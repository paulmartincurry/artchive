Isaki and Kasago Fish, from the series Uozukushi (Every Variety of Fish)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1830s · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 26 × 37.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hiroshige came from a samurai family in Edo; his grandfather taught archery. Besides the Utagawa style he studied the methods of the Kanō school, of nanga and of the Shijō school, and likely the linear perspective of Western art.
