The Demon Seated

Mikhail Vrubel · 1890 · Oil on canvas · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Vrubel moved to Moscow in 1889 and made this there as his first large canvas, more than two metres wide. The faceted surface was laid on in plain strokes with a painting knife. The subject is the Demon of Mikhail Lermontov's poem, and the painting was harshly criticized. He returned to the theme twice, in 1899 and in 1901–1902.
