Gazette du Bon Ton, 1923, No. 5, Pl. 19: Mme Ida Rubinstein dans "La Dame aux Camélias"

George Barbier · 1923 · 24.3 × 18.8 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The Gazette du Bon Ton was founded by Lucien Vogel and ran from 1912 to 1925, sold only to subscribers at 100 francs a year. Each issue carried ten full-page fashion plates printed in pochoir. This one shows the Russian actress and dancer Ida Rubinstein on stage in La Dame aux Camélias, in a black gown by Worth, one of the seven couture houses under contract to the magazine. George Barbier was about forty.
