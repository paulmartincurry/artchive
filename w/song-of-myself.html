Do I contradict myself? Very well then I contradict myself,
(I am large, I contain multitudes.)

Song of Myself (Sections 51–52)

Walt Whitman · 1855

In the first edition of Leaves of Grass, in 1855, this was the first of twelve untitled poems and had no sections. Walt Whitman published that edition at his own expense. He divided the poem into fifty-two numbered sections for the fourth edition, in 1867, and it took its present title only in the last, of 1891–2. In 1882 Boston's district attorney threatened action against the book under the state's obscenity laws and demanded changes to several passages of this poem.
