Aspidium filix mas (Urformen der Kunst, plate 56)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · image 26 × 19 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blossfeldt began his career as a sculptor. In 1926 the gallerist Karl Nierendorf showed his plant photographs paired with African sculptures, and two years later helped him publish them as Urformen der Kunst, in which this is plate 56. The gravure image is 26 by 19 centimetres.
