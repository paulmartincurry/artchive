Fruit (or Pomegranate)

William Morris · c. 1866 · Block-printed in distemper colours · 68.7 × 54.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Fruit, also called Pomegranate, was one of the three floral papers Morris made in the mid-1860s, each issued in several colourways. The number of colours and the handwork made them expensive. A block was twenty-one inches wide, cut away to leave one colour's part of the design, with fine lines made by brass wire driven edgewise into the wood.
