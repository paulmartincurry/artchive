Carnival of Souls, frame at 13:16

Herk Harvey · 1962 · Film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The face of the Man at the car window was made with an angled mirror placed on the far side of the glass. The Man is played by Herk Harvey, who also produced and directed; his day job was making industrial and educational films for the Centron Corporation in Lawrence, Kansas. With no money for a rear-projection screen, he filmed inside moving cars with a battery-powered handheld Arriflex, a camera then used mostly for newsreels.
