The Passion of Joan of Arc, frame at 5:47

Carl Theodor Dreyer · 1928 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Renée Falconetti was a stage actress, and this was her second and last film role. Dreyer first saw her backstage at a comedy, La Garçonne, and screen-tested her without make-up. No one in the cast wore make-up, which the new panchromatic film stock made possible. His script, drawn from the trial transcripts after more than a year and a half of research, condensed 29 interrogations held over 18 months into a single day.
