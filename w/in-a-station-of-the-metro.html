The apparition of these faces in the crowd;
Petals on a wet, black bough.

In a Station of the Metro

Ezra Pound · 1913

The poem records a moment at the Concorde station of the Paris Métro in 1912. Pound cut it down from thirty lines to fourteen words, none of them a verb. It was printed in the magazine Poetry in April 1913, when he was twenty-seven, with spacing between the groups of words that differs from later printings.
