Design for a Clock

Lefebvre Manufactory · 1800–1825 · Pen and gray ink, brush and yellow, green and brown washes on off-white laid paper · 51.9 × 42.9 cm (20 7/16 × 16 7/8in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The design is for a gilt table clock, drawn on a sheet 51.9 by 42.9 centimetres. A wrecked sailing ship lies at the foot of the clock, and its base carries a relief of Neptune with a trident, riding the sea with a Triton. Eleanor and Sarah Hewitt gave the drawing to the museum.
