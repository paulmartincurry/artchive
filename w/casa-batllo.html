Casa Batlló

Antoni Gaudí · 1904–06 · Stone, ceramic, metal and wood

Free to use with credit. Photo: Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 3.0

The house was built in 1877 and bought in 1903 by Josep Batlló, a textile industrialist. Antoni Gaudí redesigned it in 1904, and the work was finished in 1906. Much of the façade is covered in a mosaic of broken ceramic tiles. The arched roof has been likened to the back of a dragon.
