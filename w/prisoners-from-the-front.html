Prisoners from the Front

Winslow Homer · 1866 · Oil on canvas · 61 × 96.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Winslow Homer had worked as a war correspondent. The Union officer is identified as Brigadier General Francis Channing Barlow; the Confederate soldiers are not named. Infrared photography and studies indicate that the painting changed many times before it was finished. It was on show at the Exposition Universelle in Paris while Homer was there in 1867.
