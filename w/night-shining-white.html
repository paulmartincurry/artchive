Night-Shining White (Zhàoyèbái)

Han Gan · ca. 750 · Ink on paper · The Met

Free to use

Han Gan came from a poor family and was working in a wine shop in the capital, Chang'an, when the painter and poet Wang Wei noticed his talent. He became a court artist, and the emperor Xuanzong ordered from him a series of portraits of his horses. The drawing is unsigned and about thirty centimetres square. Later owners added so many seals and inscriptions that it was mounted on a handscroll twenty feet long to hold them.
