Anywhere Out of the World

Charles Baudelaire · 1867

Translated by Arthur Symons

The collection this belongs to holds 50 short prose poems, and Baudelaire's friends published it in 1869, two years after he died. He spent the years 1857 to 1867 working on it. He mentioned that he had read Aloysius Bertrand's Gaspard de la nuit at least twenty times before starting.
