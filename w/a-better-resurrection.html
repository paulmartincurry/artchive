The Artchive
I have no wit, no words, no tears;
My heart within me like a stone
Is numbed too much for hopes or fears.
Look right, look left, I dwell alone;

A Better Resurrection

Christina Rossetti · 1862

In her late teens Rossetti became engaged to the painter James Collinson, a founding member of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood, and she ended the engagement in 1850 when he reverted to Catholicism. From 1859 to 1870 she worked as a volunteer at the London Diocesan Penitentiary in Highgate, a refuge for fallen women.
