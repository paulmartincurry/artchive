The Trojan Women Setting Fire to Their Fleet

Claude Lorrain · c. 1643 · Oil on canvas · 105.1 × 152.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Claude was in his late thirties when he painted this canvas, by then settled in Rome near the Spanish Steps, where he lived for the rest of his life. Four paintings for Pope Urban VIII in 1635–1638 had secured his reputation. From about 1636 he kept the Liber Veritatis, a book of pen and wash drawings recording nearly all his pictures as they were finished.
