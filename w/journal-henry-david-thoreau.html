"What are you doing now?" he asked. "Do you keep a journal?" So I make my first entry to-day.

Journal (first entry, 22 October 1837)

Henry David Thoreau · 1837

Thoreau started the journal at Ralph Waldo Emerson's suggestion and made this first entry on 22 October 1837. He was twenty and had graduated from Harvard that year. He had taken a post at the Concord public school and resigned after a few weeks rather than administer corporal punishment. His first essay in The Dial, in July 1840, was made of revised passages from the journal.
