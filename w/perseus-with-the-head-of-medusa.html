Perseus with the Head of Medusa

Antonio Canova · 1804–6 · Marble · Overall: H. 95 1/2 × W. 75 1/2 × D. 40 1/2 in. · The Met

Free to use

The marble stands 242.6 centimetres high. Canova was in his late forties when he made it, in the same years he was carving Napoleon as Mars the Peacemaker, begun in 1803 and finished in 1806. His workshop turned plaster models into marble versions, and he spread his reputation by publishing engravings of what he made. Since 1802 he had also held the papal post of Inspector-General of Antiquities. The Met bought the statue in 1967.
