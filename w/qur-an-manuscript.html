Qur'an Manuscript (Ottoman Turkey, 1851–52)

Maker unknown · dated 1268 AH/1851–52 CE · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper; leather binding · H. 6 1/4 in. (15.9 cm), W. 4 1/4 in. (10.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The whole Qur'an fits in a book 15.9 centimetres tall and less than 11 wide, written in ink with opaque watercolour and gold and bound in leather. It was copied in Ottoman Turkey in the year 1268 of the Islamic calendar. Elizabeth M. Riley gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1980.
