Pain and suffering are always inevitable
for a large intelligence and a deep heart.

Crime and Punishment (Part III, Ch. 5)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1866

Deep in debt and supporting the family of his dead brother Mikhail, Dostoevsky asked the publisher Mikhail Katkov for an advance on what he then thought of as a story. An early draft was written as a diary; in November 1865 he recast it in the third person and began again from scratch. It ran in The Russian Messenger in twelve monthly installments during 1866, while he was also under contract to finish The Gambler. The stenographer who helped him, Anna Snitkina, later became his wife.
