A Knockout

George Bellows · 1921 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This lithograph of 1921 is based on a drawing Bellows had made nearly fifteen years earlier, when boxing in New York was an underground sport of tavern back rooms. For reasons unknown he printed only a few impressions. He had installed a lithography press in his studio in 1916.
