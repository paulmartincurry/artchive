Jacques and Berthe Lipchitz

Amedeo Modigliani · 1916 · Oil on canvas · 81.3 × 54.3 cm (32 × 21 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Jacques Lipchitz ordered this portrait when he married the Russian poet Berthe Kitrosser, as a way of putting money in the hands of his friend. By Lipchitz's account Modigliani painted it in two days, and Lipchitz talked him into working on it for two more weeks so that he could pay him more. Lipchitz traded it to the dealer Léonce Rosenberg around 1921. Frederick and Helen Birch Bartlett bought it in 1922, and it came to the Art Institute after her death.
