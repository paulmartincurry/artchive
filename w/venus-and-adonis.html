Venus and Adonis

Titian · 1550s · Oil on canvas · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Titian's birth date is uncertain. Most scholars now put it between 1488 and 1490, which would make him sixty or older when he painted this canvas. In old age he told Philip II of Spain in a letter that he had been born in 1474, and an age of ninety-nine at his death in 1576 was still accepted into the twentieth century. The painting is 106.7 by 133.4 centimetres and came to the Met in 1949 with the Jules Bache Collection.
