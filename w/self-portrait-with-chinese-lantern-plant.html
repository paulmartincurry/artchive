Self-Portrait with Chinese Lantern Plant

Egon Schiele · 1912 · Oil on panel · Leopold Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Schiele was twenty-one or twenty-two the year he painted this. In April 1912 he was arrested at his studio in Neulengbach, thirty-five kilometres west of Vienna, and police took more than a hundred of his drawings. The kidnapping charge was dropped, but he was found guilty of showing erotic drawings where children could see them. With twenty-one days already spent in custody, he was given three more, and in his cell he made a series of paintings of it.
