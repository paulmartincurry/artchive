Kalevala (Runo XL)

Elias Lönnrot (Finnish-Karelian Oral Tradition) · 1835 / 1849

Translated by Keith Bosley, 1989 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Lönnrot was a physician, the district health officer at Kajaani, and searched for poems in his spare time, making eleven field trips over fifteen years. On the second he was called back to Helsinki to treat cholera victims. On the fifth a singer named Arhippa Perttunen gave him some 4,000 verses in two days of continuous recitation. The first version, published in 1835, had 12,078 verses, and the 1849 version has 22,795.
