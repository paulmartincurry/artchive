Cetus (Uranometria)

Johann Bayer · 1603 · Engraving · Linda Hall Library, Kansas City

Free to use

Johann Bayer was a lawyer in Augsburg, and he was about thirty-one when his atlas was published there in 1603; he later became legal adviser to the city council. It was the first atlas to cover the whole sky, in 51 charts engraved on copper by Alexander Mair. Bayer labelled the stars of each constellation with Greek and Latin letters, a system astronomers still use.
