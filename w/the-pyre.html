The Pyre (The Miseries and Misfortunes of War, plate 13)

Jacques Callot · 1633 · Etching; first state of three (Lieure) · sheet: 9.2 x 19.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This impression is an early one, the first of three states. Callot died in Nancy in 1635, two years after the series came out, having made more than 1,400 etchings. Francisco de Goya owned a set of these prints, which are believed to have influenced his own Disasters of War almost two centuries later.
