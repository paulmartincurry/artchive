On errands of life, these letters speed to death.

Ah Bartleby! Ah humanity!

Bartleby, the Scrivener — Dead letters (ending)

Herman Melville · 1853

Melville may have written the story partly in response to the bad reviews of his previous novel, Pierre. It first ran anonymously as Bartleby, the Scrivener: A Story of Wall-Street. In May 1856 he collected it in The Piazza Tales, published by Dix and Edwards, and the book came out in Britain the next month. It was no great success at the time.
