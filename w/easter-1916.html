Too long a sacrifice
Can make a stone of the heart.

Easter, 1916 (closing section)

W. B. Yeats · 1916

The Rising began on Easter Monday, 24 April 1916, and sixteen of its leaders were executed. Yeats wrote the poem between May and September that year and had 25 copies printed privately. It was not published until 1921. The count of lines carries the date: sixteen in the first and third stanzas, twenty-four in the second and fourth, and four stanzas for the fourth month. Yeats disliked green as a political symbol so much that he forbade it for the bindings of his books.
