It was during the time I wandered about and starved in Christiania: Christiania, this singular city, from which no man departs without carrying away the traces of his sojourn there.

Hunger — It was during the time I wandered about and starved (Part I, opening)

Knut Hamsun · 1890

Translated by George Egerton

An extract from the novel was first published anonymously in a Danish magazine in November 1888, and the book followed in 1890. It is loosely based on Hamsun's own poverty before that year. George Egerton made the first English translation in 1899.
