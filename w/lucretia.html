Lucretia

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1664 · Oil on canvas · 120 × 101 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The canvas is signed and dated 1664 at the centre left. In 2015 Ernst van de Wetering of the Rembrandt Research Project said he was convinced that it could not be by Rembrandt's own hand, and the project left it out of its catalogue of that year; the National Gallery of Art lists it as Rembrandt. In 1828 the painter Sir Thomas Lawrence bought it at a London sale on behalf of the collector Hugh Munro.
