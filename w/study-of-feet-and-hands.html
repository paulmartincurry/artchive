Study of Feet and Hands

Théodore Géricault · 1818–19 · Oil on canvas · 52 × 64 cm · Musée Fabre, Montpellier

Free to use

Painted studies of severed heads and limbs such as this one have been ascribed to Géricault. It is dated to 1818 or 1819, the years of The Raft of the Medusa. He died in Paris in 1824, aged thirty-two, weakened by riding accidents and a chronic tubercular infection.
