Job 3:2–17

Book of Job (King James Version) · 540–330 BCE; KJV 1611

The book's Hebrew, post-Babylonian and touched by Aramaic, points to its composition in the Persian period, between 540 and 330 BCE. The King James Version it is given in here was published in 1611.
