Our widows weep, and our virgins mourn. Woe be unto us! Woe be unto us! Our old men wail and our young men lament. Woe be unto us! Woe be unto us! Our women shed tears and our city is laid waste.

Kebra Nagast — How Solomon Lamented for Zion

Neburä Id Yishaq of Aksum · 14th century

Translated by E. A. Wallis Budge, 1922

The Kebra Nagast traces the Solomonic dynasty, which ruled Ethiopia until 1974, back to Solomon and the Queen of Sheba through their son Menelik I. It tells how the Ark of the Covenant came to Ethiopia with Menelik; in this chapter Solomon laments its going. Many Ethiopian Christians still regard the book as historically reliable.
