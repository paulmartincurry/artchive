Pale Death, impartial, walks his round; he knocks at cottage-gate
And palace-portal. Sestius, child of bliss!
How should a mortal's hopes be long, when short his being's date?

Odes I.4 (Pale Death, Impartial)

Horace · 23 BCE

Translated by John Conington

The first three books of the Odes were published in 23 BC, when Horace was in his early forties. He took the short lyric poems of the Greeks as his models, Pindar, Sappho and Alcaeus among them. One scholar argues that the first two books probably came out singly before all three were issued together.
