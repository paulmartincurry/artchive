Head of a Skeleton with a Burning Cigarette

Vincent van Gogh · 1885–1886 · Oil on canvas · Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam

Free to use

This small canvas, 32 by 24.5 centimetres, is undated. Vincent van Gogh most likely painted it in the winter of 1885–86, while he was attending classes at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts in Antwerp, where the study of skeletons was part of learning anatomy. He later said the classes were boring and taught him nothing. He was a keen smoker himself. The painting belonged to his brother Theo and passed down through the family until 1962.
