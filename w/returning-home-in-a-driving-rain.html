Returning Home in a Driving Rain

Xia Gui · early 13th century · Fan mounted as an album leaf, ink and color on silk · 25.6 × 26.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Painted on a fan and later mounted flat, the leaf measures about 26 centimetres across. No record of Xia Gui's birth, family or training survives; he served in the Imperial Painting Academy in Hangzhou under Emperor Ningzong. His standing fell after his death along with the rest of the Southern Song academy, but Sesshū, who visited China in the fifteenth century, carried his manner to Japan.
