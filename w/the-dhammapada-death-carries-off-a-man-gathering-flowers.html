The Dhammapada · ca. 3rd century BCE; English 1881

Translated by F. Max Müller

The collection was also put into Chinese more than once. The Faju jing was translated and compiled by Weizhinan in 224 CE, the Chuyao jing by Zhu Fonian in 383, and the last of the four Chinese versions by Tianxizai between 980 and 999. One of them appears to be an expanded translation of the Pāli text.
