I, Lalla, took that teaching to heart.
From that day, I've danced naked.

Vakh (94)

Lal Ded (Lalleshwari) · 14th century

Translated by Ranjit Hoskote · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Lal Ded's verses were carried by voice for some five and a half centuries before anyone wrote them down. In 1914 George Grierson, of the Linguistic Survey of India, found no written copy to be had and commissioned one, which was taken down from the recitation of Dharma-dasa Darwesh, a storyteller living in Gush, Kashmir. She is believed to have been married at twelve and to have left home in her mid-twenties to follow a Shaivite teacher, travelling alone on foot and living on alms. 285 poems are attributed to her.
