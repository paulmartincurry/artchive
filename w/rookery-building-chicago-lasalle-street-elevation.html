Rookery Building, Chicago, LaSalle Street Elevation

Burnham and Root · 1885/87 · Multicolored hectograph print on heavy paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is a hectograph print of the architects' drawing for the LaSalle Street front, on a sheet 63 by 73 centimetres. It was given to the museum by Daniel H. Burnham, Jr.
