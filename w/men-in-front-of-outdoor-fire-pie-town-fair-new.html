Men in Front of Outdoor Fire, Pie Town Fair, New Mexico

Russell Lee · October 1940 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Lee photographed the homesteading community of Pie Town, New Mexico, in 1940 on Kodachrome colour film, and this slide is from the town fair that October. He had trained as a chemical engineer and worked as a chemist before resigning to become a painter. He first used photography as reference for his paintings.
