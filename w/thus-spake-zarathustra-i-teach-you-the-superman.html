Lo, I teach you the Superman!

The Superman is the meaning of the earth. Let your will say: The Superman shall BE the meaning of the earth!

Thus Spake Zarathustra — I Teach You the Superman (Prologue, 3)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883; English 1909

Translated by Thomas Common

Nietzsche said the central idea of the book first came to him in August 1881, beside a block of stone in the woods along Lake Silvaplana in the Upper Engadine, where he made a small note. A year and a half later he was living in Rapallo.
