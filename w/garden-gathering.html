Garden Gathering (Tile Panel)

Maker unknown · 1640–50 · Stonepaste; painted and polychrome glazed (cuerda seca technique) · Panel with tabs: H. 41 in., W. 74 in., D. 2 1/2 in. · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Each tile in the panel is 22.5 centimetres square, glazed in several colours by the cuerda seca method, and the assembled panel is nearly two metres wide and weighs 400 pounds. It was probably made in Isfahan. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1903.
