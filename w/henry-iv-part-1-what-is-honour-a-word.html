What is honour? A word. What is in that word, “honour”?

Henry IV, Part 1 — What is honour? A word. (Act V, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1597

The play was probably written in the mid-1590s and was first published in quarto in 1598. It was among Shakespeare's most popular works in his own time. The title page of the first publication advertised Falstaff and Henry Percy and did not mention Prince Hal.
