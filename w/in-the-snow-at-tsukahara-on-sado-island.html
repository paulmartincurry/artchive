In the Snow at Tsukahara on Sado Island (Concise Illustrated Biography of the Great Priest)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1830–35 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 23.7 × 37.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The print comes from a set on the life of the priest Nichiren, which the Art Institute of Chicago dates to about 1830 to 1835. Nichiren was exiled to Sado Island, in the Sea of Japan, from 1271 to 1274. In the first winter he and a few disciples endured terrible cold, food deprivation and threats from local inhabitants.
