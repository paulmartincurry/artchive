Father Duffy, Times Square

Berenice Abbott · 14 April 1937 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott made this photograph on 14 April 1937. The statue honours Francis P. Duffy, a military chaplain and the most highly decorated cleric in the history of the U.S. Army, who died in 1932. He had been pastor of Holy Cross Church in Hell's Kitchen, a block from Times Square. The back of the print carries the Federal Art Project's Changing New York stamps.
