Winter Trees Reflected in a Pond

William Henry Fox Talbot · 1841–42 · salted paper print from calotype negative · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The pond is at Lacock Abbey in Wiltshire, the thirteenth-century house that was the Talbot family seat. Talbot announced his calotype process in 1841; it cut the exposure in the camera from an hour or two to a minute or two in bright sun. The print stayed with the family until the 1930s, when it went to the Science Museum in London. Cleveland bought it in 2006.
