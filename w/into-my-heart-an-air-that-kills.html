Into my heart an air that kills
From yon far country blows:
What are those blue remembered hills,
What spires, what farms are those?

That is the land of lost content,
I see it shining plain,
The happy highways where I went
And cannot come again.

Into my heart an air that kills (A Shropshire Lad, XL)

A. E. Housman · 1896

Most of the poems in A Shropshire Lad were written in 1895, while Housman was living at Byron Cottage in Highgate. The book came out the following year, partly at his own expense, after one publisher had rejected it. The first printing of 500 copies did not clear until 1898. He declined royalties at first to keep the price down.
