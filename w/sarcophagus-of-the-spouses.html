Sarcophagus of the Spouses

Maker unknown · 530–510 BCE · Terracotta · 1.14 × 1.9 m · National Etruscan Museum of Villa Giulia, Rome

Free to use with credit. Photo: Sailko, CC BY-SA 4.0

The sarcophagus was found in the Banditaccia necropolis at Caere. It was modelled in terracotta in four separate pieces, which were fired separately and then put together, and the lid was once brightly painted. A similar sarcophagus in the Louvre goes by the same name and was very possibly made in the same workshop.
