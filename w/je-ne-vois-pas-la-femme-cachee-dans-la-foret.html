Je ne vois pas la [femme] cachée dans la forêt (La Révolution surréaliste, No. 12)

René Magritte · published 15 December 1929 · Printed magazine page (letterpress and halftone)

Free to use

Sixteen surrealists, Magritte among them, were photographed with their eyes closed and set around his painting on this page of La Révolution surréaliste, published in Paris on 15 December 1929. The same issue printed André Breton's Second Manifesto of Surrealism and the scenario of Un Chien andalou. Twelve issues of the magazine appeared between 1924 and 1929, and this was the last.
