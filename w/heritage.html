What is Africa to me:
Copper sun or scarlet sea,
Jungle star or jungle track,
Strong bronzed men, or regal black
Women from whose loins I sprang
When the birds of Eden sang?

Heritage (opening)

Countee Cullen · 1925

Heritage first appeared in The Survey on March 1, 1925, before Cullen put it in Color. Africa was not a place Cullen knew at first hand. As an undergraduate he came second in the Witter Bynner competition for undergraduate poetry in 1923 and again the next year, and won first prize in 1925.
