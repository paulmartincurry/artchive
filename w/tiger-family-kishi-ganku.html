Tiger Family

Kishi Ganku · early 1800s · Pair of six-panel folding screens; ink and color on paper · 179.4 x 384.8 cm (70 5/8 x 151 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kishi Ganku taught himself to paint and by his thirties was sought after by Kyoto patrons, including the imperial family. Tigers were his best-loved subject.
