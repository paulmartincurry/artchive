Kosode (Robe), Japan, 19th Century

Maker unknown · late Edo period (1789–1868)/ Meiji period (1868–1912), 19th century · Silk and gold-leaf-over-lacquered-paper-strip-wrapped cotton, warp-float faced 4:1 satin weave with supplementary patterning wefts bound in weft-float faced 1:4 'Z' twill interlacings; lined with silk, plain weave, creped wefts, dyed with safflower · Art Institute of Chicago

The gold in this robe is gold leaf laid over lacquered paper, cut into strips and wrapped around cotton thread, then woven into the satin as a pattern. The lining is silk dyed with safflower. The robe measures 180.5 by 125.8 centimetres and was given to the museum by Mrs. Robert D. Graff.
