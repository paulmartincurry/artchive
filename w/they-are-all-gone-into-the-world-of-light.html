They are all gone into the world of light!
And I alone sit ling’ring here;
Their very memory is fair and bright,
And my sad thoughts doth clear.

They are all gone into the world of light

Henry Vaughan · 1655

Vaughan was a Welsh poet who wrote in English. In the 1650s he began a medical practice that he kept for the rest of his life.
