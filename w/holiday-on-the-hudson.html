Holiday on the Hudson

George Luks · c. 1912 · Oil on canvas · 76.2 × 91.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Cleveland Museum of Art bought this painting four months before George Luks was beaten to death after an argument in a speakeasy. He often boasted of having been an amateur boxing champion, which later proved a tall tale. In his teens he had played the vaudeville circuit with his younger brother.
