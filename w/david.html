David

Michelangelo Buonarroti · 1501–1504 · Marble · Galleria dell'Accademia, Florence

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jörg Bittner Unna, CC BY 3.0

The block had been quarried at Carrara in the 1460s for another sculptor, who roughed out a figure and stopped; it then lay in the cathedral yard, open to the weather, for twenty-five years. Michelangelo was twenty-six when he won the contract in August 1501, at six florins a month. He made his own steel chisels. The statue, 5.17 metres high and about 8.5 tons, proved too heavy to raise to the cathedral roof it was meant for, and in 1504 more than forty men rolled it on fourteen greased logs to the Piazza della Signoria, which took four days.
