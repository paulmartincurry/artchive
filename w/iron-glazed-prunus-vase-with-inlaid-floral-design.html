Iron-glazed Prunus Vase with Inlaid Floral Design

Maker unknown · 1100s–1200s · Iron-black ware · Height 29.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The vase is iron-black ware, not celadon: three ginseng plants, leaves and roots, are inlaid in white slip against the dark ground. Ginseng is a medicinal herb, and Goryeo prunus vases were once used to store wine. It stands 29.7 centimetres high and was bought for Cleveland in 1961 from the J. H. Wade Fund.
