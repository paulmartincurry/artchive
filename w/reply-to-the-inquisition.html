You may be more afraid to bring that sentence against me than I am to accept it.

Reply to the Inquisition

Giordano Bruno · February 8, 1600

Translated by Ingrid D. Rowland, 2008 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

On 8 February 1600 Bruno was stripped of the priesthood he had held since 1572. Nine days later he was burned at the stake in the Campo de' Fiori in Rome. He had fled his Dominican monastery in Naples in 1576, when an indictment was being prepared against him; a banned book by Erasmus, with his notes in it, had been found hidden in the monastery latrine. He then taught and published in Geneva, Toulouse, Paris, London and Wittenberg before an invitation from a Venetian patrician drew him back to Italy.
