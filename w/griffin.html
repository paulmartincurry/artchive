Griffin

Martin Schongauer · 1470–91 · Engraving · Sheet: 10.2 × 9.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Schongauer's father, Caspar, was a goldsmith in Colmar and presumably taught his son to engrave. One hundred and sixteen engravings are recognised as his. All carry his monogram, and none is dated. This one is small, a sheet 10.2 by 9.4 centimetres.
