The Sea Monster

Albrecht Dürer · c. 1498 · Engraving · Sheet: 14.9 × 18.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Albrecht Dürer engraved this plate around 1498, the year his sixteen designs for the Apocalypse are dated. He was about twenty-seven and had made his first journey to Venice a few years before. The sheet measures 14.9 by 18.8 centimetres.
