Oedipus Cursing His Son Polynices

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1786 · Oil on canvas · 149.8 × 165.4 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Fuseli sold this canvas in October 1791 to William Roscoe, and it was auctioned in Liverpool on 28 September 1816. A study published in 1959–60 gave its whereabouts as unknown since that sale. It was with London and Zurich dealers in 1973, Paul Mellon bought it in 1974, and he gave it to the National Gallery of Art in 1983.
