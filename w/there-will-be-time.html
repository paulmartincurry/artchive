The Artchive
For I have known them all already, known them all:
Have known the evenings, mornings, afternoons,
I have measured out my life with coffee spoons;

There Will Be Time (The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock)

T. S. Eliot · 1915

Eliot began writing the poem in February 1910. It was first published in the June 1915 issue of Poetry: A Magazine of Verse at the instigation of Ezra Pound, the magazine's overseas editor.
