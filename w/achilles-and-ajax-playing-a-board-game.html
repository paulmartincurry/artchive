Achilles and Ajax Playing a Board Game (Vatican Amphora 344)

Exekias · c. 530 BC · Black-figure pottery · H. 61.1 cm · Vatican Museums, Vatican City

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jakob Bådagård, CC BY-SA 3.0

Exekias signed fourteen surviving vessels, twelve of them only as potter. This is one of two amphorae on which the inscription says he both made and painted the vase. Achilles and Ajax are named beside their figures, and the words written next to them have Achilles throwing a four and Ajax a three. The vase is 61.1 centimetres high.
