The tree is happy because it is scarcely sentient;
the hard rock is happier still, it feels nothing:

Lo fatal

Rubén Darío · 1905

Translated by Lysander Kemp, 1965 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Darío was thirty-eight in 1905. He had been in print since he was thirteen, when a daily newspaper published an elegy of his, and he worked as a journalist and a diplomat as well as a poet. He died in León, Nicaragua, in 1916, aged forty-nine.
