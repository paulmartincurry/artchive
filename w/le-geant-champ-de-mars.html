Le Géant, Champ de Mars

Nadar · October 18, 1863, probably printed 1880/89 · Gelatin silver printing out paper print · Image/paper: 17.1 × 16.5 cm (6 3/4 × 6 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is the second launch of the balloon Géant, two weeks after its first flight on 4 October 1863. The balloon was made with 20,000 metres of silk and had a two-storey basket, and a crowd of 200,000 came to watch. The flight ended in a crash in Hanover that left Nadar with a fractured leg. He had commissioned the balloon that year from the balloonist Eugène Godard.
