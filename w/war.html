Arnold Böcklin · 1896 · Oil on lime panel · 100 × 69.5 cm · Galerie Neue Meister, Dresden

Free to use

Böcklin left Zürich in 1892 and lived afterwards at San Domenico, near Florence, and this painting comes from that period. He put his monogram and the date 1896 at the lower left. He died at Fiesole on 16 January 1901, aged seventy-three.
