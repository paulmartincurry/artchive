Lascaux Cave Paintings

Maker unknown · ca. 17,000 years ago · Mineral pigments on limestone

An eighteen-year-old, Marcel Ravidat, found the entrance on 12 September 1940 when his dog, Robot, nosed into a hole left by an uprooted tree. The colours are mineral pigments, swabbed or blotted on, or sprayed by blowing them through a tube, and the paintings on the ceiling needed scaffolding. One bull is 5.2 metres long. The cave opened to the public in 1948; by 1955 the breath and heat of 1,200 visitors a day had visibly damaged the paintings, and it has been closed since 1963.
