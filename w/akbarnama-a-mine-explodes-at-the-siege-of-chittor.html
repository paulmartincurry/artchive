Akbarnama: A Mine Explodes at the Siege of Chittor

Miskina and Sarwan · ca. 1590–1595 · Gouache and gold on paper · Victoria and Albert Museum

In the royal studio an experienced master laid out each composition and a mostly younger painter filled in the colour. Their names were often written below the pictures, because painters were sometimes paid by the piece. Notes left on fifteen pages of this manuscript show a single picture taking between 42 and 68 days. The explosion shown happened on 17 December 1567. The widow of Major General Clarke, who found the volume in India, sold it to the museum in 1896, and it was catalogued as a different book until 1905.
