Great Blue Heron (The Birds of America, Plate 211)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1834 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 101 × 68.5 cm (39 3/4 × 26 15/16 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The watercolour this plate was engraved from is at the New-York Historical Society, which holds all 435 of Audubon's preparatory watercolours for the book. His widow, Lucy Audubon, sold them to the society after his death.
