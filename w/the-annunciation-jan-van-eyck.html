The Annunciation

Jan van Eyck · c. 1434/1436 · Oil on canvas transferred from panel · 90.2 × 34.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Van Eyck painted this on a panel; it has since been transferred to canvas. It is thought to be the left wing of a triptych whose other wings have not been seen since before 1817. Examination after a cleaning in 1998 showed that the vase of lilies was added late, that gold leaf is used only for the seven rays from the window, and that in a shadow behind the stool he worked a glaze with his fingers.
