Boating

Édouard Manet · 1874 · Oil on canvas · 38 1/4 × 51 1/4 in. (97.2 × 130.2 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Manet painted this in the summer of 1874 while staying on his family's property at Gennevilliers. X-rays show he worked on it many times, outdoors on the boat and in the studio, and changed the man's pose again and again. The man has often been identified as his brother-in-law Rodolphe Leenhoff. It was first shown five years later, at the Salon of 1879, and afterwards was sold to the banker Victor Desfosses.
