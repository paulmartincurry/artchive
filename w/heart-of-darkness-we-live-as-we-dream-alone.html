Heart of Darkness — We live, as we dream, alone (Part I)

Joseph Conrad · 1899

Blackwood's Magazine ran the story in three parts in February, March and April 1899, and the February number was the magazine's thousandth issue. Conrad put its length at 30,000 words. It did not appear in a book until 13 November 1902, in a volume with two other stories, and the critics said very little about it. Marlow, who tells it, had first appeared in the story Youth.
