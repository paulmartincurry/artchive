The moon shines in my body, but my blind eyes cannot see it:
The moon is within me, and so is the sun.

The Moon Shines in My Body

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

Kabir is believed to have been a disciple of the poet-saint Ramananda in Varanasi, whose teaching was that God was inside every person and everything. According to the historian Irfan Habib, the earliest known texts with biographical information about him are two manuscripts of the Persian Dabestan-e Mazaheb. He died at Maghar.
