Ulysses and the Sirens

John William Waterhouse · 1891 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne

Free to use

Waterhouse took the scene from the Odyssey. The crew have blocked their ears against the Sirens' song, and Odysseus has had himself tied to the mast so he can hear it without wrecking the ship.
