How was it I did not see that lofty sky before? And how happy I am to have found it at last!

War and Peace — The Sky at Austerlitz

Leo Tolstoy · 1869

Translated by Louise and Aylmer Maude

Tolstoy began War and Peace in 1863, and two years later a periodical, The Russian Messenger, printed the opening under the title 1805. He was unhappy with that version and rewrote the whole novel between 1866 and 1869. His wife, Sophia, copied out as many as seven complete manuscripts before he let it go, and the first edition ran to 1,225 pages. He knew battle at first hand: he had been an artillery officer in Sevastopol during its eleven-month siege.
