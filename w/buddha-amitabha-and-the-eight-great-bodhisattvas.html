Buddha Amitabha (Amita) and the Eight Great Bodhisattvas

Maker unknown · mid-late 14th century · Ink, color, and gold on silk · 160.3 × 86 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Of the eight bodhisattvas around Amitabha, all but one wear jewelled crowns; the exception is Kshitigarbha, who can save even those reborn in hell, in a plain hooded garment. Korean Buddhist painting reached this refinement under the patronage of the Goryeo court. The silk has turned deep brown and is worn and cracked, and the seals at lower left can no longer be read. Charles Lang Freer bought the scroll from Yamanaka and Company in 1906.
