Bridal Veil, Yosemite

Carleton Watkins · 1865–66 · albumen print from wet collodion negative · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The mount is inscribed in pencil with a height of 900 feet for the falls; the museum gives the drop as 620 feet. Watkins had known nothing about photography until 1854, when he was left minding the San Francisco studio of the daguerreotypist Robert Vance. He later lost his gallery and its negatives to a creditor, and the creditor and the photographer Isaiah Taber reprinted the pictures without credit. Watkins went back and remade the lost views as what he called his New Series.
