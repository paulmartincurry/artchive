Woman on Rose Divan

Henri Matisse · 1921 · Oil on canvas · 38 × 46 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Matisse sold this canvas to the Bernheim-Jeune gallery in Paris on 19 April 1921, the year he painted it. He had relocated to a suburb of Nice in 1917. Frederick Clay Bartlett of Chicago bought the picture in 1923 and gave it to the Art Institute in 1926. It is 38 centimetres high.
