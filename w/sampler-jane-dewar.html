Sampler

Jane Dewar · 1847 · wool embroidery on linen foundation Technique: embroidered in cross and eyelet stitches on plain weave foundation · H × W: 29.8 × 21 cm (11 3/4 × 8 1/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Jane Dewar stitched rows of alphabets in wool, then signed and dated the sampler in red and green. It is 29.8 by 21 centimetres and came to the museum in the bequest of Gertrude M. Oppenheimer.
