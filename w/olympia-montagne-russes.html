Olympia Montagne Russes

Jules Chéret · 1892 · Color lithograph · Framed: 124.8 x 90.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Montagnes Russes was a wooden roller coaster imported from England in 1888 and built in a courtyard at 28 Boulevard des Capucines in Paris. In 1892, the year of this poster, the Prefect of Police feared a fire and ordered it closed. The Olympia music hall opened on the same site in 1893.
