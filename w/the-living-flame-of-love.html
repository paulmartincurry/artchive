O Living Flame of Love,
That woundest tenderly
My soul in its inmost depth!
As thou art no longer grievous,
Perfect thy work, if it be thy will,
Break the web of this sweet encounter.

The Living Flame of Love (the stanzas)

Saint John of the Cross (San Juan de la Cruz) · 16th century

Translated by David Lewis

John of the Cross was a Spanish priest and Carmelite friar. He was born on 24 June 1542 and died on 14 December 1591, at the age of 49.
