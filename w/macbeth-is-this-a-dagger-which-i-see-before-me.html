Is this a dagger which I see before me,
the handle toward my hand?

Macbeth — Is This a Dagger Which I See Before Me (Act II, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · 1606

The only eyewitness account of Macbeth from Shakespeare's lifetime was recorded by Simon Forman, who saw it at the Globe on 20 April 1610. The play contains more musical cues than any other in the canon. Samuel Pepys recorded seeing it eight times between 1664 and 1668.
