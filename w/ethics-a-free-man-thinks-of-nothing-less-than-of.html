A free man thinks of death least of all things; and his wisdom is a meditation not of death but of life.

Ethics — A Free Man Thinks of Nothing Less than of Death (Part IV, Prop. LXVII)

Baruch Spinoza · 1677

Translated by R. H. M. Elwes

After his expulsion from the Jewish community in 1656, Spinoza supported himself by grinding lenses. He died in February 1677 at 44, and the Ethics came out after his death.
