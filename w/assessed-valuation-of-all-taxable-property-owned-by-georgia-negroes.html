Assessed Valuation of All Taxable Property Owned by Georgia Negroes (The Georgia Negro)

W. E. B. Du Bois · c. 1900 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

The Paris exhibit was put together by Daniel Murray, the Assistant Librarian of Congress, with Thomas J. Calloway and Du Bois. This chart is part of the Daniel Murray collection at the Library of Congress. It covers taxable property owned by African Americans in Georgia between 1875 and 1890, and it was shown again at Cooper Hewitt in New York in 2022 and 2023.
