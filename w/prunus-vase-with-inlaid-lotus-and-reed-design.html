Prunus Vase with Inlaid Lotus and Reed Design

Maker unknown · 1300s · Celadon ware with inlaid white and black slip decoration · Height 33 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Vases of this shape were made to store alcoholic drinks, and recent research shows they also held cooking liquids such as sesame oil. Most have lost their lids, which may once have doubled as cups. The lotus and reeds are inlaid in white and black slip in the celadon body. Langdon Warner bought the vase in Korea, and John L. Severance gave it to Cleveland in 1918.
