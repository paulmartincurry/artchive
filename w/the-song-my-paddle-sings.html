Be strong, O paddle! be brave, canoe!
The reckless waves you must plunge into.

The Song My Paddle Sings

E. Pauline Johnson (Tekahionwake) · 1892 (The White Wampum, 1895)

Johnson wrote this poem for her first show, on 19 February 1892, and made her stage debut with it. She had been signed up by Frank Yeigh after an authors' evening in Toronto that year at which she was the only woman and the only writer called back for an encore. During the 1880s she had travelled the Canadian outdoors by canoe. After her father's death in 1884 she worked to support her mother and sister, and performing became her living until she retired from the stage in August 1909.
