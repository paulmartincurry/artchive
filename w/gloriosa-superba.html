Gloriosa Superba (Climbing Lily)

Pierre-Joseph Redouté · c. 1810 · Watercolor and gilt on vellum · 38.7 × 29.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is a watercolour on vellum, with gilt, about 39 by 29 centimetres. Redouté learned to dissect flowers and record their parts exactly from the botanist Charles Louis L'Héritier de Brutelle, and took his handling of watercolour from Gerard van Spaendonck at the Jardin du Roi. He taught and painted until the day he died of a stroke, in June 1840, at eighty.
