Then the man drowsed off into what seemed to him the most comfortable and satisfying sleep he had ever known.

To Build a Fire — Then the man drowsed off (ending)

Jack London · 1908

London published two versions of this story. The first appeared in The Youth's Companion on May 29, 1902. The second, later version was published in 1908 and republished in 1910, and it is the one that is often anthologized.
