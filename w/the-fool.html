The Fool (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Pamela Colman Smith drew the seventy-eight cards of this deck in 1909, on a commission from A. E. Waite; both belonged to the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn. William Rider & Son of London published them that December in a very small first printing. This card was scanned from the much larger printing of March 1910. Smith was thirty-one.
