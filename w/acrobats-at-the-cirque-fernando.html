Acrobats at the Cirque Fernando (Francisca and Angelina Wartenberg)

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1879 · Oil on canvas · 131.2 × 99.2 cm (51 1/2 × 39 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Francisca and Angelina Wartenberg were acrobats at the Cirque Fernando in Paris, aged seventeen and fourteen. They posed in costume at Renoir's studio, in daylight, and not under the gas lighting of the circus. The oranges were thrown by audiences as a tribute. The dealer Durand-Ruel paid 2,000 francs for the picture in 1882 and sold it ten years later for 8,000 to Potter Palmer of Chicago, whose wife Bertha took it with her on her travels abroad.
