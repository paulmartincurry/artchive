The Qur'an — The Verse of Light (24:35)

The Qur'an (Surah An-Nur) · 7th century CE

Translated by M. A. S. Abdel Haleem · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

According to tradition, the first caliph, Abu Bakr, had the Quran gathered into a single volume after many of those who knew it by heart were killed in battle. Zayd ibn Thabit collected it from parchments, palm-leaf stalks, thin stones and men who had memorized it. The third caliph, Uthman, who reigned from 644 to 656, established the standard version. It has 114 chapters. Two folios of an early copy, the Birmingham Quran manuscript, are on parchment carbon-dated to about 568 to 645.
