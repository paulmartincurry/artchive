Goose Flying in Front of the Moon

Katsushika Hokusai · 1760–1849 · Ink and color on paper · 59 × 36.7 cm · National Museum of Asian Art, Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The drawing is mounted in an album, on a mount of 59 by 36.7 centimetres. Charles Lang Freer bought it from Bunshichi Kobayashi in 1904. It reached the Freer Gallery in 1920, when the building was completed and his collection was received.
