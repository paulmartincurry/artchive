I say unto you: one must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star.

Thus Spoke Zarathustra (Prologue, 5)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883

Translated by Walter Kaufmann · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

By Nietzsche's account the first part came to him on long walks around Rapallo, along the road to Zoagli and the Bay of Santa Margherita, and each part took ten days. The four parts appeared between 1883 and 1885. Of the fourth, forty copies were printed at his own expense and given to his closest friends, and he did not want it made public.
