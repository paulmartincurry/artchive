Assad Ibn Kariba Launches a Night Attack on the Camp of Malik Iraj (Hamzanama)

Attributed to Basavana, Shravana and Tara · c. 1564–69 · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on cloth; mounted on paper · Painting 68.6 × 54 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Three painters are named for this page: Basavana, Shravana and Tara. In many of Basavana's paintings he made the design while a second artist supplied the colour. He was a Yadav from Uttar Pradesh who became a court painter for Akbar, and his son Manohar Das succeeded him at court. The scene is painted on cloth, 68.6 cm tall.
