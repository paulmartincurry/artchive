The Tree of Life, Stoclet Frieze

Gustav Klimt · 1909 · Cardboard · MAK – Museum of Applied Arts

Free to use

This is a working study, not the finished frieze. Gustav Klimt made it for mosaics that cover three walls of the dining room of the Stoclet Palace in Brussels, a commission that ran from 1905 to 1911. The study is 195 centimetres high and 102 wide.
