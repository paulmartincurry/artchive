…I remember my youth and the feeling that will never come back any more—the feeling that I could last for ever, outlast the sea, the earth, and all men; the deceitful feeling that lures us on to joys, to perils, to love, to vain effort—to death…

Youth — I remember my youth

Joseph Conrad · 1898

The story was first published in Blackwood's Magazine in September 1898. Its narrator, Charles Marlow, is 42 and looks back on a voyage he made at 20 as a ship's second mate. Marlow also narrates Lord Jim, Chance and Heart of Darkness.
