The Agnew Clinic

Thomas Eakins · 1889 · Oil on canvas · University of Pennsylvania

Free to use

Three undergraduate classes at the University of Pennsylvania paid Thomas Eakins $750 for this painting, to mark Dr. Agnew's retirement from teaching. It is his largest work, 214 by 300 centimetres, and he finished it in three months. Eakins appears in it, behind the nurse, but that figure is attributed to his wife, Susan Macdowell Eakins. He carved a Latin inscription into the frame himself. The Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts refused to exhibit the picture in 1891.
