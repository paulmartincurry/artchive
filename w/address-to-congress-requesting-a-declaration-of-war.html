Yesterday, December 7, 1941—a date which will live in infamy—the United States of America was suddenly and deliberately attacked by naval and air forces of the Empire of Japan.

Address to Congress Requesting a Declaration of War (opening)

Franklin D. Roosevelt · December 8, 1941

Early in the afternoon of December 7, 1941, Roosevelt was told by telephone that the Japanese had attacked Pearl Harbor. At about 5 p.m. he dictated the request to his secretary, Grace Tully, having composed it in his head, and then marked up the typed draft. He addressed a joint session of Congress at 12:30 p.m. on December 8 and signed the declaration of war at 4 p.m.
