Stirrup-Spout Bottle with Combat Scene

Maker unknown · 200–500 CE · Ceramic · 11.4 × 9.9 × 14 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This bottle with a combat scene was made between 200 and 500 CE and is only 11.4 centimetres high. Moche pottery records war along with agriculture, metalwork and weaving, and Moche pots have been found at major sites such as Huaca de la Luna, Huaca del Sol and Sipán as well as in small villages and unrecorded burials. It came to the Met in 1979 in the bequest of Nelson A. Rockefeller.
