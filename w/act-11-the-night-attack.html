Act 11: The Night Attack (Treasury of Loyal Retainers)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; oban · 26.1 × 37.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Act 11 of the Chūshingura is the night raid. The story is drawn from the Akō incident: after their lord was sentenced to seppuku in 1701 for drawing his sword on the official Kira, some forty-six or forty-seven of his dismissed samurai broke into Kira's mansion in Edo nearly two years later and killed him. They were then sentenced to seppuku themselves. Kuniyoshi made this print around 1831 to 1832.
