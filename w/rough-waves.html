Rough Waves

Ogata Kōrin · c. 1704–9 · Two-panel folding screen; ink, color, and gold leaf on paper · Image 146.5 × 165.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kōrin moved to Edo in 1704, where lucrative commissions were more readily available. There he had the opportunity to study the ink paintings of the monk painters Sesshū and Sesson, which are seen as important influences on his work of the period, this screen among them. Ten years earlier his spending had forced him to pawn some of his possessions, and a letter of 1694 to a pawnbroker survives.
