Isenheim Altarpiece (Crucifixion Center Panel)

Matthias Grünewald · ca. 1512–1516 · Oil and tempera on limewood panel · Unterlinden Museum, Colmar

The altarpiece was painted for the Monastery of St. Anthony at Issenheim, whose monks ran a hospital and treated plague and skin diseases such as ergotism. Guy Guers commissioned it, and Nikolaus Hagenauer carved the sculptures inside. The wings were kept closed except on certain holy days, so this Crucifixion was the view the patients normally saw. Closed, it measures 3.76 by 5.34 metres. It was moved to Munich for a time during the First World War, and was cleaned and restored from 2018 to June 2022.
