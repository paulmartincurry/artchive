There's a certain slant of light,
On winter afternoons,
That oppresses, like the weight
Of cathedral tunes.

There's a certain Slant of light

Emily Dickinson · published 1890

Dickinson left this poem untitled among her unpublished manuscripts, where it was found after her death. Her first editors printed it in 1890 under the title Winter and replaced her dashes with commas. The dashes were put back in Thomas H. Johnson's edition of 1955. The manuscript is kept at Harvard's Houghton Library.
