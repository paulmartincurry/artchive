The Death of Marat

Jacques-Louis David · 1793 · Oil on canvas · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium

Jean-Paul Marat was stabbed in his bath on 13 July 1793. Jacques-Louis David, who was his friend, painted this in the months that followed, and his pupils made several copies in 1793–1794. From 1795 the painting dropped out of sight, and during David's exile in Belgium his pupil Antoine Gros hid it somewhere in France. After David's death in 1825 his family tried to sell it and failed. The Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium acquired it in 1893.
