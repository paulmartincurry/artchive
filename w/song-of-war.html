Are we watched by enemies and strangers?
Then our lightning shall strike them,
Strike and take them captive, yea, King!

Song of War (Iga'ma le 'mpi)

Zulu tradition · printed 1920

Translated by Natalie Curtis

Natalie Curtis recorded it from the singing of Madikane Cele, a Zulu student at Hampton Institute, and printed it in 1920. It was sung before a battle and at the inauguration of a king, with the war-dance. She printed a literal translation beside the Zulu and then this singing version, writing that no English could keep the rhythm of the original.
