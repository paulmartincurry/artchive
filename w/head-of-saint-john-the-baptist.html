Head of Saint John the Baptist

Spain or Northern Italy · ca. 1550–1650 · Oil on canvas · Unframed: 50 × 75.2 cm (19 11/16 × 29 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Cleveland Museum of Art bought this canvas in 1953 from Alessandro Brass in Venice as a work by Titian. The attribution was soon dropped, and after decades of research the museum has not settled on a painter; recent scholars have argued for a Spanish one. It was made for a private house, not a church. A ribbon with a Latin inscription winds round the reed cross beside the head.
