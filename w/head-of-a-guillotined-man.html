Head of a Guillotined Man

Théodore Géricault · 1818–19 · Oil on panel · 41 × 38 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The museum says Géricault likely painted this directly from the head, which probably belonged to a convicted criminal. He is known to have acquired corpses from his local morgue to study anatomy and the effects of decomposition. The panel was sold from his estate in November 1824 and later belonged to the painter Auguste Boulard. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1992.
