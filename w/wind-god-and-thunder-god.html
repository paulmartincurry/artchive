Wind God and Thunder God (after Sōtatsu)

Ogata Kōrin · ca. 1705 · Ink and color on gold-foiled paper · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

Kōrin made this pair of screens as a close replica of a seventeenth-century pair by Tawaraya Sōtatsu. A later owner, the painter Sakai Hōitsu, painted his Flowering Plants of Summer and Autumn on the back of them and also made his own version of the two gods. The two sides have since been separated to protect them. The three versions were shown together in Kyoto in 2015, for the first time in seventy-five years.
