The Kumulipo

Hawaiian creation chant · ca. 1700 Oral Canon (Trans. 1897)

Translated by Queen Liliʻuokalani, 1897

The Kumulipo runs to 2,102 lines in sixteen sections. It was made in honour of Kalaninuiamamao and handed down by word of mouth to his daughter Alapaʻiwahine. Priests recited it during the makahiki season, and Captain James Cook was greeted with it when he arrived in Kealakekua Bay in 1779. King Kalākaua had it printed for the first time in 1889, as a sixty-page pamphlet.
