Fish and Octopus (Colorful Realm of Living Beings)

Itō Jakuchū · c. 1757–65 · Color on silk · Museum of the Imperial Collections (Sannomaru Shōzōkan), Tokyo

Free to use

Jakuchū taught himself by observing and sketching what was around him: the vegetables sold at his father's shop, the fish in the Nishiki market and the chickens in his garden. This scroll is one of a set of thirty that he gave to the Kyoto temple Shōkoku-ji.
