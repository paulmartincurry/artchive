A Room of One's Own — Money and a Room (Ch. 1)

Virginia Woolf · 1929

The essay began as two papers Woolf read to student societies at Cambridge women's colleges, at Newnham on 20 October 1928 and at Girton six days later. She stayed at Newnham as the guest of the principal, Pernel Strachey, and went to Girton with Vita Sackville-West. Her own father had believed that only the boys of the family should be sent to school. The book came out on 28 September 1929.
