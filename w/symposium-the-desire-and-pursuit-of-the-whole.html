And the reason is that human nature was originally one and we were a whole, and the desire and pursuit of the whole is called love.

Symposium — The Desire and Pursuit of the Whole (Aristophanes)

Plato · after 385 BCE

Translated by Benjamin Jowett

Plato wrote the dialogue no earlier than 385 BC. A line in this speech of Aristophanes has been read, by ancient and modern scholars, as a reference to the breaking up of Mantinea by Sparta in 385 or 384 BC, an event later than the banquet the dialogue describes.
