Ineluctable modality of the visible: at least that if no more, thought through my eyes. Signatures of all things I am here to read, seaspawn and seawrack, the nearing tide, that rusty boot.

Ulysses — Ineluctable modality of the visible (Proteus)

James Joyce · 1922

The novel ran as a serial in the American journal The Little Review from 1918 until 1920, when one episode brought a prosecution for obscenity. In 1932 Random House arranged to import a copy of the French edition and have it seized by Customs, then contested the seizure, and a federal judge ruled the book was not obscene. An appeals court upheld him in 1934. While writing it in Zurich Joyce began learning Modern Greek.
