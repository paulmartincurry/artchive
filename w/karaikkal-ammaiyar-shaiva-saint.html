Karaikkal Ammaiyar, Shaiva Saint

Maker unknown · c. late 13th century · Copper alloy · 23.2 × 16.5 × 13.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Karaikkal Ammaiyar, born Punitavati, is one of only three women among the sixty-three Nayanmar saints of Shiva, and was also known as the ghoul-like one. She probably lived in the fifth century, so this figure was cast some eight hundred years after her. It is 23.2 centimetres high. The Met bought it in 1982.
