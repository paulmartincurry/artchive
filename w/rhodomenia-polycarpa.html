Rhodomenia Polycarpa

Anna Atkins · ca. 1853 · Cyanotype · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

This print dates from about the year Atkins finished Photographs of British Algae, the book of cyanotypes she had been issuing in parts since October 1843. It is counted as the first book illustrated with photographic images. Only 17 copies are known, and one sold at auction in 2004 for £229,250. The cyanotype process had been invented in 1842 by Sir John Herschel, a friend of her family.
