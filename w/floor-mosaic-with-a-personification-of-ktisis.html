Floor Mosaic with a Personification of Ktisis

Maker unknown · 500–550, with modern restoration · Marble and glass · 59 1/2 × 78 5/8 × 1 in. (151.1 × 199.7 × 2.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This fragment of a Byzantine floor is about one and a half metres tall and two metres wide, and only two and a half centimetres thick. Parts of it are modern restoration. The Metropolitan Museum acquired it in 1998, with further purchase funds credited in 1999.
