The Awakening

Henry Mayer · 20 February 1915 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

Henry Mayer drew this map as the centrefold of Puck for 20 February 1915, a special suffrage issue guest-edited by New York state suffrage groups. By the end of 1914 women had the vote on equal terms in eleven states, all in the West. A poem by Alice Duer Miller is printed below. Mayer, born in Worms in Germany, had become the magazine's chief cartoonist the year before.
