The Emperor's Carpet

Maker unknown · second half 16th century · Silk (warp and weft), wool (pile); asymmetrically knotted pile · 759.5 × 339.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The carpet is more than seven and a half metres long, its wool pile knotted on a silk foundation. The Met bought it with the Rogers Fund in 1943.
