Songs that the Hyades shall sing,
where flap the tatters of the King.

The King in Yellow — Cassilda's Song

Robert W. Chambers · 1895

Chambers trained as a painter. He studied in Paris from 1886 to 1893, showed at the Salon, and on his return to New York sold illustrations to Life, Truth and Vogue before he gave his time to writing. The King in Yellow came out in 1895, when he was about thirty. The names Carcosa, Hali and Hastur are borrowed from two short stories by Ambrose Bierce.
