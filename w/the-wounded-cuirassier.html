The Wounded Cuirassier

Théodore Géricault · 1814 · Oil on canvas · 358 × 294 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Géricault made this painting in only three weeks, after five separate sketches and drafts, for the Salon that opened at the Louvre on 5 November 1814. The critics were negative, and in his disappointment he joined the army for a time, serving in the garrison at Versailles. A smaller study for it is in the Brooklyn Museum.
