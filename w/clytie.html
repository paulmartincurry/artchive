Clytie

George Frederic Watts · 1868 · Plaster · H.: 89 cm (35 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Clytie, a water nymph, was turned into a sunflower by the gods. Watts blended male and female in the bust and left the mould lines from casting instead of smoothing them away.
