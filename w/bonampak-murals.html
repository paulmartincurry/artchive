Bonampak Murals

Maker unknown · 790

Free to use with credit. Photo: Dbannasch, CC BY-SA 4.0

The murals cover the walls of three rooms in a building 16 metres long, and show some 281 human figures. They were painted around 790 by artisans hired from the larger city of Yaxchilan, a team that would have included plasterers and pigment preparers as well as painters. About a third of the figures are named in captions, and one in ten has a caption left blank. Outsiders first saw the site in 1946, led there by Lacandon Maya who still prayed in the temples. Some later attempts at preservation did more harm than good.
