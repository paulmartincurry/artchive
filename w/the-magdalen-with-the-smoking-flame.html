The Magdalen with the Smoking Flame

Georges de La Tour · c. 1638–40 · Oil on canvas · 117 × 91.7 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

La Tour painted this composition twice. The other canvas is in the Louvre, and the two differ only in small things such as the books on the table and the shape of the flame. This one was with the La Haye family in France until 1977, when the Ahmanson Foundation bought it and gave it to the museum the same year.
