The Sombrero Galaxy (Messier 104)

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and ESA · published 2005 · Space telescope image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/STScI/AURA

Pierre Méchain discovered this galaxy on 11 May 1781. William Herschel found it again on his own in 1784 and noted a dark band across its disc, now known to be a ring of dust around the central bulge, seen from Earth almost edge-on. Using Hubble and ground telescopes, astronomers worked out that the stars near its centre could only move as fast as they do around a mass of about a billion Suns.
