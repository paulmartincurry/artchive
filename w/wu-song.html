Wu Song (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827–30 · Color woodblock print; oban · 37.5 × 25.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Before this series Kuniyoshi had published little for almost ten years, from 1818 to 1827, and at one point was reduced to selling used tatami mats. The sheet was published by Kagaya Kichiemon. It came to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1939 from the collection of Frederick W. Gookin.
