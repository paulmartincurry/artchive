Burgonet

Filippo Negroli · dated 1543 · Steel, gold, textile · The Met

Free to use

The helmet weighs 1,871 grams. Filippo Negroli specialised in repoussé, and he worked in steel rather than the iron that is easier to shape. He was in the Negroli family workshop in Milan with his younger brothers Giovan Battista and Francesco, and their father Gian Giacomo, who headed it, died in 1543, the year dated on this piece. J. Pierpont Morgan gave it to the museum in 1917.
