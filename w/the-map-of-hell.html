The Map of Hell (Divine Comedy Illustrated by Botticelli)

Sandro Botticelli · c. 1480–1490 · Silverpoint, ink and tempera on parchment · Vatican Library, Vatican City

Free to use

The sheet comes from a manuscript of Dante's Divine Comedy that Botticelli illustrated with 92 full-page pictures, of which only four are fully coloured. Each page was first drawn with a metal stylus. The drawings now in the Vatican had belonged to Queen Christina of Sweden; after her death in Rome in 1689 Pope Alexander VIII bought them.
