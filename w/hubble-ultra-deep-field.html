Hubble Ultra-Deep Field

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and ESA · 2003–2004 · Space telescope image

Free to use

The telescope was pointed at one patch of sky in the constellation Fornax for 400 orbits between September 2003 and January 2004. The picture is built from 800 exposures taken over 11.3 days and combined. The patch is about one-tenth the width of a full moon and holds an estimated 10,000 galaxies. To cover the whole sky to the same depth, Hubble would have to observe without a break for a million years.
