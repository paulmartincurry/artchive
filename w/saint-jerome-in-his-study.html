Saint Jerome in His Study

Albrecht Dürer · 1514 · Engraving · Sheet: 24.6 × 18.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Dürer engraved this copper plate in 1514, the year he turned forty-three. It is often grouped with two other engravings of 1513 and 1514, Knight, Death and the Devil and Melencolia I, as his Meisterstiche. This impression was trimmed along the plate line and measures 24.6 by 18.9 centimetres.
