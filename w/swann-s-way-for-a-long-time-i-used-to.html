For a long time I used to go to bed early. Sometimes, when I had put out my candle, my eyes would close so quickly that I had not even time to say "I'm going to sleep."

Swann's Way — For a long time I used to go to bed early (opening)

Marcel Proust · 1913

Translated by C. K. Scott Moncrieff

Proust began to shape the novel in 1909 and worked on it until his final illness in the autumn of 1922 forced him to stop. Swann's Way, published in 1913, was rejected by a number of publishers.
