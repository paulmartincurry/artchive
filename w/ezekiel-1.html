Ezekiel 1

Book of Ezekiel (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · ca. 590 BCE

The chapter was written in Hebrew and runs to 28 verses. By the book's own account Ezekiel was a priest from Jerusalem, taken into captivity by the Babylonians in 597 BCE, and its dates suggest he was 30 when he received his prophetic call by the river Chebar. Fragments of the chapter were found among the Dead Sea Scrolls.
