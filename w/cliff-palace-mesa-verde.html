Cliff Palace, Mesa Verde

Maker unknown · ca. 1190–1260 · Sandstone, mortar and wooden beams

Free to use with credit. Photo: Tobi 87, CC BY-SA

The Pueblo people farmed corn, beans and squash on the mesa and began building its large cliff dwellings at the end of the 12th century. By 1285, after a series of severe and prolonged droughts, they had left and moved south into Arizona and New Mexico. Cliff Palace is one of about 600 cliff dwellings on Mesa Verde, which has been a national park since 1906.
