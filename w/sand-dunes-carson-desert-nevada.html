Sand Dunes, Carson Desert, Nevada

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1867 · albumen print from wet collodion negative · Image: 19.7 × 27 cm (7 3/4 × 10 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The wagon in the picture is a former Civil War ambulance that O'Sullivan had turned into a portable darkroom. He framed the scene to show only two sets of footprints, though more than 165,000 people had passed near here on the way to California in the previous twenty-five years. He took it in 1867 as official photographer to Clarence King's survey of the Fortieth Parallel, which was studying the future path of the transcontinental railroad.
