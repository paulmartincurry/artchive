Moai Kavakava

Maker unknown · date not recorded · Wood · Museo Etnográfico Juan B. Ambrosetti, Buenos Aires

Kavakava is the Rapa Nui word for ribs. Travellers in the 19th century reported that these small wooden figures were worn hanging around the necks of men who took part in the ritual dances at public ceremonies. Little else is known about how they were used.
