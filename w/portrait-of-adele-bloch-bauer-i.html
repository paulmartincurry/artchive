Portrait of Adele Bloch-Bauer I

Gustav Klimt · 1907 · Oil and gold and silver on canvas · Neue Galerie, New York

Free to use

Ferdinand Bloch-Bauer, a Viennese banker and sugar producer, ordered the portrait of his wife in mid-1903 as an anniversary present for her parents that October. Gustav Klimt made more than a hundred preparatory sketches and finished only in 1907. He laid gold and silver leaf and raised the patterns on it in gesso; the face, hair and hands, in oil, cover less than a twelfth of the surface. The Nazis stole the painting in 1941. Ferdinand's niece won it back in 2006 and sold it that year for 135 million dollars.
