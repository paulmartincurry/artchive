Black Iris

Georgia O'Keeffe · 1926 · Oil on canvas · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Georgia O'Keeffe began painting the centres of flowers in 1924, and the black iris became a subject she returned to as late as 1936. This canvas was first shown at the Intimate Gallery in New York in January 1927, catalogued as Dark Iris No. 3. She kept it until 1969, with the last twenty years spent on loan to the Metropolitan Museum, which was then given it. It was called Black Iris III until 1991, when the list of her works was revised.
