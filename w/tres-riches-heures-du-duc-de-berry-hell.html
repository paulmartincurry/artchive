Très Riches Heures du Duc de Berry: Hell (folio 108r)

The Limbourg brothers · c. 1412–1416 · Tempera on vellum · 29 × 21 cm · Musée Condé, Chantilly

Free to use

A Limbourg brother painted this page. Every other miniature in its section of the book is by Jean Colombe, who was commissioned to complete the manuscript around 1485 to 1489. The scene is based on a description by the Irish monk Tundal.
