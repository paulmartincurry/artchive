April is the cruellest month, breeding
Lilacs out of the dead land

The Waste Land (opening)

T. S. Eliot · 1922

Public domain in the United States (published 1922); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

T. S. Eliot had worked at Lloyds Bank since March 1917. In the autumn of 1921 the bank gave him three months' leave after a diagnosis of a nervous disorder. He wrote some fifty lines in a shelter on Margate Sands and completed the first draft in Lausanne, under the care of Dr Roger Vittoz. Ezra Pound then helped edit the 19-page draft, and a large amount was removed. The manuscripts were believed lost for many years and were sold to the New York Public Library in 1958.
