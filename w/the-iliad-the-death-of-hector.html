When he had thus said the shrouds of death enfolded him, whereon his soul went out of him and flew down to the house of Hades, lamenting its sad fate that it should enjoy youth and strength no longer.

The Iliad — The Death of Hector (Book XXII)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by Samuel Butler

Before this, Achilles chases Hector three times around Troy, and Athena takes the shape of Hector's brother Deiphobus to make him stand and fight. Afterwards Achilles ties the body by the heels to his chariot and drags it around the city while the Trojans watch from the walls. Twelve days go by before Priam comes to ransom his son.
