Nocturne: Blue and Gold, Southampton Water

James McNeill Whistler · 1872 · Oil on canvas · 51 × 76.7 cm (20 1/16 × 30 3/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Whistler signed the canvas with his butterfly mark, painted in yellow on the water at lower right. It was probably first shown at the Dudley Gallery in London in 1872 under another title, Nocturne in Grey and Gold. Alfred Chapman of London owned it by June 1874. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1900, three years before Whistler's death.
