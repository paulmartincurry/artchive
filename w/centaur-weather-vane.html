Centaur Weather Vane

James McLellan · c. 1938 · Watercolor, graphite, and gouache on paper · 38.1 × 51.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The weather vane itself is 30 inches high and 40 wide. James McLellan recorded it in watercolour for the Index of American Design, whose artists shared a technique for exact rendering that Suzanne Chapman is credited with developing.
