The Heart Sutra

Mahāyāna Buddhist Canon · By the 7th century CE

Translated by Edward Conze · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

No author is known, and scholars still dispute whether the text was first composed in Sanskrit or in Chinese. The oldest dated copy is a stone stele of 661 CE at Yunju Temple, near Beijing. The oldest Sanskrit manuscript, on palm leaf, was found at Hōryū-ji in Japan and is kept in the Tokyo National Museum. The short version translated into Chinese by Xuanzang has 260 characters.
