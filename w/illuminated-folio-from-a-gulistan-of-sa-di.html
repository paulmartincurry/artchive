Illuminated folio from a Gulistan (Rose Garden) of Sa'di (c. 1213–1291)

Sultan 'Ali al-Mashadi · about 1475–1500; border: about 1550 · Gum tempera, ink, gold, and silver on paper · 30 x 19 cm (11 13/16 x 7 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The text is from the Gulistan, the Rose Garden of Sa'di, finished around 1258, from its chapter on the conduct of kings. The silver of the flowers and streams in the border has tarnished dark over time.
