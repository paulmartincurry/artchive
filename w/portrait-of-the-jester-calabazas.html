Portrait of the Jester Calabazas

Diego Velázquez · c. 1631–32 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 175 × 106 cm (68 7/8 × 41 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Calabazas was a jester at the court of Philip IV, and he holds a pinwheel and a miniature portrait of a woman. The canvas stayed in the royal collections at the Buen Retiro palace in Madrid until at least 1808. In 1872 it was sold in Paris under the title Le Fou for 750 francs. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought it at Christie's in London in 1965. Velázquez was in his early thirties when he painted it.
