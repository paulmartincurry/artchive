It is not the end, but the beginning; not nothingness, but eternity!

Funeral Oration for Balzac

Victor Hugo · August 21, 1850

Translated by Katharine Prescott Wormeley, 1892

Victor Hugo was forty-eight in August 1850. He sat in the National Assembly as a member for the Seine, a seat he held from April 1848 to December 1851, and had been a member of the Académie française since 1841. Five years earlier he had started writing Les Misérables, which was not published until 1862.
