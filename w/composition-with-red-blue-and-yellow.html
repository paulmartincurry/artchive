Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

Piet Mondrian · 1930 · Oil on canvas · Kunsthaus Zürich

The canvas is forty-five centimetres square, and the black bands are laid on in thick brushwork. In the same year Mondrian painted a closely similar picture, Composition II in Red, Blue, and Yellow. He turned fifty-eight in 1930. His first painting to rest almost wholly on red, yellow and blue, a tree in a field at dusk called Evening, dates from 1908.
