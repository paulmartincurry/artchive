The Gate of Yann (A Dreamer's Tales)

Sidney Sime · published 1910 · Plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

George Allen & Sons published A Dreamer's Tales in September 1910. Sime's work for Dunsany in these years was almost entirely black and white; only late in life did he work more in colour. He also designed scenery and costumes for the stage. He died in 1941 and is buried at Worplesdon in Surrey, where his widow left his remaining works to form a gallery.
