The Story of an African Farm — The Hunter

Olive Schreiner · 1883

Olive Schreiner was twenty-eight when this novel, her first to be published, came out in London in 1883 in two volumes under the name Ralph Iron. She was the ninth of twelve children of a missionary couple in the Cape Colony, had no formal schooling until she was about twelve, and had earned her living as a governess. The heroine Lyndall carries her mother's maiden name. The book sold quickly and was attacked for its frankness about free thought and sex outside marriage.
