Madonna

Edvard Munch · 1894 · Oil on canvas · Munch Museum

Free to use

Munch painted several versions of this composition between 1892 and 1895 and also made it as a lithograph. The model was his friend Dagny Juel-Przybyszewska. He used more than one title for it, Loving Woman among them. Masked men with firearms took this version and The Scream from the Munch Museum on 22 August 2004, snapping the cables that held them to the wall. Oslo police recovered both on 31 August 2006, and the Madonna came back with a hole 2.5 centimetres wide.
