Wouldst thou learn thy Lord's meaning in this thing? Learn it well: Love was His meaning. Who shewed it thee? Love. What shewed He thee? Love. Wherefore shewed it He? For Love.

Revelations of Divine Love — Love Was His Meaning (Ch. 86)

Julian of Norwich · c. 1390s–1410s

Of the book's 87 chapters this is the second to last. Julian wrote in Middle English, and she described herself as unlettered, which in the Middle Ages might have meant that she could not read or that she had had no formal education. There is no evidence that her writings were read by more than a very few people before 1670, when they were first printed.
