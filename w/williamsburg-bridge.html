Williamsburg Bridge (New York, plate 4)

Alvin Langdon Coburn · published c. 1911 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The gravure is plate 4 in Coburn's book New York, which the Library of Congress dates to 1911 with a question mark. The Williamsburg Bridge had been finished in 1903, and at the time it held the longest suspension span in the world. Coburn married in New York in October 1912, sailed for England that November, and never returned to the United States.
