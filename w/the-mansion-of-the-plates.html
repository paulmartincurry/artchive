The Mansion of the Plates (One Hundred Ghost Tales)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; chūban · 25.5 × 18.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The series takes its name from a night-time game in which people told frightening stories and put out a candle after each one until the room was dark. Okiku, the servant thrown into a well over a missing plate from a set of ten, first reached the stage in a puppet play of July 1741. In the legend this print follows, her ghost came back every night.
