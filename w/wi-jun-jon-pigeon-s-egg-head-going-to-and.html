Wi-jún-jon, Pigeon's Egg Head (The Light) Going To and Returning From Washington

George Catlin · 1837–39 · Oil on canvas · 73.6 × 60.9 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

Catlin met the Assiniboine leader in St. Louis in December 1831, on his way to Washington to meet President Andrew Jackson. Pigeon's Egg Head is Catlin's mistranslation of a name that means The Light. He came home eighteen months later, and his people in time rejected his accounts of the white man's cities as lies; he was murdered. Catlin bought his scalp from a trader and displayed it at exhibitions of his paintings.
