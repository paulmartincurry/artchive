Gas Station, Kern County, California

Dorothea Lange · November 1938 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The hand-lettered sign hangs on the air pump of a gas station in Kern County, California, where Lange photographed it in November 1938 for the Farm Security Administration. She was forty-three. The Library's digital file was made from her original negative.
