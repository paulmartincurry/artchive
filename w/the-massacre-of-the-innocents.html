The Massacre of the Innocents

Guido Reni · 1611 · Oil on canvas · Pinacoteca Nazionale di Bologna

Free to use

Guido Reni painted the canvas in 1611 for the Basilica of San Domenico in Bologna. It is now in the Pinacoteca Nazionale in the same city. The poet Giambattista Marino, his contemporary, wrote a short rhyme about it.
