Slavs in Their Original Homeland (The Slav Epic)

Alphonse Mucha · 1912 · Painting on canvas · 8.10 × 6.10 m · City of Prague (shown at Moravský Krumlov chateau)

Free to use

This is the first of the twenty canvases of The Slav Epic, finished in 1912; it measures 8.10 by 6.10 metres. An American philanthropist, Charles Richard Crane, paid for the project, and in 1910 Mucha rented part of the castle at Zbiroh to paint it. Before starting he travelled to Russia, Poland and the Balkans and consulted historians.
