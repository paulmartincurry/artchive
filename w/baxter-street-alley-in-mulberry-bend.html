Baxter Street Alley in Mulberry Bend

Jacob Riis · c. 1888–89 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Early in 1887 Riis read that a way had been found to take pictures by flashlight, using magnesium flash powder fired from a pistol-like device. With John Nagle of the city's Bureau of Vital Statistics and two other amateurs he began photographing the slums. His descriptions of Mulberry Bend led to the area being razed and replaced by a park. The title comes from old, unverified caption card data.
