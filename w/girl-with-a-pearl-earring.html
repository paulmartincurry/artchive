Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer · ca. 1665 · Oil on canvas · Mauritshuis, The Hague

Free to use

Vermeer signed this painting but did not date it. In 1881 Arnoldus Andries des Tombe bought it at an auction in The Hague for two guilders plus thirty cents buyer's premium. He had no heirs and left it to the Mauritshuis in 1902. The background was a translucent green glaze over a black underlayer, and two of the pigments in the glaze, indigo and weld, have faded. Research in 2018 found a green curtain behind the head and fine eyelashes.
