New Chest, Piegan

Edward S. Curtis · 1910 · Photogravure, plate 200 from "The North American Indian, Volume 6" (1911) · Image/paper: 39 × 27.9 cm (15 3/8 × 11 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This portrait appeared in 1911 as plate 200 in the sixth volume of The North American Indian. Over the whole project Curtis took more than 40,000 photographs of people from over eighty tribes and made more than 10,000 wax cylinder recordings of their languages and music. Short of money, he sold the rights to J. P. Morgan Jr. in 1928, and the last volume came out in 1930. About 280 sets were sold in all.
