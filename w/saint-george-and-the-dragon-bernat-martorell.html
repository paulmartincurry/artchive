Saint George and the Dragon

Bernat Martorell · 1434–35 · Tempera on panel · 155.6 × 98.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This was the central panel of an altarpiece, probably made for the chapel of Saint George in the palace of the government of Catalonia in Barcelona; the four smaller panels of the saint's martyrdom that surrounded it are now in the Louvre. Martorell built up the halo, the armour and the dragon's scales in raised gesso. Charles Deering bought the panel in 1917, and his daughters gave it to the museum in 1933.
