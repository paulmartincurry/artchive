The Moon

John Adams Whipple · 1857–60 · Salted paper print from glass negative · 21 × 16.2 cm (sheet) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

John Adams Whipple made his first daguerreotype in the winter of 1840, using a candle box for a camera. Between 1847 and 1852 he and William Cranch Bond photographed the Moon through Harvard's Great Refractor, then the largest telescope in the world, and their pictures won a prize at the Crystal Palace Exhibition in London in 1851. With William Breed Jones he worked out a way of making paper prints from glass negatives, the method used for this print.
