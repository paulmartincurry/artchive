Soon She Was Lost to Sight in the Danube (Undine)

Arthur Rackham · published 1909 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

This is the fourteenth of Rackham's fifteen colour plates for the 1909 Undine. Friedrich de la Motte Fouqué published the novella in 1811, drawing on the writings of the occultist Paracelsus. The moment shown comes on a trip down the Danube to Vienna, when the knight Huldbrand throws Undine's coral necklace overboard and she vanishes into the river.
