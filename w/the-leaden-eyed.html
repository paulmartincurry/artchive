Not that they starve, but starve so dreamlessly, … Not that they die, but that they die like sheep.

The Leaden-Eyed

Vachel Lindsay · 1914

Vachel Lindsay trained as a painter at the Art Institute of Chicago and the New York School of Art before he turned to poetry in 1905. He printed his own poems and bartered them for food and lodging on long walks, the last of them from Illinois to New Mexico between May and September 1912. He was thirty-four in 1914, the year Poetry magazine printed The Congo and he became widely known.
