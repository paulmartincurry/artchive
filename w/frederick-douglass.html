Frederick Douglass

Samuel J. Miller · 1847-52 · Daguerreotype · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Samuel J. Miller ran a daguerreotype portrait studio in Akron, Ohio, one of thousands that opened across the United States within a few years of the process being announced in 1839. Northeastern Ohio was then a centre of the campaign against slavery. Douglass commissioned or sat for a great many portraits, and he knew this one would be seen by his supporters. The image is on a silver-coated copper plate fourteen centimetres tall, kept in a case that opens and closes.
