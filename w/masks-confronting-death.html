Masks Confronting Death

James Ensor · 1888 · Oil on canvas · 81.3 × 100.3 cm · The Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Ensor was twenty-seven or twenty-eight when he painted this in 1888, the same year he made forty-five etchings and began Christ's Entry Into Brussels. He had left school at fifteen and trained at the Académie Royale des Beaux-Arts in Brussels from 1877 to 1880. The canvas is 81.3 by 100.3 centimetres and is now in the Museum of Modern Art, which showed about 120 of his works in 2009.
