The Librarian

Giuseppe Arcimboldo · 1562 · Oil on canvas · Skokloster Castle, Sweden

Free to use

The painting was taken from Prague Castle when the Swedish army looted it in 1648, and it was owned by General Hans Christoff von Königsmarck. It is thought to portray Wolfgang Lazius, historian to the Habsburg emperors, an identification first made in 1957. A scientific study published in 2011 concluded that this canvas is a later copy of Arcimboldo's original, whose whereabouts are unknown.
