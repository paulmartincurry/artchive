Mankind! Your road is mud,
These ruts are your last breaths;
While nothing proves sorrier
Than the meeting of two deaths.

Black Spring (Чёрная весна)

Innokenty Annensky · published 1910

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The poem belongs to the Spring Trefoil, one of the groups of three poems in The Cypress Chest, the book Annensky's son prepared for the press after his death; it came out in April 1910, four months after he died. Annensky had been slow to publish his own poems and was known first for translating Euripides.
