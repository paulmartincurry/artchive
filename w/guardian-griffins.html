Guardian Griffins

Maker unknown · 1150–1175 · pink limestone (called "Verona Marble") · Overall: 75.9 × 50.2 × 120.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The two griffins probably held up the columns of a porch in front of a church door. One grips a knight between its paws and the other a calf. Each is about 1.2 metres long, carved in the pink limestone known as Verona marble. They are said to have come from the house of a Cardinal Leona, east of Bologna, and were given to the Cleveland Museum of Art in 1928.
