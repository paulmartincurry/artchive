It was Robert Ford, that dirty little coward,
I wonder how he does feel,
For he ate of Jesse's bread and he slept in Jesse's bed,
Then laid poor Jesse in his grave.

Jesse James

Traditional American ballad · c. 1887

Jesse James was shot by Robert Ford, a member of his own gang; Mr. Howard was the name James was living under in Saint Joseph, Missouri, at the time. There is no evidence for the song's picture of him robbing the rich to give to the poor. Bentley Ball made the first recording in 1919. The text is from the 1918 printing of John Lomax's Cowboy Songs.
