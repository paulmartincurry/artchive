Isfandiyar's Third Course: He Slays a Dragon (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Qasim ibn 'Ali · c. 1530 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 27.9 × 26.2 cm; page 47.3 × 31.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Arthur Houghton sold the manuscript's paintings one at a time to avoid taxes, kept 118 for himself and gave 78 to the Metropolitan Museum; this leaf, folio 434 verso, is one of the 78. The painting is attributed to Qasim ibn 'Ali.
