Don Quixote (Part I, Ch. VIII)

Miguel de Cervantes · 1605

Translated by Edith Grossman · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Cervantes had been a soldier. He was badly wounded at the Battle of Lepanto in 1571 and lost the use of his left hand, and in 1575 he was captured by Barbary pirates and held for five years until he was ransomed. Back in Spain he worked as a purchasing agent and then as a government tax collector. He was fifty-seven when Part One of the novel was published in 1605.
