The Bridge, Venice (Camera Work 21, plate VII)

Alvin Langdon Coburn · published 1908 · Halftone · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Coburn travelled to Venice in the autumn of 1907 while working on frontispieces for an American edition of Henry James's novels. In January 1908 twelve of his photographs were published in Camera Work, number 21; this was plate VII.
