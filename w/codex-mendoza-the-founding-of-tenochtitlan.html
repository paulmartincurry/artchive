Codex Mendoza: The Founding of Tenochtitlan

Maker unknown · ca. 1541 · Paper · Bodleian Library, Oxford

The codex was painted in Mexico by native artists and scribes schooled at the Franciscan college in Tlatelolco, on seventy-one leaves of Spanish paper. Its last page says the Spanish interpreter was given ten days before the fleet sailed and worked in haste. According to a later owner, French privateers took the fleet and the book went to France, where the royal cosmographer signed it in 1553. Richard Hakluyt later bought it for 20 French francs. It reached the Bodleian in 1659 and lay unnoticed there until 1831.
