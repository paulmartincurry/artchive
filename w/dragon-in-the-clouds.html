Dragon in the Clouds

Totoya Hokkei · 1832 · Color woodblock print; shikishiban, surimono · 20.5 × 18.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hokkei's earliest known work, around 1800, was illustration for books of comic poems, licentious novels and storybooks. He was in his early fifties when he made this surimono, and he died in 1850 at seventy. The Art Institute bought it from the collector Florence Crane in 1939 and showed it in Dance of the Dragons in 2000.
