Stand still, you ever-moving spheres of heaven,
That time may cease, and midnight never come!

Doctor Faustus (final scene)

Christopher Marlowe · ca. 1592

Marlowe took the story from a German chapbook of 1587, which was first translated into English in 1592, so the play was probably written that year or the next. It was not printed until 1604, eleven years after his death, in a text only 1,485 lines long. A version of 1616 cut 36 lines and added 676, partly by other writers. The Admiral's Men performed it 23 times between 1594 and 1597, and a story spread that real devils once appeared on stage during a performance.
