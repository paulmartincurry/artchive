Phoenix-Headed Ewer

Maker unknown · 1000s · porcelain with pale greenish-white glaze · Overall: 38.7 cm (15 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The phoenix beak is the spout. Ewers of this kind have been dug up at Xicun in Guangdong, where local kilns fired the thick, coarse ones; thin, refined pieces like this one were probably made at Jingdezhen, in Jiangxi. It is almost thirty-nine centimetres tall. Severance and Greta Millikin of Cleveland gave it to the museum in 1965.
