Heron Maiden (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889 · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

When Yoshitoshi drew the Heron Maiden in 1889, Hiroshige, Kunisada and his own teacher Kuniyoshi had been dead for decades. Japan was turning to photography and lithography, and he kept working in the old manner.
