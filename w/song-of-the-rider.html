Córdoba.
Far away, and lonely.

Full moon, black pony,
olives against my saddle.

Song of the Rider (Canción del jinete)

Federico García Lorca · 1921–1924

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Lorca published the poem in Canciones, a collection that, as his biographers note, did not contain songs in the usual sense. In 1927 he showed a series of drawings at the Galeries Dalmau in Barcelona.
