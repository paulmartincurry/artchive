Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 7

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Merian found only two of these yellow caterpillars; one died, and the other turned into a pupa on 20 April, from which the butterfly emerged on 26 May. To pay for the engraving of the plates she took subscriptions in advance, and twelve subscribers paid for the hand-painted edition, while a cheaper black-and-white edition was also printed.
