Christ's Entry Into Brussels in 1889

James Ensor · 1888 · Oil on canvas · J. Paul Getty Museum

The canvas is 2.53 metres by 4.31, a single piece of linen too large for James Ensor to work on all at once; he nailed part of it to the wall and let the rest lie on the floor. He laid the paint on thickly with brushes, palette knives and spatulas. Les XX rejected it, and it stayed in his house in Ostend until its first exhibition in 1929. Shrapnel damaged it during the Second World War. The Getty bought it in 1987 and it left Belgium despite protests.
