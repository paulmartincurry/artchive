Terracotta Army

Maker unknown · ca. 210 BCE · Terracotta · Xi'an

Seven farmers digging a well found the first figures on 29 March 1974, about a kilometre and a half east of the emperor's tomb mound. Work on the mausoleum had begun in 246 BCE, soon after Qin Shi Huang became King of Qin at the age of thirteen. The soldiers stand between 1.75 and about 2 metres tall, with the officers taller, and were once painted. Once exposed to the dry air the lacquer under the paint can curl in fifteen seconds. The figures now on display were put back together from fragments.
