The Seven Sleepers of Ephesus (Falnama)

Maker unknown · 1550s · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · Painting 58.4 × 45.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A Falnama was a book for telling fortunes. The reader washed, recited prayers from the Qur'an and opened the book at random, then read the omen in the painting and in the text that faced it. The first such books were made in Safavid Iran under Shah Tahmasp, and they were unusually large; this painting alone is 58.4 cm high.
