I want to paint humanity, humanity and again humanity.

Letter to Émile Bernard on Painting Humanity

Vincent van Gogh · c. 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1912

Before he painted, Van Gogh worked for the art dealers Goupil & Cie in The Hague, London and Paris, and was dismissed. He then spent time as a missionary in a coal-mining district of Belgium, where he gave up his lodgings and slept on straw in a small hut. The church authorities dismissed him as well.
