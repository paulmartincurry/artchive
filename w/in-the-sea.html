In the Sea

Arnold Böcklin · 1883 · Oil on panel · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Böcklin was living and working in Florence when he painted this panel, and in the same years he was making the five versions of Isle of the Dead. It is 86.5 by 115 centimetres. The dealer Fritz Gurlitt showed it in Berlin in 1884, and a Basel collector, Adalbert Mylius-Gemuseus, owned it from 1886. Offered at Sotheby's in New York in 1990, it failed to sell at auction and was sold to the Art Institute the same year.
