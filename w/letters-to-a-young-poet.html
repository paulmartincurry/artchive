This above all, ask yourself in the silence of night: must I write?
Dig deep for an answer.

Letters to a Young Poet (First Letter)

Rainer Maria Rilke · February 17, 1903 (pub. 1929)

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2021 · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Franz Xaver Kappus was a nineteen-year-old officer cadet when he sent Rilke his poems and asked for a critique. The academy's chaplain had told him that Rilke was once a pupil at its lower school. Rilke, then twenty-seven, answered from Paris on 17 February 1903 and declined to criticise the poems. Nine more letters followed over five years. Kappus served fifteen years as an army officer and published the ten letters in 1929, three years after Rilke's death. His own side of the correspondence was found in the Rilke family archive in 2017.
