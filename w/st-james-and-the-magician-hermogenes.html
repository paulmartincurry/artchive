St. James and the Magician Hermogenes

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1565 · Engraving; first state of three · Plate: 26.2 × 34 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Pieter van der Heyden engraved the plate after Bruegel's design, and Hieronymus Cock published it in 1565. With one exception Bruegel did not work the plates himself; he made a drawing that Cock's specialists worked from. This impression is the first state of three.
