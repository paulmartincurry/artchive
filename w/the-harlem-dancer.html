But looking at her falsely-smiling face,
I knew her self was not in that strange place.

The Harlem Dancer

Claude McKay · 1922

McKay published two poems in The Seven Arts in 1917 under the pseudonym Eli Edwards. In the five years before 1919 he had managed a Brooklyn restaurant, which failed, and waited tables at a hotel in Hanover, New Hampshire. Harlem Shadows, his collection of 1922, was among the first books of the Harlem Renaissance.
