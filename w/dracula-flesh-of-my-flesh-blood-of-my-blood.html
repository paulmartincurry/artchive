And you, their best beloved one, are now to me, flesh of my flesh; blood of my blood; kin of my kin; my bountiful wine-press for a while; and shall be later on my companion and my helper.

Dracula — Flesh of my flesh, blood of my blood

Bram Stoker · 1897

Stoker built the novel from more than 100 pages of notes, with chapter summaries and plot outlines. In them the story was at first set in Styria instead of Transylvania, and one of the vampire hunters was to be killed by a werewolf. His widow, Florence, sold the notes to a New York book dealer in 1913; they later disappeared until the Rosenbach Museum and Library in Philadelphia bought them in 1970.
