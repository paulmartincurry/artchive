Rokeby Venus

Diego Velázquez · c. 1647–1651 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

No other female nude by Velázquez survives. Infra-red shows that he first painted the figure sitting more upright with her head turned to the left, and the grey sheet was once a deep mauve that has faded. The picture is first recorded in an inventory of 1 June 1651. John Morritt bought it for 500 pounds and hung it at Rokeby Park in Yorkshire. On 10 March 1914 the suffragette Mary Richardson cut it seven times with a meat cleaver; the damage was repaired.
