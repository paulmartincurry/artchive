Descent of the Ganges, Mamallapuram

Maker unknown · Mid-7th Century · Granite

Free to use with credit. Photo: Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 3.0

The relief is cut into two boulders of pink granite standing in the open air, and the natural cleft between them is part of the carving. A brick cistern at the top once released water down the cleft on festive occasions. There are about 146 figures, many of the people life size and the elephants nearly so. It was made to mark the victory of the Pallava king Narasimhavarman I, who reigned from 630 to 668, over the Chalukya emperor Pulakesin II.
