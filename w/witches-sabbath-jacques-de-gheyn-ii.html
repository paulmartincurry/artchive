Witches' Sabbath

Jacques de Gheyn II · late 16th–early 17th century · Pen and brown ink, brush and brown wash, on brownish paper; framing lines in black chalk · 23.5 × 36.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Jacques de Gheyn II drew this in pen, brown ink and wash on brownish paper. He was the son of a glass painter and engraver in Antwerp, and from 1585 he spent five years in Haarlem studying under Hendrick Goltzius. The Met bought the sheet in 1962 with money from Joseph Pulitzer's bequest.
