It's raining women's voices as if they were dead even in memory

Il pleut (It's Raining)

Guillaume Apollinaire · 1916

Translated by Ron Padgett · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

In 1916 Apollinaire was an infantry officer in the French army, and that year a piece of shrapnel struck him in the temple; he never fully recovered. He was thirty-five. Calligrammes, his collection of poems in which typography and layout are part of the effect, carries the dates 1913 to 1916 in its title and was published by Mercure de France shortly after his death. He died of influenza on 9 November 1918, and the French government declared that he had died for France.
