Working Woman with Blue Shawl

Käthe Kollwitz · 1903 · color lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kollwitz chose printmaking because prints were cheap enough to reach every social class. Her husband, Karl, whom she married in 1891, was a doctor treating the poor in Berlin, and through his practice she got to know the women who came to him for help. In 1903, when she was thirty-five, she was also working on the Peasant War cycle, which took from 1902 to 1908. This impression is the third and final state, given to Cleveland by The Print Club of Cleveland in 1932.
