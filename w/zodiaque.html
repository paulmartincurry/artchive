Zodiaque (La Plume)

Alphonse Mucha · 1896–97 · Color lithograph from multiple stones on tan wove paper · 63 × 47 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Mucha made this design for La Plume, the literary and artistic review edited by Léon Deschamps. Deschamps invited him to show at the Salon des Cent in 1896, and in 1897 gave him a retrospective there of 448 works. La Plume brought out a special edition devoted to his work, and the exhibition travelled to Vienna, Prague, Munich, Brussels, London and New York.
