Helmet (Kawari Kabuto)

Maker unknown · helmet 17th–19th century, crest 19th century · Iron, lacquer, leather, silk, wood, gesso, shakudō, bone, pigments · H. 30.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Ornamental helmets called kawari kabuto, strange helmets, were made in the Edo period with figures of animals or other objects mounted on top. The bowl of this one dates from the seventeenth to nineteenth century and its crest from the nineteenth. It is built from iron, lacquer, leather, silk, wood and gesso, and is 30.6 centimetres high.
