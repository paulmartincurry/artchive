Dracula (Ch. II)

Bram Stoker · 1897

Stoker's earliest dated notes for the novel are from 8 March 1890, and he said it took him about three years to write. He worked on it in time spare from his job as business manager of the Lyceum Theatre in London, and probably wrote most of the manuscript on summer holidays at Cruden Bay in Scotland. The Count's name came from an 1820 history of Wallachia and Moldavia. The printer's copy of the typescript is titled The Un-Dead.
