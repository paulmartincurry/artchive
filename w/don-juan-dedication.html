Cold-blooded, smooth-faced, placid miscreant!
Dabbling its sleek young hands in Erin’s gore,

Don Juan: Dedication (on Castlereagh)

Lord Byron · 1818; published 1833

The Don Juan story first appeared in a Spanish play of 1630 by Tirso de Molina, and reached the English stage in 1676 as The Libertine by Thomas Shadwell. In Byron's verse the name is said the English way, in three syllables, and rhymes with ruin. Byron had held a hereditary seat in the House of Lords since 1809.
