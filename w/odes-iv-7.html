Yet the swift moons repair Heaven's detriment:
We, soon as thrust
Where good Aeneas, Tullus, Ancus went,
What are we? dust.

Odes IV.7 (Diffugere Nives)

Horace · 13 BCE

Translated by John Conington

The fourth book of the Odes, fifteen poems, was published in 13 BC, ten years after the first three. Horace was then about fifty-two.
