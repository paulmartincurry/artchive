Orbium Planetarum Terram Complectentium Scenographia (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The atlas falls into two parts. The first is a run of copper-engraved plates setting out the rival models of the universe then under discussion, those of Ptolemy, Copernicus and Tycho Brahe; the second holds the star charts. This is the third plate of the first part.
