Sad Forebodings of What Is Going to Happen (The Disasters of War, plate 1)

Francisco de Goya · c. 1815 (published 1863) · Etching, burin, drypoint, burnisher · Plate: 17.5 × 21.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is the first plate of the series, but it was one of the last to be finished, after the war had ended. Goya was 62, in poor health and almost deaf, when he began the prints.
