The trumpet of a prophecy! O, Wind,
If Winter comes, can Spring be far behind?

Ode to the West Wind (part V)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1819

Shelley wrote the ode in 1819 in the Cascine wood near Florence, when he was twenty-seven and had been living in Italy for a year. His son William had died that year. The poem is in terza rima: five sections, each of four three-line stanzas and a closing couplet. Charles Ollier published it in London in 1820, in the same volume as Prometheus Unbound.
