Canst thou make me immortal, O thou that blamest me so
For haunting the battle and loving the pleasures that fly?
If thou hast not the power to ward me from Death, let me go
To meet him and scatter the wealth in my hand, ere I die.

Muʿallaqa of Ṭarafa — Canst Thou Make Me Immortal?

Ṭarafa ibn al-ʿAbd · mid-6th century CE

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1922

Tarafa died at about twenty-six, and Arabic tradition calls him the murdered youth. According to one legend, King Amr ibn Hind, whom he had mocked in verse, sent him with a sealed letter to the governor of Bahrayn; he could not read and refused to break the royal seal, and in Bahrayn he was imprisoned and executed. His Mu'allaqa runs to over a hundred lines, nearly thirty of them describing his she-camel.
