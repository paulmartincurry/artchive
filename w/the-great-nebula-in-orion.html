The Great Nebula in Orion (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot made the study for this plate over 1875 and 1876. He worked his observations up first as pastels, and the published plates were printed as chromolithographs. In 1877 he was elected a fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, and he won the Valz Prize of the French Academy of Sciences.
