At the round earths imagin'd corners, blow
Your trumpets, Angells, and arise, arise
From death, you numberlesse infinities

Holy Sonnet 7: At the round earth's imagined corners

John Donne · 1633

Modern scholars date most of the Holy Sonnets to 1609 and 1610, when Donne was moving from Roman Catholicism to the Church of England; he took holy orders in 1615. None of them was printed in his lifetime. They went round among friends in manuscript, one of them in at least fifteen copies, and were first published in 1633.
