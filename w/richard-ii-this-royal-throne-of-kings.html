This royal throne of kings, this sceptered isle,
This earth of majesty, this seat of Mars,
This other Eden, demi-paradise,

Richard II — This royal throne of kings (Act II, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1595

The play was entered in the Register of the Stationers Company on 29 August 1597 and the first quarto came out later that year. On 7 February 1601 supporters of the Earl of Essex's planned revolt paid for a performance at the Globe Theatre.
