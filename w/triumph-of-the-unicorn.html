Triumph of the Unicorn

Jean Duvet · 1540–50 · Engraving on paper · 23.2 × 38.6 cm (9 3/16 × 15 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Duvet shows the unicorn's triumph as the victory of Christianity, with God the Father as Jupiter leading the unicorn into paradise. The uneven ink suggests the plate was unfinished or worked on for a long time.
