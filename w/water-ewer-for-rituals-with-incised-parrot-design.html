Water Ewer for Rituals (Kundika) with Incised Parrot Design

Maker unknown · 1100s · Stoneware with celadon glaze, incised and carved decoration · Height 35.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A kundika, with its spout on one side, was used in Buddhist purification rituals. Most surviving examples are decorated with a willow tree or a lotus pond; this one is incised with flying birds. In the Goryeo period about 260 kilns, mainly in Jeolla and Chungcheong provinces, met the demand for wares like this. Cleveland bought it in 1921 from Dr. Alfred Irving Ludlow.
