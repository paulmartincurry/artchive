An Old Woman Cooking Eggs

Diego Velázquez · 1618 · Oil on canvas · 100.5 × 119.5 cm · National Galleries of Scotland, Edinburgh

Free to use

Velázquez painted this in Seville, before his move to Madrid in 1623; the date is thought to be about 1618. In these early pictures he often used his family as models, and the same old woman appears in his Christ in the House of Martha and Mary. Whether the eggs are being fried or poached is disputed. The Scottish gallery bought the painting in 1955 with the aid of the Art Fund and a Treasury grant.
