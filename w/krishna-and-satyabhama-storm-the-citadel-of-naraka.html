Krishna and Satyabhama Storm the Citadel of Naraka (Bhagavata Purana, folio 97)

Maker unknown · c. 1775 · Gum tempera and ink on paper · 32.2 × 51.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The citadel is seen from above, its rings of fortification laid out like a mandala. A caption in a Nepali vernacular runs along the bottom margin, and the red demon at lower right is labelled the Five-Headed Demon. The page, numbered 97, passed through sales at Sotheby's London in 1981 and Bonhams New York in 2012, and Cleveland bought it at Christie's New York in 2022.
