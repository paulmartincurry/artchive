Beneath Mannen Bridge at Fukagawa (Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1830–33 · Color woodblock print; oban · 25.8 × 37.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The first prints of the series were printed mostly in blue, outlines included, using Prussian blue, a pigment only recently brought to Japan from Europe. Once the publisher was sure the series would sell, the prints were made in many colours. This sheet is signed Hokusai aratame Iitsu fude.
