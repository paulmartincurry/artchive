Iznik Tile with Floral and Cloud-band Design

Maker unknown · ca. 1578 · Stonepaste; polychrome painted under transparent glaze · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The tile is a square of about 25 centimetres and less than two centimetres thick, made of stonepaste in Iznik and painted in several colours before a clear glaze went over it. William B. Osgood Field gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1902.
