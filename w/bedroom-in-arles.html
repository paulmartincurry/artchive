Bedroom in Arles

Vincent van Gogh · 1888 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

The room is Vincent van Gogh's bedroom in the Yellow House at 2, Place Lamartine in Arles, and its floor plan was a trapezoid, not a rectangle. He began the first version in mid-October 1888, after an illness that kept him in bed for days. A flood of the Rhône damaged that canvas while he was in hospital, and his brother Theo proposed having it relined and sent back so that he could copy it. He painted a second version at the same size in September 1889 and a smaller third for his mother and sister.
