God Judging Adam

William Blake · c. 1795 · Color relief etching finished with pen and ink and watercolor · Sheet: 42.5 × 52.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Metropolitan Museum bought this sheet in 1916. It is a colour print from a relief-etched plate, finished by hand in pen, ink and watercolour, and it measures 42.5 by 52.7 centimetres. Blake was about thirty-seven when he made it, a year or so after Europe a Prophecy and Songs of Experience, both of 1794.
