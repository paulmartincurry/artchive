Studioportret van Abdülaziz, 32e sultan van het Ottomaanse Rijk

Abdullah Frères · c. 1863 - c. 1866 · Albumen print · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Abdullah Frères were three Ottoman brothers of Armenian descent. In 1863 Sultan Abdülaziz, the sitter here, made them official court photographers. Their stamp is on the back.
