The Brothers Karamazov — The Grand Inquisitor

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1880

Dostoevsky began his first notes for the novel in April 1878. The next month his three-year-old son Alyosha died of epilepsy, and later that year he went to the Optina Monastery. He gave the son's name to the novel's hero. The writing took nearly two years, much of it done in Staraya Russa, and the book ran as a serial in The Russian Messenger from January 1879 to November 1880. He died less than four months after it was finished.
