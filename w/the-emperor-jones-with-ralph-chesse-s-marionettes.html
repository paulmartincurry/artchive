The Emperor Jones, with Ralph Chessé's Marionettes

Maker unknown · 1937 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The poster announced Eugene O'Neill's The Emperor Jones performed by Ralph Chessé's marionettes at the Columbia theatre. The Library of Congress dates it to 1937; Wikipedia places the Federal Theatre's marionette production in Los Angeles in 1938. O'Neill drew on his own experience prospecting for gold in Honduras in 1909, and the play became his first big box-office hit.
