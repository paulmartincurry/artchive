Down—steadily down it crept. I took a frenzied pleasure in contrasting its downward with its lateral velocity. To the right—to the left—far and wide—with the shriek of a damned spirit! to my heart with the stealthy pace of the tiger!

The Pit and the Pendulum (the descent)

Edgar Allan Poe · 1842

The story first appeared in 1842 in The Gift, an annual for 1843 edited by Eliza Leslie and published by Carey and Hart in Philadelphia. Poe revised it slightly for the Broadway Journal of 17 May 1845. The pendulum comes from one paragraph in the preface of Juan Antonio Llorente's 1826 history of the Spanish Inquisition, a second-hand account from a single released prisoner that most modern sources dismiss as fantasy.
