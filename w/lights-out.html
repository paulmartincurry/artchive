I have come to the borders of sleep,
The unfathomable deep
Forest where all must lose
Their way, however straight,

Lights Out

Edward Thomas · 1917

Thomas was killed at Arras on Easter Monday, 9 April 1917, soon after he reached France. The story long told was that the blast of one of the last shells fired killed him as he stood to light his pipe and left no mark on his body. A letter his commanding officer, Franklin Lushington, wrote in 1936, found many years later in an American archive, says he was shot clean through the chest.
