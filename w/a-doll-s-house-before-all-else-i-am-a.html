I believe that before all else I am a reasonable human being, just as you are—or, at all events, that I must try and become one.

A Doll's House — before all else I am a reasonable human being (Act III)

Henrik Ibsen · 1879

Translated by R. Farquharson Sharp

Ibsen sent a fair copy of the finished play to his publisher on 15 September 1879. It was published in Copenhagen on 4 December in an edition of 8,000 copies, which sold out within a month. In Germany an actress refused to play the ending as written, and Ibsen gave the play an alternative ending in which Nora does not leave.
