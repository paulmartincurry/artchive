The long sobs of
The violins
Of autumn
Lay waste my heart
With monotones
Of boredom.

Chanson d'automne

Paul Verlaine · 1866

Translated by Martin Sorrell · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Paul Verlaine was twenty-two and held a post in the civil service when this poem appeared in his first collection, Poèmes saturniens, published by Alphonse Lemerre. On 1 June 1944 the BBC's Radio Londres broadcast its first three lines to tell the French Resistance that the invasion of Normandy would begin within two weeks. The next three lines went out on 5 June at 23:15, the signal that it would begin within 48 hours and that sabotage of the railways should start.
