If I had everything that I could desire, and my finger ached, I should not have everything, for I should have a pain in my finger, and so long as that remained, I should not enjoy full comfort.

Rest Only in God (from Light, Life and Love)

Meister Eckhart · c. 1300–1327

Translated by W. R. Inge

In later life Eckhart was accused of heresy and brought before the local Franciscan-led Inquisition. He was tried as a heretic by Pope John XXII, whose bull is dated March 27, 1329, and he seems to have died before the verdict was received.
