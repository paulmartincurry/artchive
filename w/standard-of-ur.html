Standard of Ur

Maker unknown · ca. 2600 BCE · Wood · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: Denis Bourez, CC BY 2.0

Leonard Woolley's excavators found it in a royal tomb at Ur that robbers had plundered in ancient times, lying close to the shoulder of a man. The wooden frame had rotted away and the weight of the soil had crushed the box, but the pieces of shell, red limestone and lapis lazuli had kept their places in the earth. The excavators uncovered about three centimetres at a time and covered each patch with wax so the mosaic could be lifted whole. What is on display is a reconstruction, taken apart and reassembled in 1973. Its purpose is not known.
