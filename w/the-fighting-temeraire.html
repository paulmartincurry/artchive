The Fighting Temeraire

J. M. W. Turner · 1839 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

J. M. W. Turner had been exhibiting at the Royal Academy for 40 years when he painted this. The real ship was towed by two tugboats, not one, and her masts and rigging had been removed before the journey. Turner kept the painting in his studio until his death, and in about 1848 he refused an offer reputed to have been £5,000. He left it to the nation, but relatives contested his will after he died in 1851, and it entered the National Gallery in 1856. X-ray images suggest he reused a canvas on which he had started another sea picture.
