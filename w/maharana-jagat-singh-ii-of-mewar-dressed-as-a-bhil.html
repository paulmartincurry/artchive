Maharana Jagat Singh II of Mewar Dressed as a Bhil, Hunting Deer at Night

Maker unknown · c. 1735–40 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page: 17.2 × 24.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Jagat Singh II, Maharana of Mewar from 1734, is dressed in the leaf skirt of the Bhil people. He and a woman tracker wear girdles of bells to drive game out of the brush, and she lights the way with a lamp in a pot. On the riverbank another woman roasts kebabs on a three-pronged fork. The page is small, about 17 by 25 centimetres.
