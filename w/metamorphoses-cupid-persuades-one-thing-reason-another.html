But a new power draws me on, against my will; and Cupid persuades one thing, reason another. I see which is the more proper course, and I approve of it, while I follow the wrong one.

Metamorphoses — Cupid Persuades One Thing, Reason Another (Medea, Book VII)

Ovid · c. 8 CE

Translated by Henry T. Riley

The speaker is Medea. She first appears in Hesiod's Theogony, around 700 BC; the tragedy by Euripides and the Argonautica of Apollonius of Rhodes followed in the 5th and 3rd centuries BC. In the myth she helps Jason in his search for the Golden Fleece.
