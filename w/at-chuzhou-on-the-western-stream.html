On the spring flood of last night’s rain
The ferry-boat moves as though someone were poling.

At Chuzhou on the Western Stream

Wei Yingwu · 781 CE

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu, 1929 · Public domain in the United States (published 1929); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Wei Yingwu was born into a noble family in Chang'an and became a bodyguard to Emperor Xuanzong at fifteen. His rank meant he never had to sit the imperial examination. He served as governor of Chuzhou, then of Jiangzhou, then of Suzhou, where he died in 792. This is one of twelve poems of his in the anthology Three Hundred Tang Poems.
