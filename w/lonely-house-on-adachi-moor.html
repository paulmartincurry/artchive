Lonely House on Adachi Moor

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885 · Colour woodcut, diptych · Each sheet about 37 × 25 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The print appeared in 1885 and draws on the legend of the demon woman of Kurozuka, told in an 11th-century anthology and staged in noh, kabuki and jōruri. A heavily pregnant woman hangs bound by her feet over a fire while an old woman sharpens a knife. Its accurate drawing of upside-down suspension fascinated Itoh Seiu, and the print became part of the history of modern kinbaku. Another impression is in the National Gallery of Australia.
