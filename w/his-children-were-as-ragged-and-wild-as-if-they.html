His Children Were as Ragged and Wild as if They Belonged to Nobody (Rip Van Winkle)

Arthur Rackham · published 1905 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Rackham made 51 colour pieces for this edition of Rip Van Winkle, and their reproduction by colour-separated printing marked a turning point in how books were made. Heinemann's 1905 edition is what first brought him to wide public attention. Washington Irving wrote the story in a single night in June 1818, in his brother-in-law's house in Birmingham, England, after filing for bankruptcy; he admitted he had never been to the Catskills.
