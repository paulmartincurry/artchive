Earthrise from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter

Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, NASA/Goddard/Arizona State University · 2015 · Composite of spacecraft images

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/Goddard/Arizona State University

The orbiter sees twelve earthrises a day but is almost always busy photographing the lunar surface, so it rarely gets the chance to catch one. In this composite the centre of the Earth sits just off the coast of Liberia, with the Sahara in the upper right. On the Moon is the crater Compton, just beyond the eastern limb.
