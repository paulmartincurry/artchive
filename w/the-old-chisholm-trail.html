The Old Chisholm Trail

Traditional cowboy song · 1870s

Cowboys were singing it in the 1870s, and it grew out of an English song that dates back to 1640. John Lomax first printed it in 1910 in Cowboy Songs and Other Frontier Ballads. The text is from the 1918 printing.
