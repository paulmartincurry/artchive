Typus Selenographicus (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Before this atlas, Andreas Cellarius had published a book on building fortifications, Architectura Militaris, in 1645, and a description of Poland and Lithuania in 1659, both with the same Amsterdam house, Janssonius. He is thought to have worked in Poland as a military engineer after leaving Heidelberg in the Thirty Years' War. A second edition of the atlas followed in 1661, a year after the first.
