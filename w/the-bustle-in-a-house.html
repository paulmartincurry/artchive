The sweeping up the heart,
And putting love away
We shall not want to use again
Until eternity.

The bustle in a house

Emily Dickinson · published 1890

The poems were first published as a selection in 1890, edited by Thomas Wentworth Higginson and Mabel Loomis Todd, who significantly altered them to fit the poetic conventions of the time. This is that edition's text.
