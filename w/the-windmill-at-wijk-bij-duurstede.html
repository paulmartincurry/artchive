The Windmill at Wijk bij Duurstede

Jacob van Ruisdael · c. 1668–c. 1670 · Oil on canvas · 83 × 101 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The painting is not dated, as very few of Ruisdael's works are after 1653; it is assumed that it was painted in 1670. Adriaan van der Hoop bequeathed it to the city in 1854, and it has been on loan to the Rijksmuseum since 30 June 1885. It ranks third in the museum's card sales.
