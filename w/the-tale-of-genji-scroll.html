The Tale of Genji Scroll

Artist unknown · ca. 1120–1140 · Illustrated handscroll · Tokugawa Art Museum, Nagoya

Free to use

The original ran to twenty rolls, about 450 feet in all, with more than 100 paintings. Nineteen paintings survive, roughly 15 percent of it, split between two museums in Japan. It was made a little over a century after Murasaki Shikibu wrote the novel. Each scene was first drawn in black and white, then covered with heavy pigment, and the hidden lines were drawn back in with ink. No face in it is shown from the front. The painters are unknown.
