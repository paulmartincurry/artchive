Winchester Psalter: The Mouth of Hell (folio 39r)

Maker unknown · 12th century · Ink and coloured washes on vellum · 32 × 22.25 cm · British Library, London

Free to use

The psalter was probably made for use in Winchester, and most scholars agree that the likely patron was Henry of Blois, bishop there from 1129. It was damaged in the fire at Ashburnham House in 1731, and afterwards its leaves were cut and mounted singly.
