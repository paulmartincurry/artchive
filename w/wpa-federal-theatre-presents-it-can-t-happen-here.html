WPA Federal Theatre Presents It Can't Happen Here

Richard Halls · 1936 or 1937 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Richard Halls made the poster for the Federal Theatre's It Can't Happen Here, dramatized by Sinclair Lewis and J. C. Moffitt. It dates from 1936 or 1937 and advertised the play at the Adelphi Theatre on 54th Street.
