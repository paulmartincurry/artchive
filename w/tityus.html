Tityus

Jusepe de Ribera · 1632 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Ribera signed and dated this canvas, 227 by 301 centimetres, in 1632 in Naples, where he had settled in 1616. It was one of a series of four showing the punishments of Tityus, Sisyphus, Tantalus and Ixion; only Tityus and Ixion survive, both in the Prado. The first record of it is its purchase in 1634 by Jerónimo de Villanueva to decorate the Buen Retiro palace in Madrid.
