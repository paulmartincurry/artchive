Folio from the Blue Qur'an

Maker unknown · late 9th–early 10th century · Gold and silver on indigo-dyed parchment · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use with credit. Photo: Marie-Lan Nguyen, CC BY 2.5

Nobody knows when, where or for whom this Qur'an was made; proposals range from Spain to Persia and from the 8th century to the 10th. It is thought to have had 600 leaves of sheepskin parchment, which would have taken at least 150 sheep. The indigo was probably brushed on before the skins were stretched and dried, and the letters are gold leaf outlined in ink to tidy the edges. The leaves were dispersed under the Ottoman Empire, and about 100 are now in museums and private collections.
