Pioneer F/G Plaque

Carl Sagan, Frank Drake and Linda Salzman Sagan · 1972 · Design for an engraved aluminium plaque

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

Carl Sagan and Frank Drake designed the plaque, and Linda Salzman Sagan, then Sagan's wife, drew the artwork. Plaques of gold-anodised aluminium went into space on Pioneer 10, launched on 2 March 1972, and Pioneer 11, launched in 1973. Fourteen of the radiating lines stand for pulsars, with their periods written in binary, using the hydrogen atom drawn at top left as the unit. Sagan first meant the two figures to hold hands, but dropped it in case they were read as a single creature.
