Nursery Rhymes

Walter Crane · 1876 · Machine-printed paper · H × W × D: 35.2 × 56 cm (13 7/8 × 22 1/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

This was the second wallpaper Walter Crane designed for children, and its scenes come from nursery rhymes. He was thirty or thirty-one. From 1865 to 1876 he and the printer Edmund Evans had produced two or three toy books of nursery rhymes and fairy tales a year. The repeat on this piece is incomplete, and it came to the museum from the Essex Institute.
