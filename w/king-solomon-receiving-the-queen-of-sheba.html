King Solomon Receiving the Queen of Sheba

Master of the Antwerp Adoration Group · 1515–20 · Oil on panel transferred to canvas · 73.2 × 27.7 cm (28 7/8 × 10 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

With its companion, King David Receiving the Cistern Water, it formed the inner wings of a folding triptych with the Adoration of the Magi at its centre, by the painters now called the Antwerp Mannerists.
