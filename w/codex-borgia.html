Codex Borgia

Maker unknown · 15th century (Pre-Hispanic) · Animal skins, folded into 39 sheets · Vatican Apostolic Library

Free to use

Each sheet of the codex is a square of 27 by 27 centimetres, and unfolded the strip of hide runs to nearly 11 metres. The images were painted on both sides over a coating of white gesso, and the book is read from right to left. It is one of only a handful of Mexican codices of its kind that were not destroyed during the conquest. Cardinal Stefano Borgia owned it in the 18th century, and his collection was moved to the Vatican library in 1902.
