Utriusque Cosmi Historia, page 26: Darkness

Robert Fludd · 1617 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Robert Fludd held that everything began as dark Chaos, before divine Light acted on it. The book came out in two folio volumes in 1617 and 1618 from the de Bry press at Oppenheim, when Fludd was in his early forties.
