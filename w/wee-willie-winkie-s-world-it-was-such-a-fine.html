Wee Willie Winkie's World: It Was Such a Fine Day!

Lyonel Feininger · 1906 · Print (newspaper comic page) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Chicago Sunday Tribune printed this page on 25 November 1906. Unlike The Kin-der-Kids, Wee Willie Winkie's World had no running story; each week the boy looks out at the strange countryside around his grandfather's village. It ended on 17 February 1907, the last comic strip Feininger made. He was thirty-six when he turned to painting, and in 1919 he became the first teacher Walter Gropius appointed at the Bauhaus.
