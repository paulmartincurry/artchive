Upon the Bed There Lay a Nearly Liquid Mass of Loathsome, of Detestable Putridity (The Facts in the Case of M. Valdemar)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Poe printed this story in December 1845 without saying it was fiction, and many readers took it for a scientific report; he later called it a hoax in his letters. Clarke died of tuberculosis on 6 January 1931 in Chur, Switzerland, on his way home to Dublin. His family did not know they had to pledge to maintain the grave, and in 1946 his remains were moved to a communal grave.
