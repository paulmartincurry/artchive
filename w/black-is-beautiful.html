Black is beautiful

American civil rights movement · 1960s

A cultural movement started by African Americans in the 1960s, with roots in the Négritude movement of the 1930s. It spread to South Africa through the writings of Steve Biko and the Black Consciousness Movement. John Rock was long credited with the phrase in an 1858 speech, but the records show he did not use those words.
