Church of Saint George, Lalibela

Maker unknown · Late 12th – Early 13th Century · Volcanic tuff

Free to use with credit. Photo: Sailko, CC BY 3.0

The church was not built up but carved downwards into volcanic tuff, and that rock is its only material. It stands in a trench 25 metres by 25 metres and 30 metres deep, and is reached by a narrow man-made canyon that spirals down and becomes a tunnel near the church. King Gebre Mesqel Lalibela commissioned it, and it was among the last of the town's eleven rock-hewn churches to be made.
