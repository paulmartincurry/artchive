Book of Kells: Carpet Page (folio 33r)

Maker unknown · c. 800 · Iron gall ink and pigments on calf vellum · Trinity College Library, Dublin

Free to use

This is the only carpet page in the Book of Kells; the Lindisfarne Gospels have five and the Book of Durrow six. The Annals of Ulster record under 1007 that the book was stolen at night from the stone church at Kells. Henry Jones presented it to Trinity College in 1661.
