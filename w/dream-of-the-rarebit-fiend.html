Dream of the Rarebit Fiend

Winsor McCay · 1905 · Pen and brush and black ink on ivory illustration board with lithographed title header and printed copyright label pasted down · 54.5 × 35.4 cm · The Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

McCay began Dream of the Rarebit Fiend on 10 September 1904 for the Evening Telegram, a paper published by the New York Herald, and signed it Silas for contractual reasons. Each strip followed a dreamer who had eaten Welsh rarebit, a toasted cheese dish, and woke in the last panel regretting it. This original is drawn in ink on illustration board, with a printed title header pasted down.
