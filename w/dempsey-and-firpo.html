Dempsey and Firpo

George Bellows · 1924 · Oil on canvas · Whitney Museum of American Art

The fight took place on 14 September 1923, and Bellows painted it over the following months. He chose the moment Luis Firpo knocked Jack Dempsey out of the ring, although Dempsey won the match. The balding man at the far left is Bellows himself. The Whitney has owned the canvas since it opened in 1931.
