It is too little to call man a little world; except God, man is a diminutive to nothing. Man consists of more pieces, more parts, than the world; than the world doth, nay, than the world is.

Devotions upon Emergent Occasions — Meditation IV: It is too little to call man a little world

John Donne · 1624

The Devotions have 23 sections, each covering Donne's thoughts on a single day of his illness. The book was registered with the Stationers' Company by 9 January 1624, and it is one of only seven works attributed to Donne that were printed in his lifetime.
