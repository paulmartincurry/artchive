The eternal silence of these infinite spaces frightens me. … Man is but a reed, the most feeble thing in nature; but he is a thinking reed.

Pensées

Blaise Pascal · 1670

Translated by W. F. Trotter

Pascal died in 1662 at the age of thirty-nine, leaving draft notes for a defence of Christianity that he never completed. He is believed to have planned the order of the book and begun cutting and pasting the notes into shape. Those responsible for his effects handed them over to be edited, and they were published in 1670. Editors have disagreed ever since about the order in which the fragments should be read.
