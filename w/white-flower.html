White Flower

Georgia O'Keeffe · 1929 · Oil on canvas · 76.2 × 91.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This was the first flower painting by Georgia O'Keeffe to be purchased by an art museum. It was shown at An American Place in New York from February 1930 as White Flower, N.M., and a label on the backing gives its price as 8,000 dollars. She painted it in 1929, the year she first travelled to New Mexico.
