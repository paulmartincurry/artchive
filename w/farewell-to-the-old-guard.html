Bring me the eagle, that I may embrace it also! Ah! dear eagle, may this kiss which I give thee find an echo to the latest posterity!

Farewell to the Old Guard

Napoleon Bonaparte · April 20, 1814

Translated by Anonymous (“a member of the New York Bar”)

The coalition armies had captured Paris and forced Napoleon to abdicate, and his reign had ended on 6 April 1814, two weeks before he spoke these words. He was forty-four. He left for exile on the Mediterranean island of Elba, where he was given the title of Sovereign Prince. Ten months later he escaped with about a thousand men and marched on Paris.
