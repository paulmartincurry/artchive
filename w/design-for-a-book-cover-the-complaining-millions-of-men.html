Design for a Book Cover: The Complaining Millions of Men

Alice Cordelia Morse · ca. 1893 · Brush and gouache on paper · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Alice Cordelia Morse was about thirty when she made this design, in the years she worked as a designer at the New York Society of Decorative Art. She had started out in stained glass, under John La Farge and then Louis Comfort Tiffany, before turning to book covers. Between 1887 and 1905 she designed about eighty-one of them. A note on the back of the sheet lists the cloth for the binding: buckram or linen.
