I wept, as I remembered, how often you and I
Had tired the sun with talking and sent him down the sky.

Heraclitus

Callimachus · ca. 270 BCE

Translated by William Johnson Cory, 1858

Callimachus came from Cyrene, on the coast of modern-day Libya, and was employed at the Library of Alexandria, where he compiled a catalogue of all Greek literature. He wrote at least 60 epigrams, some of them sepulchral. Most of them survive through the Palatine Anthology, a 10th-century manuscript discovered at Heidelberg in 1606. The Suda credits him with more than 800 works, and the vast majority are lost.
