This demands an act of severe self-denial; we have to forfeit three-fourths of ourselves in order to become like other people.

Counsels and Maxims — Our Relation to Ourselves (Ch. II, Section 9)

Arthur Schopenhauer · 1851

Translated by T. Bailey Saunders

Schopenhauer settled in Frankfurt in July 1833 and stayed there for the rest of his life, apart from a few short journeys. He lived alone except for a succession of pet poodles named Atman and Butz.
