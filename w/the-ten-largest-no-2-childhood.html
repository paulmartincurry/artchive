The Ten Largest, No. 2, Childhood

Hilma af Klint · 1907 · Tempera on paper mounted on canvas · Hilma af Klint Foundation, Stockholm

Hilma af Klint was in her mid-forties in 1907 and had trained at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts in Stockholm from 1882 to 1887. She earned her income from landscapes, botanical drawings and portraits, and kept her abstract painting as a separate practice. The year after this work, she met Rudolf Steiner when he visited Stockholm. She asked that her work be kept secret for twenty years after her death.
