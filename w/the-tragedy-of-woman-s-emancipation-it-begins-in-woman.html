The right to vote, or equal civil rights, may be good demands, but true emancipation begins neither at the polls nor in courts. It begins in woman's soul.

The Tragedy of Woman's Emancipation — It Begins in Woman's Soul

Emma Goldman · 1910

Goldman turned 41 the year this book came out. In 1917 she and Alexander Berkman were sentenced to two years in jail for conspiring to induce people not to register for the new draft, and in December 1919 she was deported to Russia.
