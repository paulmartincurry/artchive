In the void is virtue, and no evil.

The Book of Five Rings — The Book of the Void

Miyamoto Musashi · 1645

Translated by Victor Harris · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Book of the Void is the short epilogue to a manual of swordsmanship in five volumes, which Miyamoto Musashi wrote between 1643 and 1645 from what he taught the students in his own school. He handed the manuscript to his student Terao Magonojō seven days before his death on 13 June 1645. By the usual count he had fought sixty-two duels without losing one, the first at twelve or thirteen.
