Frogs

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1871–89 · Pigments on paper · 184 × 47 cm · Musée Cernuschi, Paris

Free to use

One frog pulls a rickshaw, a vehicle invented in Japan in the second half of the nineteenth century, and a long lotus stalk stands in for a telegraph pole; a third frog plays the postman. Kyōsai began training at seven with Utagawa Kuniyoshi. He chose a frog-shaped stone for his own grave. The Musée Cernuschi in Paris bought the painting in 2019.
