Cross in the Mountains (Tetschen Altar)

Caspar David Friedrich · 1807–08 · Oil on canvas, in a carved and gilded frame · 115 × 110 cm · Galerie Neue Meister, Dresden

Free to use

Friedrich designed this as an altarpiece, and the frame was made by Kühn after his design. Research in 1977 found that he had conceived the painting before any commission and meant to dedicate it to King Gustav IV Adolf of Sweden. The Thun-Hohenstein family bought it and hung it in the countess's bedroom, not in their chapel. It stayed at Tetschen Castle from 1809 to 1921.
