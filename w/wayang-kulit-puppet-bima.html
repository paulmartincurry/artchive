Wayang Kulit Puppet: Bima

Maker unknown · before 1914 · Carved leather · Wereldmuseum Amsterdam

Free to use with credit. Photo: Tropenmuseum, CC BY-SA 3.0

Shadow puppets of this kind are cut from water buffalo or goat hide and fixed to sticks, and their holes are punched with small tools struck by a mallet. A large character can take months to make. The puppet represents Bima, the Mahabharata hero, whose story is named in a Javanese royal charter of 903 as the subject of a puppet show performed for the gods.
