Aconitum (Urformen der Kunst, plate 96)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · 26 × 19 cm (10 1/4 × 7 1/2 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Georges Bataille printed Blossfeldt's photographs in the magazine Documents in 1929, and Walter Benjamin wrote in praise of the book. Blossfeldt died in Berlin on 9 December 1932, aged sixty-seven.
