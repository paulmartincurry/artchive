The Grand Electric Skeleton (Gran Calavera Eléctrica)

José Guadalupe Posada · 1900 · Zincograph, type-metal engraving and letterpress on green paper · Sheet: 40 × 30.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Antonio Vanegas Arroyo published this broadside in 1900, when Posada was forty-seven or forty-eight. The sheet, 40 by 30.5 centimetres, combines a zincograph and a type-metal engraving with letterpress on green paper. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1946.
