The Repentant Magdalen

Georges de La Tour · c. 1635–40 · Oil on canvas · 113 × 92.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The stretcher carries the stencil of the Haro family, Paris restorers and sellers of art supplies, and an entry in their account book for 9 October 1877 is the earliest record of the picture. La Tour died at Lunéville in 1652 and his work was forgotten until the German art historian Hermann Voss rediscovered it in 1915. The National Gallery of Art bought this canvas in 1974.
