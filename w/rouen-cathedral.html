Rouen Cathedral

Claude Monet · 1893 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

Monet rented rooms across the street from Rouen Cathedral to use as a studio, and painted its front more than thirty times in 1892 and 1893. He reworked the canvases at home in 1894, building the paint up in thick layers. In 1895 he chose twenty for his Paris dealer's gallery and sold eight before the exhibition closed. There were calls for the state to buy the whole series and keep it together, but it was divided.
