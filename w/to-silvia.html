…and from the distance,
with your hand you pointed
towards death’s coldness and the silent grave.

To Silvia (A Silvia)

Giacomo Leopardi · 1828

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Leopardi drafted the poem in Pisa on 19 and 20 April 1828, made the final copy on 29 September, and published it in the 1831 edition of the Canti. A long tradition, resting partly on his own writings, identifies Silvia with Teresa Fattorini, daughter of the family's coachman, who died young of tuberculosis.
