Sawmill, Outskirts of Paris

Henri Rousseau · c. 1893/95 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Henri Rousseau was forty-nine in 1893 when he retired from his job collecting taxes on goods entering Paris and moved to a studio in Montparnasse. This canvas, 25.5 centimetres high, dates from his first years of painting full time. He had no academic training, and he added to a small pension with part-time jobs. Kate Brewster left the picture to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1950.
