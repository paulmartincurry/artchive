Conversion on the Way to Damascus

Caravaggio · 1601 · Oil on canvas · 230 × 175 cm · Santa Maria del Popolo, Rome

Free to use

The contract of 24 September 1600 gave Caravaggio eight months and 400 scudi to paint two pictures for the chapel of Tiberio Cerasi. Cerasi died on 3 May 1601 while the work was still going on, and the fee was cut to 300 scudi for reasons that are not known. The paintings were installed on 1 May 1605. Giovanni Baglione reported in 1642 that first versions of both had been rejected.
