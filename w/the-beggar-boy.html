The Beggar Boy (The Young Pilgrim)

Giovanni Battista Piazzetta · 1738–39 · Oil on canvas · 67.7 × 54.7 cm (26 5/8 × 21 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The painting was long called a beggar boy. It is now thought to show the artist's oldest child, Giacomo, dressed as a pilgrim bound for Santiago de Compostela; he would have been 13 years old.
