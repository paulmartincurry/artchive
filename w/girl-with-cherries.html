Girl with Cherries (Jeune Fille aux cerises)

Eva Gonzalès · ca. 1870 · Oil on canvas · 56.2 × 47.4 cm (22 1/16 × 18 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Eva Gonzalès began studying with Édouard Manet in February 1869, and she was the only formal student he had. This canvas belonged to William M. Chase of New York, who sold it at auction there in 1896 under the title Fruit Girl. Gonzalès died in childbirth in 1883, five days after Manet's death.
