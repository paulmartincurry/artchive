The father now gallops, with terror half wild,
He grasps in his arms the poor shuddering child;
He reaches his courtyard with toil and with dread,—
The child in his arms finds he motionless, dead.

The Erl-King

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1782

Translated by Edgar Alfred Bowring

Goethe wrote this ballad as part of a Singspiel of 1782, Die Fischerin. At least a hundred composers have set it to music. Franz Schubert composed his setting in 1815 at the age of 18, and a friend claimed it was written in a few hours one afternoon. When the song was sent to Goethe in the hope of his approval, he sent it back without comment.
