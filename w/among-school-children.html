O chestnut tree, great rooted blossomer,
Are you the leaf, the blossom or the bole?
O body swayed to music, O brightening glance,
How can we know the dancer from the dance?

Among School Children (stanza VIII)

W. B. Yeats · 1928

The Tower, published in 1928, was Yeats's first major collection after he received the Nobel Prize in 1923. Its title refers to Ballylee Castle, a Norman tower he bought and restored in 1917 and renamed Thoor Ballylee; he often spent summers there with his family until 1928. Every poem in the book had already appeared in print elsewhere.
