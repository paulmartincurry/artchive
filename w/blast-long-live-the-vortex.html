BLAST: Long Live the Vortex!

Wyndham Lewis · 1914

BLAST was launched at a dinner at the Dieudonné Hotel in St James's, London, on 15 July 1914. Its title had been suggested by C. R. W. Nevinson, and the first issue ran to 158 pages, most of it the work of Lewis. In August Britain declared war on Germany, and there was little appetite for avant-garde art.
