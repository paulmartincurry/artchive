Sarah, do not mourn me dead, but think I am gone and waiting for thee—for we shall meet again.

Letter to Sarah Ballou

Sullivan Ballou · 1861

Sullivan Ballou, a Rhode Island lawyer and a major in the 2nd Rhode Island Infantry, wrote this letter to his wife from Camp Clark in Washington on 14 July 1861. A week later a cannonball tore off part of his right leg at the First Battle of Bull Run, and he died of his injuries at the age of thirty-two. The letter is said to have been found in his trunk. The Governor of Rhode Island delivered it to Sarah Ballou in person. She never remarried.
