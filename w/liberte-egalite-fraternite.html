Liberté, égalité, fraternité

French Revolution · 1790

The first official mention of the phrase is in the 35th issue of Camille Desmoulins's Révolutions de France et de Brabant, published on 26 July 1790. At the Fête de la Fédération that month he described citizen-soldiers promising each other liberty, equality, fraternity. A fourth word, death, was later dropped.
