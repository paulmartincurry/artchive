Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips...
Then flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand.

Isaiah 6

Book of Isaiah (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · ca. 740 BCE (trans. 1611)

The chapter dates itself to the year that King Uzziah died, which has been estimated as around 740 BCE. It was written in Hebrew and is divided into 13 verses. The historic Isaiah lived in the Kingdom of Judah during the reigns of four kings. The Great Isaiah Scroll, the best preserved of the biblical scrolls found at Qumran, contains every verse of the chapter.
