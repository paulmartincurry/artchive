Colour is the keyboard, the eyes are the hammers, the soul is the piano with many strings. The artist is the hand which plays, touching one key or another, to cause vibrations in the soul.

Concerning the Spiritual in Art: Colour Is the Keyboard

Wassily Kandinsky · 1911

Translated by M. T. H. Sadler, 1914

Kandinsky was thirty when he gave up a career teaching law and economics in Moscow and went to Munich to study painting. The Munich Academy did not admit him at first, and he began learning on his own. This book was published in Munich in 1911, fifteen years after he arrived.
