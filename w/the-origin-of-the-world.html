The Origin of the World (L'Origine du monde)

Gustave Courbet · 1866 · Oil on canvas · 55 × 46 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Courbet painted it in 1866 for the Ottoman diplomat Khalil Bey. A later owner, the psychoanalyst Jacques Lacan, had his stepbrother André Masson build a double frame to hide it behind another picture; after Lacan's death it went to the French state in lieu of inheritance tax. In 2011 Facebook took it down, and in 2018 a French court ruled that Facebook had been wrong to close the account that posted it.
