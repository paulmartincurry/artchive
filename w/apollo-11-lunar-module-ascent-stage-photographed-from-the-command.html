Apollo 11 Lunar Module Ascent Stage Photographed from the Command Module (AS11-44-6642)

NASA (Apollo 11 crew) · 21 July 1969 · Photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

The Lunar Module ascent stage, carrying Neil Armstrong and Edwin Aldrin, was photographed from the Command and Service Modules on 21 July 1969 as it approached to dock. Michael Collins had stayed in lunar orbit while the other two explored the surface. The dark area below is Smyth's Sea, and the Earth rises above the lunar horizon.
