Demon in Chains

Muhammad Siyah Qalam · c. 1453 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Image 25.7 × 34.4 cm; overall 27 × 35.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The page once belonged to a miscellany in the library of the early Ottoman sultans. Cleveland catalogues it in the style of Muhammad Siyah Qalam, the name attached to some 80 paintings and drawings, most of them in an album in the Topkapı Palace Library; scholars now broadly agree that these are likely the work of many hands. The museum bought this one at Sotheby's in New York on 19 May 1982.
