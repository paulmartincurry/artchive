The Diet (La Diète)

Jehan Georges Vibert · late 19th century · Oil on canvas · Private collection

Free to use

Vibert's cardinals sold well, above all in America, where John Jacob Astor IV and William Vanderbilt commissioned him. A large group of his pictures given to a Catholic seminary in Miami was later kept out of view by bishops uneasy with the paintings' digs at the clergy.
