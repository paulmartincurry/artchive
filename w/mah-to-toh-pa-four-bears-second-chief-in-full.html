Máh-to-tóh-pa, Four Bears, Second Chief, in Full Dress

George Catlin · 1832 · Oil on canvas · 73.7 × 60.9 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

Four Bears agreed to stand for his portrait early in the morning and, by Catlin's account, arrived with a great crowd of women and children. In the same year, 1832, Catlin secured a robe recording the chief's deeds. Four Bears died on July 30, 1837, of smallpox, in the epidemic that killed most of the Mandan; Catlin claimed he had starved himself to death out of grief.
