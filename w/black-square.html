Black Square

Kazimir Malevich · 1915 · Oil on canvas · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

The black square first appeared in Kazimir Malevich's stage designs for the opera Victory over the Sun, performed in Saint Petersburg on 3 December 1913, and the back of this canvas is inscribed with that year. He painted it in 1915 in broad strokes of thick black oil paint on linen 79.5 cm square. X-rays taken in 2015 found two earlier compositions underneath. Under Stalin it was hidden and neglected in the Soviet archives, and it was not exhibited again until the 1980s.
