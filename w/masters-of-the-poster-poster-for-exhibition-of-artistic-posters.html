Masters of the Poster: Poster for Exhibition of Artistic Posters of Wilhelm Söborg

Paul Fischer · c. 1895 · Color lithograph · Sheet: 39.9 x 28.9 cm; Image: 27.6 x 20 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is a small plate from Masters of the Poster, the series of reduced reproductions of posters that Jules Chéret started in 1895. Paul Fischer was a Copenhagen painter who spent 1891 to 1895 in Paris and took up posters under the influence of Steinlen and Toulouse-Lautrec. The original advertised an exhibition of artistic posters held by Wilhelm Söborg.
