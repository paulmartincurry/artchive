Lambayeque (Sicán) Funerary Mask

Maker unknown · 900–1100 CE · Gold, silver-copper alloy, cinnabar · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The mask is nearly half a metre wide, made of gold and a silver-copper alloy and coloured with cinnabar. It comes from the north coast of Peru. Alice K. Bache gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in stages, as a gift in 1974 and a bequest in 1977.
