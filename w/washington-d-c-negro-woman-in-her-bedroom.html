Washington (Southwest Section), D.C. Negro Woman in Her Bedroom

Gordon Parks · November 1942 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Parks made this negative in the southwest section of Washington in November 1942. An exhibition of his photographs in 1941 had won him a photography fellowship with the Farm Security Administration, paid for by the Rosenwald Fund, and he produced photographs for the agency in 1942 and 1943.
