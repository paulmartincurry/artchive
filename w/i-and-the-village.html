I and the Village

Marc Chagall · 1911 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

The canvas is nearly two metres tall, 192.1 by 151.4 centimetres. Marc Chagall painted it in 1911, when he was twenty-three or twenty-four, in the years before the First World War when he was moving between Saint Petersburg, Paris and Berlin. He was the eldest of nine children from a Jewish family near Vitebsk. His father carried barrels for a herring merchant and his mother sold groceries from their home.
