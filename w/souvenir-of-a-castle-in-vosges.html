Souvenir of a Castle in Vosges

Victor Hugo · 1857 · Brush and iron gall washes, pen and iron gall ink, white gouache; outline of castle obtained by using a paper stencil · Sheet: 47 × 31 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hugo got the outline of the castle by using a paper stencil, then worked the sheet with brush and pen in iron gall ink and washes and with white gouache. Drawing began for him as a casual hobby. Between 1848 and 1851 it was his only creative outlet.
