Bon Voyage (Los Caprichos, plate 64)

Francisco de Goya · 1799 · Etching, burnished aquatint, burin · Plate: 21.5 × 15.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya wrote a short explanation of each print in a manuscript that is now in the Museo del Prado. The title etched under each image was his too, and he kept many of them vague on purpose to protect himself.
