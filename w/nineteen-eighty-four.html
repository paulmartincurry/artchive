Who controls the past controls the future: who controls the present controls the past.

Nineteen Eighty-Four

George Orwell · 1949

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Most of the book was written at Barnhill, a farmhouse on the Scottish island of Jura with no electricity or hot water. Orwell finished the first draft in bed on 7 November 1947 and was then diagnosed with tuberculosis. No typist could be found for the second draft, so he retyped it himself at about 4,000 words a day, with fever and coughing fits, and posted it on 4 December 1948. In the drafts the year was first 1980, then 1982. He died in January 1950, seven months after publication.
