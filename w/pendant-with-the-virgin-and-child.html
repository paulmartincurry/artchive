Pendant with the Virgin and Child

Godefroid de Huy · c. 1160–1170 · Gilded copper, champlevé enamel; the reverse decorated in vernis brun · 19.8 x 17 x 3.2 cm (7 13/16 x 6 11/16 x 1 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Four small enamels show angels for Humility, Virginity, Piety and Mercy, each named. The pendant may have held a consecrated host.
