He Must Have Died if the Little Mermaid Had Not Come to the Rescue (The Mermaid)

Edmund Dulac · published 1911 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Andersen wrote The Little Mermaid in 1836, and C. A. Reitzel published it in Copenhagen on 7 April 1837 in a collection of nine fairy tales for children. The plate shows the storm that sinks the prince's ship, when the mermaid saves him from drowning. After the First World War the market for deluxe gift books like this one shrank, and Dulac moved on to magazines, stamps and banknotes.
