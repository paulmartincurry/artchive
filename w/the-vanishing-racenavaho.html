The Vanishing Race—Navaho

Edward S. Curtis · c. 1904 · Platinum print, sepia toned · 15.7 × 20.3 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Edward S. Curtis copyrighted this photograph in 1904 and later made it plate 1 of the first supplementary portfolio of The North American Indian. In 1906 J. P. Morgan gave him $75,000 to produce that work, planned as 20 volumes with 1,500 photographs. This print reached the Library of Congress in 1934 as part of the Louise Imogen Guiney Collection.
