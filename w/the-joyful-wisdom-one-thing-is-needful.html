One Thing is Needful.—To "give style" to one's character—that is a grand and a rare art!

The Joyful Wisdom — One Thing Is Needful (290)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1882; English 1910

Translated by Thomas Common

The title was first put into English as The Joyful Wisdom. The Gay Science became the usual translation after Walter Kaufmann's version in the 1960s.
