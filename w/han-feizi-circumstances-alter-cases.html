Han Feizi — Circumstances Alter Cases

Han Fei · ca. 240 BCE (Warring States Period)

Translated by Burton Watson, 1964 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Han Fei was born into the ruling family of the state of Han. He is said to have had a stutter that kept him from arguing his case at court, so he put his advice in writing, and his king ignored it. The essays reached the king of Qin, who invited him to his court. There a former fellow student, Li Si, had him imprisoned and forced him to drink poison in 233 BC. The book that carries his name runs to 55 chapters and was first called simply the Hanzi, Master Han.
