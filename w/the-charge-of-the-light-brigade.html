Into the valley of Death
Rode the six hundred.

The Charge of the Light Brigade

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1854

The charge took place on 25 October 1854 at Balaclava, in the Crimean War, and the poem was in print six weeks later. About 670 British light cavalry rode down a valley at a Russian battery because an order from the commander-in-chief, who meant them to attack something else, was misunderstood. The officer who carried the order was killed in the first minute. Tennyson had been Poet Laureate for four years.
