Dendera Zodiac

Maker unknown · ca. 50 BCE · Sandstone · Louvre

Free to use

This relief was the ceiling of a chapel to Osiris in the temple of Hathor at Dendera. Vivant Denon drew it during Napoleon's campaign in Egypt and published engravings in 1802. An antique dealer, Sébastien Louis Saulnier, then hired Claude Lelorrain to cut it out with saws, jacks, scissors and gunpowder, and it reached Paris in 1821. It hung in the Royal Library for a century before moving to the Louvre in 1922. Two eclipses shown on it, of 52 and 51 BC, are the basis for its date.
