About Two Squares: A Suprematist Tale of Two Squares in Six Constructions

El Lissitzky · 1922 · Book with letterpress and offset lithography, in black and red, on cream wove paper, stapled along spine · 21.7 × 26.8 × 0.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Lissitzky made the book in Vitebsk in 1920 and published it in Berlin in 1922, and it then appeared in the magazine De Stijl. It is a children's book of six plates in which a red square and a black square fly to earth from space. He had left Vitebsk for Berlin in 1921 as a cultural emissary of Soviet Russia, charged with making contacts between Russian and German artists.
