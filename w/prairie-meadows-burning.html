Prairie Meadows Burning

George Catlin · 1832 · Oil on canvas · 27.8 × 35.9 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

Catlin had seen prairie fires himself. The canvas is small, 28 by 36 centimetres. In 1852 his debts forced him to sell the whole Indian Gallery, by then 607 paintings, to the industrialist Joseph Harrison, who stored it in a factory in Philadelphia. It came to the Smithsonian as the gift of Mrs. Joseph Harrison, Jr.
