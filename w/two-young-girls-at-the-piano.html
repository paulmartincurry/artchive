Two Young Girls at the Piano

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1892 · Oil on canvas · 44 × 34 in. (111.8 × 86.4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Renoir turned fifty-one in 1892, and around that year he developed rheumatoid arthritis. Two years earlier he had married Aline Charigot, a dressmaker twenty years younger than he was. He went on painting for the rest of his life; as the arthritis advanced he could still grasp a brush, but an assistant had to place it in his hand. The canvas is a little over a metre tall and came to the museum with the Robert Lehman Collection in 1975.
