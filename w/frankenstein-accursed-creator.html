Accursed creator! Why did you form a monster so hideous that even you turned from me in disgust? God, in pity, made man beautiful and alluring, after his own image; but my form is a filthy type of yours, more horrid even from the very resemblance.

Frankenstein — Accursed creator (Ch. 15)

Mary Shelley · 1818

Mary Shelley wrote the first four chapters in the weeks after her half-sister Fanny took her own life. Her first child had died in infancy, and when she began the book in 1816 she was probably nursing her second, who was also dead by the time it was published. Her manuscripts and the fair copy she made for the publisher have been in the Bodleian Library in Oxford since 2004.
