Cubiculum (Bedroom) from the Villa of P. Fannius Synistor at Boscoreale

Maker unknown · ca. 50–40 BCE · Fresco · 265.4 × 334 × 583.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The villa at Boscoreale was buried by Vesuvius in 79 AD. It was privately discovered, excavated, partly dismantled and reburied in 1900, and its frescoes were auctioned and are now scattered around the world. The Metropolitan Museum bought this bedroom in 1903. The paint survived the heat because it held no organic pigments; some of the yellow ochre turned red, which shows the temperature passed 300 degrees Celsius.
