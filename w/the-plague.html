The Plague

Arnold Böcklin · 1898 · Tempera on fir panel · 105.1 × 149.5 cm · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

Böcklin painted this in tempera on a fir panel about a metre and a half wide, in 1898, when he was seventy or seventy-one and living at San Domenico near Florence. He had nearly died of typhoid himself in 1859, and he died in Fiesole in January 1901. The Gottfried Keller Foundation placed the picture in the Basel museum in 1902.
