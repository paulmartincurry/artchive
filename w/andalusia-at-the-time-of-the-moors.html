Andalusia at the Time of the Moors

Eugène Grasset · c. 1900 · Color lithograph · Sheet: 133.2 x 88 cm; Image: 124.7 x 84.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

L'Andalousie au temps des Maures was an open-air attraction at the 1900 Paris Exposition, on the Quai Debilly at the western end of the Trocadéro. It had full-scale copies of Moorish buildings from the Alhambra, Córdoba, Toledo and Seville and an 80-metre copy of the Giralda. It was a French production with no connection to Spain's official presence at the fair.
