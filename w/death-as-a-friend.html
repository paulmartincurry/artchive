Death as a Friend (Der Tod als Freund)

Alfred Rethel · 1851 · wood engraving on wove paper [fourth edition] · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Rethel is said to have made this design in expiation for his skeleton at a masked ball, which had given his friends nightmares. The impression dates from 1851, the year he married Marie Grahl in Dresden. His mental illness had shown itself from 1846, and he died in Düsseldorf in 1859, aged forty-three, cared for by his wife and his brother Otto.
