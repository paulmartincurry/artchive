The Feast of the Gods

Giovanni Bellini and Titian · 1514/1529 · Oil on canvas · 170.2 × 188 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Giovanni Bellini signed and dated the canvas 1514 on a paper attached to the wooden tub at lower right, and his payment that year is recorded. It was made for the Duke of Ferrara's chamber of alabaster, and Dosso Dossi and Titian later repainted parts of the landscape. A cardinal acting as papal legate confiscated it from the castle at Ferrara in 1598.
