Had we lived, I should have had a tale to tell of the hardihood, endurance, and courage of my companions which would have stirred the heart of every Englishman. These rough notes and our dead bodies must tell the tale …

Message to the Public (closing)

Robert Falcon Scott · March 1912 (published 1913)

A planned meeting with supporting dog teams from the base camp failed on the return from the Pole. Scott and his companions died 162 miles from their base camp at Hut Point and about 12.5 miles from the next depot. He led two Antarctic expeditions, the Discovery Expedition of 1901–1904 and this one, the Terra Nova Expedition of 1910–1913.
