"Ah Yes, Those Comets, They Always Predict Great Misfortunes"

Honoré Daumier · October 30, 1858 · Lithograph in black on white wove paper · Image: 20.4 × 25.1 cm (8 1/16 × 9 15/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Censorship under Louis Napoleon pushed Daumier from politics to social subjects. A comet was a welcome one: he drew ten comet prints, then ten more when Parisians loved them.
