How is there laughter, how is there joy, as this world is always burning? Why do you not seek a light, ye who are surrounded by darkness?

The Dhammapada — Old Age (verses 146–151)

The Dhammapada · ca. 3rd century BCE; English 1881

Translated by F. Max Müller

The title joins two Pāli words: dhamma, the Buddha's doctrine, and pada, a foot, and so a path or a verse. The collection is part of the Khuddaka Nikāya, a division of the Pāli Canon of Theravāda Buddhism. The commentator Buddhaghosa explains that each saying was made on a different occasion, in answer to something that had happened in the life of the Buddha and his monastic community.
