Death Appearing to a Wedded Couple from an Open Grave

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1639 · Etching · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Rembrandt made this etching in 1639, the year he and his wife Saskia moved into a recently modernised house on the Breestraat in Amsterdam, among artists and art dealers. The mortgage on the 13,000-guilder purchase later became a cause of his money troubles, and the house was sold at a foreclosure auction in 1658.
