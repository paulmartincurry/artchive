Follow thine heart during thy lifetime; do not more than is commanded thee. Diminish not the time of following the heart; it is abhorred of the soul, that its time [of ease] be taken away.

The Instruction of Ptah-hotep — Follow Thine Heart (11)

Vizier Ptahhotep · Middle Kingdom (c. 2040–1782 BCE); English 1906

Translated by Battiscombe G. Gunn

Gunn was 22 or 23 when this translation, made from the Prisse Papyrus in Paris, came out in 1906; it was his first published translation from Egyptian. The papyrus, the only complete copy of the text, was found at Thebes in 1847 by Émile Prisse d'Avennes.
