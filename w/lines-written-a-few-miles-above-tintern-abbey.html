And I have felt
A presence that disturbs me with the joy
Of elevated thoughts; a sense sublime
Of something far more deeply interfused,
Whose dwelling is the light of setting suns,

Lines Written a Few Miles above Tintern Abbey (lines 89-103)

William Wordsworth · 1798

Wordsworth said he composed this poem entirely in his head, beginning as he left Tintern and not jotting down a line until he reached Bristol. He was twenty-eight, on a walking tour with his sister Dorothy, and had first visited the Wye five years earlier. Lyrical Ballads was already being published, and he had the poem inserted at the last moment as the final one in the book. The abbey itself does not appear in it.
