I too am not a bit tamed, I too am untranslatable,
I sound my barbaric yawp over the roofs of the world.

I Stop Somewhere Waiting for You (Song of Myself, 52)

Walt Whitman · 1855

Whitman began writing what became Leaves of Grass as early as 1850. At the end of June 1855 he surprised his brothers with the first edition, already printed. His father died on July 11, a few days after the book came out, at the age of 65.
