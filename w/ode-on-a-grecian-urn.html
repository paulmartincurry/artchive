Beauty is truth, truth beauty,—that is all
Ye know on earth, and all ye need to know.

Ode on a Grecian Urn

John Keats · 1819

The idea came from two articles by the painter Benjamin Haydon in the Examiner of 2 May and 9 May 1819. Keats looked at prints of Greek urns at Haydon's office and traced an engraving of the Sosibios Vase, a marble krater in the Louvre, from a book of antique vases. He was twenty-three, short of money and living with his friend Charles Brown. The ode was first printed in January 1820 in an art magazine, without his name.
