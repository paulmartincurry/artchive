Bricklayer

August Sander · 1928, published 1929 · Black-and-white photograph

Free to use

The bricklayer, from Cologne, is unnamed. Sander left his own shadow in the picture, falling across the lower left. It was one of sixty portraits in his 1929 book Face of Our Time, with an essay by Alfred Döblin; under the Nazi regime the book was seized in 1936 and its plates destroyed. A print sold at Sotheby's New York on 11 December 2014 for $749,000.
