Am I Not a Man and a Brother?

Society for the Abolition of the Slave Trade · 1787

Josiah Wedgwood produced and distributed the medallion in 1787 as a seal for the Society for the Abolition of the Slave Trade. It shows a kneeling Black man in chains with his hands raised. The figure was probably modelled by Henry Webber and William Hackwood.
