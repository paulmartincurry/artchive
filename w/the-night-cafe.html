The Night Café (Le café de nuit)

Vincent van Gogh · September 1888 · Oil on canvas · 72.4 × 92.1 cm · Yale University Art Gallery, New Haven

Free to use

In the first days of September 1888 Van Gogh sat up three nights in a row to paint this room, sleeping during the day. It is the Café de la Gare at 30 Place Lamartine in Arles, run by Joseph-Michel Ginoux and his wife Marie, where he had a room. He used the picture to settle debts with Ginoux. It later belonged to Ivan Morozov in Moscow, was nationalized and sold by the Soviet authorities in the 1930s, and in 2016 the United States Supreme Court turned down a claim to it by a man said to be Morozov's great-grandson.
