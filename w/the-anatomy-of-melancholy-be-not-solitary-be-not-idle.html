Only take this for a corollary and conclusion, as thou tenderest thine own welfare in this, and all other melancholy, thy good health of body and mind, observe this short precept, give not way to solitariness and idleness. Be not solitary, be not idle.

The Anatomy of Melancholy — Be not solitary, be not idle (closing words)

Robert Burton · 1621

Burton's epitaph in Christ Church Cathedral, which is believed to have been written by Burton himself, names him as Democritus Junior. He published five revised and expanded editions of the book during his lifetime.
