Landscape with the Fall of Icarus

After Pieter Bruegel the Elder · c. 1560 · Oil on canvas · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium

Free to use

Nothing was known of this painting until the Brussels museum bought it in 1912. After technical examination in 1996 it came to be seen as an early copy, by an unknown hand, of a lost original by Bruegel, though later research has reopened the question. A paint sample showed a chalk ground beneath the original blue, which means the picture was painted on a wooden panel and later transferred to canvas; that transfer and two relinings damaged it and led to heavy overpainting. A second version, on wood, has Daedalus still in the sky.
