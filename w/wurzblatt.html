Wurzblatt (Wallpaper Design)

Dagobert Peche · c. 1915 · Ink, on paper; screen printed · 17.3 × 41.3 cm (6 7/8 × 16 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The pattern repeats about every twelve centimetres. Dagobert Peche designed it around 1915, the year he joined the Wiener Werkstätte in his late twenties, and it was manufactured in Vienna by F. O. Schmidt. He died in 1923, aged thirty-six.
