Paris Street; Rainy Day

Gustave Caillebotte · 1877 · Oil on canvas · 212.2 × 276.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The crossing is today's Place de Dublin in the eighth arrondissement, a short walk from the family mansion where Caillebotte lived. Twenty-three studies in graphite and oil survive. He was twenty-eight when it was shown at the Third Impressionist Exhibition in April 1877, a show he helped pay for out of his inheritance. The painting then stayed with his family, almost unknown, until Walter P. Chrysler Jr. bought it in 1954; Chicago acquired it in 1964.
