Ruin by the Sea

Arnold Böcklin · 1881 · Oil on fabric · 111 × 82 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Böcklin signed the canvas with his initials at the lower left. He painted it in 1881, at fifty-three or fifty-four, while he was working in Florence, and it is 111 centimetres high. A collector in St. Gallen owned it by 1893. It was sold at auction in Zürich in 1933 under the title Ruine am Meer, and the Cleveland Museum of Art bought it in 1979.
