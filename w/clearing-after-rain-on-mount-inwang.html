Clearing After Rain on Mount Inwang (Inwang jesaekdo)

Jeong Seon · Late May 1751 · Ink on paper · National Museum of Korea

Free to use

Jeong Seon painted this view in Cheongun-dong, in what is now the Jongno District of Seoul, during the reign of King Yeongjo. It is 79.2 cm high and 138.2 cm wide. South Korea made it the 216th National Treasure on 6 August 1984. It belonged to Lee Kun-hee, and after his death in 2020 it was donated to the National Museum of Korea.
