Zhuangzi — Autumn Floods

Zhuangzi (Master Zhuang) · ca. 3rd Century BCE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The only complete version of the Zhuangzi has 33 chapters, prepared around AD 300 by the scholar Guo Xiang, who reduced it from an earlier form of 52. His text is about 63,000 characters long, around two-thirds of the length attested for the Han-era manuscript. Guo stated that he had made considerable edits to the outer and miscellaneous chapters. Fragments from the early Tang dynasty were found among the Dunhuang manuscripts and are now held at the British Library and the Bibliothèque nationale de France.
