Egyptian No. 3 (The Grammar of Ornament, Plate VI)

Owen Jones · published 1856 · Lithograph printed in colours · book: 57 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

The Grammar of Ornament came out in London in 1856 with one hundred folio plates, drawn on stone by Francis Bedford and printed in colours by Day and Son. Owen Jones, an architect, had made his name with his studies of the Alhambra, a publication that set new standards in chromolithography and strained his finances. This plate is the third of the book's Egyptian plates.
