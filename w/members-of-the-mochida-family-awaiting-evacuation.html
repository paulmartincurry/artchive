Members of the Mochida Family Awaiting Evacuation

Dorothea Lange · 8 May 1942 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

Dorothea Lange took this photograph in Hayward, California, on 8 May 1942. Her caption says the family were waiting for the evacuation bus, that the tags were used to keep each family together, and that Mochida had run a nursery and five greenhouses on a two-acre site where he raised snapdragons and sweet peas. After Pearl Harbor Lange had given up a fellowship to work for the War Relocation Authority.
