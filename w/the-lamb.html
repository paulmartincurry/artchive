Little lamb, who made thee?
Does thou know who made thee,

The Lamb

William Blake · 1789

Blake was in his early thirties when Songs of Innocence came out in 1789. He had trained as an engraver, apprenticed at fourteen to James Basire for seven years, and his wife Catherine Boucher worked on his books as printer and colourist. The poem may have been meant to be sung, but no record of Blake's own tune survives. John Tavener wrote his setting of it in a single afternoon, for his nephew's third birthday.
