Poker

Félix Vallotton · 1896 · Woodcut · Sheet: 25.3 × 31.8 cm (9 15/16 × 12 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Vallotton made more than 200 woodcuts, the first of them a portrait of Paul Verlaine in 1891. His method was to make a very precise and detailed drawing and then simplify it again and again. This one came five years in, and the image is about 18 by 22 centimetres.
