Alphabet

Lucas Kilian · 1627 · Book with 24 etchings, bound in crimson levant with marbled endpapers, outer slip case · Art Institute of Chicago

Lucas Kilian made these twenty-four etched letters ten years before his death, at about forty-eight. He had learned engraving in Augsburg under his stepfather, Dominicus Custos, and specialised in portraits, mythology and ornament; nearly 1,050 works have been identified as his. Each sheet is under seventeen centimetres tall. The year before this alphabet, he produced a bird's eye view of Augsburg.
