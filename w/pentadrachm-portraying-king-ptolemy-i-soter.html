Pentadrachm (Coin) Portraying King Ptolemy I Soter

Maker unknown · 285-247 BCE, issued by King Ptolemy II Philadelphos · Gold · Diam.: 2.4 cm (1 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Ptolemy II put his father, the founder of the dynasty, on this coin; the magical aegis he wears and the eagle and thunderbolt on the back all point to Zeus.
