Beggars

Käthe Kollwitz · 1924 · Charcoal · Approx. 38.4 × 28.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Kollwitz drew this in charcoal in 1924, when she was fifty-six or fifty-seven, on a sheet about 38 by 29 centimetres. She signed it in graphite at the lower right. It is in the Rosenwald Collection at the National Gallery of Art.
