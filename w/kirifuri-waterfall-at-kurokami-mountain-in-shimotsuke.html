Kirifuri Waterfall at Kurokami Mountain in Shimotsuke (A Tour of Waterfalls in Various Provinces)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1832 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 37.1 × 26.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Waterfalls series has eight prints, all of them upright, and was completed around 1833 to 1834. Hokusai printed it with Prussian blue, a newly imported pigment that was fashionable at the time, and set the blue against yellows, browns and greens. This impression came to the Metropolitan Museum in the bequest of Henry L. Phillips in 1939.
