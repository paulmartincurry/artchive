The Artchive
An old, mad, blind, despised, and dying king,—
Princes, the dregs of their dull race, who flow
Through public scorn,—mud from a muddy spring,—

England in 1819

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1819; published 1839

The sonnet was written in September 1819, a month after peaceful demonstrators were killed in Manchester on 16 August. George III had then been king for fifty-nine years. Shelley, then living in Italy, sent it to his friend Leigh Hunt, who judged it too risky to print, since writing in sympathy with the demonstrators counted as seditious libel. It stayed unpublished until Mary Shelley's four-volume edition of his poems, seventeen years after his death.
