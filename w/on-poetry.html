If I feel physically as if the top of my head were taken off, I know that is poetry.

On Poetry (remark to T. W. Higginson, 1870)

Emily Dickinson · 1870

Dickinson was thirty-nine for most of 1870. Her formal schooling had been seven years at Amherst Academy, where the classes included Latin, botany and geology, and ten months at Mount Holyoke Female Seminary, after which she returned home to Amherst. Her father, a lawyer, was treasurer of Amherst College from 1835 to 1873. She kept her herbarium beside her botany textbook throughout her life.
