Flying Dutchman

Albert Pinkham Ryder · completed by 1887 · Oil on canvas mounted on fiberboard · 36.1 × 43.8 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum

Free to use

Ryder was born in New Bedford, Massachusetts, a busy whaling port, the youngest of four sons. His family moved to New York City in 1867 or 1868. This painting was completed by 1887, the last year he exhibited with the Society of American Artists, of which he had been a founding member in 1877.
