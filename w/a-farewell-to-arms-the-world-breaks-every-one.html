The world breaks every one and afterward many are strong at the broken places.

A Farewell to Arms — The world breaks every one (Ch. 34)

Ernest Hemingway · 1929

The novel was first serialized in Scribner's Magazine, from the May to the October 1929 issue. The book was published on September 27, 1929, in a first printing of approximately 31,000 copies. By his own count Hemingway wrote 39 versions of the ending before he was satisfied.
