Welcome, O life! I go to encounter for the millionth time the reality of experience and to forge in the smithy of my soul the uncreated conscience of my race.

A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man — Welcome, O life! (closing)

James Joyce · 1916

The book was begun on Joyce's twenty-second birthday as a realist novel called Stephen Hero, planned in 63 chapters. He abandoned it in September 1907 after 25 chapters and 914 manuscript pages, and started again. In 1911, angry at publishers' refusals of Dubliners, he threw the new manuscript into the fire, and his sister Eileen helped pull it out. No British publisher would take the finished novel, so it was first issued in New York, on 29 December 1916.
