Demon Intoning the Name of the Buddha (Oni no Nembutsu)

Japan, Edo period · 1700s · Hanging scroll, ink and color on paper · Painting only: 59.2 × 22.1 cm (23 5/16 × 8 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Pictures of this kind are called Otsu-e. They were made as souvenirs for travellers passing through the station of Otsu on the Tokaido, the road from Edo to Kyoto, using a mix of woodblock printing, rapid brushstrokes and colour added by stencil or by hand. The earliest, from the 17th century, were Buddhist and Shinto icons. The painting itself is about 59 centimetres tall and 22 wide. Cleveland bought it in 1982 from Shogoro Yabumoto of Amagasaki.
