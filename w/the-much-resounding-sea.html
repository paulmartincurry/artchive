The Much Resounding Sea

Thomas Moran · 1884 · Oil on canvas · 63.9 × 158.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Thomas Moran crossed the Atlantic at seven, when his family left Lancashire for the United States in 1844, and he later made sketches and paintings of the sea. This canvas is more than a metre and a half wide. It was on consignment with Knoedler from 1912 until June 1919, when it went back to its owner unsold.
