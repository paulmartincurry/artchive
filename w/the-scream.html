The Scream (Skrik)

Edvard Munch · 1893 · Tempera and wax crayon on cardboard · National Museum, Oslo

Free to use

Munch made four versions of this image, two in paint and two in pastel, and a lithograph stone in 1895. This is the painted version of 1893, on cardboard. In its upper left corner is a pencil inscription in small lettering, and a study of the handwriting under infrared photography showed that Munch added it himself. Two men stole the painting on 12 February 1994, the day the Winter Olympics opened in Lillehammer. It was recovered undamaged on 7 May.
