The Unicorn Rests in a Garden (The Unicorn Tapestries)

Maker unknown · 1495–1505 · Wool warp with wool, silk, silver, and gilt wefts · 12 ft. 7/8 in. × 8 ft. 3 in. (368 × 251.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is the seventh and last tapestry of the unicorn series, although some scholars think it was made at another time by another artist and was never part of the set. The unicorn is chained to a pomegranate tree inside a small round fence. The letters AE are woven into the background, and a small frog sits at the lower right. The hanging is 3.7 metres tall and about 2.5 metres wide.
