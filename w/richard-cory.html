So on we worked, and waited for the light,
And went without the meat, and cursed the bread;
And Richard Cory, one calm summer night,
Went home and put a bullet through his head.

Richard Cory

Edwin Arlington Robinson · 1897

Robinson completed the poem in July 1897 and published it that year in The Children of the Night. Paul Simon based the song Richard Cory on it; Simon & Garfunkel recorded the song for their second studio album, Sounds of Silence.
