The Great Day of His Wrath

John Martin · 1851–1853 · Oil on canvas · Tate

Free to use

John Martin's son Leopold said his father found the idea for this picture on a night journey through the Black Country. The canvas is nearly two metres high and three wide, one of three that make up a triptych of the Last Judgement. Martin was still painting on 12 November 1853 when an attack of paralysis took his speech and the use of his right arm, and he died three months later. The Royal Academy rejected the three pictures. The Tate bought this one in 1945 from the dealer Robert Frank, who had restored it.
