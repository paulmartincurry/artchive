And now good morrow to our waking soules,
Which watch not one another out of feare;
For love, all love of other sights controules,
And makes one little roome, an every where.

The Good-Morrow

John Donne · 1633

Donne wrote the poem while he was a student at Lincoln's Inn, which makes it one of his earliest works. It was published in his 1633 collection Songs and Sonnets. Some manuscripts and a later volume give different last lines.
