The Alba Madonna

Raphael · c. 1510 · Oil on panel transferred to canvas · Diameter 94.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The round picture was painted on wood around 1510 and later transferred to canvas. It belonged to the Dukes of Alba in Spain, whose name it bears, and in 1836 it was sold to Nicholas I of Russia for the Hermitage. In 1931 the government of the Soviet Union sold it clandestinely to Andrew W. Mellon.
