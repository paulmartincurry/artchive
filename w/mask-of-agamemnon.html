Mask of Agamemnon

Maker unknown · ca. 1550–1500 BCE · Gold · National Archaeological Museum, Athens

Free to use

The mask was made from a single thick sheet of gold, heated and hammered against a wooden form, with the details chased in afterwards with a sharp tool. Heinrich Schliemann found it in 1876 in a shaft grave at Mycenae and named it after the king in the Iliad. Eight men were buried in that grave circle and five of them wore masks. Modern research dates it 300 to 400 years before the period of the Trojan War.
