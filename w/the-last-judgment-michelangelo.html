The Last Judgment

Michelangelo · 1536–1541 · Fresco · Sistine Chapel, Vatican City

Free to use

The fresco covers the whole altar wall of the Sistine Chapel and took over four years, from 1536 to 1541; Michelangelo was nearly 67 when he finished. Nearly all the men and angels were first shown nude, and many were later partly covered with painted draperies. Vasari reports that the pope's master of ceremonies called the nudes disgraceful, and that Michelangelo at once painted the man's face into the scene as Minos, with donkey ears.
