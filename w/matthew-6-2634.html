Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they toil not, neither do they spin:
And yet I say unto you, That even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.

Matthew 6:26–34 (Consider the Lilies)

Gospel of Matthew (New Testament / KJV) · 1st century CE (trans. 1611)

The Sermon on the Mount fills chapters 5 to 7 of Matthew's gospel and is the first of its five discourses. Christians have traditionally marked the Mount of Beatitudes as the place it was given, though Mount Arbel and the Horns of Hattin have also been suggested. The whole of chapter 6 survives in the Codex Vaticanus and the Codex Sinaiticus, both copied in the 4th century.
