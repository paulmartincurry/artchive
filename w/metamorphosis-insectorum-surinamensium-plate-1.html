Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 1

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Merian opened the book with the pineapple, which her text calls the foremost of all edible fruits. The insects on it are cockroaches; she wrote that they spoiled the colonists' wool, linen, food and drink, and that the young hatch from a round egg case. She was fifty-two when she sailed for Suriname with her daughter Dorothea Maria in 1699.
