Sunflowers

Vincent van Gogh · 1888 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London

This is the fourth of the sunflower canvases Vincent van Gogh painted at Arles in August 1888, the one with the yellow background. He was working on three at once, meaning them as decoration for the house he hoped to share with Paul Gauguin. He announced a dozen or fourteen flowers, and the canvas has fifteen or sixteen. It left the artist's estate in 1924, sold to the Tate Gallery. In October 2022 protesters threw tomato soup at it, and the plexiglass kept the paint unharmed.
