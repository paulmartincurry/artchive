Brother, you say there is but one way to worship and serve the Great Spirit.
If there is but one religion, why do you white people differ so much about it?

Reply to the Missionary

Red Jacket (Sagoyewatha, Seneca Orator) · 1805

Jacob Cram, a missionary from New England, asked in 1805 to work among the Seneca. The two men met at Buffalo Creek, New York, and Red Jacket gave this answer after conferring with the Seneca leaders. He was about fifty-five. The name Sagoyewatha, roughly he keeps them awake, had been given to him by the Seneca around 1780 for his skill as a speaker.
