Ethan Frome — An incarnation of its frozen woe

Edith Wharton · 1911

The story began as a French composition that Wharton had to write while studying the language in Paris. Several years later she took it up again, drawing on her ten years at The Mount, her home in Lenox, Massachusetts, and reading the work in progress each day to her friend Walter Berry. The sledding crash is probably based on an accident on Courthouse Hill in Lenox in 1904, which she learned of from a survivor, Kate Spencer, when both worked at the Lenox Library.
