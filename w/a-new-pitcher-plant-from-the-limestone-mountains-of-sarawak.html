A New Pitcher Plant from the Limestone Mountains of Sarawak, Borneo

Marianne North · ca. 1876 · Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew

Free to use

The plant was unknown to science when Marianne North painted it. Herbert Everett of the Borneo Company brought the specimens down from a tree about 1000 feet up on the limestone mountains of Sarawak, and North tied them in festoons around her verandah. She had one small half-sheet of paper left to paint them on. The nurseryman Harry Veitch saw the picture and sent a collector for seeds, and in 1881 Joseph Dalton Hooker named the species after her.
