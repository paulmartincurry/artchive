Gledhill Wall Paper Co. · 1906–07 · Machine-printed · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The Gledhill Wall Paper Company of New York was six or seven years old when this sample was printed, in greens, reds and olive on a ground of gold mica. It is 67 by 48 centimetres and was a gift of Dorwin L. Starr.
