'Late late yestreen I saw the new moone,
Wi the auld moone in hir arme,
And I feir, I feir, my deir master,
That we will cum to harme.'

Sir Patrick Spens

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1765

Thomas Percy first printed it in 1765 in his Reliques, from two manuscript copies sent from Scotland; this is that text, as Child's editors printed it in 1904. The ballad may or may not be history. One suggested source is the voyage of 1281, when knights and nobles took Margaret, daughter of Alexander III, to marry Eric, King of Norway, and many of them drowned on the way home.
