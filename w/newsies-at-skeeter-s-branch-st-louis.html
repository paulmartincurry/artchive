Newsies at Skeeter's Branch, St. Louis

Lewis Hine · 9 May 1910 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Hine left his teaching job in 1908 to become the photographer for the National Child Labor Committee. He sometimes got into workplaces as a fire inspector, postcard vendor or Bible salesman. This print comes from the committee's album on street trades, as Hine number 1386, and its title is copied from the committee's caption card.
