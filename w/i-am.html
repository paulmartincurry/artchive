I am: yet what I am none cares or knows,
My friends forsake me like a memory lost;
I am the self-consumer of my woes,

I Am

John Clare · c. 1844-45; published 1848

Clare wrote this poem in late 1844 or 1845, in his early fifties, as a patient in the Northampton General Lunatic Asylum, and the asylum's house steward, W. F. Knight, wrote it out for him. It was first published on 1 January 1848 in the Bedford Times, though some sources give a later first printing. At times during his years in asylums Clare believed he was Lord Byron, or Shakespeare, and at one point he re-edited Byron's poems.
