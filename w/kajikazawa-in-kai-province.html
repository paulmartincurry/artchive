Kajikazawa in Kai Province (Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1830–32 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 25.4 × 38.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A man stands on a rocky outcrop and casts fishing lines into the Fuji River at Kajikazawa. The first impressions of the print were made in blue alone. Later versions add colours: a red jacket for the fisherman and his son, a green-to-yellow wash on the rocks and, in some impressions, a streak of pink in the sky.
