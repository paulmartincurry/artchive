The Child's Bath

Mary Cassatt · 1893 · Oil on canvas · 101.3 × 67.3 cm (39 15/16 × 26 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Cassatt signed the canvas in blue paint on the floor at bottom left. She sold it to the dealer Durand-Ruel in Paris on 24 November 1893, and it hung as number one in his Cassatt exhibition that winter. A Connecticut collector, Harris Whittemore, bought it early in 1894 and sold it back in 1899. In 1910 the Art Institute bought it, the first work by Cassatt to enter its collection.
