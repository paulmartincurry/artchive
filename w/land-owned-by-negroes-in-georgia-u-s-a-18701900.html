Land Owned by Negroes in Georgia, U.S.A., 1870–1900 (The Georgia Negro)

W. E. B. Du Bois · c. 1900 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

This map of Georgia uses colour to mark how many acres African Americans owned in each county. Alongside Du Bois's charts, the Paris exhibit showed a statuette of Frederick Douglass, four bound volumes of nearly 400 patents by African Americans, and some five hundred photographs.
