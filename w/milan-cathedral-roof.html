Milan Cathedral, Roof

Maker unknown · 1386–1965 · Brick faced with Candoglia marble

Free to use with credit. Photo: Daniel Case, CC BY-SA 3.0

Milan Cathedral took nearly six centuries to build: work began in 1386 and the final details were finished in 1965. It is brick faced with marble from the Candoglia quarry, which Gian Galeazzo Visconti gave to the cathedral works for their exclusive use, free of tax. The spire that carries the Madonnina went up in 1762.
