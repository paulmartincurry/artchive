Carousel Giraffe

Henry Tomaszewski · c. 1939 · Watercolor and graphite on paperboard · 45.8 × 34.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Henry Tomaszewski pencilled his working dates in the corner: 2/27/39 to 4/28/39. The carousel giraffe is 72 inches long. Making the Index of American Design was also a way of creating jobs; it employed about one thousand people in thirty-four states.
