Milton Dictating to His Daughter

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1794 · Oil on canvas · 121.2 × 118.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Fuseli painted this canvas for his Milton Gallery, an enterprise he ran himself. He sent it to William Roscoe in Liverpool in 1800, apparently in exchange for funds Roscoe had already advanced. Roscoe had sold it by August 1802, possibly to the 1st Marquess of Bute. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1973.
