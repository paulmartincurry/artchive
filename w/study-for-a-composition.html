Study for a Composition

Piet Mondrian · 1940–41 · Collage of cut and pasted papers, prepared with gouache and charcoal, on pieced cream wove newsprint in three parts with charcoal on verso · Max: 33 × 27 cm (13 × 10 11/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Piet Mondrian reached a fully abstract style in 1916, reducing bridges, churches and trees to black lines and fields of red, blue and yellow. This study is made of cut and pasted papers, worked out in his last years in New York.
