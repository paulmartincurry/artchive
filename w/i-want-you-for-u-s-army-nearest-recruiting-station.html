I Want You for U.S. Army: Nearest Recruiting Station

James Montgomery Flagg · c. 1917 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

James Montgomery Flagg first drew this Uncle Sam for the cover of Leslie's Weekly of 6 July 1916, over the caption What Are You Doing for Preparedness. The pose was taken from a British recruiting poster of Lord Kitchener. More than four million copies were printed between 1917 and 1918, with a blank space at the bottom for the address of the nearest recruiting station. Flagg said later that he had used his own face for Uncle Sam's.
