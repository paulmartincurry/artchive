Glass and Shadows (Camera Work 40, plate XIII)

Adolf de Meyer · published 1912 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The photogravure was plate XIII of Camera Work, number 40, in October 1912. That year de Meyer photographed Nijinsky in Paris. The following year he moved to the United States and became the first official fashion photographer for Vogue.
