Kuba Mask (Bwoom)

Maker unknown · mid- to late 1800s · Wood and paint · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Among the Bushoong, the central Kuba group, the bwoom mask is one of three, with a female mask and another male one, that act out the founding of the Kuba kingdom. Outside the capital it is used in other settings, such as the men's initiation society. Eagle feathers were added to the top only on the occasions when it was performed. The Charles Ratton Gallery in Paris sold this mask to the museum in 1935.
