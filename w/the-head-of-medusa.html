The Head of Medusa

Peter Paul Rubens · c. 1618 · Oil on canvas · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

The snakes have been attributed to Frans Snyders, who also painted the eagle in Rubens's Prometheus Bound. This canvas, 68.5 by 118 centimetres, belonged to the Duke of Buckingham between 1635 and 1648, is recorded in Prague in 1685, and came from Prague to Vienna in 1876. Another version is in the Moravian Gallery in Brno, and the Vienna canvas is thought to be the later of the two.
