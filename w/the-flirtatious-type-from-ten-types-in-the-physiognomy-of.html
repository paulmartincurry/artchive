The Flirtatious Type, from Ten Types in the Physiognomy of Women

Kitagawa Utamaro · c. 1793 · color woodblock print · 36.9 × 25.4 cm (14 1/2 × 10 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The print is signed as physiognomy and design by Utamaro and carries the mark of his publisher, Tsutaya Jūzaburō, with whom he had lived for about five years from the mid-1780s. Only two impressions from this series have a pink mica ground. This sheet was owned by Murata Kinbei in Kanagawa until 1917, passed through the dealers Yamanaka and Company, and came to Cleveland in 1930 in the bequest of Edward L. Whittemore.
