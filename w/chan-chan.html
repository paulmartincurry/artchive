Chan Chan

Maker unknown · ca. 900–1470 · Adobe

Free to use with credit. Photo: Håkan Svensson, GFDL

Chan Chan was the capital of the Chimor kingdom, a city of twenty square kilometres with an estimated 40,000 to 60,000 people, built in a stretch of coastal desert and watered by rivers running down from the Andes. The Inca conquered it around 1470 and scattered its people, and the Spaniards later looted its tombs. Rain is now the danger to its adobe walls: the site has been on the List of World Heritage in Danger since 1986, and archaeologists are putting up coverings and drains to protect it.
