The Mortuary (Etchings of Paris)

Charles Meryon · 1854 · Etching and drypoint · Platemark: 23.1 × 20.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Meryon worked almost entirely in etching, as he had colour blindness, and by the age of 28 he had given himself wholly to drawing and printmaking. The morgue building dated from 1568 and was soon to disappear with the modernisation of Paris. He developed his plates through many states; this impression is from the fourth of seven.
