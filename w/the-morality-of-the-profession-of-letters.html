To ape a sentiment, even a good one, is to travesty a sentiment; that will not be helpful. To conceal a sentiment, if you are sure you hold it, is to take a liberty with truth.

The Morality of the Profession of Letters

Robert Louis Stevenson · 1881

Lighthouse design was the Stevenson family's profession, and he entered the University of Edinburgh to study engineering. In April 1871 he told his father he had decided on a life of letters instead. For security it was agreed he would read law; he qualified for the Scottish bar in 1875 and never practised. He was about thirty when this essay appeared.
