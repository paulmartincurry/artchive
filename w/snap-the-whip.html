Snap the Whip

Winslow Homer · 1872 · Oil on canvas · 30.5 × 50.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Homer painted two almost identical versions in 1872. This is the second: he kept the schoolhouse and took out the hills behind it. He is said to have been prompted by boys playing at the schoolhouse in Hurley, in New York's Hudson Valley. The canvas is about 30 centimetres tall.
