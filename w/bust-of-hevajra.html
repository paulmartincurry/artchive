Bust of Hevajra

Maker unknown · late 12th–early 13th century · Stone · 132.1 × 73.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The bust is 132.1 centimetres high. Hevajra practice thrived in Cambodia from the tenth to the thirteenth century, and this figure dates from the end of that span, the late twelfth or early thirteenth century. Those years include the reign of Jayavarman VII, from 1181 to 1218, the first Khmer king devoted to Buddhism and the builder of the Bayon. The Met bought the bust in 1936 with the Fletcher Fund.
