I mourn now for Adonis: he is dead, the fair Adonis;
‘Lovely Adonis is dead’, echo the servants of Love.

Lament for Adonis (opening)

Bion of Smyrna · c. 100 BCE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Bion was a bucolic poet from Smyrna, probably active around the turn of the second and first centuries BC. This long poem about Adonis and seventeen short fragments are all that survive of his work. An anonymous Epitaph to Bion says he was poisoned and implies he died young.
