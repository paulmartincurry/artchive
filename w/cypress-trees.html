Cypress Trees

Kanō Eitoku · 1590 · Colour on paper with gold leaf · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

The eight panels began as four painted sliding doors and were later remounted as folding screens, which helps account for the breaks in the image. Together they measure 170.3 by 460.5 centimetres. The painting is usually given to Kanō Eitoku, who died in 1590, but a reference to a commission in a prince's diary has led some to give it to his younger brother Kanō Sōshū. After the Meiji Restoration it passed from the Katsura-no-miya to the Imperial Household and then to the nation.
