Standing Buddha

Maker unknown · 150–200 CE · Schist · H. 119.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Gandharan art is believed to have produced the first images of the Buddha in human form. Mixing elements from the Mediterranean and South Asian worlds, its sculptors were among the first to give him a placid face, thick wavy hair and a heavy, toga-like monk's robe. This figure is 119.7 centimetres high, and a cavity at the top of the head may once have held a portion of the Buddha's relics. Cleveland bought it in 1972 from B. J. Lester-Smith of Vienna.
