The Clenched Left Hand (Study for Pierre de Wissant)

Auguste Rodin · modeled ca. 1885, cast 1974 · Bronze · Overall: 18 1/4 × 10 3/8 × 7 5/8 in. · The Met

Free to use

This bronze was cast in 1974, fifty-seven years after the death of Auguste Rodin, from a hand he modelled around 1885. It is 46 centimetres tall and weighs 10.5 kilograms.
