The Artchive
With a high sudden hum
The field tosses its mane:

Messengers

Andrei Bely · 1913

Translated by Babette Deutsch and Avrahm Yarmolinsky, 1921 · Public domain in the United States (published 1921); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Andrei Bely was the pen name of Boris Bugaev, born in Moscow, the son of a mathematician. The name was given to him by Mikhail Solovyov, the younger brother of the philosopher Vladimir Solovyov. His mother was a pianist and gave him his musical education. He was in his early thirties in 1913, the year his novel Petersburg first appeared.
