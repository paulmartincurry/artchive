Songs of Experience: London

William Blake · 1794, printed c. 1825 · Relief etching printed in orange-brown ink and hand-colored with watercolor and shell gold · Sheet: 15.7 × 14.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Blake engraved, printed and coloured each page of the Songs himself. He first bound Songs of Innocence and Songs of Experience separately and combined them in one volume only in 1794. This sheet, 15.7 by 14.1 centimetres, is plate 46 of the copy known as copy Y, which the Metropolitan Museum bought in 1917.
