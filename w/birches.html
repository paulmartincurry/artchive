Earth's the right place for love:
I don't know where it's likely to go better.

Birches (closing lines)

Robert Frost · 1915

Public domain in the United States (published 1916); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

This poem was first called Swinging Birches. It appeared in The Atlantic Monthly in August 1915, the year Frost came home from England, printed alongside The Road Not Taken. Swinging on birches was a common game for country children in New England; Frost had done it as a boy, and his daughter Lesley wrote in her journal about climbing a high birch and riding it down.
