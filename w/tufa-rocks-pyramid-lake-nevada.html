Tufa Rocks, Pyramid Lake, Nevada

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1867 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 22.1 × 29.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

From 1867 to 1869 Timothy O'Sullivan was the official photographer on the United States Geological Exploration of the Fortieth Parallel under Clarence King, and this view dates from its first year. He was about twenty-seven. O'Sullivan died of tuberculosis on Staten Island in 1882, aged 42.
