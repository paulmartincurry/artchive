Hevajra

Northeastern Thailand, former kingdom of Angkor · ca. 1200 · Bronze · Overall: 46 × 23.9 cm (18 1/8 × 9 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The bronze survived inside a clay vessel that lay under water, which gave it its grey patina. Samples from its clay core contain feldspar, a mark of clay from the Isaan plateau in present-day Thailand. Many bronzes of Hevajra were cast during the reign of the Khmer king Jayavarman VII. This one is 46 centimetres tall. It was bought from a Bangkok antiques dealer by Maxeen Stone Flower, who owned it with her husband from 1987 and left it to the museum.
