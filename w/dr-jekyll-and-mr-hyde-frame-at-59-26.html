Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, frame at 59:26

John S. Robertson · 1920 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

John Barrymore played the first stage of the change into Hyde with no make-up, by contorting his face and body. Later, when Hyde turns back into Jekyll, one of his prosthetic fingers is shaken loose and can be seen flying across the screen. The screenplay had Hyde grow uglier as the film goes on, his bald head rising to a point. The Rivoli Theatre in New York paid 10,000 dollars just to rent the film.
