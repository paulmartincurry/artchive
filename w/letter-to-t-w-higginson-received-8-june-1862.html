If fame belonged to me, I could not escape her; if she did not, the longest day would pass me on the chase, and the approbation of my dog would forsake me then. My barefoot rank is better.

Letter to T. W. Higginson, received 8 June 1862

Emily Dickinson · June 1862

Higginson received this, her third letter, on June 8, 1862. Her decision to contact him suggests that by 1862 she was contemplating publication. Over the years of their correspondence she went from addressing him as Mr Higginson to Dear friend, and signed her letters Your Gnome and Your Scholar.
