Irises

Vincent van Gogh · May 1889 · Oil on canvas · 74.3 × 94.3 cm · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Van Gogh entered the asylum at Saint-Rémy in May 1889 and started this painting within a month, working from nature in the hospital garden. He regarded it as a study, and there are no known drawings for it. One of the pollen cones that fall from the garden's umbrella pines is embedded in the paint. The Getty has owned the painting since 1990.
