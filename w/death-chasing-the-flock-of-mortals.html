Death Chasing the Flock of Mortals

James Ensor · 1896 · Etching on paper · 23.5 × 17.5 cm · MSK Gent (Museum of Fine Arts, Ghent)

Free to use

This was the first time a skeleton took the central role in Ensor's work. The etching repeats, apart from a few details, a drawing of the same name he made in 1887, the year his father and grandmother died; death recurs in his work from then on. The sheet is 23.5 by 17.5 centimetres, signed at lower left and titled there La mort qui passe.
