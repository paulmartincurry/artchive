For thee the daedal Earth bears scented flowers,
For thee waters of the unvexed deep
Smile, and the hollows of the serene sky
Glow with diffused radiance for thee!

On the Nature of Things: Invocation to Venus

Lucretius · c. 55 BCE

Translated by William Ellery Leonard

The poem opens by calling on Venus as the mother of Aeneas's line. It is addressed and dedicated to Gaius Memmius, of whom Lucretius was either a friend or a client; that is the one certain fact of his life. The three earliest manuscripts date to the ninth century. The oldest, the Codex Oblongus, was made in a Carolingian scriptorium and is kept at Leiden.
