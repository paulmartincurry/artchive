Comédie de la Mort

Rodolphe Bresdin · 1854 · Lithograph · Image: 21.7 × 15.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The stone was printed in Toulouse, at the lithographers Bertrand and Barthère in the rue St. Rome. Bresdin had walked 678 kilometres to that city after the counter-revolution and taken to living in the open air and in workmen's and fishermen's huts. He was thirty-one or thirty-two when he signed and dated the print in 1854. The image is 21.7 centimetres high.
