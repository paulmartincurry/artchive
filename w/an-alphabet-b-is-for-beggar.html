An Alphabet: B is for Beggar

William Nicholson · 1897 · color lithograph · Sheet: 30.7 × 24 cm (12 1/16 × 9 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

An Alphabet was the first of several sets of prints William Nicholson made for the publisher William Heinemann. He was about twenty-five. He cut the images in wood and they first came out as a small edition of hand-coloured woodcuts, then in large editions as colour lithographs like this one. A pencil note on the sheet identifies the beggar as Pryde, his partner in the Beggarstaff Brothers.
