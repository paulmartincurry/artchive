Nine Dragons (detail)

Chen Rong · 1244 · Handscroll, ink and some red on paper · Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Free to use

Parts of the scroll are spattered with drops of ink that were flung or blown onto the paper, and Chen Rong's own inscription ties his dragons to rain that people longed for. He was a Southern Song official as well as a painter, and was in his forties in 1244. The Qianlong Emperor had a court painter copy the scroll and stamped several seals on it. In 1917 the New York dealers Yamanaka and Co. sold it to the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, for 25,000 dollars.
