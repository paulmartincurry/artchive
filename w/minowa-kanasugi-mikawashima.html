Minowa, Kanasugi, Mikawashima (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hiroshige did not finish the series himself. He died aged 62 during the great Edo cholera epidemic of 1858, though whether the epidemic killed him is unknown, and Hiroshige II completed the set. He was buried in a Zen Buddhist temple in Asakusa.
