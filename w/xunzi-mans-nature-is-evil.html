Man's nature is evil; goodness is the result of conscious activity.

Xunzi — Man’s Nature Is Evil

Xunzi (Xun Kuang) · ca. 250 BCE (Warring States Period)

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This chapter answers Mencius, who had claimed that people have a natural inclination toward goodness. The writings attributed to Xun Kuang survived as hundreds of loose fascicles until Liu Xiang redacted them into 32 bundles of bamboo strips. The first commentary did not appear until 818 AD, when an official named Yang Liang said he had corrected the strips and transcribed them onto scrolls of silk. The text has been continuously in print since the 11th century.
