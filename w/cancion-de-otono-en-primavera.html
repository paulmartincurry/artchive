Juventud, divino tesoro,
ya te vas para no volver!
Cuando quiero llorar, no lloro...
Y a veces lloro sin querer...

Canción de otoño en primavera

Rubén Darío · 1905

Darío published Cantos de vida y esperanza in Madrid in 1905, edited by the poet Juan Ramón Jiménez, while he was in Spain on a Nicaraguan government committee settling a territorial dispute with Honduras. In the years just before, he and his companion Francisca Sánchez had lost two infant children, one a daughter who died of smallpox before he ever met her.
