The Great Gatsby — The green light (Ch. 1)

F. Scott Fitzgerald · 1925

Fitzgerald began outlining the novel in June 1922 and threw away most of the 18,000 words he had written by the middle of 1923. He finished a rough draft in 1924 after moving to the French Riviera, then revised it over the winter at his editor's urging. It came out on April 10, 1925, when he was twenty-eight, and had sold fewer than 20,000 copies by October. During the Second World War free copies were handed out to American soldiers overseas.
