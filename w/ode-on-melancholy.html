Ay, in the very temple of Delight
Veil'd Melancholy has her sovran shrine,
Though seen of none save him whose strenuous tongue
Can burst Joy's grape against his palate fine;

Ode on Melancholy

John Keats · 1819

Keats wrote this ode in the spring of 1819, one of five that season. With three stanzas it is the shortest of them. Its original first stanza, about a boat built of dead men's bones with shrouds for a sail, was cut before the poem was published in 1820.
