Tu casa está llena de lirios

Alfonsina Storni · 1919

Translated by Alice Stone Blackwell, 1929

Storni was born in Switzerland and brought to Argentina at four. Her father ran a tavern in Rosario, where she did chores, and she wrote her first verse at twelve. After his death in 1906 she went to work in a hat factory, then toured the country with a theatre company, then trained as a rural schoolteacher. In 1919, the year of this poem, she was a regular contributor to the literary magazine La Nota. Jorge Luis Borges was among the men who criticised her early books harshly.
