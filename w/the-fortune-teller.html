The Fortune-Teller

Georges de La Tour · probably 1630s · Oil on canvas · 101.9 × 123.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting was uncovered in about 1960 and bought that year by the Metropolitan Museum. A signature at the top right says that La Tour made it at Lunéville in Lorraine. Its authenticity has been questioned since, notably by the English art historian Christopher Wright, but it is generally accepted as La Tour's work.
