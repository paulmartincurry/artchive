Pandora's Box: Omoi Tsuzura and Yokubari Obasan (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889–92 · Woodcut, color · 31.9 × 21.4 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

An old woman is set upon by demons that pour out of a huge basket, her punishment for injuring a sparrow. Yoshitoshi's last years went badly: early in 1891 he invited friends to a gathering of artists that existed only in his mind, all his money was stolen in a robbery, and he was admitted to a mental hospital.
