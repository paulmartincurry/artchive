The Metamorphosis (Die Verwandlung)

Franz Kafka · 1915

Translated by David Wyllie, 2002 · Translation © David Wyllie, distributed by Project Gutenberg under its licence; not public domain.

The Metamorphosis runs to about 70 printed pages, the longest of the stories Franz Kafka considered complete and published in his lifetime. It first appeared in the October 1915 issue of the journal Die weißen Blätter, and as a book that December in Kurt Wolff's series Der jüngste Tag. On 25 October 1915 Kafka wrote to his publisher about the cover illustration and asked that the insect not be drawn, nor even shown from a distance.
