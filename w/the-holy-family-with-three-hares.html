The Holy Family with Three Hares

Albrecht Dürer · c. 1496 · Woodcut in black on off-white laid paper · 39 × 28.2 cm (15 3/8 × 11 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The three hares at the bottom point to the Virgin's miraculous fertility. The coral necklace on the Child was meant to protect against plague and foretells his death on the cross.
