Moema

Victor Meirelles · 1866 · Oil on canvas · São Paulo Museum of Art

Free to use

Moema is a character in Caramuru, an epic poem of 1781 by Santa Rita Durão. Meirelles did not paint a scene from the poem; the drowned figure is his own version of what became of her. A sketch made beforehand has her in a different position and gives more room to the group of people in the background. The painting was first shown in 1866 at the Imperial Academy of Fine Arts and has been in the São Paulo Museum of Art since 1947.
