Sick on a journey,
my dreams wander
the withered fields.

Tabi ni yande

Matsuo Bashō · November 1694

Translated by Robert Hass · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Bashō left Edo for the last time in the summer of 1694 and passed through Ueno and Kyoto before reaching Osaka. There he came down with a stomach illness, and he died on 28 November with his disciples around him, aged about fifty. He did not compose a formal death poem; this one is generally accepted as his farewell.
