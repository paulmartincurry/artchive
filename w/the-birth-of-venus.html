The Birth of Venus

Sandro Botticelli · mid-1480s · Tempera on canvas · Uffizi Gallery, Florence

Free to use

Botticelli painted this on canvas, a cheaper support than panel. It is two pieces sewn together before he started, over a gesso ground tinted blue, and he used gold as a pigment for the highlights on the hair, wings, textiles and shell. No record of the original commission exists. The title was given only in the nineteenth century. The painting stayed at the Villa di Castello, a country house outside Florence, until 1815, when it was transferred to the Uffizi.
