Church, Taos Pueblo, New Mexico, 1942

Ansel Adams · 1942 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

Ansel Adams knew Taos well. His first book, Taos Pueblo, had been published in 1930 with a text by Mary Hunter Austin. He returned in 1942 on his contract with the National Park Service and dated this negative of the church in his caption. He sent 225 small prints to the Department of the Interior and held on to the 229 negatives.
