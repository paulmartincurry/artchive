The Night Battle of Kai Khusrau and Afrasiyab (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Bashdan Qara · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 30.8 × 18.4 cm; page 47.1 × 31.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting is attributed to Bashdan Qara and stands 30.8 cm high on a page of 47.1 by 31.9 cm. Another page from the same manuscript, owned by the scholar and collector Stuart Cary Welch, sold on 6 April 2011 for 7.4 million pounds.
