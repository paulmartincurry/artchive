Calligraphy in Semi-Cursive Style

Yueshan Daozong · c. 1660–1709 · Hanging scroll; ink on paper · Image: 28.6 × 64.1 cm (11 1/4 × 25 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Yueshan Daozong was a Chan Buddhist monk from Fujian province who emigrated to Japan and took a post at Manpukuji, the headquarters of the Ōbaku school, founded in Japan by Chinese monks in the 1600s. He later became its seventh abbot. His inscription names this as calligraphy for the new spring, and it carries his two seals and a third naming the Linji school of Chan.
