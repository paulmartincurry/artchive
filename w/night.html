Night

Michelangelo · 1526–31 · Marble · Sagrestia Nuova, Basilica of San Lorenzo, Florence

Free to use with credit. Photo: George M. Groutas, CC BY 2.0

Night was among at least four statues for the Medici tombs that were completed between 1525 and 1527. Florence then rebelled against Medici rule, and Michelangelo sided with the republican faction and took charge of the fortifications during the siege of 1529–1530. Work on the sacristy resumed in April 1531. The figure lies on the left of the sarcophagus of Giuliano de' Medici, Duke of Nemours.
