Winter Flashlight Scene in the Weddell Sea, Showing Endurance Stuck Fast

Frank Hurley · 1915 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Frank Hurley was the photographer on Ernest Shackleton's expedition. The Endurance was trapped in pack ice and sank in the Weddell Sea on 21 November 1915. Hurley chose 120 of his glass-plate negatives to save and smashed about 400 others. This print was copyrighted in 1916, and the picture was published in Shackleton's book South.
