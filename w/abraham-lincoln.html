Abraham Lincoln

Alexander Gardner · 1865 · Albumen silver print · 45 × 38.6 cm · National Portrait Gallery, Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Some of Alexander Gardner's photographs of Abraham Lincoln were long thought to be the last taken of the president, four days before his assassination. That was later found to be wrong: they were taken in February 1865, the last on 5 February. This print is 45 by 38.6 centimetres and came to the National Portrait Gallery in 1981 with the Frederick Hill Meserve Collection.
