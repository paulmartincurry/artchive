George Sand

Nadar · 1864, published 1877 · Woodburytype, from Galerie Contemporaine · 23.5 × 19.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Nadar photographed George Sand in 1864, four years after he moved his studio to 35 Boulevard des Capucines in Paris. This impression is a Woodburytype issued in 1877 in the second volume of the periodical Galerie Contemporaine. Nadar had worked as a caricaturist, journalist and novelist, and in 1858 he became the first person to take aerial photographs.
