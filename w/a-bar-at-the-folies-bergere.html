A Bar at the Folies-Bergère

Édouard Manet · 1882 · Oil on canvas · Courtauld Gallery, London

Free to use

The barmaid was a real employee of the Folies-Bergère known as Suzon, but Manet posed her in his studio. The bottles with the red triangle on the label are Bass Pale Ale, and the green feet in the upper left corner belong to a trapeze artist. The painting was shown at the Paris Salon of 1882. Its first owner was the composer Emmanuel Chabrier, a close friend, who hung it over his piano.
