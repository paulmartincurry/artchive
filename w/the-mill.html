The Mill

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1645/1648 · Oil on canvas · 87.6 × 105.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The painting was in the collection of the ducs d'Orléans in Paris until 1792, when it was acquired with the rest of their Dutch, German and Flemish pictures for an English syndicate. For a long time the attribution to Rembrandt was regarded as doubtful; it has been restored in recent years, although it is not universally accepted.
