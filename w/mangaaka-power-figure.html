Mangaaka Power Figure (Nkisi N’Kondi)

Maker unknown · c. 1880–1900 · Wood, iron, resin, ceramic, plant fiber, textile, pigment · 118 × 49.5 × 39.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Each blade or nail was driven in to seal an oath, a covenant or the settling of a dispute, after the people involved had touched it, sometimes to their lips. The word nkondi comes from the verb konda, to hunt. This figure was made around 1880 to 1900 by a Yombe-Kongo artist working with an nganga, a ritual specialist. It is 118 centimetres tall and weighs 53 pounds.
