The Ramparts of Carcassonne

Gustave Le Gray · 1851 · Salted paper print from waxed paper negative · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

In 1851, the year of this print, Le Gray was one of the first five photographers hired by the French government's Missions Héliographiques to record the country's monuments and buildings. He was thirty or thirty-one. He worked from paper negatives that he waxed before exposure so they would take finer detail, an improvement of his own. The museum acquired the print in 2005 with the Gilman Collection.
