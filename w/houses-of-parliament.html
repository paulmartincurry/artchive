Houses of Parliament

Claude Monet · c. 1900–1904 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

Every canvas in this series was painted from the same spot, Monet's window or a terrace at St Thomas' Hospital across the Thames, and all are about 81 by 92 centimetres. He began the first at about a quarter to four on the afternoon of 13 February 1900. He no longer finished pictures in front of the subject: the canvases went home to Giverny, and he sent to London for photographs to help him complete them. No museum owns more than two.
