Two Old Ones Eating Soup

Francisco de Goya · c. 1819–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 49.3 × 83.4 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

This mural, 49.3 by 83.4 centimetres, probably sat above the main door of Goya's house, between La Leocadia and Two Old Men. He laid some of the paint on with a palette knife, and kept to blacks, ochres, earth tones and greys.
