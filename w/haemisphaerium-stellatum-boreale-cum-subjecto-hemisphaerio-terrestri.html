Haemisphaerium Stellatum Boreale cum Subjecto Hemisphaerio Terrestri (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The constellation figures were not designed for the atlas; they were based on earlier images by the Dutch engraver Jan Pieterszoon Saenredam. Cellarius meant the book as the introduction to a two-part work on cosmography. He died in 1665, five years after it first appeared, and the second part was never published.
