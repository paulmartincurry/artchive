Al-Khazneh (The Treasury), Petra

Maker unknown · Early 1st Century · Sandstone

The whole front, 39.1 metres high and 25.3 wide, was cut out of a sandstone cliff, probably as a tomb at the beginning of the first century AD. Behind it there is only a plain main chamber and three antechambers. Bedouin of the area named it the Treasury in the early nineteenth century, believing the stone urn on the upper level held treasure. The urn is solid sandstone and is marked by bullets. Visitors touching and leaning on the walls wore the surface back by 40 millimetres in less than ten years.
