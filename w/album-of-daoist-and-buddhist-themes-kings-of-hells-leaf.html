Album of Daoist and Buddhist Themes: Kings of Hells, Leaf 30

Maker unknown · 1200s · Album, ink on paper · 34.3 × 38.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The album holds 50 ink drawings, and 14 of them show the Ten Kings of Hell administering punishments to the dead. They were likely made by several master craftsmen as models for studio apprentices to follow when filling commissions. The painter Wang Hui once owned the album. F. R. Martin published it in Munich in 1913 as drawings after Wu Daozi, and Cleveland bought it in 2004 from the son of the collector Stephen Junkunc III.
