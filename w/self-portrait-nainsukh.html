Self-Portrait

Nainsukh · ca. 1730 · Gouache and gold on paper · Indian Museum

Free to use

Nainsukh was born around 1710 in Guler, in the foothills of the Himalayas, and was trained by his father, the painter Pandit Seu, alongside a brother about ten years older. This is one of at least two self-portraits, and it comes from early in his career, a period that is very poorly documented. Around 1740 he left the family workshop for Jasrota, where Raja Balwant Singh employed him for almost twenty years. About a hundred of his works survive, and four are signed.
