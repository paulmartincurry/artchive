Universalis Cosmographia (The Waldseemüller Map)

Martin Waldseemüller · 1507 · Woodcut on twelve sheets · 128 × 233 cm (assembled) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

This is the only known surviving copy of the first edition, of which about 1,000 copies are believed to have been printed. It is made of twelve sheets printed from woodcuts, 128 by 233 centimetres when assembled. It was once bound into the Schöner Sammelband, and the back of one sheet carries the stamps of the princely Waldburg-Wolfegg print room and library. Martin Waldseemüller, a butcher's son who had been ordained a priest in Basel, was about thirty-seven when the map came out in April 1507.
