But according to the ideal of art that I have, I think that the artist should not manifest anything of his own feelings, and that the artist should not appear any more in his work than God in nature. The man is nothing, the work is everything!

Letter to George Sand (December 1875)

Gustave Flaubert · December 1875

Translated by Aimée L. McKenzie, 1921

Flaubert had a close friendship and correspondence with George Sand. Between 1875 and 1877 he broke off the long project that became Bouvard et Pécuchet to write the Three Tales. The edition dates this letter December 1875. Their correspondence was published in 1884 with an introduction by Guy de Maupassant.
