And there appeared another wonder in heaven; and behold a great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads.

Revelation 12:1–9

King James Bible (Book of Revelation) · ca. 95 CE (trans. 1611)

The author of Revelation names himself only as John and says he is on the island of Patmos, so he is conventionally called John of Patmos. The book is usually dated to about AD 95. Arsène Heitz, one of the designers who submitted proposals for the flag of Europe, suggested that its twelve stars come from the crown of twelve stars in this chapter.
