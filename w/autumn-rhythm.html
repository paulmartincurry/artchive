Autumn Rhythm (Number 30)

Jackson Pollock · 1950 · Enamel on canvas · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

In copyright. Small image shown under fair use, for reference and commentary.

Jackson Pollock made this painting in July and August of 1950 in his studio at Springs, New York, on unprimed canvas laid on the floor. It is 17 feet wide and 8 feet high. Hans Namuth photographed him at work, and the pictures show that he began with thin black lines on the right third, added the other colours, then moved to the centre and finally the left. He first showed it as Number 30. The Metropolitan Museum bought it from his estate in 1957 for $20,000 plus another of his paintings in trade.
