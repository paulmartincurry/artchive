Nobody else could have got in this way,
as this entrance was meant only for you. Now I’ll go and close it.

The Trial — Before the Law

Franz Kafka · 1915

Translated by David Wyllie · Translation © David Wyllie, distributed by Project Gutenberg under its licence; not public domain.

The parable belongs to a novel Kafka never finished. He drafted its first sentence in August 1914 and worked on it through 1915 while holding a day job in insurance, writing scenes out of order in several notebooks at once. What survived was 161 loose pages torn from those notebooks, which his friend Max Brod took away for safekeeping and assembled into a book after Kafka's death in 1924.
