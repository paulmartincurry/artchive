Sharpening the Scythe

Käthe Kollwitz · 1905 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Peasant War cycle took Kollwitz from 1902 to 1908, with many preliminary drawings and discarded attempts in lithography, and ended as seven etchings. This is one of them. The Cleveland impression is from the twelfth and last state of the plate.
