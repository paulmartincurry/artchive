Mask Visor in Form of a Human Face

Kolman Helmschmid (attributed) · c. 1515 · Steel, gold · H. 30.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The visor is attributed to Kolman Helmschmid of Augsburg, who lived from 1471 to 1532. His family name means helmet smith, and the Helmschmids made armour for several Holy Roman Emperors and for the rulers of Spain. The visor is steel and gold and weighs 2,146 grams.
