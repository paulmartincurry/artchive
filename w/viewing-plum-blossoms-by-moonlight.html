Viewing Plum Blossoms by Moonlight

Ma Yuan · early 13th century · Fan mounted as an album leaf, ink and color on silk · 25.1 × 26.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This was painted on a fan and later mounted flat as an album leaf, about 25 by 27 centimetres. John M. Crawford Jr. gave it to the Met in 1986. Ma Yuan learned at first from the work of Li Tang before he settled into his own manner, and a writer of his time described his pine trees as strong as iron wire. Japanese painters including Shūbun and Sesshū took up his style.
