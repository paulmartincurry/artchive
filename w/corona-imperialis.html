Corona Imperialis (Hortus Eystettensis)

Basilius Besler & Raphael Custos · 1613 · Hand-coloured copper engraving · Universitätsbibliothek Eichstätt-Ingolstadt

Free to use

Spring is the fullest part of the book, with 134 plates showing 454 plants. The plates are not signed at the bottom, and some engravers hid their names in the picture: Raphael Custos signed this one on a leaf. The colour on this copy was added by hand.
