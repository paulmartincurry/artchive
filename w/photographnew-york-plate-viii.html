Photograph—New York, Plate VIII (Camera Work 49/50)

Paul Strand · published 1917 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This was plate VIII in the final issue of Camera Work, published in June 1917. Stieglitz had introduced Strand's photographs in issue 48, which did not appear until October 1916. Soon after the last issue, Stieglitz found he could no longer afford to publish the magazine or run his gallery, 291.
