I hid my love when young till I
Couldn't bear the buzzing of a fly;
I hid my love to my despite
Till I could not bear to look at light:

Secret Love

John Clare · 1842–1864 (published 1920)

Clare was committed to Northampton General Lunatic Asylum between Christmas and New Year, 1841, and died in 1864, in his 71st year. The 1920 edition this text comes from prints the poem among his asylum poems.
