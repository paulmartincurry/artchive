Better by far you should forget and smile
Than that you should remember and be sad.

Remember

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Christina Rossetti was thirty-one in 1862, when Macmillan published Goblin Market and Other Poems. Her earlier book, Verses, had been printed privately in 1847. In the 1840s her father, a poet and political exile from Vasto in Italy, lost his health and gave up his teaching post at King's College, and her mother went out to teach to support the family. At fourteen Christina had a nervous breakdown and left school, and bouts of depression followed.
