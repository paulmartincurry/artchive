The Bewitched Groom

Hans Baldung Grien · c. 1544 · Woodcut; first of two states · sheet: 33.9 × 20 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Baldung cut this design around 1544, about a year before his death in Strasbourg in September 1545, at about sixty-one. Witchcraft had held his interest since his chiaroscuro witches of 1510 and lasted to the end of his career; in Strasbourg the bishop was charged with finding and prosecuting witches. His nickname, Grien, came from his liking for the colour green.
