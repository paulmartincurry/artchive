Fireworks at Ryōgoku Bridge (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1858 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 33.7 × 22.2 cm (image) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The date seal on this print reads the eighth month of 1858. Hiroshige died that year, aged 62, during the great cholera epidemic in Edo, and was buried at a Zen temple in Asakusa. Hiroshige II finished the series after his death, bringing it to 119 prints.
