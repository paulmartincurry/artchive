Discobolus (Lancellotti Discobolus)

After Myron · Roman copy, 1st–2nd century CE, of a Greek bronze of about 460–450 BCE · Marble · Museo Nazionale Romano, Palazzo Massimo alle Terme, Rome

Free to use with credit. Photo: Livioandronico2013, CC BY-SA 4.0

Myron's original bronze of about 460 to 450 BC is lost and is known only through Roman marble copies. This one was the first to be found, in 1781, on a Massimo family property on the Esquiline Hill in Rome, and the family kept close watch over who could see it. In 1938 Galeazzo Ciano, the Foreign Minister, sold it to Adolf Hitler for five million lire over the protests of the Education Minister. It went by rail to the Glyptothek in Munich and came back to Rome in 1948.
