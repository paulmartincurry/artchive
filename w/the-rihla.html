The Rihla (Taghaza)

Ibn Battuta (Dictated to Ibn Juzayy) · 1355 CE

Translated by H. A. R. Gibb, 1929 · Public domain in the United States (published 1929); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Ibn Battuta did not write the book himself. On his final return to Morocco in 1353 he dictated it to the scholar Ibn Juzayy, whom the Marinid sultan had commissioned to take down and edit the account. He had set out for Mecca in 1325, aged twenty-one, and by his own description covered about 117,000 kilometres.
