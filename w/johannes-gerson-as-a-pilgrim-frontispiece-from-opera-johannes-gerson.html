Johannes Gerson as a Pilgrim, frontispiece from Opera Johannes Gerson

Maker unknown (German) · 1488 · Hand-colored woodcut in black on cream laid paper · Image/block: 22.2 × 14.8 cm (8 3/4 × 5 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The theologian Jean Gerson (1363–1429) is shown on an imagined pilgrimage with a dog and a walking staff. The woodblock was reissued and copied often.
