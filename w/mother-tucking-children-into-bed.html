Mother Tucking Children into Bed (Mother's Little Angels)

Norman Rockwell · 1920; The Literary Digest, 29 January 1921 · Oil on canvas, painted for a magazine cover

Free to use

Rudolph Edward Leppert Sr., art editor of The Literary Digest, commissioned it in 1920 for the magazine's cover of 29 January 1921. The model for the mother was Rockwell's wife at the time, Irene.
