The Climax (Salome with the Head of Iokanaan)

Aubrey Beardsley · 1893 · Line block print

Free to use

Aubrey Beardsley was 21 when Oscar Wilde commissioned sixteen illustrations from him for the English edition of his play Salome. This one is a second version, redrawn because the journal The Studio owned the copyright to the first. Beardsley died of tuberculosis at 25. The original pen and ink drawing went missing for 80 years and was found hanging in a bathroom in Boston, whose owner had inherited it from his grandmother. It then sold for $213,300.
