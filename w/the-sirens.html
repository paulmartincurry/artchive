The Sirens

Auguste Rodin · 1889 · Bronze · 43.2 x 41.6 x 30.5 cm (17 x 16 3/8 x 12 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Rodin made the group for The Gates of Hell, his great work based on Dante's Divine Comedy. The Gates were never finished as planned, and several of their figures were later cast on their own.
