Statue of Kūya

Kōshō · First decade of the 13th Century · Wood, coloured · Rokuharamitsu-ji Temple

Free to use

The monk Kūya, shown here, founded Rokuharamitsu-ji, the temple that holds this statue, and died there in 972. He travelled with images and added rhythm and dance to his prayers. In 963 he held a ceremony for a copy of a sutra written in gold ink, begun thirteen years earlier on community donations. The statue was made more than two centuries after his death.
