Final Study for "Bathers at Asnières"

Georges Seurat · 1883 · Oil on panel · 15.8 × 25.1 cm (6 1/4 × 9 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It is a study for Seurat's large Bathers at Asnières, now in London. The bank is in the working-class suburb of Asnières, across the river from the park he painted in A Sunday on La Grande Jatte.
