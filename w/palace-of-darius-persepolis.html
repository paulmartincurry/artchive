Palace of Darius (Tachara), Persepolis

Maker unknown · ca. 515–490 BCE · Stone

Free to use with credit. Photo: Laurens R. Krol, CC BY 4.0

Persepolis stands on a terrace of 125,000 square metres, partly built up and partly cut out of a mountain. Grey limestone was the main building material, and inscriptions on the buildings support the belief that Darius I built the terrace and the palaces. Alexander the Great's army took the complex in 330 BC, and soon afterwards its wooden parts were destroyed by fire. Much of the rubble is thought to have been reused to build the nearby city of Estakhr.
