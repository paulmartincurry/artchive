Pilgrimage to the Torinomachi Festival across the Ricefields of Asakusa (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper; vertical ōban · 33.2 × 22.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The print shows a Yoshiwara teahouse, and beyond it the Ōtori Shrine and Mount Fuji. The rooster festival is hinted at by hairpins shaped like bear's paws, which resemble the good-luck rakes carried by the pilgrims in the background. The date seal reads the eleventh month of 1857.
