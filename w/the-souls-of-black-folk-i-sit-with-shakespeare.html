I sit with Shakespeare and he winces not. Across the color line I move arm in arm with Balzac and Dumas

The Souls of Black Folk — I sit with Shakespeare (Ch. VI)

W. E. B. Du Bois · 1903

Du Bois took a first degree at Fisk University and three more at Harvard, where he was the first African American to earn a doctorate. He also studied at the Friedrich Wilhelm University. His doctoral thesis, on the suppression of the African slave trade to the United States, is dated 1896, seven years before this book.
