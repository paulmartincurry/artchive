In the Storm of the Third Year of the Five Year Plan

Gustav Klutsis · 1930 · Color offset lithograph on cream wove paper, laid down on cheesecloth · 103.5 × 73.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Klutsis had served as a Latvian rifleman in Lenin's guard at the Smolny in 1917–18 and later guarded the Kremlin. Eight years after this poster, on 16 January 1938, he was arrested in Moscow as he prepared to leave for the New York World's Fair. He was shot on 26 February 1938 at the Butovo NKVD ground near Moscow, and was rehabilitated in 1956 for lack of evidence of any crime.
