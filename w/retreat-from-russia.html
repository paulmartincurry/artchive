Retreat from Russia (Retour de Russie)

Théodore Géricault · 1818 · Lithograph · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

After his return from Italy Géricault undertook a series of lithographs on military subjects; this print is dated 1818. Years earlier he had left the classroom of his teacher Pierre-Narcisse Guérin to study at the Louvre, where from 1810 to 1815 he copied paintings by Rubens, Titian, Velázquez and Rembrandt.
