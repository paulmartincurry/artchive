Official Protest to the Treaty of Annexation

Queen Liliʻuokalani · 1897

Liliʻuokalani signed her protest in Washington on 17 June 1897, at the age of fifty-eight. She had reigned for two years before a coup removed her in January 1893. After a failed uprising in her name she was held under house arrest in ʻIolani Palace, and on 24 January 1895 she abdicated under the threat that her imprisoned supporters would be executed. The United States annexed Hawaiʻi after the outbreak of the Spanish–American War.
