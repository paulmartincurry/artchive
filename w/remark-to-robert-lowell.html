…you must say I was the loneliest person who ever lived.

Remark to Robert Lowell

Elizabeth Bishop · 1948 (recorded 1957)

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Randall Jarrell introduced Bishop to Robert Lowell in 1947, a year after her first book, North & South, was published. The two stayed friends, mostly by letter, until Lowell's death in 1977. She turned thirty-seven in 1948. Their complete correspondence was published in 2008 and runs to almost nine hundred pages.
