But let them call me crazy, and shut me in a cell,
And lock the door with seven keys, to close it fast and well;

El dulce milagro (The Sweet Miracle)

Juana de Ibarbourou · 1919

Translated by Alice Stone Blackwell, 1929

Juana de Ibarbourou had moved to Montevideo with her family in 1918, and her book Lenguas de diamante came out the next year. She was twenty-seven. Her birth year is often given as 1895, but the civil registry in Melo, signed by two witnesses, records 1892. Ten years after the book she was given the title Juana de América in the Legislative Palace of Montevideo.
