I fynde no peace and all my warre is done,
I feare and hope, I bourne and freese lyke yse;
I flye above the wynde, yet cannot ryse;

I Find No Peace (Canzoniere 134)

Petrarch · 14th century; English before 1542

Translated by Thomas Wyatt

Thomas Wyatt turned this sonnet into English at the court of Henry VIII, where he served as a diplomat; in 1526 a mission took him to Rome. His poems circulated at court and were not published under his name until Tottel's Miscellany in 1557, fifteen years after his death. Petrarch's collection holds 366 poems, written over a period of forty years.
