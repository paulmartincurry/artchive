And yet by heaven, I think my love as rare,
As any she belied with false compare.

Sonnet 130

William Shakespeare · 1609

Thomas Watson, Michael Drayton and Barnabe Barnes were among the poets of the sonnet craze whose conventions this poem plays with. The comparison of hair to wire is also found in Richard Linche's collection Diella, where the hair is set against gold wire. According to Carl Atkins, many early editors took the sonnet at face value, as a disparagement of the writer's mistress.
