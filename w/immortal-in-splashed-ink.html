Immortal in Splashed Ink

Liang Kai · late 12th–early 13th century · Album leaf, ink on paper · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Except for a few fine lines at the face, ears and chest, the figure is laid down in broad swaths of wet ink. Liang Kai was called Madman Liang for his informal pictures. He held the rank of Painter-in-Attendance at the Southern Song court and was awarded the Golden Belt, but he left it behind when he quit the court to practise Chan Buddhism. The leaf is the second in an album in the National Palace Museum, Taipei.
