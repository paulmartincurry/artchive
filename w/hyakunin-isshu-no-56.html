Hyakunin Isshu, No. 56

Izumi Shikibu · ca. 1008 CE

Translated by Joshua S. Mostow · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The name she is known by is not her own. Izumi was the province her first husband governed, and Shikibu was her father's court title. Probably in 1009 she joined the court of the imperial consort Fujiwara no Shōshi, where Murasaki Shikibu also served, then married a military commander and left with him for Tango Province. She later took Buddhist robes and wore them for the rest of her life. The year of her death is not known.
