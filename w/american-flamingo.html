American Flamingo (The Birds of America, Plate 431)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1838 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 101.3 × 68.3 cm (39 7/8 × 26 7/8 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is plate 431 of the book's 435, printed in 1838, the year the project was finished. It belongs to the National Gallery of Art's set, given in 1945 by Mrs. Walter B. James, one of only two known sets of the prints kept in their original unbound state.
