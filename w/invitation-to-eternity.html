The Artchive
Say, wilt thou go with me, sweet maid,
Say, maiden, wilt thou go with me
Through the valley-depths of shade,
Of bright and dark obscurity;

Invitation to Eternity

John Clare · 1842–1864 (this text 1920)

Clare spent his last years in Northampton General Lunatic Asylum, to which he was committed between Christmas and New Year, 1841. He died of a stroke on 20 May 1864, in his 71st year. The 1920 edition this text comes from prints the poem among his asylum poems.
