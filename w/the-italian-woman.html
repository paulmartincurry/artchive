The Italian Woman

Amedeo Modigliani · ca. 1918–19 · Oil on canvas · 40 3/8 × 26 3/8 in. (102.6 × 67 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Modigliani made this canvas, 102.6 by 67 centimetres, in the last year or so of his life. He had caught tuberculosis at sixteen, and he died of tubercular meningitis at the Hôpital de la Charité in Paris on 24 January 1920, at thirty-five. He had often given his work away in exchange for meals in restaurants, and he died destitute. The painting came to the Met in 1956 as a gift of the Chester Dale Collection.
