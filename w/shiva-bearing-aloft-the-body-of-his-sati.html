Shiva Bearing Aloft the Body of His Sati

Maker unknown · c. 1890 · Gum tempera, graphite, ink, and tin on paper · 45.6 × 27.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kalighat pictures were made by families of painters near the Kalighat Kali Temple in Kolkata, almost on an assembly line: one copied the outline in pencil, another shaded the flesh, a third laid in the colours, and the outlines were finished in lamp black. When Cleveland acquired this one it was called Shiva Bearing Aloft the Body of His Spouse Who Has Died in Childbirth, though the museum knows of no myth on that theme. William E. Ward gave it in 2003.
