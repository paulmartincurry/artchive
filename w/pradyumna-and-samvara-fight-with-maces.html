Pradyumna and Samvara Fight with Maces (Large Basohli Bhagavata Purana)

Maker unknown · c. 1760–65 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · 28 × 38.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In the story, the demon Samvara had heard a prophecy that Pradyumna would kill him, so he threw the infant into the ocean, where a fish swallowed him. The fish was caught and brought to Samvara's own kitchen, and Mayavati found the baby alive inside it. This page catches the moment before Pradyumna's mace ends the fight. The Mundane Club gave it to Cleveland in 1960.
