Drops of Rain (Camera Work 23)

Clarence H. White · 1908 · Photogravure · The Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The gravure comes from Camera Work number 23, July 1908, which Stieglitz gave over to 16 of White's photographs. It was only the third time he had devoted an issue to a single photographer, after Steichen and Coburn. That same year White began teaching at the Brooklyn Institute of Arts and Sciences, a post he kept for thirteen years.
