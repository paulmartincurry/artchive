Study for the Magnanimous Cuckold

Lyubov Popova · 1920 · Pen and brown ink and graphite (recto) and graphite and pen and green ink (verso) on cream wove paper · 17.6 × 18 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Popova designed the stage for Vsevolod Meyerhold's 1922 production of Fernand Crommelynck's play The Magnanimous Cuckold. A year earlier, at the 5x5=25 exhibition, she and four other Constructivists had declared that easel painting was to be abandoned. She died in Moscow in 1924, aged 35, of scarlet fever caught from her son, two days after his death. The sheet is drawn on both sides, in pen and graphite, and came to Chicago as a gift in 2023.
