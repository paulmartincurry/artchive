Water Lilies, 1906

Claude Monet · 1906 · Oil on canvas · 89.9 × 94.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Monet's paintings of the water garden he made at Giverny, after buying the house in 1890, number about 250, from the early pictures of the pond and its wooden bridge to the near-abstract series made for the Musée de l'Orangerie between 1914 and 1926.
