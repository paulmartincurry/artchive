God of War

Paul Klee · 1937 · Gouache and tempera · Sheet: 35.2 × 29 cm (13 7/8 × 11 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Klee painted this on a sheet of newspaper, and the printed article showing through is about the Spanish civil war. Shortly before, he had been driven out of Germany to his native Switzerland. He wrote the title in German, Kriegs Gott, on the mount below. Lockwood Thompson of Cleveland bought it from a Paris gallery in September 1938 and kept it until his death in 1992, when it passed to the museum.
