Salvador Dali A (Dalí Atomicus, Before Retouching)

Philippe Halsman · c. 1948 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This print came to the Library of Congress from the US Copyright Office in 1948 and shows the picture before retouching: the assistant holding the chair is still in the frame and the easel holds an empty frame, which Dalí later painted on the chosen print. The shoot took five to six hours in Halsman's New York studio, with a 4×5 twin-lens reflex camera he had designed. On his count of three, assistants threw the cats and a bucket of water; on four, Dalí jumped. It took at least 26 takes, and after each one the assistants collected and dried the cats.
