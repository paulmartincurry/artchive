Painting with Green Center

Wassily Kandinsky · 1913 · Oil on canvas · 108.9 × 118.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kandinsky painted this canvas in 1913, when he was forty-six. He had not begun his painting studies until the age of thirty. The next year the outbreak of the First World War sent him back from Germany to Russia.
