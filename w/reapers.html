Reapers

Jean Toomer · 1923

In 1921 Jean Toomer spent a few months as principal of a new agricultural and manual labour school for Black students in Sparta, Georgia, near where his father had lived. The stories, sketches and poems he wrote from that stay became the book Cane, published in 1923. He had taken the name Jean Toomer only in 1919. He let his publisher market him as a Negro writer, grudgingly, to help sales. The book was reissued in 1969, two years after his death.
