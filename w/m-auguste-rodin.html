M. Auguste Rodin (Camera Work 34/35, plate I)

Edward Steichen · 1911 · Photogravure (Japan tissue proof) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress copy is a proof on Japan tissue. The portrait was published as plate I of the double issue of Camera Work, numbers 34 and 35, dated April and July 1911. In 1905 Steichen and Stieglitz had opened the Little Galleries of the Photo-Secession, which gave Rodin some of his first exhibitions in America.
