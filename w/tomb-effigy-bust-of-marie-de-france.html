Tomb Effigy Bust of Marie de France

Jean de Liège · c. 1381 · Marble with lead inlays · 31.1 × 32.4 × 15.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Marie de France was a daughter of King Charles IV and Jeanne d'Évreux, and died at about fourteen. Her tomb effigy was carved by Jean de Liège, a sculptor of Flemish origin who specialised in funerary sculpture and died in 1381. The effigy was destroyed, except for this bust. It is marble with lead inlays, 31.1 centimetres high.
