The Ten Largest, No. 7, Adulthood (Group IV)

Hilma af Klint · 1907 · Tempera on paper, mounted on canvas · Hilma af Klint Foundation, Stockholm

Free to use

This is one of a series of large paintings dated 1907, each measuring about 240 by 320 centimetres. Hilma af Klint had painted her first abstract series a year earlier, at the age of 44, while earning her income from conventional landscapes, botanical drawings and portraits. She wrote in her notebook that she worked without preliminary drawings. She directed that the work remain unseen until twenty years after her death.
