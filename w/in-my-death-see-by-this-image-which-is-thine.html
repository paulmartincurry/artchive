In My Death, See by This Image, Which Is Thine Own, How Utterly Thou Hast Murdered Thyself (William Wilson)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Clarke trained in his father's church-decorating business in Dublin, which had a stained glass division, and won the gold medal for stained glass at South Kensington in 1911, 1912 and 1913. The heavy black lines of his book drawings echo his glass. Poe set this story in Stoke Newington, where he went to school from 1817 to 1820; it was the first of his works translated into French, in December 1844.
