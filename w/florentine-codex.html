Florentine Codex

Maker unknown · 1545–1577 · Biblioteca Medicea Laurenziana, Florence

The Franciscan friar Bernardino de Sahagún worked on this study from 1545 until his death in 1590, with Nahua elders and writers who had been his students. Its pages are set in two columns, the Nahuatl text on the right and a Spanish version on the left. Study of how eyes, profiles and bodies are drawn suggests that twenty-two artists made the pictures, in black outline first with colour added later. Some of the spaces left for pictures were never filled. Scholars did not know of the manuscript until a description of it was published in 1793.
