I cease not from desire till my desire
Is satisfied; or let my mouth attain
My love’s red mouth, or let my soul expire,
Sighed from those lips that sought her lips in vain.

I Cease Not from Desire

Hafez of Shiraz · 14th century

Translated by Gertrude Bell

Hafez is a pen name. It is the title given to someone who has memorised the Quran, which he did at an early age. Few details of his life are known, and one tradition has him working in a bakery, delivering bread, before he found his Sufi master. His poems were probably gathered into the Divan after his death, and no definitive version exists: editions run from 573 poems to 994.
