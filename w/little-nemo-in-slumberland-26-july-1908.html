Little Nemo in Slumberland, 26 July 1908

Winsor McCay · 26 July 1908 · Newspaper comic page, colour

Free to use

Little Nemo filled a whole Sunday newspaper page, and McCay experimented with the timing of the page and the size and shape of its panels; in the last panel Nemo always wakes up in bed. Before newspapers, McCay made posters and performed in dime museums, and he was also a popular performer of chalk talks on the vaudeville circuit.
