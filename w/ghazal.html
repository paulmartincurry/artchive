If that Shirazi Turk would take
My heart within his loving hand
I'd give for his dark mole the towns
Of Bokhara and Samarqand;

Ghazal (The Shirazi Turk)

Hafez of Shiraz · 14th Century CE

Translated by Dick Davis · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The couplet offering Samarkand and Bukhara for a mole has a tale attached to it. In the tale Timur, whose capital was Samarkand, summoned Hafez in anger to account for the verse, was pleased by his answer and sent him away with gifts. Hafez was born in Shiraz and is buried there. He lived on the patronage of a succession of local rulers.
