I had lost something which could never be restored to me while I lived. All the grace, the beauty, the poetry had gone out of the majestic river!

Life on the Mississippi — The language of this water (Ch. 9)

Mark Twain (Samuel Langhorne Clemens) · 1883

Twain was twenty-one when he began training as a steamboat pilot, as the apprentice of Horace E. Bixby. The section of the book on learning the river was first published in 1876 under the title Old Times on the Mississippi. The whole book came out in 1883, in the United States and Great Britain at once, and it was the first to be submitted to a publisher as a typewritten manuscript. Twain did not use the typewriter himself.
