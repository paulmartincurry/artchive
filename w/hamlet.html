To be, or not to be, that is the question:
Whether 'tis nobler in the mind to suffer.

Hamlet (Act III, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · 1601

Hamlet is Shakespeare's longest play, written sometime between 1599 and 1601. Three early versions survive: the First Quarto of 1603, the Second Quarto of 1604 and the First Folio of 1623. Each includes lines and passages missing from the others. Judging by the number of reprints, it was his fourth most popular play during his lifetime.
