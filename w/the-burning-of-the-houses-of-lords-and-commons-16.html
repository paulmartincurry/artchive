The Burning of the Houses of Lords and Commons, 16 October 1834

J. M. W. Turner · 1835 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 92 × 123.2 cm (36 1/4 × 48 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Parliament burned on the night of 16 October 1834; a low tide made it hard to pump water and slowed the steamers towing fire equipment, and the blaze ran for hours. Turner painted the event twice. This version looks at the fire from downriver on the southeast bank, and in it he made the flames taller than they were. John Garth Marshall of Leeds bought it from him, and John L. Severance of Cleveland bought it in 1922.
