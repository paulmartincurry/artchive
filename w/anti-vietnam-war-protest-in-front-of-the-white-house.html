Anti-Vietnam war protest in front of the White House, in support of Eartha Kitt

Warren K. Leffler · 19 January 1968 · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Warren K. Leffler made this outside the White House on 19 January 1968, in support of the singer Eartha Kitt. The Library showed the print in its exhibition Hope for America.
