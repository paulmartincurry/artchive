The Tribute Money

Masaccio · c. 1425 · Fresco · 247 × 597 cm · Brancacci Chapel, Santa Maria del Carmine, Florence

Free to use

Felice Brancacci, a silk merchant, hired Masolino to paint his family chapel in Santa Maria del Carmine some time between 1423 and 1425; Masaccio, eighteen years younger, joined him. The fresco tells three moments of the story at once, and its lines converge on the head of Christ. A fire ruined the church in 1771 and damaged the frescoes. A restoration from 1981 to 1990 found that the parts painted on dry plaster, such as the leaves on the trees, were gone.
