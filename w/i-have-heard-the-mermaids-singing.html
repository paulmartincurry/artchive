I have heard the mermaids singing, each to each.

I do not think that they will sing to me.

I Have Heard the Mermaids Singing (The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock, closing)

T. S. Eliot · 1915

The year this poem was published, Eliot was teaching English at Birkbeck College in London. He had come to England in 1914 at twenty-five on a scholarship to Merton College, Oxford, and left Merton after a year. On 22 September 1914 he called at Ezra Pound's flat in London, and Pound introduced him everywhere. On 26 June 1915 he married Vivienne Haigh-Wood, a governess, at Hampstead Register Office.
