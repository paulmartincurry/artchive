Witches' Sabbath (El Aquelarre)

Francisco de Goya y Lucientes · 1798 · Oil on canvas · Lázaro Galdiano Museum

The canvas is 43 centimetres tall and 30 wide. The Duke and Duchess of Osuna bought it in 1798 together with five other paintings of witchcraft by Goya, and the purchase is credited to the duchess. It is not known whether they commissioned the set or bought it finished. The financier José Lázaro Galdiano bought the painting in the twentieth century, and it went to the Spanish state at his death.
