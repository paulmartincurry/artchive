Witches' Kitchen

Frans Francken the Younger · c. 1610 · Oak panel · 52 × 66.1 cm · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Frans Francken the Younger painted this on an oak panel, 52 by 66.1 centimetres, when he was about twenty-nine. At lower right, inside a magic circle, is the word toverye, sorcery: what the women were accused of. He had become an independent master in the Antwerp painters' guild in 1605 and ran a large workshop that made many copies of his compositions. The panel is recorded in the secular treasury by 1773.
