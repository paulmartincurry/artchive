The Artchive

Atalanta Fugiens

Michael Maier & Matthäus Merian the Elder · 1617–1618

Free to use

The book holds fifty engravings on copper by Merian. Each comes with a Latin verse and discourse, an epigram in German, and a fugue for three voices to which the verse is sung. Forty of the fifty fugues are based on compositions by John Farmer. Johann Theodor de Bry published it at Oppenheim in 1617, and a second edition followed in 1618.
