Set me as a seal upon thine heart, as a seal upon thine arm:
for love is strong as death; jealousy is cruel as the grave.

Song of Songs 8:6

Song of Solomon (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · ca. 3rd century BCE

The book names no author and gives no date or place for its making. Its heading calls it Solomon's, but the Hebrew it is written in points to a time centuries after that king, and most scholars date it to the 3rd century BCE. Whether it is a single poem or a collection of separate songs is still not settled.
