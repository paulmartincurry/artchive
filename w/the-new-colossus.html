Give me your tired, your poor,
Your huddled masses yearning to breathe free,

The New Colossus

Emma Lazarus · 1883

Emma Lazarus was thirty-four when she was asked for a poem to be auctioned for the fund that paid for the Statue of Liberty's pedestal. She refused at first. The sonnet was the first entry read when the exhibition opened on November 2, 1883, and it played no part when the statue itself was opened in 1886. A friend, Georgina Schuyler, had it put up on a bronze plaque inside the pedestal in 1903, sixteen years after the poet's death. The plaque leaves out one comma.
