Study for the Nude Youth over the Prophet Daniel

Michelangelo · 1510–11 · red chalk over black chalk or charcoal · Sheet: 34.3 × 24.3 cm (13 1/2 × 9 9/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The figure is one of the twenty nudes Michelangelo painted at the corners of the scenes running down the centre of the Sistine ceiling, which took him four years. He worked out their poses in red chalk before painting each section of wet plaster. When he ran out of room for the left foot, he turned the sheet over and drew the foot there, with three more sketches of the big toe. In 1933 the drawing was sold in Romania as the work of his school; a Cleveland collector, Henry G. Dalton, bought it in 1937.
