Marigold (Printed Cotton)

William Morris · 20th century · plain weave cotton, printed · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Marigold was one of the first patterns Morris designed for printing on fabric, and it began as a wallpaper. It was one of only a few designs that clients could buy as both cloth and wallpaper, and it was also printed on linoleum. This length was printed in the twentieth century, after his death in 1896.
