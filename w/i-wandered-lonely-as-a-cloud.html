I wander'd lonely as a cloud
That floats on high o'er vales and hills,
When all at once I saw a crowd,
A host of golden daffodils

I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud (Daffodils)

William Wordsworth · 1807, revised 1815

The daffodils grew in a long belt on the shore of Ullswater, where Wordsworth and his sister Dorothy walked on 15 April 1802; she described them in her journal that day. He wrote the poem in 1804. His wife, Mary Hutchinson, contributed two of its lines, which he later said were the best in it. The book it first appeared in was poorly reviewed, by Lord Byron among others.
