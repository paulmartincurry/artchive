And cried, 'Before I am old
I shall have written him one
Poem maybe as cold
And passionate as the dawn.

The Fisherman

W. B. Yeats · 1917

The Wild Swans at Coole, a collection of twenty-nine poems and a play, was first published by the Cuala Press in November 1917. Macmillan republished the poems in March 1919 without the play but with seventeen more poems.
