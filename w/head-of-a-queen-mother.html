Head of a Queen Mother (Ùhúnmwèlaò ọ́ghé iyọ́bà)

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · 18th–19th century · Brass, iron · 53.3 × 26.7 × 27.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

When a queen mother died, her son the king was expected to commission art for her altar in the shrine of the kings in the palace. The title was created by King Esigie for his mother, Idia, who is reputed to have rallied support for him in a civil war. This head stands 53.3 centimetres high and was given to the Met in 1991 by Mr. and Mrs. Klaus G. Perls.
