Do not go to the garden of flowers! O Friend! go not there; In your body is the garden of flowers. Take your seat on the thousand petals of the lotus, and there gaze on the Infinite Beauty.

Songs of Kabir — Do Not Go to the Garden of Flowers (IV)

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

Tagore's translation was first published in New York in 1915 and in Britain as One Hundred Poems of Kabir. The poems had been collected from mendicants across India by Kshitimohan Sen.
