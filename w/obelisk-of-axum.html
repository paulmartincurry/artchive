Obelisk of Axum

Maker unknown · 4th Century · Phonolite

Free to use with credit. Photo: Ondřej Žváček, CC BY 2.5

The stele is a single block 24 metres tall and weighing 160 tonnes, probably carved in the fourth century to mark a royal burial chamber. In 1937 Italian forces cut it into three pieces, trucked it to the port of Massawa in five trips over two months, and set it up in Rome. Italy agreed in 1947 to give it back. The pieces were flown home in April 2005 after the runway at Axum was lengthened for the purpose, and the stele was raised again on 4 September 2008, pinned with Kevlar bars in place of steel.
