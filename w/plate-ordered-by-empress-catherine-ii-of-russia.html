Plate ordered by Empress Catherine II of Russia

Manufacture nationale de Sèvres · 1778 · Soft-paste porcelain, turquoise-blue ground, polychrome enamels, and gilding · 2.9 × 26.1 cm (1 1/8 × 10 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The plate belongs to a dinner service of about eight hundred pieces that Catherine the Great ordered from the Sèvres factory in 1776, through her ambassador in Paris, asking for it in the best and newest style. It is marked with her monogram, E II.
