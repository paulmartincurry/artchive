Young Man and Woman in an Inn

Frans Hals · 1623 · Oil on canvas · 105.4 × 79.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hals signed and dated the canvas on the edge of the chimney-piece, F. Hals 1623. Seymour Slive called it the only dated genre painting by Hals; in 1989 Claus Grimm rejected the attribution and called it a copy of a lost original. It was long taken for a portrait of Pieter Ramp, a young ensign of the Haarlem civic guard, an identification since rejected.
