The Kiss of Peace and Justice

Laurent de La Hyre · 1654 · Oil on canvas · Framed: 68.5 x 89 x 6.5 cm (26 15/16 x 35 1/16 x 2 9/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The figures of Peace and Justice may refer to Psalm 85:10, where righteousness and peace kiss; the pastoral landscape is described two verses later.
