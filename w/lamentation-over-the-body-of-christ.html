Lamentation over the Body of Christ

Gerard David · c. 1500 · Oil on panel · 55.1 × 62.8 cm (21 1/2 × 24 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It was once part of the Saint Anne Altarpiece, ten panels made by Gerard David and his workshop for export to Spain. It was likely the central panel, directly behind the altar.
