Mask for Egungun (Ere Egungun)

Maker unknown · Late 19th century · Wood and pigment · 38.1 × 24.8 × 31.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Masks like this were worn at Egungun festivals honouring lineage ancestors, and at funerals of important lineage members. This one shows a figure in a pith helmet, with a bird on the crest and five smaller birds around the rim, and the holes around its edge let it be laced to the cloth costume. The Egungun masquerade began in the Yoruba kingdom of Oyo, perhaps as early as the seventeenth century. The Art Institute bought the mask in London in 1976.
