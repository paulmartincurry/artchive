That Liberty Shall Not Perish from the Earth: Buy Liberty Bonds, Fourth Liberty Loan

Joseph Pennell · 1918 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

Joseph Pennell was a Philadelphia Quaker who had spent most of his working life in Europe and made more than 1,800 prints. Given a permit to draw the front at Verdun, he found the scenes unbearable and left without making any pictures. He was sixty-one when he drew New York in flames for the Fourth Liberty Loan.
