It was a pleasure to burn.

Fahrenheit 451

Ray Bradbury · 1953

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

The novel grew out of two of Bradbury's short stories, which he combined into The Fireman, a novella published in Galaxy Science Fiction in 1951. Stanley Kauffmann, an editor at Ballantine Books, urged him to make it a full novel. Bradbury finished the manuscript in 1953 and it was published later that year.
