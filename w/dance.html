Dance (La Danse)

Henri Matisse · 1910 · Oil on canvas · Hermitage Museum, Saint Petersburg

Free to use

The Russian businessman and collector Sergei Shchukin asked Henri Matisse for this panel and its companion, Music. Until the October Revolution of 1917 the two hung together on the staircase of Shchukin's mansion in Moscow. The canvas is 260 cm high and 391 cm wide. Matisse painted a full-size study in paler colours in March 1909, which is now in the Museum of Modern Art in New York.
