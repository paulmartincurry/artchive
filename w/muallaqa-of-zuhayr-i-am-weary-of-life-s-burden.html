I am weary of life's burden: well a man may weary be
After eighty years, and this much now is manifest to me:
Death is like a night-blind camel stumbling on:--the smitten die
But the others age and wax in weakness whom he passes by.

Muʿallaqa of Zuhayr — I Am Weary of Life's Burden

Zuhayr ibn Abī Sulmā · late 6th century CE

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1907

Zuhayr's ode refers to the feuds of the kindred tribes Banu Abs and Banu Dhubyan, and the scholar Theodor Nöldeke placed his working years at the end of the sixth century. His father was a poet, and his son Ka'b composed a eulogy on the Prophet in 630. The Saudi land artist Zahrah al Ghamdi based her installation After Illusion, shown at the Venice Biennale in 2018, on his poetry.
