And malt does more than Milton can
To justify God's ways to man.

Terence, this is stupid stuff (A Shropshire Lad, LXII, lines 19–42)

A. E. Housman · 1896

Housman failed his final examination at Oxford and in 1882 took work as a patent examiner in London. In his spare time he worked on the texts of Greek and Latin authors, and his publications earned him the professorship of Latin at University College London in 1892. He is said to have first called the book The Poems of Terence Hearsay, and changed the title at the suggestion of a colleague in the British Museum.
