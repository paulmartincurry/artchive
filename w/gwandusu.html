Gwandusu

Maker unknown · 15th–17th century · Wood (Cordyla africana or Lonchocarpus), applied organic materials · 122.6 × 32.4 × 33.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bamana Jo and Gwan association figures were shown in groups at an annual public ceremony, with supporting figures standing in front of the main sculptures, usually a seated man with a lance and medicine horn and a seated mother with a child. This one was carved by a Bamana blacksmith between the fifteenth and seventeenth centuries and is 122.6 centimetres tall.
