Fritz Lang · 1921 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The sets were designed by Walter Röhrig, Hermann Warm and Robert Herlth; Röhrig and Warm had worked on The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari the year before. German critics received the film poorly, complaining it was not German enough, and it found its audience first in France. Douglas Fairbanks was reportedly so taken with its effects, the flying carpet above all, that he bought the rights so they could be copied for The Thief of Bagdad in 1924.
