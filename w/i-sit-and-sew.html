I sit and sew--a useless task it seems,
My hands grown tired, my head weighed down with dreams--

I Sit and Sew

Alice Dunbar-Nelson · 1918

In 1918 Alice Dunbar-Nelson was a field representative for the Woman's Committee of the Council of Defense. She supported American involvement in the First World War and organised events to bring other African Americans behind it. She was in her early forties, had taught at Howard High School in Wilmington, Delaware, for more than a decade, and had married the poet and civil rights activist Robert J. Nelson two years before.
