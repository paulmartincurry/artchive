Dancing Dervishes (Divan of Hafiz)

Attributed to Bihzad · c. 1480 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Painting 16 × 10.8 cm; page 29.9 × 18.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting is attributed to Bihzad, though attributions of single paintings to him are often uncertain. According to the writer Qadi Ahmad, Bihzad was orphaned young and raised by the painter and calligrapher Mirak Naqqash, a director of the Timurid royal library in Herat. In 1486 Sultan Husayn Bayqara made Bihzad head of the royal workshops there. This leaf is dated about 1480; the painting is 16 cm high.
