But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

Isaiah 40:28–31

Book of Isaiah (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · 6th century BCE (trans. 1611)

Isaiah's name stops appearing in the book after chapter 39. In 1775 Johann Christoph Döderlein suggested that the book held the work of two prophets more than a century apart, and through most of the 20th century chapters 40 to 55 were held to be the work of an anonymous author writing during the exile in Babylon in the 6th century BCE.
