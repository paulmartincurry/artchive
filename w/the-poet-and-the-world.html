Whatever inspiration is, it's born from a continuous 'I don't know.'

The Poet and the World (Nobel Lecture)

Wisława Szymborska · 1996

Translated by Stanisław Barańczak and Clare Cavanagh · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Szymborska, born in 1923, received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1996. She had lived in Kraków since 1931, where she worked on the staff of the literary review Życie Literackie from 1953 to 1981 and wrote its book review column. Her reputation rests on fewer than 350 poems.
