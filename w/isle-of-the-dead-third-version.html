Isle of the Dead, Third Version

Arnold Böcklin · 1883 · Oil on wood · 80 × 150 cm · Alte Nationalgalerie, Staatliche Museen zu Berlin

Free to use

Böcklin painted this third version in 1883 for his dealer Fritz Gurlitt, who gave the picture its title that year. From this version on, his initials A.B. appear over one of the burial chambers in the rock on the right. Adolf Hitler bought it, hung it at the Berghof and, after 1940, in the New Reich Chancellery in Berlin; after 1945 it was missing until 1979.
