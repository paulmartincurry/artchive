The Artchive

Champ Fleury

Geoffroy Tory · 1529

Free to use

Geoffroy Tory wrote this book himself and published it in Paris in 1529, when he was about forty-nine. He had worked as a bookbinder, an editor of texts, a corrector for the press and a college professor before turning to woodcuts and bookselling. The book is in three parts and draws its letters on a square grid. The year after it appeared he became official printer to King Francis I.
