Irises at Yatsuhashi (Eight Bridges)

Ogata Kōrin · after 1709 · Pair of six-panel folding screens; ink and color on gold leaf on paper · Each screen 163.7 × 352.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kōrin had painted a first pair of iris screens about five to twelve years before these. Unlike the earlier pair, this one includes an angular bridge, a more explicit reference to the episode in The Tales of Ise that inspired both. The Metropolitan Museum of Art has held the screens since 1953.
