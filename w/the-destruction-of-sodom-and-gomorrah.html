The Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah

John Martin · 1852 · Oil on canvas · 136.3 × 212.3 cm · Laing Art Gallery, Newcastle upon Tyne

Free to use

Martin painted this in 1852. While he lived, many regarded him as a great British artist surpassed only by his older contemporary J. M. W. Turner, with whom he competed for recognition. His reputation declined after his death.
