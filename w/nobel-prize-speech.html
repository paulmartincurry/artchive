Nobel Prize Speech

William Faulkner · December 1950

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Faulkner was awarded the 1949 Nobel Prize in Literature and went to Stockholm in December 1950 to receive it. He had attended the University of Mississippi for three semesters before dropping out, and he also worked as a screenwriter.
