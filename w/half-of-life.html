The walls loom
Speechless and cold, in the wind
Weathercocks clatter.

Half of Life (Hälfte des Lebens)

Friedrich Hölderlin · 1804

Translated by Michael Hamburger · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Hölderlin was thirty-four in 1804. Two years earlier he had come home on foot from a tutoring post in Bordeaux, arriving in Nürtingen exhausted. A friend, Isaac von Sinclair, then found him a sinecure as court librarian at Homburg. On 11 September 1806 he was delivered into a clinic in Tübingen, and he spent the rest of his life in a room in a carpenter's house in that town.
