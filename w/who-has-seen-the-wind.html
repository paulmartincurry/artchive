Who has seen the wind?
Neither I nor you:
But when the leaves hang trembling
The wind is passing thro’.

Who Has Seen the Wind?

Christina Rossetti · 1893 edition (Sing-Song)

Sing-Song: A Nursery Rhyme Book was printed with one hundred and twenty illustrations by Arthur Hughes, engraved by the Brothers Dalziel. The poems in it carry no titles, and the contents list them by first line. This text is from the edition Macmillan issued in London and New York in 1893.
