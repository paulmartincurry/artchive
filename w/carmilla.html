I felt a stinging pain as if two large needles darted, an inch or two apart, deep into my breast.

Carmilla (Ch. VII)

Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu · 1872

Carmilla ran as a serial in the magazine The Dark Blue in late 1871 and early 1872, with pictures by two illustrators, David Henry Friston and Michael Fitzgerald. Later in 1872 Le Fanu reprinted it in a story collection and added a preface that turned it into a case file of his invented occult detective, Dr Hesselius. He was a Dublin journalist and newspaper owner, fifty-seven that year, and he died in February 1873. Bram Stoker's Dracula came 25 years after it.
