The rise of the sun recalls to me Sakhr my brother,
And him I remember also at every sunset.

Elegy for Ṣakhr

Al-Khansāʾ (Tumāḍir bint ʿAmr) · ca. 615 CE

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1922

Ṣakhr was the poet's brother. He was wounded avenging the killing of their other brother, Muʿāwiya, in 612, and died of the wounds a year later. Al-Khansāʾ had urged him to it. She composed more than a hundred elegies for the two of them. In her time a woman poet's part was to make elegies for the dead and perform them before the tribe in public contests. Nearly a thousand lines of her poetry remain, collected by a scholar in the ninth century.
