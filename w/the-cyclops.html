The Cyclops (Le Cyclope)

Odilon Redon · ca. 1914 · Oil on cardboard · Kröller-Müller Museum

Free to use

Odilon Redon painted this in oil on a sheet of cardboard, 65.8 by 52.7 centimetres, which was then mounted on wood. Its date is not settled, and it has been placed anywhere between 1898 and 1914. Redon grew up in the Aquitaine region, where one-eyed giants figure in the local folklore.
