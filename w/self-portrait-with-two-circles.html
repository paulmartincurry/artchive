Self-Portrait with Two Circles

Rembrandt van Rijn · c. 1665–1669 · Oil on canvas · Kenwood House, London

Free to use

Rembrandt neither signed nor dated this self-portrait, which is unusual for him. X-rays show that he first turned the body farther to the right, with his arm raised to paint on a canvas at the edge of the picture, and later repainted his left hand on his hip. Jean-Honoré Fragonard made a painted copy of it.
