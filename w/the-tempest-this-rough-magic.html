I’ll break my staff,
Bury it certain fathoms in the earth,
And deeper than did ever plummet sound
I’ll drown my book.

The Tempest — This Rough Magic (Act V, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · 1611

The Tempest was probably written in 1610 or 1611 and is thought to be one of the last plays Shakespeare wrote alone. The First Folio lists it first among his comedies. Prospero's giving up of his magic has been read as Shakespeare's own farewell to the stage, and critics have put forward two of Prospero's speeches as candidates for that retirement speech.
