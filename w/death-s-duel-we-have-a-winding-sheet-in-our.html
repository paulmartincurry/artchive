…we have a winding-sheet in our mother's womb which grows with us from our conception, and we come into the world wound up in that winding-sheet, for we come to seek a grave.

Death's Duel — We have a winding-sheet in our mother's womb

John Donne · 1631

Death's Duel was the last sermon Donne delivered as Dean of St Paul's. He preached it on 25 February 1631, the first Friday in Lent, and it was entered in the Stationers' Register on 30 September of that year.
