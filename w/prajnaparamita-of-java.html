Prajnaparamita of Java

Maker unknown · Late 13th Century · Andesite · National Museum of Indonesia

Free to use with credit. Photo: Gunawan Kartapranata, CC BY-SA 3.0

A Dutch East Indies official, D. Monnereau, found the statue in 1818 or 1819 in ruins near the Singhasari temple in East Java. It was in almost perfect condition, which suggests it had lain buried for a long time. He gave it away in 1820 and it went to the museum in Leiden, where it stayed for more than 158 years. The Netherlands returned it to Indonesia in January 1978. It is 126 centimetres high.
