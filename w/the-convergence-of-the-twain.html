And as the smart ship grew
In stature, grace, and hue,
In shadowy silent distance grew the Iceberg too.

The Convergence of the Twain

Thomas Hardy · 1912

Hardy published this poem in 1912, the year the Titanic sank, with the subtitle Lines on the loss of the Titanic. He was in his early seventies. It is built of eleven three-line stanzas numbered I to XI, each rhyming on a single sound, with a long third line. His wife Emma died the same year.
