Paracas Poncho Displaying Contorted Figures

Maker unknown · c. 200-150 BCE · Wool (camelid), plain weave; embroidered in stem stitches; edged with cross-knit loop stitches; loop and plied fringes · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The poncho was first shown at the Art Institute of Chicago in 1932, on loan from an owner whose name is not recorded, in an exhibition of Peruvian garment embroideries. By 1953 it belonged to Nasli and Alice Heeramaneck, dealers in New York, who sold it to the museum in 1958. It is camelid wool, embroidered in stem stitch.
