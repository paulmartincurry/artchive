Total Eclipse of the Sun (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot watched this eclipse at Creston, in what was then Wyoming Territory, on 29 July 1878, when he was fifty. He made about 7,000 astronomical drawings in his life. Fifteen of them, this one among them, were published as chromolithographs by Charles Scribner's Sons in New York.
