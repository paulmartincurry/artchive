Almond Blossom

Vincent van Gogh · February 1890 · Oil on canvas · 73.3 × 92.4 cm · Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam (Vincent van Gogh Foundation)

Free to use

On 31 January 1890 Theo van Gogh wrote to Vincent that his son had been born and named Vincent Willem. Van Gogh, then at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, began this painting straight away as a gift to hang in the couple's bedroom, and sent it to Paris at the end of April. It stayed with the family until 1962, when the nephew it was painted for transferred it to the Vincent van Gogh Foundation. He went on to found the Van Gogh Museum.
