Suzanne Valadon (attributed) · 1889 · Oil on fabric · Unframed: 48.8 x 60 cm (19 3/16 x 23 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The attribution to Valadon has been rejected by several authorities. Others accept it, saying the picture is atypical because it is an early work, signed and dated 1889; she did not find her mature style until 1891.
