Your Forefathers Died for Liberty in 1776: Buy Liberty Bonds

Paul A. Ickes · 1917 · Colour lithograph · 104 × 69 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Ohio Lithograph Company of Toledo printed this Liberty Bond poster in 1917. Paul Ickes was about twenty-two. The first Liberty Loan was authorised on 24 April 1917 at 3.5 percent interest, and the second followed on 1 October 1917 at 4 percent; for many Americans the bonds were their first financial securities.
