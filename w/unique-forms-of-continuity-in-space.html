Unique Forms of Continuity in Space

Umberto Boccioni · 1913, cast 1950 · Bronze · 47 3/4 × 35 × 15 3/4 in. (121.3 × 88.9 × 40 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This bronze stands 121.3 centimetres high and weighs 90.7 kilograms. It was cast in 1950, thirty-four years after Boccioni's death. He had volunteered with a cyclists' battalion when Italy entered the First World War, was drafted into an artillery regiment in May 1916, and in August was thrown from his horse during cavalry training near Verona. He died the next day, aged thirty-three.
