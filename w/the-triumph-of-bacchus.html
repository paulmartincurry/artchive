The Triumph of Bacchus (Los Borrachos)

Diego Velázquez · 1628–1629 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Velázquez painted this canvas for Philip IV, who paid him 100 ducats for it. He made it after arriving in Madrid from Seville and just before his voyage to Italy. Apart from Bacchus and the half-naked figure behind him, the drinkers wear the everyday clothes of poor people in seventeenth-century Spain.
