The Flammarion Engraving

Artist unknown · 1888 · Wood engraving

Free to use

No artist is credited. The engraving first appears in a book on meteorology that the French astronomer Camille Flammarion published in 1888, facing a passage of his text that it matches closely. Flammarion had been apprenticed to an engraver in Paris at the age of twelve and may have made it himself. Scholars of the twentieth century dated it anywhere from 1520 to the seventeenth century, a mistake made possible by cropping off its decorative border. It was cut with a burin, a tool used on wood only since the late eighteenth century.
