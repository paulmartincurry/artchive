The Salon of 1852

Gustave Le Gray · 1852 · Salted paper print from waxed-paper negative · Image: 24.1 × 38 cm (9 1/2 × 14 15/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Le Gray had trained as a painter under François-Édouard Picot and Paul Delaroche, and he showed his own paintings at the Salons of 1848 and 1853, the year after this photograph. His treatise on photography, first published in 1850, was in its third edition by 1852. Among the people he taught were Nadar, Charles Nègre and Maxime Du Camp. The print is about 24 by 38 centimetres.
