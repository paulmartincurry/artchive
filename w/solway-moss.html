Solway Moss (Liber Studiorum, no. 52)

J. M. W. Turner · published 1816 · Etching and mezzotint · Sheet: 21 × 29.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

For the Liber Studiorum Turner made the etchings himself, and collaborating engravers worked the plates in mezzotint; this one is credited to Turner and Thomas Goff Lupton. The models the printmakers followed were mainly in sepia watercolour. The original plates were part of the Turner Bequest and are now kept at Tate Britain.
