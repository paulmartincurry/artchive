The Storm on the Sea of Galilee

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1633 · Oil on canvas · Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, Boston (stolen in 1990)

Free to use

This is Rembrandt's only known seascape, painted in 1633. One of the men in the boat, the one looking straight out, is a self-portrait. Its earliest known owner was a Dutch merchant who acquired it in 1644, and in 1898 Bernard Berenson negotiated its purchase for Isabella Stewart Gardner for $6,000. On 18 March 1990 two thieves posing as police officers stole it from her museum in Boston, and its whereabouts remain unknown. The museum offers a $10 million reward, and empty frames hang in the room where the stolen works were.
