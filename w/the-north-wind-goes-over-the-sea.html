The North Wind Goes over the Sea (East of the Sun and West of the Moon)

Kay Nielsen · published 1914 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Alexander Turnbull Library, Wellington

Free to use

Kay Nielsen made 25 colour plates and more than 21 monotone images for this 1914 book, and they were printed by a four-colour process where most of his contemporaries used three. In the tale, the North Wind says he once wore himself out blowing an aspen leaf to the castle, but agrees to carry the girl there. This copy belongs to the Alexander Turnbull Library in Wellington.
