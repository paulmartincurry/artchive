No more to build on there. And they, since they
Were not the one dead, turned to their affairs.

“Out, Out—”

Robert Frost · 1916

Frost wrote the poem in memory of a neighbour's son whom he had befriended while living in Franconia, New Hampshire. The boy had died on March 24, 1910, after an accident similar to the one in the poem. It was first published in the July 1916 issue of McClure's.
