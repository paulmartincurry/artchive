Yours is the Earth and everything that’s in it,
And—which is more—you’ll be a Man, my son!

If—

Rudyard Kipling · 1910

Kipling was forty-four in 1910. Three years earlier he had been awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature, the first writer in English to receive it and, at forty-one, the youngest. He was sounded out for the Poet Laureateship and several times for a knighthood, and declined both. He had begun as a newspaperman in British India, working for papers in Lahore and Allahabad from 1883 to 1889.
