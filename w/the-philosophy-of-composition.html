Most writers—poets in especial—prefer having it understood that they compose by a species of fine phrenzy—an ecstatic intuition

The Philosophy of Composition

Edgar Allan Poe · 1846

George Rex Graham, a friend and former employer of Poe, had declined to be the first to print The Raven and offered fifteen dollars as charity instead. He then published this essay, in which Poe sets out how he says he wrote that poem, in the April 1846 issue of Graham's Magazine. It is uncertain whether Poe really followed the method he describes.
