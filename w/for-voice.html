For Voice (Dlia golosa)

El Lissitzky · 1923 · Book with twenty-four letterpress prints in red and black and one halftone reproductive print in black on ivory wove paper · 19 × 13.3 × 0.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Lissitzky designed this collection of Vladimir Mayakovsky's poems in Berlin in 1923, called himself its constructor rather than its designer, and gave it a thumb index. The book was praised for its typography in the year it came out, and Lissitzky said the work won him membership of the Gutenberg Society. The same year he published his essay Topography of Typography.
