Little Nemo in Slumberland: I Don't Like This, One Little Tiny Bit, Not One Tiny Weenie Bit

Winsor McCay · 1906 · Pen and black ink and brush and black ink over graphite on two joined sheets of paperboard · 70.5 × 54.6 cm (image) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is McCay's original drawing for the Little Nemo page dated 14 October 1906, made in pen and brush over graphite on two joined sheets of paperboard about 70 centimetres tall. It stayed in McCay's family, passing to his grandson Ray Winsor Moniz, until the National Gallery of Art bought it in 2008.
