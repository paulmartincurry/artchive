Flying Horse of Gansu

Maker unknown · ca. 2nd Century CE (Eastern Han) · Bronze · Gansu Provincial Museum

In 1969 a team of local people digging air-raid shelters near Wuwei broke into a chamber under a monastery and found more than 200 bronze figures of men, horses and chariots. They carried them home in plastic bags before reporting the find. The chamber was part of a Han general's tomb that looters had entered long before without reaching the bronzes. The horse stands 34.5 centimetres high and 45 long, balanced on one hoof on a bird that was exhibited as a swallow, though scholars pointed out it is not one.
