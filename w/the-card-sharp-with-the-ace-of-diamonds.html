The Card Sharp with the Ace of Diamonds

Georges de La Tour · c. 1636–1638 · Oil on canvas · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Nobody knows who commissioned the picture. Georges de La Tour signed it under the card sharp's elbow, in the shadow of the tablecloth. He painted the composition twice; the other version, with the ace of clubs, is in the Kimbell Art Museum in Fort Worth. The Louvre bought this one in 1972.
