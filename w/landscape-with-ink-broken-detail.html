Landscape with Ink Broken (Haboku Sansui), Detail

Sesshū Tōyō · 1495 · Ink on paper · 148.6 × 32.7 cm (whole scroll) · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

At the top of the scroll Sesshū wrote a dedication dated to the third month of 1495, giving his age as seventy-six. A pupil, Josui Sōen, was going home to Sagami and had asked for the painting as a certificate that he had properly inherited his master's method. On the way Sōen stopped in Kyoto and had six monks add poems. The painting takes up only about a third of the scroll.
