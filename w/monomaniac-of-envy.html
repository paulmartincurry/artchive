Monomaniac of Envy

Théodore Géricault · c. 1819–22 · Oil on canvas · 72.1 × 58.5 cm · Musée des Beaux-Arts de Lyon

Free to use

Géricault painted ten portraits of the mentally ill, and this is one of only five known to survive. They were commissioned by Étienne-Jean Georget, a doctor at La Salpêtrière, and show patients from that Paris hospital and from Bicêtre. The portraits lay largely unknown until they were found in an attic in Baden-Baden in 1863. The Lyon museum bought this one in 1908.
