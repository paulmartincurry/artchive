It was the whiteness of the whale that above all things appalled me.

Moby-Dick — The Whiteness of the Whale (Ch. 42)

Herman Melville · 1851

The white whale was modelled on Mocha Dick, an albino sperm whale reported off the Chilean island of Mocha and rumoured to carry twenty or so harpoons in his back. Melville knew the account of him printed in The Knickerbocker in May 1839. He had spent 18 months as an ordinary seaman on the whaler Acushnet in 1841 and 1842. Most of his facts about whales came from five books, chief among them Thomas Beale's Natural History of the Sperm Whale, which he bought on 10 July 1850.
