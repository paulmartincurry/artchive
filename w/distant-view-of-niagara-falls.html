Distant View of Niagara Falls

Thomas Cole · 1830 · Oil on wood panel · Art Institute of Chicago

Thomas Cole visited Niagara Falls in May 1829 and painted this panel the following year. He left out the factories, hotels and viewing points that already stood around the falls. The panel is 47.9 by 60.6 centimetres, signed and dated at lower right. By 1936 it was in London, and the Art Institute acquired it from Edith Mendelssohn-Bartholdy there in 1946.
