Sunday, March 19.—Dream that my little baby came to life again; that it had only been cold, and that we rubbed it before the fire, and it lived. Awake and find no baby. I think about the little thing all day.

Journal (19 March 1815)

Mary Shelley · March 19, 1815

On 22 February 1815 Mary Godwin gave birth to a girl two months premature, who was not expected to survive. The loss of the child brought on acute depression, and she was haunted by visions of the baby; she conceived again and had recovered by the summer. Her journal from May 1815 to July 1816 is lost.
