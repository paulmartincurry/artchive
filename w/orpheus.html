Orpheus

Odilon Redon · c. 1903–10 · pastel on brown paper · Sheet: 68.8 × 56.8 cm (27 1/16 × 22 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Henri Matisse bought this pastel for his father, Émile. It later belonged to Wilhelm Uhde in Paris, and in 1915 John Quinn of New York bought it. The Cleveland museum acquired it from Quinn's estate, with funds from J. H. Wade. Redon drew Orpheus several times in different media; another version of this pastel, now in Montpellier, hung in the Armory Show of 1913.
