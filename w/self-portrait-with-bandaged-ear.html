Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear

Vincent van Gogh · January 1889 · Oil on canvas · 60.5 × 50 cm · The Courtauld, London

Free to use

Van Gogh painted this in January 1889, a week after leaving hospital. He had cut off most of his left ear; he worked from a mirror, so the bandage shows on the right. The fur cap was a recent purchase, bought to hold the bandage in place and keep out the cold. The print on the wall behind him is Geishas in a Landscape by Satō Torakiyo. Samuel Courtauld bought the painting in 1928.
