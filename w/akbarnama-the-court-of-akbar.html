Akbarnama: The Court of Akbar

Basawan and others · ca. 1590–1595 · Gouache and gold on paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Akbarnama is the official chronicle of Akbar's reign, commissioned by the emperor himself and written largely by his court historian Abul Fazl, who says in the book that it took seven years to complete. The paintings in the original manuscript are thought to have been made between about 1592 and 1594 by at least forty-nine artists of the imperial workshop, Basawan among them.
