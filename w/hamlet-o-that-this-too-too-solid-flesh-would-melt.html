O that this too too solid flesh would melt,
Thaw, and resolve itself into a dew!
Or that the Everlasting had not fix’d
His canon ’gainst self-slaughter. O God! O God!

Hamlet — O That This Too Too Solid Flesh Would Melt (Act I, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · c. 1599–1601

Shakespeare wrote Hamlet sometime between 1599 and 1601, almost certainly with Richard Burbage, the chief tragedian of the Lord Chamberlain's Men, in mind for the title role. Three early versions survive, the First Quarto of 1603, the Second Quarto of 1604 and the First Folio of 1623, and each includes lines and passages missing from the others. It is his longest play.
