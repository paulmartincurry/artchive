Worship of Shiva and Devi

Maker unknown · early 1700s · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Image: 26.7 x 20.3 cm (10 1/2 x 8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In the shrine Shiva appears as a yogi, with matted locks, a chain of skulls, cobras for jewellery and a body white with the ashes of the cremated dead; his wife is the red goddess beside him. The scene with a heroine above is still unidentified.
