Income and Expenditure of 150 Negro Families in Atlanta, Ga., U.S.A. (The Georgia Negro)

W. E. B. Du Bois · c. 1900 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

Congress allocated $15,000 for the exhibit only four months before the Paris Exposition opened. The lawyer Thomas J. Calloway, its main organizer, brought in Du Bois, his former classmate at Fisk. Du Bois was then a professor at Atlanta University, and this chart covers 150 families in Atlanta.
