We Are Such Stuff as Dreams Are Made On (The Tempest)

Edmund Dulac · published 1908 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Dulac painted 40 colour illustrations for this 1908 Tempest, when he was about twenty-six. He had come to London four years earlier after studying law in Toulouse and spending a very short time at the Académie Julian in Paris. Shakespeare probably wrote the play in 1610–1611, and it is thought to be one of the last he wrote alone.
