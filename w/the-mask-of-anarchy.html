‘Rise like Lions after slumber
In unvanquishable number—
Shake your chains to earth like dew
Which in sleep had fallen on you—
Ye are many—they are few.’

The Mask of Anarchy (closing stanzas)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1819; published 1832

Shelley was living in Italy when he wrote these 372 lines in 1819, after the Peterloo Massacre of that year. He sent the manuscript to Leigh Hunt for The Examiner, and Hunt held it back. It was first printed in 1832, ten years after Shelley's death, by Edward Moxon in London, with a preface by Hunt. Shelley's manuscript spells the title Mask; the first edition spells it Masque. Students recited the poem in Tiananmen Square in 1989.
