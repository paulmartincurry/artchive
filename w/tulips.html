Tulips (The Temple of Flora)

Robert John Thornton · published 1798 · Colour aquatint, stipple and line engraving · Missouri Botanical Garden, Peter H. Raven Library

Free to use

These tulips were copied from the collection of the florist Mason. Two of them, newly raised by Davey and Mason, were named by Thornton after patrons of the book, the Duchess of Devonshire and Earl Spencer, and his text records that the tulip called Louis then sold in England for forty guineas. He set the group in Holland, where tulips were most widely grown.
