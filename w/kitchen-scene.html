Kitchen Scene

Diego Velázquez · 1618–20 · Oil on canvas · 55.9 × 104.2 cm (21 7/8 × 41 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The type is a bodegón, from the Spanish for pantry. Velázquez painted a young African woman at work, and may have used an enslaved woman from his or an associate's household as a model; slavery was widespread in Seville, and Velázquez, his father and his teacher were all part of it.
