Terracotta Eye-Cup Kylix

Greek, Attic · ca. 520 BCE · Terracotta · H. 3 3/4 in. (9.5 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The bowl of this drinking cup is nearly 29 centimetres across, and more than 36 with its handles, but it stands only 9.5 centimetres high. It was made in Attica in the Archaic period. The museum bought it in 1956.
