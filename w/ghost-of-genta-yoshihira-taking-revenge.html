Ghost of Genta Yoshihira Taking Revenge (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · December 1889 · Colour woodcut · 35.7 × 25.3 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Genta Yoshihira, as the story goes, turned into a thunder god just before Nanba no Jirō beheaded him, and then killed Nanba with a bolt of lightning. Sasaki Toyokichi published the sheet in December 1889, the year New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts began. The Rijksmuseum's impression was a gift of H. J. Herwig and A. H. Herwig-Kempers.
