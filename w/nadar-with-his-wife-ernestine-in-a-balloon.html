Nadar with His Wife, Ernestine, in a Balloon

Nadar · ca. 1865, printed 1890s · Gelatin silver print from glass negative · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

For publicity, Nadar recreated balloon flights inside his studio with his wife, Ernestine, using a rigged-up gondola. He gave up ballooning after the two of them were injured in an accident in his balloon Le Géant. This print is 9 by 7.8 centimetres and was made in the 1890s from a glass negative of about 1865.
