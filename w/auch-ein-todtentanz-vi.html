Auch ein Todtentanz VI

Alfred Rethel · 1849 · wood engraving · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is the last of the six woodcuts in Auch ein Todtentanz, first published with the title Auch ein Todtentanz aus dem Jahre 1848: Death rides over the fallen wearing a victor's laurel wreath. Rethel began work on the series in the winter of 1848, taking up the tradition of Hans Holbein's dance of death woodcuts. Each image was printed with verses by Robert Reinick, and the series drew wide comment in the newspapers.
