Dresden Codex

Maker unknown · ca. 12th century · Amate (fig-bark paper) screenfold · Saxon State Library, Dresden

Free to use

Eight scribes wrote this book, each in a different hand, using thin brushes and vegetable dyes on paper made from the pounded inner bark of a wild fig. Unfolded it is 12 feet long. The director of the Royal Library at Dresden bought it from a private owner in Vienna in 1739. In February 1945 it sat in a flooded basement during the bombing of Dresden, and some sheets went back into their glass case in the wrong order after drying.
