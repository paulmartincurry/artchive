Coyolxauhqui Stone

Maker unknown · ca. 1473 · Stone, carved in relief · Templo Mayor Museum, Mexico City

Free to use with credit. Photo: Dennis G. Jarvis, CC BY-SA 2.0

Workers for the Mexico City electric-power company struck this stone while digging on 21 February 1978. The disc is carved in high relief from a single stone more than three metres across, and it lay at the foot of the stairway of the main temple of Tenochtitlan. Finding it led to the excavation of the Templo Mayor under Eduardo Matos Moctezuma. The body was once painted yellow against a red ground, with blue on the headdress.
