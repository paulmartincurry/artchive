Self-Portrait as the Allegory of Painting (La Pittura)

Artemisia Gentileschi · 1638–1639 · Oil on canvas · Royal Collection

Free to use

Gentileschi probably painted this during her stay in England between 1638 and 1639, when she was about forty-five, and Charles I acquired it. When the royal collection was dispersed it went to John Jackson, a lawyer for Parliament's creditors, and it was recovered for Charles II in 1660. Her initials are below the palette. A cleaning in 1972 took off later overpainting. Not every art historian accepts that the figure is the painter herself.
