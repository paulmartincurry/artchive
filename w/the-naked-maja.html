The Naked Maja (La maja desnuda)

Francisco de Goya · c. 1797–1800 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya painted it around 1797 to 1800, probably for Manuel Godoy, who kept it in a separate cabinet for nude paintings; a clothed twin, La maja vestida, hangs beside it. In 1808 investigators for the Spanish Inquisition found it among Godoy's questionable pictures, and Godoy and his curator were made to name the artists. It has been in the Prado since 1901.
