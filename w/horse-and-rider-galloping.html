Horse and Rider Galloping

Eadweard Muybridge · 1883–86, printed 1887 · Collotype · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Eadweard Muybridge made the photographs for Animal Locomotion in 1884 and 1885 at the University of Pennsylvania, which commissioned the study and contributed $5,000. The thoroughbred horses were photographed at the Gentlemen's Driving Park in Philadelphia. The series was published on 9 July 1887 as 781 collotype plates holding more than 20,000 images in all, and subscribers could choose 100 plates for $100.
