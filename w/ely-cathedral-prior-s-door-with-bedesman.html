Ely Cathedral: Prior's Door, with Bedesman

Frederick H. Evans · 1891 · Lantern slide · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Frederick H. Evans was still a bookseller when he made this lantern slide; he left the trade to photograph full time in 1898. The slide is 8.2 centimetres square. His ideal was a photograph that was not retouched or modified in any way. The cost of platinum paper kept rising after he took it up, and by 1915 he had given up photography altogether.
