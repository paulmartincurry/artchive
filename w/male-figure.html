Male Figure (Moai Tangata)

Maker unknown · Early 19th century · Wood, obsidian, bone · 40.6 × 9.5 × 5.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This Rapa Nui figure was carved in the early nineteenth century in wood, with obsidian and bone, and is 40.6 centimetres tall. Faith-dorian and Martin Wright gave it to the Met in 1984 in honour of Livio Scamperle.
