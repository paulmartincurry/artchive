Bowl with Peony Sprays on Diaper Ground

Maker unknown · 1736–95 · porcelain with famille rose overglaze enamel decoration · Diameter: 15.5 cm (6 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Plain porcelain from the imperial kilns at Jingdezhen may have been carried to the palace workshops in Beijing, where court painters added the enamels. The Qianlong reign mark is written on the base in blue enamel.
