God is always ready, but we are very unready; God is near to us, but we are far from Him; God is within, but we are without; God is at home, but we are strangers.

God Is Always Ready (from Light, Life and Love)

Meister Eckhart · c. 1300–1327

Translated by W. R. Inge

Eckhart was likely born around 1260 in the village of Tambach, near Gotha, in Thuringia. Probably around 1278, when he was about eighteen, he joined the Dominican convent at Erfurt.
