Lewis Chessmen (King and Queen)

Maker unknown · c. 1150–1200 · Walrus ivory · National Museum of Scotland, Edinburgh

Free to use with credit. Photo: National Museums Scotland, CC BY-SA 4.0

These two are among the eleven pieces in Edinburgh; the British Museum has 82, for which it paid 80 guineas. The hoard of 94 objects was found on the Isle of Lewis in 1831 and first shown in Edinburgh on 11 April that year by Roderick Ririe of Stornoway, who sold ten pieces to a Scottish collector. Most are carved from walrus ivory. They were probably made between 1150 and 1200, and are commonly believed to come from Trondheim in Norway.
