They fought south of the Castle,
They died north of the wall.
They died in the moors and were not buried.
Their flesh was the food of crows.

Fighting South of the Castle

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · c. 124 BC

Translated by Arthur Waley

This anonymous ballad has been dated to about 124 BC. Ballads of this kind are called yuefu, after the Music Bureau, a Han government office that from 112 BC collected, wrote and performed folk songs. In the poem the harvest has not been gathered, which meant no corn-offerings could be made to the spirits of the dead.
