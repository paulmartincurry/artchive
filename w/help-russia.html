Käthe Kollwitz · 1921 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kollwitz made this lithograph in 1921, and it was printed in an edition of 300. This impression is from the third and last state. In the two years that followed she produced her cycle War in woodcut. Joseph M. Erdelac gave the print to the Cleveland Museum of Art.
