Aftermath

Siegfried Sassoon · 1920

From 1919 to 1925 Sassoon lived at 54 Tufton Street, Westminster; the house is gone and a plaque marks the place. In November 1918 he had travelled to Blackburn to support the Labour candidate Philip Snowden, who had been a pacifist during the war.
