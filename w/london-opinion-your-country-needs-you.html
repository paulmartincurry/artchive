London Opinion: "Your Country Needs You"

Alfred Leete · 1914 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

Alfred Leete drew Kitchener as the cover of London Opinion for 5 September 1914. The magazine cost a penny and had a circulation of around 300,000. Readers asked for reproductions, and the magazine sold postcard-sized copies at 100 for 1s 4d before the design was reused on posters. A 2013 study found no evidence that the poster was as popular or influential in the war as was later stated.
