The Fall of Satan (The Book of Job, Plate 16)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · Sheet: 42.9 × 33.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The engravings were finished in 1825 and an edition of 315 was printed in 1826. Only 20 copies were sold in Blake's lifetime, mostly to people in his own circle. The Royal Academy and the King's Library each bought one, and the Academy also awarded him £25. This is plate 16.
