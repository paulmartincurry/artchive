Where, before me, are the ages that have gone?
And where, behind me, are the coming generations?
I think of heaven and earth, without limit, without end,
And I am all alone and my tears fall down.

On a Gate-Tower at Yu-chou (Deng Youzhou Tai Ge)

Chen Zi'ang · before 702; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Chen Zi'ang came from a rich family in Shehong County, Sichuan, and did not go to the capital for his examinations until his twenties. He advised the Empress Wu Zetian and argued that poetry should reflect real life. He died in 702, after years in and out of prison.
