The falling facade was a genuine two-ton building front, with no trickery, and a nail in the ground marked where Buster Keaton had to stand for the open attic window to fit around him. It has been claimed that standing just inches off the spot could have killed him. The cyclone scene, with breakaway street sets and six Liberty-motor wind machines, cost a third of the film's budget.
