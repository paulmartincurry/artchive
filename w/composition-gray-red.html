Composition (No. 1) Gray-Red

Piet Mondrian · 1935 · Oil on canvas · 57.5 × 55.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Mondrian signed and dated this canvas PM 35, and it was shown in Hartford the same year. In January 1936 he sold it himself to Elizabeth Goodspeed of Chicago, who gave it to the Art Institute in 1949. It is 57.5 centimetres high. Five years after painting it he left Europe for New York.
