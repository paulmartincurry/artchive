The Great Comet of 1881 (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot left France with his family after Louis-Napoleon Bonaparte's coup of 1851 and settled in Medford, outside Boston, where he made his living as an artist and astronomer. He observed this comet in the early hours of 26 June 1881. By 1882 he had gone back to France to work at the Meudon Observatory, where he fell into a bitter rivalry with its director, Jules Janssen.
