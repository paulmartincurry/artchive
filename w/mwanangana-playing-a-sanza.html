Mwanangana (Lord of the Land) Playing a Sanza

Maker unknown · 19th century (before 1869) · Wood (uapaca), cloth, plant fiber, beads, dye, resinous coating · 42.5 × 12.7 × 14 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This figure was carved from uapaca wood and dressed with cloth, plant fibre and beads, and it was made before 1869. Among the Chokwe, pieces made for chiefs often show the chiefs themselves and royal ancestors, and cult objects come from professional woodcarvers called songi, whom not every village has. The figure is 42.5 centimetres tall.
