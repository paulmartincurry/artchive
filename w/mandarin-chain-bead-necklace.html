Mandarin Chain Bead Necklace

Maker unknown · 1800s · Tourmaline, jade, pearls, seed pearls, seed coral and gilt metal · 120.6 cm (47 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Court necklaces were worn by high-ranking women and men, with one string down the back and two at the front. The gilt fittings were decorated with blue kingfisher feathers.
