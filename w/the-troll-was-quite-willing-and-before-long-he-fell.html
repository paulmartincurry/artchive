The Troll Was Quite Willing, and Before Long He Fell Asleep and Began Snoring (East of the Sun and West of the Moon)

Kay Nielsen · published 1914 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Alexander Turnbull Library, Wellington

Free to use

While painting landscapes around Dover in these years, Nielsen met the Society of Tempera Painters and learned methods that cut the time each painting took. He left for New York in 1917 for an exhibition of his work, then went home to Denmark to paint stage scenery for the Royal Danish Theatre.
