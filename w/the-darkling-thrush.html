That I could think there trembled through
His happy good-night air
Some blessed Hope, whereof he knew
And I was unaware.

The Darkling Thrush

Thomas Hardy · 1900

The poem was first called By the Century's Deathbed. It was printed in The Graphic on 29 December 1900 and in The Times on 1 January 1901. A deleted 1899 on the manuscript suggests it may have been written that year. Hardy was sixty when it appeared, and his first collection of poems had come out only in 1898.
