it's easier to find
rare, unparalleled treasure
than it is to have and hold
one reliable lover

Sent to a Neighbor Woman Friend

Yu Xuanji · ca. 860s

Translated by David Young and Jiann I. Lin · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Forty-nine poems by Yu Xuanji survive. The collection published in her lifetime, Fragments of a Northern Dreamland, has been lost. At sixteen she became a concubine or lesser wife of an official named Li Yi, and they separated three years later because of his primary wife's dislike of her. She later took vows as a Daoist nun and travelled often. The one account of her death, a semi-legendary one, says she was executed at twenty-eight.
