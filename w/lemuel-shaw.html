Lemuel Shaw

Southworth and Hawes · c. 1850 · Daguerreotype · 21.6 × 16.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Southworth & Hawes was a photographic firm in Boston from 1843 to 1863, and it worked almost exclusively in the daguerreotype process. Lemuel Shaw wrote over 2,150 legal opinions as Chief Justice of Massachusetts. In 1847 he became the father-in-law of Herman Melville.
