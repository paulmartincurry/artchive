Hardware Store, 316 Bowery

Berenice Abbott · 26 January 1938 · Gelatin silver print · 35.6 × 43.2 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott photographed the shop on 26 January 1938. This large print, 14 by 17 inches, is marked as the master print and was shown in an exhibition of New York City photographs. The book Changing New York came out in 1939, with captions by the critic Elizabeth McCausland, who lived with Abbott.
