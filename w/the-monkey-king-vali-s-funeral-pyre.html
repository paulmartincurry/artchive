The Monkey King Vali's Funeral Pyre (Ramayana)

Nainsukh · c. 1780 · Ink, opaque watercolor, silver, and gold on paper · 24.8 × 34 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met dates this page to about 1780 and gives it to Nainsukh, who spent his last years at Basohli and died in 1778. By then a family workshop was carrying on his style, and he was probably no longer making the works himself but leaving them to his children and nephew; such pages are often ascribed to the Family of Nainsukh. In the Ramayana, Rama killed Vali with an arrow to the chest. The Met bought the page in 2004.
