The woman I love says that there is no one whom she would rather marry than me, not if Jupiter himself were to woo her. Says;—but what a woman says to her ardent lover should be written in wind and running water.

Carmen 70 (Written in Wind and Running Water)

Catullus · c. 58 BCE

Translated by F. W. Cornish

Catullus wrote his roughly 113 poems between 62 and 54 BC. In 966 Rather, the bishop of Verona, the poet's home town, found a manuscript of them and reproached himself for spending day and night with it. Nothing more is heard of any manuscript until about 1300.
