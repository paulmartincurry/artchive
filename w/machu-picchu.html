Machu Picchu

Maker unknown · ca. 1450–1470

Free to use with credit. Photo: Draceane, CC BY-SA 4.0

The Inca emperor Pachacuti is believed to have ordered it built as a royal estate, though no written record from the time confirms it. About 750 people lived there, most of them support staff, and much of their food was brought in from the valleys around. Landslides struck during construction, and the places where terraces shifted and were stabilized can still be seen. The topsoil on the terraces was probably carried up from the valley floor. It was used for 80 years and then abandoned.
