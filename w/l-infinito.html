So my mind sinks in this immensity:
and foundering is sweet in such a sea.

L'Infinito

Giacomo Leopardi · 1819

Translated by Jonathan Galassi, 2010 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Leopardi was twenty-one and still living in his father's house in Recanati, a hill town in the Papal States, when he wrote this, probably in the autumn of 1819. He had taught himself there, in his father's library, studying constantly from the age of twelve to nineteen, and his health had suffered for it. The year before, he had tried to escape from the town; his father caught him and brought him home, and the family watched him closely from then on. He died at thirty-eight.
