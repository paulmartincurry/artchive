The Waterfall Where Yoshitsune Washed His Horse at Yoshino in Yamato Province (A Tour of Waterfalls in Various Provinces)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1832 · Woodblock print; ink and color; vertical ōban · 37.1 × 26 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The waterfalls Hokusai chose for the series lie in the central, western and eastern parts of Honshu, in regions that travellers of his time knew as sites of pilgrimage. Hokusai was a follower of Nichiren Buddhism. He was in his seventies when the eight prints were completed.
