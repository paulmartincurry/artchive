It is not, truly speaking, the labor that is divided; but the men:--Divided into mere segments of men--broken into small fragments and crumbs of life;

The Stones of Venice: The Division of Labour

John Ruskin · 1853

Ruskin made two research trips to Venice for the book with his wife, Effie Gray, the first in the winter of 1849–50, when they stayed at the Hotel Danieli. He had his own camera by 1849 for taking daguerreotypes, though his valet appears to have taken them. The marriage was annulled in 1854, six years after the wedding.
