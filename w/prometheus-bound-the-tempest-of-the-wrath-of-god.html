O holy Earth, O mother mine!
O Sky, that biddest speed along
Thy vault the common Light divine,—
Be witness of my wrong!

Prometheus Bound — The Tempest of the Wrath of God (closing speech)

Aeschylus · 5th century BCE

Translated by E. D. A. Morshead

This is the last speech of the play. Most students of the subject think it was followed by a Prometheus Unbound, which survives only in fragments, so the author's plan for the whole is not known. Shelley used some of the material for a Prometheus Unbound of his own.
