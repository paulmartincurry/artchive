The Sphinx

Odilon Redon · 1883 · Various charcoals, with touches of black chalk, stumping, wiping, and erasing, on pale-pink wove paper with red and blue fibers altered to a golden tone · Art Institute of Chicago

The paper was pale pink when Redon drew on it and has since turned a golden tone. He was forty-two or forty-three, and worked almost only in charcoal and lithography, in black pictures he called his noirs. A year later a novel by Joris-Karl Huysmans featured an aristocrat who collected his drawings; until then he had been relatively unknown. The Art Institute bought the sheet in 1950 from Henri Petiet of Paris.
