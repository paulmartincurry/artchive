Federal Theatre Presents Faustus

Maker unknown · 1936–41 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Death beats a drum over an hourglass in this poster for Marlowe's Faustus at Maxine Elliott's Theatre, 109 West 39th Street. The Federal Art Project printed it in New York.
