Adam

Auguste Rodin · modeled 1880 or 1881, cast 1910 · Bronze · Overall: 76 3/8 × 30 3/8 × 32 1/2 in. · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Adam was modelled in 1880 or 1881, when Rodin had just taken a part-time job designing vases and table ornaments for the Sèvres porcelain factory. The bronze was not cast until 1910, the year Thomas F. Ryan gave it to the museum. It is 194 centimetres tall and weighs 377 kilograms.
