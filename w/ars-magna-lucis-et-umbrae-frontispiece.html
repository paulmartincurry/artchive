Ars Magna Lucis et Umbrae: Frontispiece

Athanasius Kircher · 1646 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Athanasius Kircher's book on light and shadow was printed in Rome in 1646 and runs to more than 900 folio pages, with engraved plates, some of them folded. Kircher, a German Jesuit, was in his mid-forties and taught at the Collegio Romano. The magic lantern has sometimes been credited to him by mistake.
