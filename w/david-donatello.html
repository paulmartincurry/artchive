David (bronze, detail of the head)

Donatello · ca. 1440 · Bronze · Museo Nazionale del Bargello, Florence

Nothing documents the making of this bronze, and proposed dates run from the 1420s to the 1460s. Most scholars assume Cosimo de' Medici commissioned it. It is first recorded in the Medici Palace in 1469, and when the family was exiled in 1494 it was moved to the courtyard of the Palazzo della Signoria. The figure is 158 centimetres tall and partly gilded. It was restored for the first time between 2007 and 2008, with scalpels on the bare bronze and lasers on the gilding.
