Hall stand

Coalbrookdale Company · ca. 1875 · Cast iron, marble, glass, brass · H × W × D: 220 × 136 × 47 cm (86 5/8 × 53 9/16 × 18 1/2in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The Coalbrookdale Company in Shropshire cast the frame of this hall stand in iron. It stands 220 centimetres tall and 136 wide, with an oval mirror and rows of hooks above a marble-topped base with a drawer and removable trays. The museum bought it with money from its Walter R. Scholz Memorial and Decorative Arts Association acquisition funds.
