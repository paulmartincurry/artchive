Danaë

Gustav Klimt · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 77 × 83 cm · Private collection

Free to use

Klimt painted Danaë in 1907, the year he also finished the last of his ceiling paintings for the University of Vienna, which had been attacked as pornographic. After that row he took no more public commissions. His father had been a gold engraver. The canvas was with the Galerie Würthle in Vienna until the gallery closed in 1995; it belonged to the collector Hans Dichand, and after his death in 2010 passed to his three children.
