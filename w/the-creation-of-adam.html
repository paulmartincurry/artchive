The Creation of Adam

Michelangelo Buonarroti · ca. 1512 · Fresco · Sistine Chapel, Vatican City

Free to use

The commission was first for the Twelve Apostles, with ornament across the middle of the ceiling. Michelangelo persuaded Pope Julius II to give him a free hand and proposed a larger scheme with nine episodes from Genesis at its centre, of which this is the fourth. The whole ceiling took him about four years, from 1508 to 1512. He drew a study of Adam in red chalk in 1511, using a male model for the pose. The fresco is 68 feet above the chapel floor.
