The Hunters Return to the Castle (from the Unicorn Tapestries)

Maker unknown · 1495–1505 · Wool warp with wool, silk, silver, and gilt wefts · 368.3 × 388.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The yellow in the tapestries came from weld, the red from madder and the blue from woad. Six of them hung in John D. Rockefeller Jr.'s house until The Cloisters was built, and he gave them to the museum.
