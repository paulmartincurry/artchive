King David Receiving the Cistern Water of Bethlehem

Master of the Antwerp Adoration Group · 1515–20 · Oil on panel transferred to canvas · 73.5 × 27.5 cm (28 7/8 × 10 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago
