Mohnköpfe (Poppyheads) (Dress or Furnishing Fabric)

Koloman Moser · 1900 · Silk, wild silk, and cotton, warp-float faced 15:1 satin weave self-patterned by ground weft floats; woven on loom with Jacquard attachment; right selvage present · 182.9 × 113 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Koloman Moser designed Poppyheads three years before he co-founded the Wiener Werkstätte, and a year after he began teaching at the School of Applied Arts in Vienna. The Viennese firm of Backhausen wove it in various colours on a Jacquard loom, for upholstery or curtains. The pattern repeats every 45.5 centimetres. The firm is still in business.
