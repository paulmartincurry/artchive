City and Rural Population, 1890 (The Georgia Negro)

W. E. B. Du Bois · c. 1900 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

W. E. B. Du Bois prepared this chart for the Exhibit of American Negroes at the Paris Exposition Universelle of 1900. It belongs to The Georgia Negro, a social study of 32 handmade graphs and charts. The exhibit was on show from April to November 1900, and more than 50 million people passed through the fair.
