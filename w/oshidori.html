Lafcadio Hearn · 1904

During fifteen months as a teacher in Matsue, on the Sea of Japan, Hearn married Setsuko Koizumi, the daughter of a local samurai family, and they had four children. In January 1896 he became a Japanese citizen under the name Yakumo Koizumi, taking his wife's surname.
