The Battle of the Sea Gods (Left Portion of Frieze)

Andrea Mantegna · before 1481 · Engraving · Sheet: 28.5 × 43 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This sheet is the left portion of a frieze, engraved before 1481. Mantegna never signed or dated any of his plates, except for a single disputed instance of 1472. By the usual reckoning he and his workshop engraved about thirty plates; it is now considered that he engraved either seven of them himself or none.
