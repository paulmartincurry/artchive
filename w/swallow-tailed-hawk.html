Swallow-Tailed Hawk (The Birds of America, Plate 72)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1829 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

About 1820, when he was around thirty-five, Audubon declared that he would paint every bird in North America. The plants behind some fifty of the birds were painted by his assistant Joseph Mason, who is not credited in the book.
