Golden lads and girls all must,
As chimney-sweepers, come to dust.

Cymbeline — Fear No More the Heat o’ the Sun (Act IV, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · c. 1611

The precise date of composition is unknown, but the play was certainly produced as early as 1611. After the performance that year mentioned by Simon Forman there is no record of a production until 1634, when it was revived at court for Charles I and Henrietta Maria.
