Blessed Ludovica Albertoni

Gian Lorenzo Bernini · 1671–74 · Marble · Over life-size · San Francesco a Ripa, Rome

Free to use with credit. Photo: Labicanense, CC BY 4.0

Bernini was seventy-one when he began this monument, and he took on the project without pay. A cardinal descended from Ludovica Albertoni commissioned it for the chapel where she had been buried in 1533. A document shows Bernini buying the white marble on 7 February 1674, and the sculpture was installed by 31 August of that year. He cut an archway into an existing wall to frame it, and windows hidden at either side light the figure.
