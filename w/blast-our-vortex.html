We are proud, handsome and predatory.

We hunt machines, they are our favourite game.

We invent them and then hunt them down.

BLAST: Our Vortex

Wyndham Lewis · 1914

Lewis was thirty-one when BLAST appeared. He had left Roger Fry's Omega Workshops after a quarrel over a commission for wall decorations at the Daily Mail Ideal Home Exhibition, which he believed Fry had misappropriated. With other Omega artists he set up the Rebel Art Centre, which ran for only four months but gave rise to the Vorticist group and its magazine. The name Vorticism came from his friend Ezra Pound.
