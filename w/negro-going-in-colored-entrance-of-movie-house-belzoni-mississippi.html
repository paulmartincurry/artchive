Negro Going in Colored Entrance of Movie House, Belzoni, Mississippi

Marion Post Wolcott · c. October 1939 · Nitrate negative, 35 mm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Wolcott was hired by Roy Stryker, head of the photography division of the Farm Security Administration, after Ralph Steiner showed him her portfolio and Paul Strand wrote a letter of recommendation. The caption card dates this frame to October 1939, with a question mark. The negative came to the Library of Congress from the Office of War Information in 1944.
