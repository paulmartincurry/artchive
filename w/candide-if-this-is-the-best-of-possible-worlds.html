Candide — If this is the best of possible worlds (Ch. VI)

Voltaire (François-Marie Arouet) · 1759

Voltaire is believed to have written part of the book in the summer of 1758. It was published in 1759. He did not openly admit to having written it until 1768, and until then signed it with a pseudonym, Doctor Ralph.
