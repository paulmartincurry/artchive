The Ball

Félix Vallotton · 1899 · Oil on card glued on wood · 48 × 61 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Vallotton painted this in oil on card, which is glued to wood, in 1899. That was the year he married Gabrielle Rodrigues-Hénriques, the widowed daughter of the art dealer Alexandre Bernheim, who brought three children from her first marriage into his household. The painting came to the French national collection in 1953 by the bequest of Carle Dreyfus.
