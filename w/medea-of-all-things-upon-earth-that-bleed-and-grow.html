Of all things upon earth that bleed and grow,
A herb most bruised is woman.

Medea — Of All Things upon Earth That Bleed and Grow

Euripides · 431 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray

Medea was first staged at the City Dionysia in 431 BC with three other plays, Philoctetes, Dictys and the satyr play Theristai, none of which survives. Euripides came third and last; Euphorion, a son of Aeschylus, won, and Sophocles came second. In the 20th century it became the most frequently performed Greek tragedy.
