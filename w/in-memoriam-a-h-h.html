’Tis better to have loved and lost
Than never to have loved at all.

In Memoriam A.H.H. (section XXVII)

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1850

Arthur Hallam died in Vienna in 1833, aged twenty-two. Tennyson worked on this elegy for him for seventeen years and published it in 1850 without his name on it: 2,916 lines in 133 cantos, of which this is the twenty-seventh. He added one more canto in 1851 and another in 1871. In the year the poem came out he married and was made Poet Laureate. Queen Victoria turned to it after her husband's death.
