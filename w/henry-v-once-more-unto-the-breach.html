Once more unto the breach, dear friends, once more,
Or close the wall up with our English dead.

Henry V — Once more unto the breach (Act III, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1599

The play is believed to have been written around 1599. It was entered in the Register of the Stationers Company on 14 August 1600, and the first quarto was published before the end of that year.
