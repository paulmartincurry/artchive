Studies in Design, Plate XII

Christopher Dresser · published 1876 · Smithsonian Libraries, Washington

Free to use

Studies in Design was published in London by Cassell, Petter and Galpin in 1876, with each plate followed by a guard sheet of printed description. A. Goater of Nottingham lithographed the plates from Dresser's designs. Dresser, born in Glasgow, had started at the Government School of Design in London at the age of thirteen.
