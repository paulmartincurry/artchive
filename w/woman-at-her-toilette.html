Woman at Her Toilette

Berthe Morisot · 1875–80 · Oil on canvas · 60.3 × 80.4 cm (23 3/4 × 31 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The signature runs along the bottom edge of the mirror. Morisot showed the painting at the fifth Impressionist exhibition in 1880. William Merritt Chase of New York owned it until his sale in 1896, and Mary Cassatt had it that same year. The Art Institute bought it from Paul Rosenberg in 1924.
