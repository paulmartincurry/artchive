The Censer

Martin Schongauer · 1470–91 · Engraving · Sheet: 10 11/16 × 8 1/8 in. (27.1 × 20.7 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Schongauer signed all 116 of his surviving engravings with his monogram and dated none of them. His contemporary Israhel van Meckenem made close copies of 58 of them, exactly half. Schongauer died in Breisach in 1491 with his Last Judgment in the cathedral there unfinished, the largest mural north of the Alps. The next year Albrecht Dürer came to Colmar to meet him and found he had died. The Met bought this sheet in 1926.
