Les mots et les images (La Révolution surréaliste, No. 12)

René Magritte · published 15 December 1929 · Printed magazine page (letterpress and halftone)

Free to use

René Magritte published this page of drawings and captions on 15 December 1929 in No. 12 of La Révolution surréaliste, the magazine's last issue, printed in 3,000 ordinary copies and sold in France for 20 francs. He was thirty-one. He had moved to Paris after his first solo exhibition, in Brussels in 1927, had poor reviews; in 1930 he returned to Brussels and resumed working in advertising.
