Kali on Shiva (Tantric Devi series)

Maker unknown · c. 1810 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · 20.3 × 26 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The page comes from a series made in the Pahari kingdom of Mandi. It was in the royal library of Mandi until 1969, when it was probably sold to the German collector Ludwig V. Habighorst. Sotheby's sold it in New York in 2009 to Nancy and Wayne Hunnicutt, who gave it to Cleveland in 2014. The museum says the scene is set in a cremation ground, with vultures, jackals and bones.
