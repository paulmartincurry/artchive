But we loved with a love that was more than love--
I and my ANNABEL LEE;
With a love that the winged seraphs of heaven
Coveted her and me.

Annabel Lee

Edgar Allan Poe · 1849

This is the last complete poem Poe composed, probably in May 1849. He gave one copy to his literary executor, Rufus Wilmot Griswold, another to John Thompson to repay a five-dollar debt, and sold a third to Sartain's Union Magazine. Griswold printed it first, on 9 October 1849, two days after Poe's death, as part of his obituary of Poe in the New-York Daily Tribune.
