Fishing Boats with Hucksters Bargaining for Fish

J. M. W. Turner · 1837–38 · Oil on canvas · 174.5 × 224.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Turner signed this canvas on the top flag of the nearest boat. He showed it in 1838 at the British Institution, in the former Boydell Shakespeare Gallery in Pall Mall. It was still his in 1851, the year he died, when John Naylor bought it from him. The painting stayed with the Naylors until 1910 and came to the Art Institute of Chicago by bequest in 1922.
