And as long as you lack this
True word: Die and Become!
You’ll be but a dismal guest
In Earth’s darkened room.

Blissful Yearning (Selige Sehnsucht)

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1814

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Goethe dated the poem 31 July 1814, in Wiesbaden. It was first printed in a ladies' pocket-book for 1817 under the title Vollendung, and then placed second to last in the Book of the Singer of the West–östlicher Divan, published in 1819, a book inspired by his reading of Hafez.
