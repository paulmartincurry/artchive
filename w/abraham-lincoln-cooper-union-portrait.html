Abraham Lincoln, Cooper Union Portrait

Mathew B. Brady · 27 February 1860, printed later · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Brady made the portrait on the morning of Monday 27 February 1860, a few hours before Lincoln gave his Cooper Union address in New York. Lincoln had a new suit fitted for the occasion, at a cost of 100 dollars. According to Lloyd Ostendorf, who compiled an album of the Lincoln photographs, Lincoln later said that the speech and this portrait put him in the White House. This print was made later.
