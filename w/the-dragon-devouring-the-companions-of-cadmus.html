The Dragon Devouring the Companions of Cadmus

Hendrick Goltzius, after Cornelis van Haarlem · 1588 · Engraving · Image: 24 × 31.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Goltzius engraved this plate in 1588 after a design by Cornelis van Haarlem; during the 1580s the two of them and Karel van Mander ran an informal academy in Haarlem where artists drew the figure from life. As a child Goltzius had fallen into burning coals, and his right hand could never fully open again, so he engraved using the larger muscles of his arm and shoulder.
