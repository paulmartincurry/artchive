Cardinal Fernando Niño de Guevara

El Greco · ca. 1600 · Oil on canvas · 67 1/4 × 42 1/2in. (170.8 × 108cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The cardinal spent several weeks in Toledo in the spring of 1600, and the portrait was probably commissioned then. He had been nominated Inquisitor General of Spain in 1599. The historian Walter Liedtke proposes that the man who ordered it was the sitter's nephew, Pedro Lasso, a patron of El Greco who owned seven or eight of his paintings. Painter and sitter were both born in 1541.
