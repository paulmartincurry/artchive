Portrait of Andreas Vesalius (Frontispiece to De humani corporis fabrica)

Jan Steven van Calcar · 1542 · Woodcut · The Met

Free to use

Jan Steven van Calcar was a pupil of Titian, entering his school in 1536, and was taken on for the accuracy of his copies of Titian's paintings. Giorgio Vasari and Carel van Mander credited him with the large woodcut illustrations in Andreas Vesalius's anatomy, On the Fabric of the Human Body in Seven Books, published in 1543. He spent most of his life in Naples. This sheet is 21.2 centimetres tall and was given to the Metropolitan Museum by Richard Day in 1980.
