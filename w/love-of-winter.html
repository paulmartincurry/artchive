Love of Winter

George Bellows · 1914 · Oil on canvas · 81.6 × 101.6 cm (32 1/2 × 40 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In January 1914 Bellows wrote to a friend that there had been no snow that winter, and that he had to paint snow at least once a year. A blizzard hit New York on 13 February, and he painted this skating scene after it. It belongs to a run of New York snow pictures he made from 1907 to 1915. The Friends of American Art bought it from him the same year and gave it to the Art Institute, which showed it that December in an exhibition of his paintings.
