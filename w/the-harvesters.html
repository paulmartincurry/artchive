The Harvesters

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1565 · Oil on oak · Overall: 46 7/8 × 63 3/4 in. (119 × 162 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Nicolaes Jonghelinck, a merchant banker and collector in Antwerp, commissioned a cycle of paintings of the seasons from Bruegel, and this is one of the five that survive. Three of the others are in Vienna and one is in Prague. Bruegel had married two years before and moved to Brussels, and he died there four years later. Strips of wood were added to the panel at the top, bottom and right at some later date. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1919.
