The Taking of Christ

Caravaggio · c. 1602 · Oil on canvas · 133.5 × 169.5 cm · National Gallery of Ireland, Dublin

Free to use

Ciriaco Mattei, a Roman nobleman, commissioned the painting in 1602. By the late eighteenth century it was thought to have disappeared, and it stayed missing for about 200 years. It had hung in the dining room of the Jesuits in Dublin since the early 1930s, taken for a copy by Gerard van Honthorst, until it was recognised in 1990. The man holding the lantern is believed to be a self-portrait.
