The Annunciation

Fra Angelico · c. 1440–1445 · Fresco · Museo di San Marco, Florence

Free to use

When Cosimo de' Medici rebuilt the convent of San Marco, Fra Angelico decorated it with around fifty frescoes, painted by himself or under his direct supervision. This one is at the top of the stairs to the friars' dormitories, one of only three he painted on the corridor walls rather than inside the cells. It was meant to be seen in low light; today lamps light it from many angles.
