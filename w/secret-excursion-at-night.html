Secret Excursion at Night (Yageum mohaeng)

Sin Yun-bok · ca. 1805 · Gansong Art Museum, Seoul

Free to use

This is one leaf of an album of thirty genre paintings by Sin Yun-bok. Jeon Hyeopil bought the album from an antique dealer in Osaka in 1930 and had it newly mounted, and he later founded the museum that holds it. The journalist and independence activist Oh Sechang wrote its subtitles and postface.
