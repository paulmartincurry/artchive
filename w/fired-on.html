The Artchive

Fired On

Frederic Remington · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 68.8 × 101.6 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

William T. Evans bought this painting in November 1909, the month before Remington died, and gave it to what is now the Smithsonian American Art Museum. It was the first painting by Remington to enter a public gallery in the United States. It hung in the Oval Office during Harry S. Truman's presidency.
