There Sat the Dog with Eyes as Big as Teacups (The Tinder-Box)

Harry Clarke · published 1916 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Clarke's first book commissions from Harrap, The Rime of the Ancient Mariner and The Rape of the Lock, were never finished; much of his Coleridge work was destroyed in the 1916 Easter Rising in Dublin. That made this Andersen, with 16 colour plates and more than 24 halftones, his first printed book, in 1916. In the tale, this is the first of three dogs guarding chambers of coins inside a hollow tree.
