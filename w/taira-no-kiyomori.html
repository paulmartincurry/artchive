Taira no Kiyomori (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889–92 · Woodcut, color · 26.9 × 21.1 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In the Library of Congress's description, Taira no Kiyomori sits holding a sword and has a vision of a demon looking through a window. The ghost series was still appearing when Yoshitoshi was admitted to a mental hospital. He left it in May 1892 and died three weeks later in a rented room, of a cerebral hemorrhage, aged fifty-three.
