Un Chien Andalou, frame at 6:47

Luis Buñuel · 1929 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Luis Buñuel was working as an assistant director to Jean Epstein when he and Salvador Dalí wrote the script, in a few days, from two of their dreams. Buñuel's mother paid for the film. It was shot in Le Havre and at the Billancourt Studios in Paris over ten days in March 1928, on 430 metres of film.
