Moses Harris · 1766 · Hand-coloured engraving · University of Alberta Library

Free to use

A second edition of The Aurelian followed in 1775. Harris later made 44 watercolour drawings of the life cycles of British butterflies and moths for a projected new edition, and they are now in the Natural History Museum.
