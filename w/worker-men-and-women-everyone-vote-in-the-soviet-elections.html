Worker Men and Women: Everyone Vote in the Soviet Elections

Gustav Klutsis · 1930 · Color offset lithograph on cream wove paper, pieced and laid down on white wove paper · 119.7 × 84.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Gustav Klutsis was 35 when this poster was printed, a sheet about 120 centimetres tall in offset lithography. He and his wife, Valentina Kulagina, worked mainly in photomontage, and to save money they often posed for their own posters, dressed as shock workers or peasants. In 1930 the state art school VKhUTEMAS, where he had taught colour theory since 1924, was closed.
