This is the way the world ends
This is the way the world ends
Not with a bang but a whimper.

The Hollow Men (V)

T. S. Eliot · 1925

Eliot built this poem from pieces. Four of its five parts had already appeared in magazines between the winter of 1924 and March 1925, and the whole was first printed as it now stands on 23 November 1925, in his Poems: 1909–1925. Only the fifth part was new. He said he made the title by combining William Morris's The Hollow Land with Rudyard Kipling's The Broken Men. Asked in 1958 whether he would write the closing lines again, he said he would not.
