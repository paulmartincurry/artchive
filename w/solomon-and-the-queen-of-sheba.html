Solomon and the Queen of Sheba

Maker unknown · 1965–1975 · Wereldmuseum Amsterdam

Free to use with credit. Photo: Tropenmuseum, CC BY-SA 3.0

In Ethiopian tradition the Queen of Sheba, called Makeda, visited King Solomon in Jerusalem and had a son by him, Menelik, who founded Ethiopia's royal line. Paintings telling the story in rows of small panels, like a comic strip, became popular in the twentieth century.
