Floor Mosaic: Adam and Eve

Maker unknown · late 400s–early 500s CE · marble and stone tesserae · Overall: 142.9 × 107.3 × 5.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This panel was cut from a much larger mosaic on the floor of an early Byzantine church in northern Syria. A Greek inscription along the top gives a line from the third chapter of Genesis. The fragment is about 1.4 metres tall. It came to Cleveland through the New York dealer J. J. Klejman and entered the collection in 1969.
