Alfred Stieglitz

Frank Eugene · c. 1901 · Photogravure · 16.5 × 11.4 cm (image) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Eugene worked over his negatives with scratches and brush strokes, a habit he brought from his training as a painter. The National Gallery dates this portrait of Stieglitz to about 1901, two years after Eugene's first show at the Camera Club of New York. In late 1902 Eugene became a founder of the Photo-Secession and sat on its governing council.
