Morphine Addict

Eugène Grasset · 1897 · Colour lithograph · 56.7 × 42.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Eugène Grasset, the son of a cabinetmaker and sculptor in Lausanne, trained as an architect and moved to Paris in 1871. This lithograph was printed by Auguste Clot and published by Ambroise Vollard in his album of original prints. Cleveland's impression is signed in graphite and carries the collector's mark of Albin Woldemar von Dietel; the museum bought it in 2018.
