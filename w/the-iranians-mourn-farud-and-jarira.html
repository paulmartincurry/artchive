The Iranians Mourn Farud and Jarira (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Mirza Muhammad Qabahat and 'Abd al-'Aziz · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.6 × 18.6 cm; page 47.3 × 31.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Two painters are named for this scene, Mirza Muhammad Qabahat and 'Abd al-'Aziz. After Arthur Houghton died in 1990, his son sold the binding, the text pages and the 118 paintings that remained; in 1994 they went to the Tehran Museum of Contemporary Art in exchange for Willem de Kooning's Woman III.
