A prince riding a composite elephant

Maker unknown · c. 1590 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page: 28.8 x 18.6 cm (11 5/16 x 7 5/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Deccan artists built on Persian habits of hiding creatures in landscapes to make composite beasts out of many figures. Two lions share one head at the top of this elephant's head.
