The Princess and the Trolls

John Bauer · 1913 · Watercolour · Nationalmuseum, Stockholm

Free to use

In July 1904 Bauer spent a month in Lappland, making pictures for a book on the region; he photographed, sketched and took notes on the tools, costumes and objects he saw among the Sami. He drew the first four volumes of Among Gnomes and Trolls, and, after a year's break, the volumes from 1912 to 1915. This watercolour is in the Nationalmuseum in Stockholm.
