Evening Snow at Kambara (Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1833 · Color woodblock print · Sheet: 22.6 × 35.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kambara, a coastal town, rarely sees snow. The flakes were made by carving their shapes out of the block, so they print as bare paper. The Cleveland Museum of Art says this may be the first print in which Hiroshige showed falling snow. In 1996 Weezer used the design as the cover of the album Pinkerton.
