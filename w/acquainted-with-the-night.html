The Artchive
I have been one acquainted with the night.
I have walked out in rain—and back in rain.
I have outwalked the furthest city light.

Acquainted with the Night

Robert Frost · 1928

The Virginia Quarterly Review printed this poem first, in its autumn 1928 issue, and Henry Holt published it later that year in the collection West-Running Brook. It has fourteen lines, like a sonnet, rhymed in terza rima. Frost was fifty-four. He had lived in San Francisco until his father's death in 1884, when the family moved to Lawrence, Massachusetts.
