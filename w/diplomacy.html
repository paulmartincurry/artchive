The Artchive
“I will bite it!” cried the man, in great anger,—“I will bite it!—I will bite”—

There was a flash, a swish, a crunching thud: the bound body bowed over the rice sacks,—two long blood-jets pumping from the shorn neck;—and the head rolled upon the sand.

Diplomacy

Lafcadio Hearn · 1904

Hearn was born on the Greek island of Lefkada and grew up in Dublin, where he was abandoned first by his mother, then by his father, and then by his father's aunt, who had been made his guardian. At nineteen he emigrated to the United States and worked as a newspaper reporter in Cincinnati and New Orleans before going to Japan in 1890.
