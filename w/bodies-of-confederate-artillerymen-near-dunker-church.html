Bodies of Confederate Artillerymen near Dunker Church

Alexander Gardner · photographed 1862, printed 1880–89 · Albumen print, half stereograph on card mount · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Alexander Gardner photographed the battlefield of Antietam in September 1862, developing his plates in a travelling darkroom. He held the honorary rank of captain. The battle remains the bloodiest day in American history, with 22,726 dead, wounded or missing on the two sides. This print was made from his negative between 1880 and 1889.
