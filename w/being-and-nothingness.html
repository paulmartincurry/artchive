Man is a useless passion.

Being and Nothingness

Jean-Paul Sartre · 1943 (English 1956)

Translated by Hazel E. Barnes · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Sartre read Martin Heidegger's Being and Time while he was a prisoner of war in 1940 and 1941, and he attributed the course of his own philosophical inquiries to that reading. Being and Nothingness followed in 1943.
