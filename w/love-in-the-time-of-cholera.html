Love in the Time of Cholera

Gabriel García Márquez · 1985 (English 1988)

Translated by Edith Grossman · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The story is a recasting of the courtship of García Márquez's own parents. His father wooed his mother with violin serenades, love poems, letters and telephone messages after her father, a colonel, sent her away to separate them, and her family gave in. The unnamed port city is based on a mix of Cartagena and Barranquilla. García Márquez was fifty-seven when the novel came out, three years after his Nobel Prize, and the first Mexican edition alone ran to 100,000 copies.
