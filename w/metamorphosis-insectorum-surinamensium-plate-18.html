Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 18

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

The German word Vogelspinne, bird spider, probably goes back to this engraving of a large spider that has caught a bird. In the same plate and its text Merian became the first European to describe army ants and leaf-cutter ants. She wrote that she never saw these spiders fly, and that they took small birds from their nests when ants were scarce.
