The Four Witches (Four Naked Women)

Albrecht Dürer · 1497 · engraving · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This engraving dates from two years after Dürer opened his own workshop in Nuremberg, which only a married man was allowed to do. He was twenty-five or twenty-six. He had learned the basics of goldsmithing from his father, a goldsmith, before he was apprenticed to a painter at fifteen. The Print Club of Cleveland gave this print to the museum.
