Gallus Indicus Auritus Tridactylus (Monstrorum Historia)

Ulisse Aldrovandi · 1642 · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Aldrovandi kept a collection of some 7,000 natural specimens in his home in Bologna, where sea animals are described as hanging from the ceiling and stuffed birds lining the walls. He willed it to the Senate of Bologna.
