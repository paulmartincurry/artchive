Bread (Brot)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1924 · Lithograph · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

In 1924, the year she turned fifty-seven, Kollwitz finished three posters: Germany's Children Starving, Bread, and Never Again War. This lithograph carries the second of those titles. Over her life she made 275 prints in etching, woodcut and lithography.
