The Lord Answering Job out of the Whirlwind (The Book of Job, Plate 13)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · Sheet: 42.6 × 32.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

In 1823 John Linnell formally commissioned Blake to engrave the Job plates. Blake worked them in intaglio and rejected the mixed method popular among commercial engravers of his time, in which guidelines were first lightly etched into the plate. This is plate 13 of the series.
