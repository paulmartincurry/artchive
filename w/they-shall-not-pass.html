No pasarán

They shall not pass

Dolores Ibárruri · 1936

The phrase spread from the Battle of Verdun in 1916, when General Robert Nivelle urged French troops not to let the enemy pass. In the Spanish Civil War the communist Dolores Ibárruri used it to rally the Republican defenders of Madrid against Franco's forces.
