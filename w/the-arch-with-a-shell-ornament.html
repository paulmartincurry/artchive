The Arch with a Shell Ornament (Carceri d'invenzione, plate XI)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching on heavy ivory laid paper · 40.8 × 55 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Plate XI is very similar, in reverse, to a Piranesi drawing in the British Museum known as Study for a Palatial Interior. It is one of the seven horizontal plates that close the set, numbered X to XVI.
