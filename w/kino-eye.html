Kino-Eye (Kino-Glaz)

Alexander Rodchenko · 1924 · Poster (lithograph)

Free to use

Rodchenko made this poster for Kino-Eye, Dziga Vertov's 1924 documentary, shot by Mikhail Kaufman without actors, sets or a studio. Vertov planned it as a six-part series but filmed only the first episode, Life Taken by Surprise. Rodchenko had given up painting in 1921 to design posters, books and films, and he had worked closely with Vertov in 1922.
