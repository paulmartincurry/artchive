The world is too much with us; late and soon,
Getting and spending, we lay waste our powers:

The World Is Too Much with Us

William Wordsworth · 1807

Wordsworth composed this sonnet around 1802, the year he married Mary Hutchinson, whom he had first met at school in Penrith. It keeps the Italian form: eight lines rhymed ABBA ABBA, then six rhymed CDCD CD. It was first published in 1807 in Poems, in Two Volumes.
