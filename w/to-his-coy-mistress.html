But at my back I always hear
Time's winged chariot hurrying near.

To His Coy Mistress

Andrew Marvell · ca. 1650 (pub. 1681)

The poem was perhaps written in the early 1650s, when Marvell was working as tutor to the daughter of Sir Thomas Fairfax, the retired commander of the New Model Army. It was not published until 1681, three years after his death. In the years between he sat in the House of Commons for Hull.
