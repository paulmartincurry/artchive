The Shadow of Death

William Holman Hunt · 1873-74 · Oil on panel · 104.5 × 82 cm (41 × 32 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The young Jesus stretches after work, and his shadow falls on a rack of tools in the shape of a cross. It is the third and final version of a composition Hunt began in Jerusalem in 1869, where he could study the setting from life.
