King Muchukunda Enters the Realm of Mount Gandhamadana (Fifth Basohli Bhagavata Purana)

Attributed to Fattu · c. 1760–70 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page: 30.3 × 40.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This page from the Fifth Basohli Bhagavata Purana is attributed to the painter Fattu and dates from around 1760 to 1770. Its inscriptions in Gurmukhi and Sanskrit number it painting 139, chapter 52. In the story, King Muchukunda slept in a cave for thousands of years, and on waking was sent by Krishna to do penance on Mount Gandhamadana. An upside-down inscription on the back suggests the painting was meant to be turned on its side.
