Taurus, from a Mandi Astrology Series

Sanju · c. 1810 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · 27 x 22.2 cm (10 5/8 x 8 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The white bull for Taurus wears bells, a jewelled diadem and capped horns, and a gold-patterned cloth sets him apart from the cattle and elephants behind. The oval format comes from the fashion for British cameos at the Mughal court.
