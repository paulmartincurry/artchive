Entrance to Manzanar, Manzanar Relocation Center

Ansel Adams · 1943 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Manzanar lies in California's Owens Valley, on the eastern side of the Sierra Nevada. It held 10,046 adults and children at its peak, and 11,070 people were incarcerated there in all. Eight guard towers with machine guns stood around a perimeter fence topped with barbed wire. Ansel Adams visited in 1943 and 1944.
