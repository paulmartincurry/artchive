Dancer (Die Tänzerin)

Egon Schiele · 1913 · Watercolor and gouache over graphite on wove paper · 47 × 30.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Schiele signed and dated the sheet in graphite at the centre right, in three lines: EGON, SCHIELE, 1913. He was twenty-two or twenty-three. It belonged in turn to Hans Ankwicz-Kleehoven, Rudolf Leopold and Serge Sabarsky, was sold at Sotheby's in New York in 1979, and was given to the National Gallery of Art in 1990.
