Très Riches Heures du Duc de Berry

The Limbourg brothers · c. 1412–1416 · Vellum, tempera · Condé Museum

Free to use

The three Limbourg brothers and the duke who employed them all died in 1416, and the book was left unfinished and unbound. Another painter added to it in the 1440s, and Jean Colombe completed it between 1485 and 1489 for the Duke of Savoy, who paid him 25 gold pieces. It has 206 leaves of calfskin, 30 centimetres by 21.5, with 66 large miniatures and 65 small ones. The painters used about ten colours besides black and white, with extremely small brushes and probably a lens. The Musée Condé withdrew the book from display in the 1980s.
