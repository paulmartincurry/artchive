Dans les Bois (L'Estampe Moderne)

Eugène Grasset · 1899 · Color lithograph from bound volume containing 50 lithographs, each with a tissue paper guard · Sheet: 21.5 x 18.4 cm; Image: 21.5 x 18.4 cm; Secondary Support: 40.1 x 30.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This print was issued in L'Estampe Moderne, a series of original colour lithographs printed by Champenois, each sheet with a tissue guard. Grasset designed furniture, wallpapers, fabrics, ceramics and jewellery after moving to Paris in 1871, and in 1897 the Peignot foundry patented a typeface of his under the name Grasset.
