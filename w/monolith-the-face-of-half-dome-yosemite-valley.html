Monolith, the Face of Half Dome, Yosemite Valley

Ansel Adams · 1927 · Gelatin silver print · 14.6 × 19.7 cm (portfolio print) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

On 17 April 1927 Adams hiked up to a ledge called the Diving Board with friends, carrying a Korona view camera, twelve glass plates and a wooden tripod, in basketball sneakers on the snow. He waited more than two hours for the light, and by 2:30 he had two plates left. He used a deep red filter to turn the sky black. The picture went into his first portfolio that August, eighteen prints made by Adams himself; the San Francisco patron Albert Bender sold 56 sets by telephone before any buyer had seen one. This print is plate 3 of that portfolio.
