The Artchive
O German mother dreaming by the fire,
While you are knitting socks to send your son
His face is trodden deeper in the mud.

Glory of Women

Siegfried Sassoon · 1918

Glory of Women came out in 1918 in Counter-Attack and Other Poems. Before refusing to return to duty in 1917, Sassoon had thrown his Military Cross ribbon into the sea at Formby beach; the medal itself was kept and passed to his family. At the end of that year he was posted to Limerick in Ireland to help train new recruits, and soon afterwards to Egypt.
