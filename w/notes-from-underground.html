I am a sick man... I am a spiteful man.
I am an unattractive man.

Notes from Underground (Part I, Ch. I)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1864

The novella first ran in the journal Epoch between January and April 1864, where Dostoevsky had announced it under the title A Confession. He was forty-two, and his first wife, Maria Isaeva, died that April. Fifteen years earlier he had been arrested for belonging to a circle that discussed banned books and sentenced to death; the sentence was commuted, and he spent four years in a Siberian hard labour camp.
