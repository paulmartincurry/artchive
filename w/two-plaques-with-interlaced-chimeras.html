Two Plaques with Interlaced Chimeras

Maker unknown · 1200–50 · Gilt copper alloy · A: 9.2 × 3.8 cm (3 5/8 × 1 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The two plaques were made in Limoges, and neither is as much as ten centimetres tall. Mr. and Mrs. James W. Alsdorf of Winnetka, Illinois, lent them to an exhibition of medieval decorative arts at the Art Institute of Chicago in October 1985, and gave them to the museum that same year.
