The City Flourishing, Tanabata Festival (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 33.8 × 22.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is the only print in the series without a place name in its title. By one argument it shows the view from Hiroshige's own house, which takes in Edo Castle and Mount Fuji.
