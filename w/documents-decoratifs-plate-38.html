Documents Décoratifs: Plate 38

Alphonse Mucha · 1901–2 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Documents Decoratifs was a book of seventy-two printed plates, published in 1902 by the Central Library of Fine Arts as a pattern book of Mucha's designs. By then he had also created a new kind of print with the printer Champenois: the decorative panel, a poster without text made purely for decoration.
