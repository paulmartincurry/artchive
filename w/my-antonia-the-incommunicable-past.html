Whatever we had missed, we possessed together the precious, the incommunicable past.

My Ántonia — The incommunicable past (closing)

Willa Cather · 1918

Cather's family moved from Virginia to Nebraska in 1883, when she was nine, and her father farmed there for eighteen months before taking them into the town of Red Cloud. The narrator of the novel makes the same journey from Virginia at the age of ten. Houghton Mifflin bought the book without having seen it and published it in 1918. For the revised edition of 1926 Cather agreed to cut the original introduction.
