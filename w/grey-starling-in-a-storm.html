Grey Starling in a Storm

Ohara Koson · 1900–10 · Color woodblock print on paper · 18.1 × 35.0 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Koson designed around 500 prints in his career. Around 1900 he taught at the Tokyo School of Fine Arts, where he met Ernest Fenollosa. Fenollosa, later curator of Japanese art at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, would ask him to send numerous bird-and-flower prints to the United States.
