The Calligrapher

Katsushika Hokusai · 1760–1849 (Edo period) · Ink and color on paper · 30.9 x 22.4 cm · Freer Gallery of Art, National Museum of Asian Art, Smithsonian, Washington

Free to use

This drawing comes from an album of sketches by Hokusai that Charles Lang Freer bought from the dealer Bunshichi Kobayashi in 1904. Freer signed the deed of gift for his collection in 1906, and it was received in 1920, when the Freer Gallery was completed. The sheet is 31 centimetres tall.
