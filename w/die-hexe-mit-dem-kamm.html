Die Hexe mit dem Kamm (Witch with a Comb)

Paul Klee · 1922 · Lithograph · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This lithograph dates from 1922, Klee's second year at the Bauhaus, where he was a master in the bookbinding, stained glass and mural painting workshops and was given two studios. Kandinsky joined the staff the same year.
