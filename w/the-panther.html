It seems to him there are a thousand bars; and behind the bars, no world.

The Panther (In the Jardin des Plantes, Paris)

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1902

Translated by Stephen Mitchell · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Rilke wrote this poem between 1902 and 1903, about an animal on show in the menagerie of the Jardin des Plantes. He was twenty-six or twenty-seven and had come to Paris to write a book on Auguste Rodin, who taught him to value objective observation. The poem has three stanzas of four lines. In the 1990 film Awakenings the main character uses it to describe his own physical disability.
