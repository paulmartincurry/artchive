L'Eclair (Lightning)

Victor Hugo · 1868 · Etching in black on laid paper · Plate: 21.7 × 14.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The plate is signed Victor Hugo 1868 and was printed in the album Sonnets et Eaux-Fortes, facing a sonnet of the same title by Paul Meurice. Hugo was sixty-six and living in exile on Guernsey, where he had been since 1855. His wife Adèle died that August, and because he was still banished from France he could not attend her funeral.
