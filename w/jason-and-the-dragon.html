Jason and the Dragon

Salvator Rosa · c. 1663–64 · Etching and drypoint · Sheet: 34.1 × 21.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Besides painting, Rosa wrote poetry and satire, acted, made music and etched. All his prints are etchings, some with drypoint. This one dates from about 1663–64, when he was in his late forties, and the sheet is 34.1 centimetres tall.
