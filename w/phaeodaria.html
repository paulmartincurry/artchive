Phaeodaria (Kunstformen der Natur, plate 1)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Colour lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

This is the first plate of the book. Ernst Haeckel signed its preface in Jena on 16 February 1899, his sixty-fifth birthday, and planned at first for fifty plates issued in five sets of ten, each with its own sheet of text, going on to a hundred only if the first sets were well received. The lithographs were made by Adolf Giltsch in Jena, and the Bibliographisches Institut in Leipzig took on the costly production and publication.
