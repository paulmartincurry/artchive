Daniel in the Lions' Den

Henry Ossawa Tanner · 1907–18 · Oil on paper mounted on canvas · 104.5 × 126.8 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

This is not the first version, which Tanner painted in 1895 and showed at the Paris Salon of 1896, where it received an honorable mention. He made this canvas between 1907 and 1918. Under the same title he also won silver medals at the Paris Exposition of 1900, at Buffalo in 1901 and at St. Louis in 1904.
