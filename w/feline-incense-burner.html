Feline Incense Burner

Maker unknown · 1150–1200 · Copper alloy, cast, engraved, chased, and pierced · 35.5 × 11 × 32.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The head was cast separately and lifts off, so the body could be filled with hot coals and incense; the smoke came out through the pierced pattern. The animal may be a caracal, a kind of lynx. Verses from the Qur'an in Kufic script run along its neck and spine, telling believers to leave trade aside for Friday prayer. It was made in Khurasan between 1150 and 1200 and is 35.5 centimetres high.
