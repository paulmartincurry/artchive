And when Rostam opened the boy’s armor and saw the clasp he tore at his own clothes in grief, saying, “All men praised your bravery, and I have killed you with my own hands.”

Shahnameh — Rostam and Sohrab

Abu'l-Qasim Ferdowsi · ca. 1010 CE

Translated by Dick Davis · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Rostam was not Ferdowsi's invention. Legends about him were known across the Iranian lands by the end of the Sasanian period, and a body of Pahlavi texts told them. Ferdowsi worked mainly from a prose Book of Kings compiled in his home town of Tus. Another poet, Daqiqi, had started turning the old chronicle into verse and died violently after 1,000 verses; Ferdowsi took those verses into his own poem and said so.
