And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.

Revelation 21:1–4 (A New Heaven and a New Earth)

King James Bible (Book of Revelation) · ca. 95 CE (trans. 1611)

Revelation was written in Koine Greek, and early copies of this chapter survive in the Codex Sinaiticus, made between 330 and 360, and the Codex Alexandrinus. Later in the chapter the New Jerusalem is measured at 12,000 stadia in each direction, and the city has no temple.
