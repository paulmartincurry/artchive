The Artchive
The body, you’ll abandon, not its cares;
They will be ash, but ash that yet feels;
Dust they will be, but an amorous dust.

The last shadow that the bright day brings me

Francisco de Quevedo · before 1645

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Quevedo was born in Madrid in 1580 and died in 1645. He and Góngora were lifelong rivals, and he mocked Góngora's prominent nose in the sonnet A una nariz.
