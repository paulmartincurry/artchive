Persische Nachtigallen (Persian Nightingales)

Paul Klee · 1917 · Gouache, watercolor, and pen and black ink over graphite on laid paper, mounted on cardboard · 22.8 × 18.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Klee made this sheet in 1917 while a conscript in the Imperial German Army. That January he had been transferred to the Royal Bavarian flying school in Gersthofen to work as a clerk for the treasurer, which let him stay in a small room outside the barracks and continue painting. On the mount he wrote the title and 1917 92.
