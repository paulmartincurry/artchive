Royal Seat (Lupona)

Ngongo ya Chintu (Buli Master) · c. 1840–1870 · Wood, metal studs · 61 × 27.9 × 27.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Ngongo ya Chintu is an honorific title meaning the great leopard, the father of sculpted things. In the 1930s the Belgian art historian Frans Olbrechts noticed that about ten figures shared one hand, two of them collected in the town of Buli, and named their unknown carver the Master of Buli. In the 1970s the sculptor was identified as an artist from the village of Kateba. This seat, carved around 1840 to 1870, is 61 centimetres tall.
