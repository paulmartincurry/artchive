Pastoral—Moonlight (Camera Work 19, plate I)

Edward Steichen · 1907 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The plate appeared as plate I of Camera Work, number 19, in July 1907. Steichen was the photographer shown most often in the quarterly, and in 1902 Stieglitz had asked him to design its logo with a custom typeface. He was also one of the earliest in the United States to use the Autochrome colour process, which only became available in 1907.
