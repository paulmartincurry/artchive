The Junction of the Thames and the Medway

J. M. W. Turner · 1807 · Oil on canvas · 108.8 × 143.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Thomas Lister Parker of Brownsholme Hall bought the painting in 1807, almost certainly from Turner's own gallery. It was put up for sale in 1808 and again in 1811 and was bought in both times. Joseph Gillott of Birmingham bought it in 1848, and Peter A. B. Widener bought it in 1894.
