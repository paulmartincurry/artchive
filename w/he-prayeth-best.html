The Artchive
He prayeth best, who loveth best
All things both great and small;
For the dear God who loveth us
He made and loveth all.

He Prayeth Best (The Rime of the Ancient Mariner, Part VII)

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1798

Coleridge wrote the poem in 1797–98 and published it in 1798 in the first edition of Lyrical Ballads. For the second edition, published in 1800, he replaced many of the archaic words.
