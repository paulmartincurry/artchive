Coat-of-Arms of Death (The Dance of Death)

Hans Holbein the Younger · c. 1526, published 1538 · Woodcut · Sheet: 6.5 × 4.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

There were eleven editions of this series before 1562, and perhaps as many as a hundred unauthorized editions and imitations over the sixteenth century. Ten more designs were added in later editions. The Met bought this impression in 1919.
