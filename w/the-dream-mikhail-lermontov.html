Noon heat, a gorge in Daghestan,
I lay still, a bullet in my chest:
The deep wound was still red-hot,
blood seeped, drop by drop.

The Dream (В полдневный жар в долине Дагестана)

Mikhail Lermontov · 1841

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Lermontov wrote this poem in 1841, shortly before his death, and it was published in 1843. An earlier poem of his with the same title dates from 1830 or 1831 and was not published until 1889.
