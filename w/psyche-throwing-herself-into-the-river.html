Psyche Throwing Herself into the River

William Morris, after Edward Burne-Jones · 1865–68 · Wood engraving · to borderline: 10.5 x 8.2 cm (4 1/8 x 3 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

It is one of the few illustrations finished for Morris and Burne-Jones's planned book of myths and legends. They engraved the pictures for one of the 24 stories, then gave up when they could not find a publisher; the experience led Morris to found the Kelmscott Press.
