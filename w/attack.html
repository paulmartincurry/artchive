While time ticks blank and busy on their wrists,
And hope, with furtive eyes and grappling fists,
Flounders in mud. O Jesu, make it stop!

Attack

Siegfried Sassoon · 1918

Attack was published in Counter-Attack and Other Poems by Heinemann in 1918. On the Western Front Sassoon once captured a German trench single-handed, armed with grenades, and his men nicknamed him Mad Jack. He was awarded the Military Cross on 27 July 1916, for bringing in the wounded during a raid under rifle and bomb fire, and was later recommended for the Victoria Cross.
