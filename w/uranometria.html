Uranometria (Title Page)

Johann Bayer · 1603 · Engraving · United States Naval Observatory Library

Free to use

Alexander Mair engraved this title page and the atlas's 51 star charts on copper plates. He signed it with a monogram in the shadows of the scroll at the bottom, and beneath the title he put a view of Augsburg, where the book was published in 1603. Johann Bayer took his star positions from Tycho Brahe's catalogue of 1,005 stars, which was then circulating only in manuscript. It was the first atlas to cover the whole sky.
