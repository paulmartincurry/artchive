A Night Scene of Shiva Puja

Attributed to Muhammad Rizavi Hindi · c. 1760–70 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · Painting only: 14.5 × 11.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting itself is tiny, 14.5 by 11.1 centimetres, and is attributed to Muhammad Rizavi Hindi, who worked at Lucknow in the mid-1700s. Before 1811 it belonged to Sir Charles Forbes, and it stayed with his descendants until his great-grandson sold it at Sotheby's in London on 10 December 1962. Ralph Benkaim bought it that day.
