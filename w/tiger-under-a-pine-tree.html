Tiger Under a Pine Tree (Songha maenghodo)

Kim Hong-do · late 18th century · Colour on silk · Ho-Am Art Museum, Yongin

Free to use

Kim Hong-do painted this tiger in colour on silk; the scroll is about 90 centimetres tall and belongs to the Ho-Am Art Museum. He grew up in Ansan, where a district is now named Danwon after him. Despite his years at court, he died lonely and poor, and even the year of his death is unknown: sources give 1806, 1810 or some time after 1814.
