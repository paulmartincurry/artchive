I, Lallā, passed in through the door of the jasmine-garden of my soul.
And there, O Joy! saw I Śiva seated united with His Śakti.
There became I absorbed in the lake of nectar.
Now, what can (existence) do unto me? For, even though alive, I shall in it be dead.

Vakh 68 (The Jasmine-Garden of My Soul)

Lal Ded (Lalleshwari) · 14th century; English 1920

Translated by George Grierson and Lionel D. Barnett

Her verses lived in oral tradition and were first written down in the twentieth century. In 1914 a manuscript was made by taking them down as they were recited by Dharma-dasa Darwesh, a storyteller living in Gush, Kashmir. Lalleshwari is estimated to have died in 1373, and a grave near Bijbehara is said to be hers, though that has never been confirmed.
