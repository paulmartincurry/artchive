Washington Crossing the Delaware

Emanuel Leutze · 1851 · Oil on canvas · 149 × 255 in. (378.5 × 647.7 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The river in the background is the Rhine. Emanuel Leutze painted the picture in Düsseldorf, using American tourists and art students as his models. His first version was damaged by a fire in his studio and destroyed in a bombing raid in 1942; this one is a full-sized replica begun in 1850. More than 50,000 people saw it in New York after it went on show in October 1851, and Marshall O. Roberts bought it for $10,000. The canvas is about 3.8 metres tall and 6.5 metres wide.
