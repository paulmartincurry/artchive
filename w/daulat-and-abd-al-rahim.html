Daulat and Abd al-Rahim (Khamsa of Nizami)

Daulat · ca. 1609–1610 (Added to 1595 Lahore Manuscript) · British Library

Free to use

The manuscript was made for the Mughal emperor Akbar, and one scribe, Abd al-Rahim, wrote all of it between 12 October 1593 and 14 December 1595. When Akbar's son Jahangir inherited the book he ordered this extra painting added. It shows the scribe at work, facing the painter Daulat, who is making a drawing of him. The date on it has an illegible last digit and falls between 1611 and 1620.
