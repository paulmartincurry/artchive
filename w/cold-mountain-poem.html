Where’s the trail to Cold Mountain?
Cold Mountain? There’s no clear way.

Cold Mountain Poem

Hanshan (Cold Mountain) · ca. 8th–9th century CE

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2006 · © A. S. Kline 2006; free to reproduce for any non-commercial purpose (poetryintranslation.com).

Hanshan means Cold Mountain, and no one knows who the poet was, when he lived, or whether he existed at all. In the poems he says he wrote them on the rocks of the mountains where he lived as a recluse. Of some 600 he is thought to have written, 313 were collected and survive. An undated preface credits a governor with gathering them from rocks, bamboo, trees and house walls, and the translator Burton Watson judged that the governor was probably a myth as well. No poem is shorter than four lines or longer than thirty-four.
