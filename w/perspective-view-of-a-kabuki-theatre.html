Perspective View of a Kabuki Theatre

Okumura Masanobu · ca. 1741–1744

Free to use

This is an uki-e, a kind of print built on geometrical perspective. Western-style perspective drawing had reached Japan in 1734 through a manual based on a Dutch text of 1644. Okumura Masanobu advertised himself as the inventor of the genre, though Torii Kiyotada is said to have made the first one. Masanobu belonged to no school and had run his own shop since 1707.
