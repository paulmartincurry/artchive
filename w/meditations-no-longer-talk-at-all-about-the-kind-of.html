No longer talk at all about the kind of man that a good man ought to be, but be such.

Meditations — No longer talk at all about the kind of man (Book X, 16)

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180

Translated by George Long

The work has no official title, and Meditations is one of several titles commonly given to it. Its Greek name means Things Unto Himself, and it records the emperor's private notes to himself.
