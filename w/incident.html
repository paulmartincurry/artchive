I saw the whole of Baltimore
From May until December;
Of all the things that happened there
That’s all that I remember.

Incident

Countee Cullen · 1925

Incident was first published in Color, Cullen's first collection, in 1925. His birthplace has never been settled: Baltimore, New York City and Louisville have all been suggested, and he claimed New York while often naming Louisville on legal applications.
