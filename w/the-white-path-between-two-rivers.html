The White Path Between Two Rivers

Maker unknown · 1200s · hanging scroll; ink, color, gold, silver, and cut gold on silk · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The scroll sets out a teaching ascribed to the Chinese Buddhist monk Shandao, who died in 681, about a narrow path between a river of fire and a river of water. Four characters at the lower right give the names Jō-shin and Kyō-shin. The painted silk is about 125 centimetres tall and 51 wide. It came to Cleveland in 1955 through a dealer in the city, Howard Hollis and Co.
