Saint Catherine with a Kneeling Male Donor

Maker unknown · 1400s · pot metal, white glass with silver stain · 158.7 x 49.2 cm (62 1/2 x 19 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

An inscription names the donor as the rector of Melle, a parish in western France, and a scroll near him reads Miserere mei deus. The panel was part of a larger window; the scene he faced, probably the Crucifixion or the Virgin, is missing.
