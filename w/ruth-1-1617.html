whither thou goest, I will go; and where thou lodgest, I will lodge: thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God:

Ruth 1:16–17

Book of Ruth (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · c. 550–330 BCE; KJV 1611

Ruth, a Moabite, says these words to her Judean mother-in-law Naomi, who is going home to Bethlehem after both their husbands have died. Rabbinic sources derive several laws of Jewish conversion from this declaration. Ruth goes on to marry Boaz, and their son Obed becomes the grandfather of King David. The book is chanted in synagogues at the festival of Shavuot.
