Women marching with gags against the war, outside the White House

Thomas J. O'Halloran · 18 March 1970 · Photographic negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Thomas J. O'Halloran photographed the women's peace march at the White House and the Capitol for U.S. News & World Report. Most of the marchers wore a cloth tied over their mouths.
