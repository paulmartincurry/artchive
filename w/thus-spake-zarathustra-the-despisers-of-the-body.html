Behind thy thoughts and feelings, my brother, there is a mighty lord, an unknown sage—it is called Self; it dwelleth in thy body, it is thy body.

There is more sagacity in thy body than in thy best wisdom.

Thus Spake Zarathustra — The Despisers of the Body (First Part, IV)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883; English 1909

Translated by Thomas Common

This translation is Thomas Common's, published in 1909. In the mid-1890s he had joined a project led by Alexander Tille to produce a complete English edition of Nietzsche. From 1903 to 1916 he also put out a quarterly journal meant to raise interest in Nietzsche.
