Coffin of Bakenmut

Maker unknown · c. 1000–900 BCE · Gessoed and painted sycamore fig · 208 × 68 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The coffin is made of sycamore fig, gessoed and painted, and is 208 centimetres long. It comes from Thebes, probably from Deir el-Bahri. A smaller coffin was originally placed inside it, and the separately attached hands are now lost. Lucy Olcott Perkins bought it in Luxor from Joseph Hassan Ahmed, and it has been in Cleveland since 1914.
