Saturday Night in a Saloon, Craigville, Minnesota

Russell Lee · September 1937 · Safety film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Russell Lee joined the Farm Security Administration's photographers in the fall of 1936 and made this in Craigville, Minnesota, a year later. He was known for using direct flash indoors, in places other photographers of the time avoided.
