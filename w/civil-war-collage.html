Civil War Collage

Maker unknown · c. 1860/70 · Photocollage (albumen prints) · Image/ paper: 33.4 × 30.7 cm (13 3/16 × 12 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Around the Civil War, small cheap studio portraits called cartes de visite were traded, kept in albums and sometimes cut up and pasted into collages like this one. Its rows of faces over a cluster of family pictures read as a kind of family tree.
