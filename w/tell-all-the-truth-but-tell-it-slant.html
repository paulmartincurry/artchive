The Truth must dazzle gradually
Or every man be blind —

Tell all the truth but tell it slant (1263)

Emily Dickinson · ca. 1872

Emily Dickinson was about forty-one when she wrote this poem. Only 10 of her nearly 1,800 poems were published during her lifetime, and few people around her knew she was writing. She was born in Amherst, Massachusetts, in a house her grandfather built in 1813, and she died in the same town.
