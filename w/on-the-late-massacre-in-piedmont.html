Avenge O lord thy slaughter'd Saints, whose bones
Lie scatter'd on the Alpine mountains cold,

On the Late Massacre in Piedmont

John Milton · 1655

The order to attack the Waldensians of Piedmont was given on 24 April 1655 by the troops of Charles Emmanuel II, Duke of Savoy. An estimated 2,000 people were killed and another 2,000 were forced to convert. Milton was forty-six and a civil servant, Secretary for Foreign Tongues to the Council of State, a post he held from March 1649 to May 1660.
