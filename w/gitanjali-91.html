O thou the last fulfilment of life, Death, my death, come and whisper to me!

Gitanjali 91

Rabindranath Tagore · 1910; English 1912

In 1912 Tagore took a sheaf of his translations to England. He spent a few months that summer at 3 Villas on the Heath in Hampstead, where a blue plaque now marks the visit, and W. B. Yeats wrote the preface to the English Gitanjali.
