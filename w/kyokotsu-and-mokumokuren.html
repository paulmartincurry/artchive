Kyōkotsu and Mokumokuren (One Hundred Monsters Ancient and Modern)

Toriyama Sekien · 1781 · Woodblock printed book; ink on paper · 22.5 × 16 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kyōkotsu is a white-haired, skeletal ghost that rises from the bottom of a well; Mokumokuren is a swarm of eyes on the torn paper screens of old buildings, said to be Sekien's own invention. Both are in Rain, the last of the book's three volumes. Sekien was in his late sixties when it was published in 1781.
