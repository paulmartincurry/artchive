Admonitions Scroll

After Gu Kaizhi · 4th–7th Century · Handscroll painting; ink and colours on silk · British Museum

Free to use

The scroll carries no signature or seal of Gu Kaizhi, and the first writer to give it to him did so in 1103, about seven centuries after his death. It illustrates a poem of eighty lines written in 292 to reprimand an empress. Three of its twelve scenes are missing. One owner, the pawnbroker and collector Xiang Yuanbian, stamped it with about fifty seals. After the Boxer Rebellion an officer in the British Indian Army acquired it and sold it to the British Museum. It is 329 centimetres long and 25 high.
