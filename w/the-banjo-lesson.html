The Banjo Lesson

Henry Ossawa Tanner · 1893 · Oil on canvas · Hampton University Museum

Free to use

Henry Ossawa Tanner signed and dated the canvas 1893, probably in Philadelphia, during a visit home from Paris to recover from typhoid fever. A photograph he took served as a study, and a closely related image of his illustrated a Christmas story in Harper's Young People that December. He sent the painting ahead to Paris in the spring of 1894, and it became his first work accepted by the Salon. Robert Curtis Ogden bought it and gave it to the Hampton Institute that November.
