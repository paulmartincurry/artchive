Le Bonheur de vivre (The Joy of Life)

Henri Matisse · 1905–06 · Oil on canvas · 176.5 × 240.7 cm · Barnes Foundation, Philadelphia

Free to use

Matisse made many preparatory sketches and a full cartoon for this canvas, first shown at the Salon des Indépendants in 1906. Paul Signac, who had bought earlier works by Matisse, complained in writing about its outlines as thick as a thumb. The painting is rich in cadmium sulfide yellow, and those areas are turning white or brown as carbon dioxide reacts with the pigment. For many years the Barnes Foundation did not allow colour reproductions of it.
