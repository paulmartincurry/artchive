Whenas in silks my Julia goes,
Then, then, methinks, how sweetly flows
The liquefaction of her clothes!

Upon Julia's Clothes

Robert Herrick · 1648

Herrick had his lyric poems printed in 1648 under the title Hesperides. He wrote over 2,500 poems, and about half of them appear in that book. In 1629 he had become the vicar of Dean Prior in Devonshire.
