Vishnu Battles Madhu and Kaitabha (Markandeya Purana)

Maker unknown · c. 1760 · Gum tempera on paper · 16.7 × 25.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

According to the text, the two demons Vishnu fights here were generated from his earwax, just before the world was created. The small kingdoms of the western Himalayan foothills paid tribute to the Mughal emperor, and their rulers began to keep workshops of court painters in the 1600s, on the model of the imperial Mughal studio. Cleveland bought this page in 1974.
