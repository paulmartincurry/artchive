Diptych with Mary and Her Son Flanked by Archangels, Apostles and a Saint

Follower of Fre Seyon · Late 15th century · Tempera on wood · 22.5 × 19.8 cm and 25.6 × 19.7 cm (two panels) · Walters Art Museum, Baltimore

Free to use with credit. Photo: Walters Art Museum, CC BY-SA 3.0

Fre Seyon, a fifteenth-century painter, left only one signed work, and the Walters considers the painter of this diptych most likely one of his followers. The Christ Child touching his mother's chin was probably inspired by fifteenth-century Italian paintings then admired at the Ethiopian royal court. The two panels are painted in tempera on wood and stand 22.5 and 25.6 centimetres tall. The Walters bought the diptych in 2001 from the Nancy and Robert Nooter Collection.
