Inside the Bar

Winslow Homer · 1883 · Watercolor and graphite on off-white wove paper · 16 × 29 in. (40.6 × 73.7 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

By 1883 Homer had been working regularly in watercolour for ten years, since a summer in Gloucester, Massachusetts. The watercolours sold well and put his finances on a much better footing. He had begun showing works on paper often the year before. This sheet is 41 by 74 centimetres, and it came to the Metropolitan Museum as a gift in 1954.
