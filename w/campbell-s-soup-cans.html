Campbell's Soup Cans

Andy Warhol · 1962 · Acrylic on canvas, 32 panels · Museum of Modern Art, New York

These are not silkscreens. Warhol traced each can from a photograph and painted it by hand in commercial-grade acrylic at his home studio on Lexington Avenue, adding the gold fleur-de-lis with a rubber stamp. The thirty-two canvases, one for each variety of soup, went on show at the Ferus Gallery in Los Angeles on 9 July 1962, lined up in a single row on narrow ledges. Five or six sold. The dealer Irving Blum then asked the buyers to give theirs back so the set could stay together, and bought all thirty-two from Warhol for $1,000, paid at $100 a month.
