Flowers of a Hundred Worlds (Momoyogusa): Wisteria (Fuji)

Kamisaka Sekka · 1909–10 · Colour woodcut with gold and silver · 29.9 × 22.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Momoyogusa was commissioned by the Kyoto publisher Unsodo and issued between 1909 and 1910: sixty images in three volumes. The prints went one by one to subscribers first and were later reissued bound. This one is a colour woodcut with gold and silver. The title comes from an eighth-century poetry collection, the Man'yōshū, where it names a many-leaved autumn herb.
