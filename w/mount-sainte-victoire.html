Mount Sainte-Victoire

Paul Cézanne · c. 1904 · oil on fabric · Cleveland Museum of Art

Cézanne painted this from life, with his easel at the edge of a small road running east from Aix toward the mountain. He made 36 paintings and 45 watercolours of Mount Sainte-Victoire and kept at it until his death, about two years after this one. The canvas was still in his estate when Ambroise Vollard bought it in March 1907. Leonard C. Hanna Jr. bought it in New York in 1939 and left it to the Cleveland museum.
