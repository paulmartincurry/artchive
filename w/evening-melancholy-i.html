Evening, Melancholy I

Edvard Munch · 1896 · Hand-colored woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The man on the shore is the art critic Jappe Nilssen, a friend of Munch's, and the beach is at Åsgårdstrand, a fishing village south of Oslo. Munch chose heavily grained boards so that the wood would print, and cut them roughly. He coloured each sheet by hand in watercolour and gouache, so no two impressions match. This is one of only two known impressions taken before the block was cut into two sections.
