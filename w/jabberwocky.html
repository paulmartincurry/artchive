’Twas brillig, and the slithy toves
Did gyre and gimble in the wabe;

Jabberwocky

Lewis Carroll · 1871

The first stanza was printed in 1855 in Mischmasch, a periodical Carroll wrote and illustrated to amuse his family, under the title Stanza of Anglo-Saxon Poetry. He was twenty-three and had written it at Croft-on-Tees, where his parents lived. The rest was written during a stay with relatives at Whitburn, near Sunderland. In 1868 he asked his publishers whether a page or two could be printed in reverse, and Macmillan answered that it would cost a great deal more.
