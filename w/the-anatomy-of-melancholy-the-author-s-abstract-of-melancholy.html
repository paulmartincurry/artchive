The Anatomy of Melancholy — The Author's Abstract of Melancholy (first two stanzas)

Robert Burton · 1621

The Anatomy of Melancholy was first published in 1621 and republished five more times over the next seventeen years, with massive alterations and expansions. Burton wrote it under the pseudonym Democritus Junior.
