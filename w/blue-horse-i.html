Blue Horse I (Blaues Pferd I)

Franz Marc · 1911 · Oil on canvas · Lenbachhaus, Munich

The composition began as a pencil drawing about the size of a postcard in one of Marc's sketchbooks, which he worked up the same year into a canvas 112 centimetres tall. The painting was derided at first, and even spat upon. It belonged to Bernhard Koehler, who supported Marc with money and took paintings in return. The Lenbachhaus has shown it since 1965.
