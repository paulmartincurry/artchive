The Sick Child I

Edvard Munch · 1896 · Transfer lithograph printed from two stones in pale blue and black ink on ivory wove paper · Image: 42 × 57.2 cm (16 9/16 × 22 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch's sister Sophie died of tuberculosis in 1877, at fifteen. Nine years later he painted the first of five versions of her on her sickbed, and when it was shown in Oslo in 1886 a group of young Realist painters surrounded him and called him a madman. This print was his first colour lithograph, made in Paris in 1896 with the printer Auguste Clot. He printed the image in many colour combinations, with up to four colour stones; this impression uses two, in pale blue and black.
