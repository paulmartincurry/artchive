Borne away by the waves,
and lost in darkness and distance.

Frankenstein (Walton's final letter)

Mary Shelley · 1818

Mary Shelley was eighteen in the summer of 1816, when cold weather kept her party indoors at Lord Byron's villa on Lake Geneva and Byron proposed that each of them write a ghost story. She began what she assumed would be a short story, and wrote much of the book in a lodging house in Bath that year. The first edition, 500 copies in three volumes, was published anonymously on 1 January 1818, when she was twenty. Her name first appeared on a French translation in 1821.
