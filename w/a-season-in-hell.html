One evening I sat Beauty on my knees – And I found her bitter – And I reviled her.

A Season in Hell (Une Saison en Enfer)

Arthur Rimbaud · 1873

Translated by A. S. Kline · © A. S. Kline; free to reproduce for non-commercial purposes (poetryintranslation.com terms).

Rimbaud began this work in April 1873, at eighteen, on his family's farm at Roche, near Charleville. That July in Brussels, when he said he planned to leave, Paul Verlaine fired two shots from a revolver and wounded him once; Verlaine was given two years of hard labour. Rimbaud went home to Roche and finished the work in a farmhouse there. It is the only work he published himself.
