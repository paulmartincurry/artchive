Lu Zhishen, Originally Named Lu Da (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827 · Color woodblock print; oban · 38.9 × 26.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1827 Kuniyoshi got his first major commission: a set of single-sheet prints of the heroes of the Chinese tale Shuihu Zhuan. He gave the heroes tattoos, a novelty that soon spread into Edo fashion. The series made his name, and demand for his warrior prints grew.
