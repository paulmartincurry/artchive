Judith Beheading Holofernes

Caravaggio · c. 1599–1602 · Oil on canvas · 145 × 195 cm · Galleria Nazionale d'Arte Antica, Palazzo Barberini, Rome

Free to use

According to the painter and biographer Giovanni Baglione, the Genoese banker Ottavio Costa commissioned this canvas. Judith was probably modelled by the Roman courtesan Fillide Melandroni, who sat for Caravaggio several times around then. X-rays show he moved the head of Holofernes as he worked, setting it slightly apart from the body. The picture was rediscovered in 1950.
