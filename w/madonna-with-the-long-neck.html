Madonna with the Long Neck

Parmigianino · 1534–1540 · Oil on panel · Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

Parmigianino began the panel in 1534 for the funerary chapel of Francesco Tagliaferri in Parma, and it was still incomplete when he died in 1540. Ferdinando de' Medici, Grand Prince of Tuscany, bought it in 1698. It has been on display at the Uffizi since 1948.
