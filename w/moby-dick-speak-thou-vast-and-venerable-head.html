O head! thou hast seen enough to split the planets and make an infidel of Abraham, and not one syllable is thine!

Moby-Dick — Speak, thou vast and venerable head (Ch. 70, The Sphynx)

Herman Melville · 1851

Melville signed on for his own whaling voyage aboard the Acushnet, a new ship of slightly less than 360 tons, after reading a magazine account of the hunt for a white sperm whale called Mocha Dick. In August 1851, months before the novel came out, the whaler Ann Alexander was holed and sunk by a whale off the Galápagos Islands, the second vessel since the Essex to go down that way. Melville remarked that he wondered whether his evil art had raised the monster.
