Roseate Spoonbill (The Birds of America, Plate 321)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1836 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · 67.3 × 100.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The complete Birds of America has 435 plates, and the prints cost each subscriber around $1,000. It is thought that no more than 120 complete sets exist today. This is plate 321, dated 1836.
