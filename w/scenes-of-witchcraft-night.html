Scenes of Witchcraft: Night

Salvator Rosa · c. 1645–49 · Oil on canvas · Diameter 54.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is one of four round canvases by Salvator Rosa showing witchcraft at different times of day, and it is 54.5 centimetres across. The museum says Rosa chose the round shape for the circle's part in the practice of magic. The paintings came from the family of the Marchese Giovanni Niccolini in Florence, and Cleveland bought them in 1977.
