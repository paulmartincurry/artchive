Chartres Cathedral, West Front

Maker unknown · Royal Portal 12th century; cathedral rebuilt 1194–1220 · Limestone

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jörg Bittner Unna, CC BY-SA 3.0

When fire swept through the cathedral on the night of 10 June 1194, only the crypt, the two towers and this west front survived, and they were built into the new church; by 1220 its roof was in place. Most of the Royal Portal sculpture was carved before it went up and was set into the walls as the south tower rose. The taller north spire, 113 metres, was added between 1507 and 1513 by the architect Jean Texier, to bring that tower closer to its 105-metre neighbour.
