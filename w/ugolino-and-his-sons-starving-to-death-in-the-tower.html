Ugolino and His Sons Starving to Death in the Tower

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1806 · Pen and black ink and brush and black, gray, and red wash, over traces of graphite, on grayish-ivory laid paper · 63.9 × 52.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Fuseli was about sixty-five in 1806 and Keeper of the Royal Academy, a post he had been given three years before. The drawing is in pen and black ink with black, grey and red washes, on a sheet about 64 centimetres high. William F. E. Gurley of Chicago bought it at auction in London in October 1914 and gave it to the Art Institute in 1922.
