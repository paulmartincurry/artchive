Three Beauties of the Present Day

Kitagawa Utamaro · ca. 1792–1793 · Woodblock print (nishiki-e); ink and color on paper · Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

The three women were not courtesans but a geisha and two teahouse waitresses known around Edo, and by tradition the waitresses were fifteen and sixteen. A separate woodblock was cut for each colour, and the background was dusted with a kind of mica so that it glitters. This first printing names all three in the top right corner, and only two copies of that state are believed to survive. Later printings dropped the title, the names, or both.
