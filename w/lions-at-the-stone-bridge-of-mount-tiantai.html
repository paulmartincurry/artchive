Lions at the Stone Bridge of Mount Tiantai

Soga Shōhaku · 1779 · Hanging scroll; ink on silk · Image 114 × 50.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Shōhaku painted this scroll in 1779, two years before his death. He was born into a wealthy merchant family, lost all his immediate family before he was eighteen and became a painter in his late twenties. He painted mostly in monochrome, in a brush style of the Muromachi period that was already passé 150 years before his birth.
