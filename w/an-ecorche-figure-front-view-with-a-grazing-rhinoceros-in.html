An Écorché Figure, Front View, with a Grazing Rhinoceros in the Background

Bernhard Siegfried Albinus & Jan Wandelaar · 1742 · Line engraving · 56.2 × 40 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

The rhinoceros is Clara, who reached the Netherlands in 1741. Albinus wrote in his text that she was two and a half years old by her keepers' account, and that so rare a beast would please more than any ornament he might invent. Jan Wandelaar drew the figure from actual bodies and engraved the plate himself, in 1742, five years before the full atlas was published in Leiden.
