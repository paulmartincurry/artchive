Let me not to the marriage of true minds
Admit impediments. Love is not love
Which alters when it alteration finds,

Sonnet 116

William Shakespeare · 1609

In the quarto where it was first published, in 1609, this sonnet was mis-numbered as 119. In 2025 Leah Veronese, a PhD student at Oxford University, found another version in a manuscript that had belonged to Elias Ashmole, a strong supporter of the monarchy during the English Civil War. That version has seven additional lines and a rewritten first quatrain, and it sits in a collection of militant writings.
