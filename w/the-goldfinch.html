The Goldfinch

Carel Fabritius · 1654 · Oil on panel · Mauritshuis, The Hague

Free to use

Carel Fabritius signed and dated the panel 1654. It is possible that it was in his workshop in Delft at the time of the gunpowder explosion that killed him. The bird is life-sized, and the panel was once part of a larger structure, perhaps a window jamb or a protective cover. The painting was unknown for more than two centuries before it came to light in 1859 in Brussels; the Mauritshuis has had it since 1896.
