The Phantom of the Opera, frame at 1:29:36

Rupert Julian · 1925 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Lon Chaney devised his own make-up, and the studio kept it secret until the premiere. He stuffed wadding in his cheeks, wore a skullcap to raise his forehead, glued back his ears and pulled his nostrils up with hidden wires, which sometimes made him bleed. The Paris Opera set was built on Stage 28 at Universal, the first soundstage made with steel girders set in concrete, to carry hundreds of extras. Parts of the set stood there until the stage was demolished in 2014.
