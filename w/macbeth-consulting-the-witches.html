Macbeth Consulting the Witches

Eugène Delacroix · 1825 · Lithograph · Image: 32.2 × 25.2 cm (12 11/16 × 9 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Delacroix was twenty-seven in 1825, the year he travelled to England and was making lithographs from Shakespeare. For this one he covered the stone with black lithographic crayon and then scratched the design out of it with a sharp tool. This impression is the third of five states.
