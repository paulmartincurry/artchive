We are the Dead. Short days ago
We lived, felt dawn, saw sunset glow,
Loved and were loved, and now we lie,
In Flanders fields.

In Flanders Fields

John McCrae · 1915

John McCrae, a Canadian physician who had enlisted at forty-one, wrote the poem on 3 May 1915, sitting in the back of an ambulance outside Ypres. The day before, he had performed the burial service for his friend Alexis Helmer. According to legend he discarded the draft and a fellow soldier retrieved it. The Spectator rejected the poem, and Punch printed it anonymously on 8 December 1915.
