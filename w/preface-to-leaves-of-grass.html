Preface to Leaves of Grass (1855)

Walt Whitman · 1855

This prose preface ran to 827 lines and opened the first edition of Leaves of Grass, ahead of twelve untitled poems. Whitman paid for the book himself and had it printed at a local shop during the employees' breaks from commercial jobs. 795 copies were made. No author was named on it; an engraved portrait faced the title page instead.
