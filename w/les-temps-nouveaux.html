Les Temps Nouveaux

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1906 · Lithograph in black on cream wove paper · Image: 54.7 × 37 cm; Sheet: 68 × 50.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Les Temps Nouveaux was an anarchist periodical edited by Jean Grave from 1895 to 1922, and it was read in literary and artistic circles. In 1893 Grave had been sentenced to two years in prison for a book prefaced by Octave Mirbeau. Steinlen produced hundreds of illustrations, some under a pseudonym to avoid political trouble.
