Kali

Eastern India, Bengal, Kolkata, Kalighat · ca. 1890 · Gum tempera, graphite, and ink on paper · Painting only: 45.4 × 28 cm (17 7/8 × 11 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting was made in the Kalighat district of Kolkata, on a sheet of paper about forty-five centimetres tall. William E. Ward of Solon, Ohio, gave it to the Cleveland Museum of Art in 2003 in memory of his wife, Evelyn Svec Ward.
