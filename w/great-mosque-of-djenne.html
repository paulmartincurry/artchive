Great Mosque of Djenné

Rebuilt under Ismaila Traoré · 13th-Century Site / Rebuilt 1907 by Guild Master Ismaila Traoré · Adobe

The present building was finished in 1907, raised by forced labour under Ismaila Traoré, head of Djenné's guild of masons. Its walls are sun-baked earth bricks under a coat of plaster, and the bundles of palm sticks that project about 60 centimetres from them serve as scaffolding for the yearly repairs. The whole stands on a platform three metres above the marketplace, clear of the floods of the Bani River. The central tower is about 16 metres high, and the spires are topped with ostrich eggs.
