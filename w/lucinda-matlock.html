Degenerate sons and daughters,
Life is too strong for you—
It takes life to love Life.

Lucinda Matlock

Edgar Lee Masters · 1915

Masters was a Chicago lawyer in his mid-forties in 1915, running the firm he had started in 1911 after eight years as a partner of the trial lawyer Clarence Darrow. In 1914 he began a series of poems about his childhood in western Illinois, printed in Reedy's Mirror of St. Louis under the pseudonym Webster Ford. The series was bound into a volume in 1915 as Spoon River Anthology.
