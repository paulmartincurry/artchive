Wild Turkey (The Birds of America, Plate 1)

John James Audubon & William Home Lizars · 1827 · Hand-colored copperplate etching, engraving and aquatint · Boston Public Library

Free to use

Audubon posed dead birds with wires and threads and drew them life-size in watercolour and pastel. He sailed to Britain in 1826 with 250 drawings to find an engraver and subscribers. William Lizars of Edinburgh engraved the first ten plates, then stopped when his colourists went on strike, and in 1827 the work passed to Robert Havell Jr. in London, who finished some of those ten and saw the series through to 1838. The sheets are 39.5 inches tall, and more than fifty colourists were hired to paint them by hand.
