The Misses Grierson

David Octavius Hill and Robert Adamson · c. 1845 · Salted paper print · 19.9 × 14 cm (7 7/8 × 5 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The sitters are the daughters of the Reverend James Grierson of Errol. The exposure was long, so the pose had to be one they could hold. Hill was about forty-three and Adamson about twenty-four; the two had been introduced in 1843 by the physicist Sir David Brewster. The mount carries the stamp of the Royal Scottish Academy, where Hill was the salaried secretary.
