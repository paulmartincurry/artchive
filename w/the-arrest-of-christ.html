The Arrest of Christ (Kiss of Judas)

Giotto di Bondone · c. 1304–1306 · Fresco · Scrovegni Chapel, Padua

Free to use

This is one of the scenes from the life of Christ with which Giotto and his team covered the inside of the Scrovegni Chapel in Padua, a cycle completed around 1305. The banker Enrico Scrovegni built the chapel as his family's private oratory and as a funerary monument for himself and his wife. It is also called the Arena Chapel because it stands on land beside the site of a Roman arena.
