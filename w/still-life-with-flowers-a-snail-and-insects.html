Still Life with Flowers, a Snail and Insects

Joris Hoefnagel · 1589 · Watercolor, gouache, and shell gold on vellum · 11.7 × 9.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sheet is vellum, less than twelve centimetres tall, painted in watercolour and gouache with shell gold. Joris Hoefnagel was a diamond dealer's son from Antwerp who left the city after Spanish troops sacked it in 1576 and took a post at the ducal court in Munich. He painted this in 1589. Two years later, as a Calvinist, he was forced to leave Munich when the court was required to profess the Catholic faith.
