Ghent Altarpiece

Hubert and Jan van Eyck · 1432 · Oil on panel · Saint Bavo's Cathedral, Ghent

Free to use

Jodocus Vijd, a merchant and mayor of Ghent, and his wife Lysbette paid for the altarpiece, and it was installed on 6 May 1432. Hubert van Eyck is generally thought to have designed it and his younger brother Jan to have painted the panels. It measures 375 by 520 centimetres. The wings were pawned in 1815 for the equivalent of 240 pounds and sold on to Berlin. One panel, the Just Judges, was stolen in 1934 and is still missing. In 1942 Hitler had the altarpiece seized, and it was later stored in a salt mine, which damaged the paint and varnish.
