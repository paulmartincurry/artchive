Manhattan Bridge, from Bowery and Canal Street

Berenice Abbott · 11 November 1936 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott photographed the bridge on 11 November 1936. Leon Moisseiff designed it, and it opened to traffic on the last day of 1909; it was the first suspension bridge to use a Warren truss. Its main span runs 1,480 feet between two towers 350 feet high.
