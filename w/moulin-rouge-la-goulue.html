Moulin Rouge: La Goulue

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1891 · Lithograph printed in four colors; three sheets of wove paper · sheet: 74 13/16 × 45 7/8 in. (190 × 116.5 cm) · The Met

Free to use

This was the first lithograph Toulouse-Lautrec ever made. The Moulin Rouge had opened in 1889, and its director, Charles Zidler, already had a poster by Jules Chéret before he hired Toulouse-Lautrec for a new one. About 3,000 copies were printed and pasted up around Paris, and most of them were lost. Each is printed across three sheets of paper and stands 190 centimetres tall.
