Fenggan, Hanshan and Shide

Ueno Jakugen · 18th century · Triptych of hanging scrolls, color on silk

Hanshan and Shide were poet-recluses of Tang dynasty China, remembered as ragged, laughing eccentrics who worked in a monastery kitchen. Fenggan was the monk who looked after them and is usually shown with a tiger. Zen painters in China and Japan returned to the three of them for centuries.
