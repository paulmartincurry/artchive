I took a deep breath and listened to the old brag of my heart.

The Bell Jar

Sylvia Plath · 1963

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Plath probably began the novel in 1961 and may have finished it that August. The fellowship that sponsored the writing was disappointed by the manuscript and withdrew. It came out in Britain on 14 January 1963 under the pseudonym Victoria Lucas, and Plath died by suicide a month later, aged thirty. It first appeared under her own name in 1966. At the wish of her husband and her mother it was not published in the United States until 1971.
