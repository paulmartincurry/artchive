Bahram Gur in the Dark Palace on Saturday (Haft Paykar, Khamsa of Nizami)

Shaikh Zada · 1524–25 (dated 931 AH) · Ink, opaque watercolor, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 20.3 × 11.4 cm; page 31.9 × 22.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In Nizami's poem, probably completed in August 1197, Bahram Gur builds a palace with seven domes, one for each day of the week, each with its own colour. This is the Dark Palace of Saturday, on folio 207 of a copy made in Herat in 1524–25, painted by Shaikh Zada.
