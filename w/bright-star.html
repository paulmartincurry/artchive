Bright star, would I were stedfast as thou art,
Not in lone splendour hung aloft the night

Bright Star

John Keats · c. 1819 (published 1838)

No one knows when Keats first drafted this sonnet, and his biographers give dates from April 1818 to October 1819. He copied the final version into a volume of Shakespeare's poems, opposite A Lover's Complaint. Joseph Severn said this was done in late September 1820, on board the Maria Crowther on the way to Rome, where Keats died of tuberculosis five months later, aged twenty-five. The poem was first printed in 1838 in The Plymouth and Devonport Weekly Journal.
