Barge Haulers on the Volga

Ilya Repin · 1870–1873 · Oil on canvas · Russian Museum

Free to use

Repin saw the haulers while on holiday on the Volga in 1870 and made his studies, mostly in oil, at a village on the river. The eleven men are people he came to know there, among them a former soldier, a painter and a defrocked priest named Kanin, who leads the line. Sitters were hard to find even for pay, because of a belief that the soul left the body once its likeness was set down. He kept reworking the canvas until 1873. Grand Duke Vladimir Alexandrovich, the Tsar's second son, bought it, and his collection was nationalized after the Revolution.
