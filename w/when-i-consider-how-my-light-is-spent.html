Thousands at his bidding speed
And post o're Land and Ocean without rest:
They also serve who only stand and waite.

When I Consider How My Light Is Spent (On His Blindness)

John Milton · 1673

Milton numbered this sonnet 19 in his notebook, the Trinity Manuscript, but it was printed as number 16, so it goes by both. The title On His Blindness is not his: Thomas Newton gave it in a 1761 edition. Most scholars date the poem to between June and October 1655, when his blindness was essentially complete, and the notebook copy is in a scribe's hand, not his own.
