At Last a Perfect Soldier!

Robert Minor · July 1916 · Cartoon

Free to use

Robert Minor drew it for the July 1916 issue of The Masses, the socialist monthly from Greenwich Village. After the United States entered the First World War, cartoons like his were treated as treasonous, and the Espionage Act cost the magazine its mailing rights.
