Songoku and Jewel Hare (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–90 · Woodcut, color · 32 × 21.8 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Sun Wukong, the monkey hero, is shown fighting the rabbit that lives in the Moon. The Library of Congress copy has been trimmed to 32 by 21.8 centimetres. Its catalogue entry was written in 2005 and 2006 by Edo print specialists working with Nichibunken.
