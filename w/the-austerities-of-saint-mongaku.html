Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885 · Color woodblock print · 25 × 35.5 cm (vertical diptych, as the holder gives it) · Art Institute of Chicago

Yoshitoshi published this vertical diptych in 1885, the same year as The Lonely House on Adachi Moor. Mr. and Mrs. Eric Youngquist gave the print to the Art Institute of Chicago.
