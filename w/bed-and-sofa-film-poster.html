Bed and Sofa, film poster

Vladimir and Georgii Stenberg · 1927 · Poster (lithograph)

Free to use

Abram Room's 1927 film began as a plot the screenwriter Viktor Shklovsky told him over lunch in a studio cafeteria; Shklovsky had found the story in the newspaper Komsomolskaya Pravda. Room wrote the script on the express train from Moscow to Sevastopol. The Stenbergs designed the poster. From 1928 they also decorated Red Square for the state celebrations, work that Vladimir said took six months of every year.
