Pierrot Laughing

Nadar and Adrien Tournachon · 1855 · Gelatin-coated salted paper print (vernis-cuir) · 27.3 × 19.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The museum credits this photograph to two brothers, Nadar and Adrien Tournachon, and names the sitter as Jean-Charles Deburau. A friend had encouraged Nadar to begin a portrait business in 1854; busy with caricature, he handed its running to his younger brother Adrien, who took it over completely and soon ran into debt. The print is 27.3 by 19.8 centimetres.
