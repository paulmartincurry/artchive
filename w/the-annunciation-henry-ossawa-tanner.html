The Annunciation

Henry Ossawa Tanner · 1898 · Oil on canvas · Philadelphia Museum of Art

Free to use

In 1897 Tanner travelled to Egypt and Palestine on a trip paid for by Rodman Wanamaker. He intended this picture for the Paris Salon of 1898 and chose the largest canvas of his career. He was not satisfied with a first version and began a new one. It has been suggested that Jessie Olssen, later his wife, was the model for Mary, but he likely did not meet her until after the Salon.
