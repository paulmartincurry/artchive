The Massacre at Chios

Eugène Delacroix · 1824 · Oil on canvas · 419 × 354 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

The canvas is more than four metres tall. Delacroix committed himself to the subject in May 1823, the year after the killings on Chios. At the Salon of 1824 it hung in the same room as Ingres's The Vow of Louis XIII, and the public rivalry between the two painters began there. Alexandre Dumas and Stendhal both took it for a picture of a plague.
