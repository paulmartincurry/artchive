The Flaying of Marsyas

Titian · c. 1570–1576 · Oil on canvas · Archbishop's Palace, Kroměříž

Free to use

Titian probably painted the canvas between about 1570 and his death in 1576, when he was in his eighties, and it may be unfinished. Nothing is known of its history before it appears in an inventory of 1655. In 1673 the Bishop of Olomouc acquired it after it had been a prize in a lottery, and it has been at Kroměříž in Moravia ever since.
