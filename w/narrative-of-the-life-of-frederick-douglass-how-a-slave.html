You have seen how a man was made a slave; you shall see how a slave was made a man.

Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass — How a slave was made a man (Ch. X)

Frederick Douglass · 1845

Douglass wrote the book in Lynn, Massachusetts, seven years after his escape from slavery in Maryland, and it was published on May 1, 1845, when he was twenty-seven. Five thousand copies sold within four months. Afraid of being recaptured by his owner, he sailed for England and Ireland and stayed two years; supporters there paid $710.96 to buy his freedom.
