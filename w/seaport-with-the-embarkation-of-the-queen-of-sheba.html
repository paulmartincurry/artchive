Seaport with the Embarkation of the Queen of Sheba

Claude Lorrain · 1648 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London

Free to use

Claude signed and dated the canvas in 1648. The Duc de Bouillon, general of the papal army, commissioned it together with its companion, Landscape with the Marriage of Isaac and Rebecca. In 1824 it was among the first works the National Gallery acquired, one of five Claudes bought from the collection of John Julius Angerstein; Turner later left two of his own paintings to the nation on condition that they hang beside this pair.
