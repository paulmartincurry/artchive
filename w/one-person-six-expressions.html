One Person, Six Expressions

Kobayashi Kiyochika · 1884 · Color woodblock print; oban · 35.9 × 24.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kiyochika published this sheet of six faces in 1884, the year he married his third wife, Tajima Yoshiko. By then he was drawing caricatures for the satirical magazine Marumaru Chinbun, which ran his work in every issue from August 1882; those caricatures are probably his best-remembered work.
