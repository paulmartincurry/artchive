…suddenly there fell upon me without any warning, just as if it came out of the darkness, a horrible fear of my own existence.

The Varieties of Religious Experience — The Sick Soul (Lectures VI and VII)

William James · 1902

James presents this account as a letter from an anonymous sufferer, but the source is in fact autobiographical. As a young man he went through periods of depression during which he contemplated suicide for months on end.
