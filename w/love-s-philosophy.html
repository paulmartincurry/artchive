Nothing in the world is single;
All things by a law divine
In one spirit meet and mingle.
Why not I with thine?

Love's Philosophy

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1819

Leigh Hunt printed the poem in his paper The Indicator on 22 December 1819. In one manuscript book Shelley headed it An Anacreontic, the name for verse in the manner of the Greek poet Anacreon. It has two stanzas of eight lines each. Mary Shelley reprinted it in Posthumous Poems in 1824, two years after her husband died in a boating accident at twenty-nine.
