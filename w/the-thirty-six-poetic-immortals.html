The Thirty-Six Poetic Immortals

Studio of Kano Takanobu · early 17th century · Pair of six-panel folding screens; ink, color, gold, and silver on paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Each of the two screens has six panels and a painted surface about 165 centimetres high and 360 wide. They come from the studio of Kano Takanobu, who was born in 1571 and died in 1618. The Met bought the pair in 2024 with a gift from Mary and Cheney Cowles.
