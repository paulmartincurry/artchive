Venise sauvée (Venice Preserved)

Jan Toorop · 1895 · Lithograph on laid paper (proof before letters) · Image: 44.5 × 28 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Toorop made this lithograph in 1895 for the Théâtre de l'Œuvre, and the image carries the theatre's address, 23 rue Turgot. This impression is a proof before letters on Arches paper, inscribed by a later hand as number 3. His name is lettered in reverse at the lower left.
