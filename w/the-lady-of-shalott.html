The Lady of Shalott

John William Waterhouse · 1888 · Oil on canvas · Tate

Free to use

The canvas is 183 centimetres high and 230 wide. John William Waterhouse returned to Tennyson's poem twice more, painting other episodes from it in 1894 and 1915. Sir Henry Tate gave this picture to the public in 1894, six years after it was painted.
