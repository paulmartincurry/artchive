The Mountain Nymph Sweet Liberty

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1866 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 36.1 × 28.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Julia Margaret Cameron took up photography at the age of 48, after her daughter gave her a camera as a present. By her own account she turned her coal-house into a darkroom and a glazed fowl house into her studio. This print is 36.1 by 28.6 centimetres.
