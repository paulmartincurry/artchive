Mutus Liber, Plate 3: Concentric Circles of Creation

Isaac Baulot (Altus) · published 1702 (first published 1677) · Engraving · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

A third edition of the book was printed in Paris around 1760 and is extremely rare. Interest faded and then returned in the second half of the nineteenth century, with new editions in 1867, 1914 and 1943. This is plate 3 of the 1702 Geneva printing.
