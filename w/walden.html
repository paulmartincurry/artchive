I went to the woods because I wished
to live deliberately.

Walden (Ch. 2)

Henry David Thoreau · 1854

The house in the woods measured 10 feet by 15, was shingled and plastered, and cost Thoreau $28.12 to build. It stood on a woodlot owned by Ralph Waldo Emerson, who let him build and plant a garden in exchange for clearing land and doing other chores. Thoreau moved in on 4 July 1845. In this chapter he recalls the places he considered first, among them the Hollowell farm, which the owner's wife decided to keep.
