Angel of the Revelation

William Blake · ca. 1803–5 · Watercolor, pen and black ink, brush and wash, over traces of graphite · Sheet: 15 7/16 × 10 1/4 in. (39.2 × 26 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The subject is chapter 10 of the Book of Revelation, drawn on a sheet 39 centimetres high. William Blake was in his mid-forties. He had left school at ten and served seven years as an engraver's apprentice, and engraving was his trade from the age of twenty-one. The Metropolitan Museum bought the drawing in 1914.
