Antaeus Setting Down Dante and Virgil in the Last Circle of Hell

William Blake · 1824–27 · Pen and ink and watercolour · National Gallery of Victoria, Melbourne

Free to use

John Linnell commissioned this watercolour and it stayed with his family until Herbert Linnell sold it at Christie's in London on 15 March 1918. It was bought there for the Felton Bequest, on the advice of Robert Ross, and reached Melbourne in 1920.
