But, for Many Minutes, the Heart Beat On with a Muffled Sound (The Tell-Tale Heart)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

The Tell-Tale Heart was first published in James Russell Lowell's magazine The Pioneer in January 1843. Clarke was the son of a Dublin church decorator whose business later added a stained glass division, and he won the gold medal for stained glass at the South Kensington National Competitions of 1911, 1912 and 1913.
