The Lovers' Whirlwind, Francesca da Rimini and Paolo Malatesta

William Blake · 1824–27 · Pen and ink and watercolour · 37.4 × 53 cm · Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery

Free to use

The Dante commission came to Blake through John Linnell and was meant to end in a set of engravings. Blake's death in 1827 cut it short, with only seven of the engravings brought as far as proofs. He worked on the series on the day he died, 12 August. The sheet measures 374 by 530 millimetres.
