Self-Portrait as the Apostle Paul

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1661 · Oil on canvas · 91 × 77 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Rembrandt signed and dated this canvas in 1661. He had first painted Saint Paul in 1627, thirty-four years earlier, with a long beard and a receding hairline. Ernst van de Wetering has speculated that Rembrandt based the likeness on earlier self-portraits rather than looking in a mirror. It came to the Rijksmuseum as a bequest from Mr and Mrs De Bruijn-van der Leeuw of Muri, Switzerland.
