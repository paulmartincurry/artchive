The Flagellation of Christ

Piero della Francesca · c. 1459–1460 · Tempera on panel · Galleria Nazionale delle Marche, Urbino

Free to use

Piero signed the panel below the seated emperor, naming himself after his home town, Borgo San Sepolcro. Originally it had a frame inscribed with a Latin phrase from the second Psalm. The three men in the foreground have been identified in widely differing ways; an interpretation still upheld in Urbino sees them as Oddantonio da Montefeltro and two advisers, murdered together on 22 July 1444.
