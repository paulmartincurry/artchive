Dervish with a Lion (Shah Jahan Album)

Padarath · c. 1630 (calligraphy on reverse c. 1500) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · 38.9 × 25.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Padarath painted this dervish around 1630, for the album Jahangir had begun around 1620 and Shah Jahan continued; the calligraphy on the other side is by Sultan 'Ali al-Mashhadi, about 1500. The album was meant for a private audience, likely the royal family and close friends. Its last Mughal owner, Aurangzeb, added only an impression of his seal.
