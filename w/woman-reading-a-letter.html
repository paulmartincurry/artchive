Woman Reading a Letter

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1663 · Oil on canvas · 46.5 × 39 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The city of Amsterdam has owned the painting since the Van der Hoop bequest of 1854, and it was the first Vermeer in the Rijksmuseum when the museum opened in 1885. At a sale in 1772 it was catalogued under the name Van der Neer and went to a dealer for 40 guilders. The map on the wall was published in 1620. No corner, floor or ceiling is visible, which is true of no other interior by Vermeer.
