Till rising and gliding out I wander’d off by myself,
In the mystical moist night-air, and from time to time,
Look’d up in perfect silence at the stars.

When I Heard the Learn'd Astronomer

Walt Whitman · 1865

During the American Civil War Whitman went to Washington, D.C., and worked in hospitals caring for the wounded. He also worked as a government clerk. Leaves of Grass, which he had published himself in 1855 as a book of twelve poems, held more than 400 by his death in 1892.
