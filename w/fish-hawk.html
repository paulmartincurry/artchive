Fish Hawk (The Birds of America, Plate 81)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1830 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · 100 × 66.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

As early as 1807 Audubon had worked out a way of holding dead birds in lifelike poses with wires and threads while he drew them. He drew mainly in watercolour and pastel crayon, not oil. Robert Havell of London engraved, printed and hand-coloured the plates, and more than fifty colourists were hired for the work.
