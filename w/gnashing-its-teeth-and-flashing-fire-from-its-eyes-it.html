Gnashing Its Teeth, and Flashing Fire from Its Eyes, It Flew upon the Body of the Girl (The Murders in the Rue Morgue)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Poe first called this story The Murders in the Rue Trianon-Bas. It appeared in Graham's Magazine in April 1841, while he was working as an editor, and he was paid an additional 56 dollars for it, an unusually high figure; The Raven brought him only 9 dollars four years later. Clarke met the artist and teacher Margaret Crilley at the art school in Dublin and married her on 31 October 1914.
