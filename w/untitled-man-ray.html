Untitled (Champs Délicieux)

Man Ray · 1922 · Gelatin silver print, from the album Champs Délicieux (Delicious Fields) · 22.9 × 17.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A rayograph is made without a camera, by laying objects straight onto light-sensitive paper and exposing it. Tristan Tzara showed Man Ray the cameraless pictures of Christian Schad in January 1922, six months after Man Ray had moved to Paris. That year he issued Champs Délicieux, a portfolio of twelve photographs made from his rayographs, in an edition of 40 with a preface by Tzara; the title puns on the Elysian Fields. This print probably passed from the artist to the New York dealer Julien Levy.
