Father, Have Pity on Me

Arapaho Ghost Dance song · 1890 (printed 1896)

Translated by James Mooney, 1896

James Mooney, who studied the Ghost Dance, wrote that this song was sung to a plaintive tune, sometimes with tears rolling down the dancers' cheeks. The Ghost Dance was first danced by the Northern Paiute of Nevada in 1889, following the teachings of Wovoka, who held that the dance would reunite the living with the spirits of the dead.
