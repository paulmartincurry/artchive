Gable Figure (Tekoteko)

Maker unknown · Mid-18th century · Wood, shell (pāua) · 109.2 × 9.2 × 11.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A tekoteko is a carved human figure set as a finial above the gable of a meeting house or a food storehouse, usually standing over a carved head where the bargeboards meet. This one, carved in the mid-eighteenth century, is 109.2 centimetres tall and inlaid with pāua shell. Before modern paints, Māori carvings were often coloured with red ochre mixed with shark-liver oil. The Met bought the figure in 2022.
