Sidney Sime · published 1912 · Plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

The Book of Wonder was made the other way round: Sidney Sime drew the pictures first, and Lord Dunsany wrote the stories to fit them. Twelve of its fourteen tales came from Sime drawings. Heinemann published it in November 1912. The originals of all Sime's work for Dunsany are kept at Dunsany Castle in County Meath.
