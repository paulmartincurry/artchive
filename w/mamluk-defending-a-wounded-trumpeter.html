Mamluk Defending a Wounded Trumpeter

Théodore Géricault · 1818 · Lithograph · Plate: 34.2 × 27.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Géricault was twenty-six or twenty-seven when he made this lithograph in 1818. He undertook a series of lithographs on military subjects after his return from Italy. The plate is about 34 centimetres by 28, and Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer bequeathed this impression to the Metropolitan Museum in 1929.
