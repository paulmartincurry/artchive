Ghost Dance (The Vision of Life)

Ralph Albert Blakelock · 1895–97 · Oil on canvas · 53.7 × 100 cm (21 1/8 × 39 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Blakelock painted the dancers from accounts in newspapers and magazines. He had travelled alone through the American West from 1869 to 1872 and had not been back in more than twenty years. He had his first mental breakdown in 1891 and his last in 1899, two years after finishing this canvas, and he spent nearly all of his remaining twenty years in mental institutions. The picture was sold at Anderson Galleries in New York in 1922 to Charles and Mary Worcester of Chicago, who gave it to the Art Institute in 1947.
