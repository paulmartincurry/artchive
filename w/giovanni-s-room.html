Giovanni's Room

James Baldwin · 1956

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Dial Press of New York published the novel in 1956, three years after James Baldwin's first. Asked in a 1980 interview whether it was autobiographical, he said no, and that it drew on what he had observed in Paris. One of the people he described was a blond Frenchman who bought drinks for his table in a bar. Two or three days later Baldwin saw the man's face in the headlines of a Paris newspaper.
