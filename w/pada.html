I have cast away fear of the world; what can any one
do to me?

Pada

Mirabai · ca. 1540

Translated by Max Arthur Macauliffe, 1909

Mirabai was born into a Rajput royal family and in 1516 was married to the crown prince of Mewar, who died of battle wounds in 1521. Her poems are padas, metric verses in the Rajasthani language, with melodies assigned so that they can be sung. Only two poems attributed to her have been found in manuscripts older than the early eighteenth century, and scholars are divided on how many of the verses under her name she composed.
