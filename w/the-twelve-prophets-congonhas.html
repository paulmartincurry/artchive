The Twelve Prophets, Congonhas

Aleijadinho (Antônio Francisco Lisboa) · 1800–1805 · Soapstone

Free to use with credit. Photo: Halleypo, CC BY-SA 3.0

The prophets stand on the outdoor stairway of a hilltop pilgrimage church in Congonhas, Minas Gerais. The church was commissioned by Feliciano Mendes, who made a vow after recovering from a grave illness in 1757. Aleijadinho and his assistants also carved the painted figures in its seven chapels of the Stations of the Cross.
