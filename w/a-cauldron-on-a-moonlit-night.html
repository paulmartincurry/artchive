A Cauldron on a Moonlit Night (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1886 · Color woodblock print; oban · 37.5 × 25.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The sheet's full title names its two figures, Kobuna no Gengo and Shimaya Hanzo. Yoshitoshi was forty-six or forty-seven when it was published in 1886. This impression belonged to the Chicago collector Frederick W. Gookin, whose daughter Nathalie gave it to the Art Institute in 1939.
