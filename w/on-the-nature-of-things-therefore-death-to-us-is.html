Therefore death to us
Is nothing, nor concerns us in the least,
Since nature of mind is mortal evermore.

On the Nature of Things — Therefore Death to Us Is Nothing (Book III)

Lucretius · c. 55 BCE

Translated by William Ellery Leonard

The poem runs to some 7,400 hexameter lines in six untitled books, written to explain the philosophy of Epicurus to a Roman audience. Epicurus held that human unhappiness arose largely from dread of the gods and terror of their wrath, and he made it his mission to remove those fears.
