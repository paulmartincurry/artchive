Tupi, or not tupi that is the question.

Manifesto Antropófago

Oswald de Andrade · May 1928

The essay first appeared in 1928 in the Revista de Antropofagia, printed with a line drawing of Abaporu, the painting by Tarsila do Amaral that had inspired it. Oswald de Andrade was thirty-eight. He came from a wealthy São Paulo family and used his money to support modernist painters, Tarsila among them. One line of the Portuguese text is written in English. No English translation was made until 1991.
