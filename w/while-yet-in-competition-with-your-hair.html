Not only turns to silver and sad violet,
But you and all of these together turn
To earth, smoke, dust, shadow, nothing.

While yet in competition with your hair

Luis de Góngora · before 1627

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Góngora was born in 1561 in Córdoba, where his father was corregidor, a judge. He became a Catholic priest at Córdoba Cathedral, and he died in 1627.
