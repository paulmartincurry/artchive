He clasps the crag with hooked hands;
Close to the sun in lonely lands,
Ring’d with the azure world, he stands.
The wrinkled sea beneath him crawls;
He watches from his mountain walls,
And like a thunderbolt he falls.

The Eagle

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1851

The poem was first published in 1851 with the subtitle Fragment. Tennyson travelled often to the Pyrenees, where he saw eagles and other birds of prey circling above him; for the poem he made up a setting of cliffs by the sea instead of the mountainside. He had been Poet Laureate since 19 November 1850.
