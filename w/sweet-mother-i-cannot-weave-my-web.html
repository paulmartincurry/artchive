Sweet Mother, I cannot weave my web, broken as I am by longing for a boy, at soft Aphrodite's will.

Sweet Mother, I Cannot Weave My Web (fragment 90)

Sappho of Lesbos · ca. 600 BCE

Translated by Henry Thornton Wharton

Hephaestion quoted these lines as an example of a metre. The scholars of Alexandria who edited Sappho in the second or third century BC probably grouped her poems by metre, in at least eight books.
