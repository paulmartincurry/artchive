Lust (The Seven Deadly Sins)

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · published 1558 · Engraving on laid paper · Plate: 22.7 × 29.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Pieter van der Heyden was one of the main engravers of Bruegel's designs from 1556 onwards and made 26 plates after them, more than any other printmaker. Bruegel seems to have preferred him for his skilled technique and his fidelity to the drawing he was copying.
