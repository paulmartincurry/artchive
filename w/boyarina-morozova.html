Boyarina Morozova

Vasily Surikov · 1884–1887 · Oil on canvas · 304 × 587.5 cm · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

Surikov painted the noblewoman's face from an Old Believer he met at the Rogozhsky cemetery, in a sketch that took two hours. The fool on the ground was drawn from a poor man in Moscow who sold cucumbers while sitting in the snow. More than a hundred preparatory sketches survive; he fastened them to the canvas with buttons, and restorers later found the holes. After its debut at the 15th Travelling Exhibition in 1887 the painting was bought for 25,000 rubles for the Tretyakov Gallery.
