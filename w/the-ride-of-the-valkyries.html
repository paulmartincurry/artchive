The Ride of the Valkyries (The Rhinegold & The Valkyrie)

Arthur Rackham · published 1910 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Arthur Rackham was in his early forties when this book came out. He began his colour plates as pen and India ink drawings over soft pencil, then built them up with thin washes of watercolour. They were photographed and printed mechanically, so no engraver had to recut his lines. Die Walküre, the opera the plate illustrates, had its first performance in Munich on 26 June 1870, at the insistence of King Ludwig II of Bavaria.
