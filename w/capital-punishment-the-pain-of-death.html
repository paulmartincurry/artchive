Capital Punishment: The Pain of Death (Todesstrafe: La peine de mort)

Félicien Rops · c. 1880 · Soft-ground etching · Platemark: 36.5 × 26.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is a soft-ground etching, and the plate mark measures 36.5 by 26.5 centimetres. Rops was in his late forties when he made it. He had been taught at home by private tutors until he was ten and then spent five years at a Jesuit school in Namur, where there were complaints about his caricatures of his teachers. John Bonebrake bequeathed this impression to the Cleveland Museum of Art.
