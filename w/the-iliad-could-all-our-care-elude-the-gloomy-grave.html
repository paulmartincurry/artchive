Could all our care elude the gloomy grave,
Which claims no less the fearful and the brave,
For lust of fame I should not vainly dare
In fighting fields, nor urge thy soul to war.

The Iliad — Could all our care elude the gloomy grave (Book XII, Pope's translation)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by Alexander Pope

Pope's Iliad was published between 1715 and 1720. The money he made from translating Homer allowed him to move in 1719 to a villa at Twickenham, where he made a grotto and gardens.
