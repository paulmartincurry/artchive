On Imagination

Phillis Wheatley · 1773

Phillis Wheatley was about twenty and still enslaved when her book of poems came out in London on 1 September 1773. Publishers in Boston had declined it. The year before, a group of Boston men including John Hancock and the governor of Massachusetts had examined her and signed a statement, printed in the preface, that she had written the poems herself. The Countess of Huntingdon paid towards the printing. The Wheatley family freed her by November of that year.
