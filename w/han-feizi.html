The Artchive

Han Feizi

Maker unknown · Qing dynasty printing · Hunan Museum

Free to use

The copy shown is a late nineteenth-century edition printed by the Hongwen Book Company. The text has fifty-five chapters, most of them written in the middle of the third century BCE. It was first called simply the Hanzi, Master Han; the longer title came in during the Tang dynasty, possibly to keep the author from being confused with the poet Han Yu. Chapter 36, with its story of a man selling spears and shields, is credited as the origin of the Chinese word for contradiction.
