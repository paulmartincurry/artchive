The soul selects her own society,
Then shuts the door;
On her divine majority
Obtrude no more.

The Soul selects her own Society

Emily Dickinson · 1890

In the summer of 1858 Dickinson began copying her poems into small booklets. The forty fascicles she made between 1858 and 1865 eventually held nearly eight hundred poems. A selection was first published in 1890, four years after her death, edited by Thomas Wentworth Higginson and Mabel Loomis Todd, who altered the poems to fit the conventions of the time.
