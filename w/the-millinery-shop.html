The Millinery Shop

Edgar Degas · 1879-86 · Oil on canvas · 100 × 110.7 cm (39 3/8 × 43 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is the largest of the paintings Degas made of milliners, and he worked up the single figure in a number of drawings and pastels first, trying out her pose and her clothes. The dates on it run over seven years. Degas kept it until February 1913, when he sold it to the dealer Paul Durand-Ruel for 50,000 francs. Annie Swan Coburn of Chicago later bought it from Durand-Ruel in New York, and it came to the Art Institute with her bequest in 1933.
