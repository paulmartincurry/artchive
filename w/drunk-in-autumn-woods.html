Drunk in Autumn Woods

Shitao · ca. 1702 · Hanging scroll, ink and color on paper · 161 × 70.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1690 Shitao went to Beijing to find a patron who could advance him in the Buddhist hierarchy. He failed, gave up Buddhism for Daoism in 1693, and went back to Yangzhou, where he took the name Da Dizi and gave it to his house, Da Di Hall. This scroll dates from those last Yangzhou years. He also wrote on painting, in a book called Sayings on Painting from Monk Bitter Gourd, one of his many names.
