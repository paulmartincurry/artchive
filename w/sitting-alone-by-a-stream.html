Sitting Alone by a Stream

Attributed to Fan Kuan · 11th century · Hanging scroll, light colour on silk · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Fan Kuan's real name was Fan Zhongzheng; Fan Kuan was a pseudonym. He was born around 960 in what is now Yaozhou, in Shaanxi, died around 1030, and was both a Daoist and a Neo-Confucianist. Travelers among Mountains and Streams may be his only surviving painting, so this scroll in Taipei carries his name by attribution rather than by proof.
