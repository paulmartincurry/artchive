Liberté: DADA DADA DADA, hurlement des couleurs crispées, entrelacement des contraires et de toutes les contradictions, des grotesques, des inconséquences: LA VIE.

Manifeste Dada 1918 (excerpts)

Tristan Tzara · 1918

Tzara was born Samuel Rosenstock in Moinești, Romania, and left for Zürich in the autumn of 1915, at nineteen. The shows he staged there often turned into scandals or riots and kept him in conflict with the Swiss police. He was twenty-two when this manifesto was published in 1918, and in 1924 he collected it with his other Dada texts as the Seven Dada Manifestos.
