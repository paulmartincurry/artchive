Sword Guard (Tsuba) with Bridge and Pine

Maker unknown · mid-1800s · Iron · 7 × 7.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This iron guard, about 7 centimetres across, was made in the mid-1800s and is signed by its maker. It came to Cleveland in 1919, a gift of D. Z. Norton, in the same year as the brass guard with plovers in waves.
