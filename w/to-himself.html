Life is a bitter, weary load,
The world a slough. And now, repose!
Despair no more, but find in Death
The only boon Fate on our race bestows!

To Himself (A se stesso)

Giacomo Leopardi · 1835

Translated by Frederick Townsend

Leopardi grew up in Recanati, in the Papal States, in a noble family whose fortunes had been destroyed by his father's gambling. Between the ages of twelve and nineteen he studied constantly in his father's library, and an illness, probably Pott's disease or ankylosing spondylitis, left him standing only 1.41 metres tall. In 1818 he tried to escape from home, but he was caught by his father and brought back.
