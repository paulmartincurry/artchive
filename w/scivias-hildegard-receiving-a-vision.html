Scivias: Hildegard Receiving a Vision

Hildegard of Bingen and her scribes · ca. 1151–1179 · Illumination on parchment

Free to use

Hildegard was forty-two and had led the women's community at Disibodenberg for five years when she began to set down her visions, in 1141. The monk Volmar and one of her nuns, Richardis von Stade, helped with the writing. She finished the book in 1151 or 1152, soon after moving her convent to Rupertsberg. This frontispiece shows her sketching on a wax tablet while dictating to Volmar. The Rupertsberg manuscript was taken to Dresden for safekeeping in World War II and lost; the colour images come from a copy painted at Eibingen in 1927-1933.
