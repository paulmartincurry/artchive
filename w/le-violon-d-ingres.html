Le Violon d'Ingres (Ingres's Violin)

Man Ray · 1924 · Gelatin silver print · 29.6 × 22.7 cm (image) · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

The model is Alice Prin, known as Kiki de Montparnasse, Man Ray's companion for most of the 1920s; he had moved to Paris in 1921. He posed her after Ingres's Valpinçon Bather, then painted the two f-holes onto a print and rephotographed it. In French a violon d'Ingres is a hobby, after Ingres's habit of playing the violin. The picture was first published in the Surrealist magazine Littérature in June 1924. A print sold at Christie's New York in May 2022 for $12,400,000, a record for a photograph.
