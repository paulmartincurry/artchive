Ibaraki (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889–92 · Woodcut, color · 27.5 × 21.4 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress describes the figure as an elderly woman or demon, possibly Ibaraki of Rashomon, flying through the air. In the legend, Watanabe no Tsuna cut Ibaraki's arm with a sword named Higekiri. The series the print comes from was published between 1889 and 1892, the last years of Yoshitoshi's life.
