Whirl up, sea—
whirl your pointed pines,
splash your great pines on our rocks.

Oread

H.D. (Hilda Doolittle) · 1915

Oread is six lines long. Hilda Doolittle had come to London from Pennsylvania on a holiday in May 1911 and stayed to make a career as a writer. In 1912 Ezra Pound suggested she sign her work H.D., and she kept the name for the rest of her career. She, Pound and Richard Aldington, whom she married in 1913, lived at numbers 6, 10 and 8 Church Walk in Kensington and met each day to work in the British Museum Reading Room.
