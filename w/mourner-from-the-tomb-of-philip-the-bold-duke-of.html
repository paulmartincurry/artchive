Mourner from the Tomb of Philip the Bold, Duke of Burgundy

Claus de Werve · 1404–10 · Vizille alabaster · 41 × 12.7 × 15 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Philip the Bold ordered his tomb in 1381 for the monastery of Champmol, near Dijon, and when he died in 1404 only two of the mourners were finished. Claus Sluter designed the figures; his nephew Claus de Werve completed them in 1410. Champmol was destroyed in 1793 and the tomb damaged. A plaster cast of this alabaster mourner, 41 centimetres high, now fills its empty niche on the tomb in Dijon.
