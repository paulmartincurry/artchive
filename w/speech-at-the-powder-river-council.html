Speech at the Powder River Council

Sitting Bull (Tȟatȟáŋka Íyotake) · Spring 1877

In the spring of 1877 Sitting Bull was one of the last Lakota leaders still refusing to surrender. The summer before, on 25 June 1876, the Lakota and Northern Cheyenne had destroyed Custer's battalion at the Little Bighorn, and the United States had sent thousands more soldiers in response. In May 1877 he led his band north into Canada, where he stayed until 1881.
