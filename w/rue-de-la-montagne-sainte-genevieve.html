Rue de la Montagne-Sainte-Geneviève

Eugène Atget · 1898 · albumen print, gold toned · Image: 22 × 17.4 cm (8 11/16 × 6 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The University of Paris was founded on this narrow street in 1112. Eugène Atget was forty-one and had been a professional photographer for eight years. He used no enlarger, so the print is the same size as its glass negative; it was exposed to the sun in a printing frame, then toned with gold. The number EA 8794-2 is written in pencil on the back.
