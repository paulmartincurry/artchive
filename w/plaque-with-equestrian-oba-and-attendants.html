Plaque with Equestrian Ọ́bà and Attendants

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · c. 1540–70 · Brass · 49.5 × 41.9 × 11.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hundreds of rectangular brass plaques like this were hung on the pillars of the oba's palace at Benin City, held by nails punched through them. British forces captured the city on 18 February 1897, and the palace building was burned three days later. Over a thousand metal plaques and sculptures were sent to Britain, where the Admiralty auctioned the booty to pay for the expedition.
