The Assassination of Khusrau Parviz (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to 'Abd al-Samad · c. 1535 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.4 × 27.3 cm; page 47 × 31.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met attributes this painting to 'Abd al-Samad, who later moved to India and from about 1572 ran the workshop of the Mughal emperor Akbar. Most works given to him date from about 1544–45 or later; the scholar Stuart Cary Welch attributed a painting in this much earlier manuscript to him.
