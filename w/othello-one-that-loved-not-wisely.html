Speak of me as I am. Nothing extenuate,
Nor set down aught in malice. Then must you speak
Of one that loved not wisely, but too well;
Of one not easily jealous, but being wrought,
Perplex’d in the extreme; of one whose hand,

Othello — one that loved not wisely (Act V, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · c. 1603

Shakespeare wrote the play around 1603. It was not published in his lifetime and survives in one quarto edition, from 1622, and in the First Folio. His main source was a 1565 novella by Cinthio.
