The Swing

Jean-Honoré Fragonard · 1767 · Oil on canvas · Wallace Collection

Free to use

According to the memoirs of the dramatist Charles Collé, a courtier first asked the painter Gabriel François Doyen for a picture of his mistress on a swing pushed by a bishop. Doyen refused and passed the job to Fragonard, who painted a layman instead. The first certain owner was a tax farmer who was guillotined in 1794, and the revolutionary government seized the painting. Lord Hertford bought it at auction in Paris after 1865. When it was cleaned in 2021 it had not been cleaned for more than a hundred years.
