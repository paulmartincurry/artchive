Samurai, Yokohama

Felice Beato · 1864–65 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · image 17.9 × 14.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Many of the photographs in Beato's albums were hand-coloured, a technique that in his studio applied the skills of Japanese watercolourists and woodblock printmakers to European photography. He may have started hand-colouring at the suggestion of Charles Wirgman, an artist and correspondent for the Illustrated London News whom he had met in Hong Kong.
