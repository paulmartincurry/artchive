Zodiac Tomb Figure: Ox

Maker unknown · 500s · gray earthenware with traces of slip · Overall: 22.6 cm (8 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The ox is the second of the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac, and figures of all twelve were placed in tombs. This one is about twenty-three centimetres tall, and the lower half of its interior is hollow. Emery May Holden Norweb of Cleveland gave it to the museum in 1972.
