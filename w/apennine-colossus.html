Apennine Colossus

Giambologna · late 1580s · Stone · H. about 11 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: Rhododendrites, CC BY-SA 4.0

The giant is about eleven metres high and was the water source for the fountains of the Villa di Pratolino, north of Florence. Giambologna built it in the late 1580s. Inside are chambers and caves on three levels: one on the upper floor is big enough for a small orchestra, and a small chamber in the head holds a fireplace whose smoke escaped through the nostrils. The villa itself was demolished in 1822.
