The Herring Net

Winslow Homer · 1885 · Oil on canvas · 76.5 × 122.9 cm (30 1/8 × 48 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Homer painted this at Prouts Neck, Maine, four years after a stay in the fishing community of Tynemouth in England. He signed it Homer 85 in red paint on the water. It was first shown at the National Academy of Design in November 1885, and for a time it went by the title Banks Fishermen. The Chicago collector Martin A. Ryerson bought it in 1915.
