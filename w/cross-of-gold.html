You shall not press down upon the brow of labor this crown of thorns;
you shall not crucify mankind upon a cross of gold!

Cross of Gold

William Jennings Bryan · July 9, 1896

Bryan gave this speech at the Chicago Coliseum on the third day of the 1896 Democratic National Convention, at the close of the debate on the party platform. He was thirty-six, a former congressman from Nebraska with little support among the delegates. They carried him on their shoulders afterwards and nominated him for president. He lost the election to William McKinley, and the United States formally adopted the gold standard in 1900.
