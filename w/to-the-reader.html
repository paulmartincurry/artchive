To the Reader (Au Lecteur — Les Fleurs du mal)

Charles Baudelaire · 1857

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2001–2010 · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

This poem prefaces Les Fleurs du mal, Baudelaire's first volume of poems, published in 1857 when he was thirty-six. He, his publisher and the printer were prosecuted for an offence against public morals and fined, and six of the poems were suppressed. The judgment was officially reversed on 11 May 1949.
