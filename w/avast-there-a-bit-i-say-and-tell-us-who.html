Avast There a Bit, I Say, and Tell Us Who the Devil Ye All Are! (King Pest)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Clarke's London publisher was Harrap. Before the Poe book he had started two commissions for the firm that were never completed, a Rime of the Ancient Mariner and a Rape of the Lock; much of his work on the first was destroyed during the 1916 Easter Rising in Dublin. The caption under this plate is printed beside the page number 70.
