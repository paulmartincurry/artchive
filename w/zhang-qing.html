Zhang Qing (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827–30 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 38.9 × 26.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kuniyoshi was twenty-nine when this series began. He drew tattoos on his heroes, a novelty that soon influenced fashion in Edo. The series became extremely popular there, and demand for his warrior prints grew.
