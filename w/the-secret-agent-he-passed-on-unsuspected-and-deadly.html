He passed on unsuspected and deadly, like a pest in the street full of men.

The Secret Agent — He passed on unsuspected and deadly (Ch. XIII, close)

Joseph Conrad · 1907

The novel was printed in eleven instalments in 1905 and 1906 and published as a book on 12 September 1907. It sold only 3,076 copies between 1907 and 1914. One character is based on Martial Bourdin, a French anarchist who died when the explosives he was carrying went off early.
