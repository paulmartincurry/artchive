Night of the Living Dead, frame at 7:13

George A. Romero · 1968 · Film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The opening sequence was shot at the Evans City Cemetery on Franklin Road, 30 miles north of Pittsburgh. George A. Romero directed, photographed and edited the film. It was his first feature, made after years of television commercials, industrial films and segments for Mister Rogers' Neighborhood. The ghouls' makeup began as white skin and blackened eyes; mortician's wax for wounds was added as filming went on.
