Roslin Chapel, South Porch

Roger Fenton · 1856 · Salted paper print from glass negative · Image: 35.8 × 43.3 cm (14 1/8 × 17 1/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The print is made from a glass negative and is about forty-three centimetres wide. Three years earlier Fenton had helped to found the London Photographic Society and became its first secretary. His whole career in photography lasted little more than a decade: in 1862 he sold his equipment, gave up the work, and went back to law as a barrister. He died seven years later, at fifty, in Potters Bar.
