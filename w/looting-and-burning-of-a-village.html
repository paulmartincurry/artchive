Looting and Burning of a Village (The Miseries and Misfortunes of War, plate 7)

Jacques Callot · 1633 · Etching; second state of three (Lieure) · sheet: 10.5 x 20.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The original plates for the series still exist, in a museum in Nancy. Seven drawings of whole compositions survive too, along with many tiny figure studies, a large group of them in the Hermitage. More than 2,000 of Callot's drawings and studies for prints survive, but no painting by him is known.
