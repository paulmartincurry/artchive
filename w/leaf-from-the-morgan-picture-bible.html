Leaf from the Morgan Picture Bible

Maker unknown · about 1250 · Tempera colors, gold leaf, and ink on parchment · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Forty-three leaves of this picture Bible are in the Morgan Library in New York, two are in Paris and this one is in Los Angeles. The book has traditionally been thought to have been made in Paris in the mid-1240s for Louis IX. Several leaves, all involving Absalom's rebellion, appear to have been deliberately removed from it.
