At fifteen I stopped scowling,
I desired my dust to be mingled with yours
Forever and forever, and forever.

The River-Merchant's Wife: A Letter

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1915

Translated by Ezra Pound

Li Bai's poem is called Changgan Xing, the Changgan song. Ezra Pound's loose English version first appeared in his collection Cathay in 1915, where eleven of the nineteen poems are Li Bai's. Li Bai's own family had lived in a Silk Road city and were likely merchants.
