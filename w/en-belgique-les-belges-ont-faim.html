En Belgique les Belges ont faim (In Belgium the Belgians Are Hungry)

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1915 · Lithograph in two colours · 125 × 94 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The title translates as In Belgium the Belgians are hungry, and the poster announced an artistic raffle in aid of Belgian food relief. Steinlen signed it in 1915, and Lapina printed it in Paris in two colours. German troops had overrun almost all of neutral Belgium before the winter of 1914.
