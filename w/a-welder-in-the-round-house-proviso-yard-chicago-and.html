A Welder in the Round-House, Proviso Yard, Chicago and North Western Railroad

Jack Delano · December 1942 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Delano made this colour transparency in December 1942 at the Proviso yard of the Chicago and North Western Railroad. He had sent sample pictures to Roy Stryker, and in 1940 Stryker offered him a job with the Farm Security Administration at 2,300 dollars a year.
