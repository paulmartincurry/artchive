“I’ve got out at last,” said I, “in spite of you and Jane! And I’ve pulled off most of the paper, so you can’t put me back!”

The Yellow Wallpaper

Charlotte Perkins Gilman · 1892

Charlotte Perkins Gilman wrote the story on 6 and 7 June 1890, when she was twenty-nine. After the birth of her daughter she had been put on a rest cure by Dr. Silas Weir Mitchell that banned reading, writing and painting, and after three months she went back to work against his orders. The editor of the Atlantic Monthly rejected the story, and it was first printed in The New England Magazine in January 1892. She sent Mitchell a copy and never received a response.
