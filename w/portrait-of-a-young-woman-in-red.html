Portrait of a Young Woman in Red

Maker unknown · 90–120 CE · Encaustic on limewood with gold leaf · 38.1 × 18.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The portrait is painted in wax on a limewood panel 38 centimetres tall, with gold leaf. Portraits of this kind covered the faces of bodies mummified for burial and were mounted into the cloth bands that wrapped them; about 900 are known, and almost all have been detached from their mummies. The museum bought this one in 1909.
