Zhuangzi — Prince Hui's Cook

Zhuangzi (Chuang Tzu) · 4th–3rd century BC

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, 1889

Guo Xiang, who edited the Zhuangzi around AD 300, said he had made considerable changes to its outer and miscellaneous chapters. His version runs to about 63,000 characters, around two-thirds of the length recorded for the Han-era text. The Han historian Sima Qian called it a work of 100,000 characters.
