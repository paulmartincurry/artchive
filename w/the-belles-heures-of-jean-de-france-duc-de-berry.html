The Belles Heures of Jean de France, duc de Berry

Limbourg Brothers · 1405–1408/1409 · Tempera, gold, and ink on vellum · Single leaf, Overall: 9 3/8 × 6 11/16 in. (23.8 × 17 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This book of hours was the first job the Limbourg brothers took on for Jean de Berry, after their earlier patron, his brother Philip the Bold, died in April 1404. The three brothers, born between about 1385 and 1388, were around twenty when they began it, and it was finished in 1409. Each page is about twenty-four centimetres tall. All three brothers and the duke died in the first half of 1416.
