You'll Come to Three Princesses, Whom You Will See Standing in the Earth up to Their Necks (East of the Sun and West of the Moon)

Kay Nielsen · published 1914 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Alexander Turnbull Library, Wellington

Free to use

Nielsen grew up in Copenhagen as the son of two actors; his father ran the Dagmarteater and his mother played at the Royal Danish Theatre. He studied in Paris at the Académie Julian and the Académie Colarossi from 1904 to 1911, then lived in England until 1916. He was twenty-eight when this book appeared.
