Reliquary Bust of Saint Juliana

Circle of Giovanni di Bartolo · c. 1376 · Copper, gilding, gesso, and tempera paint · 28.1 × 22.9 × 21.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The bust is copper, gilded and then painted in tempera over gesso, and it is 28.1 centimetres high. The museum gives it to the circle of Giovanni di Bartolo, an Italian artist active from 1364 to 1404.
