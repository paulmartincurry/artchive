A noir, E blanc, I rouge, U vert, O bleu, voyelles,
Je dirai quelque jour vos naissances latentes.

Voyelles

Arthur Rimbaud · 1871

Rimbaud wrote this sonnet by September 1871, before his 17th birthday, and it was Paul Verlaine who published it, in October 1883. At least two early manuscripts exist, one in Rimbaud's hand and one a transcript by Verlaine. They differ mainly in punctuation, though one word in the fourth line appears as bombillent in one and bombinent in the other; both mean buzz.
