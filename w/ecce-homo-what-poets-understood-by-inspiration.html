One hears—one does not seek; one takes—one does not ask who gives: a thought suddenly flashes up like lightning, it comes with necessity, without faltering—I have never had any choice in the matter.

Ecce Homo — What Poets Understood by Inspiration (Thus Spake Zarathustra, 3)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici

Nietzsche wrote Ecce Homo in 1888, the year before his collapse; it stayed unpublished until 1908. Ludovici, who translated it, had also written Nietzsche and Art, published in 1911.
