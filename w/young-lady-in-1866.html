Young Lady in 1866

Édouard Manet · 1866 · Oil on canvas · 72 7/8 × 50 5/8 in. (185.1 × 128.6 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The model was Victorine Meurent, and the canvas is 185 centimetres tall. Manet sold it to the dealer Durand-Ruel in January 1872 for 1,500 francs; in 1878 it sold at the Hôtel Drouot for 700. In March 1889 its owner, Erwin Davis, bought it back at his own New York sale for $1,350 and on 21 March wrote to the Metropolitan Museum giving it to them. It was one of the first two paintings by Manet to enter a museum collection.
