The Grapes of Wrath

John Steinbeck · 1939

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

According to The New York Times this was the best-selling book of 1939, and 430,000 copies had been printed by February 1940. It was publicly banned and burned by citizens and debated on national radio. It won the National Book Award and the Pulitzer Prize.
