Mount Vesuvius at Midnight

Albert Bierstadt · 1868 · Oil on canvas · 42.6 × 60.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Bierstadt was living in London in 1868 when he heard that Vesuvius was active again, and he went at once to Italy. It is not certain that he actually saw an eruption. This is a smaller version of a much larger canvas that is now lost.
