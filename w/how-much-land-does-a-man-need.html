His servant picked up the spade and dug a grave long enough for Pahom to lie in, and buried him in it. Six feet from his head to his heels was all he needed.

How Much Land Does a Man Need? (closing)

Leo Tolstoy · 1886

Translated by Louise and Aylmer Maude

Tolstoy published this story in 1886. Born into an aristocratic family, he had served in the Crimean War in his twenties, and in later life he became a dedicated advocate of Georgism, the economic philosophy of Henry George, which he brought into his writing.
