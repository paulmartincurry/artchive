I laugh when I hear that the fish in the water is thirsty:
You do not see that the Real is in your home, and you wander from forest to forest listlessly!

I Laugh When I Hear That the Fish in the Water Is Thirsty

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

After Kabir's death two of his disciples, Dharamdas and Suratigopal, set up centres to spread his teaching, the beginning of the Kabir Panth, the Path of Kabir. Its followers, generally considered to number in the millions, keep a strict vegetarian diet, bathe daily and allow those who wrong them up to three chances of forgiveness. They greet one another with the word Bandagi.
