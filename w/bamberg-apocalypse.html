Bamberg Apocalypse

Maker unknown · ca. 1000–1020 · Parchment · Bamberg State Library

The manuscript was made in the scriptorium at Reichenau Abbey and finished between 1000 and 1020. Scholars still argue over who ordered it: Otto III, who died suddenly in 1002, or Henry II, who may have found it unfinished and had it completed. Henry and his wife Cunigunde gave it to the new Collegiate Abbey of St. Stephan at Bamberg. It has 106 leaves, each 29.5 by 20.5 centimetres, with 57 gilded miniatures.
