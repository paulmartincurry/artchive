Self-Portrait in the Studiolo

Lavinia Fontana · 1579 · Oil on copper · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

Lavinia Fontana was about twenty-six and two years married when she painted herself at her desk. Her marriage contract said she would go on working and would not keep house, and her husband, a former pupil of her father, acted as her agent and painted draperies for her. She had started in business with small pictures on copper, which were wanted as papal and diplomatic gifts for the worth and shine of the metal.
