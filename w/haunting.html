Haunting

Odilon Redon · 1893–94 · Lithograph · Image: 36.1 × 22.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Redon took this stone through five states, and this impression is from the last. He was in his early fifties and by then also worked in pastel and oil; he made no more of his black works after 1900. The image is 36 centimetres high. The print entered the Metropolitan Museum in 1955.
