Eight Bells

Winslow Homer · 1886 · Oil on canvas · Addison Gallery of American Art

The idea began on one of three wooden panels Winslow Homer found in the cabin of his brother's sloop at Prouts Neck, Maine, where he painted a grey study of a ship's officer alone with an octant. Sales were slow the year he painted the canvas. It sold the following year for a reported 400 dollars, the first oil he had sold in more than three years, and he then gave up painting in oils until 1890.
