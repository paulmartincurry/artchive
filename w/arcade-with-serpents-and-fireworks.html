Arcade with Serpents and Fireworks

Maker unknown · ca. 1780 · Cut paper on wood frame · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

A paper label pasted on the back, written in Savoyard Italian, describes the scene as a fire machine with a serpent that throws flowers and lightning. The slide is cut paper on a wooden frame, made for a magic lantern. The museum bought it with gifts from Mary Hearn Greims and George A. Hearn and money from the Misses Hewitt Fund.
