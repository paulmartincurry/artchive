Moche Portrait Vessel

Maker unknown · 100 BCE–700 CE · Terracotta · Musée du quai Branly, Paris

Moche potters on the north coast of Peru made these vessels to hold liquids. Almost all of them show adult men, a few show young boys, and none showing an adult woman has been found. Most were recovered from graves, but wear marks and repairs show they were used in life. An estimated 95% of the more than 900 that have been photographed were dug up by looters, so where they were first found is not known.
