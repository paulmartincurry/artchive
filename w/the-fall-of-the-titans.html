The Fall of the Titans

Cornelis Cornelisz. van Haarlem · 1588–1590 · Oil on canvas · 259.3 × 227.6 centimeter · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Cornelis van Haarlem was in his twenties when he painted this. In 1583 the city of Haarlem had given him his first official commission, a militia company portrait, and two years after that Karel van Mander brought drawings by Bartholomeus Spranger to the city, which had a strong effect on the painters there. During the Spanish siege of 1573 his parents fled Haarlem and he stayed behind, raised by the painter Pieter Pietersz the Elder, his first teacher.
