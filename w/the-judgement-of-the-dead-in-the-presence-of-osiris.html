The Judgement of the Dead in the Presence of Osiris (Book of the Dead of Hunefer)

Maker unknown · c. 1275 BCE · Papyrus · British Museum, London

Free to use

Hunefer, who owned this papyrus, was a scribe of the 19th Dynasty and steward of Pharaoh Seti I; his titles included Scribe of Divine Offerings and Overseer of Royal Cattle. The scene reads from left to right. His heart, drawn as a pot, is weighed against a feather, and he is then brought before Osiris, who sits under a canopy.
