It's not catastrophes, murders, deaths, diseases, that age and kill us; it's the way people look and laugh, and run up the steps of omnibuses.

Jacob's Room — It's not catastrophes that age and kill us (Ch. 6)

Virginia Woolf · 1922

Jacob's Room was Woolf's third novel, published on 26 October 1922 by the Hogarth Press, with a cover by her sister Vanessa Bell. She was forty. She named her main character Jacob Flanders after the generation who died in Flanders Fields. One critic that year hoped she would go back to a more conventional way of writing.
