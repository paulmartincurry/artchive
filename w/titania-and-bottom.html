Titania and Bottom

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · c. 1790 · Oil on canvas · 217.2 × 275.6 cm · Tate Britain, London

Free to use

The publisher John Boydell commissioned this canvas for his Shakespeare Gallery, and Fuseli also painted a large companion, Titania's Awakening, showing a later moment of the same scene. Titania's pose is adapted from Leonardo da Vinci's Leda and the Swan. The painting has belonged to the Tate since 1887.
