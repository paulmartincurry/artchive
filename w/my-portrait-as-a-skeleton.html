My Portrait as a Skeleton

James Ensor · 1889 · Etching on paper · 11.6 × 7.5 cm · MSK Gent (Museum of Fine Arts, Ghent)

Free to use

Ensor worked this self-portrait from a photograph of himself at a window of the Rousseau family's house at 20 Rue Vautier in Brussels. The first state follows the photograph closely and survives in a single impression at Mu.ZEE in Ostend. In the second and third states he stripped his face to the skull, keeping only his hair and his eyes, and replaced the woman at the window with a skull on the sill. The print is 11.6 by 7.5 centimetres.
