Popol Vuh — The House of Gloom

Lewis Spence, retelling the Popol Vuh · ca. 1550 Transcription of Pre-Columbian Codex

The one early manuscript stayed with the Dominican Order until the clerics were expelled from Guatemala in 1829–30, and then went to the Universidad de San Carlos. In 1855 the French abbot Brasseur de Bourbourg found it in the university library and apparently took the volume back to France. After his death in 1874 it was sold to Edward E. Ayer, who gave his collection to the Newberry Library in Chicago. It lay there unnoticed until 1941.
