Abiding Nowhere, the Awakened Mind Arises

Musō Soseki · early to mid-14th century · Hanging scroll; ink on paper · Image: 39 1/16 × 22 5/8 in. (99.2 × 57.4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Musō Soseki was a Rinzai Zen monk who also designed gardens, among them those at Saihō-ji and Tenryū-ji in Kyoto, though none survives in its original form. He made up his own name after a dream, from characters meaning dream and window. The writing on this scroll is almost a metre tall, and with its mounting the scroll hangs 181 centimetres. Mary and Cheney Cowles gave it to the Met in 2018.
