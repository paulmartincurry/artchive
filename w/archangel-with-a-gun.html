Archangel with a Gun (Asiel Timor Dei)

Circle of the Master of Calamarca · 17th century · Oil on canvas · Museo Nacional de Arte, La Paz

Angels carrying an arquebus in place of the usual sword first appeared in Peru in the second half of the seventeenth century, dressed like Criollo and Andean nobles. Their poses probably came from engravings of military drill published in 1607 by Jacob de Gheyn II. The church at Calamarca, about 60 km from La Paz, holds the most complete surviving series, which includes the Asiel Timor Dei of around 1680.
