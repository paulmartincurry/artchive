I will show you something different from either
Your shadow at morning striding behind you
Or your shadow at evening rising to meet you;
I will show you fear in a handful of dust.

A Handful of Dust (The Waste Land, I)

T. S. Eliot · 1922

At a dinner in Paris on 3 January 1922 with Ezra Pound, Eliot and James Joyce, the New York publisher Horace Liveright made offers for The Waste Land and for Ulysses. Eliot had doubted the poem held together as one piece and suggested spreading it over four issues of The Dial; Pound objected. Boni and Liveright's book came out that December in a first run of 1,000 copies, followed very soon by a second 1,000.
