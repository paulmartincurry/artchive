Oath of the Horatii

Jacques-Louis David · 1784 · Oil on canvas · Department of Paintings of the Louvre

Free to use

The commission came from an assistant to Louis XVI, who had already let David live in the Louvre. David began the studies in Paris but painted the canvas in Rome, and showed it there first. He departed from the scene that had been agreed; a surviving drawing shows an earlier idea, the last Horatius standing over the sister he has killed. The painting reached the Salon of 1785 late and was hung in a poor spot, until public complaint forced the gallery to move it. It is 329.8 centimetres high and 424.8 wide.
