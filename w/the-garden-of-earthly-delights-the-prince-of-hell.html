The Garden of Earthly Delights: The Prince of Hell (detail of the Hell panel)

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1490–1510 · Oil on oak panel · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Bosch painted the triptych between 1490 and 1510, when he was between 40 and 60 years old. This bird-headed figure is sometimes called the Prince of Hell, after the cauldron it wears on its head. In 1566 the triptych was used as the model for a tapestry that hangs at El Escorial.
