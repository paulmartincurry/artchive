Earth has not any thing to shew more fair:
Dull would he be of soul who could pass by
A sight so touching in its majesty:

Composed upon Westminster Bridge

William Wordsworth · 1807

Despite its title, the date of composition is given as 31 July 1802, and the sonnet was at first dated 1803. That morning Wordsworth and his sister Dorothy crossed Westminster Bridge on the Dover coach, on their way to Calais to visit Annette Vallon and his daughter Caroline before his marriage to Mary Hutchinson. Dorothy described the view in her journal. At the end of his life he considered revising the poem after a lady objected that London could not be both bare and clothed.
