Offer the Ram of Pride instead of him.
But the old man would not so, but slew his son.

The Parable of the Old Men and the Young

Wilfred Owen · 1918; published 1920

Siegfried Sassoon first published this poem in 1920, without its last line. Owen had been killed on 4 November 1918 at the crossing of the Sambre-Oise Canal, one week before the Armistice, at the age of twenty-five. He was raised an evangelical Anglican, and before the war he spent a year and a half as lay assistant to a country vicar.
