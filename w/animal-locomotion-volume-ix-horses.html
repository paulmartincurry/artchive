Animal Locomotion, Volume IX: Horses

Eadweard Muybridge · 1887 · Photogravures · The Met

Free to use

The full title of the work calls it an electro-photographic investigation, commenced in 1872 and completed in 1885. Most of the pictures were made at the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia, where from 1883 to 1886 Muybridge produced more than 100,000 images of animals and people in motion. He was in his late fifties when it was published. This volume is the one on horses, and the Metropolitan Museum moved it from its library into the photographs department.
