The Grammar of Ornament

Owen Jones · 1856 · Chromolithography · Height: 22 7/16 in. (57 cm) × Width: 15 3/8 in. (39 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Owen Jones, a Londoner, was forty-six or forty-seven when this book was published in London. It holds 100 plates, some of them in colour, and its pages are 57 centimetres tall. The Met's copy was given to its Thomas J. Watson Library by the library's friends.
