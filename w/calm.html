Underneath some archway, the dying light
sleeps, and, like a long shroud trailing from the East,
listen, dear one, listen to the soft onset of night.

Calm (Recueillement)

Charles Baudelaire · published 1868

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The sonnet is listed among the additions to the 1868 edition of Les Fleurs du mal. The French musician Neige later used it as the words of a song with his band Amesoeurs.
