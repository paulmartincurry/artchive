Elegantly Dressed Male and Female Skeletons Arm in Arm (Vignette for the Feast of the Dead)

José Guadalupe Posada · c. 1894 · Type-metal engraving · 8.2 × 6.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Posada was apprenticed as a teenager, in 1868, in a workshop in Aguascalientes where he learned lithography and engraving. Three years later a local newspaper gave him his first job as a political cartoonist. It closed after eleven issues, reportedly because one of his cartoons had offended a local politician.
