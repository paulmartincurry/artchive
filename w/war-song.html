Comrades, kinsmen,
Now have ye spoken thus,
The earth is mine,
’Tis my domain.
’Tis said, and now anew I exert me!

War-Song (Okicize Olowan)

Dakota war song · Printed 1907

Translated by Natalie Curtis, 1907

According to The Indians' Book, the Dakotas sang this old song when they were fighting for their land. By the time Natalie Curtis took it down, chiefs sang it on various occasions, sometimes to open a ceremony or a council, and it was often sung when presents were given at a dance. It was known to other tribes as well.
