Fallen Statue at the Ramesseum, Thebes

Francis Frith · 1857 · Albumen print from wet collodion negative · image 38.3 × 48.2 cm · The Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Frith took this view on the second of his three trips to Egypt, using a mammoth plate, typically a glass negative of 18 by 22 inches. He included members of his expedition in the picture. He had been apprenticed in a Sheffield cutlery house and worked in a wholesale grocery business, and he was almost 30 when he became a photographer.
