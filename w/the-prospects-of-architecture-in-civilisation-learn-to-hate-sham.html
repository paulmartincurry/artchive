The Prospects of Architecture in Civilisation: Learn to Hate Sham Art

William Morris · 1880

In the autumn of 1879 Morris began a campaign to save St Mark's Basilica in Venice from restoration, and his petition gathered 2,000 signatures, among them Disraeli, Gladstone and Ruskin. In April 1879 he had moved his family to an eighteenth-century house on the Thames at Hammersmith, owned by the novelist George MacDonald, and renamed it Kelmscott House. In its grounds he set up a workshop for hand-knotted carpets.
