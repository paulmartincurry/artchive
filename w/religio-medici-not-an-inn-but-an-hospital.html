For the world, I count it not an inn, but an hospital; and a place not to live, but to die in. The world that I regard is myself; it is the microcosm of my own frame that I cast mine eye on: for the other, I use it but like my globe, and turn it round sometimes for my recreation.

Religio Medici — Not an inn, but an hospital (Part II, 11)

Sir Thomas Browne · 1643

Browne took his medical degree at Leiden in 1633 and settled in Norwich in 1637, where he practised medicine until his death in 1682. The book was placed on the papal Index of prohibited books.
