For men were born to pray and save:
Romantic Ireland's dead and gone,
It's with O'Leary in the grave.

September 1913

W. B. Yeats · 1913

Yeats composed the poem in response to the Hugh Lane controversy. Lane had offered his collection of paintings to the city of Dublin, provided that a municipal gallery was built to house them, and the Dublin Corporation voted against funding the gallery. The poem was first published in The Irish Times on 8 September 1913, under another title.
