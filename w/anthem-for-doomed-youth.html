What passing-bells for these who die as cattle?
Only the monstrous anger of the guns.
Only the stuttering rifles' rapid rattle

Anthem for Doomed Youth

Wilfred Owen · 1917

Owen wrote the poem in September and October 1917 as a patient at Craiglockhart War Hospital in Edinburgh, where he was being treated for shell shock. He was twenty-four. Siegfried Sassoon, whom he met there, went over the draft with him, supplied the word anthem for the title and changed dead to doomed. The manuscript survives with both men's handwriting on it.
