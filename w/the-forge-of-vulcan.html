The Forge of Vulcan (Les Visions du chevalier Tondal, folio 27, detail)

Simon Marmion · 1475 · Tempera colors, gold leaf, gold paint, and ink on parchment · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

The borders of the manuscript carry the letters CM, for Margaret of York and her husband Charles the Bold, and she owned the book until 1503. Marmion worked at Valenciennes from 1458 until his death. A near contemporary called him the prince of illuminators.
