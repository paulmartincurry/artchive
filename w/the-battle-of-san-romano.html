The Battle of San Romano

Paolo Uccello · probably about 1438–40 · Egg tempera with some oil on wood (poplar) · 182 × 320 cm · The National Gallery, London

Free to use

This is one of three battle panels Uccello painted for Leonardo Bartolini Salimbeni around the time of his second marriage in 1438; the other two are now in the Uffizi and the Louvre. In 1484 Lorenzo de' Medici had all three forcibly removed from the Salimbeni town house to his own palace, and their arched tops were cut down to rectangles. The armour was laid in real silver leaf, now tarnished, and the vermilion of the banners has turned blue-grey. The National Gallery bought this panel in 1857.
