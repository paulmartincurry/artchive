War Mask

Maker unknown · 14th–16th century · Iron, gold, copper alloy · 19.5 × 14.6 × 5.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The mask is iron, decorated with gold, and weighs 346 grams; it is 19.5 centimetres high. It is held by the Met's department of arms and armor. Steven Kossak gave it from the Kronos Collections in 2022, to mark the museum's 150th anniversary.
