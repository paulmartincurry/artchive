Alexandre Dumas père

Nadar · 1855 · salted paper print from wet collodion negative · Cleveland Museum of Art

Nadar opened his photography studio in 1854, a year before this portrait, after years of drawing caricatures for the Paris papers. He was about thirty-five. The sitter, the novelist and playwright Alexandre Dumas, was a friend of his. The print measures 23.8 by 17.8 centimetres and came from the Bibliothèque Nationale.
