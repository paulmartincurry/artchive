Thirty-six Peaks of Mount Huang Recollected

Shitao · ca. 1705 · Hanging scroll, ink on paper · 205.9 × 78.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Shitao painted this recollection of the Huangshan peaks around 1705, when he was about sixty-three and two years from his death in Yangzhou. In his late years he is said to have greeted the Kangxi Emperor when the emperor visited the city. In a colophon of 1686 he had written that he followed neither the Southern nor the Northern school of painting, but always his own method. The painted paper is just over two metres tall.
