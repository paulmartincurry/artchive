Night, street, lamp, and pharmacy,
A dull, a meaningless light.

Night, Street, Lamp, and Pharmacy

Alexander Blok · October 10, 1912

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Blok was thirty-one. He was born in Saint Petersburg and died there. Nine years later he had stopped writing poetry altogether, and told a friend that all sounds had stopped. He fell ill in 1921 with asthma and scurvy. His doctors asked for him to be sent abroad, and the permission to leave was delivered three days after his death.
