The Drawbridge (Carceri d'invenzione, plate VII)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 55.5 × 41.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In the 1761 reworking, Piranesi filled various plates with new chains, cables, gallows and dimly seen instruments of torture. Robison's catalogue counts six states of this plate, and this impression is the fourth or the fifth.
