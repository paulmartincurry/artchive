Eclectus Roratus Polychloros

Edward Lear · c. 1830–32 · Watercolor and gouache (bodycolor) over graphite or chalk · 55.7 × 37.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Edward Lear began painting parrots in 1830, at eighteen, studying live birds at the London Zoo and in private collections. The Met dates this watercolour to about 1830 to 1832. His book of parrots, 42 hand-coloured lithographs issued to subscribers in 175 copies, sold only 125 subscriptions and lost money, and he ran out of funds after twelve of the fourteen parts he had planned.
