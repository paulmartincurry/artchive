Thomas Carlyle

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1867 · Albumen silver print · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Julia Margaret Cameron met many of the people she later photographed at Little Holland House in Kensington, where her sister Sara Prinsep kept a literary and artistic salon. She made this portrait of Thomas Carlyle in 1867. Critics of the time derided her softly focused images. She took around 900 photographs over twelve years.
