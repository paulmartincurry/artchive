Still Life with Fruit

Roger Fenton · 1860 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 35.2 × 43.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Roger Fenton's Crimean War photographs did not sell well, and afterwards he travelled over Britain making landscapes and still lifes. Two years after this one, in 1862, he sold his equipment, gave up photography entirely and went back to the law as a barrister. The print is 35.2 by 43.1 centimetres.
