The Repast of the Lion

Henri Rousseau (le Douanier) · ca. 1907 · Oil on canvas · 44 3/4 in. × 63 in. (113.7 × 160 cm) · The Met

Free to use

A tiger in a storm was the jungle picture Rousseau exhibited in 1891, and more than a decade went by before he took up the subject again. This canvas dates from about 1907, when he was around sixty-three. Picasso had come across one of his paintings being sold on the street as a canvas to be painted over, and in 1908 he held a banquet for Rousseau in his studio. Sam A. Lewisohn left the picture to the Metropolitan Museum in 1951.
