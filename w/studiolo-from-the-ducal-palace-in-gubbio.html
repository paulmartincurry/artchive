Studiolo from the Ducal Palace in Gubbio

Francesco di Giorgio Martini · ca. 1478–82 · Walnut, beech, rosewood, oak and fruitwoods in walnut base · H. 15 ft. 10 15/16 in., W. 16 ft. 11 15/16 in., D. 12 ft. 7 3/16 in. · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Federico da Montefeltro ordered this room in 1478 for a palace he was rebuilding in Gubbio. Francesco di Giorgio Martini designed it, and the brothers Giuliano and Benedetto da Maiano built it in their Florence workshop before it was carried to Gubbio. It stayed there until 1874, went to a villa in Frascati and then to a dealer in Venice, and was reassembled in New York between 1939 and 1941. Its paintings were removed in the 17th century.
