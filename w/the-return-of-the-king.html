I will not say: do not weep; for not all tears are an evil.

The Return of the King

J. R. R. Tolkien · 1955

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

In 1953 Tolkien proposed titles for the six books of The Lord of the Rings to his publisher, Rayner Unwin, who instead split the work into three volumes. This third volume, holding the fifth and sixth books and the appendices, was published in the UK on 20 October 1955.
