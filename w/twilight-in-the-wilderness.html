Twilight in the Wilderness

Frederic Edwin Church · 1860 · Oil on canvas · Unframed: 101.6 × 162.6 cm (40 × 64 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Church painted this in his New York studio from sketches made near Mount Katahdin in Maine nearly two years earlier. He was thirty-four, and in the same year he married and bought a farm near Hudson, New York. Instead of sending the picture to an annual exhibition, he showed it on its own at a gallery for seven weeks. It went through eleven owners, three generations of the de Forest family among them, before Cleveland bought it in 1965.
