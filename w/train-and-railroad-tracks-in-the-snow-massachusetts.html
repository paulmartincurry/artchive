Train and Railroad Tracks in the Snow, Massachusetts

Jack Delano · December 1940 or January 1941 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress gave this Massachusetts slide its title and dates it to December 1940 or January 1941. Delano was then a photographer for the Farm Security Administration. Later in 1941 he was sent to Puerto Rico for a few days and stayed months after Pearl Harbor; he settled there for good in 1946.
