Grand Canyon from South Rim, 1941

Ansel Adams · 1941 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

Ansel Adams set off on the road trip for the Interior Department's mural project with his friend Cedric and his son Michael. He planned to combine the government work with commissions for the U.S. Potash Company and Standard Oil, and kept some days for work of his own. His caption dates this view from the South Rim to 1941.
