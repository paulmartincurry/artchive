The Art of the People: An Art Made by the People

William Morris · 1879

By 1879 Morris had been running a decorating firm for eighteen years. He started it in 1861 with Edward Burne-Jones, Dante Gabriel Rossetti, Philip Webb and others, took sole control in 1875 and renamed it Morris & Co. He designed its tapestries, wallpapers, fabrics, furniture and stained glass himself.
