Daniel in the Lions' Den

Peter Paul Rubens · c. 1614/1616 · Oil on canvas · 224.2 × 330.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The lions are life-size. Rubens traded the painting in 1618 to Sir Dudley Carleton, the English ambassador at The Hague, in exchange for antique sculpture. Carleton presented it to Charles I, who hung it in the Bear Gallery at Whitehall. It was later misattributed to two of Rubens's collaborators before it came to Washington in 1965.
