Circus Sideshow (Parade de cirque)

Georges Seurat · 1887–88 · Oil on canvas · 39 1/4 × 59 in. (99.7 × 149.9 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The scene is the sideshow of the Circus Corvi, a travelling fair set up on the place de la Nation in Paris. It was Seurat's first night scene, and he worked on the subject for nearly six years. When it was shown, marked for sale, at the Salon des Indépendants in 1888, it hung beside his much larger Models and drew little notice. It did not sell, and in 1900 his brother and brother-in-law let it go, together with another picture, for 1,000 francs.
