Tile

Grueby Faience Company · ca. 1902 · Glazed earthenware · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The rabbit was press-moulded into the clay, and raised partitions keep the matt glazes of blue, green and yellow-green apart. The tile is 10.4 centimetres square. The initials E.R. are painted in green on it. It was given to the museum by Marcia and William Goodman.
