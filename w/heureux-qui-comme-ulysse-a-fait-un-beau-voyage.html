Happy, the man who finds sweet journey’s end,
Like Ulysses, or he of the Golden Fleece,
Returning home, well-travelled, wise, to Greece:
To live life out, among his own again!

Heureux qui, comme Ulysse, a fait un beau voyage (Les Regrets)

Joachim du Bellay · 1558

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Du Bellay published Les Regrets in 1558: 191 sonnets in alexandrines. They come out of his stay in Italy from 1553 to 1557, which left him disillusioned, and he soon missed France.
