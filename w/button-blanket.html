Button Blanket

Maker unknown · c. 1880 · Wool trade cloth, shell buttons · 142.2 × 177.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A Kagani Haida blanket from Prince of Wales Island, Alaska, made of wool trade cloth and shell buttons. Such blankets are worn over the shoulders, so that the crest hangs on the wearer's back. The blankets were first acquired from Hudson's Bay Company traders in the middle of the nineteenth century.
