“In vain have I struggled. It will not do. My feelings will not be repressed. You must allow me to tell you how ardently I admire and love you.”

Pride and Prejudice — In vain have I struggled (Ch. XXXIV)

Jane Austen · 1813

Austen wrote the novel when she was 20 to 21, under the working title First Impressions. It was published in 1813. She sold the copyright to Thomas Egerton for £110, having asked for £150.
