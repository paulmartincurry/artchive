Sí, se puede

Dolores Huerta · 1972

Spanish for yes, it can be done. Dolores Huerta, co-founder of the United Farm Workers, created it in 1972 during César Chávez's 25-day fast in Phoenix, Arizona. It is the union's motto, and a registered trademark of the United Farm Workers.
