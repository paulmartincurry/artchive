Home of a Rebel Sharpshooter

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1863 · Albumen print · sheet 7½ × 9½ in. · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The photograph was staged. Timothy O'Sullivan, then assistant to Alexander Gardner, took it after the Battle of Gettysburg in July 1863. It is one of six photographs of the same body, which had first lain on the south edge of Devil's Den before being moved next to this stone wall. The rifle was not a sharpshooter's and was likely a prop. Gardner published the picture after the war in his Photographic Sketch Book and claimed credit for it.
