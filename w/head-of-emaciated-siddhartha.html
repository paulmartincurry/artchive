Head of Emaciated Siddhartha

Maker unknown · 2nd–3rd century · Gray schist · 15.7 × 9 × 7.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

James and Marilynn Alsdorf owned this head by 1990. It went on long-term loan to the Art Institute in 1997, was publicly promised to the museum that September, and was finally given in 2015. It is 15.7 centimetres high. In 1998 it travelled to Hong Kong for the exhibition In the Footsteps of the Buddha.
