Wilson Bentley Photomicrograph of Stellar Snowflake No. 1018

Wilson A. Bentley · c. 1890 · Albumen print · Smithsonian Institution Archives, Washington

Free to use

Wilson Bentley grew up on his family's farm in Jericho, Vermont, and was given an old microscope by his mother when he was fifteen. He fixed a bellows camera to a compound microscope and photographed his first snowflake on 15 January 1885, when he was nineteen. In 1903 he sent prints of his snowflakes to the Smithsonian, hoping they might interest its Secretary, Samuel P. Langley.
