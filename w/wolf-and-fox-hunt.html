Wolf and Fox Hunt

Peter Paul Rubens · ca. 1616 · Oil on canvas · 96 5/8 × 148 1/8 in. (245.4 × 376.2 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Assistants worked on this canvas, but the wolves were painted entirely by Rubens. He was thirty-eight or thirty-nine, and it began a period in which he concentrated on hunting scenes. Even after being trimmed at the top and left to fit a client's house, it is 245.4 by 376.2 centimetres. The Met bought it in 1910.
