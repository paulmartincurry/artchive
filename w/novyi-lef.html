Novyi Lef (New Lef)

Alexander Rodchenko · 1927–1928 · Periodicals with photomontage wrappers; complete set of 24 numbers in 10 parts · 22.8 × 15 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Novyi Lef ran in Moscow for 24 numbers in 1927 and 1928, with Mayakovsky as editor and Rodchenko designing the photomontage covers. Its pages carried work by Pasternak, Gorky and Kruchenykh, and pictures by Varvara Stepanova, the Stenberg brothers and Dziga Vertov. After a falling-out with Mayakovsky, the writer Sergei Tretyakov took over as editor in August 1928, and the magazine turned away from poetry towards prose.
