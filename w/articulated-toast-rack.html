Articulated Toast Rack

Christopher Dresser · 1881–1883 · silver plated, metal · H × W × D: 12.7 × 15 × 10.5 cm · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The design was registered with the British Patent Office on 9 May 1881, when Christopher Dresser was forty-six, and the registry mark is stamped under the base. Hukin & Heath of Birmingham made this rack and the Norwich retailer E.S. Pegler sold it. Its seven sets of parallel posts are linked by rods and ball joints. Toast racks to Dresser's designs are now manufactured by Alessi.
