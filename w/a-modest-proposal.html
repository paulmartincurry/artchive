I grant this food will be somewhat dear, and therefore very proper for landlords, who, as they have already devoured most of the parents, seem to have the best title to the children.

A Modest Proposal

Jonathan Swift · 1729

Swift was sixty-one when this essay appeared, and he had been dean of St Patrick's Cathedral in Dublin since 1713. Like all his works it first came out anonymously or under a false name. He set it out in the textbook order of argument taught in his day, which came from the Roman rhetorician Quintilian. It may have been in part an answer to a 1728 essay on foundlings by his rival Daniel Defoe.
