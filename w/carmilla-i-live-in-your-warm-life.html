I live in your warm life, and you shall die—die, sweetly die—into mine.

Carmilla — I Live in Your Warm Life (Ch. IV)

Joseph Sheridan Le Fanu · 1872

Carmilla is set in Styria, in a secluded Austrian castle. Le Fanu had studied law at Trinity College Dublin and was called to the bar in 1839, but he never practised and soon turned to journalism; from 1840 he owned several newspapers, among them the Dublin Evening Mail. His great-uncle was the playwright Richard Brinsley Sheridan.
