Fragment 31

Sappho of Lesbos · ca. 600 BCE

Translated by Anne Carson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Very little about the woman who wrote this is certain. Sappho came from a wealthy family on the island of Lesbos, and one ancient chronicle records that she went into exile in Sicily some time between 604 and 595 BC. In her own surviving poetry she spells her name Psappho.
