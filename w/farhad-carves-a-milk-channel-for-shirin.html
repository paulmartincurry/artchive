Farhad Carves a Milk Channel for Shirin (Khamsa of Nizami)

Shaikh Zada · 1524–25 (dated 931 AH) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · Painting 21.3 × 12.9 cm; page 32.1 × 22.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This copy of Nizami's Khamsa is dated 931 AH, 1524–25, and was made in Herat; the painting is by Shaikh Zada. He worked from Herat and often collaborated with Sultan Muhammad in Tabriz, the two probably communicating by courier, and he later left Herat for Bukhara. Alexander Smith Cochran gave the leaves to the Met in 1913.
