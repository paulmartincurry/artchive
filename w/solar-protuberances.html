Solar Protuberances (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot observed these prominences at 9.40 in the morning on 5 May 1873, the year after he joined the staff of the Harvard College Observatory. The Sun was his particular interest, and in 1875 he discovered what he called veiled spots on it.
