When shall I reach the top and hold
All mountains in a single glance?

A View of T'ai-shan

Du Fu · before 770; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Du Fu lived from 712 to 770 and served the Tang state as an official as well as writing. Chinese poems are typically concise, the historian William Hung observed, leaving out context an informed reader of the time could be assumed to know.
