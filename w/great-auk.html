Great Auk (The Birds of America, Plate 341)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1836 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The great auk is one of the birds in the book that are now extinct. All but 80 of the book's copper plates were later melted down, after Lucy Audubon, short of money, sold them for scrap to the Phelps Dodge Corporation.
