The Young Beggar

Bartolomé Esteban Murillo · c. 1645–1650 · Oil on canvas · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

This is the first known picture of a street child by Murillo, painted around 1645–1650. Its other name, The Lice-Ridden Boy, comes from the boy delousing himself. It was once kept in the royal collection of Louis XVI and is now in the Louvre.
