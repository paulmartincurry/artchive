Pain has an element of blank;
It cannot recollect
When it began, or if there were
A day when it was not.

Pain has an element of blank

Emily Dickinson · 1890

Dickinson's chief physician gave the cause of her death as Bright's disease and its duration as two and a half years. She was laid in a white coffin, and at her request it was not driven but carried through fields of buttercups to the family plot at West Cemetery.
