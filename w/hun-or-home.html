Hun or Home?

Henry Patrick Raleigh · 1918 · Color lithograph on cream wove paper · 75.2 × 49.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Liberty Bonds were sold in the United States to support the Allied cause in the First World War, and the Treasury ran an aggressive campaign to sell them. Well-known artists made the posters, film and stage stars hosted rallies, and a division of the Committee on Public Information called the Four Minute Men gave speeches. Edwards & Deutsch printed Henry Patrick Raleigh's design.
