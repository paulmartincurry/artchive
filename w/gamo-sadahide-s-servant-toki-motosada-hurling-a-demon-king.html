Gamo Sadahide's Servant, Toki Motosada, Hurling a Demon King to the Ground at Mount Inahana (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1890 · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The print appears to illustrate an obscure legend. In it, Toki Motosada, camped for the night at a temple in Kai Province, went to look into strange happenings and met a crowd of spirits and goblins, the largest in the form of a temple guardian. Once he threw it down, the rest, dancing skeletons and moths among them, vanished. Yoshitoshi made the print in 1890, two years before his death.
