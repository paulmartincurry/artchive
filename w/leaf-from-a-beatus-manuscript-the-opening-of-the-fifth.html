Leaf from a Beatus Manuscript: The Opening of the Fifth Seal

Maker unknown · c. 1180 · Tempera, gold, and ink on parchment · 44.4 × 30 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Beatus gave the year 801 as his guess for the end of the world, while warning that it was folly to guess. This leaf from a copy of his commentary was painted about four centuries later and measures 44.4 by 30 centimetres.
