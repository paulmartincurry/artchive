Woman in the Moon, frame at 1:20:21

Fritz Lang · 1929 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

In the film the rocket Friede is built inside a tall building and moved out on rails to the launch area, and before take-off the intertitles count down the seconds from six to now, often cited as the first countdown to zero. The rocket scientist Hermann Oberth advised on the film; he had meant to build a working rocket for it, but time and technical limits stopped him. It was shot at the UFA studios in Neubabelsberg between October 1928 and June 1929.
