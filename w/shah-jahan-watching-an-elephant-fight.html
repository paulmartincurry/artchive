Shah Jahan Watching an Elephant Fight (Padshahnama)

Bulaqi · probably 1639 · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · 38.2 × 24.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Padshahnama was the official history of Shah Jahan's reign. In his eighth regnal year Shah Jahan asked Muhammad Amin Qazvini to write it, and Qazvini finished his account of the first ten years in 1636; the work then passed to Abdul Hamid Lahori, who completed his two volumes in 1648. Bulaqi's painting is dated probably 1639, and the Met bought it in 1989.
