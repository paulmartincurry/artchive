Portrait of a Man (Self Portrait?)

Jan van Eyck · 1433 · Oil on oak panel · National Gallery, London

Free to use

Van Eyck completed this small panel in Bruges in 1433. The frame is original, and the inscription at its top carries his motto Als Ich Can, his first known use of it. The head is about one-third life-size. The painting probably came to England with Thomas Howard, Earl of Arundel, during his exile in Antwerp in 1642–1644, and it has been in the National Gallery since 1851.
