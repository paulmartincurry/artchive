Woman before an Aquarium

Henri Matisse · 1921–23 · Oil on canvas · 81.2 × 100.2 cm (31 15/16 × 39 7/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In Algeria and Morocco Matisse had collected pottery, textiles and tiles. He painted this canvas years later in Nice, where he had moved in 1917. The Paris dealer Bernheim-Jeune bought it from him on 12 June 1923 and two days later sold it for 11,200 francs to Frederick Clay Bartlett of Chicago, who gave it to the Art Institute in 1926.
