Now I shall go, to overtake that killer of a dear life, Hektor; then I will accept my own death, at whatever time Zeus wishes to bring it about, and the other immortals.

The Iliad (Book XVIII)

Homer · ca. 750 BCE

Translated by Richmond Lattimore · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Iliad runs to 15,693 lines in its standard edition, divided into 24 books. Professional reciters called rhapsodes performed it at Greek festivals such as the Panathenaia. The oldest complete copy to survive is Venetus A, a Byzantine manuscript made in the tenth century AD, with notes drawn from the lost works of four ancient grammarians. The poem was first printed in Florence in 1489.
