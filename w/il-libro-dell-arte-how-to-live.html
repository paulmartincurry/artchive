Il Libro dell'Arte: How to Live

Cennino Cennini · c. 1390s

Translated by Christiana J. Herringham, 1899

Alongside advice on how a young painter should live, the book covers pigments, brushes, drawing, panel painting, fresco, painting on fabric and casting. Its passing mention of oil painting helped dispel the story, spread by Giorgio Vasari, that Jan van Eyck invented it. The art forger Eric Hebborn followed its methods for preparing panels and tinting paper.
