Suprematist Composition: White on White

Kazimir Malevich · 1918 · Oil on canvas · 79.4 × 79.4 cm · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Malevich painted this the year after the October Revolution, a white square tilted on a ground of slightly warmer white. In 1927 he took it to Berlin for an exhibition and, returning to Leningrad, left it with the architect Hugo Häring. It passed to Alexander Dorner of the Provinzialmuseum in Hanover, who kept it in storage once the Nazis took power. Malevich never asked for it back and died in 1935, the year it went on show at the Museum of Modern Art in New York.
