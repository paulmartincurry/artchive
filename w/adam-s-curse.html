Adam's Curse (opening)

W. B. Yeats · 1903

Yeats drew on a meeting with Maud Gonne and her sister Kathleen Pilcher. The poem was written just before the marriage of Maud Gonne and John MacBride, and he included it in In the Seven Woods, published in 1903.
