The Snow Queen Sat in the Very Middle of It When She Sat at Home (The Snow Queen)

Edmund Dulac · published 1911 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Hans Christian Andersen first published The Snow Queen on 21 December 1844, divided into seven chapters, unlike his other tales. According to the writer Carole Rosen, he modelled the icy queen on the Swedish singer Jenny Lind after she turned him down. Dulac's plates for Stories from Hans Andersen appeared in 1911, the year he married his second wife, Elsa Arnalice Bignardi.
