Vieil océan, aux vagues de cristal, tu ressembles proportionnellement à ces marques azurées que l'on voit sur le dos meurtri des mousses; … Je te salue, vieil océan!

Les Chants de Maldoror — Vieil océan (Canto I)

Comte de Lautréamont (Isidore Ducasse) · 1868

The first canto, which holds this passage, was printed in August 1868 and published anonymously, at Ducasse's own expense, as a booklet of thirty-two pages. He had been born in Montevideo and sent to school in France at thirteen. He settled in Paris at the end of 1867 after a stay with his father in Montevideo, and it is possible that he worked on the canto during the ocean journey. He died at the age of 24.
