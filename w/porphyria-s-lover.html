And thus we sit together now,
And all night long we have not stirred,
And yet God has not said a word!

Porphyria's Lover (closing)

Robert Browning · 1836

The poem first appeared, as Porphyria, in the January 1836 issue of Monthly Repository. Browning republished it in Dramatic Lyrics in 1842, paired with Johannes Agricola in Meditation under the title Madhouse Cells. It is his first short dramatic monologue.
