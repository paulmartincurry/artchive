Night Scene in the Yoshiwara

Katsushika Ōi (O-Ei) · ca. 1850s · Color on paper · Ōta Memorial Museum of Art, Tokyo

Free to use

Ōi worked parts of her own name into this painting, spread across three lanterns marked O, i and Ei. She was Hokusai's daughter by his second wife and trained as his apprentice. She married the painter Minamizawa Tomei in 1824 and divorced him three years later, then moved back in with her father and worked beside him, and she never remarried. She also made and sold small keshi ningyō dolls for a living. Few of her works survive, and no prints.
