Early Morning After a Storm at Sea

Winslow Homer · 1900–1903 · oil on canvas · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The composition began as a watercolour, and Homer took it up in oil nearly twenty years later. He waited for the right weather and painted it in four sessions spread over two years. The coast at Prouts Neck, Maine, was his main subject in his last decades. He signed this canvas with the year 1902; the dealer Knoedler sold it in 1903, and Cleveland bought it in 1924.
