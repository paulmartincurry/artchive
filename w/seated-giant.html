Seated Giant

Francisco de Goya · by 1818 (possibly 1814–18) · Burnished aquatint, scaper, roulette, lavis (along the top of the landscape and within the landscape) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya made this print in his late sixties or early seventies, in a period when he kept a number of his works private. From the late 1810s he lived almost alone outside Madrid, in a farmhouse he had turned into a studio. The plate is 28.4 centimetres tall, and the museum bought this impression in 1935.
