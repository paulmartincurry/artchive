Scolopendrium Vulgare

Anna Atkins · 1852–54 · cyanotype · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The plant is a hart's-tongue fern, and its Latin name was written in negative so that it printed along with the fern. This sheet belonged to Anne Dixon, the friend with whom Atkins made several albums of plants, ferns, feathers and lace. Atkins had first heard of photography through her father, who sat on the Royal Society's Committee on Papers; Talbot laid out the details of his process to that committee in February 1839.
