Federal Theatre Project Presents Day Is Darkness

Maker unknown · 1939 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

George Fess's anti-Nazi play Day Is Darkness was staged at the Ocean Park Municipal Auditorium for audiences in Boyle Heights, California, sponsored by the Boyle Heights Progressive Benevolent Club, and part of the poster's text is in Yiddish. The Library of Congress dates it to 1939. Congress ended the Federal Theatre Project's funding on 30 June that year, putting 8,000 people out of work.
