Bahram Gur Slays a Dragon (Great Mongol Shahnama)

Maker unknown · 1330–35 · Opaque watercolor, ink, and gold on paper · Image 19.5 × 29.5 cm; sheet 45.8 × 34.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The dealer Georges Demotte owned this leaf around 1910. When he could not get his price for the whole manuscript, he separated its paintings for sale, pulling some pages apart so that both sides could be sold and hiring calligraphers to add text, often from the wrong part of the poem. Cleveland bought the leaf from Heeramaneck Galleries in New York in 1943.
