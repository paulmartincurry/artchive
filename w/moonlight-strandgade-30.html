Moonlight, Strandgade 30

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1900–1906 · Oil on canvas · 41 × 51.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hammershøi lived with his wife at Strandgade 30 in Copenhagen from 1898 to 1909, and many of his interiors were painted there. The Met bought this one in 2012.
