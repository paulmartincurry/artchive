He created the imhullu-wind (evil wind), the tempest, the whirlwind

Enuma Elish (Tablet IV)

Babylonian Temple Priests · ca. 1200 BCE

Translated by Stephanie Dalley · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The poem has about a thousand lines, written in Akkadian on seven clay tablets that each hold between 115 and 170 lines of cuneiform. Austen Henry Layard recovered it in fragments in 1849 from the ruined Library of Ashurbanipal at Nineveh, and George Smith first published a form of it in 1876. Most of the fifth tablet has never been recovered. The earliest manuscript, excavated at Assur, is dated to the 9th century BCE.
