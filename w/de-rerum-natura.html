De Rerum Natura (Book II)

Lucretius · ca. 55 BCE

Translated by William Ellery Leonard, 1916

De rerum natura is the only known work of Lucretius: some 7,400 hexameter lines in six untitled books. The earliest recorded comment on it is in a letter from Cicero to his brother Quintus. No manuscripts are known from between the ninth and the fifteenth centuries. Poggio Bracciolini found a copy in January 1417, probably in the Benedictine library at Fulda, and the first printed edition was produced in Brescia in 1473.
