One Hundred Horses

Giuseppe Castiglione (Lang Shining) · 1728 · Ink on silk · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

The scroll is 7.76 metres wide. Giuseppe Castiglione was a Jesuit missionary from Italy, and the imperial court commissioned the subject from him in 1724; he finished it in 1728 for the Yongzheng emperor. A preparatory sketch from around 1723 to 1725 is in the Metropolitan Museum of Art. The sketch has two nude figures, and in the finished painting he kept only one, the less conspicuous of the two.
