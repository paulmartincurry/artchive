Perseverance's First High-Resolution Panorama

Perseverance rover, NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS · 22 February 2021 · Rover camera mosaic

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS

The panorama is stitched from 79 pictures taken on the afternoon of 22 February 2021, the rover's fourth day on Mars, by turning its mast a full circle. Perseverance had landed in Jezero Crater four days earlier, after launching from Cape Canaveral on 30 July 2020. The ridge at the centre is the front of the crater's ancient river delta, about 2 kilometres away; at that distance the zoom camera can make out features about 50 centimetres across.
