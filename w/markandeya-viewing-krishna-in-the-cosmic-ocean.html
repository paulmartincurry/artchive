Markandeya Viewing Krishna in the Cosmic Ocean

Maker unknown · c. 1680 · Gum tempera on paper · 15.2 × 10 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painter, an anonymous artist at the court of Basohli, used a web of concentric abstract shapes for the cosmic ocean. In the story, the sage Markandeya, cursed with immortality, drifts alone after the world has ended and finds the infant Krishna on a banyan leaf. The painted image is only 15.2 by 10 centimetres. Sherman E. Lee, director of the Cleveland Museum of Art from 1958 to 1983, gave it with his wife Ruth in 1967.
