How do I love thee? Let me count the ways.
I love thee to the depth and breadth and height
My soul can reach.

Sonnets from the Portuguese 43

Elizabeth Barrett Browning · 1850

Robert Browning wrote to Elizabeth Barrett after reading her 1844 volume Poems, and her cousin John Kenyon arranged for him to visit her rooms on 20 May 1845. She was thirty-nine and spent most of her time in an upstairs room at 50 Wimpole Street, London. The Sonnets from the Portuguese were written after that meeting. The courtship and marriage were kept secret from her father, who disinherited her after the wedding.
