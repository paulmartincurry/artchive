Much madness is divinest sense
To a discerning eye;
Much sense the starkest madness.

Much madness is divinest sense

Emily Dickinson · 1890

Only 10 of Dickinson's nearly 1,800 poems were published in her lifetime, and few people around her knew she wrote. After her death in 1886 her younger sister Lavinia found the poems in her desk. The first selection came out in 1890.
