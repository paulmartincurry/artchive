The Astronomer

Johannes Vermeer · 1668 · Oil on canvas · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Vermeer completed the canvas in 1668. Until 1800 it was always kept together with his Geographer, and the two were sold as a pair in Rotterdam on 27 April 1713. A Paris dealer sold it to the banker Alphonse de Rothschild between 1881 and 1888. In 1940 a Nazi task force seized it from the home of his son Édouard.
