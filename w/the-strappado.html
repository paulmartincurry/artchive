The Strappado (The Miseries and Misfortunes of War, plate 10)

Jacques Callot · 1633 · Etching; second state of three (Lieure) · sheet: 10.7 x 21.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In plates 9 to 14 the marauding soldiers are rounded up and put to public torture and execution; this is plate 10. Each print in the series has a six-line verse caption below the image, written by the print collector Michel de Marolles.
