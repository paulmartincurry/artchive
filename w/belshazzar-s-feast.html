Belshazzar's Feast (First Plate)

John Martin · 1826 · Mezzotint with etching; proof · Image: 47 × 71.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Martin sold the painting of Belshazzar's Feast in 1821 for 1,000 guineas to William Collins, who made him agree not to reproduce it. When Martin prepared this mezzotint in 1826, Collins sued. Martin successfully claimed he was reproducing his own preliminary sketches. He was his own publisher, and this proof is dated 1 June 1826.
