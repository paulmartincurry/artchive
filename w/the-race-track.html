The Race Track (Death on a Pale Horse)

Albert Pinkham Ryder · ca. 1896–1908 · Oil on canvas · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Ryder rarely signed his paintings and never dated them, and he often worked on one for ten years or more. He built them up in layers of paint, resin and varnish, sometimes painting into wet varnish, and he added candle wax, bitumen and oils that do not dry. His paintings have darkened and cracked as a result, and some are still soft a century later. He completed fewer than two hundred.
