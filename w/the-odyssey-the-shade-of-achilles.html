The Odyssey — The Shade of Achilles (Book XI)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by Samuel Butler

The eleventh book of the Odyssey was called the Nekyia in antiquity, after a rite by which ghosts were called up and questioned. Odysseus sacrifices a ram and a ewe so that the dead will gather round him. He goes only as far as the entrance of the underworld; Virgil's Aeneas, later, walks through it.
