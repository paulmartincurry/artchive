The Blue Room (La Chambre bleue)

Suzanne Valadon · 1923 · Oil on canvas · Centre Pompidou, Paris

The canvas is 90 by 116 centimetres. Suzanne Valadon was in her late fifties when she painted it; she had started out at fifteen as an artists' model in Montmartre and never attended the academy. In 1923 she also had a solo exhibition at the Galerie Bernheim-Jeune in Paris.
