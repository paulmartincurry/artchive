The Moon, it is said, sent once an Insect to Men, saying, “Go thou to Men, and tell them, ‘As I die, and dying live, so ye shall also die, and dying live.

The Origin of Death

Nama (Khoekhoe) tradition · Printed 1864

Translated by W. H. I. Bleek, 1864

Wilhelm Bleek travelled to Cape Town in 1857 to catalogue the private library of Sir George Grey, Governor of the Cape. He asked missionaries and travellers for examples of African literature, and in 1861 the Reverend W. Kronlein sent him Namaqua texts.
