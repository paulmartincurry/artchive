Carnival of Souls, frame at 1:17:40

Herk Harvey · 1962 · Film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Harvey had the idea for the film after driving past the abandoned Saltair Pavilion in Salt Lake City, on his way home to Kansas from a shoot in California. He told the writer John Clifford, a colleague at Centron, that the last scene had to be ghouls dancing in its ballroom and the rest was up to him. Clifford wrote the script in three weeks. Harvey rented the pavilion for $50.
