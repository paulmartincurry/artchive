Are you too deeply occupied to say if my verse is alive?

Letter to T. W. Higginson (15 April 1862)

Emily Dickinson · 1862

Dickinson was thirty-one in April 1862. In a letter to Higginson she named Keats and Robert and Elizabeth Barrett Browning as her poets, and for prose Ruskin, Sir Thomas Browne and the Book of Revelation. Thomas Wentworth Higginson went on to edit the first selection of her poems, with Mabel Loomis Todd, in 1890, four years after her death.
