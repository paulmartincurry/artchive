Notebooks: The Cavern

Leonardo da Vinci · c. 1480

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

Leonardo was about twenty-eight and still working in Florence, eight years after he qualified as a master in the Guild of Saint Luke. An early anonymous biographer has him living with the Medici in 1480 and often working in the garden of the Piazza San Marco. Two years later he was sent to Milan.
