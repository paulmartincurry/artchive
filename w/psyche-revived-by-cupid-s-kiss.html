Psyche Revived by Cupid's Kiss

Antonio Canova · 1787–1793 · Marble · Department of Sculptures of the Louvre

Canova was raised by his grandfather, a stonemason. He was about thirty when Colonel John Campbell ordered this group in 1787. A handle near one of Psyche's feet let the statue be turned on its base, and a critic of the time complained that there was no single point from which to see both faces. Joachim Murat acquired the marble in 1800, and it entered the Louvre in 1824. Canova carved a second version, which Prince Yusupov bought from him in Rome in 1796.
