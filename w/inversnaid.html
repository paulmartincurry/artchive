What would the world be, once bereft
Of wet and of wildness? Let them be left,
O let them be left, wildness and wet;
Long live the weeds and the wilderness yet.

Inversnaid

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1881; published 1918

Inversnaid is a small community on the east bank of Loch Lomond, near the north end of the loch. Its hotel stands beside Arklet Falls and was built in 1790 as a hunting lodge for the Duke of Montrose. Hopkins is listed among its guests, along with Wordsworth, Walter Scott, Queen Victoria and Nathaniel Hawthorne.
