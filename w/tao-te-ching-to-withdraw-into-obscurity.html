When the work is done, and one's name is becoming distinguished, to withdraw into obscurity is the way of Heaven.

Tao Te Ching — To Withdraw into Obscurity (Ch. 9)

Laozi (Lao Tzu) · 4th–3rd century BC

Translated by James Legge

First translated into French in the early nineteenth century, it is among the most translated texts in world literature, after the Bible. James Legge, who made this version, was the first Professor of Chinese at Oxford, from 1876 to 1897.
