This grew; I gave commands;

My Last Duchess (lines 21-56)

Robert Browning · 1842

When this poem first appeared, in Dramatic Lyrics in 1842, its title was simply Italy. Browning was about thirty, living in his parents' house and financially dependent on them; his father paid for his poems to be published. The speaker is taken to be Alfonso II d'Este, Duke of Ferrara, whose first wife, Lucrezia de' Medici, married him at thirteen and died at sixteen. The painter and the sculptor named in the poem are invented.
