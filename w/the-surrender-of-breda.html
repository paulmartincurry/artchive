The Surrender of Breda

Diego Velázquez · 1634–1635 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Velázquez painted the canvas in 1634 and 1635 for Philip IV's palace of Buen Retiro in Madrid, where it hung in the Hall of Realms with nineteen other paintings. The Spanish general in it, Ambrogio Spinola, had taken Breda on 5 June 1625, and Velázquez had travelled to Italy with him.
