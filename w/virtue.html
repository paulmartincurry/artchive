Sweet day, so cool, so calm, so bright!
The bridal of the earth and sky—
The dew shall weep thy fall to-night;
For thou must die.

Virtue

George Herbert · 1633

In 1629 Herbert decided to enter the priesthood, and the next year he was appointed rector of the rural parish of Fugglestone St Peter with Bemerton, near Salisbury. While at Bemerton he revised and added to his collection of poems entitled The Temple. It was published in 1633.
