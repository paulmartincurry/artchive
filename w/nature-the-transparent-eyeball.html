Standing on the bare ground, — my head bathed by the blithe air, and uplifted into infinite space, — all mean egotism vanishes. I become a transparent eye-ball; I am nothing; I see all; the currents of the Universal Being circulate through me; I am part or particle of God.

Nature — The transparent eyeball (Ch. 1)

Ralph Waldo Emerson · 1836

Nature was Emerson's first published essay. He had resigned as a pastor in Boston four years earlier, and in 1833 he gave his first public lecture, on natural history, built on a visit to the Jardin des Plantes in Paris. He was living in Concord, Massachusetts, in a house he had bought in July 1835.
