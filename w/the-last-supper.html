The Last Supper

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1495–1498 · Tempera on gesso · Santa Maria delle Grazie, Milan

Free to use

Leonardo did not use the proven method for painting on walls. He worked in tempera, the medium of panel painting, over a stone wall sealed with gesso, pitch and mastic, which let him revise as he went. He worked on it from about 1495 and finished on 9 February 1498, though not continuously, and the convent's prior complained about the delay. By 1517 the paint was starting to flake. A doorway was cut through the bottom of it in 1652, and on 15 August 1943 Allied bombing struck the room while sandbags protected the wall.
