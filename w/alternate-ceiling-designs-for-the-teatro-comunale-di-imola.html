Alternate Ceiling Designs for the Teatro Comunale di Imola

Maker unknown · 1851–1856 · Pen and ink, brush and watercolor on paper · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

The design is for a horseshoe-shaped chamber with ceiling panels that open to imitate the sky, and the right half of the sheet gives an octagonal version. A ducal coat of arms is drawn below. The sheet is 41.1 by 36.3 centimetres.
