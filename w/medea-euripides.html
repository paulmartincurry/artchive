I know indeed what evil I intend to do,
But stronger than all my afterthoughts is my fury,

Medea

Euripides · 431 BCE

Translated by Rex Warner · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Euripides was about forty-nine when this play was staged in Athens. Other tellings of the story give Medea as many as fourteen children; his version has two sons and does not name them. Of the ninety or so plays credited to him, nineteen survive more or less complete, and in his whole career he won the festival prize only five times, once after his death.
