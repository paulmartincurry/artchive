Broadsheet on the Comet Seen in Mexico in November 1899

José Guadalupe Posada · c. 1899 · Zincograph and letterpress on pink paper · 20 × 29.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This sheet, printed by zincograph and letterpress on pink paper, pairs news of a comet seen over Mexico in November 1899 with the words of a song. Posada was forty-seven. He had learned to engrave in lead and zinc after moving to Mexico City at the end of 1888, and he worked for the publisher Antonio Vanegas Arroyo, whose broadsides covered the news of the day.
