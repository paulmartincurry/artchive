Business men boast of their skill and cunning
But in philosophy they are like little children.
Bragging to each other of successful depredations
They neglect to consider the ultimate fate of the body.

Business Men

Chen Zi'ang · before 702; English 1918

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Chen Zi'ang was an adviser to the Empress Wu Zetian and held that poetry should reflect real life; much of his work carries a strain of social comment. Some have suggested that his writing brought on his persecution by Wu Sansi.
