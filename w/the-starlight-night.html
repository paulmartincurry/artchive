Look at the stars! look, look up at the skies!
O look at all the fire-folk sitting in the air!
The bright boroughs, the circle-citadels there!

The Starlight Night

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

Hopkins dated his first draft of this sonnet 24 February 1877, a day after his first draft of God's Grandeur, while he was training as a Jesuit at St Beuno's in Wales. He had once given up poetry, feeling that his love of it kept him from devoting himself wholly to religion, and took it up again in 1875, when his superior asked for a poem on the wreck of a German ship. A version of this sonnet was printed in Miles's anthology Poets and Poetry of the Century.
