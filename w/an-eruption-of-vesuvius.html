An Eruption of Vesuvius

Johan Christian Dahl · 1824 · Oil on canvas · 94 × 139.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Prince Christian Frederik of Denmark invited Johan Christian Dahl to join him on the Gulf of Naples in 1820. Dahl married Emilie von Bloch quickly and left for Italy the next day, staying ten months; he was in the Bay of Naples when Vesuvius was active. He painted this canvas, nearly a metre and a half wide, in 1824, when he was living in Dresden.
