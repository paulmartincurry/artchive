Sailor and Girl at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, Washington, D.C.

John Collier Jr. · May 1943 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Collier made this transparency in May 1943, shortly before he left the Office of War Information to serve in the Merchant Marine. A car accident at the age of eight had left him with brain injuries and hearing loss, and he never completed school beyond a third-grade level. Roy Stryker had hired him for the Farm Security Administration in 1941, probably through Dorothea Lange.
