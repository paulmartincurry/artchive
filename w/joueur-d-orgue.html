Joueur d'orgue (Organ Player)

Eugène Atget · 1898/99 · Gelatin silver printing out print · Image/paper: 23 × 17.8 cm (9 1/16 × 7 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Before photography, Atget was an actor with a travelling company, and he gave up the stage in 1887 after an infection of his vocal cords. He worked with a wooden bellows camera on glass plates whose slow emulsion needed long exposures, which is why moving figures in his pictures are sometimes blurred. This print came to the museum as part of the Julien Levy Collection.
