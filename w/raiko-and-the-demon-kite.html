Raiko (Minamoto no Yorimitsu) and the Demon Kite

Totoya Hokkei · c. 1825 · Color woodblock print; shikishiban surimono · 21 × 18.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Totoya Hokkei made at least eight hundred surimono in the 1820s and 1830s, and he illustrated about a hundred books, among them a book of sketches called Hokkei Manga in the manner of the Hokusai Manga. This print is about 21 by 18 centimetres, the square shikishi format.
