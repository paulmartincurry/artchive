Suido Bridge and Surugadai (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 36 × 24.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The carp streamers place the scene at the Boys' Festival on the fifth day of the fifth month, the same month in which the censors approved the print in 1857. The view from Surugadai takes in the Kanda River, Suidō Bridge, Edo Castle and Mount Fuji. Vincent van Gogh was among the Western artists the series inspired.
