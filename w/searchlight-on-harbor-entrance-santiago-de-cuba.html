Searchlight on Harbor Entrance, Santiago de Cuba

Winslow Homer · 1902 · Oil on canvas · 77.5 × 128.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Homer worked in part from drawings of Morro Castle that he had made on a visit to Cuba in 1885, seventeen years earlier. The searchlight was the United States Navy's, used during the blockade of Santiago harbour in the Spanish–American War to keep the Spanish fleet from leaving. George A. Hearn gave the painting to the Metropolitan Museum in 1906.
