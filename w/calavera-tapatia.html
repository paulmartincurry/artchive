Calavera Tapatia

Manuel Manilla · 1910 · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Printed in 1910 at the press of Antonio Vanegas Arroyo. The verse under the picture is about the bravery of calaveras from Guadalajara, in Jalisco. The sheet reached the Library of Congress in 1977 with Caroline and Erwin Swann's collection of caricature and cartoon.
