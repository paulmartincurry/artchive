Water Lilies (Agapanthus)

Claude Monet · ca. 1915–26 · Oil on canvas · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The canvas is two metres tall and more than four metres wide. Claude Monet was in his mid-seventies when he began it, working from the water lily pond in the garden he had designed at his home in Giverny. It was still in his estate when he died in 1926, and it bears the estate stamp at lower left. His son Michel kept it at Giverny through 1950, and the Cleveland Museum of Art acquired it in 1960.
