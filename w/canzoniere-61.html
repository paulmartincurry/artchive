The Artchive
Blessed be the day, and the month, and the year,
and the season, and the time, and the hour, and the moment,
and the beautiful country, and the place where I was joined
to the two beautiful eyes that have bound me:

Canzoniere 61 (Blessed Be the Day)

Petrarch · 14th century

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Petrarch's songbook holds 366 poems in Italian, though he did most of his writing in Latin. Few of his works can be dated precisely, because he went on revising them all his life. His father, a notary, made him study law for seven years at Montpellier and Bologna, and once threw his books into a fire. From 1326 he worked in clerical offices in Avignon, which left him time to write.
