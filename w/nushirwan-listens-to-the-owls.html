Nushirwan Listens to the Owls (Khamsa of Nizami)

Maker unknown · 1555–65 · Opaque watercolor, ink, and gold on paper · Sheet: 32.7 × 21.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The scene comes from the Treasury of Secrets, the first poem of Nizami's Quintet. King Nushirwan, on a white horse, asks his minister Buzurgmihr, on a mule, what two owls on a ruin are saying; one, he answers, is offering the other as many ruined cities as he likes for his daughter's hand. The page was painted at Qazvin between 1555 and 1565.
