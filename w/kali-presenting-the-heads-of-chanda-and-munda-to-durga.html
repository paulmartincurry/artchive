Kali Presenting the Heads of Chanda and Munda to Durga (Devi Mahatmya)

Maker unknown · c. 1780 · Graphite and ink on paper · 14.3 × 19.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In the Devi Mahatmya, Kali emerges from the goddess's forehead to kill the demons Chanda and Munda, and because she has slain them she earns the name Chamunda. The drawing is 14.3 by 19.2 centimetres. The Met bought it in 1995.
