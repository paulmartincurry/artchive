Georgia O'Keeffe

Alfred Stieglitz · 1918 · Gelatin silver print · 11.4 × 9.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Georgia O'Keeffe moved to New York from Texas in early June 1918, and within a month Alfred Stieglitz began photographing her. He was fifty-four. Between 1918 and 1925 he produced more than 350 mounted prints of her. This one is 11.4 by 9.1 centimetres.
