Olympia

Édouard Manet · 1863 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

The model was Victorine Meurent, who was nineteen and later exhibited her own paintings at the Paris Salon. Manet painted the picture in 1863 and first showed it at the Salon of 1865. In 1888, hearing that his widow meant to sell it, Claude Monet began a subscription to buy it for France. Nearly 100 people gave, Monet himself 1,000 francs and Rodin 25, and Zola refused. It was bought in March 1890 for 19,415 francs, and reached the Louvre in 1907 by order of Georges Clemenceau.
