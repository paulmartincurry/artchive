The Autobiography of Malcolm X

Malcolm X · 1965

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

The journalist Alex Haley wrote the book with Malcolm X from more than 50 in-depth interviews conducted between 1963 and 1965. It was released on October 29, 1965, nine months after Malcolm X's assassination. Haley wrote the epilogue after he was killed.
