Nay, never ask this week, fair lord,
Where they are gone, nor yet this year,
Except with this for an overword, —
But where are the snows of yester-year?

The Ballad of Dead Ladies

François Villon · 1461

Translated by Dante Gabriel Rossetti

Villon simply labelled this section Ballade; the title was added by Clément Marot in his 1533 edition of the poems. It forms part of the collection he wrote in 1461. Dante Gabriel Rossetti popularized the word yesteryear to translate Villon's antan, which in the French was used in its original sense of last year.
