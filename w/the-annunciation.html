The Annunciation

Sandro Botticelli · ca. 1490 · Tempera and gold on wood · 7 1/2 × 12 3/8 in. (19.1 × 31.4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The panel is nineteen centimetres tall and thirty-one wide. The Metropolitan Museum takes it to be a single picture made for private prayer, not one of the small scenes from the base of an altarpiece. Around the same years Botticelli painted two other Annunciations, for Santa Maria Maddalena dei Pazzi and for San Barnaba in Florence. In the seventeenth century the panel belonged to the Barberini family in Rome, and it reached the museum with the Robert Lehman Collection.
