Augury of the crow (Vasakasajja Nayika)

Maker unknown · c. 1800 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · 21.4 x 14.5 cm (8 7/16 x 5 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The heroine waits for her husband, who is late, while monsoon clouds gather. The crow in her pomegranate tree is a bad omen, which makes her more anxious. A king of Kangra owned the painting, and one of Kangra's forts sits in the distance.
