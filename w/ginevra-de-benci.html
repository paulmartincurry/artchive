Ginevra de' Benci

Leonardo da Vinci · 1476 · Oil on panel · National Gallery of Art, Washington

The panel is nearly square now, 38.1 by 37 centimetres, because the bottom was cut off at some point, presumably after damage. The sitter's arms and hands are thought to have gone with it. The back is painted too, with a sprig of juniper inside a wreath of laurel and palm and a Latin motto. In February 1967 the National Gallery of Art bought the portrait from the Prince of Liechtenstein for between $5 and $6 million, then a record price for a painting.
