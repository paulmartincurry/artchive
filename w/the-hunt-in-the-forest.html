The Hunt in the Forest

Paolo Uccello · c. 1470 · Tempera, oil and gold on panel · Ashmolean Museum, Oxford

Free to use

Uccello painted this panel around 1470, and it is his last known work before his death in 1475. It was probably part of a cassone, a painted chest associated with marriage. To build the depth he laid a grid on the panel and diminished the figures, animals and objects toward a central vanishing point. William Fox-Strangways, later 4th Earl of Ilchester, gave it to the Ashmolean in 1850.
