Mademoiselle Eglantine's Troupe

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1896 · Color lithograph on tan wove paper · Image: 61.4 × 79.5 cm; Sheet: 62 × 79.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Jane Avril, a Moulin Rouge dancer whom Toulouse-Lautrec drew and painted often, travelled with a dance troupe to perform in London in 1896, the year of this poster. She was nicknamed La Mélinite, after an explosive. Lautrec himself also went to London, where he met and befriended Oscar Wilde.
