That the play is the tragedy, "Man,"
And its hero the Conqueror Worm.

The Conqueror Worm

Edgar Allan Poe · 1843

The Conqueror Worm first appeared on its own in the January 1843 issue of Graham's Magazine. In 1845 Poe wrote it into a revised printing of his story Ligeia, where the dying Ligeia is its author and teaches it to the narrator. The same year it was collected in The Raven and Other Poems.
