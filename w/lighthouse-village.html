Lighthouse Village (also known as Cape Elizabeth)

Edward Hopper · 1929 · Watercolor with graphite and scraping on paper · Sheet 40.5 × 63.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Hopper painted this watercolour in 1929, on the last of several painting excursions to Maine. He had painted the lighthouse at Cape Elizabeth once two years before. The three buildings at the bottom were where coast guard families lived. He sold the sheet to Frank K. M. Rehn of New York in October 1929, and Cleveland bought it from Rehn the same year.
