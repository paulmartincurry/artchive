Leaf from a Beatus Manuscript: The Lamb at the Foot of the Cross, Flanked by Two Angels

Maker unknown · c. 1180 · Tempera, gold, and ink on parchment · 44.4 × 30 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This leaf comes from a copy of the commentary on the Book of Revelation written around 776 by the Spanish monk Beatus of Liébana. There are 29 surviving illustrated manuscripts of the work, many of them incomplete or in fragments. The museum bought the leaf in 1991.
