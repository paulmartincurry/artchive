Oklahoma Sharecropper and Family Entering California, Stalled on the Desert near Indio

Dorothea Lange · February 1937 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Lange took this picture in February 1937 on the desert near Indio, California. The year before, twenty-two of her photographs for the Farm Security Administration had been included in John Steinbeck's The Harvest Gypsies when it first ran in The San Francisco News. Her photographs later served as inspiration for the 1940 film of The Grapes of Wrath.
