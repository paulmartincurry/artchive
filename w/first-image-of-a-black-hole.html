First Image of a Black Hole (M87*)

Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration · observed 11 April 2017, released 10 April 2019 · Radio interferometry image

Free to use with credit. Photo: EHT Collaboration, CC BY 4.0

The image comes from observations made on 11 April 2017 by eight radio telescopes spread across the planet and timed against each other with atomic clocks. Each telescope recorded about 350 terabytes of data a day on helium-filled hard drives, which were flown to supercomputers at the Max Planck Institute for Radio Astronomy and MIT's Haystack Observatory to be combined. The picture was published on 10 April 2019. The ring is about 40 microarcseconds across, roughly the size of a credit card on the Moon seen from Earth.
