Saint John the Baptist

Leonardo da Vinci · c. 1513–1516 · Oil on walnut · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

This is believed to be Leonardo's final painting, probably completed between 1513 and 1516; Antonio de Beatis saw it in his workshop at Clos Lucé before 17 October 1517. In 1625 Louis XIII gave it to Charles I of England in return for a Titian and Holbein's portrait of Erasmus. After Charles's collection was sold in 1649 it passed to the banker Eberhard Jabach and Cardinal Mazarin, and in 1661 back to the French king.
