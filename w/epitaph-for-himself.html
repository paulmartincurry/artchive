If I am a Syrian, what wonder? We all dwell in one country, O stranger, the world; one Chaos brought all mortals to birth.

Epitaph for Himself

Meleager of Gadara · ca. 95 BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail, 1890

Meleager was born in Gadara, now Umm Qais in Jordan, was educated in Tyre and spent his later life on the island of Cos, where he died at an advanced age, perhaps seventy. He wrote several short poems about his own life that name all three places. One hundred and thirty-four of his epigrams survive, and only because he included them in an anthology he compiled of forty-six Greek poets, The Garland.
