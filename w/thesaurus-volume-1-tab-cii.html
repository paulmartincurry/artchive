Albertus Seba · 1734 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Carl Linnaeus visited Seba twice in 1735 and later used many of his specimens to describe new species. Seba's inclusion of fantastic beasts such as this hydra led Linnaeus to add a section of Paradoxa, creatures that might exist but had not been found, to his Systema Naturae.
