Steal away, steal away,
Steal away to Jesus.
Steal away, steal away home,
I ain’t got long to stay here.

Steal Away

Traditional African American Spiritual · 1872

Wallace Willis and his daughter Minerva sang this song at the Spencer Academy, a Choctaw school where their owner had sent them to work before the Civil War. In 1871 the superintendent, Alexander Reid, heard the Fisk Jubilee Singers perform, judged the Willises' songs better than theirs, and gave the songs to the group. Michael Tippett set it in his oratorio A Child of Our Time, first performed in 1944.
