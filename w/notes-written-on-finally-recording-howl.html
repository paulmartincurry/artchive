Mind is shapely, art is shapely.

Notes Written on Finally Recording Howl

Allen Ginsberg · 1959

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Ginsberg wrote these notes three years after City Lights in San Francisco published Howl. In that time police and customs officers had seized copies, the shop's manager had been jailed for selling it, and a judge had ruled in 1957 that the poem was not obscene. He had moved to San Francisco in the 1950s and worked there as a market researcher, and the first public reading of the poem was at the Six Gallery in 1955. In 1957 he left the city for Morocco and then Paris.
