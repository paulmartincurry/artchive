Liberty (The Universal Penman)

George Bickham, after Joseph Champion · published 1741 · Getty Research Institute, Los Angeles

Free to use

George Bickham collected writing samples from twenty-five London writing masters, engraved them, and issued them as The Universal Penman in fifty-two parts between 1733 and 1741. The writing on this page is signed by Joseph Champion. Bickham was himself a writing master as well as an engraver, and the bound book holds 211 leaves of plates.
