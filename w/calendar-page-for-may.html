Calendar Page for May (Leaf from a Book of Hours)

Maker unknown · c. 1510 · Ink, tempera, and liquid gold on vellum · Leaf: 18.1 x 12.9 cm (7 1/8 x 5 1/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

May's labour is falconry: a couple rides through the forest with a falcon, then rests together. Saint Ouen on 5 May, popular in Rouen, shows the book was made for use there.
