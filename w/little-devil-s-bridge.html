Little Devil's Bridge (Liber Studiorum, no. 19)

J. M. W. Turner · published 1809 · Etching and mezzotint · Sheet: 20.8 × 29 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is number 19 of the 71 prints in the Liber Studiorum, a project that took Turner twelve years from 1807. He etched the plates himself and collaborating engravers worked them in mezzotint; this one is credited to him and Charles Turner. The models he gave the printmakers were mainly in sepia watercolour. The original plates were part of the Turner Bequest and are kept at Tate Britain.
