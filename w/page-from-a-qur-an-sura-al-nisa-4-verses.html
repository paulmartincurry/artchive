Page from a Qur'an, Sura al-Nisa 4, Verses 157–58

Maker unknown · 1100s · Opaque watercolor, ink, and gold on paper · 32 x 21.3 cm; Text area: 23 x 16 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The page is written in eastern Kufic script, with elongated letters, long upstrokes and low strokes that sway to the left, set against a background of foliage. Its vertical layout on paper replaced the earlier horizontal format on parchment. Kufic takes its name from Kufa, a city in southern Iraq, and until about the 11th century it was the main script used to copy the Quran.
