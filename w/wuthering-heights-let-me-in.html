Emily Brontë · 1847

There is no evidence that either house in the novel is based on a real building. Ellen Nussey, a friend of Charlotte Brontë, suggested Top Withens, a ruined farmhouse in an isolated spot near the Haworth parsonage, though its structure does not match the one described. Another candidate, High Sunderland Hall, stood near Law Hill in Halifax, where Emily Brontë worked briefly as a governess in 1838; it has since been demolished.
