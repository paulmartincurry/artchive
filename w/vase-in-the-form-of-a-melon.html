Vase in the Form of a Melon

Maker unknown · 1100s · Celadon · Height 25.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The bottle is shaped like a yellow muskmelon, a variety grown mainly in East Asia. Goryeo elites commissioned vessels like this for drinking. Celadon techniques first came to Korea from China, and Goryeo potters had their own style by the 1100s. The bottle is 25.2 centimetres high and came to Cleveland in 1944 by bequest of Elisabeth Severance Prentiss.
