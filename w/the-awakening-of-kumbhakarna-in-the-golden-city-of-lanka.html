The Awakening of Kumbhakarna in the Golden City of Lanka (Ramayana)

Maker unknown · c. 1605 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · 27.6 × 19.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kumbhakarna, Ravana's giant younger brother, had been cursed to sleep for six months at a time. In one telling, when Ravana needed him in the war against Rama, he woke only after 1,000 elephants had walked over him. The page is 27.6 by 19.1 centimetres. The Met bought it in 2002 with the Cynthia Hazen Polsky and Leon B. Polsky Fund.
