Maharana Sangram Singh Hunting Wild Boar

Maker unknown · c. 1725 · Ink and opaque watercolor on paper · 31.1 × 45.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Sangram Singh II was born in 1690 and ruled Mewar from 1710 until his death in 1734, so he was about thirty-five when this was painted. Soon after he came to the throne he defeated the invading Ranbaz Khan Mewati at the Battle of Bandanwara, in 1711. The sheet is 31.1 by 45.2 centimetres. Mary G. Hamilton gave it to the Met in 1983.
