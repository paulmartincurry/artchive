Oniwakamaru Observing the Great Carp in the Pond (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889 · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The boy Oniwakamaru keeps watch on the great carp in the pond. By the time this sheet was published in 1889, Yoshitoshi had become a master teacher, with pupils such as Toshikata Mizuno and Toshihide Migita.
