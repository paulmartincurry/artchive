Dead Mother (On Death, Part II, Plate 10)

Max Klinger · 1889 · Etching in black ink on lightweight cream wove paper, laid down on cream wove paper (chine collé) · Image: 38.6 × 29.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

On Death, Part Two, is Klinger's Opus XIII, a set of twelve plates listed under the years 1898 to 1910. The Art Institute of Chicago dates this one, the tenth, to 1889. It is etched and printed on thin paper laid down on a heavier sheet 63.4 centimetres tall.
