Foemen at morn, but friends at eve—
Fame or country least their care:
(What like a bullet can undeceive!)
But now they lie low,

Shiloh: A Requiem (April, 1862)

Herman Melville · 1866

Battle-Pieces holds 72 poems on the battles and personalities of the American Civil War and their aftermath. The book had sold only 486 copies by 1868 and recovered barely half of its publication costs.
