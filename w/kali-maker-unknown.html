Kali

Maker unknown · 900s–1000s · Bronze · 46.7 × 20.4 × 15.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Emery May Holden Norweb of Cleveland owned this bronze until 1956, when she gave it to the museum. It is 46.7 centimetres high. The museum says it could have belonged to either of two branches of Hinduism: one in which the goddess is a form of Parvati, and one in which she is the supreme creator who generated all the other gods, Shiva included.
