Gero Cross

Maker unknown · c. 965–970 · Oak, painted and partially gilded · Figure 187 cm high · Cologne Cathedral

Free to use with credit. Photo: Elya, CC BY-SA 3.0

Gero, Archbishop of Cologne, commissioned the crucifix, and he died in 976. It is the earliest known Western depiction of Christ dead on the cross. The gold sun behind the figure and the marble altar it stands in were given in 1683 by Canon Heinrich Mering.
