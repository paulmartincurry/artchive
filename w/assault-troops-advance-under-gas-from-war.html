Assault Troops Advance under Gas, from War

Otto Dix · 1924 · Drypoint, etching and aquatint on cream laid paper · plate 19.7 × 29.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is one of the 50 etchings of Der Krieg, which Karl Nierendorf published in Berlin in 1924, ten years after the war began. They were printed in an edition of 70, in five portfolios of ten, priced at 300 Reichsmarks each or 1,000 for all five. Dix had served as a machine gunner on both the Eastern and Western Fronts, and he drew on wartime photographs, his hundreds of sketches from the front and his own memories.
