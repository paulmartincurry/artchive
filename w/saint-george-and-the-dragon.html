Saint George and the Dragon

Odilon Redon · 1880s and c. 1892 · Charcoal and pastel on tan wove paper · 53.7 × 37.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The museum gives this sheet two dates, the 1880s and about 1892, and it carries two signatures, one at the upper right and one at the lower right. It joins charcoal, the medium of Redon's early work, with pastel, which he began to use in the 1890s. The earliest owner in the museum's record is Andries Bonger. The National Gallery of Art bought the drawing in 2000.
