The Cornell Farm

Edward Hicks · 1848 · Oil on canvas · 93.3 × 124.4 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Hicks lettered the subject along the bottom: the farm and stock of James C. Cornell of Northampton, Bucks County, which took the premium at the agricultural society on 12 October 1848. He added that he painted it in the sixty-ninth year of his age. He died the following August. The painting stayed with Cornell's son and grandson.
