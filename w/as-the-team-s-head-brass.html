The Artchive
One of my mates is dead. The second day
In France they killed him. It was back in March,
The very night of the blizzard, too. Now if
He had stayed here we should have moved the tree.

As the Team's Head-Brass

Edward Thomas · 1917

Thomas enlisted in the British Army in 1915, and in 1916 his family left Steep for Essex. Robert Frost, then living in England, had pressed him to write poetry when he was better known as a critic, and the two friends had planned to live side by side in the United States.
