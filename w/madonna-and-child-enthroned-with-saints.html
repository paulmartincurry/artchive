Madonna and Child Enthroned with Saints

Raphael · ca. 1504 · Oil and gold on wood · Main panel: 67 7/8 × 67 7/8 in. (172.4 × 172.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Raphael painted this altarpiece for the Franciscan convent of Sant'Antonio da Padova in Perugia when he was about twenty-one. The convent may have asked for the Christ Child and the young Baptist to be fully clothed. The small panels from its base were sold off separately, to Queen Christina of Sweden, and are now in New York, London and Boston. The main panels went to the Colonna family in Rome, and J. Pierpont Morgan bought them for a record price as the last Raphael altarpiece in private hands. He gave it to the museum in 1916.
