Hubble Deep Field

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and STScI · December 1995 · Composite of 342 exposures

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL/STScI Hubble Deep Field Team

The image was assembled from 342 separate exposures taken over ten consecutive days, from 18 to 28 December 1995. Robert Williams, then director of the Space Telescope Science Institute, gave a large part of his own discretionary observing time to it. The field is about one 24-millionth of the whole sky, the size of a tennis ball seen from 100 metres, and almost all of the 3,000 objects in it are galaxies.
