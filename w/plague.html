Plague (On Death, Part II)

Max Klinger · 1903, published 1904 · Etching · Plate: 43 × 33.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Klinger made this plate in 1903, the year he moved away from Leipzig and bought a vineyard at Großjena, and it was published in 1904. The plate is 43 centimetres high and nearly 34 wide. Dr. F. H. Hirschland gave this impression to the Metropolitan Museum in 1952.
