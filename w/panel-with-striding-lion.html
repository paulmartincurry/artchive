Panel with Striding Lion

Maker unknown · ca. 604–562 BCE · Ceramic, glaze · 97.2 × 227.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The lion is built from glazed ceramic bricks and the panel is 227 centimetres long. It comes from Babylon and is dated to the years 604 to 562 BC, the reign of Nebuchadnezzar II. The museum bought it in 1931.
