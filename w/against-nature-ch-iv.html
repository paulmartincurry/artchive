Against Nature (À rebours), Ch. IV

Joris-Karl Huysmans (Trans. John Howard, 1922) · 1884 (1922 Translation)

Translated by Robert Baldick · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Huysmans was thirty-six when the novel came out in 1884. He wrote in the hours left by a clerk's job at the French Ministry of the Interior, which he held for 32 years and found tedious. His father was a Dutch immigrant who worked in Paris as a commercial artist, and the pen name Joris-Karl honours that side of the family. Mallarmé dedicated a poem to the book's hero. Eight years later Huysmans returned to the Catholic Church he had left as a child.
