The Cat (Camera Work 48, plate I)

Frank Eugene · 1916 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The plate opened Camera Work number 48, in October 1916, near the end of the magazine's run. Frank Eugene was born in New York as Frank Eugene Smith, studied drawing and stage design at the Bavarian Academy of Fine Arts in Munich from 1886, and later taught pictorial photography at a Bavarian state institute.
