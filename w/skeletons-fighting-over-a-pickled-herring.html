Skeletons Fighting over a Pickled Herring

James Ensor · 1891 · Oil on panel · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, Brussels

James Ensor grew up in his parents' souvenir shop in Ostend, which sold carnival masks. He painted in the attic studio of their house from 1880 until 1917, and in that studio he dressed up skeletons and arranged them for his pictures. This panel dates from 1891, when he was thirty, in the four years from 1888 to 1892 when his work changed course. The next year he made a four-day trip to London, one of very few journeys he took.
