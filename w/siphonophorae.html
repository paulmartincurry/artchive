Siphonophorae (Deep-Sea Hydrozoa, Kunstformen der Natur)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1904

Free to use

This plate comes from Kunstformen der Natur, the book in which Ernst Haeckel collected over 100 of his illustrations of animals and sea creatures. He was seventy in 1904 and had been professor of zoology at the University of Jena since 1862. Siphonophores were an old subject for him: he had published a monograph on them in 1869 and another study in 1888.
