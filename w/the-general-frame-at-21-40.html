The General, frame at 21:40

Buster Keaton and Clyde Bruckman · 1926 · Silent film, tinted in this copy (frame)

The locomotives were vintage lumber engines bought from a railway in Oregon. The crew reached Cottage Grove on 27 May 1926 with 18 freight cars of Civil War cannons, rebuilt passenger cars and wagons, and hired 1,500 local people. Sparks from the wood-burning engine set fires in forests and haystacks, at 25 dollars a stack. The film cost 750,000 dollars against a first budget of 400,000, and its failure cost Keaton his independence.
