Sample book of ribbons

Maker unknown · ca. 1910 · silk, cotton, leather, paper · H × W × D: 35.5 × 24.5 cm (14 × 9 5/8 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Ninety-four wide silk ribbon samples are pasted onto the plain pages of this 184-page notebook, along with one section of coarse cotton printed with roses. The book has leather corners and back and boards covered in marbled paper. It was a gift of Augusta Dillon.
