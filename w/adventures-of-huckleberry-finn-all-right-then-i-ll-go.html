I was a-trembling, because I’d got to decide, forever, betwixt two things, and I knowed it. I studied a minute, sort of holding my breath, and then says to myself:

“All right, then, I’ll go to hell”—and tore it up.

Adventures of Huckleberry Finn — All right, then, I'll go to hell (Ch. 31)

Mark Twain (Samuel Langhorne Clemens) · 1884

The novel was composed in pen on notepaper between 1876 and 1883, and Twain set it aside for several years along the way. He gave the manuscript to the Buffalo Public Library in 1885. Half of it was believed lost until 1991, when it turned up in a steamer trunk. When the American edition came out, the public library in Concord, Massachusetts, excluded the book from its shelves.
