Manzanar Street Scene, Winter, Manzanar Relocation Center

Ansel Adams · 1943 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The barracks at Manzanar were built of pine covered with tar paper. Ansel Adams made the negative for this view in 1943 and gave the print to the Library of Congress between 1965 and 1968. He wrote in 1965 that he thought the Manzanar collection an important historical document and trusted it could be put to good use.
