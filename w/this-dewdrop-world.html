The world of dew
is the world of dew.
And yet, and yet—

This Dewdrop World (Tsuyu no Yo)

Kobayashi Issa · 1819

Translated by Robert Hass, 1994 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Issa wrote this haiku after the death of his daughter Satoyo. His first child, a son, had died shortly after birth, and she died less than two and a half years later. A third child died in 1820 and his wife Kiku in 1823, when he was sixty-one. In 1827 a fire took his house, and he lived his last months in a windowless clay-walled storehouse, which still stands.
