The Pillow Book — Stray Notes

Sei Shōnagon · ca. 1002 CE (Heian Period)

Translated by Ivan Morris · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The author entered the service of the Empress Teishi in 993, at the age of twenty-seven. Her father was a scholar and poet who worked as a provincial official. The book holds 164 lists among its anecdotes and opinions. It was passed around the court and survived for several hundred years only in handwritten copies, in which scribes changed the order of entries and added or cut passages. It was first printed in the 17th century.
