The Calumny of Apelles

Sandro Botticelli · c. 1494–1495 · Tempera on panel · Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

Botticelli based this panel on Lucian's description of a lost painting by the Greek painter Apelles, a subject Leon Battista Alberti had recommended to artists in 1435. Completed around 1494 or 1495, it is probably the last of Botticelli's secular paintings to survive. Giorgio Vasari later saw it in the collection of the son of the banker Antonio Segni, and wrote that it had been a gift from Botticelli.
