They flee from me that sometime did me seek,
With naked foot stalking within my chamber:

They Flee from Me

Sir Thomas Wyatt · 1557

Wyatt's poems were passed around the court of Henry VIII in manuscript and were not printed under his name until 1557, fifteen years after his death. This one survives in several differing versions, in two manuscripts and in that first printed book. He was a diplomat in the king's service, born in 1503, and in 1536 he spent a month as a prisoner in the Tower of London.
