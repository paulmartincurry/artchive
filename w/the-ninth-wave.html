The Ninth Wave

Ivan Aivazovsky · 1850 · Oil on canvas · Russian Museum

Free to use

Ivan Aivazovsky was thirty-two or thirty-three when he painted this canvas, which is 221 centimetres high and 332 wide. He was born in Feodosia, in Crimea, to an Armenian father. The title is an old sailors' term for a wave of great size that comes after a run of steadily larger ones.
