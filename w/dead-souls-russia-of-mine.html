And you, Russia of mine—are not you also speeding like a troika which nought can overtake?

Dead Souls — Russia of mine (Part I, Ch. XI, close)

Nikolai Gogol · 1842

Translated by D. J. Hogarth

The novel was first published in 1842. Gogol meant it to be the first part of a three-volume work, but he burned the manuscript of the second part shortly before his death.
