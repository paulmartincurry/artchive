Frog (Kaeru); Gold Beetle (Kogane mushi), from the Picture Book of Crawling Creatures (Ehon mushi erami)

Kitagawa Utamaro · 1788 · Page from woodblock-printed book; ink and color on paper · 26.7 × 18.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Utamaro's first published work may have been a picture of eggplants in a poetry anthology of 1770. He was about thirty-five when this book of insects and small creatures was printed, and each page measures about 27 by 18 centimetres.
