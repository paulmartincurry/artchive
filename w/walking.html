The West of which I speak is but another name for the Wild; and what I have been preparing to say is, that in Wildness is the preservation of the World.

Walking

Henry David Thoreau · 1862

Thoreau died of tuberculosis on 6 May 1862 in Concord, Massachusetts, the town where he was born. He was forty-four. For most of his adult life he had worked in his family's pencil factory alongside his writing, and he revived a way of making good pencils from poor graphite by binding it with clay. In April 1844 he and a friend accidentally started a fire that burned 300 acres of Walden Woods.
