Proclamation of the Irish Republic

Provisional Government of the Irish Republic · 24 April 1916 · Printed broadside

Free to use

It was printed at Liberty Hall in Dublin before the Easter Rising, in two halves on one sheet, and Patrick Pearse read it outside the General Post Office as the Rising began. The first signature is Tom Clarke's, not Pearse's. All seven signatories were executed by the British military afterwards.
