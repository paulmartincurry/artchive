Grapes, Lemons, Pears, and Apples

Vincent van Gogh · 1887 · Oil on canvas · 46.5 × 55.2 cm (18 1/4 × 21 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Van Gogh painted it in Paris in the autumn of 1887, one of a group of canvases of seasonal fruit. He set yellow against purple, blue against orange and red against green, and laid the cloth in a pulsing pattern of strokes around the fruit.
