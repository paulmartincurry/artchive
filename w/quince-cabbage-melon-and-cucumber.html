Quince, Cabbage, Melon, and Cucumber

Juan Sánchez Cotán · ca. 1602 · Oil on canvas · San Diego Museum of Art

Free to use

The quince and the cabbage hang from threads, which was a common way of keeping food in the seventeenth century. Juan Sánchez Cotán was a wealthy painter in Toledo, and in 1603 or 1604, a year or two after this picture, he joined a Carthusian monastery and gave up still lifes for religious figures. In 1818 Joseph Bonaparte, the exiled former king of Spain, sold the canvas to the Pennsylvania Academy of Fine Arts. It measures 67.8 by 88.7 centimetres.
