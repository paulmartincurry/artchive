Of all that is written, I love only what a person hath written with his blood. Write with blood, and thou wilt find that blood is spirit. … I should only believe in a God that would know how to dance.

Thus Spake Zarathustra — Reading and Writing

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883

Translated by Thomas Common

Nietzsche claimed that the whole first part of Zarathustra, which holds this chapter, was conceived while he was out walking near Rapallo, on the road to Zoagli. He said its central idea had first occurred to him in August 1881, beside a pyramidal block of stone in the woods along the shores of Lake Silvaplana. The book came out in four volumes between 1883 and 1885.
