Heart of Darkness (Part III)

Joseph Conrad · 1899

In 1890, at thirty-two, Conrad took a job on a river steamer for a Belgian trading company in the Congo. The captain fell ill on the way upriver and Conrad took command. An agent named Georges-Antoine Klein became ill and died aboard his steamer, and critics have proposed him as one source for Kurtz. Conrad began the story eight years after he came back from Africa, working from his travel journals.
