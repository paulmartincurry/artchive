Black Rooster and Nandina (Colorful Realm of Living Beings)

Itō Jakuchū · c. 1757–65 · Color on silk · Museum of the Imperial Collections (Sannomaru Shōzōkan), Tokyo

Free to use

Jakuchū began the Colorful Realm of Living Beings in 1758, when he was forty-two, as a memorial to his parents and to his youngest brother, who had died prematurely. At fifty-one he had completed thirty paintings of animals and plants and three of the Shaka Triad, and he donated them all to Shōkoku-ji.
