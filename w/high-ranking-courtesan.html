High Ranking Courtesan

Mihata Jōryū · c. 1830/43 · Ink, color, and gold on silk · 106.6 × 45.6 cm (42 × 18 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Her clothing and hairstyle mark her as a high-ranking courtesan: several layers of kimono, an outer coat and many hairpins. The flowering plum on the coat runs the full height of the garment.
