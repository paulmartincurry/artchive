Vessel with Mythological Scene

Metropolitan Painter · 600–800 CE · Ceramic, slip · 14 × 10.2 × 10.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met credits this vessel to an unnamed painter it calls the Metropolitan Painter, who worked between 600 and 800 CE. Painted Maya vessels of this straight-sided kind were drinking beakers for the elite, and many were placed in burials, which is why so many survive in good condition. It is 14 centimetres tall.
