Krishna and the Gopis on the Bank of the Yamuna River (Tehri Garhwal Gita Govinda)

Maker unknown · c. 1775–80 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · 16.2 × 25.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The poet Jayadeva composed the Gita Govinda in the twelfth century, in twelve chapters, and it is still sung every night in the Jagannatha temple. The Met calls the set this page comes from the Second or Tehri Garhwal Gita Govinda. The painting is 16.2 by 25.7 centimetres, inside a dark blue border ruled in red. Steven Kossak gave it to the Met in 2017 from the Kronos Collections.
