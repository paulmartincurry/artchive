The Windhover

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

A windhover is a kestrel, named for the way it hangs in the air while hunting. Hopkins wrote the sonnet on 30 May 1877, aged thirty-two, about four months before he was ordained a priest, and dedicated it to Christ. He judged it the best thing he had written. It was not published until 1918, twenty-nine years after his death.
