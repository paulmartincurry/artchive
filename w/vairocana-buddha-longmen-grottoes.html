Vairocana Buddha, Longmen Grottoes

Maker unknown · 672–675 · Limestone

Free to use with credit. Photo: xiquinhosilva, CC BY 2.0

Work on the Fengxian-si cave at Longmen began in 672 and was finished in 675, in the years when Emperor Gaozong and Empress Wu Zetian ruled from Luoyang. The grottoes were cut into limestone cliffs along a kilometre of the Yi River, 12 kilometres south of the city, and their statues range from 1 inch to 57 feet in height. Many of the images were once painted. The heads of many statues at the site were destroyed during the Cultural Revolution.
