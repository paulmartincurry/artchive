I'll lie in de grave and stretch out my arms;

I Know Moon-Rise

Traditional African American Spiritual · 1870

Thomas Wentworth Higginson was colonel of the 1st South Carolina Volunteers, the first federally authorized black regiment, from 1862 to 1864. He took down the songs his soldiers sang around their fires, writing in the dark with his hand in his pocket, and had men such as Corporal Robert Sutton supply the parts he had missed. He printed this one in Army Life in a Black Regiment in 1870.
