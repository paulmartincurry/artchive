Equestrienne (At the Cirque Fernando)

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1887–88 · Oil on canvas · 100.3 × 161.3 cm (39 1/2 × 63 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Joseph Oller bought this canvas in 1888 for his café concert, the Moulin Rouge, and it hung there on permanent show from that year. Toulouse-Lautrec was twenty-three. As a boy in Paris he had taken informal lessons from René Princeteau, a family friend who specialised in horses. The picture is 161.3 centimetres wide, and the Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1925 with funds from Joseph Winterbotham.
