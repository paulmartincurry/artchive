The Great Gatsby — Can't repeat the past?

F. Scott Fitzgerald · 1925

A neighbour of the Fitzgeralds on Long Island, Max Gerlach, was a bootlegger who threw lavish parties, never wore the same shirt twice and called people old sport, and these details went into Gatsby. In earlier drafts Daisy was named Ada and Nick was Dud, and the story was told by an all-knowing narrator. Fitzgerald sent a near-final manuscript to his editor on 27 October 1924 and the final version in February 1925.
