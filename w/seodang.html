Seodang (Village School)

Kim Hong-do · c. 1780 · Album leaf, ink and light colour on paper · National Museum of Korea, Seoul

Free to use

The leaf is about 27 by 23 centimetres and is thought to date from around 1780. By then Kim Hong-do was a court painter of standing: in 1771 he painted the portrait of the heir to the throne, the future King Jeongjo, and in 1773 he assisted on the royal portrait of King Yeongjo. His Nineteen Taoist Immortals of 1776 made his name, and Jeongjo, newly crowned, gave him many official commissions.
