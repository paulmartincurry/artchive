We hold these truths to be self-evident: that all men and women are created equal

Declaration of Sentiments (opening)

Elizabeth Cady Stanton · 1848

Stanton was the principal author and modeled the text on the Declaration of Independence. It was signed in 1848 at the close of a two-day convention in the Wesleyan Methodist Church in Seneca Falls, New York, by 68 women and 32 men, about a third of the roughly 300 people who attended. She was in her early thirties and was the convention's main organizer. Frederick Douglass attended and argued for putting the vote on the agenda.
