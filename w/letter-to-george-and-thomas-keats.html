Negative Capability, that is, when a man is capable of being in uncertainties, mysteries, doubts, without any irritable reaching after fact and reason.

Letter to George and Thomas Keats (December 1817)

John Keats · 1817

The letter went to Keats's two younger brothers, George, born in 1797, and Tom, born in 1799. Since April 1817 the three had shared rooms at 1 Well Walk in the village of Hampstead, where John and George nursed Tom, who had tuberculosis. Keats was twenty-two. Tom died the following year.
