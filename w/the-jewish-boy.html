The Jewish Boy

Medardo Rosso · c. 1892 · Wax on plaster · 23.2 × 18.1 × 14.9 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Medardo Rosso considered this head of a boy one of his most successful works. It is wax over plaster, about 23 centimetres high. Rosso kept his own foundry in his Paris studio at a time when most sculptors sent their moulds out to be cast, and he never made preparatory drawings. This cast stayed with the Rosso family at Barzio until 1970.
