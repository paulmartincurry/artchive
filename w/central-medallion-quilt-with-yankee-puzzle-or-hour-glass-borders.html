Central Medallion Quilt with "Yankee Puzzle" or "Hour Glass" Borders

Mary Jones · Central medallion embroidered 1795; pieced and quilted c. 1750-1810 · Center: cotton, plain weave; embroidered with silk, cotton, and wool in buttonhole, chain, cross, single cross, feather, Roumanian, satin, single satin, stem, and twined double running stitches. Squares: cotton, cotton and linen, and linen, plain weaves; block, copperplate, and roller printed · 238.5 × 252.2 cm (93 7/8 × 99 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The centre was embroidered in 1795; the more than 30 English and French printed cottons around it date from about 1780 to 1810, saved over decades when America still relied on imported cloth.
