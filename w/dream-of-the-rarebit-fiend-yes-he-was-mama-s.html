Dream of the Rarebit Fiend: Yes, He Was! Mama's Litta Dookin's!

Winsor McCay · 1907 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In this twelve-panel strip, according to the Library of Congress record, a mother dreams that the baby in her arms grows a beard; on waking, her husband asks her to stop eating rarebits. McCay signed it Silas, the name he used at the New York Herald. Rarebit Fiend was aimed at adults, unlike Little Nemo, and it touched on alcoholism, homelessness and suicide.
