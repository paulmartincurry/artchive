The Last of New England—The Beginning of New Mexico

Marsden Hartley · 1918–19 · Oil on paperboard · 61.6 × 76.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Marsden Hartley spent eighteen months in the Southwest in 1918–19, and painted this imagined scene on paperboard: fallen trees of a New England forest in front, the hills of New Mexico beyond. He was born in Lewiston, Maine. Alfred Stieglitz owned the picture until his death in 1946, and it was given to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1949.
