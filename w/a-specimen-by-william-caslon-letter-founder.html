A Specimen by William Caslon, Letter-Founder

William Caslon · dated 1734

Free to use

William Caslon trained as an engraver in Birmingham and set up in London in 1716 engraving gun locks and barrels and cutting tools for bookbinders. Printers who knew him encouraged him to start a type foundry. His first types were an Arabic, a Hebrew and a Coptic; the roman and italic followed, and this sheet of 1734 showed them fully worked out. He was about forty-one.
