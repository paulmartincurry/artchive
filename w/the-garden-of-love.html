The Artchive
And I saw it was filled with graves,
And tombstones where flowers should be;
And priests in black gowns were walking their rounds,
And binding with briars my joys and desires.

The Garden of Love

William Blake · 1794

The Garden of Love appeared in Songs of Experience in 1794. Blake relief-etched each copper plate by hand, printed it and then painted the page, often in watercolour. The first two stanzas rhyme acbc; the last two lines break the pattern and rhyme inside the line instead, gowns with rounds and briars with desires.
