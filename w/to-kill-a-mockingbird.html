To Kill a Mockingbird

Harper Lee · 1960

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Lee moved to New York City in 1950 and worked as a reservation clerk for an airline while she wrote about people in Monroeville. In 1957 she presented her writing to a literary agent recommended by Capote. The publisher who bought the manuscript advised her to quit the airline and concentrate on writing. The novel won the Pulitzer Prize a year after its release.
