The Mature Age (L'Âge mûr)

Camille Claudel · ca. 1899 (Cast 1902) · Bronze, gypsum · Musée d'Orsay

The French state commissioned this group from Camille Claudel in 1895, her first state commission, and cancelled it in June 1899 before any bronze was cast. Auguste Rodin, her former teacher and lover, had obtained the commission for her, and he cut off his support after seeing the plaster in 1899. A private client, Captain Tissier, paid for this bronze in 1902. It weighs 350 kg and stayed with his family until the Musée d'Orsay bought it in 1982.
