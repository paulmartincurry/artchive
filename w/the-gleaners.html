The Gleaners

Jean-François Millet · 1857 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Millet had already made a vertical painting of the subject in 1854 and an etching in 1855 before he showed this canvas at the Salon of 1857. Short of money afterwards, he asked 4,000 francs for it and took 3,000 from an Englishman named Binder, a price he tried to keep secret. In 1889, fourteen years after his death, it sold at auction for 300,000 francs to the champagne maker Jeanne-Alexandrine Louise Pommery. It went to the Louvre under her will in 1891.
