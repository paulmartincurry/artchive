Venus of Urbino

Titian (Tiziano Vecellio) · 1534 · Oil on canvas · Uffizi Gallery, Florence

In December 1534 Titian wrote that he was at work on a painting of a woman for Cardinal Ippolito de' Medici. The cardinal died the following August, apparently without seeing it, and the canvas stayed in the studio. Guidobaldo della Rovere, aged twenty-four, saw it there in January 1538 when he came to sit for his portrait, and bought it some months later; his letters show he was afraid Titian would sell it to someone else. He became Duke of Urbino later that year. The painting has been in the Uffizi since 1736.
