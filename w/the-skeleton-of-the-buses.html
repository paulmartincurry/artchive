The Skeleton of the Buses (Calavera Broadside)

José Guadalupe Posada · ca. 1900–10 · Relief etching and letterpress · Cleveland Museum of Art

This broadside belongs to a series in which Posada used skeletons to comment on the news, here the danger of the electric trams in Mexico City. He set image and text together and used cheap materials and mass-production methods, so the sheets could reach workers, many of whom could not read. Scholars estimate he made more than 20,000 images for broadsheets, pamphlets and chapbooks. Cleveland bought this sheet from the Tobey C. Moss Gallery in Los Angeles in 2000.
