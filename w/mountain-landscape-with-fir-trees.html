Mountain Landscape with Fir Trees

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner · 1918/19 · graphite and brush and black ink · Sheet: 34.6 × 50.7 cm (13 5/8 × 19 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kirchner first went to Davos on 19 January 1917, at the invitation of Helene Spengler, and went back to Berlin after ten days of exceptionally cold weather. After his discharge from the army in 1915 he took refuge at a sanatorium near Davos, and the Alps became his subject. The back of this sheet carries his estate stamp in violet ink.
