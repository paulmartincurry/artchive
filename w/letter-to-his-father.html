The Artchive
I cannot write poetically, for I am no poet. I cannot make fine artistic phrases that cast light and shadow, for I am no painter; I can neither by signs nor by pantomime express my thoughts and feelings, for I am no dancer; but I can by tones, for I am a musician.

Letter to His Father (Mannheim, 8 November 1777)

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart · 1777

Translated by Lady Wallace, 1865

Mozart was twenty-one and without a post when he wrote this letter. In August 1777 he had resigned as a court musician in Salzburg, where his salary was 150 florins a year, and on 23 September he set out with his mother to look for employment. Munich offered no permanent position, and they moved on through Augsburg towards Mannheim and Paris. His father Leopold, deputy Kapellmeister of the Salzburg orchestra, had been his only teacher.
