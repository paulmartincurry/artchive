Alas! alas! I sing in grief as I recall the children. Would that I could turn back again; would that I could grasp their hands once more; would that I could call them forth from the land of the dead;

Composed by a Certain Ruler in Memory of Former Rulers

Anonymous Nahua poet (Cantares Mexicanos) · Written down in the 16th century

Translated by Daniel G. Brinton, 1887

This song is one of the ninety-one in the Cantares Mexicanos, a manuscript of Nahuatl songs written down in the 16th century. The collection holds more than half of all the traditional Nahuatl songs known, and it is kept in the National Library of Mexico in Mexico City.
