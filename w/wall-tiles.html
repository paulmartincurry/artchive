Wall Tiles

Maker unknown · 1600s · Fritware with underglaze-painted design · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Each of the four tiles carries a spray of tulips, carnations and a large palmette inside a twelve-lobed medallion, ringed by an eight-pointed star of paired wavy lines. Set together, their corners form a fifth medallion with a cobalt blue centre. They came to the museum through the New York dealer Dikran Kelekian, as a gift of the John Huntington Art and Polytechnic Trust.
