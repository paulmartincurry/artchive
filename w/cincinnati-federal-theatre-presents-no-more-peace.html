Cincinnati Federal Theatre Presents No More Peace

James H. Murphy · 1937 · Colour silkscreen on board · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

James H. Murphy designed the poster for the Cincinnati Federal Theatre's 1937 production of Ernst Toller's satire No More Peace; a stamp on the back reads 4 June 1937. Toller, a German Jewish playwright, had been exiled from Germany when the Nazis came to power in 1933. He died by suicide in May 1939.
