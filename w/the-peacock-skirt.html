The Peacock Skirt (Illustration for Oscar Wilde's Salome)

Aubrey Beardsley · 1893 (Published 1894)

Free to use

Oscar Wilde commissioned Aubrey Beardsley to illustrate the first English edition of his play Salome after a Beardsley drawing inspired by the play was printed in The Studio in April 1893. This is the second of the ten plates. Beardsley drew it in pen and ink, and for the 1894 edition it was reproduced from a wood engraving made by Carl Hentschel. The publisher John Lane kept the original drawing; it was sold at auction in 1926 and has belonged to the Fogg Art Museum at Harvard since 1943.
