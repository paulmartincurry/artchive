Rose, harsh rose,
marred and with stint of petals,
meagre flower, thin,
sparse of leaf,

Sea Rose

H.D. (Hilda Doolittle) · 1916

Sea Garden, H.D.'s first book, was published in 1916. The year before, her only child with Richard Aldington, a daughter, was stillborn; when he enlisted in the British Army she took his place as assistant editor of The Egoist.
