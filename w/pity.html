Pity

William Blake · c. 1795 · Relief etching, printed in color and finished with pen and ink and watercolor · Sheet: 42.2 × 52.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is a monotype. Blake painted the design on gessoed millboard, printed from it, and finished each impression by hand in ink and watercolour. He could take up to three impressions from one painting, and three of Pity survive. This one was given to the Metropolitan Museum by Mrs. Robert W. Goelet in 1958.
