A Large Sign Reading "I Am an American" Placed in the Window of a Store, Oakland, California

Dorothea Lange · March 1942 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The sign went up in the window of the Wanto Co. store in Oakland on 8 December, the day after Pearl Harbor. Tatsuro Matsuda, a University of California graduate whose family owned the business, commissioned and installed it. Dorothea Lange photographed the closed store in March 1942 for the War Relocation Authority. Her original caption gave the wrong street; the store stood at Eighth and Franklin.
