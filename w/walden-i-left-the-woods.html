I left the woods for as good a reason as I went there. Perhaps it seemed to me that I had several more lives to live, and could not spare any more time for that one.

Walden — I left the woods (Conclusion)

Henry David Thoreau · 1854

At Walden Pond Thoreau finished a first draft of A Week on the Concord and Merrimack Rivers, an elegy to his brother John. No publisher would take it, so he printed 1,000 copies at his own expense, and fewer than 300 sold. When he left the woods, Emerson asked him to move into the Emerson house and help Lidian Emerson run it while her husband was in Europe. He revised Walden over several years while working to pay off his debts.
