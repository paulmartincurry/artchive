Tiradentes Quartered

Pedro Américo · 1893 · Oil on canvas · Mariano Procópio Museum

Free to use

Pedro Américo was about fifty when he painted this canvas. He had drawn for a scientific expedition before he was ten, entered the Imperial Academy in Rio de Janeiro as a boy, and went to Paris on a pension from Emperor Pedro II to study at the École des Beaux-Arts. His name was made in the 1870s by large battle paintings done for the monarchy. This picture is reproduced in school books across Brazil.
