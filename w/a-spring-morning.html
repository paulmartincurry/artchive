But now I remember the night, the storm,
And I wonder how many blossoms were broken.

A Spring Morning (Chūn Xiǎo)

Meng Haoran · ca. 730 CE

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu, 1929 · Public domain in the United States (published 1929); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Meng Haoran lived almost all his life in and around his home town of Xiangyang, and he never secured an official post. According to the New Book of Tang, he was granted an audience with Emperor Xuanzong and sent away from the palace after a line of the poem he recited angered the emperor. Since the late Song dynasty this quatrain has opened the Poems of a Thousand Masters, a beginner's anthology used in elementary schools.
