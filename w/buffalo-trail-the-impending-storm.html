Buffalo Trail: The Impending Storm

Albert Bierstadt · 1869 · Oil on canvas · 74.9 × 125.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Ten years before he painted this, in 1859, Bierstadt first travelled west, in the company of the government surveyor Frederick W. Lander. According to a dealer's letter the canvas was once with a J. Kugal in France, and it was with a New York gallery in 1958.
