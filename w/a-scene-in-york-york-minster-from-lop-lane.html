A Scene in York: York Minster from Lop Lane

William Henry Fox Talbot · July 28, 1845 · Salted paper print · Image: 16.2 × 20.4 cm (6 7/16 × 8 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Talbot took this view on 28 July 1845, when he was forty-five. He held the English patent on the process and sold licences to use it: £20 at first, later £4 for amateurs, and up to £300 a year for professional photographers. By his own reckoning he had spent about £5,000 on his photographic work and wanted the money back.
