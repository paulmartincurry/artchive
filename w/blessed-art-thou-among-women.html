Blessed Art Thou Among Women

Gertrude Käsebier · 1899 · Photogravure · Art Institute of Chicago

Gertrude Käsebier took the photograph at forty-six or forty-seven, only a few years after turning professional. She had started art school at thirty-seven, at the Pratt Institute in Brooklyn, where she took up the ideas of Friedrich Fröbel, the founder of the kindergarten, on the bond between mother and child. This photogravure was printed for the portfolio American Pictorial Photography, Series II, in 1901, and it is number 34 of an edition of 150.
