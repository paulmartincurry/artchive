Muqarnas Dome, Alhambra

Maker unknown · 14th century

Free to use with credit. Photo: Kent Wang, CC BY-SA 2.0

The muqarnas domes of the Alhambra are in the Palace of the Lions, built by the Nasrids in the 14th century. The dome over the Hall of the Two Sisters is made of at least 5000 cells, which run down from a central summit into sixteen miniature domes around the edge. Muqarnas in this western region was built up in vertical layers from a fixed set of eight shapes, and wood and stucco were the preferred materials.
