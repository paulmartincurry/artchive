Yoruba Head from a Figure

Maker unknown · 12th–15th century · Soapstone (schist) · 28.6 × 14 × 15.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This head comes from a full figure carved in soapstone, and it is 28.6 centimetres tall. Yoruba sculpture in stone has a long history: from around 800 CE, artists at Ife worked in terracotta, stone and copper alloy.
