"A house divided against itself cannot stand." I believe this government cannot endure permanently, half slave and half free. I do not expect the Union to be dissolved,—I do not expect the house to fall; but I do expect it will cease to be divided.

A House Divided (opening)

Abraham Lincoln · June 16, 1858

Lincoln gave this speech on June 16, 1858, at the Illinois State Capitol in Springfield, after accepting the Illinois Republican Party's nomination as candidate for US senator. The evening session met at 8 pm and he was its only speaker. The line about the house is from the Gospel of Mark. Newspapers published the address in full at once, and it came out as a pamphlet.
