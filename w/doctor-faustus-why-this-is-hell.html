Why, this is hell, nor am I out of it:
Think'st thou that I, who saw the face of God,
And tasted the eternal joys of heaven,
Am not tormented with ten thousand hells,
In being depriv'd of everlasting bliss?

Doctor Faustus — Why, This Is Hell

Christopher Marlowe · ca. 1592

This exchange comes in the scene where Faustus first conjures Mephistophilis, Act 1, Scene 3. The first edition, the 1604 quarto printed by Valentine Simmes for the bookseller Thomas Bushell, credits the play on its title page only to Ch. Marl. Philip Henslowe's diary records a payment of £4 to Samuel Rowley and William Bird for additions to the play on 22 November 1602.
