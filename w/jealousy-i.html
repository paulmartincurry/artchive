Jealousy I

Edvard Munch · 1896, printed after 1906 · Lithograph in black on ivory Japanese paper · Image: 32.6 × 46 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch drew this lithograph in 1896, a year after the first painted Jealousy, but this impression was printed after 1906 by M. W. Lassally, in black on Japanese paper. He returned to the image throughout his life, in no fewer than eleven painted versions, the last made during the 1930s, as well as four lithographs and one drypoint.
