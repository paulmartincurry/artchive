For one more picture! in a sheet of flame
I saw them and I knew them all. And yet
Dauntless the slug-horn to my lips I set,
And blew. "Childe Roland to the Dark Tower came.

Childe Roland to the Dark Tower Came (closing stanzas)

Robert Browning · 1852 (published 1855)

Browning wrote the poem on 2 January 1852 and first published it in 1855 in the collection Men and Women. He claimed that it came to him in a dream. The title, which forms the last words of the poem, is a line from King Lear.
