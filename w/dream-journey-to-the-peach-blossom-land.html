Dream Journey to the Peach Blossom Land

An Gyeon · 1447 · Handscroll, ink and colour on silk · Tenri University, Tenri, Japan

Free to use

In 1447 Grand Prince Anpyeong asked An Gyeon to paint a dream he had of visiting a paradise, and An finished it in three days. The prince added his own inscription and had 21 scholars write poems for it, about 20 metres of writing in all. Six years later his brother seized power, and Anpyeong was exiled and executed. The scroll is thought to have been looted by a Japanese general in the Imjin War; it turned up in Kagoshima in 1893 and is now at Tenri University.
