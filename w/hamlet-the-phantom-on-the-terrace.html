Hamlet: The Phantom on the Terrace

Eugène Delacroix · 1843 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Delacroix was forty-four or forty-five in 1843, the year of this lithograph, and in the same year he painted a large Pietà for the church of St. Denis du Saint Sacrement in Paris. He was widely read and drew often on British writers, especially Shakespeare, Byron and Walter Scott. This impression is from the second of two states.
