Wheatfield with Crows

Vincent van Gogh · 1890 · Oil on canvas · Van Gogh Museum

Free to use

This is often said to be Vincent van Gogh's last painting, but his letters suggest he finished it around 10 July 1890, before several others; his final painting was Tree Roots. The canvas is a double square, 50.2 centimetres by 103, a format he used only in June and July of that year, the last weeks of his life. In 1991 it was stolen along with 19 other Van Gogh paintings and quickly recovered, severely damaged.
