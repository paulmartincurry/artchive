Creation of the World (opening)

Tahitian creation chant (dictated by Paoraʼi) · Dictated 1822; printed 1928

Translated by Teuira Henry (Ancient Tahiti, 1928)

This chant was dictated in 1822 by Paoraʼi, a counsellor of Borabora, and Vaiʼau, a high priest of the same island, later recited nearly the same words. Teuira Henry, born on Tahiti in 1847, rebuilt a lost manuscript history of Tahiti by her grandfather, the missionary John Muggridge Orsmond, from his notes; most of her writing was published after her death in 1915.
