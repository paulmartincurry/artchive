Radiolaria, Plate 20: Drymosphaera, Melitomma (Report on the Scientific Results of the Voyage of H.M.S. Challenger)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1887 · Lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

The animals drawn here were gathered by HMS Challenger, which left Portsmouth on 21 December 1872 and sailed about 68,890 nautical miles around the world. Samples were preserved in brine or alcohol, or dried, and shared out among specialists in Europe. The lettering on the plate credits the drawing to Ernst Haeckel and Adolf Giltsch, and the lithography to E. Giltsch of Jena.
