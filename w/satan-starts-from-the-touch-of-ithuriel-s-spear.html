Satan Starts from the Touch of Ithuriel's Spear

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1776 · Pen and brown ink and brush and gray wash · Sheet: 30.9 × 42.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Fuseli drew this in 1776, during the eight years he spent in Italy, most of them in Rome. It is one of three almost identical drawings of the scene from Paradise Lost. The other two are in the National Gallery in Stockholm and the British Museum in London.
