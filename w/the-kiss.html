The Kiss (Der Kuss)

Gustav Klimt · 1907–1908 · Oil on canvas · Belvedere, Vienna

Free to use

Klimt's father was a travelling artisan who specialised in gold engravings. Klimt himself took up gold leaf after a trip to Italy in 1903, when he saw the Byzantine mosaics in the Church of San Vitale in Ravenna. This canvas is 180 centimetres square and carries gold leaf, silver and platinum on top of the oil paint. The Austrian government bought it, still unfinished, when it was exhibited in Vienna in 1908.
