Royal Reception in a Landscape (Shahnama frontispiece, right folio)

Maker unknown · 1444 · Opaque watercolor, gold, and silver on paper · 32.5 × 22.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is the first half of a double frontispiece painted at Shiraz in 1444 for a manuscript of the Shahnama; the other half is also in Cleveland. It does not illustrate the poem and probably shows the court for whose entertainment the book was made. On the other side of the leaf is a charm against insects that might eat the book, invoking the King of Cockroaches. The leaf was in Leipzig, with Philipp Walter Schulz, by 1914.
