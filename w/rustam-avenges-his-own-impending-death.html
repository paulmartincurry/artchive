Rustam Avenges His Own Impending Death (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Bashdan Qara · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 15.2 × 17.5 cm; page 47.5 × 32.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

When the manuscript reached the Ottoman court in 1568 it was reckoned at about 30,000 couplets, written out in nasta'liq script. This painting, on folio 472 recto, is attributed to Bashdan Qara and is only 15.2 cm high.
