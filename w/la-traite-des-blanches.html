La Traite des Blanches

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1899 · Lithograph in olive, red, yellow and black on cream wove paper · Image, incl registration marks; sheet cut within image at right margin: 79.5 × 57.6 cm; Sheet: 80.4 × 60 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The newspaper Le Journal published this poster to announce La Traite des Blanches, a novel by Jean-Louis Dubut de Laforest. Charles Verneau printed it in four colours: olive, red, yellow and black. Steinlen was born in Lausanne and had worked as a trainee designer at a textile mill in Mulhouse before moving to Montmartre.
