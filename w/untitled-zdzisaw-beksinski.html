Untitled

Zdzisław Beksiński · 1984 · Acrylic on hardboard · 98.5 × 101 cm · Private collection

Free to use with credit. Photo: Zdzisław Beksiński (rights held by Muzeum Historyczne w Sanoku), CC BY-SA 3.0

Beksiński refused to give titles to any of his drawings or paintings, and he was adamant that even he did not know what they meant. This one is painted in acrylic on board and comes from the collection of Piotr Dmochowski, the biggest private collection of his art. He was stabbed to death in his Warsaw apartment on 21 February 2005, three days before his seventy-sixth birthday.
