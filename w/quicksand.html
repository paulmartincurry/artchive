Quicksand

Nella Larsen · 1928

Nella Larsen started this novel, her first, in October 1925, while on leave for her health from her job at the Harlem branch of the New York Public Library. She had trained as a nurse and worked as one for years before becoming the first black woman to graduate from the library's school. She gave up library work in 1926, and the book came out in 1928. It is largely autobiographical, and it was praised by critics without making her much money.
