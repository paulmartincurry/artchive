Night Attack on the Sanjō Palace

Maker unknown · Second half of the 13th Century · Handscroll; ink and color on paper · Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Free to use

Neither the painters nor the person who paid for the scrolls is known. The set was probably made in one workshop between the 1250s and the 1280s, and only three scrolls and fragments of a fourth survive. The colour was laid on in three steps: the outlines were sketched, the colour was added in solid form, and the outlines were then redrawn in ink over the paint. The work was once given to a painter named Sumiyoshi Keion, whose existence is now doubted.
