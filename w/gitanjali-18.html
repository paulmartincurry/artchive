Clouds heap upon clouds and it darkens. Ah, love, why dost thou let me wait outside at the door all alone?

Gitanjali 18

Rabindranath Tagore · 1912

The title Gitanjali means Song Offerings, and Tagore was a composer as well as a poet. He left some two thousand songs, and he wrote both the words and the music of the national anthems of India and Bangladesh. He was the youngest of thirteen surviving children in a Calcutta household where his father kept professional musicians to teach the children Indian classical music.
