Mephistopheles in the Air (Faust)

Eugène Delacroix · 1828 · Lithograph in black on chine collé · Image: 30.3 × 25.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This lithograph belongs to Delacroix's series for Goethe's Faust, numbered 57 to 74 in Delteil's catalogue of his prints. He was twenty-nine or thirty when it was made in 1828. The image is about 30 centimetres high and 25 wide, and this impression carries the stamp of a collector, Henri Delacroix.
