The Calculating Old Man

Paul Klee · 1929 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Klee etched this plate in 1929, the year he turned fifty and the year the first major monograph on his work, written by Will Grohmann, was published. He travelled through Egypt from 1928 to 1929.
