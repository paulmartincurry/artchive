Two Sisters (On the Terrace)

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1881 · Oil on canvas · 100.4 × 80.9 cm (39 1/2 × 31 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The setting is the terrace of the Restaurant Fournaise at Chatou, a village on the Seine outside Paris. The two were not sisters: Renoir paid unrelated models to sit. He sold the canvas to the dealer Durand-Ruel on 7 July 1881 for 1,500 francs, and it stayed with the firm for more than forty years. In 1925 Annie Swan Coburn of Chicago bought it for 100,000 dollars, and she left it to the Art Institute in 1933.
