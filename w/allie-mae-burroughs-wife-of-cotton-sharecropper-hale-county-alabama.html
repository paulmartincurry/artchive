Allie Mae Burroughs, Wife of Cotton Sharecropper, Hale County, Alabama

Walker Evans · Summer 1936 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Walker Evans made this in Hale County, Alabama, in the summer of 1936, on an assignment from Fortune magazine with the writer James Agee. Fortune turned down Agee's article, and the work became the book Let Us Now Praise Famous Men, first published in 1941.
