Utah Copper Company Open-Pit Mine at Carr Fork, Bingham Canyon, Utah

Andreas Feininger · November 1942 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Feininger photographed the Utah Copper Company pit from the railroad in November 1942. The company had chosen open-pit mining, steam shovels and rail in 1904, and by 1912 the mine and its smelter made up what was called the largest industrial mining complex in the world. Feininger had trained as a cabinetmaker at the Bauhaus and came to the United States in 1939.
