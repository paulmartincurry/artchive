Some say horsemen, some say warriors,
Some say a fleet of ships is the loveliest
Vision in this dark world, but I say it’s
What you love.

Fragment 16 (Some Say Horsemen)

Sappho of Lesbos · c. 600 BCE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The fragments are the only source for Sappho's life that dates from her own time. The earliest biography of her to survive was written about eight centuries later. Ancient writers gave her three brothers, and two of them, Charaxos and Larichos, are named in a poem of hers that was discovered in 2014.
