The Raft of the Medusa

Théodore Géricault · 1818–1819 · Oil on canvas · Louvre

Free to use

Nobody commissioned this painting. Géricault interviewed two survivors of the wreck, built a scale model of the raft with the ship's carpenter, and drew corpses in a hospital morgue. He then shaved his head and shut himself in his studio from November 1818 to July 1819; the painting took eight months and the whole project eighteen. He was 27 when he finished. Eugène Delacroix modelled for the figure lying face down in the foreground. The bitumen in the paint has since darkened and wrinkled, and details in large areas can hardly be made out.
