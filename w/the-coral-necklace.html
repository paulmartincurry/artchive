The Coral Necklace

Frederick Sandys · 1871 · Black, brown, and red chalk · 55.9 x 39.2 cm (22 x 15 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The sitter is Mary Emma Jones, Sandys's favourite model, his partner and the mother of his nine children; the two called themselves Mr and Mrs Neville. Sandys was a friend of Rossetti and close to the Pre-Raphaelites.
