Pluto in Enhanced Colour

New Horizons, NASA/Johns Hopkins APL/Southwest Research Institute · 13 July 2015 · Composite of four LORRI images with colour data from the Ralph instrument

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute

New Horizons took the four black-and-white images on 13 July 2015, from 450,000 kilometres away, with its Long Range Reconnaissance Imager. Colour data from its Ralph instrument were added to make this enhanced-colour view. The lower right edge lacked high-resolution colour coverage when it was released.
