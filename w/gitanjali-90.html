On the day when death will knock at thy door what wilt thou offer to him?

Oh, I will set before my guest the full vessel of my life—I will never let him go with empty hands.

Gitanjali 90

Rabindranath Tagore · 1912

In November 1913 Tagore learned he had won the Nobel Prize in Literature, largely for the English Gitanjali, and became the first non-European to receive it. His telegram of thanks was read at the Nobel Banquet in Stockholm on 10 December 1913. He went to Sweden to give his acceptance speech eight years later, in 1921.
