The Dancing Fox

Maker unknown · 1766 · Color woodblock print; chuban · 25.2 × 18.8 cm (9 15/16 × 7 7/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A fox dressed as a Shinto priest dances by a shrine, probably Inari Taisha, dedicated to the fox god. The numbers on its clothes are the long months of 1766, a hidden calendar.
