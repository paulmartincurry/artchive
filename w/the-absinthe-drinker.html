The Absinthe Drinker

Félicien Rops · Undated · Heliogravure on ivory wove paper · Image: 33.5 × 22.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

By 1870 Rops was living in Paris for most of the year, among its writers. This impression is a heliogravure with an image 33.5 centimetres tall. It belongs to the Albert Roullier Memorial Collection at the Art Institute of Chicago.
