The Good Samaritan

Rodolphe Bresdin · 1861 · Lithograph on wove paper · Image: 56.5 × 44.4 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Bresdin signed the stone twice: with his name and the date 1861, which print in reverse, and with the initials RB on the camel's rump. Lemercier printed it in Paris. The image is more than half a metre high, and it is one of only twenty lithographs he made beside one hundred and forty etchings. Huysmans described the print in his novel À rebours, among those owned by its hero Des Esseintes.
