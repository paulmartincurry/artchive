The Strange Occurrence of Ukiyo Matahei and His Famous Paintings

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1853 · diptych, color woodblock prints · Overall: 37.8 × 26.4 cm (14 7/8 × 10 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Besides Kuniyoshi's signature and paulownia seal, the sheets carry the names of the publisher, Echimura Heisuke, and the engraver, Hori Take, and two censors' seals. A decade earlier the Tenpō Reforms had banned pictures of courtesans and actors, which may have pushed Kuniyoshi towards comic pictures that disguised real ones. Three years after this diptych he was struck by palsy, which made it hard for him to move his limbs.
