Dam Hetch Hetchy! As well dam for water-tanks the people's cathedrals and churches, for no holier temple has ever been consecrated by the heart of man.

The Yosemite — Dam Hetch Hetchy!

John Muir · 1912

In 1908 the Secretary of the Interior granted San Francisco the rights to develop the Tuolumne River, which provoked a seven-year struggle with the Sierra Club, led by Muir. He wrote to President Roosevelt pleading for him to scuttle the project. Woodrow Wilson signed the bill authorizing the dam on 19 December 1913, and Muir died almost exactly a year later. O'Shaughnessy Dam was finished in 1923, and the reservoir first filled in May of that year.
