After the Battle of Grunwald: The Solidarity of the Northern Slavs (The Slav Epic)

Alphonse Mucha · 1924 · Painting on canvas · 405 × 610 cm · City of Prague (shown at Moravský Krumlov chateau)

Free to use

Mucha finished this, the tenth canvas of The Slav Epic, in 1924; it measures 405 by 610 centimetres and shows the aftermath of the battle fought at Grunwald in 1410. He gave the completed cycle to the city of Prague in 1928. During the Second World War the canvases were wrapped and hidden so the Nazis could not seize them.
