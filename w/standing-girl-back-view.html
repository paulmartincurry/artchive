Standing Girl, Back View

Egon Schiele · 1908 · Gouache and watercolor over graphite on paper · 31.4 × 23.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Schiele was seventeen or eighteen in 1908, the year of this sheet and of his first exhibition, in Klosterneuburg. The year before, he had sought out Gustav Klimt, who bought his drawings and arranged models for him. Scofield Thayer bequeathed the drawing to the Metropolitan Museum in 1982.
