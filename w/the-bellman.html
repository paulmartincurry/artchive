The Bellman

Samuel Palmer · 1879 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Palmer etched this plate in 1879, aged seventy-three or seventy-four, and died two years later. It was among the etchings he made after his return to England in 1840. Mr. and Mrs. Lewis B. Williams gave this impression to the Cleveland Museum of Art in honour of Henry Sayles Francis, its curator of prints.
