Spoliarium

Juan Luna · 1884 · Oil on canvas · National Museum of the Philippines

Free to use

Juan Luna spent eight months on this canvas, which is 4.22 metres high and 7.675 wide. It took the first gold medal at the national exhibition in Madrid in 1884, and the provincial government of Barcelona bought it for 20,000 pesetas. It was in storage in a Barcelona museum when the building was burned and looted in 1937, and the restoration in Madrid took 18 years. Spain gave the painting to the Philippines in 1958, and it travelled in three pieces, each in its own crate.
