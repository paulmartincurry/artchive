Mountains and Sea

Helen Frankenthaler · 1952 · Oil and charcoal on canvas · 220 × 297.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington (on extended loan from the Helen Frankenthaler Foundation)

In copyright. Look and learn: shown for study, not for download.

Frankenthaler was 23 when she painted this, and it was the first work she exhibited professionally. She made it after a road trip to Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia, in the summer of 1952. She laid an unprimed canvas on the floor and stained it with oil paint diluted with turpentine, letting the colors bleed; it was the first time she used the technique. Critics panned it at first.
