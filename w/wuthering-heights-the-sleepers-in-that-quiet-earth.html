Wuthering Heights — The sleepers in that quiet earth (Ch. 34, close)

Emily Brontë · 1847

Emily Brontë caught a severe cold at her brother Branwell's funeral in the autumn of 1848 and refused medical help as she grew weaker. She died at about two in the afternoon on 19 December, a year after the novel came out. She had grown so thin that her coffin measured only 16 inches across, and the carpenter said he had never made a narrower one for an adult.
