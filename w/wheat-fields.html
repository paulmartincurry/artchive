Wheat Fields

Jacob van Ruisdael · c. 1670 · Oil on canvas · 100 × 130.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Jacob van Ruisdael painted twenty-seven pictures of fields of grain. This one is 100 by 130 centimetres, large for the subject, and came to the museum with Benjamin Altman's bequest.
