Myologie Complette en Couleur et Grandeur Naturelle

Jacques Fabien Gautier d'Agoty · 1746 · Colour mezzotint · 56 × 39.5 cm (volume) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Jacques Fabien Gautier d'Agoty, born in Marseille in 1711, learned colour printing from Jacob Christoph Le Blon, who invented a process using blue, yellow and red plates. D'Agoty claimed to have proposed adding black and to have been rebuffed. This plate is from the album of muscle anatomy he made in 1746 with the anatomist Duverney, printed in colour mezzotint; the volume stands 56 centimetres tall.
