Going Under (A Life)

Max Klinger · 1884 (this edition 1898) · Etching and aquatint · Plate: 27.6 × 22.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Klinger made this plate in 1884, the year after he moved to Paris, and took it through six states. This impression comes from the fourth edition of the portfolio, issued in 1898. He was twenty-six or twenty-seven, and he had first begun to sculpt about a year earlier. He would combine etching, aquatint and drypoint in a single plate.
