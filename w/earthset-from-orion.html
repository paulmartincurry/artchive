Earthset from Orion (art002e021278)

NASA (Artemis II crew) · 6 April 2026 · Digital photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

The Artemis II crew took this picture of the Earth setting as they passed behind the far side of the Moon on 6 April 2026, during a seven-hour lunar flyby. NASA's caption links it to the Earthrise photograph taken by the Apollo 8 astronauts in 1968. The mission was the first crewed flight of the Orion spacecraft, which the crew named Integrity.
