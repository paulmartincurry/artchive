Hercules and the Lernaean Hydra

Gustave Moreau · 1875–76 · Oil on canvas · 179.3 × 154 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Moreau made preliminary studies for every detail of this canvas and went to the Paris zoo to sketch live snakes. He showed it at the Salon of 1876, the first to which he had submitted paintings since the harsh press of 1869. Eleven years later, in July 1887, he sold it to Louis Mante of Marseille for 30,000 francs. It stayed with the Mante family until a Paris sale in 1956.
