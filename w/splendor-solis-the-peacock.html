Splendor Solis: The Peacock

Maker unknown · 1582 (Harley MS 3469) · Parchment · British Library

Free to use

The earliest version of this alchemical text was written in Central German, is dated 1532–1535 and is kept in Berlin. It is painted on vellum and highlighted with gold, with borders like those of a book of hours, and the London manuscript is one of the later copies. The work runs to 22 images. Its author was given as Salomon Trismosin, a name believed to be a pseudonym.
