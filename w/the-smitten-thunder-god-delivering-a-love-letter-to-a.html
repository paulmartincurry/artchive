The Smitten Thunder God Delivering a Love Letter to a Courtesan

Suzuki Harunobu · c. 1767/68 · Color woodblock print; hashira-e · Art Institute of Chicago

This print is 67.7 centimetres tall and only 11.8 wide. Suzuki Harunobu made more than a thousand colour prints between 1765 and 1770, the year of his death, and he was the first to print in full colour, using as many as ten blocks on one sheet. The German collector Alexander Mosle sold this one to Kate Buckingham of Chicago in May 1928, and she gave it to the Art Institute that year.
