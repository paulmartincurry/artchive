The Art of War (Ch. IV, V, VII)

Sun Tzu · ca. 500 BCE

Translated by Lionel Giles, 1910

The treatise has thirteen chapters and is attributed to a general called Sun Tzu, a title that means Master Sun. It is now generally accepted that it was completed between 500 and 430 BC. Chinese scholars began to doubt around the twelfth century that Sun Tzu had existed. In 1972 a copy written on bamboo slips was found in tombs near Linyi in Shandong that had been sealed between 134 and 118 BC.
