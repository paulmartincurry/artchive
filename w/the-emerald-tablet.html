What is below is like that which is above, and what is above is like that which is below, to accomplish the miracles of one thing.

The Emerald Tablet

Hermetic / Jabir ibn Hayyan Tradition · ca. 8th century CE

Translated by English version as printed by H. Stanley Redgrove, 1911 (after Thomas Thomson, 1830)

The oldest version of this text is an appendix to an Arabic treatise, the Book of the Secret of Creation, thought to have been compiled in the late eighth or early ninth century. The treatise frames it with a story in which a tablet of emerald is found in a crypt beneath a statue of Hermes, in the hands of an old man seated with a book. Europe knew it only in Latin translations, the oldest from the twelfth century, until two scholars recovered the Arabic versions in the twentieth. Isaac Newton made an English translation.
