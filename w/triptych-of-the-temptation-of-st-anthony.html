Triptych of the Temptation of St. Anthony

Hieronymus Bosch · ca. 1501 · Oil paint · National Museum of Ancient Art, Lisbon

Free to use

Tree-ring analysis of the wood dates the triptych to around 1501, later than the 1490s it was usually given. The centre panel measures 131.5 by 119 centimetres and each wing 131.5 by 53. The wings are hinged shutters, painted in grey monochrome on the outside with the Arrest of Christ and Christ Carrying the Cross. The layman holding up the saint in the left panel has traditionally been identified as Bosch himself. The painting was given to the Lisbon museum in 1911.
