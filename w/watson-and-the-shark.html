Watson and the Shark

John Singleton Copley · 1778 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Brook Watson was a 14-year-old cabin boy when a shark took his right leg below the knee in Havana Harbor in 1749. He became a merchant, befriended Copley after the painter reached London in 1774, and commissioned the picture. Copley had never been to Havana and had probably never seen a shark. Infrared analysis shows the old boatswain began as a young man. Watson left the painting to Christ's Hospital at his death in 1807, and the school sold it to the National Gallery of Art in 1963.
