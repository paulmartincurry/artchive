Virginian Partridge (The Birds of America, Plate 76)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1830 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · 67 × 100.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Audubon sailed for Britain in 1826 with 250 of his drawings, looking for subscribers and for engravers. William Lizars of Edinburgh engraved up to ten of the first plates and stopped when his colourists went on strike. In 1827 Audubon engaged Robert Havell Jr. in London, who oversaw the project until it was finished in 1838. This is plate 76.
