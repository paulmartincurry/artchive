Revelations of Divine Love — The Hazel-Nut (Ch. 5)

Julian of Norwich · c. 1390s–1410s

Julian's writings are the earliest surviving works in English attributed to a woman. The earliest record of her is the will of Roger Reed, rector of St Michael Coslany in Norwich, who on 20 March 1394 left 12 shillings to Julian, anchorite. In around 1414, when she was in her seventies, the mystic Margery Kempe travelled to Norwich to ask her advice.
