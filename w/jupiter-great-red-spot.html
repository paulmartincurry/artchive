Jupiter Great Red Spot

Voyager 1, NASA/JPL · 25 February 1979 · Spacecraft image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL

Voyager 1 recorded this view on 25 February 1979, when it was 9.2 million kilometres from Jupiter and sent back the first detailed image of the Great Red Spot. The storm has been watched regularly since 5 September 1831. A similar spot was recorded from 1665 to 1713, but a 2024 study suggests it may not have been the same one.
