Stage Design: Vaulted Hall of a Palace

Giuseppe Galli Bibiena · ca. 1740 · Pen and brown ink, gray, brown, and green wash on paper, laid down · 31 × 44.6 cm (12 3/16 × 17 9/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

When he drew this, Giuseppe Galli Bibiena was about forty-four and First Theatrical Engineer to the Habsburg court in Vienna, a post he held from 1723 to 1747. Most of the sets he designed were built to be temporary and no longer exist, though many of his drawings were published. This sheet is 31 by 44.6 centimetres.
