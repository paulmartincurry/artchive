Beach at Cabasson (Baigne-Cul)

Henri Edmond Cross · 1891–92 · Oil on canvas · 65.3 × 92.3 cm (25 3/4 × 36 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Rheumatoid arthritis took Cross to Cabasson, a hamlet on the Mediterranean coast, in 1891. After the move he took up painting in small dashes and dots of colour.
