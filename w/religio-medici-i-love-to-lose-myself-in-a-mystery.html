I love to lose myself in a mystery; to pursue my reason to an O altitudo! ’Tis my solitary recreation to pose my apprehension with those involved enigmas and riddles of the Trinity—with incarnation and resurrection.

Religio Medici — I love to lose myself in a mystery (Part I, 9)

Sir Thomas Browne · 1643

Religio Medici was published in 1643, after an unauthorised version had been distributed the year before. It was translated into Latin, French, German, Dutch and Italian and reprinted about eight times in Browne's lifetime.
