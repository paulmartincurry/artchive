The Dead Mother and Her Child

Edvard Munch · 1901 · Etching with open bite and drypoint in black ink on heavyweight cream wove paper · Plate: 32.3 × 49.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Otto Felsing, a German printer born in 1854, printed this plate for Munch in 1901. It is an etching with open bite and drypoint, about 32 by 49 centimetres. Munch's own mother died of tuberculosis in 1868, when he was a small child.
