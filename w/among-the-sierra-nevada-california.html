Among the Sierra Nevada, California

Albert Bierstadt · 1868 · Oil on canvas · Smithsonian American Art Museum

Free to use

Albert Bierstadt painted this California view in his studio in Rome in the winter of 1867–68, four years after his trip to the Sierra Nevada. He showed it in London, then in Berlin, where it won a gold medal, and sold it in Boston in 1869. In 1941 a later owner had it taken out of its frame and glued to a wall of her new house. After her death in 1976 conservators spent about 600 hours cleaning it, and the original gilded frame turned up in four pieces in a barn on the property.
