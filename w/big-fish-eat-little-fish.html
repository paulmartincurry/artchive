Big Fish Eat Little Fish

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1557 · Engraving; fourth state of four · plate: 23 × 30 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bruegel made the drawing in 1556, in grey and black ink; it is now in the Albertina in Vienna. Pieter van der Heyden engraved it, and Hieronymus Cock published it in 1557 under the name of Hieronymus Bosch, who had died in 1516, probably because Bosch was better known than Bruegel. This impression is from the fourth and last state of the plate, published by Joannes Galle.
