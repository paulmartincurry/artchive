Japanese Cranes Over Waves

Christopher Dresser · ca. 1870 · Glazed earthenware · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Christopher Dresser designed this blue and white tile about six years before he first travelled to Japan, in 1876. He had become associated with Japanese art in 1862, and from the 1860s onwards he produced ceramic designs for a string of firms, Mintons and Wedgwood among them. The tile is 15.2 by 15.4 centimetres.
