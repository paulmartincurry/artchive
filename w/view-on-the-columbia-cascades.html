View on the Columbia, Cascades

Carleton Watkins · 1867 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 40.0 × 52.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Watkins worked with a mammoth-plate camera that used glass plates of 18 by 22 inches; this print is 40 by 52 centimetres. In 1867, the year of this view, he opened his first public gallery and sent photographs to the Universal Exposition in Paris, where he won a medal.
