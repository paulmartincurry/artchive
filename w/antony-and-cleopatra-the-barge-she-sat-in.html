The barge she sat in, like a burnished throne,
Burned on the water. The poop was beaten gold;
Purple the sails, and so perfumed that
The winds were love-sick with them; the oars were silver,

Antony and Cleopatra — The barge she sat in (Act II, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · c. 1607

The play was first performed around 1607. Its plot is based on Thomas North's 1579 English translation of Plutarch's Lives. It was entered in the Stationers' Register in May 1608, but does not seem to have been printed until the First Folio of 1623.
