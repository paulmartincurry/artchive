Two Demons, Fettered

Maker unknown · 15th century · Ink, opaque watercolor and gold on paper · 14.6 × 22.1 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The drawing was in the Sakisian collection by 1931 and then with the dealer Hagop Kevorkian in New York, from whom the Freer Gallery of Art bought it in 1937. It is small, 14.6 by 22.1 cm, and is dated to the Timurid period.
