Hu Sanniang (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827–30 · Color woodblock print; oban · 39.5 × 27 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This sheet comes from the series Kuniyoshi began in 1827, his first major commission: the heroes of the Chinese tale Shuihu Zhuan, one to a sheet. He drew tattoos on his heroes, a novelty that soon influenced fashion in Edo. The series became extremely popular there, and the demand for his warrior prints gained him entry to the main ukiyo-e and literary circles. He was about thirty.
