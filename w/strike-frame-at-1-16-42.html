Sergei Eisenstein · 1925 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Strike was Sergei Eisenstein's first full-length feature, shot by Eduard Tisse, who had started as a newsreel cameraman in the Civil War. Its average shot lasts 2.5 seconds, less than half that of a typical Hollywood film. Water runs through it, from puddles and swimming early on to the firemen who turn large jets of water on the workers.
