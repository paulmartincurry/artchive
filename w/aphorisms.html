Where no gods are, spectres rule. … Where children are, there is the golden age.

Aphorisms (Where No Gods Are)

Novalis · c. 1798–1800

Translated by Frederic H. Hedge

Novalis took a law degree in 1794 and enrolled in 1797 at the mining university in Freiberg, where he studied electricity, medicine, chemistry, physics, mathematics and mineralogy. He wrote in the fragment form alongside Friedrich Schlegel and published his pieces in Schlegel's journal, Athenaeum. After his death his friends published only a small sample of his philosophical and scientific writings; his notebooks were published more extensively in the twentieth century.
