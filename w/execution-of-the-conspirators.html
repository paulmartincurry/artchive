Execution of the Conspirators

Alexander Gardner · 7 July 1865 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 16.8 × 24.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Alexander Gardner was the only photographer allowed at the hanging. Four people sentenced by a military tribunal, Lewis Powell, Mary Surratt, David Herold and George Atzerodt, were executed at the Washington Arsenal on 7 July 1865. Gardner's photographs of it were later turned into woodcuts for publication in Harper's Weekly.
