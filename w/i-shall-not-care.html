The Artchive
When I am dead and over me bright April
Shakes out her rain-drenched hair,
Though you should lean above me broken-hearted,
I shall not care.

I shall have peace, as leafy trees are peaceful
When rain bends down the bough,
And I shall be more silent and cold-hearted
Than you are now.

I Shall Not Care

Sara Teasdale · 1917

Teasdale had poor health for much of her childhood and was schooled at home until she was 9. Her first poem was published in 1907 in Reedy's Mirror, a St. Louis newspaper, and her first collection, Sonnets to Duse and Other Poems, came out that same year.
