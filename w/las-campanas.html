I love them—and I hearken
As the winds their notes prolong,

Las campanas (The Carillon)

Rosalía de Castro · 1884

Translated by Thomas Walsh (ed.), 1920

Rosalía de Castro wrote in both Galician and Spanish, and her Spanish collection En las orillas del Sar appeared in 1884. She died of uterine cancer at Padrón the following July, aged forty-eight. Her husband, the historian and journalist Manuel Murguía, edited her books.
