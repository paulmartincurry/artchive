Two-Panel Screen Depicting Wrestlers

Maker unknown · 17th century · Color and ink on paper · H x W (overall): 170 x 178 cm (66 15/16 x 70 1/16 in) · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art), Washington

Free to use

Pairs of wrestlers practise sumo in a walled courtyard. Sumo became a professional sport early in the Edo period, when this screen was painted.
