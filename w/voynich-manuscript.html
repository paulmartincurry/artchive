Voynich Manuscript

Maker unknown · ca. 1404–1438 · Vellum · Beinecke Library, Yale University

Free to use

No one has read this book. Its script has never been deciphered, and codebreakers from both world wars tried. The vellum was radiocarbon dated in 2009 to between 1404 and 1438, and it took at least fourteen or fifteen whole calfskins. The text was written with a quill in iron gall ink. About 240 pages survive of at least 272. The book dealer Wilfrid Voynich bought it in 1912 from the Jesuits, and it reached Yale in 1969 as a gift from a dealer who could not find a buyer.
