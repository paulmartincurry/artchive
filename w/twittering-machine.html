Twittering Machine (Die Zwitscher-Maschine)

Paul Klee · 1922 · Watercolor and ink; oil transfer on paper · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Klee had been teaching at the Bauhaus for a year when he made this drawing. It hung for several years in the Alte Nationalgalerie in Berlin until 1933, when Hitler declared it degenerate art. The Nazis seized it and in 1939 sold it to a Berlin dealer for $120; the Museum of Modern Art bought it the same year. Klee was the son of a musicologist, and more than 100 pieces of music have been written after this sheet.
