Nelly, I am Heathcliff! He’s always, always in my mind: not as a pleasure, any more than I am always a pleasure to myself, but as my own being.

Wuthering Heights — I am Heathcliff (Ch. 9)

Emily Brontë · 1847

This is Emily Brontë's only novel, published on 24 November 1847, when she was twenty-nine. Harper & Brothers of New York brought out the first American edition in April 1848, and she died that December, aged thirty. In 1850 her sister Charlotte edited a second edition, correcting the punctuation and spelling and thinning out the Yorkshire dialect of the servant Joseph.
