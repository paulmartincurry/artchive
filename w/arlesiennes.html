Arlésiennes (Mistral)

Paul Gauguin · 1888 · Oil on jute canvas · 73 × 92 cm (28 3/4 × 36 3/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Gauguin painted this in Arles during the two months he spent with Vincent van Gogh in the winter of 1888, one of 17 canvases he finished there. The garden is the public one directly across the street from the house the two men shared. He was forty. He sold it to Theo van Gogh for 300 francs, probably in the first half of 1889, and the Art Institute bought it in 1934.
