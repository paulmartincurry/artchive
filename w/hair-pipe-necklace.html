Hair-Pipe Necklace

Nakota (?) maker · early 1900s · Cow-bone hair pipes, glass beads, brass beads, silk ribbon, metal coin, Native-tanned hide or rawhide stringing, vegetable-tanned hide separators · 236.2 cm (93 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The tube beads, once made of shell, became popular in cow bone made almost entirely at one mill in New Jersey and traded across the Plains. Necklaces and breastplates of them are still powwow regalia.
