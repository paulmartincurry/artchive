The Swan (Svanen), No. 17, Group IX/SUW

Hilma af Klint · 1915 · Oil on Canvas · Stiftelsen Hilma af Klints Verk (The Hilma af Klint Foundation)

Free to use

The Swan is one of the series inside the Paintings for the Temple, 196 works that Hilma af Klint made between 1906 and 1915, with a break from 1908 to 1912. Most of them are in oil. She earned her income from conventional landscapes, portraits and botanical drawings, and directed that the abstract work stay unseen until twenty years after her death.
