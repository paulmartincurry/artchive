The Dancer's Reward (Aubrey Beardsley's Illustrations to Salome by Oscar Wilde)

Aubrey Beardsley · drawn 1893–94; portfolio published 1906–7 · Process print from line block · 35.2 × 27.9 cm (portfolio) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Oscar Wilde wrote Salome in French in Paris, beginning late in 1891. Sarah Bernhardt took it into rehearsal in London in June 1892, but the censor refused a licence because the play showed biblical characters, and it was not performed publicly in Britain until 1931. This print comes from a portfolio of Beardsley's Salome drawings issued by John Lane around 1906–7.
