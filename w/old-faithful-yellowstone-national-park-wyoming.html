Old Faithful, Yellowstone National Park, Wyoming

Ansel Adams · 1941–42 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

The negatives Ansel Adams made on his contract for the Department of the Interior were legally the property of the United States government. He kept them all the same, believing that the National Archives did not take proper care of photographic material, and used various subterfuges to put off its queries. The series is now in the National Archives among the records of the National Park Service.
