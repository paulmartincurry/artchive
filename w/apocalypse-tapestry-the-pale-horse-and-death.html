Apocalypse Tapestry: The Pale Horse and Death (detail)

Jean Bondol (design) · 1377–1382 · Tapestry · Château d'Angers

Free to use

Louis I, Duke of Anjou, commissioned the tapestries around 1373, and they were woven in Paris between 1377 and 1382 from designs by Jean Bondol. During the French Revolution they were cut into pieces and used as floor mats, to protect orange trees from frost and to insulate horse stables. The surviving fragments were rediscovered in 1848.
