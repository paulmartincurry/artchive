‘Because all the world is muddy and I alone am clear,’ said Qu Yuan, ‘and because all men are drunk and I alone am sober, I have been sent into exile.’

The Fisherman (Yu fu)

Qu Yuan · ca. 290 BCE

Translated by David Hawkes · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This short piece is one of the 17 sections of the Chu Ci, an anthology put together by Wang Yi, a librarian at the Han court in the 2nd century AD. Wang Yi counted it among 25 works by Qu Yuan, but most modern scholars think someone else wrote it, perhaps a person who knew him well. Qu Yuan had served King Huai of Chu as Left Minister and was twice sent into exile after rivals slandered him. Tradition says he drowned himself in the Miluo River in 278 BC.
