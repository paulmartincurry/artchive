The Little Fourteen-Year-Old Dancer

Edgar Degas · modeled 1881, cast 1922, modern tutu · Partially tinted bronze, cotton tarlatan, silk satin, and wood · Overall: H. 38 1/2 × W. 17 1/4 × D. 14 3/8 in. · The Met

Free to use

Degas modelled the figure in 1881, when he was forty-six or forty-seven. This bronze was cast in 1922, five years after his death, and it stands just under a metre tall. The bronze is partly tinted and the figure is dressed in real cotton tarlatan and silk satin; the tutu it wears now is a modern replacement. It came to the Metropolitan Museum in 1929 with the bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer.
