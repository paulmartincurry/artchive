The Gulf Stream

Winslow Homer · 1899; reworked by 1906 · Oil on canvas · 28 1/8 × 49 1/8 in. (71.4 × 124.8 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Homer started the painting by September 1899, after three months of watercolours in Nassau the winter before. It went to the Pennsylvania Academy in 1900, and when it came back he repainted the sea, broke the gunwale, added the sail and the distant ship, and made the name Anna, Key West, legible on the boat. He asked $4,000 for it. In 1906 the whole jury of the National Academy of Design petitioned the Metropolitan Museum to buy it, and the museum did that year.
