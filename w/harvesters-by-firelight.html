The Artchive

Harvesters by Firelight

Samuel Palmer · 1830 · Pen and black ink with watercolor and gouache on wove paper · 28.7 × 36.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

A pencil note on the back, possibly written by the painter's son and biographer A. H. Palmer, says the glow is the reflection of one of the incendiary fires in Kent and that the sheet was done, he thinks, the next day. The same note suggests the building is Ightham Mote. Palmer was twenty-five and living at Shoreham. Paul Mellon gave the drawing to the National Gallery of Art in 1986.
