Moses

Michelangelo · c. 1513–1515 · Marble · San Pietro in Vincoli

Free to use with credit. Photo: Livioandronico2013, CC BY-SA 4.0

Pope Julius II ordered his tomb from Michelangelo in 1505 and died in 1513, and the tomb was not finished until 1545. The first design called for more than forty statues, with Moses set on a tier about 3.74 metres up, opposite a figure of St Paul. In the tomb as built he sits at the centre of the bottom tier. The horns follow the Latin Bible of the time, which describes the face of Moses with a word meaning horned. The figure is 235 centimetres high.
