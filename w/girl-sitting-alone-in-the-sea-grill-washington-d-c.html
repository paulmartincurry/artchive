Girl Sitting Alone in the Sea Grill, Washington, D.C.

Esther Bubley · April 1943 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Bubley started at the Office of War Information in the fall of 1942 as a darkroom assistant for Roy Stryker, then moved on to photographing life on the home front. The long caption on the record gives the woman's own account of why she comes to the Sea Grill.
