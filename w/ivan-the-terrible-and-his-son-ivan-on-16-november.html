Ivan the Terrible and His Son Ivan on 16 November 1581

Ilya Repin · 1883–85 · Oil on canvas · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

Ilya Repin sold the painting to Pavel Tretyakov as soon as it was finished in 1885, and on 1 April that year viewings were forbidden. The ban was lifted on 11 July. The painter Grigoriy Myasoyedov sat for the Tsar and the writer Vsevolod Garshin for his son. A visitor slashed the canvas three times with a knife in 1913, and Repin helped restore it. In 2018 another visitor broke the protective glass with a metal bar and pierced the canvas in three places.
