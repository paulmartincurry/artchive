The Three Trees

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1643 · Etching, drypoint and burin · 213 × 279 mm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Rembrandt signed and dated the plate 1643 and worked it in etching, drypoint and burin. It is his largest landscape print, 213 by 279 millimetres. The dense parallel hatching in the upper left corner has led to debate over whether the marks are rain or rays of sunlight.
