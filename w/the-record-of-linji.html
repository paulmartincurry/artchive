The Record of Linji

Linji Yixuan (Rinzai Gigen) · ca. 866 CE

Translated by Ruth Fuller Sasaki · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Linji wrote nothing himself. What survives is said to rest on notes his students took of his sermons and dialogues, and the names of those students are not known. He taught for about ten years at a small temple on the banks of the Hutuo River in Hebei, and took his name from it. The standard edition of the sayings was printed in 1120, some two hundred and fifty years after his death.
