The Last Judgment (Vienna Triptych)

Hieronymus Bosch · ca. 1482 · Oil on oak panel · Academy of Fine Arts Vienna

Free to use

Tree-ring analysis of the oak panels shows the triptych cannot have been painted before 1482. The oldest record of it is a 1659 inventory of Archduke Leopold Wilhelm of Austria's collection. It was heavily repainted in the 17th and 18th centuries and has lost part of its colour. The outsides of the shutters are painted in grisaille, with a saint on each.
