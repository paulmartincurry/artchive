Traveller, the road is only
your footprint, and no more;
traveller, there’s no road,
the road is your travelling.

Proverbios y cantares XXIX (Caminante, son tus huellas)

Antonio Machado · 1912

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The poem is one of the Proverbios y cantares, short reflective poems resembling popular songs or sayings, in Machado's book Campos de Castilla. A few weeks after the book came out, his young wife Leonor died, on 1 August 1912.
