And sweetest – in the Gale – is heard –
And sore must be the storm –

'Hope' is the thing with feathers (314)

Emily Dickinson · ca. 1861

Only ten of Dickinson's nearly 1,800 poems were published while she was alive. Few people around her knew she was writing until after her death in 1886, when her younger sister Lavinia discovered the poems in her desk. She was thirty in 1861.
