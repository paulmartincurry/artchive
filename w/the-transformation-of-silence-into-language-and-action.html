Your silence will not protect you.

The Transformation of Silence into Language and Action

Audre Lorde · 1977

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

In 1977 Lorde was forty-three, living on Staten Island and teaching as a professor of English at John Jay College of Criminal Justice, where she fought for the creation of a black studies department. She had trained as a librarian, taking a master's degree in library science at Columbia in 1961, and was head librarian at the Town School in New York City from 1966 to 1968. She was nearsighted to the point of being legally blind.
