Art happens--no hovel is safe from it, no Prince may depend upon it, the vastest intelligence cannot bring it about, and puny efforts to make it universal end in quaint comedy, and coarse farce.

The Ten O'Clock Lecture: Art Happens

James McNeill Whistler · 1885

In 1877 Whistler sued the critic John Ruskin for libel over a review of his painting Nocturne in Black and Gold: The Falling Rocket. The jury found in his favour but awarded him one farthing in damages, and the court costs were split. The case, along with debts from building his house in Tite Street, Chelsea, left him bankrupt by May 1879, and his work, collections and house were auctioned.
