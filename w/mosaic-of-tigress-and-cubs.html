Mosaic of Tigress and Cubs

Maker unknown · 300s CE · tesserae · Overall: 142.9 × 135.4 cm (56 1/4 × 53 5/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

More than ten thousand small cut-stone tiles make up this mosaic, which is about 1.4 metres on each side. The tigress has one cub on her back and two in front of her. Tigers are relatively rare in Roman art, and the female appears more often than the male. The mosaic was sold to Cleveland by the Paris gallery of Marianne Maspero.
