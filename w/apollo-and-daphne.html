Apollo and Daphne

Gian Lorenzo Bernini · 1622–1625 · Carrara marble · Galleria Borghese

Cardinal Scipione Borghese ordered this group after giving away another Bernini he owned, The Rape of Proserpina, to the new pope's nephew. Most of the early carving was done in 1622 and 1623; Bernini then broke off to make his David and finished in 1625. He did not carve all of it himself. Giuliano Finelli, from his workshop, cut the twigs and leaves springing from Daphne's hands and her windswept hair. The group stands 243 centimetres high and was planned to be seen from a doorway, slightly from the right.
