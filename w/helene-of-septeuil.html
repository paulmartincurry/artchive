Hélène of Septeuil

Mary Cassatt · c. 1890 · Etching in dark brown ink on ivory wove paper · Image/plate: 23.6 × 15.8 cm (9 5/16 × 6 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

The plate measures 23.6 by 15.8 centimetres, and this impression is printed in dark brown ink on ivory paper. Mary Cassatt was in her mid-forties and living in France when she made it. The print was a gift to the Art Institute of Chicago from Charles L. Hutchinson.
