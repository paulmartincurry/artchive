To the Stone-Cutters

Robinson Jeffers · 1925

Jeffers learned stonemasonry in a short, informal apprenticeship and built Tor House, his granite home in Carmel, with his own hands. He kept adding to it all his life, writing in the mornings and working on the house in the afternoons. Beside it he later built a four-storey stone tower.
