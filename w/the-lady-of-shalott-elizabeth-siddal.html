The Lady of Shalott

Elizabeth Siddal · 15 December 1853 · Pen and ink

Free to use

Elizabeth Siddal was twenty-four when she made this drawing from Tennyson's ballad, and she signed it with her initials and the spelling of her surname she had adopted that year. She had been working at a millinery in London in 1849 when painters first asked her to sit as a model. Her first reading of Tennyson was a poem or two printed on a piece of paper that had been wrapped around butter.
