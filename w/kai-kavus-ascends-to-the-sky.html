Kai Kavus Ascends to the Sky (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Qadimi · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.4 × 18.3 cm; page 47.3 × 31.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is folio 134 recto, with the painting attributed to Qadimi. After the manuscript reached Istanbul in 1568 it stayed in the Topkapı Palace library for a long time, and readers added commentaries in its margins around 1800.
