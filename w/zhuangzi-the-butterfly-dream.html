But he didn't know if he was Chuang Chou who had dreamt he was a butterfly, or a butterfly dreaming he was Chuang Chou.

Zhuangzi — The Butterfly Dream

Zhuangzi (Chuang Tzu) · ca. 300 BCE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The dream closes the second chapter of the Zhuangzi, On the Equality of Things. It is one of the first seven chapters, the inner chapters, which have traditionally been taken as the work of Zhuang Zhou himself. Almost nothing is concretely known of his life. Most historians place his birth around 369 BC at Meng in the state of Song, and his death at 301, 295 or 286 BC.
