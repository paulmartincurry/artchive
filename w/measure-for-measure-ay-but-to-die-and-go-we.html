Ay, but to die, and go we know not where;
To lie in cold obstruction, and to rot;
This sensible warm motion to become
A kneaded clod; and the delighted spirit

Measure for Measure — Ay, but to die, and go we know not where (Act III, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1603–1604

The play is believed to have been written in 1603 or 1604 and first performed in 1604. It was published in the First Folio of 1623.
