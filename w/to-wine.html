Demons of care fall harmless at my feet,
Therefore I say, Be thou my spear and shield!

To Wine

Lu You · c. 1179

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, 1898

Lu You was about fifty-four. Four years earlier, in 1175, he had joined the staff of the poet Fan Chengda, decided there was no place for him in official life and taken to drinking. He gave himself the art name Fangweng, the Liberated Old Man. More than 2,400 of his poems date from the eight years between the ages of forty-six and fifty-four, after he joined the military; he wrote about eleven thousand in all.
