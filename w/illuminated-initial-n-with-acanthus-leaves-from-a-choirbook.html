Illuminated Initial N with Acanthus Leaves from a Choirbook

Maker unknown · 18th or 19th century · Manuscript cutting in tempera, gold leaf and gold paint on vellum · 9.3 × 6.6 cm (3 11/16 × 2 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A single letter cut from the page of a choirbook, 9.3 centimetres tall and 6.6 wide. The museum places its making in Europe, possibly France. It was given by Charles L. Hutchinson.
