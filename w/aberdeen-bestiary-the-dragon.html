Aberdeen Bestiary: The Dragon (folio 65v)

Maker unknown · c. 1200 · Pigment and gold leaf on parchment · Aberdeen University Library

Free to use

The book was first listed in 1542, in the inventory of the Old Royal Library at the Palace of Westminster, and it carries the shelf stamp of Henry VIII. Small colour notes in it, probably written by the painter, help to place its making near 1200 or 1210.
