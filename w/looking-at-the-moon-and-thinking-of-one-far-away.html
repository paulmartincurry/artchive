It is no darker though I blow out my candle.
It is no warmer though I put on my coat.
So I leave my message with the moon
And turn to my bed, hoping for dreams.

Looking at the Moon and Thinking of One Far Away

Zhang Jiuling · before 740; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Zhang Jiuling was a chancellor of the Tang court. He passed the jinshi examinations with the highest score under the poet Shen Quanqi, and was later demoted to secretary general of Jing Prefecture. He died on 5 June 740.
