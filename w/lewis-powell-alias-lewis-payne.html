Lewis Powell [alias Lewis Payne]

Alexander Gardner · 27 April 1865 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 22.4 × 17.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Reporters were kept away from the prisoners held for the plot against Abraham Lincoln, but Alexander Gardner received clearance. On 27 April 1865 each prisoner was brought on deck and photographed in a few positions, and Gardner took far more photographs of Lewis Powell than of anyone else. Powell had turned twenty-one five days earlier. He was executed on 7 July.
