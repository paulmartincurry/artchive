O Sky divine, O Winds of pinions swift,
O fountain-heads of Rivers, and O thou,
Illimitable laughter of the Sea!
O Earth, the Mighty Mother, and thou Sun,

Prometheus Bound — O Sky Divine, O Winds of Pinions Swift

Aeschylus · 5th century BCE

Translated by E. D. A. Morshead

These are the first words Prometheus speaks. Until then he is silent while Kratos and Bia, with the smith god Hephaestus, fetter him to a mountainside in Scythia. The play opens with four characters on stage, which no other surviving Greek tragedy does.
