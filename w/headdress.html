Headdress (Ago Egungun)

Ojerinde · Mid–late 19th century · Wood and pigment · 33.1 × 23.2 × 23.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The tufted hairstyle of this headdress copies the flap of a hunter's cap that hides protective medicines. Its carver, Ojerinde, worked at Abeokuta and died about 1914. An Egungun costume is built from layers of cloth lappets, and the layer worn next to the skin resembles the shroud in which the dead are wrapped. The Art Institute bought the headdress from a New York gallery in 1969.
