Inferno, Canto III (Göttliche Komödie, plate 10)

Gustave Doré · published 1870 · Wood engraving · 29 cm (volume) · National Library of Poland, Warsaw

Free to use

Doré drew the design; a professional block-cutter cut it into the wooden printing block and usually signed it too. At the height of his career some 40 block-cutters worked on his drawings. This impression is plate 10 in a German edition of the Inferno published in Berlin in 1870, now in the National Library of Poland.
