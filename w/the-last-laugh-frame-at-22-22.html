The Last Laugh, frame at 22:22

F. W. Murnau · 1924 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The film has a single title card, before the epilogue; the only other text on screen is the letter telling the doorman he has been fired. It was shot entirely at the UFA studios, where cinematographer Karl Freund rode a bicycle into a lift and out to the street with a camera strapped to his chest. F. W. Murnau said the story was absurd, since a washroom attendant makes more than a doorman.
