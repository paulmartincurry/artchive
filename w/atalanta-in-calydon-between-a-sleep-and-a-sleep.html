He weaves, and is clothed with derision;
Sows, and he shall not reap,
His life is a watch or a vision
Between a sleep and a sleep.

Atalanta in Calydon — Between a sleep and a sleep

Algernon Charles Swinburne · 1865

Swinburne published the poetic drama Atalanta in Calydon in 1865. It marked the breakthrough of his work as a poet.
