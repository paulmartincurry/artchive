Friends, Romans, countrymen, lend me your ears;
I come to bury Caesar, not to praise him.

Julius Caesar — Antony’s Funeral Oration (Act III, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · 1599

Shakespeare was about thirty-five in 1599, a playwright who still acted and a part-owner of a London playing company, the Lord Chamberlain's Men. That year a partnership of the company's members built their own theatre on the south bank of the Thames and named it the Globe. He had moved across the river to Southwark by then.
