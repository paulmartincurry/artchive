When to my grave thou turnest thy blessed feet,
Wine and the lute thou shalt bring in thine hand to me,
Thy voice shall ring through the folds of my winding-sheet,
And I will arise and dance to thy minstrelsy.

Where Are the Tidings of Union?

Hafiz of Shiraz · 14th century; English 1897

Translated by Gertrude Lowthian Bell

Hafez was born in Shiraz, probably in 1315 or 1317, and according to Jami he died in 1390. He was buried in Shiraz, and a tomb was raised over his grave in the Musalla Gardens twenty years after his death. The present mausoleum was designed by the French architect André Godard in the late 1930s, and his alabaster sarcophagus is inscribed with two of his poems.
