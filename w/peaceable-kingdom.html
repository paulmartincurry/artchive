Peaceable Kingdom

Edward Hicks · c. 1834 · Oil on canvas · 74.5 × 90.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Edward Hicks gave this painting in 1834 to Joseph Foulke of Three Tuns, Pennsylvania, and it came down to Foulke's great-grandson. Hicks painted 62 versions of the subject, the first around 1820. He was a Quaker minister who had learned coach painting as an apprentice, and his easel paintings were often made for family and friends, not for sale.
