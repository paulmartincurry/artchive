A Hunger Artist (Ein Hungerkünstler)

Franz Kafka · 1922

Translated by Willa and Edwin Muir · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translators. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

First printed in the periodical Die neue Rundschau in 1922, the year Kafka turned thirty-nine, the story gave its title to the last book he prepared for publication. That book came out shortly after he died of tuberculosis in 1924, aged forty. He had asked for his remaining works to be destroyed; his executor Max Brod published them instead.
