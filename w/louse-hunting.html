The Artchive
Nudes, stark and glistening,
Yelling in lurid glee. Grinning faces
And raging limbs
Whirl over the floor one fire;

Louse Hunting

Isaac Rosenberg · published 1922

Rosenberg enlisted in the British Army at the end of October 1915 and continued to write poetry while serving in the trenches. He was killed on the night of 1 April 1918, having just finished a night patrol.
