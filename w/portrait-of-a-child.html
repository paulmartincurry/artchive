Portrait of a Child (Anton Peschka Jr.)

Egon Schiele · 1916 · Gouache, watercolor, and graphite · Sheet: 49.9 × 32.2 cm (19 5/8 × 12 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The boy is Anton Peschka Jr., the son of a friend, and he sat for several of Schiele's compositions. Schiele signed and dated the sheet inside a drawn box, and the name Serena Lederer is written on the back; the Lederer family of Vienna owned it by 1917. Schiele had married Edith Harms the year before he made it. Two years after it, he died in the Spanish influenza epidemic at twenty-eight.
