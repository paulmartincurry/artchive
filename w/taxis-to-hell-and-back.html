Taxis to Hell - and Back (Into the Jaws of Death)

Robert F. Sargent · 6 June 1944 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Robert F. Sargent, a chief photographer's mate in the United States Coast Guard, took this photograph at 7:40 in the morning on 6 June 1944 at Omaha Beach. The landing craft had left the transport Samuel Chase about ten miles off the coast of Normandy at around 5:30. The caption on the back opens with the words into the jaws of death, a phrase from Tennyson's poem The Charge of the Light Brigade.
