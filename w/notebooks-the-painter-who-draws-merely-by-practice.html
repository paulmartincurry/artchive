The painter who draws merely by practice and by eye, without any reason, is like a mirror which copies every thing placed in front of it without being conscious of their existence.

Notebooks: The painter who draws merely by practice

Leonardo da Vinci · before 1519

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

An abridged version of Leonardo's writings on painting was first published in France in 1651, as A Treatise on Painting. His notebooks hold drawings and notes on subjects that include anatomy, astronomy, botany, cartography and painting.
