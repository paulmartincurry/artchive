Dog Team Resting by an Iceberg

Herbert Ponting · 1910 or 1911 · Photographic print · 16.8 × 11.9 cm (image) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The print carries Ponting's blind stamp in the lower right corner, and he registered its copyright in August 1914. Before Antarctica he had worked in a Liverpool bank, run a fruit ranch in California and published a book of photographs of Japan in 1910. Most of the money from his lectures after the expedition went to its debts and to a fund for the families of the men who died.
