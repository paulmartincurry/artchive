Christ Carrying the Cross

El Greco · ca. 1577–87 · Oil on canvas · 41 5/16 × 31 1/8 in. (105 × 79 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

El Greco painted this subject many times, and this version dates from his early years in Toledo, the city he moved to in 1577 and never left. The canvas is 105 centimetres tall. Robert Lehman bought it in 1953, and it came to the museum with his collection in 1975.
