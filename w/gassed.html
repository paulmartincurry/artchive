Gassed

John Singer Sargent · 1919 · Oil on canvas · 231 × 611.1 cm · Imperial War Museum, London

Free to use

Sargent was 62 when he went to the Western Front in July 1918, commissioned by the British War Memorials Committee. On 21 August, at Le Bac-du-Sud, he saw the aftermath of a barrage in which mustard gas had been used. He finished the canvas, more than six metres wide, in March 1919. It was meant for a Hall of Remembrance that was never built. A restoration in 2023 significantly altered its appearance.
