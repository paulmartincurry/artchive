Beloved Pan, and all ye other gods who haunt this place, give me beauty in the inward soul; and may the outward and inward man be at one.

Phaedrus — Beloved Pan (closing prayer)

Plato · c. 370 BCE

Translated by Benjamin Jowett

Plato probably wrote the Phaedrus around 370 BC, about the same time as the Republic and the Symposium. Socrates and Phaedrus walk out into the countryside and sit by a stream under a plane tree, where the whole dialogue takes place.
