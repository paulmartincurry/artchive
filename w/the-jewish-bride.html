The Jewish Bride

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1667 · Oil on canvas · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The title dates from the early nineteenth century, when an Amsterdam collector took the scene for a Jewish father giving his daughter a necklace on her wedding day. That reading is no longer accepted, and who the couple are is not known. Technical evidence suggests Rembrandt first planned a larger and more elaborate composition, but his signature at the lower left shows the canvas is close to the size it was when he finished it.
