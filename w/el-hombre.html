It’s a strange courage
you give me ancient star:

Shine alone in the sunrise
toward which you lend no part!

El Hombre

William Carlos Williams · 1917

Al Que Quiere! is a collection of 52 poems published in 1917 by the Four Seas Company of Boston. Williams paid $50 to the publisher. He translated the title as To Him Who Wants It.
