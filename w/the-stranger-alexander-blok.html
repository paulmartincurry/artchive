A treasure’s buried in my soul,
The sole key to it is mine!
You’re right, you drunken fools!
I know: ‘There’s truth in wine.’

The Stranger (Neznakomka)

Alexander Blok · 1906

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Blok wrote the poem on 24 April 1906 by the old calendar, out of his evenings in the suburb of Ozerki, where he had found a small restaurant. It was first published in 1907 in his collection Nechayannaya radost.
