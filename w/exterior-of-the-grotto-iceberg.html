Exterior of the Grotto Iceberg

Herbert Ponting · c. 1911 · Photographic print · image 16.9 × 12 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress identifies the two men at the entrance as T. Griffith Taylor and Charles S. Wright of the British Antarctic Expedition, which ran from 1910 to 1913. Ponting was its photographer and one of the first men to use a portable movie camera in Antarctica. He registered the copyright on this print in the United States on 14 August 1914.
