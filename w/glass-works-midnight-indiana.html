Glass Works. Midnight. Indiana

Lewis Hine · August 1908 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Lewis Hine gave up teaching in 1908 to become the photographer of the National Child Labor Committee, and he made this picture in August of that year. Photography was forbidden in the mills, mines and factories, so he got in under other guises: fire inspector, postcard vendor, Bible salesman, or industrial photographer recording the machinery. Factory police and foremen often threatened him with violence.
