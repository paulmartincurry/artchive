“I did not bow down to you, I bowed down to all the suffering of humanity,” he said wildly and walked away to the window.

Crime and Punishment — I did not bow down to you (Part IV, Ch. IV)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1866

Translated by Constance Garnett

In letters written in November 1865 Dostoevsky's plan changed, and what he had called a story became a novel. The first part appeared in the January 1866 issue of The Russian Messenger and the last in December.
