The Iliad — Achilles and Lycaon (Book XXI)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by Samuel Butler

Lycaon, a son of Priam and half-brother of Hector, had met Achilles once before, when he was captured while cutting branches in his father's orchard. Achilles sold him as a slave on Lemnos, and the ruler of Imbros bought him and sent him home to Troy. Twelve days later he faced Achilles again.
