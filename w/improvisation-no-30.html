Improvisation No. 30 (Cannons)

Wassily Kandinsky · 1913 · Oil on canvas · 111 × 111.3 cm (43 11/16 × 43 13/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The canvas is square, 111 centimetres on each side, and it was shown at the Royal Albert Hall in London in July 1913. Kandinsky wrote to its owner, the Chicago collector Arthur Jerome Eddy, that the cannons could probably be explained by the constant talk of war that year. When war came in 1914 he left Germany and went back to Moscow.
