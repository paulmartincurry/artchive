The Church of Souain

Félix Vallotton · 1917 · Oil on canvas · 96 × 130 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

In June 1917 the Ministry of Fine Arts sent Vallotton, with two other artists, on a three-week tour of the front lines. He had been rejected for service because of his age, forty-eight. He signed and dated this canvas at the lower right, and it stayed with his estate and then the Galerie Paul Vallotton in Lausanne until at least 1985.
