Being by Calvary's turbulence unsatisfied,
The uncontrollable mystery on the bestial floor.

The Magi

W. B. Yeats · 1914

Responsibilities and a Play was printed and published in 1914 by Yeats's sister, Elizabeth Corbet Yeats, at the Cuala Press. Macmillan published Responsibilities and Other Poems in 1916.
