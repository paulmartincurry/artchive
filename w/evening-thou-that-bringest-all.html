Evening, thou that bringest all that bright morning scattered; thou bringest the sheep, the goat, the child back to her mother.

Evening, Thou That Bringest All (fragment 95)

Sappho of Lesbos · ca. 600 BCE

Translated by Henry Thornton Wharton

The lines survive in the Etymologicum Magnum, which quoted them to show the meaning of a word for dawn, and in Demetrius, as an example of Sappho's grace. Until the last quarter of the 19th century, all of her poetry was known only through quotations like these in other ancient authors.
