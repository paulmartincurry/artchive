The Dream of the Fisherman's Wife

Katsushika Hokusai · 1814 · Wood, paper · British Museum

This is a page from Kinoe no Komatsu, a three-volume book of erotic prints that Hokusai first published in 1814. None of the designs in the book has a title, and the names this one goes by were given later. The writing that surrounds the figures is dialogue between the diver and the two octopuses. Picasso drew a private version of it in 1903.
