Fraktur: Baptismal Certificate of Maria Schaeffer

P. Otto · 1820 · Brush and watercolor, pen and ink, graphite on paper · 32.9 × 39.9 cm (12 15/16 × 15 11/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The certificate records that Maria Schaeffer was born on 20 May 1820 in Mountjoy, a township in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, to William Schaeffer and Barbara, born Hofferlin, and that the Reverend Johannes Speck baptized her. The text is in German script inside a heart, and P. Otto signed it. The museum bought it from its General Acquisitions Endowment.
