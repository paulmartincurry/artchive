The sound of her silk skirt has stopped.
On the marble pavement dust grows.
Her empty room is cold and still.
Fallen leaves are piled against the doors.
Longing for that lovely lady
How can I bring my aching heart to rest?

Li Fu-jēn

Emperor Wu of Han (attributed) · ca. 100 BC

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Lady Li was a dancer from a family of entertainers, and Emperor Wu asked to meet her after her brother sang him a song about her beauty. When she fell gravely ill she refused to let him see her face, citing the loss of her beauty. She died between 104 and 101 BC. The poem is attributed to the emperor, though its authorship is in doubt.
