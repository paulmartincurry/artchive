Figure of Death (Memento Mori)

Maker unknown · 1530–1630 · Boxwood · H. 24.7 cm · Walters Art Museum, Baltimore

Free to use with credit. Photo: Walters Art Museum, CC BY-SA 3.0

The figure is carved from boxwood and stands 24.7 centimetres tall. The carver hollowed out the gut and peeled away the skin without the form collapsing. The scroll it holds is inscribed in Latin: I am what you will be, I was what you are. Its pose is probably taken from the Adam in Albrecht Dürer's engraving of Adam and Eve. Henry Walters bequeathed it to the museum in 1931.
