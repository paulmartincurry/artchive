Nelson's Column under Construction, Trafalgar Square

William Henry Fox Talbot · first week of April 1844 · Salted paper print from paper negative · image 17.1 × 21.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Talbot made this view in the first week of April 1844. The stonework of the column, built by the contractors Grissell and Peto, had been ready for the statue of Nelson only since November 1843. The four bronze lions at its base were not added until 1867.
