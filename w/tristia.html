I have studied the Science of departures,
in night’s sorrows, when a woman’s hair falls down.

Tristia

Osip Mandelstam · published 1922

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Mandelstam's second book of poems, Tristia, was published in Berlin in 1922, the year he and his wife Nadezhda moved to Moscow. In 1938 he was arrested again and died that year in a transit camp near Vladivostok.
