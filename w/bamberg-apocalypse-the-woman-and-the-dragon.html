Bamberg Apocalypse: The Woman and the Dragon (folio 31v)

Maker unknown · c. 1000–1020 · Illuminated manuscript · Page 29.5 × 20.5 cm · Staatsbibliothek Bamberg

Free to use with credit. Photo: Staatsbibliothek Bamberg, CC BY-SA 4.0

The manuscript was made at the scriptorium at Reichenau between 1000 and 1020. It may have been commissioned by Otto III, who died suddenly in 1002, or by Henry II, who may have found it unfinished and ordered its completion.
