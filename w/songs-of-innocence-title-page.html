Songs of Innocence: Title Page

William Blake · 1789, printed c. 1825 · Relief etching printed in orange-brown ink, hand-coloured with watercolour and shell gold · 15.7 × 14.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Blake etched Songs of Innocence in relief on thirty-one plates in 1789, the date on the title page; the first printing made seventeen or possibly eighteen copies. This impression was printed about 1825 and coloured by hand with watercolour and shell gold.
