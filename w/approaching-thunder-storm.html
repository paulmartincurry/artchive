Approaching Thunder Storm

Martin Johnson Heade · 1859 · Oil on canvas · 71.1 × 111.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Heade based this on a sketch he made of a storm coming in over Narragansett Bay, Rhode Island. It was his largest painting to that date. Changes visible in the paint suggest he reworked it: the hills on the horizon were at first larger and more jagged. Records suggest it was shown at the National Academy of Design in 1860.
