Self-Portrait (Self-Portrait at Twenty-Eight)

Albrecht Dürer · 1500 · Oil on panel · Alte Pinakothek, Munich

Free to use

Albrecht Dürer finished the panel early in 1500, just before his 29th birthday; it is the last of his three painted self-portraits. He likely donated or sold it to the Nuremberg city council. It was probably on continuous public display in the city until 1805, when it was sold to the Bavarian royal collection.
