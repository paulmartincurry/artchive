Saint Sebastian (Louvre)

Andrea Mantegna · c. 1480 · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Mantegna painted Saint Sebastian three times; the other two pictures are in Vienna and Venice. He lived in a period of frequent plagues, and the saint was considered a protector against the plague. From the late 17th to the early 18th century this painting was recorded in the Sainte Chapelle of Aigueperse in the Auvergne, where its presence is related to the marriage of Chiara Gonzaga of Mantua to Gilbert de Bourbon.
