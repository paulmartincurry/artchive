Ballet at the Paris Opéra

Edgar Degas · 1877 · Pastel over monotype on cream laid paper · Plate: 35.2 × 70.6 cm (13 7/8 × 27 13/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Degas first printed this scene as a monotype, a technique few artists used then, on one of the widest plates he ever worked with, and then drew over the print in pastel. The view is from the orchestra pit, with the necks of the double basses rising into the dancers. He showed it at the third Impressionist exhibition, in 1877. It belonged to the Paris collector Albert Hecht and then his widow and daughter, and Mary and Leigh Block gave it to the Art Institute in 1981.
