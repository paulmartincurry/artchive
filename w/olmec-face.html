Olmec Face

Maker unknown · 900–400 BCE · Jadeite · 14.6 × 14 × 7.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This face was carved in jadeite between 900 and 400 BCE and is 14.6 centimetres tall. Olmec luxury objects were made from materials such as jade, obsidian and magnetite brought from distant places, and at La Venta at least 48 separate offerings of polished jade celts, pottery, figurines and mirrors lay buried. Alice K. Bache left it to the Met in 1977.
