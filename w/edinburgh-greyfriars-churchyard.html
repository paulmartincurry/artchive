Edinburgh. Greyfriars' Churchyard

David Octavius Hill and Robert Adamson · 1843–47 · Salted paper print from paper negative · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painter David Octavius Hill and the engineer Robert Adamson set up the first photography studio in Scotland in 1843, at Rock House on Calton Hill in Edinburgh. They often posed their sitters outdoors among the elaborate tombs of Greyfriars Kirkyard. The partnership made around 3,000 photographs and lasted only four years, ending with Adamson's death in 1848.
