Oiwa's Ghost (Oiwa no borei)

Utagawa Kunisada · 1860s · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Yotsuya Kaidan opened at the Nakamura-za in Edo in 1825, on a double bill with the story of the Forty-seven Rōnin. Kunisada made this print of Oiwa's ghost in the 1860s, the last years of a five-decade career; he died in January 1865 in the same neighbourhood where he was born. From 1844 he signed his prints with his master's name, Toyokuni. The print came to the Art Institute in 1990 from the estate of Bruce Goff.
