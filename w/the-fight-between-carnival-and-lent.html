The Fight Between Carnival and Lent

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1559 · Oil on oak panel · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Bruegel signed and dated the oak panel 1559 on a stone at the lower left. It holds nearly 200 figures. A likely forerunner is an etching of 1558 that Hieronymus Cock made after Frans Hogenberg. The Vienna museum's record says the panel presumably belonged to Rudolf II and moved from the treasury to the gallery in 1748.
