Fountain (photograph by Alfred Stieglitz)

Marcel Duchamp · 1917 · Porcelain urinal, shown in a gelatin silver photograph

Free to use

The urinal was submitted in April 1917, signed R. Mutt, to the first exhibition of the Society of Independent Artists in New York. The society's rules said every work would be accepted from an artist who paid the fee, but this one was never placed in the show area. Duchamp, a member of the board, resigned. Alfred Stieglitz then photographed it at his studio in front of a painting by Marsden Hartley, and the picture was published in the journal The Blind Man. The original has been lost.
