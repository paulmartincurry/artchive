axe-age, sword-age, shields are cleft asunder,

The Poetic Edda — Völuspá

Anonymous Old Norse Skalds · 10th century CE

Translated by Carolyne Larrington, 1996 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The poem dates to the tenth century, the century before Iceland became Christian. The whole of it is preserved in two Icelandic manuscripts, the Codex Regius of about 1270 and the Hauksbók of about 1334, and the order and number of the stanzas differ between the sources. In 2018 a team at the University of Cambridge suggested that it records the eruption of the volcano Eldgjá in 939.
