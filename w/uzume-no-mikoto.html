Uzume no Mikoto (The Cave Door of Spring)

Totoya Hokkei · 1825 · Third from a pentaptych of woodblock prints; ink and color on paper · Sheet: 18.8 x 21 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is the third of a set of five surimono on the legend of the sun goddess Amaterasu, who hid in a cave and left the world in darkness. Each sheet has a blue evening sky with clouds embossed and touched with gold, silver and copper. Totoya Hokkei had been a fishmonger before he studied painting, and he became one of Hokusai's first students.
