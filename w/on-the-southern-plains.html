On the Southern Plains

Frederic Remington · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 76.5 × 129.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Remington painted this in 1907, two years before he died at forty-eight. His father had been a colonel in the Union army. The Metropolitan Museum's credit line for the painting reads Gift of Several Gentlemen, 1911.
