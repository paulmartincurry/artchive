Tsimshian Headdress Frontlet

Maker unknown · c. 1820–40 · Wood, abalone shell, pigment, and nails · 17.8 × 15.2 × 7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This Tsimshian headdress frontlet is carved wood set with abalone shell, painted and fixed with nails, and it was made around 1820 to 1840. It is 17.8 centimetres tall. It came to the Met in 2019 with the Charles and Valerie Diker Collection of Native American Art.
