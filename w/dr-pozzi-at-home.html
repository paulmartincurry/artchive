Dr. Pozzi at Home

John Singer Sargent · 1881 · Oil on canvas · 201.6 × 102.2 cm · Hammer Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

This was Sargent's first large portrait of a man, and the first work he exhibited at the Royal Academy in London, where it hung in 1882 under the title A Portrait. He chose not to show it in Paris. Samuel Pozzi, a gynaecologist and art collector, was about 35 when he sat, and he kept the portrait until his death in 1918. Armand Hammer acquired it in 1967.
