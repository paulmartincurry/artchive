'O I fear you are poisoned, Lord Randal, my son!
I fear you are poisoned, my handsome young man!'
'O yes, I am poisoned; mother, mak my bed soon,
For I'm sick at the heart, and I fain wad lie down.'

Lord Randal

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1882

The ballad is a dialogue between a young lord and his mother. Related ballads have been found in Danish, German, Magyar, Irish, Swedish and Wendish, and the earliest known Italian version is a setting made in Verona in 1629. Most versions sung in England are called Henry, My Son. Bob Dylan modelled A Hard Rain's A-Gonna Fall on it in 1962.
