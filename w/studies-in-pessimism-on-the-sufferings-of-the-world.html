Unless suffering is the direct and immediate object of life, our existence must entirely fail of its aim.

Studies in Pessimism — On the Sufferings of the World

Arthur Schopenhauer · 1851

Translated by T. Bailey Saunders

The translator drew these essays from Parerga and Paralipomena, which Schopenhauer published in 1851. Because his earlier books had been received without enthusiasm, publishers were reluctant, and Hayn of Berlin took it on only after persuasion by his disciple Julius Frauenstädt, printing 750 copies and paying the author ten.
