The Gulf of Naples. Moonlight

Johan Christian Dahl · 1820–21 · Oil on canvas · 26.3 × 42.7 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

This canvas is Dahl's own repetition of a painting of the same subject that he dated 1820, which is in a private collection in Norway. It was presumably painted straight afterwards, during his stay in Italy. The picture has probably always been in Danish ownership, and the museum acquired it in 1916 from Waldorf Larsen of Copenhagen.
