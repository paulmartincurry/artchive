Notebooks: The Eye

Leonardo da Vinci · c. 1490s

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

Much of Leonardo's career was spent in Milan in the service of Ludovico Sforza, who ruled the city from 1479 to 1499. He had written Sforza a letter setting out what he could do in engineering and weapon design, and it mentioned that he could paint. He brought with him a silver string instrument, a lute or a lyre, made in the form of a horse's head.
