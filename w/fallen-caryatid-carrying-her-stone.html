Fallen Caryatid Carrying Her Stone

Auguste Rodin · 1880–81? · Bronze · Overall: 43.5 × 29.2 × 31.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The figure was designed as one of those on The Gates of Hell, Rodin's sculptural doorway. He was showing it as a sculpture in its own right as early as 1883, and produced versions in both marble and bronze. This bronze is 43.5 centimetres high and carries the mark of the Paris founder Alexis Rudier. The widow and family of Ralph King gave it in his memory.
