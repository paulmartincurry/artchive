Moonlit Night on the Dnieper (Dnipro)

Arkhip Kuindzhi · 1880 · Oil on canvas · Russian Museum, St Petersburg

Free to use

Kuindzhi began the painting in the summer and fall of 1880 and opened his studio for two hours each Sunday to anyone who wished to watch him work. Grand Duke Konstantin Konstantinovich bought it from the workshop for 5,000 roubles before it had been exhibited, and took it with him on his sea travels. It was shown as the only painting in the exhibition, with the curtains lowered and an artificial light on the canvas. The conditions in which it was later kept darkened its colours.
