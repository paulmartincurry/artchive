The End (Ende)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1897 · Aquatint, etching, and emery stone pencil · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Käthe Kollwitz made a cycle of six prints on the revolt of the Silesian weavers in Langenbielau in 1844: three lithographs, and three etchings worked with aquatint and sandpaper, of which this is one. She began it after seeing a performance of Gerhart Hauptmann's play The Weavers, in the years between the births of her sons in 1892 and 1896.
