True beauty could be discovered only by one who mentally completed the incomplete.

The Book of Tea

Okakura Kakuzō · 1906

Okakura Kakuzō wrote this essay in English, for Western readers, and it was first published in the United States in 1906, a book of 160 pages. He had learned English as a boy at a school run by a Christian missionary, at a time when he could not yet read Japanese characters. He was forty-three, and in the same year he was invited to work at the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston. He dedicated the book to the American painter John La Farge, a friend of twenty years.
