An Ancient Picture of the Boat Bridge at Sano in Kōzuke Province (Curious Views of Famous Bridges in the Provinces)

Katsushika Hokusai · early 1830s · Color woodblock print · 25.4 × 37.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Hokusai made the series Curious Views of Famous Bridges in the Provinces in the early 1830s and signed it Zen Hokusai Iitsu hitsu. The sheet carries the kiwame seal, the censor's mark of approval. Hokusai used at least thirty names during his life, more than any other major Japanese artist.
