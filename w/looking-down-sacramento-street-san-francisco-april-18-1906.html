Looking Down Sacramento Street, San Francisco, April 18, 1906

Arnold Genthe · 1906, printed later · Gelatin silver print · sheet 25 × 32 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Genthe's studio and cameras were destroyed in the earthquake of 18 April 1906, so he borrowed a hand-held camera and photographed the city that morning. In his memoir As I Remember he wrote that the picture shows the results of the earthquake, the beginning of the fire and the attitude of the people. This print was made later from the negative.
