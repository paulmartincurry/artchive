Nocturne: Blue and Silver – Chelsea

James McNeill Whistler · 1871 · Oil on panel · Tate, London

Free to use

Painted in 1871, it is the first of Whistler's London nocturnes, made from the same August evening as Variations in Violet and Green. He would study a night scene, go home to bed, and paint it the next morning from memory.
