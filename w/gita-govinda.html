The night frightens him.
Radha, you take him home!

Gīta Govinda (opening)

Jayadeva · 12th Century CE

Translated by Barbara Stoler Miller · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Little is known of Jayadeva's life, and three regions of eastern India each claim his birthplace. Temple inscriptions record that he taught at a school at Kurmapataka. The earliest commentary on the poem was written in Odisha around 1190. It is sung every night in the Jagannatha temple at Puri, where lines from it are woven into the cloth the deities wear. The Odisha State Museum keeps 210 manuscripts of it, some of them cut in the shape of a fish, a garland or a dagger.
