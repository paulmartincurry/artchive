Compound Vase with Six Trumpet-Shaped Necks

Maker unknown · Qing dynasty (1644–1911), Qianlong reign mark and period (1736–1795) · Glazed stoneware · 24.2 × 18.5 cm (9 1/2 × 7 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Five long-necked bottles are joined around a sixth, their mouths opening into a shared cavity. The Qianlong reign mark on the base of the centre bottle is in archaic seal script; pieces like this were probably made as showpieces more than flower vases.
