The Newborn Child

Georges de La Tour · c. 1645–1648 · Oil on canvas · Musée des Beaux-Arts, Rennes

Free to use

Georges de La Tour painted this canvas around 1645–1648. It is sometimes thought to show the Virgin and Child, with Saint Anne at the left, in the form of a scene from everyday life, and so it is also known as The Nativity.
