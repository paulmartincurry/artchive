Upon the threshing-floor of life I burn
Beside the Winnower a word to learn;
And only this: Man's of the soil and sun,
And to the soil and sun he shall return.

The Luzumiyat, XXV

Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri · before 1057; English 1918

Translated by Ameen Rihani

Al-Ma'arri was born in al-Ma'arra, in present-day Syria, and went blind from smallpox when young. He called himself a double prisoner, of blindness and of isolation. His translator, Ameen Rihani, was a Lebanese-born writer of the mahjar, the movement of Arab emigrant writers in North America.
