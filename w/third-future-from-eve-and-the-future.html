Third Future, from Eve and the Future

Max Klinger · 1880 · Etching on light gray wove paper, laid down on ivory wove card · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Plate six of the series. Max Klinger was twenty-two or twenty-three in 1880, three years out of art school in Berlin, and he spent part of that year in Munich. He had trained in engraving as an apprentice to Hermann Sagert. His reputation came a year later, when his etched series Paraphrase on the Finding of a Glove was published in 1881. He gave his print cycles opus numbers, as a composer would.
