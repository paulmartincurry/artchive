Codex Borgia: Mictlantecuhtli and Quetzalcoatl (page 56)

Maker unknown · Pre-Columbian · Paint on gesso over animal skin · 27 × 27 cm (each sheet) · Vatican Apostolic Library

Free to use

On this page Mictlantecuhtli, god of death and lord of the underworld, stands on the left, with Quetzalcoatl on the right. The Codex Borgia is painted on a white gesso coating over animal skins, folded into 39 sheets 27 centimetres square and nearly 11 metres long in all, and it is read from right to left. It is named after Cardinal Stefano Borgia, after whose death in 1804 it passed to the Vatican Library.
