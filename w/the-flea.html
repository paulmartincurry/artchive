Marke but this flea, and marke in this,
How little that which thou deny'st me is;
It suck'd me first, and now sucks thee,
And in this flea, our two bloods mingled bee;

The Flea

John Donne · published 1633

No one knows exactly when this poem was written. The likely date is the 1590s, when Donne was a young law student at Lincoln's Inn, which admitted him on 6 May 1592. It was first printed in 1633, two years after his death, by which time he had been Dean of St Paul's Cathedral for a decade.
