Spring and Fall

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1880 (published 1918)

Hopkins was the eldest of nine children. His father founded a marine insurance firm, published several books of verse and reviewed poetry for The Times. Of his sisters, Grace set many of his poems to music and Kate helped to bring out the first edition of them. Two brothers, Arthur and Everard, became artists.
