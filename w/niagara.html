Niagara

Frederic Edwin Church · 1857 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Frederic Edwin Church made dozens of pencil and oil studies of the Falls and visited them probably five times before starting. An account of 1879 says the painting itself took about six weeks, worked on two similar canvases at once, one of them a draft for testing ideas. In May 1857 tens of thousands of people paid 25 cents each to see it in a darkened Manhattan gallery where it alone was lit. It sold that year for 4,500 dollars, of which 2,000 was for the reproduction rights. Church repainted the sky in 1886.
