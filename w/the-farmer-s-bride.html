Alone, poor maid. ’Tis but a stair
Betwixt us. Oh! my God! the down,
The soft young down of her, the brown,
The brown of her—her eyes, her hair, her hair!

The Farmer's Bride (closing stanzas)

Charlotte Mew · 1916

Many of Mew's poems are dramatic monologues, and she often wrote from the point of view of a male persona, as she does in this one.
