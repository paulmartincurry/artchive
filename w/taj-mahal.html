Taj Mahal

Attributed to Ustad Ahmad Lahori · 1632–1648 · White marble inlaid with semi-precious stones

Free to use with credit. Photo: Yann, edited by Jim Carter, CC BY-SA

Shah Jahan ordered the tomb in 1631, the year his wife Mumtaz Mahal died giving birth to their fourteenth child. More than 20,000 people worked on it. The white marble came from Makrana in Rajasthan and was hauled to the site by oxen and elephants along an earthen ramp 15 kilometres long; the scaffold was built of brick. Deposed by his son in 1658, Shah Jahan was held at Agra Fort, within sight of the building, and was buried in it beside her in 1666.
