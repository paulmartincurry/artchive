I have looked upon those brilliant creatures,
And now my heart is sore.

The Wild Swans at Coole

W. B. Yeats · 1917

The poem first appeared in the Little Review in June 1917. That November the Cuala Press published it as the title poem of a collection of twenty-nine poems and a play, At the Hawk's Well. Macmillan reissued the book in March 1919 without the play and with seventeen more poems.
