Shakuntala Looking Back for Dushyanta

Raja Ravi Varma · 1898 · Oil on canvas · Sree Chitra Art Gallery

Free to use

In the scene Shakuntala stops as if to take a thorn out of her foot, so that she can look back for Dushyanta while her friends tease her. Ravi Varma was fifty when he painted it. Four years earlier he had opened a lithographic press at Ghatkopar in Bombay to print cheap copies of his pictures of the epics, and it was moved to Malavli, near Lonavala, in 1899. By then the press was deep in debt, and in 1901 it was sold to its German printing technician, Fritz Schleicher.
