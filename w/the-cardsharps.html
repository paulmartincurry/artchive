The Cardsharps

Caravaggio · c. 1596–1597 · Oil on canvas · Kimbell Art Museum, Fort Worth

Free to use

Caravaggio painted it around 1596–1597, when he was attempting an independent career after leaving a workshop where he had painted flowers and fruit. The picture disappeared in the 1890s and was rediscovered in 1987 in a private collection in Zürich; the Kimbell Art Museum acquired it that year. He may have painted more than one version.
