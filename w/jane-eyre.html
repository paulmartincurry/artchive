I am no bird; and no net ensnares me;
I am a free human being with an independent will.

Jane Eyre (Ch. 23)

Charlotte Brontë · 1847

Charlotte Brontë began composing the novel in Manchester, and it was published in London on 19 October 1847 under the pen name Currer Bell. She was thirty-one, and publishers had rejected her first novel, The Professor. Like Jane she had worked as a governess. Lowood School was modelled on the Clergy Daughters' School at Cowan Bridge, which she entered at eight and where her two elder sisters fell ill before their deaths.
