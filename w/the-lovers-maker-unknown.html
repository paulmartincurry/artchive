The Lovers (Cary-Yale Visconti Tarot)

Maker unknown · mid-15th century · Paint and gold on card · Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, Yale University

Free to use

This card belongs to a different and possibly older set, named after the Cary Collection of Playing Cards, which became part of Yale University Library in 1967. Sixty-seven cards survive, and Yale reckons the full deck had at least 89. One scholar dates it to between 1442 and 1447, because its coin cards copy a gold florin that Filippo Maria Visconti issued in 1442.
