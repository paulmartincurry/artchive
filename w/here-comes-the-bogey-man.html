Here Comes the Bogey-Man (Los Caprichos, plate 3)

Francisco de Goya · 1799 · Etching, burnished aquatint · Plate: 21.3 × 15.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya first planned this series as Sueños, or Dreams, and made at least 28 preparatory drawings for it. When he renamed the prints Caprichos, he kept all but three of the dream designs.
