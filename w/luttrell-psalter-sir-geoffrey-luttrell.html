Luttrell Psalter: Sir Geoffrey Luttrell

Maker unknown · ca. 1325–1340 · Parchment · British Library

Free to use

Sir Geoffrey Luttrell, a Lincolnshire landowner, paid for this psalter and had himself painted into it, armed and mounted on a war-horse. One scribe wrote it and at least five artists illustrated it, on 309 vellum leaves measuring 350 by 245 millimetres. In 1929 a court ruling on who owned it came only a few hours before it was due to be auctioned. The British Museum then bought it for £31,500 with an interest-free loan from J. P. Morgan.
