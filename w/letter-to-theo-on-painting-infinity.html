Behind his head, instead of the trivial wall of the room—I paint infinity.

Letter to Theo on Painting Infinity

Vincent van Gogh · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1912

Van Gogh moved to Arles in February 1888, ill from drink and suffering from a smoker's cough. In Arles the American painter Dodge MacKnight introduced him to Eugène Boch, a Belgian painter staying nearby at Fontvieille, and the two exchanged visits in July. When Boch came again, Van Gogh painted his portrait and a study called The Poet Against a Starry Sky.
