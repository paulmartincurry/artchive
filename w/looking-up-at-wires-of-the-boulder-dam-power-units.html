Looking Up at Wires of the Boulder Dam Power Units

Ansel Adams · 1941 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

In 1941 Ansel Adams signed a contract with the National Park Service to photograph national parks, Indian reservations and other places run by the Department of the Interior. The pictures were to be enlarged as murals for the department's new building. The contract was for 180 days. His own caption for this negative is Boulder Dam Power Units, 1941.
