The Nightmare

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1781 · Oil on canvas · Detroit Institute of Arts

Free to use

Henry Fuseli had shown at the Royal Academy in London many times without success before this painting was exhibited there in 1782. The original sold for twenty guineas, while an engraving of it that circulated from January 1783 earned its publisher more than 500 pounds. A preliminary chalk sketch does not include the horse. On the back of the canvas is an unfinished portrait of a girl. Fuseli painted other versions, of which at least three survive.
