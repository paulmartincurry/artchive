Irises

Claude Monet · 1914–17 · Oil on canvas · 200 × 200.7 cm (78 3/4 × 79 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Monet painted it during the First World War, one of a series of very large canvases about six and a half feet square. It stayed in his studio at Giverny after his death in 1926, was hit by shell splinters in the Second World War, and was found again in the 1950s.
