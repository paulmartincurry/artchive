The Little Street

Johannes Vermeer · 1658 · Oil on canvas · Rijksmuseum

Free to use

In 2015 a search of Delft's register of quay dues, which records the measurements of every house along the canals, placed this scene at what are now numbers 40 and 42 Vlamingstraat. The house on the right belonged to Vermeer's aunt, Ariaentgen Claes van der Minne, who sold tripe, and the passage beside it was called the Tripe Gate. His mother and sister lived diagonally opposite. The brick is red ochre and madder lake. The canvas is 54.3 centimetres high.
