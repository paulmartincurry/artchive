So long as men can breathe or eyes can see,
So long lives this, and this gives life to thee.

Sonnet 18

William Shakespeare · 1609

Shakespeare wrote 154 sonnets, and their accepted numbering comes from the first edition of 1609. This one belongs to the first 126, known as the Fair Youth sequence, and is the first to follow the opening group now called the procreation sonnets. It has fourteen lines of iambic pentameter, set as three quatrains and a couplet.
