(Many a soldier’s loving arms about this neck have cross’d and rested,
Many a soldier’s kiss dwells on these bearded lips.)

The Wound-Dresser (section 4)

Walt Whitman · 1865

In December 1862 Whitman went looking for his brother George, whose name he feared he had seen in a list of the wounded at Fredericksburg, and found him alive with only a superficial wound on his cheek. He left for Washington on December 28, took part-time work in the army paymaster's office, and volunteered as a nurse in the army hospitals. The poem is dated 1865.
