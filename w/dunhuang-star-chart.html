Dunhuang Star Chart

Maker unknown · ca. 700 · Ink on paper scroll · British Library, London

Free to use

The chart is part of a scroll found in the Mogao Caves in the early twentieth century and carried off with hundreds of other manuscripts by a British expedition to Dunhuang. It marks and names 1345 stars in 257 constellations. Three colours of ink separate the stars of three schools of Chinese astronomy.
