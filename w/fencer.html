Fencer

Georges Demenÿ · 1906 · Gelatin silver print · Image: 17.5 × 26.5 × 12.8 cm (6 7/8 × 10 7/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Georges Demenÿ was a gymnast and physiologist as well as a photographer and inventor, and he was in his mid-fifties when this print was made. In the years around it he published books on the mechanics of movement, on physical education and on the physiology of the violinist. The image is about 17.5 by 26.5 centimetres.
