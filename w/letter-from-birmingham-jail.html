Letter from Birmingham Jail

Martin Luther King Jr. · 1963

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

King was arrested in Birmingham, Alabama, on 12 April 1963, at the age of thirty-four. He began the letter in jail on the margins of a smuggled newspaper, went on with it on scraps of paper that his lawyers carried out, and finished it on a pad they were allowed to leave with him. He was released on 20 April. The New York Times Magazine asked for the letter and then chose not to publish it; the complete text was first published by the American Friends Service Committee in May 1963.
