Writing Box (Suzuribako) with Pine, Camellia, and Bamboo

Maker unknown · 1400s · Lacquered wood with gold and silver sprinkled powder (maki-e), stone, and metal · Overall: 24.2 × 22.6 cm (9 1/2 × 8 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A writing box of this kind held the tools for calligraphy: an inkstone, a water dropper, an ink cake and brushes. This one still has its inkstone and its water dropper, and the dropper carries the same camellia and pine as the lid. The box is about 24 by 23 centimetres. Cleveland bought it from a Tokyo dealer in 1969.
