Knight, Death, and the Devil

Albrecht Dürer · 1513 · Engraving · The Met

Free to use

The plate is 24.3 by 18.8 centimetres, and Dürer was forty-one or forty-two when he cut it. Engraving was the technique he preferred for his later prints. From the year before, the Emperor Maximilian I was his patron. The Metropolitan Museum acquired this print in 1943.
