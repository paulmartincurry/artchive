Holy Family (Doni Tondo)

Michelangelo · 1506 · Tempera on panel · Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

This is the only finished panel painting by the mature Michelangelo to survive, and it is still in its original frame. Agnolo Doni commissioned it, possibly for his marriage in January 1504 to Maddalena Strozzi, whose family's three crescent moons are carved in the frame. Michelangelo is believed to have painted it without assistants, using a limited palette of lead white, azurite, verdigris and a few other pigments and avoiding ochres.
