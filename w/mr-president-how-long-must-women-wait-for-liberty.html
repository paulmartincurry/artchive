Mr. President, how long must women wait for liberty?

Silent Sentinels, National Woman's Party · 1917

One of the banners held outside the White House by the Silent Sentinels, more than 2,000 women organised by Alice Paul and the National Woman's Party. Their picket began on 10 January 1917, the day after a meeting with President Wilson.
