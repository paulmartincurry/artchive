My heart is like a singing bird
Whose nest is in a watered shoot;

A Birthday

Christina Rossetti · 1861

Christina Rossetti was thirty in 1861 and had not yet had a book commercially printed. She had been collecting and dating her poems since 1842. Her grandfather, Gaetano Polidori, printed a small volume of them in 1847 for family and friends, and in 1850 she appeared in the Pre-Raphaelite periodical The Germ under the name Ellen Alleyne.
