O'er all the hill-tops
Is quiet now,
In all the tree-tops
Hearest thou
Hardly a breath;
The birds are asleep in the trees:
Wait; soon like these
Thou too shalt rest.

Wanderer's Night-Songs

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1776 and 1780

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

The manuscript of the first of these two poems, written in 1776, was among Goethe's letters to Charlotte von Stein. He probably wrote the second on the evening of September 6, 1780, onto the wall of a wooden gamekeeper lodge on top of the Kickelhahn mountain near Ilmenau. He went back to the cabin on August 27, 1831, about six months before his death, recognised his writing on the wall and reportedly broke down in tears. After 1831 the handwritten text vanished.
