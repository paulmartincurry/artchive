Patriotism: A Menace to Liberty

Emma Goldman · 1910

Emma Goldman reached New York from Saint Petersburg at sixteen and sewed overcoats in Rochester for more than ten hours a day at two and a half dollars a week. In 1910, the year her book Anarchism and Other Essays came out, she turned forty-one and had been running her own journal, Mother Earth, for four years. In 1917 she was sentenced to two years in jail for conspiring to persuade men not to register for the draft, and in December 1919 she was deported to Russia.
