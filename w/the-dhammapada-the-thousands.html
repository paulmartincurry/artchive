If one man conquer in battle a thousand times thousand men, and if another conquer himself, he is the greatest of conquerors.

The Dhammapada — The Thousands (verses 100–103)

The Dhammapada · Date uncertain; English 1870

Translated by F. Max Müller

The oldest surviving manuscripts of the Pāli text date to 1500 CE. Max Müller's translation from the Pāli came out in 1870; he was born in Dessau in 1823 and died in 1900.
