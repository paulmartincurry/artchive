The Monkey and the Cat

Abraham Hondius · probably 1670s · Oil on canvas · Framed: 80 x 93.5 x 6 cm (31 1/2 x 36 13/16 x 2 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting takes one of Aesop's fables, probably through La Fontaine's version: a monkey flatters a cat into pulling hot chestnuts from the fire, then eats nearly all of them.
