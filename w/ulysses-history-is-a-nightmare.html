History, Stephen said, is a nightmare from which I am trying to awake.

From the playfield the boys raised a shout. A whirring whistle: goal. What if that nightmare gave you a back kick?

Ulysses — History is a nightmare (Nestor)

James Joyce · 1922

Joyce first met the figure of Ulysses in Charles Lamb's Adventures of Ulysses, a version of the Odyssey for children, and at school he wrote an essay on him titled My Favourite Hero. He thought of writing Ulysses as one more short story for Dubliners, based on a Dubliner named Alfred H. Hunter. The idea grew from a story in 1906 to a short book in 1907 to the novel he began in 1914.
