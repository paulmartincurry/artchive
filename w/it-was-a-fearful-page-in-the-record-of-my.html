It Was a Fearful Page in the Record of My Existence (Berenice)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Berenice was first published in the Southern Literary Messenger in March 1835. In 1919 the Committee of the Irish National War Memorial commissioned Clarke to illustrate Ireland's Memorial Records, a roll of honour for the 49,435 Irish who died during the First World War. Its eight volumes were completed in 1922 and published in 1923.
