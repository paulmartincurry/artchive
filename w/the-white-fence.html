The White Fence (Camera Work 49/50, plate IX)

Paul Strand · published 1917 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The photogravure was plate IX of Camera Work, number 49/50, in June 1917. That double issue, the magazine's last, was devoted almost entirely to Strand's photographs. Strand had turned away from the soft focus of the Pictorialists toward the clear lines and forms of ordinary objects.
