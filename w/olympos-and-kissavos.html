And perched upon my highest peak there sits a mighty eagle;
A mirror, in his talon grasped, he holds on high exalted,
And in it he his charms admires, and on his beauty gazes!

Olympos and Kissavos

Traditional Greek folk song · printed 1896

Translated by J. S. Stuart-Glennie and Demosthenes

The book's editor, J. S. Stuart-Glennie, heard his servant Demosthenes sing it on an August morning while climbing Olympos, after weeks of hunting brigands; Demosthenes remembered the last lines only imperfectly. Their escort was scattered that night and they narrowly escaped capture on the mountain. A few days later their hostess at the village of Litochori completed the song, and the two men translated it there.
