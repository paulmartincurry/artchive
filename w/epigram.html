Epigram

Anyte of Tegea · ca. 300 BCE

Translated by W. R. Paton, 1917

No reliable information about Anyte's life survives, and she is dated to around 300 BC only by the style of her work. Nineteen epigrams are generally accepted as hers, more poetry than survives from any other ancient Greek woman except Sappho. She may have been the first poet to compile a book of her own epigrams. Of her five surviving epitaphs, four are for women who died young.
