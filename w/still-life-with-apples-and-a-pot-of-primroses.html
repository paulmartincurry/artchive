Still Life with Apples and a Pot of Primroses

Paul Cézanne · ca. 1890 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Free to use

Cézanne painted this still life about four years after the death of his father, a banker, whose estate left him 400,000 francs and free of money worries. He was around fifty-one. The canvas is 73 by 92.4 centimetres. It came to the Met in 1951 as a bequest from Sam A. Lewisohn.
