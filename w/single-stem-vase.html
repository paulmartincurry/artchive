Single Stem Vase

Seifū Yohei IV · 1914–46 · Porcelain with molded design and copper-red glaze · height: 17.1 cm (6 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The glaze runs from copper red at the top to purple near the foot. The box lid names its colour shinsha, after a Qing glaze made to copy a red of the Ming emperor Xuande's reign.
