The Lantern Night Excursion of Zhong Kui (detail)

Yan Hui · late 1200s–early 1300s · Handscroll, ink and slight color on silk · 24.8 × 240.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In cities of southeast China a New Year custom had poor people disguise themselves as demons and parade through the streets doing tricks to clear houses of evil spirits. Yan Hui's procession of performers led by Zhong Kui is shown here in part; the painted silk is about 2.4 metres long. Yan, from Ji'an in Jiangxi, painted mostly human, Buddhist and ghost figures. Thirty-one collectors' seals are on the scroll, eight of them belonging to the sixteenth-century collector Xiang Yuanbian.
