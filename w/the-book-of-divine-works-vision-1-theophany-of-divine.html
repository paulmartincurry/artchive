The Book of Divine Works: Vision 1, Theophany of Divine Love

Maker unknown · c. 1210–1230 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · 39 × 25.5 cm · Biblioteca Statale di Lucca

Free to use

Hildegard of Bingen began this book in 1163 or 1164 and finished it between 1172 and 1174. The Lucca manuscript can be dated to between the second and third decades of the 13th century, after her death in 1179.
