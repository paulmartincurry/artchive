A House Burgled at Night

Maker unknown · c. 1700 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · Page: 38.7 × 28.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Robbers have climbed the walls of a Mughal house and are carrying off its contents while a woman runs out the door, leaving her shoes behind. The Cleveland Museum of Art suggests the scene, painted around 1700, may reflect the violent successions that unsettled the Mughal court at the start of the 18th century. It came to Cleveland in 2013 from the Benkaim collection.
