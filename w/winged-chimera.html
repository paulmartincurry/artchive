Winged Chimera (Bixie)

Maker unknown · 400s CE · Bronze · 6.7 × 7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A bixie is a hybrid creature that was believed to ward off evil spirits. This bronze one, cast in the 400s, is less than 7 centimetres high. It belonged to Eugene and Elva Bernat, who consigned it to Sotheby Parke-Bernet, and Cleveland bought it at that sale in 1980.
