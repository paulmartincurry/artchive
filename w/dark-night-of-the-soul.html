One dark night,
fired with love’s urgent longings
—ah, the sheer grace!—
I went out unseen,
my house being now all stilled.

Dark Night of the Soul (Noche Oscura del Alma)

Saint John of the Cross (San Juan de la Cruz) · ca. 1578

Translated by Kieran Kavanaugh and Otilio Rodriguez · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

John of the Cross gave this poem no title. Neither the time nor the place of its writing is certain: it was probably composed between 1577 and 1579, and it has been proposed, on thin second-hand evidence, that he wrote it while imprisoned in Toledo. It has eight stanzas of five lines each. He later began two prose commentaries that explain it line by line, and left both unfinished.
