Après le Déluge (After the Flood)

Arthur Rimbaud · ca. 1874 (Published 1886)

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

In London, where Rimbaud and Verlaine lived in considerable poverty from September 1872, Rimbaud spent his days in the Reading Room of the British Museum, where heating, lighting, pens and ink were free. He was nineteen for most of 1874. At twenty he stopped writing literature altogether and went on to travel three continents as a merchant and explorer. He died of cancer just after his thirty-seventh birthday.
