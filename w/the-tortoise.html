The Tortoise (Aja'ib al-Makhluqat of Qazwini)

Maker unknown · second half 16th century · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Page 14.6 × 14 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Qazwini, a jurist born in Qazvin around 1203, fled to Mosul at sixteen or seventeen when an early Mongol campaign reached his city, and later served as a judge and professor near Baghdad. This page, 14.6 cm high, comes from a copy of his book made in Khurasan in the second half of the 16th century, more than 250 years after his death in 1283.
