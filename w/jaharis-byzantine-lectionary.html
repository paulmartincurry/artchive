Jaharis Byzantine Lectionary

Maker unknown · ca. 1100 · Tempera, gold, and ink on parchment; leather binding · Overall: 14 1/2 × 11 5/8 × 4 7/8 in. (36.8 × 29.6 × 12.4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This book of Gospel readings was made in Constantinople. Its pages are thirty-five centimetres tall, and the bound volume is more than twelve centimetres thick. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 2007 with a gift from Mary and Michael Jaharis, whose name it now carries.
