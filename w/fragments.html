Fragments (DK B30)

Heraclitus of Ephesus · ca. 500 BCE

Translated by Charles H. Kahn, 1979 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Heraclitus of Ephesus is said to have written a single work on papyrus, and its title is not known. One ancient account has him leaving the book in the temple of Artemis as a dedication. It could still be read in the second century AD and was later lost. What survives is a little over 100 fragments, each one a quotation in some other author's work. Later Greek writers called him the Dark, which became the Obscure in Latin.
