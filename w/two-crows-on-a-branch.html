Two Crows on a Branch

Ohara Koson · 1927 · Color woodblock print on paper · 36.5 × 23.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Koson made this print of two crows under a full moon in 1927, the year after he began working with the publisher Watanabe Shōzaburō. He designed around 500 prints, most of them of birds and flowers. Little known in Japan for most of his career, they sold well in the United States, and had to be brought back to Japan when they became popular there after 1970.
