Plaque with Warrior and Attendants

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · ca. 1540–70 · Brass · The Met

This plaque was cast by artists of the Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n, the guild of brass casters at the Court of Benin, in what is now Nigeria. This one is nearly 48 centimetres high and stands almost 11 centimetres out from its back. Klaus G. Perls and his wife gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1990.
