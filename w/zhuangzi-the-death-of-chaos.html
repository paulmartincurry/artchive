So every day they bored one hole; but on the seventh day Hun Tun died.

Zhuangzi — The Death of Chaos (ch. VII)

Zhuangzi (Chuang Tzu) · 4th century BC

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, 1889

In Chinese myth Hundun is a faceless being and the primal chaos at the centre of creation; its name means muddled confusion. The Zhuangzi was written during the late Warring States period. The only complete version, of 33 chapters, was prepared around AD 300 by the scholar Guo Xiang, who cut it down from 52.
