Saint John on Patmos

Hans Baldung Grien · ca. 1511 · Oil, gold, and white metal on spruce · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hans Baldung moved back to Strasbourg in 1509 after working as an assistant in Albrecht Dürer's studio in Nuremberg. He became a citizen, joined a guild, opened a workshop and began signing his work with the monogram HGB. This spruce panel, 89.5 centimetres tall, dates from about two years after that. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1983.
