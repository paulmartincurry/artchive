The Witch (Night Piece)

Jan van de Velde II · 1626 · Engraving · Platemark 21.6 × 29 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Jan van de Velde II engraved this plate at thirty-three. The dark ground is built from long, fine lines laid in dense parallel and crossed patterns, a manner of engraving made for prints of flickering, theatrical light. He was the son of a calligrapher, was apprenticed to the engraver Jacob Matham in 1613 and entered the Haarlem guild the following year. This impression once belonged to William Bell Scott.
