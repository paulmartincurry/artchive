Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde — Like some damned Juggernaut (Story of the Door)

Robert Louis Stevenson · 1886

Shops did not stock the book at first. That changed after The Times reviewed it favourably on 25 January 1886. The actor Richard Mansfield bought the stage rights, and his play opened at the Boston Museum in May 1887. It ran for ten weeks in London in 1888, until the Jack the Ripper murders; when newspapers named Mansfield as a possible suspect he shut the production down. By 1901 the book was estimated to have sold over 250,000 copies in the United States.
