Thunder God (Raijin)

Maker unknown · 1300s · wood with traces of gesso and red and black pigment · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A metal clasp sticks out of the figure's back, where a ring of small drums was once attached. Only traces are left of the gesso and the red and black paint that covered the wood. The figure is 66.7 centimetres tall. Its partner, a wind god, is in the Fukuoka Art Museum in Japan; this one was bought from a Tokyo dealer in 1972.
