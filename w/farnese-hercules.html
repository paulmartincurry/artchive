Farnese Hercules

Glykon of Athens, after Lysippos · early 3rd century AD · Marble · H. 3.17 m · Museo Archeologico Nazionale, Naples

Free to use with credit. Photo: Marie-Lan Nguyen, CC BY 2.5

The statue is 3.17 metres tall and is signed by Glykon, an Athenian who is otherwise unknown. It is an enlarged version of a much older Greek bronze by Lysippos or one of his circle, and was made for the Baths of Caracalla in Rome, dedicated in 216 AD. It was recovered there in 1546 and soon entered the collection of Cardinal Alessandro Farnese. The left forearm is a restoration in plaster.
