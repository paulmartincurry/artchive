The Ripley Scroll: Large Green Dragon

Maker unknown, after George Ripley · c. 1600 · Ink and colours, mounted on linen with wooden rollers · 328 × 40 cm (whole scroll) · Wellcome Collection, London (MS.692)

Free to use

The whole scroll is 328 centimetres long and 40 wide, mounted on linen with wooden rollers. It is a copy of a copy made in 1588, and a note in its border records that the earlier roll was drawn in colours at Lübeck in Germany. The dragon sits in the fourth of five panels, above a poem of 38 lines.
