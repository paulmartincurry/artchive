I say that all these are but dreams and shadows; the shadows that hide the real world from our eyes.

The Great God Pan (Ch. I)

Arthur Machen · 1894

This first chapter was published on its own in 1890, in a magazine called The Whirlwind. Once Machen decided it belonged with another of his stories, he wrote the rest of the novella in a single evening, except for the ending, which he could not think of for months; the last chapter was finished in June 1891. The publisher Blackwood rejected it. John Lane brought it out in 1894 with a cover by Aubrey Beardsley, and after the press denounced it the book went into a second edition.
