The Artchive
For my enemy is dead, a man divine as myself is dead,
I look where he lies white-faced and still in the coffin—I draw near,
Bend down and touch lightly with my lips the white face in the coffin.

Reconciliation

Walt Whitman · 1865

Eighteen poems were added to Drum-Taps later in 1865 to make Sequel to Drum-Taps. In 1867 Drum-Taps and the Sequel appeared as appendices to Leaves of Grass.
