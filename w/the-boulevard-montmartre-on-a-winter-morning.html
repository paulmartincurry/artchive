The Boulevard Montmartre on a Winter Morning

Camille Pissarro · 1897 · Oil on canvas · 25 1/2 × 32 in. (64.8 × 81.3 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Pissarro was sixty-six or sixty-seven when he painted this view, and he died in Paris six years later. He had been born on the island of Saint Thomas in the Danish West Indies, where as a young man he worked for five years as a port clerk in his father's business. He is the only artist who showed at all eight of the Paris Impressionist exhibitions. The canvas measures 65 by 81 centimetres.
