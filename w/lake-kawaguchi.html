Lake Kawaguchi

Yoshida Hiroshi · 1926 · Woodblock print · Honolulu Museum of Art

Free to use

This view of Fuji across Lake Kawaguchi dates from 1926, the year Yoshida published the first three prints of his Ten Views of Mount Fuji; the other seven followed in 1928. A mountaineer, he climbed in the Japanese Alps every summer and spent about half of each year travelling to sketch. From 1925 he ran his own studio of carvers and printers, working under his close supervision.
