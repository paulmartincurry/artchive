Dictionnaire Infernal: Béhémoth

Louis Le Breton · published 1863 · Wood engraving

Free to use

Louis Le Breton studied medicine before sailing on Dumont d'Urville's second voyage aboard the Astrolabe. When the expedition's official illustrator died, Le Breton took his place, and he later drew the demons for the 1863 edition of the Dictionnaire Infernal.
