Fritz Erler · 1917 · Bibliothèque nationale et universitaire de Strasbourg

Free to use

Fritz Erler's painting was used on the poster for Germany's sixth war loan in 1917. The campaign brought in at least 13.1 million marks more than any other. Erler had moved to Munich in 1895 and was a founding member of the magazine Jugend the next year. Under the National Socialists he painted portraits of Hitler.
