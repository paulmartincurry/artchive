He who has now no house will ne'er build one,
Who is alone will now remain alone;
He will awake, will read, will letters write
Through the long day and in the lonely night;

Autumn Day (Herbsttag)

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1902

Translated by Jessie Lemont

In the summer of 1902 Rilke left home and travelled to Paris to write a monograph on the sculptor Auguste Rodin; his daughter had been born the previous December. At first he had a difficult time in the city. For a time he acted as Rodin's secretary.
