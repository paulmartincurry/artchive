Este es su Ser dicho cuando estaba suspenso en calma, en silencio, sin moverse, sin cosa sino vacio el cielo.

Popol Vuh — Este es su ser dicho (opening)

Kʼicheʼ Maya authors (Popol Vuh) · Written down c. 1550; Spanish text by Ximénez c. 1701–1703

Translated by Francisco Ximénez (Spanish), c. 1701–1703

Francisco Ximénez, the Dominican priest who copied out the Kʼicheʼ text and set a Spanish translation beside it, was born in 1666 and reached the New World in 1688. In his first parishes he learned Kaqchikel, a Maya language, and he was later curate of Rabinal from 1704 to 1714. The name Popol Vuh is usually given as Book of the Community, after the woven mat that served as a royal throne.
