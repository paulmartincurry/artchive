Night Café

Louis Marcoussis · c. 1923 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 70.5 × 88.1 cm (27 3/4 × 34 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painter was born Ludwik Markus, and took the name Marcoussis, a village near Paris, at the suggestion of Guillaume Apollinaire. He had served in a Polish company of the French Foreign Legion from 1914 to 1919, and his first solo exhibition in Paris came in 1925, a couple of years after this canvas. A yellow hot air balloon and a French flag show through the window. A Cleveland minister bought the painting in 1948, and his daughters gave it to the museum in 2006.
