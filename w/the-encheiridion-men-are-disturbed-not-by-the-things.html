Men are disturbed not by the things which happen, but by the opinions about the things; for example, death is nothing terrible, for if it were it would have seemed so to Socrates; for the opinion about death that it is terrible, is the terrible thing.

The Encheiridion — Men are disturbed not by the things (V)

Epictetus (compiled by Arrian) · c. 125 CE

Translated by George Long

Epictetus was born into slavery at Hierapolis in Phrygia and lived in Rome until his banishment. Around AD 93, when the emperor Domitian banished all philosophers from the city, he moved to Nicopolis in Epirus and founded a school there.
