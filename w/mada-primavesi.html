Mäda Primavesi

Gustav Klimt · 1912–13 · Oil on canvas · 59 × 43 1/2 in. (149.9 × 110.5 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Mäda Primavesi was nine or ten when Klimt painted her, on a canvas about one and a half metres tall. She lived until 2000. Klimt was fifty, and he had accepted no public commissions since the dispute over his University of Vienna ceiling paintings more than a decade before; he died five years later, after a stroke and pneumonia. The painting was given to the museum in 1964 by André and Clara Mertens, in memory of Clara's mother, Jenny Pulitzer Steiner.
