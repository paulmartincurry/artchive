Corridor in the Asylum

Vincent van Gogh · September 1889 · Brush and oils over black chalk on pink laid paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Van Gogh drew the corridor in black chalk and then worked over it with oil paint and a brush, on a sheet of pink paper 65.1 by 49.1 centimetres. His friendship with Gauguin had ended after a confrontation with a razor, when he cut off part of his own left ear, and he spent time in psychiatric hospitals, among them one at Saint-Rémy. Abby Aldrich Rockefeller left the sheet to the Met in 1948.
