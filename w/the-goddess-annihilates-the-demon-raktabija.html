The Goddess Annihilates the Demon Raktabija (Devi Mahatmya)

Maker unknown · c. 1640 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · 16.2 × 25.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The demon's name, raktapiju, is written on the page. The episode is told in the eighth chapter of the Devi Mahatmya. The museum points out that Hanuman, the monkey god, also fights for the Goddess here. The page is 16.2 by 25.7 centimetres, and Cleveland acquired it in 2018, partly as a purchase and partly as a gift from the Catherine and Ralph Benkaim Collection.
