A wonderful serenity has taken possession of my entire soul, like these sweet mornings of spring which I enjoy with my whole heart.

The Sorrows of Young Werther — A wonderful serenity (Book I, 10 May)

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1774

Translated by R. D. Boylan

Goethe was 24 when he wrote the novel, in five and a half weeks of intensive writing from January to March 1774. He had spent the summer of 1772 in Wetzlar, where he fell in love with Charlotte, who was already engaged to Christian Kestner.
