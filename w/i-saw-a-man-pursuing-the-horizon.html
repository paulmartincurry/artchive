I saw a man pursuing the horizon;
Round and round they sped.
I was disturbed at this;
I accosted the man.
"It is futile," I said,
"You can never"—

"You lie," he cried,
And ran on.

I saw a man pursuing the horizon

Stephen Crane · 1895

The Black Riders and Other Lines was published in May 1895 by Copeland & Day and was Crane's first serious venture into poetry. It was his second published volume, after Maggie: A Girl of the Streets in 1893, and came before The Red Badge of Courage.
