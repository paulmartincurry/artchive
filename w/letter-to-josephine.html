I thought I loved you some days ago; but, since I saw you, I feel that I love you even a thousand times more.

Letter to Josephine (Marmirolo, 17 July 1796)

Napoleon Bonaparte · 1796

Translated by Henry Foljambe Hall, 1901

Napoleon was twenty-six and had been married to Joséphine de Beauharnais for four months when he wrote this letter. That year he was commanding the French campaign against the Austrians and their Italian allies. French was not his first language: he grew up speaking Corsican and Italian, went to the mainland at nine to learn it, and his French spelling stayed poor.
