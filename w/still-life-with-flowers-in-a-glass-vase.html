Still Life with Flowers in a Glass Vase

Rachel Ruysch · c. 1690–c. 1720 · Oil on canvas · 65 × 53.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Rachel Ruysch learned to draw among her father's collections; he was a professor of anatomy and botany. In 1679, at fifteen, she was apprenticed to the Amsterdam flower painter Willem van Aelst, and she went on painting for more than six decades. She signed this canvas at the lower left.
