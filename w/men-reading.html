Men Reading

Francisco de Goya · c. 1820–1823 · Oil on gesso transferred to linen · 125.3 × 65.2 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

X-rays show that Goya changed this mural a great deal: at one stage the background held a landscape with a rider, and the central figure in white had large horns, or possibly wings, that seemed to grow from his head. It hung beside Man Mocked by Two Women, and both pictures are upright and smaller than the other paintings in the series.
