Illustration to Quatrain XX (Rubáiyát of Omar Khayyám)

Edmund Dulac · published 1909 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Dulac's Rubáiyát of 1909 had 20 colour plates, set against Edward FitzGerald's English version of quatrains attributed to Omar Khayyam. FitzGerald first published the translation in 1859; it sold poorly at first, but by the 1880s it was so popular that Omar Khayyam clubs were formed. This copy is in the Maastricht University Library.
