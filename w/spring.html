Nothing is so beautiful as spring--
When weeds, in wheels, shoot long and lovely and lush;

Spring

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

Hopkins dated this sonnet May 1877 and noted its metre as standard rhythm opening with sprung leadings, in the terms of his own system. He was ordained a priest that September. A month later he took up duties as sub-minister and teacher of Latin and Greek at Mount St Mary's College near Sheffield, and the next July he became curate at the Jesuit church in Mount Street, London.
