The Arrest of Pangeran Diponegoro

Raden Saleh · 1857 · Oil paint · Gedung Agung

Free to use

Raden Saleh never met Prince Diponegoro and was studying in Europe when the prince was arrested in 1830, though his family had fought for him. He began the painting in 1856, the year after Diponegoro's death, and was refused permission to visit the sites of the war. There are more than forty people in the frame, and Saleh put himself in twice, as two of the soldiers. He gave the finished work to King William III of the Netherlands. It was sent to Indonesia in 1978 and restored in 2012. It measures 112 by 178 centimetres.
