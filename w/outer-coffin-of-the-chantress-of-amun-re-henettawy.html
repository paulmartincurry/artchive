Outer Coffin of the Chantress of Amun-Re Henettawy

Maker unknown · ca. 1000–945 BCE · Wood, gesso, paint, varnish · L. 203 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The coffin is 203 centimetres long, made of wood covered with gesso, paint and varnish. The museum's own excavators found it in the tomb of Henettawy at Deir el-Bahri in the season of 1923–24.
