Analects — While You Do Not Know Life (Book XI, 11)

Confucius · Warring States period (475–221 BCE)

Translated by James Legge

According to the Han historian Ban Gu, the book began as notes kept by Confucius's disciples of his conversations with them, collected and edited after his death in 479 BCE. Scholars agree that much of it was composed in the Warring States period.
