Aoi no Ue (Pictures of Noh Performances)

Tsukioka Kōgyo · 1898 · Color woodblock print · 25.2 × 37.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In the Tale of Genji, Aoi no Ue, Genji's wife, falls seriously ill, possessed by a phantom caused by Rokujō, his first lover. Kōgyo was twenty-eight or twenty-nine when this series of Noh scenes was published in 1898. Some sets of it survive as albums in their original accordion-style bindings. He died in 1927, aged 57.
