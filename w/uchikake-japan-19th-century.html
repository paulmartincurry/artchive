Uchikake (Outer Kimono), Japan, 19th Century

Maker unknown · late Edo (1789–1868)/Meiji period (1868–1912), 19th century · Silk, warp-float faced 4:1 satin weave; embroidered with silk in satin, single satin, and stem stitches; laid work and couching, and Chinese knots; painted with gold; lined with red silk (chirimen), crepe; dyed with beni, safflower · 194.2 × 126.7 cm (76 1/2 × 49 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The robe is nearly two metres long, 194.2 centimetres by 126.7 across. The red of its crepe lining was dyed with safflower, and the gold on the satin was painted on, not woven in. It came to the museum as an anonymous gift and was shown there in 1992 in an exhibition covering five centuries of Japanese kimono.
