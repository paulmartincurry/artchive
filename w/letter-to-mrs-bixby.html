I pray that our heavenly Father may assuage the anguish of your bereavement, and leave you only the cherished memory of the loved and lost, and the solemn pride that must be yours to have laid so costly a sacrifice upon the altar of freedom.

Letter to Mrs. Bixby

Abraham Lincoln · November 21, 1864

The letter was sent in November 1864 to Lydia Bixby, a widow in Boston who was thought to have lost five sons in the Union army. At least two of her sons survived the war. It was possibly written by John Hay, Lincoln's assistant private secretary. It was delivered on November 25, 1864, and printed in two Boston newspapers that afternoon. The fate of the original is unknown.
