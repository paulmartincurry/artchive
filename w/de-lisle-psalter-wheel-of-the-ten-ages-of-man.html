De Lisle Psalter: Wheel of the Ten Ages of Man (folio 126v)

Attributed to the Madonna Master · c. 1310 · Illuminated manuscript · British Library, London

Free to use

The page belongs to a collection of diagrams attributed to John of Metz, a 13th-century Franciscan friar working in Paris. The painting has been attributed to the Madonna Master, who may also have painted the oak sedilia at Westminster Abbey.
