Nie wieder Krieg (Never Again War)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1924 · Käthe Kollwitz Museum Köln

Free to use

The Socialist Workers' Youth in Leipzig commissioned this poster from Käthe Kollwitz for August 1924, when mass demonstrations across Germany marked ten years since the outbreak of the war. Her younger son Peter had been killed in October 1914. She was fifty-seven.
