I hope to see my Pilot face to face
When I have crossed the bar.

Crossing the Bar

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1889

Tennyson was about eighty and had been Poet Laureate for nearly forty years. Accounts of the writing differ: one has him composing it while crossing the Solent to Farringford on the Isle of Wight after a serious illness, another on a yacht anchored at Salcombe. He said the words came to him in a moment. Shortly before his death three years later he told his son Hallam to put the poem at the end of every edition of his work.
