The Nightmare, Frankfurt Version

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · c. 1790–1791 · Oil on canvas · Frankfurter Goethe-Haus (Freies Deutsches Hochstift), Frankfurt

Free to use

The first Nightmare, shown at the Royal Academy in 1782, sold for twenty guineas, and Fuseli went on to paint other versions. This one, completed in 1790–91, is smaller than the original. The woman's head lies to the left, a mirror faces her on the right, and the demon looks at her rather than out of the picture, with pointed, catlike ears.
