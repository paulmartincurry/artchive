The Artchive
"Yes; quaint and curious war is!
You shoot a fellow down
You'd treat if met where any bar is,
Or help to half-a-crown."

The Man He Killed

Thomas Hardy · 1902

Hardy wrote this poem in 1902, at the time of the Second Boer War, a war he and his wife Emma did not approve of. He was in his early sixties. It was first published in Harper's Weekly on 8 November 1902, and in a book in 1909, in Time's Laughingstocks and Other Verses. In 1990 the former congressman James Symington recited it extemporaneously on camera in the last episode of Ken Burns's series The Civil War.
