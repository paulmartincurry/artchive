Tomb of Giuliano de' Medici, Duke of Nemours

Michelangelo · 1520–34 · Marble · Sagrestia Nuova, Basilica of San Lorenzo, Florence

Free to use with credit. Photo: George M. Groutas, CC BY 2.0

Giuliano de' Medici, Duke of Nemours, died in 1516, and his brother Pope Leo X wanted a princely burial for him. Michelangelo chose Day and Night for his tomb. The portrait of Giuliano was handed to Giovanni Angelo Montorsoli in 1533 for finishing, and the niches at either side, meant for statues of Heaven and Earth, stayed empty. Michelangelo left Florence in 1534 and never set foot there again.
