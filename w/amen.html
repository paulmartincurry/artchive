In the stillness
An angel opens his blue poppied eyes.

Amen

Georg Trakl · 1913

Translated by Babette Deutsch and Avrahm Yarmolinsky, 1923 · Public domain in the United States (published 1923); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Trakl trained as a pharmacist and was working as one at a military hospital in Innsbruck. Ludwig von Ficker, editor of the journal Der Brenner, printed his poems regularly and found him a publisher: his collection Gedichte came out from Kurt Wolff in Leipzig in the summer of 1913, when he was twenty-six. Through Ficker, Ludwig Wittgenstein gave him a large stipend anonymously. Trakl died of a cocaine overdose in a military hospital in Kraków in November 1914.
