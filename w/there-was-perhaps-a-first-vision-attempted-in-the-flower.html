There Was Perhaps a First Vision Attempted in the Flower (Les Origines)

Odilon Redon · 1883 · Lithograph · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This lithograph belongs to Les Origines, the album of 1883 that takes up numbers 44 to 53 in Mellerio's catalogue of Redon's prints. Redon was forty-two or forty-three. He had been drafted in 1870 to serve in the Franco-Prussian War, and when it ended in 1871 he moved to Paris and went back to working almost exclusively in charcoal and lithography.
