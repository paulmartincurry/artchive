Medea (Poster for Sarah Bernhardt at Théâtre de la Renaissance)

Alphonse Mucha · 1898 · Lithograph · Private collection

Free to use

The play on this poster was Médée, a version of Euripides written by Catulle Mendès in 1898, in three acts and in verse. Sarah Bernhardt starred in it at the Théâtre de la Renaissance in Paris. Mucha was thirty-seven or thirty-eight. Twenty years before, the Academy of Fine Arts in Prague had turned him down.
