Mortuary Figures of the Zodiac Signs

Maker unknown · 500s · Gray earthenware with traces of slip · 22.6 cm (8 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The twelve animals stood for the cycle of time and were tied to fortune-telling. Placed in a tomb, they seem to have given the afterlife its order. The figures appear to have been made in moulds.
