The Virgin of the Rocks

Leonardo da Vinci · about 1491–1508 · Oil on panel · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The contract for the altarpiece was signed in Milan on 25 April 1483, with installation due that December. This panel was delivered and put into place on 18 August 1508. In between, the painters asked for a further 1,200 lire and the Confraternity of the Immaculate Conception offered 100. In 1785 the Scottish painter and dealer Gavin Hamilton paid 1,582 lire for it and took it to England. The National Gallery bought it in 1880 for 9,000 guineas.
