We may enlarge our conceptions beyond all imaginable space; we only produce atoms in comparison with the reality of things. It is an infinite sphere, the centre of which is everywhere, the circumference nowhere.

Pensées — An infinite sphere (72)

Blaise Pascal · 1670

Translated by W. F. Trotter

Pascal was a mathematician as well as a philosopher. When he died in 1662 he left behind several draft notes. They were edited by others and first published in 1670.
