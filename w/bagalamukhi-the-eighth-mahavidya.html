Bagalamukhi, the Eighth Mahavidya (Dashamahavidya series)

Maker unknown · c. 1725 · Gum tempera, gold, and silver on paper · Page: 35.6 × 26.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The museum titles this page as Bagalamukhi, one of the ten Mahavidya goddesses of tantric Hinduism, but its own description says the painting remains unidentified. Where it was made is uncertain too: the kingdom of Sirohi in Rajasthan, or Aurangabad in the northern Deccan, around 1725. The goddess holds a naked, ashen figure by the tongue; his hair standing on end suggests he is a demon.
