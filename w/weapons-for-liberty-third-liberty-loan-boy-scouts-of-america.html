Weapons for Liberty: Third Liberty Loan, Boy Scouts of America

J. C. Leyendecker · 1918 · Colour lithograph · 76 × 51 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The poster was made for the Third Liberty Loan, which opened on 5 April 1918. The Boy Scouts and Girl Scouts sold the bonds themselves, under the slogan Every Scout to Save a Soldier. Leyendecker, born in Montabaur, Germany, came to Chicago with his family in 1882 and went on to paint 322 covers for The Saturday Evening Post.
