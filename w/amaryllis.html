Amaryllis

Charles Demuth · c. 1923 · watercolor over graphite · Sheet: 45.8 × 30.4 cm (18 1/16 × 11 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Demuth made this while recovering from diabetes at home in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. Too weak to paint in oils, he kept to watercolour and to things he could study easily, such as flowers. He drew the plant in pencil, brushed on the colour, and used a blotter to work up the pebbled texture. Cleveland bought it from the Daniel Gallery in New York in 1923, and it was among the first works by Demuth to enter a public museum. He was one of the first people in the United States to be treated with insulin.
