Scenes of Witchcraft: Day

Salvator Rosa · c. 1645–49 · Oil on canvas · 54.5 cm (unframed) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This round canvas is 54.5 centimetres across. According to the Cleveland Museum of Art, Rosa chose the shape because the circle is the basis of practising magic. He was writing satirical poetry in Florence in these years, before his return to Rome in 1649. The painting belonged to the Niccolini family of Florence, and the museum bought it in 1977.
