The Evening of the Deluge

J. M. W. Turner · c. 1843 · Oil on canvas · 76 × 76 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The canvas is square, 76 centimetres each way. Its first recorded owner was the Reverend T. J. Judkin, and it was sold at Christie's in London in 1872 as The Animals going into the ark-circle. It came up there again in 1891 as The Deluge and was bought in, and in 1924 as The Eve of the Deluge. Lillian Guyer Timken bequeathed it to the National Gallery of Art in 1960.
