Upon earth there is not his like,
who is made without fear.

Job 41

Book of Job (King James Version) · ca. 500 BCE (trans. 1611)

No author is named for the Book of Job. Its language, which combines post-Babylonian Hebrew with Aramaic influences, indicates that it was composed in the Persian period, between 540 and 330 BCE. This chapter has 34 verses in English Bibles and 26 in the Hebrew Bible, which numbers them differently. The Hebrew word translated here as terror is found nowhere else in the Hebrew Bible.
