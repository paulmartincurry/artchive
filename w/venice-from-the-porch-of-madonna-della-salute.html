Venice, from the Porch of Madonna della Salute

J.M.W. Turner · ca. 1835 · Oil on canvas · The Met

The view does not match the real city: Turner put it together from one of his three visits to Venice. It hung in the Royal Academy exhibition of 1835 at Somerset House in London, when he was sixty. He was a prolific painter, with about 550 oils and more than 2,000 watercolours to his name. Cornelius Vanderbilt left the canvas to the Metropolitan Museum in 1899.
