The Thief of Bagdad, frame at 41:36

Raoul Walsh · 1924 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

The art director William Cameron Menzies was largely responsible for the design, working to requirements set by Douglas Fairbanks, who wrote the story, produced the film and starred in it. The palace sets were built full scale on a Hollywood studio lot. The film cost a reputed 1,135,654.65 dollars. The composer Mortimer Wilson wrote a theme for each character and spent many hours in the editing room fitting his score to the film.
