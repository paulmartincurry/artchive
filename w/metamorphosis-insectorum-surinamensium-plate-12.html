Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 12

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Each banana tree, Merian's text records, bears a single bunch as heavy as a man can carry, with nine or ten rings of twelve to fourteen fruits. She described the flower as five blood-red petals as thick as leather, and noted that the leaves, more than seven feet long, were laid under bread to slide it into the oven. The green caterpillar she found on the tree shed its skin and became a pupa on 21 April.
