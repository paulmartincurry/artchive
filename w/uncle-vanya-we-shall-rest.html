Ah, then dear, dear Uncle, we shall see that bright and beautiful life; we shall rejoice and look back upon our sorrow here; a tender smile—and—we shall rest.

Uncle Vanya — We Shall Rest (closing)

Anton Chekhov · 1897

Translated by Marian Fell

Uncle Vanya is a reworking of The Wood Demon, a play Chekhov had written in 1889, which two theatres refused. He cut the cast from almost two dozen to nine and changed its climactic suicide into a failed homicide. The new play was published in 1897, and its Moscow premiere came on 7 November 1899 at the Moscow Art Theatre, with Stanislavski as Astrov and Chekhov's future wife, Olga Knipper, as Yelena.
