An endless web, as by some fisher strung,
A deadly plenteousness of robe, I flung
All round him, and struck twice; and with two cries
His limbs turned water and broke;

Agamemnon — Clytemnestra over the King's Body

Aeschylus · 458 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray, 1920

The Oresteia, of which Agamemnon is the first play, won first prize at the Dionysia in 458 BC. A few years before, the reformer Ephialtes had stripped the Areopagus court of all its powers except some minor religious duties and the trial of homicide. Aeschylus made Agamemnon lord of Argos rather than Mycenae, which may be significant, since Athens had about then entered an alliance with Argos.
