What is that dirge-like murmur that I hear
Like the sea breaking on a shingle-beach?

Our Casuarina Tree

Toru Dutt · ca. 1877 (Published 1882)

Toru Dutt died of tuberculosis in Calcutta on 30 August 1877, at twenty-one. The poem was in a volume she left unfinished, Ancient Ballads and Legends of Hindustan, which was published in 1882. She grew up between a house in Calcutta and a garden house in the suburb of Baugmaree with a sister, Aru, and a brother, Abjie, who died at fourteen. In 1869, at thirteen, she sailed with her family for Europe and spent four years there, one in France and three in England. She wrote in English and French.
