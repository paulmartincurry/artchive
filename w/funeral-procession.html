Funeral Procession (Mantiq al-Tayr)

Maker unknown · 1486 (dated 892 AH) · Opaque watercolor, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 24.8 × 14 cm; page 33 × 21.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The calligrapher of this manuscript, Sultan 'Ali al-Mashhadi, lost his father at the age of seven and taught himself to write until he moved to Herat around 1468. He copied this text of Attar's poem there in the 1480s for the Timurid court. After the Timurid dynasty fell he retired to Mashhad, where in 1514 he wrote a verse treatise on calligraphy.
