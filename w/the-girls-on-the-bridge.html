The Girls on the Bridge

Edvard Munch · 1918 · Woodcut in blue and lithograph (from multiple stones) in yellow, green, orange and blue on white wove paper · Image: 50 × 43 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch combined two processes in this print: a woodcut printed in blue, and a lithograph printed from several stones in yellow, green, orange and blue. The image is 50 centimetres high and 43 wide. He was fifty-four or fifty-five in 1918.
