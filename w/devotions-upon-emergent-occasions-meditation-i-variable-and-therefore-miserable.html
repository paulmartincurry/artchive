Variable, and therefore miserable condition of man! this minute I was well, and am ill, this minute. I am surprised with a sudden change, and alteration to worse, and can impute it to no cause, nor call it by any name.

Devotions upon Emergent Occasions — Meditation I: Variable, and therefore miserable condition of man

John Donne · 1624

In November 1623 Donne fell seriously ill. The illness is believed to have been relapsing fever or typhus, and he wrote the Devotions that December as he recovered.
