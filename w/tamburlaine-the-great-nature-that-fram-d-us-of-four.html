The Artchive
Nature, that fram'd us of four elements
Warring within our breasts for regiment,
Doth teach us all to have aspiring minds:

Tamburlaine the Great — Nature, that fram'd us of four elements (Part I, Act II, Scene VII)

Christopher Marlowe · c. 1587

The play was written in 1587 or 1588. Both parts were entered in the Stationers' Register on 14 August 1590 and printed together that year. A letter of 1587 tells of a child killed during a performance when a firearm went off by accident.
