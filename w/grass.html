Pile the bodies high at Austerlitz and Waterloo.
Shovel them under and let me work —
I am the grass; I cover all.

Grass

Carl Sandburg · 1918

Grass is from Cornhuskers, the book for which Sandburg received a Pulitzer Prize in 1919; the award was funded by a special grant from the Poetry Society of America. From 1919 to 1930 he and his family lived at 331 South York Street in Elmhurst, Illinois.
