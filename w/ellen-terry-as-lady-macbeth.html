Ellen Terry as Lady Macbeth

John Singer Sargent · 1889 · Oil on canvas · 221 × 114.3 cm · Tate Britain, London

Free to use

Sargent was at the Lyceum Theatre for the opening night on 29 December 1888 and decided almost at once to paint Ellen Terry. The pose with the crown held overhead was not in the production. Her dress was crocheted from green wool and blue tinsel yarn and decorated with 1,000 wing cases of the green jewel beetle. Henry Irving, who played Macbeth, bought the portrait for the Beefsteak Room at the Lyceum.
