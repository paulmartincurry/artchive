The Manger

Gertrude Käsebier · 1899 · Platinum print · 33.7 × 24.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A print of The Manger sold for $100, the most that had ever been paid for a photograph at that time. Gertrude Käsebier was the mother of three children and had begun art school at the age of 37. Alfred Stieglitz published the picture in his journal Camera Notes and again in the opening issue of Camera Work. This print is 33.7 by 24.6 centimetres.
