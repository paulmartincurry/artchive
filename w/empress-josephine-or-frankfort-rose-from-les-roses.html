'Empress Josephine' or Frankfort Rose (Rosa turbinata), from "Les Roses"

Pierre-Joseph Redouté · 1817–24 · Color stipple engraving · 34.9 × 25.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Redouté worked from living plants, not pressed specimens. His paintings for Les Roses were bought by Charles X of France for his widowed daughter-in-law, the Duchess of Berry, and most can no longer be traced; some were sold at Sotheby's in London in 1948. He went through difficult years after Empress Joséphine's death in 1814, until the natural history museum in Paris appointed him a master of draughtsmanship in 1822.
