Green, as I love you, greenly.
Green the wind, and green the branches.

Romance Sonámbulo (opening)

Federico García Lorca · 1928

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Until he was eighteen Lorca meant to be a musician; he had six years of piano lessons and did not turn to writing until his teacher died in 1916. Gypsy Ballads, published in 1928, the year he turned thirty, made his name across Spain. He disliked being typecast as a gypsy poet, and in June 1929 he sailed for New York, where he enrolled at Columbia University at his parents' expense.
