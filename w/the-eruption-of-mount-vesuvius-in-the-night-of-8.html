The Eruption of Mount Vesuvius in the Night of 8 August 1779 (Campi Phlegraei)

Pietro Fabris · 1779 · Etching with gouache · 38.8 × 21 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

The view was taken from near the King's palace at Posillipo on the night of 8 August 1779. The Wellcome's record notes what seems to be a flash of lightning in the sky and, in the foreground, a woman apparently holding up a print of a sacred figure. The print is an etching coloured by hand in gouache.
