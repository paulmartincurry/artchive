Le Désespéré (The Desperate Man)

Gustave Courbet · 1843–1845 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

This self-portrait dates from the beginning of Gustave Courbet's stay in Paris, when he was spending time in the Louvre copying Ribera, Zurbarán, Velázquez and Rembrandt. He turned the canvas on its side, 45 cm high and 54 cm wide, instead of using the upright portrait format. He kept the painting and took it with him into exile in Switzerland in 1873. Qatar Museums acquired it in 2025, and it is to stay at the Musée d'Orsay for five years before going to Doha.
