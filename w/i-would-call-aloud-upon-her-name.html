I Would Call Aloud upon Her Name (Ligeia)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Poe's story Ligeia was first published on 18 September 1838 in the American Museum, a magazine edited by two of his friends. The first version of Clarke's illustrated Tales was restricted to halftone illustrations; a second, with eight colour plates and more than 24 halftone images, was published in 1923.
