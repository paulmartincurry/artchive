Self-Portrait in a Convex Mirror

Parmigianino · ca. 1524 · Oil on poplar panel · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Parmigianino was twenty-one when he painted himself from a convex mirror, on a wooden panel specially made with the same curve. It is 24.4 centimetres across. He took it to Rome in 1525 as one of three small paintings, and Giorgio Vasari records that it was made to show customers what he could do. It was given to Pope Clement VII and later to the writer Pietro Aretino, in whose house Vasari saw it as a child. When it entered the imperial collections in Vienna in 1777 it was attributed to Correggio.
