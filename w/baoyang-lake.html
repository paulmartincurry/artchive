Baoyang Lake (from Eighteen Views of Wuxing)

Song Xu · ca. 1588 · Album leaf; ink and color on silk · Sheet: 26.4 × 28.4 cm (10 3/8 × 11 3/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Song Xu was not from Wuxing and took this album on as a commission. He painted all eighteen views and wrote a note on each one; the note on this leaf gives the distances east to Lake Tai and north to the township of Xiangshan. The sheet is 26.4 by 28.4 centimetres. He was born a commoner in 1525, which makes him about sixty-three here, and he later became a Buddhist priest.
