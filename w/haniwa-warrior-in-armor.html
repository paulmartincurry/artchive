Haniwa Warrior in Armor

Maker unknown · 6th Century · Terracotta · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

Haniwa were built up from coils of clay, layer by layer, with the protruding parts made separately and attached. They stood on cylindrical bases set into the earth of a burial mound, and the rows of them helped to hold the mound in place. The armoured figures show the iron armour and swords worn by the cavalry of the Kofun period.
