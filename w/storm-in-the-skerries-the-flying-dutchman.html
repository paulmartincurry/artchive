Storm in the Skerries. "The Flying Dutchman"

August Strindberg · 1892 · Wax paint on millboard · 62 × 98 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Strindberg found this scene at Dalarö in the Stockholm skerries in the summer of 1892. He worked with a spatula in brief, fierce bursts and usually chose small formats; this board, nearly a metre wide, is one of his largest paintings. He wrote on it that it must never be varnished. On 2 January 1902 he gave it as a wedding present to Gustaf af Geijerstam, and it was given to the museum in Copenhagen in June 1919.
