Death and the Maiden

Egon Schiele · 1915 · Oil on canvas · 150 × 180 cm · Belvedere, Vienna

Free to use

Schiele first called this picture Man and Girl, and also Entwined People. He painted it in 1915, when he had married Edith Harms and been drafted into military service in the First World War. The canvas is signed and dated at the upper right. The Belvedere acquired it in 1930.
