Death as a Strangler (Der Tod als Erwürger)

Alfred Rethel · 1851 · wood engraving [first edition] · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Rethel had the idea for his dance of death in 1847, in several images of Death the reaper, among them this one, which refers to the cholera outbreak in Paris in 1831. His skeleton at a masked ball, scraping two bones like a violin, is said to have haunted his artist friends and disturbed their dreams. This impression is from the first edition of the wood engraving, dated 1851.
