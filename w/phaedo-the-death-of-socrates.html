Phaedo — The Death of Socrates

Plato · 4th century BCE

Translated by Benjamin Jowett

The Phaedo is the fourth and last of Plato's dialogues about the final days of Socrates, after the Euthyphro, the Apology and the Crito. It is set in an Athenian prison in 399 BC, and told by Phaedo of Elis to Echecrates. Socrates was in his seventies. A day or two earlier his friends had arranged for him to escape into exile, and he had declined.
