Judith I (Judith and the Head of Holofernes)

Gustav Klimt · 1901 · Oil and gold on canvas · Belvedere, Vienna

The canvas is twice as tall as it is wide, 84 by 42 centimetres. The face has been recognised as that of Adele Bloch-Bauer, a Viennese socialite and friend of Klimt, who painted two portraits of her in later years. Critics took the figure for Salome. To settle the matter Klimt had his brother Georg make the metal frame with the words Judith and Holofernes engraved on it. He painted the subject a second time in 1909.
