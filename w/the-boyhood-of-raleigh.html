The Boyhood of Raleigh

John Everett Millais · 1870 · Oil paint on canvas · 120.6 × 142.2 cm · Tate, London

Free to use

Millais travelled to Budleigh Salterton on the Devon coast to paint the setting. His own sons, Everett and George, posed as the young Walter Raleigh and his brother; the sailor was a professional model. The idea came from an essay by the historian James Anthony Froude on the Elizabethan seafarers. The painting was shown at the Royal Academy in 1871.
