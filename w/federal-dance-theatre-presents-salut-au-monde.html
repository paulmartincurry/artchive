Federal Dance Theatre Presents Salut au Monde

Richard Halls · 1936 or 1937 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Richard Halls designed this silkscreen for Salut au Monde, a dance adapted from Walt Whitman's poem, with choreography by Helen Tamiris and music by Genevieve Pitot. A date stamp on the back reads 16 June 1937. Tamiris was the central figure of the Federal Dance Project, which ran on its own from January 1936 until October 1937.
