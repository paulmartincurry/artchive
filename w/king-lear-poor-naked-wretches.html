O, I have ta’en
Too little care of this! Take physic, pomp;
Expose thyself to feel what wretches feel,

King Lear — Poor Naked Wretches (Act III, Scene IV)

William Shakespeare · 1606

Shakespeare wrote the part of Lear for Richard Burbage, his company's chief tragedian. The one performance known from his lifetime was given before King James I at Whitehall on 26 December 1606. On the Globe stage Lear's costume changed as he fell, from crown and regalia to a huntsman's clothes to bareheaded in the storm.
