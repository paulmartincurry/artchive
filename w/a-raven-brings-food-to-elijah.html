A Raven Brings Food to Elijah (Mirror of Holiness)

Maker unknown · 1602–04 · Gum tempera, ink, color, and gold on paper · Page: 26.2 x 15.7 cm (10 5/16 x 6 3/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Elijah sits like an Indian holy man under a tree while a raven brings him bread and meat, as God commanded. The book was written by the Jesuit Father Jerome Xavier and painted for Prince Salim, the future emperor Jahangir.
