The Threatened Swan

Jan Asselijn · c. 1650 · Oil on canvas · 144 × 171 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The swan is life-size. Some time after Jan Asselijn painted it, three inscriptions were added that turned it into a political allegory: the grand pensionary under the swan, Holland on an egg, the enemy of the state above the dog. A dealer bought it in 1800 for 100 guilders for the Nationale Konst-Gallery in The Hague, and it is documented as that institution's first purchase.
