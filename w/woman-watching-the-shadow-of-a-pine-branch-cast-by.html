Woman Watching the Shadow of a Pine Branch Cast by the Moon (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–1892 · Woodblock print

Free to use

This is one of 100 prints that Tsukioka Yoshitoshi's publisher, Akiyama Buemon, issued in batches between 1885 and 1892. The only thing linking the hundred subjects is that the Moon is in every one. People queued before dawn to buy each new design and still found the edition sold out.
