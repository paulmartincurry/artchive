Ghosts of the Taira at Daimotsu Bay

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1843–47 · Triptych of woodblock prints; ink and color on paper · Each sheet 37.5 × 25.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kuniyoshi took his warrior subjects from war tales such as the Tale of the Heike and added dreams, ghostly apparitions, omens and superhuman feats. The ghost of Taira no Tomomori at Daimotsu Bay was one of the subjects he treated this way, for a public with a growing taste for the ghastly. Each of the three sheets is 37.5 by 25.1 centimetres.
