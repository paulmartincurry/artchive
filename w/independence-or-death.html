Independence or Death (The Cry of Ipiranga)

Pedro Américo · 1888 · Oil on canvas · Museu Paulista, São Paulo

Free to use

Nobody invited Pedro Américo to paint this; he offered in 1885, and was first put off for lack of funds. The contract he signed on 14 July 1886 gave him three years and thirty contos de réis, with six more paid on signing for studies. He painted it entirely in Florence and finished a year early, 66 years after the event it shows. It was first shown at the Academy of Fine Arts of Florence on 8 April 1888.
