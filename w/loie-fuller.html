Loie Fuller

Jules Chéret · 1893 · Colour lithographic poster · 111.1 × 82.4 cm · National Portrait Gallery, Smithsonian Institution, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

Loie Fuller was born on a farm in Illinois and created her Serpentine Dance by 1891, lighting silk costumes with coloured gels fixed to revolving lamps of her own design. A court refused to protect the dance from imitators, and she left for Europe in June 1892. Chéret's poster dates from the following year. The image is more than a metre tall.
