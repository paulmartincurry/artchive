Sidewall in Floral and Foliate pattern

Maker unknown · 1860–1870 · block-printed paper · H × W (Roll): 50.2 cm (19 3/4 in.) width · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Eleven colours were block printed on a gray ground for this paper, a pattern of plants in the manner of Indian chintz. The roll is 50.2 centimetres wide, and it came to the museum from the Long Island Museum.
