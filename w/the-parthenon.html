The Parthenon

Iktinos and Callicrates · 447–432 BCE · Pentelic marble

Free to use with credit. Photo: Steve Swayne, CC BY 2.0

Construction began in 447 BC and the building was finished in 438 BC, though work on its sculpture went on until 432 BC. It was a temple to Athena that also served as the city treasury. It later became a church, and before the end of the fifteenth century a mosque. On 26 September 1687 a Venetian mortar round set off the gunpowder the Ottomans were storing inside, blowing out the centre of the building and killing about three hundred people.
