If I should die, think only this of me:

The Soldier

Rupert Brooke · 1914

The Soldier is the fifth and final sonnet in Rupert Brooke's sequence 1914. The Times Literary Supplement printed it on 11 March 1915, and it was read from the pulpit of St Paul's Cathedral on Easter Sunday. Brooke had joined the Royal Navy after war broke out in August 1914. He died that April, aged twenty-seven, of septicaemia from a mosquito bite, aboard a French hospital ship off Skyros. The manuscript is at King's College, Cambridge.
