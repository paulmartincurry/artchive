The Assumption of the Virgin

El Greco · 1577–79 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

El Greco arrived in Toledo in 1577, in his mid-thirties, and this was the centre of his first major commission there: the high altarpiece of the convent church of Santo Domingo el Antiguo, the funerary monument of the noblewoman Doña Maria de Silva. The canvas is more than four metres tall and was in place by September 1579. He signed it at lower right in Greek, calling himself a Cretan. Durand-Ruel bought it in 1904 for 100,000 francs with money from Henry O. Havemeyer, and the Art Institute bought it in 1906.
