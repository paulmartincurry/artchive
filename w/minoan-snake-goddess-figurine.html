Minoan Snake Goddess Figurine (the Smaller Figure from Knossos)

Maker unknown · c. 1600 BCE · Faience · H. about 29 cm as restored · Heraklion Archaeological Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: C messier, CC BY-SA 4.0

Arthur Evans's excavators found this figure in 1903 in a stone-lined pit in the palace at Knossos, without its head and without the left arm below the elbow. Evans and one of his restorers made the head. The crown was a fragment from the same pit and the cat or panther another loose piece, which Evans only later decided belonged to it. The figure is faience and stands about 29 centimetres tall as restored. Evans called it a Snake Priestess.
