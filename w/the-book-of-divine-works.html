I am the supreme and fiery force who has kindled all sparks of life

The Book of Divine Works (I Am That Supreme and Fiery Force)

Hildegard von Bingen · ca. 1163–1174

Translated by Sabina Flanagan · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Hildegard was in her mid-sixties when she began this book and in her mid-seventies when she finished it. She was by then head of two monasteries she had founded herself, Rupertsberg in 1150 and Eibingen in 1165. She had kept her visions almost entirely to herself until she was forty-two, and a monk named Volmar served as her scribe. In a letter written at seventy-seven she said the visions came to her wide awake, with her eyes open. The book is one of three volumes of visionary theology she left, alongside nearly 400 letters.
