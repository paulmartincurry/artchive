The Denial of Saint Peter

Hendrick ter Brugghen · c. 1626 · Oil on canvas · 132.3 × 178 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Ter Brugghen painted this canvas around 1626, about three years before his death in Utrecht on 1 November 1629, possibly of the plague. He is thought to have been in Italy in 1607, and he was the only Dutch painter in Rome during Caravaggio's lifetime. From about 1750 the picture belonged to the Reedtz-Thott family at Gavnø Castle in Denmark, who sold it in the late 1960s.
