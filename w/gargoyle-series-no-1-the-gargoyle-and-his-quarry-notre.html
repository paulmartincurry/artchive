Gargoyle Series No. 1: The Gargoyle and His Quarry, Notre Dame

John Taylor Arms · 1920 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

John Taylor Arms trained as an architect at MIT, graduating in 1912, and served as a United States Navy officer in the First World War before taking up etching full time. He published his first original etchings in 1919, the year before this plate. He worked with sewing needles and magnifying glasses to get his detail. This impression is an artist's proof, signed and dated in pencil, and was printed by Frederick Reynolds.
