Ishtar Gate

Maker unknown · ca. 575 BCE · Glazed brick · Pergamon Museum, Berlin

Free to use with credit. Photo: LBM1948, CC BY-SA 4.0

King Nebuchadnezzar II ordered this gate for the north side of Babylon and roofed it with cedar. Each animal was built from bricks pressed in reusable moulds, with the joints planned so that none fell across an eye. The bricks were fired once, painted with a blue glaze made to imitate lapis lazuli, fired again, and sealed at the seams with bitumen. German excavators numbered the pieces and shipped them packed in straw inside coal barrels. What stands in Berlin is the smaller front gate; the larger rear gate was too big for the museum and is in storage.
