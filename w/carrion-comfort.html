Not, I'll not, carrion comfort, Despair, not feast on thee;
Not untwist--slack they may be--these last strands of man
In me or, most weary, cry I can no more. I can;
Can something, hope, wish day come, not choose not to be.

Carrion Comfort

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1918

Hopkins met Robert Bridges, later Poet Laureate, while studying Classics at Balliol College, Oxford, and the two stayed friends for life. Hopkins died in 1889, and afterwards Bridges published a few of his mature poems in anthologies. His father, Manley Hopkins, also wrote poetry, as well as a history of Hawaii and a manual of marine insurance.
