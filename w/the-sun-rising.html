She'is all States, and all Princes, I,
Nothing else is.

The Sun Rising

John Donne · 1633

The Sun Rising runs to thirty lines in three stanzas. The number of stresses in a line ranges from two to six in no fixed pattern, and every stanza rhymes ABBACDCDEE. Like most of Donne's poems it was first printed in 1633, two years after his death.
