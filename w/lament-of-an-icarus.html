And, scorched by desire for the beautiful,
I will not know the bliss,
of giving my name to that abyss,
that knows my tomb and funeral.

Lament of an Icarus (Les Plaintes d'un Icare)

Charles Baudelaire · published 1868

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The poem is among the additions to the third edition of Les Fleurs du mal, published in 1868, the year after Baudelaire's death. The first edition, of 1857, had been prosecuted: six of its poems were suppressed and Baudelaire was fined 300 francs.
