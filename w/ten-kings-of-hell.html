Ten Kings of Hell

Jin Chushi · before 1195 · One of five of a set of ten hanging scrolls, ink and color on silk · 129.5 × 49.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met holds five scrolls from a set of ten, one for each of the Kings of Hell, painted by Jin Chushi before 1195; each painted silk is about 1.3 metres tall. In the Chinese account of the underworld the dead pass through ten courts, each ruled by a judge, and are sent on to be reborn once their punishment is served. The museum bought the five in 1930.
