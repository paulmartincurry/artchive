Behemoth and Leviathan (The Book of Job, Plate 15)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The Job designs began as watercolours painted in 1805–06 for Thomas Butts. Of the 315 sets of engravings printed, only 20 were sold in Blake's lifetime, mostly to people in his own circle. The Royal Academy bought one and awarded him £25. This impression is on India paper.
