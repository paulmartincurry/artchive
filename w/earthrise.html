Earthrise

William Anders · 1968 · Photograph

Free to use

William Anders took this photograph from lunar orbit on 24 December 1968, during Apollo 8, the first crewed voyage to orbit the Moon. He used a modified Hasselblad with a 250 mm lens and 70 mm colour film made by Kodak. He had been photographing the lunar surface, and he took a black-and-white frame of the Earth first, before finding colour film. For years accounts credited the picture to the mission commander, Frank Borman. The published image is rotated 95 degrees clockwise from the way the crew saw it.
