Not the law, but the land sets the limit. Desert is the name it wears upon the maps, but the Indian's is the better word.

The Land of Little Rain (opening)

Mary Austin · 1903

Houghton Mifflin first published the book in 1903, when Mary Hunter Austin was about thirty-five. Its fourteen chapters are essays and short stories about the Owens Valley and the Mojave Desert of California. An abridged edition of 1950 carried photographs by Ansel Adams.
