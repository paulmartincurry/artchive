The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1632 · Oil on canvas · Mauritshuis

Rembrandt was 26 and newly arrived in Amsterdam when the Surgeons' Guild gave him this job, his first major commission in the city. Each man shown paid to be in it. The dissection took place on 31 January 1632; the guild allowed one public dissection a year, and the body was that of Aris Kindt, hanged for armed robbery earlier the same day. In 1828 the king blocked a plan to sell the painting, and the Dutch state bought it for 32,000 guilders.
