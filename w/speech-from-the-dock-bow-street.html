We are here, not because we are law-breakers;
we are here in our efforts to become law-makers.

Speech from the Dock, Bow Street

Emmeline Pankhurst · October 1908

Emmeline Pankhurst was fifty in October 1908. Five years earlier she had founded the Women's Social and Political Union, an all-women organisation whose members smashed windows and went to prison repeatedly, she and her daughters among them. Her father, a Manchester manufacturer, had owned a theatre in Salford and played the leads in Shakespeare there.
