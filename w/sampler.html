Sampler

Mary Follet · 1802 · silk embroidery on linen foundation Technique: plain weave foundation; cross, stem, chain, running, and surface satin stitching · Warp × Weft: 53.5 × 41.4 cm (21 1/16 × 16 5/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Mary Follet was twelve when she worked this sampler, and she stitched her age and the year, 1802, along the bottom. Two scenes with twelve figures and animals sit inside a floral border, in silk on linen, 53.5 by 41.4 centimetres.
