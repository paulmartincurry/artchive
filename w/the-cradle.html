The Cradle (Le Berceau)

Berthe Morisot · 1872 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay

Free to use

The woman is the painter's sister Edma, watching her sleeping daughter Blanche. Berthe Morisot showed the picture at the first Impressionist exhibition in 1874, held in the former studio of the photographer Nadar, and priced it at 800 francs. It did not sell. It stayed in the family and passed to Blanche, the baby in the cradle, until the Louvre acquired it in 1930.
