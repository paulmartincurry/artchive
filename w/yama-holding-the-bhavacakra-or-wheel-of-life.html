Yama Holding the Bhavacakra or Wheel of Life

Maker unknown · before 1895 · Ink on cloth · 136.3 × 113.1 cm · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

An artist from Tashi Lhunpo made this drawing for Laurence Waddell, a British army surgeon stationed in Darjeeling who learned Tibetan. It copies a version at Samye Monastery believed to have been brought there by an Indian monk in the eighth century, which is in turn believed to be copied from a painting in the Ajanta caves. Waddell reproduced the drawing in his book The Buddhism of Tibet, published in 1895. The cloth is 136.3 by 113.1 centimetres.
