The Moon Beyond Shinagawa (One Hundred Views of Musashi Province)

Kobayashi Kiyochika · 1884 · Color woodblock print; oban · 35.6 × 23.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kiyochika is said to have studied Western-style painting under Charles Wirgman, and from August 1876 he made what he called light-ray pictures, prints with Western light and shade, possibly influenced by the photographs of Shimooka Renjō. By 1884, when this print came out, demand for such prints had fallen and he was drawing comic images for newspapers. He used a subdued palette, without the harsh aniline dyes then common.
