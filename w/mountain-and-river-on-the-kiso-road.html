Mountain and River on the Kiso Road

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Triptych of color woodblock prints · 36 × 25.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Late in his career Hiroshige made a set of three triptychs on the old poetic theme of snow, moon and flowers; this is the snow. It was published in 1857, the year before his death, by which time he had already retired from the world as a Buddhist monk. Edward L. Whittemore bought it from S. H. Mori of Chicago in 1925 and left it to Cleveland in 1930.
