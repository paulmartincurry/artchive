Songs of Experience: The Tyger

William Blake · 1794, printed c. 1825 · Relief etching printed in orange-brown ink and hand-colored with watercolor and shell gold · Sheet: 15.7 × 14.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Blake published the poem in 1794 and went on reprinting the book for the rest of his life. This impression was printed about 1825, in orange-brown ink, and coloured by hand with watercolour and shell gold. Only 28 copies of the combined Songs made in his lifetime are known to exist.
