Biographies of Lian Po and Lin Xiangru

Huang Tingjian · ca. 1095 · Handscroll; ink on paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The scroll is 34.3 centimetres high and, with its mounting, more than 21 metres long, and it holds 652 identifiable characters in grass script. Huang Tingjian, about fifty at the time, copied the words from Chapter 81 of the Records of the Grand Historian, written roughly a thousand years before him, and edited the text so that it stops at a key point. The New York collector John M. Crawford Jr. left it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1988.
