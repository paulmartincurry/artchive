And not one will know of the war, not one
Will care at last when it is done.

There Will Come Soft Rains

Sara Teasdale · 1918

The poem is twelve lines long and first appeared in Harper's Monthly Magazine in July 1918, two months after the Sedition Act made it a crime in the United States to publish disloyal language about the government. Teasdale revised it for her 1920 collection Flame and Shadow, adding the subtitle War Time and changing one word in the second line, calling, to circling. Ray Bradbury took the title for a short story in 1950.
