Portrait of Wally Neuzil

Egon Schiele · 1912 · Oil on panel · Leopold Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Schiele met Wally Neuzil in 1911, when he was 21 and she was 17, and she modelled for him for several years. In 1939 the painting's owner, the Jewish art dealer Lea Bondi Jaray, was made to hand it over to another dealer as she fled Austria. Rudolf Leopold bought it from the Austrian National Gallery in 1954. It was seized in New York after a loan to the Museum of Modern Art, and in July 2010 the Leopold Museum paid $19 million to Bondi's heirs to settle the claim. The panel is 32 centimetres high.
