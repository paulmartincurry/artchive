My First Summer in the Sierra (June 6)

John Muir · 1911

In March 1867 a file slipped in an Indianapolis wagon wheel factory and cut John Muir's right eye, and he spent six weeks in a darkened room waiting for his sight to return. That September he set off on a walk of about a thousand miles from Kentucky to Florida, then sailed by way of Cuba and New York to California. He reached Yosemite and worked there as a shepherd for a season. The book about those first years in the mountains came out in 1911, when he was in his seventies.
