The Toilet, from The Rape of the Lock

Aubrey Beardsley · c. 1895–96 · pen and black ink with traces of graphite underdrawing · Sheet: 25.6 × 17.4 cm (10 1/16 × 6 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This drawing is for an 1896 edition of The Rape of the Lock, the poem Alexander Pope published in 1712. Traces of pencil underdrawing show beneath the ink. The garden with its domed pavilion is on a folding screen, not seen through a window. In December 1896 Beardsley suffered a violent haemorrhage from the tuberculosis that killed him at twenty-five. The museum bought the sheet in 1953 from R. A. Walker of Bedford.
