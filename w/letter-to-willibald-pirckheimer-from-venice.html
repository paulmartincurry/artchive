How I shall freeze after this sun! Here I am a gentleman, at home a parasite.

Letter to Willibald Pirckheimer from Venice (October 1506)

Albrecht Dürer · 1506

Translated by Rudolf Tombo, 1913

Dürer was thirty-five and on his second stay in Venice, which lasted from early 1506 to the spring of 1507. The city's German community had commissioned an altarpiece from him for the church of San Bartolomeo. By then his engravings were popular enough to be copied. In Venice he began drawing on blue paper. Willibald Pirckheimer, to whom the letter is addressed, also received one in which Dürer wrote in rough terms about his own wife.
