Save Serbia Our Ally

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1916 · Colour lithograph · 92 × 60 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Steinlen signed and dated the poster in 1916, and it was printed by Lapina in Paris for the Serbian Relief Committee of America. According to the Library of Congress record, about 700,000 Serbians fled into the mountains of Albania in the winter of 1915 after their defeat by the Austro-German army, and many thousands died on the way. The same image appears on a French poster for a Serbian day of 25 June 1916.
