Bandits' Roost, 59½ Mulberry Street

Jacob Riis · 1888, printed 1958 · Gelatin silver print · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Riis was a police reporter when he began photographing the slums of the Lower East Side at night in 1887 and 1888. His pictures of Mulberry Street went into How the Other Half Lives, published by Scribner's in 1890 after an eighteen-page magazine article the Christmas before. In 1897 the city cleared the worst of Mulberry Bend and made it a park, now Columbus Park. This print of the negative was made in 1958.
