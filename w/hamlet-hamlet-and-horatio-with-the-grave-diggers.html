Hamlet: Hamlet and Horatio with the Grave Diggers

Eugène Delacroix · 1843 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The lithograph is based, with some alterations, on an oil painting Delacroix had made four years earlier and shown at the Paris Salon of 1839. He exhibited the painting again at the Salon of 1855, and the Louvre acquired it in 1884. This impression is from the third of three states of the print.
