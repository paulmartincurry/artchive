Ono Waterfall on the Kiso Road (A Tour of the Waterfalls of the Provinces)

Katsushika Hokusai · early 1830s · Color woodblock print · Sheet: 38.3 × 26.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Hokusai was a follower of Nichiren Buddhism, and the waterfalls he chose for this series of eight prints were well-known pilgrimage sites of his day. The series, finished about 1833 to 1834, was the first in ukiyo-e to take falling water as its subject, and every sheet is upright. This impression carries the mark of the publisher Nishimuraya Yohachi.
