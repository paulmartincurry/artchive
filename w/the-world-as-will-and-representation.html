Hence its life swings like a pendulum to and fro between pain and boredom

The World as Will and Representation

Arthur Schopenhauer · 1818

Translated by E. F. J. Payne · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Schopenhauer worked on this book for several years in Dresden and was thirty when the first edition came out in late 1818, with the date 1819 on its title page. It was met with near-universal silence, and the expanded second edition of 1844 also failed to attract interest. He did not need it to sell: his inheritance from his father, invested in government bonds, earned him more than double the salary of a university professor.
