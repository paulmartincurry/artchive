My peace is gone,
My heart is sore:
I never shall find it,
Ah, nevermore!

Faust, Part One — Margaret at the Spinning-Wheel

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1808

Translated by Bayard Taylor

The earliest forms of Faust were developed between 1772 and 1775; that manuscript is lost, but a copy was discovered in 1886. The play first appeared in print as a fragment in 1790. Franz Schubert set this scene to music on 19 October 1814, three months before his eighteenth birthday.
