Manuel Osorio Manrique de Zuñiga

Francisco de Goya · 1787–88 · Oil on canvas · 50 × 40 in. (127 × 101.6 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sitter was born in April 1784 and died at eight in June 1792. His father, the Count of Altamira, a director of the Banco de San Carlos, ordered the portrait. The magpie holds Goya's visiting card in its beak. The painting sold in Paris in 1878 for 1,200 francs; in 1926 Jules Bache paid $160,000 for it after his daughter Kathryn saw it at Duveen's gallery, and it was still shown in her apartment at times until her death in 1979.
