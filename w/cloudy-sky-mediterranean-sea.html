Cloudy Sky, Mediterranean Sea

Gustave Le Gray · 1857 · Albumen print, No. 16 from the album "Vistas del Mar" · Image/paper: 30.9 × 41.8 cm (12 3/16 × 16 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A single exposure of the time could not record sea and sky together: the sky washed out or the water went too dark. Gustave Le Gray, who had trained in Paris as a painter, made one negative for the sea and another for the sky at different exposure times and printed from both. This print was once bound with others in an album of his seascapes. His studio, opened in 1855, ran into debt, and he closed it and left France.
