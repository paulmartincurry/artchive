One question I ask of you:
Where flows the water of Kane?

The Water of Kane

Traditional Hawaiian (Kauai) · Printed 1909

Translated by Nathaniel B. Emerson

Nathaniel B. Emerson printed this mele, with the Hawaiian beside his English, in Unwritten Literature of Hawaii, a Smithsonian bulletin of 1909 on the songs of the hula. It closes the book's last chapter before the general review. He wrote that it came from Kauai, and that he had found the idea of the water of Kane worked into a set form nowhere else.
