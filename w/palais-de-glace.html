Palais de Glace

Jules Chéret · 1891 · Color lithograph on cream wove paper · 124 × 87.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Jules Chéret trained in lithography in London from 1859 to 1866, and he came back to France to make posters for cabarets, music halls and theatres. The French government gave him the Légion d'honneur in 1890, the year before this poster for the Palais de Glace. Imprimerie Chaix printed it.
