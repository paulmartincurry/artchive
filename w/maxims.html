Maxims

François de La Rochefoucauld · 1665

Translated by Leonard Tancock · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

La Rochefoucauld published the maxims anonymously in 1665, when he was fifty-one. He had spent years as a soldier and as an aristocratic rebel, and in 1652 he was shot through the head at the battle of the Faubourg Saint-Antoine and took a year to recover. Writing sentences and maxims was the special pastime of the salon of the Marquise de Sablé, where he was a regular. He went on altering and adding to them for the rest of his life, and editions now print 504, most of them two or three lines long.
