Without going outside his door, one understands (all that takes place) under the sky; without looking out from his window, one sees the Tao of Heaven.

Tao Te Ching — Without Going outside His Door (Ch. 47)

Laozi (Lao Tzu) · 4th–3rd century BC

Translated by James Legge

In the first of three stories told by the Han historian Sima Qian, Laozi was an official in the imperial archives. At the request of Yinxi, keeper of the Han-ku Pass, he wrote a book in two parts before leaving for the West.
