The Circle of the Corrupt Officials; the Devils Tormenting Ciampolo (Dante's Divine Comedy, Plate 2)

William Blake · 1827 · Engraving · Sheet: 39.3 × 53.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The commission to illustrate Dante's Divine Comedy came to Blake in 1826 through John Linnell. His death in 1827 cut the work short, and only seven of the engravings reached proof form. This is plate 2, for canto XXII of the Inferno, on a sheet about 39 by 54 centimetres.
