Tlingit Mask

Maker unknown · c. 1825 · Wood, paint · 21.6 × 16.5 × 15.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This painted wooden mask was made around 1825 and is 21.6 centimetres high. Tlingit society is divided into two moieties, the Raven and the Eagle, and their clans display heraldic crests on totem poles, canoes, feast dishes, house posts and other art. The Met bought the mask in 1966 with a gift from Mrs. Gertrud A. Mellon.
