Jūmantsubo Plain at Fukagawa Susaki (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1856 · Woodblock print (nishiki-e); ink and color on paper; vertical ōban · 35.7 × 24.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is print 107 of 119 in the series, and its date seal reads the intercalary fifth month of 1857, although the Met dates it 1856. The mountain on the horizon is Mount Tsukuba. Hiroshige began the series in 1856, the year he became a Buddhist monk.
