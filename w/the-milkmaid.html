The Milkmaid

Johannes Vermeer · 1660 · Oil on canvas · Rijksmuseum

Free to use

Johannes Vermeer was twenty-five when he painted this. He first put a large wall map behind the woman and a large clothes basket near the bottom of the picture, then painted over both; the basket was later found with an X-ray. For the blue he used natural ultramarine, made from crushed lapis lazuli, where other painters typically used the much cheaper azurite. At a sale in 1696 it brought 175 guilders, the second-highest price there. The Rijksmuseum acquired it in 1908 with support from the Dutch government.
