Bookbinding for a Qur'an

Maker unknown · 1460–1500 · leather over paper pasteboards; outer cover tooled, blue paint; inner covers openwork designs over colored papers · Overall: 36 × 81.6 cm (14 3/16 × 32 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The binding was made in Istanbul of leather laid over pasteboards made of paper. Like most Islamic book covers it has a triangular flap on the back cover that tucks under the front to protect the edges of the pages. The inside covers are cut leather openwork set over coloured papers, a technique that began in northeastern Iran in the 1400s. Cleveland bought it in 1944 from Antoinette Minassian of New York.
