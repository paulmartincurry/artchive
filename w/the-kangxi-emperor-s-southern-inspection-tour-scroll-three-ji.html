The Kangxi Emperor's Southern Inspection Tour, Scroll Three: Ji'nan to Mount Tai

Wang Hui · datable to 1698 · Handscroll; ink and color on silk · Image: 26 3/4 in. × 45 ft. 8 3/4 in. (67.9 × 1393.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Wang Hui was born in 1632, so he was about sixty-six when this scroll was painted. It is 68 centimetres high and almost 14 metres long. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1979.
