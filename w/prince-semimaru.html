Prince Semimaru (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 30.4 × 21.9 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Semimaru, poet and musician, tunes the strings of his lute at a mountain cottage. The Library of Congress catalogues the maker as Taiso Yoshitoshi: he took the name Taiso, meaning great resurrection, in 1873, the year after a mental breakdown.
