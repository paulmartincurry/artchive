Albertus Seba · 1735 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Three snakes carry Albertus Seba's name in their scientific names: the species Ninia sebae and Python sebae and the subspecies Oxyrhopus petola sebae. This plate is from the second volume of his catalogue, dated 1735, the year before his death.
