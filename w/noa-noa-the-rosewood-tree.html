I struck out with joy. My hands became stained with blood in my wild rage, my intense joy of satiating within me, I know not what divine brutality.

Noa Noa: The Rosewood Tree

Paul Gauguin · 1893

Translated by O. F. Theis, 1919

Parts of Noa Noa, including his account of Tehaʼamana, appeared in La Revue Blanche in 1897, without pictures. The book came out in 1901 in the La Plume edition, with poems by Charles Morice, but Gauguin refused to let his woodcuts be printed on the smooth paper the publishers wanted. He never saw a copy and turned down one hundred free ones. The manuscript is now in the Louvre.
