I heard a fly buzz when I died;
The stillness round my form
Was like the stillness in the air
Between the heaves of storm.

I heard a Fly buzz when I died

Emily Dickinson · published 1896

From about 1840 the Dickinson family lived in a house that overlooked Amherst's burial ground. When Emily was thirteen her second cousin and close friend Sophia Holland died of typhus, and her parents sent her to relatives in Boston to recover. This poem was printed ten years after her own death at fifty-five.
