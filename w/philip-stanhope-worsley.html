Philip Stanhope Worsley

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1866 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · image 30.4 × 25 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Philip Stanhope Worsley was an English poet who published a translation of the Odyssey in 1861 and of the first twelve books of the Iliad in 1865. Cameron's portrait is dated 1866. He died of tuberculosis on 8 May of that year, at 30.
