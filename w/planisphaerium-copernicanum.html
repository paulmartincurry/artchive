Planisphaerium Copernicanum (Harmonia Macrocosmica, Plate 22)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660

Free to use

This is plate 22 of a star atlas of 29 plates, published in Amsterdam in 1660 by the map publisher Johannes Janssonius. Andreas Cellarius meant the book as the first volume of a two-part work on cosmography, and the second volume never appeared. Of the engravers who cut the copper plates, only two signed their work.
