The Artchive
There is a weeping in the world
As if the Good Lord now lay dead,

End of the World (Weltende)

Else Lasker-Schüler · December 1910

Translated by Robert P. Newton, 1982 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Lasker-Schüler was forty-one in December 1910. That year she separated from her second husband, Georg Lewin, founder of the Expressionist magazine Der Sturm, whose pseudonym, Herwarth Walden, was her invention. After the divorce in 1912 she was penniless and depended on money from friends, Karl Kraus in particular. She had trained as an artist in Berlin and illustrated some of her own books of poetry.
