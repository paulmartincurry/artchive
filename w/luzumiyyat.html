Tread lightly, for the mighty that have been
Might now be breathing in the dust unseen;

Luzūmiyyāt

Abu al-ʿAlāʾ al-Maʿarrī · ca. 1030 CE

Translated by Ameen Rihani, 1918

The collection holds nearly 1600 short poems, six or seven lines each on average, set in alphabetical order. None has a title of its own. Al-Maʿarrī devised a double-consonant rhyme scheme for them, and the Arabic name of the book refers to that rule, which nothing obliged him to follow. He wrote the poems over many years and circulated them in his lifetime. He was a vegan and lived in near isolation.
