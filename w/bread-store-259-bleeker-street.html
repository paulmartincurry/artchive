Berenice Abbott · 3 February 1937 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

The photograph is dated 3 February 1937. Later that year, from October 1937 to January 1938, Changing New York was shown at the Museum of the City of New York. When Abbott resigned from the Federal Art Project in 1939, the series had produced 305 photographs, and they were deposited at that museum.
