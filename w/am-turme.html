And only in secret may have my will
And give to the wind my tresses.

Am Turme (On the Tower)

Annette von Droste-Hülshoff · 1842 (Published 1844)

Translated by Charles Wharton Stork, 1913

Between October 1841 and April 1842 Droste-Hülshoff wrote about fifty poems while staying at her brother-in-law's castle at Meersburg on Lake Constance. She was in her mid-forties. Her first book, in 1838, had sold only 74 of its 500 copies. The large collection that the publisher Cotta issued in September 1844 did well, and the profits helped her buy a small vine-covered villa in Meersburg.
