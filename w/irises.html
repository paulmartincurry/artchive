Irises

Ogata Kōrin · ca. 1701–1705 · Ink and colour on paper, with gold leaf · Nezu Museum, Tokyo

Some of the blooms on this pair of six-panel screens are so alike that a stencil was evidently used. The blue is powdered azurite, and the gold leaf was laid in squares around the painted areas. The screens were probably made for the Nijō family and were kept for over 200 years at the Nishi Honganji temple in Kyoto, which sold them in 1913. Ogata Kōrin painted a second pair of iris screens about 5 to 12 years later.
