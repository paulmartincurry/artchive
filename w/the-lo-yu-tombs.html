With twilight shadows in my heart
I have driven up among the Lo-yu Tombs
To see the sun, for all his glory,
Buried by the coming night.

The Lo-yu Tombs

Li Shangyin · before 858; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Li Shangyin lived from about 813 to 858, in the late Tang dynasty, when the court was weakened by the growing power of the eunuchs and by the feud between the Niu and Li factions. He was born in the Henei Commandery, now Qinyang in Henan.
