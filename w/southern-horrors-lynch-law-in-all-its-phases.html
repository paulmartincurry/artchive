Southern Horrors: Lynch Law in All Its Phases

Ida B. Wells · 1892

In March 1892 a mob took three Black men from the Shelby County Jail in Memphis and shot them. One of them, the grocer Thomas Moss, was a friend of Ida B. Wells, who was godmother to his first child. She was then editor and co-owner of the Memphis newspaper Free Speech and Headlight. A white mob destroyed the paper's office and presses, and she left Memphis for Chicago.
