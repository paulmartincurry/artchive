My soul, like to a ship in a black storm,
Is driven, I know not whither.

The White Devil — My soul, like to a ship in a black storm (Act V, Scene VI)

John Webster · 1612

The play was written for Queen Anne's Men and first performed at the Red Bull Theatre in Clerkenwell in the early months of 1612. According to Webster's own preface, that first performance was a notorious failure. He complained that it was acted in the dead of winter before an unreceptive audience.
