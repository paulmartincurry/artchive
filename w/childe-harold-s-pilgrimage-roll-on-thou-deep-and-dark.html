Roll on, thou deep and dark blue Ocean--roll!
Ten thousand fleets sweep over thee in vain;
Man marks the earth with ruin--his control
Stops with the shore;

Childe Harold's Pilgrimage — Roll On, Thou Deep and Dark Blue Ocean (Canto IV)

Lord Byron · 1818

These stanzas come from the fourth and last canto of Childe Harold's Pilgrimage, which Byron published in 1818. The first two cantos had come out on 3 March 1812; their first run of 500 quarto copies sold out in three days, and there were ten editions within three years. Byron was by then living in Italy, where he stayed for seven years.
