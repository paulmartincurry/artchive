The Return of the Prodigal Son

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1668 · Oil on canvas · Hermitage Museum

Free to use

Rembrandt came back to this parable for decades, in drawings, etchings and paintings, starting with an etching of 1636. This canvas, more than two and a half metres tall, is among the last things he made, probably finished within two years of his death in 1669. Some of the onlookers have no part in the parable and are carried over from those earlier works.
