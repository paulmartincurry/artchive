Queen Sembiyan Mahadevi as the Goddess Uma

Maker unknown · 10th century · Bronze · 107.3 × 33.4 × 25.7 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Sembiyan Mahadevi was widowed young and gave most of her life to temple commissions. A metal portrait of her was presented to the Shiva temple in the town named after her, where special ceremonies marked her birthday, and the Freer says it is possible this is that sculpture. The dealer Hagop Kevorkian kept it in a safety deposit vault in Paris, shipped it to New York on the S.S. Paris and sold it to the Freer in 1929.
