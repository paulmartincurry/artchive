Muisca Raft

Maker unknown · ca. 1200–1500 · Gold · Gold Museum, Bogotá

Free to use with credit. Photo: Pedro Szekely, CC BY-SA 2.0

The raft is 19.5 centimetres long and was cast in one pour from a single mould, after a model built from sheets and threads of beeswax as thin as a millimetre. A fracture during casting left part of the base as a solid sheet where openwork was intended. Nobody corrected it or polished the surface, and charcoal from the mould is still in the crevices. The metal is an alloy of gold with silver and copper. It was found in 1969 in a cave at Pasca, as part of an offering stored in a ceramic vessel.
