Alfred Stieglitz (Camera Work 21, plate VI)

Alvin Langdon Coburn · 1908 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Coburn made this profile of the editor of Camera Work, and it was printed as plate VI of issue 21 in January 1908 by the J. J. Waddington Company in London. Twelve of Coburn's photographs ran in that issue, beside an anonymous article that attacked him. The author was probably Stieglitz himself.
