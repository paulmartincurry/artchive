The Children of Heaven and Earth (the separation)

Māori tradition (Aotearoa) · Printed 1855

Translated by Sir George Grey, 1855

Sir George Grey, twice Governor of New Zealand, learned Māori to fluency and gathered Māori mythology and oral history, which he published in translation in London. In this tradition Rangi and Papa, sky father and earth mother, had more than 500 children, among them Tāne, Tangaroa and Tāwhirimātea.
