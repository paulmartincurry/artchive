Rustam Captures the Shah of Sham and the Shah of Berber (Shahnama)

Artist unknown; text by Abu'l Qasim Firdausi · ca. 1330–40 · Ink, opaque watercolor, gold, and silver on paper · The Met

The painting on this page is only 4.7 centimetres high and 10.8 wide, on a leaf of 20.5 by 13.3 centimetres. The manuscript was probably made in Isfahan, more than three hundred years after the death of Firdausi, who wrote the poem. The folio came to the Met in 1974 as a bequest of Monroe C. Gutman.
