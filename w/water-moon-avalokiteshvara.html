Water-Moon Avalokiteshvara

Maker unknown · first half of the 14th century · Hanging scroll, ink and color on silk · 114.5 × 55.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painted silk is about 1.1 metres tall, and with its mounting the scroll reaches two metres. It was painted in Korea in the first half of the fourteenth century, under the Goryeo dynasty. The Met received it in 1914 from the family of the collector Charles Stewart Smith, in his memory.
