I have spread my dreams under your feet;
Tread softly because you tread on my dreams.

He Wishes for the Cloths of Heaven

W. B. Yeats · 1899

The poem was first called Aedh Wishes for the Cloths of Heaven, after Aedh, one of three recurring characters in Yeats's work; later printings changed the name to He. It appeared in 1899 in The Wind Among the Reeds, his third volume of poetry. Its last two lines are on a blue plaque at Balscadden House in Howth, near Dublin, the cottage where he lived from 1880 to 1883.
