Where are the tidings of union? that I may arise—
Forth from the dust I will rise up to welcome thee!
My soul, like a homing bird, yearning for Paradise,
Shall arise and soar, from the snares of the world set free.

Where Are the Tidings of Union?

Hafez of Shiraz · 14th century

Translated by Gertrude Bell

A tomb was built for Hafez in the Musalla Gardens in Shiraz twenty years after his death. The mausoleum that stands there now was designed in the late 1930s by the French architect André Godard, and the alabaster sarcophagus inside is inscribed with two of his poems. Iranian families open his Divan at a random page at Nowruz and on Yalda Night and read the poem they find. William Jones made the first English translation of his work in 1771.
