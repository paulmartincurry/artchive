Thence I walked to the Tower; but, Lord! how empty the streets are and melancholy, so many poor sick people in the streets full of sores; and so many sad stories overheard as I walk, every body talking of this dead, and that man sick, and so many in this place, and so many in that.

Diary, 16 October 1665: The Plague

Samuel Pepys · 1665

In early 1665 the start of the Second Anglo-Dutch War put great pressure on Pepys at the Navy Board, and in October 1665, the month of this entry, he was made surveyor-general of victualling. King Charles II, his family and his court had left London for Salisbury and moved on to Oxford in September.
