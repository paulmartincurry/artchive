Almansor (Where They Burn Books)

Heinrich Heine · 1821

Heine wrote this tragedy while he was a law student; he had gone to the University of Bonn in 1819, after his uncle, a millionaire banker in Hamburg, realised he had no talent for trade. It had little success in the theatre. His radical political views later led to many of his works being banned by German authorities, and he spent the last 25 years of his life as an expatriate in Paris.
