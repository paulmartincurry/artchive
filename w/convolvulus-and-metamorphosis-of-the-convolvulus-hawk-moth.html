Convolvulus and Metamorphosis of the Convolvulus Hawk Moth

Maria Sibylla Merian · ca. 1670–90 · Watercolor with touches of opaque watercolor over indications in black chalk or graphite on vellum · Support: 41 × 54.7 × 0.2 cm (16 1/8 × 21 9/16 × 1/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The sheet follows the European pink-spotted hawk moth from egg through caterpillar and pupa to adult, beside the morning glory it feeds on; Maria Sibylla Merian added caterpillars of other species to fill it out. She reared caterpillars in her own home for ten years before she wrote and illustrated a book on metamorphosis. In 1670 she moved to Nuremberg, where she painted on parchment and designed embroidery. The drawing sold at Sotheby's in London in 1999 and came to Cleveland in 2019.
