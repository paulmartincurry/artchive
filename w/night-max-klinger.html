Night (On Death, Part I, Plate 1)

Max Klinger · 1889 · Etching and aquatint · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Night is the first of the ten plates in On Death, Part One, a portfolio that one critic of the time called a modern Dance of Death. In 1889, the year the museum gives for it, Klinger was invited to show with Les XX in Brussels and moved to Rome, where he stayed until 1893. The Print Club of Cleveland gave this impression to the museum in 1933.
