Discomedusae (Plate 8, Kunstformen der Natur)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Lithograph

Free to use

Ernst Haeckel was a biologist, and many of the organisms in the book were first described by him. The lithographer Adolf Giltsch turned his sketches and watercolors into prints, which were issued in sets of ten between 1899 and 1904, a hundred in all. The first set included a jellyfish Haeckel had observed and described shortly after the death of his wife, Anna Sethe.
