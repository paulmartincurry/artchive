If ever two were one then surely we,
If ever man were loved by wife, then thee;

To My Dear and Loving Husband

Anne Bradstreet · ca. 1660 (pub. 1678)

Simon Bradstreet married Anne Dudley when she was sixteen, and in 1630 the two sailed to Massachusetts aboard the Arbella. She had eight children there, and both her husband and her father later served as governors of the colony. The poem was printed in 1678, six years after her death, in a collection she had revised herself.
