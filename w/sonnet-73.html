That time of year thou mayst in me behold
When yellow leaves, or none, or few, do hang
Upon those boughs which shake against the cold,
Bare ruin’d choirs, where late the sweet birds sang.

Sonnet 73

William Shakespeare · 1609

The fire in the third quatrain has often been traced to Geoffrey Whitney's A Choice of Emblemes, a book of 1586 that shows a down-turned torch with the Latin motto Qui me alit me extinguit. The same motto is carried on a shield in a scene of Shakespeare's play Pericles. Almost all of the sonnet's lines keep to iambic pentameter without variation.
