This country holds your father's body. Never sell the bones of your father and your mother.

Dying Words to His Son

Old Chief Joseph (Tuekakas) · 1871

Tuekakas led the Wallowa band of the Nez Perce and was among the first of his people to convert to Christianity. In 1863, after a gold rush, the federal government cut the Nez Perce reservation to a tenth of its size. He refused to sign that treaty, slashed his American flag, tore up his Bible and would not move his band out of the Wallowa Valley. He died in 1871, aged about eighty-six; his gravestone gives the wrong year. The son he spoke to became known as Chief Joseph.
