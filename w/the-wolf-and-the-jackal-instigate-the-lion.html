The Wolf and the Jackal Instigate the Lion (Tuti-nama, Twenty-first Night)

Maker unknown · c. 1560 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · 20.3 x 14 cm (8 x 5 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A lion waits below the treed Brahman, egged on by his viziers the wolf and the jackal. The lion's other viziers, the deer and the gazelle, had told him to be kind, and they come back to save the Brahman.
