The Seven Deadly Sins and the Four Last Things

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1500 · Oil on wood · 120 × 150 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Philip II of Spain bought this painting before 1560, believing it was by Bosch. It was his favourite Bosch and hung in his own bedroom at El Escorial until he gave it to the monastery in 1574. In 2015 the Bosch Research and Conservation Project assigned it to a follower; the Prado, which shows it in a sealed case, still holds it to be authentic.
