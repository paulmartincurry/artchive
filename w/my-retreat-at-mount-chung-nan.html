I will walk till the water checks my path,
Then sit and watch the rising clouds

My Retreat at Mount Chung-nan

Wang Wei · 8th century; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Wang Wei came back to Chang'an from a posting on the northwestern frontier in 738 or early 739. With no official post to go to, he spent the time exploring the Zhongnan Mountains south of the capital. His wife had died in 731 and he never remarried. Later in life he became a Chan Buddhist and a vegetarian, and he studied for ten years with the Chan master Daoguang.
