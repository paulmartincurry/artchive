Ely Cathedral: Galilee Porch, Door into Nave

Frederick H. Evans · c. 1891 · Lantern slide · 8.2 × 8.2 cm (3 1/4 × 3 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is a lantern slide a little over eight centimetres square. Frederick H. Evans was still working as a bookseller when he took it, and he did not become a full-time photographer until 1898. He gave up photography altogether by 1915, after the price of platinum paper had risen.
