Sheikh Lotfollah Mosque

Muhammad Reza Isfahani · 1603–1619 · Stone, brick, mortar, tile

Free to use with credit. Photo: Nicolas Hadjisavvas, CC BY 2.5

Shah Abbas built this mosque for the private use of the royal court, which is why it is small and has no minarets. A tunnel under the square once linked it to the Ali Qapu Palace. The square runs north to south and Mecca lies to the south-west, so the architect joined the entrance to the prayer hall with an L-shaped vestibule, and from the square the dome sits 6.5 metres off the axis of the portal. His name, Muhammad Reza, son of Husayn, is on an inscription around the mihrab.
