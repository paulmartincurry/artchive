She Never Told Her Love

Henry Peach Robinson · 1857 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 18 × 23.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Henry Peach Robinson was apprenticed to the bookseller and printer Richard Jones, and in 1850 and 1851 he was working for booksellers in Bromsgrove and London. He made this photograph in 1857, when he was 26 or 27.
