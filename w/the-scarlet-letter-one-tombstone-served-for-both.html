The Artchive
It bore a device, a herald’s wording of which might serve for a motto and brief description of our now concluded legend; so sombre is it, and relieved only by one ever-glowing point of light gloomier than the shadow:—

“ON A FIELD, SABLE, THE LETTER A, GULES.”

The Scarlet Letter — one tombstone served for both (Ch. XXIV, close)

Nathaniel Hawthorne · 1850

Hawthorne wrote the manuscript at the Peter Edgerley House in Salem, Massachusetts, and delivered the final pages to his publisher in February 1850. The first mechanized printing, 2,500 volumes, sold out within ten days.
