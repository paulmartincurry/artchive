We carry with us the wonders we seek without us: there is all Africa and her prodigies in us. We are that bold and adventurous piece of nature, which he that studies wisely learns, in a compendium, what others labour at in a divided piece and endless volume.

Religio Medici — The wonders we seek without us

Sir Thomas Browne · 1643

Religio Medici was published in 1643, after an unauthorised version had been passed around the year before. It was reprinted about eight times in Browne's lifetime and translated into Latin, French, German, Dutch and Italian. He had taken his medical degree at Leiden in 1633, after studying at Padua and Montpellier, and settled in Norwich in 1637.
