And so I betake myself to that course, which is almost as much as to see myself go into my grave: for which, and all the discomforts that will accompany my being blind, the good God prepare me!

Diary, 31 May 1669: The Last Entry

Samuel Pepys · 1669

Pepys stopped writing the diary in 1669. His eyesight had begun to trouble him, and he feared that writing in dim light was damaging his eyes. He lived another 34 years without going blind, but he never took to writing his diary again, though he dictated a journal for two months in 1669–70.
