The Archangel Michael

Guido Reni · completed 1636 · Oil on canvas · Santa Maria della Concezione dei Cappuccini, Rome

Free to use

The Barberini family of Pope Urban VIII commissioned this painting from Reni in 1630 for the Capuchin church of Santa Maria della Concezione in Rome, and it was completed in 1636. An old legend holds that Reni gave Satan, crushed under the archangel's foot, the face of Cardinal Giovanni Battista Pamphilj in revenge for a slight. According to his biographer Malvasia, Reni was a compulsive gambler, often in financial distress.
