As when about the silver moon, when air is free from wind,
And stars shine clear, to whose sweet beams, high prospects, and the brows
Of all steep hills and pinnacles, thrust up themselves for shows,
And ev’n the lowly valleys joy to glitter in their sight,

The Iliad — As when about the silver moon (Book VIII, Chapman's translation)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by George Chapman

Chapman published his translation of the Iliad in instalments from 1598. In 1616 the complete Iliad and Odyssey appeared in The Whole Works of Homer, the first complete English translation.
