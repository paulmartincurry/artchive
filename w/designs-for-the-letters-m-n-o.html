Designs for the Letters M, N, O

Jean Baptiste Piauger · 1767 · Etching and engraving on laid paper · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

This plate of six letter designs, set two to a row, was published in Jean Henri Prosper Pouget's Dictionnaire des chifres, part of a series of decorative letters. It is numbered 8 in the plate. Pouget died in 1769, two years after the date it carries.
