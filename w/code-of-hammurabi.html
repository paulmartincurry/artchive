Code of Hammurabi

Maker unknown · ca. 1755–1750 BCE · Black basalt · Louvre

Free to use with credit. Photo: Mbzt, CC BY 3.0

The stele stands 2.25 metres tall and carries about 4,130 lines of cuneiform, compiled near the end of Hammurabi's reign. Some 600 years after it was made it was carried off as plunder to Susa, in present-day Iran. Seven columns of laws near the bottom were polished away in antiquity. French excavators found it there in three large pieces between December 1901 and January 1902 and put it back together.
