The Voyage of the Beagle (closing chapter)

Charles Darwin · 1839; revised 1845

The book grew from the diary Darwin kept on HMS Beagle, written in ink on board ship and worked up from pencil notes made in pocket books on land. He was twenty-two when the ship sailed, on a voyage planned for two years that lasted almost five. His account first appeared in 1839 as the third volume of the official narrative, titled Journal and Remarks. The title The Voyage of the Beagle dates from a republication in 1905.
