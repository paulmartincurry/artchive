Necklace (amazipho)

Zulu-style maker · 1800s · Glass beads, bone, and sinew · 38.1 cm (15 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Real lion claws marked royalty, and bone copies like these were worn by high-ranking people of lesser status. Large red glass beads were kept for the Nguni elite until the Zulu kingdom was destroyed in 1879.
