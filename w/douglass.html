Ah, Douglass, we have fall'n on evil days,
Such days as thou, not even thou didst know,

Douglass

Paul Laurence Dunbar · 1903

Through his poetry Dunbar came to know Frederick Douglass and Booker T. Washington. On March 5, 1897, he took part in a meeting to celebrate Douglass's memory, at which the American Negro Academy was founded. Dunbar's own father had escaped slavery in Kentucky and served in the 55th Massachusetts Infantry Regiment.
