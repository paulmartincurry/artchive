Corner of Kitchen in Floyd Burroughs' Cabin, Hale County, Alabama

Walker Evans · Summer 1936 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In the summer of 1936 Walker Evans and the writer James Agee spent eight weeks among three sharecropping families in Hale County, Alabama, on an assignment for Fortune magazine. Fortune rejected the piece, and the pictures appeared in 1941 in the book Let Us Now Praise Famous Men. The Library rated the negative's deterioration at level 4 around 1992.
