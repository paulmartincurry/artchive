Sugawara no Michizane (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 32.5 × 20 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Sugawara no Michizane stands beside a tree under a full moon, writing poetry, and the verse in the cartouche is his own. Michizane was a scholar, poet and politician of the Heian period. In 901 he was demoted to a minor post at Dazaifu, where he and his family were banished, and he died in exile in 903.
