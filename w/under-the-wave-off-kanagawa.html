Under the Wave off Kanagawa (The Great Wave)

Katsushika Hokusai · ca. 1830–32 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 10 1/8 × 14 15/16 in. (25.7 × 37.9 cm) · The Met

Free to use

About 1,000 copies of this print were made at first and an estimated 8,000 in the end, and the later ones show the wear. 113 impressions of the first edition are known to survive. Hokusai was about seventy when he arrived at the final design in late 1831, after several years of work and other drawings. The dark blue is Prussian blue, a pigment imported from Holland that reached Japan in large quantities in 1829.
