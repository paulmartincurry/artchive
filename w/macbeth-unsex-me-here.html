Come, you spirits
That tend on mortal thoughts, unsex me here,
And fill me, from the crown to the toe, top-full
Of direst cruelty!

Macbeth — Unsex Me Here (Act I, Scene V)

William Shakespeare · 1606

Macbeth was first published in the Folio of 1623 and is Shakespeare's shortest tragedy. The only eyewitness account from his lifetime is Simon Forman's, from a performance at the Globe on 20 April 1610. Samuel Pepys saw the play at least six times between November 1664 and November 1667.
