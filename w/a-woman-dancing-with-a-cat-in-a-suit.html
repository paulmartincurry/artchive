A Woman Dancing with a Cat in a Suit

José Guadalupe Posada · ca. 1880–1910 · Zincograph · Sheet: 4 5/16 × 6 7/8 in. (11 × 17.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

At the end of 1888 Posada moved from León to Mexico City, where he learned to engrave in lead and zinc. He went on to work for the publisher Antonio Vanegas Arroyo, turning out book covers and illustrations in large numbers. The image on this small sheet is about 13 centimetres wide. The print was given to the museum by Roberto Berdecio in 1960.
