The Garden of Earthly Delights

Hieronymus Bosch · ca. 1495–1505 · Oil on panel · Museo del Prado

The oak of the panels has been dated by its tree rings to between 1460 and 1466. The triptych was first documented in 1517, one year after Bosch's death, in the palace of the Counts of Nassau in Brussels. The Duke of Alba confiscated it in 1568 and brought it to Spain. Philip II bought it at auction in 1591 and two years later gave it to El Escorial, where it stayed for 342 years before it moved to the Prado in 1939. With the wings closed, the outer panels show a design in green-grey grisaille.
