So we never live, but we hope to live; and, as we are always preparing to be happy, it is inevitable we should never be so.

Pensées — We never live, but we hope to live (172)

Blaise Pascal · 1670

Translated by W. F. Trotter

The fragments were notes for a defence of Christianity that Pascal never completed. The planned work is often called the Apology for the Christian Religion, a title he never used.
