I Laïs who laughed exultant over Greece, I who held that swarm of young lovers in my porches, give my mirror to the Paphian; since such as I am I will not see myself, and such as I was I cannot.

To Aphrodite, by Laïs

Plato · before 347 BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail, 1890

The epigram is one of those the Greek Anthology gives to Plato, an attribution Mackail treats with caution. Most of the Anthology survives in two manuscripts, the Palatine Anthology of the 10th century and the Planudean of the 14th.
