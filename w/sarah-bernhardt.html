Sarah Bernhardt

Nadar · 1864 · Gelatin silver print · 21.1 × 16.2 cm · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Sarah Bernhardt was about twenty when she posed for Nadar, and had barely begun her career. Nadar was preoccupied with ballooning at the time and willing to leave most portrait work to his studio assistants. He wrapped her in a sweep of velvet that bared one shoulder. This print carries the stamp of his son Paul Nadar on the back of its mount.
