The Temptation of Saint Anthony

Martin Schongauer · c. 1469/73 · Engraving in black on cream laid paper · Plate/image: 29.9 × 22.9 cm (11 13/16 × 9 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

According to Giorgio Vasari, Michelangelo made a copy of this engraving. Martin Schongauer was the son of a goldsmith in Colmar, and his father probably taught him to engrave, a skill goldsmiths used on metal vessels. He had set up his own workshop in Colmar by 1471. The plate is just under 30 centimetres tall. The impression was given to the Art Institute by Mrs. Potter Palmer, Jr.
