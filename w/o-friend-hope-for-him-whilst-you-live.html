O friend! hope for Him whilst you live, know whilst you live, understand whilst you live: for in life deliverance abides.
If your bonds be not broken whilst living, what hope of deliverance in death?

O Friend, Hope for Him Whilst You Live

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

Kabir lived in Varanasi in the fifteenth century, though his exact dates are uncertain: some historians place his life between 1398 and 1448, others between 1440 and 1518. A few accounts say a Muslim weaver named Niru and his wife Nima found him as an infant at Lahartara Lake and raised him. His poems were composed aloud and passed on by word of mouth until the first written collection, the Kabir Bijak, was made in the seventeenth century.
