Boy on a Ram

Francisco de Goya · 1786–87 · Oil on canvas · 127.2 × 112.1 cm (50 1/16 × 44 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is a full-size cartoon, painted as a guide for the weavers at the royal tapestry factory of Santa Bárbara. The tapestry woven from it went above a door in the dining room of the palace of El Pardo, outside Madrid. Goya was forty and had just been given a salaried post as painter to Charles III. The cartoon was still with the factory's director in 1887 and was sold to Knoedler in 1911.
