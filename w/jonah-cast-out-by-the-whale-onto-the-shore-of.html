Jonah Cast Out by the Whale onto the Shore of Nineveh

Maarten van Heemskerck · 1566 · Pen and brown ink over indications in black chalk, within brown ink framing lines; indented for transfer · 19.6 x 25 cm (7 11/16 x 9 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Jonah floats in midair as the giant fish throws him onto dry land. It is the second of four drawings for prints of the story; the fish is neither shark nor whale but a creature of Heemskerck's own invention.
