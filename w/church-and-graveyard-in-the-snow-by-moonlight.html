Church and Graveyard in the Snow by Moonlight

Carl Blechen · 1827 · Lithograph, with gray tone block on cream wove paper · Image: 41.4 × 35.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Blechen made this lithograph in 1827 and printed it with a grey tone block. That year he joined the Berlin Artists' Association and lost his post as a decorator at the Royal Theater on the Alexanderplatz, dismissed over a running dispute with the singer Henriette Sontag. Afterwards he tried to support himself as a free-lance artist. The image is about 41 centimetres high.
