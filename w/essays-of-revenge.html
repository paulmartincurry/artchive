Revenge is a kind of wild justice; …
in taking revenge, a man is but even with his enemy.

Essays — Of Revenge

Francis Bacon · 1597 / 1625

In 1597 Francis Bacon was thirty-six, a lawyer of Gray's Inn and a member of Parliament who had just been made the Queen's legal counsel. The next year he was arrested for debt. By 1625 he had been Attorney General and Lord Chancellor of England, had been found guilty on corruption charges, briefly imprisoned and barred from public office. He died the year after, aged sixty-five.
