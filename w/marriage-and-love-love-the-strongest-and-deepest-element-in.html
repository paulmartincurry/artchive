Love, the strongest and deepest element in all life, the harbinger of hope, of joy, of ecstasy; love, the defier of all laws, of all conventions; love, the freest, the most powerful moulder of human destiny; how can such an all-compelling force be synonymous with that poor little State and Church-begotten weed, marriage?

Marriage and Love — Love, the Strongest and Deepest Element in All Life

Emma Goldman · 1910

The essays grew out of lectures Goldman gave on fundraising tours for her journal Mother Earth, which she founded in 1906. This was her first published book, issued by the Mother Earth Publishing Association.
