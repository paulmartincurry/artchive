Church Street El

Charles Sheeler · 1920 · Oil on canvas · 41 × 48.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Charles Sheeler based this painting on an image from Manhatta, the short film he made with the photographer Paul Strand in 1920 and 1921. It looks down on Broadway and Wall Street, with the Church Street elevated train at the right. He signed and dated it 1920. The Philadelphia collector Earl Horter owned it by 1930.
