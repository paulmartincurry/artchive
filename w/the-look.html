Strephon's kiss was lost in jest,
Robin's lost in play,
But the kiss in Colin's eyes
Haunts me night and day.

The Look

Sara Teasdale · 1915

Teasdale married Ernst Filsinger, a longtime admirer of her poetry, on 19 December 1914, after three years in which several men had courted her, the poet Vachel Lindsay among them. Her third collection, Rivers to the Sea, came out the following year, when she was thirty. It was reprinted several times.
