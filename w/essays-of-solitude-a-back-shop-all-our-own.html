Essays — Of Solitude: A back shop all our own (Book I, Ch. 38)

Michel de Montaigne · 1580

Translated by Charles Cotton

By 1571 Montaigne had given up his post as a magistrate in Bordeaux and retired to the tower of his château, where his library held some 1,500 volumes. On his thirty-eighth birthday he had an inscription put up on the bookshelves of his work chamber recording his retirement. Not long before, a rider had collided with him at full speed and thrown him from his horse, and his recovery took weeks or months. The tower still stands.
