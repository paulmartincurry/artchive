No coward soul is mine,
No trembler in the world's storm-troubled sphere.

No Coward Soul Is Mine

Emily Brontë · January 2, 1846

On 2 January 1846 Emily Brontë was twenty-seven. She spent most of her life at home in Haworth, where her father was curate, helping the family's servant with the cooking, ironing and cleaning. She taught herself German from books and became an accomplished pianist. Her poems first appeared under the pen name Ellis Bell, in a book shared with her sisters Charlotte and Anne.
