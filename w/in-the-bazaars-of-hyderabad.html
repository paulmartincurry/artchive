Crowns for the brow of a bridegroom,
Chaplets to garland his bed,
Sheets of white blossoms new-gathered
To perfume the sleep of the dead.

In the Bazaars of Hyderabad

Sarojini Naidu · 1912

Naidu was born in Hyderabad and wrote this poem from memories of her childhood there. It was published in 1912 in The Bird of Time, her second book, which came out in both London and New York, and it runs to thirty lines in five stanzas. She was thirty-three and already active in the Indian independence movement, which left her little time for poetry. The New York Times printed the first stanza on 27 April 1913.
