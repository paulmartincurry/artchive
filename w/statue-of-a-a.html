Statue of A'a

Maker unknown · Late 16th – Mid-17th Century · Sandalwood · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: Paul Hudson, CC BY 2.0

The people of Rurutu gave this figure to the London Missionary Society on 9 August 1821 to mark their conversion to Christianity. It is hollow, with a removable panel in the back, and 24 small gods were inside it when it was handed over. Thirty smaller figures are carved on its surface. Radiocarbon dating in 2015 placed its making between 1591 and 1647, and tests the same year found the wood to be sandalwood. The British Museum acquired it in 1911. Picasso and Henry Moore each owned a copy.
