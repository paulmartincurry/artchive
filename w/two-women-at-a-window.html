Two Women at a Window

Bartolomé Esteban Murillo · c. 1655/1660 · Oil on canvas · 125.1 × 104.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The earliest owner the museum records is the Duque de Almodóvar del Rio in Madrid. His heirs sold the canvas in 1823 to an Englishman, William A'Court, and Peter A. B. Widener bought it on 3 December 1894.
