The Comet of 1811, as Seen at Day Break, the 15th October, from Otterbourne Hill, near Winchester

H. R. Cook, after Pether · published 1820 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

The lettering says the comet is shown as it appeared at daybreak on 15 October 1811 from Otterbourne Hill, near Winchester, engraved by H. R. Cook from a drawing by Pether. October was the month it was brightest, and it stayed visible to the naked eye for around 260 days. R. N. Rose published the print in London on 1 May 1820. The wine of 1811 was sold for years afterwards as Comet Wine.
