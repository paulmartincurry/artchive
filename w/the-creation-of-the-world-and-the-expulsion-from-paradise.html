The Creation of the World and the Expulsion from Paradise

Giovanni di Paolo · 1445 · Tempera and gold on wood · Overall: 18 1/4 × 20 1/2 in. (46.4 × 52.1 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The panel is about 46 by 52 centimetres. Giovanni di Paolo spent nearly his whole career in Siena, where he is first recorded in 1417 illuminating manuscripts for the Dominicans, and most of his commissions came from local monastic communities. He is thought to have kept a model book of other artists' work, and to have borrowed figures from it into his own paintings. The panel came to the museum in 1975 with the Robert Lehman Collection.
