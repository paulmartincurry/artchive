A Spirit fallen from the realms of light
Above this dim world winged his weary flight,

The Demon

Mikhail Lermontov · 1839 / 1842

Translated by Francis Storr, 1894

Lermontov was first taken to the Caucasus as a small boy, in 1819 and 1820 and again in 1825, for his health and the mineral springs. As an officer of the Hussars he was exiled there twice, the first time for a poem on the death of Pushkin. He was killed in a duel with a fellow officer at the foot of Mashuk mountain on 27 July 1841, aged twenty-six. Many of his verses were found in his notebooks after his death.
