But he whom we carry to the grave
Will never more return.

Burial Song — The Dew on the Garlic-Leaf

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · Han dynasty (206 BC–220 AD)

Translated by Arthur Waley

This song was sung at the burial of kings and princes in Han China. Han poetry was brushed in ink on silk, hemp paper and bamboo slips tied with cord, and when the cords rotted the slips fell out of order. Much of it survives only because anthologies compiled centuries later, in the Six Dynasties period, kept it.
