He comes, a moon whose like the sky ne’er saw, awake or dreaming, Crowned with eternal flame no flood can lay.
Lo, from the flagon of Thy love, O Lord, my soul is swimming, And ruined all my body’s house of clay.

He comes, a moon

Jalāl al-Dīn Muḥammad Rūmī · 13th century

Translated by R. A. Nicholson, 1914
