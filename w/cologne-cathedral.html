Cologne Cathedral

Maker unknown · 1248–1560 and 1842–80 · L. 144.5 m; spires 157 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: Thomas Wolf (www.foto-tw.de), CC BY-SA 3.0 DE

Building began in 1248 and stopped in 1473, with the south tower finished only to the belfry. A huge crane stayed on top of that tower for four hundred years. Work on the nave resumed in 1814, and the cathedral was completed to its medieval plan in 1880. The builders had planned it to house the reliquary of the Three Kings. The spires rise 157 metres.
