Vitrioleuse, from L'Estampe Originale

Eugène Grasset · 1894 · Lithograph, hand-stenciled in five colors · 52.4 × 38.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The print is a lithograph with five colours stencilled on by hand. André Marty published it in the sixth album of L'Estampe Originale, and Auguste Delâtre printed it. Grasset had come to Paris from Lausanne and was an admirer of Japanese art. The Metropolitan Museum bought its set of the albums in 1922.
