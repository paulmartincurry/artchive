Heart of Darkness — The last word he pronounced (Part III)

Joseph Conrad · 1899

Researchers have proposed more than twenty real people as models for Kurtz. The historian Adam Hochschild names Léon Rom, an administrator in the Congo Free State, pointing among other things to the heads on stakes outside the station. Conrad's biographer Norman Sherry judged the main model to be Arthur Hodister, a Belgian trader who spoke three Congolese languages, though later scholars have refuted that.
