The Ice Was All Around (The Rime of the Ancient Mariner)

Gustave Doré · 1876 · Engraving

Free to use

Doré made this for an 1876 edition of Coleridge's poem. As a young man the horror writer H. P. Lovecraft drew inspiration from Doré's illustrations to the Rime of the Ancient Mariner.
