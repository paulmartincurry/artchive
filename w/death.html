Death (Visconti-Sforza Tarot)

Bonifacio Bembo · c. 1455 · Paint and gold on heavy cardboard · 17.5 × 8.7 cm · The Morgan Library & Museum, New York

Free to use

Bonifacio Bembo and his workshop painted the 78 cards of this deck for Francesco Sforza and Bianca Maria Visconti, probably around 1455. Each was painted on heavy cardboard and measures 175 by 87 millimetres. Four have been lost, among them the Devil and the Tower. The Morgan Library bought 35 of the cards in 1911 and keeps them in transparent envelopes so they can be examined without handling.
