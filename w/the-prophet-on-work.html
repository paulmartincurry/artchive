Work is love made visible.

And if you cannot work with love but only with distaste, it is better that you should leave your work and sit at the gate of the temple and take alms of those who work with joy.

The Prophet — On Work

Kahlil Gibran · 1923

With the financial help of Mary Haskell, Gibran studied art in Paris from 1908 to 1910. In 1911 he settled in New York, where Alfred A. Knopf published The Madman, his first book in English, in 1918, while the writing of The Prophet was under way. His drawings had first been shown in 1904, at the Boston studio of the photographer F. Holland Day.
