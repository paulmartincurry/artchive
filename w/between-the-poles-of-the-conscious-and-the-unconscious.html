Between the poles of the conscious and the unconscious, there has the mind made a swing:
Thereon hang all beings and all worlds, and that swing never ceases its sway.

Between the Poles of the Conscious and the Unconscious

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

Kabir composed in Sadhukkadi, a language that borrows from several dialects, among them Khadi boli, Braj, Bhojpuri, Marwari and Awadhi. In his lifetime he was threatened by both Hindus and Muslims for his views, and when he died both claimed him as their own. His verses are part of the Sikh scripture, the Guru Granth Sahib.
