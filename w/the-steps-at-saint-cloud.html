The Steps at Saint-Cloud

Eugène Atget · 1906 · Albumen silver print · overall 22 × 17.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The negative number 6606 is written in the upper left of the negative, and St. Cloud is pencilled on the back of the print. Atget became a professional photographer in Paris in 1890, supplying documents for artists: studies for painters, architects and stage designers. This albumen print is 22 centimetres high.
