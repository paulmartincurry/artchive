Elephant Battery, Jhansi

Raja Deen Dayal · 1887 · Albumen print · Image: 18.2 x 26.5 cm (7 3/16 x 10 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Elephants had been used in war in India since at least the 500s BC. With heavy artillery they moved from attack to support, hauling guns and supplies for the British Indian Army.
