Federico da Montefeltro (Diptych of Federico da Montefeltro and Battista Sforza)

Piero della Francesca · c. 1473–1475 · Oil on wood · Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

Battista Sforza died in 1472, and Piero probably painted this portrait of her husband and its pair soon after her death; Federico da Montefeltro was not made duke until 1474. On the back of each panel the sitter rides in triumph on an ancient wagon, with Latin inscriptions praising the couple. Piero was a frequent guest at the Montefeltro court in Urbino.
