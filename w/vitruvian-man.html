Vitruvian Man

Leonardo da Vinci · 1490 · Pen and ink on paper · Gallerie dell'Accademia

Free to use

Leonardo drew this in Milan on a sheet larger than most of his manuscript pages, ruling it with metalpoint, calipers and compass before going over it in pen. The text above the figure is in mirror writing. Earlier artists had centred both the circle and the square on the navel; he made the scheme work by centring the square on the genitals. The Gallerie dell'Accademia in Venice has owned the drawing since 1822 and keeps it in a locked room on the fourth floor, because light would fade it. A lawsuit tried to stop its loan to the Louvre in 2019.
