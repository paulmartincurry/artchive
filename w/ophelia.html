Ophelia

John Everett Millais · 1851–1852 · Oil on canvas · Tate

Millais painted the riverbank first, sitting beside the Hogsmill River in Surrey for up to eleven hours a day, six days a week, for five months in 1851. When snow came in November he had a straw-covered hut built to work in. The figure came second: Elizabeth Siddall, aged 22, lay clothed in a full bathtub in his London studio, and when the oil lamps warming the water went out she caught a severe cold. Her father demanded £50 for medical expenses. A dealer bought the picture for 300 guineas.
