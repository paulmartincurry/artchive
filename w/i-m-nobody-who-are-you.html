I'm nobody! Who are you?
Are you nobody, too?

I'm Nobody! Who are you?

Emily Dickinson · c. 1861 (published 1891)

The poem was first printed in 1891, five years after Dickinson's death, in Poems, Series 2, assembled and edited by Mabel Loomis Todd and Thomas Wentworth Higginson. The same two editors had brought out the first selection in 1890, with the poems significantly altered to fit the poetic conventions of the time. A complete collection closer to what she wrote did not appear until Thomas H. Johnson's in 1955.
