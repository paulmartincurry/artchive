Pendant: Face

Baule-style goldsmith · 1900s · Gold · 7.6 x 5 cm (3 x 1 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Gold objects are the only Baule art linked with ancestral spirits, kept in suitcases or pots and laid out around the body at funerals. Faces like this are said to be idealised portraits of friends or lovers.
