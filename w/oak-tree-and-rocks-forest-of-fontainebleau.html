Oak Tree and Rocks, Forest of Fontainebleau

Gustave Le Gray · 1849–52 · Salted paper print from paper negative · 25.2 × 35.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Le Gray trained as a painter under François-Édouard Picot and Paul Delaroche, and scenes of Fontainebleau Forest were among his early photographs. He was an important contributor to the development of the wax paper negative. This print from a paper negative measures 25 by 36 centimetres.
