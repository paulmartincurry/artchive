Flowers of a Hundred Worlds (Momoyogusa): River in Winter

Kamisaka Sekka · 1909–10 · color woodcuts with gold and silver · Sheet: 29.9 x 22.1 cm (11 3/4 x 8 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The print is from Sekka's three albums of nature and literature, first sent one sheet at a time to subscribers and later bound and reissued. His choices follow the Rinpa style founded by Tawaraya Sōtatsu and Hon'ami Kōetsu.
