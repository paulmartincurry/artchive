Codex Mendoza: Novice Priests and Warrior Ranks (folio 64r)

Maker unknown · c. 1541 · Ink and paint on paper · 30.6 × 20.6 cm · Bodleian Library, Oxford

Free to use

This folio, from the codex's section on daily life, shows the duties of novice priests above and the ranks awarded to warriors below. The codex is believed to date from around 1541 and is named after Antonio de Mendoza, viceroy of New Spain, who supervised its making by artists and scribes trained at the Franciscan college in Tlatelolco. According to Samuel Purchas, French privateers seized it from the Spanish fleet, and it has been at the Bodleian Library in Oxford since 1659.
