The Blinding of Samson

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1636 · Oil on canvas · 219.3 × 305 cm · Städel Museum, Frankfurt am Main

Free to use with credit. Photo: Städel Museum, Frankfurt am Main, CC BY-SA 4.0

Rembrandt gave this painting to the House of Orange, through its secretary Constantijn Huygens, as an excuse for his delay in delivering the Passion paintings it had commissioned. It later hung in the Palais Schönborn-Batthyány in Vienna until the Städel acquired it in 1905. In the background Delilah runs off with the scissors and a shock of hair in her hands.
