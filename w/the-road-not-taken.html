I took the one less travelled by,
And that has made all the difference.

The Road Not Taken

Robert Frost · 1916

Public domain in the United States (published 1916); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Robert Frost lived in England from 1912 to 1915 and took many walks there with the writer Edward Thomas, who was indecisive about which path to take and often lamented afterwards that they should have taken the other. Frost wrote the poem as a joke for him, and sent him an advance copy after returning to New Hampshire in 1915. It was first published in the Atlantic Monthly that August. Thomas was killed two years later in the Battle of Arras.
