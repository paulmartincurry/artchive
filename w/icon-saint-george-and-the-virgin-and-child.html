Icon: Saint George and the Virgin and Child

Maker unknown · 15th-16th century · Wood, paint · H x W x D: 52 × 66.8 × 3 cm, 5.4 kg (20 1/2 × 26 5/16 × 1 3/16 in., 12 lb.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

The icon was once owned by a parsonage. One panel shows the twelve apostles, Saint George being cut to pieces, and the saint killing a dragon; the other shows the Virgin Mary and Child.
