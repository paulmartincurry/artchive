Prince Amar Singh Drives His Own Elephant

Attributed to the Mewar Stipple Master · c. 1695 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · Page: 26.8 × 40 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Amar Singh was born in 1672 and became Maharana of Mewar in 1698, so he was about twenty-three and still a prince when this was painted, around 1695. The museum attributes the page to a painter known only as the Mewar Stipple Master. The prince holds a goad, and the attendant behind him holds a whisk, the royal insignia. The flat green ground is a highly corrosive pigment.
