Calligraphy Couplet

Liang Qichao · after 1911 · Pair of hanging scrolls; ink on paper · Each: 128 x 30 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Liang Qichao wrote these two scrolls in a firm regular script modelled on inscriptions cut into ancient stone steles, a style popular with reformers of his day. He was a politician and journalist, and as a young man he joined his teacher Kang Youwei in the Hundred Days' Reform of 1898. A British diplomat bought the pair while posted in Beijing. Each scroll is 128 centimetres tall.
