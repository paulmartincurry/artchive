The Qur'an — Al-Fātiḥah, The Opening (Surah 1)

The Qur'an (Surah Al-Fatiha) · 7th century CE; English 1930

Translated by Marmaduke Pickthall

Al-Fatiha is the first chapter of the Quran and is recited in every Muslim prayer, obligatory and voluntary. Most narrators recorded that it was the first complete surah revealed to Muhammad, and the most commonly accepted view places it at Mecca. Among its other names are the Mother of the Book and the Cure. There is disagreement over whether the opening invocation counts as its first verse.
