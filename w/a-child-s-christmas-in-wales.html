I said some words to the close and holy darkness, and then I slept.

A Child's Christmas in Wales

Dylan Thomas · 1952

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

The piece grew out of a talk Thomas wrote for the BBC's Welsh Children's Hour in 1945, enlarged almost five years later with parts of a 1947 magazine essay and sold to Harper's Bazaar for $300. On 22 February 1952, in New York, he recorded five poems for a new company, Caedmon Records, and chose this story to fill the other side of the LP. He arrived at the studio without the text, and a 1950 copy of Harper's Bazaar had to be found for him to read from. He died in New York the following year, aged thirty-nine.
