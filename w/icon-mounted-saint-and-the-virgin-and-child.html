Icon: Mounted Saint and the Virgin and Child

Maker unknown · Late 16th-early 17th century · Distemper and gesso on wood · H x W x D: 8.9 x 15.8 x 0.6 cm (3 1/2 x 6 1/4 x 1/4 in.) (open) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

Since the 1400s devout people have commissioned icons as gifts to the Ethiopian Orthodox Church; small icons might be worn hung from the neck, and were believed to carry prayers for mercy and healing straight to the saint shown.
