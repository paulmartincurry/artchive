Bird in Flight

Étienne-Jules Marey · 1886 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · Image: 3.3 × 17.3 cm (1 5/16 × 6 13/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Marey was a physiologist who began by studying blood circulation and the pulse. In 1869 he built an artificial insect to work out how insects fly, and then turned to birds. His chronophotographic gun, first made in 1882, took twelve frames a second. This print is a thin strip, 17 centimetres long and just over 3 high. In 1890 he published a book on the flight of birds.
