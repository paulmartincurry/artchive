Dictionnaire Infernal: Baël

Louis Le Breton · published 1863 · Wood engraving

Free to use

Baël appears on page 71 of the 1863 edition, the sixth, for which Louis Le Breton made 69 illustrations of demons. Le Breton was a marine painter, and from 1847 he worked mainly on naval subjects for the French Navy. He was forty-five when the book came out.
