The Defense of the Sampo (Sammon puolustus)

Akseli Gallen-Kallela · 1896 · Tempera on canvas · Turku Art Museum

Free to use

A wealthy Helsinki patron, Salomo Wuorio, ordered the painting for his dining room. By Gallen-Kallela's own notes the deal was dissolved on friendly terms after the patron's wife reacted badly to the finished canvas. The Turku Arts Association then bought it for 4000 marks, a price not heard of before. He painted the scene again as a fresco for the Paris World's Fair of 1900, which was destroyed when the fair closed, and once more in 1928 for the foyer of the National Museum of Finland.
