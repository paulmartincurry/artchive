Time is a river, a violent current of events, glimpsed once and already carried past us, and another follows and is gone.

Meditations (Book IV, 43 and 48)

Marcus Aurelius · ca. 175 CE

Translated by Gregory Hays · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Marcus Aurelius wrote these notes in Koine Greek, for his own guidance, while he was Roman emperor. He was in his fifties. It is unlikely he meant them to be published, and the work has no official title. Large portions may have been written at Sirmium, in modern Serbia, where he spent much of the decade from 170 planning military campaigns.
