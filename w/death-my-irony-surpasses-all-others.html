Death: My Irony Surpasses All Others! (plate 3 of 6)

Odilon Redon · 1889 · Lithograph in black on ivory China paper laid down on ivory wove paper · Image 20 × 26 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is plate 3 of a set of six lithographs Redon published in 1889, the year his son Arï was born. It belongs to his noirs, the visionary works in charcoal and lithography he made after settling in Paris; in the 1890s he turned to pastel and oil, and he made no noirs after 1900. The Art Institute's impression came with the Stickney Collection.
