Rosetta Stone

Maker unknown · 196 BCE · Granodiorite · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: Hans Hillewaert, CC BY-SA

French soldiers found the stone in July 1799 while demolishing a wall at Fort Julien, where it had been used as building material. It is a fragment of a taller stele and weighs about 760 kilograms; only the last 14 lines of the hieroglyphic text remain. The British took it to London in 1801. There the inscriptions were filled with white chalk and the surface waxed against visitors' fingers, which turned the stone dark until it was cleaned in 1999.
