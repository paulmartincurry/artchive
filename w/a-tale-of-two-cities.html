A Tale of Two Cities (opening)

Charles Dickens · 1859

The novel first appeared in 31 weekly instalments in All the Year Round, a literary periodical Dickens had just started. Its first instalment ran in the first issue, on 30 April 1859. Over the same months he republished the chapters as eight monthly sections in green covers. He was forty-seven.
