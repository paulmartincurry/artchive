The Lady and the Unicorn

Maker unknown · 1500 · Wool, silk · Musée de Cluny

Free to use

The six tapestries were woven in Flanders from designs drawn in Paris, and they carry the arms of the Le Viste family. Prosper Mérimée came across them in 1841 at Boussac Castle, where bad storage had left them damp and mouldy. The novelist George Sand saw them in 1844 and wrote them into a novel, dating them from the ladies' costumes. They stayed at risk until 1882, when the curator of the Musée de Cluny bought them.
