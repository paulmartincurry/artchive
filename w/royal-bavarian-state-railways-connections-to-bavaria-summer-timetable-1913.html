Royal Bavarian State Railways: Connections to Bavaria, Summer Timetable 1913

Ludwig Hohlwein · 1913 · Colour lithograph on paper · 71.4 × 43.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The poster gave train connections into Bavaria for the summer timetable of 1913 for the Royal Bavarian State Railways, the state railway company of the kingdom, founded in 1844. Ludwig Hohlwein had trained and worked as an architect in Munich before he switched to poster design. By 1925 he had designed 3,000 different advertisements.
