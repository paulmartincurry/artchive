Saluting Protective Spirit

Maker unknown · 883–859 BCE · Gypsum · 229.9 × 137 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Austen Henry Layard excavated this relief in 1847 at the palace of Ashurnasirpal II at Nimrud, where it once stood alongside hundreds of similar ones. Dr. Henry John Lobdell gave it to Amherst College, which later exchanged it for a lot of objects with the Joseph Brummer Gallery. The slab is gypsum, about 2.3 metres high, and is inscribed with an account of the king's conquests. Cleveland bought it in 1943.
