The Staircase with Trophies (Carceri d'invenzione, plate VIII)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 55 × 40.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Some of Piranesi's ink-wash drawings for the prisons survive, and the one for this plate is close to the finished etching. Piranesi had trained early as a stage designer, and surviving set drawings by the architects Filippo Juvarra and Ferdinando Bibiena have the same receding spaces and disappearing staircases.
