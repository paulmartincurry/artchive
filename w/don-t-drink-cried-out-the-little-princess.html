Don't Drink! Cried Out the Little Princess (In Powder and Crinoline)

Kay Nielsen · published 1913 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

In Powder and Crinoline was Nielsen's first English commission. Hodder & Stoughton asked him for 24 colour plates and more than 15 monotone illustrations for these fairy tales retold by Sir Arthur Quiller-Couch, published in 1913. The same year The Illustrated London News printed his four pictures for tales by Charles Perrault in its Christmas edition. This plate is the frontispiece to The Twelve Dancing Princesses.
