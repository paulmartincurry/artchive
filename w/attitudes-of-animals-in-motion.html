Attitudes of Animals in Motion

Eadweard Muybridge · 1879, printed 1881 · Albumen silver print · Image: 6 5/16 × 9 15/16 in. (16 × 25.3 cm) · The Met

Free to use

From 1878 Muybridge photographed animals with a row of cameras, each catching a different position in the stride. He made these pictures in 1879, at forty-nine, and this print, sixteen centimetres tall, was made two years later. He also built the zoopraxiscope, which projected painted motion pictures from glass discs before film on a perforated strip existed. Joyce F. Menschel gave the print to the Metropolitan Museum in 2015.
