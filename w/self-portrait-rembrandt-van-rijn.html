Self-Portrait

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1659 · Oil on canvas · 84.5 × 66 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Rembrandt painted more than forty self-portraits, and this is one of only two in which he is turned to the left, showing more of the right side of his face. He signed and dated it at the centre left in 1659. Its whereabouts are known only from 1767, the date of a mezzotint made after it. Andrew W. Mellon bought it in January 1929 and it was given to the National Gallery of Art in 1937.
