The Alchymist, in Search of the Philosopher's Stone, Discovers Phosphorus

William Pether, after Joseph Wright of Derby · 1775 · Mezzotint · Image: 56.1 x 45.5 cm (22 1/16 x 17 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The print copies a painting by Joseph Wright of Derby. Wright based the composition on the story of Hennig Brand, the German alchemist who discovered phosphorus by accident in 1676.
