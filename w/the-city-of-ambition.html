The City of Ambition (Camera Work 36, plate I)

Alfred Stieglitz · 1910, published 1911 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The photogravure appeared as plate I of Camera Work, number 36, in October 1911. The Library of Congress copy carries the title with the year 1910 beside it. Throughout 1911 and early 1912 Stieglitz promoted new art alongside photography in the magazine's pages.
