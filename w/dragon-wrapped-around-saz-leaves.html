Dragon Wrapped around Saz Leaves

Maker unknown · ca. 1550–70 · Main support: ink on paper Border: Gold, ink, and opaque watercolor on paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The drawing was made in Istanbul in ink alone, and the border around it was added in gold and opaque watercolour. It reached the Metropolitan Museum in 2000 as a gift from a donor who chose not to be named.
