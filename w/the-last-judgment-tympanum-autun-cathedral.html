The Last Judgment Tympanum, Autun Cathedral

Gislebertus · c. 1130 · Stone carving

Free to use with credit. Photo: Lamettrie, CC BY-SA 2.0 DE

In 1766 the canons of the cathedral decided the sculpture was mediocre and covered it with plaster. The head of Christ was removed so that the plaster could fill the tympanum completely. It was uncovered again in 1837.
