Wanderer above the Sea of Fog

Caspar David Friedrich · ca. 1818 · Oil on canvas · Hamburger Kunsthalle

The landscape is put together from separate places in the Elbe Sandstone Mountains of Saxony and Bohemia. Friedrich sketched them in the field and rearranged them in the studio, as he usually did. The rocks the figure stands on are a group on the Kaiserkrone. Who the man is remains uncertain. Nothing is known of where the painting went after it was made until 1939, when it was in the gallery of Wilhelm August Luz in Berlin, and the Hamburger Kunsthalle acquired it in 1970.
