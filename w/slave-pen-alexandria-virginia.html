Slave Pen, Alexandria, Virginia

Andrew Joseph Russell · 1863 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · image 25.6 × 36.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

During the first two years of the Civil War, Russell painted a diorama used to recruit soldiers for the Union Army. He took his first photographs with a borrowed camera. On 1 March 1863 Colonel Herman Haupt had him removed from his regiment to photograph for the United States Military Railroad and the Quartermaster Corps, which he did until September 1865.
