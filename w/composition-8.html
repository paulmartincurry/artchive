The Artchive

Composition 8

Wassily Kandinsky · July 1923 · Oil on canvas · 140.3 × 200.7 cm · Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, New York

Free to use

Kandinsky finished this canvas in July 1923, at fifty-six, while teaching at the Bauhaus in Weimar, where he ran the basic design class for beginners. In those years the circle, the half-circle, the angle and the straight line came to the front of both his teaching and his painting. His 1911 book had named three kinds of painting, impressions, improvisations and compositions, the last worked out from the most formal point of view. In 1923 he also formed the Blue Four with Paul Klee, Lyonel Feininger and Alexej von Jawlensky.
