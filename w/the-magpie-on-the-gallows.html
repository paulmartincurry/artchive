The Magpie on the Gallows

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1568 · Oil on oak panel · Hessisches Landesmuseum, Darmstadt

Free to use

Bruegel signed and dated the panel BRVEGEL 1568, the year before his death, and it may be his last painting. He asked his wife to burn some of his pictures when he died, but told her to keep this one for herself. The gallows is an impossible object: its posts stand side by side while the crossbar recedes into the distance.
