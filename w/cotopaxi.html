The Artchive

Cotopaxi

Frederic Edwin Church · 1862 · Oil on canvas · Detroit Institute of Arts

Free to use

James Lenox, a collector who owned the first Turner paintings to reach America, commissioned this canvas, and it was first shown in New York in 1863. Church had first drawn the volcano in 1853, on the first of his journeys to South America, and he painted it at least ten times. He worked in his New York studio from sketches made on the spot. The canvas measures 48 by 85 inches.
