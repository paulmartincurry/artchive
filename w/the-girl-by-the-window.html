The Girl by the Window

Edvard Munch · 1893 · Oil on canvas · 96.5 × 65.4 cm (38 × 25 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The museum's exhibition record puts this picture in Munch's show in Kristiania in September 1892, and two months later in his exhibition for the Verein Berliner Künstler, which went on to Düsseldorf, Cologne, Copenhagen and Dresden. Arthur von Franquet of Braunschweig bought it directly from Munch in 1893. In 1935 Otto Kallir of the Neue Galerie in Vienna bought it and wrote to Munch the next day to tell him. The Art Institute bought it in 2000.
