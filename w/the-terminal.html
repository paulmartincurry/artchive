The Terminal (Camera Work 36, plate XV)

Alfred Stieglitz · 1893 (dated 1892 on the plate), published 1911 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Stieglitz made this with a small 4 by 5 camera, which he could carry through the streets without the tripod his 8 by 10 view camera needed. From 1893 to 1895 he often walked downtown near the East River with it. On a bitterly cold day near the old Post Office he came on a driver watering the steaming horses of a Harlem streetcar outside the Astor House. He was 29 when he took it; it appeared in Camera Work in October 1911.
