West Rose Window, Sainte-Chapelle

Maker unknown · 1485–1498 · Stained glass

Free to use with credit. Photo: Gerd Eichmann, CC BY-SA 4.0

The first rose window on the west front of the chapel was replaced around the end of the 15th century by this one, in the Flamboyant style. Its restoration was completed in 2015.
