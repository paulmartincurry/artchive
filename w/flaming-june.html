Flaming June

Frederic Leighton · 1895 · Oil on canvas · Museo de Arte de Ponce

Free to use

The figure began as a motif on a marble bath in another of Leighton's paintings, and the angle of her right arm took him several preliminary sketches. The Graphic magazine bought the canvas to make a reproduction it gave away at Christmas 1895. Leighton died the next January, and the painting stood in the magazine's office window as his funeral passed. It was found in the early 1960s boxed in over a chimney in a Battersea house, and failed to sell at auction. Luis A. Ferré bought it in Amsterdam in 1963 for under 1,000 dollars.
