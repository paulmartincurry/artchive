Then There Appears a Singular Being, Having the Head of a Man on the Body of a Fish

Odilon Redon · 1888 · lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is plate five of the first of three portfolios Redon made after Gustave Flaubert's novel The Temptation of Saint Anthony. The Belgian publisher Edmond Deman commissioned it after seeing Redon's work exhibited in Brussels in 1886. Becquet printed it, and 58 copies were pulled of a planned edition of 60. The Print Club of Cleveland bought the set from the Paris dealer Maurice Le Garrec and gave it to the museum in 1927.
