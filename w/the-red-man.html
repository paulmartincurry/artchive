The Red Man

Gertrude Käsebier · 1902 · Platinum print with gum bichromate · 32.7 × 24.1 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In 1898 Gertrude Käsebier watched Buffalo Bill's Wild West troupe parade past her Fifth Avenue studio in New York on its way to Madison Square Garden. She wrote to William Cody asking permission to photograph the Sioux members of the show in her studio. This print is signed in the lower left corner.
