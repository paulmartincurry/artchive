Wheel of Fortune (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Every Rider edition up to 1939 could be bought with a small guide by Waite, first published in 1909 as The Key to the Tarot. A revised version of 1911, The Pictorial Key to the Tarot, reproduced all seventy-eight of Smith's drawings in black and white.
