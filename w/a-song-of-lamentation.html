The smoking stars gather together against it; the one who cares for flowers is about to be destroyed.

He who cared for books wept, he wept for the beginning of the destruction.

A Song of Lamentation (stanzas 4–8)

Anonymous Nahua poet (Cantares Mexicanos) · Written down in the 16th century

Translated by Daniel G. Brinton, 1887

In the manuscript of the Cantares Mexicanos the songs are set out as about 1,700 hanging paragraphs, short stanzas of some thirty words each. Ángel María Garibay Kintana put much of it into Spanish in 1965 and 1968, but the whole manuscript was not in Spanish until Miguel León-Portilla's two-volume edition.
