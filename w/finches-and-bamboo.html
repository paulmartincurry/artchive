Finches and Bamboo

Emperor Huizong · early 12th century · Handscroll, ink and color on silk · 33.7 × 55.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Birds and flowers were the main subjects of Emperor Huizong's own painting. He also invented a calligraphy style, Slender Gold, named for the way his strokes twisted like gold filament, and founded an imperial painting house where the best painters in China showed their work. The painting is about 55 centimetres long; with the inscriptions and seals mounted after it, the scroll stretches past eight metres.
