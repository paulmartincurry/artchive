Ancient Ruins in the Cañon de Chelle, N.M., in a Niche 50 Feet above Present Cañon Bed

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1873 · Albumen print · 27.5 × 20.2 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Timothy O'Sullivan was photographer to the survey led by Lieutenant George Wheeler, which mapped the United States west of the 100th meridian for the government. To reach this canyon he and a small group travelled thirty-five miles across a dry desert plateau. Their campsite is visible in the foreground. O'Sullivan had worked for Mathew Brady during the Civil War.
