Kyōsai's Pictures of One Hundred Demons (Kyōsai hyakki gadan)

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1890 (first edition published posthumously 1889) · Woodblock printed book (orihon, accordion-style); ink and color on paper · 19.8 × 12 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kyōsai died in April 1889, and the first edition of this book of demons came out after his death that same year; this copy is from 1890. It is an accordion-folded book, 19.8 centimetres tall, printed from woodblocks in ink and colour.
