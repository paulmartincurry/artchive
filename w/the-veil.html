The Veil

Fernand Khnopff · c. 1887 · Charcoal and graphite with stumping on ivory wove paper, laid down on wood pulp board · 40 × 21 cm (15 3/4 × 8 5/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The drawing is 40 centimetres tall, and its first recorded owner was Marguerite Freson-Khnopff of Liège. Fernand Khnopff was about twenty-nine and was illustrating novels by Joséphin Péladan. He had destroyed his 1885 cover for Péladan's Le Vice suprême after the soprano Rose Caron thought she recognised her own face in it. He went to law school to please his parents before leaving for painting.
