Human-Headed Winged Bull (Lamassu)

Assyrian · ca. 883–859 BCE · Gypsum alabaster · H. 123 1/2 × W. 26 1/2 × D. 122 in. · The Met

Free to use

John D. Rockefeller Jr. gave this bull to the Met in 1932. It stands 313.7 centimetres high, is 309.9 centimetres deep and weighs about 7,257 kilograms.
