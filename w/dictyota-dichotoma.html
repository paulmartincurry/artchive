Dictyota dichotoma (Photographs of British Algae)

Anna Atkins · 1843–53 · Cyanotype · The New York Public Library, Spencer Collection

Free to use

Anna Atkins made this print without a camera, by laying the dried seaweed straight onto cyanotype paper. Her friend Sir John Herschel had invented the process in 1842, and she was using it on algae within a year. She published the first part herself in October 1843, with handwritten text, and went on adding to the three volumes until 1853. Only 17 copies are known to exist.
