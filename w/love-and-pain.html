Love and Pain (Vampire)

Edvard Munch · 1895 · Oil on canvas · Munch Museum, Oslo

Free to use

Edvard Munch called this painting Love and Pain. The name Vampire came from his friend, the critic Stanisław Przybyszewski, who saw it on exhibition, and Munch never used it. He painted six versions of the subject between 1893 and 1895, and one of the six is unaccounted for. A version was stolen from the Munch Museum on 23 February 1988 and recovered later that year, when the thief contacted the police.
