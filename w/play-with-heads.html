Play with Heads

Oskar Schlemmer · 1923 · lithograph · Overall: 49 × 33.6 × 7 cm (19 5/16 × 13 1/4 × 2 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Each print in this portfolio uses a single colour, spattered onto the stone by drawing a blade across a toothbrush loaded with lithographic ink. Schlemmer was thirty-four or thirty-five, and that year he took over the stage workshop at the Bauhaus. As a teenager he had supported himself as an apprentice in inlay and marquetry workshops. The portfolio passed through an East German private collection and a Bern sale in 1993 before Cleveland bought it.
