Typus Orbis Terrarum (Theatrum Orbis Terrarum)

Abraham Ortelius · 1570 · Hand-coloured engraving · 43 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The atlas this map comes from was issued in Antwerp on 20 May 1570 with 53 maps. Abraham Ortelius drew almost none of them himself: he gathered the work of other mapmakers, had it engraved at one size on copper, and printed his own commentary on the back of each sheet. For the world map his sources included Giacomo Gastaldi's map of 1561 and Gerardus Mercator's of 1569. Ortelius, who had started out colouring maps for a living, was forty-three. This copy's cover bears the arms of Jean, comte de Hoya, Bishop of Münster and Osnabrück.
