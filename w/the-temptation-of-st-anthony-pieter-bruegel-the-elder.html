The Temptation of St. Anthony

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1556 · Engraving · Sheet: 26.2 × 33.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bruegel had settled in Antwerp in 1555, the year before this print, and worked there mainly as a designer of prints for Hieronymus Cock, more than forty of them. With one exception he did not work the plates himself; he made a drawing, and Cock's specialists worked from it. Cock published this sheet in 1556.
