Goe, and catche a falling starre,
Get with child a mandrake roote,
Tell me, where all past yeares are,
Or who cleft the Divels foot,

Song (Go and Catch a Falling Star)

John Donne · 1633

This song probably passed from hand to hand in manuscript during the 1590s. It was not printed until the first edition of Donne's collected poems in 1633, two years after his death. Each of its three stanzas rhymes ABABCCDDD, and the line lengths vary in a regular sequence.
