William heard me breathing, and rustling now and then, but we both lay still, and unseen by one another. He thought that it would be so sweet thus to lie in the grave, to hear the peaceful sounds of the earth, and just to know that our dear friends were near.

Grasmere Journal, 29 April 1802

Dorothy Wordsworth · 1802

Later in 1802 Dorothy Wordsworth travelled with William to Gallow Hill in Yorkshire for his marriage to Mary Hutchinson. The manuscript of the journal she kept at Alfoxden from January to May 1798 is lost. William Angus Knight edited the Grasmere Journal for its first publication in 1897.
