You see, really and truly, apart from the things anyone can pick up (the dressing and the proper way of speaking, and so on), the difference between a lady and a flower girl is not how she behaves, but how she’s treated.

Pygmalion — the difference between a lady and a flower girl (Act V)

George Bernard Shaw · 1913

Shaw wrote the play in early 1912 and read it to the actress Mrs Patrick Campbell in June. It was first staged in German, at the Hofburg Theatre in Vienna on 16 October 1913. The English-language premiere followed in London in April 1914, with Mrs Patrick Campbell as Eliza Doolittle.
