Venus and Mars

Sandro Botticelli · c. 1485 · Tempera on panel · The National Gallery, London

Free to use

The picture was probably made to mark a wedding, and set into panelling or a piece of furniture in the couple's bedroom. Its exact date is not known. An English collector and dealer bought it in Florence between 1864 and 1869, and the National Gallery bought it at the sale of his collection in June 1874 for £1,050.
