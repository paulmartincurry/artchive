The Kiss

Auguste Rodin · modelled 1882; marble ordered 1888, first shown 1898 · Marble · 181.5 × 112.5 × 117 cm · Musée Rodin, Paris

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jean-Pierre Dalbéra, CC BY 2.0

The couple first appeared among the reliefs Rodin made for The Gates of Hell, and the group was originally titled Francesca da Rimini, after the woman in Dante who falls in love with her husband's younger brother. The French government ordered this marble in 1888 for the 1889 Exposition Universelle; it was carved by Jean Turcan and first shown in public at the Salon of 1898. It moved to the Musée du Luxembourg in 1900 and to the Musée Rodin in 1918.
