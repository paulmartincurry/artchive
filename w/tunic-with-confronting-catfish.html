Tunic with Confronting Catfish

Maker unknown · 800–850 CE · Camelid hair, tapestry-weave · 54.6 × 109.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This tunic was woven in camelid-hair tapestry between 800 and 850 CE and is 109.9 centimetres wide. Wari tunics for high-ranking officials hold between six and nine miles of thread each. George D. Pratt gave it to the Met in 1929.
