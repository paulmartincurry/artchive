Aberdeen Bestiary: The Phoenix (folio 56r)

Maker unknown · c. 1200 · Pigment and gold leaf on parchment · Aberdeen University Library

Free to use

There is pouncing around the bird and the flames, probably done while the manuscript was being made. Elsewhere in the book, small pin holes were likely used to map out the pictures and copy them into a new manuscript.
