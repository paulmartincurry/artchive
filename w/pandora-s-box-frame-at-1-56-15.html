G. W. Pabst searched for months for his Lulu and settled on Louise Brooks after seeing her play a circus performer in A Girl in Every Port. His second choice, Marlene Dietrich, was waiting in his office to sign a contract when word came that Brooks was free. Brooks had danced with the Denishawn company, and Pabst choreographed her movement in each scene, limiting her to a single emotion per shot.
