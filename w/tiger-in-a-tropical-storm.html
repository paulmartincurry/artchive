Tiger in a Tropical Storm (Surprised!)

Henri Rousseau · 1891 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

This was the first of Rousseau's jungle paintings, and he never left France: the plants are thought to come from the botanical gardens of Paris and from prints and books. He showed it in 1891 at the Salon des Indépendants, which had no jury, after failing to get a painting past the jury of the Académie. The rain is his own device, strands of silver paint trailed diagonally across the canvas. Around 1908 the dealer Ambroise Vollard bought it with two other works for 190 francs. The National Gallery bought it in 1972.
