Lady with an Ermine

Leonardo da Vinci · 1490 · Oil on panel · Czartoryski Museum

Free to use

The panel is a single piece of walnut four to five millimetres thick, most likely cut from the same tree as the one Leonardo used for La Belle Ferronnière. His fingerprints are in the paint of the face and the animal's head. The sitter, Cecilia Gallerani, was about sixteen and the mistress of his employer, the Duke of Milan. The background was probably bluish-grey before it was painted over in black. The Nazis seized the picture in 1939 and Hans Frank hung it in his offices; Allied troops found it at his country home in Bavaria.
