The Beauty of Life: A Golden Rule

William Morris · 1880

Morris trained as an architect under George Edmund Street from January 1856 and gave it up to paint. In 1877 he founded the Society for the Protection of Ancient Buildings, to campaign against the damage done by restoration. From 1871 he rented Kelmscott Manor in Oxfordshire and kept a main home in London. In his lifetime he was known mainly as a poet.
