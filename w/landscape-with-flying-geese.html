Landscape with Flying Geese

Ma Lin · mid-1200s · Album leaf; ink and slight color on silk · Image: 25.5 × 26.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Ma Lin signed this leaf Your subject, Ma Lin. It also carries three half-seals, one an official seal of the Yuan dynasty and one from the Ming imperial collection, used between 1372 and 1384. The painting is in ink and a little colour on silk, about 25 by 26 centimetres. Cleveland bought it in 1952 from Vladimir Simkhovitch of New York.
