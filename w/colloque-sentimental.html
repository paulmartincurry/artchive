"How sweet was hope, the sky how blue and fair!"
"The sky grew black, the hope became despair."

Colloque sentimental

Paul Verlaine · 1869

Translated by Gertrude Hall

Verlaine was educated in Paris and then took up a post in the civil service; his first collection came out in 1866, when he was twenty-two.
