The Shadow

Hans Christian Andersen · 1847

Translated by translator not named

Andersen was forty-two in 1847 and two years into his third series of fairy tales. He was living at Nyhavn 67 in Copenhagen. That June he went to England for the first time and met Charles Dickens at a party. He was the son of a washerwoman who could not read, and before he moved to Copenhagen at fourteen to try for work as an actor he had been apprenticed to a weaver and then to a tailor.
