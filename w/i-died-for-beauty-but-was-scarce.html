And so, as kinsmen met a night,
We talked between the rooms,
Until the moss had reached our lips,
And covered up our names.

I died for beauty, but was scarce

Emily Dickinson · 1890

When Dickinson's second cousin and close friend Sophia Holland died of typhus in April 1844, the thirteen-year-old was traumatised. It has been reported that most of her poems were written during the American Civil War. Her poems were first published as a selection in 1890, four years after her death.
