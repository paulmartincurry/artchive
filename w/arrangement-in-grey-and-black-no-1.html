Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1 (Whistler's Mother)

James McNeill Whistler · 1871 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay

Free to use

Anna McNeill Whistler sat for her son while she was living with him at 96 Cheyne Walk in Chelsea. The Royal Academy came close to rejecting the picture in 1872, and it was the last painting he ever submitted there. The frame is his own design. He later pawned the painting, and the French state bought it in 1891.
