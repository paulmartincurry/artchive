Those are pearls that were his eyes:
Nothing of him that doth fade,
But doth suffer a sea-change
Into something rich and strange.

The Tempest — Ariel's Song (Act I, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · 1611

A real shipwreck stands behind the opening of the play. The Sea Venture was wrecked on the island of Bermuda in 1609 while sailing toward Virginia, and William Strachey described it in a private letter dated 15 July 1610, which Shakespeare may have seen that year. The Tempest was probably composed between late 1610 and mid-1611.
