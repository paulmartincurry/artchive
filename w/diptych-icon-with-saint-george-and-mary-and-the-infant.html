Diptych Icon with Saint George, and Mary and the Infant Christ

Maker unknown · Early 15th century · Tempera on wood · 18.7 × 27.9 cm · Walters Art Museum, Baltimore

Free to use with credit. Photo: Walters Art Museum, CC BY-SA 3.0

This early fifteenth-century diptych was made for one owner's personal use, and open it measures 18.7 by 27.9 centimetres. Saint George was often paired with Mary on such icons because he, more than any other saint, was considered her special messenger. The Walters bought it in 2004 from Sam Fogg, a London dealer in rare books and manuscripts.
