Tenth Plague of Egypt (Liber Studiorum, no. 61)

J. M. W. Turner · published 1816 · Etching and mezzotint · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is number 61 of the 71 landscape prints in the Liber Studiorum, a project to which Turner gave twelve years, from 1807 to 1819. The plate is credited to Turner and William Say and was published in 1816.
