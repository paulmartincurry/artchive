The Qur'an — Al-Qāriʿah (Surah 101)

The Qur'an (Early Meccan Surah) · 7th century CE

Translated by M. A. S. Abdel Haleem, 2004 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The revelations classed as Meccan make up two-thirds of the Quran. They were passed on by word of mouth at first, and it is thought that writing them down began towards the end of the Meccan period, on tablets, bones and the broad, flat ends of palm leaves. The Quran did not exist as a book when Muhammad died in 632.
