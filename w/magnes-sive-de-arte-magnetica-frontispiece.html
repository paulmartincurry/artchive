Magnes sive de Arte Magnetica: Frontispiece

Athanasius Kircher · 1641 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use with credit. Photo: Wellcome Collection, CC BY 4.0

Magnes was printed in Rome in 1641. Its stated subject is magnetism, but Kircher also wrote in it about other kinds of attraction, including gravity and love. His first book, ten years earlier, had also been on magnetism, and a magnetic clock is among the inventions credited to him.
