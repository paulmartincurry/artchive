The Lacemaker

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1669–70 · Oil on canvas mounted on wood · 24 × 21 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

At about 24 by 21 centimetres this is the smallest of Vermeer's paintings. The canvas was cut from the same bolt as the one he used for A Young Woman Seated at the Virginals, and it is now glued to a wooden panel. The light falls from the right, which is rare in his work. Its first owner was Pieter Claesz. van Ruijven of Delft, Vermeer's patron and main collector; the Louvre bought it in 1870.
