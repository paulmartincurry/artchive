The Apocalyptic Woman (The Apocalypse)

Albrecht Dürer · 1498, edition of 1511 · Woodcut · Block: 39.3 × 28 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This sheet is from the second Latin edition of the Apocalypse. Dürer published it in 1511, thirteen years after the first, in a combined edition with his Life of the Virgin and Large Passion. Single impressions were also printed and sold.
