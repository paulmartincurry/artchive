The final sequence was shot in Death Valley through July and August 1923, in a place with no roads, hotels or running water. The highest temperature officially recorded during the shoot was 123 degrees Fahrenheit. Of the forty-three cast and crew who worked there, one cook died of heatstroke and fourteen others fell ill and were sent back to Los Angeles.
