The Opening of the Fifth and Sixth Seal (The Apocalypse)

Albrecht Dürer · 1498 · Woodcut on laid paper · Block: 39.2 × 28.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The Apocalypse was published in Latin and in German, and this impression is from the Latin edition of 1498. It was sold at auction at Boerner in May 1926, passed to the Zurich collector Félix Somary, and was bought by the National Gallery of Art in 2008.
