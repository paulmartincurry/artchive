The Court of Gayumars (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Sultan Muhammad · ca. 1522–1525 · Gouache, ink and gold on paper · Aga Khan Museum, Toronto

Free to use

This page comes from a manuscript of 759 pages and 258 paintings, made in the royal workshop at Tabriz. Shah Ismail I ordered it and died in 1524, soon after work began; his son Tahmasp took the throne at eleven. In 1568 it was given to the Ottoman sultan Selim II, and it arrived with 34 camels and other gifts. The page is about 48 by 32 centimetres. Arthur Houghton II broke the book up in the 1970s and sold the paintings one by one. The painter Dust Muhammad, who worked on the manuscript, wrote that this page was by Sultan Muhammad.
