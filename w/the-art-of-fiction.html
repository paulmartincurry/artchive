The Art of Fiction

Henry James · 1884

Henry James was forty-one in 1884 and had been settled in London for about eight years, living in rented rooms. His novels ran as magazine serials before his publishers issued them as books. During a year in Paris he had met Flaubert, Zola, Maupassant and Turgenev, and he said he had learned more about the craft of fiction from Balzac than from anyone else. He had a stutter that appears to have shown only when he spoke English.
