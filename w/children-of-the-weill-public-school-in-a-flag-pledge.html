Children of the Weill Public School in a Flag Pledge Ceremony, San Francisco

Dorothea Lange · April 1942 · Gelatin silver print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This photograph, attributed to Dorothea Lange, was taken in April 1942 at the Raphael Weill Public School in San Francisco for the War Relocation Authority. Lange had given up a Guggenheim Fellowship to take the assignment. Two girls in plaid coats in the front row, Hideno Nakamoto and Yoko Itashiki, were forcibly removed with their families to the Tanforan Assembly Center soon afterwards.
