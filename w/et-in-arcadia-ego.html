Et in Arcadia Ego (The Arcadian Shepherds)

Nicolas Poussin · 1637–1638 · Oil on canvas · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Nicolas Poussin painted the subject twice. This version dates from 1637–38 and is in the Louvre; the earlier one, of 1627, is at Chatsworth House in England. Guercino had treated the theme before him.
