Man is a rope stretched between the animal and the Superman—a rope over an abyss.

Thus Spake Zarathustra — A Rope over an Abyss (Prologue, 4)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883

Translated by Thomas Common

Nietzsche said the book's central idea, the eternal recurrence, first came to him in August 1881, beside a pyramidal block of stone in the woods along Lake Silvaplana in the Upper Engadine. He was writing it after his marriage proposals to Lou Andreas-Salomé had been rejected, and he walked a great deal while he worked on it. Ernst Schmeitzner was its publisher.
