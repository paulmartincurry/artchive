God Commanding Noah to Build the Ark

Marco Dente da Ravenna · 1520 · Engraving in black on tan laid paper · 30 × 24.5 cm (11 13/16 × 9 11/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It reproduces Raphael's fresco in the Vatican's Stanza d'Eliodoro, in the same direction as Marcantonio Raimondi's print; both differ from the final painting and may come from the same workshop drawing.
