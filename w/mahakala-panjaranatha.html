Mahakala Panjaranatha (Protector of the Tent)

Maker unknown · late 14th–early 15th century · Steatite with color and gilding · 17.8 × 11.4 × 3.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The figure is 17.8 centimetres high, carved in steatite and then coloured and gilded. Through Tibetan influence, Mahakala's importance grew under the Mongol-led Yuan dynasty, when his likeness was displayed in the imperial palace and in Buddhist temples in and around the capital. Florence and Herbert Irving gave this carving to the Met in 2015.
