The Artchive

Nepenthaceae (Kunstformen der Natur, plate 62)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Colour lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

This pitcher plant was found on 4 January 1901 by the waterfalls of Tjiburrum, in the rainforest of Java, and painted from life in the laboratory at nearby Tjibodas. Ernst Haeckel was sixty-six.
