Witches' Sabbath

Claes Jacobsz van der Heck · 1636 · Oil on panel · 56.8 × 97.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The panel is signed and dated on the pedestal of the obelisk: C. Heck, 1636. Claes Jacobsz van der Heck, a grand-nephew of Maarten van Heemskerck, was then about sixty and specialised in views of abbeys and castles around Alkmaar, where he helped set up the painters' guild in 1631 and 1632. Ernst Heldring bought the painting at auction in 1908 and gave it to the Rijksmuseum the next year. Since 2000 it has been on loan to the museum in Alkmaar.
