Two Elephants in Combat

Maker unknown · 1700–1730 · Ink on paper · 35.6 × 53.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Elephant fights were a favourite amusement of the Rajput rulers, and certain elephants were trained for them. The two in this ink drawing are shackled at their back feet to keep them from running free. The sheet is 35.6 by 53.4 centimetres. Catherine Glynn Benkaim and Barbara Timmer sold and gave it to Cleveland in 2018.
