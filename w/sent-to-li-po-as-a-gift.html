The Artchive
You drink a great deal,
You sing wild songs,
Your days pass in emptiness.
Your nature is a spreading fire,
It is swift and strenuous.
But what does all this bravery amount to?

Sent to Li Po as a Gift

Du Fu · 8th century; English 1921

Translated by Florence Ayscough and Amy Lowell, 1921

Florence Ayscough was born in Shanghai and met the poet Amy Lowell at school in Brookline, Massachusetts. Her family went back to live in China when she was in her early twenties. She introduced Lowell to Chinese paintings and poetry in 1917, and Fir-Flower Tablets, their book of translations and Ayscough's first, followed in 1921.
