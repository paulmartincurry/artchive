Young Sick Bacchus

Caravaggio · c. 1593–94 · Oil on canvas · 67 × 53 cm · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Free to use

According to his first biographer, Giovanni Baglione, Caravaggio painted this from a mirror, in his first years in Rome after arriving from Milan in mid-1592. He was in his early twenties. The accounts of these years are thin, but they agree that he fell gravely ill and spent six months in the hospital of Santa Maria della Consolazione. The canvas belonged to his early employer Giuseppe Cesari, whose collection Cardinal Scipione Borghese seized in 1607.
