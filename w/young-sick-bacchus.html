Young Sick Bacchus

Caravaggio · c. 1593–94 · Oil on canvas · 67 × 53 cm · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Free to use

Caravaggio painted himself from a mirror, about a year after arriving in Rome from Milan in mid-1592. He was twenty-one or twenty-two. The early sources agree that around this time he fell gravely ill and spent six months in the hospital of Santa Maria della Consolazione. The canvas belonged to his early employer Giuseppe Cesari, whose collection Cardinal Scipione Borghese seized in 1607.
