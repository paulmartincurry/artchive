Little trotty wagtail he went in the rain,
And tittering, tottering sideways he neer got straight again,
He stooped to get a worm, and looked up to get a fly,
And then he flew away ere his feathers they were dry.

Little Trotty Wagtail

John Clare · 1849

Clare was committed to the Northampton General Lunatic Asylum at the end of 1841 and stayed there until his death in 1864. Earl Fitzwilliam paid for his keep, at the ordinary rate for poor people. Thomas Octavius Prichard, who ran the asylum, encouraged and helped him to write. Clare wrote in Northamptonshire dialect and often without punctuation, which his publishers tended to add.
