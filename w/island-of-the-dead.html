Island of the Dead

Arnold Böcklin · 1880 · Oil on wood · 73.7 x 121.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is the smaller version Böcklin painted on wood in June 1880 for Marie Berna, a widow who had seen the first one half finished on his easel in Florence. At her request he added the coffin and the white-clad figure, an allusion to her husband's death from diphtheria years earlier. Böcklin called these pictures Die Gräberinsel, Tomb Island. The museum bought this one in 1926.
