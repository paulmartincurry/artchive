The Artchive
To defy Power, which seems omnipotent;
To love, and bear; to hope till Hope creates
From its own wreck the thing it contemplates;

Prometheus Unbound (Act IV, closing lines)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1820

Prometheus Unbound is a lyrical drama in four acts, first published in 1820. Mary Shelley first described her husband's work on it in a letter of 5 September 1818. He stopped working on the poem after the death of their daughter Clara Everina on 24 September 1818.
