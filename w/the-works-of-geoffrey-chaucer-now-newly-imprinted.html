The Works of Geoffrey Chaucer Now Newly Imprinted (The Kelmscott Chaucer)

William Morris and Edward Burne-Jones · 1896 · Hand-printed book on Batchelor linen paper, bound with tooled white pigskin on oak board · 44.5 × 36 × 7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Kelmscott Press printed 425 copies of its Chaucer in 1896, and this is one of 48 bound in white pigskin over oak boards. Morris designed the typeface, a twelve-point version of his Troy type that he named Chaucer, along with the initials and borders. Edward Burne-Jones drew the 87 illustrations and William Harcourt Hooper cut them in wood. The paper was handmade by Joseph Batchelor and Son, and the ink was made without modern chemicals. Morris died the year the book was printed.
