Pine Trees

Hasegawa Tōhaku · ca. 1595 · Ink on wood · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

Hasegawa Tōhaku painted these two six-panel screens with a brush made of straw, on relatively coarse paper. The sheets are joined unevenly, the top and bottom pieces are half the usual size, and his seals are not in their usual forms, so the screens may have been a preparatory work. The trees at the far right are cropped, which suggests the panels were reordered or some replaced.
