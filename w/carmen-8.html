Poor Catullus, 'tis time you should cease your folly, and account as lost what you see is lost. Once the days shone bright on you, when you used to go so often where my mistress led, she who was loved by me as none will ever be loved.

Carmen 8 (Miser Catulle)

Catullus · c. 59 BCE

Translated by F. W. Cornish

The poem is known by its opening words, Miser Catulle, and is written in the choliambic metre. The scholar E. T. Merrill dated it to about 59 BC, during an estrangement that he thought Lesbia herself ended.
