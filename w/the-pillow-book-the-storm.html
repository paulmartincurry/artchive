When one thinks of it, to be in a boat at all is a terrible thing! It is bad enough, even in reasonably shallow water, to trust oneself to such a conveyance;

The Pillow-Book — The Storm (on boats)

Sei Shōnagon · c. 1002

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1928

Sei Shōnagon married a government official, Tachibana no Norimitsu, at sixteen and had a son. Courtiers of her day credited her with an excellent memory, and her accounts of court give details such as the clothes people wore, though she wrote them down years after the events. She passed lightly over harder facts, such as the death of her empress, Teishi, in childbirth in 1001.
