Candide (Ch. 30)

Voltaire (François-Marie Arouet) · 1759

Nobody knows exactly when Voltaire wrote Candide. Scholars think he began it as early as 1757 and wrote most of it in late 1758, partly at his house Les Délices near Geneva and partly on a three-week visit to the Elector Palatine at Schwetzingen. He was sixty-four when it was published in secret in five countries at once, by 15 January 1759. The title page claimed it was translated from the German of a Dr. Ralph. It was widely banned straight away.
