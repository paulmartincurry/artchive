Ambassadeurs: Aristide Bruant

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892 · Color lithograph on tan wove paper · 147.2 × 99.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Aristide Bruant left home at fifteen after his father's death and made his name singing in the bistros of Montmartre, where Toulouse-Lautrec became one of his friends. The poster was designed in 1892, when the singer moved to the Café des Ambassadeurs. The sheet is nearly a metre and a half tall.
