Jael and Sisera

Artemisia Gentileschi · 1620 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Fine Arts, Budapest

Free to use

Artemisia carved her signature and the date 1620 into the pillar, signing as Artemisia Lomi, the surname she used in Florence; it is her first known use of the imperfect tense of the Latin verb for making. The painting is first recorded in the imperial collection in Vienna in 1781 and reappeared at auction in Budapest in 1974. It was restored in 1978 after extensive paint loss in two corners.
