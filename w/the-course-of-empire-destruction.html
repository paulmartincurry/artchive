The Course of Empire: Destruction

Thomas Cole · 1836 · Oil on canvas · 100.3 × 161.3 cm · New-York Historical Society, New York

Free to use

The Course of Empire is a series of five paintings that Cole made between 1833 and 1836, tracing the growth and fall of an imaginary city. Destruction is the fourth. A large boulder on a crag above the valley appears in every canvas. Cole quoted lines from Byron's Childe Harold's Pilgrimage in his newspaper advertisements for the series.
