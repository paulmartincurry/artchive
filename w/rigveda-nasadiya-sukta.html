That one breathed, windless, by its own impulse. Other than that there was nothing beyond.

Rigveda — Nasadiya Sukta (10.129)

Rigveda (hymn 10.129) · ca. 1200 BCE

Translated by Wendy Doniger · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Book 10 of the Rigveda, where this hymn sits, is one of the two most recent of its ten books. The whole collection holds 1,028 hymns in Vedic Sanskrit, composed in the northwest of the Indian subcontinent, most likely between about 1500 and 1000 BCE. No written record survives from that time. The hymns were carried by memorisation, from father to son or from teacher to pupil.
