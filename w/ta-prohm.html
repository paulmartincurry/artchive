Ta Prohm

Maker unknown · founded 1186 · Stone

Free to use with credit. Photo: Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 3.0

King Jayavarman VII founded the temple as a Buddhist monastery dedicated to his mother; its foundation stele gives the date 1186. Almost 80,000 people were needed to maintain or attend it, among them 615 dancers. It was built without mortar, and after it was abandoned trees took root in the loosened stones. When restoration began at Angkor in the early twentieth century, the École française d'Extrême-Orient decided to leave Ta Prohm largely as it had been found.
