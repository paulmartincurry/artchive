Now listen to this reed-flute’s deep lament
About the heartache being apart has meant

Masnavi — Song of the Reed

Jalāl al-Dīn Muḥammad Rūmī · ca. 1258 CE

Translated by Jawid Mojaddedi · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

These are the opening lines of the Masnavi, and they existed before the rest of it. Rumi's scribe and student Hussam-e Chalabi suggested, on a walk through the vineyards outside Konya, that he write a long poem in the manner of Sanai and Attar. Rumi took out a sheet of paper with the first eighteen lines already written. He spent the next twelve years dictating the six volumes to Hussam.
