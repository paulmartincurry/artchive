Berthe Morisot · 1873 · Oil on canvas · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The model was the painter's sister Edma, who had trained beside her until she married a naval officer in 1869 and moved to Cherbourg. X-ray examination shows that Edma's eyes first looked straight out of the picture and were then repainted to look down at the book. Berthe Morisot was thirty-two. The canvas is 46 by 72 centimetres, and the museum bought it from a Paris dealer in 1950.
