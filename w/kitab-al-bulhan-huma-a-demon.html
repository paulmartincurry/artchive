Kitab al-Bulhan: Huma, a Demon

Maker unknown · c. 1399–1400 · Illustrated manuscript · Bodleian Libraries, Oxford (MS. Bodl. Or. 133)

Free to use

The Oxford manuscript holds 83 illustrations, many of them full-page. Parts of it were lost, and the rest was rebound in an order that does not always follow the original, so later Ottoman copies have helped scholars reconstruct what is missing.
