Rooster and Hen with Hydrangeas (Colorful Realm of Living Beings)

Itō Jakuchū · c. 1757–65 · Color on silk · Museum of the Imperial Collections (Sannomaru Shōzōkan), Tokyo

Free to use

Jakuchū's father died suddenly in 1739 and left him the family grocery shop in Kyoto at the age of twenty-three. He was so absorbed in painting that he neglected it. In 1756, at forty, he gave up managing the shop, retired and moved to the temple Shōkoku-ji to devote himself to painting.
