Villa of the Mysteries Frieze (Detail: Initiate and Dancing Maenad)

Maker unknown · c. 60–50 BCE · Fresco · H. 162 cm

Free to use

The frieze runs round one room of a villa some 400 metres outside the walls of Pompeii, and this stretch of it is 162 centimetres high. The house was buried by Vesuvius in 79 AD under metres of pumice and ash and took only minor damage; it was uncovered between 1909 and 1910. By then it had become a working farm that made and sold wine, after the earthquake of 62 AD. Who owned it is unknown.
