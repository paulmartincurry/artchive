Mother died today.

The Stranger

Albert Camus · 1942 (English 1946)

Translated by Stuart Gilbert · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Camus completed the first manuscript by May 1941. Gallimard published the book in Paris on 19 May 1942, during the Nazi occupation of France, and only 4,400 copies were printed. Camus was ill with tuberculosis in Oran and could not travel to France. Twenty copies were sent to him and never arrived, and he did not receive one until 17 June.
