To burn always with this hard, gemlike flame, to maintain this ecstasy, is success in life.

The Renaissance (Conclusion)

Walter Pater · 1873

Pater made this Conclusion by reworking the last paragraphs of an essay on the poems of William Morris that he had published in 1868. He had been a fellow of Brasenose College, Oxford, since 1864. After the book came out in 1873 the college chaplain and the Bishop of Oxford both objected to it. He took the Conclusion out of the second edition in 1877 and put it back, slightly changed, in 1888.
