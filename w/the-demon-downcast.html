The Demon Downcast

Mikhail Vrubel · 1902 · Oil on canvas · 139 × 387 cm · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

Vrubel began the sketches in 1901 and squared up the giant canvas with a rope and charcoal. He asked a friend for photographs of the Caucasus and painted the peaks behind the demon the night they arrived. When the picture was shown in Saint Petersburg in early 1902 he came every morning to rework it, changing the face most of all. The Tretyakov Gallery decided not to buy it in 1902 and acquired it in 1908.
