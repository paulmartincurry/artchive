All, save I, were at rest or in enjoyment; I, like the arch-fiend, bore a hell within me, and finding myself unsympathised with, wished to tear up the trees, spread havoc and destruction around me, and then to have sat down and enjoyed the ruin.

Frankenstein — I, like the arch-fiend, bore a hell within me (Ch. 16)

Mary Shelley · 1818

Mary Shelley, Percy Shelley and their son reached Geneva on 14 May 1816. Byron arrived on 25 May and rented the Villa Diodati at Cologny, while Percy rented a smaller house on the waterfront nearby. In 2011 the astronomer Donald Olson, working from the motion of the moon and stars, dated the waking dream that gave her the story to between two and three in the morning on 16 June 1816.
