Napoleon Crossing the Alps

Jacques-Louis David · 1801 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

Napoleon refused to sit for this portrait. Jacques-Louis David worked from a bust for the face, put his own son on top of a ladder for the pose, and borrowed the uniform and hat Napoleon had worn at Marengo. The real crossing was made in fine weather, on a mule led by a guide. The King of Spain commissioned the picture, and the first version took four months, from October 1800 to January 1801. David painted five in all, each about 2.6 by 2.2 metres.
