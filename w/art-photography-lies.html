Art: Photography Lies

Auguste Rodin · 1911

Translated by Mrs. Romilly Fedden (Katharine Waldo Douglas Fedden), 1912

When The Age of Bronze was shown in 1877, it looked so lifelike that Rodin was accused of having cast it from a living model. He had photographs taken of the model to show how the sculpture differed, and a committee of sculptors cleared him. He made his next male nude, St. John the Baptist Preaching, larger than life to avoid the same charge.
