Eleftheria i thanatos

Greek War of Independence · 1820s

The motto arose in the 1820s as a war cry of the Greeks who rose against Ottoman rule, and it is now the motto of Greece. One account of the nine stripes on the Greek flag is that they stand for its nine syllables in Greek.
