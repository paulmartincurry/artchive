Pomona

Julia Margaret Cameron · September 1872 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 36.3 × 26.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1860 Julia Margaret Cameron bought the house next door to Alfred Tennyson at Freshwater on the Isle of Wight and named it Dimbola, after one of her family's coffee plantations in Ceylon. A private gate joined the two properties. She lived there until 1875, three years after she made this photograph, and sold her prints through the London dealers P. & D. Colnaghi.
