“But I don’t want to go among mad people,” Alice remarked.

“Oh, you can’t help that,” said the Cat: “we’re all mad here. I’m mad. You’re mad.”

Alice's Adventures in Wonderland — We're all mad here (Ch. 6)

Lewis Carroll · 1865

The story was first told aloud on 4 July 1862, during a rowing trip of five miles up the river from Oxford with the three young daughters of Henry Liddell. Carroll, thirty years old and a mathematician at Christ Church, began writing it out the next day. Alice Liddell was given the manuscript, with his own illustrations, on 26 November 1864. The printed book is about twice as long and has 42 wood-engraved illustrations by John Tenniel.
