Steps in a Garden

William Henry Millais · 1860 · Watercolor and gouache with scratch-away technique to create highlights on sturdy-weight wove paper mounted to same by artist with color tests in margins of mount · 33.8 x 26 cm (13 5/16 x 10 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

William Henry Millais, brother of John Everett Millais, was close to the founders of the Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood and sketched a great deal from nature in the early 1850s.
