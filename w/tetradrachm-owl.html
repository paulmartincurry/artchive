Tetradrachm: Owl

Maker unknown · 449–407 BCE · Silver · Diameter: 2.3 cm (7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The letters ΑΘΕ are the first three of the city's name, beside Athena's owl. The olive leaves on Athena's helmet and the small crescent moon behind the owl date the coin after the Persian Wars. The same design is back in use on the Greek one-euro coin.
