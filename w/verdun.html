The Artchive

Verdun

Félix Vallotton · 1917 · Oil on canvas · 114 × 146 cm · Musée de l'Armée, Paris

Free to use

When the war began in August 1914 Vallotton volunteered for the army and was rejected because of his age, forty-eight. In June 1917 the Ministry of Fine Arts sent him and two other artists on a three-week tour of the front lines. He painted this in Paris that year and it was shown at the Musée du Luxembourg in October. The Musée de l'Armée bought it in 1976.
