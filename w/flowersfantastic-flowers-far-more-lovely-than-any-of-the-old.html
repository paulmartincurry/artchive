Flowers—Fantastic Flowers, Far More Lovely Than Any of the Old Earth (The Colloquy of Monos and Una)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Harry Clarke was born on 17 March 1889, the younger son of a Dublin church decorator. His way of making the leading of a window part of its design was originally inspired by an early visit to see the stained glass of Chartres Cathedral. The caption under this plate is printed beside the page number 240, and the scan comes from a copy in the Maastricht University Library.
