Studies in Design, Plate XXX

Christopher Dresser · published 1876 · Smithsonian Libraries, Washington

Free to use

Dresser published Studies in Design in 1876, the year the British government sent him to Japan. On the way he gave three lectures in Philadelphia, and over four months in 1876 and 1877 he travelled about 2,000 miles in Japan. This copy of the book is stamped by the Social Art Club of Philadelphia.
