Chimera, Notre-Dame de Paris

Eugène Viollet-le-Duc (restoration design) · 1844–64

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jawed Karim, CC BY-SA 3.0

The creatures on the Galerie des Chimères are not medieval. They were carved during the restoration of 1844 to 1864, directed by Jean-Baptiste Lassus and Eugène Viollet-le-Duc, which followed the interest in the cathedral raised by Victor Hugo's novel of 1831. Unlike gargoyles, chimeras do not serve as rainspouts.
