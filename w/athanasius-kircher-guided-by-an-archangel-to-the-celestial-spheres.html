Athanasius Kircher Guided by an Archangel to the Celestial Spheres

Johann Friedrich Fleischberger · 1660 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Johann Friedrich Fleischberger engraved this image, about 17 by 12 centimetres, for a Latin book printed at Würzburg in 1660; the holder's record names Gaspar Schott alongside him. Schott, also a German Jesuit, had joined the order at nineteen and lived at Würzburg from 1655 until his death in 1666. Kircher was in his late fifties.
