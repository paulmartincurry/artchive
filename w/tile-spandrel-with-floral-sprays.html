Tile Spandrel with Floral Sprays

Maker unknown · c. 1570–75 · Fritware with red slip and underglaze design · 76 x 29.9 x 2.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This spandrel and its missing right half once framed the arch over a niche, window or door. It was made in Iznik, in northwest Turkey, where potters produced tiles for the Ottoman sultans; in the 1550s and 1560s they made tiles for the Süleymaniye Mosque and the mausoleum of Süleyman I. The strong red was a technical feat, achieved with a thick, iron-rich clay slip.
