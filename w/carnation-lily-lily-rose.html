Carnation, Lily, Lily, Rose

John Singer Sargent · 1885–1886 · Oil on canvas · Tate

Free to use

Sargent painted this outdoors in a garden in the Cotswolds, working only in the few minutes each evening when the light was right, from September to November 1885. When the flowers died in the autumn he replaced them with artificial ones. He took it up again the next summer and finished by the end of October 1886, having cut about two feet off the left side of the canvas along the way. The girls are Dolly and Polly Barnard, aged eleven and seven, daughters of an illustrator friend.
