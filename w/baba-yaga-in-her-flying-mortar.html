Baba Yaga in Her Flying Mortar (Vasilisa the Beautiful)

Ivan Bilibin · 1900

Free to use

In 1900, the year of this illustration, Ivan Bilibin finished his law course at the University of Saint Petersburg and also took a lawyer's diploma. He was twenty-three or twenty-four and had been studying painting under Ilya Repin at Princess Maria Tenisheva's school since 1898. His first illustrations for Russian fairy tales had come out the year before. The tale itself was collected by Alexander Afanasyev.
