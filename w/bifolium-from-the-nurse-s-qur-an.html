Bifolium from the Nurse's Qur'an (Mushaf al-Hadina)

Maker unknown · c. 1019–20 (410 AH) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on parchment · 44.5 × 60 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The manuscript was copied and illuminated about 1020 by the calligrapher 'Ali ibn Ahmad al-Warraq for the governess of the Zirid prince al-Mu'izz ibn Badis, and was probably made in Qairawan. Parts of it were kept in the mosque library there before moving to the Raqqada museum. This opened sheet is 44.5 by 60 cm.
