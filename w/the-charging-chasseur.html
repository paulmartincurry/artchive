The Charging Chasseur

Théodore Géricault · c. 1812 · Oil on canvas · 349 × 266 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

This was Géricault's first exhibited work, shown at the Salon of 1812, when he was in his early twenties, and again at the Salon of 1814. He had learned the anatomy and movement of horses in the palace stables at Versailles. The canvas is 349 by 266 centimetres.
