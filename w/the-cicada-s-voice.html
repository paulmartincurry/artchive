Nothing in the cicada’s voice
Gives token of a speedy death.

The Cicada's Voice (Yagate shinu)

Matsuo Bashō · 1690

Translated by Basil Hall Chamberlain, 1902

In 1690 Bashō was about forty-six and away from Edo, where he made his living as a teacher of poetry; he had left the year before and did not come back until the winter of 1691. He thought his best work was not his hokku but the linked verse he composed with other poets.
