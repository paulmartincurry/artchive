Analects (Book I, 1)

Confucius · ca. 5th century BCE

Translated by D. C. Lau, 1979 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

By the traditional account, the Analects began as records that Confucius's disciples kept of their conversations with him, collected and edited together after his death in 479 BCE. Many modern scholars think the book was compiled over about two hundred years. In the second century BCE a version written in characters no longer in use was found hidden in a wall of the house believed to have been his, while the house was being destroyed to enlarge a king's palace.
