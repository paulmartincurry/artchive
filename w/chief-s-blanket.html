Chief's Blanket

Maker unknown · 1855–60 · Wool · 157.5 × 186.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This wool blanket was woven by an unidentified Diné weaver in 1855 to 1860, when such blankets were prized trade goods: in 1850 one sold for $50 in gold. Until 1880 Navajo textiles were all blankets rather than rugs. It measures 157.5 by 186.7 centimetres and was given to the Met in 2019 for its 150th anniversary.
