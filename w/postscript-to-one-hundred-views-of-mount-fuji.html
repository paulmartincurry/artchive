Postscript to One Hundred Views of Mount Fuji

Katsushika Hokusai · 1834

Translated by Henry D. Smith II · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

These lines are the colophon to One Hundred Views of Mount Fuji, a picture book. Hokusai was about seventy-three, and in 1834 he had begun working under a new name, Gakyō Rōjin Manji, the Old Man Mad About Art; he used at least thirty names in his lifetime. Five years later a fire destroyed his studio and much of his work. He went on working almost until his death in 1849, aged 88.
