The Ancient of Days (Frontispiece to Europe a Prophecy)

William Blake · 1794 · Ink on paper · Fitzwilliam Museum, Cambridge

Free to use

The design was first published in 1794 as the frontispiece to Europe a Prophecy, of which thirteen copies are known. Blake coloured each print by hand, so no two are the same. He made many versions of this image. The last was for Frederick Tatham, who paid more for it than Blake had ever been paid for a work, and Blake tinted it propped up in bed only weeks before his death.
