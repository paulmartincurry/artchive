But never met this fellow,
Attended or alone,
Without a tighter breathing,
And zero at the bone.

A narrow Fellow in the Grass

Emily Dickinson · 1865; published 1866

Dickinson kept about two thirds of her poems in manuscript books she made herself, from sheets folded in half, laid one on top of another and bound with string. There are forty of these, and fifteen more sets of folded sheets that were never bound. The ten poems printed in her lifetime appeared anonymously. The rest were left in manuscript at her death in 1886 and published only gradually over the next seven decades.
