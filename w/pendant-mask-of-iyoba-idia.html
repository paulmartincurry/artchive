Pendant Mask of Ìyọ́bà Idià (Queen Mother)

Ìgbèsànmwà (ivory- and wood-carving guild) artists · 16th century · Ivory, iron, brass, coal, traces of iron oxides · H. 9 3/8 × W. 5 × D. 2 1/2 in. (23.8 × 12.7 × 6.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The mask was carved by artists of the Ìgbèsànmwà, the guild of ivory and wood carvers at the Court of Benin, in what is now Nigeria. It is 24 centimetres high. Nelson A. Rockefeller gave it to the Metropolitan Museum as part of the Michael C. Rockefeller Memorial Collection in 1972.
