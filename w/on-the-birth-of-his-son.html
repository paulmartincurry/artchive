Families, when a child is born
Want it to be intelligent.
I, through intelligence,
Having wrecked my whole life,
Only hope the baby will prove
Ignorant and stupid.
Then he will crown a tranquil life
By becoming a Cabinet Minister.

On the Birth of His Son

Su Shi (Su Dongpo) · before 1101; English 1918

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Su Shi, also called Dongpo, came from a literary family in Meishan, Sichuan. He wanted high office but was caught again and again in the court's fights between reformers and conservatives, and spent much of his career in exile; his first, from 1080 to 1086, was to Huangzhou. He once wrote a poem attacking Wang Anshi's government monopoly on salt.
