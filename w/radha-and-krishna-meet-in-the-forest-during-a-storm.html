Radha and Krishna Meet in the Forest during a Storm

Maker unknown · c. 1770 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · Page: 26.1 × 37.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The village women hold a cover over Krishna to keep off the rain, not knowing he can lift a mountain to shelter them all. The page was probably painted around 1770 for a Hindu ruler in eastern India, after Mughal artists had scattered from Delhi following the invasion of 1739. The white cow's belly is coloured orange with henna.
