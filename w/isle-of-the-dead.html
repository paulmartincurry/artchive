Isle of the Dead

Arnold Böcklin · 1880 · Oil on canvas · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

This is the first version, which Arnold Böcklin completed in May 1880 in his studio in Florence. It was painted for his patron Alexander Günther, but Böcklin kept it for himself. A widow, Marie Berna, saw it half finished on the easel and ordered a smaller one with a coffin and a female figure, and Böcklin then added both to this canvas as well. He painted five versions between 1880 and 1886. The title was given by his dealer, Fritz Gurlitt, in 1883.
