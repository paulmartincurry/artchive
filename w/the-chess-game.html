The Chess Game

Sofonisba Anguissola · 1555 · Oil and tempera on canvas · National Museum, Poznań

Free to use

Sofonisba Anguissola was about twenty-three in 1555, the daughter of a noble family in Cremona that had little money. She had trained under two local painters, Bernardino Campi and Bernardino Gatti. Four years later she was recruited to Madrid to teach painting to the Spanish queen, with the rank of lady-in-waiting.
