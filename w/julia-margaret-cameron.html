Julia Margaret Cameron

Henry Herschel Hay Cameron · 1870 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · The Met

Free to use

The sitter is the photographer herself, in her mid-fifties, and the man behind the camera was her son Henry, who also became a photographer. She had taken up the work at 48, after her daughter gave her a camera, and turned a glazed chicken house into her studio at Freshwater on the Isle of Wight. Five years after this portrait she and her husband left for Ceylon, taking a cow, her photographic equipment and two coffins.
