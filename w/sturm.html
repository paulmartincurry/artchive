Sturm (Ein Weberaufstand, Sheet 5)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1897–98 · Etching · Sheet: 37 × 47.9 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Between the births of her sons, Hans in 1892 and Peter in 1896, Kollwitz saw a performance of Gerhart Hauptmann's play The Weavers, about the failed revolt of the Silesian weavers in 1844. She made a cycle of six prints on the theme, three lithographs and three etchings. A pencil note at the lower left of this impression marks it as sheet 5.
