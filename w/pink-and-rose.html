Pink and Rose

William Morris · ca. 1890 · Block-printed in distemper colors · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is a sheet of wallpaper, printed by hand from blocks. Morris was in his mid-fifties around the time of the design, and in 1890 he published his utopian novel News from Nowhere. The next year he founded the Kelmscott Press, which took up his final years. The Metropolitan Museum bought the sheet in 1923.
