Great Footed Hawk (The Birds of America, Plate 16)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1827 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 67.2 × 99.7 cm (26 7/16 × 39 1/4 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The Edinburgh engraver William Lizars cut the first ten plates of the book before his colourists went on strike. In 1827, the year of this plate, Audubon took the work to Robert Havell Jr. in London, who saw it through to its completion in 1838.
