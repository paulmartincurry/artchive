Ship in a Storm

Henry Ossawa Tanner · 1879 · Oil on academy board · H x W: 10 1/16 x 6 in. (25.6 x 15.2 cm) | H x W x D (frame): 13 1/2 × 9 1/2 × 2 in. (34.3 × 24.1 × 5.1 cm) · National Museum of African American History and Culture, Washington

Free to use

It is an early student work, painted for Daniel A. Payne, a leader of the A.M.E. Church and a friend of Tanner's family, and inscribed to him on the back.
