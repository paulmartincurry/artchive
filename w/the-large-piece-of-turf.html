The Large Piece of Turf

Albrecht Dürer · 1503 · Watercolour and body colour, heightened with white body colour · 40.3 × 31.1 cm · Albertina, Vienna

Free to use

The plants shown are those found in late April to early May, which dates the work to that time of year in 1503. It is painted in watercolour and body colour, heightened with white, on a sheet 40.3 centimetres tall. The edges of the paper are damaged.
