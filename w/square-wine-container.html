Square Wine Container (Fangyou)

Maker unknown · c. 1250–1046 BCE · Bronze · 26.7 × 13.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This ritual wine vessel was cast in bronze in the last phase of the Shang dynasty, probably at Anyang in Henan province. Its square body is a rare type among the usual round and ovoid vessels, and its decoration of crested birds alone, without dragons, is uncommon. It stands 26.7 centimetres high. Cleveland bought it in 1963 from S. Kriger, Inc. of Washington.
