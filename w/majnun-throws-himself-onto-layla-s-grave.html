Majnun Throws Himself onto Layla's Grave (Khamsa of Amir Khusraw Dihlavi)

Maker unknown · c. 1450 · Ink and color on paper · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Amir Khusraw finished his Khamsa, five long poems modelled on Nizami's, in 1298; the third of them is the romance of Laila and Majnun. This leaf comes from a copy painted in northern India around 1450, about 150 years later.
