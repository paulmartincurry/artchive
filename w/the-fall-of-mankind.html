The Fall of Mankind

Hans Baldung Grien · 1511 · Chiaroscuro woodcut in two blocks, printed in gray-brown and black; second of two states · Sheet: 38 × 25.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Baldung spent 1503 to 1507 as an assistant in Dürer's studio in Nuremberg, moved back to Strasbourg in 1509, and opened his own workshop. This print of 1511 is a chiaroscuro woodcut, with a second block printing tone. He had first added a tone block to a woodcut the year before.
