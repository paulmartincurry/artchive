Something there is that doesn't love a wall,
That sends the frozen-ground-swell under it,
And spills the upper boulders in the sun;

Mending Wall

Robert Frost · 1914

Public domain in the United States (published 1914); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

The stone wall in this poem stood on Frost's farm in Derry, New Hampshire. The poem was first published in England. In 1912, after years of rejection by American magazines, he had taken his family there, and the publisher David Nutt brought out North of Boston in 1914 with this poem placed first. An American edition followed the next year.
