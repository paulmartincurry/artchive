The Garden of Death

Hugo Simberg · 1896 · Watercolour and gouache on paper · Ateneum Art Museum, Helsinki

Free to use

The sheet is only 16 by 17 centimetres. Hugo Simberg was twenty-two or twenty-three and was studying privately with Akseli Gallen-Kallela at his wilderness studio in Ruovesi, and the same year he went to London. He came back to the subject several times in different techniques, including a larger fresco in Tampere Cathedral. Many authorities in the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland opposed the work.
