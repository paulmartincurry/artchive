Saishoin Temple at Hirosaki

Kawase Hasui · c. 1920 · Woodblock print (ukiyo-e) on mulberry paper (washi), ink with color · 39.3 × 26.4 cm · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

His teacher, Kiyokata Kaburagi, gave him the name Hasui, which can be translated as water gushing from a spring. In 1956 he was named a Living National Treasure of Japan. This print was a gift to the Cooper Hewitt from Dr. Asaka Matsuoka.
