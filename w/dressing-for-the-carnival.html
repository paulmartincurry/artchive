Dressing for the Carnival

Winslow Homer · 1877 · Oil on canvas · 50.8 × 76.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The people in the picture are preparing for the African-American festival known in the South as Jonkonnu and in the North as Pinkster, which involved the costuming of a Harlequin-like figure. Homer painted it in 1877, when he was forty or forty-one. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1922 with the Amelia B. Lazarus Fund.
