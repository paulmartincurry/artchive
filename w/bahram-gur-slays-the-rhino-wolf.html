Bahram Gur Slays the Rhino-Wolf (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to 'Abd al-'Aziz, under Aqa Mirak · c. 1530–35 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.5 × 18.7 cm; page 47.1 × 31.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met attributes the painting to 'Abd al-'Aziz, with Aqa Mirak as director of the workshop. Aqa Mirak was a painter and companion to Shah Tahmasp; he later lived in Mashhad from 1555 to 1565 and then in Qazvin, where he died in 1576.
