Harmony in Blue and Gold: The Peacock Room

James McNeill Whistler · 1876-1877 · Oil paint and gold leaf on canvas, leather, mosaic tile, glass, and wood · H x W x D (overall): 421.6 x 613.4 x 1026.2 cm (166 x 241 1/2 x 404 in) · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art), Washington

Free to use

It was the dining room of Frederick Richards Leyland, a Liverpool shipowner and Whistler's chief patron, in his London house. The architect Thomas Jeckyll designed it, but illness made him abandon the work, and Whistler, who was decorating the entrance hall, offered to finish it.
