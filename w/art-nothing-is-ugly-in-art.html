There is nothing ugly in art except that which is without character, that is to say, that which offers no outer or inner truth.

Art: Nothing Is Ugly in Art

Auguste Rodin · 1911

Translated by Mrs. Romilly Fedden (Katharine Waldo Douglas Fedden), 1912

Rodin and Rose Beuret bought a country estate at Meudon in 1897, and their guests there included King Edward VII, the dancer Isadora Duncan and the harpsichordist Wanda Landowska. The poet Rainer Maria Rilke stayed with Rodin in 1905 and 1906 and did administrative work for him. Rodin married Beuret on 29 January 1917, after fifty-three years together, and she died two weeks later.
