Akbar with Lion and Calf (Shah Jahan Album)

Govardhan · c. 1630 (calligraphy on reverse c. 1530–50) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · 38.9 × 25.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Govardhan painted this leaf around 1630. His father, Bhavani Das, had been a minor painter in the imperial workshop; Govardhan joined it under Akbar and worked on into the reign of Shah Jahan, and the painter Daulat made a portrait of him. On the facing side is calligraphy by Mir 'Ali Haravi, a century older.
