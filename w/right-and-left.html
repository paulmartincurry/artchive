Right and Left

Winslow Homer · 1909 · Oil on canvas · 71.8 × 122.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Homer had a mild stroke in May 1908, and this is probably the picture he told his brother he was painting in a letter of 8 December that year. He consigned it to his New York dealer in January 1909. His biographers disagree about the ducks: one says he bought them for his Thanksgiving dinner, another that a friend shot them and hung them on his studio door. He died less than two years after finishing it.
