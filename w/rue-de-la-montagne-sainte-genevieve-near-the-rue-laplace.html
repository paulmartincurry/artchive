Rue de la Montagne-Sainte-Geneviève near the Rue Laplace

Charles Marville · 1865/69 · Albumen print · Image/paper: 31.6 × 26.9 cm (12 1/2 × 10 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The city commissioned Marville to photograph streets marked for demolition under Haussmann, and also the gas lamps and kiosks of the new city; he made more than 400 such pictures. His old-street views were later shown beside the new ones at the Exposition Universelle of 1878. The mount carries his blind stamp as photographer to the Imperial Museum of the Louvre. He died in 1879 while documenting the rebuilding of the Hôtel de Ville, destroyed during the Paris Commune.
