The World was all before them, where to choose
Thir place of rest, and Providence thir guide:
They hand in hand with wandring steps and slow,
Through Eden took thir solitarie way.

Paradise Lost — The World Was All Before Them (Book XII, closing lines)

John Milton · 1667

Milton went blind in 1652 and composed Paradise Lost entirely through dictation, with the help of amanuenses and friends. The first version, published in 1667, had ten books; the second edition of 1674 rearranged it into twelve.
