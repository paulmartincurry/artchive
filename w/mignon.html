Do you know the land where the lemon-trees grow,
In darkened leaves the gold-oranges glow,
A soft wind blows from the pure blue sky,
The myrtle stands mute, and the bay tree high?

Mignon (Kennst du das Land)

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · published 1795–96

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The song opens the third book of Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship, published in 1795–96. Goethe wrote it before his journey to Italy, and it already stood in the early draft of the novel, Wilhelm Meister's Theatrical Mission, which was not published in his lifetime.
