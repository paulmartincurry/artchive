The Hand of God

Auguste Rodin · original model before 1895, carved ca. 1907 · Marble · The Met

Free to use

Carved in marble around 1907 from a model made before 1895, the block weighs 230 kilograms and stands 74 centimetres high. Edward D. Adams gave it to the museum in 1908, when Rodin was in his late sixties.
