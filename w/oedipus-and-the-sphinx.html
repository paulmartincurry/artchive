Oedipus and the Sphinx

Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres · 1808 (enlarged 1827) · Oil on canvas · Louvre

Free to use

This began as a student exercise. Ingres was in Rome on the Prix de Rome and had to send work back to Paris to be judged, and in 1808 he sent a life-size figure of Oedipus; the academicians found fault with the light and thought the figure not idealized enough. He kept the canvas in his studio for years. Around 1825 he added 20 centimetres to the left edge and 31 to the top and the right, painted in the fleeing man and the human remains, and showed the result at the Salon of 1827. He sold it two years later.
