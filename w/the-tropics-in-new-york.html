A wave of longing through my body swept,
And, hungry for the old, familiar ways,
I turned aside and bowed my head and wept.

The Tropics in New York

Claude McKay · 1922

McKay was born in Jamaica and first travelled to the United States to attend college. Between 1914 and 1919 he managed a Brooklyn restaurant, which failed, then worked as a waiter at a hotel in Hanover, New Hampshire, at a Manhattan women's club, and longest on the Pennsylvania Railroad. Harlem Shadows, his collection of 1922, was among the first books published during the Harlem Renaissance.
