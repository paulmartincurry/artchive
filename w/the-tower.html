The Tower (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Smith gave every card in the deck a full scene with figures, the numbered suit cards included. Before the tarot she had illustrated the verses of W. B. Yeats and a book by Bram Stoker, written two collections of Jamaican folk tales, and run her own magazine, The Green Sheaf, for thirteen issues.
