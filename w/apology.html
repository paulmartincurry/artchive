The difficulty, my friends, is not to avoid death, but to avoid unrighteousness;
for that runs faster than death.

Apology

Socrates (via Plato) · 399 BCE

Plato, who was about thirty at the time of the trial, was in the court, and Socrates mentions his presence twice in the speech. The jury was perhaps 500 Athenian men, and the three accusers were Anytus, Meletus and Lycon. Defences of Socrates by several writers appeared in the decade after his trial, and Plato's is one of them. Socrates was the son of a stoneworker and a midwife, and twenty-four years earlier Aristophanes had mocked him on stage in The Clouds.
