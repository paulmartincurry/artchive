Leaf from a Beatus Manuscript: Christ in Majesty with Angels

Maker unknown · ca. 1180 · Tempera, gold, and ink on parchment · Overall (folio): 17 1/2 × 11 13/16 in. (44.4 × 30 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This single leaf was cut from a Spanish manuscript and is about forty-four centimetres tall. Besides Christ in Majesty, it shows an angel directing Saint John to write the Book of Revelation. It was bought for The Cloisters in 1991.
