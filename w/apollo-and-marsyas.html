Apollo and Marsyas

Jusepe de Ribera · 1637 · Oil on canvas · 182 × 232 cm · Museo di Capodimonte, Naples

Free to use

Ribera painted this subject twice in 1637; the other canvas is in Brussels. This one is signed and dated at the bottom right. It is first recorded in a catalogue of the Avalos collection made between 1650 and 1700, and Giovanni d'Avalos was probably the man who commissioned it. Why Ribera painted several versions at the same time is not known.
