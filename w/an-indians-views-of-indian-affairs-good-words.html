Words do not pay for my dead people. They do not pay for my country, now overrun by white men. They do not protect my father's grave. They do not pay for all my horses and cattle. Good words will not give me back my children.

An Indian’s Views of Indian Affairs — Good Words

Chief Joseph (Hin-mah-too-yah-lat-kekt) · 1879

Chief Joseph gave this account in Washington, and the North American Review printed it in April 1879 with an introduction by the Missionary Bishop of Niobrara. After the surrender that ended the war of 1877, the 418 Nez Perce who had given themselves up, women and children among them, were sent by train to Fort Leavenworth, Kansas, as prisoners.
