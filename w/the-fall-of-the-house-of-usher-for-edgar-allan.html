The Fall of the House of Usher, for Edgar Allan Poe's Tales of Mystery and the Imagination

Aubrey Beardsley · 1894 · Pen, brush and India ink over graphite · 25.4 × 16.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This drawing was one of a set Beardsley made in 1894 for a Chicago edition of Poe's tales. Four years later he died of tuberculosis in a hotel in Menton, aged twenty-five. His last letter begged his publisher Leonard Smithers to destroy his obscene drawings; Smithers kept selling reproductions and forgeries of his work instead.
