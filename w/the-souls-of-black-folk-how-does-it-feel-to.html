To the real question, How does it feel to be a problem? I answer seldom a word.

The Souls of Black Folk — How does it feel to be a problem? (Ch. I)

W. E. B. Du Bois · 1903

Du Bois had been professor of history and economics at Atlanta University since July 1897 when the book came out. Each of its fourteen chapters opens with two epigraphs, lines of poetry and the musical score of a spiritual, printed without the spiritual's words. James Weldon Johnson said the book's effect on African Americans was comparable to that of Uncle Tom's Cabin.
