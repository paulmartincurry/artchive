Hawk at Sunrise

Jeong Hong-rae · second half 18th century–first half 19th century · Hanging scroll, ink and color on silk · 113 × 59.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Jeong Hong-rae was born in 1720, and the Met dates this scroll broadly, from the second half of the eighteenth century to the first half of the nineteenth. The painted silk is 113 centimetres tall. The museum bought it in 2022.
