Notre-Dame de Paris, West Façade

Maker unknown · 1163–1345 · Limestone and marble · L. 128 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: DXR / Daniel Vorndran, CC BY-SA 3.0

The cornerstone was laid in 1163 under Bishop Maurice de Sully, and the cathedral was largely complete by 1260. The names of the masters who built it have been lost. In the 1790s, during the French Revolution, much of its religious imagery was damaged or destroyed. A fire in April 2019 closed it for repairs until December 2024; the stones of the fallen vaults were found to have been quarried in the Vexin, north-west of Paris.
