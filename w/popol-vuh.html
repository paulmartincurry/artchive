Over a universe wrapped in the gloom of a dense and primeval night
passed the god Hurakan, the mighty wind. He called out "earth,"
and the solid land appeared.

Popol Vuh

Lewis Spence, retelling the Popol Vuh · Pre-Columbian (transcribed ca. 1550)

The Popol Vuh was passed on by word of mouth among the Kʼicheʼ people of Guatemala until about 1550, when it was written down. The oldest text known is a manuscript by Francisco Ximénez, a Dominican priest who came to the town of Santo Tomás Chichicastenango in 1701. He set the Kʼicheʼ and his Spanish translation side by side in parallel columns. The manuscript he is thought to have borrowed from a parishioner has never been found.
