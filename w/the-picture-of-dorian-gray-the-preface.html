We can forgive a man for making a useful thing as long as he does not admire it. The only excuse for making a useless thing is that one admires it intensely.

All art is quite useless.

The Picture of Dorian Gray — The Preface

Oscar Wilde · 1891

The preface was not part of the story as first printed in a magazine in 1890. Wilde wrote it after that version was attacked, answering his critics indirectly in a string of epigrams, and published it in The Fortnightly Review. A month later, in April 1891, it appeared at the front of the book, which he had enlarged from thirteen chapters to twenty. Before writing it he had reviewed a translation of the Chinese philosopher Zhuang Zhou.
