The Death of Sardanapalus

Eugène Delacroix · 1827 · Oil on canvas · Department of Paintings of the Louvre

Free to use

Delacroix took the subject from Lord Byron's play Sardanapalus, published six years before he showed the painting at the Paris Salon of 1827, where it divided opinion. In 1844 he made a smaller replica, now in Philadelphia. Hector Berlioz wrote a cantata on the subject in 1830, and Franz Liszt began an opera after seeing the painting and abandoned it with one act complete.
