Merz 3 Portfolio, Plate 1

Kurt Schwitters · 1923 · lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The six lithographs of the Merz portfolio were made at the Molling printing plant, where Schwitters salvaged freshly printed scraps: patterned paper, pieces of illustrations, cigarette wrappers, advertisements and letters. He collaged them while the oily ink was still wet and transferred the images to the lithographic stone. He had taken the name Merz from a fragment of the words Commerz und Privatbank in Das Merzbild, a collage finished in the winter of 1918–19. Cleveland bought this plate in 1995.
