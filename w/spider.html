Spider

Odilon Redon · 1887 · Lithograph on ivory China paper laid down on ivory wove paper · Chine: 33 × 27 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Redon called his works in shades of black his noirs. He was little known until 1884, when a novel by Joris-Karl Huysmans gave its hero a collection of his drawings, three years before this lithograph. It is printed on China paper laid down on a wove sheet 44.7 centimetres tall.
