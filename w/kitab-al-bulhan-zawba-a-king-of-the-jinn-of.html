Kitab al-Bulhan: Zawba'a, King of the Jinn of Friday

Maker unknown · c. 1399–1400 · Illustrated manuscript · Bodleian Libraries, Oxford (MS. Bodl. Or. 133)

Free to use

Zawba'a is the king of the jinn for Friday. In the early seventeenth century the manuscript belonged to Yahya ibn Muhammad in Edirne. It reached England and entered the Bodleian Library in 1717 with the bequest of Nathaniel Palmer.
