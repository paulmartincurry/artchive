Prometheus Bound

Peter Paul Rubens and Frans Snyders · c. 1611–1612, completed by 1618 · Oil on canvas · 242.6 × 209.6 cm · Philadelphia Museum of Art

Free to use

Rubens began this canvas, 242.6 by 209.6 centimetres, around 1611–1612 and finished it by 1618; Frans Snyders painted the eagle. In 1618 Rubens traded it with a group of other paintings to the English collector Sir Dudley Carleton for his collection of classical statues, pricing it in a letter at 500 florins. The museum bought it in 1950.
