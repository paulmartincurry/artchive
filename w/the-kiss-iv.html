The Kiss IV

Edvard Munch · 1902 · Woodcut printed from two blocks (one sawn into two sections) in gray-brown and black ink on cream Japanese paper · Image: 47.1 × 47.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch worked on the motif of a kissing couple from as early as 1888–89. This version of 1902 was printed from two woodblocks, one of them sawn into two sections, in grey-brown and black ink on Japanese paper. The image is almost square, 47.1 by 47.2 centimetres.
