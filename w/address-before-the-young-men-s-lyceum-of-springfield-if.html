If destruction be our lot, we must ourselves be its author and finisher. As a nation of freemen, we must live through all time, or die by suicide.

Address before the Young Men's Lyceum of Springfield — If destruction be our lot

Abraham Lincoln · January 27, 1838

Lincoln was twenty-eight when he gave this address to the Young Men's Lyceum of Springfield, Illinois, on January 27, 1838. Its title was The Perpetuation of Our Political Institutions. Three months earlier a pro-slavery mob in nearby Alton had murdered the newspaper editor Elijah Lovejoy, and Lincoln referred to the death in the speech.
