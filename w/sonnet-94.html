For sweetest things turn sourest by their deeds;
Lilies that fester, smell far worse than weeds.

Sonnet 94

William Shakespeare · 1609

The sonnets were first published all together in a quarto in 1609. There is evidence, in a note on the title page of one of the extant copies, that the actor Edward Alleyn bought a copy in June 1609 for one shilling.
