A crust of bread and a corner to sleep in,
A minute to smile and an hour to weep in,
A pint of joy to a peck of trouble,
And never a laugh but the moans come double;
And that is life!

Life

Paul Laurence Dunbar · 1896 (Lyrics of Lowly Life)

Dunbar wrote his first poem at six and published his first poems at sixteen, in Dayton's The Herald. In 1890 he wrote and edited The Tattler, Dayton's first weekly African-American newspaper, printed by his high-school acquaintances Wilbur and Orville Wright; it lasted six weeks.
