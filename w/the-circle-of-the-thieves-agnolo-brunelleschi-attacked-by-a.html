The Circle of the Thieves; Agnolo Brunelleschi Attacked by a Six-Footed Serpent (Dante's Divine Comedy, Plate 4)

William Blake · 1827 · Engraving · Sheet: 39 × 54.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is plate 4 of the Dante engravings and illustrates canto XXV of the Inferno. The commission reached Blake in 1826, in his late sixties, when he was living at Fountain Court off the Strand, a property demolished in the 1880s when the Savoy Hotel was built. He was still working on the Dante series on 12 August 1827, the day he died.
