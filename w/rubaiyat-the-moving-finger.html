Rubáiyát — The Moving Finger

Omar Khayyam (trans. Edward FitzGerald) · ca. 1100 CE (trans. 1859)

A Latin version of one quatrain, printed in 1700, was the first notice Europe took of Omar Khayyam. Edward FitzGerald's English Rubaiyat of 1859 did not sell at first; it was taken up from 1861 onward, admired by the Pre-Raphaelites, and by the 1880s there were Omar Khayyam Clubs across the English-speaking world. Agatha Christie took the title of a 1942 novel from this quatrain, and Martin Luther King cited it in his speech Beyond Vietnam.
