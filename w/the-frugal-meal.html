The Frugal Meal (The Saltimbanques)

Pablo Picasso · September 1904, printed 1913 · Etching · Plate 46.3 × 37.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

To make this etching, Pablo Picasso recycled a zinc plate previously used by a friend. He worked on it in September 1904, the month before he turned twenty-three. This impression was printed in 1913 by Louis Fort and published by Ambroise Vollard. Cleveland bought it from the Kraushaar galleries in New York in 1929.
