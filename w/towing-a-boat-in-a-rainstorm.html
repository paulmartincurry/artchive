Towing a Boat in a Rainstorm

Maker unknown · late 12th century · Fan mounted as an album leaf, ink and color on silk · 24.4 × 26 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

An unidentified Song painter made this scene on a silk fan about 24 by 26 centimetres, later mounted flat as an album leaf. It belonged to the collector A. W. Bahr, and the Met bought it from his collection in 1947.
