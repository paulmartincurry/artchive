More Maxims

François de La Rochefoucauld · 1665 (1678 Definitive Edition)

Translated by J. W. Willis Bund and J. Hain Friswell, 1871

La Rochefoucauld was a duke and a soldier. In 1652, fighting in the Faubourg Saint-Antoine, he was shot through the head; it was feared he would lose his sight, and he was a year recovering. The maxims came out of the salon of Madame de Sablé, whose literary work was the writing of such sentences. He published them anonymously in 1665 and kept altering and adding to them through four more editions, the last in 1678. The full count now printed is 504, most of them two or three lines long.
