Max Schmitt in a Single Scull (The Champion Single Sculls)

Thomas Eakins · 1871 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Free to use

Thomas Eakins came home to Philadelphia in July 1870 after four years of study in Paris, and that October he watched his school friend Max Schmitt win the single sculls race on the Schuylkill in a record twenty minutes. He put himself in the picture as the rower in the middle distance and signed it on the stern of his own boat. It was shown once in his lifetime, for four days in April 1871. He gave it to Schmitt, and the Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1934.
