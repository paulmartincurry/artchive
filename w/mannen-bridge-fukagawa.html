Mannen Bridge, Fukagawa (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A turtle hangs from the handle of a tub, offered for life release. Beyond it are Mannen Bridge and Mount Fuji. The print dates from 1857, the year before Hiroshige's death.
