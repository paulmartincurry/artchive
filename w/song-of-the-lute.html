Song of the Lute (Pipa xing)

Bai Juyi (Po Chü-i) · 816 CE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

After the prime minister was assassinated in July 815, Bai Juyi sent a memorial to the emperor, a breach of protocol for an official of his minor rank. He was demoted to Sub-Prefect and banished from the court and capital to Jiujiang, then called Xun Yang, on the southern shore of the Yangtze River. He was about forty-four in 816 and stayed there three years before being sent on to govern a remote place in Sichuan.
