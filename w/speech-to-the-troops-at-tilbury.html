I know I have the body but of a weak and feeble woman; but I have the heart and stomach of a king, and of a king of England too

Speech to the Troops at Tilbury

Queen Elizabeth I · August 9, 1588

Elizabeth I was fifty-four when she spoke to her army at Tilbury in Essex. The Armada had been driven from the Strait of Dover eleven days before, and the troops were sent home two days afterwards. She went among them with an escort of six men, dressed in white with a silver breastplate and riding a grey gelding. The wording comes from a letter by Leonel Sharp, a clergyman who was at Tilbury that day, and was not printed until 1654.
