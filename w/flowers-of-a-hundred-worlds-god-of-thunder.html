Flowers of a Hundred Worlds (Momoyogusa): God of Thunder (Raijin)

Kamisaka Sekka · 1909 · Ink and colour on paper · 27.3 × 39.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is one of Kamisaka Sekka's preparatory drawings for Momoyogusa, made in ink and colour on tracing paper. The museum notes that the sketches are much looser than the printed compositions. Sekka was born in Kyoto to a samurai family, and in 1901 the Japanese government sent him to Glasgow. Cleveland bought the drawing from a Kyoto dealer in 1989.
