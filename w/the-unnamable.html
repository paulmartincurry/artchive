The Unnamable

Samuel Beckett · 1953 (English 1958)

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Samuel Beckett was forty-seven when this novel appeared in 1953. He was a Dubliner who had settled permanently in Paris before the war, and he wrote in both French and English. During the Second World War he had been a member of a French Resistance group, for which he was awarded the Croix de Guerre in 1945.
