I am the man of gloom — the widower — the unconsoled

El Desdichado (The Disinherited)

Gérard de Nerval · 1854

Translated by Richard Sieburth · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This poem was printed in 1854 in Les Filles du feu, a volume of short stories that also carried a sequence of twelve sonnets. Gérard de Nerval was the pen name of Gérard Labrunie, taken from a property near Loisy that had belonged to his family. He had spent months in a Montmartre clinic after two breakdowns in 1841, and his last years were spent in poverty. He hanged himself in Paris during the night of 26 January 1855, aged forty-six.
