To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.

Ulysses

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1833 (pub. 1842)

On 1 October 1833 Tennyson, aged twenty-four, heard that his friend Arthur Hallam had died. He was living at Somersby in Lincolnshire in cramped rooms with his mother and nine of his siblings. He finished these seventy lines of blank verse nineteen days later, on 20 October, and kept them back for nine years, until his Poems of 1842. Unlike much of his work, the poem was never revised after it was printed. His main model for the hero was Dante's Ulisse rather than Homer's.
