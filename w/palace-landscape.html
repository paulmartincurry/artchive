Palace Landscape

Maker unknown · 1000s · Album leaf; ink, color, and gold on silk · 33 × 41.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painter of this Northern Song leaf is not known; it is painted in ink, colour and gold on silk. It was lot 1 in a Sotheby's sale in New York on 5 March 1971, consigned by the collector Jiang Ershi, and Cleveland bought it through Peter Marks the same year.
