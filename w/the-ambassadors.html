The Ambassadors

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1533 · Oil on oak panel · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The panel is just over two metres each way, 207 by 209.5 centimetres. Inscriptions in the picture give the sitters' ages: 29 on Jean de Dinteville's dagger and 25 on the book under Georges de Selve's arm. Dinteville was French ambassador to the court of Henry VIII for most of 1533, and de Selve visited him in London that spring. The National Gallery bought the painting in 1890. Part of the skull's jaw and nasal cavity had been lost by the 1997 restoration, and restorers rebuilt it from a distorted photograph of a modern skull.
