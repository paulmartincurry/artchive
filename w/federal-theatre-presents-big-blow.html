Federal Theatre Presents Big Blow

Richard Halls · 1936–39 · Colour silkscreen on board · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Richard Halls made this silkscreen for Theodore Pratt's hurricane drama Big Blow at Maxine Elliott's Theatre on West 39th Street in New York. A date stamp on the back reads 28 June 1939, two days before Congress cut off the Federal Theatre Project's funding. The sheet measures 56 by 35.5 centimetres.
