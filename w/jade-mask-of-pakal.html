Jade Mask of Pakal

Maker unknown · 683 · Jade, with shell, nacre and obsidian · National Museum of Anthropology, Mexico City

More than 346 pieces of green jade make up this mask, which is 25.6 cm high. The king K'inich Janaab' Pakal was still wearing it when the archaeologist Alberto Ruz Lhuillier opened his stone sarcophagus in 1952, in a burial chamber inside the Temple of the Inscriptions at Palenque. Jade necklaces and bracelets were buried with him, and more jade was in his hands.
