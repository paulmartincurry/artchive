Witches at their Incantations

Salvator Rosa · about 1646 · Oil on canvas · 72 × 132 cm · National Gallery, London

Free to use

Salvator Rosa probably painted this for the Florentine banker and collector Carlo Rossi. A letter by Rosa records that it hung behind a curtain at the end of a long gallery, so that it could be revealed suddenly to visitors who did not know it was there. Rosa had settled in Florence in 1640, among a circle of intellectuals, scientists and artists drawn to the occult. The canvas is 72 by 132 centimetres, and the National Gallery bought it in 1984.
