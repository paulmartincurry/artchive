Doge Leonardo Loredan

Giovanni Bellini · c. 1501–02 · Oil on poplar panel · National Gallery, London

Free to use

Leonardo Loredan was Doge of Venice from 1501 to 1521, and Bellini painted him around 1501–02. He signed the small paper fixed to the parapet with the Latin form of his name. The panel was probably looted when Napoleon took Venice; William Beckford bought it in 1807 for thirteen guineas and sold it to the National Gallery in 1844 for £630.
