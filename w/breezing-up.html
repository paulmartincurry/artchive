Breezing Up (A Fair Wind)

Winslow Homer · 1873–1876 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Homer began the canvas in New York in 1873, working from sketches made at Gloucester, Massachusetts, and kept at it for three years. Infrared imaging shows what he took out along the way: a fourth boy near the mast and a second schooner in the distance. An anchor now sits in the bow where a boy had been. His own title was A Fair Wind; by 1879 the picture was being called Breezing Up, a name he did not give it.
