We must not look at goblin men,
We must not buy their fruits.

Goblin Market

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Rossetti composed the poem in the spring of 1859, at twenty-eight, and shortly afterwards began volunteering at Highgate Penitentiary. In public she often said it was written for children; in a letter to her publisher she said it was not. Her brother Dante Gabriel designed the binding, frontispiece and title page of the 1862 first edition. In 1880 she gave the composer Emanuel Aguilar the exclusive right to set it to music, and his cantata was the only setting made in the nineteenth century.
