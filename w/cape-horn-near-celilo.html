Cape Horn near Celilo

Carleton Watkins · 1867 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 40 × 52.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Carleton Watkins worked with a mammoth-plate camera that took glass plates of 18 by 22 inches, and this print is 40 by 52.4 centimetres. In 1867, the year of this view, he opened his first public gallery and sent photographs to the Universal Exposition in Paris, where he won a medal. He had come to San Francisco in 1851 hoping to find gold.
