Such as eternity at last transforms into Himself,
The Poet rouses with two-edged naked sword,
His century terrified at having ignored
Death triumphant in so strange a voice!

The Tomb of Edgar Allan Poe (Le Tombeau d'Edgar Poe)

Stéphane Mallarmé · published 1887

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The sonnet was collected in Poésies, first published in 1887; enlarged editions followed in 1899 and 1913. Mallarmé died at Valvins, near Fontainebleau, on 9 September 1898.
