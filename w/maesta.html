Maestà (front)

Duccio di Buoninsegna · 1308–1311 · Tempera and gold on wood · Museo dell'Opera del Duomo, Siena

Free to use

The city of Siena commissioned the altarpiece from Duccio in 1308, and he painted it with assistants in a studio on Via Stalloreggi, close to the cathedral. It was painted on both sides so it could be seen from front and back on the high altar. On 9 June 1311 it was carried in procession around the city and into the cathedral, and the workshops stayed closed for the day. In 1771 it was sawn apart to be shared between two altars; panels from it are now in London, Washington, New York, Madrid and Fort Worth.
