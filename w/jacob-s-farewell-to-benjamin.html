Jacob's Farewell to Benjamin

Follower of Rembrandt · c. 1655 · Oil on canvas · 113 × 110.5 cm (52 9/16 × 43 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The scene is read as Jacob letting his youngest son Benjamin go to Egypt with his brothers; the boy's turned back conveys the father's mixed feelings.
