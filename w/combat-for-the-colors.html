Combat for the Colors

Édouard Detaille · 1874 · Oil on wood panel · Unframed: 32.5 × 22.6 cm (12 13/16 × 8 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The panel is small, under 33 centimetres high, and Detaille signed and dated it in black paint. He was about twenty-five. Four years earlier he had enlisted in the Eighth Battalion of the Mobiles when the Franco-Prussian War broke out, and he recorded enemy positions for General Appert. In 1872 the Salon refused two of his war paintings to avoid embarrassing the German government.
