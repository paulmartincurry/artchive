Ruins of an Ancient City

John Martin · c. 1810–20 · Oil on paper, mounted on canvas · 95.6 × 118.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

John Martin began his career as a coach and ceramics painter. This picture is in oil on paper mounted on canvas, about 96 by 119 centimetres without its frame, and he signed it at the lower right. He sent his first oil painting to the Royal Academy in 1810, and it was not hung.
