I want to go on living even after my death!

The Diary of a Young Girl

Anne Frank · 1942–1944 (English 1952)

Translated by B. M. Mooyaart-Doubleday · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Anne Frank kept the diary in Dutch during two years in hiding with her family under the Nazi occupation of the Netherlands. The family was apprehended in 1944, and she died of typhus in the Bergen-Belsen concentration camp in 1945. Miep Gies and Bep Voskuijl retrieved the diaries, and the book was first published in Amsterdam in 1947.
