Robert Fludd · 1617 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Fludd was a physician who made repeated attempts to enter the College of Physicians in London, where examiners objected to his contempt for traditional medical authorities. He died in London on 8 September 1637 and was buried at Bearsted in Kent, where he was born.
