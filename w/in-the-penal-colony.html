In the Penal Colony

Franz Kafka · 1914 (Published 1919)

Translated by Ian Johnston · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line on purpose.

Kafka wrote the story in October 1914, two months after the war began and after he had moved, at thirty-one, into his sister's empty apartment to live alone for the first time. He revised it in November 1918, and Kurt Wolff published it as a book in October 1919, five years after it was written.
