Now is the winter of our discontent
Made glorious summer by this son of York;

Richard III — Now Is the Winter of Our Discontent (Act I, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · 1593

Richard III was probably written between about 1592 and 1594. By word count it is the second longest of Shakespeare's plays and the longest in the First Folio. The only contemporary mention of a deformity in the real king is that his right shoulder sat slightly higher than his left; his remains, found in 2012, showed scoliosis of the spine.
