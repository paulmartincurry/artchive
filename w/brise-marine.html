The flesh is sad, alas! – and I’ve read all the books.

Brise Marine (Sea Breeze)

Stéphane Mallarmé · 1865

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Stéphane Mallarmé was twenty-two or twenty-three in the year of this poem, and had been married for two years. His daughter Geneviève had been born the year before. He earned his living as an English teacher and spent much of his life in relative poverty.
