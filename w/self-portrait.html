Self-Portrait

José María Velasco · 1877 · Oil on Canvas · Museo Nacional de Arte, Mexico City

Free to use with credit. Photo: José Luiz Bernardes Ribeiro, CC BY-SA 4.0

José María Velasco studied zoology, botany, mathematics, geology and surveying before he became a student of painting. His landscape teacher at the Academy of San Carlos, the Italian Eugenio Landesio, left in 1877, when Velasco was in his late thirties. Two years later Velasco described a new species of salamander from a lake north of Mexico City and published it in a scientific journal.
