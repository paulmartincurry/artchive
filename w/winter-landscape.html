Winter Landscape (Autumn and Winter Landscapes)

Sesshū Tōyō · late 15th–early 16th century · Ink on paper · 47.7 × 30.2 cm · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

The two scrolls were handed down in the Kyoto temple Manshu-in as Summer and Winter Landscapes, and it is thought that they were two of four showing the four seasons. In this one a single figure, who has just left a boat, climbs towards the buildings. Each scroll measures 47.7 by 30.2 centimetres.
