Sample Book Containing 125 Swatches

Wiener Werkstätte · about 1910 · Silk or wool, plain weave; screen printed · 13.1 × 19.1 × 4.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This sample book holds 125 screen-printed swatches of silk and wool, some still with the makers' tags naming the design and designer. It reportedly belonged to Bruno Paul. The Wiener Werkstätte was founded in 1903 by Koloman Moser, Josef Hoffmann and their backer Fritz Waerndorfer, and it began in three small rooms in Vienna.
