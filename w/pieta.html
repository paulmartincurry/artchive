Pietà

Michelangelo · 1499 · Marble · St. Peter's Basilica

Free to use with credit. Photo: Stanislav Traykov, CC BY 2.5

A French cardinal, then his country's ambassador in Rome, ordered this sculpture for his funeral chapel in Old St Peter's. It is the only work Michelangelo ever signed, and he carved the words on the sash across Mary's chest. Four fingers of her left hand broke during a move and were restored in 1736. On 21 May 1972 a man struck the marble fifteen times with a geologist's hammer. Mary's nose was not among the pieces returned, and it was rebuilt from a block cut out of her back.
