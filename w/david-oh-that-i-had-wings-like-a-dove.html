David: Oh, That I Had Wings like a Dove

Frederic Leighton · 1865 · Oil on fabric · Framed: 125 x 152.4 x 4.5 cm (49 3/16 x 60 x 1 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Leighton chose David's moment of gravest doubt: he has just heard that his rebellious son Absalom has been killed by his own army, and has gone up to a chamber over the city gate. His crown lies on the floor.
