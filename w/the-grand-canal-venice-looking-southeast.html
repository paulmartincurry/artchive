The Grand Canal, Venice, Looking Southeast

Canaletto · 1730s · Oil on canvas · 18 1/2 × 30 5/8 in. (47 × 77.8 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Canaletto was in his thirties when he painted this view, which is 47 by 78 centimetres. In the late 1720s Owen Swiny had encouraged him to paint small views of Venice with an appeal for tourists and foreign visitors, and many of his pictures were sold to Englishmen on the Grand Tour. He was the son of a theatrical scene painter and had begun by designing sets for operas. The painting came to the museum in 2019 as a bequest from Mrs. Charles Wrightsman.
