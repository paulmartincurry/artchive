Utriusque Cosmi Historia: The Monochord

Robert Fludd · 1617–18 · Engraving · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Fludd believed, like Pythagoras and his followers, that numbers held great hidden secrets, and that certainty in religion could be found only through the study of numbers and ratios. That view later brought him into conflict with Johannes Kepler, and the two exchanged views on the scientific and hermetic approaches to knowledge.
