Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge and Atake

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · The Met

Free to use

Hiroshige began the One Hundred Famous Views of Edo in 1856, the year he became a Buddhist monk, and this sheet came out the following year. The whole series was paid for in advance by a wealthy Buddhist priest who was in love with the daughter of the publisher, Uoya Eikichi, a former fishmonger. Hiroshige died in 1858, aged sixty-two, during a cholera epidemic in Edo. The Met bought this impression in 1936 from the Howard Mansfield Collection.
