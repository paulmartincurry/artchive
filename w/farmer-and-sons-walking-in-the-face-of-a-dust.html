Farmer and Sons Walking in the Face of a Dust Storm, Cimarron County, Oklahoma

Arthur Rothstein · April 1936 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Arthur Rothstein was 20 when he made this in Cimarron County, Oklahoma, in April 1936, working for the Resettlement Administration. The negative was printed so often that it wore out, and Rothstein made the Library a copy negative from his own print. The original was later thrown away.
