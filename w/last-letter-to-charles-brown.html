I have an habitual feeling of my real life having passed, and that I am leading a posthumous existence.

Last letter, to Charles Brown (30 November 1820)

John Keats · 1820

This is the last letter Keats wrote, sent to Charles Armitage Brown on 30 November 1820, sixteen days after he reached Rome. He and Joseph Severn had sailed from England on 17 September and been held in quarantine at Naples for ten days. His doctor put him on a diet of an anchovy and a piece of bread a day and bled him. He died on 23 February 1821. Severn's letters home are the only surviving account of his final months.
