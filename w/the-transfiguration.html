The Transfiguration

Raphael · 1516–1520 · Oil on panel · Pinacoteca Vaticana

Free to use with credit. Photo: Alvesgaspar, CC BY-SA 4.0

Cardinal Giulio de' Medici ordered two altarpieces for Narbonne Cathedral, this one from Raphael and a Raising of Lazarus from Sebastiano del Piombo, for which Michelangelo supplied drawings. Raphael was still at work on his when he died on 6 April 1520, and for a couple of days the panel lay at the head of his catafalque. The cardinal never sent it to France. French troops took it to Paris in 1797, and it came back after the fall of Napoleon. A cleaning in the 1970s found that assistants had finished only some figures at the lower left.
