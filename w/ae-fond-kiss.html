Had we never loved sae kindly,
Had we never loved sae blindly,
Never met--or never parted,
We had ne’er been broken-hearted.

Ae Fond Kiss

Robert Burns · 1791

Burns was thirty-two in 1791 and had been married to Jean Armour for three years. He died five years later in Dumfries, aged thirty-seven.
