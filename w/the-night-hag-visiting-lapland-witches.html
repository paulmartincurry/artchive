The Night-Hag Visiting Lapland Witches

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1796 · Oil on canvas · 101.6 × 126.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Henry Fuseli painted this canvas in 1796, at the age of 55. He did not paint in oil until he was 25, and he often took his pigments as dry powder and mixed them with oil or turpentine on the end of the brush. The Met bought the painting in 1980.
