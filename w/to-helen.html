On desperate seas long wont to roam,
Thy hyacinth hair, thy classic face,
Thy Naiad airs have brought me home
To the glory that was Greece,
To the grandeur that was Rome.

To Helen

Edgar Allan Poe · 1831

Poe wrote To Helen in honour of Jane Stanard, the mother of a childhood friend. The fifteen-line poem was first printed in Poems of Edgar A. Poe in 1831 and again in the Southern Literary Messenger in March 1836. For The Raven and Other Poems in 1845 he rewrote the closing lines of the second stanza, which had first spoken of fair Greece and old Rome.
