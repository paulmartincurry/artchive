The Yellow River Breaches Its Course (Water Album)

Ma Yuan · late 12th–early 13th century · Album leaf, ink on silk · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

This leaf is one of a series of paintings of water by Ma Yuan, each about 27 by 42 centimetres, now kept together in the Palace Museum in Beijing. Painting was the family trade. His great-grandfather served as painter in attendance at the Northern Song court, his grandfather and father held the same post in Hangzhou, and Ma Yuan took it up himself some time after 1189.
