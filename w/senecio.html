Senecio

Paul Klee · 1922 · Oil on gauze · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

The painting is also known as Head of a Man Going Senile. Klee made it in 1922, aged forty-two, a year into his ten years of teaching at the Bauhaus, where he was form master in the bookbinding, stained glass and mural workshops and had two studios. Kandinsky joined the staff that year, and Klee designed advertising for the school's first exhibition and festival. Between 1916 and 1925 he also made fifty hand puppets for his son Felix.
