Moon over the Waters at Daimotsu Bay (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1886 · Woodcut, color · 37.2 × 25 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Benkei, the warrior monk who served Minamoto no Yoshitsune, faces the ghosts of the Taira clan in a storm at sea. The sheet was published in 1886. This impression at the Library of Congress keeps its full sheet, 37.2 by 25 centimetres.
