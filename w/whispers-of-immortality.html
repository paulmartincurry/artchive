Webster was much possessed by death
And saw the skull beneath the skin;
And breastless creatures under ground
Leaned backward with a lipless grin.

Whispers of Immortality (first four stanzas)

T. S. Eliot · 1918–1919

The poem was written sometime between 1915 and 1918 and first appeared in the Little Review. It was first collected in June 1919 in Poems, published by Leonard and Virginia Woolf's Hogarth Press.
