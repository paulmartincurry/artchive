Portrait of John Churchill, 1st Duke of Marlborough

Christian Friedrich Zincke · 1715 · enamel on copper in a gilt metal frame · Framed: 6.4 x 5.1 cm (2 1/2 x 2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Enamel portraits were fired in many layers of metal oxide at different temperatures, which made a likeness hard to get. Unlike miniatures in watercolour on vellum or ivory, they do not crack or fade.
