Mung and the Beast of Mung (The Gods of Pegāna)

Sidney Sime · published 1905 · Plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Lord Dunsany approached Sidney Sime in 1904 to illustrate his first book, The Gods of Pegāna, published the next year on a commission basis by Elkin Mathews. In the book's pantheon Mung is Lord of all Deaths. Sime had worked five years in the Yorkshire coal mines as a scoop pusher, and nearly died there, before he studied at the Liverpool School of Art.
