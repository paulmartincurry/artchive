Let us go then, you and I,
When the evening is spread out against the sky
Like a patient etherized upon a table;

The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock (opening)

T. S. Eliot · 1915

Public domain in the United States (published 1917); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Eliot began the poem in February 1910 and finished it in July or August 1911, when he was twenty-two. It waited four years for a printer. Ezra Pound, overseas editor of the magazine Poetry, recommended it to the founder, Harriet Monroe, and it ran in the June 1915 issue as Eliot's first professionally published poem. In 1912 he had added a 38-line middle section, then removed it on the advice of Conrad Aiken.
