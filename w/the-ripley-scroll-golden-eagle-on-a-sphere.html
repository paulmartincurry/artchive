The Ripley Scroll: Golden Eagle on a Sphere

Maker unknown, after George Ripley · c. 1600 · Ink and colours, mounted on linen with wooden rollers · 328 × 40 cm (whole scroll) · Wellcome Collection, London (MS.692)

Free to use

The golden bird on its sphere is the third of five panels; a legend beside it names it the bird of Hermes. A sale catalogue for the library's second copy suggested the copying was commissioned by John Dee. George Ripley, whose name the scroll carries, was an Augustinian canon who died in 1490.
