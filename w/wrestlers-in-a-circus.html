Wrestlers in a Circus

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner · 1909 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 80.5 × 94 cm (31 11/16 × 37 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting stayed in Kirchner's estate from his death in 1938 until 1957, when it was sold to the Galerie Grosshennig in Düsseldorf. It went to a New York dealer the same year, was sent back to Düsseldorf on commission and returned unsold, and changed hands twice more before Cleveland bought it in 1966. During the 1930s, 639 works by Kirchner were removed from German museums.
