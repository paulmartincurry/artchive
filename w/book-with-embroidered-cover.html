Book with embroidered cover

Maker unknown · 1635 · paper, silk, metallic thread Technique: embroidered on satin weave · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

The book inside this cover is a copy of The Psalmes, and its title page is missing. The whole volume is 8 centimetres tall and 5 wide. Its cream satin cover was embroidered in coloured silks and metallic thread. It came to the museum as a gift of Elizabeth Riley.
