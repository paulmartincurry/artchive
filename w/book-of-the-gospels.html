Book of the Gospels

Northern Highlands artist, Ethiopia · late 14th–early 15th century · Parchment (vellum), acacia wood, tempera, ink · H. 16 1/2 × W. 11 1/4 × D. 4 in. (41.9 × 28.6 × 10.2 cm) · The Met

This Gospel book comes from the Lake Tana region of Ethiopia. It measures 41.9 centimetres high, 28.6 wide and 10.2 thick. It entered the Met in 1998 through the Rogers Fund.
