Woman with Red Hair and Green Eyes (The Sin)

Edvard Munch · 1901 · color lithograph · Sheet: 77.7 × 49.2 cm (30 9/16 × 19 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The sheet is nearly eighty centimetres tall. Munch was in his late thirties, and his paintings and prints were then circulating widely in Paris, shown at exhibitions and sold through dealers and other artists. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought this impression in 1983.
