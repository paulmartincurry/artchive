The Steerage

Alfred Stieglitz · 1907 · photogravure · Image: 32 × 25.7 cm (12 5/8 × 10 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This was Stieglitz's own copy of the picture, which he kept for the rest of his life. It then passed to Georgia O'Keeffe, and in 2019 Thomas and Diann Mann gave it to the Cleveland Museum of Art. The passengers are poor immigrants returning to Europe. Stieglitz was forty-three, and he signed the mount and wrote the title and year on it in pencil. The image is 32 by 25.7 centimetres.
