'You crave one kiss of my clay-cold lips;
But my breath smells earthy strong;
If you have one kiss of my clay-cold lips,
Your time will not be long.

The Unquiet Grave

Anonymous English Ballad · 1868

This version was written down from the lips of a girl in Sussex and printed in the Folk-Lore Record in 1868. Several of its verses also turn up in other ballads, among them Sweet William's Ghost and some versions of The Twa Brothers. Ralph Vaughan Williams arranged it for voice, violin and piano in 1912.
