Losbruch

Käthe Kollwitz · 1898–1903 · Chalk on paper · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

This chalk drawing is a design for a print, a preliminary study for an etching in Kollwitz's Peasant War series. That cycle of seven etchings was inspired by the German Peasants' War of 1524–1525. The sheet is inscribed with its title in French and German, La Révolte and Losbruch, and the Rijksmuseum bought it with the support of the F.G. Waller-Fonds.
