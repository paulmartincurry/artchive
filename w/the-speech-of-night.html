Sweet is my sleep, but more to be mere stone,
So long as ruin and dishonour reign;
To bear nought, to feel nought, is my great gain;
Then wake me not, speak in an undertone!

The Speech of Night

Michelangelo Buonarroti · 16th century

Translated by John Addington Symonds, 1878

Night is one of the figures Michelangelo carved for the tomb of Giuliano de' Medici in the New Sacristy, also known as the Medici Chapel. At least four of the chapel's statues, Night among them, were completed between 1525 and 1527.
