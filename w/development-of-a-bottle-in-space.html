Development of a Bottle in Space

Umberto Boccioni · 1913, cast 1950 · Bronze · 15 1/2 × 23 3/4 × 12 1/2 in. (39.4 × 60.3 × 31.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Boccioni had worked as a painter and a commercial illustrator, and decided to become a sculptor after visiting Paris studios in 1912, among them those of Brâncuși, Archipenko and Duchamp-Villon. The following year he showed his sculptures at the Galerie La Boétie in Paris. He was thirty or thirty-one when he made this one. The bronze is 60.3 centimetres long and came to the Met in the bequest of Lydia Winston Malbin.
