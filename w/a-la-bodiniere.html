À la Bodinière

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1894 · Lithograph in black, olive, orange and blue on tan wove paper · 60.4 × 82 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Steinlen put cats into many of his illustrations and published a book of cat drawings, Dessins Sans Paroles Des Chats. This poster is printed in black, olive, orange and blue on tan paper. He was thirty-four in 1894, and some of the hundreds of illustrations he produced between 1883 and 1920 were signed with a pseudonym to avoid political trouble.
