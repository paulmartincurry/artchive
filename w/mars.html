Mars

Diego Velázquez · c. 1639–1640 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Velázquez painted Mars around 1639–1640, like all the pictures he made for the Torre de la Parada of King Philip IV. The pose draws on the ancient sculpture known as the Ludovisi Ares and on Il Pensieroso, one of Michelangelo's figures for the Medici tombs in the New Sacristy of San Lorenzo in Florence.
