Fish and Rocks

Bada Shanren · dated 1699 · Hanging scroll, ink on paper · 135.3 × 61 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bada Shanren was born Zhu Da, a prince of the Ming imperial house. When the Qing purged the Zhu family he fled to a Buddhist temple near Nanchang, took vows in 1645 at nineteen, and spent about thirty years as a monk. From 1690 he turned to fish, often painting one alone in the middle of an empty sheet. He was seventy-three when he made this scroll.
