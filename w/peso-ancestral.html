Tú me dijiste: no lloró mi padre;
Tú me dijiste: no lloró mi abuelo;

Peso ancestral

Alfonsina Storni · 1919 (Irremediablemente)

Peso ancestral appeared in Irremediablemente in 1919. Storni's next book, Languidez, won the first Municipal Poetry Prize and the second National Literature Prize. In 1923, when the magazine Nosotros asked which poets under thirty its readers most admired, she had just turned thirty-one and was too old to be counted.
