The Judgment of Paris

Lucas Cranach the Elder · c. 1528 · Oil on beech · 101.9 × 71.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The picture is painted in oil on a beech panel and dates from around 1528. Cranach was court painter to the Electors of Saxony for most of his career and ran a large workshop, and many of his works exist in different versions. The museum bought this panel in 1928.
