Pandora

Odilon Redon · ca. 1914 · Oil on canvas · 56 1/2 × 24 1/2 in. (143.5 × 62.2 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Redon was seventy-three or seventy-four when he painted Pandora, about two years before his death in Paris. The year before, he had been given more space than any other artist at the Armory Show in New York, Chicago and Boston. The canvas is 143.5 centimetres tall and 62.2 wide. It came to the Met in 1959 as a bequest from Alexander M. Bing.
