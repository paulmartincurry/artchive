Djenné Terracotta Archer

Maker unknown · 13th–15th Century · Ceramic · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

Djenné-Djenno, in the inland delta of the Niger in Mali, is one of the oldest known cities in sub-Saharan Africa. Clay figures like this were made in the region over several centuries. Many were dug up illegally and sold abroad in the twentieth century, so little is known about how they were used.
