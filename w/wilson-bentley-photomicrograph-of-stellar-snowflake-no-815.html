Wilson Bentley Photomicrograph of Stellar Snowflake No. 815

Wilson A. Bentley · c. 1890 · Photographic print · Smithsonian Institution Archives, Washington

Free to use

Each crystal was caught on a blackboard and moved quickly to a microscope slide before it could melt. Wilson Bentley made more than five thousand such photomicrographs. His book Snow Crystals, with 2,500 of them, was published shortly before he died of pneumonia at his farm on 23 December 1931; he may have caught it walking home in the snow.
