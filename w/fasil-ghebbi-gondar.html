Fasil Ghebbi, Gondar

Maker unknown · 1636–1667 · Brown basalt with local tuff

Free to use with credit. Photo: Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 3.0

Ethiopian emperors had ruled from a travelling camp of tents until Fasilides founded Gondar as a fixed capital and ordered this castle built. A Yemeni ambassador who visited in 1648, when it was a few years old, recorded that the architect was an Indian named Abdal Kerim. Most of the manual work was done by local Ethiopian Jews, who traditionally worked as masons, metalsmiths and carpenters. Later emperors added their own buildings inside the same enclosure, which has a wall 900 metres long with twelve gates.
