Seven Trees

Paula Modersohn-Becker · ca. 1900–1902 · Charcoal on paper · 8 5/8 × 11 3/4 in. (21.9 × 29.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sheet is small, 21.9 by 29.8 centimetres. Paula Becker was in her mid-twenties and moving between Paris, where she studied anatomy at the Académie Colarossi in 1900, and the artists' colony at Worpswede. In May 1901 she married the painter Otto Modersohn, eleven years older than her, and set up a small studio on a nearby farm where she worked several hours a day.
