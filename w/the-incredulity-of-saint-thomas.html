The Incredulity of Saint Thomas

Caravaggio · c. 1601–1602 · Oil on canvas · Sanssouci Picture Gallery, Potsdam

Free to use

Caravaggio painted the canvas around 1601–1602 for the banker Vincenzo Giustiniani, and it appears in the Giustiniani inventory of 1638. After that collection was dispersed the painting was sent to Prussia, where the state bought it in 1816. It survived the Second World War unscathed and hangs in the Sanssouci Picture Gallery in Potsdam.
