View of Toledo

El Greco (Domenikos Theotokopoulos) · ca. 1599–1600 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Only two landscapes by El Greco survive, and this is one of them. The Castle of San Servando, on the left, stands where it really does, but other landmarks of the city have been moved. Both landscapes were found in the collection of Pedro Salazar de Mendoza after his death, and he is believed to have commissioned them. The signature in the lower-right corner gives the painter's birth name, Domenikos Theotokopoulos.
