Au Pied de l'Echafaud

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1893 · Lithograph poster on cream wove paper · Image: 82.5 × 58.1 cm; Sheet: 82.5 × 59.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

When the Moulin Rouge opened in 1889, Toulouse-Lautrec was commissioned to make its posters. He had a regular income from his family, but posters gave him a living of his own. He was in his late twenties when he made this one, printed in black on cream paper.
