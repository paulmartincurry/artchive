Eat the rich

Attributed to Jean-Jacques Rousseau · 1790s

The line is commonly attributed to Rousseau and was popularised during the French Revolution. The Paris Commune's president, Pierre Gaspard Chaumette, is said to have used it in a speech during the Terror on 14 October 1793, though the account is doubtful.
