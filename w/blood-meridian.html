Blood Meridian

Cormac McCarthy · 1985

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

McCarthy began writing the book in the mid-1970s. In a letter sent around 1979 he said that he had not touched it in six months out of frustration. The Judge was added to the manuscript in the late 1970s. It was his fifth book, and Random House published it in 1985.
