The Veteran in a New Field

Winslow Homer · 1865 · Oil on canvas · 24 1/8 × 38 1/8in. (61.3 × 96.8cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Homer finished the painting in 1865, the last year of the Civil War, when he was twenty-nine. The jacket and canteen lying in the cut wheat at the lower right belong to the division he had travelled with while covering the war. Reviews at its first showing that year were mostly bad: critics said it was painted fast and loosely, and that a real harvest needed a cradle as well as a scythe.
