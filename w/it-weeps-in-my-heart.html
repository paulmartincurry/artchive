It weeps in my heart
As it rains on the town.
What is this dull smart
Possessing my heart?

It Weeps in My Heart (Il pleure dans mon cœur)

Paul Verlaine · 1874

Translated by Gertrude Hall

The poems of Romances sans paroles were written between 1872 and 1873, the years of Verlaine's affair with Arthur Rimbaud, which took the two of them to London. The book was published in 1874 while Verlaine was in prison at Mons. He had been arrested after firing two shots at Rimbaud in Brussels on July 12, 1873, wounding his left wrist.
