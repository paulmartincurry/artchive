Oh, cease! must hate and death return?
Cease! must men kill and die?
Cease! drain not to its dregs the urn
Of bitter prophecy.

Hellas (final chorus)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1822

Shelley wrote Hellas in 1821 while living in Pisa, with a view to raising money for the Greek War of Independence. It was published in 1822 by Charles and James Ollier in London.
