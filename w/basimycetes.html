Basimycetes (Kunstformen der Natur, plate 63)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Book plate (printing method not confirmed) · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

The veiled fungus in figure 1 was a new species when Haeckel drew it at natural size. It was found in December 1900 in the botanical garden at Buitenzorg on Java, and he named it Dictyophora madonna. His text notes that its net-like veil unfolds within a few hours, with a crackling sound.
