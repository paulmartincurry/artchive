A Lovely Garland (Tamakazura): Tamatori-Ama (Scenes amid Genji Clouds Matched with Ukiyo-e Pictures)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1845–46 · Woodblock ōban print (nishiki-e); ink and color on paper · Image 36.5 × 25.1 cm; sheet 54.6 × 40 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Metropolitan Museum of Art bought this sheet in 2019 with a gift from Alan and Barbara Medaugh. Kuniyoshi trained many pupils, who began by making warrior prints in their master's style before going their own way. The most important of them was Yoshitoshi.
