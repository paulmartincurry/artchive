Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding.

Job 38:4-11

Book of Job (King James Version) · c. 6th-4th century BCE; KJV 1611

The author of Job was almost certainly an Israelite, although the story is set outside Israel, in southern Edom or northern Arabia. The book uses an exceptionally large number of words and word forms found nowhere else in the Bible. It begins and ends in prose, with the dialogues and monologues between them in verse. From chapter 38 God speaks from a whirlwind.
