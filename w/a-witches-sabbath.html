A Witches' Sabbath

Cornelis Saftleven · c. 1650 · Oil on panel · 54.3 × 78.2 cm (21 3/8 × 30 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The number 804GS is still visible on the back of this panel, from the months in 1938 and 1939 when it was held at Christie's in London. Before that it was probably at Castle Dioszegh, in the collection of Baron Kuffner, whose son was married to Tamara de Lempicka. The Art Institute bought it in 1945. Cornelis Saftleven was in his early forties when he painted it. Rubens owned eight of his paintings and had added figures to four of them.
