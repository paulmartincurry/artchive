River-Snow (Jiāng Xuě)

Liu Zongyuan · ca. 808 CE

Translated by Burton Watson, 1984 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Liu Zongyuan had been a rising official in the capital until 805, when a reform movement he was tied to failed and he was exiled to Yongzhou in Hunan. He was about thirty-two. The poem's Chinese title is Jiangxue, and Chinese painters have often taken it as a subject. Around 180 of his poems survive.
