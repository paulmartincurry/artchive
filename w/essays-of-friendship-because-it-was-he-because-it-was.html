If a man should importune me to give a reason why I loved him, I find it could no otherwise be expressed, than by making answer: because it was he, because it was I.

Essays — Of Friendship: Because it was he, because it was I (Book I, Ch. 27)

Michel de Montaigne · 1580

Translated by Charles Cotton

The friend was Étienne de La Boétie, a poet three years older than Montaigne, whom he met while serving at the Bordeaux Parlement. La Boétie died in 1563, just four years after they met, likely of plague or tuberculosis. The line about why he loved him was added afterwards in the margins of Montaigne's own copy of the 1588 edition, in two stages and two different inks, and was printed in the posthumous edition of 1595.
