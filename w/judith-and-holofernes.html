Judith and Holofernes

Francisco de Goya · 1819–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 143.5 × 81.4 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya painted this in oil straight onto the plaster, probably beside Saturn Devouring His Son in his house. Judith is the only historical figure in the Black Paintings who can be identified with certainty. Neither Holofernes nor his blood is shown.
