Delphinium (Urformen der Kunst, plate 45)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · image 26 × 19 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blossfeldt was 62 or 63 when Urformen der Kunst came out in 1928. His pictures of the geometry of plants later fed into the photographs of industrial architecture by Bernd and Hilla Becher. The plates were printed as photogravures, each image 26 by 19 centimetres.
