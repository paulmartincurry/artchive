Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

Psalm 46

Book of Psalms (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · Date uncertain (trans. 1611)

Martin Luther paraphrased this psalm in his hymn Ein feste Burg ist unser Gott, known in English as A Mighty Fortress Is Our God. Bach's cantata BWV 80 is among the many works that quote Luther's hymn.
