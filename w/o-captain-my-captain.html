O Captain! my Captain! our fearful trip is done,
The ship has weather'd every rack, the prize we sought is won.

O Captain! My Captain!

Walt Whitman · 1865

Whitman saw Abraham Lincoln several times between 1861 and 1865 but never met him. The poem was printed in The Saturday Press on November 4, 1865, less than seven months after Lincoln's death, and an early draft of it is in free verse. It was the only poem of his to be anthologized in his lifetime. In the late 1880s he earned money selling autographed copies of it.
