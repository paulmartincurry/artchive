Mystic Mountain

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and ESA · 1–2 February 2010 · Space telescope image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/ESA/STScI

Hubble's Wide Field Camera 3 photographed this pillar on 1 and 2 February 2010, and the picture marked the telescope's twentieth anniversary. The column of gas and dust is about three light-years tall. It stands in the Carina Nebula, which Nicolas-Louis de Lacaille discovered from the Cape of Good Hope on 25 January 1752.
