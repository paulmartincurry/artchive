Las Virgenes Cristianas Expuestas al Populacho

Félix Resurrección Hidalgo · 1884 · Oil on canvas · Metropolitan Museum of Manila

This painting took a silver medal, the ninth of forty-five, at the 1884 Exposición General de Bellas Artes in Madrid. Filipino expatriates in the city held a banquet for Hidalgo and his fellow medallist Juan Luna, and José Rizal gave a speech there. The original canvas was destroyed in a fire at the University of Valladolid in Spain. A copy is in the collection of the Central Bank of the Philippines, and it spent 2015 to 2020 on loan in Singapore.
