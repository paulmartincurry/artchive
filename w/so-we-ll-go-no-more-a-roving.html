For the sword outwears its sheath,
And the soul wears out the breast,
And the heart must pause to breathe,
And Love itself have rest.

So We'll Go No More a Roving

Lord Byron · 1817

Byron was twenty-nine in 1817. His marriage to Anne Isabella Milbanke, made in 1815, had ended in separation the year before. He had been forced to leave England after threats of lynching, and lived for seven years in Italy, in Venice, Ravenna, Pisa and Genoa.
