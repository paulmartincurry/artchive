Sappho

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1865 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 34.4 × 26.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Cameron took up photography at the age of 48, after her daughter gave her a camera as a present. The gift was meant as a diversion while her husband was in Ceylon. She converted a chicken coop into studio space and, by her own account, turned her coal-house into a dark room.
