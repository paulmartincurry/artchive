The Rock Bridge at Mount Tiantai

Zhou Jichang · 1178 · Ink and color on silk · 109.9 × 52.7 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The scene follows the story of the fourth-century monk Tanyou, who found the rock bridge at Mount Tiantai blocked by a great stone and prayed and fasted for several days until the luohan opened a way into their temple. The bridge was said to narrow to a few centimetres. Zhou Jichang painted the scroll in 1178 for a set of the five hundred luohan that later belonged to the Daitokuji temple in Kyoto. Charles Lang Freer bought it in Tokyo in 1907.
