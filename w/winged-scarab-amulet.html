Winged Scarab Amulet

Maker unknown · Late Period, Dynasty 26 (664–525 BCE) · Faience · Scarab: 5.9 × 3.5 × 0.8 cm (2 3/8 × 1 7/16 × 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A scarab amulet was often laid over a mummy's heart as a protector. Its wings let it join the daily rising and setting of the sun, a cycle equated with death and rebirth.
