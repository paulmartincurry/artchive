La Calavera Oaxaqueña

José Guadalupe Posada · 1903 · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The press of Antonio Vanegas Arroyo issued this sheet in 1903, with song lyrics set under the picture. Posada had moved to Mexico City at the end of 1888 and learned there to engrave in lead and zinc.
