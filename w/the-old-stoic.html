Yes, as my swift days near their goal:
'Tis all that I implore;
In life and death a chainless soul,
With courage to endure.

The Old Stoic

Emily Brontë · 1846

Emily Brontë printed this poem as Ellis Bell in 1846, in the book of poems she shared with her sisters Charlotte and Anne, their first work in print. A year later, Charlotte wrote, the publishers had sold two copies. The publishers, Aylott and Jones, were mainly stationers, and they bound the book with a blank spine.
