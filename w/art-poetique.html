De la musique avant toute chose,
Et pour cela préfère l'Impair …
Et tout le reste est littérature.

Art poétique

Paul Verlaine · 1884

After his release from prison Verlaine worked for some years in England as a teacher, giving lessons in French, Latin, Greek and drawing at a school in Stickney in Lincolnshire. He returned to France in 1877. Composers who set his poems to music include Fauré, Debussy, Ravel and Stravinsky, and Verlaine was aware of this and apparently pleased. In 1894 his peers elected him Prince of Poets.
