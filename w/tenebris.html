The black hand plucks and plucks
At the bricks.
The bricks are the color of blood and very small.

Tenebris

Angelina Weld Grimké · 1927

Grimké was forty-seven in 1927. She had begun teaching English in 1902 at a black school in the segregated system of Washington, D.C., and moved to Dunbar High School in 1916. A train wreck at Bridgeport, Connecticut, in 1911 left her with a back injury that never fully healed. Her poems were published in The Crisis, the newspaper of the NAACP, and in Opportunity.
