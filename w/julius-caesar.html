Cowards die many times before their deaths;
The valiant never taste of death but once.

Julius Caesar (Act II, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · 1599

In 1599 a partnership of men from Shakespeare's company built a theatre of their own on the south bank of the Thames and called it the Globe. He had moved across the river to Southwark by that year. His name had begun to appear on the title pages of printed plays the year before, and two years earlier he had bought New Place, the second-largest house in Stratford.
