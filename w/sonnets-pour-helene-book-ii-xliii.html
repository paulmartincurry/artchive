So live, believe me: don’t wait for another day,
Gather them now the roses of life, and desire.

Sonnets pour Hélène, Book II: XLIII

Pierre de Ronsard · 1578

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Sonnets pour Hélène was published in 1578. Catherine de' Medici commissioned the book for Hélène de Fonsèque, her lady-in-waiting, to console her for the loss of her lover. Ronsard was nearly 45 when they met.
