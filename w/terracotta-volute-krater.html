Terracotta Volute-Krater (Bowl for Mixing Wine and Water)

Attributed to the Painter of the Woolly Satyrs · ca. 450 BCE · Terracotta · H. 63.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The museum attributes the painting to an Athenian known only as the Painter of the Woolly Satyrs. The bowl stands 63.5 centimetres high and was used for mixing wine and water. It was bought in 1907.
