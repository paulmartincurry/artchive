Mona Lisa

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1503–1506 · Oil on poplar panel · Louvre Museum, Paris

Free to use

The portrait is thought to have been commissioned for the Giocondo family's new home and for the birth of their second son, but Leonardo never gave it to them. It is believed he took it to France in 1516 and went on working on it there. King Francis I acquired it after Leonardo's death in 1519. On 21 August 1911 it was stolen from the Louvre by an employee who had helped build its glass case. He kept it in his apartment for two years and served six months in prison.
