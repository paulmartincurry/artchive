Strandgade, Sunshine

Vilhelm Hammershøi · c. 1906 · Oil on canvas · 50.5 × 56.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Hammershøi signed the canvas at the lower right in black paint. The Copenhagen dealers Winkel & Magnussen had it by 1916, the year he died, and Dr. Axel Heine owned it by 1918. It was sold from Heine's collection in 1945 and again at Sotheby's in London in 1989. Nancy and Joseph Keithley of Cleveland bought it in 2001 and later gave it to the Cleveland Museum of Art.
