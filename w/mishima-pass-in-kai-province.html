Mishima Pass in Kai Province (Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1830–33 · Color woodblock print; oban · 26 × 38.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hokusai made the Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji in answer to a boom in domestic travel in Japan. The series sold so well that ten more prints were added to it, which is why it has forty-six. The Art Institute's record names the publisher as Hibino Yohachi.
