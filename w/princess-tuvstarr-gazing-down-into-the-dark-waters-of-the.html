Princess Tuvstarr Gazing Down into the Dark Waters of the Forest Tarn

John Bauer · 1913 · Watercolour, gouache and pencil on paper · Malmö Konstmuseum

Free to use

Bauer painted this for the 1913 volume of Among Gnomes and Trolls, to accompany Helge Kjellin's story of the old bull Skutt and the little princess Tuvstarr. It is 25 centimetres square, in watercolour, gouache and pencil. Malmö Konstmuseum bought it at the Baltic Exhibition in Malmö in 1914.
