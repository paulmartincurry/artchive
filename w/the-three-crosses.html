The Three Crosses (Christ Crucified between the Two Thieves)

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1653 · Drypoint and burin · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Most of Rembrandt's prints are mainly etched, but this one is drypoint, with burin work added from the third state onwards. He made around sixty impressions from the plate in its first three stages and then went back into it. In the reworked plate a man on horseback in a large hat has been added and the light falling from above is considerably darker. The print measures 394 by 456 millimetres.
