Art when really understood is the province of every human being.

It is simply a question of doing things, anything, well.

The Art Spirit (opening)

Robert Henri · 1923

The Art Spirit was put together by a former pupil, Margery Ryerson, who collected Henri's remarks on art and the practice of it. It was published in 1923, when he was in his late fifties and teaching at the Art Students League of New York. He had been teaching since 1892, and his students included Edward Hopper, George Bellows, Rockwell Kent and Stuart Davis. The book stayed in print for several decades.
