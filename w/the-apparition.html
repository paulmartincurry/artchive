The Apparition (L'Apparition)

Gustave Moreau · 1876 · Watercolor · Musée d'Orsay

This is a watercolour, 106 centimetres high and 72 wide, worked with scraping and incisions, and Gustave Moreau spent from 1874 to 1876 on it. He returned to the subject in 19 paintings, 6 watercolours and more than 150 drawings. The severed head recalls a Japanese print he had copied in 1869. The Belgian dealer Léon Gauchez bought it at the Salon of 1876, and the next year sent it to the Grosvenor Gallery in London, where it was hung in the main gallery with the oil paintings.
