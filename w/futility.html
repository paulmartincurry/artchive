Was it for this the clay grew tall?
--O what made fatuous sunbeams toil
To break earth's sleep at all?

Futility

Wilfred Owen · 1918

Owen wrote this poem in May 1918, while posted to the Northern Command Depot at Ripon. He had turned twenty-five there in March, and spent the day quietly at Ripon Cathedral. In July he returned to active service in France, although he might have stayed on home duty indefinitely. In 1982 the singer Virginia Astley set the poem to music she had composed.
