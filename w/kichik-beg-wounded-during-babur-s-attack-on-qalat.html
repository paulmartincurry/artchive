Kichik Beg Wounded during Babur's Attack on Qalat (Baburnama)

Designed by Kanha, painted by Mansur · c. 1590 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Image 23.9 × 12.9 cm; paper 31.7 × 21.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kanha designed this scene and Mansur painted it, around 1590. Akbar ordered illustrated copies of his grandfather Babur's memoirs as soon as he was given the finished Persian translation in November 1589, and four were made over the following decade or so; the first was broken up for sale in 1913.
