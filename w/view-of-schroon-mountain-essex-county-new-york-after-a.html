View of Schroon Mountain, Essex County, New York, After a Storm

Thomas Cole · 1838 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 99.8 × 160.6 cm (39 5/16 × 63 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Cole sketched this Adirondack peak in early summer and painted it in autumn colours back in his studio. He signed it T. Cole, Catskill, 1838; two years earlier he had married Maria Bartow and settled year-round at the Cedar Grove farm there. Schroon Mountain was his own name for the peak, which is officially Hoffman Mountain. The first owner was Dr. George Ackerly of New York, and Cleveland bought the canvas in 1917.
