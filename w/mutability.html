It is the same!—For, be it joy or sorrow,
The path of its departure still is free:
Man’s yesterday may ne’er be like his morrow;
Nought may endure but Mutability.

Mutability (We are as clouds)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1816

The poem first appeared in the 1816 collection Alastor, or The Spirit of Solitude: And Other Poems, published in London by Baldwin, Cradock, and Joy. Mary Shelley quoted half of it in Frankenstein in 1818 without naming her husband as its author.
