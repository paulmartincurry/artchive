Le Bain (The Bath)

Félix Vallotton · 1894 · Woodcut · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Vallotton made this woodcut in 1894, at the age of twenty-eight, during his association with Les Nabis. The woodcuts he produced in the 1890s brought him international recognition. This impression is in the Rosenwald Collection at the National Gallery of Art.
