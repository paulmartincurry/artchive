He Made Me Stop That He Might Gather Fruit (Arabian Nights)

Virginia Frances Sterrett · published 1928 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

From 1923 Sterrett's tuberculosis let her work only for short stretches, and this Arabian Nights of 1928 was the one commission she finished before her death in Monrovia, California, in 1931; she was about thirty. The plate belongs to the voyages of Sinbad, a cycle added to the Thousand and One Nights only around the early 17th century.
