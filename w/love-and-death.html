Love and Death (Los Caprichos, plate 10)

Francisco de Goya · 1799 · Etching, burnished aquatint, burin · Plate: 21.3 × 15.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya had to learn printmaking for this series, since painters of his day did not engrave. He worked each plate in etching and aquatint together, which was new; the aquatint gave him tones from white to black, like a wash of paint.
