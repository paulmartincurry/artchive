A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains

Wang Ximeng · 1113 · Ink and color on silk · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

Wang Ximeng was eighteen, a student at the court painting school with a post in the documents storehouse. The emperor Huizong taught him personally, and in less than half a year he handed in this scroll, nearly twelve metres long and coloured with azurite and malachite. It is his only surviving work. He died at twenty-three. The last emperor, Puyi, gave the scroll to his brother in 1922, and it entered the Palace Museum in 1953.
