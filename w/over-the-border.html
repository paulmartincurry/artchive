The moon goes back to the time of Ch'in, the wall to the time of Han,
And the road our troops are travelling goes back three hundred miles. . . .
Oh, for the Winged General at the Dragon City —
That never a Tartar horseman might cross the Yin Mountains!

Over the Border

Wang Changling · before 757; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Wang Changling wrote mostly about imagined battles on China's western frontier. He died during the An Lushan Rebellion, put to death by the Tang official Lü Qiuxiao some time between 755 and 757.
