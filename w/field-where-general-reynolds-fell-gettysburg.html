Field Where General Reynolds Fell, Gettysburg

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1863 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 17.8 × 22.7 cm (7 × 8 15/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Early in the war Timothy O'Sullivan was a civilian photographer attached to the Topographical Engineers, copying maps and plans and taking pictures in his own time. Forty-four of his photographs went into Gardner's Photographic Sketch Book of the War, the first published collection of Civil War photographs. This print is 17.8 by 22.7 centimetres. The Met bought it in 2005 as part of the Gilman Collection.
