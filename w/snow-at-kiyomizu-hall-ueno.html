Snow at Kiyomizu Hall, Ueno

Kawase Hasui · 1929 · Woodblock print on paper · 40.9 × 27.7 cm · Museum Plantin-Moretus, Antwerp

Free to use

This snow scene at Kiyomizu Hall in Ueno is dated the seventh month of 1929. Hasui had turned to art after the family rope and thread business went bankrupt when he was 26. His teacher Kaburagi Kiyokata gave him the name Hasui, which can be translated as water gushing from a spring. He designed almost a thousand woodblock prints over nearly forty years.
