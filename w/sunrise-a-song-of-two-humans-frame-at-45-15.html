Sunrise: A Song of Two Humans, frame at 45:15

F. W. Murnau · 1927 · Film with recorded score, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Charles Rosher and Karl Struss shot the film and won the Academy Award for cinematography at the first ceremony, in 1929. The city street set alone reportedly cost more than 200,000 dollars, and Fox used it again in later films, including John Ford's Four Sons. The original negative was destroyed in the Fox vault fire of 1937, and a new one was made from a surviving print.
