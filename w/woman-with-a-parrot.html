Woman with a Parrot

Gustave Courbet · 1866 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Free to use

In 1864 the Paris Salon had turned down a nude by Courbet as indecent; two years later it accepted this one, his first nude to be shown there. The model was probably Joanna Hiffernan, who posed for him several times, including for Le Sommeil the same year. The canvas is nearly two metres wide. It came to the museum in 1929 as part of the bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer.
