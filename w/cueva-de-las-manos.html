Cueva de las Manos

Maker unknown · ca. 7300 BCE – 700 · Ignimbrite

Free to use

The hands were stencilled by blowing paint through bone pipes, and the remains of those pipes helped to date the work. There are more than 2,000 handprints in and around the cave, and 829 left hands to 31 right ones, which suggests most of the painters held the pipe in the right hand. The people who made them hunted guanacos, and the painting went on in waves from about 7,300 BC to AD 700. An Italian missionary, Alberto Maria de Agostini, was the first to write about the site, in 1941.
