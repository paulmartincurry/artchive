Adiantum pedatum (Urformen der Kunst, plate 54)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · 26 × 19 cm (10 1/4 × 7 1/2 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Karl Blossfeldt started out in metal: at sixteen he was apprenticed as an iron caster at the art ironworks and foundry in Mägdesprung. He then studied in Berlin and from 1890 worked for the designer Moritz Meurer, travelling through Europe and North Africa to photograph plants as models for Meurer's ornament.
