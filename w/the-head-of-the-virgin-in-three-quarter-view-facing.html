The Head of the Virgin in Three-Quarter View Facing Right

Leonardo da Vinci · 1510–13 · Black chalk, charcoal, and red chalk, with some traces of white chalk (?); some remains of framing lines in pen and brown ink at upper right (not by Leonardo) · Sheet: 8 × 6 1/8 in. (20.3 × 15.6 cm) · The Met

Free to use

This sheet is a study for the head of the Virgin in The Virgin and Child with Saint Anne, the painting now in the Louvre that Leonardo had begun in 1502 or 1503. Some years after it was drawn, wide strips were cut from all four sides. Its attribution was long disputed. In April 2002 Carmen C. Bambach found parallel hatching of the kind made by a left-handed draughtsman, and in 2005 the mark of the Melzi-Leoni collection was found on the back. The Metropolitan Museum bought it at Sotheby's in May 1951.
