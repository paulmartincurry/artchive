Pyramid of Skulls

Paul Cézanne · c. 1898–1901 · Oil on canvas · Private collection

Free to use

Four skulls, stacked, on a canvas 37 by 45.5 centimetres. Cézanne painted it in his studio in Aix before he moved into the new Les Lauves studio in September 1902. His mother had died in October 1897, and between 1898 and 1905 he made a number of skull still lifes in oil and watercolour. A visitor in July 1902 found three human skulls on a narrow table in his bedroom.
