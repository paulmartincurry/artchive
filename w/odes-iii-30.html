And now 'tis done: more durable than brass
My monument shall be, and raise its head
O'er royal pyramids: it shall not dread
Corroding rain or angry Boreas,

Odes III.30 (Exegi Monumentum)

Horace · 23 BCE

Translated by John Conington

Horace's father was probably taken captive by the Romans in the Social War, and managed to gain his freedom. The rhetorician Quintilian judged the Odes the only Latin lyrics worth reading.
