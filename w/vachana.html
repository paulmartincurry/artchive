The Artchive
My legs are pillars,
the body the shrine,
the head a cupola
of gold.

Vachana

Basava (Basavanna) · ca. 1160 CE

Translated by A. K. Ramanujan · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Basava began as an accountant at the court of the Kalachuri king Bijjala and became chief minister of the kingdom. He composed in Kannada, the local language, at a time when the literary scene was dominated by Sanskrit. A vachana ends with one of the local names of Shiva, and the name used identifies the writer; his is Kudala Sangama Deva. Scholars state that the poems were written down long after his death.
