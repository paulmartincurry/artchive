The poet is a pretender,
who pretends so completely
he even pretends to pain
the pain he really feels.

Autopsychography

Fernando Pessoa · 1932

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain. The Portuguese original (1932) may still be in copyright in the United States.

Pessoa wrote under his own name and about seventy-five others, which he called heteronyms, a word he made up. He was forty-three or forty-four in 1932. Schooled in English in Durban, where his stepfather was the Portuguese consul, he published four books in English during his life and only one in Portuguese. At his death in Lisbon in 1935 he left 25,574 manuscript and typed pages in a domed wooden trunk.
