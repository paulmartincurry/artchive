Posters Exhibition: Executed under Federal Art Project Poster Division

Richard Floethe · c. 1941 · Print on board (poster) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

This poster announced a show of the Federal Art Project poster division's own work at the Federal Art Gallery, 7 East 38th Street, New York. The Federal Art Project ran from 1935 to 1943 as a relief measure, paying artists to make murals, paintings, prints and posters. The poster is stamped on the back with the date 21 March 1941.
