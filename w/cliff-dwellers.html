The Artchive

Cliff Dwellers

George Bellows · 1913 · Oil on canvas · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

The street is East Broadway on New York's Lower East Side. Bellows painted it with a colour system sold by the paint manufacturer Hardesty Maratta, who gave each of his ready-mixed oil colours the value of a musical note and advised painters to combine them like chords. Bellows had used the system since 1909 or 1910, and here he worked with three chords, each kept to its own part of the canvas. The Los Angeles County Museum of Art acquired the painting in 1916.
