The Merchant Georg Gisze

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1532 · Oak panel · 97.5 × 86.2 cm · Gemäldegalerie, Berlin

Free to use

Holbein painted this large portrait in 1532, at the start of his second stay in England, when the German merchants of the Steelyard on the north bank of the Thames were among his first clients; he rented a house nearby in Maiden Lane. Georg Gisze came from Danzig. The carnations on the table may refer to his betrothal, and he married Christine Krüger in Danzig in 1535.
