And my love came behind me—
He came from the South;
His breast to my bosom,
His mouth to my mouth.

My Grief on the Sea

Traditional Irish song (Connacht) · printed 1893

Translated by Douglas Hyde

Douglas Hyde, who later served as the first president of Ireland, printed it in 1893 in Love Songs of Connacht. He gave his own English verse, then the Irish, with a word-for-word prose version in a footnote.
