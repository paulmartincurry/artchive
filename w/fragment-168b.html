Moon has set
and Pleiades: middle
night, the hour goes by,
alone I lie.

Fragment 168B (The Moon Has Set)

Sappho of Lesbos · ca. 600 BCE

Translated by Anne Carson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Sappho's poems were written to be sung to music. She probably composed around 10,000 lines, of which only about 650 survive, and only one poem, the Ode to Aphrodite, is certainly complete.
