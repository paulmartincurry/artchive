Chinese Garden

Maker unknown · ca. 1780 · Cut paper on wood frame · H × W: 44.6 × 50 cm (17 9/16 × 19 11/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The colours in this optical toy slide come from white, yellow, green, blue and red transparencies set into the cut paper. On the back, a label in Savoyard Italian describes a Chinese garden full of pine trees and a fireworks machine that runs without end. The frame is wood, 50 centimetres wide.
