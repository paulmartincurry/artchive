Through caverns measureless to man
down to a sunless sea.

Kubla Khan

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1797 (pub. 1816)

By Coleridge's own account the poem came from a dream at a farmhouse between Porlock and Linton, after two grains of opium taken for dysentery, and the writing stopped when a visitor on business from Porlock interrupted him. It was likely written in October 1797 and was then set aside. On 10 April 1816 he read it to Lord Byron, who persuaded him to publish it, and two days later a contract for 80 pounds was drawn up with the publisher John Murray. The manuscript is on permanent exhibit at the British Library.
