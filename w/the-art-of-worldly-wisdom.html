Think with the Few and speak with the Many. … Truth is for the few, error is both common and vulgar.

The Art of Worldly Wisdom

Baltasar Gracián · 1647

Translated by Joseph Jacobs, 1892

The book is a collection of 300 maxims, each with a commentary. Baltasar Gracián was a Jesuit priest in his mid-forties when he wrote it, and the year before he had been chaplain to the Spanish army that lifted the French siege of Lleida. Eleven years later his order sanctioned and exiled him for publishing a novel without his superiors' permission.
