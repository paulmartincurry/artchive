The Artchive
Before the last sea and the hapless stars;
Whatever mourns when many leave these shores;
Whatever shares
The eternal reciprocity of tears.

Insensibility (section VI)

Wilfred Owen · 1917–1918 (published 1920)

Owen was diagnosed with neurasthenia or shell shock and sent to Craiglockhart War Hospital in Edinburgh for treatment. While recuperating there he met the poet Siegfried Sassoon.
