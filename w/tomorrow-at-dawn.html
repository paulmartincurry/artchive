I’ll view neither the gold of descending evening
Nor the sails nearing Harfleur, in tranquil weather,
But I will place on your grave there, on arriving,
A bouquet of green holly and flowering heather.

Tomorrow, At Dawn (Demain, dès l'aube)

Victor Hugo · published 1856

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Hugo wrote the poem about a visit to the grave of his daughter, Léopoldine, four years after her death. It was published in 1856 in his collection Les Contemplations.
