Moai at Rano Raraku

Maker unknown · c. 1250–1500 · Volcanic tuff

Free to use

The Rapa Nui carved more than 900 moai from the soft volcanic tuff of Rano Raraku, mostly between 1250 and 1500, and nearly half of them are still at the quarry. Oral histories say the quarry was divided into a territory for each clan. It appears to have been abandoned abruptly, leaving stone tools, finished statues waiting to be moved, and almost as many unfinished ones still in the rock. The heads make up about three-eighths of each figure.
