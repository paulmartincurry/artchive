Confessions of an English Opium-Eater

Thomas De Quincey · 1821

De Quincey went to London in 1821 to sell some translations from German and was persuaded to write up his opium experiences first. He was thirty-six and had been using the drug since 1804, when he was a student at Oxford. The account ran unsigned in two parts in The London Magazine that September and October, and came out as a book with a new appendix in 1822. In 1856 he revised it and more than doubled its length.
