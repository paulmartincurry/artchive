Seeking the Tao in Autumn Mountains

Attributed to Juran · 10th century · Hanging scroll, ink and light colour on silk · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Juran was a Buddhist monk, and not even his family name is known; Juran is his name in religion. He worked at the Southern Tang court in Nanjing, and when the last Southern Tang ruler surrendered to the Song around 975, he followed the court north to Kaifeng and lived at the Kaibao temple. Only a few hanging scrolls are attributed to him, and this one is among them.
