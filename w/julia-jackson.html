The Artchive

Julia Jackson

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1867 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 27.4 × 20.6 cm (10 13/16 × 8 1/8 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sitter was Cameron's niece and goddaughter, the child of her sister Maria and Dr John Jackson. Cameron had taken up photography only four years earlier, at forty-eight, when her daughter and son-in-law gave her a sliding-box camera for Christmas. At her house at Freshwater on the Isle of Wight she turned the coal-house into a darkroom and a glazed hen house into her studio. The museum bought this print in 1996.
