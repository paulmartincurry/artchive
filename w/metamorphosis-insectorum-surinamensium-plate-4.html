Metamorphosis insectorum Surinamensium, Plate 4

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

The lizard on the stem was added, Merian wrote, to decorate the plate. Her text says the animal grows as large as a crocodile, lives on dead animals and does not attack living people. She found the brown hairy caterpillar eating cassava leaves in June 1700 and fed it until 12 June, when it turned into a pupa. She saw many of the moths flying in the cassava fields of a Mr van Vreedenburg.
