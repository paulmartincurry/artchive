Cover for "The World’s Almaniac for 1879"

Frederick Stuart Church · 1878 · Printed in black and red ink on paper · 30.2 × 23.5 cm (11 7/8 × 9 1/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Frederick Stuart Church drew this cover eight years after he moved to New York to study at the National Academy of Design. He had left school at thirteen to work for the newly founded American Express Company in Chicago, and served in the Union Army before he took up art. The sheet measures about 30 by 23 centimetres and came to the museum as a bequest of Erskine Hewitt.
