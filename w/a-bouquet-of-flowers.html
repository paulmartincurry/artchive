A Bouquet of Flowers

Clara Peeters · ca. 1612 · Oil on wood · 18 1/8 × 12 5/8 in. (46 × 32 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Fewer than ten flower paintings made in the Netherlands can be dated before 1608, the year after Clara Peeters signed her first known picture. She was one of the few women then working professionally as painters, yet no guild has a record of her, and her birth year and death are both uncertain. She had settled in Amsterdam by 1611. She signed thirty-one works, and the museum bought this panel in 2020.
