Cats Suggested as the Fifty-Three Stations of the Tōkaidō

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1847–50 · Colour woodcut · 35.4 × 23.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Kuniyoshi loved cats, and in the late 1840s he began putting them in place of people in his kabuki and satirical prints. On this sheet each cat is matched with the name of a station on the Tōkaidō road. It carries the seals of two censors, Murata Heiemon and Mera Taichiro, and was published by Ibaya Senzaburō. An inscription on the back records that it was once in the Bing collection.
