The Stones of Venice: The Nature of Gothic

John Ruskin · 1853

The Stones of Venice appeared in three volumes between 1851 and 1853, when John Ruskin was in his early thirties. He had first seen Venice in 1835, at sixteen, on one of the continental tours his family made; his father was a sherry and wine importer. He drew as well as wrote, and his early sketchbooks are full of drawings of landscapes and buildings.
