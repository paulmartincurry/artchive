Last she said,
“When I carried them in my arms they had no clothes
And now their jackets have no linings.” [She dies.

The Sick Wife

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · Han dynasty (206 BC–220 AD)

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Poems in this style take their name, yuefu, from the Music Bureau of the Han court, which began collecting, writing and performing folk songs and ballads in 112 BC. Many of the Bureau's songs are anonymous and are written in the voice of a type of person from Han society.
