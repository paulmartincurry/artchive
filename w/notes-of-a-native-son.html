Notes of a Native Son

James Baldwin · 1955

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

This was Baldwin's first book of non-fiction, published by Beacon Press in 1955. Its ten essays had previously appeared in magazines including Harper's Magazine, Partisan Review and The New Leader. In 1958 the poet Langston Hughes reviewed it in the New York Times Book Review.
