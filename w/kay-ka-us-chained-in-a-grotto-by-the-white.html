Kay-Ka'us Chained in a Grotto by the White Demon (Shahnama of Shah Isma'il II)

Siyavush Beg · 1576–77 · Opaque watercolor, ink and gold on paper · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Siyavush Beg painted this leaf for the Shahnama of Shah Isma'il II, made in Qazvin in 1576–77, when he was about forty. According to the chronicler Qazi Ahmad, he had been brought to Tabriz as a child, a Georgian slave, and trained in the royal studio under Muzaffar 'Ali. Isma'il II reigned for little more than a year and died on 24 November 1577, and the general view at the time was that he had been poisoned.
