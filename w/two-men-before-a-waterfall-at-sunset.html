Two Men before a Waterfall at Sunset

Johan Christian Dahl · 1823 · Oil on canvas · 38.1 × 35.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1823, the year of this small canvas, Dahl moved in with Caspar David Friedrich in Dresden. He was about thirty-five, the son of a fisherman in Bergen, and had been accepted into the Dresden academy three years earlier. He kept painting imaginary landscapes with forests, mountains and waterfalls alongside the subjects he drew around the city. Christen Sveaas gave the picture to the Metropolitan Museum in 2019.
