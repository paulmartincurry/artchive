Sir Thomas More

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1527 · Oil on oak panel · The Frick Collection, New York

Free to use

Hans Holbein painted the oak panel in 1527, during the stay in London that began in 1526. Erasmus had recommended that he befriend More. A closely related drawing is in the Royal Collection, though it was probably not directly preparatory.
