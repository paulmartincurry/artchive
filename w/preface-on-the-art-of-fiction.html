My task which I am trying to achieve is, by the power of the written word to make you hear, to make you feel—it is, before all, to make you see.

Preface on the Art of Fiction (1897)

Joseph Conrad · 1897

English was not Conrad's first language. He was born Józef Teodor Konrad Korzeniowski in Berdychiv, in what was then the Russian Empire, to a Polish family, and he did not speak English fluently until his twenties. He was orphaned at eleven and at thirteen announced that he meant to become a sailor. He was thirty-nine in 1897, and had married Jessie George the year before.
