Las Meninas

Diego Velázquez · 1656 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado

Free to use

The setting is Velázquez's own studio in the Alcázar palace in Madrid, a room Philip IV gave him in the early 1650s. The king kept a chair there and would often sit and watch him work. On the painter's chest is the red cross of the Order of Santiago, which he did not receive until 1659, three years after the painting was completed. The canvas was damaged in the fire that destroyed the Alcázar in 1734, and it has been cut down on the left and right sides.
