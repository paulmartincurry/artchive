Notebooks

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1490–1508

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1888

Leonardo filled his notebooks with drawings and notes on anatomy, astronomy, botany, cartography, painting and palaeontology. He did not publish his findings. His own schooling had been basic and informal: reading, mathematics and writing in the vernacular.
