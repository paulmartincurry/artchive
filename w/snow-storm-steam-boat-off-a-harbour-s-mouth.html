Snow Storm – Steam-Boat off a Harbour's Mouth

J. M. W. Turner · exhibited 1842 · Oil on canvas · 91.5 × 122 cm · Tate Britain, London

Free to use

Turner was 67 and in poor health when this was first shown at the Royal Academy in 1842. He said sailors had lashed him to the mast for four hours so that he could watch the storm, an account modern art historians doubt. One critic called the picture soapsuds and whitewash. Turner kept it all his life and it went to the nation in his bequest.
