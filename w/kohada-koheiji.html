Kohada Koheiji (One Hundred Ghost Tales)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; chūban · 25.6 × 18.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In the tale, Kohada Koheiji was a kabuki actor drowned by his wife's lover who came back to haunt the pair; the story was popular in the 18th century and went on to inspire a novel and several plays. The print was published by Tsuruya Kiemon, whose mark is in the cartouche. It measures 25.6 by 18.1 centimetres. The Art Institute bought this impression from the Evanston collector Charles Hibbard Chandler in March 1943.
