In the councils of government, we must guard against the acquisition of unwarranted influence, whether sought or unsought, by the military-industrial complex. The potential for the disastrous rise of misplaced power exists and will persist.

Farewell Address — The military-industrial complex

Dwight D. Eisenhower · January 17, 1961

Eisenhower gave this speech on national television from the Oval Office on January 17, 1961, and it lasted less than 10 minutes. As President for two terms he had slowed the push for increased defense spending, and yet the military services and the defense industry had expanded a great deal in the 1950s. He eventually described that growth as a necessary evil.
