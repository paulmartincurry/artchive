Belshazzar's Feast

Rembrandt van Rijn · c. 1635–38 · Oil on canvas · 167.6 × 209.2 cm · The National Gallery, London

Free to use

Rembrandt lived in the Jewish quarter of Amsterdam and took the form of the Hebrew inscription from a book by his friend Menasseh ben Israel, a rabbi and printer. He miscopied one of the characters and set them in columns. The date is unknown, and most sources put it between 1635 and 1638. The painting was with the Earls of Derby at Knowsley Hall until the National Gallery acquired it in 1964.
