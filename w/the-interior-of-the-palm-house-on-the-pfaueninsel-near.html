The Interior of the Palm House on the Pfaueninsel Near Potsdam

Carl Blechen · 1834 · Oil on canvas · 135 × 126 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

King Frederick William III of Prussia commissioned this view and paid Blechen 1,000 Taler for it in 1834. The palm house had been designed by Karl Friedrich Schinkel to show the Prussian royal collection of tropical plants. The king possibly gave the canvas that November to his daughter Charlotte, the wife of Tsar Nicholas I. Blechen had worked in banking until 1822, when an interest in art led him to the Berlin Academy.
