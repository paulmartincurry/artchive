Discomedusae, Plate 28 (Kunstformen der Natur)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Colour lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

One of the jellyfish on this plate was drawn from life, at its natural size, at Belligemma on Ceylon in December 1881. Haeckel was forty-seven then, and the plate was published about twenty years later.
