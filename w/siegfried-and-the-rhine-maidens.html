Siegfried and the Rhine Maidens

Albert Pinkham Ryder · 1888/1891 · Oil on canvas · 50.5 × 52 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Richard Haines Halsted, a New York stockbroker, owned this painting by 1891; he also owned Ryder's Jonah. By 1895 it belonged to Sir William Van Horne of Montreal, a Canadian railroad magnate and amateur artist. The National Gallery of Art bought it at the sale of Van Horne's estate in 1946. Ryder signed it at the lower left.
