True!—nervous—very, very dreadfully nervous I had been and am;
but why will you say that I am mad?

The Tell-Tale Heart

Edgar Allan Poe · 1843

Poe wrote the story in Philadelphia, where he lived at various addresses from 1838 to 1844. It appeared in January 1843 in the first issue of The Pioneer, a short-lived Boston magazine edited by James Russell Lowell and Robert Carter, and he was probably paid ten dollars. The first printing opened with lines from a Longfellow poem. Poe cut them in 1845 because he believed the poem was plagiarized.
