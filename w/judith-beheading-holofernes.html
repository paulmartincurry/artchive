Judith Beheading Holofernes (Capodimonte)

Artemisia Gentileschi · c. 1612–1613 · Oil on canvas · 158.8 × 125.5 cm · Museo di Capodimonte, Naples

Free to use

Artemisia Gentileschi was around twenty when she painted this, and X-rays show she moved Judith's arms and changed the drapery as she worked. At some point the left and top of the canvas were cut off. Nothing is known of its whereabouts until 1827, when it was recorded in a collection in Naples. She painted a second version, now in the Uffizi, between 1613 and 1621.
