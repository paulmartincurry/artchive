Self-Portrait with a Straw Hat

Vincent van Gogh · 1887 · Oil on canvas · 16 × 12 1/2 in. (40.6 × 31.8 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The self-portrait is painted on the back of another canvas by Van Gogh, The Potato Peeler. He had moved to Paris the year before, where he met Émile Bernard and Paul Gauguin and his colours grew brighter. He was thirty-three or thirty-four, and his younger brother Theo was paying his way. The canvas is 40.6 by 31.8 centimetres and came to the Met in 1967 in the bequest of Adelaide Milton de Groot.
