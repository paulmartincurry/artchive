The Lion Bas-Reliefs (Carceri d'invenzione, plate V)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 56.6 × 41.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Plate V was one of two new etchings added for the 1761 edition. Its giant relief, in a late Imperial style, shows a prisoner in chains being led to punishment. The plate measures 56.6 by 41.4 centimetres, and this impression is the second of the three states in Robison's catalogue.
