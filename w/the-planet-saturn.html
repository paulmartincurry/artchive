The Planet Saturn (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot drew Saturn on 30 November 1874. The following year he was given the use of the 26-inch refracting telescope at the US Naval Observatory for a year. Besides his drawings he published about fifty scientific papers, and craters on the Moon and on Mars carry his name.
