Book of the Dead for the Chantress of Amun, Nauny

Maker unknown · ca. 1050 BCE · Papyrus, paint · L. 521.5 cm; H. 35 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The papyrus is more than five metres long and 35 centimetres high. The museum's excavators found it in 1928–29 at Deir el-Bahri, rolled inside a hollow figure of Osiris in Nauny's burial. Nauny was probably a daughter of Pinedjem I and was about seventy when she died; she was buried in a tomb first made for an earlier queen, in coffins originally made for her own mother.
