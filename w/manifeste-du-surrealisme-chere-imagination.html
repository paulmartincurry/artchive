Manifeste du surréalisme: Chère imagination

André Breton · 1924

Breton's manifesto was published by Éditions du Sagittaire on 15 October 1924, two weeks after Yvan Goll brought out a rival Manifeste du surréalisme of his own. The two camps quarrelled at the Comédie des Champs-Élysées over the right to the word. Breton was twenty-eight.
