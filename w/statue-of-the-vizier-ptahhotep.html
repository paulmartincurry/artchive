Statue of the Vizier Ptahhotep

Maker unknown · ca. 2400–2350 BCE · Imhotep Museum, Saqqara

Free to use with credit. Photo: Kurohito, CC BY-SA 3.0

Ptahhotep was city administrator and vizier to King Djedkare Isesi in the Fifth Dynasty, and also overseer of the treasury, of the double granary and of all royal works. He was buried in a mastaba at North Saqqara, where the lower parts of the wall reliefs survive. Depictions of him in different wigs were taken from the tomb to the museum on the same site.
