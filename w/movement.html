The Artchive

Movement

Marsden Hartley · 1913 · Oil on canvas · 119.5 × 119 cm (47 × 46 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Marsden Hartley painted this in Berlin in 1913, on the eve of the First World War, the year after his first trip to Europe. He was thirty-six. The canvas is almost square, 119.5 by 119 centimetres. It was shown in New York in 1916 at the Forum Exhibition of Modern American Painters, and it belonged to Alfred Stieglitz, the photographer who had given Hartley his first solo show. After Stieglitz's death it reached the Art Institute in 1949 through Georgia O'Keeffe.
