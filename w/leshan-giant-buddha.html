Leshan Giant Buddha

Begun by the monk Haitong · 713–803 · Sandstone, carved from a cliff face

Free to use with credit. Photo: xiquinhosilva, CC BY 2.0

The statue is 71 metres tall and was cut from a red sandstone cliff where two rivers meet. The monk Hai Tong, who led the work, died when it was finished only from the shoulders up, and his disciples completed it. The ears are not stone: they were made of wood, coated with clay and attached to the head. Gutters and channels hidden in the hair, collar and chest carry rainwater away and still work. A thirteen-story wooden shelter once covered the whole figure.
