'The curse of hell frae me sall ye beir,
Mither, mither,
The curse of hell frae me sall ye beir,
Sic counseils ye gave to me O.'

Edward

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1765

This version first appeared in print in Bishop Thomas Percy's Reliques of Ancient English Poetry in 1765. Percy had it from Sir David Dalrymple, who said he heard it from an unnamed lady. In most other versions the son has killed his brother, and the name Edward seems to come from Percy's text. Franz Schubert set it to music in 1827.
