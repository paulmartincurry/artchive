Zhong Kui

Ren Yi (Ren Bonian) · dated 1883 · Hanging scroll, ink and color on paper · 171.8 × 93.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Ren Yi, also called Ren Bonian, was the son of a rice merchant who made extra money painting portraits. After his father's death in 1855 he moved to Shanghai and became one of the Shanghai School. He was forty-three when he painted this scroll. Early on he followed the Song painters, and later he favoured a freer manner drawn from the work of Zhu Da. Images of Zhong Kui were often painted on household gates as a guardian.
