The Bronco Buster

Frederic Remington · 1895 · Bronze · Metropolitan Museum of Art

Free to use

This was Frederic Remington's first sculpture. He modelled it in the summer of 1895 and copyrighted it that autumn, taking the subject from his own magazine illustrations, the earliest of them drawn in 1888 for an article by Theodore Roosevelt. Ten years after that drawing, in September 1898, the Rough Riders presented Roosevelt with a cast.
