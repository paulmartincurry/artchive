The Farm

Joan Miró · 1921–1922 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Miró began the picture in the summer of 1921 at his family's farmhouse in Mont-roig del Camp and finished it in Paris in the winter of 1922, at twenty-nine. By his own account it took nine months of seven or eight hours a day, and in Paris he had grasses from Mont-roig posted to him in an envelope so he could go on. One dealer proposed cutting the canvas into eight pieces to sell. Ernest Hemingway bought it in instalments for 5,000 francs as a birthday present for his wife Hadley, and Mary Hemingway gave it to the National Gallery of Art in 1987.
