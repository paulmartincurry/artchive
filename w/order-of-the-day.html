You are about to embark upon the Great Crusade, toward which we have striven these many months. The eyes of the world are upon you.

Order of the Day (6 June 1944)

Dwight D. Eisenhower · June 6, 1944

Eisenhower began drafting this order in February 1944 and decided before dawn on June 5 to proceed with the invasion. It was distributed to the 175,000-member expeditionary force on the eve of the landings. That same afternoon he scribbled a second note, accepting full blame should the effort to create a beachhead on the Normandy coast fail.
