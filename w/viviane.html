Viviane

Jules Chéret · 1886 · Color lithograph · Sheet: 78.2 x 55.9 cm; Image: 66.5 x 48.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Viviane was a ballet in five acts and nine tableaux by Edmond Gondinet, with music by Raoul Pugno and Clément Lippacher, staged at the Eden Théâtre. In the early 1870s Chéret and the brothers Léon and Alfred Choubrac had brought down the cost of colour lithography with technical advances. The poster collector Ernest Maindron published the first book on the illustrated poster in 1886, the year of this one.
