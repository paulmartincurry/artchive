Innocence is the child, and forgetfulness, a new beginning, a game, a self-rolling wheel, a first movement, a holy Yea.

Thus Spake Zarathustra — The Three Metamorphoses (First Part, I)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1883; English 1909

Translated by Thomas Common

Nietzsche claimed that the whole first part of the book was conceived while he was walking. In a letter he said it came to him in the course of strenuous hiking. By his own account in Ecce Homo, each part took ten days.
