I taught to them withal that art of arts,
The lore of number, and the written word
That giveth sense to sound, the tool wherewith
The gift of memory was wrought in all,

Prometheus Bound — I Taught to Them (the gifts to mortals)

Aeschylus · 5th century BCE

Translated by E. D. A. Morshead

Whether Aeschylus wrote this play is not settled. In 1977 two scholars, working independently, argued against it on grounds of language, technique and stagecraft, and no consensus has been reached. It cannot be later than 430 BC, and a reference to the eruption of Mount Aetna in 479 suggests it may be later than that.
