Zhang Shun, the White Splash in the Waves, and Li Kui, the Black Whirlwind, in an Underwater Struggle

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · September 1887 · Color woodblock prints; oban diptych · Each sheet 25.2 × 37.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Two sheets, each 25.2 by 37.8 centimetres, make up this picture of an underwater fight between two heroes. It was published in September 1887, when Yoshitoshi was forty-eight.
