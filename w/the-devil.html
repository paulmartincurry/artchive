The Devil (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Before the tarot, Smith worked with the Lyceum Theatre company of Ellen Terry, Henry Irving and Bram Stoker, travelling with them and working on costumes and stage design. In 1907 Alfred Stieglitz showed her paintings at his gallery in New York, which until then had shown only photographs.
