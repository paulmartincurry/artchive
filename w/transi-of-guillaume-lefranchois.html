Maker unknown · 1446 · Black stone · Musée des Beaux-Arts, Arras

Guillaume Lefranchois was a physician and a canon at Béthune. His effigy was carved in black stone in 1446 for the church of Saint-Barthélemy in Béthune, and it is now in the Musée des Beaux-Arts in Arras. Monuments of this kind, showing the dead as a skeleton or a decomposing body, first appeared in the 1380s and stayed in use for two hundred years.
