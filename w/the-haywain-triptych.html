The Haywain Triptych

Hieronymus Bosch · 1510 · Oil on panel · Museo del Prado

Free to use

Tree-ring study of the oak panels puts the triptych at about 1516. Philip II of Spain acquired it in 1570 with five other paintings and sent it to El Escorial four years later. It was later sold and split into three, and the panels ended up in three different places; they were put back together at the Prado in 1914. Bosch painted the outside of the shutters in full colour, where most triptychs of the time had grey monochrome. His pigments were few: azurite, lead-tin yellow, vermilion and ochres.
