The Great Wave, Sète

Gustave Le Gray · 1857 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 33.7 × 41.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Le Gray's seascapes date from 1856 to 1858. Among his technical innovations was combination printing: seascapes made by using one negative for the water and one negative for the sky. He later closed his studio and fled the country to escape his creditors.
