The difference between us is very marked. Most that I have done and suffered in the service of our cause has been in public, and I have received much encouragement at every step of the way. You on the other hand have labored in a private way. I have wrought in the day—you in the night.

Letter to Harriet Tubman (29 August 1868)

Frederick Douglass · August 29, 1868

Douglass wrote this letter from Rochester on August 29, 1868, for Sarah Hopkins Bradford's biography of Harriet Tubman, which was planned as a fundraiser for her. Bradford solicited letters from Tubman's friends and supporters and worked quickly, to finish before a trip to Europe. The book was published in November 1868 with the date 1869 printed in it, and it brought Tubman some $1,200.
