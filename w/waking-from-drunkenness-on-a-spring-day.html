“Life in the World is but a big dream:
I will not spoil it by any labour or care.”
So saying, I was drunk all the day,
Lying helpless at the porch in front of my door.

Waking from Drunkenness on a Spring Day

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1919

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

In 756 Li Bai became a staff adviser to Prince Yong, a son of Emperor Xuanzong. When the prince's forces were defeated in 757 he escaped, was captured, imprisoned in Jiujiang and sentenced to death. The sentence was commuted to exile in Yelang, in the remote south-west of the empire, and notice of an imperial pardon reached him before he got near it.
