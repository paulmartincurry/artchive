Nighthawks

Edward Hopper · 1942 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Edward Hopper worked on this canvas for about a month and a half and finished it on 21 January 1942. He posed in a mirror for the two men, and his wife Jo posed for the woman. That May it was sold to the Art Institute of Chicago for $3,000, of which $1,971 went to Hopper after commission and costs. He said the scene was suggested by a restaurant on Greenwich Avenue in New York, and that he simplified it and made the restaurant bigger.
