Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in. I drink at it; but while I drink I see the sandy bottom and detect how shallow it is. Its thin current slides away, but eternity remains.

Walden — Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in (Ch. 2)

Henry David Thoreau · 1854

Thoreau used his time at Walden Pond, from July 1845 to September 1847, to write his first book, A Week on the Concord and Merrimack Rivers, which came out in 1849. He went there partly to escape what he called over-civilization, and spent a good deal of his time reading and writing. This passage closes the second chapter, Where I Lived, and What I Lived For; the chapter after it is about reading.
