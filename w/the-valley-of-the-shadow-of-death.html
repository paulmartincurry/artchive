The Valley of the Shadow of Death

Roger Fenton · 1855 · Salted paper print, from the album "Photographic Pictures of the Seat of War in the Crimea" (1856) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Fenton spent March to June 1855 with the British army in the Crimea and made 360 wet-plate glass negatives, working from a horse-drawn darkroom. He took two pictures of this road, one empty and one strewn with cannonballs. In 2007 the film-maker Errol Morris went to Sevastopol, found the spot, and concluded that the empty road was photographed first. This print was published by Thomas Agnew & Sons of Manchester on 1 January 1856, as number 218.
