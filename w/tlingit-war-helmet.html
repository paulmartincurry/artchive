Tlingit War Helmet

Maker unknown · Late 18th Century · Wood · Museo de América, Madrid

Free to use with credit. Photo: Luis García, CC BY-SA 3.0

Tlingit warriors of southeastern Alaska went into battle in thick wooden helmets carved as animals or fierce faces, with a wooden collar to protect the neck and armour of wooden slats. They wore this armour against Russian fur traders and soldiers in the fighting around Sitka in the early 1800s.
