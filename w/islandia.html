Islandia (Theatrum Orbis Terrarum)

Abraham Ortelius · 1585 (this printing 1603) · Engraved map, hand-coloured · 34 × 48 cm · National Library of Israel, Jerusalem (Eran Laor Cartographic Collection)

Free to use

The plate is signed as issued by Ortelius in 1585. This impression is map number 106 in the Latin edition printed in Antwerp in 1603 by Johannes Baptista Vrients, five years after Ortelius's death. The atlas kept growing after him: from 53 maps in 1570 it reached 167 maps across thirty-one editions by 1612.
