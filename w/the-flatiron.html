The Flatiron

Edward Steichen · 1904, printed 1909 · Blue-green pigment gum bichromate over platinum print · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Steichen photographed the Flatiron Building at dusk from the west side of Madison Square Park in 1904, two years after it opened. From the one negative he made platinum prints and brushed pigment mixed with gum arabic and potassium bichromate over them by hand, producing three prints in different colours. This blue-green one was printed in 1909, and all three were given to the Metropolitan Museum of Art in 1933 with the Alfred Stieglitz Collection. A fourth gum print sold at Christie's in 2022 for $11,840,000.
