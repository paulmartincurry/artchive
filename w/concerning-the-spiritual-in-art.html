Every work of art is the child of its age and, in many cases, the mother of our emotions. It follows that each period of culture produces an art of its own which can never be repeated.

Concerning the Spiritual in Art (opening)

Wassily Kandinsky · 1911

Translated by M. T. H. Sadler, 1914

The book came out in Munich in 1911. In the same year Kandinsky was divorced from his first wife and started the Blue Rider group with several other artists. He was the son of a Moscow tea merchant, and two years earlier he had founded the New Artists' Association in Munich.
