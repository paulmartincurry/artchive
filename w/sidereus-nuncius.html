Sidereus Nuncius

Galileo Galilei · March 13, 1610 · Houghton Library, Harvard University

Free to use

Galileo was a mathematician at the University of Padua when this pamphlet came out in Venice, and he had ground the lenses of his early telescopes himself. The one behind these observations magnified twenty times. The book holds more than seventy drawings and diagrams, and his nightly sketches of Jupiter run from late January to early March 1610, days before publication. He dedicated it to a former pupil, Cosimo II de' Medici, and named Jupiter's four moons for the Medici brothers, which helped him win a post at their court.
