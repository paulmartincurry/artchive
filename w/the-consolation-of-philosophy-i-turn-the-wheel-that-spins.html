I turn the wheel that spins. I delight to see the high come down and the low ascend.

The Consolation of Philosophy — I Turn the Wheel That Spins (Book II, Prose II)

Boethius · 523; English 1897

Translated by H. R. James

Boethius wrote the book in 523, during a year in prison while he awaited trial, and then execution, for alleged treason under the Ostrogothic king Theodoric the Great.
