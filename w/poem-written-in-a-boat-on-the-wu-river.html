Poem Written in a Boat on the Wu River (detail)

Mi Fu · ca. 1095 · Handscroll, ink on paper · 31.1 × 556.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The scroll is more than five and a half metres long, ink on paper, and Mi Fu wrote it around 1095, in his forties. His mother had worked as a wet nurse to Emperor Shenzong, which brought him into the court as a boy. He held posts as a reviser of books in the imperial library, a provincial governor and, from 1104, Professor of Painting and Calligraphy in the capital. He kept part of his collection of old writing secret, shown only to a chosen few.
