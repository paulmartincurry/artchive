Jain Cosmological Map of Adhaidvipa

Maker unknown · 1900 · Lithograph · 50.2 × 40 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Jain texts hold that human beings can live on only two and a half of the continents of the middle world. The same texts describe a universe that no creator made, with neither beginning nor end. This lithograph was printed in India in 1900 and measures 50.2 by 40 centimetres.
