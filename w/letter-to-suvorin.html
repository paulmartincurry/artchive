You confuse two things: solving a problem and stating a problem correctly. It is only the second that is obligatory for the artist.

Letter to Suvorin (27 October 1888)

Anton Chekhov · October 27, 1888

Translated by Constance Garnett, 1920

Alexey Suvorin owned and edited the St. Petersburg paper Novoye Vremya. Early in 1886 he invited Chekhov to write for it, at double the rate per line his previous publisher paid and with three times the space. He became a lifelong friend, perhaps Chekhov's closest. Chekhov was twenty-eight when he wrote him this letter.
