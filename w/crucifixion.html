Crucifixion (Tauberbischofsheim Altarpiece)

Matthias Grünewald · c. 1523–1525 · Oil on panel · Staatliche Kunsthalle Karlsruhe

Free to use

This Crucifixion was painted on one side of a wooden panel 196 by 152 centimetres, with a second scene on the back. To show both in a museum, the panel was split in half during its first restoration, in 1883. It is first recorded in the eighteenth century in the Church of St. Martin in Tauberbischofsheim, and it has been in Karlsruhe since 1900.
