The Tempest

Giorgione · c. 1506–1508 · Oil on canvas · Gallerie dell'Accademia, Venice

Free to use

The Venetian noble Gabriele Vendramin commissioned the painting, which is 83 by 73 centimetres, a size made for a collector's house. X-rays show that Giorgione first painted a second female nude where the standing man now is. He died of plague in 1510, a few years after finishing it, and only about six paintings are firmly given to him. In September 1943 an Italian arts inspector, Pasquale Rotondi, hid it under his bed to keep it from German troops.
