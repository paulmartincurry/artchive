The Large Operation, plate eighteen from Gesichter

Max Beckmann · 1914, published 1919 · Drypoint on ivory wove paper · plate 29.7 × 43.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Beckmann drew this drypoint in 1914, and it was published five years later in his portfolio Faces (Gesichter), printed by Franz Hanfstaengl for the Marées Society and R. Piper and Company. In 1925 he began teaching a master class at the Städelschule in Frankfurt; in 1933 the Nazi government called him a cultural Bolshevik and dismissed him. He spent the next ten years in exile in Amsterdam.
