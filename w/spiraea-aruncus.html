Spiraea aruncus (Tyrol)

Anna Atkins · 1851–54 · Cyanotype · Image: 35.1 × 24.6 cm (13 13/16 × 9 11/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

No camera was used. The plant was laid on light-sensitive paper and left in the sun, so the image is the size of the specimen, here 35.1 by 24.6 centimetres. In the 1850s Atkins and her friend Anne Dixon made at least three albums of these prints as presents. She was in her fifties. She kept the plants she used and gave the collection to the British Museum in 1865.
