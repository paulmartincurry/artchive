Job's Evil Dreams (The Book of Job, Plate 11)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · Sheet: 43.4 × 33.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blake had painted this design twice in watercolour before he engraved it as plate 11: for Thomas Butts in 1805–06, and in 1821 for John Linnell, who traced the Butts watercolours and had Blake colour the tracings. The borders, with their biblical quotations and paraphrases, are what chiefly sets the engravings apart from the watercolours. Ralph Vaughan Williams based his 1931 ballet Job: A Masque for Dancing on the series.
