That if poetry comes not as naturally as the leaves to a tree, it had better not come at all.

Letter to John Taylor (27 February 1818): Axioms in poetry

John Keats · 1818

John Taylor was a partner in Taylor and Hessey of Fleet Street, the publishers Keats moved to after his first book failed, and they brought out Endymion in 1818. Through them Keats met their lawyer, Richard Woodhouse, who began collecting everything he could about the poetry. That archive survives as one of the main sources of information on Keats's work.
