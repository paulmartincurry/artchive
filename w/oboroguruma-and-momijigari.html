Oboroguruma and Momijigari (One Hundred Monsters Ancient and Modern)

Toriyama Sekien · 1781 · Woodblock printed book; ink on paper · 22.5 × 16 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Oboroguruma is an ox cart with a huge human face on its front, which appears on hazy moonlit nights in Kyoto; Momiji is the demon-woman of Mount Togakushi. This book is the third of Sekien's four bestiaries of ghosts and monsters: the first sold well enough to spawn three sequels. The Met bought this copy, three books bound as one, in 2013.
