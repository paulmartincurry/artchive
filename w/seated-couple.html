Seated Couple

Barnes Foundation Master · 18th–early 19th century · Wood, copper and iron alloy, applied organic materials · 73 × 21.9 × 20.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met attributes this pair to the carver it calls the Barnes Foundation Master. Carved in wood with copper and iron alloy in the eighteenth or early nineteenth century, it is 73 centimetres tall. Lester Wunderman gave it to the Met in 1977.
