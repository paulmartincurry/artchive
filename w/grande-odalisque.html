Grande Odalisque

Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres · 1814 · Oil on canvas · Department of Paintings of the Louvre

Free to use

Caroline Murat, Queen of Naples and sister of Napoleon, commissioned the painting, and Ingres finished it in 1814. He took the pose, a reclining figure looking back over her shoulder, from a portrait David had painted in 1800. Early critics said the figure had two or three vertebrae too many. A later study, measuring against real women, put the excess at five, and found the left arm shorter than the right. The Louvre bought the canvas in 1899.
