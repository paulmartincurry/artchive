Not even the best of artists has any conception
that a single marble block does not contain
within its excess

Sonnet 151

Michelangelo Buonarroti · ca. 1538–1544

Translated by James M. Saslow, 1991 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This sonnet dates from Michelangelo's sixties. He had worked mostly in Rome since his thirties, and between 1536 and 1541 he painted The Last Judgment on the altar wall of the Sistine Chapel. Poetry is listed among his fields alongside sculpture, painting and architecture.
