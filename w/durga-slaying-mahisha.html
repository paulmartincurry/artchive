Durga Slaying Mahisha

Maker unknown · c. 1700–1710 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · 15.5 × 21.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Cleveland bought this page from Heeramaneck Galleries in New York in 1960, and that winter lent it to the Rajput Painting exhibition at Asia House. It is 15.5 by 21.2 centimetres. Painters in the foothills of the western Himalayas, where it was made, gave Durga a tiger to ride rather than a lion.
