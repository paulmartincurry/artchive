Divan Japonais

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892–1893 · Ink on wove paper · Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

This poster was made to advertise a café-chantant in Montmartre then called the Divan Japonais. The woman in the audience is the dancer Jane Avril, and beside her is the writer Édouard Dujardin. The performer they are watching is Yvette Guilbert: her face is left out of the poster, and she is identified by her tall, thin frame and long black gloves.
