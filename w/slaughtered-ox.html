Slaughtered Ox

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1655 · Oil on beech panel · 95.5 × 68.8 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Rembrandt painted the carcass on a beech panel, signed and dated it 1655, and built up its texture with thick impasto. A woman looks out from behind a half-open door at the back. Joshua Reynolds saw the picture in an Amsterdam collection in 1781, and Louis Viardot sold it to the Louvre in 1857 for 5,000 francs. Francis Bacon's Figure with Meat of 1954 sets a pope beside what is likely a version of this carcass.
