Portrait of Innocent X

Diego Velázquez · c. 1650 · Oil on canvas · Galleria Doria Pamphilj

Free to use

Velázquez painted the pope on his second trip to Italy. The light linen vestments point to summer, most likely that of 1650. He signed the portrait on the sheet of paper in the pope's hand, where the date can no longer be read. The pope's family, the Pamphilj, kept the picture, and for most of the next two centuries few people saw it. Francis Bacon painted more than forty-five variants of it and avoided seeing the original.
