Old pond—frogs jumping in—sound of water.

The Old Pond (Furu ike ya)

Matsuo Bashō · 1686

Translated by Lafcadio Hearn, 1898

This hokku was composed in early 1686, when Bashō was about forty-two and teaching poetry at his hut in Edo. In April of that year the poets of the city gathered at the hut for a verse contest on the subject of frogs, and his poem was placed at the top of the compilation.
