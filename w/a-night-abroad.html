Through the night, to my motionless tall mast,
The stars lean down from open space.
And the moon comes running up the river.

A Night Abroad

Du Fu · c. 765; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

In the spring of 765 Du Fu and his family set off by boat down the Yangtze, apparently meaning to reach Luoyang, the region where he was born. He was about fifty-three, with failing eyesight, deafness and lung trouble that had begun in 754, and they travelled slowly. From late spring 766 they stopped for almost two years at Kuizhou, at the entrance to the Three Gorges, where he wrote 400 poems. He died in 770 without reaching home.
