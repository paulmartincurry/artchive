The veil has been torn asunder. We have already seen the light, and it is not our desire to be thrust back into darkness.

The Jamaica Letter

Simón Bolívar · September 6, 1815

Translated by Lewis Bertrand · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Bolívar was thirty-two and in exile on Jamaica when he wrote this letter, forced there after Spanish forces subdued New Granada in 1815. He had been fighting for five years, since starting as a militia officer in 1810. From Jamaica he went on to Haiti, where its leader Alexandre Pétion gave him military support in return for a promise to abolish slavery in Spanish America. He died of tuberculosis in 1830.
