Chang'e Flees to the Moon (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 27.1 × 17.3 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The print shows Chang'e holding a vial of the elixir of immortality as she rides on clouds to the moon. It belongs to a series of 100 prints that Yoshitoshi issued in batches from 1885 until 1892. This copy has been trimmed and measures 27.1 by 17.3 centimetres.
