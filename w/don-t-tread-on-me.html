Don't Tread on Me

Christopher Gadsden · 1775

Christopher Gadsden, a South Carolina delegate to the Continental Congress, designed the flag in 1775: a timber rattlesnake coiled on a yellow field. In December 1775 Benjamin Franklin, writing as American Guesser in the Pennsylvania Journal, described a rattlesnake painted with this motto under it.
