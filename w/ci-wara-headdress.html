Ci Wara Headdress

Maker unknown · late 19th–early 20th century · Wood, metal · Brooklyn Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: a Wikipedia Loves Art participant, CC BY 2.5

Headdresses of this kind are carved in wood by the Bambara of Mali and danced in male and female pairs at sowing and harvest. Each carving is tied to a basket worn on the dancer's head, and the dancer's body is hidden under a heap of raffia. In 1917 Guillaume Apollinaire and the collector Paul Guillaume published pictures of ci wara figures in their book Sculptures nègres.
