Center of Town, Woodstock, Vermont (Snowy Night)

Marion Post Wolcott · March 1940 · Safety film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The words Snowy night come from an annotation on the original negative jacket. Wolcott made the photograph in March 1940, when she was 29, on a sheet of safety film about 3 by 4 inches. The following year she met Leon Oliver Wolcott, deputy director of war relations for the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
