Wall Painting from Room H of the Villa of P. Fannius Synistor at Boscoreale

Maker unknown · ca. 50–40 BCE · Fresco · 186.7 × 186.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting is a square of wall plaster 186.7 centimetres on each side, cut from Room H of a villa at Boscoreale. The museum bought it in 1903.
