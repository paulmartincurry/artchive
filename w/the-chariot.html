The Chariot (Cary-Yale Visconti Tarot)

Maker unknown · mid-15th century · Paint and gold on card · Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, Yale University

Free to use

The Cary-Yale is the only historical Western deck with six ranks of face cards, adding a Damsel and a Lady on horseback to the usual four. Its trumps include Faith, Hope and Charity. Every trump is painted on a gilt ground, and the number cards on silver.
