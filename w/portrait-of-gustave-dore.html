Portrait of Gustave Doré

Nadar (Gaspard-Félix Tournachon) · ca. 1856–1858 · Salted paper print · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Gustave Doré was in his mid-twenties when he sat for this photograph. He had started work at fifteen as a caricaturist for the Paris paper Le journal pour rire, and by 1854 he had illustrated Rabelais. He never married, and after the death of his father in 1849 he went on living with his mother.
