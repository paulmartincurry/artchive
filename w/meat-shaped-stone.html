Meat-Shaped Stone

Maker unknown · 18th–19th Century · Agate · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use with credit. Photo: National Palace Museum, Taipei, Open Government Data License

The stone was carved in the Qing dynasty to look like a piece of Dongpo pork, a Chinese way of cooking pork belly. Its layers are the natural bands of the stone. It is 5.73 centimetres high.
