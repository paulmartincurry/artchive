What! Sell a country! Why not sell the air, the clouds and the great sea, as well as the earth?

Speech at Vincennes

Tecumseh (Shawnee Chief) · August 1810

Tecumseh was about forty-two in August 1810. Two years earlier he and his brother Tenskwatawa had founded Prophetstown, a multi-tribal village in present-day Indiana. The argument that Native lands were held in common, and that none should be ceded without the consent of all the tribes, he had first heard at fifteen, at an intertribal conference at Lower Sandusky in 1783. He was killed at the Battle of the Thames on October 5, 1813.
