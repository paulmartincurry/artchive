My candle burns at both ends;
It will not last the night;
But ah, my foes, and oh, my friends—
It gives a lovely light!

First Fig

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1920

In 1920, the year Millay turned twenty-eight, her collection A Few Figs From Thistles was published and drew controversy. She was living in Greenwich Village, where her addresses included 75½ Bedford Street, known as the narrowest house in New York City. Floyd Dell, an editor of The Masses, and the critic Edmund Wilson both proposed marriage to her and were refused.
