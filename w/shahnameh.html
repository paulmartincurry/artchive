Shahnameh (conclusion)

Abu'l-Qasim Ferdowsi · March 8, 1010 CE

Translated by Dick Davis · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Ferdowsi began the poem in 977 and finished it on 8 March 1010, thirty-three years later. It runs to some 50,000 couplets, the longest epic poem by a single author. Most of it recasts in verse the accounts of Iran's ancient kings that already existed in prose; a small part, in passages scattered through the book, is his own invention.
