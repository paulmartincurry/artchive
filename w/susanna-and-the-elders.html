Susanna and the Elders

Artemisia Gentileschi · 1610 · Oil on canvas · Schloss Weißenstein, Pommersfelden

Free to use

This is Artemisia Gentileschi's earliest known signed and dated work. The last digit of the date was questioned from 1845, and an x-ray made in 1970 confirmed that it is written as 1610. Her father, Orazio, was her first teacher. The painting hangs at Schloss Weißenstein in Pommersfelden.
