Pale Blue Dot

Voyager 1 · 1990 · Spacecraft image

Free to use

Voyager 1 took this picture at 04:48 GMT on 14 February 1990, from about six billion kilometres away, and its cameras were powered down afterwards. Carl Sagan had proposed the shot in 1980, and it waited nine years because of fears that pointing so near the Sun would ruin the camera. The image combines three frames shot through blue, green and violet filters, each exposed for under a second. Earth fills 0.12 of one pixel out of 640,000. The bands of light are sunlight reflecting off the camera and its sunshade.
