Thus I; faltering forward,
Leaves around me falling,
Wind oozing thin through the thorn from norward
And the woman calling.

The Voice

Thomas Hardy · 1914

Emma Hardy died at Max Gate on 27 November 1912, at the age of 72, and was buried three days later at Stinsford. From 1899 she had lived as a virtual recluse, spending much of her time in attic rooms she had asked Hardy to build for her. In her attic bedroom he found a notebook titled What I Think of My Husband. The poem was published in Satires of Circumstance in 1914.
