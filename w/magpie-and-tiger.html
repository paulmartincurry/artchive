Magpie and Tiger

Maker unknown · 19th century

Free to use

Korean folk paintings of this kind, called minhwa, were mostly made by travelling or unknown painters without formal training. The pigments were made from natural ingredients and laid on hanji, Korean paper, and the finished picture was hung by the front door. In the magpie and tiger genre the tiger is drawn to look foolish on purpose, and the type is nicknamed the idiot tiger.
