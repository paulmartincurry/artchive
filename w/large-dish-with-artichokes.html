Large Dish with Artichokes

Maker unknown · c. 1535–40 · Fritware with underglaze-painted design · Diameter 36.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Potters at Iznik made vessels and tiles like this for the Ottoman court in Istanbul. The artichoke pattern at the centre was probably borrowed from luxury Italian textiles brought into the court. The dish was painted around 1535 to 1540, in the reign of Suleyman the Magnificent, and is 36.4 centimetres across. It passed through the Sulzbach, Stora and Scarisbrick collections before Cleveland bought it in 1995.
