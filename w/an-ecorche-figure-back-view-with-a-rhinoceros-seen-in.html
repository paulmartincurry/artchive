An Écorché Figure, Back View, with a Rhinoceros Seen in the Background

Bernhard Siegfried Albinus & Jan Wandelaar · 1742 · Line engraving · 56 × 40 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

To copy the body accurately, Albinus and Wandelaar hung nets of square mesh at set distances between the artist and the specimen and drew by the grid. The scenery behind the figures was Wandelaar's addition; scholars such as Petrus Camper criticised it and Albinus defended him. Albinus is thought to have spent 24,000 florins on the atlas, and from 1746 Wandelaar lived in his house.
