Mind like a floating wide cloud.
Sunset like the parting of old acquaintances
Who bow over their clasped hands at a distance.
Our horses neigh to each other as we are departing.

Taking Leave of a Friend

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1915

Translated by Ezra Pound

Mary McNeil Fenollosa met Ezra Pound at a literary salon in London late in 1913 and asked him to organise and edit her late husband's notes, the fruit of two decades of living and teaching in Asia. Pound first used them to rewrite Fenollosa's versions of Noh plays, then turned to the Chinese poems that became Cathay.
