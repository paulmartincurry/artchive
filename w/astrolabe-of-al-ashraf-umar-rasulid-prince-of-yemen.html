Astrolabe of al-Ashraf Umar, Rasulid Prince of Yemen

Umar ibn Yusuf al-Muzaffari · dated 690 AH/1291 CE · Brass; cast and hammered, pierced, chased, inlaid with silver · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The maker was a prince of Yemen, Umar ibn Yusuf, and the instrument is dated 1291. Four years later he became ruler himself, and his reign ended the following year. The brass case is 15.6 centimetres across and holds four plates, each 12.7 centimetres wide. It came to the Met in 1891 in the bequest of Edward C. Moore.
