The Martyrdom of Saint Bartholomew

Jusepe de Ribera · 1634 · Oil on canvas · 104 × 113 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Ribera signed the canvas at the lower right in 1634 and wrote español after his name. He had moved to Naples in late 1616 and stayed there for the rest of his life. The painting was bought in Italy about 1810 for George Cranstoun, Lord Corehouse, and remained with the family in Scotland until a London sale in 1983.
