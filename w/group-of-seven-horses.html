Group of Seven Horses

Hans Baldung Grien · 1534 · Woodcut · Sheet: 21.8 × 32.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Baldung worked mainly in woodcut. Most of his hundreds of woodcuts were commissioned for books, and fewer than 100 are single sheets like this one. He dated it 1534, when he was about fifty, eleven years before his death in Strasbourg.
