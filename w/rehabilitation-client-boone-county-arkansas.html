Rehabilitation Client, Boone County, Arkansas

Ben Shahn · October 1935 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Ben Shahn joined the Resettlement Administration's photographers in 1935 on the recommendation of his friend and former roommate Walker Evans, and took this in Boone County, Arkansas, that October. It is a 35 mm negative. For candid pictures Shahn often fitted a right-angle viewfinder to his Leica.
