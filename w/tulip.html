Tulip

Ustad Mansur · early 1600s

Free to use

Mansur was a court painter to the Mughal emperor Jahangir, who visited Kashmir in 1620. Around that time Mansur painted at least one hundred flowers from the Kashmir valley, and this red tulip is among them. Which species it shows is still debated. He began as an assistant and colourist on manuscripts in the 1590s and gained the title of ustad, or master, towards the end of Akbar's reign. He died in 1624.
