Retiring a Locomotive Driver Wheel, Santa Fe Railway Shops, Shopton, Iowa

Jack Delano · March 1943 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The caption describes the job: the steel tyre is heated with gas until it can be slipped over the driving wheel, and it grips tight as it cools. Delano made the picture in March 1943 at the Santa Fe shops near Fort Madison, Iowa. He was born Yakov Ovcharov in the Russian Empire and was twenty-eight at the time.
