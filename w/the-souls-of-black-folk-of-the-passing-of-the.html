The Souls of Black Folk — Of the Passing of the First-Born (Ch. XI)

W. E. B. Du Bois · 1903

The chapter tells of the death of Du Bois's first child, his son Burghardt, as an infant. The boy had diphtheria, and white doctors in Atlanta refused to treat Black patients. Du Bois had married Nina Gomer in 1896; their daughter Yolande was born after Burghardt's death.
