Elephant Mask (mbap mteng)

Bamileke-style maker · early 1900s · Cotton, burlap, glass beads, twine, leather, and wood · 139.7 x 50.8 x 19.1 cm (55 x 20 x 7 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The name mbap mteng means animal with huge ears. The beads came from Venice and Bohemia, and the triangles stand for the leopard; leopard and elephant both meant royal power. Members of the Kuosi society wore such masks for dances and funerals.
