Nocturne: Palaces

James McNeill Whistler · 1879–80 · Etching and drypoint; printed in dark brown ink on drum mounted medium weight ivory laid paper. · Sheet: 11 1/2 × 7 13/16 in. (29.2 × 19.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Whistler first learned to etch as a young man at the United States Coast Survey, moved to its etching division after he was caught drawing sea serpents and mermaids in the margins of maps; he lasted there two months. He was in his mid-forties when he made this plate. It was published in 1886 in his Second Venice Set, a portfolio of twenty-six etchings. This impression is printed in dark brown ink, and Harold K. Hochschild gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1940.
