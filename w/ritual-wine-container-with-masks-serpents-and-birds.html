Ritual Wine Container (Fangyi) with Masks (Taotie), Serpents, and Birds

Maker unknown · c. 1100 BCE · Bronze · 35.3 × 24.8 × 23.3 cm · National Museum of Asian Art, Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The vessel carries a cast inscription of 187 characters, one of the longest from the early Zhou period, repeated inside the body and the lid. A court scribe named Ling might have composed the text himself. It commemorates three days of meetings and ceremonies at Chengzhou, after which Ling was awarded ritual wine, metal and oxen. The Freer Gallery of Art bought the bronze in 1930 from Tonying and Company, New York.
