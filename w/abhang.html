Abhang

Sant Tukārām · ca. 1640 CE

Translated by Dilip Chitre · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

An abhang is a metrical Marathi verse form, and tradition holds that Tukaram composed some 4,500 of them, probably between 1632 and 1650. They were first printed in a modern edition in 1869, with a subsidy from the British colonial government in Bombay. That edition noted that some of the manuscripts it relied on had been corrected and rearranged in the two centuries after his death.
