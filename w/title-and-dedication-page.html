Title and Dedication Page (Imaginary Voyage to the Moon)

Filippo Morghen · about 1769 · etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is the title page of a set of ten etchings; the nine plates after it show the imagined life of people on the moon, with sailboats driven by giant bellows and pumpkins used as boats and houses. Filippo Morghen based the series on three books about voyages to the moon, among them Francis Godwin's Man in the Moone of 1640. The set is dedicated to Sir William Hamilton, ambassador to the Court of Naples. Cleveland bought it in 2023.
