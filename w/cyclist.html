Cyclist

Natalia Goncharova · 1913 · Oil on canvas · Russian Museum, St Petersburg

Free to use

Natalia Goncharova first trained as a sculptor in Moscow and won a silver medal for sculpture in 1903–04. She was thirty-one or thirty-two when she painted Cyclist. Her exhibitions in Moscow and St Petersburg in 1913 and 1914 were the first in which an independent gallery promoted a new artist. In 1921 she moved to Paris, where she lived until her death.
