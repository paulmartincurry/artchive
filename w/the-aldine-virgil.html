The Aldine Virgil

Aldus Manutius & Punchcutter Francesco Griffo · 1501 · Ink and paint on vellum · British Library, London

Free to use

This edition of Virgil, issued in Venice in April 1501, was the first book printed in italic type. Aldus Manutius, a scholar and teacher before he was a printer, had hired Francesco Griffo to design and cut his typefaces. Early italic faces were designed mainly to save on the cost of paper. The dolphin and anchor that Aldus took as his device in the same year came from a Roman silver coin given to him by Pietro Bembo.
