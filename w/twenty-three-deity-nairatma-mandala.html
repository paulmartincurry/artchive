Twenty-three Deity Nairatma Mandala

Maker unknown · c. 1375 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on cloth · Overall: 82.5 × 72.4 cm (32 1/2 × 28 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

An inscription along the bottom edge names the subject: a mandala of Nairatma with twenty-three deities. The outermost ring holds eight cremation grounds, and the teacher Virupa is painted twice, once among the lineage masters along the top and once in the ring, being served a cup of liquor. The cloth was painted in central Tibet and is 82.5 centimetres tall. Cleveland bought it from a London dealer in 1993.
