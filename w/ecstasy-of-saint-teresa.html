Ecstasy of Saint Teresa

Gian Lorenzo Bernini · 1647–1652 · Marble · Santa Maria della Vittoria, Rome

Pope Innocent X had shunned Bernini, which left the sculptor's studio free to work for Cardinal Federico Cornaro, who wanted a burial chapel. Bernini designed the whole chapel as well as carving the group, and lit the figures from a hidden window above them. The work was finished in 1652 and cost 12,000 scudi. A terracotta model about 47 centimetres high, made between 1644 and 1647, still shows his fingerprints; it is in the Hermitage.
