Emblematic Still Life with Flagon, Glass, Jug and Bridle

Johannes Torrentius · 1614 · Oil on panel · Diameter 51 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Torrentius signed and dated the bit of the bridle, JT 1614. A 1629 note in the state papers of Charles I of England listed seven of his pictures at a friend's house in Lisse and called this round panel his finest piece, and the king's branded monogram is on the back. Torrentius was later convicted of blasphemy and his works were ordered to be burned; few of his paintings survive.
