Two Eagles

Bada Shanren · dated 1702 · Hanging scroll, ink on paper · 187.3 × 90.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bada Shanren painted this scroll, nearly two metres tall, in 1702, when he was seventy-six. Of 179 works by him that survive, 167 were made between 1684 and his death in 1705, in his sixties and seventies. He had begun on small album leaves and in these late years preferred large hanging scrolls, where his brushwork could show at full scale.
