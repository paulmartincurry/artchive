Awake! for Morning in the Bowl of Night
Has flung the Stone that puts the Stars to Flight:
And Lo! the Hunter of the East has caught
The Sultan's Turret in a Noose of Light.

Rubáiyát — Awake! for Morning in the Bowl of Night (I, first edition)

Omar Khayyam (trans. Edward FitzGerald) · 1859

FitzGerald's second source was a transcript of a manuscript that Edward Cowell found in 1857 in the library of the Royal Asiatic Society of Bengal in Calcutta. He made a revised draft in January 1859 and had 250 copies privately printed. The first edition held 75 quatrains.
