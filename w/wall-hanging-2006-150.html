Wall Hanging (pardah), 2006.150

Maker unknown · 1800–1850 · Silk: warp-faced plain weave, warp-ikat; cotton weft · 211.8 x 142.2 cm (83 3/8 x 56 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

It is made of five loom widths, the three in the middle patterned with amulets and the two outer ones with palmettes, in five colours. Hangings like this decorated the reception rooms of the urban elite in Central Asia; it is among the earliest known.
