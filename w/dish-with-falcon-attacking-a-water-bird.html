Dish with Falcon Attacking a Water Bird

Maker unknown · 1100s · Fritware with carved decoration and underglaze design, Lakabi ware · Diameter 40 cm, height 8.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The dish was possibly made at Tell Minis in Syria in the 1100s. The design was carved with raised outlines so that glazes of different colours could sit side by side without running together in the kiln. Falconry, sanctioned by the Qur'an, was a favourite sport of Muslim rulers for centuries. The dish is 40 centimetres across; Cleveland bought it in 1938.
