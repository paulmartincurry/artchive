Narmer Palette

Maker unknown · ca. 3100 BCE · Greywacke · Egyptian Museum, Cairo

Free to use

James Quibell and Frederick W. Green dug this palette out of the Temple of Horus at Nekhen in the season of 1897–98, and did not record the exact spot clearly. It is carved in low relief on both sides from a single piece of stone, 63 centimetres by 42, from a quarry at Wadi Hammamat. Palettes of this kind were used for grinding cosmetics, but this one is too large and heavy for personal use and was probably made for a temple. It has survived about five thousand years in almost perfect condition.
