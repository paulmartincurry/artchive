Young Farmers

August Sander · 1914, published 1929 · Black-and-white photograph

Free to use

Later research identified the three young men as Otto Krieger, August Klein and his cousin Ewald Klein, from the village of Dünebusch, probably on their way to a dance in Halscheid. They were not farmers: two worked in an iron-ore mine and the third in the mine's office. All three were sent to fight in Belgium soon after Sander took the picture in 1914, and August Klein was killed. Sander published it in 1929 in his book Face of Our Time.
