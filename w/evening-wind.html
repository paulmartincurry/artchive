Evening Wind

Edward Hopper · 1921 · Etching · Plate 17.8 × 21 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Hopper made this etching in 1921, when he was still bound to illustration by economic necessity, work he had come to detest. His etchings began to receive public recognition in the early 1920s, and in 1923 they won him two awards. This impression is signed in pencil at the lower right.
