Self-Portrait at the Easel

Judith Leyster · ca. 1630 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Leyster offered this painting to the Haarlem painters' guild in 1633, when she was applying to become a master; she was the guild's first woman member. X-rays show that the canvas on her easel first held a portrait of a young girl, which she changed to a fiddler from another of her paintings. She holds eighteen brushes. For centuries the picture was attributed to Frans Hals, and it was given back to her only when the museum acquired it in 1949.
