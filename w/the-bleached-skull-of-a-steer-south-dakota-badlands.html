The Bleached Skull of a Steer, South Dakota Badlands

Arthur Rothstein · May 1936 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Arthur Rothstein took this in the South Dakota Badlands in May 1936, about two months before he turned 21. It is on a small square nitrate negative, 2 1/4 inches a side or less.
