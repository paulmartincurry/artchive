Ejiri in Suruga Province (Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1830–32 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 25.1 × 37.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

For each print in the series, Hokusai's drawing on paper was glued to a woodblock to guide the carving, so the original design was lost. Every colour in the image needed a separate block. The publisher, Nishimura Yohachi, had planned to take the series past a hundred prints, but publication stopped at forty-six.
