Saturn's Hexagon in Infrared

Cassini, NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona · 2013 · False-colour infrared image, polar projection (frame from a movie)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona

The hexagon is a six-sided jet stream circling Saturn's north pole. This is a frame from a Cassini movie that maps it in infrared, seen from straight above.
