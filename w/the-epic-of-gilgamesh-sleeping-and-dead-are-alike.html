Sleeping and dead are alike, from Death they mark no distinction

The Epic of Gilgamesh — Sleeping and dead are alike (Tablet X)

Anonymous (Standard Babylonian) · c. 1200 BCE

Translated by R. Campbell Thompson, 1928

About 15,000 fragments of Assyrian cuneiform tablets were found in the Library of Ashurbanipal at Nineveh. The British Museum later hired George Smith to study them, and in 1872 he read translated fragments before the Society of Biblical Archaeology.
