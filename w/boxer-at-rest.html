Boxer at Rest

Maker unknown · ca. 100 BCE · Bronze · Palazzo Massimo alle Terme, Rome

The statue was dug up on the slopes of the Quirinal in Rome in 1885, and appears to have been buried with care in antiquity. It was cast in eight pieces by the lost-wax process and soldered together, with the joins filed until they barely show. Copper inlays mark the lips, the wounds on the face and the drops of blood on the shoulder, forearm and thigh. The fingers and toes were worn down by the hands of passers-by in ancient times. There is no signature on it.
