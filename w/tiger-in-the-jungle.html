Tiger in the Jungle

Paul Elie Ranson · 1893 · color lithograph · Sheet: 58.6 x 41.4 cm (23 1/16 x 16 5/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The yellow is printed, not the colour of the paper. Ranson made it for L'Estampe originale, a series that issued original prints by living artists to collectors in the 1890s.
