The Unicorn Purifies Water (The Unicorn Tapestries)

Maker unknown · 1495–1505 · Wool warp with wool, silk, silver, and gilt wefts · Overall: 145 × 149in. (368.3 × 378.5cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The design for this tapestry was drawn in France, and the weaving was done in the Southern Netherlands, in wool and silk with threads of silver and gilt. It is about 3.7 metres tall and 3.8 metres wide. John D. Rockefeller Jr. gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1937.
