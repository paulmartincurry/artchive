Fog everywhere. Fog up the river, where it flows among green aits and meadows; fog down the river, where it rolls defiled among the tiers of shipping and the waterside pollutions of a great (and dirty) city.

Bleak House — Fog everywhere (Ch. I, opening)

Charles Dickens · 1852–1853

The novel first appeared as a serial between 12 March 1852 and 12 September 1853. It came out in 20 monthly instalments, each with 32 pages of text and two illustrations by Phiz, the last two issued together as a double number.
