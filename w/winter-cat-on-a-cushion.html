Winter: Cat on a Cushion

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1909 · Lithograph in 6 colors (red, ochre, yellow, black, gray-brown, brown) from two stones, with scraping on stone, on ivory wove paper · 50.3 × 62.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Steinlen printed this cat in six colours from only two stones, with scraping on the stone. Born in Lausanne, he trained as a designer at a textile mill in Mulhouse before moving to Montmartre, and he also modelled cats in sculpture. He was about fifty when he made the print.
