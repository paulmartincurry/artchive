Ulysses — The heaventree of stars

James Joyce · 1922

Joyce wrote the novel over seven years, from 1914 to 1921. He had resolved that it would come out on his fortieth birthday, 2 February 1922, and his publisher in Paris, Sylvia Beach, received the first three copies from the printer that morning.
