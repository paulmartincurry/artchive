The Artchive
Hug me, kiss me, suck my juices
Squeezed from goblin fruits for you,
Goblin pulp and goblin dew.
Eat me, drink me, love me;

Goblin Market — Hug Me, Kiss Me

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Rossetti wrote Goblin Market in the spring of 1859. Shortly afterwards she began volunteering at Highgate Penitentiary, a refuge for women. In her first book, in 1862, it was illustrated by her brother Dante Gabriel Rossetti.
