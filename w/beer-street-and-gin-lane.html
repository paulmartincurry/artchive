Beer Street and Gin Lane

William Hogarth · 1751 · Etching and engraving on paper · Victoria and Albert Museum

Free to use

William Hogarth priced the two prints at one shilling each, with a better impression at one shilling and sixpence, and advertised them in the London Evening Post in February 1751. They were engraved directly from drawings, and no paintings of the scenes exist. The verses beneath were composed by the Reverend James Townley. Hogarth never sold the plates, and in 1759 he reworked Beer Street, replacing the Frenchman lifted by the blacksmith.
