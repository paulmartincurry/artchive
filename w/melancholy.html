Melancholy

Lucas Cranach the Elder · 1532 · Oil on panel · 51 × 97 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Cranach, who was probably born in 1472, painted this panel in 1532; it measures 51 by 97 centimetres. He was court painter to the Electors of Saxony in Wittenberg from 1505 to 1550. The SMK records the painting's acquisition in 1759.
