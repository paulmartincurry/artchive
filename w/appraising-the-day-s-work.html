Appraising the Day's Work

Anna Ancher & Michael Ancher · 1883 · Oil on canvas · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Anna and Michael Ancher had been married for three years when this picture was painted, and she was twenty-three or twenty-four. She was the only one of the Skagen painters who was born and grew up in Skagen, where her father owned the Brøndums Hotel. She kept painting after her marriage, against the pressure on married women to give their time to the household. The couple bought their house in Skagen the following year.
