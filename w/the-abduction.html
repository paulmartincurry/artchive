The Abduction (A Glove)

Max Klinger · 1880 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Glove was Klinger's first work to tell a story in a sequence of pictures. In this ninth plate the glove is stolen from him in the night by a dreamlike creature. The tenth and last shows it lying on a table with Cupid beside it.
