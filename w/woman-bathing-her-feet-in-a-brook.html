Woman Bathing Her Feet in a Brook

Camille Pissarro · 1895 · Oil on canvas · 73 × 92 cm (28 1/2 × 36 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In November 1894 Pissarro wrote to his son Lucien that the picture of a little peasant girl dipping her feet in the water was almost done but lacked that little something. His reworking may explain its heavily built-up surface.
