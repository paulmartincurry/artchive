El Castillo, Chichén Itzá

Maker unknown · ca. 900–1200 · Limestone

The pyramid is 24 metres high, with a temple of another 6 metres on top, and its square base measures 55.3 metres across. It was built over an older temple. Excavators who began digging inside in April 1931 found a Chac Mool figure in 1935 and, the next year, a second room holding a jaguar painted red with cinnabar. Each of the four stairways has about 91 steps. Visitors have not been allowed to climb it since around 2006, after a climber fell to her death.
