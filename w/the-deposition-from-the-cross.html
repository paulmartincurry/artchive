The Deposition from the Cross

Jacopo Pontormo · c. 1525–1528 · Tempera on wood · Church of Santa Felicita, Florence

Free to use

Pontormo completed the altarpiece in 1528, in tempera on wood. It is still above the altar of the Capponi Chapel in the church of Santa Felicita in Florence. Legend has it that he painted himself at the extreme right. The decoration he made for the chapel's dome is lost.
