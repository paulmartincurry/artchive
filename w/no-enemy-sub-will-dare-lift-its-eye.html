No Enemy Sub Will Dare Lift Its Eye

Maker unknown · 1941–43 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Made under the WPA in Washington, D.C., between 1941 and 1943, to ask people to lend their binoculars to the Navy. They were to be packed carefully and sent to the Naval Observatory. The Library of Congress conservation division treated it in 1976.
