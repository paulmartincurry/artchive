Anger (The Seven Deadly Sins)

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · published 1558 · Engraving on laid paper · Plate: 22.5 × 29.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Pieter Bruegel drew the design and Pieter van der Heyden engraved it for the Antwerp publisher Hieronymus Cock, who issued the plate in 1558. The Latin line in the lower margin says that anger makes the face swell and the veins grow black with blood.
