Ravages of War (The Disasters of War, plate 30)

Francisco de Goya · 1810 (published 1863) · Etching, drypoint, burin, burnisher · Plate: 14 × 16.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Short of copper during the war, Goya cut two of his earlier etched landscapes in half to make four plates for this series. Partly for that reason the plates vary in size, from 142 by 168 millimetres to 163 by 260.
