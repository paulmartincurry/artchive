Vishnu Riding on Garuda

Maker unknown · dated 1004 · Gilt copper repoussé · H. 16 1/2 in., W. 11 5/8 in., D. 3 3/8 in. · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This figure from Nepal was hammered from copper sheet and gilded, and stands about forty-two centimetres tall. It carries a date of 1004. It was in the Zimmerman Family Collection before the Metropolitan Museum bought it in 2012.
