Sample book

S.A. Maxwell & Co. · 1915 · Machine-printed, airbrushed, embossed, oatmeal paper · Overall: 72 × 46.5 × 5.5 cm (28 3/8 × 18 5/16 × 2 3/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The year 1915 is stamped on the front cover of this sample book. Inside are papers for living rooms, halls, dining rooms and bedrooms, each design in several colourways, some with a fabric swatch attached, and the more expensive samples are at the front. Closed, the book is 72 by 46.5 centimetres and 5.5 deep.
