Wall Street, New York (Camera Work 48, plate I)

Paul Strand · 1915, published 1916 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Strand photographed people walking past the J.P. Morgan bank at 23 Wall Street in 1915, as he and Stieglitz were turning from soft-focus pictorial work toward straight photography. Stieglitz printed it as the first plate of Camera Work number 48, in October 1916. Only two vintage prints are known, one at the Philadelphia Museum of Art with the negatives. In 1921 Strand and Charles Sheeler included a similar shot in their film Manhatta.
