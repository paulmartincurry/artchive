The Sacrifice of Isaac

Caravaggio · 1603–1604 · Oil on canvas · Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

According to the early biographer Giovanni Pietro Bellori, Caravaggio painted this subject for Cardinal Maffeo Barberini, the future Pope Urban VIII, who paid him one hundred scudi in instalments between May 1603 and January 1604. Isaac has been identified as Cecco Boneri, who modelled for Caravaggio in several pictures. X-rays show that Cecco also sat for the angel, whose profile and hair were later changed to hide the likeness.
