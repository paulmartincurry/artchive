I Lock My Door Upon Myself

Fernand Khnopff · 1891 · Oil on canvas · Neue Pinakothek, Munich

Free to use with credit. Photo: Yelkrokoyade, CC BY-SA 4.0

Khnopff made his first contacts with England in 1889, two years before he painted this, and went on staying and exhibiting there regularly. Edward Burne-Jones became a friend. From 1895 until the outbreak of war in 1914 he wrote the Brussels column of the British art journal The Studio.
