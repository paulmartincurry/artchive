Great Buddha of Kamakura

Maker uncertain · ca. 1252 · Bronze

Free to use with credit. Photo: ProtoplasmaKid, CC BY 4.0

A wooden Buddha stood here first, finished in 1243 after ten years of work and damaged by a storm five years later. The bronze that replaced it dates from around 1252 and is hollow, 13.35 metres tall with its base. It was once gilded, and traces of gold leaf remain near the ears. Storms wrecked its hall twice, and a tsunami carried the last one away in 1498; the statue has stood in the open air ever since.
