And I will come again, my Luve,
Tho' it ware ten thousand mile.

A Red, Red Rose

Robert Burns · 1794

Burns described this as a simple old Scots song that he had picked up, and he gave it to the Italian musician Pietro Urbani to set to music. Urbani was the first to print it, in 1794. The tune it is now sung to, Low Down in the Broom, was not joined to the words until 1821. In his last years Burns worked on traditional Scottish songs and saw to the preservation of more than 300.
