Love not Pleasure; love God. This is the Everlasting Yea, wherein all contradiction is solved: wherein whoso walks and works, it is well with him.

Sartor Resartus — Love Not Pleasure; Love God (Book II, Ch. IX, The Everlasting Yea)

Thomas Carlyle · 1833–1834

The first edition in book form appeared in Boston in 1836, arranged by Ralph Waldo Emerson, who much admired Carlyle. Emerson's dealings with the American publishers earned Carlyle a high payment, which the novel never brought him in Britain.
