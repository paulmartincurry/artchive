The raison d'être of language is an idea to be expressed. When the idea is expressed, the language may be ignored. But where shall I find a man to ignore language, with whom I may be able to converse?

Zhuangzi — The Fish-Trap and the Words (ch. XXVI)

Zhuangzi (Chuang Tzu) · 4th century BC

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, 1889

Like other Warring States texts of its kind, the Zhuangzi probably grew through several hands over time. Its influence can already be traced in late Warring States works such as the Han Feizi.
