Death, Tarot de Marseille (XIII)

Jean Dodal · 1701–15 · Woodcut, coloured by hand or with stencils · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

Card XIII is usually left without a name in the Marseilles pattern. The deck made by Noblet around 1650 is an exception and labels it as Death. The cards were printed from woodcuts and coloured by hand or through stencils.
