Whistler's Mother (Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1)

James McNeill Whistler · 1871 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay

Free to use

Anna McNeill Whistler sat for her son at 96 Cheyne Walk in Chelsea, where they were living. The Royal Academy came close to rejecting the picture in 1872, and it was the last painting he ever submitted there. The words about the painter's mother were added to the title for the exhibition, and the number came later, after Thomas Carlyle saw the picture and agreed to sit for a second arrangement. Whistler designed the frame himself. He eventually pawned the painting, and it was acquired for the Musée du Luxembourg in Paris in 1891.
