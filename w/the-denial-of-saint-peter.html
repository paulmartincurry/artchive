The Denial of Saint Peter

Caravaggio (Michelangelo Merisi) · 1610 · Oil on canvas · 37 × 49 3/8 in. (94 × 125.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

One of the last two paintings Caravaggio made, probably finished in Naples in the summer of 1610; he died in July of that year. In 1613 Guido Reni accepted the canvas, valued at 240 scudi, in part payment of a debt owed to him. After the Second World War a Neapolitan collector bought it from an antiques dealer as an anonymous work. It was attributed to Caravaggio only after a restoration in 1959 to 1964.
