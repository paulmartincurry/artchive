Smart lad, to slip betimes away
From fields where glory does not stay
And early though the laurel grows
It withers quicker than the rose.

To an Athlete Dying Young

A. E. Housman · 1896

This is poem XIX of the 63 in A Shropshire Lad. Housman wrote most of them in 1895 while living at Byron Cottage in Highgate. One publisher rejected the book, and it came out the following year partly at his own expense. The first printing was 500 copies, which took until 1898 to sell. He declined royalties at first so that the price could be kept down.
