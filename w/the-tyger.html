Tyger, tyger, burning bright
In the forests of the night,

The Tyger

William Blake · 1794

Songs of Experience, where this poem first appeared in 1794, followed William Blake's Songs of Innocence of 1789. The two books were then issued together in 54 illustrated plates, under a title that names Blake as both author and printer. He reprinted the work throughout his life, sometimes with the plates in a different order. Only 28 copies made in his lifetime are known to exist, and Copy A is held by the British Museum.
