Slag Run-Off from an Open Hearth Furnace, Republic Steel, Youngstown, Ohio

Alfred T. Palmer · November 1941 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The agency caption explains the moment: slag is drawn off the furnace just before the molten steel is poured into ladles for ingots. Palmer made the colour transparency at Republic Steel in Youngstown, Ohio, in November 1941. It reached the Library of Congress from the Office of War Information in 1944.
