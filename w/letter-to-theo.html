Why, I ask myself, should the shining dots of the sky not be as accessible as the black dots on the map of France? If we take the train to get to Tarascon or Rouen, we take death to reach a star.

Letter to Theo (July 1888)

Vincent van Gogh · July 9–10, 1888

Translated by As published by WebExhibits (ed. Robert Harrison); wording of The Complete Letters of Vincent van Gogh (1958), trans. Johanna van Gogh-Bonger and C. de Dood · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Van Gogh was thirty-five and living in Arles, in the south of France, when he wrote this letter. Theo, his younger brother by four years, supported him financially, and the two kept up a long correspondence.
