Studies for the Libyan Sibyl (Sistine Chapel Ceiling)

Michelangelo Buonarroti · ca. 1510–11 · Red chalk, with small accents of white chalk on the left shoulder of the figure in the main study (recto); soft black chalk, or less probably charcoal (verso) · Sheet: 11 3/8 × 8 7/16 in. (28.9 × 21.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The model for the Libyan Sibyl was a young man; in the painted ceiling the figure is a woman holding open a large book. Here he twists over his left shoulder with his hands raised around an object that is not drawn. The back of the sheet has a seated figure, a knee and a small sketch in black chalk. Michelangelo was thirty-five or thirty-six. The Met bought the drawing in 1924 with money from the bequest of Joseph Pulitzer.
