No taxation without representation

American colonists · 1768

In the dispute over British taxes on the American colonies, the slogan first appeared in a headline of the London Magazine in February 1768, over a printing of Lord Camden's speech to Parliament. The colonists argued that, unrepresented in Parliament, they could not lawfully be taxed by it, under acts such as the Stamp Act and the Townshend Acts.
