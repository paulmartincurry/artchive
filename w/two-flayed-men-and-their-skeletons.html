Two Flayed Men and Their Skeletons

Domenico del Barbiere · c. 1540–45 · Engraving · 24.1 × 33.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Domenico del Barbiere was a Florentine who settled and married at Troyes, in France, between 1530 and 1533. He joined the Italian artists working under Primaticcio and Rosso Fiorentino at Fontainebleau, on both stucco and fresco, and made engravings as well. In 1541 he went back to Troyes and worked as a sculptor for its churches.
