Keelmen Heaving in Coals by Moonlight

J. M. W. Turner · 1835 · Oil on canvas · 92.3 × 122.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Henry McConnel, a Manchester textile manufacturer, had this painted as a companion to a view of Venice that Turner had exhibited the year before. McConnel sold both pictures in 1849, at a time of business adversity. In 1861 he tried to buy at least one of them back and failed.
