Nemesis (The Great Fortune)

Albrecht Dürer · 1501–2 · Engraving · Sheet: 33.3 × 23.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Dürer made watercolour sketches as he crossed the Alps on his way to Italy. Some survive, and others may be deduced from the landscapes of real places in later works, this engraving among them. The impression is trimmed nearly to the plate line and is 33.3 centimetres tall.
