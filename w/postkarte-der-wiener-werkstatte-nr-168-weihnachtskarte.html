Postkarte der Wiener Werkstätte Nr. 168: Weihnachtskarte

József Divéky · 1908 · Wien Museum, Vienna

Free to use

The Wiener Werkstätte began in 1903 in three small rooms, with money from the industrialist Fritz Wärndorfer, and grew to fill a three-storey building. Postcards were one of its product lines beside metalwork, bookbinding, textiles and ceramics. The cards were numbered; this is number 168, issued in 1908.
