Kana Preface to the Kokinshū

Ki no Tsurayuki · 905 CE

Translated by Helen Craig McCullough · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

In 905 Emperor Daigo ordered four poets to compile the Kokin Wakashū, the first anthology of waka sponsored by an emperor. Ki no Tsurayuki was its chief editor and wrote this preface in Japanese; a second preface is in Chinese. His mother was a court dancer whose name is not recorded, and he was either thirty-three or thirty-nine that year. He later spent five years as governor of Tosa Province.
