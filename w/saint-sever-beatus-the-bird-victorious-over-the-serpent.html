Saint-Sever Beatus: The Bird Victorious over the Serpent (folio 13)

Attributed to Stephanus Garsia · 11th century · Illuminated manuscript · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

The manuscript was made at Saint-Sever Abbey in Gascony under Gregory of Montaner, abbot between 1028 and 1072. Stephanus Garsia, who signed his name on folio 6, is believed to have been its main artist. Picasso's Guernica was inspired by this book.
