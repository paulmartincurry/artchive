Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains

Huang Gongwang · 1347–1350 · Paper · National Palace Museum, Taipei, and Zhejiang Provincial Museum

Free to use

Huang Gongwang took up painting seriously only at fifty, and he was about eighty when he finished this scroll and gave it to a Taoist priest in 1350. Three centuries later an owner, Wu Hongyu, had it put on the fire shortly before he died so that he could take it with him. His nephew pulled it out, already burning and torn in two. The pieces measure 51.4 and 636.9 centimetres, and they were shown together again in Taipei in 2011.
