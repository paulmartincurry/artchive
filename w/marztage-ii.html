Märztage II (March Days II)

Max Klinger · 1883 · Etching with aquatint on wove paper · Plate: 45.5 × 35.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Klinger made this plate for his series Dramen in 1883, the year he moved to Paris, where he lived until 1886 or 1887. He first began sculpting at about the same time. This impression belonged to Georg Hirzel of Leipzig, was sold in Berlin in 1993, and was bought by the National Gallery of Art in 2011.
