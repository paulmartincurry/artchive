The film was long said to have cost about $20,000; Noah Isenberg, researching his book on it, found the final cost was closer to $100,000. Billy Halop was cast as Al Roberts and replaced by Tom Neal three days before filming began. The Production Code did not allow a murderer to go free, so Ulmer ended the film with Al picked up by a police car. The film was released in November 1945.
