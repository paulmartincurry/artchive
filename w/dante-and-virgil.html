Dante and Virgil

William-Adolphe Bouguereau · 1850 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Bouguereau was twenty-four when he painted this canvas, 281 centimetres high, for his third attempt at the Prix de Rome. It did not win. Five months later he heard that another painting, Shepherds Find Zenobia on the Banks of the Araxes, had taken a joint first prize, and in January 1851 he arrived at the Villa Medici in Rome. Dante and Virgil was shown at the Salon of 1850 at the Palais-Royal.
