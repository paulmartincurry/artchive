Corn Fields

Félix Vallotton · 1900 · oil on board · Unframed: 26.2 × 46.2 cm (10 5/16 × 18 3/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This small board, 26 centimetres high and 46 wide, is signed and dated twice in the lower right corner. Vallotton was thirty-four and had spent the 1890s mostly on prints; with the new century he moved over to oil painting. The picture was with a Paris gallery by 1928, sold at auction in Zurich in 2008, and was given to the museum in 2020.
