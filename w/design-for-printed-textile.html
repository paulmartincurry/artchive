Design for Printed Textile

Oberkampf & Cie. · ca. 1759–1815 · Woodcut on cream laid paper, lined · 35.7 × 31 cm (14 1/16 × 12 3/16 in.), irregular · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The number 9383 is inked at the upper left of this design from Oberkampf & Cie. at Jouy-en-Josas. The woodcut carries an allover floral pattern in six colours on a sheet 35.7 by 31 centimetres. Part of the money for its purchase was a gift from Brunschwig & Fils.
