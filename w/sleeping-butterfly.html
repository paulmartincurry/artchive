Awake! awake! I'll make of thee
My comrade, sleeping butterfly.

Sleeping Butterfly (Oki yo oki yo)

Matsuo Bashō · 17th century

Translated by Basil Hall Chamberlain, 1902

Bashō was born in 1644 near Ueno, in Iga Province, to a family of samurai descent. In his late teens he became a servant to Tōdō Yoshitada, who shared his love of collaborative verse, and his first surviving poem was published in 1662. Yoshitada died suddenly in 1666. In about the spring of 1672 Bashō moved to Edo to further his study of poetry.
