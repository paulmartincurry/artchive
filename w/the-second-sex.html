One is not born, but rather becomes a woman.

The Second Sex

Simone de Beauvoir · 1949

Translated by H. M. Parshley · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Beauvoir researched and wrote the book in about 14 months between 1946 and 1949, and some chapters first appeared in the journal Les Temps modernes. The first French publication sold around 22,000 copies in a week. The Vatican placed it on its List of Prohibited Books.
