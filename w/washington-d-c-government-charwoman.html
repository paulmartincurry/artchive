Washington, D.C. Government Charwoman

Gordon Parks · August 1942 · Safety film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Gordon Parks made this in August 1942, months after a fellowship brought him to Washington and a job in the Farm Security Administration's photography section. The woman is Ella Watson, who cleaned government offices; Parks posed her with a broom and mop in front of the flag. He later named it American Gothic, after Grant Wood's painting.
