Darab Sleeping in the Ruined Vault (Great Mongol Shahnama)

Maker unknown · c. 1330–40 · Opaque watercolor, ink and gold on paper · 25.2 × 20.4 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The Freer Gallery of Art in Washington, which owns this leaf, holds 16 pages from the manuscript, the largest group; 57 of its paintings have been identified in collections around the world. In the late 19th century the book was still bound in the library of the shahs, where it was photographed and then restored, its folios trimmed, remargined and renumbered.
