Although she feeds me bread of bitterness,
And sinks into my throat her tiger’s tooth,
Stealing my breath of life, I will confess
I love this cultured hell that tests my youth!

America

Claude McKay · 1921

Claude McKay left Jamaica for the United States in 1912 to attend Tuskegee Institute, and moved to New York in 1914. Over the next five years he managed a Brooklyn restaurant, which failed, and waited tables at a hotel in Hanover, New Hampshire, and at a Manhattan women's club. In 1917 he published two poems in The Seven Arts under the name Eli Edwards. He turned thirty-one in 1921 and was co-executive editor of The Liberator until 1922.
