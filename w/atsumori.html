The Artchive
So Atsumori fell and was slain, but now the Wheel of Fate
Has turned and brought him back.

Atsumori (closing scene)

Seami (Zeami Motokiyo) · c. 1400

Translated by Arthur Waley

Atsumori, nephew of the Taira chief Kiyomori, fell at the Battle of Ichi-no-Tani in 1184. Kumagai, who killed him, found a bamboo flute wrapped in brocade beside the body and gave it to his son. In the play Kumagai has become the priest Rensei, and the ghost of Atsumori comes back to him.
