Song of Songs 1:2-6

Song of Solomon (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · c. 3rd century BCE; KJV 1611

The title the song of songs follows a Hebrew idiom for the greatest of its kind, as in Holy of Holies. The other name, the Song of Solomon, can mean a song by, for or dedicated to Solomon. The French rabbi Rashi did not believe it was an erotic poem. Marc Chagall, Carl Theodor Dreyer, Toni Morrison, John Steinbeck and Neil Diamond have all made work drawn from it.
