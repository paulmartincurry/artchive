White Pansy

Georgia O'Keeffe · 1927 · Oil on canvas · 91.7 × 76.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

O'Keeffe kept this painting for nearly sixty years and displayed it above her living room couch before bequeathing it to the Cleveland Museum of Art. It was painted in 1927 and shown at the Museum of Modern Art in December 1929 in Paintings by Nineteen Living Americans. The small blue flowers beside the pansy are forget-me-nots.
