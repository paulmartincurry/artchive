Asiel Timor Dei, Arcángel Arcabucero

Circle of the Master of Calamarca · c. 1680 · Oil on canvas · Museo Nacional de Arte, La Paz

Free to use

Angels armed with an arquebus in place of a sword first appeared in Peru in the second half of the seventeenth century, dressed like Criollo and Andean nobles. Their poses probably come in part from the engravings in Jacob de Gheyn's Exercise in Arms of 1607. The most complete surviving series hangs in the church of Calamarca, about 60 kilometres from La Paz.
