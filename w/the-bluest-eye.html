The Bluest Eye

Toni Morrison · 1970

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

This was Morrison's first novel, published in 1970 and set in Lorain, Ohio, her hometown. It received minimal critical attention when first published, but it was placed on many university reading lists in black-studies departments. An extremely positive review appeared in The New York Times in November 1970.
