First Photograph Taken on Mars Surface

Viking 1 lander, NASA · 20 July 1976 · Spacecraft photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL

Viking 1 took this picture minutes after it landed on Mars on 20 July 1976. The centre of the frame is about 1.4 metres from the camera, and the large rock in the middle is about 10 centimetres across. At right is part of the lander's own footpad, with sand and dust on it. The dark vertical band at the upper left may be a minute in which cloud or dust passed between the Sun and the ground.
