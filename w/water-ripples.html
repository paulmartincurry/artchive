Water Ripples

Maker unknown · 1780–1830 · Mulberry paper (kozo washi) treated with fermented persimmon tannin (kakishibu), and silk threads (itoire) · Sheet: 27.3 × 41 cm (10 3/4 × 16 1/8 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The small squares around the two ripples were punched out of the paper. Two written characters sit on the lower half of the stencil. The museum bought it in memory of Mrs. Edward F. Caldwell.
