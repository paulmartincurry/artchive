Since we parted the time is already long,
Daily my clothes hang looser round my waist.
Floating clouds obscure the white sun,
The wandering one has quite forgotten home.
Thinking of you has made me suddenly old,
The months and years swiftly draw to their close.

Old Poems (1) — On and On, Always On and On

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · Han dynasty (1st century BC–1st century AD)

Translated by Arthur Waley

This is the first of the Nineteen Old Poems, a Han dynasty series. Some of them have been attributed to Mei Sheng, of the first century BC, and one to Fu I, of the first century AD, but they are not all by one hand or of one date, and internal evidence puts at least one after Mei Sheng's death. Like the poets behind the Music Bureau songs, their makers are mostly anonymous.
