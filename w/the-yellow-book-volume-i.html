The Yellow Book was a quarterly published in London at the Bodley Head from 1894 to 1897, priced at five shillings and edited by the American Henry Harland. Aubrey Beardsley was its first art editor and has been credited with the idea of the yellow cover, a colour then associated with illicit French novels. He was dismissed soon after Oscar Wilde's arrest in 1895, and died three years later at twenty-five.
