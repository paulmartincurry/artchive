Strike, frame at 1:19:45

Sergei Eisenstein · 1925 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

After two days of test shoots, the Goskino board decided to remove Eisenstein from the film, and he was allowed to go on only after studio head Boris Mikhin and the cameraman Eduard Tisse personally guaranteed it would be finished. Crowd scenes were filled with factory workers from Moscow. Completed in late 1924, the film waited for positive film stock and premiered in Leningrad on 1 February 1925.
