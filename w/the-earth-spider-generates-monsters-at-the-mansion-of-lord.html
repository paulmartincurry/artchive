The Earth Spider Generates Monsters at the Mansion of Lord Minamoto Yorimitsu

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1843 · Color woodblock print triptych · 35 × 67.7 cm (sight) · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

Kuniyoshi published this design in 1843, during the Tenpō Reforms, when prints of actors and courtesans were banned. It shows Minamoto no Yorimitsu asleep and haunted by the Earth Spider and its demons, and it was read as a veiled attack on the shogunate. It became popular with a public unhappy with the government.
