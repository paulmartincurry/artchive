The Fool, Tarot de Marseille

Jean Dodal · 1701–15 · Woodcut, coloured by hand or with stencils · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

Jean Dodal's deck is dated between 1701 and 1715, and the Bibliothèque nationale's set has been published in photographs. In the Marseilles pattern the Fool carries no number and stands outside the 21 numbered trumps, because it usually cannot win a trick.
