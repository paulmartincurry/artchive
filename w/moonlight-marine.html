Moonlight Marine

Albert Pinkham Ryder · 1870–90 · Oil and possibly wax on wood panel · 29.2 × 30.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Ryder shared a studio with the painter Robert Loftin Newman, who is believed to have influenced his turn from pastoral landscapes to more mystical subjects. He wrote poetry to accompany many of his paintings. In 1913 ten of his pictures were shown together in the Armory Show. The Met acquired this panel in 1934.
