La Reine Hortense, the Emperor's Yacht, Le Havre

Gustave Le Gray · 1856 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · Image: 31.8 × 41.3 cm (12 1/2 × 16 1/4 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Reine Hortense was the yacht of Napoleon III, and around this time Le Gray was becoming the emperor's official photographer. A year earlier he had opened a lavishly furnished portrait studio. This print, about 32 by 41 centimetres, was made from a glass negative, not the waxed paper he had used earlier in the decade.
