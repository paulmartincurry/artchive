The Red Studio

Henri Matisse · 1911 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art

Free to use

The room is the studio Matisse had built for himself in 1909. He first painted the canvas a pale yellow, then went over it with a thin coat of red. The pale outlines of the furniture are not drawn lines at all: they are narrow bands of the yellow underneath that he left uncovered. Shortly before he painted it he had seen an exhibition of Islamic art in Munich and travelled to Seville, Córdoba, Granada, St. Petersburg and Moscow.
