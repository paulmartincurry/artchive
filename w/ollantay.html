Cuzco! O thou beautiful city!
Henceforth behold thine enemy.
I’ll bare thy breast to stab thy heart,
And throw it as food for condors;

Ollantay (Act I, Scene 4)

Anonymous Quechua playwright · 18th-century manuscript; first printed 1857

Translated by Clements R. Markham, 1910

The oldest known manuscript of Ollantay belonged to an 18th-century priest, Antonio Valdés, who was for a time thought to be its author. The play was first printed in 1857, in Quechua with a German translation by Johann Jakob von Tschudi, and the first Spanish version appeared in Lima in 1868. Some scholars hold that it is Inca in origin; others that it was written under Spanish rule.
