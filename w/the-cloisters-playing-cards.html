The Cloisters Playing Cards

Maker unknown · ca. 1475–80 · Paper (four layers of pasteboard) with pen and ink, opaque paint, glazes, and applied silver and gold · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

At a Paris auction in 1978 this deck was catalogued as an incomplete tarot set of the sixteenth century. Harrie Kenter, an antiques dealer from Amsterdam, paid 8,000 guilders for it and for a time cycled around the city with it in his coat pocket. It is a complete deck of fifty-two, with suits of nooses, tethers, horns and dog collars, cut out with shears from sheets of pasted paper. The Metropolitan Museum bought it at Sotheby's in London in 1983 for $143,000.
