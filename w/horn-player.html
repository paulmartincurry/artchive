Horn Player

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · 1550–1680 · Brass, iron · 62.9 × 29.2 × 17.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Late nineteenth-century scholars wrongly concluded that Benin learned metalworking from Portuguese traders. Bronzes were made in Benin before the Portuguese arrived, and the tradition is thought to derive from, or to have been influenced by, the older kingdom of Ife. This horn player is 62.9 centimetres tall.
