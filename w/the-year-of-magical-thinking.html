The Year of Magical Thinking

Joan Didion · 2005

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Didion wrote the book between October 4 and December 31, 2004, completing it a year and a day after her husband, John Gregory Dunne, died. Quintana Roo Dunne Michael died of pancreatitis on August 26, 2005, before the book's publication, and Didion did not revise the manuscript. Knopf published it that October.
