The Hours of Jeanne d'Evreux, Queen of France

Jean Pucelle · ca. 1324–28 · Grisaille, tempera, and ink on vellum · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Each page of this prayer book is about nine centimetres tall and six wide, on vellum so thin it is almost transparent. It has 209 leaves and more than 700 illustrations have been counted in it. By the usual account King Charles IV had it made for his third wife, Jeanne d'Evreux, whom he married in 1325. The Germans confiscated it from Maurice de Rothschild in 1940 and sent it to Neuschwanstein castle. It was returned to him in 1948, and he sold it to the museum in 1954.
