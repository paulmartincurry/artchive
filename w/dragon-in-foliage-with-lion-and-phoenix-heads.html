Dragon in Foliage with Lion and Phoenix Heads

Shahkulu · mid-1500s · Ink, gum tempera, and gold on paper · Sheet: 17.3 × 40.2 cm (6 13/16 × 15 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The drawing is attributed to Shahkulu, a painter trained by Persian artists who joined the Ottoman imperial painting workshop in Istanbul in 1526 and became its head in 1545. The pearl the dragon reaches for is drawn as a seal, inscribed in Naskh script with a verse of the Qur'an. The sheet is about forty centimetres wide. It was later owned by Charles Ricketts and Charles Shannon, and the Cleveland Museum of Art bought it from Philip Hofer in 1944.
