The Artchive
Methinks, I am thrice imprisoned--ask not me
Of news that need no telling--
By loss of sight, confinement to my house,
And this vile body for my spirit's dwelling.

Methinks, I Am Thrice Imprisoned (two pieces from the Luzūmiyyāt)

Abu al-ʿAlāʾ al-Maʿarrī · ca. 1030 CE

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1907

Al-Maʿarrī lost his sight to smallpox at the age of four. After eighteen months in Baghdad, where he refused to sell his poems, he came home to Ma'arra in about 1010 and learned that his mother had died before he arrived. He stayed there for the rest of his life, and left his house only once, to go to Aleppo and plead for his brother's release.
