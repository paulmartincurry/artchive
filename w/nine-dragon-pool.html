Nine Dragon Pool

Mei Qing · 1690s · Hanging scroll, ink and light color on paper · 92 × 43.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Mei Qing probably sketched this waterfall on his second trip to Huangshan, the Yellow Mountain, in 1690, when he was 68 by the Chinese count of years. The falls drop through nine curves, each ending in a pool. He was a friend of Shitao and influenced some of the younger painter's early work. The scroll was later owned by the painter Dai Xi, whose seal is on it, and was shown in Tokyo in 1928 before Cleveland bought it in 1979.
