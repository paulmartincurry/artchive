Georgia O'Keeffe - Hands and Horse Skull

Alfred Stieglitz · 1931 · Gelatin silver print · Image 19.3 × 24.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Alfred Stieglitz was sixty-seven when he made this print in 1931. The following year he hung recent photographs of Georgia O'Keeffe in a forty-year retrospective of 127 of his works. The print came to the Art Institute of Chicago with the Alfred Stieglitz Collection.
