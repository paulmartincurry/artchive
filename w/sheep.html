Sheep

Rosa Bonheur · 1861 · Brush and brown wash over graphite, heightened with lead white · Sheet: 20.8 × 31.8 cm (8 3/16 × 12 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The highlights are in lead white, and parts of it have oxidised to pink. Bonheur signed and dated the sheet in brown wash. Two years earlier she had bought the Château de By at Thomery, near Fontainebleau, where she and Nathalie Micas lived largely apart from the world, working on commissions. In 1865 Empress Eugénie visited her studio to make her a knight of the Legion of Honour, the first woman to hold the title.
