The Martyrdom, plate four from Die Hölle

Max Beckmann · 1919 · Transfer lithograph on tan imitation Japanese paper · image 54.5 × 75 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Art Institute of Chicago identifies the woman being tortured here as Rosa Luxemburg, a co-founder of the German Communist Party, who was murdered by right-wing vigilantes in January 1919, the year of this print. The image is drawn on transfer paper and printed on tan imitation Japanese paper, 54.5 by 75 centimetres. In 1937 the Nazi government confiscated more than 500 of Beckmann's works from German museums.
