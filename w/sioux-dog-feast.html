Sioux Dog Feast

George Catlin · 1832-1837 · Oil on canvas · 24 x 29 in. (60.9 x 73.7 cm) · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

Catlin took part in a Sioux ceremony of friendship centred on a meal of dog meat, which he described in his journal as a religious ceremony and a mark of friendship.
