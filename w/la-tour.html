La Tour

Robert Delaunay · 1926 · lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Delaunay began his series of paintings of Paris and the Eiffel Tower in 1909, seventeen years before this print. When the Armory Show turned down his La ville de Paris for being too big, he had all of his works taken out of the exhibition. He was declared unfit for military duty in 1916, at the French consulate in Vigo. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought this lithograph through its John L. Severance Fund in 1989.
