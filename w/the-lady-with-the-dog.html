And it seemed as though in a little while the solution would be found, and then a new and splendid life would begin; and it was clear to both of them that they had still a long, long road before them, and that the most complicated and difficult part of it was only just beginning.

The Lady with the Dog (closing)

Anton Chekhov · 1899

Translated by Constance Garnett

The story was written in Yalta, where Chekhov had moved on his doctor's advice because of his advancing tuberculosis. It first appeared in the December 1899 issue of the magazine Russkaya Mysl. The winter before, while living in Yalta, he had fallen in love with the actress Olga Knipper, whom he married in 1901.
