Folio with Verses in Nasta'liq Script

Mir Imad al-Hasani · 1608–9 (dated 1017 AH) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · 28.6 × 19.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Mir 'Imad wrote out these verses in 1608–9, when he was about fifty-four. Born in Qazvin in 1554, he worked as a scribe in Shah 'Abbas's library and then at the court in Isfahan. In 1615 he was accused of being a Sunni and was murdered at night on his way to the bathhouse, and for several days no one dared to bury him.
