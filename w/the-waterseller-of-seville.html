The Waterseller of Seville

Diego Velázquez · c. 1618–1622 · Oil on canvas · Apsley House, London

Free to use

Velázquez painted the subject three times, and this, the original version, was made when he was in his late teens or early twenties; according to José Gudiol it was painted shortly before he moved to Madrid. A fig sits in the glass the boy is handed, put there to make the water taste fresher.
