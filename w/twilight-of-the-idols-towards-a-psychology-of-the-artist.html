This state of ecstasy must first have intensified the susceptibility of the whole machine: otherwise, no art is possible.

Twilight of the Idols — Towards a Psychology of the Artist (Skirmishes, 8)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici

Nietzsche wrote Twilight of the Idols in just over a week, between 26 August and 3 September 1888, on holiday in Sils Maria; it came out in 1889. The German title, Götzen-Dämmerung, puns on Wagner's opera Götterdämmerung. Ludovici's English appeared in the Complete Works edited by Oscar Levy, the first translation of Nietzsche into English.
