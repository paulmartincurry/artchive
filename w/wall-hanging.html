Wall Hanging

Maker unknown · 1801/50 · Silk and cotton; warp-dyed, warp-faced plain weave (ikat) · 171.5 × 144.2 cm (67 1/2 × 56 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It is an ikat: the warp threads were dyed in the pattern before the cloth was woven. Silks like this were given at weddings, worn and hung on walls, often several at once to show the owner's wealth.
