Painting nor sculpture now can lull to rest
My soul that turns to His great love on high,
Whose arms to clasp us on the cross were spread.

On the Brink of Death (Sonnet LXV, to Giorgio Vasari)

Michelangelo Buonarroti · 1554

Translated by John Addington Symonds, 1878

The sonnet is addressed to Giorgio Vasari, who four years earlier had published a life of Michelangelo, the only living artist in his book. No Western artist had had a biography published in his lifetime before. Michelangelo was seventy-nine in 1554 and had been the architect of St. Peter's Basilica since the age of seventy-one. He lived another ten years.
