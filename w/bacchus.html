Bacchus

Caravaggio · c. 1596–1598 · Oil on canvas · Uffizi Gallery

Free to use with credit. Photo: Commonists, CC BY-SA 4.0

Cardinal Francesco Maria del Monte commissioned this painting soon after Caravaggio joined his household. The painter moved into the cardinal's Palazzo Madama in 1596 and stayed five years. The model may have been his pupil Mario Minniti; his rival Giovanni Baglione believed Caravaggio painted himself with the help of a mirror. The cardinal gave the picture to the Medici family, and it has been in Florence ever since.
