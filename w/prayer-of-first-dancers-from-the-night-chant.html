In the house made of the dawn,
In the house made of the evening twilight,
In the house made of the dark cloud,

Prayer of First Dancers, from the Night Chant

Navajo (Diné) Night Chant · Printed 1897

Translated by Washington Matthews, 1897

The prayer is said on the last night of the Night Chant, the klédzi hatál. The chanter speaks it verse by verse and one of the first dancers repeats each verse after him. It is addressed to a thunderbird, spoken of as a male divinity who lives with other gods at Tse`gíhi. The closing words, said twice or four times, ended every Navajo prayer recorded up to that time.
