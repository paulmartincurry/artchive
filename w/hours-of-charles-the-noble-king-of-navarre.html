Hours of Charles the Noble, King of Navarre

Master of the Brussels Initials and Associates · c. 1404 · ink, tempera, and gold on vellum · Codex: 20.3 × 15.7 × 7 cm (8 × 6 3/16 × 2 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

King Charles the Noble of Navarre probably used this book of hours at his castle of Olite in northern Spain. A book like it took years to make, on vellum prepared from animal skins and written with a quill. In one margin a griffin holds an open book with a faint inscription, once read as Zebo da Firenze and taken for the illuminator's name; that identification has since been rejected. The book passed through three members of the Rothschild family in Paris before Cleveland bought it in 1964.
