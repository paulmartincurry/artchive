A Scenery on Dano Day (Dano pungjeong)

Sin Yun-bok · late 18th–early 19th century · Album leaf, colour on paper · Gansong Art Museum, Seoul

Free to use

Sin Yun-bok worked in the shadow of Kim Hong-do, but where Kim painted peasants with humour, Sin painted townspeople and kisaeng, in bright colours, often filling the whole sheet instead of leaving the customary empty space. By one account the erotic turn of his work helped get him expelled from the royal painting bureau, though some studies doubt he was ever a member. The album this leaf comes from was made a National Treasure of South Korea in 1970.
