The Action (A Glove)

Max Klinger · 1880 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Klinger was twenty-one when he made the ten Glove designs in 1877–78, and they were first exhibited at the Berlin Art Association in 1878. The story opens at a skating rink in Berlin, and Klinger put himself into it. This impression comes from the fourth edition of the etchings.
