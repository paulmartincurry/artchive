Lindisfarne Gospels: Carpet Page

Eadfrith of Lindisfarne · ca. 715–720 · Ink and pigments on vellum · British Library, London

Free to use

A carpet page is a full page of patterned ornament with no figures, usually placed at the beginning of a Gospel Book. The designs borrow from the metalwork of the same period. The earliest one to survive is in the Bobbio Orosius, a manuscript of the early seventh century.
