Street, Berlin (Straße, Berlin)

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner · 1913 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Kirchner painted several Berlin street scenes in 1913, and this is one of them. He had moved to the city from Dresden two years earlier with other members of Die Brücke, the group he helped found in 1905. The group formally broke up a few months before he painted this canvas, which is 120.6 centimetres tall and 91.1 wide.
