To the tintinnabulation that so musically wells
From the bells, bells, bells, bells,
Bells, bells, bells--
From the jingling and the tinkling of the bells.

The Bells (part I)

Edgar Allan Poe · 1848 (published 1849)

Poe is believed to have written The Bells in May 1848, at his cottage in Fordham, New York, near the bell tower of the Fordham college church. He sent it three times to Sartain's Union Magazine before it was taken, and was paid fifteen dollars. It did not appear until after his death in October 1849. The New York Daily Tribune ran it on its front page that month, and Sartain's printed it in November.
