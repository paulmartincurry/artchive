At the Moulin Rouge

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892–1895 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Toulouse-Lautrec put himself in the background, in profile, standing in front of Dr. Gabriel Tapié de Céleyran. The people at the table are a writer, a dancer and two photographers, with Jane Avril seen from behind. The woman fixing her hair at the back is La Goulue, who danced at the Moulin Rouge, and the face lit green at the right edge is the English dancer May Milton. The cabaret had been built in 1889, three years before he began the canvas.
