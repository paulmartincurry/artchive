The Advance-Guard, or The Military Sacrifice (The Ambush)

Frederic Remington · 1890 · Oil on canvas · 87.3 × 123.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Remington signed and dated this in red paint on the ground at lower left, in 1890. He showed it in New York in 1893, and that September Harper's Weekly reproduced it with a text under his name. It came to the Art Institute of Chicago with the George F. Harding Collection.
