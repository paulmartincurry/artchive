Astronaut's Bootprint in Lunar Soil (AS11-40-5878)

NASA (Apollo 11 crew) · 20 July 1969 · Photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

The bootprint was photographed with a 70 mm lunar surface camera during the moonwalk of 20 July 1969, in the Sea of Tranquility. Armstrong and Aldrin spent more than 21 hours on the surface in all before rejoining Michael Collins, who had stayed in lunar orbit aboard Columbia. The crew splashed down in the Pacific Ocean on 24 July.
