Last night I dreamed that angels stood without
The tavern door, and knocked in vain, and wept;
They took the clay of Adam, and, methought,
Moulded a cup therewith while all men slept.

Last Night I Dreamed That Angels Stood

Hafez of Shiraz · 14th century; English 1897

Translated by Gertrude Bell

Hafez was supported by several successive rulers of Shiraz, from Shah Abu Ishaq, who came to power while the poet was in his teens, to Timur at the end of his life; his work flourished most under the 27-year rule of Shah Shuja. He was also known as the Tongue of the Unseen. Goethe wrote his West-Eastern Divan, published in 1819, after reading Hafez in Joseph von Hammer's translation.
