Garden Pavilion

Maker unknown · ca. 1780 · Cut paper on wood frame · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

This is a magic lantern slide, made of cut paper on a wooden frame 50 centimetres wide. The pavilion is set on a black ground, with a set piece of fireworks and a cipher above its top storey and pinwheel fireworks to either side.
