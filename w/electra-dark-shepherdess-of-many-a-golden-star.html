Dark shepherdess of many a golden star,
Dost see me, Mother Night? And how this jar
Hath worn my earth-bowed head, as forth and fro
For water to the hillward springs I go?

Electra — Dark Shepherdess of Many a Golden Star

Euripides · c. 415 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray

Euripides' Electra was probably written in the mid 410s BC, likely before 413. It reworks the story Aeschylus told in The Libation Bearers, and Sophocles also wrote an Electra; which of the two came first is still uncertain. In this version Aegisthus has married Electra off to a poor farmer so that she will bear no noble heirs to avenge her father.
