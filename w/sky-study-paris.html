Sky Study, Paris

Charles Marville · 1856–57 · albumen print from wet collodion negative · Image: 15.3 × 21.4 cm (6 × 8 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The plates of the 1850s could not record land and sky properly in one exposure. Marville set his camera for the clouds, and the city under them, with the dome of Les Invalides, went almost black. He made a series of these sky views over Paris, some from rooftops and some from his own apartment window, three decades before the Eiffel Tower went up. The print is 15 by 21 centimetres.
