In Endymion, I leaped headlong into the sea, and thereby have become better acquainted with the Soundings, the quicksands, and the rocks, than if I had stayed upon the green shore, and piped a silly pipe, and took tea and comfortable advice.

Letter to James Augustus Hessey (9 October 1818)

John Keats · October 9, 1818

Endymion was first published in 1818 by Taylor and Hessey of Fleet Street in London, and this letter is to Hessey. The edition dates it Hampstead, October 9, 1818. Keats also expressed regret at making the poem public.
