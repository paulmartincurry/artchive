Bonaventure Cemetery, Four Miles from Savannah

George N. Barnard · 1866 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · image 34 × 26.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Barnard was the official army photographer for the Military Division of the Mississippi, commanded by General Sherman. His 1866 book, Photographic Views of Sherman's Campaign, holds 61 albumen prints of places that include Savannah. He had started in business in New York State in 1843, one of the first in the United States to use the daguerreotype.
