May Milton

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1895 · Colour lithograph · 79.5 × 61.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

May Milton was an English dancer who came to the Moulin Rouge in 1895 with a dance troupe. Jane Avril asked Toulouse-Lautrec for this poster ahead of an American tour, which never took place. A copy hangs on the wall in the background of Picasso's 1901 painting The Blue Room.
