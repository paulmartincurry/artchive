The gale, it plies the saplings double,
It blows so hard, 'twill soon be gone:
To-day the Roman and his trouble
Are ashes under Uricon.

On Wenlock Edge the wood's in trouble (A Shropshire Lad, XXXI)

A. E. Housman · 1896

A Shropshire Lad was published partly at Housman's own expense, after one publisher had rejected it. The first printing of 500 copies, some 160 of which were sent to the United States, did not clear until 1898. Sales revived during the Second Boer War.
