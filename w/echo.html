Come back to me in dreams, that I may give
Pulse for pulse, breath for breath:
Speak low, lean low,
As long ago, my love, how long ago!

Echo

Christina Rossetti · 1862

In 1848, at seventeen, Christina Rossetti sat for the Virgin Mary in her brother Dante Gabriel's first completed oil painting, The Girlhood of Mary Virgin. In 1853 she helped her mother run a school in Frome, which did not succeed, and the next year they returned to London, where her father died. After her collection of 1862, Gabriel named her the natural successor to Elizabeth Barrett Browning, who had died the year before.
