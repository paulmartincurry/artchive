Evening on the Chikugo River

Yoshida Hiroshi · 1927 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 27.6 × 41.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Yoshida gave this print to the Cleveland Museum of Art himself, three years after he made it in 1927. The engraver's mark at the upper right is a jizuri stamp. He had begun selling his work in the United States on a trip in 1923, after the Great Kantō earthquake, and held exhibitions across the country from a base in Boston.
