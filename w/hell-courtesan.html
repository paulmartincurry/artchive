Hell Courtesan

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1871–89 · Hanging scroll; ink, color, gold and silver on silk · 144.2 × 67.6 cm (image) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kyōsai painted this large composition again and again, with variations. In this version the courtesan stands in for the goddess Benzaiten, and the other six Gods of Good Fortune appear on her outer robe, one of them reporting sins to Enma, the King of Hell. The word for longevity is written several times in gold and silver across the robe. Her story first appeared in print in 1672.
