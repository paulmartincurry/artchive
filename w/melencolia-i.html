Melencolia I

Albrecht Dürer · 1514 · Engraving · Sheet: 9 1/2 × 7 3/8 in. (24.1 × 18.7 cm) · The Met

Free to use

On the wall of the building is a four-by-four magic square, the first printed in Europe. The two middle cells of its bottom row give the year of the engraving, 1514. Dürer's mother died on 17 May of that year. The plate exists in two states, and in the first the nine in the square is backward. He made a few pencil studies for it, and he sometimes distributed it with his St. Jerome in His Study.
