Thunderstorm Beneath the Summit (Kurofuji / Black Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · ca. 1831 · Woodblock print · Sumida Hokusai Museum, Tokyo

Free to use with credit. Photo: Sailko, CC BY 3.0

This print belongs to the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, published around 1830 to 1832, when Hokusai was about seventy. Soon after publication the blocks were damaged, losing one of the brown dots below the summit and the end of a character in his signature. For a later impression the publisher changed the sky to a purplish grey with a yellow band at the top and added a group of pine trees cut from a new block.
