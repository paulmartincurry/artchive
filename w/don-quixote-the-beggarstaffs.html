Don Quixote (Les Maîtres de l'Affiche, Plate 63)

The Beggarstaffs (William Nicholson and James Pryde) · designed 1895 · Colour lithograph · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

The Beggarstaffs were the brothers-in-law William Nicholson and James Pryde, who took the name from a sack of fodder in an old stable. They worked in collage, moving cut pieces of paper around on a board. Don Quixote was designed in 1895 for Henry Irving's production at the Lyceum Theatre and was never printed as a poster, because the client thought it a bad likeness. This is the reduced plate from Les Maîtres de l'Affiche, the Paris series of 256 colour lithographs issued monthly until November 1900.
