Wait till we empty one more cup —
West of Yang Gate there'll be no old friends.

A Song at Wei-ch'eng

Wang Wei · before 761; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Wang Wei was a musician and a painter as well as a poet. His playing of the pipa won him favour at court before he sat the palace examination, which he passed in first place in 721. None of his music survives.
