Self-Reliance — Our own rejected thoughts (opening)

Ralph Waldo Emerson · 1841

Essays, published in 1841, was Emerson's second book, and it gained favourable reviews in London and Paris. In March 1837 he had given a series of lectures at the Masonic Temple in Boston, the first he managed on his own, and found the profits much larger than when an organisation paid him to talk. He eventually gave as many as 80 lectures a year, travelling as far as California.
