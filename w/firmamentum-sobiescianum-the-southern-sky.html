Firmamentum Sobiescianum: The Southern Sky

Johannes Hevelius · 1690

Free to use

The atlas has 56 sheets of constellations and was printed in Hevelius's own house in Danzig at lavish expense, from plates he engraved many of himself. He died on his 76th birthday in January 1687, and his wife Elisabeth published it after his death. It introduced the constellation Scutum, named for the shield of King John III Sobieski after the Battle of Vienna, and Sextans, named for an instrument lost when his observatory burned in 1679.
