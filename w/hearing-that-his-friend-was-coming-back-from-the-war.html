Oh that I could shrink the surface of the World,
So that suddenly I might find you standing at my side.

Hearing that his friend was coming back from the war

Wang Jian · Tang dynasty

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

Wang Jian, whose dates are given as 766 to 831 with question marks, was a poet of the Tang dynasty. One of his poems was included in the anthology Three Hundred Tang Poems.
