Rest at pale evening....
A tall, slim tree....
Night coming tenderly
Black like me.

Dream Variations

Langston Hughes · 1926

Public domain in the United States (published 1926); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

In 1926 Langston Hughes was twenty-five and had dropped out of Columbia University. His first collection of poems came out that year, and so did his essay The Negro Artist and the Racial Mountain, printed in The Nation. The following spring he was asked to read at Fisk University in Nashville and travelled through the South, his costs met by a patron, Charlotte Osgood Mason. He later graduated from Lincoln University.
