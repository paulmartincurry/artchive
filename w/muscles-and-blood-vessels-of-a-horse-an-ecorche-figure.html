Muscles and Blood-Vessels of a Horse: An Écorché Figure, Side View (The Anatomy of the Horse)

George Stubbs · 1766 · Engraving with etching · 37.7 × 47.8 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

George Stubbs taught himself as an artist. In York, between 1745 and 1753, he painted portraits and studied human anatomy under the surgeon Charles Atkinson at the county hospital. He engraved and etched this plate himself and published it in London in 1766; the platemark measures 37.7 by 47.8 centimetres.
