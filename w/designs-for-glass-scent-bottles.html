Designs for Glass Scent Bottles

Maker unknown · ca. 1850 · Pen and brown ink, brush and gouache, gold paint over graphite on cream wove paper · Sheet: 43.2 × 29.4 cm (17 in. × 11 9/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Twelve scent bottles are drawn on this one sheet, six on the front and six on the back, each with its stopper. A style number is written in brown ink under every design. The sheet is 43.2 by 29.4 centimetres and was a gift of Virginia Hamill.
