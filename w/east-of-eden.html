East of Eden

John Steinbeck · 1952

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Steinbeck first addressed this novel to his sons Thom and John, then six and four. The working title was The Salinas Valley, and he only settled on East of Eden while copying out the sixteen verses of Cain and Abel into the text. Samuel Hamilton in the book is based on his maternal grandfather, an Irish immigrant with a ranch near King City. Viking brought out the first edition in September 1952 in two print runs, one of 1,500 signed copies, and both carry a misprint on page 281.
