One Thousand and One Nights (frame tale)

Anonymous Medieval Arabic Storytellers · 9th–14th Century Canon

Translated by Husain Haddawy, 1990 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The frame story comes from a Persian book, Hezār Afsān, of which no physical trace survives. A bookseller in tenth-century Baghdad, Ibn al-Nadim, described it in his catalogue and thought it poorly told. The earliest surviving fragment of the Arabic Nights is a few lines from the ninth century. Aladdin and Ali Baba were not in the Arabic versions: the French translator Antoine Galland added them after hearing them from the Syrian writer Hanna Diyab in Paris in 1709.
