Prisoners on a Projecting Platform (Carceri, Plate 10)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1749–50 · etching, engraving, sulphur tint or open bite, burnishing · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Piranesi had begun the prison series in 1745, and the first edition came out in 1750 as fourteen etchings with no titles or numbers. He was twenty-nine or thirty. The scenes drew on his training as a stage designer, not on any real prison. The plate is about 41 by 54 centimetres, and this is an early state. In 1821 Thomas De Quincey compared the series to his own opium addiction in Confessions of an English Opium-Eater.
