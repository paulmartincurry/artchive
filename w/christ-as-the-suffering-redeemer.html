Christ as the Suffering Redeemer

Andrea Mantegna · c. 1485–1495 · Tempera on panel · 78 × 48 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Mantegna signed the panel in gold letters on the edge of the base, ANDREAS MANTINIA. It appears in a Gonzaga family inventory of 1627, and in 1763 it passed from Cardinal Silvio Valenti Gonzaga into the Danish royal collection. In the quarry at the right of the background, two men are working a slab, a column and a statue.
