Ushibori

Kawase Hasui · 1930 · Color woodblock print on paper · 26 × 38.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

A rower takes a passenger across the river to the village of Ushibori during a snow shower at night, past houses with lit windows. Hasui made the print in 1930, the year he built a new house in Magome. Seven years earlier the Great Kantō earthquake had destroyed his publisher's workshop and 188 of his sketchbooks.
