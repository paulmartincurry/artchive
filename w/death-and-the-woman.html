Death and the Woman

Hans Baldung Grien · c. 1520–25 · Mixed technique on limewood · 29.8 × 17.1 cm · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

The panel is limewood, 29.8 by 17.1 centimetres, painted in a mixed technique. It came to the Basel museum from the Museum Faesch in 1823. As the Protestant Reformation took hold, the number of Baldung's religious pictures fell, and the nearness of death and the relation between the sexes became among his recurring subjects; he painted Death seizing a young woman several times.
