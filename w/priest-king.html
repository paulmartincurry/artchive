Priest-King

Maker unknown · ca. 2200–1900 BCE · Steatite · National Museum of Pakistan

Free to use with credit. Photo: Mamoon Mengal, CC BY-SA 1.0

The archaeologist Kashinath Dikshit found the figure at Mohenjo-daro in 1925 or 1926, at a depth of 1.37 metres, in a passage between two walls. It is 17.5 centimetres high, broken off at the bottom, and was fired at over 1,000 °C to harden the soft stone. The shapes on the robe were once filled with a red material. The figure went to New Delhi and was returned to Pakistan only after the Simla Agreement of 1972. The museum normally shows a replica and keeps the original secure.
