Coronation of the Virgin

Gentile da Fabriano · c. 1420 · Tempera and gold leaf on panel · 93 × 64.1 cm · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Gentile painted it about 1420 as the front of a processional banner for a confraternity at San Francesco in Fabriano, his home town. The back, Saint Francis Receiving the Stigmata, is now in Parma.
