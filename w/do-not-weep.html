Do Not Weep

Chippewa love song · Printed 1910

Translated by Frances Densmore, 1910

Frances Densmore wrote that this love song was widely known among the Chippewa. She began recording music for the Smithsonian's Bureau of American Ethnology in 1907, often on wax cylinders, and many of the recordings she made for the Bureau are now in the Library of Congress.
