Under a government which imprisons any unjustly, the true place for a just man is also a prison.

Civil Disobedience — The true place for a just man

Henry David Thoreau · 1849

The essay was first printed in 1849 under the title Resistance to Civil Government. In it Thoreau gave his reasons for refusing to pay taxes, as a protest against slavery and the Mexican-American War. The title Civil Disobedience was first put on it in 1866, four years after his death. In a letter of 10 September 1935 Gandhi wrote that his own resistance in South Africa was well advanced before he read the essay, and that he borrowed its title to explain his struggle to English readers.
