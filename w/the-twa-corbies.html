Oer his white banes, when they are bare,
The wind sall blaw for evermair.

The Twa Corbies

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1803

The Twa Corbies was written down from the recitation of an old woman at Alva and sent to Walter Scott in a letter of August 1802. Scott printed it the next year in his Minstrelsy of the Scottish Border. Its lines come from an English ballad, The Three Ravens, printed by Thomas Ravenscroft in 1611. Pushkin published a partial Russian version in 1828.
