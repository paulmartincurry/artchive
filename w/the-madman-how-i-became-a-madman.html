And I have found both freedom and safety in my madness; the freedom of loneliness and the safety from being understood, for those who understand us enslave something in us.

The Madman — How I Became a Madman (prologue)

Kahlil Gibran · 1918

The Madman was Gibran's first book in English, published by Alfred A. Knopf in 1918 with illustrations reproduced from his own drawings. Gibran had first shown his drawings in Boston in 1904. This prologue opens the book.
