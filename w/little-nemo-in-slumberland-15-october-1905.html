Little Nemo in Slumberland, 15 October 1905

Winsor McCay · 15 October 1905 · Newspaper comic page, colour

Free to use

This is the first Little Nemo page, printed on the last page of the New York Herald's Sunday comics section on 15 October 1905. McCay based Nemo's looks on his son Robert. The strip ran in the Herald until July 1911, when McCay moved to William Randolph Hearst's New York American; the Herald held the copyright, but McCay won a lawsuit that let him keep the characters.
