The Baba-Yaga Came Riding in Her Mortar (Russian Wonder Tales)

Ivan Bilibin · published 1912 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Between 1902 and 1904 Bilibin travelled the northern provinces of Vologda, Olonets and Arkhangelsk for the Russian Museum, studying wooden architecture and folk art. Old Russian art, Japanese prints and Renaissance woodcuts all shaped his drawing. In 1906 the magazine Zhupel was banned over his cartoon showing the emperor as a donkey.
