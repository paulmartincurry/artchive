Roadside Stand near Birmingham, Alabama

Walker Evans · 1936 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The signs on this roadside store near Birmingham, Alabama, advertise a house mover and river fish: catfish, trout, perch, drum, buffalo and eel. Evans photographed it in 1936. The Library's digital file was made from the preservation duplicate negative, not the original, and the collection was transferred from the Office of War Information in 1944.
