It is sweeter than honey, and it maketh
one to rejoice more than wine, and it illumineth more
than the sun

Kebra Nagast

Neburä Id Yishaq of Aksum · ca. 1314–1322 CE

Translated by E. A. Wallis Budge, 1922

A note attached to most surviving copies says the book was first written in Coptic, put into Arabic in 1225, and then into Geʽez by a team of clerics in Ethiopia; scholars date that last step to between 1314 and 1322 and doubt there was ever a Coptic version. The Scottish traveller James Bruce was given a manuscript on leaving Gondar, and it is now in the Bodleian Library. The whole text was first printed in 1905, and the first English translation followed in 1922.
