Design for Case Clocks

Maker unknown · ca. 1805 · Graphite, pen and ink, brush and watercolor on paper · 27.9 × 26.6 cm (11 in. × 10 1/2 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Three case clocks are drawn side by side. The middle one has painted landscape panels and a figure of Chronos on its hood, and the two beside it have plain wooden cases. The paper is watermarked with a Strassburg lily in a shield and the name C & I Honig.
