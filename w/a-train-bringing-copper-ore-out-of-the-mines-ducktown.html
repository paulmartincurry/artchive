A Train Bringing Copper Ore out of the Mines, Ducktown, Tennessee

Marion Post Wolcott · September 1939 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The original caption reads that fumes from smelting copper for sulfuric acid had destroyed all vegetation and eroded the land. Wolcott made the slide at Ducktown, Tennessee, in September 1939. Acid rain from the smelters, together with logging to feed them, left the Copper Basin barren for more than a century; the last mine closed in 1987.
