Metamorphosis of Narcissus

Salvador Dalí · 1937 · Oil on canvas · 51.2 × 78.1 cm · Tate, London

In copyright. Look and learn: shown for study, not for download.

Dalí began this painting in the spring of 1937 at Zürs, in the Austrian Alps, and exhibited it that year alongside a poem he had written. On 19 July 1938 he took the canvas to London to a meeting with Sigmund Freud, whom he had admired since the 1920s. His benefactor Edward James was at the meeting and ultimately became the painting's owner.
