If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles.

The Art of War — Know the enemy and know yourself (Ch. III, 18)

Sun Tzu · c. 5th century BCE

Translated by Lionel Giles

For almost 1,500 years this was the lead text in an anthology that an emperor of the Song dynasty formalized in 1080 as the Seven Military Classics. A French Jesuit priest, Jean Joseph Marie Amiot, made the first translation into a European language in 1772. The first annotated English translation followed in 1910. The earliest account of Sun Tzu's life was written around 97 BC, some four centuries after he is said to have lived.
