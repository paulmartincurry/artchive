I don't love you an atom; on the contrary, I detest you. You are a good for nothing, very ungraceful, very tactless, very tatterdemalion. You never write to me; you don't care for your husband; you know the pleasure your letters give him, and you write him barely half-a-dozen lines, thrown off anyhow.

Letter to Josephine, Verona, 23 November 1796

Napoleon Bonaparte · 1796

Translated by Henry Foljambe Hall, 1901

The couple had married on 9 March 1796 in a civil ceremony, and two days later Bonaparte left Paris to take command of the Army of Italy. The French laid siege to Mantua, and he defeated every Austrian effort to relieve it, winning among others the Battle of Arcole. The letter is headed Verona, November 23, 1796.
