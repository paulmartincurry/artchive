Satan Sowing Tare (Satan semant l'ivraie)

Félicien Rops · Undated · Aquatint and etching · Plate: 27.7 × 20.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This impression belonged to Claude Schaefer of Montevideo. In 1950 Lessing Rosenwald, travelling in South America, was offered a collection of some 1,100 prints and drawings that the seller's father had brought from Germany. He bought it all, kept about a quarter for the National Gallery of Art, and gave the rest to other institutions.
