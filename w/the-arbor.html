The Arbor (Camera Work 23)

Clarence H. White · 1908 · Photogravure · The Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

White was entirely self-taught. He took up photography as a hobby in 1893, the year he married, and kept working long hours as a bookkeeper in Newark, Ohio; his student Ralph Steiner later recalled that White could afford only two glass plate negatives a week. This plate was printed in Camera Work number 23, in July 1908.
