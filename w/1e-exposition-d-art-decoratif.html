1e Exposition d'Art Décoratif

Eugène Grasset · 1894 · Colour lithograph · 130 × 82 cm (image) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

G. de Malherbe printed the poster in Paris in 1894 for the first exhibition of decorative art held by the Société des artistes décorateurs. Grasset was the son of a cabinetmaker and sculptor and studied architecture in Zürich. In 1898 he drew an italic typeface for his posters, which the Peignot foundry released at the Paris exhibition of 1900.
