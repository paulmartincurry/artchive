Self-Portrait with Two Pupils, Marie Gabrielle Capet and Marie Marguerite Carraux de Rosemond

Adélaïde Labille-Guiard · 1785 · Oil on canvas · 83 × 59 1/2 in. (210.8 × 151.1 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Labille-Guiard was thirty-six when she painted this, two years after she was accepted into the Académie Royale on the same day as her rival Vigée Le Brun. She had been taking students since 1780, all of them women. A chalk study exists in which she worked out the two pupils' heads, and the bust in the background is of her father, a haberdasher. The canvas is more than two metres tall, almost life-size. It stayed with her family until 1905 and was given to the Met by Julia Berwind in 1953.
