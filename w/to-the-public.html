I am in earnest--I will not equivocate--I will not excuse--I will not retreat a single inch--AND I WILL BE HEARD.

To the Public (The Liberator, No. 1)

William Lloyd Garrison · 1831

Garrison was a trained typesetter who composed his editorials directly in type, without writing them out on paper first. He started The Liberator in Boston in 1831 with his friend Isaac Knapp, after serving seven weeks in a Baltimore jail for refusing to pay a fifty-dollar libel fine. The paper came out weekly until December 29, 1865, when he closed it with issue number 1,820.
