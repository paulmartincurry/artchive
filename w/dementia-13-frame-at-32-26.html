Dementia 13, frame at 32:26

Francis Ford Coppola · 1963 · Film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Francis Ford Coppola wrote a story outline in one night and the next morning described this sequence to Roger Corman: a woman ties dolls to the bottom of a lake, then surfaces to find an axe murderer. Corman gave him $22,000 left over from The Young Racers, on which Coppola had worked as a sound man. Coppola raised another $20,000 by pre-selling the European rights, without telling Corman. It was his first mainstream feature as director.
