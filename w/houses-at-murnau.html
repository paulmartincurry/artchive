Houses at Murnau

Wassily Kandinsky · 1909 · Oil on cardboard · 49 × 64 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Murnau is a small Bavarian town where Kandinsky settled after two years of travelling across Europe, from 1906 to 1908. He painted this view on cardboard in 1909. Katharine Kuh bequeathed it to the Art Institute of Chicago.
