Seba (Sixty-nine Stations of the Kisokaidō)

Utagawa Hiroshige · c. 1835–38 · Color woodblock print; oban · 23.4 × 37.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Sixty-nine Stations of the Kisokaidō was begun by Keisai Eisen, who made the first eleven prints; Hiroshige then took over and designed 47 of the 71. Iseya Rihei published the series from about 1834 to 1842. The Kisokaidō, also called the Nakasendō, was the inland alternative to the coastal Tōkaidō between Edo and Kyoto.
