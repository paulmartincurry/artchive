The Bathers

Paul Cézanne · 1899-1904 · Oil on canvas · 51.3 × 61.7 cm (20 3/16 × 24 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Cézanne set the bathers in a frame of foliage and rendered ground, water, trees and sky in patches of hatching. He painted the subject many times over his career, inventing some poses and taking others from his own earlier works.
