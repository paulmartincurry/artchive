The Sun Also Rises — You can't get away from yourself

Ernest Hemingway · 1926

Robert Cohn was drawn from Harold Loeb, an American writer in Paris who had a brief affair with Duff Twysden, the model for Lady Brett Ashley. Both were in the party Hemingway took to Pamplona in June 1925. He was jealous of Loeb, and by the end of the week the two men had a public fistfight. Loeb gave his own account of the period in a memoir in 1959.
