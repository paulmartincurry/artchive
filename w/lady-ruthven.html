Lady Ruthven

David Octavius Hill and Robert Adamson · 1843–47 · Salted paper print from paper negative · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Robert Adamson's studio, Rock House on the Calton Hill in Edinburgh, was the centre of the partnership's photographic experiments. Using the calotype process, Hill and Adamson produced around 3,000 different photographs in four years, before Adamson's ill health and his death in 1848 cut the partnership short.
