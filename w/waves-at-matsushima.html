Waves at Matsushima

Tawaraya Sōtatsu · 17th century · Ink, color, gold, and silver on paper · Each screen 166 × 369.9 cm · National Museum of Asian Art, Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The screens were probably painted in the early Kan'ei era, which ran from 1624 to 1644. They were likely commissioned by Tani Shōan, a wealthy sea captain and merchant, to celebrate the opening of the Shōunji temple at the port of Sakai. The silver in the clouds and embankments has darkened to a soft black. Charles Lang Freer bought the pair from Bunshichi Kobayashi in 1906, and the present name dates from the early twentieth century.
