I placed a jar in Tennessee,
And round it was, upon a hill.
It made the slovenly wilderness
Surround that hill.

Anecdote of the Jar

Wallace Stevens · 1919

Anecdote of the Jar was first published in 1919. Four years later it was collected in Harmonium, which Knopf issued in an edition of 1,500 copies. When the book was reissued in 1931, three poems were left out and fourteen new ones added.
