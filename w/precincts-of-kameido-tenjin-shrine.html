Precincts of Kameido Tenjin Shrine (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1856 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 36.5 × 24.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The drum bridge and garden belong to Kameido Tenjin Shrine, and the flowering wisteria sets the scene in summer. From the age of twelve Hiroshige had been a fire warden at Edo Castle, a duty inherited from his father that left him much leisure time. He passed the post to his son in 1823.
