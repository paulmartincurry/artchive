Nītiśataka

Bhartṛhari · 5th–6th Century CE

Translated by B. Hale Wortham, 1886

Indian tradition gives these 100 verses to King Bharthari of Ujjain, who is said to have left the pleasures of the royal court for complete renunciation. Yi-Jing, a Chinese pilgrim of the seventh century, wrote that he wavered seven times between joining the Buddhist orders and living as a layman. The verses are believed to have been written over the course of his life.
