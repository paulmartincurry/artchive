Native Texan Farmer on Relief, Goodliet, Texas

Dorothea Lange · June 1938 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Dorothea Lange photographed this farmer in Goodliet, Hardeman County, Texas, in June 1938. Her caption says he had been tractored out in late 1937, was close to going on relief, and had a wife and two children. It then runs on in his own words, about debts, departed neighbours and the big landowners who spoke for the town.
