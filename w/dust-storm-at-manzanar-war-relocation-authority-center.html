Dust Storm at Manzanar War Relocation Authority Center

Dorothea Lange · 3 July 1942 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

Dorothea Lange visited several temporary assembly centres as they opened and then fixed on Manzanar, the first of the permanent camps, some 300 miles from the coast. She made this negative there on 3 July 1942. The authorities impounded most of her photographs of the internment, and they were not seen publicly during the war.
