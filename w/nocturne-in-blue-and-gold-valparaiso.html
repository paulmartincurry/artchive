Nocturne in Blue and Gold: Valparaiso

James McNeill Whistler · 1866–c. 1874 · Oil on canvas · 76.4 × 50.7 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

Whistler began this canvas in Valparaiso, Chile, where he watched a Spanish fleet attack the city. He probably started it as a daylight scene and turned it into a night scene when he reworked it in 1874. At a Christie's auction in 1889 it was sold as Nocturne in Blue and Silver. Charles Lang Freer bought it in London in 1909.
