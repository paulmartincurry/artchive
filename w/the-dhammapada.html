The Dhammapada (verse 1)

The Dhammapada · ca. 3rd century BCE

Translated by F. Max Müller, 1881

The collection holds 423 verses in 26 chapters, and no compiler is named. By tradition the Buddha spoke the verses on various occasions, and more than half of them also occur elsewhere in the Pāli Canon. The oldest manuscripts available date to 1500 CE. In 1855 the Danish scholar Viggo Fausbøll produced a critical edition in Latin, the first Pāli text to be examined in that way by European scholars.
