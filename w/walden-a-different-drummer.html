If a man does not keep pace with his companions, perhaps it is because he hears a different drummer. Let him step to the music which he hears, however measured or far away.

Walden — A different drummer (Conclusion)

Henry David Thoreau · 1854

Ticknor and Fields of Boston published Walden on 9 August 1854, with a picture on the title page drawn by Thoreau's sister Sophia. Of the 66 early reviews that have been found, 46 were strongly favourable, and more than half used the word quaint or eccentric. The book took five years to sell 2,000 copies and then went out of print until Thoreau's death in 1862.
