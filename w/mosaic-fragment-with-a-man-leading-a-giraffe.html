Mosaic Fragment with a Man Leading a Giraffe

Maker unknown · 5th century · Stone in mortar · 170.8 × 167 × 6.4 cm (67 1/4 × 65 3/4 × 2 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The fragment once paved the floor of a wealthy family home, and is one of only a few images of a giraffe from the Early Byzantine period. Texts of the fifth century describe giraffes being brought to Constantinople as gifts for the emperor. Robert B. Mayer and his wife, of Winnetka, bought the piece from dealers in Beirut in 1968. It was on loan to the Art Institute from 1972 and was given in 1993.
