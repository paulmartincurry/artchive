The world is charged with the grandeur of God.
It will flame out, like shining from shook foil;

God's Grandeur

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

John Henry Newman received Hopkins into the Roman Catholic Church on 21 October 1866, a step that estranged him from his family. In May 1868, within a week of resolving to enter religious life, he made a bonfire of his poems, and he wrote almost none for the next seven years. He began his Jesuit training that September. His ordination came in September 1877, when he was thirty-three.
