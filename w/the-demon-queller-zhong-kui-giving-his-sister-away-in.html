The Demon Queller Zhong Kui Giving His Sister Away in Marriage (detail)

Yan Geng · Yuan dynasty (1271–1368) · Handscroll, ink on silk · 24.4 × 253.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The handscroll is more than two and a half metres long and painted in ink alone. Pictures of Zhong Kui marrying off his sister became a standard subject, probably because of a pun: in Chinese, marrying off a younger sister sounds almost the same as sending away an evil spirit. Yan Geng was active in the late thirteenth century, and the Met bought the scroll in 1990.
