Arab Horseman Attacked by a Lion

Eugène Delacroix · 1849–50 · Oil on paperboard · 46 × 38 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Delacroix painted this on paperboard, 46 centimetres by 38, in the years when he was also painting the ceiling of the Galerie d'Apollon in the Louvre. Baron Michel de Trétaigne owned it by 1864, and it sold in Paris in 1872 for 17,000 francs. Potter Palmer bought it at auction in New York in 1892 for 6,350 dollars, and his sons gave it to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1922.
