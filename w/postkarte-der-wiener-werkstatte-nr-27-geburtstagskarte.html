Postkarte der Wiener Werkstätte Nr. 27: Geburtstagskarte

Rudolf Kalvach · 1907 · Wien Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Rudolf Kalvach designed this birthday card, number 27 in the Wiener Werkstätte's postcard series, in 1907. The workshop had been founded four years earlier by Koloman Moser, Josef Hoffmann and the patron Fritz Waerndorfer. It came close to bankruptcy in 1913, and it closed in 1932.
