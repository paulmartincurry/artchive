Tile with Pious Inscription

Maker unknown · Ilkhanid dynasty (1256–1353), Late 13th/early 14th century · Fritware with molded decoration, painted in blue under opaque turquoise glaze · 19.4 × 35.8 × 3.7 cm (7 5/8 × 14 1/8 × 1 7/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The tile was once part of a longer band of pious inscriptions, almost certainly on a religious building. The script is thuluth, known for the long horizontal strokes at the ends of its letters; it first appeared in the eleventh century.
