You cannot step twice into the same rivers; for fresh waters are ever flowing in upon you.

Fragments — You Cannot Step Twice into the Same Rivers

Heraclitus of Ephesus · ca. 500 BCE

Translated by John Burnet

Heraclitus wrote a single work, and only fragments of it survive. According to Diogenes Laërtius he deposited the book in the temple of Artemis at Ephesus as a dedication. It could still be read in the 2nd century AD, when Plutarch and Clement quoted directly from it.
