American Gothic

Grant Wood · 1930 · Oil on beaverboard · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Grant Wood sketched the house in Eldon, Iowa, in August 1930, in oil on paperboard from the front yard. He added the figures later in his studio in Cedar Rapids, using his sister Nan and the family dentist, Byron McKeeby, as models. Nan made the apron herself and took the rickrack trim from their mother's old dresses. The painting won a bronze medal and a $300 prize in a competition at the Art Institute of Chicago, which then bought it.
