The way is to the destructive element submit yourself, and with the exertions of your hands and feet in the water make the deep, deep sea keep you up.

Lord Jim — In the destructive element immerse (Ch. 20)

Joseph Conrad · 1900

Behind the novel is a real abandonment. On 17 July 1880 the steamer Jeddah left Singapore with 778 men, 147 women and 67 children, pilgrims bound for Mecca; when the hull sprang a leak the captain and officers left them, and a French steamship towed the vessel into Aden on 8 August with the passengers alive. The chief mate, Austin Podmore Williams, was the model for Jim. Stein is based on the naturalist Alfred Russel Wallace. The novel ran in Blackwood's Magazine from October 1899 to November 1900.
