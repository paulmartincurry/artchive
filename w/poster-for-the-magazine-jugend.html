Poster for the Magazine Jugend

Maker unknown · 1896 · Paper · 63.5 × 44.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Georg Hirth founded Jugend in Munich in 1896 to launch a new cultural renaissance in the city, and he edited it for twenty years, until his death in 1916. Albert Langen's rival magazine Simplicissimus was founded the same year. The poster is 63.5 centimetres tall.
