The Sword Spring, Tiger Hill (Twelve Views of Tiger Hill, Suzhou)

Shen Zhou · after 1490 · Album leaf; ink on paper or ink and slight color on paper · Image: 31.2 × 40.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Tiger Hill is a historic site a few miles northwest of Suzhou, and Shen Zhou's twelve views follow a visit from the boat landing up to the Cloudy Cliff Monastery. Shen Zhou came from a wealthy family, gave up the official examinations after his father's death to care for his widowed mother, and took up painting in midlife. The album passed through at least four collectors, among them Binliang, before Cleveland bought it in 1964.
