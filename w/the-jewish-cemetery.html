The Jewish Cemetery

Jacob van Ruisdael · 1654 or 1655 · Oil on canvas · Detroit Institute of Arts

Free to use

The canvas is twice the size of a typical landscape painting of the 17th century. It shows Beth Haim, the cemetery of Amsterdam's Portuguese-Jewish community at Ouderkerk aan de Amstel, though Ruisdael changed its central elements. After it was catalogued in England in 1835 it disappeared from public display for many years. The Detroit Institute of Arts acquired it in 1926 from Julius H. Haass, who gave it in memory of his brother.
