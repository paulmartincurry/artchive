Extravehicular Activity during STS 41-B: Bruce McCandless II (S84-27031)

NASA · 7 February 1984 · Colour photograph, 70 mm film

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

On 7 February 1984 Bruce McCandless II flew free of the Space Shuttle Challenger on a manned maneuvering unit, the first spacewalk made without a tether. This frame catches him at his greatest distance from the shuttle before he reversed and came back. A crewmate took it from inside the cabin with a 70 mm camera. McCandless had been the capsule communicator in Mission Control during the first moonwalk of Apollo 11.
