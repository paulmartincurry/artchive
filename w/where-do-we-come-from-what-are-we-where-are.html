Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going?

Paul Gauguin · 1897–1898 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Free to use

Gauguin painted this canvas, 139 by 375 centimetres, in Tahiti while ill and in debt, and while working on it he learned of the death of his daughter Aline. In December 1897 he wrote to his friend Daniel de Monfreid that he planned to kill himself; after finishing the painting he tried to, with arsenic. He wrote the French title in capitals, with no question marks, at upper left. Gabriel Frizeau bought it from Vollard for 2,500 francs in 1901.
