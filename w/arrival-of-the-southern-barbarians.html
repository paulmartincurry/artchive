Arrival of the Southern Barbarians (Nanban Screen)

Maker unknown · c. 1600 · One of a pair of six-panel folding screens; ink, color, and gold on paper · Image: 146.7 × 337.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The screen is one of a pair painted around 1600, showing Portuguese merchants arriving in Japan on their great ship with a mixed crew. The Japanese called them Southern Barbarians because their ships came from the south; no European had reached Japan before 1543. The Portuguese were forced out in 1639. More than 90 pairs of these screens survive. Before Cleveland bought this one in 1960, it belonged to Nagami Takutaro of Nagasaki.
