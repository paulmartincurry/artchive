Space Butterfly (W40)

Spitzer Space Telescope, NASA/JPL-Caltech · 2019 · Infrared space telescope image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech

The butterfly is a nebula officially named W40, a nursery for hundreds of young stars about 1,400 light-years from the Sun. Its two wings are giant bubbles of hot gas blown out by the hottest, most massive stars in the region. The material was thrown out from a dense cluster of stars between the wings.
