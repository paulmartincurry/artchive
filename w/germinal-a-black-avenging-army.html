Men were springing forth, a black avenging army, germinating slowly in the furrows, growing towards the harvests of the next century, and their germination would soon overturn the earth.

Germinal — a black avenging army (Part Seven, Ch. VI, close)

Émile Zola · 1885

Translated by Havelock Ellis

Zola wrote the novel between April 1884 and January 1885. For it he made long visits to mining towns in northern France in 1884, saw the after-effects of a miners' strike at Anzin and went down a working coal pit at Denain.
