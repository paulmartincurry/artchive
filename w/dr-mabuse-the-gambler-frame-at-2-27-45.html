Dr. Mabuse the Gambler, frame at 2:27:45

Fritz Lang · 1922 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Fritz Lang began filming while Norbert Jacques's novel was still being serialised, and with Thea von Harbou he added things the book does not have, including Mabuse's counterfeiting and the 20-minute opening. The full film runs four and a half hours in two parts, first released a month apart. When it reached the United States in 1927, it was a single film less than half that length.
