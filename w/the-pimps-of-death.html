The Pimps of Death

George Grosz · 1919, published 1920 · Lithograph in black on ivory laid paper · image 38.5 × 30.2 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Grosz volunteered for the army in November 1914, hoping that by doing so he would avoid being sent to the front, and was discharged in 1915 after a stay in hospital with sinusitis. In 1916 he changed Georg to George and Groß to Grosz in protest against German nationalism. This lithograph was drawn in 1919 and published in 1920, the year his album Gott mit uns brought him a 300-mark fine for insulting the army and the seizure of its printing plates.
