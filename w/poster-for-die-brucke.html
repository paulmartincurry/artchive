Poster for Die Brücke

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner · 1907 · Lithograph in green and gold on paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner was twenty-seven when he made this lithograph, two years after he and three other architecture students in Dresden founded Die Brücke in 1905. The group met at first in his studio, which had been a butcher's shop. The sheet is 80 centimetres tall.
