At evening, casual flocks of pigeons make
Ambiguous undulations as they sink,
Downward to darkness, on extended wings.

Sunday Morning (stanza VIII)

Wallace Stevens · 1923

Part of Sunday Morning was published in the magazine Poetry in November 1915. The full poem waited until 1923 and Harmonium, Stevens's first book, which Knopf printed in an edition of 1,500 copies when he was forty-four.
