I, too, sing America.

I am the darker brother.

I, Too (Epilogue)

Langston Hughes · 1925

I, Too was first published in the March 1925 issue of Survey Graphic. The next year Hughes collected it in his first book of poems, The Weary Blues. Its opening line answers Walt Whitman's poem I Hear America Singing.
