A Maid Asleep

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1656–57 · Oil on canvas · 87.6 × 76.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Technical examination found that Vermeer first painted a man in the second room with a dog looking at him; he later replaced them with a mirror and a chair. The painting was very likely owned by his patron Pieter van Ruijven and was sold from the estate of Jacob Dissius on 16 May 1696. Benjamin Altman left it to the museum in 1913, and the terms of his bequest stop it from being lent.
