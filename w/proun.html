Proun

El Lissitzky · 1924–25 · Collage of cut-and-tipped wove papers prepared with gouache, graphite and varnish, with brush and pen and black ink and red watercolor, on cream laminate cardboard · 49.8 × 65 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Lissitzky began his Prouns in 1919–20 and named them after the UNOVIS Project; he meant them to have neither top nor bottom. This one, made in 1924–25, is built from cut papers prepared with gouache, graphite and varnish, laid on cardboard, and is inscribed Proun N 92 on the back. Only about 25 Prouns are known to survive.
