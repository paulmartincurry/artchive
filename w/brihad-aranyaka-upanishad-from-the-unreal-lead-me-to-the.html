From the unreal (asat) lead me to the real (sat)!
From darkness lead me to light!
From death lead me to immortality!

Brihad-Aranyaka Upanishad — From the Unreal Lead Me to the Real (1.3.28)

Vedic Upanishadic Seers · c. 7th–6th century BCE; English 1921

Translated by Robert Ernest Hume

Brihadaranyaka means great wilderness or forest. The Upanishad is estimated to have been composed in about the 7th or 6th century BCE, and its oldest surviving manuscripts, on palm leaf, date to the 12th century CE. In 1997 Ravi Shankar and George Harrison arranged this mantra for the album Chants of India, and in 2003 it was worked into the score of The Matrix Revolutions.
