Rustam Brings the Div King to Kai Kavus for Execution (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Mir Musavvir and Qasim ibn 'Ali · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.4 × 18.1 cm; page 47.3 × 31.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting is attributed to Mir Musavvir working with Qasim ibn 'Ali. Mir Musavvir had moved to Tabriz from Termez or Badakhshan, and in another painting in this manuscript, Manuchihr Enthroned, his signature is on a courtier's turban. By November 1549 he and his son Mir Sayyid Ali had left Iran for the Mughal court at Kabul.
