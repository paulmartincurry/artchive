Gravity and Grace

Simone Weil · before May 1942 (English 1952)

Translated by Arthur Wills · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Gravity and Grace was not intended to be a book. It consists of passages selected from Weil's notebooks and arranged topically by her friend Gustave Thibon, who owned a farm in the Ardèche where she worked the grape harvest. She had given him some of her notebooks written before May 1942, with no intent to publish them. She died on 24 August 1943.
