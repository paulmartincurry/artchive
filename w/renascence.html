The Artchive
The world stands out on either side
No wider than the heart is wide;
Above the world is stretched the sky,--
No higher than the soul is high.

Renascence (closing lines)

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1912

Millay wrote the poem while looking out from the summit of Mount Battie in Camden, Maine. Her mother saw the announcement of a contest run by The Lyric Year, an annual volume of poetry, and encouraged her to enter it in 1912. The contest's backer chose it as the winner from thousands of entries before consulting the other judges, and it finally placed fourth. The first-place winner said the award embarrassed him, and a second-prize winner offered her his $250.
