We live on a placid island of ignorance
in the midst of black seas of infinity.

The Call of Cthulhu (opening)

H. P. Lovecraft · 1926 (Weird Tales, February 1928)

The first chapter grew out of a dream Lovecraft had in 1919, in which he tried to sell a bas-relief he had sculpted to a museum curator in Providence. He outlined the story in his commonplace book in August 1925 and wrote it in the summer of 1926, the year he moved back to Providence from New York. The editor of Weird Tales rejected it at first. He took it only after a friend of Lovecraft's falsely claimed it was about to be sent elsewhere, and it ran in February 1928.
