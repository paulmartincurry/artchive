It was a wonderful thing,
the way it all melted as ice melts
when the Father eases the fetters off the frost

Beowulf — The Giants' Sword

Anonymous Anglo-Saxon Poet · ca. 975 CE

Translated by Seamus Heaney · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Beowulf survives in a single manuscript, copied between 975 and 1025, and has no title there. Nobody knows who composed it or when. In 1731 a fire at Ashburnham House in London, where the manuscript was kept, charred its margins and some readings were lost. The poem was first transcribed in 1786 and first printed in 1815.
