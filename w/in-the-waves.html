In the Waves (Dans les Vagues)

Paul Gauguin · 1889 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 92.5 × 72.4 cm (36 7/16 × 28 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Painted at Pont-Aven in Brittany, the canvas was shown at the Café Volpini in Paris the year it was made. In February 1891 it was lot 14 in the sale of his own paintings that Gauguin held to pay for his journey to Tahiti. Later owners included Paul Éluard, who probably bought it in 1923. Mr. and Mrs. William Powell Jones of Gates Mills, Ohio, gave it to the museum in 1978.
