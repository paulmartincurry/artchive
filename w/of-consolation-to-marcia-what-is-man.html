What is man? a potter’s vessel, to be broken by the slightest shake or toss: it requires no great storm to rend you asunder: you fall to pieces wherever you strike.

Of Consolation: To Marcia — What is man? (XI)

Seneca the Younger · c. 40–45 CE

Translated by Aubrey Stewart

Seneca wrote this consolation to Marcia, who had mourned the death of her son Metilius for over three years. His three consolations were written around 40 to 45 AD, and they read more like essays than personal letters.
