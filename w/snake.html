And so, I missed my chance with one of the lords
Of life.

Snake (ending)

D. H. Lawrence · 1921

Lawrence's father was a barely literate miner at Brinsley Colliery in Nottinghamshire who wanted his sons to go down the pit. Lawrence was the first pupil from his local school to win a county council scholarship to Nottingham High School. He worked three months as a junior clerk in a surgical appliances factory and then as a schoolteacher, giving up teaching to write full time after pneumonia in November 1911. He turned thirty-six in 1921.
