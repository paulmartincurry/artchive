View from River Valley, Canyon de Chelly National Monument, Arizona

Ansel Adams · 1941–42 · Photographic negative · National Archives at College Park, Maryland

Free to use

Ansel Adams made this negative for the Department of the Interior, which wanted mural-sized prints for its new building. The project ended on 30 June 1942 and, because of the war, the murals were never made. Canyon de Chelly had been a national monument since 1 April 1931; it lies within the Navajo Nation in north-eastern Arizona.
