The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse

Albrecht Dürer · c. 1497/98 · Woodcut in black on ivory laid paper · Art Institute of Chicago

This is one of sixteen woodcut designs for the Apocalypse, a series dated 1498. It is now thought unlikely that Dürer cut any of the blocks himself; a specialist craftsman would have done it. He either drew straight onto the block or glued a paper drawing to it, and either way the drawing was destroyed in the cutting. The sheet is 39.4 by 28.4 centimetres.
