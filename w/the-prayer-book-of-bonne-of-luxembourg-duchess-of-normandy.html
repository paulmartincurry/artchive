The Prayer Book of Bonne of Luxembourg, Duchess of Normandy

Attributed to Jean Le Noir · before 1349 · Tempera, grisaille, ink, and gold on vellum · 13.2 × 9.7 × 4.2 cm (closed) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The book was probably commissioned for Bonne of Luxembourg, wife of John II of France, around 1348 or 1349. It is dated to just before her death in 1349, when the Black Death was beginning to spread across Europe. Closed, it is 13.2 centimetres tall.
