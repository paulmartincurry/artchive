Design for a Stained Glass Window with the Story of Aaron

Jean Cousin the Younger · n.d. · Pen and brown ink and brush and brown wash, on tan laid paper, laid down on cream laid paper · 38.2 × 26.2 cm (15 1/16 × 10 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Jean Cousin the Younger was born in Sens around 1522 and trained under his father, the painter and sculptor Jean Cousin the Elder. The work of the two is nearly indistinguishable even to experts. This sheet is about 38 centimetres tall and 26 wide.
