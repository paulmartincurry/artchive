Sherlock Jr., frame at 21:10

Buster Keaton · 1924 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The screen in the dream sequence is a stage with a big black cut-out, lit to look like a projected film, with front-row seats and an orchestra pit built in front of it. As the scenery behind Keaton cuts from park to lake to desert, he and the camera were set at exactly the right distances with surveyor's instruments. Filming took four months, twice his usual time for a feature. Keaton was also his own editor.
