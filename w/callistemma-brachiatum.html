Callistemma brachiatum (Urformen der Kunst, plate 11)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · 26 × 19 cm (10 1/4 × 7 1/2 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blossfeldt taught a class in modelling from plants at the Kunstgewerbeschule in Berlin for thirty-one years, from 1899. Photographs like this one were made as reference material for his teaching and for his own work.
