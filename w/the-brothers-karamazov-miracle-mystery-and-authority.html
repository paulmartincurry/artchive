There are three powers, three powers alone, able to conquer and to hold captive for ever the conscience of these impotent rebels for their happiness—those forces are miracle, mystery and authority.

The Brothers Karamazov — Miracle, mystery and authority (The Grand Inquisitor)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1880

Translated by Constance Garnett

This was the sixteenth and last of Dostoevsky's novels. It took in material from a project he had begun in 1869 and left unfinished, The Life of a Great Sinner. A sketch dated 13 September 1874, about a soldier thought to have murdered his father, was an early draft of its first chapter; the character was based on a real soldier from Omsk. Tolstoy asked for the book to be with him on his deathbed, and Wittgenstein took a copy to the front in the First World War.
