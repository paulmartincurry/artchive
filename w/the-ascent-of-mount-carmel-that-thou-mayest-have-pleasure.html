That thou mayest have pleasure in everything,
seek pleasure in nothing.
That thou mayest know everything, seek to know nothing.
That thou mayest possess all things, seek to possess nothing.

The Ascent of Mount Carmel — That thou mayest have pleasure in everything (Book I, Ch. 13)

Saint John of the Cross (San Juan de la Cruz) · 1578–1579

Translated by David Lewis

The Ascent of Mount Carmel was written between 1578 and 1579 in Granada, after John's escape from prison. The work is set out as a commentary on four stanzas of his own verse.
