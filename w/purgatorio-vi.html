Ah! servile Italy, grief’s hostelry!
A ship without a pilot in great tempest!
No Lady thou of Provinces, but brothel!

Purgatorio VI (Ah! Servile Italy)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1315

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

Dante was exiled from Florence in 1301 and spent his last years in Ravenna, where the city's prince invited him in 1318. He died there in 1321 of malaria caught on the way back from a diplomatic mission to Venice. Florence later asked repeatedly for his remains, and the custodians in Ravenna refused, at one point hiding the bones in a false wall of the monastery. The tomb Florence built for him in 1829 has been empty ever since.
