Shield (Grere'o)

Maker unknown · Early to mid-19th century · Fiber, parinarium-nut paste, chambered-nautilus shell, pigment · 86.4 × 26.7 × 2.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This shield is made of fibre, a paste of parinarium nuts, chambered-nautilus shell and pigment. It was made in the early to mid-nineteenth century, possibly on New Georgia or Guadalcanal, and is 86.4 centimetres tall. Nelson A. Rockefeller gave it in 1972.
