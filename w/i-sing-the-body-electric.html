I sing the body electric,
The armies of those I love engirth me and I engirth them,

I Sing the Body Electric (opening)

Walt Whitman · 1855

None of the poems in the 1855 Leaves of Grass had a title, this one included. The line that now opens it and gives it its name was not added until the 1867 edition. Whitman paid for the first printing with his own money.
