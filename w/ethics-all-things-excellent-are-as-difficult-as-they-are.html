How would it be possible, if salvation were ready to our hand, and could without great labour be found, that it should be by almost all men neglected? But all things excellent are as difficult as they are rare.

Ethics — All Things Excellent Are as Difficult as They Are Rare (Part V, Prop. XLII, Note)

Baruch Spinoza · 1677

Translated by R. H. M. Elwes

Spinoza wrote the Ethics in Latin between 1661 and 1675, and it was published only after his death, in 1677. Shortly afterwards his works were placed on the Catholic Church's Index of prohibited books.
