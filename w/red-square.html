Red Square (Painterly Realism of a Peasant Woman in Two Dimensions)

Kazimir Malevich · 1915 · Oil on canvas · 53 × 53 cm · State Russian Museum, Saint Petersburg

Free to use

The canvas is inscribed on the back Peasant Woman (Suprematism), one of the first times Malevich used the word. He showed it in Petrograd at the exhibition 0,10, beside his Black Square. Only the red shape is varnished, not the white ground. The paint began to flake, probably because Malevich had repainted part of it, and it was restored shortly before his death; the largest later restorations were in 1976 and 1988.
