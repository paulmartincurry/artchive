General Robert E. Lee

Mathew B. Brady · 1865 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 14 × 9.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Mathew Brady spent more than $100,000 during the Civil War to make over 10,000 plates. He expected the government to buy them; it declined, and he had to sell his New York studio and file for bankruptcy. Congress granted him $25,000 in 1875, but he stayed in debt and died penniless in a charity ward in New York in 1896. This print is 14 by 9.3 centimetres.
