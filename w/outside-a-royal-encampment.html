Outside a Royal Encampment (left side of a double-page album folio)

Style of Bihzad · c. 1485 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Image: 24.4 × 14.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting was left unfinished, so the artist's underdrawing still shows where the colour was never filled in. It is the left half of a double composition, made around 1485 in Afghanistan or Iran: musicians play, a cook grills meat over a brazier, and a man in blue has fallen down drunk. The leaf belonged to Philip Hofer of New York by 1931.
