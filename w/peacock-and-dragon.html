Peacock and Dragon

William Morris · designed 1878 · Wool, three-colored complementary weft, weft-float faced 3:1 twill weave; lined with linen, plain weave; two panels pieced · 217.5 × 171.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Morris designed Peacock and Dragon in 1878, during the two years he gave over to textiles after a spell of incessant work at the dye-vat. It is a heavy wool twill in three colours, and one repeat of the pattern is more than a metre high. This hanging is two panels pieced together and lined with linen; the Art Institute bought it in 1985.
