The Four Evangelists, within a Border of Flowers, Birds, and Insects

Circle of Giulio Clovio · 1572 · Tempera and gold paint on vellum, in paper montage with pen and brown ink and touches of watercolor · 36.7 × 25 cm (14 1/2 × 9 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This miniature is a collage. The coat of arms of Pope Gregory XIII suggests it came from a manuscript kept in the Sistine Chapel; when Napoleon's troops raided Rome in the late 1700s, sixty manuscripts were taken from the papal chapel, and many were cut up and reassembled.
