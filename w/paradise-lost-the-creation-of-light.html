Paradise Lost: The Creation of Light

John Martin · 1824, published 1825 · Mezzotint · Platemark: 25.3 × 35.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In 1823 the publisher Samuel Prowett commissioned Martin to illustrate Paradise Lost and paid him 2,000 guineas for 24 mezzotints. Before they were finished he paid 1,500 guineas more for a second set on smaller plates. Martin had begun his working life as a painter of coaches and ceramics.
