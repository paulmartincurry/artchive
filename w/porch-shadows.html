Porch Shadows (Camera Work 49/50, plate IX)

Paul Strand · 1916, published 1917 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress describes this plate as a variant of Strand's 1916 photograph Abstraction, Twin Lakes, Connecticut: an abstract pattern of porch shadows on a table and floor. It appeared in Camera Work 49/50 in June 1917. Stieglitz tipped every photogravure into the magazine himself, touching up dust spots and scratches, and once hung gravures from it in an exhibition in Brussels when a set of prints failed to arrive.
