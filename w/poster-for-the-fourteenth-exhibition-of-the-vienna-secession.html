Poster for the Fourteenth Exhibition of the Vienna Secession (Klinger, Beethoven)

Alfred Roller · 1902 · Lithograph · 95.5 × 62.5 cm · MAK – Museum of Applied Arts, Vienna

Free to use

Alfred Roller designed the posters for the fourth, fourteenth and sixteenth Secession exhibitions, and laid out the exhibitions as well. The fourteenth, in 1902, was built around Max Klinger's statue of Beethoven, and in the same year Roller became president of the Secession. Also in 1902 he showed Gustav Mahler sketches for Tristan und Isolde, and Mahler hired him to design the sets. The sheet is 95.5 centimetres tall.
