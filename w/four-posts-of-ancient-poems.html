Four Posts of Ancient Poems (detail)

Zhang Xu · 8th century · Handscroll, cursive script, ink on paper · Liaoning Provincial Museum, Shenyang

Free to use

Zhang Xu was counted among the Eight Immortals of the Wine Cup. Legend has it that when drunk he wrote with his own hair as the brush, and on waking could not match the work sober. An anecdote says he learned cursive by watching porters push past a princess's guard and the sword dancer Lady Gongsun perform. He flung off his cap in front of princes and was called Zhang the Madman. The full scroll holds forty lines and is about 1.9 metres long.
