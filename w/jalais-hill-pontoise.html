Jalais Hill, Pontoise

Camille Pissarro · 1867 · Oil on canvas · 34 1/4 × 45 1/4 in. (87 × 114.9 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

At the Salons of 1865 and 1866 Camille Pissarro had still listed Corot and Melbye as his masters in the catalogue; by the exhibition of 1868 he credited no one. This canvas, 87 by 115 centimetres, dates from the year between, when he was about thirty-seven. He preferred to finish his paintings outdoors, often at one sitting. William Church Osborn left the picture to the museum in 1951.
