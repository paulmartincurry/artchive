It avails not, time nor place—distance avails not, I am with you, you men and women of a generation, or ever so many generations hence

Crossing Brooklyn Ferry

Walt Whitman · 1856

The poem first appeared in 1856 as Sun-Down Poem and took its present title in 1860. Lines from it are in Whitman's notebooks from early 1855. It runs to 132 lines in nine sections and describes the ferry crossing of the East River from Manhattan to Brooklyn, in the decades before the Brooklyn Bridge was built. Whitman kept making small revisions until 1881.
