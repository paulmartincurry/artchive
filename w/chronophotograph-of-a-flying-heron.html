Chronophotograph of a Flying Heron

Étienne-Jules Marey · c. 1883–87 · gelatin silver print · Image: 8.3 × 9.4 cm (3 1/4 × 3 11/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In 1880 Marey bought a villa at Posillipo, near Naples, and did part of his work on motion there. Local people found it strange to watch him aim his photographic gun at birds without ever firing, and gave him a mocking nickname. This print is only 8 centimetres high, with VII pencilled on the back. Cleveland bought it in 2009.
