The Marsh Flower, a Sad Human Head (Homage to Goya)

Odilon Redon · 1885 · Lithograph on China paper laid on wove paper · Image: 27.5 × 20.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is the second plate of Homage to Goya, a portfolio of six lithographs printed by Lemercier in Paris in an edition of 50. Redon sent one set to the poet Stéphane Mallarmé, whose literary salon he had begun to attend. The Print Club of Cleveland bought this set from the Paris dealer Maurice Le Garrec and gave it to the museum in 1927.
