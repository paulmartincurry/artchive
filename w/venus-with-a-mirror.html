Venus with a Mirror

Titian · c. 1555 · Oil on canvas · 124.5 × 105.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

X-rays show that Titian painted this Venus over a double portrait he had abandoned, keeping one figure's red cloak for the cloth under her arm. It is believed to be the earliest of his versions of the subject, and it stayed in his house in Venice until his death in 1576. Czar Nicholas I bought it in 1850; in 1931 the Soviet government sold it in secret to raise foreign currency, and Andrew Mellon acquired it.
