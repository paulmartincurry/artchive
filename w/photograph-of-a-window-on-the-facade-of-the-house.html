Photograph of a Window on the Facade of the House of Hector Guimard, 22 Rue Mozart

Hector Guimard · 1910 · Photographic print on sensitized paper · 22.6 × 10.6 cm (8 7/8 × 4 3/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The window is on an upper storey of Hector Guimard's own house, and has a metalwork balustrade. Guimard was forty-three in 1910. Between 1890 and 1930 he designed and built some 50 buildings and 141 entrances for the Paris Métro, but Art Nouveau went out of fashion in the 1910s, and by the 1960s most of his work had been demolished. Madame Hector Guimard gave the print to the museum.
