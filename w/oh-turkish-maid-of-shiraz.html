Oh Turkish maid of Shiraz ! in thy hand
If thou’lt take my heart, for the mole on thy cheek
I would barter Bokhara and Samarkand.

Oh Turkish Maid of Shiraz

Hafez of Shiraz · 14th century; English 1897

Translated by Gertrude Lowthian Bell

Hafez took his name, which means the memorizer, from having learned the Quran by heart at an early age. Many tales were told about him after his death, and in one of them Timur, whose capital was Samarkand, summons him to account for the opening lines of this poem. In the story Hafez answers with a joke about his own poverty and is sent away with gifts.
