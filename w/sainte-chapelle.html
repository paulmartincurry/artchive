Sainte-Chapelle

Maker unknown · 1238–1248 · Stone and stained glass

Free to use with credit. Photo: Didier B (Sam67fr), CC BY-SA 2.5

King Louis IX paid 135,000 livres for the Crown of Thorns and other relics, and another 100,000 for the silver chest that held them. The chapel he built to house them cost 40,000 livres to build and glaze, and it was consecrated on 26 April 1248. During the French Revolution it was used as a storehouse for grain and its spire was pulled down, but nearly two-thirds of the original stained glass survived. A restoration begun in 1840 went on for twenty-eight years.
