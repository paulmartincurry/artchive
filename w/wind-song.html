But the youth I love is gone to war, far hence.
Weary, lonely, for me he longs.

Wind-Song (Gomda Daagya)

Kiowa wind-song · Printed 1907

Translated by Natalie Curtis, 1907

Kiowa wind-songs are war songs made while the men were away on the war-path, sung by those at home or by the warriors thinking of home. The name comes from the loneliness of the open prairie, where there is only the sweep of the wind. Apiatan, or Wooden Lance, sang this one; Curtis noted that it was very ancient and its words archaic.
