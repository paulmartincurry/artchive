Aurora Borealis (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Trouvelot's interest in astronomy seems to have begun when he saw several auroras in 1870. He observed this one on the evening of 1 March 1872, the year Joseph Winlock, director of the Harvard College Observatory, saw his drawings and invited him to join the staff there.
