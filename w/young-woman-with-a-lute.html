Young Woman with a Lute

Johannes Vermeer · ca. 1662–63 · Oil on canvas · 20 1/4 × 18 in. (51.4 × 45.7 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The canvas was almost certainly cut from the same bolt of cloth as the one Vermeer used for Lady Writing a Letter with her Maid. The picture was probably painted shortly after Young Woman with a Water Pitcher. The objects in the foreground have darkened with age and the paint there is abraded. The railroad industrialist Collis P. Huntington left it to the museum in his will in 1900.
