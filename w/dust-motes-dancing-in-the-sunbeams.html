Dust Motes Dancing in the Sunbeams

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1900 · Oil on Canvas · Ordrupgaard, Copenhagen

Free to use

From 1898 to 1909 Vilhelm Hammershøi and his wife, Ida, lived at Strandgade 30 in Copenhagen, and many of his interiors were painted there. He was thirty-five or thirty-six in 1900. He kept to a narrow range of colours, mostly greys with muted yellows and greens. He had begun drawing lessons at eight and later trained at the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts.
