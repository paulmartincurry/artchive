The Art of Painting

Johannes Vermeer · 1667 · Oil on canvas · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Vermeer signed the canvas but did not date it, and he never sold it, even when he was in debt. After his death his widow made it over to her mother to keep it from the creditors; the executor ruled the transfer illegal. Until 1860 it passed as a Pieter de Hooch, with a forged signature to match, and in 1813 it changed hands for 50 florins. Hitler acquired it in 1940 for 1.82 million Reichsmark. It was recovered in 1945 from a salt mine near Altaussee.
