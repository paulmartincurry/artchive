The Earth Only Endures

Teton Sioux war song · Printed 1918

Translated by Frances Densmore, 1918

Used-as-a-Shield sang this war song for Frances Densmore, who noted that it was sung after a fight. Densmore grew up in Red Wing, Minnesota, where as a child she listened to the music of the Dakota living nearby; in more than fifty years of work she collected thousands of recordings.
