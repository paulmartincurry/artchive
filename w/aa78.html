Zdzisław Beksiński · 1978 · Oil on hardboard · 87 × 87 cm · Muzeum Historyczne w Sanoku (Historical Museum in Sanok)

Free to use with credit. Photo: Zdzisław Beksiński (rights held by Muzeum Historyczne w Sanoku), CC BY-SA 3.0

Beksiński painted this in oil on a board 87 centimetres square in 1978. The year before, he had moved to Warsaw, and before leaving he destroyed a selection of his works in his own backyard without leaving any record of them. The painting belongs to what he called his fantastic period, which ran from the late 1960s into the mid-1980s.
