Malagan Masks

Maker unknown · Late 19th Century · Carved wood · Ethnological Museum, Berlin

Free to use

Malagan carvings are made in northern New Ireland, Papua New Guinea, for ceremonies held in the name of people who have recently died. The ceremonies last several days and take months or years to prepare. By tradition the carvings were burnt or left in a cave to rot once the event was over.
