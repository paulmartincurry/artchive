Cadaver Tomb of René of Chalon

Ligier Richier (attributed) · c. 1544–57 · Limestone and black marble · H. 177 cm · Church of Saint-Étienne, Bar-le-Duc

Free to use

René of Chalon, Prince of Orange, died at twenty-five on 15 July 1544, of a wound taken the day before at the siege of St. Dizier. His alleged deathbed wish was that his tomb show his body as it would be three years after death. The limestone figure is attributed to Ligier Richier, and at one point the raised hand supposedly held a reliquary containing the prince's heart. During the First World War the tomb was moved to the Panthéon in Paris for safekeeping, and it returned to Bar-le-Duc in 1920.
