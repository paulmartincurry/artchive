Saturn and Its Rings, Natural Colour

Cassini, NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute · 6 October 2004 · Mosaic of 126 images

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute

The mosaic is made of 126 images taken over two hours on 6 October 2004, while Cassini was about 6.3 million kilometres from Saturn. The view changed so little in that time that none of the images had to be re-projected or altered. Each tile was taken through red, green and blue filters.
