Li Kui (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 38.8 × 26.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Water Margin heroes were Kuniyoshi's first major commission, received in 1827. Before it he had produced little for nearly ten years, probably for lack of commissions from publishers, and at one point he was forced to sell used tatami mats. He showed each hero alone on a single sheet.
