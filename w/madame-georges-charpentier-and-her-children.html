Madame Georges Charpentier and Her Children

Auguste Renoir · 1878 · Oil on canvas · 60 1/2 × 74 7/8 in. (153.7 × 190.2 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Georges Charpentier, a Paris publisher, commissioned this portrait of his wife and children for 1,000 francs. Renoir painted it in a month, from September to October 1878, in a private sitting room of their house on the rue de Grenelle. The boy on the sofa is Paul, dressed like his sister, who sits on the family's Newfoundland dog, Porthos. After both parents had died the daughters sold the collection, and the Metropolitan Museum bought the painting in April 1907 for 84,000 francs.
