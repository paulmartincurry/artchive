The Artchive

Violette Heymann

Odilon Redon · 1910 · pastel on gray wove paper · Unframed: 72 x 92 cm (28 3/8 x 36 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The sitter was the young niece of the Paris collector Marcel Kapferer. Redon, known early for drawings and lithographs in black and white, turned to pastel later, and made commissioned portraits of women with flowers like this one.
