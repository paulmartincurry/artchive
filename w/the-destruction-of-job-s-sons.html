The Destruction of Job's Sons (The Book of Job, Plate 3)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · Plate: 21.6 × 17.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blake had sketched ink studies for an illustration to Job as early as 1785, and he made two sets of watercolours of the subject, in 1806 and 1821, before the engravings. This is plate 3 of the twenty-two. The engraved plate is about 21.6 by 17.2 centimetres, on a sheet nearly twice as large each way.
