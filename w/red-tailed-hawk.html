Red-Tailed Hawk (The Birds of America, Plate 51)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1829 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Audubon shot his birds with fine shot and then propped them on wires in natural positions to draw them, instead of stuffing them stiffly as other ornithologists did. He had worked out the method of wires and threads as early as 1807.
