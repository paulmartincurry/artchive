When thou art quiet or silent, then thou art That which God was before Nature and Creature, and whereof he made thy Nature and Creature; then thou hearest and seest with That wherewith God saw and heard in thee before thy own Willing, Seeing, and Hearing began.

Of the Supersensual Life (The Way to Christ)

Jacob Boehme · 1622; published 1624

Jacob Böhme was a shoemaker in Görlitz before he gave up the trade in 1613 and sold woollen gloves. This dialogue was among the short works he wrote in 1622 that went into his first published book, The Way to Christ, on New Year's Day 1624. The book caused a scandal; that March the town council warned him to leave Görlitz, and he died in November of the same year.
