The Giant Wheel (Carceri d'invenzione, plate IX)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 55.3 × 40.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The first nine plates of the set are upright and the last seven are horizontal, so this is the last of the upright ones. Piranesi seems to have been unsure how the prints would be received: the 1750 title page did not name him, and neither did most of the plates. Robison counts eight states of this one.
