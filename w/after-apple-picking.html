My long two-pointed ladder's sticking through a tree
Toward heaven still,
And there's a barrel that I didn't fill
Beside it, and there may be two or three
Apples I didn't pick upon some bough.
But I am done with apple-picking now.

After Apple-Picking

Robert Frost · 1914

Frost placed this poem at the centre of North of Boston, his second book. It runs to 42 lines and keeps to no fixed form or rhyme scheme. He had grown up in the city, and before the book he earned his living teaching school and then working a farm in Derry, New Hampshire. His first book had come out when he was around forty.
