Dictionnaire Infernal: Belphégor

Louis Le Breton · published 1863 · Wood engraving

Free to use

Jacques Collin de Plancy first published his dictionary of demons in 1818, as a sceptic influenced by Voltaire. By the end of 1830 he had become a devout Roman Catholic, and he rewrote the book to fit Catholic theology; the sixth and final edition of 1863, with these engravings, shows the change most clearly. Belphégor is on page 89.
