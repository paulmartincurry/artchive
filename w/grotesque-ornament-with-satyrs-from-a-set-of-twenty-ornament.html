Grotesque Ornament with Satyrs, from a set of twenty ornament panels

Agostino Veneziano · ca. 1530–1535 · Engraving on laid paper · 20.2 × 14.4 cm (7 15/16 × 5 11/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The monogram AV is engraved in tiny letters at the bottom of this panel. Of the 188 prints once attributed to Agostino Veneziano, 141 carry that monogram. He had worked in Marcantonio Raimondi's printmaking workshop in Rome until the sack of the city in 1527 broke it up, and he went back to Rome in 1531, staying until at least 1536. The sheet is 20.2 by 14.4 centimetres.
