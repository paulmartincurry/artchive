Maffet Ledger: Drawing

Maker unknown · c. 1874–81 · Graphite, watercolor, and crayon on paper · 29.8 × 13.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A page from a ledger drawn in by Southern and Northern Cheyenne artists. Account books became a common source of paper for Plains artists in the late nineteenth century; the older pictures of this kind had been painted on buffalo hides, and the buffalo had become scarce. In 1877 a Northern Arapaho man named Friday said it was extremely common for close friends to draw in each other's books.
