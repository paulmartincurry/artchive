Success is counted sweetest
By those who ne'er succeed.
To comprehend a nectar
Requires sorest need.

Success is counted sweetest

Emily Dickinson · 1859 (published 1864)

Written in 1859, when Dickinson was twenty-eight, the poem first appeared without her name in the Brooklyn Daily Union on April 27, 1864. Helen Hunt Jackson, who knew it by heart, pressed her for two years to let it go into an 1878 anthology of unsigned work. The publisher admitted the wording had been slightly changed. Readers believed Ralph Waldo Emerson had written it.
