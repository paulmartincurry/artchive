Bessemer Converter, Allegheny Ludlum Steel Corp., Brackenridge, Pennsylvania

Alfred T. Palmer · c. 1941 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In a Bessemer converter, air is blown through molten pig iron to burn off the excess carbon, and the oxidation itself keeps the iron molten. Henry Bessemer patented the process in 1856. The Library of Congress dates Palmer's transparency, made at the Allegheny Ludlum plant in Brackenridge, Pennsylvania, to 1941 with a question mark.
