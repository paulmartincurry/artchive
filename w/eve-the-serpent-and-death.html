Eve, the Serpent and Death

Hans Baldung Grien · c. 1510–15 · Oil on wood (probably lime) · 64 × 32.5 cm · National Gallery of Canada, Ottawa

Free to use

For about a century this panel was taken for a Cranach: it was auctioned at Christie's in 1875 under his name, after leaving the collection of the British politician William Angerstein. Sotheby's in Scotland identified it as Baldung's work before selling it in 1969, and the National Gallery of Canada bought it in 1972. Its date is still debated, from the early 1510s to the late 1520s.
