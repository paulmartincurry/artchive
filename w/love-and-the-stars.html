On the stars thou gazest, my Star; would I were heaven, that I might look on thee with many eyes.

Love and the Stars

Plato · before 347 BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail, 1890

Thirty-two epigrams in the Greek Anthology go under the name of Plato, some of them doubtfully. Mackail, who translated them in 1890, notes the tradition that the philosopher, who lived from 428 to 347 BCE, wrote poetry as a very young man. Meleager included him in the Garland.
