Cottages in a Misty Grove in Autumn

Li Anzhong · 1117 · Album leaf; ink and color on silk · 24.2 × 26.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Li Anzhong dated this leaf himself: his inscription says he painted it in the ting-yu year, 1117. He was a court painter who lived through the move of the capital from Kaifeng to Hangzhou. The leaf, about 24 by 26 centimetres, later belonged to Gao Shiqi of Hangzhou and to C. C. Wang in New York.
