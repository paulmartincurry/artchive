Blossom Restaurant, 103 Bowery

Berenice Abbott · 3 October 1935 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Berenice Abbott took this photograph on 3 October 1935, the year the Federal Art Project hired her as supervisor of her own project, Changing New York. The print is an 8 by 10 inch contact print and carries the project's stamp on the back. By the time she resigned in 1939 she had made 305 photographs for it.
