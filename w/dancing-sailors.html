Dancing Sailors

Charles Demuth · 1917 · Watercolor over graphite · Sheet 20.4 × 25.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Charles Demuth signed and dated this watercolour in 1917 and sold it to Albert Rothbart of Ridgefield, Connecticut. He made two almost identical versions; the other is in the Museum of Modern Art. This one was exhibited only once in his lifetime. The sheet is 20.4 by 25.7 centimetres.
