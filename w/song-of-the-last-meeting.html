My heart was chilled and numb,
But my feet were light.

Song of the Last Meeting

Anna Akhmatova · 1911

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2005 · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Akhmatova dated this poem 29 September 1911 and noted that it was her two hundredth. She was twenty-two. Her first book, Evening, came out the following year with only 35 of those 200 poems in it, and the edition of 500 copies quickly sold out. She took her grandmother's surname as a pen name because her father did not want verses printed under his own.
