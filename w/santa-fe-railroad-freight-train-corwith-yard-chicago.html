Santa Fe Railroad Freight Train, Corwith Yard, Chicago

Jack Delano · March 1943 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This is a colour transparency, made in March 1943 and transferred from the Office of War Information to the Library of Congress in 1944. The Farm Security Administration, where Delano had worked, was eliminated as budget waste that year. Later in 1943 he was drafted into the Army Air Corps and served in the South Pacific until 1946.
