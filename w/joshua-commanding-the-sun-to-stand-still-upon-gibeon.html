Joshua Commanding the Sun to Stand Still upon Gibeon

John Martin · 1816 · Oil on canvas · 150 × 231 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Martin showed this canvas at the Royal Academy's Summer Exhibition of 1816 and at the British Institution the following year, where it took a premium of £100. William Collins bought it in 1822. In 1924 an anonymous donor gave it to the United Grand Lodge of England, which kept it for eighty years until the National Gallery of Art bought it in 2004.
