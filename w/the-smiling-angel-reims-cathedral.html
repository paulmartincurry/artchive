The Smiling Angel, Reims Cathedral

Maker unknown · 1236–45 · Stone

Free to use

The angel was carved between 1236 and 1245. On 19 September 1914 a German shell set fire to Reims Cathedral, and the head fell four metres and broke into several pieces. An abbot collected the pieces the next day and stored them in the archbishop's cellars, where an architect found them on 30 November 1915. The restored sculpture was put back on the cathedral on 13 February 1926.
