Destroy This Mad Brute: Enlist, U.S. Army

Harry R. Hopps · 1918 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

Harry R. Hopps ran a stained-glass firm in San Francisco with his brother; his father was a stage set designer. The poster was copyrighted by the San Francisco Army Recruiting District on 4 April 1918, and one of the Library of Congress copies has the address 660 Market St. stamped over the word Enlist. Hopps later moved to Los Angeles and worked as an art director on films, among them The Thief of Bagdad.
