Faridun Strikes Zahhak with the Ox-Headed Mace (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Sultan Muhammad · c. 1525 · Ink, opaque watercolor and gold on paper · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

This leaf is attributed to Sultan Muhammad and is now in the Freer Gallery of Art in Washington. The manuscript it comes from turned up in the collection of Edmond James de Rothschild after the Ottoman Empire fell apart, and was later bought by Arthur Houghton, who sold its paintings one by one to avoid taxes.
