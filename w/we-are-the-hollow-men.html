Shape without form, shade without colour,
Paralysed force, gesture without motion;

We Are the Hollow Men (The Hollow Men, I)

T. S. Eliot · 1925

The first part of The Hollow Men was printed in The Dial in March 1925, alongside the parts that became II and IV. Two months earlier Eliot had run three related poems in his own magazine, The Criterion. One of the poem's two epigraphs names Kurtz, from Joseph Conrad's Heart of Darkness.
