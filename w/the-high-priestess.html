The High Priestess (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Waite met Smith through the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, which she joined in 1901 after W. B. Yeats introduced her to it. The imagery of the deck drew on the nineteenth-century occultist Eliphas Levi and on the order's teachings.
