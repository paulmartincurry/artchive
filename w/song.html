When I am dead, my dearest,
Sing no sad songs for me;
Plant thou no roses at my head,
Nor shady cypress tree:

Song (When I am dead, my dearest)

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Many of Christina Rossetti's letters, diary entries and papers were destroyed after her death by her brother William. She had turned down three offers of marriage, ending an engagement to the painter James Collinson in 1850. She died of cancer on 29 December 1894, aged sixty-four, at 30 Torrington Square in Bloomsbury, and was buried in the family grave at Highgate Cemetery.
