The Artchive
The fog comes
on little cat feet.

It sits looking
over harbor and city
on silent haunches
and then moves on.

Fog

Carl Sandburg · 1916

Sandburg left school at thirteen to drive a milk wagon. He went on to work as a porter in a barbershop, a bricklayer, a farm laborer on the wheat plains of Kansas, a hotel servant in Denver and a coal-heaver in Omaha. He began his writing career as a journalist for the Chicago Daily News, and he was thirty-eight in the year of this poem.
