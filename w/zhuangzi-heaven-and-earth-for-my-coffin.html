Zhuangzi — Heaven and Earth for My Coffin

Zhuangzi (Chuang Tzu) · 4th–3rd century BC

Translated by Herbert A. Giles, 1889

The scholar Russell Kirkland has argued that there is no reliable historical record of Zhuang Zhou at all, and that most of what is known about the Zhuangzi comes from its third-century commentator, Guo Xiang. Sima Qian wrote that Zhuang Zhou is thought to have spent time in the southern state of Chu and in Linzi, the capital of Qi.
