The Monkey Leader Angada Steals Ravana's Crown from His Fortress (Siege of Lanka series)

Manaku · c. 1725 · Ink and opaque watercolor on paper · 56.7 × 83.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Manaku, born about 1700, was around twenty-five when this page was made; the sheet measures 56.7 by 83.8 centimetres. He almost never signed his work, and only four surviving pieces carry his signature. After his death he was mostly forgotten, overshadowed by his younger brother Nainsukh, until art historians such as B. N. Goswamy brought him back to notice. The Met bought this page in 1919.
