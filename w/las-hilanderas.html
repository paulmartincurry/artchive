Las Hilanderas (The Spinners)

Diego Velázquez · c. 1655–1660 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

The painting was made for Don Pedro de Arce, huntsman to Philip IV, and entered the royal collection in the eighteenth century. It was probably damaged in the fire at the Alcázar in Madrid in 1734, after which strips were added to the sides and more than fifty centimetres to the top. The tapestry hanging at the back is a copy of a Titian that was in the Spanish royal collection. Most scholars date the canvas to about 1657, when Velázquez was in his late fifties.
