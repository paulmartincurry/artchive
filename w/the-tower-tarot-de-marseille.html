The Tower, Tarot de Marseille (XVI, La Maison Dieu)

Jean Dodal · 1701–15 · Woodcut, coloured by hand or with stencils · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

The French deck calls this card the Maison Dieu, the House of God. Early Italian packs gave it other names, among them the Arrow, Lightning, the House of the Devil and the Fire. The name Tarot de Marseille itself was coined only in 1856, by the card historian Romain Merlin.
