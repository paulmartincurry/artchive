Wolf-Head Barge Fixture

Maker unknown · 1–100 CE · Bronze · 21.9 x 16.8 cm (8 5/8 x 6 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Wolf heads very like this one capped the beam ends of a pleasure barge moored on Lake Nemi, near Rome, a floating platform for parties. Each held a large bronze ring between its teeth.
