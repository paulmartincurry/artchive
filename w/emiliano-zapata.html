Emiliano Zapata

José Guadalupe Posada · ca. 1910–1913 (a later impression) · Zincograph · Sheet: 6 7/8 × 4 9/16 in. (17.5 × 11.6 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Posada's career had begun in 1871, before he was twenty, as political cartoonist for El Jicote, a newspaper in Aguascalientes that closed after eleven issues, reportedly because one of his cartoons offended a local politician. From the outbreak of the Mexican Revolution in 1910 until his death in 1913 he worked steadily at his press. This sheet is a later impression from his plate.
