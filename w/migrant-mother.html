Migrant Mother

Dorothea Lange · 1936 · Photograph · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Dorothea Lange was driving home on a cold, rainy afternoon in March 1936 when she passed a sign for a pea-pickers' camp at Nipomo, California. She kept going for twenty miles, then turned around. By her own account she spent ten minutes at the camp, and this was the last frame she took there. The mother's left thumb was later retouched out of the foreground. Lange was working for a federal agency, so the picture was in the public domain from the start. The woman was not identified until 1978: Florence Owens Thompson.
