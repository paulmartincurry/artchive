Your pain is the breaking of the shell that encloses your understanding.

Even as the stone of the fruit must break, that its heart may stand in the sun, so must you know pain.

The Prophet — On Pain

Kahlil Gibran · 1923

The book is 26 prose poetry fables, written in English. The prophet Almustafa has lived in the city of Orphalese for 12 years when the ship that will carry him home arrives, and before he leaves, the seeress Almitra asks him to speak to the crowd.
