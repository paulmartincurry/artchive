While this America settles in the mould of its vulgarity, heavily thickening to empire,
And protest, only a bubble in the molten mass, pops and sighs out, and the mass hardens,

Shine, Perishing Republic

Robinson Jeffers · 1925

Jeffers first published this poem in 1925, in Roan Stallion, Tamar, and Other Poems. In the 1930s he wrote two companion pieces, Shine, Republic and Shine, Empire. He and his wife had settled in Carmel-by-the-Sea, California, in 1914.
