Five Grotesque Heads (Teste Pittoriche)

Gaetano Gandolfi · 1750–1800 · Pen and brown ink and brush and brown wash over black chalk · Sheet: 29.5 × 20.2 cm (11 5/8 × 7 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Gaetano Gandolfi devised this kind of drawing, the pictorial head, in the 1770s, drawing on academic theories that emotions could be classified by facial expression. Many of his drawings were made as finished works in their own right and collected by Italian and British patrons. He trained at the Accademia Clementina in Bologna, where he won prizes for portraiture and sculpture, and he travelled to England in 1788.
