Guardian Spirit of the Waters

Odilon Redon · 1878 · Various charcoals, with touches of black chalk, stumping, erasing, incising, and subtractive sponge work, heightened with traces of white chalk, on cream wove paper altered to a golden tone · 46.6 × 37.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Redon was thirty-eight and had worked in charcoal for years when this drawing brought him his first recognition, in 1878. He used several charcoals with black chalk, rubbed, erased, scratched and sponged, on cream paper altered to a golden tone. He sold it to the dealer Ambroise Vollard in December 1899, according to the Art Institute's 1994 catalogue.
