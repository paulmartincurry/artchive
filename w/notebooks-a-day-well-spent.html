As a day well spent procures a happy sleep, so a life well employed procures a happy death.

Notebooks: A Day Well Spent

Leonardo da Vinci · before 1519

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

Leonardo spent his last years in France, in the service of King Francis I, who gave him the manor house of Clos Lucé near the royal château at Amboise and a pension. A visitor in October 1517 recorded that his right hand was paralysed. He died at Clos Lucé on 2 May 1519, aged sixty-seven, and by his will sixty beggars carrying tapers followed his coffin.
