The Artchive

Nebuchadnezzar

William Blake · 1795–ca. 1805 · Colour print, ink and watercolour on paper · Tate, London

Free to use

The design was painted on millboard and run through Blake's press against a sheet of dampened paper. He and his wife Catherine then worked over each impression in ink and watercolour. Four impressions existed, and one has been missing since 1887. In the late summer of 1805 Blake sold this one to Thomas Butts with seven others from the series, for £1.1s each.
