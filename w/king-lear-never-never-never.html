Why should a dog, a horse, a rat have life,
And thou no breath at all? Thou’lt come no more,
Never, never, never, never, never!

King Lear — Never, Never, Never (Act V, Scene III)

William Shakespeare · 1606

For a century and a half audiences did not see this ending. From 1681 the stage used Nahum Tate's rewrite, in which Lear and Cordelia survive. Edmund Kean tried Shakespeare's tragic ending in 1823 and went back to Tate after three performances; William Macready restored Shakespeare's text at Covent Garden in 1838.
