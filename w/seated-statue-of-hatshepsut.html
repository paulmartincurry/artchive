Seated Statue of Hatshepsut

Maker unknown · ca. 1473–1458 BCE · Indurated limestone, paint · H. 197 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The statue is 197 centimetres tall and weighs about 1,247 kilograms. It comes from the Senenmut Quarry at Deir el-Bahri in Thebes, and the museum's record names two excavations for it: Lepsius in 1843–45 and its own in 1926–28. It was bought through the Rogers Fund in 1929.
