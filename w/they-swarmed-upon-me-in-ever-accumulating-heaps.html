They Swarmed upon Me in Ever Accumulating Heaps (The Pit and the Pendulum)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Clarke's mother died in 1903, when he was fourteen, and he was apprenticed in his father's studio while taking evening classes in Dublin. Poe's story first appeared in the annual The Gift for 1843. Its pendulum came from one paragraph in an 1826 history of the Spanish Inquisition by the priest Juan Antonio Llorente, retelling the account of a single prisoner.
