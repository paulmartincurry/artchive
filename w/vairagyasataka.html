We have gained no pleasure, but pleasure has taken
us captive

Vairāgyaśataka

Bhartṛhari · ca. 5th century CE

Translated by B. Hale Wortham, 1886

This is one of three collections of 100 verses each, on ethics, love and detachment, known together as the Śatakatraya. The date of composition is unknown. Tradition names the Sanskrit grammarian Bhartṛhari as the author, but modern scholars generally treat the grammarian and the poet as two different people.
