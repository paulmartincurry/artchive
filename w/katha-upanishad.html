Arise! Awake! Pay attention, when you've obtained your wishes! A razor's sharp edge is hard to cross— that, poets say, is the difficulty of the path.

Katha Upanishad

Vedic Upanishadic Seers · ca. 500 BCE

Translated by Patrick Olivelle · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

No one knows who composed this text or exactly when: scholars date it anywhere from the fifth to the first centuries BCE. It has two chapters of three sections each, and the first chapter is thought to be the older. The story it tells of the boy Nachiketa is older still and appears in the Taittiriya Brahmana. It was translated into Persian in the seventeenth century, and copies of that version were put into Latin and circulated in Europe.
