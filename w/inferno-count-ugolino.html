Already blind, to groping over each,
And three days called them after they were dead;
Then hunger did what sorrow could not do.

Inferno — Count Ugolino (Canto XXXIII)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1306–1310

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

Ugolino della Gherardesca had himself proclaimed lord of Pisa in 1287. In July 1288 he was seized and shut in the Muda, a tower of the Gualandi family, with his sons Gaddo and Uguccione and two grandsons. In March 1289, on the orders of Archbishop Ruggieri, the keys were thrown into the Arno and the prisoners left to starve. Their bodies lay in the cloister of the church of Saint Francis until 1902.
