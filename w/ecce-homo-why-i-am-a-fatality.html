I know my destiny. … I am not a man, I am dynamite.

Ecce Homo — Why I Am a Fatality

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici

Ecce Homo was the last original book Nietzsche wrote. He wrote it in 1888, and it was not published until 1908, eight years after his death. Its other chapters carry titles such as Why I Am So Wise and Why I Am So Clever, and he signed the book Dionysus versus the Crucified.
