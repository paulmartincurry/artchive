The Fourth King of Hell

Maker unknown · late 1300s · Hanging scroll, ink, color, and gold ink on silk · 63 × 45.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This Korean scroll was one of a set of ten, one king to each; the First King from the same set is at Harvard. Below the king and his secretary, sinners suffer in a giant cauldron of boiling water while a guard drives burning spears into them. Ready-made Buddhist paintings from workshops in the Chinese port of Ningbo are believed to have been one of the models for scenes like this. The set belonged to Harry Packard; this scroll reached Cleveland in 2019.
