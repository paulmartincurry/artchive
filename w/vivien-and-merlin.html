Vivien and Merlin

Julia Margaret Cameron · 1874 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 30.4 × 25.3 cm (11 15/16 × 9 15/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Cameron made this print in 1874, the year before she left Dimbola, the house at Freshwater she had bought in 1860 next door to Alfred Tennyson and named after one of her husband's coffee plantations in Ceylon. A private gate joined the two houses. In about twelve years with a camera she took around 900 photographs. She died at Kalutara in Ceylon in 1879. The museum bought this print in 1952.
