Night View of Matsuchi Hill and the San'ya Canal (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 35.8 × 24.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is the only print in the series with a large human figure, purportedly Hiroshige's favourite geisha. The three-coloured square title cartouche marks this impression as a first edition, and the grain of the woodblock shows in the dark grey sky and the blue water. In later printings, made from worn blocks, the grain stands out less.
