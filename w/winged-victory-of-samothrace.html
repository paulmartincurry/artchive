Winged Victory of Samothrace

Maker unknown · ca. 190 BCE · Parian marble · Louvre

Charles Champoiseau, a French consular official, dug the body out of the sanctuary on Samothrace on 13 April 1863 and shipped it to the Louvre. He left behind the grey marble blocks found beside it, taking them for a tomb. Austrian excavators who studied the spot from 1875 worked out that they formed the bow of a warship, and Champoiseau went back for them in 1879. The right wing is a plaster model made between 1880 and 1883. The palm of the right hand turned up in 1950, and traces of blue paint have been found on the wings.
