Happy families are all alike; every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.

Anna Karenina (opening)

Leo Tolstoy · 1877

Translated by Constance Garnett, 1901

Anna Karenina first reached readers in instalments between 1875 and 1877, all but the last of them in The Russian Messenger. The book followed in 1878: eight parts, 239 chapters, 964 pages. Tolstoy called it his first true novel. By the time he was writing the final instalments he had come to hate it, and he finished it unwillingly. Dostoevsky published an essay on it in 1877, the year before the book appeared.
