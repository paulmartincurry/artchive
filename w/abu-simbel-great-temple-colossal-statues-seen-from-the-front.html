Abu Simbel, Great Temple, Colossal Statues Seen from the Front

Félix Teynard · 1851–52 · Salted paper print from paper negative · Image: 24.3 × 30.8 cm (9 9/16 × 12 1/8 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The French title Teynard gave this print says it shows the lower part of the colossal statues, seen from the front. The image is about 24 by 31 centimetres on a mount of 38 by 50. It was part of the Gilman Collection, which the museum acquired in 2005.
