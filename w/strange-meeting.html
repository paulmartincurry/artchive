I am the enemy you killed, my friend.
I knew you in this dark; for so you frowned
Yesterday through me as you jabbed and killed.

Strange Meeting

Wilfred Owen · 1918 (pub. 1919)

Owen composed or revised this poem at Ripon, where he was posted to the Northern Command Depot in March 1918. It was published in 1919, after his death. The whole poem is written in pararhyming couplets. Benjamin Britten set it as the last of the Owen poems in his War Requiem, and a line from it appears on the memorial sculpture to Owen at Shrewsbury Abbey, put up for his centenary in 1993.
