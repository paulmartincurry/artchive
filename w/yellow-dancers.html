Yellow Dancers (In the Wings)

Edgar Degas · 1874–76 · Oil on canvas · 73.5 × 59.5 cm (28 15/16 × 23 7/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Degas first painted dancers as a subject in their own right in 1871 and gave almost half his output to it. Here the viewer stands in the wings of the Paris Opéra, where subscribers roamed backstage.
