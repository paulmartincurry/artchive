Liberty Leading the People

Eugène Delacroix · 1830 · Oil on canvas · Louvre

Free to use

Delacroix painted this in the autumn of 1830, the year of the July Revolution it shows. The French government bought it in 1831 for 3,000 francs, meaning to hang it in the throne room of the Palais du Luxembourg. It hung in the palace's museum gallery for a few months instead, and after the June Rebellion of 1832 it was returned to the artist. The state acquired it for the Louvre in 1874.
