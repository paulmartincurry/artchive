The Floor Scrapers (Les raboteurs de parquet)

Gustave Caillebotte · 1875 · Oil on canvas · 102 × 146.5 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

The room is now believed to be Caillebotte's own studio at 77 rue de Miromesnil in Paris, with three workers stripped to the waist scraping its wooden floor. The Salon jury of 1875 rejected the painting. Caillebotte showed it the next year at the second Impressionist exhibition instead, beside a second version of the same subject. His family gave it to the Musée du Luxembourg in 1894; it has been at the Musée d'Orsay since 1986.
