Isenheim Altarpiece: The Resurrection

Matthias Grünewald · 1512–1516 · Oil and tempera on limewood panel · Unterlinden Museum, Colmar

Free to use

The Resurrection sits on the right wing of the second view, shown when the outer wings were opened for the great feasts, especially those of the Virgin. Grünewald worked on the altarpiece from 1512 to 1514 or 1515. He seems to have left Isenheim in a hurry, and his later poverty suggests he was never fully paid.
