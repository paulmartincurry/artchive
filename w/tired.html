I am tired of work; I am tired of building up somebody else's civilization.

Tired

Fenton Johnson · 1919

Fenton Johnson was thirty or thirty-one when this poem appeared in 1919 in Others, the magazine of new verse edited by Alfred Kreymborg. He had self-published his first three books of poems, and back in Chicago he had founded two monthlies with his cousin, The Champion in 1916 and The Favorite Magazine in 1918. It was during the war years that he turned from rhymed and dialect verse to free verse. The poem was reprinted in The Book of American Negro Poetry in 1922.
