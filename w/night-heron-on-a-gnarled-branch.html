Night Heron on a Gnarled Branch

Ohara Koson · 1900–10 · Color woodblock print on paper · 18.3 × 35.0 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Koson worked at first with the publishers Akiyama Buemon and Matsuki Heikichi and signed these prints Koson. Most of what he made was prints of birds and flowers, typically flat in colour and in the borderless naga-ōban format. This impression was a gift to the Rijksmuseum from J. Perrée of Eindhoven.
