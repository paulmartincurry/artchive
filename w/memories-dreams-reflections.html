My life is a story of the self-realization of the unconscious.

Memories, Dreams, Reflections

Carl Jung · 1962 (English 1963)

Translated by Richard and Clara Winston · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

In 1956 Kurt Wolff, the publisher and owner of Pantheon Books, expressed a desire to publish a biography of Jung. Jung was at first reluctant to cooperate with Aniela Jaffé, but he became engrossed in the project and wrote the first three chapters himself. The rest was written by Jaffé in collaboration with him. The book was published in English in 1963, two years after Jung's death.
