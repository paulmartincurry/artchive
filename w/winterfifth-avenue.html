Winter—Fifth Avenue (Camera Work 12, plate II)

Alfred Stieglitz · 1893 (dated 1892 on the plate), published 1905 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Stieglitz stood for three hours at the corner of Fifth Avenue and Thirty-Fifth Street in a blizzard on 22 February 1893, with a hand camera borrowed from a fellow member of the Society of Amateur Photographers, waiting for an omnibus. He later misdated it to 1892, as the caption on this plate does. Within a day of developing the negative he cropped it on all four sides, and the picture was exhibited and published at least twenty-five times before 1900.
