Woman's Head with Roses

Koloman Moser · 1899 · Color pochoir on paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Koloman Moser was thirty-one in 1899, the year he began teaching at the Vienna School of Applied Arts, where he had studied. He was also the lead designer of Ver Sacrum, the magazine of the Vienna Secession, from 1898 to 1903. In 1903 he and Josef Hoffmann founded the Wiener Werkstätte.
