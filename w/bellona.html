Bellona

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1633 · Oil on canvas · 127 × 97.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Rembrandt painted Bellona in 1633, at twenty-seven, little more than a year after moving from Leiden to Amsterdam, where he first lodged with the art dealer Hendrick van Uylenburgh. In 1634 he married the dealer's cousin, Saskia van Uylenburgh. The canvas came to the museum in 1931 with Michael Friedsam's bequest.
