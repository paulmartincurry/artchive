Naito Shinjuku at Yotsuya (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 35.6 × 24.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Naitō Shinjuku was a small post town, the first station on the Kōshū Kaidō road out of Edo. Hiroshige's depiction of horse dung was criticized as vulgar. He likely alludes to a line in a book of 1775 about flowers thriving on the horse dung of Yotsuya, which referred to the prostitutes of Shinjuku.
