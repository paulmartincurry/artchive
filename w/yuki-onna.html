Even as she screamed, her voice became thin, like a crying of wind;—then she melted into a bright white mist that spired to the roof-beams, and shuddered away through the smoke-hole.

Yuki-Onna

Lafcadio Hearn · 1904

According to Hearn's introduction, a farmer in Musashi Province told him this tale. Snow-woman legends are found all over Japan, but Hearn did not know they had been written down before, and his version, in which she is harmful, is original to him. He had lived in Japan since 1890, and he died in Tokyo in September 1904, the year the book came out.
