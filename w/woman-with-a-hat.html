Woman with a Hat

Henri Matisse · 1905 · Oil on canvas · San Francisco Museum of Modern Art

Free to use

The sitter is the painter's wife, Amélie Matisse. He started the picture in September 1905 and finished it in time for the Salon d'Automne in October, where it drew the largest crowd of any entry and was dismissed as madness. Gertrude and Leo Stein bought it for 500 francs. It later passed to Michael and Sarah Stein, who sold it in 1948 to Elise S. Haas, and she left it to the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art in 1990.
