Dress or Furnishing Fabric Panel

Wiener Werkstätte · 1903/05 · Linen, plain weave; block printed; edged with cotton, plain weave; two widths joined · 190.5 × 143.5 cm (75 × 56 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Wiener Werkstätte opened in Vienna in 1903 in three small rooms, with money from the industrialist Fritz Wärndorfer, and by 1905 it employed about 100 people. This linen was printed by hand from blocks. The panel is two widths of cloth joined, 190 centimetres high, and the pattern repeats every 73 centimetres. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1991.
