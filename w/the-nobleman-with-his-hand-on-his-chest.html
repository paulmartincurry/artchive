The Nobleman with His Hand on His Chest

El Greco · c. 1580 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

El Greco painted the portrait in Toledo around 1580; it is one of his earliest works in Spain. Nobody knows for certain who the sitter is. Cervantes has been proposed, and so has a self-portrait; the Prado finds the most convincing suggestion to be Juan de Silva y de Ribera, Marquis of Montemayor.
