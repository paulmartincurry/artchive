Dado

Christopher Dresser · 1880 · Block-printed paper · H × W: 40 × 75.5 cm (15 3/4 × 29 3/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Printed in the margin of this paper is DR. DRESSER: INV., his mark as its designer. It was block printed in red, browns, black and metallic gold on a gray ground, and the sample is 40 by 75.5 centimetres. In 1880, the year of this design, Dresser was promoting the Art Furnishers' Alliance, whose shop opened at 157 New Bond Street in London the following May.
