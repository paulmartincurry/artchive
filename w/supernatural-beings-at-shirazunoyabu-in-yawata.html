Supernatural Beings at Shirazunoyabu in Yawata

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1881 · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Yoshitoshi published this print in 1881, during the years when he was making his series of 51 prints of great Japanese generals, from 1877 to 1882. When the Satsuma Rebellion of 1877 sent newspaper sales up, woodblock artists were in demand and Yoshitoshi drew much attention, but he was not financially secure until 1882. This impression belonged to Bruce Goff and came to the Art Institute in 1990 with his archive.
