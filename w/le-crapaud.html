See it: shorn poet, wingless forever,
A nightingale in the mud...

Le Crapaud (The Toad)

Tristan Corbière · 1873

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Tristan Corbière was born Édouard-Joachim Corbière and took the name Tristan in his twenties. This poem is from Les Amours jaunes of 1873, the only verse he published in his lifetime. He was twenty-seven or twenty-eight, living in Brittany, disfigured by the severe rheumatism he had contracted as a schoolboy. The book went almost unnoticed. He died two years later at twenty-nine, and it was Paul Verlaine who afterwards brought him readers.
