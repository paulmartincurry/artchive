Family Scene

Pierre Bonnard · 1893 · color lithograph on beige wove paper · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The woman is Bonnard's sister Andrée and the baby is her son Jean, born the year before. Bonnard put himself in the lower right corner, looking at his nephew. He was about twenty-five, had qualified in law, and had begun making lithographs only in 1892. The print was issued in an edition of 100 by L'Estampe Originale, a monthly publication of original prints, whose embossed stamp is below the lower left corner.
