He is not here; but far away
The noise of life begins again,
And ghastly thro’ the drizzling rain
On the bald street breaks the blank day.

Dark House (In Memoriam A.H.H., section VII)

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1850

Arthur Henry Hallam, Tennyson's Cambridge friend, died of cerebral haemorrhage in Vienna in 1833, at the age of twenty-two. Tennyson spent seventeen years composing, writing and editing the poem, and published it anonymously.
