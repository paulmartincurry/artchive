Zwei Männer, einander in höherer Stellung vermutend, begegnen sich (Two Men Meet, Each Believing the Other to Be of Higher Rank)

Paul Klee · 1903 · Etching on zinc · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is the sixth of the Inventions, a cycle of ten etchings Klee made between 1903 and 1905 in Bern, where he was living with his parents after a journey through Italy. He was twenty-three when he etched it. The cycle was shown at the Munich Secession in June 1906, his first public appearance as an artist.
