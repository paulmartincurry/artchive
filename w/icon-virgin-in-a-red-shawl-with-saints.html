Icon: Virgin in a Red Shawl, with Saints

Maker unknown · Early 16th century · Distemper and gesso on wood · H x W x D: 16.7 x 26.7 x 1.5 cm (6 9/16 x 10 1/2 x 9/16 in.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

Mary usually wears blue, but in the mid-1500s Ethiopian artists began painting her shawl red, a change tied to their study of Cretan icons. The Ge'ez inscriptions help identify the figures.
