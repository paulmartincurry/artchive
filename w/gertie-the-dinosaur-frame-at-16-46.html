Winsor McCay · 1914 · Silent animated film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Working in his spare time, Winsor McCay drew thousands of frames on sheets of mulberry paper, and an art student next door, John A. Fitzsimmons, retraced the backgrounds. The film began as part of his vaudeville act, first performed at the Palace Theater in Chicago on 8 February 1914. For the finale McCay walked offstage and reappeared in the film, where Gertie lifted him onto her back.
