For within the hollow crown
That rounds the mortal temples of a king
Keeps Death his court; and there the antic sits,

Richard II (Act III, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · 1595

From 1594 Shakespeare's plays were acted only by the Lord Chamberlain's Men, a company owned by its players, of whom he was one. They performed at The Theatre in Shoreditch. He was about thirty-one in 1595 and had three children. The year after, he was living in the London parish of St Helen's, Bishopsgate, and his son Hamnet died at the age of eleven.
