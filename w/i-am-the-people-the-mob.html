I am the people — the mob — the crowd — the mass.

Do you know that all the great work of the world is done through me?

I Am the People, the Mob

Carl Sandburg · 1916

In Milwaukee Sandburg worked for a newspaper and joined the Wisconsin Social Democratic Party. From 1910 to 1912 he was secretary to Emil Seidel, the city's socialist mayor. He met his wife, Lilian Steichen, at the party office in 1907. In Chicago he was a reporter for the Chicago Daily News and The Day Book, and his collection Chicago Poems came out in 1916.
