Kshitigarbha

Maker unknown · first half of the 14th century · Hanging scroll, ink, color, and gold on silk · 84.5 × 36.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kshitigarbha, Jijang in Korean, vowed not to become a Buddha until all the hells are emptied, and is often regarded as the bodhisattva of beings in hell. Unlike most bodhisattvas he is usually shown as a monk with a shaved head, carrying a staff that opens the gates of hell and a jewel that lights the darkness. This Goryeo painting is in ink, colour and gold on silk. It came to the Met in 1929 with the H. O. Havemeyer Collection.
