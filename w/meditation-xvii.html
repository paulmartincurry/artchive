No man is an island, entire of itself; every man is a piece of the continent, a part of the main

Meditation XVII

John Donne · 1624

In November 1623 Donne, then Dean of St Paul's, fell so ill that he came close to death. Ordered to rest, he insisted on being given pen and paper and wrote down the course of the disease. The book has twenty-three sections, one for each day of the illness, and this is the seventeenth. It was handed to the printers a month after he recovered and registered by 9 January 1624. It is one of only seven works printed in his lifetime.
