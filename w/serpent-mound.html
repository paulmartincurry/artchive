Serpent Mound

Maker unknown · ca. 300 BCE / Reworked ca. 1070 · Clay and ash, with rocks and soil

Free to use with credit. Photo: Stephanie A. Terry, CC BY-SA 4.0

The mound is 1,348 feet long and nowhere much more than three feet high, built of yellowish clay and ash under a layer of rocks and soil. Archaeologists still disagree over who made it and when: one set of radiocarbon dates gives 300 BCE, another about 1070 CE. In 1886 Frederic Ward Putnam of Harvard, finding that ploughing was destroying Ohio's mounds, raised money from a group of women in Boston to buy 60 acres around it. He then spent four years excavating, and afterwards restored the mounds.
