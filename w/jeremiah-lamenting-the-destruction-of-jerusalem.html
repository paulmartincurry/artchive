Jeremiah Lamenting the Destruction of Jerusalem

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1630 · Oil on panel · 58 × 46 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Rembrandt painted this panel, 58 by 46 centimetres, in 1630, during his Leiden period. The Rijksmuseum bought it with the support of private collectors, the Vereniging Rembrandt and a foundation for the museum.
