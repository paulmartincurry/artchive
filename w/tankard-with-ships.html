Tankard (Hanap) with Ships

Maker unknown · late 16th century · Iznik ware, fritware with underglaze painting in blue, lavender, red, and black · 21.1 × 16.8 × 12.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Iznik potters began making fine fritware in the last quarter of the 15th century, almost certainly at the urging of the Ottoman court, which prized Chinese blue-and-white porcelain. Fritware is mostly quartz sand bound with ground glass and a little clay; at Iznik lead oxide was added to the frit. This tankard of the late 1500s is painted under the glaze in blue, lavender, red and black and stands 21.1 centimetres high.
