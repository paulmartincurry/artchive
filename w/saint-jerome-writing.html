Saint Jerome Writing

Caravaggio · 1607–1608 · Oil on canvas · St John's Co-Cathedral, Valletta

The coat of arms in the bottom right corner belongs to Ippolito Malaspina, prior of the Knights of Malta in Naples. Caravaggio painted it after he arrived on Malta on 12 July 1607, a year after killing a man in a brawl in Rome. In December 1984 thieves cut the canvas out of its frame in St John's Co-Cathedral. It came back damaged two years later, after telephone negotiations with the Director of Museums, and had to be restored before it went on show again.
