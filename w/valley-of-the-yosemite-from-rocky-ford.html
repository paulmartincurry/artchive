Valley of the Yosemite, from Rocky Ford

Eadweard Muybridge · 1872 · albumen print from wet collodion negative · Cleveland Museum of Art

This view was taken in early morning light during Muybridge's second trip to Yosemite, which lasted from June to November 1872. Many of the photographs from that trip were made on glass plate negatives twenty by twenty-four inches. He was forty-two, and worked under the name Helios from a mobile darkroom wagon he called the Flying Studio. Twelve years earlier a stagecoach crash in Texas had left him with serious head injuries, and he took up photography during his recovery in England.
