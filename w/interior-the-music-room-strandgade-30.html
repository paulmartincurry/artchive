Interior. The Music Room, Strandgade 30

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 70 × 59 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hammershøi treated his apartment like a stage set and moved the furniture about as props. Here he placed a chair, a piano, a cello and a violin in a corner of the drawing room, a corner he painted regularly, with and without his wife Ida. The canvas was sold in Copenhagen in October 1910 and belonged to Ole Olsen by 1916. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it at Sotheby's in New York in May 2023.
