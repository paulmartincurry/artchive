Vegetable Nirvana

Itō Jakuchū · 18th century · Ink on paper · Kyoto National Museum

Free to use

The death of the Buddha Shakyamuni is painted in ink with all the traditional figures replaced by vegetables. Jakuchū was the eldest son of a Kyoto grocer whose shop, Masuya, lay in the Nishiki food district.
