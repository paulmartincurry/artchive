The mind is its own place, and in it self
Can make a Heav’n of Hell, a Hell of Heav’n.

Paradise Lost (Book I)

John Milton · 1667

Milton went blind in 1652 and composed the whole poem by dictation, with friends and assistants writing it down. His contemporary John Aubrey says it was written between 1658 and 1663. He had first meant to write an epic about a legendary British king such as Arthur. The first edition of 1667 had ten books and more than ten thousand lines, and it was the first book the printer Samuel Simmons registered in his own name. Milton rearranged it into twelve books in 1674, the year he died.
