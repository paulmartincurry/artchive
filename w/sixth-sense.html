So, age after age – How long, O Lord, how long? –
The spirit cries out under the knife
Of nature and art, exhausts the flesh,
To give birth to a sixth sense.

Sixth Sense

Nikolay Gumilev · 1920

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Gumilev wrote the poem in 1920, and it was published in 1921 in his collection Ognenny stolp. That year he was arrested and executed by the Cheka. He had married Anna Akhmatova in 1910.
