Climbing into America

Lewis Hine · 1905, printed 1930s · Gelatin silver print · image 24.45 × 19.37 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Hine first photographed at Ellis Island while teaching, leading his sociology classes there to photograph the thousands of immigrants who arrived each day. He wrote on the back of this print Slovaks, Climbing into America and Just arrived at Ellis Island. The negative dates from 1905; the print was made in the 1930s and later carries stamps from the Look magazine library.
