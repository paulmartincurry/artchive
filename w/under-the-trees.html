Under the Trees (from The Public Gardens)

Édouard Vuillard · 1894 · distemper on fabric · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This panel, more than two metres tall, is one of nine that Vuillard painted for the dining room of Alexandre Natanson in Paris, among his first major commissions. He was twenty-five. The series shows children playing in the Tuileries Gardens and the Bois de Boulogne. Natanson kept the panel until his sale at the Hôtel Drouot in 1929, and Cleveland bought it in 1953.
