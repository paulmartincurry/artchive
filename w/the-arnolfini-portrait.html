The Arnolfini Portrait

Jan van Eyck · 1434 · Oil on oak panel · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The panel is three vertical oak boards, 82.2 by 60 centimetres, and Jan van Eyck signed and dated it in lettering on the painted wall above the mirror. Infrared images show that he changed both faces and the mirror in the underdrawing. The picture spent centuries in the Spanish royal collection and was in London by 1816 with a Scottish soldier, Colonel James Hay. The Prince Regent had it on approval for two years and sent it back in 1818. The National Gallery bought it in 1842 for 600 pounds, by which time its shutters and original frame were gone.
