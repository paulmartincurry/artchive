Art: Conversations with Paul Gsell (Realism in Art)

Auguste Rodin · 1911

Translated by Mrs. Romilly Fedden (Katharine Waldo Douglas Fedden), 1912

Rodin was seventy in 1911. The École des Beaux-Arts had rejected him three times, and for most of two decades he earned his living as a craftsman and ornamenter, producing decorative objects and architectural embellishments. Public attention and commissions of his own came only in his late thirties. After 1900 his income from portrait commissions alone was probably 200,000 francs a year.
