Whalers

J. M. W. Turner · ca. 1845 · Oil on canvas · 36 1/8 × 48 1/4 in. (91.8 × 122.6 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Turner painted this canvas hoping that the collector Elhanan Bicknell, a wealthy investor in whaling, would buy it. It was one of two different paintings with the same title that he showed at the Royal Academy in 1845, and he hoped to sell Bicknell two more whaling pictures shown there the following year. Bicknell bought only one of them. The other 1845 canvas went to the nation in the Turner Bequest of 1856.
