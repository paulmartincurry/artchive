Who trusted God was love indeed
And love Creation’s final law--
Tho’ Nature, red in tooth and claw
With ravine, shriek’d against his creed--

Nature, Red in Tooth and Claw (In Memoriam A.H.H., section LVI)

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1850

Tennyson spent seventeen years, from 1833 to 1850, on In Memoriam, an elegy for his Cambridge friend Arthur Henry Hallam, who died of a cerebral haemorrhage in Vienna at twenty-two. He published it anonymously in 1850, in 2,916 lines and 133 cantos. Advocates of natural selection later took up the phrase red in tooth and claw from this canto.
