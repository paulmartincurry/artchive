From childhood’s hour I have not been
As others were—I have not seen
As others saw—I could not bring
My passions from a common spring—

Alone

Edgar Allan Poe · 1829 (published 1875)

Poe was twenty when he wrote these twenty-two lines in 1829, the year his foster mother Frances Allan died. He never gave the poem a title or published it. The signed manuscript stayed with a family in Baltimore until September 1875, when Scribner's Monthly printed it with a title and a facsimile. The editor, E. L. Didier, admitted he had added the date on it himself.
