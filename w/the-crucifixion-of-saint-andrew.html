The Crucifixion of Saint Andrew

Caravaggio · 1606–7 · Oil on canvas · Unframed: 202.5 × 152.7 cm (79 3/4 × 60 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Painted in Naples, the canvas belonged first to the Count of Benavente, the Spanish viceroy there. His grandson inherited it in Valladolid, and it later passed to an unidentified convent in Castile and to dealers in Madrid before Cleveland bought it in 1976. Andrew is shown on an upright cross rather than the X-shaped one usually given him. It is the only altarpiece by Caravaggio in America, and it is a little over two metres tall.
