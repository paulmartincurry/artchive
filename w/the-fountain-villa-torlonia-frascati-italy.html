The Fountain, Villa Torlonia, Frascati, Italy

John Singer Sargent · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 71.4 × 56.5 cm (28 1/8 × 22 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1907, at fifty-one, Sargent stepped away from his portrait practice to paint outdoors. The two figures are Wilfrid and Jane Emmet de Glehn, both professional painters and his frequent travelling companions. Rain kept interrupting him, so the picture took several days and his friends had to pose at length. Sargent sold it to the Art Institute of Chicago himself in 1914.
