Écorché (Flayed Man)

Jean-Antoine Houdon · 1767 · Friedenstein Stiftung Gotha

Free to use with credit. Photo: Julius Fröbus GmbH / Friedenstein Stiftung Gotha, CC BY 4.0

Jean-Antoine Houdon won the Prix de Rome in 1761 and made this flayed figure in 1767, during his four years in Italy. He was twenty-six. It is an anatomical model showing the muscles of the body without skin, and it has served artists as a guide since his day.
