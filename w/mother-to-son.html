Well, son, I’ll tell you:
Life for me ain’t been no crystal stair.

Mother to Son

Langston Hughes · 1922

The Crisis, the magazine of the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People, first printed these twenty lines in 1922, the year Langston Hughes turned twenty-one. More of his poems appeared there than in any other journal, his first published poem and his last among them. Martin Luther King Jr. drew on this one at least thirteen times in his public appearances.
