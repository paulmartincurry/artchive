Gate of the Sun, Tiwanaku

Maker unknown · ca. 500–900 · Stone (single block)

Free to use

The gateway was carved from a single piece of stone about three metres tall and four metres wide, with an estimated weight of 10 tons. European explorers who came upon it in the middle of the nineteenth century found it lying flat with a large crack through it. It stands today where it was found, which is not thought to be its original site. The figures at the edges of the frieze were added later: 48 attendants run towards the centre, where there were first 30.
