Man with a Movie Camera, frame at 58:52

Dziga Vertov · 1929 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The film has about 1,775 separate shots, cut more than four times faster than a typical feature of 1929, and not a single intertitle. It is in six parts, one for each reel it was first printed on. The music approved for the premiere in April 1929 was lost for decades. The 2015 restoration was made from a 35mm print of the only known complete version.
