Prisse Papyrus

Maker unknown · ca. 19th Century BCE · Papyrus · Bibliothèque nationale de France

Free to use with credit. Photo: Zunkir, CC BY-SA 4.0

Émile Prisse d'Avennes found this papyrus at Thebes in 1847, and it carries his name. It held three texts titled as instructions or teachings. Only one of them is complete, the Instruction of Ptahhotep, and of the four known copies of that work this is the only complete one. The copy is dated to the Twelfth Dynasty.
