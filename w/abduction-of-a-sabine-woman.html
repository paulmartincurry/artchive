Abduction of a Sabine Woman

Giambologna · 1579–83 · Marble · H. 410 cm · Loggia dei Lanzi, Piazza della Signoria, Florence

Free to use with credit. Photo: Arnold Paul, CC BY-SA 3.0

The three figures were carved from a single block of white marble, the largest block ever transported to Florence. Giambologna made the group not on commission but to impress Francesco I de' Medici, and it had no title until after it was finished; Vincenzo Borghini suggested the subject, and a bronze relief was added to the pedestal to tie it to the Roman story. It has stood in the Loggia dei Lanzi since August 1582. The original plaster model is in the Galleria dell'Accademia.
