The Englishman at the Moulin Rouge

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892 · Oil on cardboard · 33 3/4 × 26 in. (85.7 × 66 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The man in the top hat is the British artist William Tom Warrener, a close friend of Toulouse-Lautrec. Painted on cardboard, the picture was a preparatory study for a colour lithograph of 1892. Adelaide Milton de Groot left it to the Metropolitan Museum at her death, and it entered the collection in 1967.
