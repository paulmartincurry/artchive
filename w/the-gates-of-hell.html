The Gates of Hell

Auguste Rodin · 1880 · Bronze, cast from the plaster original

Free to use with credit. Photo: Till Niermann, CC BY-SA 3.0

The Directorate of Fine Arts commissioned these doors in 1880 as the entrance to a planned Decorative Arts Museum, and left the theme to Auguste Rodin. The museum was never built. He worked on the doors on and off for thirty-seven years, until his death in 1917, adding, removing and altering more than two hundred figures. The group that became The Kiss began on the doors, and he took it off because it seemed to conflict with the suffering figures around it. The work is 6 metres high and 4 metres wide.
