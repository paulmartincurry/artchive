Death and the Gravedigger

Carlos Schwabe · 1890s

Free to use

Carlos Schwabe was twenty-eight when his close friend, the musician Guillaume Lekeu, died in 1894, and death became one of his subjects from then on. The figure of Death in this picture, dated 1895, resembles his first wife, who also sat for his angels and virgins. Born in Altona and raised in Switzerland, he had come to Paris as a young man and worked there as a wallpaper designer.
