Butterfly (Chō); Dragonfly (Kagerō or Tonbo), from the Picture Book of Crawling Creatures (Ehon mushi erami)

Kitagawa Utamaro · 1788 · Page from woodblock printed book; ink and color on paper · 26.7 × 18.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

About three years after this book appeared, around 1791, Utamaro gave up designing prints for books and turned to single half-length portraits of women. He is now known best for those, but he also made nature studies, especially illustrated books of insects.
