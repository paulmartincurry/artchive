A Hunting Scene

Piero di Cosimo · c. 1494–1500 · Tempera and oil transferred to Masonite · 70.5 × 169.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The picture was painted in tempera and oil around 1494–1500 and has since been transferred to Masonite. Robert Gordon gave it to the museum in 1875. Its painter was reportedly so afraid of fire that he rarely cooked his food.
