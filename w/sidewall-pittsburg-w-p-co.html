Sidewall

Pittsburg W.P. Co. · 1900 · Machine-printed on paper · 59 × 49.5 cm (23 1/4 × 19 1/2 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Printed paper here imitates stained glass, down to dark brown lines standing in for the lead. The selvedge is marked with the maker's name and its town, New Brighton, Pennsylvania. Sylvia Goodrich gave the sheet in memory of her grandparents, Orlando R. and Elizabeth Boyd Ballou.
