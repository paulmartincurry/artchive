Woman's Head against the Shore

Edvard Munch · 1899 · Woodcut printed from two blocks (each sawn into two sections) in light green, orange, blue-green, and red-brown ink on ivory Japanese paper · Image: 46.5 × 41.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch printed this woodcut from two blocks, each sawn into two sections, in light green, orange, blue-green and red-brown ink on Japanese paper. The image is about 46 by 41 centimetres. He made it in 1899, at the age of thirty-five or thirty-six.
