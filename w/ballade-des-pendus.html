Ballade des Pendus (Ballad of the Hanged Men)

François Villon · 1462

Translated by Galway Kinnell, 1977 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Villon gave this ballade no title; the one it carries now was added by the Romantics. It is often said that he wrote it in a Paris prison late in 1462, aged about thirty-one, after a street quarrel for which he was tortured and condemned to hang, though that is not established. The sentence was commuted to banishment on 5 January 1463, and nothing is known of him afterwards. The poem was first printed in 1489.
