For the Love of God! Montresor! (The Cask of Amontillado)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

The story first appeared in the November 1846 issue of Godey's Lady's Book, at the time the most popular periodical in America, and was printed only once more in Poe's lifetime. Poe may have been inspired to write it by his own desire for revenge on literary rivals. Clarke's illustration dates from 1919. Two years later his father died, and he and his brother Walter took over the family studio.
