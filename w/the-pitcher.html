That was why the tears suddenly streamed from my eyes,
Streamed from my eyes and fell on the collar of my dress.

The Pitcher

Yuan Zhen · Tang dynasty (Yuan Zhen, 779–831)

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

Yuan Zhen, who lived from 779 to 831, was a poet and official of the middle Tang dynasty and a member of Bai Juyi's literary circle. He is also known for a prose tale, Yingying's Biography, which has often been adapted, including for opera.
