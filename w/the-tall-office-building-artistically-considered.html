The Tall Office Building Artistically Considered

Louis H. Sullivan · 1896

Sullivan wrote this in 1896, at about forty. His partnership with Dankmar Adler had been dissolved by 1894, after the Panic of 1893 cut into their practice, and Adler had been the partner who brought in the work. Their last major project, the Guaranty Building in Buffalo, New York, dates from 1895–96. He credited the principle he set out here to the Roman architect Vitruvius.
