Come to me, Arthur. Leave these others and come to me. My arms are hungry for you. Come, and we can rest together. Come, my husband, come!

Dracula — My arms are hungry for you

Bram Stoker · 1897

The first edition was bound in yellow cloth with the title in red letters and cost six shillings, and Stoker earned no royalties on its first thousand sales. In the week before it came out he staged a reading of his own stage version at the Lyceum Theatre to protect his rights, with Edith Craig as Mina. Lucy's surname comes from the Westenra family of Ireland, descended from Dutch Protestant settlers.
