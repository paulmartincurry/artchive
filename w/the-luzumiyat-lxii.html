Now, mosques and churches—even a Kaaba Stone,
Korans and Bibles—even a martyr's bone,—
All these and more my heart can tolerate,
For my religion's love, and love alone.

The Luzumiyat, LXII

Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri · before 1057; English 1918

Translated by Ameen Rihani

The Luzumiyat holds nearly 1,600 short poems, arranged alphabetically and none with a title, written over many years in a double-consonant rhyme al-Ma'arri devised for himself. He set himself other constraints too, living vegan and almost cut off from company.
