Make no parley--stop for no expostulation,
Mind not the timid--mind not the weeper or prayer,

Beat! Beat! Drums!

Walt Whitman · 1865

Whitman's Civil War poems were gathered in Drum-Taps. Its first 500 copies were printed in early April 1865, the month Lincoln was assassinated, and the book came out that May. Through the war Whitman worked in army hospitals, where he dressed wounds, helped at amputations and gave out medicines.
