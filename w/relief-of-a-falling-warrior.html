Relief of a Falling Warrior

Maker unknown · 2nd century · Marble · 53.3 × 81 × 17.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The figure repeats one from the shield of the gold-and-ivory Athena that the sculptor Pheidias made for the Parthenon around 435 BCE, enlarged some five centuries later into a marble relief. This relief and a number of others were found in the harbour of Piraeus, where they had been lost, likely while awaiting shipment. It is said to have been found in 1925, and Alfred E. Hamill gave it to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1928.
