Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Andreas Cellarius was born Keller, around 1596, near Worms, and spent the second half of his life as rector of the Latin school in Hoorn, north of Amsterdam, from 1637. All his known books were written there. He was about sixty-four when this atlas appeared. This plate shows the older model of Claudius Ptolemy, with the Earth at the centre of the universe.
