A Low Tide Pentaptych

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1830 · Pentaptych of color woodblock prints · Each: 21.2 × 18.4 cm (8 3/8 × 7 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The scene runs across five surimono, prints commissioned privately rather than made for sale, each 21.2 by 18.4 centimetres. Members of a poetry salon wrote the ten poems along the top; nine of them use the words low tide. Kuniyoshi was the son of a silk-dyer and seems to have worked as a pattern designer in his father's business. J. H. Wade and his wife gave the set to Cleveland in 1916.
