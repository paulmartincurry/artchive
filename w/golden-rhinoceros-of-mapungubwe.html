Golden Rhinoceros of Mapungubwe

Maker unknown · ca. 1220–1290 · Gold · Mapungubwe Museum, Pretoria

Free to use with credit. Photo: Sian Tiley-Nel, CC BY-SA 4.0

The rhinoceros is carved from wood and covered in thin sheets of gold, and it is small enough to stand in the palm of a hand. Archaeologists from the University of Pretoria found it in 1932 in a royal grave on Mapungubwe Hill. It was lent to the British Museum for four months from October 2016.
