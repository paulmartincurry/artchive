Karnak (Thebes), Palace, Rear Part, Fragment of Colonnades

Félix Teynard · 1851–52, printed 1853–54 · Salted paper print from paper negative · 23.9 × 30.6 cm. (9 7/16 × 12 1/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Félix Teynard was in his mid-thirties when he photographed the temple at Karnak on paper negatives. The print was made a year or two after the negative, in 1853–54, and measures about 24 by 31 centimetres. The museum bought it in 1976.
