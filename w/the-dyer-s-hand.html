The Dyer's Hand

W. H. Auden · 1962

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Many of these essays began as the lectures Auden gave as Professor of Poetry at Oxford from 1956 to 1961. Random House published the book in the United States in 1962, and Faber & Faber in Britain the following year. He dedicated it to Nevill Coghill, his tutor at Oxford, where he had started in biology, switched to English and left in 1928 with a third-class degree.
