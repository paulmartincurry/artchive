Mangosteen

Berthe Hoola van Nooten · 1863–64 · Chromolithograph

Berthe Hoola van Nooten was widowed in 1847 with five children and ran girls' schools in Louisiana and Texas before sailing to Java with her brother. There she gave drawing lessons and drew forty plates of the island's plants. The book was published in Brussels in 1863, with the chromolithographs made by Pieter De Pannemaeker in Ghent and a dedication to the Queen of the Netherlands. It went through several editions, and she died in poverty at seventy-four.
