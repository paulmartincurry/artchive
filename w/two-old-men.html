Two Old Men

Francisco de Goya · 1819–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 146 × 66 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

In 1819 Goya paid about 60,000 reales for a house on seventeen acres south-west of Madrid, near the Manzanares, and painted the Black Paintings straight onto its interior walls. Late that year an illness nearly killed him, and a friend, Dr Arrieta, saved his life. This mural probably stood on the first floor, across the room from Judith and Holofernes.
