The Last Judgment

Hans Memling · c. 1467–1471 · Oil on panel · National Museum, Gdańsk

Free to use

Angelo Tani, an agent of the Medici in Bruges, commissioned the triptych for a chapel at the Badia Fiesolana, but it was captured at sea by Paul Beneke, a privateer from Danzig. It stayed in a Danzig church until 1807, then spent eight years in the Musée Napoléon in Paris, labelled as a work by Jan van Eyck. In 1946 the Red Army found it and sent it to the Hermitage in Leningrad, where it remained for ten years.
