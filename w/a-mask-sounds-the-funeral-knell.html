A Mask Sounds the Funeral Knell (To Edgar Poe, plate 3)

Odilon Redon · 1882 · Lithograph in black on ivory China paper, laid down on white wove paper · Image: 26 × 19.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is the third plate of the album To Edgar Poe, printed in black on thin China paper laid onto a heavier sheet. Redon was conceived in New Orleans, and his mother, a French Creole, made the crossing back to France while pregnant with him. He failed the entrance examinations in architecture at the École des Beaux-Arts in Paris and studied painting there only briefly, under Jean-Léon Gérôme, in 1864.
