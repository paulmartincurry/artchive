He may well be loved, but not thought. By love may He be gotten and holden; but by thought never.

The Cloud of Unknowing — By Love, Not Thought (Chapter 6)

Anonymous English Mystic · late 14th century

In its prologue the author tells the reader not to read, copy or speak of the book to anyone not fully committed to following Christ. The text became known to English Catholics in the mid-seventeenth century through the Benedictine monk Augustine Baker, who worked from a manuscript in the monastery library at Cambrai. In the 1970s three Trappist monks, William Meninger, Basil Pennington and Thomas Keating, based the practice of Centering Prayer on its advice.
