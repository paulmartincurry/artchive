My beard points to heaven, and I feel the nape of my neck on my hump; I bend my breast like a harpy’s

Sonnet V (To Giovanni da Pistoia)

Michelangelo Buonarroti · ca. 1509

Translated by Christopher Ryan · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Michelangelo was about thirty-four when he wrote this sonnet, and he was painting the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel in Rome, the work of 1508 to 1512. He did not consider himself a painter. The Pietà and the David, both carved before he was thirty, were already behind him.
