Watch in the Form of a Human Skull

Isaac Penard · case c. 1810–20, movement c. 1650 · Case: silver; Dial: silver, partly nielloed; Movement: gilded brass and steel · 5.1 × 3.5 × 4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The movement inside is by the Swiss watchmaker Isaac Penard and dates from about 1650. The silver skull that holds it is more than a century and a half younger, made in the 1810s, probably by the firm of Moulinié, Bautte & Moynier. The whole watch is 5.1 centimetres high.
