When all has been tried, yet Justice is not in sight,
It is then right to pick up the sword,
It is then right to fight.

Zafarnama (verse 22)

Guru Gobind Singh · 1705 CE

Translated by Navtej Sarna · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Zafarnama is a letter that Guru Gobind Singh, the tenth Sikh Guru, sent to the Mughal emperor Aurangzeb after the Battle of Chamkaur. Aurangzeb had ordered the execution of the Guru's father in 1675, when Gobind Singh was nine. The Guru had learned Persian in a year at the age of seven.
