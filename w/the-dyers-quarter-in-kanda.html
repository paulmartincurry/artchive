The Dyers' Quarter in Kanda (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The characters on the first two blue-and-white cloths read sakana, a reference to the publisher, Sakanaya Eikichi. The last two cloths carry Hiroshige's monogram. Behind the dye works are Edo Castle and Mount Fuji.
