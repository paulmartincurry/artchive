What have you done, O you there
Who endlessly cry,
Say: what have you done there
With Youth gone by?

The Sky's Above the Roof (Le ciel est, par-dessus le toit)

Paul Verlaine · 1873–1874 (published 1880)

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Verlaine wrote the poem in prison, after he wounded Arthur Rimbaud with a revolver in 1873 and was sentenced to two years. It was collected in Sagesse, published in November 1880, and he reworked it after his return to Catholicism.
