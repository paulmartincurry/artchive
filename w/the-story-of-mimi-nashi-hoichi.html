At that instant Hōïchi felt his ears gripped by fingers of iron, and torn off! Great as the pain was, he gave no cry.

The Story of Mimi-Nashi-Hōïchi

Lafcadio Hearn · 1904

The source Hearn used has been identified as a tale by Isseki Sanjin, printed in 1782 in the second volume of the series Gayū kidan. Its temple, Amidaji at Akama-seki, stood where the Akama Shrine in Shimonoseki is today. Masaki Kobayashi filmed the story in 1964 as part of his film Kwaidan.
