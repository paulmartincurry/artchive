Mask of Tutankhamun

Maker unknown · ca. 1323 BCE · Gold · Grand Egyptian Museum, Giza

Free to use

Two layers of gold between 1.5 and 3 millimetres thick make up the mask, which stands 54 centimetres tall and weighs 10.23 kilograms. The blue stripes of the headcloth are glass. Howard Carter's team uncovered it on 28 October 1925, when they opened the innermost of three coffins. The beard had already come away and was fixed back with a wooden dowel in 1944. It came off again during cleaning in August 2014, and museum workers glued it on crooked with quick-drying epoxy; a German and Egyptian team reattached it with beeswax.
