Chinese Lions (Karajishi)

Kanō Eitoku · 16th century · Six-fold screen; colour, ink and gold leaf on paper · Museum of the Imperial Collections (Sannomaru Shōzōkan), Tokyo

Free to use

Eitoku was born in Kyoto, the grandson of Kanō Motonobu, an official painter for the Ashikaga shogunate, and at the age of ten he had an audience with the shōgun alongside his grandfather. Oda Nobunaga hired him at thirty-four, and Toyotomi Hideyoshi was also a patron. Most of his works were destroyed in the turmoil of the Sengoku period.
