Fight Between a Tiger and a Buffalo

Henri Rousseau · 1908 · oil on fabric · Unframed: 170 × 189.5 cm (66 15/16 × 74 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In December 1907 Rousseau was in prison for fraud and still working on this painting. Officials released him early so that he could finish it for the Salon des Indépendants, where it was shown in March 1908. He never left France: the jungle came from travel books and the Paris botanical garden, and the bananas grow upside down. The canvas measures 170 by 189.5 centimetres. The dealer Ambroise Vollard bought it from him on 14 December 1909.
