Wild Horse Felled by a Tiger

Eugène Delacroix · 1828 · Watercolor and gouache over pen and ink, with touches of gum arabic · 13.5 × 20.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sheet is small, about 13.5 by 20 centimetres, worked in watercolour and gouache over pen and ink with touches of gum arabic. Delacroix was twenty-nine or thirty when he made it in 1828. It came to the Metropolitan Museum in 2024 from the Karen B. Cohen Collection of his work.
