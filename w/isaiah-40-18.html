All flesh is grass, and all the goodliness thereof is as the flower of the field:
The grass withereth, the flower fadeth: because the spirit of the LORD bloweth upon it: surely the people is grass.

Isaiah 40:1–8

Book of Isaiah (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · 6th century BCE (trans. 1611)

This chapter opens the section scholars call Deutero-Isaiah, chapters 40 to 55, which dates from the Israelites' exile in Babylon. The Great Isaiah Scroll, found at Qumran and made in the second century BC, holds every verse of it. Verse 3 is cited in all four gospels, and Handel set verses 1 to 5 in his oratorio Messiah.
