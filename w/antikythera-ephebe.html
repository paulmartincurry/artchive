Antikythera Ephebe (Detail of the Head)

Maker unknown · c. 340–330 BC · Bronze · H. of the whole statue 1.96 m · National Archaeological Museum, Athens

Free to use with credit. Photo: Nikos Kitsakis, CC BY 4.0

Sponge-divers found the statue in 1900 in a shipwreck off the island of Antikythera, the same wreck that held the Antikythera mechanism. It came up in many fragments, and the first restoration was redone in the 1950s under Christos Karouzos, who changed the eyes, the abdomen and the position of the right arm. The whole figure is 1.96 metres tall. The right hand once held something round, and there is no agreement about who the young man is.
