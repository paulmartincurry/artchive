Too late loved I Thee, O Thou Beauty of ancient days, yet ever new! too late I loved Thee! And behold, Thou wert within, and I abroad, and there I searched for Thee; deformed I, plunging amid those fair forms which Thou hadst made.

Confessions — Too Late Loved I Thee (Book X)

Augustine of Hippo · 397–400

Translated by E. B. Pusey

Augustine was born in 354 in Thagaste, in the Roman province of Numidia, now Souk Ahras in Algeria. He was baptised by Saint Ambrose in 386 and later became Bishop of Hippo Regius.
