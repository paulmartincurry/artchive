Archangel Eliel with Harquebus

Maker unknown · c. 1690–1720 · Oil on canvas · Museo de Arte de Lima

Free to use

At the beginning of the eighteenth century hundreds of Cuzco paintings, many of them of angels with guns, were shipped to Lima, Upper Peru, Chile and northern Argentina. Large workshops, most of them indigenous, were set up to meet the demand. This one belongs to the Museo de Arte de Lima, a gift of Pedro de Osma.
