Witch Scene

David Teniers the Younger · between 1630 and 1690 · Oil on panel · 28.4 × 27.3 cm · Staatliche Kunsthalle Karlsruhe

Free to use

This small panel is 28.4 by 27.3 centimetres. David Teniers the Younger was taught by his father, a painter of altarpieces and small cabinet pictures, whose debts sometimes landed him in jail. In 1637 the son married Anna Brueghel, daughter of Jan Brueghel the Elder, with Rubens as a witness. Around 1650 he moved to Brussels as court painter and keeper of the gallery of Archduke Leopold Wilhelm.
