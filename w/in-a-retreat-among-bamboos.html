Leaning alone in the close bamboos,
I am playing my lute and humming a song
Too softly for anyone to hear —
Except my comrade, the bright moon.

In a Retreat Among Bamboos

Wang Wei · before 761; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Late in life Wang Wei became a devout Chan Buddhist and gave up meat. In 756 he was in the capital, Chang'an, when the rebels took the city, and they captured him.
