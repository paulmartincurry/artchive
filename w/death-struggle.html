Death Struggle

Edvard Munch · 1915 · Oil on canvas · 140.3 × 182.4 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Munch signed and dated this canvas 1915, and the Statens Museum for Kunst in Copenhagen acquired it in the same year. It is about 140 centimetres high and 182 wide. His sister Johanne Sophie died of tuberculosis in 1877, at the age of fifteen.
