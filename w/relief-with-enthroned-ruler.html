Relief with Enthroned Ruler

Chakalte' · 770 CE · Limestone, paint · 88.9 × 87.6 × 7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met names the sculptor of this relief as Chakalte', who was active around 750 to 800 CE, and dates the relief to 770 CE. Carved in limestone and painted, it measures 88.9 by 87.6 centimetres. It came to the Met in 1979 in the bequest of Nelson A. Rockefeller.
