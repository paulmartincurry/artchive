Lady Lilith

Dante Gabriel Rossetti · 1867 · Watercolor and gouache (bodycolor) · 20 3/16 X 17 5/16 in. (51.3 × 44 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Rossetti was thirty-eight or thirty-nine when he painted this watercolour, which is about fifty centimetres tall. He had turned to watercolour after the criticism of an oil painting he exhibited in 1850, because watercolours could be sold privately, and he rarely exhibited after that. His own method used thick pigments mixed with gum. The Metropolitan Museum bought the sheet in 1908.
