As soon as one paused to part the petals, to discover the under-side of the leaf,
along came Life and one was swept away.

At the Bay

Katherine Mansfield · 1922

Katherine Mansfield wrote this story at the Chalet des Sapins in Montana, Switzerland, drawing on her childhood in New Zealand. She had left that country at nineteen, and tuberculosis, diagnosed in 1917, kept her from ever going back. The London Mercury printed the story in twelve sections in January 1922, when she was thirty-three; a short thirteenth was added for the book. She died in France a year later.
