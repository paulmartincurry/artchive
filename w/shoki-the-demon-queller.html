Shoki the Demon Queller

Katsukawa Shun'ei · c. 1793 · Color woodblock print; hashira-e · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is a pillar print, a sheet 62 centimetres tall and only 12 wide. Katsukawa Shun'ei, whose real surname was Isoda and whose father was a landlord, mostly designed portraits of kabuki actors. Around 1800 he succeeded his teacher Shunshō as head of the Katsukawa school. In 1804 he, Utamaro, Toyokuni and others were jailed and manacled for 50 days over prints showing Toyotomi Hideyoshi. James and Mari Michener gave this print to the Art Institute in 1958.
