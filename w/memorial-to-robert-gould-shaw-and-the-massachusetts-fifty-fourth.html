Memorial to Robert Gould Shaw and the Massachusetts Fifty-Fourth Regiment

Augustus Saint-Gaudens · 1884–1897 · Bronze · Boston Common

Free to use with credit. Photo: Rhododendrites, CC BY-SA 4.0

Saint-Gaudens hired live models for the twenty-three Black soldiers and gave each a face of his own. The colonel rides on horseback while the regiment is in low relief, marching down Beacon Street as it did on 28 May 1863; Shaw died at Fort Wagner seven weeks later. The bronze, about eleven feet by fourteen, was unveiled on 31 May 1897 on the street it shows. It was taken away for repair in 2020 and set back on a new concrete foundation the next year.
