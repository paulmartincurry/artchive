I love, but who is it for whom I sigh?
Not Muslim, yet not heathen; who am I?

The Conference of the Birds — The Valley of Bewilderment

Farid ud-Din Attar · 1177 CE

Translated by Afkham Darbandi and Dick Davis, 1984 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The birds of the poem must cross seven valleys, and only thirty of them arrive. Its author gave up his pharmacy and travelled to Baghdad, Mecca, Damascus and India before coming home to Nishapur. One scholar holds that he was little known as a poet outside his own town during his life. He died in April 1221, in the massacre the Mongols carried out there.
