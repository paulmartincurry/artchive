With a sudden intensity, as if she saw it clear for a second, she drew a line there, in the centre. It was done; it was finished. Yes, she thought, laying down her brush in extreme fatigue, I have had my vision.

To the Lighthouse — I have had my vision (close)

Virginia Woolf · 1927

From the spring of 1882 Woolf's father rented a large white house in St Ives, Cornwall, and the family spent three months there each summer for the first thirteen years of her life. Its main attraction was the view across the bay to the Godrevy Lighthouse. They did not go back after 1894: a hotel had been built in front of the house, blocking the view of the sea, and her mother died the following May.
