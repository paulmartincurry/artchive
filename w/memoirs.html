Memoirs

Pablo Neruda · 1974 (English 1977)

Translated by Hardie St. Martin · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Pablo Neruda died in Santiago on 23 September 1973, the day before he was to leave Chile for exile in Mexico, and the memoirs came out the following year. His first published work was an essay in a local daily newspaper, printed when he was thirteen. In 1927, out of financial desperation, he took an honorary consulship in Rangoon, and he went on to hold many diplomatic posts, the last as Chile's ambassador to France.
