Courtesan

Keisai Eisen · before 1848

Free to use

Keisai Eisen was born in Edo in 1790, the son of a samurai who was a painter and calligrapher. He took the name Keisai from the first master he was apprenticed to, Kanō Hakkeisai. Besides designing prints he wrote books, among them a supplement to a history of ukiyo-e artists in which he describes himself as a hard drinker and claims to have owned a brothel in Nezu that burned down.
