Self-Portrait

Salvator Rosa · c. 1647 · Oil on canvas · 99.1 × 79.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Metropolitan Museum dates this canvas to about 1647, during the years Rosa spent in Florence. He had moved there around 1640 at the invitation of Giovanni Carlo de' Medici and stayed until 1649. The painting came to the museum in 1921 by the bequest of Mary L. Harrison.
