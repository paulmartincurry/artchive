Ewer in the Form of a Phoenix

Maker unknown · ca. 15th–16th century · Stoneware with underglaze cobalt blue decoration · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

A Vietnamese potter made this phoenix ewer, decorated in cobalt blue under the glaze. It stands 29 centimetres high. Betty and John R. Menke gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1992.
