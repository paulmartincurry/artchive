Fight with Cudgels

Francisco de Goya · c. 1820–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 123 × 266 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya painted this on a wall of the upper room of his house, reusing a landscape that was already there. Photographs taken before the transfer show the two men standing in high grass. Large areas were lost when the mural was moved to canvas, and the damage was covered over below their knees.
