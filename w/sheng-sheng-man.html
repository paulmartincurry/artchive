Sheng Sheng Man (Slow, Slow Tune)

Li Qingzhao · ca. 1129 CE

Translated by Kenneth Rexroth and Ling Chung · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Kaifeng, the Northern Song capital, fell in 1127, and Li Qingzhao's house in Shandong was burned in the fighting. She and her husband, Zhao Mingcheng, fled south of the Yangtze to Nanjing with what they could carry of their collection of inscriptions and books. He died of typhoid fever in 1129 on the way to an official post, when she was about forty-five. Only around a hundred of her poems are known to survive.
