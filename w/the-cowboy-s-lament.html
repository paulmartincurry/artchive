As I walked out in the streets of Laredo,
As I walked out in Laredo one day,
I spied a poor cowboy wrapped up in white linen,
Wrapped up in white linen as cold as the clay.

The Cowboy's Lament

Traditional cowboy ballad · by 1910

It comes from an older folk song, The Unfortunate Rake, and is also sung as The Dying Cowboy and Streets of Laredo. Frank H. Maynard of Colorado Springs claimed it as his own in 1911, and cowboys up and down the trail kept revising it. These are the first three verses of John Lomax's text, from the 1918 printing of Cowboy Songs.
