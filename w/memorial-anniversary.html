Memorial Anniversary (One Hundred Ghost Tales)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; chūban · 24.3 × 18.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A snake crawls over the things laid out for a memorial service: a box of sweets, a spirit tablet carrying the dead person's Buddhist name, and a leaf floating in a bowl of fresh water. The parlour game behind the series title was traditionally played in summer, the season of Obon, the festival of the dead. Hokusai lived another seventeen years and died in 1849, aged 88.
