Elle n'a pas oublié sa proie, et demain la terre cachera le peu qui est resté de lui...

Journal: 27 January 1824

Eugène Delacroix · 1824

Delacroix was twenty-five when he wrote this. Two years earlier, Géricault's The Raft of the Medusa had pushed him to paint his first major picture, The Barque of Dante. It was accepted by the Salon of 1822 and derided by the public and officials, but the State bought it for the Luxembourg Galleries.
