We had fed the heart on fantasies,
The heart’s grown brutal from the fare,
More substance in our enmities
Than in our love; oh, honey-bees
Come build in the empty house of the stare.

The Stare's Nest by My Window (Meditations in Time of Civil War, VI)

W. B. Yeats · 1922 (published 1928)

The Tower takes its title from Ballylee Castle, a Norman tower which Yeats bought and restored in 1917 and where he often spent summers with his family until 1928. It was his first major collection after he received the Nobel Prize in 1923.
