A Vase of Flowers

Margareta Haverman · 1716 · Oil on wood · 31 1/4 × 23 3/4 in. (79.4 × 60.3 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Only two signed flower paintings by Margareta Haverman are known, and this is one of them; the other is in the Statens Museum for Kunst. She was twenty-two or twenty-three when she signed and dated it in 1716, and she was registered as a pupil of Jan van Huysum, who was known for guarding his methods. The greens have faded towards blue, possibly because of a yellow lake pigment that did not last. The Met bought the panel in 1871 for 2100 francs, among its first 174 paintings.
