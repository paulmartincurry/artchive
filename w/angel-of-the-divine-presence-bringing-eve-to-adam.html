Angel of the Divine Presence Bringing Eve to Adam

William Blake · c. 1803 · Watercolor, pen and black ink, over graphite · Sheet: 41.6 × 33.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The back of this sheet carries a sketch for the same composition. Blake drew the design in graphite and worked over it in pen, black ink and watercolour when he was about forty-five. At ten he had been enrolled in drawing classes at Henry Pars's drawing school in the Strand instead of being sent to school. The Metropolitan Museum bought the drawing in 1906.
