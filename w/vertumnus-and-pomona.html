Vertumnus and Pomona (Sakuntala)

Camille Claudel · 1905 · Marble · Musée Rodin, Paris

Free to use with credit. Photo: Pierre André Leclercq, CC BY-SA 4.0

Camille Claudel first modelled this couple in 1886 and finished a full plaster in 1888, 190 centimetres tall, which won an honourable mention at the Salon. No commission for a marble or bronze followed. Rodin tried to obtain a government order for a marble in 1895, and that failed too. This marble was completed only in 1905, under a new title, Vertumnus and Pomona.
