Valley of Aosta: Snowstorm, Avalanche, and Thunderstorm

J. M. W. Turner · 1836–37 · Oil on canvas · 92.2 × 123 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1836, when he was sixty-one, Turner travelled through France and Switzerland and across the Alps to the Aosta Valley. The painting sold at Christie's in London for 910 guineas in 1878 and for 4,000 guineas in 1895, the second time to a dealer acting for Sir Donald Currie. Currie's granddaughter sold it in 1947, and the Art Institute of Chicago bought it that year.
