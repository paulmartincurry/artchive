Vir Heroicus Sublimis

Barnett Newman · 1950–51 · Oil on canvas · 242.3 × 541.7 cm · Museum of Modern Art, New York

In copyright. Look and learn: shown for study, not for download.

At about 242 by 542 centimetres, this was the largest canvas Barnett Newman had made when he finished it in 1951. The Latin title means Man, Heroic and Sublime. Newman called the thin vertical bands zips. He was in his mid-forties, and he and his wife were living almost entirely on her teaching salary, which they did until his paintings began to sell steadily in the late 1950s.
