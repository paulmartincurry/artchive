It began as a mistake.

Post Office

Charles Bukowski · 1971

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Bukowski worked for the post office in Los Angeles twice, first as a fill-in letter carrier in the early 1950s and then, for more than a decade, as a letter filing clerk. In 1969, aged 49, he accepted an offer from John Martin of Black Sparrow Press and quit to write full time. He finished this novel, his first, less than one month after leaving.
