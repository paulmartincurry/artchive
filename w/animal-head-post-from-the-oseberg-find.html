Animal-Head Post from the Oseberg Find (Detail of the Head)

Maker unknown · buried 834 · Carved wood · Viking Ship Museum, Oslo

Free to use with credit. Photo: William Allen, CC BY-SA 2.0

Five wooden posts with carved animal heads were found in the Oseberg burial mound near Tønsberg in Norway when it was excavated in 1904. The mound also held the Oseberg ship, textiles, and animals killed as offerings to the two women buried there. Four of the posts are on show in Oslo; the fifth survives only in pieces.
