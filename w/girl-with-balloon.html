Girl with Balloon

Banksy · 2002 · Stencil and spray paint on a wall

In copyright. Small image shown under fair use, for reference and commentary.

Banksy began stencilling this image around London in 2002, on Waterloo Bridge, in Shoreditch and elsewhere, and none of those murals is still in place. One, from a shop wall in Shoreditch, was cut out in 2014 and sold the following year for 500,000 pounds. He also issued it as prints, the first a signed set of 25 in 2003. In 2018 a framed copy sold at Sotheby's for 1,042,000 pounds and, moments after the last bid, was half shredded by a device he had built into the frame.
