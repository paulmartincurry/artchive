Frieze of Dancers

Edgar Degas · c. 1895 · oil on fabric · Unframed: 70 × 200.5 cm (27 9/16 × 78 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The canvas is two metres wide, and the paint around the figure was put on so loosely that it ran and dripped. Degas made the circles in the foreground with his thumb. He sold it to Durand-Ruel on 19 September 1904, and the dealer sold it to Paul Cassirer in Berlin the same day. Two months later it went to Max Liebermann, whose daughter Käthe Riezler inherited it and lent it to the Art Institute of Chicago from 1941 to 1942. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought it in 1946.
