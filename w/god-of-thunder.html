God of Thunder (Raijin)

Tawaraya Sōtatsu workshop · mid-1600s · six-panel folding screen; ink, color, and gold on paper · Image: 150.4 × 343 cm (59 3/16 × 135 1/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painter of this screen is not known. The paper was covered with gold dust, a method used by Tawaraya Sōtatsu and his followers, and together with an Inen seal it ties the work to his studio. A matching screen of the God of Wind would have stood beside it; that screen cannot be found. This one is 162.4 centimetres high and 355.8 wide, and Cleveland bought it from a New York dealer in 2004.
