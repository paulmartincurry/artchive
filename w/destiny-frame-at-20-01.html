Destiny, frame at 20:01

Fritz Lang · 1921 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

According to his biographer Patrick McGilligan, Fritz Lang made the film on the heels of his mother's death. Lang reportedly came up with his figure of Death during a fever he had as a child, and he recalled seeing a dark stranger in a wide-brimmed hat lit by moonlight from a half-open window. Bernhard Goetzke plays Death.
