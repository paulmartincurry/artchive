Senufo Helmet Mask

Maker unknown · Early to mid-1900s · Wood · 122 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The carver combined a ram's horns, a warthog's tusks, a crocodile's mouth and a hyena's snout, and set a small chameleon between the long antelope horns. The museum's record describes the mask as a device against sorcery. It is 122 centimetres long. Cleveland showed it in 1968 in an exhibition of Katherine White Reswick's collection, and she later gave it to the museum.
