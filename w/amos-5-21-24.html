But let judgment run down as waters, and righteousness as a mighty stream.

Amos 5:21-24

Book of Amos (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · c. 8th century BCE; KJV 1611

According to the book's opening line, Amos lived in Tekoa, a town in Judah south of Jerusalem, and carried out his mission in the northern kingdom of Israel. The book calls him a shepherd and a dresser of sycamore trees. He prophesied in the first half of the 8th century BC, during the reigns of Jeroboam II of Israel and Uzziah of Judah. The book has nine chapters.
