A sincere man am I
From the land where palm trees grow,
And I want before I die
My soul’s verses to bestow.

Yo soy un hombre sincero (Versos Sencillos I)

José Martí · 1891

Translated by Manuel A. Tellechea · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Versos sencillos was published in 1891, when José Martí was thirty-eight. He was a journalist who wrote for newspapers across the Americas and founded several of his own, among them Patria, which he used in the campaign for Cuban independence. Four years after the book he was killed in action at the Battle of Dos Ríos, on 19 May 1895. After his death verses from it were adapted into the song Guantanamera.
