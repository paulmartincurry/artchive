Street Arabs in "Sleeping Quarters"

Jacob Riis · c. 1888, printed later · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Riis had emigrated from Denmark in 1870 and once earned extra money projecting advertisements in Brooklyn with a magic lantern. As a night-shift police reporter on the Lower East Side he could not draw, and the lenses and plates of the time were too slow for dark corners. In early 1887 he read of a new German flash powder of magnesium and potassium chlorate, fired from a pistol-like device, and took it into the slums.
