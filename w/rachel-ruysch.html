Rachel Ruysch (1664–1750)

Rachel Ruysch & Michiel van Musscher · 1692 · Oil on canvas · 30 × 25 in. (76.2 × 63.5 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Rachel Ruysch was twenty-eight in 1692 and had been selling signed flower paintings of her own since she was eighteen. Her training began at fifteen, as an apprentice to the Amsterdam flower painter Willem van Aelst, and her first models were the plants and insects in her father's scientific collection. The year after this picture she married the portrait painter Juriaen Pool, and they had ten children. The Metropolitan Museum bought the canvas in 2023.
