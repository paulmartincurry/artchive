Calavera of Francisco Madero

José Guadalupe Posada · Design before 1913 (this impression printed 1944) · Relief etching from a zinc plate on grayish-ivory China paper · Image: 29.7 × 13.2 cm (11 3/4 × 5 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This impression was pulled from Posada's zinc plate in 1944, thirty-one years after his death, and published by the Print and Drawing Club of the Art Institute of Chicago. Posada died of gastroenteritis in January 1913, largely forgotten and reportedly penniless. Three neighbours certified his death, only one of whom knew his full name, and he was buried in an unmarked pauper's grave.
