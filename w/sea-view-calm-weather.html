Sea View, Calm Weather (Vue de mer, temps calme)

Édouard Manet · 1864 · Oil on canvas · 73.6 × 92.6 cm (29 × 36 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Manet painted it in Paris from sketches made on a family holiday at Boulogne-sur-Mer, one of his earliest pictures of the sea. Right of centre a side-wheel packet steamer heads up the Channel past the slower sailing boats.
