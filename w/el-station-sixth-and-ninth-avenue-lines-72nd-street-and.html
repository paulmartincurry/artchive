El Station, Sixth and Ninth Avenue Lines, 72nd Street and Columbus Avenue

Berenice Abbott · 6 February 1936 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott took this station on 6 February 1936. The Ninth Avenue line was the first elevated railway in New York, opened in 1868 as an experimental cable-powered single track. Most of it closed on 11 June 1940 and was taken down, four years after this photograph.
