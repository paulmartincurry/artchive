Blind Orion Searching for the Rising Sun

Nicolas Poussin · 1658 · Oil on canvas · 119.1 × 182.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Nicolas Poussin painted the canvas in 1658 for Michel Passart, a patron of landscape painting. The scene was inspired by the retellings of Greek myth by Lucian, a Syrian writer of the 2nd century CE. The museum acquired it in 1924.
