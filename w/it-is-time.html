It Is Time (Los Caprichos, plate 80)

Francisco de Goya · 1799 · Etching, burnished aquatint, drypoint, burin · Plate: 21.5 × 15.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is the last plate of the series. The prints were made in 1797 and 1798; in April 1797 Goya resigned as Director of the Academy because of his long illness. This impression was given to the Met by M. Knoedler & Co. in 1918.
