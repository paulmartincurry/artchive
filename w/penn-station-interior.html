Penn. Station, Interior

Berenice Abbott · 1930–39 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott was hired by the Federal Art Project in 1935 to supervise Changing New York. She worked with a Century Universal camera that made 8 by 10 inch negatives, and this is a contact print from one. Her note on the back reads 173-4 Penn Sta.
