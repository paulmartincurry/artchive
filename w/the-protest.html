The Protest (La Manifestation)

Félix Vallotton · 1893 · Woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

The print shows police breaking up a political demonstration in Paris, where street riots were common in the 1890s. It was made in an edition of 100 for L'Estampe Originale, and this impression is signed and numbered 90 in pencil. Vallotton had seen the gallery of Japanese prints at the Paris Universal Exposition of 1889, four years before he cut the block.
