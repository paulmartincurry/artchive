Geronimo's Story of His Life

Geronimo (Goyaałé) · 1905–1906

Geronimo was about seventy-six and had been held by the United States for nineteen years when he gave this account of his life in 1905. That same year the Indian Office supplied him for the parade at Theodore Roosevelt's second inauguration; in 1898 he had been exhibited at the Trans-Mississippi Exposition in Omaha. Among the Bedonkohe Apache he was a shaman and war leader, not a chief. He died at the Fort Sill hospital in 1909, still a prisoner of war.
