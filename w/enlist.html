Fred Spear · 1915 or 1916 · Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.

Free to use

The Boston Public Safety Committee issued this poster in 1915 or 1916. The Library of Congress identifies the woman under the water as a passenger from the Lusitania. This copy came to the library with the Willard and Dorothy Straight Collection.
