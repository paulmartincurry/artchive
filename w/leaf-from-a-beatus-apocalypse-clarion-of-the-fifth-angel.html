Leaf from a Beatus Apocalypse: Clarion of the Fifth Angel's Trumpet

Spanish · ca. 1180 · Tempera, gold, and ink on parchment · The Met

On this single leaf a star falls from the sky, a pit is opened with a key, and locusts come out of the smoke. The parchment is 44.5 centimetres tall and 30 wide. The Met bought it in 1991 for The Cloisters.
