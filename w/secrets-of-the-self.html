If the world does not comply with his humour,
He will try the hazard of war with Heaven;

Secrets of the Self (Asrār-i-Khudī)

Muhammad Iqbal · 1915 (Trans. 1920)

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1920

This was the first book of poetry Muhammad Iqbal published, in 1915, when he was thirty-seven and had a law practice in Lahore. His mother tongue was Punjabi and he had written poems in Urdu, but he wrote this one in Persian because he felt the language suited the ideas. Many of his own friends and admirers were shocked by it when it came out. His father was a tailor with no formal education.
