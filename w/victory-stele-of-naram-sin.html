Victory Stele of Naram-Sin

Maker unknown · ca. 2254–2218 BCE · Sandstone · Louvre

Free to use

The stele was carved for Naram-Sin of Akkad and is believed to have stood at Sippar. In the 12th century BC the Elamite king Shutruk-Nakhunte sacked the city and carried it off to Susa. He left the old inscription in place and added one of his own, about a thousand years after the first, saying he had taken it. Jacques de Morgan excavated it at Susa in 1898. It now stands about two metres high; estimates of its original height run up to three.
