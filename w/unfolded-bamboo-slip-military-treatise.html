Unfolded Bamboo-Slip Military Treatise (Sunzi Bingfa Scroll)

Maker unknown · Classical Warring States Format / Qing Archival Edition · Bamboo · University of California, Riverside

Free to use with credit. Photo: vlasta2, bluefootedbooby on flickr.com, CC BY 2.0

The text is The Art of War, thirteen chapters attributed to Sun Tzu and now generally thought to have been completed between 500 and 430 BC. In 1972 bamboo slips carrying it were found in two Han dynasty tombs in Shandong that had been sealed between 134 and 118 BC. A French Jesuit, Jean Joseph Marie Amiot, made the first translation into a European language in 1772.
