The Two Fridas

Frida Kahlo · 1939 · Oil on canvas · Museo de Arte Moderno, Mexico City

In copyright. Small image shown under fair use, for reference and commentary.

This was the first large painting Frida Kahlo made, a canvas about 173 centimetres square. She painted it in 1939, the year she divorced Diego Rivera; they remarried a year later. Her friend Fernando Gamboa said it came out of two double portraits she had seen at the Louvre earlier that year. It was shown in Mexico City in January 1940 and stayed with her until the Instituto Nacional de Bellas Artes acquired it in 1947.
