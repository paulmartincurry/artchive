Piri Reis Map

Piri Reis · 1513 · Gazelle-skin parchment · Topkapı Palace

Free to use

Piri Reis had sailed with his uncle as a Barbary pirate before both joined the Ottoman navy. He began the map at Gallipoli in 1511, after his uncle's death, and one of his sources was a map by Columbus that is now lost. He presented it to Sultan Selim I after the conquest of Egypt in 1517, and it then disappeared. The surviving piece, the western third, about 87 by 63 centimetres, was found in a bundle in the Topkapı Palace library in 1929.
