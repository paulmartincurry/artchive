Of human life the time is a point, and the substance is in a flux, and the perception dull, and the composition of the whole body subject to putrefaction, and the soul a whirl, and fortune hard to divine, and fame a thing devoid of judgment.

Meditations — Of Human Life the Time Is a Point (Book II, 17)

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180

Translated by George Long

A note in the text says the second book was written at Carnuntum, and the first in the country of the Quadi, on the river Granova, so part of the work was written on campaign. Marcus Aurelius wrote in Greek, for his own guidance.
