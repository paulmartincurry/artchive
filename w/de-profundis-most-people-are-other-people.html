Most people are other people. Their thoughts are some one else's opinions, their lives a mimicry, their passions a quotation.

De Profundis — Most people are other people

Oscar Wilde · 1897 (pub. 1905)

In prison Wilde was allowed only one page at a time, and he never revised the letter after his release. Robert Ross was told to send the original to Lord Alfred Douglas but, fearing Douglas would destroy it, sent a typed copy instead. The first book edition was a German translation published in Berlin on 11 February 1905, about two weeks ahead of Ross's English one, which left out every reference to the Queensberry family. The unabridged text was read aloud in the High Court in 1913.
