The Dance of Life

Edvard Munch · 1899–1900 · Oil on canvas · National Museum, Oslo

Free to use

Munch was thirty-five when he began this canvas, which is nearly two metres wide, and it belongs to the series he later called The Frieze of Life. He kept a copy of Helge Rode's 1898 play Dansen gaar in his own library. The collector Olaf Schou bought the painting in Oslo in 1910 and gave it straight away to the National Gallery.
