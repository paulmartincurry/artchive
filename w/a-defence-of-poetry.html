Poets are the hierophants of an unapprehended inspiration; the mirrors of the gigantic shadows which futurity casts upon the present… Poets are the unacknowledged legislators of the world.

A Defence of Poetry (closing)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1821

The essay is an answer to The Four Ages of Poetry, an article by Shelley's friend Thomas Love Peacock that had come out in 1820. Shelley wrote it in February and March 1821, put it aside and never completed it. It was published in 1840, eighteen years after his death, after some editing by John Hunt and by Mary Shelley.
