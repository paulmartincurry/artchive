Anxiety

Edvard Munch · 1896 · Woodcut in black ink on lightweight ivory laid proofing paper · Image: 46.3 × 37.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The painting Anxiety dates from 1894. Two years later, in 1896, the year he moved to Paris, Munch made a lithograph and a woodcut of the motif. This impression of the woodcut was printed by Lassally in black ink on thin proofing paper.
