Takiyasha the Witch and the Skeleton Spectre

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · ca. 1844 · Woodblock print on paper · British Museum

Free to use

The print is a triptych. Kuniyoshi set it in the tenth century, in the ruined manor of the warlord Taira no Masakado, whose rebellion against the emperor was put down in 939. The princess is Masakado's daughter, and the man she is trying to frighten is an official of the emperor sent to look for surviving conspirators.
