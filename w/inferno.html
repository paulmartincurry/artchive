Inferno (Cantos III & XXXIV)

Dante Alighieri · ca. 1314

Translated by John Ciardi, 1954 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Many scholars date the writing of Inferno to between about 1306 and 1310, with revisions continuing for years. Dante was by then an exile from Florence, where his political faction, the White Guelphs, had been defeated. The death of Pope Clement V in 1314 is the only event mentioned in it that happened after 1310. The first part circulated in fragments and excerpts long before the whole poem was published, and the earliest citations of it date from as early as 1315.
