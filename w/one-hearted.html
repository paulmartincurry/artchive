When those red berries come in springtime,
Flushing on your southland branches,
Take home an armful, for my sake,
As a symbol of our love.

One-Hearted

Wang Wei · before 761; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Wang Wei lived from 699 to 761, in the middle Tang dynasty. He was as much a landscape painter as a nature poet, and his courtesy name was Mojie.
