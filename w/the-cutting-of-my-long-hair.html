The Cutting of My Long Hair

Zitkala-Ša (Gertrude Simmons Bonnin) · 1900 (American Indian Stories, 1921)

In 1884, when Zitkala-Ša was eight, missionaries came to the Yankton reservation and took her and several other children to White's Indiana Manual Labor Institute, a Quaker boarding school in Wabash, Indiana. There she was made to cut her traditionally long hair. She stayed three years. Her account of the school appeared in the Atlantic Monthly in 1900, when she was twenty-four, and was collected in American Indian Stories in 1921.
