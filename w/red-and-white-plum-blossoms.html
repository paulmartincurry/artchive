Red and White Plum Blossoms

Ogata Kōrin · 18th century · Pair of two-panel folding screens; coloured pigments on paper · MOA Museum of Art, Atami

Free to use

The screens are undated. They are believed to come from Kōrin's later period and are probably one of his final works; the art historian Yūzō Yamane dates them to 1714 or 1715, just before his death. No documentation of them exists from before the twentieth century. A journal article of 1907 is the first known publication about them, and they were first shown in public in 1915.
