One Hundred Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku Hyakkei — Monochrome Spread)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1834–1835 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · Metropolitan Museum of Art

The blocks for the first two volumes were cut by the workshop of Egawa Tomekichi and printed in greys, and the books came out in 1834 and 1835 in embossed pink covers. A third volume followed from a different publisher in Nagoya and is not as well printed. This was the first major project Hokusai signed with the name Manji, written with a character meaning ten thousand. He added a colophon about his own life to a later pressing, at the age of seventy-five.
