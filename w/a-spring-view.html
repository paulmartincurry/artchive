The nation shattered, mountains and rivers remain;
city in spring, grass and trees burgeoning.

A Spring View (Chūn Wàng)

Du Fu · 757 CE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Du Fu wrote this as a captive. When the An Lushan rebellion drove the emperor from the capital in 756, he took his family to safety and set out to join the new court, but the rebels caught him and took him to occupied Chang'an. He was in his mid-forties, and a son was born to him that autumn. He escaped the following year and rejoined the court in May 757.
