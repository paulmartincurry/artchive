Edge of the Woods Near L'Hermitage, Pontoise

Camille Pissarro · 1879 · oil on fabric · Framed: 166 x 204 x 16.5 cm (65 3/8 x 80 5/16 x 6 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Pissarro painted it at the Hermitage near Pontoise, where he had lived since 1872, at a time when he was poor and struggling to sell. He laid the strokes in diagonal rows, which he compared to knitting. It was shown at the fourth Impressionist exhibition in 1879.
