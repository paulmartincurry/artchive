Dürer's Rhinoceros

Albrecht Dürer · 1515 · Laid paper

Free to use

The inscription above the animal gives the wrong year for its arrival in Lisbon, 1513 for 1515. Dürer drew it in pen and ink from a letter and a sketch sent to Nuremberg, without seeing the rhinoceros, and the print is a reversed image of his drawing. The block went through eight editions, most of them after his death in 1528, and by the seventh, issued in Amsterdam after 1620, it had woodworm holes and a crack through the legs. German school textbooks printed the image as a true likeness until the late 1930s.
