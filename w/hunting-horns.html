Memories are hunting horns alas
whose note along the wind is dying

Hunting Horns (Cors de chasse)

Guillaume Apollinaire · published 1913

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Cors de chasse was published in Alcools in April 1913. Apollinaire was wounded in the First World War, and two years later he died in the Spanish flu pandemic of 1918.
