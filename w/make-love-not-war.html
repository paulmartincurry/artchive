Make love, not war

Anti-Vietnam War movement · 1965

Several people claimed to have invented it, among them Gershon Legman, Rod McKuen, Penelope and Franklin Rosemont, Tor Faegre and Diane Newell Meyer, a senior at the University of Oregon in 1965. The earliest uses in print come from anti-war protests in Berkeley.
