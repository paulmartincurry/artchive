Behold a pale horse:
and his name that sat on him was Death.

Revelation 6:8

King James Bible (Book of Revelation) · ca. 95 CE (trans. 1611)

The Book of Revelation was written in Koine Greek. Tradition gives it to John the Apostle, but who wrote it is still debated. One of the early copies of this passage is a papyrus of the fourth century that carries verses 5 to 8 of the chapter. The English is that of the King James Version, which was commissioned in 1604 and published in 1611 and took the Greek text known as the Textus Receptus as its basis for these books.
