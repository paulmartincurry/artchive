Charlotte Brontë · 1847

Charlotte Brontë sent the manuscript to Smith, Elder and Co. in August 1847, and the firm published it soon afterwards. It was an immediate commercial success. When Wuthering Heights and Agnes Grey followed under the other Bell names, critics began to suspect that Currer Bell was a woman and to call the writing coarse, but sales kept growing. She made the drawings for the second edition herself.
