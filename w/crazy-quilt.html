Crazy Quilt

Louisa Joiner · c. 1887–1900 · silk, ribbons, lace, velvet, embroidered, heat transfer · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Louisa Joiner was married to a captain who sailed on the Great Lakes, and some of the pieces in this quilt are thought to be scraps cut from clothing worn on lake cruises. It was made in Watertown, New York, and she put her initials on a panel at lower right. The quilt is close to two metres square. She was in her forties or fifties when she made it.
