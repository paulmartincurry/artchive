Remember that thou art an actor in a play, of such a kind as the teacher (author) may choose; if short, of a short one; if long, of a long one: if he wishes you to act the part of a poor man, see that you act the part naturally; if the part of a lame man, of a magistrate, of a private person, (do the same).

The Encheiridion — Remember that thou art an actor in a play (XVII)

Epictetus (compiled by Arrian) · c. 125 CE

Translated by George Long

The division of the handbook into fifty-three chapters was first adopted by Johann Schweighäuser in his edition of 1798. Earlier editions tended to divide the text into more chapters.
