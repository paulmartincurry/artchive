The Death of the Virgin

Caravaggio · c. 1604–1606 · Oil on canvas · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

A papal lawyer, Laerzio Cherubini, commissioned the canvas for his chapel in a Carmelite church in Rome, and the parish rejected it as unfit. On the recommendation of Peter Paul Rubens, the Duke of Mantua bought it. The duke's collection was sold to Charles I of England in 1627, and in 1671 a later owner sold the painting to Louis XIV.
