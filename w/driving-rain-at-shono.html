Driving Rain at Shōno (Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1833 · Color woodblock print · 38 × 25.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Three separate blocks were cut to print the three greys of the bamboo thickets behind the running travellers. The publisher's name, Takenouchi, and part of the series title are written on one traveller's umbrella. Shōno was a minor way station on the Tōkaidō, the largely coastal road from Kyoto to Edo.
