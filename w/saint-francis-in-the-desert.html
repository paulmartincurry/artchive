Saint Francis in the Desert

Giovanni Bellini · c. 1475–1480 · Oil on panel · The Frick Collection, New York

Free to use

Zuan Michiel commissioned the panel from Giovanni Bellini. It might have left Venice for the first time at some point between 1796 and 1852, the year it was offered for sale at Christie's. Henry Clay Frick bought it in 1915 for $170,000 and hung it in his living room; he had little interest in religious paintings but valued this one for its landscape.
