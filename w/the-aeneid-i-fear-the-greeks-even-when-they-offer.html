The Aeneid — I Fear the Greeks Even When They Offer Gifts (Book II)

Virgil · 29–19 BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail

The speaker is Laocoön, a Trojan priest, and the gift is the wooden horse. Straight after the warning he throws a spear into its side. Soon afterwards two serpents come out of the sea and kill his sons and then him, and the Trojans take his death as a sign and wheel the horse into the city. The Latin is timeo Danaos et dona ferentes.
