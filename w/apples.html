Apples

Henri Matisse · 1916 · Oil on canvas · 116.5 × 89.5 cm (45 7/8 × 35 3/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Matisse painted this still life twice: this version and a more traditionally representational one. He was forty-five when the First World War broke out and was rejected for military service. He sold the canvas to the Galerie Bernheim-Jeune in Paris on 13 November 1916. John Quinn of New York bought it after December 1919, and it was given to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1948.
