The Pillow Book (opening)

Sei Shōnagon · ca. 1002 CE

Translated by Ivan Morris · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Sei Shōnagon kept these notes while she was a court lady to the Empress Teishi, and finished the book in the year 1002. She meant it to be private. Part of it reached the court when she left it on a cushion put out for a visitor, who carried it off. It is written almost entirely in hiragana, the Japanese syllabic script that women of the court used, at a time when men typically wrote in Chinese.
