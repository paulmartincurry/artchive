The Artchive
I have come three thousand miles away. Sad now with autumn
And with my hundred years of woe, I climb this height alone.
Ill fortune has laid a bitter frost on my temples,
Heart-ache and weariness are a thick dust in my wine.

A Long Climb

Du Fu · before 770; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Du Fu was a poet and a government official. The An Lushan rebellion, which began in 755, drove him and his family from home, and much of what he wrote afterwards came out of it. He died in 770; accounts of where and how differ.
