The Tale of Genji (opening)

Murasaki Shikibu · ca. 1008 CE

Translated by Royall Tyler · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The original manuscript no longer exists; more than 300 later copies do, of varying reliability. Murasaki Shikibu probably began the tale while she was married or soon after she was widowed, and may have delivered it a chapter at a time to the women of the court. She wrote it mostly in the Japanese phonetic script, at a time when writing in Chinese characters was a pursuit for men. The story runs to fifty-four chapters and some four hundred characters, almost none of whom is given a name.
