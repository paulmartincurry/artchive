Comte de Lautréamont (Isidore Ducasse) · 1870

In April and June 1870 Ducasse published two small brochures, Poésies I and II, this time under his real name instead of his pseudonym. They were meant as the preface to a planned book that would sing of good, which remained a fragment. He died that November at the age of 24.
