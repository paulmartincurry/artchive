Umbrella Movement, Harcourt Road

Studio Kanu · 28 October 2014

Free to use with credit. Photo: Studio Kanu, CC BY-SA 2.0

On the thirty-first day of the occupation, Hong Kong protesters held up their umbrellas on Harcourt Road while singing. Volunteers in the camp there worked in shifts on rubbish, recycling, security and medical care.
