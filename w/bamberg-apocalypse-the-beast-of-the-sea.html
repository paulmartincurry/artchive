Bamberg Apocalypse: The Beast of the Sea (folio 32v)

Maker unknown · c. 1000–1020 · Illuminated manuscript · Page 29.5 × 20.5 cm · Staatsbibliothek Bamberg

Free to use with credit. Photo: Staatsbibliothek Bamberg, CC BY-SA 4.0

Henry II and his wife Cunigunde gave the book to the Collegiate Abbey of St. Stephan at Bamberg. It has 106 folios and 57 gilded miniatures, and it is the only illustrated Ottonian Apocalypse manuscript still in existence.
