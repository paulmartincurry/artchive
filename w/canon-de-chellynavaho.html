Cañon de Chelly—Navaho (The North American Indian, Portfolio I, Plate 28)

Edward S. Curtis · 1904 · Photogravure · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is plate 28 of the first portfolio of The North American Indian. The money for the fieldwork came from J. P. Morgan, who was to receive 25 sets and 500 original prints in repayment; in the end 222 complete sets were published. Over the project Curtis took more than 40,000 photographs of members of over 80 tribes and made over 10,000 wax cylinder recordings of language and music.
