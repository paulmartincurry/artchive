Journey of the Magi

T. S. Eliot · 1927

Eliot was baptised into the Anglican faith on 29 June 1927 at Finstock in Oxfordshire. Two months later Faber and Gwyer issued this poem as the eighth pamphlet in their Ariel series, with illustrations by the American-born artist Edward McKnight Kauffer. It was the first of five Ariel poems Eliot wrote, and it works in quotations from Lancelot Andrewes.
