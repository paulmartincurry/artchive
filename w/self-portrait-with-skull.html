Self-Portrait with Skull (Autorretrato con calavera)

Saturnino Herrán · ca. 1918 · Charcoal and Colored Crayon on Toned Paper · Private collection

Free to use

Saturnino Herrán died in Mexico City on 8 October 1918, at the age of thirty-one, and this drawing is dated to about that year. His father died when he was sixteen, and after the move to Mexico City he worked in a telegraph office to support his mother while taking classes at the Academy of San Carlos. In 1909 he became a professor of drawing.
