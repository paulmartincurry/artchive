In the Mountains of Tōtōmi Province (Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · c. 1830–33 · Color woodblock print; oban · 25.9 × 37.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hokusai was in his seventies when he made the Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, between about 1830 and 1832. The publisher, Nishimura Yohachi, planned more than a hundred prints, but the series stopped at forty-six. Clarence Buckingham bought this impression from Yamanaka & Company in December 1905, and his sisters gave it to the Art Institute in 1925.
