The Love Letter

Jean-Honoré Fragonard · early 1770s · Oil on canvas · 32 3/4 × 26 3/8 in. (83.2 × 67 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Fragonard produced more than 550 paintings and dated only five of them. He was a glover's son from Grasse who had difficulty completing official commissions and painted mostly for private patrons or for himself. In October 1773 he left Paris for a second trip to Italy and did not return until the following September. The canvas is about 83 centimetres tall and came to the museum in 1949 with the Jules Bache Collection.
