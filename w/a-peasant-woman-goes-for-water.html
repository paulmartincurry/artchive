A Peasant Woman Goes for Water

Kazimir Malevich · 1913 · Lithograph in black on wove paper · Sheet 13 × 12 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The sheet is 13 by 12 centimetres. Malevich wrote his name and the title in Russian across the bottom of the stone. He made the lithograph in 1913, two years before he introduced Suprematism.
