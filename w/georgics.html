let the country
and the flowing streams in the valleys please me,
let me love the rivers and the woods, unknown.

Georgics (from Book II)

Virgil · c. 29 BCE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Virgil composed the Georgics against a backdrop of almost fifteen years of civil war in Rome. For the farming he relied on Varro's Res rusticae, which had already been published. According to Suetonius, he recited the poem to Octavian in 29 BCE, when he was about forty.
