Earth-worms, though in appearance a small and despicable link in the chain of nature, yet, if lost, would make a lamentable chasm.

The Natural History of Selborne (Letter on Earth-worms)

Gilbert White · 1777; published 1789

White was a country curate in Selborne, the Hampshire village where he was born. His family moved into a house there, The Wakes, when he was about eight, and it stayed his home for life. The book is set out as 110 letters to two naturalists, Thomas Pennant and Daines Barrington, though many were never posted and were written for the book. His brother Benjamin published it in 1789, four years before White's death. Charles Darwin's 1881 book on earthworms carries no footnote to White's earlier work on them.
