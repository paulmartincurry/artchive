The Artchive
I am the Empire in the last of its decline,
That sees the tall, fair-haired Barbarians pass,—the while
Composing indolent acrostics, in a style
Of gold, with languid sunshine dancing in each line.

Langueur

Paul Verlaine · 1884

Translated by Gertrude Hall

Verlaine composed the collection this sonnet belongs to during his prison sentence, and it was published in 1884. It was not until that year that Maurice Barrès referred to a particular group of writers as Decadents. In his novel of the same year, Joris-Karl Huysmans named Verlaine among the likely candidates for the core of the movement.
