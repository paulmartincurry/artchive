Battleship Potemkin, frame at 1:09:08

Sergei Eisenstein · 1925 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The real Potemkin was derelict and being scrapped when the film was made. The footage most closely matches the battleship Rostislav, scuttled in 1920 but with its superstructure still above water, and the interiors were filmed on the cruiser Komintern. The red flag would have shown black on the film stock of the time, so a white one was flown and Eisenstein tinted it red by hand in 108 frames. The film was banned in Britain until 1954.
