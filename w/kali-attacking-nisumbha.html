Kali Attacking Nisumbha

Maker unknown · c. 1740 · Gum tempera and gold on paper · 22 × 33 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Cleveland bought this painting in 1968 with the Edward L. Whittemore Fund, and showed it in its Sacred India exhibition in 1985. The museum describes the demon as eight-armed, spotted and green, and Kali's trident as impossibly long. His chariot horses and two attendants lie beneath them. The painted image is 22 by 33 centimetres.
