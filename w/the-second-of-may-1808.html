The Second of May 1808 (The Charge of the Mamelukes)

Francisco de Goya · 1814 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado

Free to use

The council governing Spain commissioned this canvas and its companion, The Third of May 1808, in 1814, after Napoleon's army had been driven out, and Goya finished both within two months. He was probably not present at the charge it shows. The returning king, Ferdinand VII, was not pleased, and the pair were not hung in public for many years. In 1936 the truck evacuating it from the Prado had an accident that tore the canvas and left pieces missing. It was restored in 1941 and again between 2007 and 2008.
