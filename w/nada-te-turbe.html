Let nothing disturb thee,
Nothing affright thee;
All things are passing;
God never changeth;

Nada te turbe

Saint Teresa of Ávila · ca. 1570 (trans. H.W. Longfellow)

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

Teresa of Ávila was a Carmelite nun who had entered the convent in her home town in 1534, aged twenty. By about 1570 she was in her fifties and reforming the Carmelite orders of both women and men. The Spanish Inquisition took an interest in her reforms, but no charges were laid against her. She was canonized in 1622, forty years after her death.
