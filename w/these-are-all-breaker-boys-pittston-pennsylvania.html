These Are All Breaker-Boys, Pittston, Pennsylvania

Lewis Hine · January 1911 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Breaker boys sat over the chutes and conveyor belts of a coal breaker for ten hours a day, six days a week, picking slate out of the coal. They worked without gloves. Pennsylvania had outlawed the work for anyone under 12 in 1885. Lewis Hine noted on his caption in January 1911 that these boys were very suspicious of his motives, and that several claimed to be fourteen and did not look it.
