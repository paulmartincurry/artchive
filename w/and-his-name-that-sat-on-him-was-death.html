And His Name That Sat on Him Was Death (The Apocalypse of Saint John)

Odilon Redon · 1899 · Lithograph · Image: 31 × 22.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This comes from the last of Redon's lithographic portfolios, twelve prints on the Book of Revelation issued in 1899 in an edition of 100. Blanchard printed it and the dealer Ambroise Vollard published it, about a year after he began selling Redon's work. It is one of the last things Redon made in black alone.
