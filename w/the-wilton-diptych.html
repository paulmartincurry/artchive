The Wilton Diptych (right panel)

Maker unknown · c. 1395–99 · Tempera on oak panel · 36.8 × 26.7 cm · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The diptych was painted for King Richard II of England, who kneels on the other panel before the Virgin and Child. The painter is unknown, probably French or English. It is named after Wilton House in Wiltshire, where the Earls of Pembroke kept it until the National Gallery bought it in 1929.
