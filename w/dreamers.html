Soldiers are dreamers; when the guns begin
They think of firelit homes, clean beds, and wives.

Dreamers

Siegfried Sassoon · 1918

In July 1917 Sassoon made a lone protest against the continuation of the war with his Soldier's Declaration, which resulted in his being sent to the Craiglockhart War Hospital. There he met Wilfred Owen.
