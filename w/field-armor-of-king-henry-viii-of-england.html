Field Armor of King Henry VIII of England

Italian, Milan or Brescia · ca. 1544 · Steel, gold, textile, leather · H. 72 1/2 in., W. 33 in., D. 14 1/2 in. · The Met

Free to use

The armour was made in Italy, in Milan or Brescia, about three years before the end of Henry VIII's reign in 1547. Mounted, it stands 184.2 centimetres high and weighs 22.91 kilograms. The Metropolitan Museum bought it through the Harris Brisbane Dick Fund in 1932.
