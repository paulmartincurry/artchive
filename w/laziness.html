Laziness

Félix Vallotton · 1896 · Woodcut printed in black on ivory wove paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

By the middle of the nineteenth century the woodcut had fallen out of use among artists. Vallotton designed his blocks, carved them and printed from them himself, in black only. Commissions for illustrations from newspapers and magazines between 1893 and 1897 made him financially secure, and after 1901 he made few prints and turned to painting.
