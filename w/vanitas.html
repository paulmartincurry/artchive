Vanitas

Willem Claesz. Heda · 1628 · Oil on panel · Museum Bredius, The Hague

Free to use

Heda was the son of Haarlem's city architect and painted nothing but still life. In 1628, the year on this panel, the Haarlem minister and poet Samuel Ampzing praised him by name in his book on the city, and Heda went on to join the Haarlem Guild of St. Luke. He was about thirty-four. The earliest work of his that is known is a vanitas.
