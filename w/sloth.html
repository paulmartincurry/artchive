Sloth (The Seven Deadly Sins)

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · published 1558 · Engraving on laid paper · Plate: 22.8 × 29.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Hieronymus Cock published this plate in 1558 from his shop in Antwerp, the House of the Four Winds, where prints of many sorts were produced and distributed. The Latin line in the margin says that sluggishness breaks strength and long idleness the nerves.
