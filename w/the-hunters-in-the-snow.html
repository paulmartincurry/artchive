The Hunters in the Snow (Winter)

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1565 · Oil on panel · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

This panel, 117 by 162 centimetres, belongs to a series on the times of the year that Bruegel painted in 1565; five of them survive. It stands for December and January. The jagged peaks across the valley are not found anywhere in the Low Countries. The inn sign, hanging loose from its fittings, reads in Dutch that this is the Golden Hart, with one letter worn away.
