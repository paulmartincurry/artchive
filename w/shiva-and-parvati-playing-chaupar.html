Shiva and Parvati Playing Chaupar (Rasamanjari)

Devidasa of Nurpur · dated 1694–95 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · 16.5 × 27.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Devidasa of Nurpur painted this page, which is dated 1694–95. Basohli, on the right bank of the River Ravi, had been founded by Raja Bhupat Pal in 1635, and by the middle of the next century its painting had grown into the Kangra school. Silver and gold are laid in with the watercolour, and the image is 16.5 by 27.6 centimetres. Dr. J. C. Burnett gave it to the Met in 1957.
