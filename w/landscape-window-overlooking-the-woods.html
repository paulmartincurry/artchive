Landscape: Window Overlooking the Woods

Édouard Vuillard · 1899 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Adam Natanson commissioned this canvas in 1899 for the library of his apartment at 85 rue Jouffroy in Paris. It is nearly four metres wide. After his death in 1906 it passed to his son Thadée, whose sale in 1908 sold it to Alexandre Natanson for 2,600 francs. From 1910 to 1929 it was on loan to Léon Blum, who bought it at the Alexandre Natanson sale in 1929.
