Sorrowing Old Man ('At Eternity's Gate')

Vincent van Gogh · May 1890 · Oil on canvas · 81.8 × 65.5 cm · Kröller-Müller Museum, Otterlo

Free to use

Van Gogh painted this at the asylum in Saint-Rémy in early May 1890, after a relapse that had lasted some nine weeks. He was reworking a lithograph he had made in 1882 from studies of Adrianus Jacobus Zuyderland, a pensioner and war veteran he drew at an almshouse in The Hague. For the new version he probably used the asylum's own chair and stove, and a patient may have posed. He died about two months later.
