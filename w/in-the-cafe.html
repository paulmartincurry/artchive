In the Café

Fernand Lungren · 1882–84 · Oil on canvas · 79.7 × 104.8 cm (31 3/8 × 41 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Fernand Lungren went to Paris in 1882 and soon left classes for direct observation of the city. Café culture gave women new chances for leisure in public, which is part of what this lone woman stands for.
