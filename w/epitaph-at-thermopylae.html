Go tell the Spartans, passerby,
That here, obedient to their laws, we lie.

Epitaph at Thermopylae

Attributed to Simonides of Ceos · 480 BCE

Translated by traditional English version, as given by Paul Cartledge · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This epitaph commemorates the Spartans who died at the Battle of Thermopylae. After the Greek states defeated the second Persian invasion, Simonides was in great demand among them and composed epitaphs for Athenians, Spartans and Corinthians. By the dates most scholars accept he was then in his seventies. As an author of inscriptions he was the first major poet to compose verses to be read rather than recited.
