Wake Up, America! Civilization Calls Every Man, Woman and Child!

James Montgomery Flagg · 1917 · Poster · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

The poster was made for Wake Up, America Day in New York on 19 April 1917, thirteen days after the United States entered the war. The actress Mary Arthur posed for Columbia, asleep in a Phrygian cap against a fluted column while storm clouds gather.
