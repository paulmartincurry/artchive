Reliquary Figure (Kota peoples, Mbete group)

Maker unknown · 19th century · Wood, pigment, metal, cowrie shells · 81.9 × 19.1 × 18.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kota reliquary guardian figures belong to a religious order known as Bwete. As people in Gabon converted to Christianity in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, missionaries and colonial officials began to collect them, and most are now in museums in Europe and North America. This one, by a Mbete-Kota artist in wood with metal and cowrie shells, is 81.9 centimetres tall and came to the Met in 2002 from the Pierre and Maria-Gaetana Matisse Collection.
