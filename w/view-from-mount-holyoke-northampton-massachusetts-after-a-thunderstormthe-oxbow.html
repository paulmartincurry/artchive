View from Mount Holyoke, Northampton, Massachusetts, after a Thunderstorm—The Oxbow

Thomas Cole · 1836 · Oil on canvas · 51 1/2 × 76 in. (130.8 × 193 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Cole made this view for the National Academy of Design exhibition of April 1836, at his patron Luman Reed's suggestion, as a break from The Course of Empire, a five-painting series that was going slowly. He stitched together two separate views from Mount Holyoke and put himself in, small, at his easel on the rocks. The canvas is 130.8 by 193 centimetres. He sold it at the exhibition to Charles Nicoll Talbot, a China-trade merchant; Olivia Sage gave it to the Met in 1908.
