The Burghers of Calais

Auguste Rodin · 1884–1889 · Bronze

Free to use

Calais had been trying since 1845 to put up a statue of one man, Eustache de Saint Pierre; one sculptor died first and the Franco-Prussian War stopped another. Rodin was invited to submit in 1884 and gave the city all six burghers instead. He wanted the group at ground level, but in 1895 it was installed on a large pedestal against his wishes, and only later moved to a much lower base in front of the town hall. French law allows no more than 12 original casts of a Rodin; the twelfth of this group was cast in 1995.
