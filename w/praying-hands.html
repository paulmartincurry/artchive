Praying Hands (Study on Blue Venetian Paper)

Albrecht Dürer · 1508 · Ink on paper · Albertina, Vienna

Free to use

Albrecht Dürer made the blue paper himself. The sheet, 29.1 by 19.7 centimetres, once also carried a sketch of the apostle's head, which has since been separated from it. The hands match those of an apostle in the central panel of the Heller Altarpiece in Frankfurt, a panel destroyed by fire in 1729. The drawing was first exhibited in 1871, in Vienna.
