Stag at Sharkey's

George Bellows · 1909 · Oil on canvas · Unframed: 92 × 122.6 cm (36 1/4 × 48 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Sharkey's Athletic Club was a saloon with a boxing ring in the back room, near Bellows's studio, run by the former fighter and Navy man Tom Sharkey. Public boxing was illegal in New York then, so bouts were private and open only to club members; an outsider who fought was given temporary membership and called a stag. On the back of the canvas Bellows wrote his address on East 19th Street and the title Club Night. The museum bought it from the dealer Marie Sterner in 1922, while he was still alive.
