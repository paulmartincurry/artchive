Tao Yuanming Returning Home

Chen Hongshou · 1650 · Ink and colors on silk · Honolulu Museum of Art

Free to use

Chen Hongshou painted this two years before his death. In 1645 he had passed the government examinations and won official status, only to flee the advancing Manchus, and the next year he became a Buddhist monk. His subject, the poet Tao Yuanming, had served in government in five stints over more than ten years before withdrawing to farm in the countryside.
