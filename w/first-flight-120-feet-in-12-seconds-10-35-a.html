First Flight, 120 Feet in 12 Seconds, 10:35 a.m., Kitty Hawk, North Carolina

Orville Wright and John T. Daniels · 17 December 1903 · Glass negative, dry plate · 18 × 12.5 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Orville Wright set up the camera and had John T. Daniels of the local life-saving station squeeze the rubber bulb that tripped the shutter. Daniels had never seen a camera before and was not sure afterwards that he had squeezed it. The camera was a Gundlach Korona V, which took 5-by-7-inch glass-plate negatives; this is the original plate.
