Study for Bathers at Asnières

Georges Seurat · c. 1883–84 · oil on wood panel · Unframed: 15.7 × 24.9 cm (6 3/16 × 9 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The panel is about 16 by 25 centimetres, one of many oil sketches Seurat made for Bathers at Asnières, which he then painted in the studio rather than outdoors. The Paris Salon rejected the big painting, and he showed it instead at the Groupe des Artistes Indépendants in May 1884. After his death this sketch went to the painter Théo van Rysselberghe and later belonged to the critic Félix Fénéon.
