Book of Kells: Christ Enthroned (folio 32v)

Maker unknown · ca. 800 · Vellum · Trinity College Dublin

Free to use

The page is calf vellum, lettered in iron gall ink, and it is one of ten full-page illustrations in the book. At least three scribes wrote the text. In 1007 the book was stolen from the church at Kells for its gold and jewelled cover and found a few months later under a sod, without the cover. It stayed at Kells until 1654, when Cromwell's cavalry was quartered in the church and the governor of the town sent it to Dublin. Trinity College has had it since 1661.
