Germany's Children are Hungry! (Deutschlands Kinder Hungern!)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1924 · Lithograph · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Kollwitz was fifty-six or fifty-seven in 1924, the year she finished three posters: Germany's Children Starving, Bread, and Never Again War. This lithograph came to the National Gallery of Art with the Rosenwald Collection.
