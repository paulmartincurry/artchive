Tughra (Imperial Insignia) of Sultan Süleiman the Magnificent, Met 38.149.2

Ottoman court workshop · ca. 1555–60 · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · Tughra: H. 20 1/2 in. (52.1 cm), W. 23 1/2 in. (59.7cm) · The Met

Free to use

The tughra is 52.1 centimetres high and 59.7 wide, and is thought to have been made in Istanbul late in the reign of Süleiman, who ruled from 1520 to 1566. The Met bought it in 1938 through the Rogers Fund.
