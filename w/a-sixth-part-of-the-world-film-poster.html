A Sixth Part of the World, film poster

Vladimir and Georgii Stenberg · 1926 · Poster (lithograph)

Free to use

Dziga Vertov built A Sixth Part of the World from sequences filmed by eight teams of his kinoks on their trips to remote parts of the Soviet Union, and it was released in 1926. The Stenberg brothers, sons of a Swedish painter and a Latvian mother, were born in Moscow but stayed Swedish citizens until 1933. In nine years of working together they produced some 300 posters.
