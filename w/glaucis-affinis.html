Glaucis affinis (A Monograph of the Trochilidae, Volume 1, Plate 7)

John Gould & Henry Constantine Richter · 1849–61 · Coloured lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

The birds are drawn at life size, from specimens that came from Bogotá, according to Gould's text for the plate. The lettering credits the drawing and the lithography to Gould and Henry Constantine Richter, who was born in Brompton, London, in 1821 into a family of artists; his grandfather, an engraver, had come to England from Dresden.
