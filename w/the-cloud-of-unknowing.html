For at the first time when thou dost it, thou findest but a darkness; and as it were a cloud of unknowing, thou knowest not what, saving that thou feelest in thy will a naked intent unto God.

The Cloud of Unknowing (Chapter 3)

Anonymous English Mystic · late 14th century

The author of this book is unknown; the English mystic Walter Hilton has been suggested, but that is generally doubted. It was written in Middle English in the second half of the fourteenth century and survives in only seventeen manuscripts. A Carthusian at Mount Grace in Yorkshire, Richard Methley, finished a Latin translation in 1491, but the English text was not printed until 1877.
