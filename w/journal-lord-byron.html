The Artchive
… and neither the music of the shepherd, the crashing of the avalanche, nor the torrent, the mountain, the glacier, the forest, nor the cloud, have for one moment lightened the weight upon my heart, nor enabled me to lose my own wretched identity in the majesty, and the power, and the glory, around, above, and beneath me.

Journal (29 September 1816)

Lord Byron · September 29, 1816

Byron's separation from his wife was made legal in March 1816, and in April he left England, never to return. In the summer of 1816 he settled at the Villa Diodati by Lake Geneva. Thomas Moore printed extracts from the journal Byron began on September 18, 1816; this is the end of the last one.
