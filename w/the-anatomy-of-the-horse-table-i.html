The Anatomy of the Horse, Table I

George Stubbs · 1766 · Etching · 46.4 × 58.4 cm (volume) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

George Stubbs rented a farmhouse at Horkstow in Lincolnshire in 1756 and spent eighteen months dissecting horses there, helped by his common-law wife, Mary Spencer. He moved to London in about 1759 and published the book in 1766; his original drawings are now at the Royal Academy. Aristocratic patrons who saw the drawings before publication began commissioning horse paintings from him.
