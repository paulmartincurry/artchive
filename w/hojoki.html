The flow of the river is ceaseless and its water is never the same.

Hōjōki (opening)

Kamo no Chōmei · 1212 CE

Translated by Donald Keene · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The hut of the title was ten feet square and seven feet high, and Kamo no Chōmei built it when he was sixty. He came from a line of Shinto priests and had been passed over for his father's post at the Kamo shrine. He worked in the Imperial Poetry Office until 1204, spent five years at Ohara, then moved to Hino, in the hills southeast of the capital. He wrote this account there in March 1212.
