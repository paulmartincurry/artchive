A Book of Verses underneath the Bough,
A Jug of Wine, a Loaf of Bread--and Thou
Beside me singing in the Wilderness--
Oh, Wilderness were Paradise enow!

Rubáiyát — A Book of Verses underneath the Bough (XII)

Omar Khayyam (trans. Edward FitzGerald) · 12th century; FitzGerald's fifth edition

FitzGerald studied Persian literature from 1853 with Edward Byles Cowell, who in March 1857 found a set of Persian quatrains by Omar Khayyám in the Asiatic Society library in Calcutta and sent them to him. On 15 January 1859 the translation appeared as an anonymous pamphlet. The publisher let it sink to a fourpenny box on the bookstalls, or even, as he later boasted, a penny one.
