Ten Assassinations for a Penny

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1897 · Lithograph · Sheet: 60.6 x 46.5 cm; Image: 60.6 x 46.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This print was reproduced in La Feuille, an anarchist paper edited by Zo d'Axa, where Steinlen's drawings answered the articles. A French law of 1881 granting freedom of the press had led to many magazines of humour and social criticism. This impression is numbered 41 of 50.
