The Night Revels of Han Xizai

After Gu Hongzhong · ca. 960s Design / 12th-Century Song Handscroll · Silk · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

By the reported account, the ruler Li Yu sent two court painters, Gu Hongzhong and Zhou Wenju, to one of his minister Han Xizai's night parties to paint what they saw. Zhou's painting was later lost. Gu's survives in two copies, this one from the Song dynasty and a Ming replica in Chongqing. The scroll is 28.7 centimetres high and more than three metres long, read from right to left in five scenes with more than forty people.
