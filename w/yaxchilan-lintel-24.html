Yaxchilan Lintel 24

Mo' Chaak · c. 723–726 · Limestone · British Museum, London

Free to use with credit. Photo: Michel wal, CC BY-SA 3.0

The lintel shows the ruler Itzamnaaj Bahlam III, known as Shield Jaguar, holding a torch above Lady K'abal Xoc as she pulls a rope through her tongue in a bloodletting rite. It dates to about 723 to 726 and carries, unusually for a Maya monument, the signature of its sculptor, Mo' Chaak. Alfred Maudslay had it cut from a doorway of Structure 23 at Yaxchilan in 1882 and shipped to Britain. A burial attributed to Lady Xoc was later found in the same building with 146 obsidian blades.
