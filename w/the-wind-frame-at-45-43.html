Although it was finished by the summer of 1927, MGM held the film back until 23 November 1928, a year after The Jazz Singer. It went out with a recorded score and sound effects but no audible dialogue, and it lost 87,000 dollars. In the novel the heroine wanders into a windstorm to die; the film's happier ending was used against the wishes of both Gish and Sjöström.
