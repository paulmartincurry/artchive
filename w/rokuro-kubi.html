Rokuro-Kubi

Lafcadio Hearn · 1904

Japanese tales tell of two kinds of rokurokubi, one whose neck stretches and one whose head comes off and flies about, called nukekubi. Hearn's are the second kind, posing as a family of woodcutters. Kwaidan was published by Houghton Mifflin on 2 April 1904, with pictures by Keishū Takénouchi.
