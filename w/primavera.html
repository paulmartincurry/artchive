Primavera

Sandro Botticelli · 1480 · Panel, plaster, tempera · Uffizi Gallery

Free to use

An inventory of 1499 records this panel in the Florence palace of Lorenzo di Pierfrancesco de' Medici, hanging above a large piece of furniture with its bottom edge at about eye level. Most scholars now connect it to his marriage on 19 July 1482. Botticelli painted some 500 identified species of plant into it, including about 190 different flowers. A second inventory in 1503 notes a large white frame. The name Primavera comes from Giorgio Vasari, who saw the painting at the Medici villa at Castello by 1550.
