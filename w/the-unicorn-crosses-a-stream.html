The Unicorn Crosses a Stream (from the Unicorn Tapestries)

Maker unknown · 1495–1505 · Wool warp with wool, silk, silver, and gilt wefts · 368.3 × 426.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

During the French Revolution the tapestries were looted from the Château de Verteuil and reportedly used to cover potatoes. They were rediscovered in a barn in the 1850s.
