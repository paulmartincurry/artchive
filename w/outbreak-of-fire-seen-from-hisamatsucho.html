Outbreak of Fire Seen from Hisamatsuchō

Kobayashi Kiyochika · 1881 · Color woodblock print; oban · 24.5 × 36.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

On 26 January 1881 Kiyochika's own house burned down in the great fire at Ryōgoku while he was out sketching. Two weeks later, on 11 February, he sketched another great fire at Hisamatsu-chō, and the drawings became this print. He was thirty-three. His specialty was night scenes lit from sources inside the picture.
