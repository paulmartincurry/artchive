My head is bloody, but unbowed. … I am the captain of my soul.

Invictus

William Ernest Henley · 1875

At sixteen William Ernest Henley had his left leg amputated below the knee, after complications of tuberculosis. Told later that the other leg needed the same operation, he went to Edinburgh in August 1873, where the surgeon Joseph Lister saved it after several operations on the foot. He wrote these verses in 1875, while recovering in the infirmary. They were published in 1888 with no title. The editor Arthur Quiller-Couch added the title in 1900, for the Oxford Book of English Verse.
