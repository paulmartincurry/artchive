The great revelation had never come. The great revelation perhaps never did come. Instead there were little daily miracles, illuminations, matches struck unexpectedly in the dark; here was one.

To the Lighthouse — Little Daily Miracles (The Lighthouse, 3)

Virginia Woolf · 1927

The house in the novel was modelled on Talland House in St Ives, Cornwall, which Woolf's father rented for family summers from 1882. Her mother died when she was thirteen. The Hogarth Press printed 3,000 copies in May 1927, in light blue cloth with gold lettering and a dust cover by her sister Vanessa Bell. With the early sales Woolf bought a car.
