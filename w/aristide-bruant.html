Aristide Bruant

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1893 · Color lithograph poster on tan wove paper, laid down on white wove Japan tissue · Image: 81.5 × 58.5 cm; Primary support: 81.7 × 60.8 cm; Secondary support: 83.4 × 62.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Bruant opened his own Montmartre club, Le Mirliton, in 1885, and Toulouse-Lautrec began showing his work there the same year. Bruant also published a magazine called Le Mirliton that printed Lautrec's illustrations. On stage he wore a red shirt, a black velvet jacket, high boots and a long red scarf.
