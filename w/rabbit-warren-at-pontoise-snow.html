Rabbit Warren at Pontoise, Snow

Camille Pissarro · 1879 · Oil on canvas · 59.2 × 72.3 cm (23 5/16 × 28 7/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Pissarro painted nearly 100 snow scenes. France had an unusually severe winter in 1879, and he painted this one at his home in Pontoise, 30 miles west of Paris.
