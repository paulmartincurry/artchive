The Penitent Magdalen

Georges de La Tour · ca. 1640 · Oil on canvas · 52 1/2 × 40 1/4 in. (133.4 × 102.2 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The canvas is not dated. Georges de La Tour, a baker's son from Lorraine, painted several candlelit versions of Mary Magdalene, and his surviving output is small. Around the time of this one he was about forty-seven, had been named Painter to the King of France in 1638, and is not recorded in his home town of Lunéville between 1639 and 1642. After his death in an epidemic in 1652 his work was forgotten until a German art historian rediscovered it in 1915.
