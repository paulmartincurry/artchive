Blow, winds, and crack your cheeks! rage! blow!
You cataracts and hurricanoes, spout!

King Lear (Act III, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · 1606

Only one performance during Shakespeare's lifetime is known, before the court of King James I at Whitehall on 26 December 1606. In 1681 Nahum Tate produced a version that left out the Fool and let Lear and Cordelia survive, and it displaced Shakespeare's text from the professional stage until 1838. London's two professional theatres did not perform the play at all from 1811 to 1820, during the madness of George III.
