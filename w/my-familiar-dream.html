Her eyes are the same as a statue’s eyes,
And in her voice, distant, serious, mild,
The tone of dear voices, those that have died.

My Familiar Dream (Mon rêve familier)

Paul Verlaine · 1866

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Verlaine published the sonnet in 1866, at 22, in his first collection, Poèmes saturniens. It is the sixth poem of the book's opening section, Melancholia.
