American Landscape

Edward Hopper · 1920 · Etching · Plate 18.5 × 31.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This etching dates from 1920, the year Hopper had his first one-man exhibition, at the Whitney Studio Club in January. The plate is 18.5 by 31.3 centimetres. Lessing J. Rosenwald owned this impression, and the National Gallery of Art acquired it in 1949.
