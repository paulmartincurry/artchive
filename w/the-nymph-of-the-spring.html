The Nymph of the Spring

Lucas Cranach the Elder · after 1537 · Oil on panel · 48.4 × 72.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Cranach, probably born in 1472, painted this panel after 1537, and he painted nude subjects from mythology throughout his career. The Latin inscription at upper left is written in the voice of the nymph of a sacred spring; beside it is the artist's device, a winged serpent holding a ring in its mouth. Clarence Y. Palitz gave the painting to the gallery, a gift completed in 1958.
