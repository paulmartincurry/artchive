I do, as I say, love these books with all my heart—and I love you too.

First Letter to Elizabeth Barrett, 10 January 1845

Robert Browning · 10 January 1845

It is the first letter Robert Browning wrote to Elizabeth Barrett, postmarked 10 January 1845, from New Cross, Hatcham. They had not yet met; John Kenyon arranged their first meeting on 20 May 1845. They married privately at St Marylebone Parish Church and moved to Italy in September 1846.
