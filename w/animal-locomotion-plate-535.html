Animal Locomotion, Plate 535

Eadweard Muybridge · 1887 · Collotype, from "Animal Locomotion" · Image: 24 × 30.1 cm (9 1/2 × 11 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This plate breaks down the hand movements of someone keeping time for a musical performance, and it is one of five plates in Animal Locomotion given over to hands. The sequences were shot at the University of Pennsylvania between 1884 and 1886. The photographer was born Edward James Muggeridge, and took the first name Eadweard as the old Anglo-Saxon form of Edward. Robert A. Taub gave the print to the Art Institute.
