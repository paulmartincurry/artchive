The Yellow Christ

Paul Gauguin · 1889 · Oil on canvas · Buffalo AKG Art Museum

Gauguin painted this in the Breton village of Pont-Aven in 1889, soon after a short summer trip to Paris to see the Exposition universelle and arrange an exhibition. He had left Arles and Vincent van Gogh the winter before, after little more than two months there. A pencil study for the picture is in the Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum and a watercolour version is in the Art Institute of Chicago. The canvas is 91 centimetres high.
