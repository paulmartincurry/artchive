Battle of Ravana and Jatayu (Burnt Ramayana)

Maker unknown · c. 1605 · Gum tempera and ink on paper · 26.1 × 15.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The page comes from the set known as the Burnt Ramayana, damaged by fire in India decades ago; Sita is now barely visible in the bottom right corner, in Ravana's donkey-drawn chariot. The Sanskrit text of the episode is written on the back. It was made at the court of Raja Bir Singh Deo of Datia, who reigned from 1605 to 1627, and stayed in the Datia palace collection until 1947.
