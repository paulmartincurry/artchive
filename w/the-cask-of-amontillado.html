Nemo me impune lacessit.
… 'For the love of God, Montresor!' 'Yes,' I said, 'for the love of God!'

The Cask of Amontillado

Edgar Allan Poe · 1846

Godey's Lady's Book, at the time the most popular periodical in America, printed this story in its November 1846 issue. It appeared only once more in Poe's lifetime, in the New England Weekly Review that same month. He was thirty-seven.
