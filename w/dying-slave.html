Dying Slave

Michelangelo · 1513–16 · Marble · H. 215 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

Michelangelo carved this figure for the tomb of Pope Julius II, a project that was redesigned several times over decades. By the 1540s the slaves had been dropped from the tomb altogether. Around 1546 he gave this one and the Rebellious Slave to Ruberto Strozzi, in gratitude for hospitality during periods of illness. Both passed to King Francis I and other French owners, and were acquired for the French state during the revolutionary period.
