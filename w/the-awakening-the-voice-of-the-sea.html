The Awakening — The voice of the sea (Ch. 6)

Kate Chopin · 1899

The novel's working title was A Solitary Soul. Kate Chopin was forty-nine, a widow in St. Louis with six children, when Herbert S. Stone published it in April 1899. Her husband had died in 1882 leaving her 2,000 in debt, and she took up writing on the suggestion of her doctor. She never wrote another novel, her next book was cancelled when the publisher ran out of money, and she died five years later. A Norwegian scholar, Per Seyersted, revived her work in the 1960s.
