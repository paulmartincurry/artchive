Could I take me to some cavern for mine hiding,
In the hill-tops where the Sun scarce hath trod;
Or a cloud make the home of mine abiding,
As a bird among the bird-droves of God!

Hippolytus — Could I Take Me to Some Cavern (chorus)

Euripides · 428 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray

Hippolytus won first prize at the City Dionysia in Athens in 428 BC as part of a trilogy. It was Euripides' second play on the story. The first, now lost, is thought to have shown Phaedra offering herself directly to Hippolytus, which may have offended Athenians. To tell them apart, the plays are called Hippolytus Veiled and Hippolytus the Wreath-Bearer, after the garlands he wears as a worshipper of Artemis.
