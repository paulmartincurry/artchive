Portrait of a Rabbit

Yabu Chōsui · 1867 · Color woodblock print; surimono · 24.9 × 18.3 cm (9 13/16 × 7 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A rabbit this big marks the year of the rabbit. Its round body also suggests the bag of Hotei, a god of good fortune, a lopsided moon where the rice-pounding rabbit lives, and the rising sun of New Year.
