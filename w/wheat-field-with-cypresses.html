Wheat Field with Cypresses

Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · Oil on canvas · 28 13/16 × 36 3/4 in. (73.2 × 93.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

In just over a decade Van Gogh made around 860 oil paintings, most of them in the last two years of his life, and this canvas is one of them. Wheat fields had become one of his series in the south of France, alongside olive trees and sunflowers. He was thirty-five or thirty-six. The Met bought the painting, 73.2 by 93.4 centimetres, in 1993 with a gift from the Annenberg Foundation.
