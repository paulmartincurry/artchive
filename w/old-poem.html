Soup and porridge are both cooked,
But there is no one to eat them with.
I went out and looked towards the east,
While tears fell and wetted my clothes.

Old Poem (At Fifteen I Went with the Army)

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · Han dynasty (206 BC–220 AD)

Translated by Arthur Waley

This anonymous ballad, known in English also as The Old Soldier's Return, is counted among the main yuefu of the Han dynasty and the centuries after it. Lines of a yuefu can run to uneven lengths, a trace of the lost folk tunes they were sung to. The word yuefu was not used for this kind of poem until the late fifth century.
