The One remains, the many change and pass;
Heaven’s light forever shines, Earth’s shadows fly;
Life, like a dome of many-coloured glass,
Stains the white radiance of Eternity,

Adonais (stanza LII)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1821

Shelley wrote the 55 Spenserian stanzas of Adonais in the spring of 1821, straight after he heard on 11 April that Keats had died in Rome seven weeks before. Charles Ollier published it that July. Its preface wrongly claimed that rage at harsh reviews had brought on Keats's death.
