Swift Dog Strikes an Enemy

Swift Dog · c. 1880 · Watercolor and ink on paper · 21.1 × 34.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Swift Dog, a Hunkpapa Lakota artist who lived from 1845 to 1925, made this drawing in watercolour and ink around 1880, when he was about thirty-five. Plains men had painted their feats in battle on buffalo hides, and when the buffalo became scarce after the federal government's eradication programmes, they turned to paper, often pages of accounting ledgers from traders, agents and army officers. Battle exploits were the most common subject.
