As soon as, by means of this prayer, the soul has become entirely dead to the world, it comes forth like a lovely little white butterfly!

The Interior Castle — The Silkworm Becomes a Butterfly (Fifth Mansions, ch. 2)

Saint Teresa of Ávila · 1577; English 1912

Translated by Benedictines of Stanbrook, rev. Benedict Zimmerman

Teresa of Ávila wrote The Interior Castle as a guide for the nuns of her Carmelite order, picturing the soul as a castle with seven mansions; this passage is from the fifth. Over twenty years her reforms produced seventeen convents, all but one founded by her. She died in 1582 at Alba de Tormes, and nine months later her coffin was opened and one of her hands was cut off and sent to Ávila.
