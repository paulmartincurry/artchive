He who knows other men is discerning; he who knows himself is intelligent. He who overcomes others is strong; he who overcomes himself is mighty.

Tao Te Ching — He who knows himself (Ch. 33)

Laozi (Lao Tzu) · c. 4th century BCE

Translated by James Legge

In 1973 archaeologists opened a tomb at Mawangdui dated to 168 BCE and found two nearly complete copies of this book written in ink on silk. Both put its two halves in the opposite order to the one handed down, with the Te section first and the Tao second. Twenty years later an older version still was found, written on bamboo slips. More than fifty manuscripts of the text, whole and partial, had already come out of the Mogao Caves near Dunhuang.
