Blind Woman, New York (Camera Work 49/50, plate III)

Paul Strand · 1916, published 1917 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Strand made this photograph in New York in 1916, when he was 25 or 26. For street portraits he often fixed a false brass lens to the side of his camera and shot through a working lens hidden under his arm, so his subjects likely had no idea they were being photographed, a practice some criticised. Alfred Stieglitz published it as a photogravure in the double issue 49/50 of Camera Work in June 1917; he printed only 500 copies of the last two issues, half the usual run.
