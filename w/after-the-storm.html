After the Storm

Caspar David Friedrich · 1817 · Oil on canvas · 22.2 × 30.8 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

The canvas is 22 centimetres high and 31 wide. An old inscription in a hand that is not Friedrich's gives Dresden and 1817, and the museum finds that date convincing. Friedrich grew up in Greifswald near the Baltic Sea and visited the nearby island of Rügen regularly throughout his life. The museum in Copenhagen, the city where he studied from 1794 to 1798, acquired the painting in 2016.
