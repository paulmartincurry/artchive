The Ardabil Carpet

Maqsud of Kashan · 1539–1540 · Wool · Victoria and Albert Museum

Free to use

There were two matching carpets, woven over about four years. A British dealer who bought them worn in 1890 had one restored with sections cut from the other, and the leftover pieces became the smaller carpet now in Los Angeles. William Morris persuaded the museum to raise £2,000 to buy this one in March 1893. It is more than ten metres long and has about 26 million knots. The light on it is kept very low and raised for a short time each hour.
