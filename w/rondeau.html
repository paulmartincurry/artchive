Death, I cry out at your harshness,
That stole my girl away from me,
Yet you’re not satisfied I see
Until I languish in distress.

Rondeau (Le Testament)

François Villon · 1461

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Villon wrote that he spent the summer of 1461 in the bishop's prison at Meung-sur-Loire. The same year he composed his long poem of 2,023 lines that includes sixteen ballades and three rondeaux, of which this is one.
