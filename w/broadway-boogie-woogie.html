Broadway Boogie Woogie

Piet Mondrian · 1942–43 · Oil on canvas · 127 × 127 cm · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Mondrian finished this canvas in New York in 1943, three years after he had left London for Manhattan to get away from the war. He was seventy-one that year. Its lines are built from small blocks of colour, worked out in part with small pieces of coloured paper tape. The Brazilian sculptor Maria Martins bought it for $800 at the Valentine Gallery, where both of them showed work in 1943, and she later gave it to the Museum of Modern Art. Mondrian died of pneumonia in February 1944.
