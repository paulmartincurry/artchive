Alone, alone, all, all alone,
Alone on a wide wide sea!
And never a saint took pity on
My soul in agony.

Alone, Alone, All, All Alone (The Rime of the Ancient Mariner, Part IV)

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1798

Five years before the date of this poem, in December 1793, Coleridge walked out of Jesus College, Cambridge, and enlisted in the 15th Light Dragoons under a false name. His brothers arranged his discharge a few months later. He went back to the college but never received a degree. He was the youngest of ten children of a Devon vicar, and had been sent to a London charity school at the age of eight, after his father's death.
