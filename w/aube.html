Aube (Illuminations)

Arthur Rimbaud · c. 1873–1875

Rimbaud wrote most of the prose poems of Illuminations during his stay in the United Kingdom with Paul Verlaine, and in February 1875 he gave the manuscript to Verlaine. They were first published in 1886 in La Vogue, a Paris literary review. By then Rimbaud was earning his living as a trader in the Horn of Africa, and he was never personally involved in the publication.
