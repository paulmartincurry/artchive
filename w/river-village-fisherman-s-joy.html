River Village: Fisherman's Joy

Zhao Mengfu · c. 1302–3 · Fan painting mounted as album leaf; ink and color on silk · 28.6 × 30 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Zhao Mengfu was a descendant of the Song imperial family, yet in 1286 he went to serve the Mongol court of Khubilai Khan at Dadu, modern Beijing. He painted this fan, about 29 by 30 centimetres, around 1302 to 1303, when he was in his late forties, and signed it with his courtesy name, Zi'ang. His wife, Guan Daosheng, was also a painter and calligrapher.
