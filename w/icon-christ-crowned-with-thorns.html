Icon: Christ Crowned with Thorns

Maker unknown · After 1700 · Wood, paint, leather, iron · H x W x D: 67 x 81 x 3.4 cm (26 3/8 x 31 7/8 x 1 5/16 in.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

A large icon meant to be seen by many, it was carried in church processions on holy days. The subject, Christ crowned with thorns among his tormentors, follows Ethiopia's famous imperial icon, which the emperor's army carried into battle.
