Orchid and Rock

Ma Shouzhen · dated 1572 · Hanging scroll; ink on paper · The Met

Free to use

Ma Shouzhen was about twenty-four when she painted this scroll. She was a courtesan in Nanjing, living in the entertainment district along the Qinhuai River, and her courtesy name, Xianglan, came from her orchid paintings; buyers came from as far as Thailand. She also wrote plays and was the only woman known to own a theatre troupe in the late Ming period. The painted image is about fifty-three centimetres tall.
