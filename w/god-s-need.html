We say God needeth naught,
For gifts He doth not pine;
If that be true, why wants
He this poor heart of mine?

God's Need (Cherubinic Wanderer III, 123)

Angelus Silesius · 1657; English 1909

Translated by Paul Carus

Scheffler was baptised in Breslau on Christmas Day 1624 and studied medicine at Strasbourg, Leiden and Padua. In Leiden he came to know the writings of Jacob Böhme through Böhme's friend Abraham von Franckenberg. He took vows as a Franciscan in 1661 and was ordained a priest three months later. After his death in 1677 his Protestant opponents spread the false rumour that he had hanged himself.
