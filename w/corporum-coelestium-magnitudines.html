Corporum Coelestium Magnitudines (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Every plate in the atlas was engraved on copper, but only two of the engravers who worked on it put their names to their work. One was Johannes van Loon, who cut ten of the plates. The other, Frederik Hendrik van den Hove, made the frontispiece.
