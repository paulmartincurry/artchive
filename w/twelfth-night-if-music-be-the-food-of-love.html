If music be the food of love, play on,
Give me excess of it; that, surfeiting,
The appetite may sicken and so die.

Twelfth Night — If music be the food of love (Act I, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1601–1602

The play is believed to have been written around 1601–1602 as an entertainment for the close of the Christmas season. The earliest public performance took place at Middle Temple Hall on 2 February 1602. It was not published until the First Folio of 1623.
