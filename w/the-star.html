The Star (Major Arcana XVII)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1909

Free to use

Arthur Edward Waite commissioned Pamela Colman Smith to draw a 78-card tarot deck in 1909, when she was thirty or thirty-one. The two had met through the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, which Yeats introduced her to. She drew full scenes with figures on every card, the numbered pips included, and William Rider & Son of London published the deck. When she died in Bude, her possessions were auctioned to pay her debts, and the place of her grave is unknown.
