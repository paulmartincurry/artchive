Woman Seen from the Back

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1888 · Oil on canvas · 63.5 × 55.5 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Hammershøi painted this in 1888, at twenty-three or twenty-four, three years after his debut at the Charlottenborg Spring Exhibition and three years before he married Ida Ilsted. The museum, which acquired the canvas in 1924, formerly called it Figure of a Woman. In his later interiors his wife, like many of his figures, is usually shown from behind.
