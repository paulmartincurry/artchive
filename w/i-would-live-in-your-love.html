I would live in your love as the sea-grasses live in the sea,
Borne up by each wave as it passes, drawn down by each wave that recedes;
I would empty my soul of the dreams that have gathered in me,
I would beat with your heart as it beats, I would follow your soul as it leads.

I Would Live in Your Love

Sara Teasdale · 1917

Teasdale's collection Love Songs came out in 1917, and won her a Pulitzer Prize the next year. The sponsoring organization now lists it as the earliest Pulitzer Prize for Poetry, although that category was inaugurated only in 1922. From 1904 to 1907 she had belonged to The Potters, a group of young women artists in St. Louis who published a monthly magazine, The Potter's Wheel.
