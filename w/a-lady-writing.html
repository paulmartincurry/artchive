A Lady Writing

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1665 · Oil on canvas · 45 × 39.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Vermeer signed the canvas on the frame of the picture hanging on the back wall. That picture, of which only part of a large string instrument shows, is likely a still life by Cornelis van der Meulen; the inventory of Vermeer's estate lists a vanitas still life with a double bass and a skull. The painting measures 45 by 39.9 centimetres.
