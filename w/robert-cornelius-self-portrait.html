Robert Cornelius, Self-Portrait

Robert Cornelius · October or November 1839 · Daguerreotype · visible plate 9 × 6.9 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Cornelius made this daguerreotype outside his family's store in Philadelphia in October or November 1839, a few months after Daguerre announced his process in Paris. He used a box fitted with a lens from an opera glass. The paper backing is inscribed The first light picture ever taken. 1839. He was 30.
