Humayun Defeating the Afghans (Akbarnama)

Maker unknown · c. 1590 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Image 31.2 × 18.6 cm; overall 36.5 × 23.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Akbar put this scene of his father Humayun's victory of 1555 near the beginning of his own official biography, though Humayun did not take part in this battle; Cleveland describes his presence here as symbolic. The painting is 31.2 cm high. Cleveland bought the leaf in 1971 from John D. MacDonald of Cambridge, Massachusetts.
