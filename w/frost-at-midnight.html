Or if the secret ministry of frost
Shall hang them up in silent icicles,
Quietly shining to the quiet Moon.

Frost at Midnight (closing)

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1798

Written in February 1798, when Coleridge was twenty-five. The child in the poem is his son Hartley. It first came out in a small volume with two of his other poems, and he went on rewriting it: seven different versions were printed. The 1798 text ended with six more lines, which he cut from later versions.
