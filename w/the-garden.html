Annihilating all that’s made
To a green thought in a green shade.

The Garden (stanzas 5–7)

Andrew Marvell · 1681

The Garden was first printed in Miscellaneous Poems in 1681, three years after Marvell's death. He wrote much of it again in Latin as a poem called Hortus, which follows it in that book. When it was written is argued over; one critic places it after the Restoration rather than in the years 1650 to 1653, when Marvell was closest to Lord General Thomas Fairfax.
