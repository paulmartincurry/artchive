The woods are lovely, dark and deep,
But I have promises to keep,
And miles to go before I sleep,
And miles to go before I sleep.

Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening

Robert Frost · 1923

This poem about a snowy evening was written on a June morning in 1922, at Robert Frost's house in Shaftsbury, Vermont. He had been up the whole night writing the long poem New Hampshire and went out to see the sunrise when the idea came. By his own account the writing took a few minutes. It was published in 1923 in the collection New Hampshire.
