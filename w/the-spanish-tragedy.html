Where words prevail not, violence prevails.
But gold doth more than either of them both.

The Spanish Tragedy

Thomas Kyd · ca. 1587

Every early edition of the play is anonymous, and Thomas Kyd was not named as its author until 1773. The most likely year for its completion is 1587, when Kyd, a London scrivener's son, was twenty-eight. The first quarto, of 1592, survives in a single copy: two booksellers had each printed works belonging to the other, both were fined 10 shillings, and the offending books were destroyed. There were eleven editions between 1592 and 1633.
