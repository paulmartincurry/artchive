The Gross Clinic

Thomas Eakins · 1875 · Oil on canvas · Philadelphia Museum of Art and Pennsylvania Academy of the Fine Arts

Free to use

Eakins based the picture on an operation he watched, in which Samuel Gross, then seventy, treated a young man's thigh bone for osteomyelitis. He put himself in at the right-hand edge, sketching or writing. The selection committee for the 1876 Centennial Exhibition turned the canvas down, and it was shown in an army hospital; it sold then for 200 dollars. In 2006 its owner agreed to sell it out of Philadelphia for 68 million dollars, and a fund raised 30 million within weeks to keep it. A restoration begun in 1917 left the secondary figures too red.
