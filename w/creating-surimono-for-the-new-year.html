Creating Surimono for the New Year

Katsushika Hokusai · 1825 · Color woodblock print; shikishiban, surimono · 21.1 × 18.5 cm; Mat: 45.8 × 35.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hokusai was in his mid-sixties when he made this print, close to the standard surimono size of about 20 centimetres square. Surimono called for great skill from the block cutter, especially in carving the characters of the poems in relief. On one book project Hokusai wrote to the publisher that the blockcutter Egawa Tomekichi had strayed from his style in cutting some of the heads.
