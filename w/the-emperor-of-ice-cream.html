Let be be finale of seem.
The only emperor is the emperor of ice-cream.

The Emperor of Ice-Cream

Wallace Stevens · 1922

According to his biographer Paul Mariani, this was one of Stevens's own favourites among the poems in Harmonium. He had trained as a lawyer, taking his degree at New York Law School in 1903 after three years at Harvard and a short spell as a journalist. His insurance work took him on business trips to Florida, and from there he visited Cuba.
