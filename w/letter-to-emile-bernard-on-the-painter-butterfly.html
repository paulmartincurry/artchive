The scene of this existence for the painter-butterfly could be one of the innumerable stars which, when we are dead, might perhaps be as accessible to us as are the black spots that in this terrestrial life represent the cities and towns on our maps.

Letter to Émile Bernard on the Painter-Butterfly

Vincent van Gogh · c. 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1912

Van Gogh met Émile Bernard after moving to Paris in 1886, among painters who were reacting against Impressionism. By 1888 he had left for Arles in the south of France, and the two were in touch by letter.
