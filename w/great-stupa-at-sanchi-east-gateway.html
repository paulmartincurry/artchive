Great Stupa at Sanchi: East Gateway

Maker unknown · 1st Century BCE (Stupa Core 3rd c. BCE) · Stone

Free to use with credit. Photo: Biswarup Ganguly, CC BY 3.0

The stupa began in the third century BCE as a brick dome over relics of the Buddha, commissioned by the emperor Ashoka and about half as wide as it is now. Under the Shungas it was enlarged and cased in stone slabs. The four carved gateways were added in the first century BCE, and inscriptions place them in the Satavahana period. The dome today is 16.46 metres high and 36.6 metres across.
