Dragon

Muqi Fachang · c. 1250–79 · Hanging scroll, ink on silk · 125.2 × 57.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This dragon and its companion tiger were most likely hung on either side of a central image, such as the Bodhisattva Guanyin, in a Chan Buddhist setting. The pair went to Japan, where the shogun Ashikaga Yoshimitsu owned them, then later Ashikaga shoguns and Tokugawa Iemitsu. They reached Cleveland through a local dealer in 1958. The silk has darkened over the centuries.
