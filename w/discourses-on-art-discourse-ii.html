Discourses on Art: Discourse II

Joshua Reynolds · 1769

The Discourses were lectures that Joshua Reynolds gave at the Royal Academy of Arts between 1769 and 1790. He had become the Academy's first president in 1768 and was knighted in 1769, at forty-five or forty-six. By then he was London's busiest portrait painter, charging 100 guineas for a portrait, and a severe cold caught in Rome had left him partly deaf, so that he carried an ear trumpet. William Blake published a hostile set of annotations to the Discourses in 1808.
