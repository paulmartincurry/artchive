When one sends a poem or a kayeshi (‘return-poem’) to someone and, after it has gone, thinks of some small alteration—perhaps only a couple of letters—that would have improved it.

The Pillow-Book — Annoying Things

Sei Shōnagon · c. 1002

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1928

The Pillow Book is built partly from lists with headings such as hateful things and things of beauty, which modern writers have called Borgesian. Later editors divided it into numbered sections: the Sankanbon text, one of the two versions thought most complete, runs to 297, with 29 more that may have been added later by the author or by copyists.
