The Three Ages of Woman

Gustav Klimt · 1905 · Oil on canvas · 180 × 180 cm · Galleria Nazionale d'Arte Moderna, Rome

Free to use

Klimt completed the painting in 1905, at forty-two or forty-three, and showed it that year in Berlin at the second exhibition of the Deutscher Künstlerbund, under the title The Three Ages. He had been a founding member of the Vienna Secession and its first president, from 1897 to 1903.
