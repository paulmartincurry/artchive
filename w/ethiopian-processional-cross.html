Ethiopian Processional Cross

Maker unknown · Early 15th century · Copper alloy · 35.6 × 24.8 × 2.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Processional crosses like this were made with a socket at the base so they could be mounted on a staff and carried in church ceremonies, and the makers were free to choose their own shape and pattern. This copper-alloy cross comes from Lasta, the province of the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, and dates from the early fifteenth century. It is 35.6 centimetres tall.
