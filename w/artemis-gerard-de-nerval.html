The thirteenth returns…She’s the first, as ever;
And always the sole one – or the sole instant;
For are you queen, O you, the first or the last?
Are you king, are you, the sole or last lover?...

Artemis (Artémis)

Gérard de Nerval · published 1854

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Artemis is one of the eight poems of The Chimeras, which were published at the end of Nerval's book Les Filles du feu in January 1854. He spent time in psychiatric hospitals in 1840–1841, 1851, 1853 and 1854, and took his own life in January 1855.
