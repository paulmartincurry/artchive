Flower-Shaped Vase

Louis Comfort Tiffany · ca. 1906 · Favrile glass · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The name Favrile was first used in 1893, for the blown glass from Tiffany's new factory in Corona, Queens, which the Englishman Arthur J. Nash was hired to oversee. Louis Comfort Tiffany was about fifty-eight when this vase was made. It is opal and amber glass, 26.8 centimetres tall, and the museum bought it through a gift of Georgiana L. McClellan.
