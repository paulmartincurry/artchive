The hour of departure has arrived, and we go our ways—I to die, and you to live. Which is better God only knows.

Apology — The Hour of Departure

Socrates (via Plato) · c. 399 BCE

Translated by Benjamin Jowett

These are the closing words of the speech as Plato wrote it. Socrates spoke before a jury of perhaps 500 Athenian men, and twice in the speech he mentions that Plato was present. Most accounts of the defence, of which this is one of many, were published in the decade after the trial of 399 BC.
