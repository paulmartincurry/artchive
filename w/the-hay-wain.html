The Hay Wain

John Constable · 1821 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The mill at Flatford belonged to Constable's father, and the cottage on the left to a neighbour, the tenant farmer Willy Lott. Constable painted a full-size oil sketch with a palette knife before the finished canvas, which he showed at the Royal Academy in 1821 as Landscape: Noon. Nobody bought it. Three years later at the Paris Salon it won a gold medal from Charles X, and a cast of the medal is set into the frame. The cottage still stands; none of the trees do.
