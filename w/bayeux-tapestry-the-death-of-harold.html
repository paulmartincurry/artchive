Bayeux Tapestry: The Death of Harold (scene 57)

Maker unknown · 1070s · Wool thread on linen · Bayeux Museum, Bayeux

Free to use

The Bayeux Tapestry is an embroidery, not a woven tapestry. It was worked in wool thread on a coarse linen ground, using four different stitches and ten natural dye colours. The cloth is nearly 70 metres long and 50 centimetres tall.
