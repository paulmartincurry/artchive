Rohan Hours: The Dead Man Before His Judge

The Rohan Master · c. 1418–1425 · Tempera and gold leaf · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

The Rohan Master painted the book probably between 1418 and 1425. It was probably commissioned by Yolande of Aragon, Duchess of Anjou. Ten of its eleven surviving full-page miniatures have been ascribed to the master himself.
