The Brocade Bridge in Suō Province (Curious Views of Famous Bridges in the Provinces)

Katsushika Hokusai · early 1830s · Color woodblock print · 38 × 25.6 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Brocade Bridge is the Kintai Bridge at Iwakuni, five wooden arches across the Nishiki River, first built in 1673 and destroyed by a flood the following year. Rebuilt on stronger piers and renewed span by span, it stood for 276 years until a typhoon flood washed it away in 1950. For nearly three centuries its versions were put together without metal nails. Hokusai's print was published by Nishimuraya Yohachi.
