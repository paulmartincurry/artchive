The Old Guitarist

Pablo Picasso · 1903–1904 · Oil on panel · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

There are at least two other paintings under this one. In 1998 an infrared camera showed a young mother seated at the centre, reaching toward a kneeling child, with a calf or sheep beside her; x-rays also found an old woman with her head bent forward. Picasso had sketched the mother and child in a letter to his friend Max Jacob a few months before he began the guitarist. He was very poor at the time and living in Barcelona. The panel is 122.9 by 82.6 centimetres.
