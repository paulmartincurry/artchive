Tiger

Franz Marc · 1912 · woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In 1912 Marc also painted the subject in oil, the canvas known as The Tiger. Two years later he was in the German army as a cavalryman, and he was killed at Verdun on 4 March 1916, aged thirty-six. Cleveland's impression of this woodcut was a gift from The Print Club of Cleveland in 1961.
