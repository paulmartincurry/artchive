Krazy Kat: The Moon Is Full To-night

George Herriman · 1917 · Drawing · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Herriman's full-page Sunday Krazy Kat began on 23 April 1916 in black and white, and at first appeared in the art and drama sections of Hearst's papers rather than the comics. This sixteen-panel original, according to the Library of Congress record, has Krazy singing to the moon on a fence and waiting for Ignatz's brick. It was shown in the Herriman exhibition at the Reina Sofía in Madrid in 2017–18.
