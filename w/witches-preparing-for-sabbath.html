Witches Preparing for Sabbath

Andries Stock, after Jacques de Gheyn II · c. 1610 · Engraving · Sheet 43.8 × 65.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sheet is 43.8 by 65.4 centimetres. Jacques de Gheyn II had given up engraving around 1600, so his design was cut into the plate by Andries Stock, an engraver who worked in The Hague, and published by Nicolaes de Clerck. Stock registered as a master engraver in the Hague guild of Saint Luke on 9 October 1613.
