These salt-pans caked and dry
Are not more parched than I!

Waka (Hyakunin Isshu 97)

Fujiwara no Teika · 1216 CE

Translated by William N. Porter, 1909

Teika was about fifty-four in 1216, a court noble from a minor branch of the Fujiwara clan that specialised in poetry. He had served twenty years in the Palace Guards of the Left, the last ten without promotion, when a hundred-poem sequence he wrote in 1200 won him admission to the court of the Retired Emperor Go-Toba. He helped compile one Imperial anthology of waka and later compiled another by himself, the first person to work on two.
