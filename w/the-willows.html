The Willows

Algernon Blackwood · 1907

The novella first appeared in 1907 in Blackwood's collection The Listener and Other Stories, when he was thirty-seven or thirty-eight. He had only lately moved back to England and begun writing supernatural stories, after years in which he worked as a dairy farmer and hotel keeper in Canada, a bartender, a model, a reporter for The New York Times and a violin teacher. The story follows two men on a canoe trip down the Danube who camp on a sandy island near Bratislava.
