If in the first chapter you say that a gun hung on the wall, in the second or third chapter it must without fail be discharged.

Advice to a Young Writer (Chekhov's Gun)

Anton Chekhov · before 1904 (English 1927)

Translated by S. S. Koteliansky, 1927

The principle known as Chekhov's gun comes from the late 1880s, the period of his play Ivanov. A theatre manager named Korsh commissioned that play in the autumn of 1887; Chekhov wrote it in a fortnight and it was produced that November. He disliked the experience of the production, and the play was a hit.
