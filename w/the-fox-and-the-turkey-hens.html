The Fox and the Turkey Hens

Jean-Baptiste Oudry · 1733 · Brush and black ink and gray wash heightened with white gouache, blue ink in margin; framing lines in brown ink · Image: 24 x 18.8 cm (9 7/16 x 7 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The fable is about turkeys whose fear of a fox makes them easy prey. It is one of 276 designs Oudry made for La Fontaine's Fables, published in four volumes between 1755 and 1759.
