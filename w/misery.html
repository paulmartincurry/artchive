The little mare munches, listens, and breathes on her master’s hands. Iona is carried away and tells her all about it.

Misery (closing)

Anton Chekhov · 1886

Translated by Constance Garnett

Chekhov published this story on 16 January 1886 in the Peterburgskaya Gazeta, signed A. Chekhonte. Leo Tolstoy included it in his personal list of Chekhov's best stories. Chekhov put a slightly revised version into the Collected Works that came out between 1899 and 1901.
