Sonnets from the Portuguese 14

Elizabeth Barrett Browning · 1850

By 1850 Elizabeth Barrett Browning had lived in Italy for four years with her husband, Robert Browning, and their son, known as Pen, had been born the year before. On the death of Wordsworth that year she was a rival to Tennyson as a candidate for poet laureate. She had suffered intense head and spinal pain since the age of fifteen and took laudanum, and later morphine, for it.
