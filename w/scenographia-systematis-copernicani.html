Scenographia Systematis Copernicani (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The plate sets out the system of Nicolaus Copernicus, with the Sun at the centre. This impression comes from the edition published in Amsterdam in 1708 by Gerard Valk and Peter Schenk, 48 years after the first. The Library of Congress copy stands 52 centimetres tall, has its plates coloured by hand, and was rebound and conserved in July 2004.
