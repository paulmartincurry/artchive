Adeline Ravoux

Vincent van Gogh · 1890 · oil on fabric · Unframed: 50.2 × 50.5 cm (19 3/4 × 19 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Adeline Ravoux was thirteen, the daughter of the innkeeper at Auvers, a small town north of Paris, where Vincent van Gogh rented a room in May 1890. He died in Auvers on 29 July, aged thirty-seven. She did not think the portrait looked like her. The canvas is about 50 centimetres square.
