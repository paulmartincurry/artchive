Saint Serapion

Francisco de Zurbarán · 1628 · Oil on canvas · 120 × 103 cm · Wadsworth Atheneum, Hartford

Free to use

The Mercedarian Order commissioned the painting in 1628 for the De Profundis hall, the funerary chapel of its monastery in Seville. Serapion was a Mercedarian friar who was martyred in 1240, and a small note painted beside his chest gives his name. Zurbarán was about thirty.
