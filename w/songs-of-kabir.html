O servant, where dost thou seek Me? Lo! I am beside thee.

Songs of Kabir (I)

Kabir · 15th century

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore, 1915

Kabir's poems were composed aloud in the fifteenth century and passed on by voice through the seventeenth. The Hindi collection behind Songs of Kabir was gathered by Kshitimohan Sen from books, from manuscripts and from the singing of wandering ascetics and minstrels. One scholar, V. C. Mishra, has argued that only six of the poems are Kabir's own and that many were composed by later admirers.
