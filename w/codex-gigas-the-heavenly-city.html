Codex Gigas: The Heavenly City (folio 289v)

Maker unknown · c. 1204–30 · Ink and pigments on vellum · 92 × 50 × 22 cm (whole book) · National Library of Sweden, Stockholm

Free to use

The Heavenly City faces the full-page Devil across the opening of folios 289 and 290. The manuscript holds 310 leaves of vellum, said to come from the skins of 160 donkeys, or perhaps calves. Handwriting analysis points to a single scribe, and the work is thought to have taken twenty to thirty years. The pages leading up to this spread have darkened from light as readers turned toward the Devil.
