Icon: Saint George, Virgin and Child and Archangels

Maker unknown · Late 17th century · Distemper and gesso on wood · H x W x D: 19.8 x 33.6 x 2.1 cm (7 13/16 x 13 1/4 x 13/16 in.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

The tender pose of Mary and the child comes from an Italian engraving of the Santa Maria Maggiore icon, which Jesuit missionaries brought to Ethiopia early in the 1600s.
