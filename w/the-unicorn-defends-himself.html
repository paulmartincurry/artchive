The Unicorn Defends Himself (from the Unicorn Tapestries)

French (design) and South Netherlandish (weaving) · 1495–1505 · Wool warp with wool, silk, silver, and gilt wefts · Overall: 145 × 158 in. (368.3 × 401.3cm) · The Met

Free to use

The tapestry is about 3.7 metres high and 4 metres wide. It was designed in France and woven in the Southern Netherlands, with silver and gilt threads worked in among the wool and silk. John D. Rockefeller Jr. gave it to the museum in 1937, and it belongs to the Cloisters department.
