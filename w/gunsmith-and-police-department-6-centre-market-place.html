Gunsmith and Police Department, 6 Centre Market Place

Berenice Abbott · 4 February 1937 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Berenice Abbott photographed this sign on 4 February 1937 for Changing New York, her project for the Federal Art Project. A decade earlier in Paris she had persuaded Eugène Atget to sit for a portrait, and after his death in 1927 she acquired the prints and negatives left in his studio. The print is an 8 by 10 inch contact print.
