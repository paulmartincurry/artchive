Music, when soft voices die,
Vibrates in the memory—
Odours, when sweet violets sicken,
Live within the sense they quicken.

Music, when soft voices die

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1821 (published 1824)

Shelley wrote the poem in 1821. It was first published in 1824 in Posthumous Poems of Percy Bysshe Shelley, under the title To—. Mary Shelley edited the collection and wrote its preface.
