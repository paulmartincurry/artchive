Poppy Field (Giverny)

Claude Monet · 1890–91 · Oil on canvas · 61.2 × 93.4 cm (24 1/16 × 36 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Monet began four canvases of the poppy fields near his house at Giverny in July 1890, all almost the same size. That summer, after the harvest, he also started the 25 paintings of stacks of wheat.
