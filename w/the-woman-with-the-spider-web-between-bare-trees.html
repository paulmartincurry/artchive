The Woman with the Spider Web between Bare Trees

Caspar David Friedrich · 1803 · woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

In his early Dresden years Friedrich drew designs for woodcuts and his brother, a furniture maker, cut the blocks. By 1804 he had made only four woodcuts and eighteen etchings, apparently printed in small numbers and given only to friends. He was twenty-eight or twenty-nine when this one was made, and had not yet begun to work much in oils.
