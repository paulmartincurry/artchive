Workers on Their Way Home

Edvard Munch · 1914 · Oil on canvas · 130.7 × 160.8 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

The year beside the signature was long read as 1919; Gerd Woll's catalogue of 2008 reads it as 1914. The canvas is about 131 centimetres high and 161 wide, and the Statens Museum for Kunst acquired it in 1928. It was earlier titled Workers Coming Home.
