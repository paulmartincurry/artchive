God Shiva in His Ferocious Aspect as Mahakala Dancing

Maker unknown · c. 1700–1710 · Gum tempera and ink on paper · 28 × 20.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Mahakala, whose name means great time or death, is the fierce form of Shiva; one tantric text describes him dwelling among eight cremation grounds, adorned with skulls and ashes. This Mewar page was painted around 1700 to 1710. George and Clara Bickford of Cleveland gave it to the museum in 1955.
