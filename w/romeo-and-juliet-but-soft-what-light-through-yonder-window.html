But soft, what light through yonder window breaks?
It is the east, and Juliet is the sun!

Romeo and Juliet — But soft, what light through yonder window breaks? (Act II, Scene II)

William Shakespeare · c. 1591–1595

The play is believed to have been written between 1591 and 1595 and was first published in a quarto in 1597. The plot comes from an Italian tale by Matteo Bandello, which Arthur Brooke put into English verse in 1562.
