It is not enough that yearly, down this hill,
April
Comes like an idiot, babbling and strewing flowers.

Spring

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1921

In 1921 Millay wrote her first verse drama, The Lamp and the Bell, at the request of the drama department of Vassar. She also wrote prose, and insisted that this more popular work appear under the pseudonym Nancy Boyd, not her own name.
