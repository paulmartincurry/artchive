Casting a Mantra on the Waves at Kakuta on His Exile to Sado Island (Concise Illustrated Biography of the Great Priest)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1830–35 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 23.4 × 36.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kuniyoshi was the son of a silk-dyer and apparently helped in his father's business as a pattern designer. He was admitted to Utagawa Toyokuni's studio in 1811 and set out as an independent artist in 1814. This impression was a gift to the Art Institute of Chicago from Mr. and Mrs. James A. Michener.
