All at Once the Door Opened, and an Old, Old Woman Came Hobbling Out (Hansel and Grethel)

Arthur Rackham · published 1909 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Rackham had already illustrated the Grimms' tales in black and white in 1900; this colour edition followed in 1909, the year after his daughter Barbara was born. The Grimms first printed Hansel and Gretel in 1812. Scholars have argued that the brothers heard it in 1809 from the family of Dortchen Wild, who later married Wilhelm Grimm.
