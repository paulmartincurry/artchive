Town with Four Towers

Hercules Seghers · ca. 1631 · Etching and drypoint · Cincinnati Art Museum

Free to use

This impression is printed in green on coloured cotton and worked over with grey and black washes. Hercules Seghers often printed on coloured paper or cloth and cropped his prints by hand to different sizes. In 1631, about the year of this etching, debt forced him to sell his large house in Amsterdam. Only 183 impressions survive from his fifty-four plates. Rembrandt collected his prints and owned eight of his paintings.
