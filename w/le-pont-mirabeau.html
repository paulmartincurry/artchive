Comes the night sounds the hour
The days go by I endure

Le Pont Mirabeau

Guillaume Apollinaire · 1912 (Alcools, 1913)

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Apollinaire was born in Rome, grew up speaking French, Italian and Polish, and moved to Paris in 1900. In September 1911 the police jailed him for a week on suspicion of helping in the theft of the Mona Lisa, then released him. The following year, when he was thirty-one, he cofounded the magazine Les Soirées de Paris. He wrote his poems without punctuation. Alcools, published in 1913, made his reputation.
