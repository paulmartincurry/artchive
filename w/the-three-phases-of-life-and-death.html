The Three Phases of Life and Death

Hans Baldung Grien · c. 1509–10 · Oil on limewood · 48.2 × 32.8 cm · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Baldung painted this small limewood panel, 48.2 by 32.8 centimetres, around 1509 or 1510, just after he had finished his years in Dürer's Nuremberg workshop and moved back to Strasbourg. Death is not a skeleton here but a rotting body with strands of hair and flaps of skin hanging from it. The picture belonged to Guidobald Graf Thun, Archbishop of Salzburg, and came to the gallery in Vienna in 1806.
