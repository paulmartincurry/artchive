Wind in Pines Among a Myriad Valleys

Li Tang · 1124 · Hanging scroll, ink and colour on silk · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

This is the only painting by Li Tang that carries a clear signature, written on one of the peaks near the top, and it is dated to the spring of 1124, when he was about seventy-four. Some time after 1100 he had reached the highest rank in Emperor Huizong's Painting Academy. Two years after this scroll the Jurchen invasion drove the court south, and he went with it to Hangzhou.
