Plan of Chicago, Plate 75: Diagram of the City with Inner Circuits

Daniel Burnham and Edward Bennett · 1909 · Colored ink on a printed map · 75.4 × 89.4 cm (29 3/4 × 35 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This plate is on permanent loan to the Art Institute from the City of Chicago. The circuits are drawn in coloured ink over a printed map about ninety centimetres wide. In 1909 Daniel Burnham was in his early sixties and Edward Bennett, who was born in England, was in his mid-thirties. Burnham had moved to Chicago at the age of eight, and he died three years after the date on the Plan, in Heidelberg.
