Kitab al-Bulhan: Al-Ahmar, the Red King of the Jinn (folio 31r)

Maker unknown · c. 1399–1400 · Illustrated manuscript · Bodleian Libraries, Oxford (MS. Bodl. Or. 133)

Free to use

Al-Ahmar, the Red King of Tuesday, is one of a series of jinn kings tied to the days of the week. The main part of the manuscript was made around 1399 to 1400, probably in Baghdad. David Pingree names its author as al-Isfahani, who compiled it for Husayn ibn Ahmad al-Irbili.
