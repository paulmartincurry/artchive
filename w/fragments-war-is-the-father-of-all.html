Fragments — War Is the Father of All

Heraclitus of Ephesus · ca. 500 BCE

Translated by John Burnet

Heraclitus came from Ephesus, a port on the western coast of Asia Minor, then under Persian rule. Even in ancient times his cryptic sayings earned him the names the dark and the obscure. By the 6th century AD his book was so rare that Simplicius, who mentions him 32 times, never quotes from it.
