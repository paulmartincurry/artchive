Notebooks: The Deluge

Leonardo da Vinci · before 1519

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

Leonardo's notebooks began as loose papers of different types and sizes. Most passed to his pupil Francesco Melzi at his death, and after Melzi died in 1570 his son Orazio took little interest in them. In 1587 a household tutor carried 13 of the manuscripts off to Pisa, and after Orazio's death his heirs sold the rest, which scattered the collection.
