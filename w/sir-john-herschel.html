Sir John Herschel

Julia Margaret Cameron · April 1867 · Carbon print · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

John Herschel was the person who told Julia Margaret Cameron about the invention of photography, in 1839. In 1842 he sent her two dozen calotypes and daguerreotypes, the first photographs she ever saw. She had met the astronomer at the Cape of Good Hope in 1835, and he was godfather to her first child. Herschel died four years after this sitting, at the age of 79.
