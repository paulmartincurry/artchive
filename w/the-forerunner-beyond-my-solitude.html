Beyond my solitude is another solitude, and to him who dwells therein my aloneness is a crowded market-place and my silence a confusion of sounds.

The Forerunner — Beyond My Solitude

Kahlil Gibran · 1920

Gibran was a Lebanese-American writer who published in English and in Arabic; his first book in Arabic came out in New York in 1905. This piece is from The Forerunner, issued by Knopf in 1920, two years after The Madman.
