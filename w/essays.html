And on the loftiest throne in the world we are still sitting only on our own rump.

Essays (Book III, Ch. 13)

Michel de Montaigne · 1588

Translated by Donald M. Frame, 1957 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Latin was Montaigne's first language. His father gave him a German tutor who spoke no French and hired only servants who could speak to the boy in Latin. In 1588 he was fifty-five, and in his own lifetime he was admired more as a statesman than as an author. He went on revising the essays he had published and writing new ones for the rest of his life, and he died in 1592 at the château where he was born.
