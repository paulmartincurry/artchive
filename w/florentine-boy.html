Florentine Boy (American Pictorial Photography, series 1, plate 4)

Gertrude Käsebier · c. 1900 · Photogravure · 15.2 × 10.2 cm (image) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This gravure is plate 4 of the first series of American Pictorial Photography, a portfolio published by the Camera Club of New York around 1900. Käsebier signed the grey mount. She had started art school at 37, and in 1899 Stieglitz called her the leading artistic portrait photographer of the day. The print came to the Library of Congress in 1948 as a gift from Frances Benjamin Johnston.
