The Artchive
He did not love me living; but once dead
He pitied me; and very sweet it is
To know he still is warm though I am cold.

After Death

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Rossetti was born on 5 December 1830 at 38 Charlotte Street, London, the youngest sister of the artist and poet Dante Gabriel Rossetti. Goblin Market and Other Poems, the book this sonnet appeared in, was her first commercially printed collection and came out under her own name from Macmillan.
