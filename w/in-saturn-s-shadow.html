In Saturn's Shadow

Cassini, NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute · 15 September 2006 · Mosaic of 165 wide-angle camera images

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute

The Cassini spacecraft drifted in the shadow of Saturn for about twelve hours on 15 September 2006, the planet blocking the Sun. This view combines 165 wide-angle images taken over nearly three hours. Two faint rings were discovered during the observation. The pale dot above the bright main rings is Earth, more than a billion kilometres away.
