Helmets, Swords, Bows and Arrows

Maker unknown · 1780–1830 · Mulberry paper (kozo washi) treated with fermented persimmon tannin (kakishibu), and silk threads (itoire) · 26.1 × 42.5 cm (10 1/4 × 16 3/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Cut into this stencil is the gear of a samurai: helmet, long sword, twelve-inch dagger, bow and arrows. The helmet is fitted with a pair of deer antlers. Silk threads support the paper, and the stencil was a gift of Helen Snyder.
