Even as his mouth was crying his father’s name, it vanished into the dark blue sea, the Icarian Sea, called after him.

Metamorphoses — Daedalus and Icarus (Book VIII)

Ovid · c. 8 CE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Ovid had completed the Metamorphoses by AD 8, when he was about fifty. In that same year the emperor Augustus banished him to Tomis on the Black Sea, where he lived for the rest of his life. The poem runs to 11,995 lines in fifteen books and holds more than 250 myths. William Caxton made the first English translation in 1480.
