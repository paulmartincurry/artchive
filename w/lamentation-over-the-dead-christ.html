Lamentation over the Dead Christ

Andrea Mantegna · c. 1480 · Tempera, canvas · Pinacoteca di Brera, Milan

The painting was still among Mantegna's possessions when he died, and its date is argued over; it was finished some time between 1475 and 1501. Mantegna made the feet smaller than true size, since at full size they would have covered much of the body. Lorenzo Mantegna sold it to Cardinal Sigismondo Gonzaga. It was probably in Mantua until 1630, when much of the collection there was stolen and sold off, and it next turns up in Rome with Cardinal Mazarin. The Bossi family of Milan sold it to the Brera.
