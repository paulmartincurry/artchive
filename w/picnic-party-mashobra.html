Picnic Party, Mashobra

Raja Deen Dayal · 1887 · Albumen print · Image: 19.3 x 26.1 cm (7 5/8 x 10 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The party is one of many held by the caterer and hotelier Federico Peliti at his estate outside Shimla; Peliti is on horseback and his wife Judith on the swing. He and Deen Dayal both belonged to the Photographic Society of Bombay.
