Book I, Proposition XXXVI (The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid)

Oliver Byrne · published 1847 · Getty Research Institute, Los Angeles

Free to use

Oliver Byrne's Euclid replaced the letters of the usual diagrams with coloured shapes and symbols. William Pickering published it in London in 1847, and Charles Whittingham printed it at Chiswick. Byrne was a civil engineer who wrote on mathematics, geometry and engineering, and he was appointed Professor of Mathematics at the College for Civil Engineers at Putney.
