Lozenge Composition with Yellow, Black, Blue, Red, and Gray

Piet Mondrian · 1921 · Oil on canvas · 60 × 60 cm (23 5/8 × 23 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The canvas is a square sixty centimetres a side, hung off axis so that its edges run on the diagonal, and Mondrian wanted such paintings hung well above eye level. He signed it at lower centre with his initials and the year, 21. In 1921 it went on consignment to Léonce Rosenberg's Paris gallery, L'Effort Moderne. Edgar Kaufmann Jr. of New York acquired it on 1 March 1957 and gave it to the Art Institute that same year.
