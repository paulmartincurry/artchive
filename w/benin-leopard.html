Benin Leopard

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · 1550–1680 · Brass, iron · 39.4 × 12.7 × 39.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The leopard is the animal of the oba, and it appears throughout the Benin Bronzes. Leopard figures were among the works British forces took from Benin City in 1897, along with heads of kings and queen mothers and bells. This one is 39.4 centimetres long and was given by Nelson A. Rockefeller in 1972.
