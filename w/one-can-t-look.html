One Can't Look (The Disasters of War, plate 26)

Francisco de Goya · 1810 (published 1863) · Etching, burnished lavis, drypoint, burin · Plate: 14.5 × 21 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya used a sketchbook when he visited battle sites and worked on the copper plates afterwards in his studio. The title of this plate, One can't look, is one of the captions taken as a sign that he was there.
