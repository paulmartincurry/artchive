But let there be spaces in your togetherness,

And let the winds of the heavens dance between you.

The Prophet — On Marriage

Kahlil Gibran · 1923

The Prophet is a book of 26 prose poetry fables that Gibran wrote in English; it was first published in 1923. In it the prophet Almustafa, who has lived in the city of Orphalese for 12 years, sees the ship arrive that will carry him home. The seeress Almitra, the first person in the city to believe in him, asks him to speak to the crowd before he leaves.
