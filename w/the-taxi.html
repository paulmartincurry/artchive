When I go away from you
The world beats dead
Like a slackened drum.

The Taxi

Amy Lowell · 1914

Lowell never attended college, because her family did not consider it proper for a woman, and made up for it with avid reading and book collecting. She turned to poetry in 1902, aged 28, after seeing Eleonora Duse perform in Europe. Her first published work appeared in the Atlantic Monthly in 1910. In 1912 she met the actress Ada Dwyer Russell, who became her lover.
