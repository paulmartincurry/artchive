The jewelled steps are already quite white with dew,
It is so late that the dew soaks my gauze stockings,
And I let down the crystal curtain
And watch the moon through the clear autumn.

The Jewel Stairs' Grievance

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1915

Translated by Ezra Pound

Ezra Pound worked on Cathay while he was moving from imagism to vorticism. The critic Zhaoming Qian has called The Jewel Stairs' Grievance, together with The River Merchant's Wife and Exile's Letter, imagist and vorticist work. In 1909 Pound had been in London as secretary to W. B. Yeats, and the two often visited the Asian galleries of the British Museum.
