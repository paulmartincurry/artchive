The Masque of the Red Death, for Edgar Allan Poe's Tales of Mystery and the Imagination

Aubrey Beardsley · 1894 · Pen and black ink, brush and India ink over graphite · 25.4 × 16.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Beardsley caught tuberculosis at the age of seven, and throughout his short career he suffered lung haemorrhages that often kept him from working. He made this drawing in 1894 for a Chicago edition of Poe. Poe first published the story in Graham's Magazine in May 1842 as The Mask of the Red Death, and was paid $12 for it.
