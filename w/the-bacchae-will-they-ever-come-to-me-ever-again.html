What else is Wisdom? What of man's endeavour
Or God's high grace, so lovely and so great?
To stand from fear set free, to breathe and wait;
To hold a hand uplifted over Hate;
And shall not Loveliness be loved for ever?

The Bacchae — Will They Ever Come to Me, Ever Again (chorus)

Euripides · 405 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray

The Bacchae was staged in 405 BC alongside Iphigeneia at Aulis and Alcmaeon in Corinth. Unusually, its chorus is part of the plot, and the god Dionysus is not a distant presence but a character on stage, indeed the protagonist. Ingmar Bergman directed it three times: as an opera in 1991, a television film in 1993 and a stage play in 1996.
