A Bus Passenger Reading the Sunday Comics, Louisville, Kentucky

Esther Bubley · September 1943 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Esther Bubley was 22 when she made this in September 1943, for her series on bus travel in the Midwest and South. The caption says the woman waited from 11 p.m. to 8 a.m. in the Louisville Greyhound station, sleeping on the comics because the benches were full, on her way to nursing school in Nebraska.
