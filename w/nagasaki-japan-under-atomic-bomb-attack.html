Nagasaki, Japan under Atomic Bomb Attack

United States Army Air Forces · 9 August 1945 · Gelatin silver print · sheet 26 × 21 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The print is credited to the United States Army Air Forces and carries the number A-58450 AC. The bomb dropped on Nagasaki on 9 August 1945 was a Fat Man, a plutonium implosion-type weapon, three days after Hiroshima. Kokura, home to one of Japan's largest munitions plants, had been on the target list with it.
