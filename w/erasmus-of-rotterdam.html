Erasmus of Rotterdam

Hans Holbein the Younger · ca. 1532 · Oil on linden panel · 7 1/4 × 5 9/16 in. (18.4 × 14.2 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The panel is 18.4 centimetres tall. Erasmus needed likenesses to send to friends and admirers across Europe, and Holbein had first painted him in 1523. A recommendation from Erasmus to Thomas More helped Holbein find work in England in 1526. In 1532, about the date of this portrait, Holbein left Basel for England a second time.
