Behold Priam! Here too is the meed of honour, here mortal estate touches the soul to tears.

The Aeneid — Sunt Lacrimae Rerum (Book I)

Virgil · 29–19 BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail

The Latin behind this is sunt lacrimae rerum, line 462 of the first book. Aeneas, a refugee on the shore of Carthage, says it in front of a mural in a temple of Juno that shows the battles of the Trojan War and the deaths of his friends. The phrase has since been carved on war memorials.
