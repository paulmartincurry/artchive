Notebooks: How to Represent a Battle

Leonardo da Vinci · before 1519

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1883

In 1505 Leonardo was commissioned to paint The Battle of Anghiari on a wall of the Hall of the Five Hundred in the Palazzo Vecchio, Florence. His design showed four men on raging war horses fighting over a standard, and Michelangelo was given the opposite wall. The painting deteriorated quickly and is now known from a copy by Rubens.
