Pilgrim Flask

Maker unknown · Tang dynasty (618–907), first half of 8th century · Earthenware with three-color (sancai) lead glazes · 19.2 × 15.5 × 14.5 cm (7 5/8 × 6 1/8 × 5 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The pilgrim flask shape may copy vessels first made in glass, leather or metal. The moulded scene shows a boy in a billowing scarf dancing with a lion.
