What is a poet? An unhappy person who conceals profound anguish in his heart but whose lips are so formed that as sighs and cries pass over them they sound like beautiful music.

Either/Or — Diapsalmata

Søren Kierkegaard · 1843

Translated by Howard V. Hong and Edna H. Hong · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Kierkegaard drafted much of this book in Berlin in the winter of 1841 to 1842, where he had gone to hear Schelling lecture and found the lectures unbearable. He came home to Copenhagen in March 1842, finished the manuscript late that year, and published it in two volumes on 20 February 1843 under the invented editor Victor Eremita. He later wrote that the whole book took eleven months, though one page of the Diapsalmata was older. It was among the last of his books to reach English, in 1944.
