A Harvest of Death, Gettysburg, Pennsylvania

Timothy H. O'Sullivan · 1863, printed 1866 · Albumen silver print

Free to use

Timothy O'Sullivan exposed the glass negative sometime between 4 and 7 July 1863, in the days after the battle. He was about twenty-three. That year he and Alexander Gardner had left Mathew Brady's team, because Brady wanted full credit for the work. Gardner made the prints and published this one as plate 36 of his Photographic Sketch Book of the War in 1865; most original prints measure 45.2 by 57.2 centimetres.
