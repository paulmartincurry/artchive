The Blanket Weaver, Navaho

Edward S. Curtis · 1904 · photogravure · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Curtis made this photograph in 1904, two years before J. P. Morgan agreed to fund The North American Indian with $75,000, and it was printed as plate 34 in the first of the portfolios that accompanied the volumes. He was in his mid-thirties. He had left school in the sixth grade and built his own camera, and five years before this picture he had been the official photographer of the Harriman Alaska Expedition.
