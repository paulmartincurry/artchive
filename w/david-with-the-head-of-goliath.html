David with the Head of Goliath

Caravaggio · c. 1605–10 · Oil on canvas · 125 × 101 cm · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Free to use

The severed head of Goliath is Caravaggio's own face. The blade carries the abbreviated inscription H-AS OS. Scholars date the canvas anywhere from 1605 to 1610, and it was in the collection of Cardinal Scipione Borghese by 1650; the cardinal was the papal official with the power to pardon Caravaggio for murder. He painted the subject at least twice more, on canvas around 1600 and on a poplar panel around 1607.
