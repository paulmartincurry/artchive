The Artchive
Often a hidden god inhabits obscure being;
And like an eye, born, covered by its eyelids,
Pure spirit grows beneath the surface of stones!

Golden Lines (Vers dorés)

Gérard de Nerval · 1845

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The sonnet first appeared in the magazine L'Artiste on 16 March 1845, under the title Pensée antique. Nine years later it was collected among The Chimeras in Les Filles du feu. In 1853 Nerval had temporarily lost the ability to speak and write.
