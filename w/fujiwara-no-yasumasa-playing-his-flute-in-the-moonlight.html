Fujiwara no Yasumasa Playing His Flute in the Moonlight

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · c. 1883 · Color woodblock print; ōban triptych · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Yoshitoshi based this 1883 triptych on a drawing he had shown the year before at an exhibition of Japanese paintings. The story comes from tale collections compiled in the 12th and 13th centuries: the bandit Hakamadare stalks the flute-playing Fujiwara no Yasumasa but cannot bring himself to strike. In the early 1870s Yoshitoshi had been so poor that he and his mistress burned the floorboards for warmth.
