Last Moments of the Endurance

Frank Hurley · 1915, copyrighted c. 1916 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

When Endurance was lost in 1915, Hurley had to leave most of his cameras and equipment behind. He kept 120 of his glass-plate negatives and smashed about 400 others. This picture was printed in Shackleton's book South, published in London in 1920.
