Miniatures from a Manuscript of the Apocalypse

Maker unknown · c. 1295 · ink, tempera, and gold on vellum · Sheet: 10.2 × 14.7 cm (4 × 5 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

These miniatures come from a manuscript made in Lorraine, at a time when painters there were working from English models. Each sheet is about 10 by 15 centimetres. Daniel Burkhardt-Wildt of Basel owned them until his death in 1819, and they stayed with his descendants until a Sotheby's sale in London in April 1983, where Cleveland bought them.
