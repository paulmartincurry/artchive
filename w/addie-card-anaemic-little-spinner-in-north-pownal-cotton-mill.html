Addie Card, Anaemic Little Spinner in North Pownal Cotton Mill, Vermont

Lewis Hine · August 1910 · Photographic print · 11.5 × 8.5 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Lewis Hine photographed this spinner at the North Pownal Cotton Mill in Vermont in August 1910. The caption card recorded no name; the Library of Congress identified her as Addie Card from a similar picture in the same album. Hine worked for the National Child Labor Committee for about a decade, to support its lobbying against child labour. The attribution to him rests on provenance.
