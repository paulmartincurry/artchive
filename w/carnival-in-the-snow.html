Carnival in the Snow

Paul Klee · 1923 · Watercolor · Sheet 24 × 23.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Klee wrote the title, Karneval im Schnee, and the number 1923 / 40 in brown ink on the mount. He was teaching at the Bauhaus that year. The watercolour was found in the estate of a young graphic designer after his death in 1944; a letter from the Munich auction house that sold it in 1968 says he must have been a personal acquaintance of Klee's.
