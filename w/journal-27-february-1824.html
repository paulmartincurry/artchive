Ce qu'il y a de plus réel en moi, ce sont ces illusions que je crée avec ma peinture. Le reste est un sable mouvant.

Journal: 27 February 1824

Eugène Delacroix · 1824

Delacroix's next success, The Massacre at Chios of 1824, was bought by the state. It showed Greek civilians rounded up for enslavement by the Ottoman Empire, and many critics deplored its despairing tone. After seeing paintings by John Constable, Delacroix made extensive, freely painted changes to its sky and distant landscape. He had been an orphan since sixteen.
