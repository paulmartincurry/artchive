Tikal Temple I

Maker unknown · ca. 732 · Limestone · Tikal

Free to use with credit. Photo: Raymond Ostertag, CC BY-SA 2.5

The tomb was built first and the pyramid raised over it. It holds Jasaw Chan K'awiil I, who ruled Tikal from 682 to 734, and his son oversaw the building of both. The lintels of the shrine at the top were carved from sapodilla, a hard red-brown local wood; two planks of the central one were given to the British Museum by the explorer John Boddam-Whetham, and two others were removed in the nineteenth century and are lost. Archaeologists did not find the tomb until 1962, by tunnelling in from the foot of the stairway.
