Notebooks — On Painting

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1498

Translated by Jean Paul Richter, 1888

At about fourteen Leonardo entered the Florence workshop of Andrea del Verrocchio as a studio boy. He was an apprentice by seventeen and stayed in training for seven years. The workshop taught drafting, chemistry, metalworking, plaster casting, leather working, mechanics and woodwork along with drawing, painting, sculpting and modelling. Around 1498, at about forty-six, he was finishing The Last Supper.
