Édouard Manet, Seated, Holding His Hat

Edgar Degas · ca. 1868 · Graphite and black chalk · Sheet: 13 1/16 × 9 1/16 in. (33.1 × 23 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Degas had met Édouard Manet in 1864, and in the years after it his own subjects moved from history painting to contemporary life, largely by Manet's example. He was in his early thirties when he made this drawing. Later, once sales of his own work allowed it, he collected paintings by Manet. The Metropolitan Museum bought the sheet in 1918, the year after Degas's death.
