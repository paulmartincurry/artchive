This is the true joy in life, the being used for a purpose recognized by yourself as a mighty one…

Man and Superman — This is the true joy in life (Epistle Dedicatory)

George Bernard Shaw · 1903

Shaw wrote the play in 1903. It was first given at the Court Theatre in London on 21 May 1905, without the third-act dream sequence, Don Juan in Hell. In May 1900 he had begun sketching a Parliament in Hell between Don Juan and the Devil.
