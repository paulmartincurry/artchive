Hakuin Ekaku · 18th century · Ink and color on paper · Manju-ji, Ōita

Free to use

Hakuin took up painting seriously only late in his life, at almost sixty. His paintings served as a kind of visual sermon and were extremely popular among the laypeople of his time, many of whom were illiterate. This one belongs to the temple Manju-ji in Ōita.
