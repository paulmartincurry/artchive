Isolde

Aubrey Beardsley · 1895 · color lithograph · Image: 22.8 × 14.2 cm (9 × 5 9/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The art journal The Studio published this print as a bonus for its readers. It was printed in colour by William Griggs, the inventor of photo-chromo-lithography, at a time when Beardsley's illustrations were in black and white. Beardsley was twenty-two or twenty-three. John Bonebrake owned the sheet from 1993 and gave it to the Cleveland Museum of Art.
