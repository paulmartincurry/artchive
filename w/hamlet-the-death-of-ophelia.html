Eugène Delacroix · 1843 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

This is an impression of the third and last state of the lithograph, which Delacroix made in 1843 at the age of forty-four or forty-five. The Cleveland Museum of Art received it in 1941 with the Mr. and Mrs. Lewis B. Williams Collection.
