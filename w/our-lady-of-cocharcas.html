Our Lady of Cocharcas

Maker unknown · 1759 · Oil and gold on canvas · 153.2 × 112.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Painted in Cuzco in 1759, in oil and gold on a canvas about a metre and a half tall. In 1688 a split in the city's corporation of painters had ended with its Quechua and mestizo painters outside it; by their account, their Spanish colleagues had exploited them.
