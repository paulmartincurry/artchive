The Feast in the House of Simon

El Greco · c. 1608–14 · Oil on canvas · 143.3 × 100.4 cm (56 7/16 × 39 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Parts were painted by El Greco's workshop to his design; the heads show different hands, probably including his son Jorge Manuel. The architecture is unlike any other in his work.
