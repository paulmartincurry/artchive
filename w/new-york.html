New York

George Bellows · 1911 · Oil on canvas · 106.7 × 152.4 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

On the back of the canvas Bellows wrote the title and his address, 146 East 19th Street. He was twenty-eight or twenty-nine. The painting stayed with him and then with his wife, Emma; Paul Mellon bought it from her estate in 1961 and gave it to the National Gallery of Art in 1986.
