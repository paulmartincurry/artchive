Bhairava

Maker unknown · 16th century · Gilded copper, rock crystal, paint · 81.3 × 91.4 × 35.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sculpture is 81.3 centimetres high and 91.4 wide, with rock crystal set into the gilded copper. Bhairava is an important deity of the Newars of the Kathmandu Valley: every traditional Newar settlement has at least one Bhairava temple, most kept by Tantric Newar priests, and chariot processions are held for him in Newar towns each year. The Zimmerman family gave this piece to the Met in 2012.
