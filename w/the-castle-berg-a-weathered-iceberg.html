The Castle Berg, a Weathered Iceberg

Herbert Ponting · 1910 or 1911 · Photographic print · 11.9 × 16.5 cm (image) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Ponting was the first professional photographer taken on an Antarctic expedition, signed for Scott's Terra Nova voyage. He preferred glass plates to film. He registered the copyright of this picture in May 1913, and it later appeared in his book The Great White South, published in New York in 1922.
