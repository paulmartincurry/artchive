The Conference of the Birds

Habiballah of Sava · ca. 1600 · Ink, opaque watercolor, gold, and silver on paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The page measures 25.4 by 11.4 centimetres and comes from a Persian manuscript of a poem by Attar of Nishapur. Attar wrote the poem in 1177, more than four hundred years before Habiballah of Sava painted this page for it. Habiballah is recorded as active from about 1590 to 1610.
