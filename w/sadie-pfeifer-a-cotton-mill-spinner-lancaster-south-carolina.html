Sadie Pfeifer, a Cotton Mill Spinner, Lancaster, South Carolina

Lewis Hine · 1908 · Gelatin silver print · Image/paper: 11.6 × 15.3 cm (4 5/8 × 6 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1908, the year of this photograph, Lewis Hine left his teaching position to become the photographer for the National Child Labor Committee. To get into mills, mines and factories he posed at times as a fire inspector, a postcard vendor, a Bible salesman or an industrial photographer recording machinery. This print is about twelve by fifteen centimetres. A pencil inscription on the back places the mill in North Carolina.
