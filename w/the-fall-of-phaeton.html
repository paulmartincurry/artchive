The Fall of Phaeton

Peter Paul Rubens · c. 1604/1605, probably reworked c. 1606/1608 · Oil on canvas · 98.4 × 131.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Rubens painted the canvas in Rome around 1604–05 and probably reworked it a few years later. In 1939 it was sold at Christie's in London as the work of another painter, A. J. van Diepenbeck. The National Gallery of Art has had it since 5 January 1990.
