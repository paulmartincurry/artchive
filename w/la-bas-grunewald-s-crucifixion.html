The laboured tendons of the armpits seemed ready to snap. The fingers, wide apart, were contorted in an arrested gesture in which were supplication and reproach but also benediction.

Là-bas — Grünewald's Crucifixion (Ch. I)

Joris-Karl Huysmans · 1891

Translated by Keene Wallis

Là-bas first ran as a serial in the newspaper L'Écho de Paris, beginning on February 15, 1891, and came out as a book that April. Its first chapter contains the first critical appreciation of Matthias Grünewald's Tauberbischofsheim altarpiece. Conservative readers urged the editor to halt the serial, and sale of the book was prohibited from French railway stations.
