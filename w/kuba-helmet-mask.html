Kuba Helmet Mask

Maker unknown · Early–mid-18th century · Wood · 50.8 × 25.4 × 24.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This wooden helmet mask was carved by a Ngeende-Kuba artist in the Sankuru River region in the early to mid-eighteenth century. The Kuba Kingdom flourished between the seventeenth and nineteenth centuries, and its kings kept the most elaborate works for court ceremony and were buried with them. The mask is 50.8 centimetres high and was given to the Met in 2018 for its 150th anniversary.
