The Abduction of Rebecca

Eugène Delacroix · 1846 · Oil on canvas · 39 1/2 × 32 1/4 in. (100.3 × 81.9 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The scene is from Walter Scott's novel Ivanhoe, and Delacroix showed the painting at the Paris Salon of 1846. Twelve years later he painted an almost identical version for the Salon of 1859, which is now in the Louvre. This first one came to New York in 1903 through the Wolfe Fund.
