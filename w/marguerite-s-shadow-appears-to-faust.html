Marguerite's Shadow Appears to Faust (Faust)

Eugène Delacroix · 1828 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Delacroix studied under the history painter Pierre-Narcisse Guérin and at the École des Beaux-Arts, where he did not succeed in the competitions. He showed at his first Salon in 1822, six years before this lithograph for Albert Stapfer's French translation of Faust.
