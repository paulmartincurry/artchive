On Photography

Susan Sontag · 1977

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

The book grew from a series of essays Sontag published in The New York Review of Books between 1973 and 1977. In 2003 she published Regarding the Pain of Others, which reassesses some of the views she had put forward in it.
