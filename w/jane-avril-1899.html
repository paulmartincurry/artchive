Jane Avril, 1899

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1899 · Color lithograph on tan wove paper · 55.6 × 37.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This was Toulouse-Lautrec's last image of Jane Avril. The snake that winds around the black dress is appliquéd to it. He made the poster in 1899, the year he collapsed from exhaustion and was committed by his family, and he died two years later.
