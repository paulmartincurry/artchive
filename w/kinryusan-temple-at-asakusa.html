Kinryūsan Temple at Asakusa (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1856 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 35.7 × 24.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The print was published in the seventh month of 1856, probably to mark the restoration of the temple's pagoda two months earlier, after damage in the earthquake of 1855. The view runs from the Kaminarimon gate to the Hōzōmon gate and the pagoda of Sensō-ji. Hiroshige never lived in financial comfort; his will left instructions for paying his debts.
