Dogon Priest with Raised Arms

Maker unknown · 14th–17th century · Wood, applied organic materials · 210.5 × 36.2 × 21.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A Dogon blacksmith carved this figure sometime between the fourteenth and seventeenth centuries, and it stands 210.5 centimetres tall. Figures with raised arms are one of the recurring themes of Dogon sculpture. Such sculptures were commonly kept out of public view, in family houses, in sanctuaries or with the Hogon. Nelson A. Rockefeller gave it in 1969.
