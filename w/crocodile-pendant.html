Crocodile Pendant

Maker unknown · 1000–1550 · Cast gold, modern greenstone · 6.5 x 5.5 x 4.4 cm (2 9/16 x 2 3/16 x 1 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Creatures mixing crocodile and lizard features mattered greatly in ancient Panama. The greenstone is a modern replacement for the stone or shell that once completed the body.
