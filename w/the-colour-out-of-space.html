It was just a colour out of space--a frightful messenger from unformed realms of infinity beyond all Nature as we know it;

The Colour Out of Space (closing)

H. P. Lovecraft · 1927

Lovecraft began this story in March 1927, straight after finishing The Case of Charles Dexter Ward, while he was also typing the final draft of his essay Supernatural Horror in Literature. He claimed the idea came from the newly built Scituate Reservoir in Rhode Island. Hugo Gernsback printed it in Amazing Stories that September, paid only $25 and paid late, and Lovecraft never sent the magazine anything again. It remained his own favourite of his short stories.
