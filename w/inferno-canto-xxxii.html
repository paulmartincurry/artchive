Inferno, Canto XXXII (Göttliche Komödie, plate 68)

Gustave Doré · published 1870 · Wood engraving · 29 cm (volume) · National Library of Poland, Warsaw

Free to use

Doré started work at fifteen as a caricaturist for the paper Le journal pour rire, working mainly in wood engraving. France made him a Knight of the Legion of Honour in 1861. This is plate 68 of the 74 in a Berlin edition of the Inferno printed in 1870.
