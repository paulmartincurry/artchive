I sent my Soul through the Invisible,
Some letter of that After-life to spell:
And by and by my Soul return'd to me,
And answer'd "I Myself am Heav'n and Hell:"

Rubáiyát — I Sent My Soul through the Invisible (LXVI–LXVII)

Omar Khayyam (trans. Edward FitzGerald) · 12th century; FitzGerald's fifth edition

Omar Khayyam was born in Nishapur on 18 May 1048 and died there on 4 December 1131, aged 83. His main interests were mathematics, astronomy, philosophy and poetry, and in one treatise he tried to work out approximate numerical solutions for cubic equations using trigonometric tables. The quatrains are attributed to him.
