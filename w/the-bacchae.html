And whose head do you hold in your hands?

The Bacchae

Euripides · 405 BCE

Translated by William Arrowsmith, 1959 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The play was written in Euripides' last years, by tradition at the court of King Archelaus in Macedonia, and he did not live to see it performed. It was first staged at the Theatre of Dionysus in Athens with two of his other plays, probably directed by his son or nephew, and it won first prize. Plutarch records a later production, after the battle of Carrhae, in which the severed head of the Roman general Crassus was used as the stage prop for the head of Pentheus.
