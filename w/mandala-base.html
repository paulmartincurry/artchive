Mandala Base

Maker unknown · early 1400s · cloisonné enamel · Diameter: 30.8 cm (12 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A ritual object of Tibetan Buddhism, the disc was the base for a three-dimensional mandala of miniature buildings. Around the lotus flowers are the Eight Buddhist Treasures, among them the wheel, the conch, the paired fish and the endless knot.
