Rustam Kills the White Div (Shahnama)

Artist unknown; text by Abu'l Qasim Firdausi · ca. 1330–40 · Ink, opaque watercolor, gold, and silver on paper · Page: H. 8 1/16 in. (20.5 cm), W. 5 1/4 in. (13.3 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Firdausi, the author of the poem, was born in Paj around 940 and died in Tus in 1020. This page is attributed to Iran, probably Isfahan, and the picture on it is a strip 4.9 centimetres high and 10.9 wide. Monroe C. Gutman left it to the Met in his will, and it arrived in 1974.
