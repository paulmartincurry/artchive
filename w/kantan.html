Ambition, length of days,
Revels and kingly rule,
All, all has ended thus, all was a dream
Dreamed while the millet cooked.

Kantan (the waking)

Attributed to Seami (Zeami Motokiyo) · c. 1400

Translated by Arthur Waley

Rosei sleeps on a wizard's pillow at an inn in Kantan and lives fifty years as an emperor while his millet cooks. The story goes back to the Pillow Tale of the Chinese writer Li Pi, who lived from 722 to 789. The play is usually given to Seami, but it is not in his own works nor in the list of his plays drawn up by his great-grandson in 1524.
