Two Pupils in Greek Dress

Thomas Eakins · 1883 · Platinum print · 36.8 × 26.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Eakins has been credited with having introduced the camera to the American art studio. He encouraged his students to use photography as an aid to understanding anatomy and the study of motion. This platinum print is dated 1883; in the late 1870s he had been introduced to Eadweard Muybridge's motion studies.
