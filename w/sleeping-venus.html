Sleeping Venus

Giorgione · c. 1510 · Oil on canvas · Gemäldegalerie Alte Meister, Dresden

Free to use

The painting is traditionally attributed to Giorgione, although it has long been widely thought that Titian completed it after Giorgione's death in 1510, and much recent opinion gives Titian the figure of Venus as well. A sitting Cupid beside her feet was painted over in the 19th century. After World War II the painting was briefly in the possession of the Soviet Union.
