Brave New World

Aldous Huxley · 1932

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Huxley wrote the novel in four months, from May to August 1931, while living in Sanary-sur-Mer in France. It was his fifth novel. He said it was inspired by the utopian novels of H. G. Wells and began as a parody of one of them. Shortly before writing it he visited Sir Alfred Mond's factory near Billingham in north-east England.
