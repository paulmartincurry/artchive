Seated Hollow Figure with Helmet

Maker unknown · 1200–800 BCE · Ceramic, cinnabar, red ochre · 34 × 31.8 × 14.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This hollow ceramic figure, coloured with cinnabar and red ochre, was made between 1200 and 800 BCE and is 34 centimetres tall. The Met attributes it to Las Bocas, a minor site in Puebla that looters plundered in the 1960s for Olmec pottery and figurines. The name was then attached to so many objects of unknown origin, some of them forgeries, that it now has little archaeological meaning.
