Modo de volar (A Way of Flying — Los Disparates, Plate 13)

Francisco de Goya y Lucientes · ca. 1815–1823 · Etching, aquatint and drypoint · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya never published this print or the rest of the series. He left the copper plates in Madrid when he moved to Bordeaux in 1824, and they stayed with his descendants until the middle of the century. The Royal Academy of Fine Arts of San Fernando bought eighteen of them in 1862 and printed 360 sets two years later. The plates and paper come from the same batch he had used for his bullfighting series, and the image measures 24.4 by 35.3 centimetres.
