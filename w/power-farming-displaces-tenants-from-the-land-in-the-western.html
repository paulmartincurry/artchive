Power Farming Displaces Tenants from the Land in the Western Dry Cotton Area, Childress County, Texas Panhandle

Dorothea Lange · June 1938 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Dorothea Lange was forty-three when she took this photograph in Childress County, in the Texas Panhandle, in June 1938. The Library of Congress published it in 2013 in the Images of America chapter of its ebook Great Photographs from the Library of Congress.
