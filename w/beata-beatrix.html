Beata Beatrix

Dante Gabriel Rossetti · 1871–72 · Oil on canvas · 87.5 × 69.3 cm (34 7/16 × 27 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Rossetti took the subject from Dante's La vita nuova and set Dante's love for the dead Beatrice beside his own for his wife and model Elizabeth Siddal, who had recently died. The Dove was his nickname for her. He insisted the picture does not show her death but a trance.
