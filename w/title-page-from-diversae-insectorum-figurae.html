Title Page from Diversae Insectorum Figurae

Wenceslaus Hollar · after 1644 · Etching on ivory laid paper · Art Institute of Chicago

This etching is the title page to a set of six plates of insects and other small creatures. Wenceslaus Hollar had been meant for the law until the Sack of Prague ruined his family. In England he set his price at fourpence an hour and timed his work with a sand-glass. Parliamentary forces captured him in 1645 at the siege of Basing House; he escaped, and worked in Antwerp until 1652.
