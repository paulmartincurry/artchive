Relief Panel

Maker unknown · ca. 883–859 BCE · Gypsum alabaster · 234.3 × 233.7 × 11.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The panel is a slab of gypsum alabaster more than 2.3 metres high and as wide, only 11 centimetres thick. It comes from Nimrud, the ancient Kalhu. John D. Rockefeller Jr. gave it to the museum in 1932.
