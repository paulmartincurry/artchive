Master of the Codex Manesse · c. 1305–1315 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · Universitätsbibliothek Heidelberg

In 1888, after long bargaining, the codex was sold to the Bibliotheca Palatina of Heidelberg. The money came from a public subscription headed by William I and Otto von Bismarck.
