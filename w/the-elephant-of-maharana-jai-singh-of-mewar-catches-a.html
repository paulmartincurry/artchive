The Elephant of Maharana Jai Singh of Mewar Catches a Horse by the Tail

Maker unknown · c. 1680 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page: 31.1 × 49.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A word written above the scene, Pakharao, is probably the elephant's name; the inscription on the back says the painting shows Maharana Jai Singh's attack. Jai Singh ruled Mewar from 1680 to 1698 and built the Dhebar lake, also called Jaisamand. In the picture a servant tries to light a firecracker to frighten the elephant off the horse.
