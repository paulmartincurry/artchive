Je sais les cieux crevant en éclairs, et les trombes,
Et les ressacs, et les courants, je sais le soir,
L'aube exaltée ainsi qu'un peuple de colombes,
Et j'ai vu quelquefois ce que l'homme a cru voir.

Le Bateau ivre (opening)

Arthur Rimbaud · 1871

Rimbaud wrote the poem in the summer of 1871, aged sixteen, at his childhood home in Charleville. In September he sent it in a letter to Paul Verlaine to introduce himself, and soon afterwards joined Verlaine in Paris. Jules Verne's Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas, published in book form in 1870, is known to have been the source of many of its images. Vladimir Nabokov translated it into Russian in 1928.
