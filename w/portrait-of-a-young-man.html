Portrait of a Young Man

Agnolo Bronzino · 1530s · Oil on wood · 95.6 × 74.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

It is unclear who the sitter is. Infrared examination shows that Bronzino made deliberate changes to the portrait as he worked. The panel came to the museum in 1929 with the bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer.
