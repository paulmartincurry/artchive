A Word to Tramps

Lucy Parsons · October 4, 1884

This article ran in The Alarm, the Chicago newspaper of the International Working People's Association, in the paper's first year, when Parsons was one of its main contributors. Reprinted on its own, it sold more than 10,000 copies between May and November 1885. She was about thirty-three and earned her living as a seamstress, with her own business making women's and children's clothing.
