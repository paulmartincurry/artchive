Stray Birds

Rabindranath Tagore · 1916

As a boy Tagore was taught at home, and English was his least favourite subject. At seventeen he was sent to school in Brighton because his father wanted him to become a barrister. He read law briefly at University College London and went back to Bengal in 1880 without a degree. From 1890 he managed the family's estates, travelling the Padma River on the family barge to collect rents. He was fifty-five in 1916.
