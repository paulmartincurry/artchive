The Artchive
Go to the Blue Sea, look along the shore
At all the old white bones forsaken —
New ghosts are wailing there now with the old,
Loudest in the dark sky of a stormy day.

A Song of War-Chariots (closing)

Du Fu · c. 750; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Du Fu wrote this poem around 750, and it is one of his earliest surviving works. It gives voice to a conscript soldier in the imperial army. He had taken the imperial examination, never attempted it again, and instead petitioned the emperor directly in 751, 754 and probably again in 755.
