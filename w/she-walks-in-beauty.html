She walks in Beauty, like the night
Of cloudless climes and starry skies;
And all that's best of dark and bright
Meet in her aspect and her eyes:

She Walks in Beauty

George Gordon, Lord Byron · 1814

On 11 June 1814 Byron went to a party in London where one of the guests was Anne Beatrix Wilmot, the wife of his first cousin Sir Robert Wilmot. The poem is believed to have been written the next morning. He was twenty-six. It was printed the following year in Hebrew Melodies, published in London by John Murray.
