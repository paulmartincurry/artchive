Catching Fireflies Beneath a Willow Tree

Kitagawa Utamaro · c. 1796–97 · Triptych: color woodblock print · Sheet: 38.2 × 76.8 cm (15 1/16 × 30 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is the left sheet of a three-sheet print, issued by the publisher Izumiya Ichibei and stamped with the censor's seal kiwame. A law of 1790 required every print offered for sale to carry such a seal, and the rules tightened over the following years. In 1804 Utamaro was arrested and manacled for fifty days over prints showing the military ruler Toyotomi Hideyoshi, and he died two years later. Jeptha H. Wade gave this sheet to Cleveland in 1975.
