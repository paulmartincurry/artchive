Impression, Sunrise

Claude Monet · 1872 · Oil on canvas · Musée Marmottan Monet, Paris

Free to use

In 2014 an astronomer and his colleagues worked out from the position of the Sun, the tide tables, weather records and the direction of the smoke that Claude Monet painted this view of Le Havre, his home town, on the morning of 13 November 1872 at about 7:35. Asked for a title for the 1874 exhibition catalogue, he had the word Impression put down, and a critic's review took the name of the movement from it. The canvas was stolen in 1985 and recovered in 1990. Measured with a photometer, the Sun is exactly as bright as the sky around it.
