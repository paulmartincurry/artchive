Ivory-Billed Woodpecker (The Birds of America, Plate 66)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1829 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 99.7 × 67.2 cm (39 1/4 × 26 7/16 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The ivory-billed woodpecker is one of three birds in the book that may now be extinct. Every bird in the book was drawn life size, and this sheet measures 99.7 by 67.2 centimetres.
