Riders to the Sea — No man at all can be living for ever (close)

J. M. Synge · 1904

The play was first performed on 25 February 1904 at the Molesworth Hall in Dublin. It is a tragedy in one act, set at Inishmaan in the Aran Islands. Synge had been encouraged to visit the islands by his friend William Butler Yeats.
