Build your cities on the slope of Vesuvius! Send your ships into unexplored seas! Live in war with your equals and with yourselves!

The Joyful Wisdom — Live in Danger (283)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1882; English 1910

Translated by Thomas Common

The book was first published in 1882. For a second edition in 1887, after Zarathustra and Beyond Good and Evil, Nietzsche added a fifth book and an appendix of songs.
