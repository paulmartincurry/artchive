Therefore, O Man, beware, and look toward the end of things that be,
The last of sights, the last of days; and no man's life account as gain
Ere the full tale be finished and the darkness find him without pain.

Oedipus the King — The Last Chorus

Sophocles · c. 429 BC

Translated by Gilbert Murray, 1911

Oedipus the King is probably the fourth of Sophocles' seven surviving plays. In his Poetics, Aristotle cites it several times to show how tragedy works. The name Oedipus, given to him as an infant by the shepherd who unbound his pinned feet, means swollen foot.
