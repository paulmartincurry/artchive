Manzanar from Guard Tower, View West

Ansel Adams · 1943 · Safety film negative · 4 × 5 in. · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Adams photographed Manzanar in 1943 at the invitation of the newly appointed camp director, his friend Ralph Merritt. The title is copied from Adams's caption on the negative sleeve; there is no original print, and the Library of Congress made one. Adams gave his Manzanar collection to the Library in 1965.
