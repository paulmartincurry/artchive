Henry Sprang upon the Wolf's Back (Old French Fairy Tales)

Virginia Frances Sterrett · published 1920 · Plate (line block print)

Free to use

Virginia Frances Sterrett got her first commission, this book of tales by the Comtesse de Ségur, at nineteen, shortly after she was diagnosed with tuberculosis. She had left the Art Institute of Chicago after a year to support her family when her mother fell ill, and was working at an advertising agency. The Penn Publishing Company brought the book out in 1920.
