‘At no town will I stop to lodge, nor near cool fountain tarry;
The mothers would for water come, and recognise their children;
And know each other man and wife; nor would there be more parting.’

Charon and the Souls

Traditional Greek folk song · printed 1896

Translated by Lucy M. J. Garnett

Lucy Garnett translated this song from Thessaly, from the collection of Oikonomides, for Greek Folk Poesy in 1896. It is one of several songs in the book about Charon, who comes for the dead on horseback.
