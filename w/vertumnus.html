Vertumnus (Emperor Rudolf II as Roman God of the Seasons)

Giuseppe Arcimboldo · 1590–1591 · Oil on panel · Skokloster Castle, Sweden

Free to use

Rudolf II commissioned this portrait of himself while Arcimboldo was employed at his court, and it was presented to him in 1591. The ear is an ear of corn, a crop from the New World. The Swedish army seized the panel after the Thirty Years' War, and there is no trace of it again until 1845, when it was identified at Skokloster Castle. It was in poor condition by 1988 and was treated with enzymes taken from Antarctic krill. The panel measures 70 by 58 centimetres.
