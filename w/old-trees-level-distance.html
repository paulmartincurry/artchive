Old Trees, Level Distance (detail)

Guo Xi · ca. 1080 · Handscroll, ink and color on silk · 35.6 × 104.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting itself is about one metre long, but with the inscriptions and seals added by later owners the scroll runs past eight and a half metres. Its first recorded owner was Emperor Huizong, whose seal is on it. Zhao Mengfu saw it in Yuan-dynasty Beijing, the Qing emperors Qianlong, Jiaqing and Xuantong stamped it, and after the Qing court it passed to the painter Zhang Daqian and then to John M. Crawford Jr., who gave it to the Met in 1981.
