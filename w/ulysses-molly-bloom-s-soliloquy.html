Ulysses — Molly Bloom's soliloquy (closing)

James Joyce · 1922

Joyce wrote Ulysses over seven years, from 1914 to 1921, much of it in Zurich during the war. He set it on 16 June 1904, the day of his first outing with Nora Barnacle. He was set on publishing it on his fortieth birthday, and Sylvia Beach got the first three copies from the printer on the morning of 2 February 1922, bound in a blue chosen to match the Greek flag. Through the 1920s the United States Post Office burned copies, and Britain banned the book until 1936.
