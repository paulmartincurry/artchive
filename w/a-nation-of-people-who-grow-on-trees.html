A Nation of People Who Grow on Trees (Aja'ib al-Makhluqat of Qazwini)

Maker unknown · c. 1550–60 · Ink, opaque watercolor and gold on paper · 22.2 × 10.8 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

In the opening essay of his book, Qazwini argues that the strange things it describes should be met with wonder rather than fear, whatever they look like. He wrote while serving as a judge and professor at Wasit, near Baghdad, and died there in 1283. This page, 22.2 cm high, comes from a copy made around 1550–60, perhaps in Iran.
