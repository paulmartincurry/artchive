Young Bull

Albrecht Dürer · c. 1496, erroneously inscribed in another hand 1508 · Pen and black ink on ivory laid paper · 17.5 × 14 cm (6 15/16 × 5 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The drawing was probably made from a live animal. Loose lines around the right hind foot, back, muzzle and horns show that Dürer first drew the steer in a slightly different position, with its mouth open. He reused its hindquarters in the farmyard of his engraving The Prodigal Son amid the Swine. The date 1508 on the sheet was written by someone else and is wrong. The paper is 17.5 by 14 centimetres.
