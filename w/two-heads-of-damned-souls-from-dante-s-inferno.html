Two Heads of Damned Souls from Dante's Inferno

Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · 1770–78 · Oil on canvas · Approx. 40.6 × 29.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Fuseli probably painted these head studies while living in Italy between 1770 and 1778, one on each side of an unprimed canvas. Both were engraved as illustrations for Johann Caspar Lavater's book on physiognomy. William F. E. Gurley of Chicago bought the canvas at a London sale in 1914, in a group of nineteen works by Fuseli, and bequeathed it to the Art Institute in 1943.
