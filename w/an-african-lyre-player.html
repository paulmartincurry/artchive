An African Lyre Player

Maker unknown · c. 1640–60 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page: 40.5 × 28.9 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The instrument is a bowl lyre called an ndongo. Many Africans, mainly from Ethiopia, settled in the Deccan and worked there as soldiers, mercenaries and administrators. Whether this album page shows a particular person or a type of musician is not known. On the back are six lines of Persian poetry. The page belonged to Jacob Goldschmidt in Berlin in the early 1900s and to Ralph and Catherine Benkaim from 1966.
