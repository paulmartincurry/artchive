I know the truth! Renounce all others!
There’s no need for anyone to fight.

I Know the Truth

Marina Tsvetaeva · October 3, 1915 (Published 1921)

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2010 · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Marina Tsvetaeva wrote this poem in 1915, the year she turned twenty-three. Her husband, Sergei Efron, had volunteered for the front the year before. She was living in the Moscow apartment she kept from 1914 to 1922, which is now a museum.
