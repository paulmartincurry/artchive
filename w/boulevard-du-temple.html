Boulevard du Temple

Louis-Jacques-Mandé Daguerre · Spring 1838 · Daguerreotype

Free to use

Daguerre took this from a window of his studio at eight in the morning. The exposure needed four to five minutes, so of everyone on a busy street only two who kept still were recorded, a bootblack and his customer at the lower left. In October 1839 he gave the plate to King Ludwig I of Bavaria as part of a framed triptych. A restoration attempted after 1970 ruined the original, and facsimiles are now made from copies Beaumont Newhall took in 1936 or 1937.
