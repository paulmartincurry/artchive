I ne'er was struck before that hour
With love so sudden and so sweet.
Her face it bloomed like a sweet flower
And stole my heart away complete.

First Love

John Clare · c. 1841-1864; published 1920

John Clare spent the last twenty-two years of his life in Northampton General Lunatic Asylum, committed at the end of 1841, and the asylum's head, Thomas Octavius Prichard, encouraged him to keep writing. Earl Fitzwilliam paid for his keep at the rate charged for the poor. Months before he was committed, Clare had walked about 80 miles home from an asylum in Essex, believing he was married to his childhood love Mary Joyce, who had died three years earlier. He died in Northampton in 1864.
