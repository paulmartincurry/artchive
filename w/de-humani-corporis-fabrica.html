De humani corporis fabrica (Of the Structure of the Human Body)

Andreas Vesalius & Jan Steven van Calcar · 1555 · Woodcut · 38.7 × 26 cm (page) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This page comes from the second edition of the book, printed in 1555. Vesalius was twenty-eight when the first edition came out; he had the woodblocks carried to Basel so that Johannes Oporinus could print it. The Met names Jan Steven van Calcar as the artist, though modern scholars generally attribute the illustrations to the studio of Titian.
