Four Captive Demons

Maker unknown · 1470–1500 · Watercolor and gold on silk · Painting 34 × 20.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met dates this painting on silk to 1470–1500 and places it in Iran or Central Asia; it is 34 cm high. Chained demons also fill the album of paintings signed Muhammad Siyah Qalam, some of them on silk, which the Topkapı Palace inventories record as treasure from Sultan Selim I's Persian campaign of 1514.
