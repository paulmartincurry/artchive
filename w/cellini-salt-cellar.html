Cellini Salt Cellar

Benvenuto Cellini · 1540–1543 · Gold, ebony, ivory, vitreous enamel · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use with credit. Photo: Jononmac46, CC BY-SA 4.0

The gold was hammered into shape by hand, not cast in a mould. Benvenuto Cellini made the piece at the court of Francis I of France with five assistants, two Italian, two French and one German, and finished it in 1543; by his own account the price was 1,000 scudi. It sits on bearings so that it could be rolled along a banquet table. It was almost melted down in 1562. On 11 May 2003 it was stolen from the museum in Vienna, and it was found in January 2006 in a lead box buried in a forest about 90 kilometres away.
