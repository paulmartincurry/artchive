Moon Lute (Yueqin)

Maker unknown · 1800s · Rosewood, wutong (paulownia) wood, and gut · 58.4 x 35.6 cm (23 x 14 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Its likeness to the full moon, yue, gives the yueqin its name. It was played in folk ensembles and in Beijing and folk operas, and is known in Japan as gekkin.
