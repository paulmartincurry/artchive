Astrolabe

Regiomontanus · 1462 · Copper alloy · 13.5 cm high · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1462 Regiomontanus was about twenty-six and travelling through northern Italy in the household of the papal legate Basilios Bessarion, copying mathematical and astronomical manuscripts for the largest private library in Europe. Later, at the court of Matthias Corvinus in Buda, he built another astrolabe for the king. This one is 13.5 centimetres high, weighs 282.7 grams and is catalogued in ten parts. The museum acquired it for The Cloisters in 2025.
