These are the times that try men's souls. The summer soldier and the sunshine patriot will, in this crisis, shrink from the service of their country; but he that stands it now, deserves the love and thanks of man and woman.

The American Crisis, No. I (opening)

Thomas Paine · 1776

Thomas Paine had been in America just over two years when he began this series of pamphlets, which ran from 1776 to 1783. He landed in Philadelphia on November 30, 1774, with a letter of recommendation from Benjamin Franklin, after a voyage on which typhoid fever killed five passengers, and was so sick that he had to be carried off the ship. In England he had been a staymaker and an excise officer, twice dismissed, and had sold his household goods to stay out of debtors' prison. He was thirty-nine in 1776.
