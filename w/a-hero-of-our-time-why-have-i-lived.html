My whole past life I live again in memory, and, involuntarily, I ask myself: ‘why have I lived—for what purpose was I born?’...

A Hero of Our Time — Why Have I Lived? (Princess Mary)

Mikhail Lermontov · 1840

Translated by J. H. Wisdom and Marr Murray

Lermontov was an officer of the Life-Guard Hussars, and a painter as well as a writer. A poem he wrote after the death of Pushkin in 1837 led to his first exile to the Caucasus, and it was during that exile that he worked on this novel. In 1841 he was killed in a duel with a fellow officer, Nikolai Martynov. He was twenty-six.
