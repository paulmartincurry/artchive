So farwel Hope, and with Hope farwel Fear,
Farwel Remorse: all Good to me is lost;
Evil be thou my Good;

Paradise Lost — Evil Be Thou My Good (Book IV)

John Milton · 1667

Paradise Lost was the first book Samuel Simmons registered for publication in his own name, in 1667. The printing business had been run by his mother, Mary, since the death of his father, Matthew, in 1654; Matthew had printed Milton's earlier work. In 1674 Milton rearranged the poem's ten books into twelve.
