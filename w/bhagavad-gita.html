Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds.

Bhagavad Gita (Chapter XI)

Vyasa (Sanskrit Epic) · ca. 200 BCE

Translated by J. Robert Oppenheimer (his own rendering of Gita 11.32) · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Gita is 700 verses in eighteen chapters, and it sits inside the sixth book of the Mahabharata. Tradition gives it to the sage Vyasa, but scholars regard it as the work of several authors, and Vyasa is also a title meaning compiler. It came out of an oral tradition, and its date of composition is unresolved. The manuscripts of the Gita vary only slightly from one another, unlike those of the rest of the epic.
