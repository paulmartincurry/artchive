Theseus Fighting the Centaur

Antonio Canova · 1804–19 · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Napoleon ordered this group for the Corso in Milan. Emperor Franz I bought it instead for the Theseus Temple in the Volksgarten in Vienna, and it moved to the Kunsthistorisches Museum in 1891. Antonio Canova was the son of a stonecutter and was raised by his grandfather, a stonemason.
