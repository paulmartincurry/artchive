The Rhinoceros

Albrecht Dürer · 1515 · Laid paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington, DC

Albrecht Dürer never saw the rhinoceros. He worked in Nuremberg from a letter and a sketch by an unknown artist, sent from Lisbon, where the animal had landed on 20 May 1515 after 120 days at sea from Goa. It drowned later that year, chained to the deck of a ship wrecked off the coast of Liguria on its way to Pope Leo X. The sheet is 23.5 by 29.8 centimetres.
