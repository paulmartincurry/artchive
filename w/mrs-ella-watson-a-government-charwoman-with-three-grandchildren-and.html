Mrs. Ella Watson, a Government Charwoman, with Three Grandchildren and Her Adopted Daughter

Gordon Parks · August 1942 · Safety film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Parks joined the Historical Section of the Farm Security Administration under Roy Stryker in January 1942. That year he photographed Ella Watson, who worked on the cleaning crew of the FSA building, and another picture from the series is called American Gothic. Watson was born in Washington, D.C., in 1883.
