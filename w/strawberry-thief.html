Strawberry Thief

William Morris · 1883 · Indigo-discharged and block-printed cotton · Victoria and Albert Museum, London

The birds are the thrushes William Morris found stealing fruit in the kitchen garden at Kelmscott Manor, his house in Oxfordshire. He first tried indigo discharge printing in 1875 and did not succeed until 1881, after moving into his factory at Merton Abbey. This was the first design in the technique to add red and yellow to the blue and white ground. Printing took days, which made it one of Morris & Co.'s most expensive cottons, and it sold well all the same.
