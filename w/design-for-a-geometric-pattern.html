Design for a Geometric Pattern

Liubov Popova · 1923–24 · Watercolor and gouache, pen and ink over a graphite underdrawing · Sheet: 12 3/16 × 14 1/16 in. (31 × 35.7 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1923 Liubov Popova began making fabric designs for the First State Textile Printing Works in Moscow, and this sheet, about thirty-one by thirty-six centimetres, is probably one of them. Two years earlier she and four fellow Constructivists had declared that easel painting was to be abandoned. Her father had been a textile merchant. She died of scarlet fever in Moscow in May 1924, aged thirty-five, two days after her son.
