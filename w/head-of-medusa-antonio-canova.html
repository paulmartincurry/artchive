Head of Medusa

Antonio Canova · c. 1801 · Plaster · 31 × 31.8 × 30.5 cm (12 1/4 × 12 9/16 × 12 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It is a partial model for Canova's marble Perseus Holding the Head of Medusa. Perseus cut off her head while she slept, approaching her by watching a mirrored shield.
