The Sultanganj Buddha

Maker unknown · ca. 500–700 · Bronze · Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery

Free to use

A railway engineer, E. B. Harris, found this figure in 1861 while building a station at Sultanganj; its right foot lay ten feet down, under a floor he thought had been laid to hide the statue after it was toppled. It stands 2.3 metres high, weighs over 500 kilograms and was cast in unrefined copper by the lost wax method. Rice husks in the clay core allowed it to be radiocarbon dated. A Birmingham ironmongery manufacturer, Samuel Thornton, paid to ship it to England and offered it to the town's planned art museum in 1864.
