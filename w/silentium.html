A thought once spoken is a lie:
troubling the streams, you cloud them –
drink from them – and be silent.

Silentium!

Fyodor Tyutchev · 1830

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Tyutchev wrote the poem in 1830. He had joined the Foreign Office after university and in 1822 went to Munich as a trainee diplomat at the Russian legation. Early in the twentieth century the composer Georgi Catoire set the poem to music.
