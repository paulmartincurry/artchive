Letitia Felix (Camera Work 3)

Clarence H. White · 1903 · Photogravure · The Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This was one of five White photographs reproduced in Camera Work number 3, in July 1903. Stieglitz had invited White and ten others to be charter members of the Photo-Secession shortly before starting the magazine. White moved to New York in 1906 and taught at Columbia University Teachers College until 1925; Margaret Bourke-White and Dorothea Lange were among his students.
