Enamel Plaque from the Altar Frontal of Saint Martin at Orense (Spain)

Maker unknown · 1174–1213 (probably before 1188) · copper: engraved, stippled, gilded; champlevé enamel · Average: 13.7 x 8.9 cm (5 3/8 x 3 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

A nearly identical plaque is in the cathedral treasury of Orense in Galicia, so this one was probably part of the great altar frontal Bishop Alfonso commissioned there. Fifty-three plaques survive at the cathedral; others, this one among them, may have been taken away in the Napoleonic era.
