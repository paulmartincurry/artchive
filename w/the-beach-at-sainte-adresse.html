The Beach at Sainte-Adresse

Claude Monet · 1867 · Oil on canvas · 75.8 × 102.5 cm (29 13/16 × 40 5/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Monet spent the summer of 1867 with his aunt at Sainte-Adresse, near Le Havre, where he had grown up. The railway was turning the fishing village into a seaside resort for tourists.
