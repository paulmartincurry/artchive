Odilon Redon · 1885 · Lithograph on paper · 29 × 22.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Rodolphe Bresdin taught Redon etching and lithography in Bordeaux. Redon published his first album of lithographs, Dans le Rêve, in 1879, six years before this print. The image measures 29 by 22.5 centimetres.
