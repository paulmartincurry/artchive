Manuel, the Young Shrimp-Picker, Biloxi, Mississippi

Lewis Hine · February 1911 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The caption is the National Child Labor Committee's own, from its card for Hine number 1984, in the album on canneries. The attribution to Hine rests on the print's provenance. His photographs supported the committee's lobbying against child labour, and the federal Children's Bureau was created in 1912.
