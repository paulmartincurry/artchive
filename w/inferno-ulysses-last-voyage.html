Consider ye the seed from which ye sprang;
Ye were not made to live like unto brutes,
But for pursuit of virtue and of knowledge.

Inferno — Ulysses' Last Voyage (Canto XXVI)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1306–1310

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

Dante was exiled from Florence in 1302, after his faction was defeated by the Black Guelphs, and he spent nearly all of the last fifteen years of his life on the Comedy. Many, but not all, scholars agree that the Inferno was begun in 1306 or 1307 and completed by 1310; some argue that he started as late as 1313. It circulated in fragments for years before the whole poem was published, and the earliest citations of it were written as early as 1315.
