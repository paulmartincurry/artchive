The Bull

Franz Marc · 1912 · Woodcut on ivory wove Japanese paper · Image/block: 15.9 × 22.1 cm (6 5/16 × 8 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Marc made some sixty prints in all, in woodcut and lithography. In 1912, the year of this one, he met Robert Delaunay, whose use of colour and the Cubist method became a major influence on his work. The block is 22 centimetres wide.
