Majuscole No. 106 (Manuale Tipografico, page 250)

Giambattista Bodoni · published 1818

Free to use

Giambattista Bodoni died in 1813, and this specimen book of his typefaces was published five years later by his widow, Margherita Dall’Aglio Bodoni. He had worked as a printer and type designer in Parma, and he designed many typefaces, each one in a large range of type sizes. An earlier Manuale tipografico had appeared in 1788.
