The Tetons and the Snake River

Ansel Adams · 1942 · Photograph

Ansel Adams took this photograph from high ground in Grand Teton National Park, Wyoming. It was encoded into the grooves of the Voyager Golden Record, two time capsules that NASA launched in 1977 and sent into interstellar space. A mural-sized print sold for $988,000 at Sotheby's New York on 14 December 2020, the highest price paid for any work by Adams.
