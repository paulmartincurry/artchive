Seventeen years ago you said
Something that sounded like Good-bye;
And everybody thinks that you are dead,
But I.

À Quoi Bon Dire

Charlotte Mew · 1916

The Farmer's Bride, Mew's first collection, was published in 1916 in chapbook format by the Poetry Bookshop. In the United States it was entitled Saturday Market and published in 1921 by Macmillan.
