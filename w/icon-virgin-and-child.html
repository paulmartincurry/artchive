Icon: Virgin and Child (after Santa Maria Maggiore)

Maker unknown · Late 17th-early 18th century · Distemper, gesso and cloth on wood · H x W x D: 34.2 x 47 x 17 cm (13 7/16 x 18 1/2 x 6 11/16 in.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

The deep green and blue are typical of the period. Mary's gesture, the child's book and the star-ornamented shawl come from a Jesuit copy of an Italian engraving of the Santa Maria Maggiore icon in Rome.
