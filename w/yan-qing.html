Yan Qing (One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin)

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1827–30 · Color woodblock print; oban · 38.2 × 25.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kuniyoshi's prints went on to Europe. Claude Monet owned twelve of them among the 231 Japanese prints in his house at Giverny, and Auguste Rodin's collection held 44 sheets by him. The Art Institute of Chicago bought this sheet through the Kate S. Buckingham Endowment.
