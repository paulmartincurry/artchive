The wound caused by fire will heal within,
But not the scar left by the tongue.

Tirukkuṛaḷ

Thiruvalluvar · ca. 1st–5th Century CE

Translated by P. S. Sundaram, 1990 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Kural is made of 1,330 couplets of seven words each, in three books on virtue, wealth and love. The text does not name its author, and almost nothing certain is known of the man tradition calls Valluvar. For centuries it was passed on by palm-leaf manuscript and by word of mouth. It was first printed in 1812, from wooden blocks made from palm-leaf scripts, and has not been out of print since.
