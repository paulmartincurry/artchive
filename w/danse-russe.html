“I am lonely, lonely.
I was born to be lonely.
I am best so!”

Danse Russe

William Carlos Williams · 1917

In 1915 Williams began to associate with the New York group of artists and writers known as The Others, founded by the poet Alfred Kreymborg and the artist Man Ray; Wallace Stevens, Mina Loy, Marianne Moore and Marcel Duchamp were among them. He and his wife lived at 9 Ridge Road in Rutherford, New Jersey, and he had trained in pediatrics, with advanced study in Leipzig.
