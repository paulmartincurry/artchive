Perseus Starting from the Cave of the Gorgons

Attributed to Henry Fuseli (Johann Heinrich Füssli) · c. 1816 · Oil and oil wash, over graphite and with touches of pen and black ink, on tan laid paper, laid down on off-white Japanese paper · 55.2 × 67.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Art Institute of Chicago gives this unfinished painting to Fuseli by attribution. Its source was Hesiod's Shield of Heracles, which was translated into English in 1815, about a year before the date assigned to the picture. It is painted in oil and oil wash over graphite on paper. William F. E. Gurley of Chicago bought it at auction in London in October 1914, and it was given to the museum in 1944.
