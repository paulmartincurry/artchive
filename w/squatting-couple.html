Squatting Couple (The Family)

Egon Schiele · 1918 · Oil on canvas · 152.5 × 162.5 cm · Belvedere, Vienna

Free to use with credit. Photo: Belvedere, Vienna, CC BY-SA 4.0

This was one of the last oil paintings Schiele made, and parts of it appear unfinished. He first called it Crouching Couple. His wife Edith was expecting their first child, and at a later point he painted a child, modelled by his nephew Toni, over a bouquet of flowers. Edith died of Spanish flu on 28 October 1918, six months into her pregnancy, and Schiele died of it three days later.
