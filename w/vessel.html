Christopher Dresser · 1879–82 · Glazed earthenware · H × diam.: 24.5 × 14 cm (9 5/8 × 5 1/2 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

This stirrup-spout bottle is 24.5 centimetres tall. In the years Christopher Dresser designed for Linthorpe, he also ran a wholesale import business with Charles Holme, from a warehouse at 7 Farringdon Road in London. A large collection of his work is now held in Middlesbrough, the pottery's home town, at the Dorman Museum. Cooper Hewitt bought this bottle through its Decorative Arts Association Acquisition Fund.
