The Marriage of Heaven and Hell — The Voice of the Devil

William Blake · 1790–1793

Blake composed the work between 1790 and 1793. Like his other books it was published as printed sheets from etched plates, and the plates were then coloured by Blake and his wife, Catherine. The title refers to Emanuel Swedenborg's Heaven and Hell, published in Latin 33 years earlier.
