Death and Life (Tod und Leben)

Gustav Klimt · 1910 (reworked 1915) · Oil on canvas · Leopold Museum, Vienna

Klimt began this canvas in 1908 and did not call it finished until 1915. It had already won first prize at the world exhibition in Rome in 1911 and been shown five times when he went back into it, changing the background from gold to grey and adding mosaic patterns. The canvas is nearly square, about 1.8 metres high and 2 metres wide.
