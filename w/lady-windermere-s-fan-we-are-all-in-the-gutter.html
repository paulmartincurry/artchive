No, we are all in the gutter, but some of us are looking at the stars.

Lady Windermere's Fan — we are all in the gutter

Oscar Wilde · 1892

The play was first performed on Saturday, 20 February 1892, at the St James's Theatre in London. Wilde's two earlier plays had found little success. For this one he took a percentage, and earned £7,000 from it in the first year alone.
