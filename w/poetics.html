Poetics (VI)

Aristotle · ca. 335 BCE

Translated by S. H. Butcher, 1895

The Poetics was lost to the Western world for centuries. Medieval readers had it only through a Latin translation, made in 1256, of an Arabic commentary by Averroes, and an accurate translation from the Greek made in 1278 was virtually ignored. A Latin version was first published in 1498, and the Greek original was printed in 1508. Only around a third of what Aristotle wrote has survived, none of it intended for publication.
