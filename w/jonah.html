Jonah

Albert Pinkham Ryder · c. 1885–95 · Oil on canvas mounted on fiberboard · 69.2 × 87.3 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum

Free to use

Ryder reworked this image so many times that the paint layers are still soft to the touch after more than a century. He often spent ten years or more on a painting, building it up in layers of paint, resin and varnish laid one on top of another. The collector John Gellatly gave it to the Smithsonian.
