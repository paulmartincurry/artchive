The Poor (The Saltimbanques)

Pablo Picasso · 1905, printed 1913 · Etching · Plate 23.5 × 17.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Poor was etched in 1905 and printed in 1913 as part of the series called The Saltimbanques. The plate measures 23.5 by 17.7 centimetres and sits on a sheet 50.8 centimetres tall, watermarked Van Gelder Zonen. Picasso was a struggling artist in these early years and frequently depicted figures living in poverty.
