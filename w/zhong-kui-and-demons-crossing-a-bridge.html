Zhong Kui and Demons Crossing a Bridge

Attributed to Dai Jin · 16th century · Ink and color on silk · 197.4 × 118.6 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

According to Song sources, the Tang emperor Xuanzong fell gravely ill and dreamed of a large ghost in an official's hat who caught a smaller ghost, tore out its eye and ate it, and named himself Zhong Kui. The emperor woke up well and ordered the court painter Wu Daozi to paint the demon queller. This Ming scroll, nearly two metres tall, was bought by Charles Lang Freer in Beijing in 1911, probably from a shop in the antiques district of Liulichang.
