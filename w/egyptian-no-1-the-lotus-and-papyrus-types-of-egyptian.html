Egyptian No. 1: The Lotus and Papyrus, Types of Egyptian Ornament (The Grammar of Ornament)

Owen Jones · 1856 · Lithograph · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Owen Jones published The Grammar of Ornament in 1856, with twenty chapters of plates and a preface of 37 general principles on form and colour. Francis Bedford drew the plates on stone and Day & Son printed them in London. Jones had travelled to Egypt as a young architect with the Frenchman Jules Goury to study the ancient sites.
