Oculus of the Pantheon, Rome

Architect unknown; built under Hadrian · c. AD 126 · Roman concrete · Diameter and height 142 feet

Free to use with credit. Photo: Ank Kumar, CC BY-SA 4.0

The dome is Roman concrete, and almost two thousand years after it was built it is still the largest unreinforced concrete dome in the world. The opening at the top is the main source of natural light. The height to the oculus and the diameter of the interior circle are the same, 142 feet. The present building was completed under the emperor Hadrian and probably dedicated around AD 126.
