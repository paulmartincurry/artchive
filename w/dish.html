Dish

Maker unknown · c. 1600 · Fritware with underglaze design in black slip, Kubachi ware · Diameter: 34.2 cm (13 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Many dishes like this were found in peasant houses in the town of Kubachi in the Caucasus, so the whole group is called Kubachi ware. They were probably made in northwestern Iran.
