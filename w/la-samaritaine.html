La Samaritaine

Alphonse Mucha · 1897 · Color lithograph from multiple stones on paper · Sheet: 175.2 × 59.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Edmond Rostand's La Samaritaine, a verse retelling of the story of the Samaritan woman at the well, was one of the plays Sarah Bernhardt staged at the Théâtre de la Renaissance. She had paid 700,000 francs for the theatre and was its artistic director and lead actress from 1893 until 1899. The poster was printed from multiple lithographic stones and stands 175 centimetres tall.
