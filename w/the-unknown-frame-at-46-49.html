The Unknown, frame at 46:49

Tod Browning · 1927 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Tod Browning shot the film between 7 February and 18 March 1927 on a budget of 217,000 dollars. For the knife and cigarette work, Lon Chaney was paired with a real armless double, Paul Desmuke, whose feet were filmed in frame with Chaney's upper body and face. The film was long thought lost until a print turned up at the Cinémathèque Française in 1968, where hundreds of film cans were marked l'inconnu.
