Tod (Ein Weberaufstand)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1897–98 · Lithograph · Sheet: 42.3 × 32.9 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Death is one of the three lithographs in Kollwitz's Weavers cycle; the other three sheets are etchings. The Statens Museum for Kunst dates this one 1897–1898 and notes that the cataloguer Sievers gives 1897. She was about thirty, and the cycle became her most widely acclaimed work.
