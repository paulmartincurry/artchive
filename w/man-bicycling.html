Man Bicycling

Étienne-Jules Marey · 1890s · Gelatin silver lantern slide (chronophotograph) · 8.5 × 10 cm (3 3/8 × 3 15/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

For these pictures Marey fitted an ordinary camera with a rotating metal disk, slotted at regular intervals, behind an open lens. The subject moved in front of a dark background, and each pass of a slot added another image to the same plate. He was a member of the Académie Nationale de Médecine and probably made this one for a campaign to design sports clothing that would reduce fatigue. In 1894 he published Le Mouvement.
