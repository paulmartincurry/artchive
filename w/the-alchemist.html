The Alchemist

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · after 1558 · Engraving; first state of three · Sheet 33.5 × 44.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bruegel drew the design and Philips Galle cut it into the copper; it was published in Antwerp by Hieronymus Cock, the most important print publisher in northern Europe. From 1555 to 1563 Bruegel worked mainly as a designer of prints for Cock, more than forty of them, and with one exception he never worked the plates himself. This is a first state, of three.
