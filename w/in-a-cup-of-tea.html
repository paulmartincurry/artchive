In a Cup of Tea

Lafcadio Hearn · 1902

Kottō came out in 1902, the last year of Hearn's lectures on English literature at Tokyo Imperial University; selections from those lectures were published after his death, in 1915 and 1916. He died of heart failure in Tokyo in 1904, at fifty-four, and is buried in Zōshigaya Cemetery.
