The astronomer is as blind to the significant phenomena, or the significance of phenomena, as the wood-sawyer who wears glasses to defend his eyes from sawdust. The question is not what you look at, but what you see.

Journal, 5 August 1851

Henry David Thoreau · August 5, 1851

In 1850 Thoreau moved into a house at 255 Main Street, where he lived until his death. He worked in his family's pencil factory alongside his writing for most of his adult life. He dated this entry August 5, 7.30 in the evening, with the moon half full.
