Madame Cézanne in a Red Dress

Paul Cézanne · 1888–90 · Oil on canvas · 45 7/8 × 35 1/4 in. (116.5 × 89.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sitter is Hortense Fiquet, born in 1850, who was in her late thirties when Cézanne painted her here; she outlived him by sixteen years. The canvas is 116.5 by 89.5 centimetres. The Met bought it in 1962 with the Mr. and Mrs. Henry Ittleson Jr. Purchase Fund.
