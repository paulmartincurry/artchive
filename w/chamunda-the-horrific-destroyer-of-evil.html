Chamunda, the Horrific Destroyer of Evil

Maker unknown · 10th–11th century · Sandstone · H. 113 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sandstone figure is 113 centimetres high. Early images of Chamunda give her a ferocious face on a feminine figure with elegant features; later ones generally show her as a skeletal old woman wearing an elephant or tiger hide. She is often seen as a form of Kali. The Met bought this sculpture in 1989.
