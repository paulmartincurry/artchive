You will eat, bye and bye,
In that glorious land above the sky;
Work and pray, live on hay,
You'll get pie in the sky when you die.

The Preacher and the Slave

Joe Hill · 1911

Joe Hill wrote it in 1911 as a parody of the hymn In the Sweet By-and-By, aimed at the Salvation Army, which the IWW called the Starvation Army; the phrase pie in the sky comes from this song. Hill, born in Gävle, Sweden, organised for the IWW and was executed in Utah on November 19, 1915, after a murder conviction on questionable evidence. This text is from the IWW songbook of October 1919.
