Brig on the Water

Gustave Le Gray · 1856 · Albumen print, from the album "Vistas del Mar" · Art Institute of Chicago

This print comes from an album titled Vistas del Mar, and the page carries the blind stamp of his firm, Gustave Le Gray and Company, Paris. For some seascapes he printed the water from one negative and the sky from another. The studio ran into debt, and by 1860 he had closed it, left his wife and children, and fled France to escape his creditors. He spent his last twenty years in Cairo, where he died in 1884.
