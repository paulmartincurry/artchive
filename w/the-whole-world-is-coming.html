The whole world is coming,
A nation is coming, a nation is coming,
The Eagle has brought the message to the tribe.
The father says so, the father says so.
Over the whole earth they are coming.

The Whole World Is Coming

Lakota Ghost Dance song · 1890 (printed 1896)

Translated by James Mooney, 1896

Kicking Bear and Short Bull, the Lakota envoys to Wovoka, taught that dancers should wear special Ghost Dance shirts, and Kicking Bear said the shirts could repel bullets. By 1890 nearly a third of the Lakota had joined the new religion. On 29 December 1890, at Wounded Knee Creek on the Pine Ridge Reservation, between 250 and 300 Lakota were killed by the United States Army.
