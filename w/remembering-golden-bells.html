Since my heart forgot her, many days have passed
And three times winter has changed to spring.
This morning, for a little, the old grief came back,
Because, in the road, I met her foster-nurse.

Remembering Golden Bells

Bai Juyi (Po Chü-i) · 9th century

Translated by Arthur Waley

Bai Juyi twice withdrew from court to the Wei River near Chang'an to mourn a parent, the second time after his mother's death in 811, and wrote a 260-line temple poem during that retreat. His mother had died after falling into a well while looking at flowers. Enemies at court later used two of his poems, on flowers and on a new well, as evidence that he lacked filial piety.
