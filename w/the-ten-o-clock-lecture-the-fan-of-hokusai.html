The Ten O'Clock Lecture: The Fan of Hokusai

James McNeill Whistler · 1885

After his bankruptcy Whistler took a commission for twelve etchings of Venice, where he shared rooms in a run-down palazzo with other artists, John Singer Sargent among them. The three-month assignment stretched to fourteen months. He came back with more than fifty etchings, several nocturnes, some watercolours and over 100 pastels.
