And Flitted Away as Far as They Could from the Castle That Lay East of the Sun and West of the Moon

Kay Nielsen · published 1914 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Alexander Turnbull Library, Wellington

Free to use

Asbjørnsen and Moe collected this Norwegian tale in Ringerike and Telemark and first printed it in 1844. Nielsen's later career took him to Disney, where his work was used in the Night on Bald Mountain sequence of Fantasia; he was let go after four years because his work was seen as too dark. He spent his last years in poverty and died in 1957.
