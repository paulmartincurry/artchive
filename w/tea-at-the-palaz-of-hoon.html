I was the world in which I walked, and what I saw
Or heard or felt came not but from myself;
And there I found myself more truly and more strange.

Tea at the Palaz of Hoon

Wallace Stevens · 1921

The poem was first published in 1921. Harmonium, Stevens's first book, came out in 1923 from Knopf in an edition of 1,500 copies, when he was forty-four. The first edition sold only a hundred copies before being remaindered.
