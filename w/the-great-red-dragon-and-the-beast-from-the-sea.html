The Great Red Dragon and the Beast from the Sea

William Blake · c. 1805 · Pen and ink with watercolor over graphite · 40.1 × 35.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blake made four Great Red Dragon watercolours between 1805 and 1810, during a commission for more than a hundred paintings illustrating the Bible. The National Gallery of Art files this one in the series Bible for Thomas Butts. The image is 40.1 centimetres high and 35.6 wide.
