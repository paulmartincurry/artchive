David

Gian Lorenzo Bernini · 1623–24 · Marble · H. 170 cm · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Free to use with credit. Photo: Burkhard Mücke, CC BY-SA 4.0

Bernini was twenty-four and at work on Apollo and Daphne when he set that group aside to carve this one. Payment records show he had started by mid-1623, and his biographer Filippo Baldinucci states that he finished it in seven months. According to Baldinucci and Domenico Bernini, Maffeo Barberini held a mirror up to Bernini's face so that he could model David on himself. It was the last work Cardinal Scipione Borghese commissioned from him.
