Arthur Rackham · published 1908 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Heinemann published Rackham's Midsummer-Night's Dream in 1908, between his gold medal at the Milan International Exhibition of 1906 and another at Barcelona in 1912. At that time stage productions of the play still cast women as Oberon and Puck, a custom that ran for seventy years after Madame Vestris revived it at Covent Garden in 1840.
