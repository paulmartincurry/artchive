Cucurbita (Urformen der Kunst, plate 26)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · image 26 × 19 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Urformen der Kunst was followed by a second book, Wundergarten der Natur, in 1932, the year Blossfeldt died. A third collection of images he had never released was put together after his death and published in 1942 as Wunder in der Natur. This photogravure came to the National Gallery of Art in 2006 from Mary and Dan Solomon.
