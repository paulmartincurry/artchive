The Intrigue

James Ensor · 1890 · Oil on canvas · 89.5 × 149 cm · Royal Museum of Fine Arts Antwerp (KMSKA)

Free to use

To paint scenes like this, Ensor often staged them first with real masks, costumes and props. Grouping half-length figures linked by their gestures was unusual in nineteenth-century painting; the museum points to Rubens as a precedent. The canvas is 89.5 by 149 centimetres, signed and dated 1890 at lower right. In 1929 King Albert made Ensor a baron.
