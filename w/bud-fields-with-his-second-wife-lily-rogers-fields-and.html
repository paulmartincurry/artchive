Bud Fields with His Second Wife, Lily Rogers Fields, and Their Daughter

Walker Evans · Summer 1936 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Bud Fields with his second wife, Lily, and their daughter, in Hale County, Alabama, in the summer of 1936. Agee and Evans spent eight weeks there that summer, mainly among three white sharecropping families. The title follows the 1988 edition of their book.
