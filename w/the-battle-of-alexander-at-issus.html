The Battle of Alexander at Issus

Albrecht Altdorfer · 1529 · Oil on limewood panel · Alte Pinakothek, Munich

Free to use

Duke William IV of Bavaria commissioned the limewood panel in 1528. That September Albrecht Altdorfer was offered the post of burgomaster of Regensburg and declined it. Napoleon's armies took the painting to Paris in 1800, and when the Prussians captured the Château de Saint-Cloud in 1814 they supposedly found it hanging in his bathroom.
