The Large Plane Trees (Road Menders at Saint-Rémy)

Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · oil on fabric · Unframed: 73.4 × 91.8 cm (28 7/8 × 36 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Van Gogh painted this street scene while he was a patient at a psychiatric hospital near Saint-Rémy; his doctor let him work outdoors because painting from nature eased his symptoms. Workmen were repairing the pavements under the plane trees. He later painted a repetition of it, now in the Phillips Collection in Washington, and conservation research has shown that this canvas came first. Theo van Gogh owned it in 1890, and Cleveland bought it in 1947.
