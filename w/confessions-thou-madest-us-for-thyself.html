Thou awakest us to delight in Thy praise; for Thou madest us for Thyself, and our heart is restless, until it repose in Thee.

Confessions — Thou Madest Us for Thyself (Book I)

Augustine of Hippo · 397–400

Translated by E. B. Pusey

Augustine wrote the Confessions in Latin, in thirteen books, between 397 and 400. The first nine books tell his life and the last four are commentary.
