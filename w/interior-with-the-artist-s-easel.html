Interior with the Artist's Easel

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1910 · Oil on canvas · 84 × 69 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Hammershøi painted his own easel from the back, in a room of his Copenhagen home. The rooms of that home served both as his favourite motif and as his studio. The date of 1910 follows Alfred Bramsen's 1918 catalogue of his work. The canvas is 84 centimetres by 69, and the Statens Museum for Kunst acquired it in 1988.
