Riverbank

Dong Yuan · Southern Tang dynasty (937–76) · Hanging scroll, ink and color on silk · 220.3 × 109.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painted silk is more than two metres tall, and with its mounting the scroll reaches over three. The Met holds it as the work of Dong Yuan, but some scholars believe it may be a modern forgery by Zhang Daqian. It came from the collection of the C. C. Wang family and was given to the museum in 2016 by the family of the New York financier Oscar L. Tang.
