I too, dislike it: there are things that are important beyond all this fiddle. … imaginary gardens with real toads in them

Poetry

Marianne Moore · 1919 (Poems, 1921)

Moore kept reworking this poem for decades. It exists in eleven versions, and in her Complete Poems of 1967 she cut it from twenty-nine lines to three. When it first appeared, in 1919, she was thirty-one and had been living for a year with her mother in Greenwich Village. Her first book, Poems, was published in 1921 without her permission by the poet H.D. and the novelist Bryher.
