Slow days passing, accumulating,
How distant they are,
The things of the past!

Haiku (Memories of the Past)

Yosa Buson · ca. 1765

Translated by R. H. Blyth, 1950 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Buson earned his living mainly as a painter. He had settled in Kyoto at the age of forty-two, after years of travel that included a journey through northern Honshū in the footsteps of Bashō, and he married at forty-five. The name Yosa came from his mother's birthplace. In 1770 he took the pen name Yahantei II, which had belonged to his teacher Hajin.
