Bindo Altoviti

Raphael · c. 1515 · Oil on panel · 59.7 × 43.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Bindo Altoviti, a banker born in Rome in 1491, commissioned this portrait, so he was in his twenties when Raphael painted it. It stayed with his descendants until 1808, when it was sold to Ludwig I of Bavaria. After long debate over its attribution, the Alte Pinakothek in Munich traded it to a London dealer in September 1938.
