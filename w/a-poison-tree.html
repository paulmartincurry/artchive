The Artchive
I was angry with my friend:
I told my wrath, my wrath did end.
I was angry with my foe:
I told it not, my wrath did grow.

A Poison Tree

William Blake · 1794

The poem's original title was Christian Forbearance. The draft has a line ruled under the first stanza, which may mean he first intended it to stop after four lines. He printed it on a plate with a picture of a body lying under a bare tree. Only 28 copies of the collection made in his lifetime are known, and the poem was not printed on its own until 1830, in the London University Magazine.
