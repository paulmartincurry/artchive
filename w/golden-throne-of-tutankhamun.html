Golden Throne of Tutankhamun (Back Panel)

Maker unknown · c. 1336–1327 BC · Wood, gold, glass, carnelian · Grand Egyptian Museum, Giza

Free to use with credit. Photo: Djehouty, CC BY-SA 4.0

The throne is wood covered with gold and inlaid with glass and carnelian. It stood in the antechamber of the tomb, and Carter thought it the most beautiful thing that had yet been found in Egypt. The king and queen are labelled with the later forms of their names, which refer to Amun, but there are signs that the labels were altered after the throne was made; the openwork arms and back still carry the king's first name, Tutankhaten.
