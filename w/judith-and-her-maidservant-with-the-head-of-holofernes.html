Judith and Her Maidservant with the Head of Holofernes

Artemisia Gentileschi · c. 1623–25 · Oil on canvas · Detroit Institute of Arts

Free to use

Artemisia Gentileschi painted Judith and her maidservant four times. This version is dated to about 1623 to 1625; the precise date is debated because she was travelling around Italy in those years. Nothing is known of its owners before 1952, when Leslie H. Green bought it and gave it to the Detroit Institute of Arts.
