Under the wide and starry sky,
Dig the grave and let me lie.
Glad did I live and gladly die,
And I laid me down with a will.

This be the verse you grave for me:
Here he lies where he longed to be;
Home is the sailor, home from sea,
And the hunter home from the hill.

Requiem

Robert Louis Stevenson · 1887

Illness recurred throughout Stevenson's adult life and left him extraordinarily thin. In 1887 he was thirty-six, and his father, a lighthouse engineer, died that year. Three years later Stevenson settled in Samoa. He died there of a stroke in 1894, aged 44, and his resting place is Mount Vaea.
