The Musicians

Caravaggio · 1597 · Oil on canvas · 36 1/4 × 46 5/8 in. (92.1 × 118.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

This is thought to be the first painting Caravaggio made expressly for Cardinal Francesco Maria del Monte, whose household he entered in 1595; the cardinal owned costly instruments the painter could use as models. The lutenist has been identified as his companion Mario Minniti. After the cardinal's death the canvas was sold in 1628, was inventoried for Cardinal Richelieu in 1638, and later dropped out of sight. It reached the museum in 1952 and was extensively restored in 1983.
