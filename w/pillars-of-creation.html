Pillars of Creation

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and ESA · 1995 · Space telescope image

Free to use

The photograph was taken on April 1, 1995, for the astronomers Jeff Hester and Paul Scowen of Arizona State University. It is assembled from 32 separate images from the four sensors of one camera, and its colours stand for elements: green for hydrogen, red for sulfur, blue for oxygen. The stair-shaped gap at the top right is there because one of the four sensors had a magnified view and its images had to be scaled down to fit the others. The leftmost pillar is about four light-years long.
