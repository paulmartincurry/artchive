Loggia of the Ducal Palace, Venice

John Ruskin · 1849–50 · Watercolor over graphite · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Ruskin was thirty when he made this watercolour. He had married Effie Gray in 1848, and the marriage was annulled in 1854. He had first seen Venice at sixteen, on one of his family's tours of the Continent, and his three-volume book The Stones of Venice came out between 1851 and 1853. The sheet is 46 centimetres tall, and the museum bought it in 1908.
