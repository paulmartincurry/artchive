The Stones of Venice: Ugly Goblins and Formless Monsters

John Ruskin · 1853

William Morris took up the call in this chapter for a return to traditional craftsmanship, and his Kelmscott Press later printed The Nature of Gothic in an edition with ornamented borders. Ruskin once went to Bradford and told its citizens they had not understood what he said about architecture and society. They still chose a Venetian Gothic design for their Wool Exchange.
