My holy of holies is the human body, health, intelligence, talent, inspiration, love, and the most absolute freedom--freedom from violence and lying, whatever forms they may take.

Letter to Alexei Pleshcheyev on Being a Free Artist

Anton Chekhov · 1888

Translated by Constance Garnett, 1920

In 1888 Chekhov was twenty-eight, a qualified physician who made little money from medicine and treated the poor free of charge. That year his story collection At Dusk won the Pushkin Prize. His long story The Steppe, begun after a trip to Ukraine in 1887, had won him publication in a literary journal rather than a newspaper.
