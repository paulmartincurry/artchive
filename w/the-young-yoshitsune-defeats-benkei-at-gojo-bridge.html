The Young Yoshitsune Defeats Benkei at Gojō Bridge

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1848 · Color woodblock prints; oban triptych · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The tale of the young Yoshitsune beating the giant monk Benkei on Gojō Bridge in Kyoto is a folk legend; no contemporary record of Benkei survives. In the story Benkei had already taken 999 swords from samurai and was looking for his thousandth. Kuniyoshi's triptych, made around 1848, adds the eight tengu said to have helped Yoshitsune, with their king Sōjōbō dressed as a monk at the top of the centre sheet.
