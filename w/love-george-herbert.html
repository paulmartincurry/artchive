‘You must sit down,’ says Love, ‘and taste my meat.’
So I did sit and eat.

Love (III)

George Herbert · 1633

Shortly before his death Herbert sent a manuscript to his friend Nicholas Ferrar, reportedly leaving it to him to publish the poems or burn them. All of his English poems were published in 1633 in The Temple, with a preface by Ferrar. Herbert had died of consumption at 39, only three years after taking holy orders.
