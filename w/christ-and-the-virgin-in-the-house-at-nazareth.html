Christ and the Virgin in the House at Nazareth

Francisco de Zurbarán · c. 1640 · Oil on canvas · 165 × 218.2 cm · The Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Zurbarán appears to have invented this subject himself; it does not come from a story in the Bible. He painted the canvas around 1640, and it is more than two metres wide. The museum bought it in 1960.
