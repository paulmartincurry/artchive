Elle est retrouvée,
Quoi? L'éternité.
C'est la mer allée
Avec le soleil.

L'Éternité

Arthur Rimbaud · 1872

Rimbaud produced the bulk of his writing in late adolescence and early adulthood. In spring 1872 he and the poet Paul Verlaine began a relationship that lasted nearly two years; it ended in Brussels in July 1873, when Verlaine fired two shots from his revolver and wounded him once. Rimbaud stopped writing literature completely at the age of 20.
