Bai Juyi (Po Chü-i) · before 846; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Bai Juyi was born in 772 in Taiyuan and lived through the reigns of eight or nine emperors. When he died in 846 he left orders for a plain burial at a monastery and asked that no posthumous title be given him.
