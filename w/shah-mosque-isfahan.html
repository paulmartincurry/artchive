Shah Mosque, Isfahan

Ali Akbar Isfahani · 1611–1629 · Brick, stone, mortar, tile

Free to use with credit. Photo: Patrickringgenberg, CC BY-SA 4.0

Building began in 1611, perhaps held up by delays in buying the land, and finished around 1630, after the death of Shah Abbas, who had ordered it. He wanted it done in his lifetime and ignored an architect's warning that the foundations would subside; in 1662 the mosque needed major repairs. The tiles were made by a newer method in which each tile was painted in several colours and fired once, cheaper and quicker than cutting mosaic. The building is said to hold 18 million bricks and 475,000 tiles.
