The Combat of the Giaour and Hassan

Eugène Delacroix · 1826 · Oil on canvas · 59.6 × 73.4 cm (23 1/2 × 28 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Alexandre Dumas may have owned this canvas from about 1827 until 1848. It later belonged to Potter Palmer of Chicago and passed down three generations of his family, whose heirs gave it to the museum in installments between 1962 and 1967. Delacroix painted it six years before his first visit to North Africa, a trip to Morocco in 1832.
