The Artchive

Esther before Ahasuerus

Artemisia Gentileschi · 1620s · Oil on canvas · 82 in. × 8 ft. 11 3/4 in. (208.3 × 273.7 cm) · The Met

Free to use

X-rays taken by the Met found a boy holding back a dog near the king's knee, which Artemisia painted out; a faint shadow of him still shows. The canvas is 208.3 by 273.7 centimetres. Nobody knows who ordered it, and it may have been begun in Venice before she moved to Naples. It is first recorded in 1856 in the Harrach collection in Vienna, and Elinor Dorrance Ingersoll gave it to the Met in 1969.
