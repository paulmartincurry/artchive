République Française Liberté Égalité

Maker unknown · ca. 1792 · Block printed on handmade paper · H × W: 45.5 × 77.5 cm (17 15/16 × 30 1/2 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The wallpaper was designed for the French Revolution: a wreath of oak leaves holds crossed fasces, a sword and a Phrygian cap, and ribbons in the tricolour wind round a fluted column bearing a motto. It was block printed on handmade paper, 45.5 by 77.5 centimetres, and came from the John Jay Ide Collection.
