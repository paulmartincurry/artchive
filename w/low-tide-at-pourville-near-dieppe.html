Low Tide at Pourville, near Dieppe

Claude Monet · 1882 · Oil on fabric · Unframed: 59.9 × 81.3 cm (23 9/16 × 32 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In the spring of 1882 Monet lodged in Pourville, a fishing village on the Normandy coast, in a small hotel like the houses at the foot of the cliff on the right. His letters say the village itself was nothing special. The thin black strokes along the water at the base of the cliff are bathers. At the time he was living at Poissy with Alice Hoschedé and her children, and within two years he had moved to Giverny.
