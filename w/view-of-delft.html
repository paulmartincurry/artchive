View of Delft

Johannes Vermeer · 1661 · Oil on canvas · Mauritshuis

Free to use

Pieter Claeszoon van Ruijven, a Delft collector who had lent Johannes Vermeer 200 guilders in 1657, commissioned this view. Vermeer painted the town from its south side, the only part where the buildings had been left untouched by the gunpowder explosion of 1654. He put his initials, VM, on the red interior of the barge, and painted out a third figure beside the two women. The picture was auctioned in 1696 for 200 guilders and sold to the Dutch government in 1822 for 2900.
