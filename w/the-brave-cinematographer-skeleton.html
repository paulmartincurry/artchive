The Brave Cinematographer Skeleton

José Guadalupe Posada · 1906 · Zincograph and letterpress on orange paper (printed off-centre on sheet) · 40 × 30 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Antonio Vanegas Arroyo published this sheet in 1906, on orange paper and printed off-centre. Posada had joined the staff of his publishing firm in Mexico City, and much of his work came out in broadsides about current events.
