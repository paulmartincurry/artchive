I’ve gathered this sprig of heather
Autumn is dead you will remember
On earth we’ll see no more of each other

The Farewell (L'Adieu)

Guillaume Apollinaire · published 1913

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

L'Adieu is one of the poems of Alcools, Apollinaire's first major collection, printed in 567 copies by the Mercure de France in April 1913. He had meant to call the book Eau de Vie and changed the title while correcting the first proofs.
