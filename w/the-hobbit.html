The Hobbit

J. R. R. Tolkien · 1937

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

The Hobbit was published in 1937. It was nominated for the Carnegie Medal and was awarded a prize from the New York Herald Tribune for best juvenile fiction. Encouraged by its critical and financial success, the publisher requested a sequel.
