Stacks of Wheat (End of Summer)

Claude Monet · 1890–91 · Oil on canvas · 60 × 100.5 cm (23 5/8 × 39 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The stacks stood fifteen to twenty feet high just outside Monet's farmhouse at Giverny, and he worked on several canvases at once at easels in the field. In May 1891 he hung fifteen of them side by side in one small room at the Galerie Durand-Ruel in Paris, and this was number one in the catalogue. Durand-Ruel paid him 3,000 francs for it that month. A Boston buyer took it in June and traded it back in August, and Potter Palmer of Chicago bought it the same day for 6,000 francs.
