Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas

Hunter S. Thompson · 1971

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Thompson spent some 36 hours alone in a hotel room writing in his notebook at the end of a trip to Las Vegas in March 1971, and began the full manuscript on April 29, 1971, in a hotel room in Arcadia, California, in his spare time from a magazine article. The book first appeared as a two-part series in Rolling Stone that year, illustrated by Ralph Steadman, and came out in book form in 1972.
