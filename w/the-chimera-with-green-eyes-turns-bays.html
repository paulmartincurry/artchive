The Chimera with Green Eyes Turns, Bays

Odilon Redon · 1888 · lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This is plate seven of Redon's first portfolio after Flaubert's The Temptation of Saint Anthony. He first made charcoal drawings and then tried to match the charcoal's dense black in lithography, a way of transferring his drawings he had found in 1876. The sheet is printed on China paper. He made twelve lithograph albums before giving up the medium in the late 1890s, and these prints were largely misunderstood in his own time.
