Manuscript Page: King David Playing the Harp

Maker unknown · 15th century · Parchment, pigment · H x W x D: 26 x 19.5 cm (10 1/4 x 7 11/16 in.) · National Museum of African Art, Washington

Free to use

The picture of King David with his harp would have been bound into a Bible with the Psalms. The museum normally does not buy single manuscript pages, so as not to encourage cutting up books, but made an exception for this old fragment.
