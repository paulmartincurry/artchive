The Deformed Polyp Floated on the Shores, a Sort of Smiling and Hideous Cyclops (Les Origines, Plate 3)

Odilon Redon · 1883 · Lithograph on chine appliqué on wove paper · Image: 21.4 × 20 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is plate 3 of Les Origines, a set of lithographs of 1883. Redon signed his name in the stone at the lower right, and the printer's line under the image names Lemercier and Company in Paris. The image, 21.4 by 20 centimetres, sits on a sheet 54.7 centimetres tall. Lessing Rosenwald gave it to the National Gallery of Art in 1951.
