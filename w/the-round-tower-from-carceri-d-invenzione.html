The Round Tower, from Carceri d'invenzione (Imaginary Prisons)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · ca. 1749–50 · Etching, engraving, sulphur tint or open bite, burnishing; first state of four (Robison) · Sheet: 24 13/16 × 19 1/2 in. (63 × 49.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This impression is the first of four states of the plate. When the series was published again in 1761, Piranesi reworked every etching and numbered them I to XVI, and some were changed to show impossible geometries. He was a stonemason's son, apprenticed to his uncle, an architect, and had gone to Rome in 1740 as a draughtsman for the Venetian ambassador. The museum bought the print in 1937.
