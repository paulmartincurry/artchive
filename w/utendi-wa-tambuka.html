Utendi wa Tambuka

Bwana Mwengo · 1728 · SOAS Library, University of London

The oldest manuscript of this epic is dated 1728 and was written at Yunga, a royal palace in the old city of Pate that has since been destroyed. The Sultan of Pate asked for the poem, and the author names himself near the end as Mwengo, son of Athumani. He was no longer a young man, and little else is known about him. The Swahili is written in Arabic script, in four-line stanzas of eight-syllable lines, with one closing rhyme carried through the whole poem. His son later wrote an imitation of it.
