Belle, Bonne, Sage

Baude Cordier · ca. 1395 · Parchment · Musée Condé, Chantilly

Free to use

The staves of this love song are drawn in the shape of a heart. It is one of two songs by Baude Cordier in the Chantilly Manuscript; the other is written out in a circle. Almost nothing is known of his life beyond an inscription on one of his works, which says he was born in Rheims and held a Master of Arts. Ten of his secular pieces survive.
