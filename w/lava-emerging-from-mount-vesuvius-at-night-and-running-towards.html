Lava Emerging from Mount Vesuvius at Night and Running towards Resina, 11 May 1771 (Campi Phlegraei)

Pietro Fabris · 1776 · Etching with gouache · 21.2 × 39.1 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

Pietro Fabris drew this flow of lava on the spot in 1771 and etched the plate five years later, colouring the print with gouache. According to the Wellcome's record, the foreground shows Sir William Hamilton guiding the King and Queen of Naples to the lava, and Fabris himself at work drawing it. Hamilton was the British envoy at Naples from 1764 to 1800 and took Fabris with him on his visits to the volcanoes.
