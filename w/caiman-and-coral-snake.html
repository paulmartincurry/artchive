Caiman and Coral Snake (Insects of Suriname)

Maria Sibylla Merian · 1705 (1719 folio) · University of Groningen Library

Free to use

To pay for the voyage to Suriname, Maria Sibylla Merian sold 255 of her own paintings. She sailed from Amsterdam in July 1699, aged fifty-two, with her younger daughter, and spent two years travelling the colony and sketching its animals and plants. An illness, possibly malaria, forced her home in June 1701. Back in the Netherlands she opened a shop selling her specimens and engravings, and she published the book in 1705.
