Tea Screen

Tiffany & Company · ca. 1905 · glass, bronze · H × W: 19.2 × 30.9 cm (7 9/16 × 12 3/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The screen is three panels of marbled glass in lavender, blue and green, each edged in narrow copper that has oxidised to green and orange-red. A cut-out copper overlay in a grapevine pattern lies over the glass, and the panels are hinged together with wire. It is 30.9 centimetres wide. No maker's marks were found on it.
