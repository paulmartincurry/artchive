To light a candle is to cast a shadow.

A Wizard of Earthsea

Ursula K. Le Guin · 1968

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

In 1967 Herman Schein, the publisher of the small Parnassus Press, asked Le Guin to try writing a book for older kids and gave her complete freedom over the subject and the approach. She had already set two short stories of 1964 in Earthsea. Parnassus published the novel in 1968, and she wrote five more Earthsea books.
