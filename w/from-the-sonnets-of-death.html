Save him, O Saviour, from the grip of wraths,
And plunge him in the dream Thine arms provide!

From the Sonnets of Death (Los sonetos de la muerte)

Gabriela Mistral · 1914 (Desolación, 1922)

Translated by Thomas Walsh (ed.), 1920

In 1914 Mistral was twenty-five and teaching at a high school in Los Andes; her own formal schooling had ended in 1900. After she won first prize in the national literary contest, the Juegos Florales in Santiago, she rarely published under her given name, Lucila Godoy. Desolación, her first book, did not appear until 1922, with help from Federico de Onis, the director of the Hispanic Institute of New York.
