Maker unknown · ca. 2300–2000 BCE · Copper alloy · 34.4 × 21.3 × 23.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The head is copper alloy and stands 34.4 centimetres high. The museum's record places it in Mesopotamia in the Early Bronze Age and names no site. It was bought in 1947.
