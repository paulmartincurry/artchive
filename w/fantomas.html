Fantômas

Juan Gris · 1915 · Oil on canvas · 59.8 × 73.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Juan Gris dated the canvas August 1915 at the lower left, and the dealer Léonce Rosenberg acquired it from him the same year. Fantômas, a character of French crime fiction, had been created in 1911. The painting was reproduced in the Bulletin de L'Effort Moderne in June 1927, and by 1929–30 it was in the house of the Philadelphia collector Earl Horter.
