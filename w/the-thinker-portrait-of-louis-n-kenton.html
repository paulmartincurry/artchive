The Thinker: Portrait of Louis N. Kenton

Thomas Eakins · 1900 · Oil on canvas · 208.3 × 106.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Louis Kenton married Eakins's sister-in-law, Elizabeth Macdowell, on May 31, 1899, and Eakins had given her the portrait by 1901. Between 1900 and 1916 he exhibited it at least thirteen times. The words The Thinker were added after his death, from an inscription on the stretcher most likely written by Mrs. Eakins. The Metropolitan Museum bought the painting in 1917.
