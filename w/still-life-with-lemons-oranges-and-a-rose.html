Still Life with Lemons, Oranges and a Rose

Francisco de Zurbarán · 1633 · Oil on canvas · Norton Simon Museum, Pasadena

Free to use

Zurbarán completed the canvas in 1633. It is the only still life he signed and dated. It hangs in the Norton Simon Museum in Pasadena, California.
