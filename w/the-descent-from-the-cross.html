The Descent from the Cross

Rogier van der Weyden · c. 1435 · Oil on panel · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

The Greater Guild of Crossbowmen of Leuven commissioned this panel for their Chapel of Our Lady Without the Walls, and tiny crossbows are painted in the corners above. Around 1548 it was exchanged for a copy by Michael Coxcie and an organ; the new owner was Mary of Austria, who governed the Habsburg Netherlands. Philip II inherited it from her in 1558 and took it to Spain.
