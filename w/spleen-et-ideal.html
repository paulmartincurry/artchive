Spleen et Idéal

Carlos Schwabe · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 146 × 97 cm · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, Brussels

Free to use

Schwabe illustrated Charles Baudelaire's Les Fleurs du mal in 1900, and in 1907 he painted this canvas, which is based on the same book. He was born in Altona and took Swiss citizenship in 1888. After studying art in Geneva he moved to Paris as a young man and worked there as a wallpaper designer.
