My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?

Psalm 22:1–15

Book of Psalms (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · Date uncertain (trans. 1611)

The psalm's Hebrew title names the hind of the dawn. It belongs to the Psalms of David, traditionally attributed to King David, an attribution scholars have qualified or challenged since the late 19th century.
