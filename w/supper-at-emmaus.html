Supper at Emmaus

Caravaggio · 1601 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

Ciriaco Mattei, the brother of a cardinal, commissioned this painting and paid for it, and Caravaggio completed it in 1601. The figures are life-sized, on a canvas of 141 by 196.2 centimetres. He painted the subject again in 1606, after he had fled Rome as an outlaw following the death of Ranuccio Tomassoni; that version is in Milan.
