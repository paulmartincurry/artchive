Astronaut Harrison Schmitt next to Deployed U.S. Flag on Lunar Surface (AS17-134-20384)

Eugene A. Cernan · December 1972 · Colour photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

Eugene Cernan, the commander of Apollo 17, took this photograph of Harrison Schmitt at the Taurus-Littrow landing site in December 1972. Schmitt was the only geologist in the astronaut corps and remains the only professional scientist to have landed on the Moon. Apollo 17 was the last crewed mission of the programme and the most recent time anyone has stood there.
