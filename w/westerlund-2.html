Westerlund 2 (Hubble 25th Anniversary Image)

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA/ESA/STScI · released April 2015 · Space telescope image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA, ESA, the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA), A. Nota (ESA/STScI) and the Westerlund 2 Science Team

NASA released the image to mark Hubble's 25 years in space; the telescope was launched on 24 April 1990. At its centre is a giant cluster of young stars called Westerlund 2.
