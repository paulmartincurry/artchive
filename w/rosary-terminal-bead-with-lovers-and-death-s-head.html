Rosary Terminal Bead with Lovers and Death's Head

Maker unknown · c. 1500–25 · Elephant ivory, with emerald pendant, gilded metal mount · 13.6 × 4 × 4.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The carved ivory is 7.2 centimetres high, and an emerald hangs below it from a gilded metal mount. It was made in northern France or the southern Netherlands and was the end bead of a rosary. J. Pierpont Morgan's gift brought it to the museum in 1917.
