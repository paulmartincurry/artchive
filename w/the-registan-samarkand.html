The Registan, Samarkand

Built under Ulugh Beg and later rulers · 1417–1660 · Ceramic tile, brick

Free to use with credit. Photo: Gustavo Jeronimo, CC BY 2.0

The three schools on the square went up over nearly two and a half centuries. Ulugh Beg built the first between 1417 and 1420 and lectured there himself. The Sherdar followed in 1619 to 1636, on the orders of the Uzbek ruler Yalangtoʻsh Bakhodir, and takes its name from the tigers in mosaic on its portal. The Tilakari, finished in 1660, served as a mosque as well as a college, and its main hall is gilded. Royal proclamations were read out on the square, and public executions were held there.
