I Saw the Figure 5 in Gold

Charles Demuth · 1928 · Oil on cardboard · The Met

Free to use

This is a portrait of the poet William Carlos Williams, one of a series Demuth painted of his friends between 1924 and 1929. The two met in a Philadelphia boarding house, when Demuth was an art student and Williams was studying medicine. The title comes from a poem Williams wrote on the sidewalk after a fire engine passed him. Demuth worked the name Bill and both men's initials into the picture, in oil, graphite, ink and gold leaf. He left it to Georgia O'Keeffe, who gave it to the Met in 1948; it has never been sold.
