My three sons married into good families
And my five daughters wedded to steady husbands.
Then I could jog through a happy five-score years
And, at the end, need no Paradise.

Tell Me Now

Wang Ji · c. 700; English 1918

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Waley translated the poem for A Hundred and Seventy Chinese Poems, published in 1918. He had been Assistant Keeper of Oriental Prints and Manuscripts at the British Museum since 1913. He never learned to speak modern Chinese and never went to China.
