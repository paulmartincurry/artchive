Map of Atlantis (Mundus Subterraneus)

Athanasius Kircher · 1664 (this printing 1678)

Free to use

The map comes from Mundus Subterraneus, first published in 1664, when Kircher was in his early sixties. He was a German Jesuit who had been based in Rome since 1634, published around forty major works and studied volcanoes and fossils. For Atlantis he had only two primary sources, both by Plato: the Timaeus and the Critias, which place the island beyond the Pillars of Hercules.
