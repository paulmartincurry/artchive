The Rape of Proserpina

Gian Lorenzo Bernini · 1621–22 · Carrara marble · H. 225 cm · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Free to use with credit. Photo: Alvesgaspar, CC BY-SA 4.0

Bernini was twenty-three when he finished this group. Cardinal Scipione Borghese commissioned it and then gave it to Cardinal Ludovico Ludovisi, the new pope's nephew, possibly as a means of gaining favour. It is 225 centimetres tall and cut from Carrara marble. The pedestal it first stood on, which carried a poem by Maffeo Barberini, has since been destroyed.
