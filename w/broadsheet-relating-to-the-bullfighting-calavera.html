Broadsheet Relating to the Bullfighting Calavera (La Calavera Taurina)

José Guadalupe Posada · 1908 · Photorelief and letterpress on pink paper · Sheet: 40 × 30.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This broadsheet was printed in photorelief and letterpress on pink paper in 1908 and published by Antonio Vanegas Arroyo. As a teenager in 1868 Posada had been apprenticed in the workshop of Jose Trinidad Pedroza, who taught him lithography and engraving.
