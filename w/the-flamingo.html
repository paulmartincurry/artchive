The Flamingo (The Natural History of Carolina, Florida and the Bahama Islands, T. 73)

Mark Catesby · 1729–32 · Coloured etching · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Catesby wrote that the flamingo takes two years to reach its full colour, stands five feet high when full grown, and nests on hillocks in shallow water, sitting with its legs hanging down like a man on a stool. The first volume of the book, in five parts, was finished in November 1732, and he spent twenty years in all preparing and publishing the work, paid for by subscribers and an interest-free loan from Peter Collinson.
