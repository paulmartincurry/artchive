Alfred Peats Prize Wallpaper

Alfred Peats Company · ca. 1910 · Machine-printed paper · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The Alfred Peats sample book this sheet belongs to holds mostly floral medallion papers, sold in matched sets of sidewall, frieze and ceiling paper, several of them in more than one colourway. The sheet is 47 by 39.5 centimetres, a gift of Jack and Martha Brown.
