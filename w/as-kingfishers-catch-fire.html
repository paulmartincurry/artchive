As kingfishers catch fire, dragonflies draw flame;
As tumbled over rim in roundy wells

As Kingfishers Catch Fire

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1918

Hopkins was ordained in 1877, and a month later he took up duties as sub-minister and teacher of Latin and Greek at Mount St Mary's College near Sheffield. His poetry was not widely known during his lifetime. Robert Bridges, a friend from his years at Balliol College, Oxford, edited the first edition, which came out in 1918.
