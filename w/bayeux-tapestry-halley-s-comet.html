Bayeux Tapestry: Halley's Comet (scene 32)

Maker unknown · 1070s · Wool thread on linen · Bayeux Museum, Bayeux

Free to use

This is the first known picture of Halley's Comet. The tapestry is generally thought to have been commissioned by Bishop Odo of Bayeux, half-brother of William, and made in England in the years following the Conquest.
