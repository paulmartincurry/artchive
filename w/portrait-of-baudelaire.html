Portrait of Baudelaire

Nadar · 1856–58 · Albumen print, cabinet card · 14.6 × 10.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Charles Baudelaire wrote scathingly about photography and was nonetheless a close friend of Nadar. The portrait was printed as a cabinet card, a format slightly larger than the carte-de-visite that displaced it in popularity around 1865 and that collectors of celebrity portraits sought out. The image is 14.6 by 10.8 centimetres.
