We wear the mask that grins and lies,
it hides our cheeks and shades our eyes.

We Wear the Mask

Paul Laurence Dunbar · 1895 (Lyrics of Lowly Life, 1896)

The poem first appeared in Majors and Minors, Paul Laurence Dunbar's second book of poems, when he was in his early twenties. A lawyer and a psychiatrist supported the printing. After leaving school in 1891 Dunbar had taken a job as an elevator operator in Dayton at four dollars a week, and he sold copies of his first book to his passengers. The poem is a rondeau, and unlike much of his popular work it is not in dialect.
