Volpini Suite: Laundresses (Les Laveuses)

Paul Gauguin · 1889 · zincograph · Sheet: 50 x 64.9 cm (19 11/16 x 25 9/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The twelve lithographs in the set were Gauguin's first prints, drawn on zinc plates instead of stone and printed on large sheets of bright yellow paper. The cropped cow and stylised water come from Japanese woodblock prints.
