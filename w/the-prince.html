The Artchive
it is much safer to be feared than loved

The Prince (Ch. XVII)

Niccolò Machiavelli · 1532

Translated by W. K. Marriott, 1908

Machiavelli had served the Florentine republic for fourteen years as secretary to its second chancery when the Medici returned to power in 1512 and removed him; he was soon suspected of treason and sent into exile. His letters show a version of this book under way in 1513, under a Latin title, and he prefaced it with a letter to Lorenzo de' Medici. It was not printed until 1532, five years after his death, with the permission of the Medici pope Clement VII.
