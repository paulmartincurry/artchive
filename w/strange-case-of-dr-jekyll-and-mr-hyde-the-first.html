Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde — The First Transformation (Henry Jekyll's Full Statement)

Robert Louis Stevenson · 1886

As a teenager Stevenson drafted a play about William Brodie, an Edinburgh cabinet-maker and city councillor who led a secret life as a burglar; reworked with W. E. Henley, it was first staged in 1882. According to Fanny Stevenson, as quoted by the biographer Graham Balfour, she woke her husband from a nightmare at the first transformation scene. Scribner's issued the book in New York on 5 January 1886, four days before the first London edition.
