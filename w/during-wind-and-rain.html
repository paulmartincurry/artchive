And brightest things that are theirs . . .
Ah, no; the years, the years;
Down their carved names the rain-drop ploughs.

During Wind and Rain

Thomas Hardy · 1917

Hardy regarded himself primarily as a poet, though his first collection was not published until 1898, three years after Jude the Obscure. In 1870, on an architectural mission to restore the parish church of St Juliot in Cornwall, he met Emma Gifford, and they married in 1874. After her death in 1912 he went back to Cornwall to revisit the places linked with their courtship.
