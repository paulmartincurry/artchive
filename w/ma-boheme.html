Où, rimant au milieu des ombres fantastiques,
Comme des lyres, je tirais les élastiques
De mes souliers blessés, un pied contre mon cœur!

Ma Bohème

Arthur Rimbaud · 1870

By the end of August 1870 Rimbaud, fifteen years old, was bored and restless in Charleville, and he ran away by train to Paris without the money for his ticket. He was arrested on arrival and locked up in Mazas Prison to await trial for fare evasion and vagrancy. His teacher Georges Izambard arranged with the prison governor for his release.
