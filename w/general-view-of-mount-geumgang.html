General View of Mount Geumgang (Geumgang jeondo)

Jeong Seon · c. 1734 · Ink and light colour on paper · Ho-Am Art Museum, Yongin

Free to use

Jeong Seon painted around a hundred surviving pictures of the Diamond Mountains, and this, about 1.3 metres tall, is the largest. It shows the Inner Geumgang, with the highest peak, Birobong, at the back. An inscription has been used to date it to the winter of 1734, when he was 59, but Gang Gwan-sik has argued that the inscription is not in Jeong Seon's hand and may have been added in 1794 by an owner. He had become the leading painter of his day at 37, on the strength of his Geumgang pictures.
