Yungang Grottoes: The Cave 20 Buddha

Maker unknown · ca. 460–494 · Sandstone

Cave 20 belongs to the first group carved at Yungang, caves 16 to 20, where work lasted until the year 465. The grottoes are cut into the south face of a sandstone cliff about 2,600 feet long, near what was then the Northern Wei capital. The court paid for the carving until it moved to Luoyang in 494. The sandstone has weathered heavily in the open air, and during the Cultural Revolution Red Guards damaged many of the carvings.
