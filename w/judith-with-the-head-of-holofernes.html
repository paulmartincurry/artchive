Judith with the Head of Holofernes

Lucas Cranach the Elder · ca. 1530 · Oil on linden · The Met

Free to use

Lucas Cranach was about fifty-eight when he painted this panel, and had been court painter to the Electors of Saxony in Wittenberg for some twenty-five years. Besides the painting workshop, he held the town monopoly on the sale of medicines and a printing patent for Bibles, and in 1531 he served as burgomaster. His large workshop turned out many works in more than one version. The Metropolitan Museum bought this one in 1911.
