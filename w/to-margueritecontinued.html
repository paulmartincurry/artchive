God, a God their severance ruled!
And bade betwixt their shores to be
The unplumb'd, salt, estranging sea.

To Marguerite—Continued

Matthew Arnold · 1852

The poem was first published in Empedocles on Etna in 1852 under a longer title. In the 1857 edition it is printed as a sequel to Isolation: To Marguerite, and there it first took the simplified title.
