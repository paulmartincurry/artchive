Girl at Gee's Bend

Arthur Rothstein · April 1937 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The book Documenting America, published in 1988, names the girl as Artelia Bendolph. Many of the Black tenants Rothstein photographed at Gee's Bend were named Pettway. In 1937, the year of this picture, the average rural rehabilitation loan to Gee's Bend families from the Resettlement Administration was 353 dollars and 41 cents.
