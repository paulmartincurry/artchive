I die just when I see the dawn break,
Through the gloom of night, to herald the day;

My Last Farewell (Mi último adiós)

José Rizal · December 29, 1896

Translated by Charles Derbyshire, 1911

José Rizal, an eye doctor aged thirty-five, wrote this poem in his cell before he was shot by a Spanish firing squad on December 30, 1896. On the afternoon before, when his mother and sisters visited, he told his sister Trinidad in English that there was something in the small alcohol stove. At home the family found a folded paper with fourteen five-line stanzas, unsigned, untitled and undated. The title it now carries was given to it by others after his death.
