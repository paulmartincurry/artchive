Summer Kimono (Hitoe)

Maker unknown · About 1900–1916 · Silk, plain weave self-patterned by rows of gauze crossings (yoko-ro); resist- dyed (shibori) and stenciled (kata yûzen-zome) · 141.9 × 126.4 cm (55 7/8 × 49 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A hitoe is an unlined summer kimono. The tie-dyed shibori ripples under the flat stencilling, so the irises look like their own reflection in moving water.
