Haneda Ferry and Benten Shrine (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1858 · Color woodblock print; ōban · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The view crosses the Tama River towards the Benten Shrine and Edo Bay. The ground of the shrine is now occupied by Haneda Airport. The print was published in 1858, the year Hiroshige died.
