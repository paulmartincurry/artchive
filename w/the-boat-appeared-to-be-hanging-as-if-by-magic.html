The Boat Appeared to Be Hanging, as if by Magic, upon the Interior Surface of a Funnel (A Descent into the Maelström)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Harry Clarke was thirty when Harrap first published his Poe in 1919, illustrated only in black and white; a second edition in 1923 added eight colour plates and made his name. Poe rushed to finish this story for Graham's Magazine in 1841 and later admitted the ending was imperfect. Readers took it for a true account, and one passage was reprinted in the Encyclopædia Britannica.
