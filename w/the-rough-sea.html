A wild sea—
and flowing out toward Sado Island,
the Milky Way.

The Rough Sea (Araumi ya)

Matsuo Bashō · 1689

Translated by Robert Hass · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The poem comes from the journey through northern Honshū that Bashō made in 1689 with his student Kawai Sora, some 2,400 kilometres in 150 days. After touring Kisakata on 30 July they turned back along the western coastline at a leisurely pace. Bashō spent three years editing his log of the trip and wrote the final version in 1694, the year he died. It was first published in 1702.
