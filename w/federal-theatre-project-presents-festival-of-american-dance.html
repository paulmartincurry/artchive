Federal Theatre Project Presents Festival of American Dance

Maker unknown · 1938 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The poster announced a Festival of American Dance featuring An American Exodus, staged by the Federal Theatre Project in Los Angeles in 1938. Los Angeles was one of the project's five regional centres. Its national director, Hallie Flanagan, built it into a federation of regional theatres that let millions of Americans see live theatre for the first time.
