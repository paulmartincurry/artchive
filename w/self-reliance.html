Trust thyself: every heart vibrates
to that iron string.

Self-Reliance

Ralph Waldo Emerson · 1841

The first trace of this essay is in a sermon Emerson preached in September 1830. More of it came from a series of lectures on the philosophy of history that he gave at Boston's Masonic Temple from 1836 into 1837; the lectures were never published on their own. The essay was first printed in 1841, in the collection Essays: First Series.
