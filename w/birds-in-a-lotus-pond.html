Birds in a Lotus Pond (detail)

Bada Shanren · ca. 1690 · Handscroll, ink on satin · 27.3 × 205.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bada Shanren painted this handscroll in ink on satin, a little over two metres long. Between 1684 and 1690 he painted myna birds, lotuses and rocks more than anything else. He had left the monastery around 1680, remarried and parted within a few years, and behaved so strangely that many believed he was feigning madness to keep out of politics. From 1684 he stayed in Nanchang and lived on scanty earnings.
