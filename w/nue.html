Nue (One Hundred Noh Dramas)

Tsukioka Kōgyo · 1898–1903 · Color woodblock print · 37.8 × 25.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Noh play Nue is by Zeami Motokiyo and is based on the Tale of the Heike, which describes the creature with a monkey's head, a tiger's limbs, a raccoon dog's body and the front half of a snake for a tail. Kōgyo was the student and adopted son of Tsukioka Yoshitoshi, and for most of his career made pictures of Noh. This series of one hundred plays appeared between 1898 and 1903.
