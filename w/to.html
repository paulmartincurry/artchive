The Artchive
I remember the marvellous moment
you appeared before me,
like a transient vision,
like pure beauty’s spirit.

To *** (Я помню чудное мгновенье)

Alexander Pushkin · 1825

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Pushkin wrote the poem no later than 19 July 1825, while he was confined to his family estate of Mikhailovskoye. By the generally accepted account it is addressed to Anna Kern, who was staying that year with her aunt on the neighbouring estate of Trigorskoye.
