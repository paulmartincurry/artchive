I pass through the pores of the ocean and shores;
I change, but I cannot die.

The Cloud (closing stanza)

Percy Bysshe Shelley · 1820

Shelley wrote The Cloud during late 1819 or early 1820 and submitted it for publication on 12 July 1820. It came out that August in the collection Prometheus Unbound, A Lyrical Drama, in Four Acts, With Other Poems.
