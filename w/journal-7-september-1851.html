Nothing is so much to be feared as fear. Atheism may comparatively be popular with God himself.

Journal, 7 September 1851

Henry David Thoreau · September 7, 1851

Thoreau began the journal at Emerson's suggestion, and his first essay in print was made of revised passages from it. His books, journals and poetry amount to more than 20 volumes. This entry is dated September 7 in the volume that runs from 1850 to September 15, 1851.
