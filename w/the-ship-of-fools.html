The Ship of Fools

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1490–1500 · Oil on wood · 58 × 33 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use

This panel is a fragment: about two thirds of one wing of a triptych that was cut into parts. The bottom third is at the Yale University Art Gallery, and the other wing is Death and the Miser in Washington; tree-ring study shows the two were painted on wood from the same tree. Camille Benoit gave the panel to the Louvre in 1918.
