Large Wind-up Automaton of Asahina Saburo

Utagawa Sadahide · c. 1847/48 · Color woodblock print; oban diptych · Art Institute of Chicago

The design is printed across two sheets, each 25 by 37.3 centimetres. Sadahide, born Hashimoto Kenjirō in 1807, was about forty. He had joined Kunisada's studio in the 1820s, and at twenty-one his name was fourth on a stone monument listing the master's students. Most of his early prints were of beautiful women; in the 1830s and 1840s he moved on to landscapes and warriors.
