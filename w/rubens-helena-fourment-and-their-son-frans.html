Rubens, Helena Fourment, and Their Son Frans

Peter Paul Rubens · ca. 1635 · Oil on wood · 80 1/4 × 62 1/4 in. (203.8 × 158.1 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Rubens married Helena Fourment in 1630. He was born in 1577 and she in 1614, and their son Frans followed in 1633. The portrait is painted on wood and stands just over two metres tall. Rubens was one of the last major artists to keep using wooden panels, even for very large works. He died in Antwerp in 1640.
