Les parfums ne font pas frissonner sa narine;
Il dort dans le soleil, la main sur sa poitrine
Tranquille. Il a deux trous rouges au côté droit.

Le Dormeur du val

Arthur Rimbaud · 1870

The Franco-Prussian War broke out on 19 July 1870, when Rimbaud was fifteen, and his school in Charleville became a military hospital. At that school he had won eight first prizes in the French academic competitions of 1869. He completely stopped writing literature at the age of 20.
