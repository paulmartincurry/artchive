The Card Players

Paul Cézanne · 1890–92 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Cézanne was in his early fifties when he painted this version, on a canvas 65.4 by 81.9 centimetres. Until the late 1890s his buyers were mostly fellow artists such as Camille Pissarro and the dealer Ambroise Vollard, who gave him his first solo exhibition in Paris in 1895. The painting came to the Met in 1960 as a bequest from Stephen C. Clark.
