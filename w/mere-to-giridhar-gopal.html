He is my very Lord, and He alone.
Mother and Father, kinsfolk I disown,

Mere to Giridhar Gopal

Mirabai · 16th century

Translated by Inayat Khan and Jessie Duncan Westbrook, 1915

Mirabai was born into a Rajput royal family and married in 1516 to the crown prince of Mewar, who died of battle wounds in 1521. Her poems are sung verses in the Rajasthani language, each with a melody assigned to it. Only two poems attributed to her have been found in manuscripts older than the early eighteenth century, and scholars are divided on how many of the hundreds under her name she wrote.
