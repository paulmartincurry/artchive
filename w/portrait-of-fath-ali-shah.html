Portrait of Fath-Ali Shah

Mihr 'Ali · 1809–1810 · Oil on canvas · Hermitage Museum

Free to use

Fath-Ali Shah was in his late thirties and had ruled Iran for about twelve years when Mihr 'Ali painted him. Twenty-five portraits of the shah are known, and he ordered many of them himself to magnify his rule. His black beard, which reached below his waist, is in all of them. Another portrait of him by the same painter is in the Brooklyn Museum.
