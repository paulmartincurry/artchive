The Vision of Saint John

El Greco · c. 1608–14 · Oil on canvas · 222.3 × 193 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

El Greco painted this in the last years of his life for a side altar of a church outside the walls of Toledo, and the Met calls it unfinished. An attempted restoration in 1880 took at least 175 cm off the top of the canvas. The painter Ignacio Zuloaga bought it for 1,000 pesetas after 1897, and Picasso studied it in Zuloaga's Paris studio. The Met acquired it in 1956.
