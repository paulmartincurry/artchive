Alfred, Lord Tennyson

Julia Margaret Cameron · May 1865 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · image 22.9 × 18.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1860, after an extended visit to Tennyson at Freshwater on the Isle of Wight, Cameron bought a house next door. She met many of the subjects of her later portraits there. This print, made in May 1865, has a rounded top and is 23 centimetres high.
