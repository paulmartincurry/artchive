Lilac

Philipp Otto Runge · 1800–1805 · Silhouette cut from off-white laid paper mounted to modern blue laid paper · Sheet: 39.4 x 27 cm (15 1/2 x 10 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Runge learned paper cutting, a folk craft, from his mother, and made well over a hundred plant silhouettes like this one, sometimes as gifts. This one was given to the Speckter family of artists in Hamburg.
