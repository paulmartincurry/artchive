Alone, I come to the road.
The stony track gleams in the mist:
the calm night listens to God,
and star is speaking to star.

Alone (Выхожу один я на дорогу)

Mikhail Lermontov · 1841

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Lermontov wrote the poem in Stavropol at the end of May or the beginning of June 1841. That year he was killed in a duel with a fellow officer, Nikolai Martynov. It was first published in 1843.
