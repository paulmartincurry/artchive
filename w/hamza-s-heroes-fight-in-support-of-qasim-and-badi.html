Hamza's Heroes Fight in Support of Qasim and Badi'uzzaman (Hamzanama)

Attributed to Shravana, Dasavanta and Tara · c. 1564–69 · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on cloth; mounted on paper · 68.6 × 54 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Dasavanta, one of the three painters named for this page, was the son of a palanquin-bearer. The emperor learned of him after he showed his talent by drawing on walls, and set him to study under the Persian master 'Abd al-Samad. Akbar's administrative record names him among the three most important artists of the time. He wounded himself with a dagger in 1584 and died of it.
