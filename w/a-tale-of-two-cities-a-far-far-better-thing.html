It is a far, far better thing that I do, than I have ever done; it is a far, far better rest that I go to than I have ever known.

A Tale of Two Cities — A far, far better thing (closing)

Charles Dickens · 1859

These lines closed the last weekly instalment, which ran on 26 November 1859, 30 weeks after the first. The novel has 45 chapters. Dickens had left school at twelve to work in a boot-blacking factory while his father was held in a debtors' prison, and he began his writing life as a journalist. He edited a weekly journal for 20 years.
