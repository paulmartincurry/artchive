Are you sorry for having stolen the potion that has set you
Over purple seas and blue skies, to brood through the long nights?

To the Moon Goddess

Li Shangyin · before 858; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Li Shangyin was born in 812 or 813 and died in 858. Among his poems are many left without titles, and their meaning is still disputed.
