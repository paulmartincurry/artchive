London

William Blake · 1794

William Blake etched the words and pictures of Songs of Experience onto copper plates and printed and hand-coloured the copies himself, so no two are the same. He kept changing the wording and the order of the poems from one printing to the next. Apart from three years in Felpham, he lived in London his whole life. Ralph Vaughan Williams set this poem to music in 1958, and Benjamin Britten in 1965.
