Middlemarch (Finale)

George Eliot (Mary Ann Evans) · 1872

The novel was made from two unfinished pieces. Eliot began the first in August 1869 and stopped that autumn, when her partner's son Thornie died of tuberculosis. In December she started a separate story, Miss Brooke, and wrote 100 pages of it in a month; by March 1871 she was joining the two. The result ran too long for the usual three volumes, so it was sold in eight paper-bound parts from December 1871 to December 1872. She turned fifty-three in the month before the last part came out.
