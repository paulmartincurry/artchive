Wolf, Apsaroke

Edward S. Curtis · 1908 · Photogravure, plate 142 from "The North American Indian, Volume 4" (1909) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Curtis took this portrait in 1908, and it was published the next year as plate 142 in the fourth volume of The North American Indian. Morgan's money could be spent only on fieldwork, not on writing or printing the books, and Curtis drew no salary from the project, which ran for more than twenty years. His field assistants included Alexander B. Upshaw, a member of the Apsáalooke tribe.
