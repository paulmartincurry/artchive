Notre-Dame

Eugène Atget · 1922 · Albumen silver print · sheet 22.6 × 17.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Atget used a large-format wooden bellows camera with a rapid rectilinear lens, and went on using it after hand-held cameras became available. This print carries his stamp on the back. It passed in 1927 to Berenice Abbott and Julien Levy in New York, and in 1968 to the Museum of Modern Art.
