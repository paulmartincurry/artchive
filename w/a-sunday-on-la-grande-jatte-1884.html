A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — 1884

Georges Seurat · 1884–86, border added 1888–89 · Oil on canvas · 207.5 × 308.1 cm (81 3/4 × 121 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The canvas is about two metres high and three wide. Two years after starting it, Seurat went back in and added figures, among them the monkey on the leash, and later still he painted the border. It was first shown at the eighth Impressionist exhibition in Paris in May 1886. After his death his family sold it in 1900 for 800 francs; in 1924 Frederic Clay and Helen Birch Bartlett of Chicago bought it for 20,000 dollars, and they gave it to the Art Institute in 1926.
