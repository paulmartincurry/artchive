Le Déjeuner sur l'herbe

Édouard Manet · 1862–1863 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

The Salon jury of 1863 rejected this painting, and Manet showed it that year at the Salon des Refusés under the title Le Bain; he gave it its present name in 1867, taking it from a painting by Claude Monet. The nude is thought to be his regular model Victorine Meurent, and the two men were based on his brothers and his brother-in-law, the sculptor Ferdinand Leenhoff. The canvas, 208 by 264.5 centimetres, was a size normally kept for history painting. It stayed unsold until 1878, when the opera singer Jean-Baptiste Faure bought it for 2,600 francs.
