“All nations striving strong to make
Red war yet redder. Mad as hatters
They do no more for Christés sake
Than you who are helpless in such matters.

Channel Firing

Thomas Hardy · 1914

Channel Firing was collected in Satires of Circumstance, published in 1914. The title of the book was picked by the publisher, and Hardy disapproved of it. In the same year Hardy, then in his seventies, was one of 53 British authors, among them H. G. Wells and Rudyard Kipling, who signed the Authors' Declaration justifying Britain's part in the First World War.
