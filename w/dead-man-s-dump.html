Earth has waited for them,
All the time of their growth
Fretting for their decay:
Now she has them at last!

Dead Man's Dump (opening)

Isaac Rosenberg · 1917

In January 1917, after his family and friends asked that he be taken out of the front line, Rosenberg was moved to a works battalion and set to delivering barbed wire to the trenches. He wrote this poem during that period. Before the war he had trained as a painter at the Slade School of Fine Art in London, alongside Stanley Spencer and Paul Nash, and his self-portraits now hang in the National Portrait Gallery and Tate Britain.
