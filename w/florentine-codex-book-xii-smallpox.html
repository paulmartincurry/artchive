Florentine Codex, Book XII: Smallpox (folio 53 verso)

Bernardino de Sahagún · 1577 · Ink and paint on paper · 31 × 21.2 cm · Biblioteca Medicea Laurenziana, Florence

Free to use

The figures on this page of Book XII are identified as victims of the smallpox epidemic, probably the one that struck the Valley of Mexico during the Spanish conquest. The disease broke out in Tenochtitlan in late October 1520 and lasted sixty days, and the account in the codex records people who starved in their beds because everyone else was too sick to care for them. The ruler Cuitlahuac caught it and died after ruling for eighty days.
