The Whore of Babylon (The Apocalypse)

Albrecht Dürer · 1498 · Woodcut · Sheet: 39.5 × 28.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Figures in turbans appear in four of the fifteen Apocalypse woodcuts, and this is one of them. Dürer did not know the Ottoman Turks at first hand. He learned about them from the painter Gentile Bellini, who had been at the court of Mehmed II.
