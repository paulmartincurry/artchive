La Bièvre

Charles Marville · ca. 1862 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · 27.8 × 37.6 cm. (10 15/16 × 14 13/16 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Marville was born Charles-François Bossu, and since bossu is French for hunchback he took a new name around 1832. For about ten years he drew illustrations on wooden blocks for engravers, then took up photography around 1850. His real name was only discovered recently, by Sarah Kennel of the National Gallery of Art and the researcher Daniel Catan. He was named photographer of the city of Paris in 1862, the year this print is dated.
