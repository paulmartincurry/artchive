Voynich Manuscript: The Rosettes Foldout

Maker unknown · early 15th century · Manuscript on parchment · Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, Yale University

Free to use

One foldout in the manuscript spans six pages and is commonly called the Rosettes folio. The parchment was radiocarbon dated at the University of Arizona in 2009 to between 1404 and 1438. Wilfrid Voynich, a Polish book dealer, bought the manuscript in 1912.
