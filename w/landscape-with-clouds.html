Landscape with Clouds

Roger Fenton · probably 1856 · Salted paper print from glass negative · 31.4 × 44.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The museum dates this print to probably 1856. Roger Fenton had qualified as a solicitor in 1847, and in 1853 he became a founder and the first secretary of the London Photographic Society, later the Royal Photographic Society. The image is 31.4 by 44.3 centimetres and irregular in outline.
