Double-Sided Gospel Leaf

Maker unknown · First half 14th century · Tempera on parchment · 27.8 × 19 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This leaf, painted in tempera on parchment in the first half of the fourteenth century, is painted on both sides and measures 27.8 by 19 centimetres. Illuminated manuscripts are older than any surviving church painting in Ethiopia: the Garima Gospels of the fourth to sixth centuries already contain painted scenes. The Met bought the leaf in 2006 with a gift from Oscar de la Renta Ltd.
