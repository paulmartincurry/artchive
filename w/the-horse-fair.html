The Horse Fair

Rosa Bonheur · 1852–55 · Oil on canvas · 96 1/4 × 199 1/2 in. (244.5 × 506.7 cm) · The Met

Free to use

For a year and a half, from the summer of 1850 to the end of 1851, Rosa Bonheur went twice a week to sketch at the horse market on the Boulevard de l'Hôpital in Paris. She got a permit from the Paris police to dress as a man so as not to draw attention. The canvas is about five metres wide. The city of Bordeaux declined to buy it for 15,000 francs, and in 1854 the dealer Ernest Gambart paid 40,000. Cornelius Vanderbilt II bought it at auction for 53,000 dollars in March 1887 and gave it to the Metropolitan Museum straight away.
