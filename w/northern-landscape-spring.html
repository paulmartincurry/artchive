Northern Landscape, Spring

Caspar David Friedrich · c. 1825 · Oil on canvas · 35.3 × 49.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

At an estate sale in Munich in 2003 this canvas was sold as the work of an anonymous artist. It was auctioned in London in June 2004, and the National Gallery of Art bought it that October. Friedrich made his outdoor studies almost exclusively in pencil and rendered the atmospheric effects of his paintings from memory.
