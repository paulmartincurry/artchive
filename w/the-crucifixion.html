The Crucifixion

Francisco de Zurbarán · 1627 · Oil on canvas · 290.3 × 165.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1626 the Dominican monastery of San Pablo el Real in Seville commissioned a cycle of paintings from Zurbarán, and this was one of them. It was installed in a dimly lit space, where visitors saw it through a grille. He signed and dated it 1627 on a scrap of paper painted at the foot of the cross. He was twenty-eight or twenty-nine.
