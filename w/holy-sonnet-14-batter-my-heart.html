Batter my heart, three person'd God; for, you
As yet but knocke, breathe, shine, and seeke to mend;

Holy Sonnet 14: Batter my heart

John Donne · published 1633

Scholars date most of the Holy Sonnets to 1609 and 1610, when Donne was short of money and moving from the Catholic faith of his family to the Church of England. None was printed while he lived; they passed among friends in manuscript and reached print in 1633. J. Robert Oppenheimer is thought to have named the Trinity test site after a phrase in this sonnet, and Benjamin Britten set it to music in August 1945.
