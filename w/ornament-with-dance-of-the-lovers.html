Ornament with Dance of the Lovers

Israhel van Meckenem · c. 1490 · engraving · Cleveland Museum of Art

Ornament prints like this one were often used by other artists and craftsmen as a source of motifs for decorative arts and furniture. Israhel van Meckenem was a goldsmith as well as an engraver, back in Bocholt by about 1480 and taking commissions from the town council. He made over 620 engravings, most of them copies of other prints, and around 150 of his own design. This impression, 26.8 centimetres wide, is the second state, and Cleveland bought it from a New York gallery in 1952.
