Thou hast made me endless, such is thy pleasure. This frail vessel thou emptiest again and again, and fillest it ever with fresh life.

Gitanjali 1

Rabindranath Tagore · 1912

Tagore made this English version himself, one of 103 poems he rendered freely from his own Bengali before a visit to England in 1912. The India Society of London published the first edition that year, priced at ten and a half shillings.
