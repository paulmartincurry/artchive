Vase

Ferdinand Barbedienne · c. 1862 · gilt bronze with enamel decoration · 78.7 x 27.3 cm (31 x 10 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The model was introduced at the International Exhibition in London in 1862 and was so popular it was made in several colours and shapes. In champlevé, hollows are cut into the metal and filled with enamel.
