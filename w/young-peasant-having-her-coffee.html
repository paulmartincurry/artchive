Young Peasant Having Her Coffee

Camille Pissarro · 1881 · Oil on canvas · 65.3 × 54.8 cm (25 11/16 × 21 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In the 1880s Camille Pissarro worked to keep the Impressionists together, persuading Caillebotte and Monet to show at the seventh exhibition in 1882, where he hung several of his own paintings of peasant girls.
