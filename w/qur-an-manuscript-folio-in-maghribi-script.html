Qur'an Manuscript Folio in Maghribi Script

Maker unknown · 1200s–1300s · ink, gold, and color on parchment · Sheet: 52.7 × 55.4 cm (20 3/4 × 21 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The page is more than half a metre in each direction and carries three verses from the Chapter of Light. Vowels are marked in red and diacritics in blue, as a guide for reading the text aloud to an assembly. It was written in southern Spain. Cleveland bought the folio in 1933 from Mehmet Aga-Oglu of Ann Arbor, Michigan.
