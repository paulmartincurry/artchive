Carolina Parrot (The Birds of America, Plate 26)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1827 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 99.7 × 66.7 cm (39 1/4 × 26 1/4 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The Carolina parakeet is one of five birds in the book that are now extinct. Audubon remarked on its decline as early as 1832, and noted that cats seemed to die from eating the birds.
