Zora Neale Hurston · 1942

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Hurston's publishers forced extensive changes on this autobiography of 1942. She had to remove a lengthy attack on American imperialism in Asia, tone down sexually explicit anthropological content and take out some libellous passages.
