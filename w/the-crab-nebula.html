The Crab Nebula

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA and ESA · 1999–2000, released 2005 · Space telescope image

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/ESA/JPL/Arizona State Univ.

The picture was assembled from 24 exposures made with the Hubble Space Telescope's Wide Field and Planetary Camera 2 in October 1999, January 2000 and December 2000. The nebula is what remains of a star that exploded in 1054. Chinese astronomers recorded it on 4 July of that year as a new star bright enough to be seen in daylight.
