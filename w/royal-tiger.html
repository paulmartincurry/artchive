Royal Tiger

Eugène Delacroix · 1829 · Lithograph · Image: 32.8 × 46.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Delacroix made this lithograph in 1829. The impression is from the second of four states, with an image about 33 centimetres high and 47 wide. Susan Dwight Bliss bequeathed it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1966.
