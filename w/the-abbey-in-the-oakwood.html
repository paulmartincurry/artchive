The Abbey in the Oakwood (Abtei im Eichwald)

Caspar David Friedrich · 1809–1810 · Oil on canvas · Alte Nationalgalerie, Berlin

Free to use

Friedrich painted his landscapes indoors, in his Dresden studio, working from sketches drawn outside. The ruin here comes from his studies of Eldena Abbey, and the same oaks turn up, slightly altered, in other pictures. He may have started in June 1809, and a visitor found the canvas nearly finished on 24 September 1810. At the Berlin exhibition that year it hung, at his request, beneath The Monk by the Sea. King Frederick William III bought both.
