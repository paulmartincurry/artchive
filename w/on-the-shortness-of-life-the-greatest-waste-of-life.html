…yet postponement is the greatest waste of life: it wrings day after day from us, and takes away the present by promising something hereafter: there is no such obstacle to true living as waiting, which loses to-day while it is depending on the morrow.

On the Shortness of Life — The greatest waste of life (IX)

Seneca the Younger · c. 49 CE

Translated by Aubrey Stewart

On the Shortness of Life is a moral essay that Seneca wrote sometime around the year 49 AD. It is addressed to his father-in-law, Paulinus.
