But toward which corner of the mountain,
How can I tell, through all these clouds?

A Note Left for an Absent Recluse

Jia Dao (Tang Dynasty) · ca. 820 CE

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu, 1929 · Public domain in the United States (published 1929); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Jia Dao was born near what is now Beijing and spent a period as a Buddhist monk before going to the capital, Chang'an. There he became a disciple of the writer Han Yu. He sat the jinshi examination for the civil service several times and failed each time.
