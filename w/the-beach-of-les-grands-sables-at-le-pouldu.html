The Beach of Les Grands Sables at Le Pouldu

Paul Sérusier · 1890 · Oil on canvas · 60 × 70 cm (23 5/8 × 27 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Sérusier went to Le Pouldu in Brittany in the summer of 1890 and was struck by its sweeping dunes and wind-bent trees. The tiny figure among the dunes shows how big they are.
