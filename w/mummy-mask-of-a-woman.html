Mummy Mask of a Woman

Maker unknown · 60–70 CE · Cartonnage, plaster, paint, plant fibers · L. 63 cm; H. 53 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The mask is built from cartonnage, plaster, paint and plant fibres and is 63 centimetres long. It is possibly from Meir in Middle Egypt. The museum bought it in 1919.
