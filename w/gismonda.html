Gismonda

Alphonse Mucha · 1895 · Colour lithograph (poster); proof of the final printing (Épreuve du tirage définitif) · 217 x 75 cm · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

Sarah Bernhardt wanted a poster to announce that Gismonda would run on after the Christmas break, and she insisted it be ready by 1 January 1895. Because of the holidays none of the regular artists at the printer Lemercier was free, and the job went to Mucha. Bernhardt was pleased: she ordered four thousand copies in 1895 and 1896 and gave him a six-year contract. The poster is more than two metres tall.
