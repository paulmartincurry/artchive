Geschrei (The Scream)

Edvard Munch · 1895 · Lithograph · Sheet: 48.3 × 38.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Besides two painted versions and two in pastel, Munch drew the image on a lithograph stone, and several prints from it survive. This impression, dated 1895, carries the German title Geschrei. The sheet is 48.3 by 38.8 centimetres.
