Is It a Crime for a U.S. Citizen to Vote?

Susan B. Anthony · 1873

In 1872 Susan B. Anthony was arrested in Rochester, New York, her home town, for voting in violation of laws that allowed only men to vote. She was fifty-two. She was convicted and fined $100, refused to pay, and the authorities declined to take further action. She gave as many as 75 to 100 speeches a year and lived almost entirely on the fees she earned as a speaker.
