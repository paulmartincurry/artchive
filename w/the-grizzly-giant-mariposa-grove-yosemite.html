The Grizzly Giant, Mariposa Grove, Yosemite

Carleton Watkins · 1861 · Albumen silver print from glass negative · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Watkins took this on his first trip to Yosemite, in July 1861, when he was thirty-one. He carried a mammoth-plate camera that used glass plates 18 by 22 inches, large enough to take in the whole sequoia, which had not been done before. He came back with thirty mammoth plates and one hundred stereo negatives. Ten years earlier he had come to San Francisco from New York to look for gold.
