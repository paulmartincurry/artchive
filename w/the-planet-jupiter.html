The Planet Jupiter (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

Jupiter was drawn as Trouvelot saw it at half past nine on the evening of 1 November 1880. It is among the latest of the observations in the set, made about a year before the plates were published and not long before he went back to France, where he was by 1882.
