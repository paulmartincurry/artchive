Battle between Alexander and Darius (Khamsa of Nizami)

Maker unknown · 1524–25 (dated 931 AH) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · Painting 18.7 × 12.2 cm; page 32.1 × 22.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Nizami wrote his Book of Alexander in 1194, or between 1196 and 1202, as one of the five poems of the Khamsa. This copy was written out in Herat in 1524–25 by the calligrapher Sultan Muhammad Nur, and the battle fills a painting 18.7 cm high on folio 279.
