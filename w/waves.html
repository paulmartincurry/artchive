Waves

Nakamura Hōchū · late 1700s-early 1800s · fan painting mounted as a hanging scroll; ink and color with gold on paper · Image: 39.8 × 53 cm (15 11/16 × 20 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This began as a folding paper fan to be carried in the hand. Someone later took the painting off its folding frame and mounted it as a hanging scroll. The fan leaf is 39.8 by 53 centimetres. Nakamura Hōchū, who died in 1819, took his lead from the earlier painter Ogata Kōrin. The museum bought the scroll from a Tokyo gallery in 1999.
