We French Workers Warn You - Defeat Means Slavery, Starvation, Death

Ben Shahn · 1942 · Poster, color lithograph · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Ben Shahn worked for the Office of War Information during the war years of 1942 and 1943, and only two of his posters for it were published. This one is dated 1942. Shahn had trained as a lithographer in Brooklyn, where his family settled after immigrating from the Russian Empire in 1906.
