Frances Benjamin Johnston, Self-Portrait in Her Studio

Frances Benjamin Johnston · c. 1896 · Gelatin silver print · 19.7 × 15.7 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Johnston opened her own studio in Washington, D.C., in 1894 and was then the only woman photographer in the city. On the mantelpiece stand six of her portraits of men, among them the poet Bliss Carman. The original glass negative is broken; this print was made by the Library of Congress.
