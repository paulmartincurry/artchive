To her fair works did nature link
The human soul that through me ran;
And much it griev’d my heart to think
What man has made of man.

Lines Written in Early Spring

William Wordsworth · 1798

In 1798 Wordsworth and his sister Dorothy were living at Alfoxton House in Somerset, a few miles from Coleridge's home in Nether Stowey. A legacy of £900 from Raisley Calvert three years earlier had allowed him to pursue a career as a poet. Lyrical Ballads, which he and Coleridge produced that year, gave neither man's name as author.
