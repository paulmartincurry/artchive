Amulet of a Lion-Headed Goddess with Kitten

Maker unknown · Third Intermediate Period, Dynasty 21–25 (c. 1069–664 BCE) · Faience · 7.7 × 1.8 × 2.7 cm (3 1/16 × 3/4 × 1 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Egyptians, living and dead, wore amulets of gods to bring protection and health. This one probably shows the lioness goddess Sekhmet or the cat goddess Bastet, both of whom acted as the Eye of Re, the sun god's protector.
