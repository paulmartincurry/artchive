Mont-Saint-Michel

Maker unknown · 11th century and later

Free to use with credit. Photo: Lynx1211, CC BY-SA 4.0

According to legend, the archangel Michael appeared to Aubert, bishop of Avranches, in 708 and told him to build a church on the rock. The tides here rise and fall by roughly fourteen metres, and the island was never taken during the Hundred Years' War. After the Revolution the abbey was closed and turned into a prison, which shut in 1863. Victor Hugo was among those who campaigned for its restoration, and it was declared a historic monument in 1874.
