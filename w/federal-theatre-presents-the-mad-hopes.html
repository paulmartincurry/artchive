Federal Theatre Presents The Mad Hopes

Maker unknown · 1938 · Colour silkscreen on board · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The poster advertised a Federal Theatre Project staging of Romney Brent's comedy The Mad Hopes at the Avery Memorial theatre in Connecticut in 1938, and it promises a Surrealist setting. It is a silkscreen print on board. The project was set up as a relief measure to employ theatre workers during the Depression, not as a cultural programme.
