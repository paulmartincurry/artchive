Satire X

Juvenal (Decimus Junius Juvenalis) · ca. 110–127 CE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Satire 10 opens the fourth of the five books into which Juvenal's sixteen satires are grouped. The first book probably dates to 100 AD and the fifth to some time after 127, so this poem falls between them. The poems are in dactylic hexameter and run from 130 to 695 lines. The traditional biographies say he practised rhetoric until he was middle-aged and turned to satire late, though little about his life can be confirmed.
