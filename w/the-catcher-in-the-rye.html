The Catcher in the Rye

J. D. Salinger · 1951

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Holden Caulfield first appeared in a short story that J. D. Salinger sold to The New Yorker in November 1941. The magazine held it until December 1946 because of the war. That same year it accepted a 90-page manuscript about Holden, which Salinger then withdrew. The novel came out on July 16, 1951, when he was thirty-two, and has sold more than 65 million copies.
