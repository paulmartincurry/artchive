Pause you who read this, and think for a moment of the long chain of iron or gold, of thorns or flowers, that would never have bound you, but for the formation of the first link on one memorable day.

Great Expectations — The long chain of iron or gold (Ch. 9)

Charles Dickens · 1861

Sales of Dickens's weekly magazine, All the Year Round, fell in September 1860, and he turned this story into its new serial to save it. It ran in thirty-six episodes starting on 1 December. He worked without his usual number plans, with only a few notes on the characters' ages, the tide ranges for chapter 54 and the draft of an ending. He rewrote that ending before publication after Edward Bulwer-Lytton said it was too sad.
