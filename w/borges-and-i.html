Borges and I

Jorge Luis Borges · 1960 (English 1962)

Translated by James E. Irby · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The piece comes from The Maker, a collection first published in 1960. Borges was about sixty then and had been completely blind since the age of fifty-five. He had worked as a librarian, and since 1955 he had been director of Argentina's National Public Library. The year after the book came out he shared the first International Formentor Prize with Samuel Beckett.
