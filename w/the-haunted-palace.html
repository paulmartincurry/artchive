The Haunted Palace (The Bells and Other Poems)

Edmund Dulac · published 1912 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

Poe published The Haunted Palace in 1839 as a poem of six stanzas, and later set it inside his story The Fall of the House of Usher. Dulac's pictures were commissioned by the Leicester Galleries, which sold the originals at a yearly exhibition, while Hodder & Stoughton bought the right to reproduce them, publishing one of his gift books each year.
