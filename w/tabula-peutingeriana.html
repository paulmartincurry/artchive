Tabula Peutingeriana

Maker unknown · 4th century / ca. 1200 copy · Parchment · Austrian National Library, Vienna

Free to use

The scroll is 6.75 metres long and only 0.35 metres high, made of eleven sections of parchment; a twelfth, at the western end, is lost. It names no fewer than 555 cities and 3,500 other places. Conrad Celtes came upon it in a library in Worms in 1494, and several scholars hold that he stole it. He left it in 1508 to Konrad Peutinger, whose family kept it for more than two hundred years. It has been in Vienna since 1738 and is too fragile to be on public display.
