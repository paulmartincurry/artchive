Death is not a sleeping-room that can be entered and come out of again.

Ashanti Proverbs on Death (Nos. 59–71)

Ashanti (Asante) proverbs · Printed 1916

Translated by R. S. Rattray, 1916

R. S. Rattray joined the Gold Coast Customs Service in 1906 and became Assistant District Commissioner at Ejura in 1911. He noted that the Ashanti pictured Death as a skeleton, a skull with empty eye sockets but with ears, and that Death is blind but can hear: when he hears that someone alone remains, he wants to finish his work.
