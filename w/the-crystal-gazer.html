The Crystal Gazer

Gertrude Käsebier · 1904, printed 1910 · Photogravure · 11.3 × 8.9 cm (image) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Käsebier made the photograph in 1904, and this gravure was printed in 1910, the year her husband Eduard died. Stieglitz had turned against her commercial work by then, but he still hung 22 of her earlier pictures in the exhibition of pictorialists at the Albright-Knox Art Gallery that year. In 1912 she became the first member to resign from the Photo-Secession.
