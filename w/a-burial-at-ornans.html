A Burial at Ornans

Gustave Courbet · 1849–50 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

The funeral takes place in Ornans, the small town where Courbet was born, and the townspeople posed for him. The figure at the back on the far left has been identified as his grandfather Oudot, who had died the year before. Courbet called the two old men beside the white dog veterans of the Revolution of 1793; they have since been identified as Secrétan and Cardet, friends of his grandfather. Shown at the Paris Salon of 1850, the canvas caused an uproar, largely because a size reserved for history painting had been spent on a country burial.
