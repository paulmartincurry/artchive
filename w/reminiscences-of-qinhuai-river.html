Reminiscences of Qinhuai River (album leaf)

Shitao · 1642–1707 · Album leaf, ink and color on Song paper · 25.5 × 20.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Shitao painted this album leaf on paper made in the Song dynasty, already centuries old when he used it. He had visited the Qinhuai River and the country around Nanjing in the 1680s, but it is not known whether the album shows particular places. The leaf is about 25 by 20 centimetres. The album belonged to C. C. Wang in New York, who sold it to Cleveland in 1966.
