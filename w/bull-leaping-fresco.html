Bull-Leaping Fresco

Maker unknown · c. 1450 BC · Stucco panel with scene in relief · 78.2 × 104.5 cm · Heraklion Archaeological Museum

Free to use

The panel is the most completely restored of several that were set high on the east wall of the palace at Knossos. They were painted on stucco modelled in relief while it was still fresh. The flakes fell from the upper storey when the palace was destroyed, probably by an earthquake. There are more fragments than the reconstruction uses, and it is generally thought that there were several bull-leaping scenes.
