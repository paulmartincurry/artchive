Old Nick, the Devil

Florian Rokita · c. 1936 · Watercolor, gouache, and graphite on paperboard · 50.8 × 32.1 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Florian Rokita painted this record of a figure 17 inches high for the Index of American Design, a programme of the Federal Art Project. About 400 artists made 18,257 watercolours of American decorative and folk objects for it between 1935 and 1942.
