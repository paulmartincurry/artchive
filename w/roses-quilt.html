Roses Quilt

Letticia Keziah Vernon · c. 1830/35 · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Letticia Keziah Vernon was in her late thirties when she made this quilt. It passed to her daughter, Elizabeth Thomas Freeland, and then to her granddaughter, Lela M. Riley of Chicago, who gave it to the Art Institute in 1956 in memory of her mother.
