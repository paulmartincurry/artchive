The Sampling Officials of the Amsterdam Drapers' Guild, Known as 'The Syndics'

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1662 · Oil on canvas · 191.5 × 279 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The five men were the wardens who inspected cloth for the Amsterdam drapers' guild, shown with their servant. They held the unpaid post from Good Friday 1661 to Good Friday 1662 and met three times a week. Two were Catholics, one a Mennonite, one a Remonstrant and one a Reformed Protestant. The canvas is on loan from the City of Amsterdam.
