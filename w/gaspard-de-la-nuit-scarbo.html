But it’s Scarbo, the goblin, who bites my neck

Gaspard de la Nuit — Scarbo

Aloysius Bertrand · 1842

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Bertrand finished the book in 1836 and never saw it printed. One publisher announced it for October 1833 and did not deliver; another printed flyers for it in 1839 and then went out of business. He died of tuberculosis in a Paris hospital in April 1841, aged thirty-four. The book came out in November 1842, from a faulty copy of the manuscript, and sold 20 copies. In 1908 Maurice Ravel based a three-movement piano suite on three of its poems, with Scarbo as the last.
