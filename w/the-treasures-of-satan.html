The Treasures of Satan

Jean Delville · 1895 · Oil on canvas · 258 × 268 cm · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, Brussels

Free to use

Delville was twenty-eight when he painted this canvas, which is more than two and a half metres on each side. According to the art historian Brendan Cole it was one of his first breakthrough works. In the same year, 1895, he published Dialogue entre Nous, one of his books on esoteric subjects.
