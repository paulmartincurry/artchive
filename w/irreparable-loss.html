When my rude hammer to the stubborn stone
Gives human shape, now that, now this, at will,
Following his hand who wields and guides it still,
It moves upon another's feet alone:

Irreparable Loss (Sonnet LXI, after the death of Vittoria Colonna)

Michelangelo Buonarroti · after 1547

Translated by John Addington Symonds, 1878

Vittoria Colonna, Marchioness of Pescara, was a noblewoman and a poet. She was born at Marino, near Rome, in 1492 and died on 25 February 1547.
