Ghatotkacha and Three Demons Chase Bhagadatta (Razmnama)

Fazl · 1616–17 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Page 36.7 × 24 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This page comes from a copy of the Persian Mahabharata made in 1616–17 for 'Abd al-Rahim, the Khan Khanan, chief minister to Akbar and Jahangir, who paid for his own illustrated copy. Akbar had ordered the translation in 1582; Naqib Khan wrote the Persian text from explanations given by Hindu scholars, a task one inscription says took him a year and a half. The page later belonged to Hagop Kevorkian and was sold at Sotheby's in London in 1970.
