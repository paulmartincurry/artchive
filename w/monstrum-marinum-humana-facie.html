Monstrum Marinum Humana Facie (Monstrorum Historia)

Ulisse Aldrovandi · 1642 · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Ulisse Aldrovandi died in 1605, and this book was printed in Bologna by Nicolò Tebaldini in 1642, thirty-seven years later. He filled thirteen volumes with his notes and observations, and only four of them were published during his lifetime.
