Rondanini Pietà

Michelangelo · 1552–64 · Marble · H. 195 cm · Museo della Pietà Rondanini, Castello Sforzesco, Milan

Free to use with credit. Photo: Paolo da Reggio, CC BY-SA 3.0

Michelangelo worked on this marble from 1552 until the last days of his life, and he spent a whole day on it six days before his death in 1564. He cut the block back so far that, of the sculpture as first conceived, only a detached right arm of Christ survives. The name comes from the Palazzo Rondanini in Rome, where it stood in the courtyard for centuries.
