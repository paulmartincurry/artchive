The Eye, Like a Strange Balloon Moves Towards Infinity (To Edgar Poe, plate 1)

Odilon Redon · 1882 · Lithograph in black on ivory China paper, laid down on white wove paper · Image 26.3 × 19.8 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Odilon Redon was forty-two when he published this lithograph as the first plate of an album dedicated to Edgar Poe. He had learned etching and lithography from Rodolphe Bresdin in Bordeaux, and his first album of lithographs, Dans le Rêve, had appeared three years earlier. Wider recognition came only in 1884, when Joris-Karl Huysmans described his drawings in the novel À rebours.
