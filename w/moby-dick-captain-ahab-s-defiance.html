From hell’s heart I stab at thee;
for hate’s sake I spit my last breath.

Moby-Dick — Captain Ahab's Defiance (Ch. 135)

Herman Melville · 1851

The novel took eighteen months. Herman Melville began it in February 1850 and finished a year later than he had expected, writing most of it over the winter of 1850–1851 at Arrowhead, a house in Pittsfield, Massachusetts. He had been a common sailor from 1841 to 1844, including on whalers, and he based the ending on the sinking of the whaleship Essex in 1820. The book was a commercial failure and was out of print when he died in 1891.
