Ia Orana Maria (Hail Mary)

Paul Gauguin · 1891 · Oil on canvas · 44 3/4 × 34 1/2 in. (113.7 × 87.6 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Gauguin made this soon after he arrived in Tahiti, and it is the only openly Christian painting from his first stay there. The praying women at left are modelled on two monks in a relief at Borobudur in Java, which he knew from photographs he owned. After an exhibition in Paris in 1893 it sold to the collector Michel Manzi for 2,000 francs, the most Gauguin had yet been paid for a work. It came to the Met in 1951 with Sam A. Lewisohn's bequest.
