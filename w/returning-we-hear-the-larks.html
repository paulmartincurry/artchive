The Artchive
Death could drop from the dark
As easily as song—
But song only dropped,

Returning, We Hear the Larks

Isaac Rosenberg · 1922

On 21 March 1918 the German army began its Spring Offensive on the Western Front. Rosenberg was killed on the night of 1 April, just after finishing a night patrol, at Fampoux, north-east of Arras; whether by a sniper or in close combat is disputed. He was twenty-seven. The previous September he had spent ten days in London on leave.
