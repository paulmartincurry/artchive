Starry Night Over the Rhône

Vincent van Gogh · 1888 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Van Gogh painted this at night in September 1888 on the bank of the Rhône, a minute or two on foot from the Yellow House he was renting, and by his own account under a gas lamp. He reworked the foreground wet-in-wet as soon as the first state was done. The Great Bear in his sky could not be seen in the direction he was facing, only to the north. His brother Theo showed the canvas in Paris in 1889, though Vincent had proposed a different painting.
