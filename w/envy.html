Envy (The Seven Deadly Sins)

Pieter van der Heyden, after Pieter Bruegel the Elder · published 1558 · Engraving on laid paper · Plate: 22.7 × 29.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

The engraver's monogram is in the lower centre of the plate. Pieter van der Heyden, who is believed to have been born in Antwerp in about 1530, often marked his prints with the initials of his Latinised name, Peter Mercinus.
