What if I were to return to life again? What an eternity of days, and all mine! How I should grudge and count up every minute of it, so as to waste not a single instant!

The Idiot — Five Minutes to Live (Part I, Ch. V)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1869

Translated by Eva Martin

In 1849 Dostoevsky was arrested with other members of the Petrashevsky Circle and sent with a large group of prisoners to Semyonov Place for execution. As they stood waiting to be shot, a messenger interrupted with notice of a reprieve, which the Tsar had planned in advance. He began this novel in September 1867 in Switzerland, where he was living in extreme poverty after leaving Russia to escape his creditors.
