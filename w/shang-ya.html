When the hills are all flat
And the rivers are all dry,
When it lightens and thunders in winter,
When it rains and snows in summer,
When Heaven and Earth mingle--
Not till then will I part from you.

Shang Ya! (Oath)

Anonymous Han Dynasty Ballad · Han dynasty (206 BC–220 AD)

Translated by Arthur Waley

In Chinese this vow is Shang Ye, one of the Han dynasty songs grouped as yuefu. The Music Bureau that gave the form its name was a government office, and the word yuefu itself first appears in the Qin dynasty, which ruled from 221 to 206 BC.
