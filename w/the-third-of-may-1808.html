The Third of May 1808

Francisco de Goya · 1814 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

This painting was Goya's own suggestion. In February 1814, after the French had been expelled, he approached the provisional government of Spain, which commissioned it together with a companion of the same size, The Second of May 1808. By some accounts it then lay in storage for thirty to forty years before the public saw it, and the Prado did not list it in a published catalogue until 1872. Both canvases were damaged in a road accident while being taken by truck to Valencia for safety during the Spanish Civil War.
