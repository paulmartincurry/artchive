Hurt Hawks

Robinson Jeffers · 1928

Jeffers named the stone tower he built beside his house in Carmel Hawk Tower, after a hawk that appeared while he was working on it and disappeared the day it was finished. At the height of his fame he was one of the few poets to appear on the cover of Time magazine.
