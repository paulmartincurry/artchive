Seated Woman, Back View

Egon Schiele · 1917 · Watercolor, gouache, and graphite on paper · 46.4 × 29.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Schiele drew this in graphite and coloured it in watercolour and gouache in 1917, the year before his death. As a child he had spent many hours drawing trains, and his father, a station master, destroyed the sketchbooks because he saw the drawing as a detriment to his schoolwork. Scofield Thayer bequeathed the sheet to the Metropolitan Museum.
