The Child is Father of the Man;
And I could wish my days to be
Bound each to each by natural piety.

My Heart Leaps Up

William Wordsworth · 1802

These nine lines were written on the night of 26 March 1802 at Dove Cottage in Grasmere, where Wordsworth was staying with his sister Dorothy. He was thirty-two. He often thought about altering the poem afterwards but left it as first written. The next day he began his ode on Intimations of Immortality, which uses the last three lines as part of its epigraph.
