Death and the Miser

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1485–1490 · Oil on panel · National Gallery of Art, Washington

This narrow panel, 93 centimetres tall and 31 wide, was once one wing of a triptych whose centre is now missing. Bosch was influenced by the Ars moriendi, handbooks of text and woodcuts on how a Christian should die. He lived all his life in and near 's-Hertogenbosch and left no letters or diaries. Only about 25 paintings are now confidently identified as his.
