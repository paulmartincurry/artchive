Moonlight, Wood Island Light

Winslow Homer · 1894 · Oil on canvas · 78.1 × 102.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

According to his biographer William Howe Downes, Homer painted this on an impulse in less than five hours, by the light of the moon. The small spot of red on the horizon is the lighthouse on Wood Island, south of Prouts Neck in Maine.
