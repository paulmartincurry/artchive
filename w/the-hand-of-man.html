The Hand of Man

Alfred Stieglitz · 1902, printed 1920/39 · Gelatin silver print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Stieglitz took this photograph from the back of a moving train as it pulled out of the yard at Long Island City, New York. He was thirty-eight. It was published in January 1903 in the first issue of Camera Work, the journal he edited. This print was made between 1920 and 1939, and the image is 8.9 by 11.9 centimetres.
