Fishing in Spring, the Pont de Clichy (Asnières)

Vincent van Gogh · 1887 · Oil on canvas · 50.5 × 60 cm (19 7/8 × 23 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The setting is the Seine at the Clichy bridge near Asnières, north of Paris, where Van Gogh painted several times with his friend Paul Signac.
