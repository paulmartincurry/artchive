Thesaurus, Volume 1, Tab. LXII

Albertus Seba · 1734 · Hand-coloured engraving · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Seba sold his first collection outright to Peter the Great of Russia in 1716; together with Frederik Ruysch's collection it so enlarged the tsar's cabinet that a new building was put up for the Kunstkammer. Seba then built a second collection, larger than the first, and it is that collection his four-volume catalogue records.
