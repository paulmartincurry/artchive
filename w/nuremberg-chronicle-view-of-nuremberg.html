Nuremberg Chronicle: View of Nuremberg

Michael Wolgemut & Wilhelm Pleydenwurff · 1493 · Woodcut

Nuremberg is the only city in the book given a double-page illustration with no text, and it is the city where the book was printed. Two Nuremberg merchants paid for the project and agreed to advance 1,000 gulden for paper, printing and sales. Wolgemut and his stepson Pleydenwurff were first commissioned in 1487–1488, and craftsmen in their workshop cut the blocks from their designs. Albrecht Dürer was an apprentice there from 1486 to 1489. The printer, Anton Koberger, was Dürer's godfather.
