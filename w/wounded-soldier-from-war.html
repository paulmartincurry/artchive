Wounded Soldier (Autumn 1916, Bapaume), from War

Otto Dix · 1924 · Etching and aquatint, with open bite, on cream laid paper · plate 19.7 × 29 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Dix was posted to a machine-gun unit on the Western Front in the autumn of 1915 and fought in the Battle of the Somme. He was wounded in the neck in August 1918. Afterwards he described a recurring nightmare of crawling through destroyed houses. For Der Krieg he gave every plate a title, a place and a description, and arranged the fifty without regard to chronology.
