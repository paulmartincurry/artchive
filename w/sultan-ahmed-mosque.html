Sultan Ahmed Mosque (The Blue Mosque)

Sedefkâr Mehmed Ağa · 1609–1616

Free to use with credit. Photo: Pedro Szekely, CC BY-SA 2.0

Sultan Ahmed I had won no major victories, so he paid for the mosque from the treasury, and the legal scholars who objected forbade Muslims to pray in it. Building began in 1609 on the site of the Hippodrome's seating. The tiles took up the whole output of Iznik, and in 1613 the sultan banned making or selling tiles for any other purpose; there are 21,043 inside. The architect, Sedefkar Mehmed Agha, had trained under Sinan. The mosque opened in 1617, and Ahmed died that year.
