I walk down the patterned garden-paths
In my stiff, brocaded gown. … Christ! What are patterns for?

Patterns

Amy Lowell · 1915

Amy Lowell was forty-one in 1915, the year of this poem. She never attended college, because her family did not consider it proper for a woman, and she turned to poetry at twenty-eight after seeing the actress Eleonora Duse perform in Europe. Her first published work had appeared in the Atlantic Monthly only five years earlier.
