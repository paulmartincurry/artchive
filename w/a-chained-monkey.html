A Chained Monkey

Franz Brun · 1559/1596 · Engraving in black on ivory laid paper · 7.2 × 6.5 cm (2 7/8 × 2 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It is a reversed copy of the pet monkey in Dürer's Madonna with the Monkey. One impression was pasted into a Mughal album of the late 1500s, now in Berlin.
