I died as mineral and became a plant,
I died as plant and rose to animal,
I died as animal and I was man.
Why should I fear? When was I less by dying?

I died as mineral

Jalāl al-Dīn Muḥammad Rūmī · 13th century

Translated by R. A. Nicholson, 1914

Rūmī died on 17 December 1273, at the age of 66. His works include the Masnavi, a poem in six books that he began dictating around the year 1258, and the Divan-i Shams-i Tabrizi.
