A man can be destroyed but not defeated.

The Old Man and the Sea

Ernest Hemingway · 1952

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Hemingway wrote the novella in Cuba between December 1950 and February 1951, at up to a thousand words a day, and completed the 26,531-word manuscript in six weeks. The story had been told to him fifteen years earlier. Life magazine published it whole in its issue of September 1, 1952, and sold a record 5.3 million copies in two days.
