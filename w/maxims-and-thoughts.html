The worst wasted of all days is that during which one has not laughed.

Maxims and Thoughts

Sébastien-Roch Nicolas Chamfort · 1795 (posthumous)

Translated by William G. Hutchison, 1902

Chamfort was born in Clermont-Ferrand in 1741 and went to Paris at nine as a scholarship student. He lived for years by teaching, hack writing and the hospitality of people who liked his conversation, and later held posts as secretary to the Prince de Condé and to the sister of Louis XVI. During the Revolution he was secretary of the Jacobin club, then was imprisoned for his sarcasms about the new order. He died in Paris in April 1794, and his collected works appeared in four volumes in 1795.
