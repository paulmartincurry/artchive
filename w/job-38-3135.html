Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?

Job 38:31–35

Book of Job (King James Version) · c. 6th-4th century BCE; KJV 1611

The Hebrew word for the cluster of the Pleiades in this passage occurs elsewhere only in 1 Samuel 15:32, and possibly once more in Job. The Pleiades are also known as the Seven Stars. The Book of Job does not name its author; most scholars believe it was written around the 6th century BCE.
