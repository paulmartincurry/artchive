The Night Watch

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1642 · Oil on canvas · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam (on loan from the Amsterdam Museum)

Free to use

Rembrandt was paid 1,600 guilders for this militia portrait, and the hired drummer was added for free. It is thought he finished it in a lean-to in his garden because it was too large to fit into his Amsterdam studio. In 1715 the painting was moved to the Amsterdam Town Hall and trimmed on all four sides, and the missing pieces have not been found. A dark varnish, removed only in the 1940s, made it look like a night scene and gave it the name it goes by.
