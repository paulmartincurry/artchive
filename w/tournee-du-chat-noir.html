Tournée du Chat Noir

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1896 · Color lithograph on paper · Bibliothèque nationale de France

Free to use

Théophile Steinlen was born in Lausanne and first worked as a trainee designer at a textile mill in Mulhouse. He moved to Montmartre in his early twenties, and the painter Adolphe Willette brought him into the circle at Le Chat Noir, which led to poster commissions. He was thirty-six when he made this one in 1896. Cats run through his illustrations, he modelled them in sculpture, and he published a book of his cat drawings.
