A Hedgehog (Erinaceus roumanicus)

Hans Hoffmann · before 1584 · Watercolor and gouache · 20.7 × 30.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hans Hoffmann was a Nuremberg painter who became known for his copies after Albrecht Dürer and for nature studies in watercolour and gouache. The Met dates this sheet to before 1584, the year he left for Munich to work for the Duke of Bavaria. In 1585 Emperor Rudolf II made him a court painter in Prague. A Nuremberg collector, Paulus Praun, owned more than a hundred of his works.
