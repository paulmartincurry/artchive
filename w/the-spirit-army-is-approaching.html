The Spirit Army Is Approaching

Kiowa Ghost Dance song · 1890 (printed 1896)

Translated by James Mooney, 1896

James Mooney worked for the Bureau of American Ethnology for thirty-six years and studied the Kiowa closely. He explained that the Kiowa verb in this song pictures the spirits coming on like an army or a great herd of animals, and that its ending marks the news as report or common belief, not something the singer had seen.
