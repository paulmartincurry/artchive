Giuliano de' Medici

Sandro Botticelli · c. 1478/1480 · Tempera on panel · 75.5 × 52.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Giuliano de' Medici was killed in Florence Cathedral on 26 April 1478, at the opening of the Pazzi conspiracy; he was twenty-four. Several versions of his portrait must have been painted, and a number survive. The queen bee emblem on the back of this panel also appears on a medal made in 1588 for Grand Duke Ferdinand I de' Medici, the first owner in the gallery's provenance.
