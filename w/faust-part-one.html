Two souls, alas, are dwelling in my breast,
And one is striving to forsake its brother.

Faust, Part One (Before the City Gate)

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1808

Translated by Walter Kaufmann · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Part One was first published in 1808, when Goethe was in his late fifties. He had lived in Weimar since 1775, sitting on the duke's privy council and on the war and highway commissions, and had managed the Weimar theatre since 1791. The play is not divided into acts and runs as a sequence of scenes. Six years after it appeared, Franz Schubert set Gretchen's scene at the spinning wheel to music.
