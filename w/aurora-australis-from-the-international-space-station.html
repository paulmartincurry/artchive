Aurora Australis from the International Space Station (ISS023-E-058455)

NASA (Expedition 23 crew) · 29 May 2010 · Digital photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

A crew member on the International Space Station took the photograph during Expedition 23. The southern aurora is seen from above, as a ribbon of green light over the curve of the Earth.
