Gently I stir a white feather fan,
With open shirt, sitting in a green wood.
I take off my cap and hang it on a jutting stone:
A wind from the pine-trees trickles on my bare head.

In the Mountains on a Summer Day

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1919

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

Arthur Waley was appointed Assistant Keeper of Oriental Prints and Manuscripts at the British Museum in 1913, and taught himself to read Classical Chinese and Classical Japanese there, partly to help catalogue the museum's paintings. He never visited China or Japan. He gave this translation in a paper read to the China Society on November 21, 1918.
