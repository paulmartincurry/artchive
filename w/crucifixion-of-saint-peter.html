Crucifixion of Saint Peter

Caravaggio · 1601 · Oil on canvas · 230 × 175 cm · Cerasi Chapel, Santa Maria del Popolo, Rome

Free to use

The contract, signed on 24 September 1600, asked for two large cypress panels in eight months for 400 scudi. Those first versions displeased the patron, Monsignor Tiberio Cerasi, and were taken by Cardinal Sannesio; Caravaggio painted this canvas in their place. Cerasi died in May 1601 while the work was under way, the fee was cut to 300 scudi, and the picture was installed in the chapel only on 1 May 1605.
