Still Life with a Skull and a Writing Quill

Pieter Claesz · 1628 · Oil on wood · 24.1 × 35.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Pieter Claesz painted this small panel, 24.1 by 35.9 centimetres, in 1628. He had moved to Haarlem in 1620, where he and Willem Claeszoon Heda painted still lifes in subdued, virtually monochromatic palettes.
