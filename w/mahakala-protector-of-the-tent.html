Mahakala, Protector of the Tent

Maker unknown · c. 1500 · Distemper on cloth · 162.6 × 134.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This form of Mahakala, Lord of the Tent, is a protector of the Sakya school of Tibetan Buddhism. The painted image is 162.6 by 134.6 centimetres. Paintings like this were traditionally kept unframed and rolled up when not on display, mounted on a textile backing with a silk cover over the front. The Zimmerman family gave this one to the Met in 2012.
