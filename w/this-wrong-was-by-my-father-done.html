This wrong was by my father done
To me, but ne'er by me to one.

This Wrong Was by My Father Done (the epitaph)

Abu al-ʿAlāʾ al-Maʿarrī · before 1057

Translated by Reynold A. Nicholson, 1907

Al-Maʿarrī composed these lines as his own epitaph, and they are on his tomb. He never married, and he held that no children should be begotten, so as to spare them the pains of life. He died in his home town in May 1057, aged eighty-three.
