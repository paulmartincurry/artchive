Torso of Clotho

Camille Claudel · c. 1893 · Plaster · 44 × 25 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Camille Claudel showed her Clotho at the Salon of the Société Nationale des Beaux-Arts in 1893, when she was twenty-eight. Clotho is one of the three Fates, and Claudel made her a very elderly woman. This plaster torso measures 44 by 25 centimetres.
