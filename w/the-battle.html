The Battle (The Miseries and Misfortunes of War, plate 3)

Jacques Callot · 1633 · Etching; second state of three (Lieure) · sheet: 10.7 x 21.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Callot let acid bite the copper several times, covering the parts he wanted shallow with ground between baths, so distant figures print lighter than near ones. He also developed the échoppe, an etching needle with a slanting oval tip that could swell a line the way an engraver's tool did. This battle is the third plate of eighteen.
