Maples at Mama (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · c. 1857 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 35.9 × 23 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The maple leaves of this design were printed in orange. Over time the colour has oxidised and turned brownish-black. The view looks out over the Edogawa River, the Tekona Shrine and Tsugihashi Bridge.
