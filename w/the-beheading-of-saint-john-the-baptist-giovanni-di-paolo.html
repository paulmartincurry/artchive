The Beheading of Saint John the Baptist

Giovanni di Paolo · 1455–60 · Tempera on panel · 68.6 × 39.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Giovanni di Paolo told the life of John the Baptist in a series of twelve panels; eleven survive, and six of them are in Chicago. Set in three rows, the series probably served as the doors of a shrine holding a relic of the saint. Martin A. Ryerson bought the six panels in 1914 from the dealer Kleinberger, who had paid 160,000 francs for them at the Aynard sale in Paris.
