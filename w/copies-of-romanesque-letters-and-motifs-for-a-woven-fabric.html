Copies of Romanesque Letters and Motifs for a Woven Fabric

Maker unknown · 1825–1850 · Brush and lateritious, purple, blue, and light green gouache on oily tracing paper · 30.2 × 23.3 cm (11 7/8 × 9 3/16 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Three Romanesque letters, F, B and O, are copied on this sheet among quatrefoils, lozenges and a shield. It is 30.2 by 23.3 centimetres and was a gift of Eleanor and Sarah Hewitt.
