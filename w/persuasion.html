I can listen no longer in silence.

Persuasion (Ch. 23)

Jane Austen · 1817 (pub. 1818)

Persuasion was one of two novels published after Austen's death at Winchester in July 1817, when she was forty-one. The four novels printed in her lifetime had all appeared anonymously. The first account of her life was a notice by her brother Henry, printed with the other posthumous novel, Northanger Abbey. Of some 3,000 letters she may have written, about 160 survive, and her sister Cassandra is believed to have destroyed most of those she received.
