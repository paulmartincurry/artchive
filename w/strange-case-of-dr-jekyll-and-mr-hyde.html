Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde

Robert Louis Stevenson · 1886

Stevenson was confined to bed after a haemorrhage, in Bournemouth, when a dream gave him three scenes of the story. His stepson doubted that the first draft took as long as three days. By the family's account Stevenson burnt it after his wife's criticism and wrote the story again in three to six days, though there is no direct evidence of the burning. It was sold as a one-shilling paperback, and close to 40 thousand copies went in six months.
