Faust and Méphistophélés Horse Riding on the Sabbath (Faust)

Eugène Delacroix · 1828 · Lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The plate was made for Albert Stapfer's French translation of Goethe's Faust, issued in 1828 by the publisher Ch. Motte and distributed by the bookseller Sautelet. By 1825 Delacroix was producing lithographs illustrating Shakespeare, and the Faust subjects followed soon after. The Print Club of Cleveland gave this impression in 1933.
