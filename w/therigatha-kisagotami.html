For the whole world—ay, and the gods in heav’n—
This is the Law: All is impermanent!

Therīgāthā (Kisāgotamī)

Kisāgotamī (Elder Buddhist Nun) · ca. 5th–3rd Century BCE

Translated by C. A. F. Rhys Davids, 1909

In the Buddhist account Kisāgotamī was the wife of a wealthy man of Shravasti who lost her only child. She became a nun, and the Buddha named her foremost in discipline among the nuns. The Therīgāthā, the Verses of the Elder Nuns in the Pali Canon, gives one version of her story. Others have come down by word of mouth and in local plays across much of Asia.
