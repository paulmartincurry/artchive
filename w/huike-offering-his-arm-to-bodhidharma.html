Huike Offering His Arm to Bodhidharma

Sesshū Tōyō · 1496 · Ink on paper · Sainen-ji, Tokoname, Aichi

Free to use

Sesshū painted this in 1496, ten years before his death. He had entered the Shōkoku-ji temple in Kyoto as an acolyte at twelve or thirteen and eventually became a Zen monk. In 1467 he travelled to China as a member of a Japanese envoy, funded by a wealthy warrior clan that traded with China, and spent three years there.
