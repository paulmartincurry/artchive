Atropos (The Fates)

Francisco de Goya · 1819–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 123 × 266 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

In Goya's house this mural probably hung on the upper floor, beside Fight with Cudgels and across from Asmodea. It was moved to canvas in 1873–74 under Salvador Martínez Cubells, a curator at the Prado. Its colours are cut down almost entirely to ochres and blacks.
