The Calling of Saint Matthew

Caravaggio · 1600 · Oil on canvas · San Luigi dei Francesi

Free to use

This was Caravaggio's first major church commission, and his first painting with more than a handful of figures. More than a decade earlier, Cardinal Matthieu Cointerel had left money and instructions in his will for a chapel decorated with the story of his namesake, Saint Matthew. The commission for the two side paintings is dated July 1599, and the final payment was made in July 1600. The canvas is more than three metres wide and still hangs in that chapel, in San Luigi dei Francesi in Rome.
