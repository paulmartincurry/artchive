Konark Sun Temple

Maker unknown · ca. 1250 · Khondalite, chlorite and laterite

Free to use with credit. Photo: Subham9423, CC BY-SA 4.0

The temple is built as a stone chariot with 24 wheels, each 9 feet 9 inches across. Its planning and construction records survive on palm leaves, found in a village in the 1960s; they name King Narasimhadeva I as sponsor and Shiva Samantaraya Mahapatra as overseer. The main tower, thought to have stood some 225 feet, came down in the late 16th century or after, and the last standing piece of it fell in 1848. In 1903 the surviving hall was sealed and filled with sand to keep it from collapsing.
