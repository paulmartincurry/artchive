Wheatfield with a Reaper

Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · Oil on canvas · Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Van Gogh painted it at the asylum of Saint-Paul-de-Mausole in Saint-Rémy, where he had gone in May 1889. He wrote that he saw in the reaper an image of death, with nothing sad in it, under a sun flooding everything with light of fine gold.
