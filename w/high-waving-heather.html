Earth rising to heaven and heaven descending;
Man’s spirit away from the drear dungeon sending, —
Bursting the fetters and breaking the bars.

High Waving Heather

Emily Brontë · 1836

Emily Brontë turned eighteen in 1836. At seventeen she had gone to Roe Head Girls' School, where her sister Charlotte was a teacher, and left after only a few months. Since the age of thirteen she and her younger sister Anne had been writing about Gondal, a fictional island. Apart from the poems, those writings were largely not preserved.
