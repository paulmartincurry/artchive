Gravediggers (Figurines for Victory over the Sun, plate nine)

El Lissitzky · 1920–21, published 1923 · Color lithograph on wove paper · 53.2 × 45 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is plate nine of Lissitzky's portfolio of figures for Victory over the Sun, drawn in 1920–21 and printed as colour lithographs in 1923. The opera's plot follows a group who set out to destroy reason by doing away with time and capturing the sun, and its cast included Nero and Caligula in the Same Person and a Telephone Talker. The audience at the first performance in 1913 reacted negatively and even violently.
