Lamentation (The Mourning of Christ)

Giotto di Bondone · ca. 1305 · Fresco · Scrovegni Chapel, Padua

Free to use with credit. Photo: Gennadii Saus i Segura, CC BY-SA 4.0

The Scrovegni family, who were wealthy, built the chapel for their private use beside a monastery in Padua, and it was consecrated in 1305. Giotto painted its interior walls between 1304 and 1306 with scenes from the life of Jesus, and this is one of them.
