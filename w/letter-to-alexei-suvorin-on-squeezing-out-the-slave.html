Letter to Alexei Suvorin on Squeezing Out the Slave (7 January 1889)

Anton Chekhov · 1889

Translated by Constance Garnett, 1920

Chekhov's grandfather was a former serf, and his father ran a grocery store in Taganrog. When the father was declared bankrupt in 1876 and fled to Moscow, Chekhov, then sixteen, stayed behind for three more years and paid for his own schooling by private tutoring, catching and selling goldfinches, and selling short sketches to the newspapers. He wrote this letter three weeks before his twenty-ninth birthday.
