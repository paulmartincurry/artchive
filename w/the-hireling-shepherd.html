The Hireling Shepherd

William Holman Hunt · 1851 · Oil on canvas · 76.4 × 109.5 cm · Manchester Art Gallery

Free to use

Hunt painted this in Surrey while working closely with Millais, who was painting Ophelia beside the Hogsmill River at the same time. The young woman was Emma Watkins, a local country girl, who later travelled to London to model for him so he could finish it. The shepherd is showing her a death's-head hawkmoth. When the painting went on show at the Royal Academy, critics attacked the red faces and wiry hair of the two figures.
