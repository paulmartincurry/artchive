A Bear Walking

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1482–85 · silverpoint on light buff prepared paper · 4 1/16 × 5 1/4 in. (10.3 × 13.3 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This drawing is about ten centimetres tall and thirteen wide. Leonardo da Vinci was in his early thirties when he made it. It came to the Metropolitan Museum in 1975 with the Robert Lehman Collection.
