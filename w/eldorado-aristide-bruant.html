Eldorado: Aristide Bruant

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892 · Color lithograph on tan wove paper · Image: 137.5 × 96.4 cm; Sheet, sight: 145.2 × 98.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Aristide Bruant commissioned this poster in 1892 to advertise his shows at the Ambassadeurs and Eldorado clubs. Toulouse-Lautrec cut the singer down to a wide-brimmed hat, a black cloak and a red scarf, and he went on reusing the image for Bruant's own cabaret, Le Mirliton. Bruant had left home at fifteen to find work and made his name in the bistros of Montmartre.
