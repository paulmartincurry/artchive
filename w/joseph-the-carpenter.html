Joseph the Carpenter

Georges de La Tour · c. 1642 · Oil on canvas · 130 × 100 cm · Musée du Louvre, Paris

Free to use with credit. Photo: Philippe Lelong, CC BY-SA 4.0

La Tour painted this about 1642. Joseph is drilling a piece of wood with an auger, and the candlelight shines through the flesh of the child's raised hand.
