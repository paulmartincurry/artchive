Radha and Krishna in the Rain

Maker unknown · c. 1780 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · 25.7 × 17.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

By 1975 the painting belonged to George and Clara Louise Bickford of Cleveland Heights, and that year it hung in the museum's exhibition of Indian art from their collection. It later passed to Dorothea Swope of New York, who gave it to Cleveland in 1988 in memory of Mahadevan Natesan.
