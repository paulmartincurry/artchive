In the bleak mid-winter
Frosty wind made moan,
Earth stood hard as iron,
Water like a stone;

In the Bleak Midwinter

Christina Rossetti · 1872

The poem first appeared as A Christmas Carol in the January 1872 issue of Scribner's Monthly, when Christina Rossetti was forty-one, and was not collected in a book until 1875. The music came after her death in 1894. Gustav Holst wrote his tune, Cranham, for the English Hymnal of 1906. Harold Darke composed his setting in 1909 while a student at the Royal College of Music, and left out her fourth verse.
