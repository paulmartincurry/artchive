Mina'i Beaker with Seated Princes

Maker unknown · 1180–1220 · Fritware with overglaze design, mina’i ware · 13.3 × 12.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Mina'i ware, made at Kashan, was the first pottery painted with overglaze enamels: the colours went on after the main firing and were fixed by a second, cooler one. No illustrated manuscripts or murals survive from Iran before the Mongol conquest, so painted pots like this beaker, 13.3 centimetres high, are the best evidence of that style of painting. Bands of Kufic script run round the rim inside and out.
