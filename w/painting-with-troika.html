Painting with Troika

Wassily Kandinsky · January 18, 1911 · Oil on cardboard; in artist's painted frame · 69.7 × 97.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The painted frame is Kandinsky's own, made especially for this picture, which is dated 18 January 1911 and painted on cardboard. A troika is a traditional carriage drawn by three horses. The Chicago lawyer Arthur Jerome Eddy owned it by 1920, and his widow and son gave it to the Art Institute in 1931.
