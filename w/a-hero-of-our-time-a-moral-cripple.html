I became a moral cripple. One half of my soul ceased to exist; it dried up, evaporated, died, and I cut it off and cast it from me.

A Hero of Our Time — A Moral Cripple (Princess Mary)

Mikhail Lermontov · 1840

Translated by J. H. Wisdom and Marr Murray

Lermontov wrote A Hero of Our Time in 1839, published it in 1840 and revised it in 1841. He took the name of its hero, Pechorin, from the Pechora River in the far north, as a homage to Pushkin's Eugene Onegin, who is named after the Onega River.
