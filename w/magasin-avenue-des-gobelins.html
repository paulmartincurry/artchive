Magasin, Avenue des Gobelins

Eugène Atget · 1925 · Gelatin silver print · 22.5 × 17.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Man Ray argued that Atget's pictures with window reflections, where the street mixes with the shop and the mannequins look ready to step out, had a Dada or Surrealist quality; Atget did not see himself as a Surrealist. He made this one in 1925, the year the American photographer Berenice Abbott visited him and bought some of his prints. He was 67 or 68. His companion Valentine died the next year, and he died in Paris on 4 August 1927.
