When I am dead, bury me
In my beloved Ukraine,

My Testament (Zapovit)

Taras Shevchenko · 25 December 1845

Translated by John Weir · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Taras Shevchenko was thirty-one on the day this poem is dated. He was born a serf in the village of Morintsy, was orphaned at eleven, and worked as a swineherd, a shepherd and a kitchen servant to his landlord. The document that freed him is dated 22 April 1838, and he went on to become a fellow of the Imperial Academy of Arts in Saint Petersburg. He wrote his poetry in Ukrainian and his prose in Russian.
