When she rises in the morning
I linger to watch her;
She spreads the bath-cloth underneath the window
And the sunbeams catch her

Gloire de Dijon

D. H. Lawrence · 1917

In March 1912 Lawrence met Frieda Weekley, who was six years older than he was, the mother of three young children and married to his former modern languages professor at Nottingham. They eloped to Germany, where he was arrested as a suspected British spy and released after her father intervened. The love poems that came out of the months that followed were published in 1917 as Look! We Have Come Through. The two had married on 13 July 1914.
