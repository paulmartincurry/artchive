The Garden of Earthly Delights: Hell (right panel)

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1490–1510 · Oil on oak panel · 220 × 97.5 cm (right panel) · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

This is the right wing of the triptych, 220 by 97.5 centimetres. The work was first recorded in 1517, a year after Bosch's death, in the Nassau palace in Brussels. The Duke of Alba seized it in 1568 and took it to Spain; Philip II bought it at auction in 1591 and gave it to El Escorial, where it stayed for 342 years before moving to the Prado in 1939.
