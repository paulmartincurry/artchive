Wrack

William Henry Fox Talbot · 1839 · Salted paper print · 22 × 17.5 cm (8 11/16 × 6 7/8 in.), irregularly trimmed · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The paper for a print like this was ordinary writing paper, bathed in a weak solution of table salt, dried, and brushed on one side with silver nitrate. Talbot had begun his experiments early in 1834. He showed his paper photographs at the Royal Institution on 25 January 1839, a few weeks after Daguerre's invention was announced without details. The Metropolitan Museum bought this sheet in 1936.
