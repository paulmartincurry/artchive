The Abduction of the Sabine Women

Nicolas Poussin · probably 1633–34 · Oil on canvas · 154.6 × 209.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Poussin painted the subject twice: this first version, probably in 1633 or 1634, and a second in 1637 or 1638 that is now in the Louvre. The canvas belonged to the Maréchal Charles de Créquy, French ambassador to Rome in the 1630s, and then to Cardinal Richelieu. The Cook family showed it at Doughty House in Richmond until it was sold in 1946.
