The Age of Innocence (Ch. VI)

Edith Wharton · 1920

Wharton was fifty-eight when this novel, her eighth, was published. It ran first in four parts in the magazine Pictorial Review in 1920 and came out as a book that October. It won the 1921 Pulitzer Prize for Fiction, the first awarded to a woman, after the judges rejected the committee's first choice, Sinclair Lewis's Main Street, on political grounds. She had spent the whole of the First World War in Europe.
