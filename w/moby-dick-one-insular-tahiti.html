For as this appalling ocean surrounds the verdant land, so in the soul of man there lies one insular Tahiti, full of peace and joy, but encompassed by all the horrors of the half known life.

Moby-Dick — one insular Tahiti (Ch. 58)

Herman Melville · 1851

The novel was published to mixed reviews and was a commercial failure. It was out of print when Melville died in 1891. For the London edition the publisher, Richard Bentley, censored or changed sensitive passages.
