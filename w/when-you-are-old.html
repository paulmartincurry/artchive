When You Are Old

W. B. Yeats · 1892

In 1892 Yeats was twenty-six or twenty-seven and living with his family at 3 Blenheim Road in Bedford Park, in England, a house they had taken in March 1888 at a rent of £50 a year. His earliest volume of verse had been published three years before, in 1889.
