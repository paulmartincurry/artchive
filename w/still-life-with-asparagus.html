Still Life with Asparagus

Adriaen Coorte · 1697 · Oil on paper on panel · 25 × 20.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Adriaen Coorte painted the asparagus in oil on paper, which is mounted on a panel 25 by 20.5 centimetres. He signed and dated it 1697 on the plinth. Two years earlier he had been fined in Middelburg for selling a painting before he was a member of the painters' guild.
