The chiefest sanctity of a temple is that it is a place to which men go to weep in common. A miserere sung in common by a multitude tormented by destiny has as much value as a philosophy.

Tragic Sense of Life — The Man of Flesh and Bone (Ch. I)

Miguel de Unamuno · 1913; English 1921

Translated by J. E. Crawford Flitch

The book appeared in 1913, while Unamuno was rector of the University of Salamanca, and in English in 1921. The Catholic Church in Spain condemned it as dangerous, and in 1957 it was placed on the Index of prohibited books.
