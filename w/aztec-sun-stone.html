Aztec Sun Stone

Maker unknown · ca. 1502–1520 · Basalt · National Museum of Anthropology, Mexico City

Free to use with credit. Photo: El Comandante, CC BY-SA 3.0

The stone is 3.6 metres across and 98 centimetres thick, and it weighs 24,590 kilograms. The rock came from the Xitle volcano and was probably dragged up to 22 kilometres to the centre of Tenochtitlan. After the conquest an archbishop of Mexico ordered it buried. Workers repaving the main square found it on 17 December 1790, about 40 centimetres down, and it then hung on the outside of the cathedral until 1885. A study published in 1939 gave evidence that it was once painted bright blue, red, green and yellow.
