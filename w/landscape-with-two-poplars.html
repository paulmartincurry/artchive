Landscape with Two Poplars

Wassily Kandinsky · 1912 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Kandinsky sold this painting in 1913 to Arthur Jerome Eddy, a collector in Chicago, and Eddy's widow and son gave it to the Art Institute in 1931. He had come to painting late. In 1896, at thirty, he gave up a career teaching law and economics in Moscow and moved to Munich to study art.
