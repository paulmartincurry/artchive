Still Life with a Green Flower Vase

Paula Modersohn-Becker · c. 1902 · Oil on brown wood pulp board, varnished · 37.7 × 29.1 cm (14 7/8 × 11 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Only about ten still lifes by Modersohn-Becker can be traced from before 1905; this small board, 37.7 by 29.1 centimetres, is one of them. The back is inscribed in pencil with her name and the year 1900, though the museum dates it to about 1902. She died at thirty-one in 1907, and from 1908 the picture belonged to Wanda Frischen-Carus of Bremen. The Art Institute bought it from a New York gallery in 1962.
