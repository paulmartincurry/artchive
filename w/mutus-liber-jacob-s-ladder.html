Mutus Liber: Jacob's Ladder (frontispiece)

Isaac Baulot (Altus) · published 1702 (first published 1677) · Engraving · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The Mutus Liber, the Silent Book, is told almost entirely in pictures. It was first printed at La Rochelle in 1677 by Pierre Savouret in no more than a few dozen copies, and twelve of the originals survive in major libraries. This plate comes from the Geneva reprint of 1702, for which new plates were engraved; there it served as a frontispiece in Jean-Jacques Manget's Bibliotheca chemica curiosa.
