"Thee now no more
The joyful house and best of wives shall welcome,
Nor little sons run up to snatch their kisses
And touch with silent happiness thy heart.

On the Nature of Things — Thee Now No More the Joyful House Shall Welcome (Book III)

Lucretius · c. 55 BCE

Translated by William Ellery Leonard

Very little is known about the life of Lucretius. The one certainty is that he was a friend or client of Gaius Memmius, to whom the poem is addressed and dedicated. Jerome, writing four centuries after his death, pictured him as a lovesick, mad poet, a report now accepted as inaccurate.
