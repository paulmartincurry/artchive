Zodiac Surimono

Indai · 1866 · Color woodblock print; surimono · 24.7 × 18.5 cm (9 3/4 × 7 5/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The twelve zodiac animals are printed in brass-dust pigment, so they show as gold silhouettes on the paper, in order from the tiger at the far right, the animal of 1866. Surimono like this were often exchanged at New Year and commissioned by poetry clubs. Indai ran a painting workshop. The host of this poetry group, Soshu, was also an artist. The print is 24.7 centimetres tall and came to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1972 from Charles H. Mitchell of Tokyo.
