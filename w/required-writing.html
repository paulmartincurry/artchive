Required Writing

Philip Larkin · 1983

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Philip Larkin turned sixty-one in 1983. He had been University Librarian at Hull since 1955 and kept the post until his death two years later; his office there was also the study where he wrote. From 1961 to 1971 he was the jazz critic of The Daily Telegraph. In 1984 he was offered the position of Poet Laureate and declined it.
