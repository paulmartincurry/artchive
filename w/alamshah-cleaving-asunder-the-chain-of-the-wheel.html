Alamshah Cleaving Asunder the Chain of the Wheel (Hamzanama)

Maker unknown · c. 1560s–70s · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on cotton and paper · Page 83.7 × 67 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This page comes from volume 11 of the manuscript, the first major undertaking of the painting workshop Akbar set up. The text for each scene was written on the back of the page, presumably to be read to the emperor as he looked at the picture. A little more than 10 percent of the pages survive, and George and Clara Bickford of Cleveland Heights gave this one to the museum in 1976.
