If I were fierce, and bald, and short of breath,
I'd live with scarlet Majors at the Base,
And speed glum heroes up the line to death.

Base Details

Siegfried Sassoon · 1917

Sassoon wrote the poem into his diary on 4 March 1917, when he was thirty and an officer in the British Army in France. Before the war he had left Cambridge without a degree and lived on a small private income, hunting, playing cricket and writing verse. There was no German ancestry in his family: his mother named him Siegfried because she loved Wagner's operas.
