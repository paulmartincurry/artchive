Suns may set, and suns may rise again:
but when our brief light has set,
night is one long everlasting sleep.

Carmen 5 (Let Us Live, My Lesbia)

Catullus · c. 60 BCE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The Lesbia of the poems is usually identified with Clodia Metelli, a woman of the aristocratic Claudii Pulchri whose husband was consul in 60 BC. Catullus came from a leading equestrian family of Verona and appears to have spent most of his young adult years in Rome. Poem 5 belongs to a group that follows the course of the affair. Ben Jonson's translation of it was set to music in 1606.
