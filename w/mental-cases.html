--These are men whose minds the Dead have ravished.
Memory fingers in their hair of murders,
Multitudinous murders they once witnessed.

Mental Cases

Wilfred Owen · 1920

Owen based the poem on Craiglockhart, the military hospital near Edinburgh where he was a patient with neurasthenia in the summer of 1917. While there he did some teaching at Tynecastle High School, in a poor area of the city. His mother received the telegram reporting his death on Armistice Day, as the church bells in Shrewsbury were ringing.
