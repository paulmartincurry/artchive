Federal Theatre Negro Unit: Macbeth

Anthony Velonis · 1936–38 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Anthony Velonis, born in 1911, made this colour silkscreen for the Federal Theatre's Negro Unit production of Macbeth, between 1936 and 1938. The sheet is 55.8 by 35.5 centimetres.
