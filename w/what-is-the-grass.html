A child said What is the grass? fetching it to me with full hands;
How could I answer the child? I do not know what it is any more than he.

What Is the Grass? (Song of Myself, Section 6)

Walt Whitman · 1855

The poem later called Song of Myself had no title in 1855. It was the first of twelve poems in the book and took up 1336 of their 2315 lines. Ralph Waldo Emerson answered the book with a five-page letter, and Whitman had a phrase from that letter stamped in gold leaf on the spine of the second edition.
