Arghan Div Brings the Chest of Armor to Hamza (Hamzanama)

Maker unknown · 1562–77 · Opaque watercolor and gold on cotton · 79.1 × 63.3 cm · Brooklyn Museum

Free to use

Akbar's Hamzanama was divided into 14 volumes of 100 paintings each, and about 140 of the paintings survive; this one is in the Brooklyn Museum. One account holds that when Nadir Shah carried off the treasures of Delhi in 1739, the Peacock Throne among them, the Mughal emperor Muhammad Shah pleaded for the return of this manuscript alone.
