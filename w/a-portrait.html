A Portrait

George H. Seeley · 1910 · Photogravure · 12 × 9.3 cm (image) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

George Seeley trained as a painter at the Massachusetts Normal Art School and made his photographs in his home town of Stockbridge, Massachusetts. Stieglitz saw his work at the First American Photographic Salon in 1904 and asked him to join the Photo-Secession, where he stayed from 1906 to 1910. This gravure dates from his last year in the group.
