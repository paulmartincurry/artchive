When I Am Parted from My Beloved

Kabir · 15th century; English 1915

Translated by Rabindranath Tagore

The years of Kabir's life are uncertain: some historians favour 1398 to 1448, others 1440 to 1518. A few accounts say he was found as an infant at Lahartara Lake by a Muslim weaver named Niru and his wife Nima, who raised him in Varanasi. He composed in Sadhukkadi, a speech that borrows from several dialects. He was threatened in his lifetime by both Hindus and Muslims, and when he died both claimed him as theirs.
