Picture of the Lake at Hakone (Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1833 · Color woodblock print · Sheet: 22.6 × 35.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Scholars still debate whether Hiroshige saw every station he drew: some of the prints borrow directly from an illustrated guide to the Tōkaidō published in 1797. The first print of the series was issued jointly by the houses of Hōeidō and Senkakudō, and Hōeidō published the rest alone. This impression came to Cleveland in 1940 by bequest of James Parmelee.
