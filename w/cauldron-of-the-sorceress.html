Cauldron of the Sorceress

Odilon Redon · 1879 · Various charcoals, with stumping, erasing, and incising, on pale-pink wove paper with red and blue fibers altered to a golden tone · 40.8 × 37.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Redon drew this in several kinds of charcoal, stumping, erasing and incising the surface, and signed it in black chalk. It stayed in his hands for most of twenty years. In February 1896 it went to the Galerie Laffitte in Paris, on deposit or sold, and came back to him. In December 1899 he sold it to the dealer Ambroise Vollard. The Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1950.
