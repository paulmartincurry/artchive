Ebony and Ivory

F. Holland Day · 1897, published 1899 · Photogravure · 16.8 × 14.4 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

F. Holland Day was a publisher as well as a photographer. He co-founded and paid for the Boston firm of Copeland and Day, which issued about a hundred titles from 1893 through 1899, among them the American edition of Oscar Wilde's Salomé. This photogravure is number 146 of an edition of 150, plate 8 of the portfolio American Pictorial Photography, Series I, of 1899.
