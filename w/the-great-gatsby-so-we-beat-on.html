The Great Gatsby — So we beat on (closing)

F. Scott Fitzgerald · 1925

F. Scott Fitzgerald completed a rough draft in 1924, after a move to the French Riviera, and revised it in Rome through the following winter at the urging of his editor, Maxwell Perkins. Perkins also convinced him to give up his original title, Trimalchio in West Egg. The novel was published in April 1925 and had sold fewer than 20,000 copies by October. Fitzgerald died in 1940 believing his work forgotten. During the Second World War free copies were distributed to American soldiers serving overseas.
