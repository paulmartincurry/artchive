Now the soldiers he smiled at are most of 'em dead,
And we're cursing his staff for incompetent swine.
…
But he did for them both by his plan of attack.

The General

Siegfried Sassoon · 1917 (pub. 1918)

Sassoon wrote this poem in April 1917 in a hospital bed in London, where he was recovering from a shoulder wound received while leading a bombing assault. The poem is set during the Battle of Arras. A copy he wrote out by hand on 7 February 1919 went on display at the Imperial War Museum in London in 2017, in an anti-war protest exhibition.
