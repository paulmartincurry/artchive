A Female Ghost with Long Hair

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1883 · Sumi ink and pigment on silk, mounted as a hanging scroll · 153.5 × 30.6 cm · Wereldmuseum (Nationaal Museum van Wereldculturen), Netherlands

Free to use

This ghost was painted on silk in 1883, when Kyōsai was fifty-one or fifty-two. The scroll is 153.5 centimetres long and only 30.6 wide. He died six years later; Erwin Bälz wrote in his diary that the cause was gastric cancer.
