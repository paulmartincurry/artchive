When Napoleon III came to power in 1851, Charles Hugo joined his father in voluntary exile on the island of Jersey. With Vacquerie he photographed family and friends, aiming to publish a volume titled Jersey et les îles de la Manche. Instead, the photographs were compiled into private albums and shared with friends.
