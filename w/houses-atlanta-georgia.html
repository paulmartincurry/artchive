Houses. Atlanta, Georgia

Walker Evans · March 1936 · Film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The two posters in front of the houses are for Carole Lombard in Love Before Breakfast and Anne Shirley in Chatterbox. Evans took the picture in Atlanta in March 1936, the month Love Before Breakfast was released.
