The poorest man may in his cottage bid defiance to all the forces of the Crown. … the wind may blow through it — the storm may enter — the rain may enter — but the King of England cannot enter!

Speech on the Excise Bill

William Pitt the Elder (1st Earl of Chatham) · March 1763

Pitt was fifty-four and sat in the House of Commons as member for Bath. He had been out of office since October 1761, after leading the cabinet from 1756 during the Seven Years' War. He had suffered from gout since his schooldays at Eton, and the attacks grew worse throughout his life. He refused a title until 1766.
