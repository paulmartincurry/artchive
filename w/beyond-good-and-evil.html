And when you look long into an abyss, the abyss also looks into you.

Beyond Good and Evil (Aphorism 146)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1886

Translated by Walter Kaufmann, 1966 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The first edition was printed at Nietzsche's own expense, by the house of C. G. Naumann in Leipzig. The book has a short preface dated 1885, then 296 numbered sections arranged in nine parts, and ends with an aftersong called From High Mountains. It came directly after Thus Spoke Zarathustra and covers that book's ideas in a more polemical way.
