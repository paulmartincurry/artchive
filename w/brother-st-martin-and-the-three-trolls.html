Brother St. Martin and the Three Trolls

John Bauer · 1913 · Watercolour · 25 × 25 cm · Nationalmuseum, Stockholm

Free to use

Bauer signed and dated this watercolour in 1913; it is 25 centimetres square and illustrates Emil Eliason's tale Brother Martin in that year's Among Gnomes and Trolls. Five years later, at thirty-six, Bauer drowned with his wife Ester and their son Bengt when their boat sank on Lake Vättern.
