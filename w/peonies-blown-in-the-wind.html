Peonies Blown in the Wind

John La Farge · ca. 1880 · Leaded opalescent glass · 75 × 45 in. (190.5 × 114.3 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The window is about 190 centimetres tall and 114 wide. John La Farge had begun making stained glass in 1878 with a large commission for Trinity Church in Boston, and in 1880, when he was forty-five, he was granted a patent for superimposing panes of glass. That patent became central to his dispute with his rival Louis Comfort Tiffany. Susan Dwight Bliss gave the window to the museum in 1930.
