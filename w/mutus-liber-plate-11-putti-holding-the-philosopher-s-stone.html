Mutus Liber, Plate 11: Putti Holding the Philosopher's Stone

Isaac Baulot (Altus) · published 1702 (first published 1677) · Engraving · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

The book names its author only as Altus. Jean Flouret established that he was Isaac Baulot, and Abraham Thévenin probably worked on the plates of the first edition. This is plate 11 of the 1702 Geneva edition.
