Animal Locomotion, Plate 758

Eadweard Muybridge · 1887 · Collotype, from "Animal Locomotion" · Image: 20.5 × 36.8 cm (8 1/8 × 14 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Twenty-four cameras took this sequence of a cockatoo in flight. They were linked by a clockwork mechanism that fired the shutters one after another at regular intervals. Muybridge first worked out the method to study horses, and showed that all four hooves leave the ground at once in a gallop. The plate was published in Animal Locomotion, a portfolio of 781 separate series.
