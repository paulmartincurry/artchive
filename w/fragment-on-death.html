O woman's body found so tender,
Smooth, sweet, so precious in men's eyes,
Must thou too bear such count to render?
Yes; or pass quick into the skies.

Fragment on Death (from Le Testament)

François Villon · 1461

Translated by Algernon Charles Swinburne

The collection these stanzas come from runs to 2,023 lines, composed in 1461 under the immediate prospect of death by hanging. Villon had taken a master's degree at the University of Paris in 1452. In 1455 a priest died of his wounds after a scuffle with him, and around Christmas 1456 he was involved in the robbery of five hundred gold crowns from the chapel of the Collège de Navarre. For four years after that he was a wanderer.
