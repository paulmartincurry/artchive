The Artchive
O the mind, mind has mountains; cliffs of fall
Frightful, sheer, no-man-fathomed.

No worst, there is none

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1885

Almost none of Hopkins's poems were printed while he lived. A Jesuit publication accepted his long poem on the wreck of the Deutschland and then did not print it. He had left Oxford with first-class honours but failed his final theology exam, which made advancement in the order unlikely. He was about forty-one in 1885 and died in Dublin four years later. Robert Bridges, a friend from Balliol, edited the first edition of the poems in 1918.
