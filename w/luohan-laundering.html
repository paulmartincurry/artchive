Luohan Laundering

Lin Tinggui · 1178 · Ink and color on silk · 112.3 × 53.5 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

A Buddhist abbot commissioned a set of one hundred scrolls of the five hundred luohan in 1175, and Lin Tinggui and another painter finished them three years later. The set went to Japan in the thirteenth century and came to the Daitokuji temple in Kyoto. Needing money for repairs, the temple showed forty-four of the scrolls in Boston in 1894, and this one was given to the tour's organiser, who sold it to Charles Lang Freer in 1902. A signed inscription in gold is almost invisible in the lower right corner.
