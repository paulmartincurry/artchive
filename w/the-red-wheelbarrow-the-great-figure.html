The Red Wheelbarrow & The Great Figure

William Carlos Williams · 1921 & 1923

Williams was a family doctor, living in Rutherford, New Jersey, who practised medicine by day and wrote at night. Spanish was the language of his parents' house and his first language. The Red Wheelbarrow appeared in Spring and All in 1923, a book he dedicated to the painter Charles Demuth. Five years later Demuth painted I Saw the Figure 5 in Gold in homage to The Great Figure.
