I do not wish them to have power over men;
but over themselves.

A Vindication of the Rights of Woman (Ch. IV)

Mary Wollstonecraft · 1792

What set the book off was a report on public education that Talleyrand presented to the French National Assembly in 1791, which recommended that women be educated at home. Wollstonecraft dedicated her reply to him and hurried to finish it; it was published in January 1792, when she was thirty-two, and went into a second edition the same year. She meant to write a second volume and died before completing it.
