And for that minute a blackbird sang
Close by, and round him, mistier,
Farther and farther, all the birds
Of Oxfordshire and Gloucestershire.

Adlestrop

Edward Thomas · 1917

The poem describes a journey Thomas took on 24 June 1914 on the Oxford to Worcester express. The train made a scheduled stop at Adlestrop station, which he thought was unscheduled, and he did not get off. The poem was published in 1917, the year of his death. British Railways closed the station in 1966, and one of its signs and a platform bench now stand in the village bus shelter.
