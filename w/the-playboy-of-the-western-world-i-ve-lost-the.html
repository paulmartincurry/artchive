The Playboy of the Western World — I've lost the only Playboy of the Western World (Act III, close)

J. M. Synge · 1907

The play was first performed at the Abbey Theatre in Dublin on 26 January 1907, where it was met with riots and protests. According to Synge, its hero was at least partially based on a convicted criminal who assaulted a woman on Achill Island.
