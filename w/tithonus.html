The woods decay, the woods decay and fall,
The vapors weep their burthen to the ground,
Man comes and tills the field and lies beneath,
And after many a summer dies the swan.

Tithonus (opening)

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1860

The first version, named Tithon, was written in 1833 shortly after the death of Tennyson's friend Arthur Henry Hallam and was not published. When Thackeray asked him for a submission to the Cornhill Magazine, Tennyson made substantial revisions, and the poem appeared in its February edition in 1860.
