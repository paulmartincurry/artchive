An aged man is but a paltry thing,
A tattered coat upon a stick, unless
Soul clap its hands and sing, and louder sing

Sailing to Byzantium

W. B. Yeats · 1928

Yeats was sixty or sixty-one when he wrote this poem in 1926. It is four stanzas of ottava rima, eight lines each. He dedicated it to the artist Norah McGuinness and printed it first in October Blast in 1927, a year before The Tower.
