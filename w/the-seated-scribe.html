The Seated Scribe

Maker unknown · ca. 2600–2350 BCE · Limestone · Louvre

Auguste Mariette found the statue at Saqqara on 19 November 1850. The exact spot is unknown, because his excavation journal was lost. The eyes are white magnesite with red veins, inlaid with polished rock crystal and held in place by two copper clips each; the nipples are wood. Nobody knows who the sitter was. The half-round base suggests the figure once fitted into a larger block that would have carried his name and title.
