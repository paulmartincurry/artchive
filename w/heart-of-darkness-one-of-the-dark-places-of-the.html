Heart of Darkness — One of the dark places of the earth (Part I)

Joseph Conrad · 1899

Conrad joined the British merchant marine in 1878 and served for fifteen years, working his way up from steward and apprentice to captain. His only command was the barque Otago, which he took from Sydney to Mauritius in 1888–89. He had spent just over eight years of his working life at sea when he signed off his last ship in January 1894.
