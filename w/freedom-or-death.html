Freedom or Death

Emmeline Pankhurst · 1913

The speech (1913) is public domain in the United States; the line is quoted as printed in a 2016 reprint.

Pankhurst founded the Women's Social and Political Union in 1903, and its members smashed windows and fought with police. She was arrested many times and starved herself in prison in protest, and the Asquith government answered with the Cat and Mouse Act. In 1913 Emily Davison threw herself under the King's horse at the Epsom Derby, and 55,000 people attended her funeral.
