Bird in Space

Constantin Brâncuși · 1923 onward · Polished bronze

Free to use with credit. Photo: Art Poskanzer, CC BY 2.0

Brâncuși made the first Bird in Space in marble in 1923, and the series runs to seven marbles and nine polished bronzes. In October 1926 one of the bronzes arrived in New York by steamship with 19 of his other sculptures. Customs officials refused to accept it as art and charged the 40 per cent tariff for manufactured metal objects, about $230. The appeal went to court, and in November 1928 the judges ruled for the sculptor. He stayed in his Paris studio and did not attend the trial.
