Tarocchi (E-Series)

Master of the E-Series Tarocchi · before 1467 · engraving hand-colored with gold · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The set has 50 engravings in five groups of ten, climbing from the beggar through the Muses, the liberal arts and the virtues to the planets and the First Cause. Despite the name, they were never playing or fortune-telling cards. Until the nineteenth century they were credited to Andrea Mantegna; the engraver is now unknown, probably working in Ferrara around 1465. Some images are copied in a manuscript dated 1467, which sets the latest possible date. Gold hand-colouring like this is very rare on prints.
