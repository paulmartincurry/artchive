Plate 43 from Los Caprichos: The Sleep of Reason Produces Monsters

Francisco de Goya · 1799 · Etching, aquatint · Plate: 8 3/8 × 5 15/16 in. (21.2 × 15.1 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Los Caprichos came out in 1799, the year Goya was made Prime Court Painter, the highest rank a painter could hold at the Spanish court. He was fifty-three and had been deaf for six years, since an illness in 1793. Manuel Godoy, the prime minister, saw himself as instrumental in getting the series published. M. Knoedler & Co. gave this impression to the museum in 1918.
