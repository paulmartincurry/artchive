Swinging Gibbon

Attributed to Xia Gui · late 1100s–early 1200s · Album leaf, ink and color on silk · 24.8 × 26.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Cleveland catalogues this leaf as attributed to Xia Gui; it carries a signature that is only partly legible. Most of Xia Gui's surviving works are small album leaves like this one, the favourite format of the Song academy painters. The museum bought it in 1978 from the collector Cheng Qi, and it travelled to Kansas City and Tokyo in the exhibition Eight Dynasties of Chinese Painting between 1980 and 1982.
