Inro, Ojime and Netsuke

Shibata Zeshin · later 1800s · lacquer with sprinkled gold, silver, and mother-of-pearl design, coral, and wood · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The set measures eight centimetres overall. Shibata Zeshin was the son and grandson of shrine carpenters in Edo and was apprenticed to the lacquerer Koma Kansai II at eleven; he took over the Koma workshop when his master died in 1835. From 1869 he worked on commissions for the Imperial government, and in 1890 he was made a member of the new Imperial Art Committee. D. Z. Norton gave the set to the Cleveland Museum of Art in 1916.
