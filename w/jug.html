Jug

Christopher Dresser · 1880–82 · Glazed earthenware · H × W × D: 21.5 × 12 × 13.4 cm (8 7/16 × 4 3/4 × 5 1/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

As Art Superintendent of the Linthorpe Art Pottery in Middlesbrough, Christopher Dresser designed more than 1,000 pots between 1879 and 1882. This jug was made there in those years. Impressed into its underside are the pottery's name, Dresser's signature in script, the number 611 and an HT monogram. It stands 21.5 centimetres tall.
