Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, all is vanity.
A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to break down, and a time to build up.

Ecclesiastes 1 & 3

Qoheleth (Book of Ecclesiastes) · ca. 300 BCE (trans. 1611)

Rabbinic tradition holds that King Solomon wrote this book in his old age, though the text never uses his name. Its Persian loanwords and Aramaic turns of phrase point to a date no earlier than about 450 BCE, and it cannot be later than 180 BCE, when the writer Ben Sira quotes from it. The Hebrew word rendered as vanity means vapour or breath. Most modern commentators regard the closing verses as the addition of a later scribe.
