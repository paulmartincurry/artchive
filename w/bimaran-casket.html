Bimaran Casket

Maker unknown · 1st Century · Gold and garnet · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: World Imaging, GFDL

The casket is gold worked in repoussé, 6.7 centimetres high, set with two rows of rubies from Badakhshan. Charles Masson dug it out of the core of a stupa at Bimaran, near Jalalabad, between 1833 and 1838; an earlier digger had given up before reaching the relic chamber. It was found without its lid, inside a stone box inscribed as holding relics of the Buddha. The box held burnt pearls, beads and four coins that were nearly new when buried, and the casket is dated from them.
