Branch of the Seine near Giverny (Mist)

Claude Monet · 1897 · Oil on canvas · 89.9 × 92.7 cm (35 3/8 × 36 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

For months Monet rose before dawn and rowed to the same spot on the Seine, working on fourteen canvases at once as the light changed. He ordered the nearly square canvas specially; half of it is reflection.
