Codex Gigas: The Devil (folio 290r)

Maker unknown · c. 1204–30 · Ink and pigments on vellum · 92 × 50 × 22 cm (whole book) · National Library of Sweden, Stockholm

Free to use

The Devil fills folio 290 recto, which is otherwise empty, and stands about 50 centimetres tall. The book is 92 centimetres long and weighs 74.8 kilograms. Swedish troops took it from Prague as war booty in 1648. When fire swept the royal castle in Stockholm on 7 May 1697, it was saved by being thrown out of a window.
