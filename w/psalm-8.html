When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;
What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?

Psalm 8

Book of Psalms (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · Date uncertain (trans. 1611)

On his way back to Earth from the first human landing on the Moon, the astronaut Buzz Aldrin recited verses 4 and 5. The psalm opens with a direction to the chief musician to play upon the gittith, which may be an instrument, a style of performance, or a reference to people and places in biblical history.
