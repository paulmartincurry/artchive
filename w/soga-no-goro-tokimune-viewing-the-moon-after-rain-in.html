Soga no Gorō Tokimune Viewing the Moon after Rain in the Mountains (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · December 1885 · Color woodblock print · Sheet 35.2 × 24 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

Yoshitoshi was forty-six when this sheet came out in December 1885, the year the moon series began. That same year the art and fashion magazine Tokyo Hayari Hosomiki ranked him the number-one ukiyo-e artist. The impression at LACMA came to the museum in the Karl O. Tuschka Bequest.
