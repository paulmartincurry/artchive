But no word has reached me from kin or friends.
I am old and sick and alone with my boat.
North of this wall there are wars and mountains —
And here by the rail how can I help crying?

On the Gate-Tower at Yo-chou

Du Fu · before 770; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

Du Fu's later writing grew out of the An Lushan rebellion, which broke out in 755 and drove him and his family from their home. Accounts disagree about where and how he died in 770.
