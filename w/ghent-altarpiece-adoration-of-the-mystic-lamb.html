Ghent Altarpiece: Adoration of the Mystic Lamb

Hubert and Jan van Eyck · 1432 · Oil on panel · St Bavo's Cathedral, Ghent

Free to use

The installation of the altarpiece was marked officially on 6 May 1432. Art historians generally agree that Hubert van Eyck designed the structure and that his younger brother Jan painted the panels. An examination after restoration found 75 plants in the altarpiece, 44 of them on this panel.
