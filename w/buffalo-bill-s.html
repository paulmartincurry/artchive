Buffalo Bill ’s

E. E. Cummings · 1920

Cummings was twenty-five in 1920. He had written poetry daily from the age of eight to twenty-two. In 1917, while a volunteer ambulance driver in France, he was arrested by the French military on suspicion of espionage and held for three and a half months in a detention camp in Normandy; his father wrote to President Wilson to get him out. His first collection of poems, Tulips and Chimneys, did not appear until 1923, and it was heavily cut by his editor.
