'Tears, idle tears, I know not what they mean,
Tears from the depth of some divine despair
Rise in the heart, and gather to the eyes,
In looking on the happy Autumn-fields,
And thinking of the days that are no more.

Tears, Idle Tears

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1847

Tennyson wrote this lyric in 1847 and published it among the songs of The Princess. A visit to Tintern Abbey in Monmouthshire, abandoned since 1536, may have prompted it; Wordsworth had written a poem inspired by the same place in 1798. It is in blank verse and does not rhyme.
