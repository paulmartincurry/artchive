Lucifer

Franz von Stuck · 1890 · Oil on canvas · 161 × 152.5 cm · National Gallery for Foreign Art, Sofia

Free to use

Ferdinand I of Bulgaria bought this painting from Stuck's studio in Munich in 1891, the year after it was made, for the royal collection in Sofia. King Boris III added it to the National Museum on 25 December 1930. Stuck was one of the founders of the Munich Secession.
