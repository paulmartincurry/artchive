Pascal had his Void that went with him day and night.
- Alas! It’s all Abyss, - action, longing, dream,
the Word! And I feel Panic’s storm-wind stream
through my hair, and make it stand upright.

The Void (Le Gouffre)

Charles Baudelaire · published 1868

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Baudelaire died on 31 August 1867, aged 46. The sonnet is among the poems added to Les Fleurs du mal in the posthumous third edition of 1868, which carried a preface by Théophile Gautier.
