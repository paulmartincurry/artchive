Yellow Cow

Franz Marc · 1911 · Oil on canvas · 140.5 × 189.2 cm · Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, New York

Free to use

Franz Marc painted it in 1911, in the years he was working out a colour scheme of his own: blue for spirituality and maleness, yellow for femininity and sensuality, red for the earth.
