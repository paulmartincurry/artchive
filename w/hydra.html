Hydra (Uranometria)

Johann Bayer · 1603 · Engraving · ETH-Bibliothek Zürich (e-rara)

Free to use

Bayer took his star positions from Tycho Brahe's catalogue of 1,005 stars, which had circulated in manuscript since 1598, yet his charts show more than 1,200. In the first edition a table of stars was printed on the back of each chart, and its lettering showed through the paper. Later editions moved the tables into a separate catalogue, which was full of misprints.
