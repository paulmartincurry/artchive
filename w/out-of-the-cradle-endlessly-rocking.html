Whereto answering, the sea,
Delaying not, hurrying not,
Whisper’d me through the night, and very plainly before daybreak,
Lisp’d to me the low and delicious word death,
And again death, death, death, death

Out of the Cradle Endlessly Rocking (closing)

Walt Whitman · 1859

The poem was first published in the Saturday Press on December 24, 1859, under the title A Child's Reminiscence. A reviewer for the Cincinnati Daily Commercial called it unmixed and hopeless drivel and a disgrace to its publisher. It went into the 1860 edition of Leaves of Grass as A Word Out of the Sea. Whitman was born in Huntington on Long Island.
