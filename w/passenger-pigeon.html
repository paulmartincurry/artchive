Passenger Pigeon (The Birds of America, Plate 62)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1829 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 101 × 68 cm (39 3/4 × 26 3/4 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Audubon claimed to have brought 350 live passenger pigeons to England in 1830, handing them out among noblemen. The species is now extinct, and this plate is the most often reproduced picture of it.
