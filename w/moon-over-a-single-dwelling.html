Moon over a Single Dwelling (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 28 × 16.2 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The old woman creeping round the doorway with a torch is the onibaba, the demon hag who lives in Asajigahara. The Library of Congress copy was trimmed at some point and now measures 28 by 16.2 centimetres.
