Remember that I am thy creature; I ought to be thy Adam, but I am rather the fallen angel, whom thou drivest from joy for no misdeed. Everywhere I see bliss, from which I alone am irrevocably excluded. I was benevolent and good; misery made me a fiend. Make me happy, and I shall again be virtuous.

Frankenstein — I ought to be thy Adam (Ch. 10)

Mary Shelley · 1818

The first edition came out in London on 1 January 1818 in three volumes, in a run of just 500 copies. It carried no author's name; Mary Shelley was twenty. The preface was written for her by Percy Bysshe Shelley, and the book was dedicated to her father, William Godwin. Her name went on the title page of the second English edition in 1823. The manuscripts have been in the Bodleian Library in Oxford since 2004.
