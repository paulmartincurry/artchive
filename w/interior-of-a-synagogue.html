Interior of a Synagogue

Alessandro Magnasco · c. 1725–35 · Oil on canvas · 122 × 148.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The synagogue was among the subjects Magnasco painted most often, though he was not Jewish. He worked in Milan until 1735, when he went home to Genoa. This canvas was sold at the Hôtel Drouot in Paris in 1928, and the Cleveland Museum of Art bought it in 1930 from the Venetian collector Italico Brass.
