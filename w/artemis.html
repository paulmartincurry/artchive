Artemis

Vilhelm Hammershøi · 1893–94 · Oil on canvas · 193 × 251.5 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Hammershøi said that he chose the title only shortly before he first exhibited the painting, in 1894. It is his only mythological painting, a canvas 193 centimetres high and more than two and a half metres wide. The Statens Museum for Kunst acquired it in 1916, the year of his death.
