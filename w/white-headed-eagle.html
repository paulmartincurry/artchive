White-Headed Eagle (The Birds of America, Plate 31)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1828 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · 67.7 × 100.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Plate 31 of The Birds of America, which came out in parts between 1827 and 1838. Subscribers received five plates at a time: one large bird, one of middle size and three smaller ones. The birds are printed life size, here on a sheet a metre wide.
