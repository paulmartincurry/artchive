Ichikawa Danjūrō VII as Kan Shōjō in the Mt. Tenpai Scene (Famous Kabuki Plays)

Utagawa Kunisada · 1814 · Color woodblock print with mica · 38 × 25.9 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Danjūrō VII plays the exiled statesman who turns into a vengeful thunder god, in red-and-white kumadori make-up and the crossed eyes of a mie pose, with a plum branch added for this role. Kunisada was in his late twenties and signed it Gototei, a studio name that refers to his father's ferry-boat business. The print is printed with mica and carries the publisher's mark of Kawaguchiya Uhei and a censor's seal.
