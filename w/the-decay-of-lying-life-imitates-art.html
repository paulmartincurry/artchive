Life imitates Art far more than Art imitates Life

The Decay of Lying: Life Imitates Art

Oscar Wilde · 1889

The essay first appeared in the January 1889 issue of The Nineteenth Century, when Wilde was thirty-four, and he revised it heavily for his 1891 collection Intentions. He wrote it as a dialogue between two speakers, Vivian and Cyril, and gave them the names of his own two sons.
