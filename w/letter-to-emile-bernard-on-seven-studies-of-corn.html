They are all of a yellow tone, and were executed at a frantic speed, just as the reaper works silently in the sweltering sun, with only one thought in his mind—to cut down as much as possible.

Letter to Émile Bernard on Seven Studies of Corn

Vincent van Gogh · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1912

During his time in Arles, Van Gogh completed 200 paintings and more than 100 drawings and watercolours, among them harvests and wheat fields. On 1 May 1888 he signed a lease on four unfurnished rooms at 2 Place Lamartine, the Yellow House, for 15 francs a month. On 4 October he sent seven canvases to Pont-Aven in an exchange of works with Gauguin, Bernard and others.
