The Night, plate seven from Die Hölle

Max Beckmann · 1919 · Transfer lithograph on tan imitation Japanese paper · image 55.4 × 69 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This plate, the seventh in Beckmann's Hell portfolio, repeats the composition of his painting The Night, made in March 1919. The portfolio was printed by C. Naumann's Druckerei and published by I. B. Neumann that year, when Beckmann was 35. He had volunteered as a medical orderly in the First World War and was discharged after a nervous breakdown.
