First View of the Yosemite Valley from the Mariposa Trail

Carleton Watkins · 1865/66 · Albumen print · Image/paper: 39.9 × 52.4 cm (15 3/4 × 20 11/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Watkins hauled his camera and fragile glass plates into the valley by mule or carriage. His mammoth negatives gave prints of the same size as the plate, here about 40 by 52 centimetres. In 1867 he opened his Yosemite Art Gallery in San Francisco and won a medal at the Paris Universal Exposition. He printed more than 1,100 mammoth-plate photographs in his career, and most of his negatives burned in the San Francisco earthquake and fire of 1906.
