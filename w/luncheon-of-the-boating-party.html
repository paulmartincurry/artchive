Luncheon of the Boating Party

Auguste Renoir · 1880 · Oil on canvas · The Phillips Collection

Free to use

The people at the table are Renoir's friends, on the balcony of the Maison Fournaise restaurant beside the Seine at Chatou. The woman with the small dog is the seamstress Aline Charigot, whom he married in 1890; she took the place of an earlier sitter who had annoyed him. The painter Gustave Caillebotte sits backwards on his chair at the right, and the owner's son and daughter are at the railing. The collector Duncan Phillips pursued the picture for a decade and paid $125,000 for it in 1923.
