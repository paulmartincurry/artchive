When you came, you were like red wine and honey,
And the taste of you burnt my mouth with its sweetness.
Now you are like morning bread,
Smooth and pleasant.

A Decade

Amy Lowell · 1919

Lowell was forty-five in 1919 and had been writing poetry for seventeen years. D. H. Lawrence had dedicated his New Poems to her the year before. Ezra Pound threatened to sue her for bringing out her three-volume series Some Imagist Poets, and took to calling the American Imagists the Amygist movement.
