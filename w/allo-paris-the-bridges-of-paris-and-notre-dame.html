Allo Paris: The Bridges of Paris and Notre-Dame

Robert Delaunay · 1926 · lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Robert Delaunay was forty-one in 1926. He and Sonia Delaunay had spent the First World War in Madrid and Portugal, and they returned to Paris in 1921, where André Breton and Tristan Tzara introduced him to the Dadaists and Surrealists. This lithograph came to the Cleveland Museum of Art as a gift of The Print Club of Cleveland in 1963.
