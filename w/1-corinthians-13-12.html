For now we see through a glass, darkly;
but then face to face.

1 Corinthians 13:12

Paul of Tarsus (New Testament / KJV) · ca. 54 CE (trans. 1611)

Paul wrote his letters in Koine Greek. According to the Acts of the Apostles he worked with his hands, at leather or tent-making, and was a Roman citizen by birth, from the city of Tarsus. Fourteen books of the Bible have traditionally been attributed to him. The First Epistle to the Corinthians is one of the seven that scholars accept without dispute as his.
