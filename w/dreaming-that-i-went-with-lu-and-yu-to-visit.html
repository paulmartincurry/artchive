Dreaming That I Went with Lu and Yu to Visit Yüan Chēn

Bai Juyi (Po Chü-i) · 815–819 (in exile)

Translated by Arthur Waley

Bai Juyi formed a long friendship with the scholar Yuan Zhen soon after he settled in Chang'an, possibly in 801. Both men were later in exile at the same time, and on Bai's journey up the Yangtze to a post in Sichuan the two spent a few days together exploring the rock caves at Yichang. Later, when Bai was governor of Hangzhou, Yuan Zhen was serving nearby at Ningbo.
