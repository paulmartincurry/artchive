Young man I think I know you—I think this face is the face of the Christ himself,
Dead and divine and brother of all, and here again he lies.

A Sight in Camp in the Daybreak Gray and Dim

Walt Whitman · 1865

The first 500 copies of Drum-Taps were printed in early April 1865, the month President Abraham Lincoln was assassinated. During the war many considered Whitman a nurse and he acted as one, dressing wounds, assisting in amputations and administering medications.
