Letters to a Young Poet — Live the questions now

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1903–1908 (published 1929)

Translated by Stephen Mitchell · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the author and translator. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Rilke wrote ten letters between 1903 and 1908 to Franz Xaver Kappus, a 19-year-old officer cadet at the Theresian Military Academy in Wiener Neustadt, who was deciding between a literary career and a career as an army officer. Rilke had studied at the academy's lower school in the 1890s. Kappus published the letters in 1929, three years after Rilke's death from leukemia.
