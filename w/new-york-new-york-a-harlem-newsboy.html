New York, New York. A Harlem Newsboy

Gordon Parks · May 1943 · Nitrate film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Gordon Parks was thirty when he photographed this newsboy in Harlem in May 1943, on nitrate film. He had bought his first camera, a Voigtländer Brillant, for 12.50 dollars at a Seattle pawnshop at the age of twenty-eight and taught himself how to take photographs. From 1943 to 1945 he worked for the Office of War Information.
