Life is not a series of gig lamps symmetrically arranged; but a luminous halo, a semi-transparent envelope surrounding us from the beginning of consciousness to the end.

Modern Fiction — Life is a luminous halo

Virginia Woolf · 1925

Woolf had been publishing professionally since 1904, more than twenty years before this essay. Her early money came from reviews, some of them in the church paper The Guardian and the National Review, and she used her father's literary reputation to win the commissions. By 1925 she and her husband Leonard owned the Hogarth Press, which published much of her work.
