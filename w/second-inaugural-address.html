Until every drop of blood drawn with the lash,
shall be paid by another drawn with the sword.

Second Inaugural Address

Abraham Lincoln · March 4, 1865

The address was given on a Saturday, with the end of the Civil War days away. Lincoln told Thurlow Weed afterwards that he did not expect it to be popular at once. The actor John Wilkes Booth was in the crowd that day; he ran at Lincoln's procession and was stopped by Benjamin Brown French. Booth shot Lincoln on 14 April, just over a month later. The text is now cut into the Lincoln Memorial alongside the Gettysburg Address.
