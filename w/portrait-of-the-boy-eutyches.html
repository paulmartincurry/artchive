Portrait of the Boy Eutyches

Maker unknown · 100–150 CE · Encaustic on wood · 38 × 19 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A Greek inscription in dark purple pigment below the neckline of the tunic names the boy as Eutyches, freedman of Kasanios. The portrait was made after his death, in wax paint on a wooden panel 38 centimetres tall, to be placed over the face of his mummy. Edward Harkness gave it to the museum in 1918.
