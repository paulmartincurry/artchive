Jadeite Cabbage

Maker unknown · Late 19th Century · Jadeite · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use with credit. Photo: National Palace Museum, Taipei, Open Government Data License

The carver worked from a single piece of jadeite, half white and half green, that was full of cracks and discoloured blotches, and turned those flaws into the veins of the stalks and leaves. The piece is 18.7 centimetres tall. It was first displayed in the Forbidden City rooms of Consort Jin, who probably received it in her dowry when she married the Guangxu Emperor in 1889. The sculptor is not known.
