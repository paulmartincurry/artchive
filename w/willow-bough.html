Willow Bough

William Morris · 1887 · Block-printed in distemper colours · 68.6 × 54.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Morris was fifty-three when he designed Willow Bough, thirteen years after an earlier paper called Willow. It was block-printed in distemper by Jeffrey & Co. for Morris & Company. In the same year Queen Victoria commissioned a wallpaper from the firm for Balmoral Castle. The Metropolitan Museum bought this sheet in 1923.
