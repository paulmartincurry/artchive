Seokguram Grotto

Maker unknown · 751–774 CE (Unified Silla) · Granite

Free to use with credit. Photo: Han Seok-Hong, Cultural Heritage Administration, KOGL Type 1

The grotto is not a cave but a built chamber, assembled from hundreds of granite stones without mortar and held together by stone rivets, on a mountain 750 metres above sea level. Work began in 751 and the Silla court completed it in 774, shortly after the death of Kim Daeseong, who is said to have built it. The seated Buddha is 3.5 metres tall. Repairs from 1913 to 1915 took the structure almost wholly apart and encased it in concrete, which trapped moisture and eroded the carvings.
