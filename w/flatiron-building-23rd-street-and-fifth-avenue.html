Flatiron Building, 23rd Street and Fifth Avenue

Berenice Abbott · 18 May 1938 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott photographed the building on 18 May 1938. It opened in 1902 as the Fuller Building, headquarters of the Fuller construction company, and locals at first called it Burnham's Folly because they doubted it would stand. In 1933 the Equitable Life Assurance Society took it at a foreclosure auction for 100,000 dollars.
