Empty hills, no one in sight,
only the sound of someone talking;

Deer-Park Hermitage (Lù Zhài)

Wang Wei · ca. 750 CE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This is one of twenty quatrains Wang Wei wrote about places on and around his estate at Lantian, south of the capital. His friend Pei Di wrote a matching poem for each one, under the same title. Wang Wei bought the estate, which had belonged to the poet Song Zhiwen, partly with money he earned as an artist on top of his government salary. He was buried there.
