Coeli Stellati Christiani Haemisphaerium Prius (Harmonia Macrocosmica)

Andreas Cellarius · 1660 (this edition 1708) · Hand-coloured engraving · 52 cm (atlas height) · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

On this chart the classical constellations are replaced by Christian and biblical figures, following the scheme Julius Schiller published in Coelum stellatum christianum in 1627. Cellarius printed these alongside the traditional figures rather than instead of them. The 1708 edition holds five plates beyond the 29 listed in its table of contents.
