C. Don Powell · c. 1938 · Colour screen print · 48 × 36 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Old Faithful, in a poster designed by C. Don Powell and printed by Dale Miller for the National Park Service around 1938. The text at the top lists what the rangers offered: nature walks, field trips, campfire programmes and nature talks.
