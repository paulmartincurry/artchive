Then stretched I forth my hand a little forward,
And plucked a branchlet off from a great thorn;
And the trunk cried, “Why dost thou mangle me?”

Inferno — The Wood of the Suicides (Canto XIII)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1306–1310

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

The voice in the tree is Pietro della Vigna, chancellor and secretary to the Emperor Frederick II. He was arrested as a traitor in March 1249 and blinded, and he died soon afterwards; some sources say he killed himself in a prison tower at San Miniato, others that he was tortured to death or died of the blinding. Dante puts him among the suicides rather than the traitors. William Blake drew him in this wood for his illustrations to the Divine Comedy.
