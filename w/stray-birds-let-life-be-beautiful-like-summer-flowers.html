Stray Birds — Let Life Be Beautiful Like Summer Flowers (6, 75, 82, 176, 268)

Rabindranath Tagore · 1916

Stray Birds came out in New York from Macmillan in 1916, with a colour frontispiece by Willy Pogány, and is dedicated to T. Hara of Yokohama. Tagore was then on a lecture tour of Japan and the United States that ran from May 1916 to April 1917.
