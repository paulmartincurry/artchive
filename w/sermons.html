The eye with which I see God is the same eye with which God sees me: my eye and God's eye are one eye, one seeing, one knowing and one love.

Sermons

Meister Eckhart · ca. 1310 CE

Translated by Maurice O'C. Walshe · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Eckhart was a Dominican friar, and he preached these sermons in German rather than the usual Latin. Around 1310, aged about fifty, he was the order's Provincial for Saxony, with forty-seven convents in his charge; the next year he was called back to Paris to teach for a second time. In 1326 the archbishop of Cologne ordered an inquisitorial trial. Eckhart appealed to the pope and set off for Avignon in 1327, and he died before the verdict was received.
