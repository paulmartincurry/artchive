Fra Mauro Map

Fra Mauro · ca. 1450 · Vellum, set in a gilded wooden frame · Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana

Free to use

Fra Mauro was a monk on the island of Murano who worked as an accountant and a professional cartographer. He led a team of cartographers, artists and copyists, and the map took them several years. It carries about 3000 inscriptions and is drawn with south at the top. This is the copy made for the government of Venice; a second, finished on 24 April 1459 for King Afonso V of Portugal, was sent to Lisbon and later disappeared.
