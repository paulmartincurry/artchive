Who among them is a man like Han-rei
Who departed alone with his mistress,
With her hair unbound, and he his own skiffs-man!

Poem by the Bridge at Ten-Shin

Li Bai · 8th century

Translated by Ezra Pound

Pound made the fifteen translations of Cathay, published in 1915, from the notes of Ernest Fenollosa, which were sent to him after he met Fenollosa's widow, Mary, in London late in 1913. Neither Pound nor Fenollosa read Chinese proficiently.
