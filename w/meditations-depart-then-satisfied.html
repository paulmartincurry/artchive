Depart then satisfied, for he also who releases thee is satisfied.

Meditations — Depart Then Satisfied (Book XII, 36)

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180

Translated by George Long

This is the last entry of the twelfth and last book. The division into twelve books is first mentioned in the Suda, a lexicon published in the late 10th century.
