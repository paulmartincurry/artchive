The Anarchist

Félix Vallotton · 1892 · Woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Vallotton was twenty-six in 1892, the year of this woodcut and the year he joined the Nabis, who called him the Foreign Nabi. He was sympathetic to the anarchist movement in his youth, and several of his woodcuts show police attacking anarchists. To earn a living he worked as an art restorer for the gallery owner Henri Haro, and from 1890 he wrote on the Paris art world for La Gazette de Lausanne.
