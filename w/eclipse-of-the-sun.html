Eclipse of the Sun

W. & F. Langenheim · 1854 · Daguerreotype · 3.2 × 2.5 cm to 7.2 × 5.9 cm (seven plates) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is a set of seven daguerreotypes, the smallest 3.2 by 2.5 centimetres and the largest 7.2 by 5.9. The Langenheim studio photographed the partial phases of the annular eclipse of 26 May 1854, whose path ran from the northwestern states across Minnesota, New York and New England. The set came to the museum in 2005 as part of the Gilman Collection.
