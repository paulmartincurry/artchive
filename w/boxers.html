Boxers

Théodore Géricault · 1818 · Lithograph · Image: 35.4 × 41.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Géricault was twenty-six or twenty-seven when he made this lithograph in 1818, the year after he came back from Italy. He had begun his training in 1808 in the studio of Carle Vernet, where he was schooled in the tradition of English sporting art. The image measures about 35 by 42 centimetres.
