The Crowd, frame at 1:32:58

King Vidor · 1928 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

Louis B. Mayer reportedly disliked the film for its bleak subject and its lack of a happy ending, and MGM held it back for almost a year. According to Vidor, seven upbeat endings were shot at the studio's insistence; the film went out with two, and exhibitors could choose which to show. In the months after its release in 1928 it grossed twice what it cost to make.
