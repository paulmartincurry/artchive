I Hear America Singing

Walt Whitman · 1860

Money was short for Whitman after the first editions of Leaves of Grass, and in May 1857 he went back to journalism as editor of Brooklyn's Daily Times. He left that job in 1859. The year before, he had published a 47,000-word series on men's health under the pen name Mose Velsor, taken from his mother's family name. Leaves of Grass was revised and reissued in 1860.
