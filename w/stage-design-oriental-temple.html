Stage Design, Oriental Temple

Antonio Giuseppe Basoli · 1810–30 · Pen and gray ink, brush and gray and black wash on paper · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Antonio Giuseppe Basoli was somewhere between about thirty-six and fifty-six when he made this stage design, on a sheet 53 centimetres wide. Gifts from various donors and the Eleanor G. Hewitt Fund paid for its purchase.
