The Red Room, Etretat

Félix Vallotton · 1899 · Oil on artist's board · Art Institute of Chicago

The painting was worked up from one of Vallotton's own photographs. He had bought a Kodak camera in 1898 and used it as the basis for at least five interiors, and some of his first photographs were taken at the Château d'Etretat. He sold the picture to the Galerie Bernheim-Jeune in Paris in 1907. Mary Runnells bought it in 1968 and left it to the Art Institute at her death in 1977.
