Bust of Nefertiti

Attributed to the workshop of Thutmose · ca. 1345 BCE · Limestone, quartz, stucco, beeswax · Egyptian Museum of Berlin

Free to use with credit. Photo: Philip Pikart, CC BY-SA 3.0

A German expedition found the bust in early December 1912 on the floor of a storeroom, in a ruined house and studio in the deserted city of Akhetaten. The building was identified as the sculptor Thutmose's from an ivory horse blinker in a rubbish pit, inscribed with his name and job title. The bust appears to have been a master study for others to copy. Twenty-three plaster casts of faces were found in the same studio.
