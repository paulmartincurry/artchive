For the listener, who listens in the snow,
And, nothing himself, beholds
Nothing that is not there and the nothing that is.

The Snow Man

Wallace Stevens · October 1921 (Harmonium, 1923)

First printed in the October 1921 issue of the journal Poetry. Stevens wrote in his leisure time. By day he was a lawyer at an insurance company in Hartford, where his work included evaluating surety claims. He and his wife had moved to 210 Farmington Avenue in 1917, and it was there that he completed Harmonium, his first book of poems, published in 1923.
