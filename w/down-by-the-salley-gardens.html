She bid me take life easy, as the grass grows on the weirs;
But I was young and foolish, and now am full of tears.

Down by the Salley Gardens

W. B. Yeats · 1889

Yeats published the poem in The Wanderings of Oisin and Other Poems in 1889. A note of his refers to an old song behind it, which may have been the ballad The Rambling Boys of Pleasure.
