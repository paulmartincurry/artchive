Nataraja, Shiva as the Lord of Dance

South India, Tamil Nadu, Chola period · 1000s · Bronze · Overall: 113 × 102 × 30 cm (44 1/2 × 40 3/16 × 11 13/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The bronze stands 113 centimetres high and 102 wide. Under the dancer's foot is a small figure who holds a serpent in his left hand and points up at Shiva with his right. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought the sculpture in 1930 from C. T. Loo, a dealer in New York.
