Revelations of Divine Love (Ch. 27)

Julian of Norwich · ca. 1395 CE

In May 1373 Julian was thirty years old, seriously ill and thought to be on her deathbed. Sixteen visions came to her, all but one over several hours in a single night. After she recovered she wrote a short account of them, then worked on a far longer one for decades while living enclosed in a cell attached to St Julian's Church in Norwich. The book was not printed until 1670, when a Benedictine monk, Serenus de Cressy, published it.
