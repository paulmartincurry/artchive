About That (Pro Eto), cover

Alexander Rodchenko · 1923 · Book cover with photomontage

Free to use

Vladimir Mayakovsky wrote the poem About That during a two-month separation from Lilya Brik that followed a quarrel, working 16 to 20 hours a day, and finished it on 11 February 1923. Rodchenko was among the guests when Mayakovsky first read it in public, at the Briks' home in early March. For the poem's first separate edition Rodchenko made photomontages, his first to be published, using photographs made by Mayakovsky and Lilya Brik.
