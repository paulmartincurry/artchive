What More Can Be Done? (The Disasters of War, plate 33)

Francisco de Goya · 1810 (published 1863) · Etching, lavis, drypoint, burin, burnisher · Plate: 15.5 × 20.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Goya gave the only complete album of proofs for the series to his friend Juan Agustín Ceán Bermúdez; it is now in the British Museum. He wrote the numbers and titles of the prints in it by hand, and they were copied onto the plates for the 1863 edition, spelling mistakes included.
