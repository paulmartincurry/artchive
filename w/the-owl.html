The Artchive
And salted was my food, and my repose,
Salted and sobered, too, by the bird's voice
Speaking for all who lay under the stars,
Soldiers and poor, unable to rejoice.

The Owl

Edward Thomas · 1917

In 1906 the Thomas family moved to Steep in East Hampshire, on the edge of Petersfield, drawn by the landscape, the link to London and the school Bedales. They lived in three different houses there over ten years. Thomas first published his poems under the name Edward Eastaway; Six Poems came out under it from the Pear Tree Press in 1916.
