Rainy Season in the Tropics

Frederic Edwin Church · 1866 · Oil on canvas · 142.8 × 213.9 cm · de Young Museum, San Francisco

Free to use

In March 1865 Church's two-year-old son and five-month-old daughter both died of diphtheria. He and his wife then travelled to Jamaica with a young artist they had befriended, and Church sketched there. This painting is dated 1866, the year their son Frederic Joseph was born.
