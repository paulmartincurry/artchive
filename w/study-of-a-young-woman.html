Study of a Young Woman

Johannes Vermeer · ca. 1665–67 · Oil on canvas · 17 1/2 × 15 3/4 in. (44.5 × 40 cm) · The Met

Free to use

At a sale in Rotterdam in 1816 this painting went for 3 Dutch guilders, a tiny sum even then. By 1829 it belonged to Prince Auguste d'Arenberg of Brussels, and it stayed in his family until the early 1950s. Charles and Jayne Wrightsman bought it from the family privately for around £125,000 and gave it to the museum in 1979. It is almost the same size as Girl with a Pearl Earring, painted around the same time.
