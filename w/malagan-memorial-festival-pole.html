Malagan Memorial Festival Pole

Maker unknown · 19th century · Painted wood, sea snail opercula · 92.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Malagan carvings are made for ceremonies held in the name of people who have died, events that last several days and take months or years to prepare. Traditionally the carvings were burned or left in a cave to rot when the ceremony ended. This painted pole, made in the nineteenth century with sea-snail opercula, is 92.7 centimetres tall and was given to Cleveland by Dr. and Mrs. Thomas Munro.
