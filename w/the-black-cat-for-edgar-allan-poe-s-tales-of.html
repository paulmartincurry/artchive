The Black Cat, for Edgar Allan Poe's Tales of Mystery and the Imagination

Aubrey Beardsley · 1894 · Pen, brush and India ink over graphite · 25.4 × 16.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Beardsley drew this in 1894, when he was about twenty-two, for an edition of Poe's tales published in Chicago in 1895–96. It is 25.4 centimetres tall, in pen, brush and India ink over graphite. Poe's story first appeared in The Saturday Evening Post on 19 August 1843. The drawing came to the Met as a gift from Jacqueline Loewe Fowler in 2020.
