Pandemonium

John Martin · 1841 · Oil on canvas · Louvre

Free to use

The scene comes from John Milton's poem Paradise Lost, and John Martin also made engravings of a similar view. The canvas, 123 cm by 185 cm, was shown at the Royal Academy exhibition of 1841 with a companion painting, The Celestial City and the River of Bliss. The Louvre bought it in 2006.
