‘Bring the eyes! Bring the eyes!’ cried Coppelius in a hollow rumbling voice.

The Sandman

E. T. A. Hoffmann · 1816

Translated by Ritchie Robertson, 1992 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Hoffmann wrote the story in 1816, when he was forty. He was a jurist by profession, as his ancestors on both sides had been, and also a composer, music critic and artist. In 1802, when he was a young official at Posen, caricatures of military officers were handed round at a carnival ball and he was at once identified as the one who had drawn them; the authorities settled the matter by moving him to Płock. He died in Berlin in 1822.
