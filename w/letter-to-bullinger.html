Mourn with me! This has been the most melancholy day of my life; I am now writing at two o'clock in the morning. I must tell you that my mother, my darling mother, is no more.

Letter to Bullinger (Paris, 3 July 1778)

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart · July 3, 1778

Translated by Lady Wallace, 1865

Mozart had left Mannheim for Paris on 14 March 1778 with his mother, still searching for a post. She was taken ill there and died on 3 July 1778, the date on this letter. It is addressed to Bullinger, a tutor in Count Lodron's family in Salzburg. Mozart left Paris in September.
