Marble Statue of a Kouros (Youth)

Maker unknown · ca. 590–580 BCE · Marble, Naxian · H. 194.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The statue was carved in Attica from marble quarried on Naxos and stands 194.6 centimetres tall without its plinth. According to the Metropolitan Museum it marked the grave of a young Athenian aristocrat. The stance, with the left leg forward and the arms at the sides, was borrowed from Egyptian statues. The museum bought it in 1932.
