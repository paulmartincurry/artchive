Hours of Catherine of Cleves: The Mouth of Hell (MS M.945, folio 168v)

Master of Catherine of Cleves · c. 1440 · Illuminated manuscript · The Morgan Library & Museum, New York

Free to use

Hell was not usually shown in books of hours. This page stands at the beginning of the Office of the Dead and shows three animal mouths. The book was produced in Utrecht for Catherine, Duchess of Guelders, and finished probably around 1440.
