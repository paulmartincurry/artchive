The Man on the Rack (Carceri d'invenzione, plate II)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 56.2 × 41.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Plate II did not exist in the first edition of 1750. Piranesi added it, with Plate V, when he reissued the series in 1761, and it carries a scene of torture near the bottom. He also put in the names and busts of victims punished unjustly by Nero, as recorded by Tacitus.
