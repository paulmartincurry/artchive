The Bay of Marseille, Seen from L'Estaque

Paul Cézanne · c. 1885 · Oil on canvas · 80.2 × 100.6 cm (31 5/8 × 39 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is one of more than a dozen views of the sea from L'Estaque that Cézanne painted in the 1880s. He kept it for over a decade, then sold it to the dealer Ambroise Vollard around 1899, and it went on to a collector in Amsterdam. Martin A. Ryerson of Chicago bought it in June 1920 and left it to the Art Institute in 1933. The canvas is 80.2 by 100.6 centimetres.
