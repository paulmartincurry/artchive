Season of mists and mellow fruitfulness,
Close bosom-friend of the maturing sun;
Conspiring with him how to load and bless
With fruit the vines that round the thatch-eves run;

To Autumn

John Keats · 1819

The poem was composed on 19 September 1819, after a walk along the River Itchen near Winchester. John Keats was twenty-three. Two days later he described the stubble fields of that walk in a letter to his friend John Hamilton Reynolds. It was the last of his 1819 odes: he could no longer afford to give his time to poems and turned to better-paid work. He died in Rome on 23 February 1821.
