The Red Record

Ida B. Wells · 1895

Ida B. Wells was born into slavery in Mississippi in 1862 and was supporting her younger siblings as a schoolteacher at sixteen, after yellow fever killed both her parents. The Memphis Board of Education dismissed her in 1891 for articles criticising conditions in the city's Black schools. She married Ferdinand L. Barnett in 1895, the year of this pamphlet. In 2020 she was awarded a Pulitzer Prize special citation, eighty-nine years after her death.
