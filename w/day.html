Day (Truth)

Ferdinand Hodler · 1896/98 · Oil on canvas · 200.5 × 105 cm (79 × 41 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

After Hodler's death in 1918 the painting went to his son Hector, who had founded the World Esperanto Association in 1908, and it stayed in the family until 1965. It was in a Swiss private collection until the Art Institute bought it at Sotheby's in May 2003. Hodler first set out his theory of parallelism in a lecture manuscript of 1897, in the years he was working on this canvas.
