Dialogue between Nature and an Icelander (closing)

Giacomo Leopardi · 1824

Translated by Charles Edwardes

Leopardi conceived the Operette morali in a single year, 1824, at a time when he believed his lyrical voice had vanished and had turned to philosophical prose. The collection holds twenty-four dialogues and novellas, and he took the comic style of Lucian as a model. Readers frequently confused the book with a similar project by his father, Monaldo, which achieved considerable success, and often cited Giacomo as its author.
