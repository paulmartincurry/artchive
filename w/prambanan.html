Prambanan

Maker unknown · ca. 850

Free to use with credit. Photo: CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0

An inscription dates the inauguration of the Shiva temple here to 12 November 856. While it was being built, a nearby river was cut into a new course along the outer wall and the old bed filled in to make room for more temples. The compound once held 240 structures around a central tower 47 metres high. It was abandoned after about eighty years, and the temples collapsed in an earthquake in the sixteenth century. The rebuilt Shiva temple was completed in 1953, and a shrine is rebuilt only if at least three quarters of its original stone survives.
