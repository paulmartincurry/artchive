Do not act as if thou wert going to live ten thousand years. Death hangs over thee. While thou livest, while it is in thy power, be good.

Meditations — Do not act as if thou wert going to live ten thousand years (Book IV, 17)

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180

Translated by George Long

Part of the work was written on campaign. Internal notes say the first book was written in the country of the Quadi, at the Granova, the modern Hron River in Slovakia, and the second at Carnuntum.
