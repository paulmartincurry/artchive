Woman with a Parasol – Madame Monet and Her Son

Claude Monet · 1875 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Monet painted this outdoors, probably in a single session of a few hours. The figures are his wife Camille and their seven-year-old son Jean, at Argenteuil, where the family was living. At a metre tall it is the largest canvas he painted in the 1870s. It was one of 18 works he showed at the Second Impressionist Exhibition in April 1876, and that November he sold it to Georges de Bellio, his homeopath, who was regularly paid in paintings.
