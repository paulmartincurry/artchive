And we are here as on a darkling plain
Swept with confused alarms of struggle and flight,
Where ignorant armies clash by night.

Dover Beach

Matthew Arnold · 1867

A draft of the first twenty-eight lines survives in pencil on the back of a folded sheet of notes for another poem. The most likely date for it is 1851, the year Arnold, then twenty-eight, was appointed an inspector of schools, married two months later, and stopped at Dover on his honeymoon. He did not publish the poem until 1867, in the collection New Poems.
