The Artchive
Because I could not stop for Death,
He kindly stopped for me;

Because I could not stop for Death (479)

Emily Dickinson · ca. 1863

Emily Dickinson never authorized the publication of her work, and it is not known whether she considered this poem finished or had abandoned it. It was first printed in 1890, after her death, in Poems: Series 1, a collection assembled and edited by her friends Mabel Loomis Todd and Thomas Wentworth Higginson. There it carried the title The Chariot.
