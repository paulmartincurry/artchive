Head of an Ọ́bà (Ùhúnmwèlaò)

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · Early 15th–mid-16th century · Brass, iron · 23.5 × 21.9 × 22.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Brass heads of kings were made for the palace of the oba at Benin City, where the royal ancestral altars were produced. The pieces were cast by the lost-wax method, and the casters' guild is now based on Igun Street in the city. This head came to the Met in 1979 in the bequest of Nelson A. Rockefeller.
