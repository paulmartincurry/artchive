Sawmill at the Greensboro Lumber Co., Greensboro, Georgia

Jack Delano (attributed) · June 1941? · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress credits this slide to Jack Delano but notes that the photographer may not be Delano, and it dates the picture to June 1941 with a question mark. Delano had come to the Farm Security Administration after proposing a study of anthracite mining in Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania. At the agency he no longer developed or printed his own film.
