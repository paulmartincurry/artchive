Two Mercenaries and a Woman

Urs Graf · 1524 · Woodcut · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Urs Graf was a goldsmith who also served for long periods as a mercenary soldier. In 1518 he fled Basel, accused of attempted murder, and was allowed back the following year. He made this woodcut in 1524. After 1527 the record of his life becomes unclear.
