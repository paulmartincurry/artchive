And she to me: “There is no greater sorrow
Than to be mindful of the happy time
In misery, and that thy Teacher knows.

Inferno — Paolo and Francesca (Canto V)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1306–1310

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

The real Francesca da Polenta was married in or around 1275 to Giovanni Malatesta, son of the lord of Rimini, to seal a peace between two families. Her affair with his younger brother Paolo lasted some ten years, until Giovanni surprised them in her bedroom and killed them both, some time between 1283 and 1286. Dante most likely knew the story from her nephew, Guido Novello da Polenta, his host in Ravenna at the end of his life.
