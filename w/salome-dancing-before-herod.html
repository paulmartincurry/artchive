Salome Dancing before Herod

Gustave Moreau · 1876 · Oil on canvas · Hammer Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Seven years went into this canvas before Gustave Moreau showed it for the first time at the Paris Salon of 1876. The palace borrows from buildings in Istanbul, Granada and Córdoba, and motifs in it have been traced to Etruscan, Roman, Egyptian, Indian and Chinese art.
