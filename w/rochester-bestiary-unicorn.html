Rochester Bestiary: Unicorn (folio 10v)

Maker unknown · c. 1230–1240 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · British Library, London

Free to use

The book appears to have been stolen from the priory at Rochester at some point. A fourteenth-century inscription records its return by a brother John Malling, and a man of that name was excommunicated in 1387 as an apostate and thief.
