The Mocking Bird (The Birds of America, Plate 21)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1827 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 99.5 × 66.5 cm (39 3/16 × 26 3/16 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Subscribers received the prints in sets of five every month or two, packed in tin cases, usually one very large bird, one medium-sized bird and three small ones. The plates came unbound and without any text, so that free copies did not have to go to the British legal deposit libraries.
