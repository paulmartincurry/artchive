for here there is no place that does not see you. You must change your life.

Archaic Torso of Apollo

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1908

Translated by Stephen Mitchell · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The sonnet opens the second part of Rilke's New Poems, published in 1908, and comes directly after that volume's dedication to Auguste Rodin. The first part, a year earlier, had opened with a poem on an earlier statue of Apollo. Which torso Rilke had in mind is not known for certain. In 1947 the archaeologist Ulrich Hausmann proposed the Miletus torso in the Louvre, a figure of a young man carved about 480 to 470 BC, and another scholar has called that improbable.
