The Water Fan

Winslow Homer · 1898/99 · Watercolor, with blotting and touches of scraping, over graphite, on thick, rough twill-textured, ivory wove paper · 37.4 × 53.4 cm (14 3/4 × 21 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The man is looking for coral through a glass-bottomed bucket, called a water glass or sponge glass, which steadies the surface so he can see down. Homer painted it in the Bahamas; red washes in the water that showed the coral below have faded.
