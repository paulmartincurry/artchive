O Mother, even a dullard becomes a poet who meditates upon Thee raimented with space

Karpuradi Stotra

Traditional Tantric Sanskrit Hymn · Medieval Period

Translated by Arthur Avalon (Sir John Woodroffe), 1922

A Sanskrit hymn of twenty-two verses to the goddess Kali, used in Tantric worship in Bengal. Each verse describes her form and promises rewards to the person who meditates on it. It reached English readers in the early twentieth century through the translations of Sir John Woodroffe.
