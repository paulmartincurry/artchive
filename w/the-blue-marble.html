The Blue Marble

The crew of Apollo 17 · 1972 · Photograph

Free to use

The frame was shot on 7 December 1972, about five hours after Apollo 17 launched, with a Hasselblad and an 80 mm Zeiss lens on Kodak Ektachrome, at f/2.8 and 1/250 of a second. It is the third of three nearly identical exposures. In the original the South Pole is at the top; the published versions are cropped and rotated. NASA credits the whole crew, and each of the three astronauts has claimed the picture, though the evidence points to Harrison Schmitt. It was released on 23 December.
