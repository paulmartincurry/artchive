Cattleya Orchid and Three Hummingbirds

Martin Johnson Heade · 1871 · Oil on wood · 34.8 × 45.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Heade travelled in Brazil from 1863 to 1864 and painted more than forty small pictures of hummingbirds for a book that was never published. This panel, signed and dated 1871, is about 35 centimetres tall.
