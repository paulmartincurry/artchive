L'Absinthe

Edgar Degas · 1876 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay

Free to use

Neither figure was a stranger in a bar. The woman is Ellen Andrée, an actress, and the man is Marcellin Desboutin, a painter and etcher; both modelled for Degas. The setting is the Café de la Nouvelle-Athènes in Paris. The picture was first called Dans un Café and was renamed L'Absinthe only when it was shown in London in 1893. Its first owner was English, Captain Henry Hill of Brighton, who bought it from a London dealer in 1876.
