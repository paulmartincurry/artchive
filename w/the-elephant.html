The Elephant

Martin Schongauer · c. 1485 · Engraving on ivory laid paper · Image: 10.2 × 12.8 cm (4 1/16 × 5 1/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Schongauer may have made his Griffin and Elephant as a pair. His elephant is far from accurate, but by the mid-1500s it was the model for the illustration in Conrad Gessner's 1551 book on animals.
