Flüelen, from the Lake of Lucerne

J. M. W. Turner · 1845 · watercolor with gouache and scratch-away · Sheet: 29.2 × 47.9 cm (11 1/2 × 18 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Turner was seventy in 1845. Flüelen is a small Swiss town, and this sheet belongs to a group of finished watercolours he made on several summer trips to Switzerland between 1841 and 1845, more than forty years after his first visit there in 1802. John Ruskin called the group the sunset drawings, because they came at the end of Turner's career. Before reaching Cleveland in 1954 the drawing went through Christie's in London twice, in 1884 and 1922.
