The Vicarello Goblet

Maker unknown · 25 BCE–25 CE · Silver · 12.2 × 7.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This silver cup, 12.2 centimetres high and worked in relief, was found north of Rome at Vicarello, the ancient Aquae Apollinares, probably in 1862. Sir William Drake of London owned it from 1867 or later, and Baron Edmond de Rothschild of Paris had it by 1912. Cleveland bought it at Christie's in London in 1966.
