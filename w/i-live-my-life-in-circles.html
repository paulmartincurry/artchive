I live my life in circles that grow wide
And endlessly unroll, …
Am I a bird that skims the clouds along,
Or am I a wild storm, or a great song?

I Live My Life in Circles (The Book of Hours)

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1899

Translated by Jessie Lemont

Rilke wrote the first part of The Book of Hours between 20 September and 14 October 1899 in Berlin-Schmargendorf, after a summer journey to Russia. He was twenty-three. He claimed that the verses came to him on waking in the morning, or in the evening, as words he needed only to transcribe afterwards. The collection was first published in Leipzig in 1905.
