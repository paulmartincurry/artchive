Saint John Devouring the Book (The Apocalypse)

Albrecht Dürer · 1498 · Woodcut on laid paper · Block: 39.3 × 28.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Work on the Apocalypse began during Dürer's first trip to Italy, which he made in 1494–95. Printing the picture on one side of the leaf and the text on the other had been done before by his godfather, the printer Anton Koberger.
