The Artchive

East Side Interior

Edward Hopper · 1922 · Etching · Plate 20 × 22.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

At an impasse over his oil paintings, Hopper turned to etching in 1915, and by 1923 he had produced most of his approximately seventy prints, many of them urban scenes of Paris and New York. This one dates from 1922. A year later he took up watercolour, and after selling every sheet at a one-man show he decided to put illustration behind him.
