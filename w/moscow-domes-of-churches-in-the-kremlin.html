Moscow, Domes of Churches in the Kremlin

Roger Fenton · 1852 · Salted paper print from paper negative · Image: 17.9 × 21.6 cm (7 1/16 × 8 1/2 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Fenton first saw photographs at the Great Exhibition in London in 1851, and within a year he was taking his own. He went to Paris to learn the waxed paper calotype process, most likely from Gustave Le Gray, and in 1852 he travelled to photograph Kiev, Moscow and St. Petersburg. He was thirty-three, a trained painter who had also read law. The same year he and Antoine Claudet published a proposal for founding a Photographic Society.
