Sleeping Woman and Child (Schlafende mit Kind)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1929 · Woodcut · Paper: 40.6 × 50.2 cm (16 × 19 3/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Käthe Kollwitz was sixty-one or sixty-two when she made this woodcut, on a sheet of paper about forty by fifty centimetres. She had taken up the medium after seeing woodcuts by Ernst Barlach at the Secession exhibitions, having found etching insufficient for the sheet she was making for Karl Liebknecht. This impression is signed in graphite in the lower margin. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought it in 1959 from a dealer in Santa Barbara.
