Old Man with a Gold Chain

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1631 · Oil on panel · 83.1 × 75.7 cm (32 3/4 × 29 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This is a tronie, a character study rather than a commissioned portrait, a type Rembrandt and Jan Lievens made into a cheaper alternative to portraits in the studio they shared in Leiden. The sitter was long called the artist's father, but he may have been the keeper of a Leiden almshouse. Rembrandt was twenty-five, signed the panel RHL, and moved to Amsterdam at the end of the year. The panel was later cut down slightly in height. Mrs. W. W. Kimball of Chicago left it to the Art Institute in 1922.
