Shwedagon Pagoda

Maker unknown · 6th–15th Century (Raised by Queen Shin Sawbu, 1450s–1470s)

Free to use with credit. Photo: Bjørn Christian Tørrissen, CC BY-SA 4.0

The royal chronicles first mention the pagoda in 1362/63, when King Binnya U raised it to 18 metres. Queen Shin Saw Pu took it to 40 metres and gilded it. An earthquake in 1768 brought down the top, and in 1775 King Hsinbyushin rebuilt it to its present 99 metres. The structure is brick, covered in gold plates fixed with rivets. Its crown is set with 5,448 diamonds and 2,317 rubies, under a single 76-carat diamond at the tip.
