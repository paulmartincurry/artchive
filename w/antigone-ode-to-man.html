Numberless wonders
terrible wonders walk the world but none the match for man

Antigone — Ode to Man

Sophocles · ca. 441 BCE

Translated by Robert Fagles · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Sophocles wrote more than 120 plays, and Antigone is one of only seven that survive complete. His father was an armour manufacturer. In 441 BC he was elected one of the ten generals of Athens and served in the campaign against Samos. An ancient life of him says the post was a reward for his production of this play, a story one modern classicist calls most improbable.
