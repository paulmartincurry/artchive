Fate of the Animals

Franz Marc · 1913 · Oil on canvas · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

In 1916, after Franz Marc's death, the whole right side of this canvas was burned beyond recognition. His friend Paul Klee restored it from old photographs, and painted the lost section in a dull brown instead of matching the original colours. The painting is 195 centimetres high and 268 wide, and its lines are all diagonals. It first had a different title, about trees showing their rings and animals their veins.
