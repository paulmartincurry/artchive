Snowy Owl (The Birds of America, Plate 121)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1831 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 100.7 × 68 cm (39 5/8 × 26 3/4 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Audubon painted his birds mainly in watercolour, adding coloured chalk or pastel to soften the feathers, especially those of owls and herons. Robert Havell Jr. engraved the plates in aquatint from those drawings.
