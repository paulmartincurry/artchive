For they are a fragrance, and I am a flint and a fire,
I am an answer, they are only a call.

What Do I Care

Sara Teasdale · 1920

By 1920 Teasdale was living in an apartment on Central Park West in New York, where she and her husband had moved four years earlier. In 1918 she had won a Pulitzer Prize for her collection Love Songs, paid for by a special grant from the Poetry Society. Her next book, Flame and Shadow, was published in 1920, the year she turned thirty-six.
