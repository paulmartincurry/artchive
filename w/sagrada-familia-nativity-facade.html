Sagrada Família, Nativity Façade

Antoni Gaudí · 1893–1936 · Stone

Free to use

This was the first of the three façades to be finished, built between 1893 and 1936. Gaudí knew he would not live to complete the church and chose this front to set the example for those who came after him; he had intended every statue on it to be painted. Some of the figures were destroyed in 1936, during the Spanish Civil War, and were remade by the Japanese sculptor Etsuro Sotoo. Gaudí, struck by a tram in June 1926, is buried in the crypt.
