Bahram Chubina Slays the Lion-Ape (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Mir Musavvir and Qasim ibn 'Ali · c. 1530–35 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.1 × 19.7 cm; page 47 × 28.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting, on folio 715 verso, is attributed to Mir Musavvir with Qasim ibn 'Ali. According to the chronicler Dust Muhammad, Mir Musavvir and Aqa Mirak worked closely together, painting walls in the palace of Prince Sam Mirza and illustrating the royal Khamsa of Nizami of 1539–43. Mir Musavvir died in 1555.
