The poetry of earth is never dead:
When all the birds are faint with the hot sun,
And hide in cooling trees, a voice will run
From hedge to hedge about the new-mown mead;

On the Grasshopper and Cricket

John Keats · 1816

In 1816 Keats was a medical student at Guy's Hospital, working as a dresser who assisted the surgeons during operations. He received his apothecary's licence that year. In May Leigh Hunt printed one of his sonnets in The Examiner, the first time his poetry appeared in print, and in early December he told his guardian that he was giving up medicine for poetry.
