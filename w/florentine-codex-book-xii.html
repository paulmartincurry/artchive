Florentine Codex, Book XII (folio 40 verso)

Bernardino de Sahagún · 1577 · Ink and paint on paper · 31 × 21.2 cm · Biblioteca Medicea Laurenziana, Florence

Free to use

Book XII of the Florentine Codex tells the Spanish conquest of Mexico, from 1519 to the fall of Tenochtitlan in 1521, as recounted by Indigenous elders who had lived in the city and seen the events. Bernardino de Sahagún gathered their accounts around 1553 to 1555 at the College of Santa Cruz in Tlatelolco. The manuscript's more than 2,000 illustrations were drawn by native artists, and its three volumes are in the Biblioteca Medicea Laurenziana in Florence.
