The Kin-der-Kids (Abroad): The Kids' Terrible Adventure with a Whale!

Lyonel Feininger · 1906 · Print (newspaper comic page) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

In February 1906, when a quarter of Chicago's population was of German descent, the Chicago Tribune's editor James Keeley went to Germany to hire popular humour artists, and recruited Feininger, a New Yorker who had moved to Germany at sixteen. The Kin-der-Kids began on 29 April 1906 and follows three brothers, their dog and a clockwork boy sailing round the world in an antique bathtub. The Tribune cancelled it on 18 November 1906.
