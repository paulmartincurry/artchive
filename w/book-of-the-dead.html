Book of the Dead (Spell 30B)

Papyrus of Ani (New Kingdom Scribes) · ca. 1250 BCE

Translated by Raymond O. Faulkner · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This copy of the Book of the Dead was compiled about 1250 BCE for Ani, a scribe of Thebes. The scroll is 78 feet long and 13 inches high. It was found in his tomb in 1888 by Egyptians trading in illegal antiquities and bought by E. A. Wallis Budge. When the police sealed the dealers' houses, Budge gave the guards a meal while local men tunnelled under a wall to bring the papyrus out. It was smuggled to the British Museum, and the Treasury paid Budge 150 pounds.
