Ah, Postumus! they fleet away,
Our years, nor piety one hour
Can win from wrinkles and decay,
And Death's indomitable power;

Odes II.14 (Eheu Fugaces)

Horace · 23 BCE

Translated by John Conington

Horace had been an officer in the republican army that was defeated at Philippi in 42 BC. Afterwards he was befriended by Maecenas, the right-hand man of Octavian in civil affairs. This ode comes from the second of the three books he published in 23 BC.
