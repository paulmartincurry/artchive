The Artchive
Gentile or Jew
O you who turn the wheel and look to windward,
Consider Phlebas, who was once handsome and tall as you.

Death by Water (The Waste Land, IV)

T. S. Eliot · 1922

The Waste Land was published in 1922. Ezra Pound, whom Eliot came to know in London in 1914, edited the poem.
