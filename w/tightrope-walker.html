Tightrope Walker

Paul Klee · 1923 · color lithograph · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Klee made this print while teaching at the Bauhaus, where he spent a decade on the faculty; he was forty-three or forty-four. He drew tightrope walkers many times. On the stone he combined fine pen lines with a smooth gradient of tone above and below the figure. This impression is the fourth state, and the Print Club of Cleveland bought it from a New York gallery and gave it to the museum in 1961.
