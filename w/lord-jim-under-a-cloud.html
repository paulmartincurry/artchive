He passes away under a cloud, inscrutable at heart, forgotten, unforgiven, and excessively romantic.

Lord Jim — Under a cloud (Ch. 45, close)

Joseph Conrad · 1900

The second half of the novel draws in some part on the life of James Brooke, an Indian-born English adventurer who in the 1840s made himself the first Rajah of Sarawak, on Borneo; Conrad said so in a letter to Margaret Brooke. The ship's mate who was the model for Jim, Austin Podmore Williams, went back to Singapore and became a successful ship's chandler. The writer Gavin Young later traced his grave to the Bidadari Cemetery there.
