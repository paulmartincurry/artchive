Scholar Viewing a Waterfall

Ma Yuan · early 13th century · Album leaf, ink and color on silk · 25.1 × 26 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The leaf is about 25 centimetres on each side and came to the Met in 1973 from the collection of the C. C. Wang family. Ma Yuan often pushed the subject of a picture into one corner and left the rest of the surface nearly empty. He was so popular in his own day that many painters imitated him, and his authorship is disputed for all but a few of the album leaves given to him.
