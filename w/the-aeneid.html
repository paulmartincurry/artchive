The Aeneid (Book VI)

Virgil · ca. 19 BCE

Translated by Robert Fagles · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Virgil is said to have recited Book VI to Augustus, along with Books II and IV. Its mention of Marcellus, the son of Augustus' sister Octavia, apparently caused her to faint. According to tradition Virgil travelled to Greece around 19 BC to revise the poem, caught a fever in a town near Megara, and died in the harbour at Brundisium on 21 September, aged fifty.
