The Rescue (A Glove)

Max Klinger · 1880 · Etching · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This impression comes from the fourth edition of the cycle. When Klinger published the Glove etchings in 1881 they were an immediate success and established his reputation, and the critic Jules Laforgue promoted his prints in Paris, where he moved in 1883. He had learned engraving as an apprentice to Hermann Sagert after graduating from the Berlin academy in 1877.
