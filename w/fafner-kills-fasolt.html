Fafner Kills Fasolt (The Rhinegold & The Valkyrie)

Arthur Rackham · published 1910 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

In the Norse Eddas that Wagner drew on, one of two brothers kills the other and turns himself into a dragon to guard the gold. Wagner wrote the libretto of Das Rheingold last of the four Ring texts, between 15 September and 3 November 1852. Rackham's income from books like this one was greatly increased by yearly exhibitions of the original drawings at the Leicester Galleries.
