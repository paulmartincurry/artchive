When Will You Marry? (Nafea Faa Ipoipo?)

Paul Gauguin · 1892 · Oil on canvas · 101 × 77 cm · Private collection

Free to use

Gauguin painted it in 1892, during his first stay in Tahiti. It hung on loan at the Kunstmuseum Basel for nearly half a century, until the Staechelin family sold it privately in February 2015 for close to US$210 million.
