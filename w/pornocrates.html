Pornocrates (La Dame au cochon)

Félicien Rops · 1878 · Pastel, gouache, watercolor · Musée Félicien Rops, Namur

Free to use

Félicien Rops was 45 and living in Paris with Léontine Duluc and her sister Aurélie, with both of whom he had children, when he made this picture. It is 75 cm high and 48 cm wide. The Belgian jurist and collector Edmond Picard was an early owner. When it was shown in 1886 at the exhibition of the Cercle des XX, the group Rops belonged to, it was met with indignation and scandal.
