Borobudur

Maker unknown · ca. 780–825 · Gray andesite-like stone

No record survives of who built Borobudur or what it was built for. It has nine stacked platforms, six square and three circular, with 2,672 relief panels and, originally, 504 statues of the Buddha. It lay for centuries under volcanic ash and jungle until 1814, when a Dutch engineer and 200 men spent two months cutting trees and digging away earth to uncover it. In 1896 the King of Siam was allowed to take home eight cartloads of its sculpture. A restoration that began in 1975 took down more than a million stones to be cleaned and catalogued.
