Automaton Clock in the Shape of a Lion

Maker unknown · c. 1630 · Gilt bronze, ebony, and metal · 20.3 × 12.7 × 22.9 cm (8 × 5 × 9 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The lion was made in Augsburg from gilt bronze and ebony, with the clock's metal works inside. It is about 20 centimetres high and 23 long. The clock came to the Art Institute with the George F. Harding Collection.
