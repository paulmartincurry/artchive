Proserpine

Dante Gabriel Rossetti · 1874 · Oil on canvas · Tate Britain, London

This is the seventh of at least eight versions of the subject that Rossetti painted between 1871 and his death in 1882, and it was commissioned by Frederick Richards Leyland, who ordered eighteen paintings from him in all. The model was Jane Morris. A scroll at lower left says in Italian that it was painted at the beginning of 1874. Rossetti wrote a sonnet for it in Italian on the canvas and in English on the frame, which he designed himself, with roundels shaped like a cut pomegranate.
