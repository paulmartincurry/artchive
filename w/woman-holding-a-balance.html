Woman Holding a Balance

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1664 · Oil on canvas · 39.7 × 35.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

When Jacob Dissius's estate was sold in Amsterdam on 16 May 1696 this painting fetched 155 guilders, more than twice the price of Vermeer's Girl Asleep at a Table. It was once called Woman Weighing Gold, until examination under the microscope showed that the balance is empty. In 1826 it was sold from the estate of the King of Bavaria as a work by Gabriel Metsu. About five centimetres were added to every side at a much later date.
