Girl with the Red Hat

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1669 · Oil on panel · 22.8 × 18 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The picture is painted on a recycled wood panel, not on canvas, and its painted surface is 22.8 by 18 centimetres. Its attribution to Vermeer has been a matter of controversy. A baron bought it at a Paris sale in 1822 for 200 French francs, and Andrew W. Mellon bought it in November 1925 for $290,000.
