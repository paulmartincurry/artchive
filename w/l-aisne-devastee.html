L'Aisne dévastée (Devastated Aisne)

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1919 · Lithograph in two colours · 114 × 79 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The poster raised money for a war fund to rebuild destroyed homes in the Aisne, a department of northern France. H. Chachoin printed it in Paris in 1919, four years before Steinlen's death. The Library of Congress copy has pieces missing at the folds.
