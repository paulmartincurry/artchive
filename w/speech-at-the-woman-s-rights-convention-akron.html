Well, if woman upset the world, do give her a chance to set it right side up again.

Speech at the Woman's Rights Convention, Akron (“Ain't I a Woman?”)

Sojourner Truth · 1851

Truth gave this speech without a prepared text in May 1851, at the Ohio Women's Rights Convention in Akron. The first written version was printed a month later in the Anti-Slavery Bugle. The title it now goes by comes from a different version, published in 1863, which put her words into a stereotypical Southern dialect. Her first language was Dutch, and she spoke English with a Dutch accent all her life.
