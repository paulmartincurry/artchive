Self-Portrait (Camera Work 2, plate III)

Edward Steichen · published 1903 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Steichen trained himself as a painter, and here he poses with palette and brush. The photogravure was plate III of the second issue of Camera Work, in April 1903, when he was 24. The year before, Stieglitz had asked him to design the magazine's logo, with a custom typeface.
