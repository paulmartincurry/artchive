Upstream

Mary Oliver · 2016

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Mary Oliver turned eighty-one in 2016. From the 1960s she had lived in Provincetown, Massachusetts, with the photographer Molly Malone Cook, who was also her literary agent and who died in 2005. Oliver worked on foot. She carried a hand-sewn notebook of three inches by five on her walks, and she said she hid pencils in the trees after once finding herself in the woods without a pen.
