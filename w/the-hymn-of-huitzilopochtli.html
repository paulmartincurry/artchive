The Hymn of Huitzilopochtli

Anonymous Aztec hymn (recorded for Bernardino de Sahagún) · Written down in the 16th century

Translated by Daniel G. Brinton, 1890

This is probably the hymn sung at the feast of Huitzilopochtli, the war god and patron of Tenochtitlan, in the fifteenth month of the Aztec calendar; it was begun at sunset and repeated until sunrise. The hymns survive in two Nahuatl manuscripts of Sahagún's history, one in Madrid and one in Florence. Beside them the Florence copy warns that the Devil hides inside such songs, which only those they are addressed to can understand.
