The Diary of Murasaki Shikibu

Murasaki Shikibu · c. 1010

Translated by Annie Shepley Omori and Kochi Doi, 1920

Fujiwara no Michinaga brought Murasaki Shikibu to court in 1005 or 1006 as companion and tutor to his daughter, the Empress Shōshi, who was then in her late teens. Murasaki was in her early thirties and a widow with a daughter. In the diary she records how she learned classical Chinese as a child by listening to her brother's lessons, and she writes about the other women writers at court. She stayed five or six years.
