Poster for the First Exhibition of the Vienna Secession (Theseus and the Minotaur)

Gustav Klimt · 1898 · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

The Secession had fifty members at the start and elected Gustav Klimt its first president. He designed the poster for its first exhibition in 1898, and it was printed in Vienna by the lithographic firm of A. Berger. The Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogues this impression as the version before censorship.
