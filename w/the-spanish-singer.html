The Spanish Singer

Édouard Manet · 1860 · Oil on canvas · 58 × 45 in. (147.3 × 114.3 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Manet was twenty-eight and staged the scene in his studio, dressing the model in a mix of clothes he had on hand. The figure is life-size. In 1861 this canvas got him into the Paris Salon for the first time, beside a portrait of his parents. It was first hung very high, and was moved lower because visitors liked it so much.
