Human-Headed Winged Lion (Lamassu)

Assyrian · ca. 883–859 BCE · Gypsum alabaster · H. 122 1/2 × W. 24 1/2 × D. 109 in. · The Met

Free to use

The lion is 311.2 centimetres tall and 276.9 centimetres from front to back, and it weighs about 7,257 kilograms. It came to the Met in 1932 as a gift of John D. Rockefeller Jr.
