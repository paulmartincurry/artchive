When the Morning Stars Sang Together (The Book of Job, Plate 14)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

John Linnell formally commissioned the Job engravings from Blake in 1823. Blake cut the plates in pure line, without the preliminary etching that commercial engravers of his time used. They were finished in 1825 and printed in an edition of 315 in 1826, the year before his death.
