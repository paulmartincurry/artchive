O Death, old Captain, it is time, put forth!
We have grown weary of the gloomy north; …
That we would wander Hell and Heaven through,
Deep in the Unknown seeking something new!

The Voyage (VIII: O Death, Old Captain)

Charles Baudelaire · 1859

Translated by F. P. Sturm

In 1841 Baudelaire's stepfather sent him on a voyage to Calcutta, hoping to end his dissolute habits. He never reached India: he stopped for a few weeks on Mauritius and Réunion and returned to Paris. The trip gave him impressions of the sea, sailing and exotic ports that he later used in his poetry, and he later exaggerated it into a legend that included riding on elephants.
