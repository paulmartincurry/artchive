Parting is all we know of heaven,
And all we need of hell.

My life closed twice before its close

Emily Dickinson · 1896

Dickinson made her sister Lavinia promise to burn her papers. After her death Lavinia kept the promise and burned most of the correspondence, but there were no instructions about the 40 notebooks and loose sheets gathered in a locked chest. A third series of the poems appeared in 1896.
