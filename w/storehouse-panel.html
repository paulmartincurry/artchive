Storehouse Panel (Epa)

Maker unknown · Mid-18th century or earlier · Wood · 149.9 × 34.3 × 10.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This panel was carved for a Māori storehouse in the mid-eighteenth century or earlier and is 149.9 centimetres tall. Māori carvers worked with tools of stone, preferably the very hard greenstone called pounamu, and the designs on carvings depict tribal ancestors. The Met bought the panel in 2022.
