Part of that Power, not understood,
Which always wills the Bad, and always works the Good. …
I am the Spirit that Denies!

Faust, Part One — The Spirit That Denies

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · 1808

Translated by Bayard Taylor

The devil's name was invented by the anonymous author of the first Faust book, published in 1587, where it is spelled Mephostophiles; the form Mephistopheles was coined and popularised by Goethe. Goethe completed a preliminary version of Part One in 1806 and published it in 1808. The complete Part One was first staged on January 29, 1829, in Braunschweig.
