But man is a noble animal, splendid in ashes, and pompous in the grave, solemnizing nativities and deaths with equal lustre, nor omitting ceremonies of bravery in the infamy of his nature.

Hydriotaphia, Urn Burial — Splendid in ashes (Ch. V)

Sir Thomas Browne · 1658

Browne studied medicine at Padua and Montpellier and took his degree at Leiden in 1633. He settled in Norwich in 1637 and practised as a physician there until his death in 1682, which fell on his seventy-seventh birthday. He and his wife, Dorothy Mileham, had ten children, six of whom died before their parents.
