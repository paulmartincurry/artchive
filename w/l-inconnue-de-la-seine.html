L'Inconnue de la Seine

Maker unknown · late 1880s · Plaster cast

Free to use

According to an often-repeated story, a young woman's body was pulled from the Seine at the Quai du Louvre in the late 1880s, and a pathologist at the Paris Morgue made a cast of her face. Copies of the mask became a fashionable fixture in Parisian bohemian homes. The face was later used for the head of Resusci Anne, the first aid mannequin.
