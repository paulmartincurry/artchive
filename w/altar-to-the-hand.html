Altar to the Hand (Ikẹ̀gòbọ̀)

Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nmwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · Late 18th century · Bronze · 21 × 25.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This altar was cast in the late eighteenth century and stands 21 centimetres high. Two golden ages of Benin metalwork are believed to have come under Esigie, who flourished around 1550, and Eresoyen, who reigned from 1735 to 1750. The altar came to the Met in 1979 in the bequest of Nelson A. Rockefeller.
