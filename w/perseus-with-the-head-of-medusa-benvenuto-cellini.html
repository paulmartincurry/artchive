Perseus with the Head of Medusa

Benvenuto Cellini · 1545–54 · Bronze · Loggia dei Lanzi, Piazza della Signoria, Florence

Free to use

Cosimo I de' Medici, the second duke of Florence, commissioned this bronze, and Benvenuto Cellini worked on it from 1545 to 1554. It was shown to the public on 27 April 1554 in a piazza where Michelangelo's David and Donatello's Judith and Holofernes already stood. Donatello's Judith had been cast in several sections and joined. On the back of Perseus's helmet Cellini put a portrait of himself.
