Symphony in White, No. 1: The White Girl

James McNeill Whistler · 1861–1862 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Whistler was twenty-seven when he began this canvas in December 1861, and he had it finished by April despite bouts of illness. The model is Joanna Hiffernan, his mistress. The Royal Academy turned it down, then the Paris Salon, and it was finally hung at the Salon des Refusés in 1863. Between 1867 and 1872 he went back and reworked it. The painting, more than two metres tall, stayed in his family until 1896.
