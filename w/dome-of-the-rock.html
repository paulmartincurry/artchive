Dome of the Rock

Built for Caliph Abd al-Malik · 691–692 · Wood

Free to use with credit. Photo: Ludvig14, CC BY-SA 4.0

The caliph hired expert workers from Syria and Egypt, presumably Christians, and the building reportedly cost seven times Egypt's yearly tax income. The dome is 20.20 metres across, close to the measurements of the Church of the Holy Sepulchre. A later caliph, al-Ma'mun, had Abd al-Malik's name in the dedication replaced with his own but left the date untouched. The upper dome fell in an earthquake in 1015 and was rebuilt in 1022–1023. The outside tiles are Ottoman, and the gold-plated roof was added in 1959–64.
