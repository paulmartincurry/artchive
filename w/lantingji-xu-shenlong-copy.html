Lantingji Xu (Orchid Pavilion Preface), Shenlong Copy

Attributed to Feng Chengsu, after Wang Xizhi · c. 627–650 (copy of a text of 353) · Handscroll, ink on paper · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

Wang Xizhi wrote the preface on the spot in 353, at a gathering of forty-two scholars who floated cups of wine down a stream and had to write a poem or drink. He is said to have been drunk and unable to match it when he tried again. Emperor Taizong of Tang had the original taken from a monk by a trusted official, ordered copies, and is said to have been buried with it. This copy, attributed to Feng Chengsu, was made by tracing in a dark room against a sunlit window.
