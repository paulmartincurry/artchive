Mishima: Morning Mist (Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō)

Utagawa Hiroshige · c. 1833–34 · Color woodblock print; oban · 24.3 × 37.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

In 1832 Hiroshige, then a fire warden with the minor privileges of a low-ranking samurai, joined an official procession taking horses from the shogun to the imperial court in Kyoto. He sketched along the road and began the series as soon as he was home. It ran to 55 prints, and each sold new for 12 to 16 copper coins, about the price of a pair of straw sandals.
