BORE, n. A person who talks
when you wish him to listen.

The Devil's Dictionary

Ambrose Bierce · 1906 / 1911

Bierce wrote these definitions over three decades as newspaper and magazine columns; the column first ran under this title in the San Francisco magazine The Wasp on 5 March 1881. His publisher refused the title in 1906, so the first book came out as The Cynic's Word Book and stopped at the letter L. Of the 1,341 copies printed, 1,070 were sold. The whole alphabet appeared under his own title in 1911, as the seventh volume of his collected works.
