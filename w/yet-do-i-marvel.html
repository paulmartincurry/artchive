Yet do I marvel at this curious thing:
To make a poet black, and bid him sing!

Yet Do I Marvel

Countee Cullen · 1925

The sonnet dates from 1925, when Countee Cullen was twenty-two. That year he graduated from New York University, one of eleven students elected to Phi Beta Kappa, entered Harvard for a master's degree in English and published Color, his first collection. He had been brought to Harlem at nine and was taken in at fifteen by the Reverend Frederick A. Cullen, pastor of Salem Methodist Episcopal Church, Harlem's largest congregation.
