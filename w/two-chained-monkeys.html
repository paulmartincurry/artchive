Two Chained Monkeys

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1562 · Oil on oak panel · Gemäldegalerie, Berlin

Free to use

Bruegel signed and dated this small panel BRVEGEL MDLXII, that is 1562. The city behind the arch is Antwerp, and the monkeys are red-capped mangabeys, probably brought there through the port by animal traders. The first record of the picture is the 1668 inventory of the Antwerp collector Peter Stevens; Berlin bought it on the Paris art market in 1931.
