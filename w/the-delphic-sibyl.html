The Delphic Sibyl (Sistine Chapel Ceiling)

Michelangelo · c. 1509 · Fresco · Sistine Chapel, Vatican City

Free to use

The sibyl is one of the figures on the Sistine Chapel ceiling, for which Michelangelo signed a contract on 8 May 1508. The first scaffolding hung on ropes from holes in the ceiling; he disliked it and had a freestanding one built. He looked for assistants practised in fresco, found none who suited him, and decided to paint the whole ceiling alone. The finished work was revealed on 31 October 1512.
