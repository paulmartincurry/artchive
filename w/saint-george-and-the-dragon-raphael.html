Saint George and the Dragon

Raphael · c. 1506 · Oil on panel · 28.5 × 21.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The panel is 28.5 by 21.5 centimetres. The saint wears the garter of the English Order of the Garter, which Henry VII had awarded in 1504 to Raphael's patron, the Duke of Urbino; the duke presumably commissioned the picture. It later belonged to Charles I of England and was sold from his collection on 19 December 1651.
