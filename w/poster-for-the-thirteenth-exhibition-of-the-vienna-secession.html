Poster for the Thirteenth Exhibition of the Vienna Secession

Koloman Moser · 1902 · Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris

Free to use

Koloman Moser was the chief designer of Ver Sacrum, the Secession's magazine, and the lettering at the top of this poster names its fifth year. He was thirty-three in 1902. The next year he and Josef Hoffmann founded the Wiener Werkstätte, and in 1905 he left the Secession with the Klimt group.
