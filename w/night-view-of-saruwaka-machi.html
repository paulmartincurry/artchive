Night View of Saruwaka-machi (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1856 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 35.4 × 23.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Saruwaka-machi was the street of Edo's kabuki and puppet theatres, and Hiroshige drew the people on it with their shadows cast like puppets on a stage. The date seal reads the ninth month of 1856. He used a vertical format for the whole series, one he had first tried in his previous series of views of the provinces.
