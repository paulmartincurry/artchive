The Voyage of Life: Manhood

Thomas Cole · 1842 · Oil on canvas · 134.3 × 202.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Cole painted the four pictures of The Voyage of Life on commission in 1840, and a disagreement arose with the owner about exhibiting them. He painted the series again with minor alterations in 1842, and this canvas is from that set. He sold it to George K. Shoenberger of Cincinnati. In 1908 it was given to a Methodist hospital in that city, which kept it until the National Gallery of Art bought it in 1971.
