The Toltecs have been taken, alas, the book of their souls has come to an end, alas, everything of the Toltecs has reached its conclusion, no longer do I care to live here.

A Song Lamenting the Toltecs (stanzas 1–3)

Anonymous Nahua poet (Cantares Mexicanos) · Written down in the 16th century

Translated by Daniel G. Brinton, 1887

The Toltec state was centred on Tula, in what is now Hidalgo, and was at its height from 950 to 1150. The Aztecs took the Toltecs to be their cultural forebears, and in Nahuatl the word for a Toltec came to mean artisan. In the Cantares Mexicanos many songs have eight stanzas, most have more, and the longest has 114.
