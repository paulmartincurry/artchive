Proverbs of Hell

William Blake · 1790–1793

The Proverbs are part of The Marriage of Heaven and Hell, which Blake composed between 1790 and 1793. He took the title from Emanuel Swedenborg's Heaven and Hell, a work of theology published in Latin thirty-three years earlier, and he criticises Swedenborg by name in several places. The book was printed as sheets from etched plates, and Blake and his wife Catherine then coloured them by hand.
