The Artchive
The brain is wider than the sky,
For, put them side by side,
The one the other will include
With ease, and you beside.

The brain is wider than the sky

Emily Dickinson · 1896

Thomas Wentworth Higginson and Mabel Loomis Todd, who edited the first books of Dickinson's poems, altered them significantly to fit the conventions of the time. Her work was restored closer to what she intended only in 1955, when Thomas H. Johnson compiled a complete collection.
