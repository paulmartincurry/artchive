Sometimes I Feel Like a Motherless Child

Traditional African American Spiritual · 1870s

The song dates back to slavery in the United States, and the Fisk Jubilee Singers were performing it in the 1870s. Samuel Coleridge-Taylor used it as the first movement of his Trio in E minor in 1893. Paul Robeson recorded it several times, starting in 1926, and Richie Havens sang a version of it to open the Woodstock festival on 15 August 1969.
