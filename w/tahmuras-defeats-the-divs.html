Tahmuras Defeats the Divs (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Sultan Muhammad · c. 1525 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.3 × 18.6 cm; page 47 × 32.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Shah Ismail I ordered this copy of the Shahnama in Tabriz and died in 1524, shortly after work began; his son Tahmasp, who took the throne, was eleven. The painting is attributed to Sultan Muhammad. Only two paintings are signed by him, so attributions like this one rest on style and other circumstantial evidence.
