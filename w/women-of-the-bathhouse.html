Women of the Bathhouse

Torii Kiyonaga · ca. 1787 · Woodcut, color · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Torii Kiyonaga was the son of an Edo bookseller and was adopted into the Torii family of artists, where he began training at fourteen. He printed on larger sheets than his predecessor Harunobu. In 1787 he took over the Torii school, which held a near monopoly on kabuki signboards, having put the job off for two years. He nearly stopped making art in the early 1790s.
