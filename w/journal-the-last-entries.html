We shall stick it out to the end, but we are getting weaker, of course, and the end cannot be far.

It seems a pity, but I do not think I can write more.

Journal: The Last Entries (22 to 29 March 1912)

Robert Falcon Scott · March 1912

Scott and four companions reached the Pole on 17 January 1912 and found that a Norwegian team led by Roald Amundsen had preceded them by 34 days. All five died on the return journey. A search party found some of their bodies, journals and photographs eight months later. The entry of 29 March is the last in the journal.
