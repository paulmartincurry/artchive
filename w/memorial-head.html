Memorial Head (Nsodie)

Maker unknown · 17th–mid-18th century · Terracotta · 20.3 × 14 × 12.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This terracotta memorial head comes from the Twifo-Heman traditional area of Ghana and was made between the seventeenth and the mid-eighteenth century. It is 20.3 centimetres tall. It was bought in 1967 with a gift from Nelson A. Rockefeller and is now in the Met's Michael C. Rockefeller Memorial Collection.
