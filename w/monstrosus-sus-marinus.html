Monstrosus Sus Marinus (Monstrorum Historia)

Ulisse Aldrovandi · 1642 · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Aldrovandi was arrested for heresy in June 1549 and held in Rome, in custody or under house arrest, until he was absolved in April 1550. It was during that time that he took up botany, zoology and geology. In 1561 he became the first professor of natural sciences at Bologna.
