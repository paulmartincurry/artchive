Sistine Madonna

Raphael · 1512 · Oil on canvas · Staatliche Kunstsammlungen Dresden

Free to use

Pope Julius II ordered this altarpiece in 1512 for the monastery church of San Sisto in Piacenza, in honour of his late uncle, Pope Sixtus IV, and the commission required both Saint Sixtus and Saint Barbara to appear. In 1754 Augustus III of Poland bought it for 110,000 to 120,000 francs, for decades the highest price paid for any painting, and took it to Dresden. During the Second World War it was stored in a tunnel, where the Red Army found it. It was held in Moscow until 1955.
