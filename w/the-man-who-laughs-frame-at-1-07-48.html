The Man Who Laughs, frame at 1:07:48

Paul Leni · 1928 · Film with recorded score, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The part of Gwynplaine was first meant for Lon Chaney, but Universal failed to get the film rights to the novel, and Chaney made The Phantom of the Opera instead. Conrad Veidt took the role, and Jack Pierce, Universal's head makeup artist, made the face. The three men behind the Joker in 1940 disagreed about much, but agreed that his smile was influenced by a photograph of Veidt from this film.
