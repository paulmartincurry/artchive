Reclining Odalisque

Roger Fenton · 1858 · Salted paper print from glass negative · 28.5 × 39 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

In 1858 Roger Fenton made a group of studio studies based on romantically imagined ideas of Muslim life, using friends and models who were not always convincing in their roles. He had studied painting in Paris and London before he took up the camera. The print is 28.5 by 39 centimetres.
