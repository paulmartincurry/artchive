El Jaleo

John Singer Sargent · 1882 · Oil on canvas · Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum

Free to use

A five-month trip through Spain and North Africa in 1879 gave Sargent the subject. He planned the composition for at least a year and made a series of studies of the dancer's pose, and the canvas itself, almost twelve feet wide, may have taken no more than a few days. Thomas Jefferson Coolidge of Boston bought it at the Paris Salon of 1882. In 1914 Isabella Stewart Gardner borrowed it and built a gallery with a Moorish arch for it, and Coolidge then gave her the painting.
