Le Carceri d'Invenzione (Imaginary Prisons, Second State, Title Plate)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 (Second State) · Paper · Princeton University Art Museum

Free to use

Piranesi trained early as a set designer for the stage, and began this series in 1745. The first edition, published in 1750, had fourteen plates and a title page that did not name him as the artist. For the second edition in 1761 he reworked every plate and added two new ones. He went on printing individual plates at least into the 1770s, experimenting with the ink.
