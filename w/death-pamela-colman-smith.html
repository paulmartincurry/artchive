Death (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

The deck this card was scanned from is the 1910 printing, known as the A deck, which has a cracked-mud pattern on its backs; the tiny first printing of December 1909 had roses and lilies. Rider kept the A deck in print until 1920. Estimates put the number of copies of the deck in circulation at over 100 million.
