The Potato Eaters

Vincent van Gogh · 1885 · Oil on canvas · Van Gogh Museum

Van Gogh was living with his parents in Nuenen, a town of farmers, labourers and weavers, when he painted this between 13 April and the start of May 1885. Before starting he made a lithograph of the composition from memory in a single day. His brother Theo was unimpressed by the sketches, and his friend Anthon van Rappard criticized the finished picture. On 14 April 1991 it was stolen with nineteen other paintings; the getaway car blew a tire and all of them were recovered thirty-five minutes later.
