Reims Cathedral, West Front

Jean d'Orbais, Jean-le-Loup, Gaucher of Reims and Bernard de Soissons · 1211–1345 · L. 149.17 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: Tournasol7, CC BY-SA 4.0

The cathedral was built to replace a church destroyed by fire on 6 May 1210, and the first stone was laid in 1211. It was the traditional place for the coronation of the kings of France. It came through the French Revolution little damaged and was severely damaged during the First World War, then restored. The French state pays for its upkeep.
