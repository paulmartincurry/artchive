Does the road wind up-hill all the way?
Yes, to the very end.
Will the day's journey take the whole long day?
From morn to night, my friend.

Up-Hill

Christina Rossetti · 1862

Christina Rossetti was affected all her life by ill health and depression. She never married, though she had three offers. She trained at the North London Drawing School in the early 1850s and later made her own drawings as guides for the illustrators of her books.
