Boy in a Blue Coat

George Bellows · 1915 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 91.5 × 61 cm (36 × 24 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Bellows painted this in July 1915 at Ogunquit, Maine, where frequent rain kept him indoors and he turned to portraits. No one knows who the boy was. Bellows painted more than 140 portraits in all. The picture was sold at auction in New York in 1923 and came to Cleveland in 1938 as a bequest from Josephine Everett.
