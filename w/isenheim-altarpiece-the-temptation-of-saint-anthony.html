Isenheim Altarpiece: The Temptation of Saint Anthony

Matthias Grünewald · 1512–1516 · Oil and tempera on limewood panel · Unterlinden Museum, Colmar

Free to use

This panel belongs to the innermost view of the altarpiece, which opens onto Nikolaus Hagenauer's carved shrine of Saint Anthony. The work was made for the monastery of Saint Anthony at Issenheim, whose Antonine monks cared for plague sufferers and people with ergotism. Fully opened, the altarpiece is 2.65 metres high and more than 5 metres wide.
