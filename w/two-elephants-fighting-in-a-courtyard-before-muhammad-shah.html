Two Elephants Fighting in a Courtyard Before Muhammad Shah

Nainsukh · c. 1730–40 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · 62.5 × 42 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Nainsukh was born around 1710 in Guler, a small hill state, so he was about twenty to thirty when he made this. The museum says it was painted by an artist from the hills who stayed at the Mughal court in Delhi and painted what he saw there. The emperor Muhammad Shah, who reigned from 1719 to 1748, watches from his window of public audience, smoking a hookah. Around 1740 Nainsukh left his father's workshop in Guler for Jasrota.
