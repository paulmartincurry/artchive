Andreas Cellarius was a Protestant educated in Heidelberg, and may have left the city when the Thirty Years' War began in 1618 or when it fell into Catholic hands in 1622. He married Catharina Eltemans in Amsterdam in 1625 and worked there as a schoolmaster before moving to Hoorn.
