The premeditation of death is the premeditation of liberty; he who has learned to die has unlearned to serve.

Essays — That to study philosophy is to learn to die (Book I, Ch. 19)

Michel de Montaigne · 1580

Translated by Charles Cotton

Montaigne began composing his essays in 1572, after Jacques Amyot's French translation of Plutarch's moral works had come out. The first edition appeared in two volumes in 1580, when he was forty-seven. He was the first author to call such writings essays, from the French verb meaning to try or to attempt.
