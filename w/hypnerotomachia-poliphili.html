Hypnerotomachia Poliphili

Francesco Colonna · December 1499 · Printed book with woodcut illustrations · 11 5/8 × 8 11/16 × 1 9/16 in. (29.5 × 22 × 4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The book was printed in Venice, and this copy is 29.5 centimetres tall. Francesco Colonna, its author, was in his mid-forties when it came out. J. Pierpont Morgan gave this copy to the museum in 1923.
