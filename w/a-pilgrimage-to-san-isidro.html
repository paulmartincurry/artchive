A Pilgrimage to San Isidro

Francisco de Goya · 1819–1823 · Oil mural transferred to canvas · 140 × 438 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya had painted the same pilgrimage about thirty years earlier, in The Meadow of San Isidro; this version is set at night. In his house it faced Witches' Sabbath across the room. Baron Émile d'Erlanger had the murals moved to canvas to sell at the Paris World's Fair of 1878, and in 1881 he gave them to the Spanish state.
