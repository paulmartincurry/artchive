Chalk Cliffs on Rügen

Caspar David Friedrich · 1818 · Oil on canvas · 90.5 × 71 cm · Kunst Museum Winterthur – Reinhart am Stadtgarten

Free to use

In January 1818 Friedrich married Christiane Caroline Bommer, who was about twenty years younger than he was. On their honeymoon that summer the couple went over to the island of Rügen with his brother Christian. The view is from the chalk cliffs of the Stubbenkammer.
