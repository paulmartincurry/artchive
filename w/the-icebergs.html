The Icebergs

Frederic Edwin Church · 1861 · Oil on canvas · Dallas Museum of Art

Free to use

In June 1859 Church sailed to Newfoundland, chartered a schooner and went out in a rowboat to sketch icebergs, seasick, and came back with about a hundred sketches. The painting took about six months. It opened in New York in April 1861 as a one-painting show at 25 cents a head, under the title The North. He added the ship's mast before showing it in London in 1863, where Edward Watkin bought it. After Watkin's death in 1901 it dropped out of sight until 1979, when it was found in a house in Manchester and sold for $2.5 million.
