On Lake Como, Number Two

John Taylor Arms · 1919 · Color etching and aquatint · Zimmerli Art Museum at Rutgers University

Free to use

John Taylor Arms made this colour etching and aquatint, 32 by 16.7 centimetres, in 1919, the year he published his first original etchings. He had trained as an architect at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, graduating in 1912, and served as an officer in the United States Navy during the First World War before giving himself to etching full time.
