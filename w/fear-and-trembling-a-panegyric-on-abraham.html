If a consciousness of the eternal were not implanted in man; … if under everything there lay a bottomless void never to be filled—what else were life but despair?

Fear and Trembling — A Panegyric on Abraham (opening)

Søren Kierkegaard · 1843

Translated by Lee M. Hollander

Kierkegaard published Fear and Trembling in 1843 under the pseudonym Johannes de silentio, Latin for John of the Silence. The book is an extended meditation on Genesis 22, the binding of Isaac. Its title comes from a verse of the letter to the Philippians.
