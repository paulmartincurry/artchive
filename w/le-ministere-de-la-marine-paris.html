Le Ministère de la Marine, Paris (The Admiralty, Paris)

Charles Meryon · 1865 · Etching on laid paper · Image: 14.1 × 12.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is the last of Meryon's Paris scenes. Among the creatures he etched into the sky are fishing boats from Oceania and whales hunted or ridden by horsemen with harpoons. He lettered the plate with a subtitle, Fictions & Voeux, and Delâtre printed it in the Rue St Jacques. Meryon worked almost entirely in etching because he was colour blind. He died in an asylum three years later, in 1868.
