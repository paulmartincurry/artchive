The Garden Court

Edward Burne-Jones · 1870–75 · Graphite and watercolor, heightened with white gouache · Matted: 54.3 x 81.4 x 0.8 cm (21 3/8 x 32 1/16 x 5/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Briar Rose, the story of Sleeping Beauty, occupied Burne-Jones for much of his career, and he painted three sets of it over 30 years. This drawing probably belongs to the first, now in the Museo de Arte de Ponce, Puerto Rico.
