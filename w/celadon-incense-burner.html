Celadon Incense Burner

Maker unknown · 12th century · Celadon with openwork design · National Museum of Korea

Free to use with credit. Photo: Steve46814, CC BY-SA 3.0

Celadon techniques came to Korea from China, and by the 12th century the potters of the Goryeo dynasty had a style of their own. Their kilns were smaller than the Chinese ones, so firing and cooling went quickly and the glaze came out closer to grey. Many pieces were made at the Gangjin kilns in the southwest, which were attacked by Japanese pirates and closed in the late 14th century.
