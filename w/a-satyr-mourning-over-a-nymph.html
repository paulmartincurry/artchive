A Satyr Mourning over a Nymph

Piero di Cosimo · c. 1495 · Oil on poplar · National Gallery, London

Free to use

Piero di Cosimo never signed his works, and this panel is undated. The National Gallery has rejected the title The Death of Procris since at least 1951. Its long, low shape suggests it was made for the front of a bridal chest or to be set into panelling, and the artist's fingerprints are in the paint of the upper part. The gallery bought it in 1862.
