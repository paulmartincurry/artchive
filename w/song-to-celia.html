Drink to me only with thine eyes,
And I will pledge with mine;
Or leave a kiss but in the cup
And I’ll not look for wine.

Song: To Celia

Ben Jonson · 1616

Before he wrote for the stage Ben Jonson was apprenticed to his stepfather as a bricklayer, then volunteered as a soldier with the English regiments in Flanders. In 1598 he killed the actor Gabriel Spenser in a duel, pleaded guilty to manslaughter, and was released with a brand on his left thumb. He was in his forties when this song was printed.
