Exhibition: Index of American Design

Erel Osborn · 1941 · Print on board (poster) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

The Index of American Design, a Federal Art Project programme, paid about 400 artists between 1935 and 1942 to record American crafts and decorative arts from colonial times to 1900. They made 18,257 watercolour drawings, now held by the National Gallery of Art in Washington. This poster advertised an exhibition of the Index at the Chicago Historical Society.
