Skull, from War

Otto Dix · 1924 · Drypoint on cream laid paper · plate 25.7 × 19.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Dix had seen Goya's Disasters of War in Basel and took up its mix of etching and aquatint for his own war series; this sheet is in drypoint alone. Before starting the plates he studied in Düsseldorf to improve his etching. Some scenes in the series draw on his visit to the catacombs of Palermo in 1923–24, others on preparatory sketches for his painting The Trench, which the Nazis later confiscated and which was lost in the Second World War.
