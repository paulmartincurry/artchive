The Utter Defeat of the Taira Clan at Akama Bay in Nagato Province

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1845 · Color woodblock print; oban triptych · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The battle at Akama Bay, Dan-no-ura, was fought at sea in 1185, when the Minamoto fleet under Minamoto no Yoshitsune destroyed the Taira. The young Emperor Antoku, about six years old, died with his grandmother. Kuniyoshi designed the triptych around 1845 for the publisher Enshuya Matabei. Florence Ayscough MacNair, who lived in Shanghai and then Chicago, owned this impression.
