Igbo-Ukwu Bronze Staff Head

Maker unknown · 9th Century · Bronze · Nigerian National Museum, Lagos

Free to use with credit. Photo: Ochiwar, CC BY-SA 3.0

Isaiah Anozie, a villager, turned up the first of the Igbo-Ukwu bronzes in 1938 while digging beside his house. The archaeologist Thurstan Shaw excavated in 1959 at the request of the Nigerian government and found two more sites, a burial chamber and a cache. Radiocarbon dating put them at about 850, centuries before the other known bronzes of the region. The town was a centre of lost-wax casting.
