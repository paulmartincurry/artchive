Arrival of the Normandy Train, Gare Saint-Lazare

Claude Monet · 1877 · Oil on canvas · Art Institute of Chicago

Monet painted the Gare Saint-Lazare twelve times and had eight of the canvases ready for the third Impressionist exhibition in April 1877. He sold this one the month before to his patron Ernest Hoschedé, and the catalogue listed it as his. It was the last time Monet took city life as a subject; after this he turned almost entirely to landscape. In 1911 Durand-Ruel bought it for 13,000 francs and sold it in New York to Martin A. Ryerson for 7,000 dollars.
