Drawing of an Elephant

Maker unknown · c. 1700 · Ink on paper · Image: 12.8 x 18.1 cm (5 1/16 x 7 1/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The Mughal emperors, from Babur and especially Akbar on, kept many elephants at the palace, for work, war, processions, hunting and fighting. Royal elephants had their tusks, necks and ankles decorated.
