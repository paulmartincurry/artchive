God damn it, you've got to be kind.

God Bless You, Mr. Rosewater

Kurt Vonnegut · 1965

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

This was Vonnegut's fifth novel, published on 5 April 1965 by Holt, Rinehart and Winston. Its central character, Eliot Rosewater, reappears in Slaughterhouse-Five and in Breakfast of Champions.
