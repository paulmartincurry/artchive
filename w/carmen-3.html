Now he goes along the dark road, thither whence they say no one returns. But curse upon you, cursed shades of Orcus, which devour all pretty things!

Carmen 3 (Mourn, Ye Graces and Loves)

Catullus · c. 60 BCE

Translated by F. W. Cornish

All of Catullus came down from antiquity in a single manuscript discovered in Verona, of which three copies survive. In it this poem and the one before were a single text; scholars separated them in the 16th century. Birds were common love-gifts in his world, and several scholars have speculated that the poet gave this one to the woman himself.
