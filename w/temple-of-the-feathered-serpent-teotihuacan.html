Temple of the Feathered Serpent, Teotihuacan

Maker unknown · ca. 150–250

Free to use with credit. Photo: Wojciech Kocot, CC BY-SA 4.0

The pyramid has six levels and stands twenty metres high. In antiquity the whole of it was painted, with a blue ground behind the carved sea shells, and the eye sockets of the serpent heads were made to hold obsidian. Two hundred or more people were buried in it, in sacrifices thought to have been carried out as it was built. In late 2003, after days of heavy rain, a sinkhole near its foot led archaeologists to a tunnel beneath it that had been sealed with boulders nearly 1,800 years before.
