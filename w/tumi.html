Tumi

Maker unknown · ca. 900–1100 · Gold, turquoise · The Met

Free to use

Tumi is the Quechua word for knife. The ornate ones made on the north coast of Peru were for ritual use by the elite and went into their burials, and the soft metals they were made of would not have cut flesh. Until 2006, when archaeologists unearthed some in graves in northern Peru, every known metal tumi with a semicircular blade had been recovered from grave looters.
