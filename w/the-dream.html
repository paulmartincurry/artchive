The Dream (Le Rêve)

Henri Rousseau · 1910 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

The Dream was the last painting Henri Rousseau completed. The dealer Ambroise Vollard bought it from him in February 1910, it went on show at the Salon des Indépendants that March, and Rousseau died on 2 September. He based the jungle plants on what he saw at the Paris Museum of Natural History and its Jardin des Plantes. At about two metres by three, it is the largest of his more than 25 jungle pictures.
