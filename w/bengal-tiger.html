Bengal Tiger

Eugène Delacroix · 1829 · Lithograph in black on light gray China paper laid down on ivory wove paper (chine collé) · Image: 32.5 × 46.4 cm (12 13/16 × 18 5/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Delacroix's largest pair of prints shows a lion and this tiger facing off. Into the late 1800s the two were still set against each other for entertainment, and the tiger usually won.
