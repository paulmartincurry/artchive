Bacchus and Ariadne

Titian · 1520 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

The commission first went to Raphael, who took an advance payment and had made only a preliminary drawing when he died in 1520. It passed to Titian, who painted the picture for a private room in the palace of Alfonso d'Este, Duke of Ferrara. The cheetahs were probably modelled on animals in the duke's menagerie, and Titian signed his name on the gold urn. The canvas was rolled up twice in its first hundred years and has needed repeated treatment for flaking paint since. It came to England in 1806.
