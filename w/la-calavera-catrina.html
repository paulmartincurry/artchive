La Calavera Catrina

José Guadalupe Posada · ca. 1910–13 · Zinc etching · Museum of José Guadalupe Posada

Free to use

José Guadalupe Posada made the skull as a zinc etching aimed at the wealthy women of his day. The first dated printing is a broadside sold for the Day of the Dead in 1913, the year he died, with a text he did not write. The name Calavera Catrina was attached only in 1930, when the image first appeared in a book. In 1947 Diego Rivera gave the figure a full body in a mural for the Hotel del Prado in Mexico City.
