Dark, horror of darkness
my darkness, drowning, swirling around me
crashing wave on wave—unspeakable, irresistible

Oedipus the King

Sophocles · ca. 429 BCE

Translated by Robert Fagles · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

At its first performance in Athens the set of plays that included this one took second prize. First went to Philocles, a nephew of Aeschylus. The date, around 429 BC, is highly uncertain, and it came about a dozen years after Antigone. The Greeks knew the play simply as Oedipus. Over his career Sophocles entered thirty dramatic competitions, won twenty-four, and was never placed lower than second.
