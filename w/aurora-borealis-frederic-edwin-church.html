Aurora Borealis

Frederic Edwin Church · 1865 · Oil on canvas · 142.3 × 212.2 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum

Free to use

The polar explorer Isaac Israel Hayes was a friend of Church, and when he came back from the Arctic in 1861 he gave the painter his sketches. The ship frozen in the pack ice is Hayes's SS United States. Church completed the canvas in New York and showed it in London in 1865 with two of his paintings of volcanoes in Ecuador.
