Pensive Bodhisattva (National Treasure 83)

Maker unknown · Early 7th Century · Bronze · National Museum of Korea

At 93.5 centimetres this is one of the few large bronzes to survive from its period, and it was once plated in gold. Which kingdom cast it is not known. The slender body points to Baekje, but the drapery over the pedestal has led most scholars to a Silla caster. The halo is lost, though part of its fitting is still attached. The statue is insured for an estimated 50 billion won. A near twin carved in red pine is kept at Kōryū-ji in Kyoto.
