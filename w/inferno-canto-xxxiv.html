Inferno, Canto XXXIV (Göttliche Komödie, plate 73)

Gustave Doré · published 1870 · Wood engraving · 29 cm (volume) · National Library of Poland, Warsaw

Free to use

Lucifer appears on plate 73, the second to last of the 74 plates in this Berlin edition of 1870. Doré never married and lived with his mother after his father's death in 1849. He died of a heart attack in Paris in January 1883, at fifty-one, while working on illustrations for Shakespeare's plays.
