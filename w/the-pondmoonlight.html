The Pond—Moonlight

Edward Steichen · 1904 · Platinum print

Free to use

Steichen made the photograph in 1904 at Mamaroneck, New York, near the home of his friend the art critic Charles Caffin. He applied light-sensitive gums to the print by hand, which gave it more than one colour and made each print different. Three are known. Alfred Stieglitz owned one and reproduced it in Camera Work in 1906. In February 2006 another sold for $2.9 million, at that time the highest price paid for a photograph at auction.
