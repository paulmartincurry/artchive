Zahhak Is Told His Fate (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Sultan Muhammad · c. 1524 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 18.7 × 32.9 cm; page 31.9 × 47 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Met dates this leaf, folio 29 verso, to about 1524, the year Shah Ismail I died soon after work on the book had begun. The painting is attributed to Sultan Muhammad, a native of Tabriz. His son Mirza Ali also painted for the same manuscript, and Sultan Muhammad died before 1555.
