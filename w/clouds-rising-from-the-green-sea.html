Clouds Rising from the Green Sea (Water Album)

Ma Yuan · late 12th–early 13th century · Album leaf, ink on silk · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

Ma Yuan painted this sheet as part of a set of studies of water, now in the Palace Museum in Beijing. He was a favourite of Emperor Ningzong, who wrote several poems inspired by his paintings. Little else is known of his life beyond his death in 1225. His son Ma Lin rose to painter-in-waiting at court and was the last painter in a family that had served the Song emperors for four generations.
