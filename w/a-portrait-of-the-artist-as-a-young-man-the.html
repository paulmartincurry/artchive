To live, to err, to fall, to triumph, to recreate life out of life! A wild angel had appeared to him, the angel of mortal youth and beauty, an envoy from the fair courts of life, to throw open before him in an instant of ecstasy the gates of all the ways of error and glory.

A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man — The girl in midstream (Ch. 4)

James Joyce · 1916

Joyce entered University College in Dublin in 1898 to study English, French and Italian. His first publication, a review of a play by Henrik Ibsen, was printed in The Fortnightly Review in 1900, and he sent Ibsen a fan letter in Norwegian. He wrote a play of his own, A Brilliant Career, which he later destroyed. After graduating in 1902 he went to Paris to study medicine and had given up the plan by the end of January 1903.
