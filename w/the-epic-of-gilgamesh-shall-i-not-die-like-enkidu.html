Gilgamish bitterly wept for his comrade, (for) Enkidu, ranging
Over the desert: “I, too—shall I not die like Enkidu also?

The Epic of Gilgamesh — Shall I not die like Enkidu also? (Tablet IX)

Anonymous (Standard Babylonian) · c. 1200 BCE

Translated by R. Campbell Thompson, 1928

The Standard Babylonian version of the epic was compiled by Sîn-lēqi-unninni and dates to somewhere between the 13th and 10th centuries BCE. Some of the best copies were found in the ruins of the library of the Assyrian king Ashurbanipal.
