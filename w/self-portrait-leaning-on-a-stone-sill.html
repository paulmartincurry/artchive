Self-Portrait Leaning on a Stone Sill

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1639 · Etching, drypoint and burin; second state of two · plate: 20 x 16 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Rembrandt was about thirty-three. The pose draws on two Renaissance portraits, Titian's A Man with a Quilted Sleeve and Raphael's Baldassare Castiglione, and his painted self-portrait of the next year, now in London, takes a related pose. Because printing reverses the plate, the etching shows his face the right way round, as people saw him.
