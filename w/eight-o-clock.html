The Artchive
Strapped, noosed, nighing his hour,
He stood and counted them and cursed his luck;
And then the clock collected in the tower
Its strength, and struck.

Eight O'Clock

A. E. Housman · 1922

In the 1920s, when Moses Jackson, his roommate at Oxford in 1877–82, was dying in Canada, Housman compiled forty-two poems into Last Poems for him to read. The book came out in 1922.
