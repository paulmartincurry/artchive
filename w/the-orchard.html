The Orchard

Clarence H. White · 1902, printed 1905 · Photogravure · 20.5 × 15.7 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Clarence H. White was a bookkeeper at a wholesale grocery firm in Newark, Ohio, and entirely self-taught as a photographer. This photogravure was printed in 1905 for Camera Work, number 9. In 1908 Alfred Stieglitz gave a whole issue of the magazine to White and sixteen of his photographs. The print came to the museum from the estate of Julia McCune Flory.
