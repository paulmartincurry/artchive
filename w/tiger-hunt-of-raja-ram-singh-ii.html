Tiger Hunt of Raja Ram Singh II

Maker unknown · c. 1830–40 · Gum tempera, gold, and silver on paper · 64.8 × 48.9 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

One tiger appears three times in quick succession: lured out of the forest by a dead bull, driven to the river with drums, trumpets, arrows and muskets, and shot by the king from the prow of a boat. Ram Singh II ruled Kota from 1827 to 1866. According to the museum, a tiger killed this way was taken from the river and offered to a religious leader. The sheet is 64.8 by 48.9 centimetres, and John and Maxeen Flower left it to Cleveland in 2011.
