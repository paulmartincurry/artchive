Look in my face; my name is Might-have-been;
I am also called No-more, Too-late, Farewell;

A Superscription

Dante Gabriel Rossetti · 1869 (Published 1870)

Rossetti was a painter as well as a poet, and often wrote sonnets to go with his pictures. His family and friends called him Gabriel; in print he preferred Dante. He was forty-one in 1869. He had married Elizabeth Siddal, one of his models, in 1860, and she died in 1862.
