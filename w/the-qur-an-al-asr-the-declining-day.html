By the declining day,
Lo! man is in a state of loss,
Save those who believe and do good works, and exhort one another to truth and exhort one another to endurance.

The Qur'an — Al-ʿAṣr, The Declining Day (Surah 103)

The Qur'an (Surah Al-Asr) · 7th century CE; English 1930

Translated by Marmaduke Pickthall

Al-Asr has three verses and is the third shortest chapter of the Quran. It is counted among the earlier Meccan surahs. The jurist Imam Shafi held that if God had revealed only this surah it would have been enough for the guidance of all humankind, and one account says the Prophet's companions would not part from one another until one of them had recited it.
