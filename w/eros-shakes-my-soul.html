Now Eros shakes my soul, a wind on the mountain falling on the oaks.

Eros Shakes My Soul (fragment)

Sappho of Lesbos · ca. 600 BCE

Translated by Henry Thornton Wharton

Until the last quarter of the 19th century, Sappho's poetry was known only through quotations in the works of other ancient authors, and many surviving fragments contain only a single word. Her work was probably lost because there was too little demand to copy it onto parchment when codices replaced papyrus scrolls. Her Aeolic dialect, considered provincial, may have contributed. The legend that the church destroyed her poems appears to have begun in the Renaissance.
