The old Lie:
Dulce et decorum est
pro patria mori.

Dulce et Decorum Est

Wilfred Owen · 1917 (pub. 1920)

The earliest known manuscript is dated 8 October 1917 and is addressed to the poet's mother, Susan Owen. Wilfred Owen drafted the poem at Craiglockhart hospital near Edinburgh, where he had been sent that May with shell shock and where he met the poet Siegfried Sassoon. The first draft was dedicated to Jessie Pope, whose verse encouraged young men to join the war. Owen was killed in action in 1918, aged 25, and the poem was published in 1920.
