Edwin Aldrin Walks on the Lunar Surface (AS11-40-5903)

Neil Armstrong · 20 July 1969 · Photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

Neil Armstrong took this photograph of Buzz Aldrin with a 70 mm lunar surface camera, and Armstrong can be seen reflected in Aldrin's visor along with the lunar module Eagle. Aldrin stepped onto the Moon nineteen minutes after Armstrong, on 20 July 1969, and the two spent about two and a half hours walking on the surface. Aldrin was thirty-nine.
