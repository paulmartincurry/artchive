The Four Avenging Angels (The Apocalypse)

Albrecht Dürer · 1498 · Woodcut on laid paper · Block: 39.2 × 28.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The blocks for the Apocalypse were cut in pear wood. Each picture took a right-hand page, and the text of the Book of Revelation was printed on the back in two columns. This block measures 39.2 by 28.2 centimetres.
