What lips my lips have kissed, and where, and why,
I have forgotten, and what arms have lain
Under my head till morning.

What lips my lips have kissed

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1920 / 1923

Millay won the Pulitzer Prize for Poetry in 1923, the first woman to win it. The same year, at thirty-one, she married Eugen Jan Boissevain, a widower of forty-three who took over most of the running of the household. In 1925 the couple bought Steepletop, a former blueberry farm of 635 acres near Austerlitz, New York, and built a barn there from a Sears Roebuck kit, followed by a writing cabin.
