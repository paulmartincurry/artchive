At last he rose, and twitch'd his Mantle blew:
To morrow to fresh Woods, and Pastures new.

Lycidas (closing lines)

John Milton · 1637

Milton wrote the poem in 1637 for Edward King, a friend at Cambridge who drowned in August of that year when his ship sank in the Irish Sea off the coast of Wales. It first appeared in 1638 in a collection of elegies dedicated to King's memory.
