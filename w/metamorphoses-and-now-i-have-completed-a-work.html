And now I have completed a work, which neither the anger of Jove, nor fire, nor steel, nor consuming time will be able to destroy!

Metamorphoses — And Now I Have Completed a Work (Book XV, epilogue)

Ovid · c. 8 CE

Translated by Henry T. Riley

These are the closing lines of the poem. The emperor Augustus exiled Ovid to Tomis, on the Black Sea, and he stayed there for the last nine or ten years of his life. Ovid put his banishment down to a poem and a mistake, and was reluctant to say more.
