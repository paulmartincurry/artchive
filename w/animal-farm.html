ALL ANIMALS ARE EQUAL BUT SOME ANIMALS ARE MORE EQUAL THAN OTHERS

Animal Farm

George Orwell · 1945

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Orwell wrote the book between November 1943 and February 1944. In June 1944 the manuscript was almost lost when a German V-1 flying bomb destroyed his London flat, and he spent hours sifting through the rubble to find the pages. Four publishers refused it before Secker & Warburg published the first edition on 17 August 1945.
