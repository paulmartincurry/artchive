Crown Imperial (Fritillaria imperialis), from "Les Liliacées"

Pierre-Joseph Redouté · 1802–16 · Color stipple engraving · 54 × 35.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Pierre-Joseph Redouté left home at thirteen to earn a living as a travelling painter of interiors, portraits and church commissions, and came to Paris in 1782 to paint theatre scenery with his brother. Empress Joséphine became his patron in 1798. Most of his watercolours for Les Liliacées passed from her to her son, Eugène de Beauharnais.
