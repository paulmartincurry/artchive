Design for a Chair

Jean Démosthène Dugourc · 1790 · Brush and watercolor, grey wash, pen and black ink on white laid paper · 13.7 × 11.5cm (5 3/8 × 4 1/2in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Jean Démosthène Dugourc was forty-one when he drew this side chair, on a sheet only 13.7 by 11.5 centimetres. The paper carries part of a Pro Patria watermark. An unidentified mark, an N in a circle, is inked in blue on the front, and that mark probably dates from the time of the French Revolution. It was bought for the museum by its Advisory Council.
