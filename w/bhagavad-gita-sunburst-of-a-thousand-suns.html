If there should rise
Suddenly within the skies
Sunburst of a thousand suns
Flooding earth with beams undeemed-of,
Then might be that Holy One's
Majesty and radiance dreamed of!

Bhagavad Gita — Sunburst of a Thousand Suns (Chapter XI)

Vyasa (Sanskrit Epic) · 2nd–1st century BCE; English 1885

Translated by Edwin Arnold

In chapters 10 and 11 Krishna reveals himself to Arjuna as the Supreme Being and shows him his universal form, the Vishvarupa, which Arjuna sees with a divine vision Krishna gives him. J. Robert Oppenheimer is said to have recalled this verse at the Trinity nuclear test, and Robert Jungk took from it the title of his 1956 history of the atomic scientists, Brighter than a Thousand Suns.
