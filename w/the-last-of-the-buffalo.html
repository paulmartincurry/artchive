The Last of the Buffalo

Albert Bierstadt · 1888–89 · Oil on canvas · 180.3 × 301.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is Bierstadt's last large canvas, three metres wide, and it combines elements he had sketched on his expeditions to the West. His wife, Mary Stewart Bierstadt, acquired it by February 1909, and the Corcoran Gallery of Art received it from her that April. It passed to the National Gallery of Art in 2014.
