The Wind, frame at 1:10:32

Victor Sjöström · 1928 · Film with recorded score, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The film was Lillian Gish's idea. She chose Lars Hanson as her leading man after seeing him in a Swedish film with Greta Garbo, and she picked Victor Sjöström to direct, having worked with him on The Scarlet Letter. It was shot partly near Bakersfield and in the Mojave Desert, and it was the last silent film Gish starred in.
