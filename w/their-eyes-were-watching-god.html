Their Eyes Were Watching God

Zora Neale Hurston · 1937

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Hurston wrote the novel in Haiti in the span of seven weeks. She described herself as under internal pressure while writing it and wished she could write it again. Richard Wright condemned the book in a review for New Masses in 1937.
