This Is Worse (The Disasters of War, plate 37)

Francisco de Goya · 1810 (published 1863) · Etching, lavis, drypoint · Plate: 15.3 × 20.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The dead man on the tree is based in part on the Belvedere Torso, an ancient marble fragment of a male nude. Goya had made a black wash drawing of the statue on an earlier visit to Rome.
