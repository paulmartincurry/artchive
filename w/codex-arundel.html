Codex Arundel

Leonardo da Vinci · ca. 1480–1518 · Paper · British Library

Free to use

The codex holds 283 paper leaves, most about 22 by 16 centimetres, from every period of Leonardo da Vinci's working life. He kept one folded sheet to a subject, and later binders cut the sheets into pages and stacked them, so the order now looks random. It is named for the Earl of Arundel, who acquired it in Spain in the 1630s. His grandson gave it to the Royal Society in 1667, and the British Museum bought it in 1831 with 549 other Arundel manuscripts.
