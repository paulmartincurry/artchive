Krishna and His Family Admire a Solar Eclipse

First generation after Nainsukh · 1775–80 · Opaque watercolor on paper · 20 × 28.3 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The back of the page carries the personal seal of Abdur Rahman Chughtai, one of Pakistan's leading twentieth-century artists, who built up a large collection of Indian paintings. The painting is likely to have left Himachal Pradesh for Pakistan before 1972. After Chughtai's death in 1975 it passed to his son Arif, who sold it to Catherine Glynn Benkaim in 2008, and the Freer acquired it from her in 2017.
