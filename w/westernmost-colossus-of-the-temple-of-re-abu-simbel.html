Westernmost Colossus of the Temple of Re, Abu Simbel

Maxime Du Camp · 1850 · Salted paper print from paper negative · image 22.8 × 16.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Du Camp was an amateur who learned photography from Gustave Le Gray shortly before he left for Egypt in 1849. He made the journey in the company of Gustave Flaubert. This salted paper print from a paper negative is dated 1850.
