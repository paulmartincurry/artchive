Study for A Sunday on La Grande Jatte

Georges Seurat · 1884 · Oil on canvas · 27 3/4 × 41 in. (70.5 × 104.1 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Seurat began work on La Grande Jatte in the summer of 1884, when he was twenty-four, and made about sixty studies for it, spending much of two years sketching in the park. This study is a little over a metre wide; the finished painting is about three metres. It came to the museum in 1951 as a bequest from Sam A. Lewisohn.
