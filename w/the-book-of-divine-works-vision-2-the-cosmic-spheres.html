The Book of Divine Works: Vision 2, The Cosmic Spheres and Human Being

Maker unknown · c. 1210–1230 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · 39 × 25.5 cm · Biblioteca Statale di Lucca

Free to use

This is one of three transcriptions of the work and the only one that is illuminated. It has ten full-page miniatures, each marking the start of one of the ten visions.
