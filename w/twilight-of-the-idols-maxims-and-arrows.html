Twilight of the Idols — Maxims and Arrows

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1888 (pub. 1889)

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1911

The whole book was written in just over a week, between 26 August and 3 September 1888, while Nietzsche was on holiday in Sils Maria. This section is made up of 44 short maxims. The German title, Götzen-Dämmerung, is a pun on the title of Wagner's opera Götterdämmerung. The book was published in 1889, the year Nietzsche, aged 44, suffered a collapse and lost his mental faculties.
