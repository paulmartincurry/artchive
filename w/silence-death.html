Silence=Death

Silence=Death Project · 1986

Six men in New York, Avram Finkelstein, Brian Howard, Oliver Johnston, Charles Kreloff, Chris Lione and Jorge Socárras, formed the collective in 1985 during the AIDS crisis. In 1986 they made a poster of the phrase over a pink triangle, the badge forced on homosexuals in Nazi Germany. The first printing misspelled the names of two government agencies in its small print.
