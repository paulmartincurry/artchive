The Alchemist's Laboratory (Amphiteatrum Sapientiae Aeternae)

Attributed to Peter van der Doort, after Hans Vredeman de Vries · n.d. (book first published 1595) · Engraving · Diameter 41 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This round plate, 41 centimetres across, comes from Heinrich Khunrath's Amphitheatrum Sapientiae Aeternae, first published at Hamburg in 1595 with four circular plates, hand-coloured and heightened with gold and silver. Khunrath was a physician from Dresden who had met John Dee at Bremen in 1589. He died in 1605, and an expanded edition came out at Hanau in 1609; the Sorbonne condemned the book in 1625.
