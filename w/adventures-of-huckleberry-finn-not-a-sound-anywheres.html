Not a sound anywheres—perfectly still—just like the whole world was asleep, only sometimes the bullfrogs a-cluttering, maybe.

Adventures of Huckleberry Finn — Not a sound anywheres (Ch. XIX)

Mark Twain (Samuel Langhorne Clemens) · 1884

Twain composed the story in pen on notepaper between 1876 and 1883. He seems to have lost interest in the manuscript while it was in progress and set it aside for several years.
