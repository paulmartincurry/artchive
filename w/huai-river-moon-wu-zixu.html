Huai River Moon: Wu Zixu (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 30.9 × 19.7 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Wu Zixu, a Chinese general, talks to a fisherman sitting on the shore of the Huai River. According to the Library of Congress's record, the fisherman is telling him where the man who murdered his father is buried. The library numbers the print 22 in the series.
