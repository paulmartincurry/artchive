Iskandar and the Talking Tree (Great Mongol Shahnama)

Maker unknown · c. 1330 · Opaque watercolor, ink and gold on paper · 40.8 × 30.1 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The manuscript was probably planned with about 280 folios and 190 paintings in two volumes, and it is thought it was never finished. A seminar at Harvard in 1975 proposed that work began between November 1335 and May 1336 for Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad, son of the vizier Rashid al-Din, and probably stopped with his murder in May 1336, though the lack of evidence for both points has been noted.
