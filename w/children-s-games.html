Children's Games

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1560 · Oil on panel · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use

Bruegel painted the panel in 1560. Archduke Ernest of Austria acquired it in 1594, and Karel van Mander was the first to mention it, in 1604. It has been suggested that it was the first of a planned series on the Ages of Man; if so, it is unlikely the series went any further.
