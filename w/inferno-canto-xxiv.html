Inferno, Canto XXIV (Göttliche Komödie, plate 53)

Gustave Doré · published 1870 · Wood engraving · 29 cm (volume) · National Library of Poland, Warsaw

Free to use

Doré made more than 10,000 illustrations in his life. The most important were copied by electrotype and printed on cylinder presses, so that very large editions could come out in several countries at once. This is plate 53 in a Berlin edition of the Inferno from 1870.
