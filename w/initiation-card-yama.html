Initiation Card (Tsakalis): Yama

Maker unknown · early 15th century · Opaque watercolor on paper · 16 × 14.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Cards like this were painted in sets of six to nearly 100 and used in rituals, as initiation cards and in training monks. Before a temple was built, the ground could be marked with cards of protective deities mounted on small wooden sticks, and a card could also be carried in an amulet box for protection on a journey. This one is 16 by 14.5 centimetres. The Met bought it in 2000.
