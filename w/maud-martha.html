What she wanted was to donate to the world a good Maud Martha.

Maud Martha

Gwendolyn Brooks · 1953

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Maud Martha is a novel in thirty-four vignettes, set in Chicago, where Gwendolyn Brooks had lived since she was six weeks old. She published it in 1953, at about thirty-six. Three years earlier she had won the Pulitzer Prize for Poetry, the first African American to receive a Pulitzer. Her first job had been as a typist.
