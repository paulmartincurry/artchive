Part of the Milky Way (The Trouvelot Astronomical Drawings)

Étienne Léopold Trouvelot · 1881–82 · Chromolithograph · New York Public Library

Free to use

This view of the Milky Way came out of a study Trouvelot carried on through 1874, 1875 and 1876, while he was living in Medford, a suburb of Boston, and supporting his family as an artist and astronomer.
