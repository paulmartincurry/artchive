One must imagine Sisyphus happy.

The Myth of Sisyphus

Albert Camus · 1942 (English 1955)

Translated by Justin O'Brien · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Camus began this essay in 1940, at twenty-six, during the Fall of France, while millions of refugees were fleeing the advancing German armies. He was in Paris when the invasion came. The book came out in 1942, the same year as his novel The Stranger, and he dedicated it to Pascal Pia.
