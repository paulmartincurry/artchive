Ceremonial Garment (Naaxein) (Chilkat Blanket)

Maker unknown · late 1800s · Weft twining: cedar bark wrapped with mountain goat hair · 137.8 × 175.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Cedar bark wrapped with mountain goat hair, twined on a loom that has no bottom frame, so the warp threads hang freely. The weaver works in vertical sections, and a robe can take a year. Chiefs traditionally wore these robes at potlatches, and the long fringe sways when the wearer dances.
