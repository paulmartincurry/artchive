Glory be to God for dappled things--
For skies of couple-colour as a brinded cow;

Pied Beauty

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

Before he turned to poetry Hopkins meant to be a painter. An aunt, Maria Smith Giberne, taught him to sketch, and he went on drawing all his life. This poem of 1877 is a curtal sonnet, written in his own sprung rhythm. It stayed unpublished until 1918.
