Operating a Hand Drill at Vultee-Nashville

Alfred T. Palmer · February 1943 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Palmer photographed American industry for the Office of War Information in 1942 and 1943; this colour transparency dates from February 1943. The Vengeance dive bomber was ordered for the Royal Air Force in 1940. When production ended in 1944, 1,931 had been built, most of them at the Vultee plant in Nashville.
