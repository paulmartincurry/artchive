I sat drinking and did not notice the dusk,
Till falling petals filled the folds of my dress.
Drunken I rose and walked to the moonlit stream;
The birds were gone, and men also few.

Self-Abandonment

Li Bai · 8th century; English 1919

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

Li Bai was known by the nickname Wine Immortal. In Shandong he became one of the Six Idlers of the Bamboo Brook, an informal group dedicated to literature and wine. A well-known tale has it that he drowned when he reached from his boat to grasp the moon's reflection in the river while he was drunk.
