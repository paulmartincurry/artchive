Have the gates of death been opened unto thee? or hast thou seen the doors of the shadow of death?

Job 38:16–24

Book of Job (King James Version) · c. 6th-4th century BCE; KJV 1611

These verses belong to the first of God's two speeches to Job from the whirlwind, which runs from chapter 38 into chapter 40. Earlier in the book a storm is one of the causes of Job's suffering; here a storm is where God speaks from. In this stretch the questions move from the outer limits of the earth to light and darkness, then to snow and hail.
