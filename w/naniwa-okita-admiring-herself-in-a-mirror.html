Naniwa Okita Admiring Herself in a Mirror

Kitagawa Utamaro · ca. 1790–95 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper, mica ground · 14 1/2 × 9 7/8 in. (36.8 × 25.1 cm) · The Met

Free to use

In about 1791 Utamaro stopped designing prints for books and concentrated on single women shown half-length, at a time when other ukiyo-e artists favoured groups. The glittering mica dusted into the background was a device the Katsukawa school had popularised for actor prints. By 1793 his reputation was established, and he went on to make several series on the women of the Yoshiwara district. The Metropolitan Museum bought this impression in 1946.
