Le Stryge (The Vampire)

Charles Meryon · 1853 · Etching on Japanese laid paper · Plate: 17.1 × 12.9 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Meryon etched two lines of verse under the image and dated the plate 1853 in Roman numerals; he was thirty-one or thirty-two. This impression, on Japanese paper, was printed by A. Delâtre and is initialled by Meryon in pencil. An impression of the first state, the one with the verses, sold at auction for 5 pounds in 1873 and for 100 pounds in 1905. Lessing Rosenwald bought this sheet in New York in 1940.
