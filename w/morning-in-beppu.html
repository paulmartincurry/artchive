Morning in Beppu

Kawase Hasui · 1928 · Color woodblock print on paper · 38.8 × 25.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The Great Kantō earthquake of 1923 destroyed the workshop of Hasui's publisher, with finished woodblocks and 188 of his sketchbooks. Later that year he set off on a sketching trip that lasted 102 days, the longest of his life. Many of the sketches from it became the basis of later prints. This one dates from 1928.
