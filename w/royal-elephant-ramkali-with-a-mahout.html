Royal Elephant Ramkali with a Mahout

Maker unknown · c. 1761 · Gum tempera, ink, and gold on paper · Painting: 20.6 x 21.4 cm (8 1/8 x 8 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painting belongs to a series of the elephants in the royal stables at Udaipur, each named in the upper margin. Ramkali is also a popular nickname for young girls in northern India.
