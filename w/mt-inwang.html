Mt. Inwang (Inwangsan)

Jeong Seon · 18th century · Hanging scroll; ink and color on paper · The Met

The painting is about 91 centimetres tall and 50 wide, and came to the museum as a gift from Mary and Cheney Cowles in 2025. Jeong Seon's family was too poor for him to become a scholar-painter. On the recommendation of well-placed neighbours he worked for the Joseon Bureau of Paintings and painted landscapes for patrons, and between 1721 and 1745 he served as magistrate of three different districts.
