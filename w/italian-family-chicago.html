Italian Family, Chicago

Lewis Hine · 1910 · Gelatin silver print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hine had enrolled at the University of Chicago in 1900, ten years before he took this photograph in the city. After his death in 1940 his son Corydon gave his prints and negatives to the Photo League, which was dismantled in 1951. The Museum of Modern Art was offered the pictures and did not accept them; the George Eastman Museum did. The Art Institute acquired this print through an exchange with George Eastman House.
