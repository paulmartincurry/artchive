Comtesse de la Châtre (Marie Charlotte Louise Perrette Aglaé Bontemps)

Élisabeth Louise Vigée Le Brun · 1789 · Oil on canvas · 45 × 34 1/2 in. (114.3 × 87.6 cm) · The Met

Free to use

It was through the Comtesse de la Châtre and her family connections at court that Vigée Le Brun was introduced to Marie Antoinette, for whom she became a favourite portraitist. This was one of the last commissions she finished before she fled France in 1789. She was thirty-four. The sitter went into exile the following year and ended up in England.
