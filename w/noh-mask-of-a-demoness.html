Noh Mask of a Demoness (Hannya)

Ogawa Haritsu (Ritsuō) · 18th century · Matted painting; color on papier mache in relief, against a paper background · Diam. 27.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is not a mask to be worn. Ritsuō modelled it in relief in papier mâché, coloured it and set it against a paper background. The hannya mask of the Noh theatre represents a jealous female demon, with two sharp bull-like horns, metallic eyes and a leering mouth. The piece came to the Metropolitan Museum in 1929 in the bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer.
