This Breaks the Back of a Book!

Arlington Gregg · 1936–40 · Colour silkscreen · 35.6 × 28 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Arlington Gregg made this for the Illinois W.P.A. Art Project in Chicago, as a warning about the care of books. It is a colour silkscreen on board, 35.6 by 28 centimetres.
