Dragonfly Lamp with Mosaic and Turtleback Base

Clara Driscoll for Tiffany Studios · By 1906 · Favrile glass and bronze · 86.4 × 57.2 cm (34 × 22 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Louis Comfort Tiffany approved every pattern but made few lamps himself. Clara Driscoll, head of the Women's Glass Cutting Department, designed most of the firm's lamps between 1888 and her departure in 1908 or 1909, and at least eight dragonfly shades. The design was made until 1924, with the colours changed from one lamp to the next. This one is 86 centimetres tall, and the shade is stamped with its model number, 1507.
