The Peasant Wedding

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1568 · Oil on panel · Kunsthistorisches Museum

Free to use

The feast is set in a barn in summer, and the porters carry the dishes on a door taken off its hinges. Nobody has settled which figure is the groom, or whether he is in the picture at all. The servant in red at the right seems to have a third foot; when Bruegel's son copied the panel in 1620 he left it out. The painting is on wood, 114 centimetres high and 164 wide.
