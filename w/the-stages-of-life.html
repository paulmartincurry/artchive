The Stages of Life

Caspar David Friedrich · c. 1834–35 · Oil on canvas · 72.5 × 94 cm · Museum der bildenden Künste, Leipzig

Free to use

The shore is Utkiek, near Greifswald, the town where Friedrich was born. The figures have been identified as the painter and his family, and the two children hold a Swedish pennant: Greifswald had passed from Sweden to Prussia in 1815. He completed the picture five years before his death. The title was probably not his own.
