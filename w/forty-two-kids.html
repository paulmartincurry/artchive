Forty-two Kids

George Bellows · 1907 · Oil on canvas · 106.7 × 153 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Bellows sold this painting for $300 to Robert C. Hall of Pittsburgh while it hung in the Carnegie International exhibition of 1909. According to the Corcoran Gallery's records it was later found in storage at the Carnegie Institute. The Corcoran bought it in November 1931.
