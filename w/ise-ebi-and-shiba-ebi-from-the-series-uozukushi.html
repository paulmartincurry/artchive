Ise-ebi and Shiba-ebi, from the series Uozukushi (Every Variety of Fish)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1830s · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 25.3 × 36.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hiroshige had hoped to study under Toyokuni of the Utagawa school, who had too many pupils to take him, and a librarian introduced him to Toyohiro instead. By 1812 he was allowed to sign his work. When Toyohiro died in 1828 Hiroshige was offered the succession and declined it.
