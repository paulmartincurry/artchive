The small children wail for their own death
and the flesh roasted that their father fed upon.

Agamemnon

Aeschylus · 458 BCE

Translated by Richmond Lattimore · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Agamemnon is the first play of the Oresteia, the only connected trilogy by any Greek tragedian that survives. A satyr play, Proteus, followed the three tragedies. Aeschylus was in his late sixties and had fought the Persians at Marathon, Salamis and Plataea. He died in Sicily about two years afterwards. Of his seventy to ninety plays, seven survive whole.
