Song of Songs 2:8-13

Song of Solomon (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · c. 3rd century BCE; KJV 1611

Many Jews read the Song of Songs aloud at Passover, a custom first recorded in the 8th century. There is a tradition that it was nearly left out of the Hebrew Bible. Rabbi Akiva, in the first and second centuries, defended its place there and forbade the singing of it in wine taverns.
