The Torment of Unbelievers and Heretics (Les Visions du chevalier Tondal, folio 14v, detail)

Simon Marmion · 1475 · Tempera colors, gold leaf, gold paint, and ink on parchment · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Margaret of York, Duchess of Burgundy, commissioned the manuscript in 1475. David Aubert wrote out the text in Ghent, and Simon Marmion painted its 20 miniatures in Valenciennes, where he was based.
