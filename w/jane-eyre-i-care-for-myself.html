I care for myself. The more solitary, the more friendless, the more unsustained I am, the more I will respect myself.

Jane Eyre — I care for myself

Charlotte Brontë · 1847

In 1842 Charlotte Brontë went with Emily to the Pensionnat Heger in Brussels, teaching English in return for board and lodging. Her attachment to her tutor, Constantin Heger, a married man, grew into an unrequited passion, and she went home in January 1844. Heger tried to destroy the letters she sent him afterwards, but his wife retrieved them, and The Times printed four of them in 1913.
