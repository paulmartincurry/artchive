Hereford Mappa Mundi

Attributed to Richard of Haldingham · ca. 1300 · Vellum · Hereford Cathedral

Free to use

No larger medieval map is known to survive. It was the central panel of a triptych whose side panels, an angel Gabriel and a Virgin, are lost. During the Commonwealth it was hidden under the floor of a chantry in the cathedral, and in 1855 it was cleaned and repaired at the British Museum. Hereford itself has nearly disappeared from the map, presumably from repeated touching. The cathedral proposed to sell it in 1988, and donations kept it there and paid for the building that has housed it since 1996.
