Faust, frame at 25:33

F. W. Murnau · 1926 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

A short sequence of the contract being written in fire on parchment took a whole day to film. Any book or plan with writing on it was shot twice, once in German and once in English for the American version. The film took six months and cost 2 million Reichsmarks, of which only half came back at the box office. It was Murnau's last German film; by the Berlin premiere he was already shooting in Hollywood.
