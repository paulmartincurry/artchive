Alexander Mosaic

Maker unknown · c. 120–100 BC · Mosaic · 272 × 513 cm · National Archaeological Museum, Naples

Free to use

The mosaic was a floor in the House of the Faun at Pompeii, laid where the owner received guests. It is made of roughly one and a half million tiny tiles in reds, yellows, black and white, and is believed to copy a Greek painting of the late fourth or early third century BC. It measures 272 by 513 centimetres. It was rediscovered in 1831 and moved to Naples in September 1843.
