The Wind

Félix Vallotton · 1910 · Oil on canvas · 89.2 × 116.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Vallotton signed and dated this canvas at the lower left in 1910, when he was forty-four. His later landscapes were painted from memory. Richard Bühler of Winterthur acquired the picture in 1915, and in 1967 it was sold to Mr. and Mrs. Paul Mellon, who gave it to the National Gallery of Art in 1985.
