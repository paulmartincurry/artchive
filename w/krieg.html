Krieg (On Death, Part II)

Max Klinger · undated · Plate: 51.6 × 33.7 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Metropolitan Museum's record gives no date for this plate. It is nearly 52 centimetres high and 34 wide, and it came to the museum in 1952 as the gift of Dr. F. H. Hirschland. Klinger was born in Leipzig to a wealthy and prominent family, and sculpture slowly came to dominate his output in his later years.
