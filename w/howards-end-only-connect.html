Only connect! That was the whole of her sermon. Only connect the prose and the passion, and both will be exalted, and human love will be seen at its height. Live in fragments no longer.

Howards End — Only connect! (Ch. XXII)

E. M. Forster · 1910

Forster conceived the book in June 1908, worked on it throughout the following year and completed it in July 1910. It was published on 18 October 1910.
