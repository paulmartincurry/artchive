Celtic Head

Maker unknown · 100–300 CE · Sandstone with traces of original red paint · 30 x 31 x 24 cm (11 13/16 x 12 3/16 x 9 7/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The head was never attached to a body. The Celts venerated the human head, and stone heads like this survive in large numbers in the uplands of northern England, where British Celts made this one under Roman rule.
