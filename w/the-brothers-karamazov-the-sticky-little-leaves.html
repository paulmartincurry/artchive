Though I may not believe in the order of the universe, yet I love the sticky little leaves as they open in spring.

The Brothers Karamazov — the sticky little leaves (Book V, Ch. III)

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1880

Translated by Constance Garnett

Dostoevsky began his first notes for the novel in April 1878. It also took in themes from an unfinished project he had begun in 1869, The Life of a Great Sinner.
