Ceremonial Board (Malu)

Maker unknown · 19th century · Wood · 190.5 × 61 × 16.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This Sawos ceremonial board was carved in wood in the nineteenth century and stands 190.5 centimetres tall. Nelson A. Rockefeller gave it to the Michael C. Rockefeller Memorial Collection in 1969.
