The Artchive

Mirror with Interlacing Floral Motifs and Twelve Zodiac Animals

Maker unknown · late 500s–early 600s · Bronze · Diameter: 15.4 cm (6 1/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

An inscription in the inner band reads, in translation: The mirror's light shows the man inside; may you live long and renew yourself. The twelve animals of the zodiac run clockwise in the outer band.
