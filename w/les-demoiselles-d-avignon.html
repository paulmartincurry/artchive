Les Demoiselles d'Avignon

Pablo Picasso · 1907 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Pablo Picasso made hundreds of sketches and studies for this painting, which is more than 2.4 metres tall. He painted it in his studio in the Bateau-Lavoir in Montmartre, and it stayed in his studio for many years. The public first saw it in July 1916, nine years after it was painted. The title came from the poet André Salmon, who organised that exhibition; Picasso never liked it. Afterwards the canvas was rolled up, and in 1924 he sold it to the designer Jacques Doucet for 25,000 francs.
