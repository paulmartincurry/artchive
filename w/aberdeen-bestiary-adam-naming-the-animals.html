Aberdeen Bestiary: Adam Naming the Animals

Maker unknown · ca. 1200 · Pigment and gold leaf on parchment · University of Aberdeen

Free to use

This page, folio 5 recto, opens the bestiary section of the manuscript, with Adam surrounded by gold leaf. Seventeen of the book's pages are pricked with small holes so that the pictures could be copied into another manuscript. The painter left small colour notes in the margins. The book was first listed in 1542 in the inventory of the Old Royal Library at Westminster and carries the shelf stamp of Henry VIII; how he came by it is not known.
