The Small Crucifixion

Matthias Grünewald · c. 1511/1520 · Oil on panel · 61.3 × 46 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Only ten paintings by Grünewald survive, and apart from this one and a Crucifixion in Basel they are all in Germany. Its first recorded owner was Duke Wilhelm V of Bavaria, who died in 1597. Joachim von Sandrart wrote in 1675 that the duke loved the little picture without knowing who had painted it.
