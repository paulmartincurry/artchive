Stag Beetle

Albrecht Dürer · 1505 · Watercolour and gouache · J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Dürer dated and signed this sheet 1505 at lower left, when he was in his mid-thirties, and painted the beetle in watercolour and gouache. The upper left corner of the paper is a later addition, and the tip of the beetle's left antenna was painted in by another hand.
