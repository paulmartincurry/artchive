Man Mocked by Two Women

Francisco de Goya · c. 1820–1823 · Oil on plaster transferred to linen · 125.4 × 65.4 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Goya was 75 when he made this mural. He began each of the Black Paintings with a thick layer of black and worked the figures over it in lighter whites, greys, blues and greens. X-rays of this one show the front figure's hand was moved, and the two women may once have been reading a book resting on a man's knees.
