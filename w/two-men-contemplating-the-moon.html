Two Men Contemplating the Moon

Caspar David Friedrich · ca. 1825–30 · Oil on canvas · 13 3/4 × 17 1/4 in. (34.9 × 43.8 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The canvas is small, 35 by 44 centimetres, and Friedrich painted it in his fifties in Dresden. The Saxon Academy had made him a member in 1818 with a yearly payment of 150 thalers, but the full professorship he hoped for never came. His work fell from favour in his later years and he died in obscurity in 1840. Interest returned in 1906, when thirty-two of his paintings were shown in Berlin.
