Oh, how the gorgeous sunlight gets to one’s head here in the country! I do not doubt but what it can drive a man a little crazy.

Letter to Émile Bernard on Half a Dozen Sunflowers

Vincent van Gogh · 1888

Translated by Anthony M. Ludovici, 1912

Before Gauguin came to stay, Van Gogh painted four versions of Sunflowers in one week to decorate the Yellow House, and hung them in the guest room. Gauguin arrived in Arles on 23 October 1888 and later acquired two of the earlier Paris versions. Van Gogh wrote 22 letters to Émile Bernard over about a year from 1888, more about technique and colour than his letters to Theo.
