Monks Mound, Cahokia

Maker unknown · ca. 950–1100 · Earth (soil and clay)

The mound is made of soil and clay carried to the site basket by basket, some 22 million cubic feet of it, and rises in four terraces to about 100 feet. Work began around 900 to 950 and the summit was finished around 1100; the sides were already slumping while it was being built. A wooden building more than 100 feet long stood on top. The name comes from French Trappist monks who lived on a smaller mound nearby from 1809 to 1813 and grew wheat on its terraces.
