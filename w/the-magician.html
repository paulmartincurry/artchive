The Magician (Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot)

Pamela Colman Smith · 1910 printing (first published 1909)

Free to use

Smith was born in London to an American father and spent part of her childhood in Jamaica. At fifteen she enrolled at the Pratt Institute in Brooklyn, where she studied under Arthur Wesley Dow, and she left in 1897 without a degree to work as an illustrator. The card is number one of the twenty-two Major Arcana.
