Federal Theatre for Youth: A Christmas Carol

Maker unknown · 1936–41 · Colour silkscreen · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Works Progress Administration in Ohio put on A Christmas Carol as part of its Federal Theatre for youth, and the Federal Art Project printed the poster on board.
