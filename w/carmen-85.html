I hate and love. And why, perhaps you’ll ask.
I don’t know: but I feel, and I’m tormented.

Carmen 85 (I Hate and Love)

Catullus · c. 55 BCE

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

No ancient biography of Catullus has survived, and the dates of his birth and death are uncertain; Jerome stated that he died in Rome in his 30th year. His poems come down as an anthology of 116. The text used today is based on a manuscript that surfaced in Verona in 1305 and disappeared again after two copies were made. One of those copies is now in the Bodleian Library, Oxford.
