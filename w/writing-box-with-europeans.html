Writing Box (Suzuribako) with Europeans

Maker unknown · 1800s · lacquered wood with gold maki-e, silver inlay and red lacquer on black ground · 23 × 20.2 cm (9 1/16 × 7 15/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The two figures on the left of the lid are Portuguese traders, drawn in balloon-shaped trousers, and the man facing them is a Jesuit priest in a long robe. Portuguese ships reached Japan in the 1540s, and pictures first made to record what the foreigners looked like lasted as decoration on later objects such as this box. The lid measures 23 by 20.2 centimetres. The museum bought the box from Robert Rousset of New York.
