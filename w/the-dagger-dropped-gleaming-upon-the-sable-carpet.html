The Dagger Dropped Gleaming upon the Sable Carpet (The Masque of the Red Death)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Free to use

Poe earned 12 dollars when this story first appeared, in Graham's Magazine in May 1842, as The Mask of the Red Death, with the tagline A Fantasy; the now-standard title came with a revised version in 1845. In the story the last room is lit by a scarlet light cast from stained glass windows. Clarke was a stained-glass artist as well as an illustrator, and produced more than 130 windows.
