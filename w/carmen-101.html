The Artchive
take them, wet with many tears of a brother, and for ever, O my brother, hail and farewell!

Carmen 101 (Ave atque Vale)

Catullus · ca. 57 BCE

Translated by F. W. Cornish, 1913

From the summer of 57 BC to the summer of 56 BC Catullus served in Bithynia on the staff of the commander Gaius Memmius. While he was in the East he travelled to the Troad to perform rites at his brother's tomb, and this poem records the visit. The American composer Ned Rorem set it for voice and piano.
