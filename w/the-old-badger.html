The Old Badger (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889 · Color woodblock print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This impression belonged to Bruce Goff of Tyler, Texas, who died in 1982. His estate passed it through Shin'enKan, Inc., which gave it to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1990 with the rest of the Bruce Goff Archive. Yoshitoshi published the sheet in 1889.
