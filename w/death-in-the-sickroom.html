Death in the Sickroom

Edvard Munch · 1896 · Lithograph in black ink on lightweight cream Japanese paper · Image: 41.5 × 55.9 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The subject is the death of Munch's sister Sophie, who died of tuberculosis in 1877 at the age of fifteen. Their mother had died of the same disease in 1868. Munch made this lithograph in 1896, nineteen years after his sister's death, and returned to the scene in many later variations.
