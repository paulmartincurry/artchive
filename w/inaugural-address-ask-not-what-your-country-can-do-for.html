And so, my fellow Americans: ask not what your country can do for you—ask what you can do for your country.

Inaugural Address — Ask not what your country can do for you

John F. Kennedy · January 20, 1961

Kennedy began constructing the speech in late November, working from a speech file kept by his secretary and asking friends and advisors for suggestions. He wrote in longhand on a yellow legal pad, and aides recounted that every sentence was worked, reworked and reduced. He delivered it on January 20, 1961. Less than six weeks later he issued the executive order establishing the Peace Corps.
