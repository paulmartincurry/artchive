Kyōsai's Treatise on Painting (Kyōsai gadan)

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1887 · Set of four woodblock printed books; ink and color on paper · 25.3 × 17.5 cm (each) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kyōsai wrote this book about his own art and life, half autobiography and half painting manual. It was printed from woodblocks as a set of four volumes in 1887, two years before his death.
