Saint Francis in Meditation

Francisco de Zurbarán · 1635–39 · Oil on canvas · 152 × 99 cm · The National Gallery, London

Free to use

In 1629 the elders of Seville invited Zurbarán to move to the city for good, and he came with his wife, the three children of his first marriage, a relative and eight servants. This canvas is dated between 1635 and 1639. The National Gallery in London bought it in 1853.
