Reader, I married him. A quiet wedding we had: he and I, the parson and clerk, were alone present.

Jane Eyre — Reader, I married him

Charlotte Brontë · 1847

Charlotte Brontë married her father's curate, Arthur Bell Nicholls, on 29 June 1854, after first refusing him. Her father decided at the last minute that he could not give her away, so her former teacher, Miss Wooler, did. She became pregnant soon after the wedding and died, with her unborn child, on 31 March 1855, three weeks before her thirty-ninth birthday.
