The Wounded Angel

Hugo Simberg · 1903 · Oil on canvas · Ateneum Art Museum, Helsinki

Free to use

In the autumn of 1902 Simberg fell ill with meningitis and spent much of the winter in bed, and he painted this canvas after he recovered. The road is a real one, in Eläintarha park in Helsinki, with Töölönlahti Bay behind; the park then held many charity institutions, and the boys are heading towards the Blind Girls' School. The canvas, 127 by 154 centimetres, passed a strict jury for the Ateneum autumn exhibition of 1903. In 2006 a public vote held by the Ateneum named it Finland's national painting.
