Gandhi picking up salt at Dandi, end of the Salt March

Photographer unknown · April 1930

Free to use

The Salt March ran for 24 days in 1930, about 387 kilometres from Sabarmati Ashram to the sea at Dandi, against the British salt monopoly. When Gandhi broke the salt laws on the beach, millions of Indians followed with acts of civil disobedience. Behind him are his son Manilal and Mithuben Petit.
