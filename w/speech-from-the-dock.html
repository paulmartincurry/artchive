Let no man write my epitaph … When my country takes her place among the nations of the earth, then and not till then, let my epitaph be written. I have done.

Speech from the Dock

Robert Emmet · September 19, 1803

Emmet was twenty-five and on trial for the rising he had led in Dublin on the evening of 23 July 1803, which failed. He was hanged on 20 September, the day after the date this speech carries. Part of the money for the rising had been a legacy of £2,000 from his father, who was physician to the Lord Lieutenant. A watercolour miniature of him was made during the trial.
