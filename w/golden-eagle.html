Golden Eagle (The Birds of America, Plate 181)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1833 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

For a large bird such as an eagle, Audubon could spend up to four fifteen-hour days preparing, studying and drawing the specimen. Every bird in the book is shown life size, which is why the larger ones are often bent to fit the page.
