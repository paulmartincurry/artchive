Finally, good painting is a music and a melody which intellect only can appreciate, and with great difficulty.

Dialogues on Painting — A music and a melody (First Dialogue; words given to Michelangelo)

Francisco de Holanda · c. 1538–1548

Translated by Charles Holroyd, 1903

Francisco de Holanda was born in Lisbon in 1517 and began his career as an illuminator at the age of 20. He studied in Italy between 1538 and 1547, where he frequented the circle of Vittoria Colonna, who gave him access to Michelangelo.
