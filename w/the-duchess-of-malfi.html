I am Duchess of Malfi still.
… Pull, and pull strongly, for your able strength must pull down heaven upon me.

The Duchess of Malfi (Act IV, Scene II)

John Webster · 1612–13

John Webster was the son of a London carriage maker. He wrote the play for the King's Men, who gave it first at the indoor Blackfriars Theatre and later at the Globe, with Richard Burbage as Ferdinand. Robert Johnson, a regular composer for Blackfriars, set the madmen's song in Act 4 to music. The story follows a real Duchess of Amalfi, Giovanna d'Aragona, who secretly married Antonio Beccadelli di Bologna after her first husband died. The text was printed in 1623.
