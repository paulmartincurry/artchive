Nude Descending a Staircase, No. 2

Marcel Duchamp · 1912 · Oil on canvas · Philadelphia Museum of Art

Free to use

Duchamp entered the painting for the 1912 Salon des Indépendants in Paris, and the hanging committee sent his own two brothers to ask him to withdraw it or change the title. By his account he said nothing, went to the show and took it home in a taxi. It was first exhibited in Barcelona that spring. At the 1913 Armory Show in New York a San Francisco lawyer, Frederic C. Torrey, bought it; in 1919 he had a full-size copy made and sold the original to Louise and Walter Arensberg, who left it to the Philadelphia museum in 1954.
