Flower Clouds

Odilon Redon · c. 1903 · Pastel, with touches of stumping, incising, and brushwork, on blue-gray wove paper with multi-colored fibers altered to tan, perimeter mounted to cardboard · Art Institute of Chicago

Redon sold this pastel to Charles Waltner of Paris in February 1904. It was shown at the Salon d'automne in 1905 under the title Marine, and in 1920 as Barque au soleil couchant. For years he had worked only in black and white before he took up pastel. The sheet is 44.5 by 54.2 centimetres, and the Art Institute bought it from a New York dealer in 1990.
