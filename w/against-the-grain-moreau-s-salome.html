She became, in a sense, the symbolic deity of indestructible lust, the goddess of immortal Hysteria, of accursed Beauty, distinguished from all others by the catalepsy which stiffens her flesh and hardens her muscles;

Against the Grain — Moreau's Salome

Joris-Karl Huysmans · 1884

Translated by John Howard

Huysmans wrote this novel on a modest civil-service salary and partly based its hero on himself. In a letter of November 1882 he told Émile Zola that he had embarked on a wild and gloomy fantasy.
