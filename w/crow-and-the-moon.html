Crow and the Moon

Kawanabe Kyōsai · c. 1887 · Album leaf; ink and color on silk · 36.2 × 27.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This crow is a single album leaf, painted in ink and colour on silk around 1887, when Kyōsai was in his mid-fifties. The leaf is 36.2 centimetres tall. It came to the Met in 1914 with the Charles Stewart Smith Collection, given in his memory.
