Guernica

Pablo Picasso · 1937 · Oil on canvas · Museo Reina Sofía, Madrid

In copyright. Look and learn: shown for study, not for download.

Thirty-five days of work went into this canvas, which Picasso finished on 4 June 1937. The Spanish Republican government had commissioned a large work from him in January for its pavilion at the Paris exposition, and he dropped his first idea on 1 May after reading an eyewitness account of the bombing. The paint is a matte house paint made at his request to have the least possible gloss. He asked the Museum of Modern Art to keep the painting until liberty and democracy were established in Spain, and it stayed there until 1981.
