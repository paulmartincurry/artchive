Capitoline Wolf

Maker unknown · 5th century BC or 11th–12th century AD (wolf); late 15th century (twins) · Bronze · 75 × 114 cm · Capitoline Museums, Rome

Free to use

The wolf was long taken for an Etruscan bronze of the fifth century BC. Radiocarbon and thermoluminescence dating now indicate that it may have been cast between 1021 and 1153, a result that is contested, and there is no agreed date. Anna Maria Carruba, who restored it in 1997, saw that it had been cast in a single piece. The twins were added in the late 15th century, probably by Antonio del Pollaiuolo. The sculpture has been kept in the Palazzo dei Conservatori in Rome since 1471.
