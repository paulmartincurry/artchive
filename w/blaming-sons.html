White hair covers my temples,
I am wrinkled and seared beyond repair,
And though I have got five sons,
They all hate paper and brush.

Blaming Sons

Tao Yuanming (Tao Qian) · early 5th century

Translated by Arthur Waley

Tao Yuanming spent more than ten years in government service, in five separate stints, before withdrawing to the countryside, where he spent much of his life farming, reading and drinking wine. His father had died when he was eight. He owned a guqin with no strings, saying he did not need to hear the strings to value the instrument.
