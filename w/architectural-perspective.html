Architectural Perspective

Hans Vredeman de Vries · 16th century · Etching

Free to use

Born in Leeuwarden in 1527, Hans Vredeman de Vries became a specialist in perspective by studying Vitruvius and Sebastiano Serlio. In Antwerp he was appointed city architect and fortification engineer. He was a Protestant, and after 1585 he fled the city because of the Spanish occupation, working afterwards in Wolfenbüttel, Hamburg, Danzig, Prague and Amsterdam. His book on perspective came out in 1604.
