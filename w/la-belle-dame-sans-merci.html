O what can ail thee, knight-at-arms,
Alone and palely loitering?

La Belle Dame sans Merci

John Keats · 1819

The first version sits inside a long letter that Keats wrote between 14 February and 3 May 1819 to his brother and sister-in-law in America, and he revised the lines as he copied them out. A second version followed in 1820. He took the title from a fifteenth-century poem by Alain Chartier. Rachel Carson used two lines from the first verse as the epigraph to Silent Spring in 1962.
