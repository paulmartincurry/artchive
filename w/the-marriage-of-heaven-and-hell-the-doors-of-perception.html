If the doors of perception were cleansed everything would appear to man as it is, infinite.

For man has closed himself up, till he sees all things through narrow chinks of his cavern.

The Marriage of Heaven and Hell — The Doors of Perception

William Blake · 1790–1793

Aldous Huxley took the title of his book The Doors of Perception from The Marriage of Heaven and Hell, and Huxley's book in turn gave the American rock band The Doors its name. C. S. Lewis wrote The Great Divorce in response to Blake's book. Apart from its opening Argument and its closing Song of Liberty, Blake wrote the whole book in prose.
