Defeat, my Defeat, my solitude and my aloofness;
You are dearer to me than a thousand triumphs,
And sweeter to my heart than all world-glory.

The Madman — Defeat

Kahlil Gibran · 1918

Defeat is from The Madman, Gibran's first book written in English. The poet May Ziadeh, his correspondent since 1912, reviewed the book in Al-Hilal, a magazine in Egypt. Mary Haskell, his benefactress, had paid for him to study art in Paris from 1908 to 1910.
