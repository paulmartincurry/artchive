One short sleep past, we wake eternally
And death shall be no more; Death, thou shalt die.

Holy Sonnet 10: Death, be not proud

John Donne · ca. 1609 (pub. 1633)

Donne never printed the Holy Sonnets. He passed them among friends in manuscript, and they were published in 1633, two years after his death. Scholars date most of them to 1609 and 1610, years when he was poor, unwell and moving from the Roman Catholic faith he was born into towards the Church of England. He was ordained in 1615, unwillingly, at the king's insistence. In August 1945 Benjamin Britten set nine of the sonnets for voice and piano, placing this one last.
