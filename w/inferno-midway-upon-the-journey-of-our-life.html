Midway upon the journey of our life
I found myself within a forest dark,
For the straightforward pathway had been lost.

Inferno — Midway upon the journey of our life (Canto I, opening)

Dante Alighieri · c. 1306–1310

Translated by Henry Wadsworth Longfellow

Dante wrote the Inferno in exile, after his faction lost power in Florence in 1302 and he was banished. Many scholars date it to between 1306 and 1310, though he kept revising. He wrote it in his own Tuscan dialect rather than Latin. The poem opens in spring 1300, when its narrator is thirty-five, roughly Dante's own age that year. The Inferno circulated in pieces for years, quoted as early as 1315, and the whole Comedy came out only after his death in Ravenna in 1321.
