Gutenberg Bible

Johannes Gutenberg · ca. 1454–1455 · Printed book on paper · New York Public Library

A complete copy has 1,288 pages and took 322 sheets of linen paper, thought to have been imported from Caselle in Piedmont. Gutenberg developed an oil-based ink, closer to a varnish, that would stick to his metal type. The first pages printed have 40 lines; he then raised the count to 42 by tightening the space between lines, presumably to save paper. Some copies sold for 30 florins, about three years' wages for a clerk. The New York Public Library's copy was the first to reach North America, in 1847.
