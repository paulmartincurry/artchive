Bear and Elf (Intermezzi, Plate 1)

Max Klinger · published 1881 · Etching and aquatint · Plate: 41.7 × 28.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is the first plate of Intermezzi, Klinger's Opus IV, published in 1881. In that year he published two sets of etchings, among them Paraphrase on the Finding of a Glove. He signed the plate at the lower left and numbered it I at the lower right. The Epstein family gave this impression to the National Gallery of Art in 2001.
