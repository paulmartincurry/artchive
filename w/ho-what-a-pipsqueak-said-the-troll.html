Ho, What a Pipsqueak, Said the Troll

John Bauer · 1912 · Drawing · Nationalmuseum, Stockholm

Free to use

Bauer was sixteen when the Royal Swedish Academy of Arts turned him down as too young; he got in at eighteen, one of three students admitted in 1900. The bent knives, shoes, spears, pots and belts he had sketched among the Sami in Lappland became part of the clothes and ornaments of his trolls. He relied on his parents for money all his life.
