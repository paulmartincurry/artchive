La Nuit à Souchez

Théophile Alexandre Steinlen · 1917 · Etching · 60 × 50 cm (platemark) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Steinlen made this etching in 1917, and it was printed in an edition of ten; the Library of Congress holds number 2. Souchez is a village in the Pas-de-Calais, about three kilometres from the Canadian memorial at Vimy Ridge. Between 1883 and 1920 Steinlen made hundreds of illustrations, some under a pseudonym to avoid political trouble.
