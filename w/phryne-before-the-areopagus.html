Phryne before the Areopagus

Jean-Léon Gérôme · 1861 · Oil on canvas · 80 × 128 cm · Hamburger Kunsthalle, Hamburg

Free to use

The painting shows the courtesan Phryne on trial for impiety in ancient Athens; legend says she was acquitted after her defender pulled away her robe before the judges. Gérôme showed it at the 1861 Salon.
