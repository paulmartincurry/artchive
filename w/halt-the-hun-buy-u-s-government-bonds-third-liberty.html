Halt the Hun! Buy U.S. Government Bonds, Third Liberty Loan

Henry Patrick Raleigh · 1918 · Color lithograph on cream wove paper · 74.8 × 50.6 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Third Liberty Loan opened on 5 April 1918, offering $4.1 billion in bonds at 4.15 percent. Nine million posters were produced and distributed for it, along with five million window stickers and ten million buttons. Henry Patrick Raleigh, born in 1880, drew this one.
