The Apotheosis of War

Vasily Vereshchagin · 1871 · Oil on canvas · State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

Vereshchagin had gone to Central Asia with the Russian army and was decorated for his part in the siege of Samarkand in June 1868; the city appears at far right. He painted this canvas, 127 by 197 centimetres, in his studio in Munich, and wrote a dedication to conquerors on the frame. It was refused a showing at his St Petersburg exhibition of 1874, and in Germany Field Marshal von Moltke ordered that German soldiers be barred from seeing it.
