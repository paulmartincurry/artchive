I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings

Maya Angelou · 1969

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Angelou was forty when she wrote the book, and had worked as a singer, actor, journalist and civil rights organiser. She refused at first when Robert Loomis of Random House asked for it, since she thought of herself as a poet and playwright. She wrote in a hotel room with the pictures taken off the walls, lying on the bed with yellow legal pads, and cut ten or twelve pages a day down to three or four in the evening. The title is from a poem by Paul Laurence Dunbar and was suggested by the jazz singer Abbey Lincoln.
