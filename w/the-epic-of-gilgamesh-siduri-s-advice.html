The Epic of Gilgamesh — Siduri's Advice

Anonymous (Old Babylonian) · ca. 1800 BCE

Translated by Andrew George, 1999 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The Old Babylonian tablets, from about 1800 BCE, are the earliest that tell a single Gilgamesh story, and only a few of them have survived. The epic was unknown to modern readers until about 15,000 fragments of cuneiform tablets were found at Nineveh in the early 1850s. The British Museum hired George Smith to study them, and he read the first translated fragments in public in 1872.
