Boating in Moonlight

Maker unknown · 1600s · Album leaf; ink on silk · Image: 30 × 26.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The painter of this small Joseon leaf, ink on silk about 30 by 26 centimetres, is not known. A boat trip under a cliff by moonlight follows the tradition of illustrating the Red Cliff poems of the Song poet Su Shi. Packed into the boat are a potted plant, a book, a lute, a tiered lunch box, a teapot and a portable stove.
