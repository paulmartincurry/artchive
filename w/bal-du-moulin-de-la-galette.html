Bal du moulin de la Galette

Pierre-Auguste Renoir · 1876 · Oil on canvas · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Renoir took an abandoned cottage in the rue Cortot as a studio to be near the dance hall. His friend Georges Rivière recorded that the picture was painted on the spot, with the wind threatening to blow the canvas away. Fellow painters and Rivière himself sat for the group at the table. A smaller version exists, and it is not known which of the two came first. The painter Gustave Caillebotte owned this one from 1879 until his death, when it went to the French state in payment of death duties.
