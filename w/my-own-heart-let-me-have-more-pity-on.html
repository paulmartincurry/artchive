My own heart let me have more pity on; let
Me live to my sad self hereafter kind,
Charitable; not live this tormented mind
With this tormented mind tormenting yet.

My own heart let me have more pity on

Gerard Manley Hopkins · published 1918

Hopkins spent the last five years of his life in Dublin, where he worked as a professor of Latin and Greek. He died of typhoid fever in 1889. Robert Bridges, a friend from Balliol College, Oxford, edited the first edition of his poems, which appeared in 1918.
