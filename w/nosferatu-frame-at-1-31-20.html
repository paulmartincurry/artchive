Nosferatu, frame at 1:31:20

F. W. Murnau · 1922 · Silent film, tinted in this copy (frame)

Free to use

Murnau rewrote twelve pages of the script himself for the final scene, in which the vampire dies in the first rays of the sun. No earlier film had shown a vampire killed by sunlight. There was only one camera on the shoot, and so only one original negative. Stoker's widow Florence sued, and a court ordered every copy destroyed, but several prints survived. Prana Film went bankrupt soon after the release, and this was the only film it ever made.
