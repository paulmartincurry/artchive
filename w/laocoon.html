Laocoön

El Greco · c. 1610–14 · Oil on canvas · 137.5 × 172.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The inventory of El Greco's estate in 1614 listed three paintings of Laocoön, two small and one large, and this is likely one of the small ones. It was probably still in his possession when he died. At the right is an unfinished figure made up of only a head and a leg. The pianist Edwin Fischer owned the picture by 1923, and Prince Paul of Yugoslavia sold it in 1946.
