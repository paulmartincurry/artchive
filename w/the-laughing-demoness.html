The Laughing Demoness (One Hundred Ghost Tales)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; chūban · 25.3 × 18 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The demoness refers to the legend of Kishimojin, known in Sanskrit as Hariti, an ogress set on eating the babies of the town of Rajgir until the Buddha hid one of her own children. She went on to become a protector of children. Hokusai used at least thirty names in his lifetime; this print is signed Zen Hokusai.
