The Sin

Franz von Stuck · 1893 · Oil on canvas · Neue Pinakothek, Munich

Free to use

The model was Anna Maria Brandmaier, a childhood sweetheart of the painter. Stuck first showed the picture in 1893 at the opening exhibition of the Munich Secession, and the Neue Pinakothek bought it the same year. He designed the gilded frame himself, with Doric half-columns at the sides and the title lettered on it, so that the picture would stand out on a crowded exhibition wall. He painted at least eleven versions, and from 1901 one of them hung on a kind of altar in his studio at the Villa Stuck.
