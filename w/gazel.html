Fuzūlī, through the parting night, alas, how dark my fortune grew!

Gazel

Fuzuli · 1535

Translated by E. J. W. Gibb, 1882

Fuzuli lived in Iraq in the sixteenth century, first under Persian and then under Ottoman rule, and wrote in Turkish, Persian and Arabic. His Turkish poems are classics in both Turkey and Azerbaijan. E. J. W. Gibb's translation appeared in 1882.
