Shiva, Parvati, and Their Son Skanda (Somaskandamurti)

Maker unknown · early 11th century · Copper alloy · W. 55.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This family group of Shiva, his consort Uma and their child Skanda first appeared under the Pallava kings of South India, between the sixth and eighth centuries. The Chola casting here was made later, in the early eleventh century, and is 55.6 centimetres wide. The Met bought it in 1982 with a gift from the Lita Annenberg Hazen Charitable Trust.
