it was so simple to paint things as I saw them; to put without special calculation a red close to a blue.

Noa Noa: A Red Close to a Blue

Paul Gauguin · 1893

Translated by O. F. Theis, 1919

Gauguin sailed for Tahiti on 1 April 1891, with money raised by an auction of his paintings at the Hôtel Drouot in Paris. After three months in Papeete he set up his studio in a bamboo hut in Mataiea, some 45 kilometres away. He wrote Noa Noa later as a travelogue, first conceived as commentary on his paintings, and it was first published in 1901.
