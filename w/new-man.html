New Man (Figurines for Victory over the Sun, plate ten)

El Lissitzky · 1920–21, published 1923 · Color lithograph on wove paper · 53.2 × 45 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Lissitzky designed these figures in 1920–21 for an electro-mechanical staging of Victory over the Sun, a Futurist opera first performed in 1913 at Luna Park in Saint Petersburg, and published them as a portfolio of lithographs in 1923. The original production had a libretto in the invented zaum language by Aleksei Kruchonykh and music by Mikhail Matyushin. Its stage designer was Kazimir Malevich, who made his first Black Square there, as part of a design for a curtain.
