Self-Portrait with Hand on Forehead (Selbstbildnis mit der Hand an der Stirn)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1910 · Etching and drypoint in black-brown ink on wove paper · plate: 15.5 × 13.6 cm (6 1/8 × 5 3/8 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This impression is numbered 37 of an edition of 50 and signed in graphite below the image. The plate measures 15.5 by 13.6 centimetres. Kollwitz made self-portraits throughout her life, and this one dates from the same year as Runover, among the works that followed her sculpture classes at the Académie Julian in Paris in 1904.
