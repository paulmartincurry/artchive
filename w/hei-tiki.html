Hei Tiki (Greenstone Pendant)

Māori, Aotearoa New Zealand · 19th century · Nephrite jade (pounamu), shell, pigment, and wax · H. 6 1/8 in. (15.5 cm), W. 3 in. (7.6 cm), D. 1 in. (2.5 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The pendant is 15.5 centimetres tall, cut from nephrite jade, pounamu, and finished with shell, pigment and wax. Heber R. Bishop gave it to the Met in 1902, and it is kept with the jades in the Asian Art department.
