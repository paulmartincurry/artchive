The Artchive
Among the worlds, the one bright sphere,
One star’s name alone I utter,
It’s not that to me she is dear,
It’s that I pine amidst the others.

And, when I’m torn with doubt, alone
It’s to her, I pray, for clear sight.
It’s not that she shines brighter,
Rather, with her, I need no light.

Among the Worlds (Среди миров)

Innokenty Annensky · written 1901, published 1910

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Annensky wrote the poem in 1901, while he was director of the Imperial lyceum at Tsarskoye Selo. It was first printed after his death, in The Cypress Chest, which came out in April 1910. He died of a heart attack at the Tsarskoe Selo railway station in 1909.
