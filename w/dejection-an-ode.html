A grief without a pang, void, dark, and drear,
A stifled, drowsy, unimpassioned grief,
Which finds no natural outlet, no relief,
In word, or sigh, or tear—

Dejection: An Ode (stanza II)

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1802

Coleridge wrote the poem in 1802 and published it the same year in The Morning Post, a London daily newspaper. In its original form it was written to Sara Hutchinson, a woman who was not his wife.
