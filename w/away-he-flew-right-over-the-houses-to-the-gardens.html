Away He Flew, Right over the Houses to the Gardens (Peter Pan in Kensington Gardens)

Arthur Rackham · published 1906 · Colour plate (photomechanical print) · Houghton Library, Harvard University

Free to use with credit. Photo: Houghton Library, Harvard University

Hodder & Stoughton published the book in late November or early December 1906. Most of its text had already appeared in 1902 as six chapters of J. M. Barrie's adult novel The Little White Bird. The scholar Jacqueline Rose has argued that, because of Rackham's plates, the 1906 edition was less a children's book than one for art collectors. This copy belongs to Houghton Library at Harvard.
