The Artchive

Up the Hudson

George Bellows · 1908 · Oil on canvas · 35 7/8 × 48 1/8 in. (91.1 × 122.2 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

George Bellows was twenty-five or twenty-six when he painted this canvas, four years after leaving Ohio State University, just before he was to graduate, to study art in New York under Robert Henri. The year he painted it was the year he first drew wide notice, in an exhibition organised by Henri's pupils. Hugo Reisinger gave it to the museum in 1911, while Bellows was still alive.
