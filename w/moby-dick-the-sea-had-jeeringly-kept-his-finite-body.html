So man’s insanity is heaven’s sense; and wandering from all mortal reason, man comes at last to that celestial thought, which, to reason, is absurd and frantic; and weal or woe, feels then uncompromised, indifferent as his God.

Moby-Dick — The sea had jeeringly kept his finite body up (Ch. 93)

Herman Melville · 1851

Melville signed on as a green hand for the maiden voyage of the whaler Acushnet on December 30, 1840. He spent 18 months aboard as an ordinary seaman.
