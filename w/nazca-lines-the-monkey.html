Nazca Lines: The Monkey

Maker unknown · ca. 100 BCE – 500 · Geoglyph cut into the desert floor

Free to use with credit. Photo: Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0

The monkey measures 93 by 58 metres. The lines were made by clearing away the reddish-brown pebbles that cover the desert to show the lighter ground beneath, and a figure is usually one continuous line. Wooden stakes have been found in the ground at the ends of some lines. The exposed layer is high in lime and hardens with the morning mist, which protects the lines from the wind.
