Apology — The unexamined life

Socrates (via Plato) · c. 399 BCE

Translated by Benjamin Jowett

Socrates was about seventy when he stood trial in Athens in 399 BC, charged with impiety and corrupting the young. He made his defence before a jury of perhaps 500 men, the trial lasted a single day, and he was sentenced to death. Socrates wrote nothing himself. This speech is Plato's account of it, and twice in it Socrates notes that Plato is in the court.
