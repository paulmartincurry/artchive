Still Life with a Silver Jug and a Porcelain Bowl

Willem Kalf · 1655–1660 · Oil on canvas · 73.8 × 65.2 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Willem Kalf, born in Rotterdam in 1619, painted this canvas between 1655 and 1660. The bowl of lemons and oranges is Chinese Wanli porcelain, and a watch lies on the table. In the 1640s he had spent about five years in Paris, where his paintings of kitchens and barns were popular enough to be widely copied.
