Mississippi Bear

Antoine-Louis Barye · 1836 · Lithograph on buff wove paper · Image: 17.3 × 23.1 cm (6 13/16 × 9 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The print shows a black bear from Mississippi, where they were plentiful until about 1900. On a hunt in the Mississippi Delta in 1902 Theodore Roosevelt refused to shoot an injured, lassoed bear.
