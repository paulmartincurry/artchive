Truth becomes fiction when the fiction's true;
Real becomes not-real where the unreal's real.

Dream of the Red Chamber (Ch. 1)

Cao Xueqin · 1791

Translated by David Hawkes, 1973 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Cao Xueqin began the novel in the 1740s and worked on it until his death in 1763 or 1764, living in poverty in the countryside west of Beijing and selling off his paintings. Hand-copied manuscripts of the first 80 chapters circulated among his friends. The book was not printed until 1791, nearly three decades after his death, when Gao E and Cheng Weiyuan brought out an edition that added 40 chapters.
