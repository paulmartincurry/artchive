For beauty is nothing but
the beginning of terror, that we are still able to bear

Duino Elegies (First Elegy)

Rainer Maria Rilke · 1912 (pub. 1923)

Translated by A. S. Kline, 2004 · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Rilke was a guest at Duino Castle on the Adriatic in January 1912 when, by his own account, he heard the opening words in the wind while walking along the cliffs. He wrote them in his notebook and finished a draft of the First Elegy that night. The set of ten then lay unfinished for long stretches, through depression and conscription into the Austro-Hungarian army, and was completed in February 1922 at a manor house in Switzerland that had no gas or electricity.
