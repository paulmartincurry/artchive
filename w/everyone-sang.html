Everyone suddenly burst out singing;
And I was filled with such delight
As prisoned birds must find in freedom,

Everyone Sang

Siegfried Sassoon · 1919

Everyone Sang was collected in Picture-Show in 1919. On 13 July 1918 Sassoon had been shot in the head near Arras, reportedly by a British soldier who mistook him for a German, and he spent the rest of the war in Britain. In 1919 he became literary editor of the socialist Daily Herald and moved to 54 Tufton Street in Westminster, where he lived until 1925.
