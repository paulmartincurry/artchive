Rustam Slays Isfandiyar (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Qasim ibn 'Ali · c. 1525–30 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 19.7 × 17 cm; page 47.3 × 32.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Qasim ibn 'Ali, to whom this painting is attributed, was one of many hands on the book. Others identified include Sultan Muhammad and his son Mirza Ali, Aqa Mirak, Mir Musavvir, Mir Sayyid Ali and Dust Muhammad, and likely Abd al-Samad. The painting measures 19.7 by 17 cm.
