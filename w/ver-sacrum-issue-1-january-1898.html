Ver Sacrum, Issue 1, January 1898 (Cover)

Alfred Roller · January 1898

Free to use

Ver Sacrum, Latin for sacred spring, was the magazine of the Vienna Secession, founded by Gustav Klimt and Max Kurzweil and published from 1898 to 1903. Alfred Roller designed the cover of this first issue. Born in Brünn in Moravia, he had studied painting at the Vienna Academy, and he was thirty-three. Rilke, Hofmannsthal and Maeterlinck were among the writers the magazine printed.
