Maharao Umed Singh Hunting Tigers

Hans Raj Joshi · 1787 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · 43.2 × 70.2 cm · National Museum of Asian Art (Freer Gallery of Art and Arthur M. Sackler Gallery), Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

An inscription on the back dates the hunt to 1787 and names the painter, Hans Raj Joshi, then about thirty-seven. The tiger appears more than once: on the right it mauls a servant of the hunt, and on the left it charges Umed Singh, who shoots from a platform. The nobleman shooting at top centre is probably Zalim Singh, and the knobby lavender rocks suggest the hunting grounds of Kaithun. Ralph Benkaim bought the painting in New Delhi in 1971.
