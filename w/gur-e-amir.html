Gur-e-Amir

Muhammad ibn Mahmud al-Isfahani · 1403–1404 · Brick

Timur ordered this tomb for his grandson and heir Muhammad Sultan, who died in 1403. He had built a smaller tomb for himself at Shahrisabz, but when he died on campaign in 1405 the passes there were snowed in, and he was buried here in the unfinished building. Another grandson, Ulugh Beg, completed it and set a single block of dark green jade over the grave. In 1740 Nader Shah tried to carry off the sarcophagus and it broke in two. The dome is 15 metres across.
