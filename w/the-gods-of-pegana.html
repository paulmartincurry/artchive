The Gods of Pegāna

Lord Dunsany (Edward Plunkett) · 1905

This was Dunsany's first book, published in London in 1905 by Elkin Mathews on a commission basis. He was in his late twenties, had married the year before, and had served as a second lieutenant in the Coldstream Guards in the Second Boer War. He was reading Nietzsche while he wrote it. Sidney Sime drew the illustrations, and the originals are kept at Dunsany Castle.
