And it will happen that if nature has bestowed on you any invention, you will acquire a manner of your own, which cannot be other than good, because your hand and your understanding being always accustomed to gather the flowers, would scarcely know how to take thorns.

Il Libro dell'Arte: Follow One Master

Cennino Cennini · c. 1390s

Translated by Christiana J. Herringham, 1899

Cennini trained in Florence under Agnolo Gaddi, whose father, Taddeo Gaddi, had trained with Giotto. He later worked for some years at the court of Francesco Novello da Carrara in Padua. His handbook was probably written in the 1390s.
