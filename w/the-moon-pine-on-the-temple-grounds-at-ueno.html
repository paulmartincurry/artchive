The Moon Pine on the Temple Grounds at Ueno (One Hundred Famous Views of Edo)

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Color woodblock print; ōban · 35.5 × 24.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The print looks past the Moon Pine to Shinobazu Pond and the Benten Shrine; its date seal reads the eighth month of 1857. Hiroshige, then about sixty, was paid little per series, earning about twice a day labourer's wage. This impression came to the Art Institute in 1939 from the collection of Frederick W. Gookin, by way of his daughter Nathalie.
