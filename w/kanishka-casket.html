Kanishka Casket

Maker unknown · 127 · Gilded copper · British Museum

Excavators found this reliquary in 1908-1909 in a chamber under the Kanishka stupa at Shah-ji-Dheri, on the edge of Peshawar. It is said to have held three fragments of bone, which the British sent on to Burma in 1910 and which are now shown in Mandalay. An inscription on it was long read as the signature of a Greek artist named Agesilas, until a cleaning showed that the word names the monastery's refectory. The original is in the Peshawar Museum, and the British Museum has a copy.
