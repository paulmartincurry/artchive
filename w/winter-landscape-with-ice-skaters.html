Winter Landscape with Ice Skaters

Hendrick Avercamp · c. 1608 · Oil on panel · 77.3 × 131.9 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Avercamp signed this panel at the right, on the wall of a wooden shed covered with graffiti. He painted it around 1608, the year he moved from Amsterdam to Kampen; he was mute and probably deaf, and was known as the mute of Kampen. The Rijksmuseum bought it in 1897 for 550 guilders, with support from the Vereniging Rembrandt.
