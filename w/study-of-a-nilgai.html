Study of a Nilgai (Shah Jahan Album)

Mansur · c. 1620 (calligraphy on reverse c. 1540) · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · 38.9 × 25.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Mansur painted this nilgai around 1620; the calligraphy on the other side of the leaf is by Mir 'Ali Haravi, about 1540. Jahangir gave Mansur the title Nadir al-'Asr, Unequalled of the Age, and set him to record animals that came to court, among them a turkey cock sent from Goa in 1612 and a zebra given in 1621. He died in 1624.
