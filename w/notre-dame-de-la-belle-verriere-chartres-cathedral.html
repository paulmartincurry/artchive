Notre-Dame de la Belle Verrière, Chartres Cathedral

Maker unknown · c. 1180 and 13th century · Stained glass

Free to use

The window was almost lost in the fire of 1194, when only its central panel of the Madonna and Child survived. After Reims Cathedral was destroyed in 1914, all of the windows at Chartres were taken out and stored through both world wars.
