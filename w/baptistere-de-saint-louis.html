Baptistère de Saint Louis

Muhammad ibn al-Zayn · ca. 1320–1340 · Brass · Louvre

Free to use

The basin was hammered from a single sheet of brass and inlaid with silver, gold and niello by the coppersmith Muhammad ibn al-Zayn. Who ordered it, and when it came to France, is not known; it first appears around 1440 in an inventory of the treasury of the Sainte-Chapelle de Vincennes. It has no connection with Saint Louis. The future Louis XIII was baptised in it in 1606, and it was last used for a baptism in 1856, at Notre Dame. During the German invasion it was hidden at the château of Chambord.
