Recovering the Stolen Jewel from the Palace of the Dragon King

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · 1853 · Triptych of woodblock prints; ink and color on paper · Each sheet 36.5 × 24.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kuniyoshi was fifty-five when this triptych was published in 1853. Three years later palsy made it hard for him to move his limbs. He died at his home in Genyadana in April 1861, aged sixty-three, having lived to see Yokohama opened to foreigners.
