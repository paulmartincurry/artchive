What song the Syrens sang, or what name Achilles assumed when he hid himself among women, though puzzling questions, are not beyond all conjecture.

Hydriotaphia, Urn Burial — What song the Syrens sang (Ch. V)

Sir Thomas Browne · 1658

Urn Burial was published in 1658 as the first part of a two-part work that ends with The Garden of Cyrus. Its nominal subject was the discovery of some 40 to 50 Anglo-Saxon pots in Norfolk.
