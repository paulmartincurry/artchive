Mask (Sōmen)

Maker unknown · 18th century · Lacquered iron, copper, gold · H. 23.9 cm; W. 17.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

A sōmen covered the entire face. Full face masks of this kind are believed to have been introduced around the mid to late fifteenth century. Face armour gave a way to secure the top-heavy helmet, whose cord was tied under the chin, and it had a small hole beneath the chin to drain sweat. This mask is lacquered iron with copper and gold and weighs 680 grams.
