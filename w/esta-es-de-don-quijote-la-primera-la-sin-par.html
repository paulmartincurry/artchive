Esta es de Don Quijote la primera, la sin par la gigante calavera

José Guadalupe Posada and Manuel Manilla · c. 1910–13 · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress names two artists for this broadside, Posada and Manuel Manilla. A second large picture is printed on the back. The library bought the sheet in 1978 and showed it in its exhibition Posada's Mexico, which opened the following year.
