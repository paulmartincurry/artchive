Hymns to the Night (First Hymn)

Novalis · 1800

Translated by Paul Bernard Thomas

Novalis wrote the six Hymns to the Night in response to the death of his fiancée, Sophie von Kühn, in 1797; she died just after her 15th birthday. The third hymn contains phrases lifted directly from his diary of that year. The hymns were published in 1800, when he was serving as a director of salt mines, and he died on 25 March 1801 at the age of 28.
