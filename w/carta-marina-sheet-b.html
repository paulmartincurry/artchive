Carta Marina, Sheet B (Detail)

Olaus Magnus · 1539 · Woodcut on paper, one of nine sheets · 56 × 41 cm (sheet); 170 × 125 cm (whole map) · Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich

Free to use

The map was printed in black and white from nine woodcut blocks, each about 55 by 40 centimetres and lettered A to I; this serpent comes from block B. For the seas and coasts Olaus Magnus drew on Ptolemy, on Jacob Ziegler's Scondia of 1532, on sailing directions and on what sailors told him. He wrote about the beasts of the northern seas again in his Historia de Gentibus Septentrionalibus, printed in Rome in 1555. That book was not translated into Swedish until 1909.
