The Knight (The Dance of Death)

Hans Holbein the Younger · c. 1526, published 1538 · Woodcut · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hans Lützelburger, who cut these blocks, had finished only 41 of the scenes when he died in Basel in June 1526, and the blocks became part of his estate. He cut them but, as far as is known, was not an artist himself.
