Nok Head

Maker unknown · 20–620 CE (thermoluminescence date) · Terracotta · 38.2 × 20 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Thermoluminescence testing dates this head to between 20 and 620 CE. Nok terracottas were first found in 1928 in a tin mine near the village of Nok, and a head found in 1943 served for a year as a scarecrow in a yam field. The museum's record notes that the figures use building and firing techniques practised only by women. Cleveland bought the head from a Brussels dealer in 1995.
