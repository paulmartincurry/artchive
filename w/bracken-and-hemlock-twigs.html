Bracken and Hemlock Twigs

Maker unknown · 1838–68 · Mulberry paper (kozo washi) treated with fermented persimmon tannin (kakishibu), and silk threads (itoire) · 30.3 × 41.9 cm (11 15/16 × 16 1/2 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Silk threads were added to this paper stencil to hold its cut structure together. The sheet is 30.3 by 41.9 centimetres, and it was a gift of Jules R. Breuchaud.
