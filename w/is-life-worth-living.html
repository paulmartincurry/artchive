These, then, are my last words to you: Be not afraid of life. Believe that life is worth living, and your belief will help create the fact.

Is Life Worth Living? (closing)

William James · 1897

James first gave this as an address to the Harvard Young Men's Christian Association. The book that collects it takes its title from The Will to Believe, a lecture first published in 1896.
