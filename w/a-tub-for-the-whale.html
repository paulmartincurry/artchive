A Tub for the Whale!

James Gillray · March 14, 1806 · Hand-colored etching and aquatint on cream wove paper · Image: 23.2 × 35 cm (9 3/16 × 13 13/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The whale with John Bull horns stands for the British nation, attacking a packet boat steered by an unpopular new coalition. Gillray's long subtitle calls it an empty barrel tossed to amuse the great Leviathan.
