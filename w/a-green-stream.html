Oh, to remain on a broad flat rock
And to cast a fishing-line forever!

A Green Stream

Wang Wei · 8th century; English 1929

Translated by Witter Bynner and Kiang Kang-hu

About 400 of Wang Wei's poems survive. His younger brother Wang Jin gathered and edited them into a collection at the emperor's command. Wang Wei was also a painter and a musician, but no authenticated painting by him survives and none of his music was preserved.
