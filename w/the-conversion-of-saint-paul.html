The Conversion of Saint Paul

Jacopo Tintoretto · c. 1544 · Oil on canvas · 152.7 × 236.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Tintoretto was about twenty-six when he painted this canvas, which measures 152.7 by 236.3 centimetres. It is probably the Conversion of Saint Paul listed in 1809 in the picture gallery of the Palazzo Pisani in Venice as a work by Andrea Schiavone; Tintoretto's early paintings have often been given to Schiavone, a painter four years younger who was much in his company.
