Great Zimbabwe

Maker unknown · 11th–15th Century · Granite, dry stone walls

Free to use with credit. Photo: Andrew Moore, CC BY-SA 2.0

Ancestors of the Shona people built this city in stone over more than 300 years, starting in the 11th century. The walls are granite laid without mortar, and those of the Great Enclosure rise 11 metres and run for about 250. The Conical Tower between its inner and outer walls is 30 feet high. Most of the people lived in houses of mud on wooden frames, and recent work puts the population at no more than 10,000. The city was abandoned in the 16th or 17th century.
