The Artchive
Raising my cup I beckon the bright moon,
For he, with my shadow, will make three men.

Drinking Alone by Moonlight

Li Bai · ca. 744 CE

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1919

In the early 740s Emperor Xuanzong called Li Bai to the court at Chang'an, used him as a translator and gave him a post at the Hanlin Academy, which supplied the emperor with poetry. When ordered to the palace he was often drunk and could still perform on the spot. The emperor later sent him away with large gifts of gold and silver. In the autumn of 744, aged about forty-three, he met the poet Du Fu and the two shared a single room.
