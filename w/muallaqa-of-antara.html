Have the poets left a single spot for a patch to be sewn?

Muʿallaqa of ʿAntara

Antarah ibn Shaddad · Late 6th Century CE

Translated by A. J. Arberry · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Antarah grew up a slave in Najd, the son of a warrior of the Banu Abs and an Ethiopian slave woman named Zabibah. He earned his freedom by fighting for the tribe when the Tayy invaded its lands. This is the poem of his included in the Mu'allaqat, the seven odes that legend says were hung in the Kaaba at Mecca. The time and manner of his death are disputed.
