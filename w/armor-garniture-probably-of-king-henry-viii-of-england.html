Armor Garniture, Probably of King Henry VIII of England

Royal Workshops at Greenwich · dated 1527 · Steel, gold, leather, copper alloys · Overall H. 73 in. (185.4 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Assembled, the armour stands 185 centimetres tall and weighs about twenty-eight and a half kilograms. Among its pieces are a reinforcing breastplate, a lance rest and a reinforcing plate for the abdomen. It was made in the royal armour workshops at Greenwich, which worked from 1511 into the 1640s, and is dated 1527, eighteen years into Henry VIII's reign. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1919.
