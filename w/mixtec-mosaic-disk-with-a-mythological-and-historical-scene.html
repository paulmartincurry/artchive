Mixtec Mosaic Disk with a Mythological and Historical Scene

Maker unknown · 1400–1500 · Turquoise, earthenware, stucco, spondylus shell, mother of pearl, and iron pyrite, with pigment · Diam.: 30.5 cm (12 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Thirty centimetres across, the disk is built up from turquoise, spondylus shell, mother of pearl and iron pyrite laid on earthenware and stucco. Two dates are written on it, 3 Flint-Knife and 4 House, which correspond to 1456 and 1457, the end of one 52-year cycle of the calendar and the start of the next. Mixtec mosaic makers from northern Oaxaca were working in the Aztec capital, Tenochtitlan, by then. The Art Institute bought the disk in 1997.
