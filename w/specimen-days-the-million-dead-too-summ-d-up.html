Specimen Days — The Million Dead, Too, Summ'd Up

Walt Whitman · 1882

Whitman left for Washington on December 28, 1862, after seeing the wounded soldiers and the heaps of their amputated limbs at Fredericksburg, with the intention of never returning to New York. A friend, Charley Eldridge, helped him get part-time work in the army paymaster's office, which left him time to volunteer as a nurse in the army hospitals.
