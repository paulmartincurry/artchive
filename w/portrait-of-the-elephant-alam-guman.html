Portrait of the Elephant 'Alam Guman

Attributed to Bichitr · c. 1640 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Page 46 × 32 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Bichitr, to whom this portrait is attributed, painted mostly formal portraits, and Stuart Cary Welch noted that he recorded the likeness of nearly every important person of his day; he also painted animals, this elephant among them. One of his court scenes later served Rembrandt as the model for a sketch. He was active until at least the 1640s.
