Swaraj is my birthright

Bal Gangadhar Tilak · c. 1916

Tilak said it in Marathi; swaraj means self-rule, and he was one of its first advocates. The British government of India tried him for sedition three times, in 1897, 1909 and 1916; in 1916, with Muhammad Ali Jinnah as his lawyer, he was acquitted.
