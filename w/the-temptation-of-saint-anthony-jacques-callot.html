The Temptation of Saint Anthony (second version)

Jacques Callot · 1635 · Etching · Platemark 31 × 45.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Callot etched this second version in 1635, the year of his death, on a plate 31 by 45.8 centimetres. He made more than 1,400 etchings in his life and developed the échoppe, an etching needle with a slanting oval tip that let an etcher swell a line the way an engraver could. He also used a harder ground of lute-makers' varnish, which allowed deeper biting and longer print runs from a plate.
