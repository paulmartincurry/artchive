In the Omnibus

Mary Cassatt · 1890–91 · color drypoint and aquatint · Platemark: 36.6 × 26.8 cm (14 7/16 × 10 9/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

One of a series of colour etchings Cassatt made in 1890 and 1891 after studying Japanese woodblock prints. A pencil note under the plate says it was printed by the artist and M. Leroy in an edition of 25. Camille Pissarro wrote that the series was as beautiful as Japanese work. This impression is from the last state of the plate, counted as the seventh in one catalogue of her prints and the fourth in another.
