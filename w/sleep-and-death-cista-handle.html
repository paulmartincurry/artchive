Sleep and Death Cista Handle

Maker unknown · 400–375 BCE · Bronze · With base: 18.5 × 18.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The three figures were cast in eight separate pieces, then welded together and attached to the rectangular base. The group was designed to be fixed to the lid of a large bronze cista, a cylindrical lidded box. The figures probably represent Sleep and Death holding the body of Sarpedon; if not Sarpedon, the fallen warrior could be Memnon. Without the base it stands 14 centimetres high.
