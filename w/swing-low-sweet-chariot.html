I look over Jordan, what do I see?
Comin’ for to carry me home.
A band of angels comin’ after me,
Comin’ for to carry me home.

Swing Low, Sweet Chariot (first verse)

Traditional African American Spiritual · 1872

The song is probably the work of Wallace Willis and his daughter Minerva Willis, Choctaw Freedmen in what is now Choctaw County, Oklahoma. Alexander Reid, a minister at a Choctaw boarding school, heard them sing it, wrote down the words and melody, and sent them to the Fisk Jubilee Singers. The earliest known recording was made in 1894 by the Standard Quartette. In 1939 Nazi Germany put it on a list of undesired and harmful music.
