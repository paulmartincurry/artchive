Dragons and Clouds (Right Screen)

Tawaraya Sōtatsu · 1590–1640 · Ink and pink tint on paper · 171.5 × 374.6 cm · National Museum of Asian Art, Smithsonian Institution, Washington

Free to use

The earliest paintings attributed to Sōtatsu are his repairs, in 1602, to twelfth-century sutra scrolls at the Itsukushima shrine. In Kyoto he opened a shop named Tawaraya, which sold fans, lantern paper, screens, dolls and patterns for kimono. Charles Lang Freer bought this screen from Bunshichi Kobayashi in 1905.
