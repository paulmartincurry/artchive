The Titan's Goblet

Thomas Cole · 1833 · Oil on canvas · 49.2 × 41 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Cole painted this without a commission and asked $100 for it, at a time when his full-scale landscapes fetched $250 to $500. He wrote the title on the back of the canvas and left no comment on the picture. In 1936 it was the only American painting from before the twentieth century in the Museum of Modern Art's exhibition Fantastic Art, Dada, Surrealism.
