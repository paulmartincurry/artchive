On Heroes, Hero-Worship, and the Heroic in History — The Hero as Divinity (Lecture I)

Thomas Carlyle · 1840 (lectures); 1841

The French Revolution had brought Carlyle recognition but little money, so friends organised courses of public lectures and sold tickets at a guinea each. This book comes from six lectures he gave in May 1840.
