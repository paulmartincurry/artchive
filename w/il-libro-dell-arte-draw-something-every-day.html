And continue always, and without fail, to draw something every day, not too little to be enough, and it will do you excellent service.

Il Libro dell'Arte: Draw Something Every Day

Cennino Cennini · c. 1390s

Translated by Christiana J. Herringham, 1899

The book was not printed until 1821, when Giuseppe Tambroni published it in Rome. Four manuscript copies survive, and the date 1437 on the earliest of them was long taken as the date it was written. A document from Colle di Val d'Elsa dated 1427 probably refers to Cennini's son and indicates he was dead by then.
