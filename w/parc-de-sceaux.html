Parc de Sceaux

Eugène Atget · 1925 · Gelatin silver print · sheet 22.2 × 17.8 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Berenice Abbott, then working with Man Ray, visited Atget in 1925, the year of this photograph, and bought some of his prints. Atget died in Paris on 4 August 1927. In the same year this print passed to Abbott and Julien Levy in New York.
