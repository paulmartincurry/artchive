The Artchive

Honoré de Balzac

Auguste Rodin · 1891 · Terracotta · Art only: 9 1/4 in. (23.5 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The head alone is 23.5 centimetres high. Rodin was about fifty when he modelled it; as a young man he had been turned down three times by the École des Beaux-Arts. The museum bought the terracotta in 1912, five years before his death.
