Portrait of a Halberdier

Jacopo Pontormo · 1529–1530 or 1537 · Oil on panel transferred to canvas · The J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Free to use

Pontormo painted the portrait on panel, and it was later transferred to canvas. It was long thought to show Francesco Guardi, a very young soldier of the Florentine Republic; others have argued that it shows a young Cosimo I de' Medici. In May 1989 it was auctioned for 32.5 million dollars, at that date the highest price for an old master painting.
