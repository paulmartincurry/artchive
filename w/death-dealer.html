Death Dealer

Frank Frazetta · 1973

In copyright. Look and learn: shown for study, not for download.

Frazetta painted Death Dealer in 1973, and it was used as the cover of Molly Hatchet's debut album in 1978. He later painted several other Death Dealer pictures, some of them as covers for a comic book series. Since 1985 the figure has been the mascot of III Corps of the United States Army, and a life-size metal statue of it was unveiled outside the corps headquarters at Fort Hood, Texas, in September 2009.
