Venus of Willendorf

Maker unknown · c. 30,000 years ago · Oolitic limestone, tinted with red ochre · H. 11.1 cm · Naturhistorisches Museum, Vienna

Free to use with credit. Photo: MatthiasKabel, CC BY 2.5

A workman found the figure on 7 August 1908 during a dig led by Josef Szombathy, Hugo Obermaier and Josef Bayer near Willendorf in Lower Austria. It is 11.1 centimetres tall, carved from an oolitic limestone that does not occur locally, and tinted with red ochre. It probably never had feet and could not have stood by itself. Radiocarbon dates from the surrounding layers put it in the ground around 30,000 years ago.
