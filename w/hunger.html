Hunger (Proletariat)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1925 · Woodcut in black on Japanese paper · Image: 58.5 × 43.2 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Under her pencil signature at the lower right Kollwitz wrote the words Proletariat: Hunger. The woodcut is printed in black on fibrous Japanese paper, and the image is about 58 centimetres high and 43 wide. The Statens Museum for Kunst dates it 1925 and acquired it in 2020.
