Saint Eustace

Albrecht Dürer · c. 1501 · Engraving · Sheet: 35 × 25.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Albrecht Dürer most likely learned to use the burin during his early training with his father, since it was also an essential skill of the goldsmith. He engraved this plate around 1501, about three years after the designs for his Apocalypse. The sheet measures 35 by 25.9 centimetres.
