Recuerdo

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1919

After graduating from Vassar in 1917, Millay moved to New York and lived in Greenwich Village. In 1919, the year she turned twenty-seven, she wrote the anti-war play Aria da Capo, which starred her sister Norma at the Provincetown Playhouse. To support herself she wrote short stories for Ainslee's Magazine, published under the pseudonym Nancy Boyd.
