The Artchive
My little songs do I utter
From out of my great, great sorrow;

Aus meinen großen Schmerzen

Heinrich Heine · 1827

Translated by Edgar Alfred Bowring, 1859

In 1827 the Hamburg publisher Julius Campe brought out Heine's Buch der Lieder, a gathering of poems that had already been printed elsewhere. Heine was twenty-nine. He had been trained for the law at three universities and had been baptised a Lutheran two years before to improve his chances of a post, which never came. The book sold slowly at first, and sales picked up once composers began setting the poems as songs.
