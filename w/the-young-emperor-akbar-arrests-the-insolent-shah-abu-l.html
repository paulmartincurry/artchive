The Young Emperor Akbar Arrests the Insolent Shah Abu'l-Maali (Akbarnama)

Designed by Basawan, painted by Shankar · c. 1590–95 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Image: 32 × 19.3 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Akbar commissioned the Akbarnama, the history of his own reign, in 1589, and about fifty artists illustrated it while Abu'l Fazl was writing it. This page shows Akbar at thirteen, three days after he came to the throne, watching his late father's favourite courtier being arrested. Basawan designed the page and painted the faces; according to the signature, Shankar painted the rest.
