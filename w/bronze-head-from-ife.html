Bronze Head from Ife

Maker unknown · ca. 14th–early 15th century · Copper-based alloy, brass · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: sailko, CC BY-SA 3.0

Builders putting up a house at the Wunmonije Compound in Ife found this head by accident in 1938, along with sixteen other brass and copper heads and the upper half of a brass figure. It was cast by the lost wax technique, stands 35 centimetres high and weighs 5.1 kilograms, and its crown still carries traces of red and black paint. The editor of the Daily Times of Nigeria took it out of the country, and it reached the British Museum in 1939.
