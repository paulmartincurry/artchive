The Artchive
They wander through the cities night enshrouds:
The cities cower, black, beneath their feet.

The Demons of the Cities (Die Dämonen der Städte)

Georg Heym · December 1910

Translated by Babette Deutsch and Avrahm Yarmolinsky, 1923 · Public domain in the United States (published 1923); may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Heym had studied law at Würzburg and was twenty-three in December 1910. That year he had joined Der Neue Club in Berlin, and it was at the club's cabaret evenings that his poems were first noticed. Ernst Rowohlt published his first book in January 1911; it was the only one to appear in his lifetime. On 16 January 1912 he went skating on the frozen river Havel with a friend, Ernst Balcke, and both drowned. It appeared that Heym had fallen in trying to save him.
