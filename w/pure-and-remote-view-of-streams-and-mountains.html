Pure and Remote View of Streams and Mountains (detail)

Xia Gui · 13th century · Handscroll, ink on paper · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

The whole scroll is almost nine metres long, ink on paper, and what is shown here is one stretch of it. It survives incomplete: the final section, which bore Xia Gui's signature, is missing. Old documents describe him as a painter who worked very fast, and paper takes up ink quickly. Some sources say he preferred brushes with worn tips, and split brushes that made two or more strokes at once.
