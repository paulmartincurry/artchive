Should auld acquaintance be forgot,
And never brought to mind?
Should auld acquaintance be forgot,
And auld lang syne!

Auld Lang Syne

Robert Burns · 1788

Burns sent this song to the Scots Musical Museum in 1788, saying it was an old song that had never been in print and that he had taken it down from an old man. A ballad printed by James Watson in 1711 has much the same first verse and chorus. The tune now used was first printed with it in 1799, three years after his death; Burns had thought the song's earlier melody mediocre.
