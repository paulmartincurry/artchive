Spider-Webs (Camera Work 21, plate XI)

Alvin Langdon Coburn · 1908 · Halftone · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The Library of Congress catalogues this plate as a halftone, not a photogravure. It ran as plate XI of issue 21 in January 1908, one of twelve Coburn photographs in that number. Coburn was twenty-five, and the year before George Bernard Shaw had called him the greatest photographer in the world.
