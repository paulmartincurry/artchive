The Love Letter

Johannes Vermeer · c. 1669–1670 · Oil on canvas · 44 × 38.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

On 23 September 1971 the painting was stolen while on loan to the Centre for Fine Arts in Brussels. The 21-year-old thief cut the canvas from its frame with a potato peeler and then demanded 200 million Belgian francs for refugees in East Pakistan. It was returned to the Rijksmuseum on 8 October in very poor condition, and the restoration took almost a year.
