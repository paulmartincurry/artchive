Look for me in the whirlwind or the storm,
look for me all around you.

First Message from Atlanta Prison

Marcus Garvey · February 10, 1925

Garvey was thirty-seven and newly held in the United States Penitentiary in Atlanta. He had been convicted in 1923 of mail fraud, for selling stock in the Black Star Line, the shipping company he had headed since 1919. He was in prison for nearly two years, until President Calvin Coolidge commuted the sentence, and in 1927 he was deported to Jamaica. He had begun his working life as a printer's apprentice.
