Christ Presented to the People

Rembrandt van Rijn · 1655 · Drypoint on Japanese paper; second state of eight · plate: 38.4 x 44.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Rembrandt scratched this straight into the copper in drypoint and printed it in eight states, some on expensive imported Japanese paper. This is the second, which adds cross-hatching to the doorway on the left. By the sixth state the soft plate was wearing, and he scraped away the whole crowd in front of the platform. It was his last print of the Passion.
