Four Tiles with a Figural Scene

Maker unknown · Safavid dynasty (1501–1722), 17th century · Fritware with polychrom overglaze decoration · Each: 24.2 × 24.2 × 2.2 cm (9 1/2 × 9 1/2 × 7/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The colours were kept apart in the kiln by a greasy black line, a technique the Spanish called cuerda seca, dry cord. These four tiles are a piece of a larger scene from the life of the Persian hero Bahram Gur.
