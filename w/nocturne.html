The Artchive
And your shadow,
Languid, delicate;
And my shadow,

Nocturne (Nocturno III)

José Asunción Silva · Published 1908

Translated by Thomas Walsh (ed.), 1920

Silva's sister Elvira died in 1892, and the poem appears to have been written in answer to her death. He came from a wealthy Bogotá family that fell into heavy debt after his father died. In 1895 a shipwreck took most of his unpublished manuscripts, and on the evening of 23 May 1896 he shot himself in the heart, aged thirty. Until 2016 Colombia's 5000 peso banknote carried his portrait, with the whole of this poem in micro-text on the back.
