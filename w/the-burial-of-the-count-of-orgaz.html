The Burial of the Count of Orgaz

El Greco · 1586 · Oil on canvas · Church of Santo Tomé

The parish priest of Santo Tomé, Andrés Núñez, signed the contract with El Greco on 18 March 1586. It set out what the picture had to show, made the painter pay for his own materials, and left the price to be fixed by appraisal. After a long dispute El Greco settled for 13,200 reales. The priest is in the painting, reading, at the right. The boy at the left is the painter's son Jorge Manuel, and the handkerchief in his pocket carries the signature and 1578, the year the boy was born. The canvas is 4.8 metres tall and hangs in the church it was made for.
