Portrait of a Noblewoman Dressed in Mourning

Jacopo da Empoli · c. 1600 · Oil on canvas · 221 × 122.5 cm (87 × 48 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Her black dress and mourning veil mark her as an aristocratic widow; the crucifix and small prayer book point to her piety. Who she was is no longer known.
