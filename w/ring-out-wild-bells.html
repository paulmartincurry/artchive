Ring out wild bells to the wild sky,
The flying cloud, the frosty light:
The year is dying in the night;
Ring out, wild bells, and let him die.

Ring Out, Wild Bells (In Memoriam A.H.H., section CIV of the 1850 edition)

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1850

After seventeen years of composing, writing and editing, Tennyson published In Memoriam anonymously in 1850, under a Latin title.
