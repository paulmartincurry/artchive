Suffering is one very long moment. We cannot divide it by seasons. We can only record its moods, and chronicle their return.

De Profundis — Suffering is one very long moment (opening)

Oscar Wilde · 1897

Wilde wrote this letter to Lord Alfred Douglas in Reading Gaol between January and March 1897, near the end of a two-year sentence with hard labour. It filled eighty close-written pages on twenty sheets of thin blue prison paper, and he was not allowed to send it. The governor handed it to him on his release on 18 May. Robert Ross gave the title, from Psalm 130, to a cut version in 1905, and gave the manuscript to the British Museum on condition it stay closed until 1960. The full, accurate text came out in 1962.
