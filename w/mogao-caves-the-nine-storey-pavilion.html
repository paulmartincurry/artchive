Mogao Caves: The Nine-Storey Pavilion

Maker unknown · 695 · Wooden structure

Free to use with credit. Photo: 慕尼黑啤酒, CC BY-SA 3.0

Monks built caves at this site from the fourth century to the fourteenth, paid for by donors who ranged from emperors to merchants and women's groups. The larger of the site's two giant Buddha statues was made in 695, a year after Empress Wu Zetian ordered giant statues built across the country. In 1941 the painter Zhang Daqian arrived with a small team and stayed two and a half years, repairing and copying the murals.
