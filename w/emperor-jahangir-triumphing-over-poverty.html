Emperor Jahangir Triumphing over Poverty

Attributed to Abu'l Hasan · c. 1620–25; border 16th century · Opaque watercolor, gold and ink on paper · Image 23.8 × 15.2 cm; sheet 36.8 × 24.6 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

Abu'l Hasan, to whom the painting is attributed, was the son of the Herat painter Aqa Riza; in 1618 Jahangir gave him the title Nadir al-Zaman, Wonder of the Age, for the frontispiece of the emperor's memoirs. The painting belongs to a series of allegorical portraits Jahangir commissioned in his later years, a time of unrest, famine and long illness. Its border is older, made in Bukhara in the 16th century.
