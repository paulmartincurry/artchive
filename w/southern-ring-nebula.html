Southern Ring Nebula (NIRCam and MIRI Images Side by Side)

James Webb Space Telescope, NASA, ESA, CSA and STScI · 12 July 2022 · Near- and mid-infrared composite images

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI

The Southern Ring Nebula was one of five objects in the first official science images from the James Webb Space Telescope, released on 12 July 2022. The left view is in near-infrared light and the right in mid-infrared. The nebula is the cast-off outer layers of a dying star, now a white dwarf, about 2,000 light-years from Earth in the constellation Vela.
