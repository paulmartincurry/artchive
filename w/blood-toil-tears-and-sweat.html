I have nothing to offer but blood, toil, tears and sweat.

Blood, Toil, Tears and Sweat

Winston Churchill · 13 May 1940

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Churchill gave this speech to the House of Commons on 13 May 1940, three days after replacing Neville Chamberlain. It was his first speech as prime minister, and in it he asked the House to declare its confidence in his Government. As a young man he had considered writing a biography of Garibaldi, and the sentence has been called a paraphrase of one Garibaldi uttered in 1849.
