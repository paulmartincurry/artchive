The Great Red Dragon and the Woman Clothed with the Sun

William Blake · ca. 1805–1810 · Watercolor · Brooklyn Museum

Free to use

William Blake painted this watercolour during the years when he was commissioned to make over one hundred paintings illustrating books of the Bible. The sheet is 43.7 cm high and 34.8 cm wide. The Brooklyn Museum keeps it behind closed doors because it is sensitive to light. A second version, with the figures seen from a different viewpoint, is in the National Gallery of Art in Washington.
