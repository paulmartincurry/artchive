Prisoners Exercising (after Gustave Doré)

Vincent van Gogh · February 1890 · Oil on canvas · 80 × 64 cm · Pushkin Museum, Moscow

Free to use

Van Gogh painted this in February 1890 in the asylum at Saint-Rémy, where he could not go out to paint from life and turned to copying prints. He worked from a woodblock reproduction, printed in a Dutch magazine, of Gustave Doré's 1872 engraving of the exercise yard at Newgate Prison. The canvas was one of the works displayed around his coffin. Ivan Morozov held it in Russia by 1909.
