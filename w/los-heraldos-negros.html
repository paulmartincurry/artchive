The Artchive
There are blows in life, so powerful… I don’t know!

Los heraldos negros (The Black Heralds)

César Vallejo · 1918 (Distributed July 1919)

Translated by Clayton Eshleman · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This is the first poem of Vallejo's first book, written between 1915 and 1918. The book was printed on the presses of the Lima Penitentiary. It was meant to come out in 1918 but was held back while Vallejo waited, in vain, for his friend Abraham Valdelomar to write an introduction, and it appeared in July 1919 in a short print run. Vallejo was twenty-seven.
