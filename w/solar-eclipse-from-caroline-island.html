Solar Eclipse from Caroline Island

H. A. Lawrence · 6 May 1883 · Gelatin silver print · 13.3 × 13.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The eclipse of 6 May 1883 was total only along a narrow path across the South Pacific Ocean, and this view of it was taken from Caroline Island. The photographer, H. A. Lawrence, was British and active in the 1880s. The print came to the museum as a gift in 2018.
