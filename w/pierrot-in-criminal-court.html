Pierrot in Criminal Court

Thomas Couture · c. 1864–70 · Oil on wood panel · Framed: 45.8 x 52.1 x 5.2 cm (18 1/16 x 20 1/2 x 2 1/16 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Pierrot is a poor fool on trial for stealing food from a restaurant; the stolen food lies on the courtroom floor. Harlequin argues for the defence while the judges sleep, Couture's jab at the courts and the legal profession.
