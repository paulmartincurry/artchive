Aldine Press Device: Dolphin and Anchor

Aldus Manutius · ca. 1502

Free to use with credit. Photo: Arminiuzz, CC BY 3.0

Aldus Manutius took the dolphin and anchor as his printer's mark. The pairing is one of several images used for a classical adage about making haste slowly, a saying Augustus was fond of. Manutius showed Erasmus, whose books he published, a Roman silver coin with the same device on its reverse, a gift to him from Cardinal Bembo.
