Servicing a Floodlight at the Construction of a New Steel Plant, Geneva, Utah

Andreas Feininger · November 1942 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The agency caption describes floodlights that turned night into day on the construction site of a new Columbia Steel plant at Geneva, Utah; Feininger was there in November 1942. The mill was built with federal money between 1941 and 1944, far inland to keep it away from possible Japanese attack on the West Coast. At its peak it was the largest steel mill west of the Mississippi.
