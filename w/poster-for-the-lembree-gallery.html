Poster for the Lembrée Gallery

Théo van Rysselberghe · 1897 · Color lithograph · Image: 62.5 x 44.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Théo van Rysselberghe was a founding member of the Belgian avant-garde group Les XX and one of the leading Neo-Impressionist painters outside France. In 1895 he travelled to Athens, Constantinople, Moscow and Saint Petersburg to make posters for the Compagnie des Wagons-lits. He moved to Paris in 1897, the year of this poster for a gallery.
