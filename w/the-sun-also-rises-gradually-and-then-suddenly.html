The Sun Also Rises — Gradually and then suddenly

Ernest Hemingway · 1926

To move the novel to Scribner's, Hemingway quickly wrote a satire of Sherwood Anderson, one of his own publisher's most saleable authors, and sent it in; Boni and Liveright rejected it, which ended his contract. He signed with Scribner's within weeks. Two months after publication the book was in a second printing, with 7,000 copies sold. The British edition of 1927 was titled Fiesta and left out the two epigraphs.
