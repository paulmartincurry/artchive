The Book of Urizen (Copy G, Object 1)

William Blake · 1794, printed c. 1818 · Relief etching, colour printed · Library of Congress, Washington

Free to use

Blake wrote, designed, printed, coloured and sold this book from his house in Lambeth, in south London, where he had moved in autumn 1790. It first appeared in 1794 as The First Book of Urizen. Only eight copies survive, all of them colour-printed, and this one, copy G, was printed about 1818.
