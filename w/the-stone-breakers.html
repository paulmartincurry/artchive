The Stone Breakers

Gustave Courbet · 1849 · Oil on canvas

Free to use

In November 1849 Courbet was driving out to paint a landscape when he passed two men breaking stones on the road near Maisières. He asked them to come to his studio the next morning and began the picture. The canvas, 1.5 by 2.6 metres, went to the Dresden gallery around 1882. It is generally believed to have burned in February 1945 on a transport caught in the bombing of the city, though a 1988 study found the museum lists it as missing after a transfer in 1944. A smaller, darker mirror-image version survives in Winterthur.
