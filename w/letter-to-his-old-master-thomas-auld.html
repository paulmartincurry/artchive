The morality of the act I dispose of as follows: I am myself; you are yourself; we are two distinct persons, equal persons. What you are, I am. You are a man, and so am I. God created both, and made us separate beings. I am not by nature bond to you, or you to me.

Letter to His Old Master, Thomas Auld

Frederick Douglass · September 1848

Douglass published this open letter in September 1848, on the tenth anniversary of his escape from slavery. It was addressed to Thomas Auld, his former master, and in it he also asked after members of his family whom Auld still held.
