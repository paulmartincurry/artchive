Shiva Nataraja

Maker unknown · c. 1100–1200 · Bronze · 153 × 114.5 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

The figure weighs 255 kilograms and stands 153 centimetres high, and its pedestal weighs another 40. In 1935 the Royal Asian Art Society in the Netherlands bought it from the Paris dealer C.T. Loo for 16,000 guilders, with help from the Vereniging Rembrandt. The society has lent it to the Rijksmuseum since 1972.
