Farewell! Oh! love me forever, and never doubt the faithful heart of your lover, L.

Ever thine. Ever mine. Ever each other's.

Letter to the Immortal Beloved

Ludwig van Beethoven · 1812

Translated by Lady Wallace, 1866

The letter dates from 1812, when Beethoven was forty-one and had made Vienna his base for twenty years. His Emperor piano concerto had been given its premiere the year before, without the composer as soloist.
