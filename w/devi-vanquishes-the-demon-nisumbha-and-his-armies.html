Devi Vanquishes the Demon Nisumbha and His Armies (Devi Mahatmya)

Maker unknown · 18th century · Opaque pigments with gold on paper · 17.7 × 22.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Devi Mahatmya has 700 verses in 13 chapters, and was added to the Markandeya Purana in the fifth or sixth century. This page illustrating it was painted in Guler more than a thousand years later, in the eighteenth century. The painting is 17.7 by 22.8 centimetres, set in a blue margin decorated with gold and a pink speckled border. The Met acquired it in 2024.
