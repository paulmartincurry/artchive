Kastchey (Russian Wonder Tales)

Ivan Bilibin · published 1912 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

In Russian tales Koshchei cannot be killed because he hides his death inside nested objects: a needle in an egg, the egg in a duck, the duck in a hare, the hare in a chest on the far island of Buyan. Ivan Bilibin first published pictures for Russian fairy tales in 1899, and this American edition of 1912 reprinted twelve of them in colour.
