He who enduréd all hardships with me, whom I lovéd dearly,
Enkidu,—he who enduréd all hardships with me (is now perish’d),
Gone to the common lot of mankind! (And) I have bewail’d him
Day and night long: (and) unto the tomb I have not consign’d him.

The Epic of Gilgamesh — Death which I dread (Tablet X, Old Babylonian version)

Anonymous (Old Babylonian) · c. 1800 BCE

Translated by R. Campbell Thompson, 1928

The Old Babylonian version dates to the 18th century BCE, and only a few of its tablets have survived. The older tablets are mainly used to fill gaps in the later text.
