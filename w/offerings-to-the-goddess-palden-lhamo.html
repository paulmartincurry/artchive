Offerings to the Goddess Palden Lhamo

Maker unknown · late 16th century · Distemper and ink on cloth · 170.2 × 112 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Palden Lhamo is the special protector of the Dalai Lamas, and the only female among the traditional Eight Guardians of the Law. As guardian spirit of the lake Lhamo La-tso, she promised the 1st Dalai Lama in a vision to protect his line of reincarnations, and since the 2nd Dalai Lama monks have gone to the lake to seek visions when choosing the next. The cloth is 170.2 by 112 centimetres. Frances Gould-Naftal and Marvin Naftal gave it to the Met in 1983.
