The Angelus (L'Angélus)

Jean-François Millet · 1857–1859 · Oil on canvas · 55.5 × 66 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

The American collector Thomas Gold Appleton commissioned it and never came to collect it. Millet then added the steeple of Chailly-en-Bière on the horizon and changed the title from Prayer for the Potato Crop. In 1860 it sold for 1,000 francs. At the Secrétan sale in 1889 the Louvre and the American Art Association bid it up to 553,000 francs, and a year later Alfred Chauchard paid 750,000 francs for it and left it to the French state. In 1932 it was vandalized.
