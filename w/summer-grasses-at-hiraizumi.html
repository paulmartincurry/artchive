Summer grass—
all that's left
of warriors' dreams.

Summer Grasses at Hiraizumi

Matsuo Bashō · 1689

Translated by Robert Hass · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Bashō left Edo on 16 May 1689 with his student Sora, heading for the northern provinces of Honshū, which were relatively unsettled at the time. They reached Hiraizumi on 29 June. He was about forty-five. Five years earlier he had set out on the first of his four major wanderings expecting to die on the road or be killed by bandits.
