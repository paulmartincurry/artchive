Lovers Under the Moon (Wolha jeongin)

Sin Yun-bok · ca. 1805 · Color on paper · Gansong Art Museum, Seoul

Free to use

Sin Yun-bok's father and grandfather were both court painters in Joseon Korea. He put a short verse and his seal on most of his paintings but never a date, so the order in which he made them is hard to establish. Not much else is known about his life.
