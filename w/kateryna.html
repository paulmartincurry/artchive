Kateryna

Taras Shevchenko · 1842 · Oil on Canvas · Taras Shevchenko National Museum

Taras Shevchenko was born a serf, and the paper that freed him is dated 22 April 1838. That winter, in a flat he shared on Vasilyevsky Island in Saint Petersburg, he wrote a poem also called Kateryna, dedicated to Vasily Zhukovsky, who had helped win his freedom. It was published in his collection Kobzar in 1840. He was about twenty-eight when he painted this canvas.
