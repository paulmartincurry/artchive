I never saw daffodils so beautiful. … the rest tossed and reeled and danced, and seemed as if they verily laughed with the wind, that blew upon them over the lake; they looked so gay, ever glancing, ever changing.

Grasmere Journal (15 April 1802)

Dorothy Wordsworth · 1802

Dorothy Wordsworth was thirty and had been living with her brother William at Dove Cottage in Grasmere since December 1799. She kept the journal from 1800 until she filled a notebook in 1803, for William and a few close friends and family, with no ambition to be a public author. William drew on this entry for his poem I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud. The journal was first published in 1897, forty-two years after her death.
