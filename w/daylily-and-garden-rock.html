Daylily and Garden Rock

Wen Shu · 1630 · Hanging scroll, ink and colour on paper · Palace Museum, Beijing

Wen Shu was taught to paint as a child by her father, a landscape painter, and lived in a villa near the Hanshan Temple outside Suzhou. Most of her surviving work is dated 1626 or later, after her father-in-law died and the family's money ran down, so she probably painted for sale. Demand was high enough that she trained women as assistants, and other artists forged her work while she was alive. She died at about thirty-nine.
