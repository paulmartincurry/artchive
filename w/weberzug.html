Weberzug (Ein Weberaufstand, Sheet 4)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1897–98 · Etching · Sheet: 37 × 47.8 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

This is sheet 4 of the six in Kollwitz's Weavers cycle, according to the pencil note at its lower left, and one of the three etchings in the set. She signed it in pencil at the lower right. The Statens Museum for Kunst, which dates it 1897–1898, acquired this impression in 1953.
