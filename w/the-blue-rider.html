The Blue Rider (Der Blaue Reiter)

Wassily Kandinsky · 1903 · Oil painting · Private collection, Zurich

Free to use

Kandinsky had studied law and political economy in Moscow and decided only at thirty to give himself to art, moving to Munich in 1896. In 1901 he co-founded the Phalanx there, an artists' association that opened a school; Gabriele Münter was one of his students. In December 1911, after his Composition V was turned down for an exhibition, he and Franz Marc founded the group Der Blaue Reiter, whose name may have come from this 1903 painting.
