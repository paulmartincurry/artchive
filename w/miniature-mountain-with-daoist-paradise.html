Miniature Mountain with Daoist Paradise

Maker unknown · 1736–95 · Green jade with brown markings · Height 17.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Carved from a boulder of green jade with brown markings during the reign of the Qianlong emperor, the mountain is 17.5 centimetres high. A bearded immortal walks with a servant carrying peaches, a sign of long life. Daoist practitioners are recorded as drinking morning dew with scraps of jade as an elixir. The carving came to Cleveland in 1941 as an anonymous memorial gift.
