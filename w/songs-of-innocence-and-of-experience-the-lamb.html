Songs of Innocence and of Experience: The Lamb

William Blake · 1789–94 · Relief etching, hand-coloured · Library of Congress, Rosenwald Collection

Free to use

In 1794 Blake joined Songs of Innocence and Songs of Experience in one volume, which he called Songs of Innocence and of Experience Shewing the Two Contrary States of the Human Soul. This copy is in the Rosenwald Collection at the Library of Congress.
