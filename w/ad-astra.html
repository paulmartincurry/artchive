Ad Astra

Akseli Gallen-Kallela · 1894–96 · Oil on canvas, in artist's painted and gilded wood shrine · Canvas: 76 × 85 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Gallen-Kallela designed and made the gilded wooden frame himself, in the form of an altarpiece, and the painting served as one at his daughter's baptism. He painted it in rural Finland between 1894 and 1896; in March 1895 his first child, Impi Marjatta, died of diphtheria. The work hung in his wilderness studio and stayed with his descendants until the Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 2017.
