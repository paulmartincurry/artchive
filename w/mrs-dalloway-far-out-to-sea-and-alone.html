She had a perpetual sense, as she watched the taxi cabs, of being out, out, far out to sea and alone; she always had the feeling that it was very, very dangerous to live even one day.

Mrs Dalloway — Far out to sea and alone

Virginia Woolf · 1925

The novel was published on 14 May 1925, and in it Clarissa Dalloway is fifty-one. Eight years earlier the Woolfs had set up a hand press on their dining-room table at Hogarth House. Leonard meant the press to give Virginia a rest from the strain of writing and to spare her the stress of submitting her books to an outside firm. Its first publication took two and a half months to print, in a run of 150 copies.
