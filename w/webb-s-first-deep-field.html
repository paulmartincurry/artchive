Webb's First Deep Field (SMACS 0723)

James Webb Space Telescope, NASA, ESA, CSA and STScI · released 11 July 2022 · Space telescope image (near-infrared)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI

This was the first full-colour image released from the James Webb Space Telescope, shown to the public on 11 July 2022, seven months after launch. It combines exposures totalling 12.5 hours. The patch of sky is about the size of a grain of sand held at arm's length, and some of the galaxies in it are as old as 13 billion years. The spikes around the stars come from the edges of the mirror's 18 hexagonal segments.
