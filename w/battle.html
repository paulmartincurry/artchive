The warriors are all dead: they lie on the moor-field.
They issued but shall not enter: they went but shall not return.

Battle

Qu Yuan · c. 300 BC

Translated by Arthur Waley

The poem is attributed to Qu Yuan, a minister of the state of Chu who was twice sent into exile after rival ministers slandered him to the king. In 278 BC, learning that a Qin general had taken the Chu capital, he is said to have written a long lament for the city; he died by wading into the Miluo River holding a rock. Of the poems tied to his name, only The Lament is generally accepted as his own.
