The Whirlpools of Awa

Utagawa Hiroshige · 1857 · Triptych of color woodblock prints · 38.4 × 25.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This triptych stands for flowers in Hiroshige's set on snow, moon and flowers: the whirlpools were meant to call flowers to mind. The whirlpools form in the Naruto Strait, about 1.3 kilometres wide, where the tide rushes between the Inland Sea and the Pacific four times a day. At spring tide the vortices can reach 20 metres across.
