O Negro slaves, dark purple ripened plums,
Squeezed, and bursting in the pine-wood air,

Song of the Son

Jean Toomer · 1923

Toomer began the sketches that became the first section of Cane in November 1921, on a train from Georgia to Washington. By Christmas the first draft of them was done. His close friend Waldo Frank suggested he combine the sketches into a book, which came out in 1923.
