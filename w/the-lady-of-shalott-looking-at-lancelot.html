The Lady of Shalott Looking at Lancelot

John William Waterhouse · 1894 · Oil on canvas · Leeds Art Gallery

Free to use

Waterhouse painted Tennyson's Lady of Shalott three times, in 1888, 1894 and 1915, and this is the second. He gave it to Leeds Art Gallery the year after he finished it. In his oil sketch the dress was red; in the finished canvas it is white. The sketch was sold after his death in 1917 and vanished until 2003, when it turned up in Iceland, bought in London years earlier by a fisherman.
