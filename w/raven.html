Raven (The Birds of America, Plate 101)

John James Audubon & Robert Havell Jr. · 1831 · Hand-colored engraving and aquatint on Whatman wove paper · sheet: 99.6 × 66.4 cm (39 3/16 × 26 1/8 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Printing the whole book cost $115,640. Audubon paid for it with advance subscriptions, exhibitions, commissions for oil paintings and the sale of animal skins from animals he hunted himself.
