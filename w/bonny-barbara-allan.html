'O mother, mother, make my bed!
O make it saft and narrow!
Since my love died for me to-day,
I'll die for him to-morrow.'

Bonny Barbara Allan

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1740

The earliest surviving mention of the song is in Samuel Pepys's diary for 2 January 1666, where he heard the actress Elizabeth Knepp sing it at a New Year party. Allan Ramsay printed this Scottish version in his Tea-Table Miscellany in 1740. Joseph Haydn arranged the song in 1792, and the ballad was first printed in the United States in 1836.
