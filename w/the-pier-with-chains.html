The Pier with Chains (Carceri d'invenzione, plate XVI)

Giovanni Battista Piranesi · 1761 · Etching and engraving on heavy ivory laid paper · 40.9 × 55 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Plate XVI was extensively reworked for the 1761 edition. Its inscriptions are quotations or paraphrases from Livy's history of the early Roman Republic, showing the justice of early Roman law. In earlier works Piranesi had argued against Greek influence on ancient Rome and stressed its Etruscan heritage.
