Portrait of Bodhidharma

Hakuin Ekaku · mid-18th century · Hanging scroll; ink on paper · Image 117.5 × 54 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Hakuin was a Zen master of the Rinzai school. Asked why he had become a monk, he said it was out of terror of falling into hell. At forty-one he experienced a decisive awakening while reading the Lotus Sutra, a text he had disregarded as a young student. Florence and Herbert Irving gave this scroll to the Metropolitan Museum in 2015.
