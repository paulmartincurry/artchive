Spend all you have for loveliness,
Buy it and never count the cost;
For one white singing hour of peace
Count many a year of strife well lost,
And for a breath of ecstasy
Give all you have been, or could be.

Barter

Sara Teasdale · 1917

In 1918 Teasdale won a Pulitzer Prize for Love Songs, her collection of 1917. She had married Ernst Filsinger, a longtime admirer of her poetry, on December 19, 1914, and in 1916 the two moved to New York City, to an apartment on Central Park West.
