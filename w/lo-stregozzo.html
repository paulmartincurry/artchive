Lo Stregozzo

Agostino Veneziano · c. 1515–25 · Engraving, first state before the engraver's initials on the horn · Sheet 30 × 62.3 cm (trimmed) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This impression is a first state, printed before the engraver's initials were added on the horn. The attribution to Agostino Veneziano is disputed, and the print sits apart from the rest of his work. From 1516 he was one of the leading engravers in Marcantonio Raimondi's workshop in Rome, until the sack of the city in 1527 broke it up. The sheet is 62.3 centimetres wide and has been trimmed at the sides.
