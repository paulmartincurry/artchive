Luttrell Psalter: Marginal Grotesque (folio 27r)

Maker unknown · c. 1320–1340 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · British Library, London

Free to use

Sir Geoffrey Luttrell, lord of the manor of Irnham in Lincolnshire, commissioned the psalter. One scribe and at least five artists worked on it, and the one called the Luttrell Master was skilled in grotesques. The British Museum acquired the book in 1929 for £31,500.
