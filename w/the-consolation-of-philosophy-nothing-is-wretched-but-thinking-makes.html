So true is it that nothing is wretched, but thinking makes it so, and conversely every lot is happy if borne with equanimity.

The Consolation of Philosophy — Nothing Is Wretched but Thinking Makes It So (Book II, Prose IV)

Boethius · 523; English 1897

Translated by H. R. James

Boethius had been a senator by 25 and a consul by 33, and became a personal adviser to Theodoric the Great. He was imprisoned around 523 after publicly defending a fellow consul against charges of conspiracy, and was tortured and executed in 524.
