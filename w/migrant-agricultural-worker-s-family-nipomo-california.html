Migrant Agricultural Worker's Family, Nipomo, California

Dorothea Lange · March 1936 · Nitrate negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Dorothea Lange made this at Nipomo, California, in March 1936, as part of the series that includes Migrant Mother. The woman is Florence Owens Thompson, who was not identified by name until 1978. Lange's caption gives her age as thirty-two and counts seven hungry children.
