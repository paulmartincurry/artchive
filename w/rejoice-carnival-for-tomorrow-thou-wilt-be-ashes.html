Rejoice, Carnival, for Tomorrow Thou Wilt Be Ashes (Los Proverbios, plate 14)

Francisco de Goya · 1815/24 · Etching and aquatint printed in sepia ink on ivory wove paper · Image: 21.1 × 32.4 cm (8 5/16 × 12 13/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Goya took the common proverb of the title to compare Carnival's indulgence with the fate of Catholicism itself. The Proverbs, like the Disasters of War, were published only after his death.
