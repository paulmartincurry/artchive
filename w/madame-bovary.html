The Artchive
human speech is like a cracked tin kettle, on which we hammer out tunes to make bears dance when we long to move the stars.

Madame Bovary (Part II, Ch. 12)

Gustave Flaubert · 1857

Translated by Eleanor Marx-Aveling, 1886

Flaubert began his first novel in 1850, after returning from Egypt, and it took him five years to write. It ran in the Revue de Paris from 1 October to 15 December 1856, and public prosecutors charged that it was obscene. He was acquitted on 7 February 1857, and the book came out in two volumes that April. The story was partly drawn from the life of a schoolfriend who became a doctor.
