The Hanging (The Miseries and Misfortunes of War, plate 11)

Jacques Callot · 1633 · Etching; second state of three (Lieure) · sheet: 10.5 x 21 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Callot was living in Nancy in 1633 when Cardinal Richelieu sent an army to occupy his native Lorraine, and the eighteen prints of this series were published that year in Paris under royal licence. Each image is only about 8 by 18 centimetres. The hanging tree was later parodied in William Hogarth's early engraving on the South Sea Scheme, with a wheel of fortune in its place.
