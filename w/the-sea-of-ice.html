The Sea of Ice

Caspar David Friedrich · 1823 · Oil on canvas · Hamburger Kunsthalle

Free to use

Caspar David Friedrich never visited the Arctic. In the winter of 1820–21 he made oil studies of ice floes on the river Elbe near Dresden, and those studies were probably worked into this picture. It came out of a commission from the collector Johann Gottlob von Quandt, who wanted one painting of southern nature and one of northern. The canvas was first exhibited in Prague in 1824, and it was still unsold when Friedrich died in 1840.
