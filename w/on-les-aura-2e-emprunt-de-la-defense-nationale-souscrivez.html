On les aura! 2e Emprunt de la Défense Nationale. Souscrivez

Abel Faivre · 1916 · Lithograph · 113.8 × 80.2 cm · Musée Carnavalet, Paris

Free to use

Abel Faivre signed and dated this poster in 1916 for France's second national defence loan, and Devambez printed it in Paris. Faivre was born in Lyon and drew cartoons for Le Rire, L'Écho de Paris and Le Figaro. The sheet is 113.8 centimetres tall. This impression is in the Musée Carnavalet.
