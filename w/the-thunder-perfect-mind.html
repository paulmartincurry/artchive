The Artchive
For I am the first and the last.
I am the honored one and the scorned one.

The Thunder, Perfect Mind

Anonymous Gnostic Visionary · ca. 2nd–3rd century CE

Translated by George W. MacRae · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

This text was found in 1945 in the Nag Hammadi library, written in Coptic. Its metre and phrasing have led to the theory that it was first composed in Greek. Estimates of its age run from the first or second century to no later than about 350 CE. Some scholars argue that it should not be classed as Gnostic.
