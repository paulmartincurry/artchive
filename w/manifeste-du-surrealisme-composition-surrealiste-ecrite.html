Manifeste du surréalisme: Composition surréaliste écrite

André Breton · 1924

Breton had first put automatic writing into practice in Les Champs magnétiques, written with Philippe Soupault. He had launched the review Littérature with Soupault and Louis Aragon in 1919. The manifesto's publication in 1924 came with the founding of the magazine La Révolution surréaliste and the Bureau of Surrealist Research.
