The Tower of Babel (Vienna)

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1563 · Oil paint · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Pieter Bruegel signed and dated this panel on a stone block in front of the king. He painted it for the Antwerp banker Nicolaes Jonghelinck, who owned no fewer than 16 of his paintings. Bruegel had been in Rome in 1552–1553, where he painted an earlier Tower of Babel in miniature on ivory, now lost, and the arches of this tower recall the Colosseum. A second surviving version in Rotterdam is half as wide and half as high.
