Curiosity Self-Portrait at John Klein

Curiosity rover, NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS · February and May 2013 · Mosaic of digital exposures (Mars Hand Lens Imager)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS

Curiosity took this self-portrait with the Mars Hand Lens Imager, a camera on a turret at the end of its arm. It combines dozens of exposures from 3 February 2013 with three from 10 May 2013. The gray powder and two holes are where the rover drilled into the rock target John Klein.
