Lacertilia (Kunstformen der Natur, plate 79)

Ernst Haeckel & Adolf Giltsch · 1899–1904 · Colour lithograph · Smithsonian Libraries and Archives

Free to use

Figure 3 is the flying dragon lizard of Java. Haeckel's text for the plate explains that these lizards fly from tree to tree on a broad fold of skin at each side, stretched open by movable ribs.
