Ballet Mécanique, frame at 0:49

Fernand Léger and Dudley Murphy · 1924 · Silent film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

The film premiered in a silent version in Vienna on 24 September 1924, at an exhibition of new theatre technique presented by Frederick Kiesler. Its opening moments credit the painter Fernand Léger and the filmmaker Dudley Murphy equally, with Man Ray contributing to the camerawork. Léger had almost died in a mustard gas attack at Verdun in September 1916. George Antheil's score for the film runs close to 30 minutes against its 19, and Paul Lehrman first joined the two on one print in 2000.
