'Sir, I exist!' — 'However,' replied the universe,
'the fact has not created in me a sense of obligation.'

A Man Said to the Universe

Stephen Crane · 1899

In 1899 Crane was twenty-seven, living in England, short of money and in poor health. He had been a war correspondent, and on the way to Cuba his ship, the SS Commodore, had sunk off the coast of Florida and left him adrift for thirty hours in a dinghy. He died of tuberculosis in a sanatorium in the Black Forest in June 1900, aged twenty-eight. He was the fourteenth and last child of a Methodist minister.
