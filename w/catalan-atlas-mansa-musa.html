Catalan Atlas: Mansa Musa

Attributed to Abraham Cresques · 1375 · Pen with coloured inks on parchment · Bibliothèque nationale de France

The king on sheet 6 of the atlas is Mansa Musa, ruler of the Mali Empire, who had been dead for about forty years when the map was drawn in 1375. He went on pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324 with a vast supply of gold, and his gifts in Cairo are said to have affected the value of gold in Egypt. After that, traders from Venice, Granada and Genoa added Timbuktu to their maps.
