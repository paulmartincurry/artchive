Reliquary Bust of Saint Yrieix

Maker unknown · ca. 1220–40, with later grill · Silver and gilded silver with rock crystal, gems, and glass · Overall: 15 × 9 3/16 × 10 1/4 in. · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The silver head is built over a wooden core almost as large as the bust itself, about thirty-seven centimetres tall. The grill was added later. J. Pierpont Morgan gave the reliquary to the Metropolitan Museum in 1917.
