Master Shi Planting Pines

Shitao · 1674 · Handscroll, light colour on paper · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Shitao was born in 1642 into the Ming imperial clan, two years before the dynasty fell to the Manchus. He had become a Buddhist monk by 1651 and moved from Wuchang to Anhui in the 1660s. Over his life he used more than two dozen names.
