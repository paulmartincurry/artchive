Flos Solis Prolifer (Hortus Eystettensis)

Basilius Besler & Heinrich Ulrich · 1613 · Hand-coloured copper engraving · Universitätsbibliothek Eichstätt-Ingolstadt

Free to use

Summer fills 184 plates with 505 plants. After the bishop who commissioned the book died, the work moved to Nuremberg and a new team of engravers, among them Heinrich Ulrich, who put his signature on a leaf in this plate.
