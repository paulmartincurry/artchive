O my Lord, if I worship Thee from fear of Hell, burn me in Hell, and if I worship Thee from hope of Paradise, exclude me thence, but if I worship Thee for Thine own sake then withhold not from me Thine Eternal Beauty.

If I Worship Thee from Fear of Hell (Prayers)

Rābiʻa al-ʻAdawiyya (attributed) · 8th century; English 1928

Translated by Margaret Smith

Very little is known about Rabi'a's life, and no written work has been linked to her for certain; stories about her grew over the centuries, and much of the poetry given under her name is of unknown origin. She was a freed slave of the tribe of Qays ibn ʿAdī and lived in Basra, where she is said to have died in 801 at about eighty years old.
