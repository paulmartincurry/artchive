Codex Borgia, Page 29

Maker unknown · Pre-Columbian · Paint on gesso over animal skin · 27 × 27 cm (each sheet) · Vatican Apostolic Library

Free to use

Pages 29 to 46 of the Codex Borgia are turned at right angles to the rest of the book: the top of the section is the right side of this page, so a reader must rotate the manuscript 90 degrees to see it. All but the end sheets are painted on both sides, giving 76 pages. A gloss on page 68 in faulty Italian suggests a sixteenth-century Spanish priest had the book in hand.
