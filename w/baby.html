Baby (Cradle)

Gustav Klimt · 1917–18 · Oil on canvas · 110.9 × 110.4 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The canvas is almost exactly square, about 111 centimetres each way, and is dated 1917 or 1918. On 11 January 1918 Klimt suffered a stroke that paralysed his right side, and he died on 6 February, aged fifty-five. A Vienna gallery sold the painting in 1919 to Otto and Eugenia Primavesi. Josef Urban brought it to the United States, and it came to the National Gallery of Art in 1978.
