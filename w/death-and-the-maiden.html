Death and the Maiden

Hans Baldung Grien · 1517 · Tempera on limewood · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

The panel is limewood and small, 30.3 by 14.7 centimetres, painted in tempera. Hans Baldung came back to this subject several times. Above Death's head he wrote a line in German telling the woman that this is where she must go.
