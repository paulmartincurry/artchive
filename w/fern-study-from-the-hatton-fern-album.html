Fern Study from the Hatton Fern Album

Maker unknown · c. 1850 · Cyanotype and fern · Template paper: 37.4 × 26 cm (14 3/4 × 10 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Along with the blue cyanotype print, the album page holds a fern itself. The print measures about 23 by 18 centimetres on a mount sheet 37 centimetres tall. It was made in England by a maker whose name is not recorded, and the Art Institute bought it with the Henry Foundation Fund.
