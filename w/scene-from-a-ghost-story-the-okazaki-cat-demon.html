Scene from a Ghost Story: The Okazaki Cat Demon

Utagawa Kuniyoshi · c. 1850 · Diptych of woodblock prints; ink and color on paper · Each sheet 36.5 × 25.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Kuniyoshi made this diptych of a ghost-story scene around 1850. A few years later, in 1856, palsy left him with great difficulty moving his limbs, and his line is said to have grown weaker afterwards. The Metropolitan Museum of Art bought the print in 1929 through the Fletcher Fund.
