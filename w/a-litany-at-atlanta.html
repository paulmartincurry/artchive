Sit not longer blind, Lord God, deaf to our prayer and dumb to our dumb suffering. Surely Thou, too, art not white, O Lord, a pale, bloodless, heartless thing!

A Litany at Atlanta (closing)

W. E. B. Du Bois · 1906 (Darkwater, 1920)

Du Bois wrote the litany after white mobs rampaged through Atlanta in September 1906, leaving more than 25 people dead. He was teaching at Atlanta University, and when the rioting broke out he bought a Winchester double-barrelled shotgun and two dozen rounds of buckshot. The piece was later collected in Darkwater, published in 1920.
