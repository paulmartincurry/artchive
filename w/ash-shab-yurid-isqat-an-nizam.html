Ash-shaʻb yurīd isqāṭ an-niẓām

Arab Spring · 2010–11

Arabic for the people want to topple the regime. The slogan of the Arab Spring first emerged in the Tunisian Revolution, and the chant echoed for weeks along Avenue Habib Bourguiba in Tunis. In Egypt after Mubarak, some protesters changed its last word to aim it at Field Marshal Mohamed Tantawi, head of the ruling military council.
