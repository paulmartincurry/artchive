And they did to it what is always done
To the learned and eloquent.
They took a cage with stout bars
And shut it up inside.

The Red Cockatoo

Bai Juyi (Po Chü-i) · 9th century

Translated by Arthur Waley, 1918

Bai Juyi spent his working life as a government official in Tang China, including terms as governor of three provinces. He wrote over 2,800 poems and had them copied and distributed so that they would survive. It is said that he would rewrite any part of a poem that one of his servants could not understand.
