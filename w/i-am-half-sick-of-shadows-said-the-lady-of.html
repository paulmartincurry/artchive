I Am Half-Sick of Shadows, Said the Lady of Shalott

John William Waterhouse · 1915 · Oil on canvas · 100.3 × 73.7 cm · Art Gallery of Ontario, Toronto

Free to use

It is the third painting Waterhouse made from Tennyson's poem The Lady of Shalott, and shows an earlier moment than his pictures of 1888 and 1894: she is still in her tower, weaving, seeing the world only in the mirror. The title quotes the poem.
