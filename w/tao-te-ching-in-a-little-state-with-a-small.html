There should be a neighbouring state within sight, and the voices of the fowls and dogs should be heard all the way from it to us, but I would make the people to old age, even to death, not have any intercourse with it.

Tao Te Ching — In a little state with a small population (Ch. 80)

Laozi (Lao Tzu) · 4th–3rd century BC

Translated by James Legge

The oldest manuscripts of the book, bamboo slips found in October 1993 in a tomb at Guodian in Hubei and dated to around 300 BC, hold 31 of its chapters, none later than chapter 66. Chapters 70 to 81 were possibly not yet composed when the slips were copied. The man buried in the tomb was an elderly noble scholar who taught a royal prince.
