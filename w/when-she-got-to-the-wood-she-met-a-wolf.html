When She Got to the Wood, She Met a Wolf (Red Riding Hood)

Arthur Rackham · published 1909 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

French-speaking peasants were telling this tale in the 10th century, when the cathedral schoolmaster Egbert of Liège wrote it down. Charles Perrault was the first to put it into literary French, and the Brothers Grimm retold it in the 19th century. Rackham's plate was printed in the 1909 edition of the Grimms' tales; a digitized copy of that book is missing several plates, this one among them.
