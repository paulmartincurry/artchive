Edward H. White II, Gemini IV Extravehicular Activity

NASA · 3 June 1965 · Photograph

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA

On 3 June 1965 Ed White became the first American to walk in space, floating outside Gemini IV for about 23 minutes while James McDivitt stayed inside. He was held by a 25-foot umbilical line and a 23-foot tether, wrapped together in gold tape, and moved himself with a hand-held manoeuvring unit; his visor was plated with gold against the sun. White was thirty-four. He died in the Apollo fire at Cape Kennedy on 27 January 1967.
