Travelers Among Mountains and Streams

Fan Kuan · ca. 1000 · Ink on silk · National Palace Museum

Free to use

This hanging scroll is about six and three-quarter feet tall and two and a half feet wide, and it may be the only work by Fan Kuan that survives. A packhorse train comes out of the wood at the foot of the cliff. His signature is half hidden in the painting and was found only in 1958.
