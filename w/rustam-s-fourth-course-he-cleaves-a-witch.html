Rustam's Fourth Course: He Cleaves a Witch (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Qadimi · c. 1525 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 28.5 × 18.6 cm; page 47.3 × 31.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The painting, on folio 120 verso, is attributed to Qadimi. When the finished book was sent as a gift to the Ottoman sultan Selim II, Ottoman sources record that it arrived in February 1568 with 34 camels and other gifts.
