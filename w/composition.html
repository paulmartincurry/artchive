Jaromír Funke · 1927–1929 · gelatin silver print, silver bromide, photogram · Image: 38.2 × 27.7 cm (15 1/16 × 10 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

In the late 1920s Jaromír Funke arranged panes and spheres of glass, bottles and other objects and photographed the reflected light and the shadows they cast. He had studied medicine and law without graduating, and in 1922 decided to work as a freelance photographer. He died in Kolín in March 1945, when the electricity to the hospital where he was undergoing abdominal surgery was cut off after an Allied airstrike. He was forty-eight.
