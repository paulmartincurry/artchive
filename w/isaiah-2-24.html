they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.

Isaiah 2:2–4

Book of Isaiah (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · ca. 740 BCE (trans. 1611)

Almost the same words appear in the Book of Micah, chapter 4, verses 1 to 3. The Book of Joel turns verse 4 around, calling for ploughshares and pruning hooks to be beaten into swords and spears. A bronze statue in the garden of the United Nations, a gift from the Soviet Union sculpted by Evgeniy Vuchetich, shows a man hammering a sword into a ploughshare.
