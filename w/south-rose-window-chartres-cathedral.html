South Rose Window, Chartres Cathedral

Maker unknown · 13th century · Stained glass

Free to use with credit. Photo: PtrQs, CC BY-SA 3.0

Pierre de Dreux paid for the windows of the south transept, and his family's coat of arms is set in quatrefoils within the rose. Most of the cathedral's windows were probably made between 1205 and 1240.
