Eryngium giganteum (Urformen der Kunst, plate 66)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · 26 × 19 cm (10 1/4 × 7 1/2 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blossfeldt kept a log of the common and scientific name of every plant he photographed, and made roughly 6,000 photographs in all. Recognition came only in his sixties, after the gallerist Karl Nierendorf began to represent him in 1925.
