Chrysanthemum

Piet Mondrian · 1906–42 · Oil on canvas · 41.5 × 38.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Mondrian painted chrysanthemums throughout his career. He added the initials and the date 1906 near the bottom edge some time after painting the flower, and the museum says the picture may have been produced as late as 1942, the year he exhibited it in New York. Leonard C. Hanna Jr. bought it from the Valentine Gallery in 1947 and bequeathed it to Cleveland.
