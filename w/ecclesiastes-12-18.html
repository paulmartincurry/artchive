Ecclesiastes 12:1–8

Qoheleth (Book of Ecclesiastes) · c. 450–180 BCE; KJV 1611

Verses 1 to 7 of this chapter are a single sentence in the Hebrew, and a Sumerian poem treats the same subject in the same style. Verse 8 repeats the words of Ecclesiastes 1:2, so one line frames the body of the book. The six verses after it are an epilogue. The speaker of the book is called Qoheleth, the Teacher, and it was probably composed between the 5th and 2nd centuries BCE.
