“You are loosed from your moorings, and are free; I am fast in my chains, and am a slave!

Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass — You are loosed from your moorings (Ch. X)

Frederick Douglass · 1845

In 1833 Douglass was sent to the Mount Misery plantation of Edward Covey, who had a reputation as a slave-breaker and whipped him so often that his wounds had little time to heal. Douglass was sixteen when he fought back. He escaped on September 3, 1838, on a northbound train from Baltimore, dressed in a sailor's uniform, and the whole journey to New York took less than 24 hours.
