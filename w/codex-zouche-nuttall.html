Codex Zouche-Nuttall (page 20)

Maker unknown · Probably 14th century · Paint on animal skin · 19 × 23.5 cm (each section) · British Museum, London

Free to use

The Codex Zouche-Nuttall was probably made in the fourteenth century on 47 sections of animal skin, each 19 by 23.5 centimetres, folded like a screen and painted on both sides. It records the genealogies, alliances and conquests of rulers of Tilantongo in highland Oaxaca, especially the warrior Lord Eight Deer Jaguar Claw. It was identified at the Monastery of San Marco in Florence in 1854, and the British Museum acquired it in 1917.
