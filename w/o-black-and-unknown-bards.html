O black and unknown bards of long ago,
How came your lips to touch the sacred fire?
How, in your darkness, did you come to know
The power and beauty of the minstrel's lyre?

O Black and Unknown Bards

James Weldon Johnson · 1917 (Fifty Years and Other Poems)

Johnson spent 1906 to 1913 as a United States consul, first at Puerto Cabello in Venezuela and then at Corinto in Nicaragua, and wrote much of his first poetry collection, Fifty Years, during those years. Before that he had written the lyrics of roughly 200 songs for Broadway with his brother Rosamond and Bob Cole.
