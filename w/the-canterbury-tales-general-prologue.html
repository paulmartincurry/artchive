Whan that Aprille with his shoures sote
The droghte of Marche hath perced to the rote,
And bathed every veyne in swich licour,
Of which vertu engendred is the flour;

The Canterbury Tales — General Prologue (opening)

Geoffrey Chaucer · c. 1387–1400

Chaucer was a civil servant, the son of a London wine merchant. He began the Tales in the years when he became Controller of Customs and Justice of the Peace, and then Clerk of the King's Works. The Prologue introduces some thirty pilgrims and sets out a plan of four stories from each, about 120 in all. Twenty-four were written, and the work was unfinished at his death in 1400.
