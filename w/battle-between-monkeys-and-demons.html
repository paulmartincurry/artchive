Battle Between Monkeys and Demons (Shangri Ramayana)

Maker unknown · c. 1700 · Gum tempera and ink on paper · 22.2 × 32.1 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The back of the page is numbered 92 and marked Lanka, in Devanagari and in Takri script. It was in the royal library of Mandi until 1969; Amrit Kaur, Maharani of Mandi, then sold it in London to a German scientist. It passed through Sotheby's London in 2009, and the London dealer Francesca Galloway sold it to Cleveland in 2025. Rama's army, mostly monkeys from the forest, fought with nothing but trees and rocks.
