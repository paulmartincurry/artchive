Mother and Child Figure

Kongo-style maker · late 1800s–early 1900s · Wood and paint · 64.7 cm (25 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The figure may have been carved in honour of a woman after her death, or after the death of a chief. Kongo society is matrilineal, so a chief's right to power passes through the female line.
