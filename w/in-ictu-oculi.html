In Ictu Oculi (In the Blink of an Eye)

Juan de Valdés Leal · 1670–1672 · Oil on canvas · Hospital de la Caridad, Seville

Free to use

The Brotherhood of Charity in Seville ordered this painting for its hospital, a home for the old and a burial ground for paupers. It is one of a pair, and both canvases still hang just inside the hospital entrance. The open book at the front shows Rubens's designs for the triumphal arches put up in Antwerp in 1634. A close copy made in the 1860s or 1870s is in the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
