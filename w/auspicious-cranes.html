Auspicious Cranes

Emperor Huizong · 1112 · Handscroll, ink and colour on silk · Liaoning Provincial Museum, Shenyang

Free to use

The inscription records that a flock of cranes circled and settled on one of the palace buildings, and Emperor Huizong wrote a poem to mark the omen. He was twenty-nine in 1112, and his collection held more than 6,000 paintings. Fifteen years later the Jurchens took his capital; he spent his last eight years as a prisoner in the far north and died there in 1135.
