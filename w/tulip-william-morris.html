Tulip

William Morris · 1875 · roller printed cotton · Overall: 76.1 × 95.3 cm (29 15/16 × 37 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

William Morris designed this pattern in 1875, the year he took total control of his firm and renamed it Morris & Co. He was forty-one. Textiles were among the most profitable goods the company sold. This piece of cotton, 76 by 95 centimetres, was given to Cleveland in 1937.
