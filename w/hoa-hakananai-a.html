Hoa Hakananai'a

Maker unknown · ca. 1000–1200 CE (Birdman Reliefs Added ca. 1600) · Basalt · British Museum

Free to use with credit. Photo: Osama Shukir Muhammed Amin FRCP(Glasg), CC BY-SA 4.0

The crew of HMS Topaze found the statue in November 1868, buried to about half its height inside a stone house at Orongo. They dug it out, dragged it downhill on a sledge and rafted it to the ship. The carvings on its back were painted red on white when Europeans first saw them, and most of the paint washed off on the raft. It stands 2.42 metres high and weighs 4.2 tonnes. Most statues on the island are of reddish tuff, and no laboratory analysis has confirmed that this one is basalt. The Governor of Easter Island asked for its return in 2018.
