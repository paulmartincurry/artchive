Café Terrace at Night

Vincent van Gogh · 1888 · Oil on canvas · Kröller-Müller Museum

Free to use

Vincent van Gogh painted this on the spot at night, in mid-September 1888, with his easel at the north-eastern corner of the Place du Forum in Arles. It is the first painting in which he used a starry background. He did not sign it, but he described it in three letters. It was first exhibited in 1891, under the title Coffeehouse, in the evening.
