House by the Railroad

Edward Hopper · 1925 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

The house said to be the model for this painting is a Victorian mansion in Haverstraw, New York, and it is still standing. The canvas hung in the Museum of Modern Art's first show made up solely of American art, in 1929–30. Stephen Clark, an heir to the Singer sewing machine company, gave it to the museum in 1930, among the first works to enter the collection.
