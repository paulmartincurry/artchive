The Monkey Bridge

Katsushika Taito II · early 1830s · Color woodblock print · 26 x 23.5 cm (10 1/4 x 9 1/4 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Taito II studied with Hokusai. The bridge is the Monkey Bridge in Kai Province, west of Tokyo, and the moon set below it shows how deep the gorge is. This impression was trimmed before it reached the museum.
