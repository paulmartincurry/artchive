Below the thunders of the upper deep;
Far, far beneath in the abysmal sea.

The Kraken

Alfred, Lord Tennyson · 1830

Tennyson was about twenty and a student at Trinity College, Cambridge, when this appeared in Poems, Chiefly Lyrical, the first collection he published on his own. The year before, he had won the Chancellor's Gold Medal at Cambridge for a poem. He left the university without a degree. The Kraken has fifteen lines and is still counted as a sonnet.
