Vision After the Sermon (Jacob Wrestling with the Angel)

Paul Gauguin · 1888 · Oil on canvas · National Galleries Scotland

Free to use

Paul Gauguin painted this at Pont-Aven in Brittany in 1888, the year he met the young painter Émile Bernard there. Bernard had begun working in a simplified style drawn from Japanese prints, and Gauguin followed his example. Gauguin owned woodblock prints by Hiroshige and Hokusai, and Bernard named the sumo wrestlers in Hokusai's Manga as the source of the two wrestling figures. The canvas is 72.2 by 91 centimetres.
