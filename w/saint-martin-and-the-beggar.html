Saint Martin and the Beggar

El Greco · 1597/1599 · Oil on canvas · 193.5 × 103 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The Chapel of Saint Joseph in Toledo commissioned the canvas on 9 November 1597, and it stayed there for three centuries. The directors of the chapel sold it in 1906 to a Paris dealer, who sold it the same year to Peter A. B. Widener. El Greco also painted a smaller version, now in the Art Institute of Chicago.
