The Dream of the Shepherd

Ferdinand Hodler · 1896 · Oil on canvas · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The canvas is two and a half metres tall, and it was long dated to 1893. Ferdinand Hodler was forty-three when he showed it at the Swiss national exhibition in Geneva in 1896. It belonged to the Kunsthandlung Rath in Basel before going to a private collector in the 1920s, and the Met bought it in 2013.
