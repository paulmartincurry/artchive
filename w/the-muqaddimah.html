It should be known that history is a discipline that has a great number of approaches. Its useful aspects are very many. Its goal is distinguished.

The Muqaddimah (On the Science of History)

Ibn Khaldun · 1377

Translated by Franz Rosenthal, 1958 · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The first version was written at Qalʿat ibn Salama, where Ibn Khaldun shut himself away for almost four years after withdrawing from political life. By his own account the writing took six months. He was forty-five when the draft was finished in 1377, and it was meant only as the introduction to a world history. It was not printed until 1857, in Cairo.
