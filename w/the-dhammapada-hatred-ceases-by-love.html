For hatred does not cease by hatred at any time: hatred ceases by love, this is an old rule.

The Dhammapada — Hatred Ceases by Love (verses 3–6)

The Dhammapada · Date uncertain; English 1870

Translated by F. Max Müller

According to tradition, the Buddha spoke these verses on various occasions. A commentary of the 4th or 5th century CE, attributed to Buddhaghosa, tells 305 stories that give the setting for them.
