Hitched to Everything Else in the Universe

John Muir · 1911

Muir built himself a small cabin on Yosemite Creek and laid it out so that part of the stream ran through a corner of the room. He lived in it for two years, unmarried and often out of work. He had been born in Dunbar, Scotland, came to a Wisconsin farm with his family at eleven, and left university without a degree. The book he made from this period was published in 1911, three years before his death.
