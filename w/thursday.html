And if I loved you Wednesday,
Well, what is that to you?
I do not love you Thursday--
So much is true.

And why you come complaining
Is more than I can see.
I loved you Wednesday,--yes--but what
Is that to me?

Thursday

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1920

Thursday is from A Few Figs From Thistles, Millay's collection of 1920, which drew controversy for its treatment of female sexuality and feminism. She took her poems on highly successful reading tours across the country. In January 1921 she went to Paris, where she befriended the sculptors Constantin Brâncuși and Thelma Wood and the photographer Man Ray.
