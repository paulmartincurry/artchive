Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl

Harriet Jacobs (Linda Brent) · 1861

Harriet Jacobs wrote the book at Idlewild, the house in New York state where she worked for the writer Nathaniel Parker Willis. She took the manuscript to England in May 1858 and found no publisher. Two American firms then accepted it and failed in turn, the second after the plates had been cast, so Jacobs bought the plates and had the book printed and bound herself. It came out in January 1861, nearly four years after she finished it, with no author's name on the title page and every personal name but two changed.
