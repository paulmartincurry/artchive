South Wind, Clear Sky (Red Fuji)

Katsushika Hokusai · ca. 1830–32 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · The Met

Hokusai was about seventy when this sheet was published as part of Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji, a series he made in answer to a boom in domestic travel in Japan. He had begun painting at around six and made more than 30,000 paintings, sketches, prints and book images over his life, under at least thirty different names. The Metropolitan Museum bought this impression in 1914.
