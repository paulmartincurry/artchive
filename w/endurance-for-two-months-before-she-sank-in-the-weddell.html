Endurance for Two Months before She Sank in the Weddell Sea

Frank Hurley · 1915, copyrighted c. 1916 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Planks prop the listing ship in the ice. Shackleton gave the order to abandon Endurance on 27 October 1915, the date the Library of Congress title gives for her sinking. In Shackleton's book South the picture is titled The Beginning of the End. Hurley had run away from home at fourteen to work at the Lithgow steel mill.
