When Abraham Lincoln was shoveled into the tombs, he forgot the copperheads and the assassin... in the dust, in the cool tombs.

Cool Tombs

Carl Sandburg · 1918

Sandburg won three Pulitzer Prizes, two for poetry and one for his biography of Abraham Lincoln, whose first part, The Prairie Years, came out in 1926. He had left Lombard College in Galesburg without a degree in 1903.
