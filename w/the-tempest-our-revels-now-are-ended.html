We are such stuff
As dreams are made on, and our little life
Is rounded with a sleep.

The Tempest — Our Revels Now Are Ended (Act IV, Scene I)

William Shakespeare · c. 1611

The Tempest was one of the last plays Shakespeare wrote alone, and only his collaborations with John Fletcher came after it. Edward Blount entered it into the Stationers' Register on 8 November 1623, one of sixteen Shakespeare plays he registered that day. The First Folio lists it first among the comedies.
