Bharat Mata (Banga Mata)

Abanindranath Tagore · 1905 · Watercolor on paper · Victoria Memorial

Free to use

This watercolour is 26.67 cm high and 15.24 cm wide. When it was first published, in the periodical Prabasi, the caption read Matrimurti, the figure of the Mother, and the artist was not named. Abanindranath Tagore had thought of calling it Banga Mata, Mother of Bengal, and it was Sister Nivedita who renamed it Bharat Mata. He had learned the pale wash he used here from the Japanese painter Yokoyama Taikan, whom he met in Calcutta around 1902-1903. In 1905-1906 an enlarged copy on a silk banner was used to raise donations for the Swadeshi movement.
