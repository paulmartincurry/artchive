Candlestand in the Shape of a Chimera (Bixie)

Maker unknown · 265–316 CE · Glazed stoneware with molded and incised decoration, Yue ware · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The candlestand is just over eight centimetres tall and was moulded in two parts, with incised lines on each side of the body for wings. Candlestands of this shape have been found in tombs, though they may also have been used in daily life. The Cleveland Museum of Art bought this one in 1983 from the New York dealer Walter Hochstadter.
