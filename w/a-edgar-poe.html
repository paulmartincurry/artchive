The Artchive

A Edgar Poe

Félix Vallotton · 1894 · Woodcut on brown wove paper · Block: 15.9 × 12.2 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The block is 15.9 centimetres high and is printed on brown wove paper, with the dedication A EDGAR POE cut into it under the head. Vallotton was twenty-eight in 1894. Five years earlier, at the Paris Universal Exposition of 1889, he had seen a gallery of Japanese prints, particularly works by Hokusai, which were to influence his work greatly. Frank Anderson Trapp gave this impression to the National Gallery of Art in 2003.
