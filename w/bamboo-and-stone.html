Bamboo and Stone

Guan Daosheng · Yuan dynasty (before 1319) · Ink on paper · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Guan Daosheng seems to have taken up painting around 1296, ten years after her marriage to the court official and painter Zhao Mengfu. Bamboo was an unusual subject for a woman at the time. The emperor commissioned her to copy the Thousand Character Classic, and in 1317 the court gave her the title Madam of the Wei Kingdom. She died in 1319, aged 58, on an official boat on the way home. Few of her paintings remain.
