The Bathers

Edgar Degas · 1885/95 · Pastel and charcoal on tan wove paper · 113.4 × 115.7 cm (44 11/16 × 45 9/16 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Degas built it by laying pastel in layers of fixative on translucent tracing paper, which he pieced together and mounted. It was found in his studio at his death, perhaps deliberately unfinished.
