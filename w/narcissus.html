Narcissus

Caravaggio · 1600 · Oil on canvas · Galleria Nazionale d'Arte Antica

No one attached Caravaggio's name to this canvas until 1916, when the art historian Roberto Longhi attributed it to him. It measures 110 by 92 centimetres. The poet Giambattista Marino, a friend of the painter, wrote a description of Narcissus.
