Ceiling Design with Fretwork Balcony and Open Sky, possibly for Conservatory/ Music Room, or Dining Room

Frederick Crace · ca. 1820 · Pen and black ink, brush and grey wash, watercolor on thick white wove paper · 40.3 × 35.0 cm (15 7/8 × 13 3/4 in.) · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

Frederick Crace, about forty-one at the time, was the decorator who made the chinoiserie interiors of the Brighton Pavilion for George IV. His father, the London decorator John C. Crace, had been hired for the Pavilion in 1788. This sheet carries a J. Whatman watermark, and the original album that held the group of drawings also survives in the museum.
