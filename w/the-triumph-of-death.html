The Triumph of Death

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · ca. 1562 · Oil on panel · Museo del Prado

Free to use

Five copies of this panel are known, all by later painters of the Bruegel family: three by Pieter Brueghel the Younger, one by Jan Brueghel the Elder and one by Jan Brueghel the Younger. Close study of them suggests the copyists did not work from the original but traced sketches from Bruegel's estate. The sketch is now lost and most likely ran over two sheets of paper. The original has been in the Museo del Prado in Madrid since 1827.
