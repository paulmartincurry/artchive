Joseph—Nez Percé

Edward S. Curtis · 1903 · Gelatin silver print · mount 22 × 16.3 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Edward S. Curtis registered the copyright on this portrait on 28 November 1903. Chief Joseph visited Seattle that year as the guest of Edmond Meany, a history professor at the University of Washington, and stayed in the Lincoln Hotel. He was sixty-three. He died on 21 September 1904 on the Colville Indian Reservation in Washington, where he had been sent far from his homeland in the Wallowa Valley.
