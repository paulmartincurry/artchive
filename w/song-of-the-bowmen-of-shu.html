When we set out, the willows were drooping with spring,
We come back in the snow,
We go slowly, we are hungry and thirsty,
Our mind is full of sorrow, who will know of our grief?

Song of the Bowmen of Shu

Ezra Pound · 1915

Song of the Bowmen of Shu is the opening poem of Cathay, which Elkin Mathews published in London on 6 April 1915 in a run of 1,000 copies. Ezra Pound made its fifteen poems from the notes of the American scholar Ernest Fenollosa, without himself knowing Chinese. Fenollosa had died two years before his widow gave Pound the papers late in 1913.
