Little Girl in a Blue Armchair

Mary Cassatt · 1878 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The girl was the daughter of friends of Edgar Degas, who found the model, advised on the background and painted part of it himself; cleaning and infra-red photography have since confirmed his hand. Cassatt sent the canvas to the American pavilion at the 1878 World's Fair in Paris and it was refused, by a jury of three that she said included a pharmacist. The dog is a Brussels Griffon, a breed she kept for the rest of her life. She held on to the painting until about 1903, when she sold it to the dealer Ambroise Vollard.
