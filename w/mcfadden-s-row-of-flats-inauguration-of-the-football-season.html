McFadden's Row of Flats: Inauguration of the Football Season in McFadden's Row

Richard F. Outcault · 1896 · Drawing on bristol board · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This is one of the first pages Outcault drew after Hearst's New York Journal hired him away from Pulitzer's New York World in 1896. The World owned the rights to Hogan's Alley, so he moved the Yellow Kid and his gang to McFadden's Row; a version of this drawing was printed on 15 November 1896. Outcault failed to copyright the Kid, and for about a year he appeared in both papers at once.
