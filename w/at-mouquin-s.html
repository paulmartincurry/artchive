At Mouquin's

William Glackens · 1905 · Oil on canvas · 122.4 × 92.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Mouquin's was the New York restaurant where William Glackens and his circle met. At the table are Jeanne Mouquin, the owner's wife, and James B. Moore, a restaurateur; the painter's wife, Edith, and the critic Charles Fitzgerald are reflected in the mirror behind them. The painting hung in the exhibition of The Eight in New York in February 1908, and the Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1925.
