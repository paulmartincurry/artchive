Before the Storm

Edward S. Curtis · 1906 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

The photograph was copyrighted on 19 December 1906 and printed as plate 9 in a supplement to the first volume of The North American Indian. That year J. P. Morgan gave Curtis 75,000 dollars for the series, to be paid over five years for fieldwork only. Morgan was to receive 25 sets and 500 original prints in return.
