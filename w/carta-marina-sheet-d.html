Carta Marina, Sheet D

Olaus Magnus · 1539 · Woodcut on paper, one of nine sheets · 56 × 41 cm (sheet); 170 × 125 cm (whole map) · Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Munich

Free to use

Olaus Magnus was a Catholic priest who settled in Rome in 1537 as secretary to his brother Johannes, the exiled Archbishop of Uppsala. When Johannes died in 1544, Pope Paul III made Olaus archbishop in his place, a title with nothing behind it because Sweden was no longer Catholic. The same pope gave the map a ten-year copyright. Oceanographers have since published studies on how truthfully it shows the currents between Iceland and the Faroe Islands.
