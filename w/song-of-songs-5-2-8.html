Song of Songs 5:2-8

Song of Solomon (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · c. 3rd century BCE; KJV 1611

The Song of Songs is one of the five scrolls, the megillot, in the Writings, the last section of the Hebrew Bible, and unlike any other book in it, it is erotic poetry. Scholars still debate whether it drew on Mesopotamian, Egyptian or Greek love poetry. For the Kabbalah, the Jewish mystical tradition, it is one of the overtly mystical books of the Bible.
