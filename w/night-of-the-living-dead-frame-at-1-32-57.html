Night of the Living Dead, frame at 1:32:57

George A. Romero · 1968 · Film, black and white (frame)

Free to use

American International Pictures turned the film down after the producers refused to soften it and reshoot the final scene. The Walter Reade Organization took it, changing the title from Night of the Flesh Eaters, and the copyright notice was accidentally deleted along with the old title, so the film entered the public domain on release. It premiered in Pittsburgh on 1 October 1968 and earned more than 250 times its budget.
