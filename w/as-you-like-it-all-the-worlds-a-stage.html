All the world’s a stage,
And all the men and women merely players;
They have their exits and their entrances,
And one man in his time plays many parts,

As You Like It — All the World’s a Stage (Act II, Scene VII)

William Shakespeare · c. 1599

The story comes from Thomas Lodge's pastoral romance Rosalynde, first published in 1590. The play was entered in the Register of the Stationers' Company on 4 August 1600 as a work to be stayed, and it was not printed until the First Folio of 1623, seven years after Shakespeare's death. There is no certain record of any performance before the Restoration.
