Girl in a Sailor's Blouse

Amedeo Modigliani · 1918 · Oil on canvas · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The only solo exhibition Modigliani had in his lifetime opened at the Galerie Berthe Weill in Paris in December 1917, the year before this picture. He was thirty-three or thirty-four when he painted it, and he had been with Jeanne Hébuterne since 1917. The canvas is 65.4 by 46.4 centimetres. Charles F. Iklé gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1960.
