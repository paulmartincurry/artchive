Death is like the balmy night,
Life is like the sultry day;
It is dark, and I am sleepy.
I am weary of the light.

Death Is Like the Balmy Night (Der Tod, das ist die kühle Nacht)

Heinrich Heine · 1826

Translated by Emma Lazarus

Heine's Book of Songs was published in Hamburg in 1827 and holds 237 poems written between 1817 and 1826, of which only 8 were new. The publisher, Julius Campe, was initially against the book but later changed his mind. Of the 237 poems, 142 deal with unrequited love.
