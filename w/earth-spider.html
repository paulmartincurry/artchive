Earth Spider (New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1889–92 · Woodcut, color · 31 × 20.8 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

A swordsman turns on the earth spider as it attacks. Yoshitoshi worked on New Forms of Thirty-Six Ghosts from 1889 to 1892, alongside the moon series, in the last and among the most productive years of his life.
