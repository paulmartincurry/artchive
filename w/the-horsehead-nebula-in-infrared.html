The Horsehead Nebula in Infrared

Hubble Space Telescope, NASA/ESA/STScI · released 19 April 2013 · Digital image in infrared light (Wide Field Camera 3)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/ESA/STScI

Hubble photographed the Horsehead Nebula in infrared light to mark the 23rd anniversary of its launch aboard the space shuttle Discovery. The picture was taken with the Wide Field Camera 3, added in 2009. The wisps along the upper ridge are lit from behind by Sigma Orionis, a young five-star system just above the frame.
