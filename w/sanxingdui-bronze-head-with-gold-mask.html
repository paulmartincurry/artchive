Sanxingdui Bronze Head with Gold Mask

Maker unknown · ca. 1200–1100 BCE

Free to use with credit. Photo: momo, CC BY 2.0

Local workers in Sichuan came upon the sacrificial pits at Sanxingdui by accident in 1986. The thousands of gold, bronze, jade and pottery objects inside had been broken, burned and carefully buried. At least six of the bronze heads originally had gold foil masks attached. Archaeologists believe the heads were mounted on wooden supports, perhaps dressed in clothing.
