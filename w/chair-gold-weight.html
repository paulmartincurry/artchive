Chair Gold Weight

Maker unknown · 18th–19th century · Brass · 4.8 × 2.9 × 3.2 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Akan goldweights were cast in brass and used as a system of measure in trade, and a complete small set was given to a newly married man so he could enter the merchant trade. Geometric weights are the oldest; figurative ones, showing people, animals and objects, first appear around 1600. Some four million are supposed to have been cast between 1400 and 1900. This chair is 4.8 centimetres high.
