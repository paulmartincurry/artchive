You, Song-jin, now think yourself reality, and your past life a dream only; you do not reckon yourself one and the same as the dream. Which shall I label the dream, you Song-jin, or you So-yoo?

The Cloud Dream of the Nine (the Teacher on the dream)

Kim Man-jung · 1600s

Translated by James S. Gale

Kim Man-jung lived from 1637 to 1692. Scholars disagree over whether the novel was first written in Korean or in classical Chinese; the oldest surviving text is in classical Chinese. James Gale's English translation was published in 1922.
