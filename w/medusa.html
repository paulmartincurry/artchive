Medusa

Caravaggio · c. 1597 · Oil on canvas mounted on wood (poplar shield) · 60 × 55 cm · Gallerie degli Uffizi, Florence

Free to use

Caravaggio painted on linen stretched over a round, convex shield of poplar wood, and mixed verdigris with lead-tin yellow for the green ground. Francesco Maria del Monte commissioned the shield as a gift for Ferdinando I de' Medici. This is the second of two versions, 60 by 55 centimetres and unsigned; the first, signed and slightly smaller, is privately owned.
