Saturn Devouring His Son

Francisco de Goya · 1819–1823 · Mural, transferred to canvas · Museo del Prado

Free to use

This was one of six pictures Goya painted on the walls of his dining room, in a house outside Madrid that he bought in 1819. He gave none of them a title; the names came from others after his death. The murals stayed on the plaster for fifty years and were deteriorating badly when a later owner, Baron Émile d'Erlanger, had them transferred to canvas. He showed them in Paris in 1878 and then gave them to the Spanish state.
