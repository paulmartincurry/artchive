Agnus Dei (Lamb of God)

Francisco de Zurbarán · 1635–1640 · Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado

Free to use

Francisco de Zurbarán painted the bound ram at least five times, and the earliest dated canvas is signed 1631. This one carries neither a date nor a signature. A wax seal still on the back shows that it once belonged to Ferdinand VII. It measures 38 centimetres by 62, and the Prado acquired it in 1986.
