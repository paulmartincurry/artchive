Rat Snake (Hebi); Lizard or Skink (Tokage), from the Picture Book of Crawling Creatures (Ehon mushi erami)

Kitagawa Utamaro · 1788 · Page from woodblock-printed book; ink and color on paper · 26.7 × 18.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is a page from a woodblock-printed book of 1788. In the mid-1780s Utamaro mostly drew illustrations for books of kyōka, a comic verse that parodied the classical waka. He was then a principal artist for the publisher Tsutaya Jūzaburō, in whose house he is estimated to have lived for about five years.
