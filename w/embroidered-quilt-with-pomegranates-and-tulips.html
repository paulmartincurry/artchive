Embroidered Quilt with Pomegranates and Tulips

Maker unknown · 1600s-1700s · plain weave: linen; embroidery, running stitch: silk · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Most of the dark brown silk that stitched the stems has rotted away, leaving the black ink outlines drawn on the linen underneath. Yellow and white thread stand in for the gilt and silver metal thread used in costly Ottoman court silks. The quilt is nearly two metres long, worked in running stitch. J. H. Wade and his wife gave it to Cleveland in 1916.
