Adam and Eve

Albrecht Dürer · 1504 · Engraving · 9 7/8 × 7 7/8in. (25.1 × 20cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

About ten years separate this plate from Dürer's first journey over the Alps to Venice, where he went to study and took an interest in the proportions of the body. He was thirty-two or thirty-three when he engraved it, and his father had died two years before. The print is 25.1 by 20 centimetres. It entered the Metropolitan Museum in 1919.
