As in the pebble sleeps the fiery spark,
The statue in the clay, inert and dark,
So slumbers the divine, O soul! in thee;

Deity (Deidad)

Amado Nervo · April 20, 1915 (Published 1916)

Translated by Alice Stone Blackwell, 1929

Nervo was forty-four when he wrote this. In his youth he had studied at the Catholic seminary in Zamora and meant to become a priest, until lack of money pushed him into a desk job in Tepic. His wife, Ana Cecilia Luisa Dailliez, whom he had met and married in Paris, had died in 1912. He died in Montevideo four years after the poem, and the president of Uruguay had his body carried home to Mexico on a cruiser.
