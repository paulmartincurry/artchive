The Melian Dialogue

Thucydides · ca. 416–400 BCE

Translated by Richard Crawley, 1874

Thucydides was an Athenian general who failed to save the city of Amphipolis from the Spartans in the winter of 424–423 BC and was exiled for twenty years. Exile let him travel among the Peloponnesian allies and see the war from both sides, and an income from his family's gold mines in Thrace paid for the work. By his own account the long speeches in his history are literary reconstructions and not records of what was said. The narrative breaks off in the middle of the year 411 BC.
