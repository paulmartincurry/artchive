Scenic Wallpaper Panel: Vues de Lyon

Sauvinet · 1821-25 · Color woodblock print on paper with canvas lining · 170.2 × 94.6 cm (67 × 37 1/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The Sauvinet firm printed this wallpaper panel in Paris. It stands 170 centimetres tall, and David Adler gave it to the Art Institute of Chicago.
