Album Page with Calligraphy Exercise (Siyah Mashq)

Muhammad Shah Qajar · October 1844 (Shawwal 1260 AH) · Ink, gold, and opaque watercolor on paper · 31 × 19.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Muhammad Shah, the third Qajar shah of Iran, wrote this practice page in October 1844, ten years into his reign, at the age of thirty-six. Born in Tabriz in 1808, he had become a skilled calligrapher and painter in the course of his princely education there. He died in 1848.
