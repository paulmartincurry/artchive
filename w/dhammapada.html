O house-builder, you are seen! You will not build this house again. For your rafters are broken and your ridgepole shattered.

Dhammapada (153–154)

Pāli Buddhist Canon · ca. 300 BCE

Translated by Acharya Buddharakkhita, 1985

The Dhammapada has 423 verses in 26 chapters, and no compiler is named. More than half of the verses also occur elsewhere in the Pāli Canon. The oldest manuscripts available date to about 1500 CE. A commentary of the fourth or fifth century, attributed to Buddhaghosa, adds 305 stories giving the occasion for the verses. In 1855 the Danish scholar Viggo Fausbøll produced an edition in Latin, the first critical edition of a Pāli text made in Europe.
