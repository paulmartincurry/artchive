Young Woman with a Water Pitcher

Johannes Vermeer · ca. 1662 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Vermeer was about thirty when he painted this, and he earned his living mainly as an art dealer rather than from his own few pictures. Henry Gurdon Marquand bought the painting at a Paris gallery in 1887 for $800. When he brought it to the United States it was the first Vermeer in America, and he gave it to the Metropolitan Museum two years later.
