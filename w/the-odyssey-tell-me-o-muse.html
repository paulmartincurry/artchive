Tell me, O Muse, of that ingenious hero who travelled far and wide after he had sacked the famous town of Troy.

The Odyssey — Tell me, O Muse (opening)

Homer · c. 8th century BCE

Translated by Samuel Butler

The Odyssey is 12,109 lines long. Its split into 24 books was probably made long after the poem itself. Because few people could read, audiences heard it performed by a singer or rhapsode, and it was probably recited without music. The oldest piece of a written copy known is a fragment made in Egypt in the third century BC. The first complete English translation appeared in 1616.
