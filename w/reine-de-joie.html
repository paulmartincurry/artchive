Reine de Joie

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec · 1892 · Color lithograph on tan wove paper · Image: 136.2 × 93 cm; Sheets as pieced, sight: 143.8 × 99 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The poster advertised Victor Joze's novel Reine de joie, whose banker character was loosely based on Baron de Rothschild and drew on anti-Semitic stereotypes of the 1890s. Protesters tore the posters off the walls of Paris bookshops. It was printed in sheets pieced together to a height of about 1.4 metres.
