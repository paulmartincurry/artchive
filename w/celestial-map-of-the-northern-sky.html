Celestial Map of the Northern Sky

Albrecht Dürer · 1515 · Woodcut in black on ivory laid paper · Art Institute of Chicago

Dürer's maps of the northern and southern skies were made as a presentation gift, along with a view of the terrestrial globe, for a humanist adviser to the Emperor Maximilian. They show the stars as if seen from space. They were not meant to be cut up and pasted onto spheres, but they were copied hundreds of times for that purpose. The printed image is 43.2 by 43 centimetres.
