Sneak home and pray you'll never know
The hell where youth and laughter go.

Suicide in the Trenches

Siegfried Sassoon · 1918

First published in the Cambridge Magazine on 23 February 1918, then in the collection Counter-Attack and Other Poems. It is twelve lines long. Seven months earlier Sassoon, a serving officer decorated for bravery on the Western Front, had made a lone public protest against the continuation of the war, and the army sent him to Craiglockhart War Hospital. There he met Wilfred Owen.
