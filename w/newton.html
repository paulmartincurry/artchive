Newton

William Blake · 1795–ca. 1805 · Colour print, ink and watercolour on paper · Tate

This is a monotype, one of twelve large colour prints William Blake made between 1795 and 1805. He first completed it in 1795, then reworked and reprinted it ten years later. Eduardo Paolozzi based a bronze sculpture on the print in 1995, and it stands in the piazza of the British Library.
