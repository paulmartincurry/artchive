Printer's Mark of Richard Pynson

Richard Pynson · ca. 1500

Free to use

Richard Pynson was born in Normandy and moved to London, where his first dated book came off the press in 1492. In 1501 he set up shop at the sign of the George in Fleet Street and stayed at that address until his death. He became King's Printer in 1506, on an annuity of two pounds, and published some 400 titles.
