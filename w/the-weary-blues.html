And far into the night he crooned that tune;
the stars went out and so did the moon.

The Weary Blues

Langston Hughes · 1925 (book pub. 1926)

Opportunity, the magazine of the Urban League, published this poem and gave it its prize for the best poem of the year. Langston Hughes wrote it in 1925, the year he turned twenty-four, and used its title for his first book the year after. In March 1958 he recorded it for an MGM album, reading over two jazz groups, one led by Leonard Feather and the other by Charles Mingus.
