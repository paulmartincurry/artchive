Pour me wine and tell me it is wine. Do not pour for me in secret if doing it in the open is possible

Wine Poem (Pour Me Wine)

Abu Nuwas · ca. 800 CE

Translated by Andras Hamori · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Abu Nuwas had an Arab father who had been a soldier and a Persian mother, and he learned his trade as the apprentice of a poet in Kufa. His wine poems were probably written to entertain the elite of Baghdad. He was jailed for his drunken exploits under the caliph al-Amin. A contemporary recorded that when his house was searched after his death it held a single book-cover with one quire of paper inside.
