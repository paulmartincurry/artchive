Splendor Solis, folio 33v

Maker unknown · 1582 · Parchment · British Library, London

Free to use

The painters of the first Splendor Solis took figures and settings from the work of Albrecht Dürer, Hans Holbein and Lucas Cranach. This red sun belongs to the Fifth Treatise in the London copy, dated 1582.
