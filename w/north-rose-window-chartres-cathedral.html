North Rose Window, Chartres Cathedral

Maker unknown · c. 1230 · Stained glass · 10.5 m in diameter

Free to use with credit. Photo: PtrQs, CC BY-SA 4.0

The window is 10.5 metres across, one of three great roses among the 167 windows of the cathedral. It sits over the north transept and, like the sculpture of the porch below, is dedicated to the Virgin. A ring of lozenges carries the arms of France and of Castile, the arms of the king and of his mother Blanche of Castile, which are taken as a sign that the royal family paid for it. The five lancets beneath it are each 7.5 metres high.
