Come now—if you are wounded, strip and show your wounds, and I will show mine.

Speech at Opis

Alexander the Great (via Arrian, Anabasis VII.9–10) · 324 BCE

Translated by Aubrey de Sélincourt · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The speech is dated to the year before Alexander's death. He had been on campaign for ten years, since the invasion of the Persian empire, and in India his troops had already forced him to turn back at the Beas River by threatening mutiny. He died in Babylon in June of the following year, aged thirty-two.
