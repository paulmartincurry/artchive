The Age of Bronze

Auguste Rodin · 1875–76 · bronze · Cleveland Museum of Art

Rodin began this figure in Belgium after two months in Italy, working from a Belgian soldier named August Neyt. It originally held a spear, which he removed. Critics alleged that he had cast it from a living model. This cast was commissioned from Rodin in 1916 by Ralph King, a trustee of the museum, and Rodin supervised the casting himself and gave it a deep reddish patina. With its base it stands 182 centimetres tall.
