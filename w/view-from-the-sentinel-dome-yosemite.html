View from the Sentinel Dome, Yosemite

Carleton Watkins · 1865–66 · Albumen silver prints from glass negatives · Image 1: 40.3 × 52.1 cm (15 7/8 × 20 1/2 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The view is made of three prints, each about 40 by 52 centimetres, laid side by side. In 1864 the California State Geological Survey had hired Watkins to photograph Yosemite. That same year Abraham Lincoln signed the Yosemite Grant, and Senator John Conness is said to have passed Watkins's photographs around Congress. A mountain in the park, Mount Watkins, is named after him.
