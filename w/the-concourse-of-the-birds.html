The Concourse of the Birds (Mantiq al-Tayr)

Habiballah of Sava · c. 1600 · Ink, opaque watercolor, gold, and silver on paper · Painting 25.4 × 11.4 cm; page 33 × 20.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Attar wrote the poem in 1177. This copy of it was written out in Herat in the 1480s by the calligrapher Sultan 'Ali al-Mashhadi, and around 1600 more paintings were added in Isfahan, this one by Habiballah of Sava, who was active from about 1590 to 1610.
