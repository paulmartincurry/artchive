The Temple of Dendur

Maker unknown · completed by 10 CE · Aeolian sandstone · Temple: H. 6.40 m; L. 12.50 m. Gate: H. 8.08 m · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Petronius, the Roman governor of Egypt, built the temple at the request of Augustus on the west bank of the Nile in Nubia. After the Aswan Low Dam was raised in 1933 it stood under water for nine months of each year, and the last paint on its carvings washed away. It was dismantled in 1963 ahead of the Aswan High Dam, given by Egypt to the United States in 1965 and awarded to the Metropolitan Museum in 1967. A British naval officer carved A L Corry RN 1817 into it.
