The Day the Earth Smiled

Cassini, NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute · 19 July 2013, released 12 November 2013 · Spacecraft image mosaic

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SSI

Cassini took this mosaic on 19 July 2013 from inside Saturn's shadow, with the planet blocking the Sun. Earth is the pale dot at lower right, about 1.44 billion kilometres away. The planetary scientist Carolyn Porco, who led the imaging team, asked people on Earth to look up and smile at the moment the pictures were taken; it was the first time they had been told in advance. Of 323 images captured over four hours, 141 went into the mosaic.
