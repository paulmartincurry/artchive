You are ice and fire,
The touch of you burns my hands like snow.

Opal

Amy Lowell · 1919

Opal is one of the love poems Lowell wrote to the actress Ada Dwyer Russell, whom she met in 1912 and who was her partner until her death. Lowell wanted to dedicate her books to Russell, who allowed it only once. Most of the letters between the two women were destroyed by Russell at Lowell's request.
