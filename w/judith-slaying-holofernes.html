Judith Slaying Holofernes (Uffizi Version)

Artemisia Gentileschi · ca. 1620 · Oil on canvas · Uffizi, Florence

Artemisia Gentileschi painted two versions of this composition. The first, completed in 1612–13 when she was around twenty, is in the Museo Capodimonte in Naples. This is the second, painted somewhere between 1613 and 1621. The Naples canvas uses deeper primary colours than this one. Her father, the painter Orazio Gentileschi, made his own version of the story.
