Temple of Bel, Palmyra

Maker unknown · dedicated AD 32 · Stone · H. 15 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: Bernard Gagnon, CC BY-SA 3.0

The temple was dedicated in AD 32. It was later turned into a church and, in 1132, into a mosque, and mud-brick houses stood among its ruins until archaeologists cleared the grounds in the 1920s. Its ruins were considered among the best preserved at Palmyra until the Islamic State destroyed more of them in August 2015; the arched main entrance is still intact. This photograph was taken in April 2010.
