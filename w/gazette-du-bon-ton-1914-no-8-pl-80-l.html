Gazette du Bon Ton, 1914, No. 8, Pl. 80: L'Arbre de science / Robe du soir de Doeuillet

George Barbier · 1914 · Lithograph, coloured with stencils and by hand; letterpress text · 24.5 × 19 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Plate 80 of the Gazette du Bon Ton for August 1914 shows an evening gown by Doeuillet. It was printed after a drawing by George Barbier, as a lithograph coloured with stencils and by hand. Barbier, born in Nantes, had held his first exhibition three years earlier, at twenty-nine.
