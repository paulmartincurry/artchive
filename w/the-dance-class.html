The Dance Class

Edgar Degas · 1874 · Oil on canvas · 32 7/8 × 30 3/8 in. (83.5 × 77.2 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The class is imaginary. It is set in the old Paris Opera, which had burnt down the year before Degas painted it, and the ballet master taking it is Jules Perrot. The singer Jean-Baptiste Faure commissioned the picture, and the poster on the wall for Rossini's Guillaume Tell is there for him. Degas painted a companion to it, now in the Musée d'Orsay. The same year his father died and he learned that his brother René had run up large business debts.
