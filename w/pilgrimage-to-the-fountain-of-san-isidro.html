Pilgrimage to the Fountain of San Isidro (The Holy Office)

Francisco de Goya · c. 1821–1823 · Oil on plaster wall transferred to canvas · 127 × 266 cm · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

Neither title of this mural is Goya's, and the shrine of San Isidro that it may show was close to his house. It hung on the upper floor, on the far side of a window from Asmodea. The bright sky on the left is among the lightest passages in the whole series.
