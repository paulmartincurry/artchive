Reading the I Ching in a Mountain Study

Chen Shu · before 1736 · National Palace Museum, Taipei

Free to use

Chen Shu was born into an elite family in Jiaxing and taught herself to paint as a girl; her father was an artist. Her husband died early, and she raised her son alone and sold her paintings to help support the family. The son, Qian Chenqun, became a statesman at the court of the Qianlong Emperor and showed the emperor his mother's work, and many of her paintings entered the imperial collection. She turned to landscapes late in her life.
