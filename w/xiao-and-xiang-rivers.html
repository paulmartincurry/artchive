Xiao and Xiang Rivers

Dong Yuan · 10th century · Handscroll, ink and colour on silk · Palace Museum, Beijing

Free to use

Dong Yuan painted this landscape on silk, about 50 by 141 centimetres. He was from Nanjing, the cultural centre of the Southern Tang kingdom, and like many Chinese painters he earned his living as an official. With his pupil Juran he founded what became the Southern, or Jiangnan, style of landscape painting.
