Twelve-Point Star Tile

Maker unknown · Timurid dynasty (1370-1507), Late 15th century · Glazed fritware tiles cut and reassembled as a mosaic · H.: 59.7 cm (23 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

It is a mosaic of separately cut tiles; most star tiles of the period were fired in one piece. It may have been the centre of a mosque pulpit or decorated the wall of a hall or madrasa.
