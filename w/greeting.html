Greeting (Begrüssung)

August Macke · 1912 · Linoleum cut · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

August Macke was twenty-five and living in Bonn when he cut this block, which measures 24 by 19 centimetres. In the same year he met Robert Delaunay in Paris. He died two years later at the front in Champagne, in the second month of the First World War.
