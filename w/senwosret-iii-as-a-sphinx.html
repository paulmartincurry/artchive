Senwosret III as a Sphinx

Maker unknown · ca. 1878–1840 B.C. · Gneiss · L. 73 × W. 29.5 × H. 42.5 cm (28 3/4 × 11 5/8 × 16 3/4 in.) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The sphinx is seventy-three centimetres long and weighs about 114 kilograms. It may have come from Thebes, in Upper Egypt. Edward S. Harkness gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1917.
