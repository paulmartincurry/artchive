Scrolls of Frolicking Animals (Chōjū-jinbutsu-giga)

Traditionally attributed to Toba Sōjō · Mid-12th Century · Ink on paper · Tokyo National Museum

Free to use

There are four scrolls, and none of them carries a word of writing. The first is the largest, 11 metres long and 30 centimetres wide. All were drawn with the ordinary brush used for writing. The attribution to Toba Sōjō is hard to verify, and the style shows more than one hand; the third and fourth scrolls may date from the thirteenth century. They belong to Kōzan-ji temple in Kyoto, which now displays reproductions.
