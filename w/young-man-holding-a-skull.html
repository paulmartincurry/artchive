Young Man Holding a Skull (Vanitas)

Frans Hals · 1626–1628 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London

Free to use

The painting was long described as Hamlet holding the skull of Yorick. Seymour Slive rejected that, because Shakespeare's plays are not recorded in the northern Netherlands in the 1620s. It was shown on loan at the National Gallery in Dublin in 1895, and the National Gallery in London bought it in 1980.
