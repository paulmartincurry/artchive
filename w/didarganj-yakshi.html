Didarganj Yakshi

Maker unknown · 3rd–2nd Century BCE · Sandstone · Bihar Museum, Patna

Free to use with credit. Photo: Shivam Setu, CC BY-SA 4.0

Villagers and an archaeologist dug this figure out of the bank of the Ganges at Didarganj, in Patna, on 18 October 1917. She stands five feet tall on a one-foot pedestal, in Chunar sandstone polished to a mirror finish, and the back is flattened with little modelling. The left hand is broken. Her nose was damaged on the way to an exhibition in Washington, and it was decided not to send her abroad again. Her date is disputed: the polish points to the 3rd century BCE, the shape and ornament to about the 2nd century CE.
