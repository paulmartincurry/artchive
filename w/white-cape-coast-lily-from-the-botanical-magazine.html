White Cape-Coast Lily, from The Botanical Magazine

Sydenham Edwards · 1806 · engraving, hand-colored · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Sydenham Edwards had been copying botanical plates for his own enjoyment at eleven, and William Curtis, who founded the Botanical Magazine in 1787, had him trained in botany and botanical illustration. Between 1787 and 1815 he made more than 1,700 watercolours for the magazine alone. He was thirty-seven or thirty-eight when this plate appeared.
