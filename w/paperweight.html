Paperweight

Clichy Glasshouse · c. 1845/60 · Glass · Diam.: 6.7 cm (2 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Millefiori, Italian for a thousand flowers, was first made in Venice in the fifteenth century. French glass houses such as Clichy, Baccarat and Saint-Louis revived it for paperweights from the late 1840s to the early 1860s.
