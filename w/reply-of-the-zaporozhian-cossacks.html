Reply of the Zaporozhian Cossacks

Ilya Repin · 1880–91 · Oil on canvas · 203 × 358 cm · State Russian Museum, Saint Petersburg

Free to use

Repin began the canvas in 1880 and finished it in 1891, and he recorded the years of work along its lower edge. Alexander III bought it for 35,000 rubles. The scene is set in 1676, and whether the Cossacks ever wrote such a letter to the Sultan is not known. Repin painted another version, which is in Kharkiv.
