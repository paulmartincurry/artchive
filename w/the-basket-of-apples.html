The Basket of Apples

Paul Cézanne · c. 1893 · Oil on canvas · 65 × 80 cm (25 7/16 × 31 1/2 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The biscuits stacked on the plate are langues de chat. Cézanne rarely signed his paintings, but this one carries his name at lower left. It was in the dealer Ambroise Vollard's hands by November 1895 and changed owners in Paris, Berlin and Lübeck before Frederick Clay Bartlett gave it to the Art Institute in 1926. It was the first work by Cézanne to enter the museum.
