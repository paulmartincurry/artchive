His dreams are of a palm-tree,
Who, far in eastern lands,
Weeps, all alone and silent,
Among the burning sands.

Ein Fichtenbaum (Lyrisches Intermezzo)

Heinrich Heine · 1822

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

The poem is dated to the spring of 1822. It was first published in Berlin in 1823, in Heine's Tragödien, nebst einem lyrischen Intermezzo, and then in 1827 in his first book of poems, Buch der Lieder.
