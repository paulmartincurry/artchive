The Garden of Earthly Delights: The Tree Man (detail of the Hell panel)

Hieronymus Bosch · c. 1490–1510 · Oil on oak panel · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Free to use

The oak of the panels has been dated by its tree rings to between 1460 and 1466, and the wood was usually stored for years to season before it was painted. The art historian Hans Belting proposed that the face of the Tree Man is a self-portrait of Bosch. Its body is a broken eggshell, with a tavern inside.
