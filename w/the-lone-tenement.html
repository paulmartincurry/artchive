The Lone Tenement

George Bellows · 1909 · Oil on canvas · 91.8 × 122.3 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Begun in December 1909. It shows the nearly finished Blackwell's Island Bridge and the last apartment building left standing at the site. From 1907 to 1909 Bellows had been painting New York's construction sites, among them the excavation for Pennsylvania Station. The Tenement House Law of 1901 was leading to the gradual replacement of buildings like this one.
