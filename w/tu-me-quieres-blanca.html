Tu me quieres alba,
Me quieres de espumas,
Me quieres de nácar.

Tú me quieres blanca

Alfonsina Storni · 1918 (El dulce daño)

The poem comes from El dulce daño, the first of three books of poetry Storni published in three years. She was about twenty-six, living in Buenos Aires as a single mother and supporting herself by teaching and newspaper journalism. While writing for the magazine Caras y Caretas she had also worked as a cashier in a shop.
