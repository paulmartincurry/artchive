Boulevard de Strasbourg (Corsets)

Eugène Atget · 1912 · Gelatin silver printing-out paper print · 22.9 × 18 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Atget was an actor until an infection of his vocal cords ended that career in 1887, and he made his first photographs the next year, at thirty. His old-fashioned camera often recorded moving things as a blur, as with the corset hanging in the doorway here. In the 1930s the New York gallery owner Julien Levy bought half of Berenice Abbott's Atget collection, and this print came to the Art Institute of Chicago as a gift from Jean and Julien Levy.
