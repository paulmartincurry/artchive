Looking Toward the Chicago and North Western Railroad Classification Yard

Jack Delano · December 1942 · Colour transparency · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

A colour transparency Jack Delano made in December 1942 at the Proviso yards of the Chicago and North Western railroad. The caption says the old cars in the foreground were living quarters for yard workers and itinerant help, and that Melrose Park is behind.
