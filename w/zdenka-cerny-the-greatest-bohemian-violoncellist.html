Zdeňka Černý: The Greatest Bohemian Violoncellist

Alphonse Mucha · 1913 · Print (poster) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division

Free to use

Mucha designed this poster for the Czech cellist Zdeňka Černý's European concert tour of 1914 and 1915, which was cancelled because of the First World War. In the years before, he had lived in the United States, where his main income came from teaching; he was a visiting professor at the Art Institute of Chicago. The Library of Congress bought the poster in 2015.
