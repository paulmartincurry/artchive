Frankenstein — You are my creator, but I am your master (Ch. 20)

Mary Shelley · 1818

Mary Shelley finished writing in the spring of 1817, and the novel came out from the small London house of Lackington, Hughes, Harding, Mavor and Jones. Walter Scott reviewed it in Blackwood's Edinburgh Magazine, though he was unconvinced by the way the creature learns language. In 1823 a stage version, Presumption, was performed thirty-seven times at the English Opera House, and its success led to a second printing of the book. She saw it on 29 August that year.
