I Had Walled the Monster Up within the Tomb! (The Black Cat)

Harry Clarke · published 1919 · Plate (line block print) · Maastricht University Library

Poe's story The Black Cat was first published in The Saturday Evening Post on 19 August 1843. The caption under Clarke's plate is printed beside the page number 308. Clarke's final book, Selected Poems of Algernon Charles Swinburne, was published in 1928.
