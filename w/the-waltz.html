The Waltz (La Valse)

Camille Claudel · 1889–1905 · Plastilina · Musée Rodin, Paris

Free to use

Camille Claudel began this sculpture about 1889, when she was working in Rodin's studio. The dancers were first naked, and in 1892 a ministry inspector judged the plaster unfit for public display. She added the skirt and showed the new version at the Paris Salon in 1893, but the ministry still declined to commission it in marble. She gave versions to friends, Claude Debussy among them. The founder Eugène Blot cast it in bronze in 1905, planning an edition of 50, and made 25.
