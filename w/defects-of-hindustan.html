Hindūstān is a country of few charms. Its people have no good looks; of social intercourse, paying and receiving visits there is none; of genius and capacity none; of manners none …

Defects of Hindūstān (Bābur-nāma)

Babur · 1525–26

Translated by Annette Susannah Beveridge

Babur wrote his memoirs in Chagatai, which he called Türki, the spoken language of the Timurids. Akbar's courtier Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khanan translated them into Persian in 1589–90. This passage sits in the section for the year 932 AH, October 1525 to October 1526.
