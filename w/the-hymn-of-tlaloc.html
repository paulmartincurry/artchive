Go ye forth to seek me, seek for the words which I have said, as I rise, a terrible one, and cry aloud.

The Hymn of Tlaloc

Anonymous Aztec hymn (recorded for Bernardino de Sahagún) · Written down in the 16th century

Translated by Daniel G. Brinton, 1890

Tlaloc, the Aztec god of rain, shared the highest place among the Mexican gods with Huitzilopochtli. His yearly festival came at about the time the corn was planted, and was held on a mountain east of Tenochtitlan called Tlalocan, the place of Tlaloc. The hymn survives because the friar Bernardino de Sahagún preserved it, and neither of the two manuscripts that hold it gives a translation.
