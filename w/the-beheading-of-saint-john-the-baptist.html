The Beheading of Saint John the Baptist

Caravaggio · 1608 · Oil on canvas · 370 × 520 cm · St. John's Co-Cathedral, Valletta

Free to use

This is the only work that bears Caravaggio's signature, which he placed in the blood running from the Baptist's throat. The Knights of Malta commissioned it for the oratory in Valletta where it still hangs, and at 3.7 by 5.2 metres it is the largest altarpiece he painted. About six months after his induction as a knight the Order expelled him in his absence, in a ceremony held in front of this painting.
