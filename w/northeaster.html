Northeaster

Winslow Homer · 1895; reworked by 1901 · Oil on canvas · 87.6 × 127 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Homer settled at Prouts Neck in Maine, where a studio was finished for him in 1884. He painted this in 1895 and it went to dealers and a collector in New York. It was then returned to him in Maine, and he reworked it by 1901. George A. Hearn owned it from 1901 and gave it to the Met in 1910.
