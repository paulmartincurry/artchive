Beethoven Frieze: The Hostile Forces

Gustav Klimt · 1901–02 · Casein colours, gold, chalk, graphite and applied materials on mortar render · Belvedere, Vienna (on loan to the Secession)

Free to use with credit. Photo: Belvedere, Vienna, CC BY-SA 4.0

Klimt painted the frieze in 1902 for the fourteenth exhibition of the Vienna Secession. It was meant to last only as long as the show, so he painted it directly on the walls with light materials. The whole work is 2.15 metres high and 34.14 metres long and weighs four tons. A collector acquired it in 1903 to prevent its destruction. It was recovered from the Altaussee salt mine in 1945 and has been on public display in the Secession building since 1986.
