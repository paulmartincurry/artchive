The Artchive
Never say about anything, I have lost it, but say I have restored it. Is your child dead? It has been restored. Is your wife dead? She has been restored.

The Encheiridion — Never say about anything, I have lost it (XI)

Epictetus (compiled by Arrian) · c. 125 CE

Translated by George Long

The handbook was compiled by Arrian, who studied under Epictetus as a young man, around AD 108. In a letter that prefaced the text, Arrian said he had selected it from the Discourses according to what he considered most useful and most necessary.
