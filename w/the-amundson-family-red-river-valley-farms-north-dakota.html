The Amundson Family, Red River Valley Farms, North Dakota

John Vachon · October 1940 · Safety film negative · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

John Vachon was 26 when he photographed the Amundson family at Red River Valley Farms, North Dakota, in October 1940. The caption says they had been farming in the drought area of western North Dakota.
