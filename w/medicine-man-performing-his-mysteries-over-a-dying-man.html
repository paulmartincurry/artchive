Medicine Man, Performing His Mysteries over a Dying Man

George Catlin · 1832 · Oil on canvas · 73.7 × 60.9 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

Catlin watched this healer at Fort Union in 1832. The costume was the skin of a yellow bear with the hides of snakes attached. Catlin owned it, and sometimes wore it himself at showings of his Indian Gallery.
