I live, I die, I burn and drown.
I’m boiling hot, suffering from cold.
Life’s too soft for me, too hard to mould:
I feel great pain for all the joy I’ve found.

Sonnet 8 (Je vis, je meurs: je me brule et me noye)

Louise Labé · 1555

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

Louise Labé's father was a successful ropemaker in Lyon, and she was known as La Belle Cordière, the Fair Ropemaker. Her Œuvres, with three elegies and twenty-four sonnets, were printed in 1555 by the Lyon printer Jean de Tournes.
