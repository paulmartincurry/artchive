Camille Monet on Her Deathbed

Claude Monet · 1879 · Oil on canvas · 90 × 68 cm · Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Free to use

Camille Doncieux, Monet's first wife, died at Vétheuil on 5 September 1879, at the age of 32, and he painted her on her deathbed. The cause of her death remains uncertain. She had met Monet in 1865 and posed for him from then on.
