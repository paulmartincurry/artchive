Winged Feline and Bull; Two Winged Angels (Aja'ib al-Makhluqat of Qazwini)

Maker unknown · c. 1571–75 · Opaque watercolor, gold and ink on paper · Image 25.1 × 18.7 cm; sheet 27.9 × 22.5 cm · Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Free to use

This leaf comes from a copy of Qazwini's book made in Bijapur around 1571–75, under Sultan 'Ali 'Adil Shah I; other leaves from it are in San Diego and Hyderabad. Qazwini wrote the treatise in Arabic in 1279–80, and its first volume deals with the heavens and their inhabitants, angels and demons among them.
