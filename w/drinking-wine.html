I pick chrysanthemums by the eastern hedge,
see the southern mountain, calm and still.

Drinking Wine (V)

Tao Yuanming (Tao Qian) · ca. 417 CE

Translated by Burton Watson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

Tao Yuanming is generally believed to have been born in 365, which would make him about fifty-two at the date given for this poem. He had served more than ten years in government, in five stints, the first taken partly because his family was poor. The last, as magistrate of Penze county, lasted about eighty days. He then went back to his home region and lived in the countryside, farming, reading and drinking wine.
