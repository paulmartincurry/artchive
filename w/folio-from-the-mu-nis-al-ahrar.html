Folio from the Mu'nis al-ahrar (The Free Man's Companion to the Subtleties of Poems)

Muhammad ibn Badr al-Din Jajarmi · dated 741 AH/1340–41 CE · Ink, opaque watercolor, and gold on paper · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Muhammad ibn Badr al-Din Jajarmi is recorded as the author of the book this page comes from, not as its painter. The manuscript was made in Isfahan and is dated 741 in the Islamic calendar, the year 1340 to 1341. The page is 19.1 centimetres tall. The Met bought it in 1919.
