Sita

Odilon Redon · c. 1893 · Pastel, with touches of black Conté crayon, over various charcoals, on cream wove paper altered to a golden tone · 53.6 × 37.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Redon laid pastel and touches of black Conté crayon over a charcoal drawing. He sold the sheet to the Durand-Ruel gallery in Paris in April 1899, the year he exhibited there with the Nabis. In 1928 it was auctioned in Paris under another title, L'évocation. Joseph Winterbotham, Jr. owned it by 1929 and bequeathed it to the Art Institute of Chicago in 1954.
