Igbo-Ukwu Bronze Pendant

Maker unknown · 9th Century · Bronze

The first Igbo-Ukwu bronzes were dug up in 1938 by Isaiah Anozie in his own compound. He gave some away to friends and neighbours and used some of the vessels to water his goats. The smiths who made them cast by the lost wax process, probably with latex in place of beeswax, and did not know wire making, soldering or riveting; details that look attached were cast in one piece. About 85 to 90 per cent of the ore came from old mines at Abakiliki, about 100 kilometres away.
