Marley was dead: to begin with. There is no doubt whatever about that.

A Christmas Carol — dead as a door-nail (Stave I, opening)

Charles Dickens · 1843

Dickens began the book in October 1843 and finished it in six weeks, writing the last pages in early December. It was published on 19 December, and the first edition had sold out by Christmas Eve.
