Paradise Lost: Adam and Eve Driven out of Paradise

John Martin · 1824–27 · Mezzotint · Platemark: 25.3 × 34.9 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Martin engraved the Paradise Lost plates himself in mezzotint, and Samuel Prowett issued them in monthly instalments, the first on 20 March 1825. For long stretches of his life Martin earned more from his engravings than from his paintings. This plate mark measures 25.3 by 34.9 centimetres.
