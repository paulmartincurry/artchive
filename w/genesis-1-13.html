And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep.
And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.

Genesis 1:1–3

Book of Genesis (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · Final form c. 5th century BCE (trans. 1611)

Genesis was written anonymously, and the original manuscripts are lost. Tradition credits Moses as its author, but most scholars agree that the book reached its final form after the Babylonian exile, possibly in the fifth century BCE. In Hebrew the book takes its title from its first word. The oldest surviving copies are fragments found among the Dead Sea Scrolls, which cover only a small part of the book.
