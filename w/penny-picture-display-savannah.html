Penny Picture Display, Savannah

Walker Evans · 1936, printed c. 1962 · Gelatin silver print · 21.7 × 17.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Walker Evans was one of several photographers hired by the Farm Security Administration to document the Depression. He photographed this display of portraits at a photographer's studio in 1936, and two years later he was given a one-person show at the Museum of Modern Art. This print was made about 1962.
