Étretat: The Beach and the Falaise d'Amont

Claude Monet · 1885 · Oil on canvas · 69.3 × 66.1 cm (27 1/4 × 26 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Monet went to Étretat with his family in the off season, the autumn and winter of 1885, so the beach is almost empty but for local fishing boats.
