Sewing bag

H.E. Verran Company · ca. 1912 · linen foundation, silk embroidery and drawstring Technique: embroidered on plain weave · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

The H.E. Verran Company of New York made this linen sewing bag. A front flap forms a pocket inside, and a green silk ribbon runs through a casing stitched an inch below the top. It came from the collection of the American Textile History Museum.
