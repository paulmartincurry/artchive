Venice (Camera Work 44, plate I)

Edward Steichen · published 1914 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

This plate opened Camera Work number 44, an issue dated October 1913 that did not come out until March 1914. In 1914 Steichen and his family left France just ahead of invading German troops. He returned in 1917 with the Photography Division of the American Army Signal Corps.
