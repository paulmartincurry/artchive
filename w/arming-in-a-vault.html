Arming in a Vault (Peasants' War)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1906 · Etching and aquatint · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Arming in the Vault is one of the seven etchings of Kollwitz's Peasant War cycle, which took its subject from the German Peasants' War of 1524–1525. She visited Paris twice while working on the cycle, and in 1904 took classes at the Académie Julian to learn to sculpt. This impression is from the eighth of nine states.
