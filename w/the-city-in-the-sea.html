The City in the Sea (The Bells and Other Poems)

Edmund Dulac · published 1912 · Colour plate (photomechanical print)

Free to use

The Bells and Other Poems came out in 1912 with 28 colour plates by Dulac and many monotone drawings. That February, Dulac, born in Toulouse, became a naturalised British citizen. Poe first printed this poem as The Doomed City in 1831 and gave it its final title in the American Review in April 1845.
