Rue Transnonain, le 15 Avril, 1834

Honoré Daumier · July 1834 · Lithograph · The Met

The print was plate 24 of l'Association mensuelle. Honoré Daumier was twenty-six, and he had been out of Sainte-Pélagie prison for about seventeen months, after six months served for a caricature of King Louis Philippe. He learned lithography as a young man and had worked at it since the mid-1820s. The September Laws of 1835 limited the freedom of the press, and after them his political cartoons became oblique and veiled.
