Letter to George and Georgiana Keats (April 1819): The Vale of Soul-Making

John Keats · 1819

George Keats and his wife Georgiana had left England in June 1818, sailing from Liverpool for America, where they lived in Ohio and in Louisville, Kentucky. Tom, the youngest of the three brothers, died that same year. Keats was twenty-three when he wrote this letter. He lent George 700 pounds, and after that could no longer cover the interest on his own debts.
