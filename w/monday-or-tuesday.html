Lazy and indifferent, shaking space easily from his wings, knowing his way,
the heron passes over the church beneath the sky.

Monday or Tuesday

Virginia Woolf · 1921

This story is one of eight in Woolf's first collection, which has the same title and was printed in 1921 by the Hogarth Press, the firm she and her husband Leonard had set up four years earlier. A thousand copies were made, with four full-page woodcuts by her sister Vanessa Bell. Leonard Woolf called it one of the worst printed books ever published because of its typographical mistakes, most of which were corrected for the American edition.
