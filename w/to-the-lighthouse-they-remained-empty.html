To the Lighthouse — They remained empty (Time Passes, 3)

Virginia Woolf · 1927

Woolf's sister, the painter Vanessa Bell, wrote that reading the parts of the novel about Mrs Ramsay was like seeing their mother raised from the dead. Their brother Adrian was once kept from an expedition to Godrevy Lighthouse, as James is in the book. The American edition came out the same month as the English one, in 4,000 copies bound in pale green cloth. In 1928 the novel won the Prix Femina–Vie Heureuse.
