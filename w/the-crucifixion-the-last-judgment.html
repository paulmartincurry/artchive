The Crucifixion; The Last Judgment

Jan van Eyck · ca. 1436–38 · Oil on canvas, transferred from wood · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Each panel is only 56.5 centimetres high and 19.7 wide. A date written in Russian on the back records that the paintings were moved from their wooden panels onto canvas in 1867, and they came into the Hermitage in 1917. When the Metropolitan Museum bought them in 1933 it credited them to Jan's brother Hubert. The Moon in the Crucifixion is the first known naturalistic depiction of its surface features.
