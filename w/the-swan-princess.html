The Swan Princess

Mikhail Vrubel · 1900 · Oil on canvas · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

Free to use

The figure comes from Rimsky-Korsakov's opera The Tale of Tsar Saltan, after Pushkin's fairy tale, for which Vrubel designed the sets and costumes. His wife, Nadezhda Zabela-Vrubel, sang the Swan Princess.
