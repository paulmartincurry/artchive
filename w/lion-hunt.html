Lion Hunt

Eugène Delacroix · 1860–61 · Oil on canvas · 76.5 × 98.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Delacroix had never witnessed a lion hunt. He put the scene together from studies of landscapes, costume and zoo animals, almost thirty years after his journey to North Africa in 1832. He signed and dated the canvas 1861. It was possibly sold by him for 2,500 francs, and at a sale in 1868 it made 14,505 francs. Mr. and Mrs. Potter Palmer of Chicago bought it at auction in New York in 1892.
