Crediting Poetry (Nobel Lecture)

Seamus Heaney · 1995

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Heaney was fifty-six when he was awarded the Nobel Prize in 1995. He was on holiday in Greece with his wife when the news broke, and neither journalists nor his own children could reach him until he landed at Dublin Airport two days later. The couple were taken straight from the airport to drink champagne with the Irish president, Mary Robinson. He was the eldest of nine children of a County Londonderry farmer and cattle dealer.
