Hylas and the Nymphs

John William Waterhouse · 1896 · Oil on canvas · 98.2 × 163.3 cm · Manchester Art Gallery

Free to use

Manchester Art Gallery bought it from Waterhouse in 1896. In January 2018 the gallery took it off display, a decision made with the artist Sonia Boyce; after a week Manchester City Council, which runs the gallery, put it back on the wall.
