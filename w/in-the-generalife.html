In the Generalife

John Singer Sargent · 1912 · Watercolor, wax crayon, and graphite on white wove paper · 14 3/4 × 17 7/8 in. (37.5 × 45.4 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Sargent made more than 2,000 watercolours over his career, against roughly 900 oil paintings. He was fifty-six when he painted this one. The Metropolitan Museum bought it in 1915, ten years before his death, with money from the Joseph Pulitzer Bequest.
