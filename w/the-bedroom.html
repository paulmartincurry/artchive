The Bedroom

Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · Oil on canvas · 73.6 × 92.3 cm (29 × 36 5/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Van Gogh first painted his bedroom in the Yellow House in Arles in October 1888, a month after moving in. This is the second of three versions, made in September 1889 while he was in Saint-Rémy; the third, smaller one was a gift for his mother and sister. He sent this canvas to his brother Theo in Paris on 18 December 1889. The Chicago collector Frederic Clay Bartlett bought it in 1926 and gave it to the Art Institute the same year.
