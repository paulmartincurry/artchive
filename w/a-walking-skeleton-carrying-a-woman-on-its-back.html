A Walking Skeleton Carrying a Woman on Its Back

Kawanabe Kyōsai · 1886 · Sumi ink and pigment on paper, mounted as a hanging scroll · 220 × 78.2 cm · Wereldmuseum (Nationaal Museum van Wereldculturen), Netherlands

Free to use

Kyōsai painted this scroll in 1886, three years before his death; it is 220 centimetres tall. His pupil Josiah Conder, turned away at first, stayed with him for ten years until his death, and in 1911 published a book of his paintings and studies. Kyōsai had been nine when he picked up a human head, separated from a corpse, in the Kanda river.
