Initial I with Entwined Beast and Serpent, Leaf from a Bible

Maker unknown · c. 1225–50 · tempera and ink on vellum · Leaf: 20 × 15.2 cm (7 7/8 × 6 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This leaf was cut from a one-volume Latin Bible, where it opened the Book of Ruth. The Bible has traditionally been linked to the Benedictine abbey of Glastonbury, which was dissolved in 1539. It later belonged to the collector Roger Gale, whose library held 450 manuscripts. The leaf was sold at Sotheby's in London in 1982, and Cleveland owns a second leaf from the same book.
