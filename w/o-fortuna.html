…since by ill fortune
strength is brought down,
all join with me in weeping!

O Fortuna

Anonymous (Carmina Burana) · early 13th century

Translated by A. S. Kline · Free to reproduce for non-commercial use (poetryintranslation.com terms); not public domain.

O Fortuna is a Goliardic poem in Latin from the early 13th century, part of the Carmina Burana manuscript found in 1803 in the monastery of Benediktbeuern in Bavaria. Carl Orff made it the opening and closing movement of his cantata Carmina Burana, first staged by the Frankfurt Opera in 1937.
