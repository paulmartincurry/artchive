The Vampire II

Edvard Munch · 1895–1902 · Color lithograph and woodcut on tan wove paper with fibrous inclusions · Plate: 40.8 × 57.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Munch hated to part with his paintings, so he turned to printmaking to reproduce them and earn an income. This print repeats the painting Love and Pain of 1893–94, later retitled Vampire. It combines colour lithography with woodcut on a tan sheet 61.8 by 70 centimetres.
