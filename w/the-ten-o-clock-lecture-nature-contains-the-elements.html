To say to the painter, that Nature is to be taken as she is, is to say to the player, that he may sit on the piano.

The Ten O'Clock Lecture: Nature Contains the Elements

James McNeill Whistler · 1885

The lecturer was a painter and printmaker, American by birth, who had made London his home in 1859 and never went back to the United States. His training had begun in the army. West Point dismissed him after three years, and he then drew coastal maps for the government until he was found adding sea serpents, mermaids and whales to the margins. Two months in the etching division of the Coast Survey taught him to etch.
