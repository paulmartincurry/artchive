Oedipus and the Sphinx

Gustave Moreau · 1864 · Oil on canvas · The Met

Free to use

Gustave Moreau was obscure before this painting went on show at the French Salon of 1864, where it was an immediate success. Prince Napoléon Bonaparte bought it that year for 8,000 francs. Moreau had sketched Ingres's version of the same subject, and his father had recently died when he painted it. The canvas is just over two metres tall. It was sold twice in 1868, the second time to William H. Herriman of Rome, who gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 1920.
