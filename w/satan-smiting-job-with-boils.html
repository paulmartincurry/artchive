Satan Smiting Job with Boils (The Book of Job, Plate 6)

William Blake · 1825 · Engraving on India paper · Sheet: 42.5 × 33.6 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is plate 6 of the twenty-two Job engravings. Blake finished engraving the series in 1825, and in 1826 he took up this subject once more in a tempera painting. The Royal Academy and the King's Library each bought a copy of the set, and the Academy awarded him 25 pounds.
