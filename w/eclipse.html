Eclipse

Eugène Atget · 1912 (the holder's title says 1911), printed c. 1956 · Photographic print (from the 1956 portfolio of 20 photographs printed by Berenice Abbott) · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Man Ray, who lived on the same street as Atget in Montparnasse, published Atget's picture of a crowd watching a solar eclipse at the Colonne de Juillet in La Révolution surréaliste on 15 June 1926. When Man Ray asked to use it, Atget told him to leave his name off, as these were simply documents he made. This print comes from 20 Photographs by Eugène Atget, a portfolio Berenice Abbott issued in New York in 1956.
