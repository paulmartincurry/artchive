Diary: The Great Fire of London (2 September 1666)

Samuel Pepys · 1666

Pepys was thirty-three and Clerk of the Acts to the Navy Board when London burned. He had begun the diary on 1 January 1660 and kept it for almost ten years, until fears for his eyesight made him stop in 1669. It was written in shorthand and was not published until 1825, after a transcriber had spent three years on it without knowing that the key to the shorthand was a few shelves above the diary in Pepys's own library.
