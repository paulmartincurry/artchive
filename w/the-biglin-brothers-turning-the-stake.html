The Biglin Brothers Turning the Stake

Thomas Eakins · 1873 · Oil on canvas · 101.3 × 151.4 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The race was rowed on the Schuylkill River in Philadelphia in May 1872. Barney and John Biglin are turning at the stake that marked the halfway point, and they won. Eakins, who had rowed as a boy, signed the canvas the next year. It was unsold when he died in 1916, and his widow sold it to the Cleveland Museum of Art in 1927.
