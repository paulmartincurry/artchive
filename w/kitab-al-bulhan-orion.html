Kitab al-Bulhan: Orion (folio 84v)

Maker unknown · c. 1399–1400 · Illustrated manuscript · Bodleian Libraries, Oxford (MS. Bodl. Or. 133)

Free to use

The astrological sections draw on the Book of Nativities attributed to the Persian astrologer Abu Ma'shar. Besides the stars and the twelve signs of the zodiac, the manuscript pictures the twenty-eight lunar mansions.
