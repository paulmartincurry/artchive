Flora (Fresco from the Villa Arianna, Stabiae)

Maker unknown · 1st century AD · Fresco · National Archaeological Museum, Naples

Free to use

The painting comes from room W26 of the Villa Arianna at Stabiae, the oldest leisure villa there, which stood on a ridge over the Bay of Naples. The villa was first excavated between 1757 and 1762, and one of its bedrooms held eighteen frescoes. The record for the painting calls the figure the so-called Flora.
