I was utterly alone with the sun and the earth. Lying down on the grass, I spoke in my soul to the earth, the sun, the air, and the distant sea far beyond sight.

The Story of My Heart (Chapter 1)

Richard Jefferies · 1883

Richard Jefferies was a farmer's son from Wiltshire who had started work as a newspaper reporter at seventeen. In this book he set down the development of his thoughts since the age of eighteen. Its atheism caused a scandal on publication, and it sold poorly beside the other books he published in his lifetime. He had tuberculosis for much of his adult life and died four years after the book appeared, aged thirty-eight.
