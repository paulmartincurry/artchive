Egyptian Funerary Mask

Maker unknown · Late Ptolemaic Period-early Roman Period, 1st century BCE · Cartonnage, gold leaf, and pigment · 44.5 × 30.5 × 29.8 cm (17 1/2 × 12 × 11 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

A mask like this covered the head and chest of a mummified body. It is made of cartonnage, with gold leaf for the skin, and stands about forty-five centimetres tall. Along the bottom, birds of prey with outstretched wings flank Osiris on a throne. The mask probably comes from Hawara, and the Art Institute acquired it in 1910.
