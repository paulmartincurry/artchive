He saith among the trumpets, Ha, ha; and he smelleth the battle afar off, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting.

Job 39:19–25

Book of Job (King James Version) · c. 6th-4th century BCE; KJV 1611

God's survey of animals in this speech begins with the lions and ravens at the end of chapter 38 and moves through the wild goat, the wild donkey, the wild ox and the ostrich before it reaches the warhorse; it closes with the hawk and the eagle. The wild ox of verse 9 is generally identified with the aurochs, whose last known herd died in Poland's Jaktorów Forest in 1627.
