Power Figure: Male (Nkisi)

Maker unknown · 19th–mid-20th century · Wood, pigment, nails, cloth, beads, shells, arrows, leather, nuts, twine · The Met

Figures of this kind were made by a nganga, a healer and diviner who also acted as mediator between a client and the spirit held in the object. Each nail or piece of metal hammered into the surface stands for a vow or a signed treaty. Nailed figures were probably made mostly in the northern part of the Kongo cultural zone, in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.
