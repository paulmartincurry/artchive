Owl at Half Moon

Ohara Koson · 1900–30 · Color woodblock print on paper · 35 × 19 cm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

An owl spreads its wings on a branch under a half moon. The holder's record names the publisher Matsuki Heikichi, one of the two houses Koson worked with first, when he signed himself Koson. The name means son of Kason, after his teacher Suzuki Kason. Ernest Fenollosa, later curator of Japanese art at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, asked him to send prints of this kind to the United States.
