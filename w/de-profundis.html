Where there is sorrow there is holy ground.

De Profundis

Oscar Wilde · 1897 (pub. 1905)

Wilde wrote this letter to Lord Alfred Douglas in Reading Gaol between January and March 1897, after a new governor allowed him writing materials. The manuscript runs to eighty close-written pages on twenty sheets of thin blue prison paper, and he was handed it only on his release. His own title was Epistola: In Carcere et Vinculis. Robert Ross chose the name De Profundis for a cut version in 1905, then gave the manuscript to the British Museum on condition that no one see it until 1960.
