The Monster of Gog and Magog (Aja'ib al-Makhluqat of Qazwini)

Maker unknown · 1717 (1129 AH) · Ink and pigments on European laid paper · 33 × 20 cm · Walters Art Museum, Baltimore

Free to use

This copy of Qazwini's Wonders of Creation was written out in 1717, in the Ottoman period, by the scribe Muhammad ibn Muhammad Shakir Ruzmah-'i Nathani, on European laid paper. Henry Walters acquired it, and it came to the Walters Art Museum in Baltimore with his bequest in 1931.
