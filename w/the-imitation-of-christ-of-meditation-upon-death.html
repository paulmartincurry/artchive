To-day man is, and to-morrow he will be seen no more. And being removed out of sight, quickly also he is out of mind.

The Imitation of Christ — Of Meditation upon Death (Book I, ch. 23)

Thomas à Kempis · c. 1418–1427

Translated by William Benham

Thomas à Kempis was born Thomas Hemerken at Kempen in the Rhineland, the son of a blacksmith and a schoolmistress. As sub-prior of the monastery of Mount St Agnes near Zwolle he was charged with teaching novices, and for them he wrote, between 1418 and 1427, the four booklets later collected as The Imitation of Christ. By 1650 it had been printed in 745 editions. He copied the Bible by hand four times.
