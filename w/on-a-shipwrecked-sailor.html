I am the tomb of one shipwrecked; but sail thou; for when we were perishing, the other ships sailed on over the sea.

On a Shipwrecked Sailor

Theodorides of Syracuse · late 3rd century BCE

Translated by J. W. Mackail, 1890

Theodorides of Syracuse wrote nineteen epigrams that survive in the Anthology and flourished towards the end of the third century BCE. He also wrote lyric poetry, including a dithyramb called the Centaurs and a Hymn to Love.
