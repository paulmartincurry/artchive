Composition with Red, Yellow, and Blue

Piet Mondrian · 1927 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 49.5 × 49.5 cm (19 1/2 × 19 1/2 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The thin strip of wood around the edge, painted the same grey as the canvas, is the frame Mondrian made for it. The canvas is just under half a metre square, signed at lower centre P M '27. Around the turn of 1927 he was also laying out a Polish translation of his book on Neoplasticism, working with Polish avant-garde artists, and Henryk Stażewski designed its cover. By 1955 the painting belonged to J. J. P. Oud, in Wassenaar.
