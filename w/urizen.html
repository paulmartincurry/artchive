The Artchive

Urizen

William Blake · 1794 · Monotype, with pen and brown and gray inks and watercolor, on cream wove paper · 17 × 12.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Blake made this as an independent work, a single hand-coloured print based on one of his plates from The Book of Urizen, published in 1794. It is a monotype finished with pen, brown and gray inks and watercolour, on a sheet 17 centimetres high. In autumn 1790 he had moved to Lambeth in south London, and from that house he designed, printed, coloured and sold his books.
