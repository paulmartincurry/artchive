Poppies whose roots are in man's veins
Drop, and are ever dropping;
But mine in my ear is safe,
Just a little white with the dust.

Break of Day in the Trenches

Isaac Rosenberg · 1916

Rosenberg wrote this poem in the trenches of the Western Front, where he had arrived on 3 June 1916. He had enlisted at the end of October 1915, unable to find a permanent job, and asked that half of his pay be sent to his mother. His first unit was a bantam battalion of the Suffolk Regiment, for men under the usual minimum height of five feet three inches. He was killed at Fampoux, northeast of Arras, on 1 April 1918, aged twenty-seven.
