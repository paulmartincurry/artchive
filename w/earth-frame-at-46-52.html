Vasyl's dance was scripted as a Cossack-style hopak, but Svashenko changed it after consulting local farmers. It was shot in soft focus in the early morning, so that the low light lit the clouds of dust his feet raised from the dirt road. The film was released on 8 April 1930 and banned by Soviet authorities nine days later. Its original negative was destroyed by a German air raid during the First Battle of Kiev in 1941.
