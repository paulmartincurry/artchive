I like the dust on the nettles, never lost
Except to prove the sweetness of a shower.

Tall Nettles

Edward Thomas · 1917

Edward Thomas had made his living for two decades as a critic and reviewer, at times reviewing fifteen books a week, before he wrote his first poem at the end of 1914, aged thirty-six. Robert Frost urged him to start. He wrote his poems in under three years, first published them under the name Edward Eastaway, and was killed at Arras on Easter Monday, 9 April 1917, soon after reaching France.
