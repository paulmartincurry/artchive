And after the earthquake a fire; but the Lord was not in the fire:
and after the fire a still small voice.

1 Kings 19:11–12

First Book of Kings (Hebrew Bible / KJV) · ca. 600 BCE (trans. 1611)

These verses come from the stories about the prophet Elijah, one of the older sources the editors of Kings worked into their history. Scholars date a first edition of the book to the late seventh century BC and a final one to the middle of the sixth. In the Hebrew Bible First and Second Kings are a single book; the division used today dates from 1517. The oldest complete copy to survive is in the Aleppo Codex, of the tenth century CE.
