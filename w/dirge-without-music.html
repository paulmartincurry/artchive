Down, down, down into the darkness of the grave
Gently they go, the beautiful, the tender, the kind;
Quietly they go, the intelligent, the witty, the brave.
I know. But I do not approve. And I am not resigned.

Dirge Without Music

Edna St. Vincent Millay · 1928

In 1925 Millay and her husband, Boissevain, bought Steepletop near Austerlitz, New York, which had once been a 635-acre blueberry farm. In August 1927 she was arrested with a number of other writers while protesting against the coming executions of Nicola Sacco and Bartolomeo Vanzetti. She is buried at Steepletop beside her husband.
