Cross Streets of New York

Everett Shinn · 1899 · Pastel, watercolor, charcoal, and gouache on paperboard · 55.09 × 74.93 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Everett Shinn signed this in pastel in 1899, at twenty-two, on a sheet of Winsor & Newton illustration board. That year he left newspaper work for a magazine and began to show his paintings and pastels in public. He was the only Ashcan artist who preferred pastel.
