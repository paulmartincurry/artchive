Out of Work (Proletariat, No. 1)

Käthe Kollwitz · 1925 · Woodcut · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Kollwitz turned to woodcut after seeing Ernst Barlach's woodcuts at the Secession exhibitions, having found etching insufficient for what she wanted, and she made about thirty by 1926. This one dates from 1925. The Print Club of Cleveland gave it to the museum in 1950.
