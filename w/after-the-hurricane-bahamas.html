After the Hurricane, Bahamas

Winslow Homer · 1899 · Transparent watercolor, with touches of opaque watercolor, rewetting, blotting and scraping, over graphite, on moderately thick, moderately textured (twill texture on verso), ivory wove paper · 37.2 × 54.2 cm (14 11/16 × 21 3/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This watercolour was made on Homer's second trip to the Bahamas, in the winter of 1898 to 1899. The seaweed on the sand is laid on thickly in opaque red and yellow, while the waves are thin washes. On the back an older title, After the Tornado, is written in pencil. By 1910 the sheet belonged to his brother Charles.
