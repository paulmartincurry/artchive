Dulle Griet (Mad Meg)

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1563 · Oil on panel · 117.4 × 162 cm · Museum Mayer van den Bergh, Antwerp

Free to use

A restoration in 2018 made the signature and date readable and showed that Bruegel painted the panel in 1563, shortly after moving to Brussels. For Mad Meg's robe he used cheap smalt instead of costly ultramarine. The picture passed into the collection of Emperor Rudolf II, was looted by Swedish troops in 1648 and resurfaced in Stockholm in 1800. Fritz Mayer van den Bergh bought it for very little at a Cologne auction in 1897 and learned who had painted it a few days later.
