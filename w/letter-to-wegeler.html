How often have I cursed my existence! Plutarch led me to resignation. I shall strive if possible to set Fate at defiance, although there must be moments in my life when I cannot fail to be the most unhappy of God's creatures.

Letter to Wegeler (Vienna, 29 June)

Ludwig van Beethoven · 1800 or 1801

Translated by Lady Wallace, 1866

Franz Wegeler was a young medical student when Beethoven met him; he became a lifelong friend and married one of the von Breuning daughters. Beethoven told the English pianist Charles Neate in 1815 that his hearing loss had begun in 1798. In 1802, on his doctor's advice, he spent April to October in Heiligenstadt, a small town just outside Vienna.
