Jack Whinery, Homesteader, and His Family, Pie Town, New Mexico

Russell Lee · October 1940 · Colour slide · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Jack Whinery with his wife Edith and their five children, Wanda, Edith, Velva, Jack and Lawrence, the youngest on his father's lap. Russell Lee took it as a colour slide in Pie Town, New Mexico, in October 1940. Whinery lived until 1994.
