Poem by Ariwara no Narihira (One Hundred Poems Explained by the Nurse)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1839 · Woodblock print; ink and color on paper · 25.7 × 38.1 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

One Hundred Poems Explained by a Nurse was Hokusai's final print series, produced around 1835 to 1836. It was never published in full, perhaps because of the financial hardships his publishers faced in the economic downturn of the mid-1830s. Each print carries its poem in a square and the series title in a vertical rectangle.
