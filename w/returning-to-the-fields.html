At gate and courtyard--no murmur of the World’s dust:
In the empty rooms--leisure and deep stillness.
Long I lived checked by the bars of a cage:
Now I have turned again to Nature and Freedom.

Returning to the Fields

Tao Yuanming (Tao Qian) · early 5th century

Translated by Arthur Waley

Tao Yuanming was born in 365 in Chaisang, a name that means Mulberry-Bramble, near Mount Lu and Poyang Lake. His great-grandfather Tao Kan had been a Jin dynasty general and governor, but Tao grew up in moderate poverty. From the Tang dynasty on he was closely tied to the figure of the farmer-recluse, and in the Northern Song the writer Su Shi held him up as a model of authenticity in poetry.
