Sphinx of Hatshepsut

Maker unknown · ca. 1473–1458 BCE · Granite, paint · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

This granite sphinx is more than three metres long and weighs close to seven tonnes. It was found in the Senenmut Quarry at Thebes during the Metropolitan Museum's own excavations in the late 1920s, and came into the collection in 1931. It was carved during the years Hatshepsut ruled jointly with Thutmose III.
