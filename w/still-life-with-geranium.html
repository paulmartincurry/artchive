Still Life with Geranium

Henri Matisse · 1906 · Oil on canvas · 100.3 × 81.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The two small figures beside the geranium are Henri Matisse's own sculptures, probably shown as plaster casts; he later made them in bronze. He sold the painting to the Bernheim-Jeune gallery in Paris on 1 March 1907. It was shown in Berlin that autumn and in Moscow in 1908, and the Art Institute of Chicago bought it in 1932.
