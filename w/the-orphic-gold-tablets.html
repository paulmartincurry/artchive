The Orphic Gold Tablets

Orphic Mystery Initiates · ca. 400 BCE

Translated by Fritz Graf and Sarah Iles Johnston · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The texts are scratched in Greek on sheets of gold foil as thin as paper, most of them small rectangles, and were buried with the dead. Some were laid on the body and others rolled into a capsule worn at the neck. One woman's grave in Thessaly, from the late 4th century BC, held a pair cut in the shape of ivy leaves and set on her chest, with a gold coin sealing her lips. Tablets have also been found at Thurii and on Crete; a survey of all those known by 1971 sorted them into three groups, and that grouping is still used.
