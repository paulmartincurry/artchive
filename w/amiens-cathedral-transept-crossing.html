Amiens Cathedral, Transept Crossing

Robert of Luzarches, Thomas and Regnault de Cormont · c. 1220–70 · Vault H. 42.30 m

Free to use with credit. Photo: Diliff, CC BY-SA 3.0

A fire destroyed the earlier cathedral at Amiens in 1218, and this one was built almost entirely between 1220 and about 1270, a short span for a Gothic cathedral. The vault is 42.30 metres high. By volume it is the largest cathedral in France, more than double Notre-Dame of Paris. In 1206 the town had received the reputed head of John the Baptist, brought from Constantinople after the Fourth Crusade.
