The Nile at Night

Suomi NPP satellite (VIIRS), NASA · 13 October 2012 · Satellite image (VIIRS day-night band)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA Earth Observatory

The Nile valley and delta make up less than 5 percent of Egypt's land area and are home to about 97 percent of its people. The satellite's day-night band picked up the lights of the valley and delta on the night of 13 October 2012. The band detects light from green to near-infrared and is used to watch city lights, gas flares, auroras and wildfires.
