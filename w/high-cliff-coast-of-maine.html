The Artchive

High Cliff, Coast of Maine

Winslow Homer · 1894 · Oil on canvas · 76.8 × 97.2 cm · Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington

Free to use

The cliff is at Prout's Neck in Maine, one of Homer's favourite places on the coast and where he died in 1910. Two small figures stand at the upper right. William T. Evans gave the painting to the Smithsonian.
