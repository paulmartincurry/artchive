The Great Red Dragon Paintings

William Blake · 1805 · Watercolor paint

There are four of these watercolours, made between 1805 and 1810, in years when William Blake had a commission for more than a hundred paintings to illustrate books of the Bible. Each sheet is about 40 centimetres high and 34 wide. Two are in the National Gallery of Art in Washington, one is at the Rosenbach Museum and Library, and one is at the Brooklyn Museum, which keeps it out of the light.
