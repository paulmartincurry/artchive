I had a terror since September, I could tell to none; and so I sing, as the boy does of the burying ground, because I am afraid.

Letter to T. W. Higginson, received 26 April 1862

Emily Dickinson · April 1862

In April 1862 Thomas Wentworth Higginson, a literary critic, radical abolitionist and ex-minister, wrote the lead piece for The Atlantic Monthly, Letter to a Young Contributor, and Dickinson wrote to him. This is her second letter, which he received on April 26, 1862. The two did not meet until he came to Amherst in 1870.
