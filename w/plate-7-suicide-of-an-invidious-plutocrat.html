Plate 7: Suicide of an Invidious Plutocrat

José Guadalupe Posada · c. 1894 · Type-metal engraving · 12 × 17 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Posada died in Mexico City in 1913, of gastroenteritis. Three neighbours certified the death, and only one of them knew his full name. He reportedly died penniless and was buried in an unmarked pauper's grave.
