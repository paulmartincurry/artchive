Armor of Henry II, King of France

Design attributed in part to Jean Cousin the Elder · c. 1555 · Steel, gold, silver, leather, textile · H. 187.96 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The armour stands 188 centimetres high and weighs 24.2 kilograms. Part of its decoration was designed by Jean Cousin the Elder, and part possibly by Baptiste Pellerin. Henry II took part in jousts and tournaments. In June 1559 a fragment of a splintered lance wounded him in the eye during a jousting match, and he died ten days later.
