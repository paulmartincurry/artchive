New York Central Yard

Alfred Stieglitz · 1903, printed 1910 · Photogravure · image 11.8 × 10 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

The photograph dates from 1903, the year Stieglitz began publishing Camera Work; this photogravure was printed in 1910. Below the image is the printed line New York Central, by A. Stieglitz. The print came to the National Gallery of Art as a gift in 2025.
