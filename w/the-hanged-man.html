The Hanged Man

Victor Hugo · c. 1855–60 · Brush and ink wash on wove paper · Sheet: 30.5 × 19.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Victor Hugo made more than 4,000 drawings, of which about 3,000 survive, and he campaigned for the abolition of capital punishment. This sheet is brushed in ink wash on wove paper, 30.5 centimetres tall. Kristina and Guy Wildenstein gave it to the Metropolitan Museum in 2003.
