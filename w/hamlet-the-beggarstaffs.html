Hamlet

The Beggarstaffs (William Nicholson and James Pryde) · 1894 · Museum für Kunst und Gewerbe Hamburg

Free to use

The Beggarstaffs made this poster in 1894 for W. S. Hardy's touring Shakespeare company, with Edward Gordon Craig as Hamlet. The design began as a collage, the hair and clothing cut from plain black paper; William Nicholson then stencilled the life-sized figure onto brown wrapping paper and added details by hand. It was shown at the poster exhibition at the Royal Aquarium in Westminster that November. The original design is untraced.
