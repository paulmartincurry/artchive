Hell is—other people!

No Exit

Jean-Paul Sartre · 1944 (English 1946)

Translated by Stuart Gilbert · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The play opened at the Théâtre du Vieux-Colombier in Paris in May 1944, while France was still occupied. Sartre was thirty-eight. Earlier in the war he had been drafted, captured and released, and had helped found a resistance group that soon dissolved. The French title, Huis clos, is a legal term for a hearing held behind closed doors. The first American production followed in 1946 at the Biltmore Theatre.
