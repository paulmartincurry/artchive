Study of Head of Christ

F. Holland Day · 1898 · Platinum print · 13 × 11.7 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

From 1896 through 1898 F. Holland Day photographed Christian subjects with himself as the model for Jesus. Neighbours in Norwood, Massachusetts, helped him stage the crucifixion outdoors. He printed only in platinum and lost interest in photography when the metal became unobtainable after the Russian Revolution. Two thousand of his prints and negatives were lost in a fire in 1904. This one belonged to Alfred Stieglitz.
