Mirror-Bearer

Maker unknown · 410–650 CE · Cordia wood (bocote), red hematite · 35.9 × 22.9 × 22.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This Maya figure was carved from bocote, a cordia wood, and coloured with red hematite between 410 and 650 CE, in Guatemala or Mexico. It is 35.9 centimetres tall and came to the Met in 1979 in the bequest of Nelson A. Rockefeller.
