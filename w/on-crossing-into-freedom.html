On Crossing into Freedom

Harriet Tubman (Recorded by Sarah H. Bradford) · 1869

Harriet Tubman escaped from Maryland to Philadelphia in 1849, at about twenty-seven. She hoped to learn to read and write and set down her own memoirs, and never did. She authorised Sarah Hopkins Bradford to put her recollections together with newspaper accounts and letters from friends, and the 132-page book came out in 1869. It earned Tubman about $1,200. Paying off the mortgage on her farm in Auburn, New York, in May 1873 used up her savings.
