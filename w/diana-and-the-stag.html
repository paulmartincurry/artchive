Diana and the Stag

Joachim Friess · c. 1620 · Partially gilded silver, enamel, jewels (case); iron, wood (movement) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Joachim Friess, who became a master in 1610, made this automaton around 1620. The case is partly gilded silver with enamel and jewels, and it hides a movement of iron and wood. Without the mechanism it weighs 2,250 grams and stands 37.5 centimetres high. It came to the museum in 1917 as a gift of J. Pierpont Morgan.
