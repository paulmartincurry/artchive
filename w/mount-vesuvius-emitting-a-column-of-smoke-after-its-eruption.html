Mount Vesuvius Emitting a Column of Smoke after Its Eruption on 8 August 1779 (Campi Phlegraei)

Pietro Fabris · 1779 · Etching with gouache · 38.9 × 21 cm (platemark) · Wellcome Collection, London

Free to use

This is the following day: the view from Posillipo on 9 August 1779, with smoke standing over Vesuvius following the eruption of the night before. Little is recorded of Pietro Fabris's life. He is suspected to have been born in England, the son of a stage designer trained in Venice, and he showed paintings and drawings in London in 1768 and 1772.
