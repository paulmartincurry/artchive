Fallingwater

Frank Lloyd Wright · 1936–37 · Local stone, reinforced concrete, steel and plate glass

Free to use with credit. Photo: lachrimae72 (Pixabay)

Frank Lloyd Wright was in his late sixties when Edgar J. Kaufmann, owner of a Pittsburgh department store, hired him in 1934 to design a weekend house over the falls on Bear Run. Kaufmann's own engineers said the cantilevered terraces were unsound; Wright overruled them, and the contractor secretly added extra steel. The house cost $155,000, including Wright's fee of $8,000. The terraces sagged, and in March 2002 the lowest one was post-tensioned to hold it.
