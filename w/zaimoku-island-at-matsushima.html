Zaimoku Island at Matsushima

Kawase Hasui · 1920 · Woodblock print in colored ink on paper · 39.3 × 26.5 cm · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The Cooper Hewitt dates this print to 8 May 1920. Hasui's first three prints for the publisher Shōzaburō Watanabe had come out in August 1918. He had turned to art after the family business, a rope and thread wholesaler, went bankrupt when he was twenty-six.
