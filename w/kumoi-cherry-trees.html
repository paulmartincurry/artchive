Kumoi Cherry Trees (Kumoi-zakura)

Yoshida Hiroshi · 1926 · Color woodblock print · 29.4 × 45.1 cm · Toledo Museum of Art

Free to use

Yoshida published 41 prints in 1926, more than in any other year of his life, and this was one of them. He had made his first woodcut only six years before, at the age of 44, after a career as an oil and watercolour painter. The Toledo Museum of Art, which owns this impression, later held two shin-hanga exhibitions that he helped organise, in 1930 and 1936.
