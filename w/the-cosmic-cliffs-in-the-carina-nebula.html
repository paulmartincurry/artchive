The Cosmic Cliffs in the Carina Nebula

James Webb Space Telescope, NASA, ESA, CSA and STScI · released 12 July 2022 · Space telescope image (near-infrared)

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI

The Carina Nebula was one of five subjects chosen for the James Webb Space Telescope's first official science images, released on 12 July 2022. The picture was taken in infrared light by the telescope's Near-Infrared Camera, built by a team at the University of Arizona and Lockheed Martin. The cliffs are the edge of a cavity in the star-forming region NGC 3324, about 7,600 light-years away, which James Dunlop first catalogued in 1826.
