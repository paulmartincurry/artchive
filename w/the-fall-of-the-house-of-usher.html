The Fall of the House of Usher (ending)

Edgar Allan Poe · 1839

Poe first published the story in Burton's Gentleman's Magazine in September 1839 and revised it slightly the next year for Tales of the Grotesque and Arabesque. It contains his poem The Haunted Palace, printed on its own that April in the Baltimore Museum. The Ushers may take their name from Mr. and Mrs. Luke Usher, actors who worked with Poe's mother and cared for her three children during her illness and death.
