Wild nights! Wild nights!
Were I with thee,
Wild nights should be
Our luxury!

Wild Nights! Wild Nights!

Emily Dickinson · published 1891

This poem was first printed five years after Dickinson's death. Her sister Lavinia had found the poems in her desk, and the two editors of the early selections, Thomas Wentworth Higginson and Mabel Loomis Todd, altered them heavily to suit the poetic conventions of the day. A complete collection closer to what she wrote did not appear until 1955.
