Her lips were red, her looks were free,
Her locks were yellow as gold:
Her skin was as white as leprosy,
The Night-Mare LIFE-IN-DEATH was she,
Who thicks man's blood with cold.

Life-in-Death (The Rime of the Ancient Mariner, Part III)

Samuel Taylor Coleridge · 1798

Coleridge wrote the poem in 1797 and 1798, and it was published in 1798 in the first edition of Lyrical Ballads, under the title The Rime of the Ancyent Marinere. Critics objected to its archaic spelling, and for the second edition, in 1800, he replaced many of the old words. In 1815 and 1816 he added marginal glosses in the same archaic style, and a revised version appeared in Sibylline Leaves in 1817.
