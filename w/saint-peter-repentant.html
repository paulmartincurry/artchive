Saint Peter Repentant

Georges de La Tour · 1645 · Oil on canvas · 114 × 95 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Georges de La Tour signed and dated this canvas 1645 at upper right, at the age of about fifty-two; he and his family died in an epidemic in Lunéville seven years later. The picture may once have belonged to Alleyn's College of God's Gift at Dulwich, and the heirs of the Reverend William Lucas Chafy kept it in Bath from 1878 until 1951.
