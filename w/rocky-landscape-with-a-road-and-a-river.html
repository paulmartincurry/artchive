Rocky Landscape with a Road and a River

Hercules Seghers · c. 1622–c. 1625 · Line etching printed in black on prepared paper, coloured with paint · 236 × 283 mm · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

Free to use

Hercules Segers printed the etching in black on paper he had prepared with a pinkish-white, lead-based ground, then coloured it with paint in white, greenish-blue, greenish-yellow and brown. The plate shows spots where the etching ground failed. The sheet came to the Rijksmuseum from the Koninklijke Bibliotheek in The Hague in 1816.
