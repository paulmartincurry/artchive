Now I am alone—oh, how alone! The stars may behold my tears, and the wind drink my sighs, but my thoughts are a sealed treasure which I can confide to none.

Journal, 2 October 1822

Mary Shelley · October 2, 1822

Percy Shelley drowned in 1822 when his sailing boat sank during a storm near Viareggio. He had set out on 8 July, and ten days after the storm three bodies washed up on the coast. Mary Shelley dated this entry 2 October. After his death she lived for a year with Leigh Hunt and his family in Genoa.
