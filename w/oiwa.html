Oiwa (One Hundred Ghost Tales)

Katsushika Hokusai · 1831–32 · Color woodblock print; chūban · 25.6 × 16.1 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Hokusai seems to have planned a hundred prints for this series of ghost tales in 1831 and 1832, when he was in his early seventies, but only five are known. The blue came from Berlin blue, a pigment that had become affordable shortly before. Oiwa is the murdered wife in Yotsuya Kaidan, a kabuki play by Tsuruya Nanboku IV first staged in 1825. The lantern's inscription asks for prayers for her soul.
