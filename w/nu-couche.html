Nu couché

Amedeo Modigliani · 1917 · Oil on canvas · Private collection

Free to use

Modigliani painted a series of nudes in 1917 with the backing of his Polish dealer, Léopold Zborowski, and this canvas, 60 by 92 centimetres, was among them. It is believed to have hung in the only exhibition he ever had, at the Galerie Berthe Weill that year, which the police shut down. On 9 November 2015 it sold at Christie's in New York for $170,405,000 to the Chinese businessman Liu Yiqian, who paid with his American Express card.
