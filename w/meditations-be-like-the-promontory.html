Be like the promontory against which the waves continually break, but it stands firm and tames the fury of the water around it.

Meditations — Be Like the Promontory (Book IV, 49)

Marcus Aurelius · c. 170–180

Translated by George Long

It is unlikely that Marcus Aurelius ever meant these writings to be published. The work has no official title; Meditations is one of several that have been given to it. It is possible that large portions were written at Sirmium, in modern Serbia, where he spent much time planning military campaigns.
