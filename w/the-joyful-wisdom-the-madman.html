God is dead! God remains dead! And we have killed him! How shall we console ourselves, the most murderous of all murderers?

The Joyful Wisdom — The Madman (125)

Friedrich Nietzsche · 1882

Translated by Thomas Common

Nietzsche was thirty-seven when this book came out. He had resigned his professorship at Basel three years earlier because of his health, and was living as an independent writer, moving between Switzerland, Italy and southern France. His father had been a Lutheran pastor. Nietzsche himself began university studying theology and gave it up after one semester.
