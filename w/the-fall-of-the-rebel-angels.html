The Fall of the Rebel Angels

Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1562 · Oil on oak panel · Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium

Free to use

No signature could be found on this panel, and the Brussels museum first took it in as a work by Hieronymus Bosch. In 1900 the name Pieter Bruegel was found under the frame, and it was then assigned to Pieter the Younger until a date found on the painting settled it as the work of Pieter the Elder. Like Bosch, Bruegel sketched first with a brush, then laid on a thin layer of pigment and built up further layers. The trumpeting angels come from a drawing of the Last Judgement he made in 1558.
