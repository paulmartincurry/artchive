Silence is the general consecration of the universe. Silence is the invisible laying on of the Divine Pontiff's hands upon the world. Silence is at once the most harmless and the most awful thing in all nature. It speaks of the Reserved Forces of Fate. Silence is the only Voice of our God.

Pierre; or, The Ambiguities — Silence (Book XIV)

Herman Melville · 1852

Melville offered the unfinished novel to his London publisher, Richard Bentley, in February 1852. Bentley would pay no advance, citing the poor sales of the earlier books, and Melville asked for £100. He also suggested it might come out anonymously or under an assumed name. It was published in July 1852 under his own name, and one review in the New York Day Book ran under the heading Herman Melville Crazy.
