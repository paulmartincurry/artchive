A Titanic Upheaval (Shackleton's Expedition to the Antarctic)

Frank Hurley · 1915, copyrighted c. 1916 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Endurance was built to resist being crushed by the floes until the ice cracked and relieved the pressure. On 1 August 1915 a pressure wave lifted the 400-ton ship bodily and heeled her sharply to port. She sank in the Weddell Sea on 21 November 1915, and her wreck was found on 5 March 2022.
