The Body of the Dead Christ in the Tomb

Hans Holbein the Younger · 1520–1522 · Oil and tempera on limewood · 30.5 × 200 cm · Kunstmuseum Basel

Free to use

The panel is 30.5 centimetres high and two metres long. It was painted for Bonifacius Amerbach and listed in his collection, the Amerbach Cabinet, as a picture of a dead man by H. Holbein. Holbein's father had taken him to see Grünewald's altarpiece at Isenheim. In 1867 Fyodor Dostoevsky stood before this painting until his wife pulled him away, fearing it would bring on an epileptic seizure.
