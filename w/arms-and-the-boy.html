Let the boy try along this bayonet-blade
How cold steel is, and keen with hunger of blood;
Blue with all malice, like a madman's flash;
And thinly drawn with famishing for flesh.

Arms and the Boy

Wilfred Owen · 1920

Owen enlisted in the Artists Rifles on 21 October 1915 and trained for seven months at Hare Hall Camp in Essex. On 4 June 1916 he was commissioned as a second lieutenant in the Manchester Regiment. In France he fell into a shell hole and suffered concussion, and the blast of a trench mortar shell left him unconscious for several days.
