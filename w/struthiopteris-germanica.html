Struthiopteris germanica (Urformen der Kunst, plate 46)

Karl Blossfeldt · 1928 · Photogravure · 26 × 19 cm (10 1/4 × 7 1/2 in.) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Blossfeldt built his own camera lenses, which let him magnify plants up to thirty times. He set his specimens against plain grey or white backgrounds so that their shapes stood out clearly.
