Their walls have crumbled,
Their places are gone,
As though they had never been!

Harper's Song from the Tomb of King Intef

Middle Kingdom Egyptian Blind Harper Tradition · ca. 1950 BCE

Translated by Miriam Lichtheim · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The song survives in two later copies: in the tomb of Paatenemheb, from the reign of Akhenaten, and on a New Kingdom papyrus known as Harris 500. Its opening line says it was copied from the tomb of a King Intef, a name borne by several kings of the 11th and 17th dynasties. Scholars accept it as a copy of a real Middle Kingdom text. Such songs were written beside drawings of blind harpists, which is why they are thought to have been sung.
