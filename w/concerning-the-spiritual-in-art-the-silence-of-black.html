Black is something burnt out, like the ashes of a funeral pyre, something motionless like a corpse. The silence of black is the silence of death.

Concerning the Spiritual in Art: The Silence of Black

Wassily Kandinsky · 1911

Translated by M. T. H. Sadler, 1914

Before leaving Moscow for Munich in 1896, Kandinsky saw an exhibition of paintings by Monet and could not tell that one of them showed a haystack until he read the catalogue. In 1908 he bought a copy of Thought-Forms by Annie Besant and Charles Webster Leadbeater, and in 1909 he joined the Theosophical Society. He was living in the Bavarian town of Murnau by then.
