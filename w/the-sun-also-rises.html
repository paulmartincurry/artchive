The Sun Also Rises (ending)

Ernest Hemingway · 1926

Hemingway began the novel on his twenty-sixth birthday, 21 July 1925, a few days after the Pamplona fiesta it describes, and finished the draft two months later. On F. Scott Fitzgerald's advice to trim the opening, he wired the publisher to cut the first 30 pages altogether. The first edition was 5,090 copies at two dollars each. He gave the royalties to his wife Hadley when she asked for a divorce after the book came out.
