Kūkāʻilimoku (Feathered Image of Kū)

Maker unknown · 18th century · Feathers · Ethnographic Collection, University of Göttingen

Free to use with credit. Photo: Lebrac, CC BY-SA 3.0

This eighteenth-century feather image of Kū, the Hawaiian god of war, belongs to the Cook/Forster collection of the University of Göttingen's Institute of Ethnology. Kū was worshipped under many names, among them Kūkāʻilimoku, the Snatcher of Land. Rituals for Kūkāʻilimoku included human sacrifice, which was not part of the worship of other gods.
