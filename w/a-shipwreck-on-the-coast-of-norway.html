A Shipwreck on the Coast of Norway

Johan Christian Dahl · 1831–32 · Oil on canvas · 173.4 × 206 cm · SMK – Copenhagen

Free to use

Dahl signed and dated this canvas twice, in 1831 and again in 1832. It is more than two metres wide. The museum records its acquisition in 1832, and the painting was shown at Charlottenborg in 1833 under the plain title A Shipwreck. It was formerly called A Shipwreck on the Coast near Bergen; Dahl's father was a fisherman in that town.
