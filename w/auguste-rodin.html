Auguste Rodin

Gertrude Käsebier · c. 1906 · Platinum print on Japanese paper · 33 × 24.5 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Gertrude Käsebier was able to photograph the reclusive Auguste Rodin through connections provided by Edward Steichen. The Library of Congress also holds her original negative for this portrait. In 1912, after several years of protest at its practices, she became the first member to resign from the Photo-Secession.
