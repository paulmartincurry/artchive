You took my heart in your hand
With a friendly smile,
With a critical eye you scanned,
Then set it down,
And said: It is still unripe,
Better wait awhile;

Twice

Christina Rossetti · 1866

Dante Gabriel Rossetti made woodcut illustrations for his sister's first collection, and in 1861 he sent Goblin Market to the art critic John Ruskin, who was severely critical of its irregular measure. After publication it was lauded by Hopkins, Swinburne and Tennyson. This poem is from her next collection, The Prince's Progress and Other Poems, first published in 1866.
