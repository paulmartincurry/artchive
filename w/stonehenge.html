Stonehenge

Maker unknown · c. 3100–1600 BCE; sarsen circle c. 2600–2400 BCE · Sarsen and bluestone

Free to use with credit. Photo: garethwiscombe, CC BY 2.0

Stonehenge was built in phases over some 1,500 years, from a circular bank and ditch dug around 3100 BC; the great circle of sarsens went up between 2600 and 2400 BC. Each outer sarsen weighs around 25 tons, and the lintels are held on with mortise and tenon joints, as in carpentry. The smaller bluestones are generally accepted to have come from the Preseli Hills in Wales, 150 miles away. The cremated remains of 63 people were found buried at the site.
