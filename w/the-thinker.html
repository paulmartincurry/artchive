The Thinker

Auguste Rodin · modelled ca. 1881; first cast 1904 · Bronze · Kunsthalle Bielefeld (the cast shown)

Free to use

The figure began as part of The Gates of Hell, a doorway commissioned from Auguste Rodin in 1880, where it sat over the door and was called The Poet. Foundry workers gave it its present name. The model was Jean Baud, a French prizefighter and wrestler. The first small plaster dates from around 1881 and the original is 72 centimetres high; it was enlarged in 1902 to 181 centimetres. A public subscription paid for a bronze cast of the large version, which stood in front of the Panthéon until 1922.
