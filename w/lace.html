Lace

William Henry Fox Talbot · 1844/45 · Salted paper print · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

No camera was needed for the negative. It was probably made by laying a piece of lace on sensitised paper and leaving it in the sun. Talbot hoped photographs of lace would make intricate patterns easier to copy in manufacturing. In 1844 he helped set up a works in Reading to turn out salted paper prints in quantity.
