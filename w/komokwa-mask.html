Komokwa Mask

Maker unknown · 1880–90 · Wood, pigment · 38.1 × 30.5 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Komokwa is the god of the undersea world revered by the Kwakwakaʼwakw, called Copper-Maker, master of the seals, whose house under the sea is held up by living sea lions. His masks often give him fish eyes, gills at the corners of the mouth and fins around the head. This one, of painted wood, was made in the 1880s and is 38.1 centimetres tall; it came to the Met in 2011 with the Ralph T. Coe Collection.
