Wind God and Thunder God

Tawaraya Sōtatsu · ca. 1620s · Pair of two-fold screens; ink and color on gold-foiled paper · Kyoto National Museum

Free to use

Neither screen carries an inscription or a seal, yet the attribution to Tawaraya Sōtatsu is not questioned. He kept a shop in Kyoto, the Tawaraya, that sold decorated fans, lantern paper, screens, dolls and kimono patterns. Two later painters, Ogata Kōrin and Sakai Hōitsu, each made their own copy of the pair. The screens belong to the Zen temple Kennin-ji in Kyoto.
