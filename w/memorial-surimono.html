Memorial Surimono

Shibata Zeshin · 1883 · Color woodblock print; surimono · 58.2 × 44.5 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

This print marked the seventh anniversary of the death of Sasaki Chikuju, a little-known haiku poet from Osaka, and 202 people wrote commemorative poems for it. Copies were given to those at the memorial service. Shibata Zeshin, then seventy-five, was known above all for lacquer, which he began to learn at eleven as apprentice to the lacquerer Koma Kansai II.
