Two Plant Specimens

William Henry Fox Talbot · 1839 · Photogenic drawing · 22.1 × 18 cm (8 3/4 × 7 1/8 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Talbot made it by laying the plants on sensitised paper and leaving them in the light, a photogenic drawing. The idea came to him after his honeymoon at Lake Como in 1833, when he could not draw the views to his satisfaction with a camera lucida.
