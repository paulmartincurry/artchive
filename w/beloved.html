Beloved

Toni Morrison · 1987

Short quotation from a work still in copyright, credited to the author. Not public domain; kept to one line on purpose.

Toni Morrison came across the story behind this novel in 1974, while working as an editor at Random House on The Black Book, a collection of texts on Black history. It was an 1856 newspaper article about Margaret Garner, who escaped slavery in Kentucky that January and, when slave catchers found her family in Cincinnati, killed her two-year-old daughter. Knopf published the novel in September 1987, when Morrison was fifty-six. It won the Pulitzer Prize for Fiction the following year.
