Madame X (Virginie Amélie Avegno Gautreau)

John Singer Sargent · 1883–84 · Oil on canvas · 82 1/8 × 43 1/4 in. (208.6 × 109.9 cm) · The Met

Free to use

Nobody commissioned this portrait. Sargent asked to paint Virginie Gautreau, and in February 1883 she agreed, after turning down many other painters. He made about thirty preparatory drawings at her estate in Brittany. At the Paris Salon of 1884, where it hung as Portrait de Mme ***, one strap of her dress had slipped off her shoulder; after the outcry he repainted it in place. Sargent was twenty-eight, and within a year he had left Paris for England.
