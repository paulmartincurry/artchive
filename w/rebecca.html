Rebecca

Frank Eugene · 1901, printed 1910 · Photogravure · 13 × 8.8 cm (image) · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Eugene made the negative in 1901, and this gravure was printed in 1910. That year 27 of his photographs hung in a large exhibition in Buffalo, whose catalogue called him the first photographer to make successful platinum prints on Japan tissue. Camera Work published ten of his gravures in April 1910 and fourteen more that July.
