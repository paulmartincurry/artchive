The Open Door

William Henry Fox Talbot · before May 1844 · Salted paper print from paper negative · 14.3 × 19.4 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The Open Door is Plate VI of The Pencil of Nature, the first commercially published book to be illustrated with photographs. Longman issued it in six parts between 1844 and 1846, with 24 calotype prints each pasted in by hand. William Henry Fox Talbot had planned many more instalments, but the book did not sell and he ended it after six.
