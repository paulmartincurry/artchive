Design for a Silver Platter

Caspar-Gottlieb Eisler · ca. 1750 · Engraving on white laid paper · Open: 12.3 × 38 × 50.7 cm · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, New York

Free to use

The names of Caspar-Gottlieb Eisler and Martin Engelbrecht are both engraved in the plate. The design is for an asymmetrical silver serving dish with two twisting handles, ornamented with root vegetables. The plate is numbered 3 and 312. It was bought for the museum by its Advisory Council.
