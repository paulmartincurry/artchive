The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh · June 1889 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Van Gogh admitted himself to the asylum at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence on 8 May 1889 and painted this about six weeks later, by around 18 June. The view is the one from his bedroom window, facing east, which he painted no fewer than twenty-one times. The hospital staff did not allow him to paint in the bedroom, where he made sketches in ink or charcoal. The village is an addition based on sketches. In letters to his brother Theo he called the painting a failure.
