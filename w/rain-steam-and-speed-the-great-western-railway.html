Rain, Steam and Speed – The Great Western Railway

J. M. W. Turner · 1844 · Oil on canvas · National Gallery

Free to use

The bridge is Isambard Kingdom Brunel's Maidenhead Railway Bridge over the Thames, which had opened in 1839, and the locomotive is of the Firefly Class, in service from 1840. J. M. W. Turner was in his late sixties when he showed the picture at the Royal Academy in 1844. He put on thick paint with stiff bristle brushes and the blade of a palette knife. Technical examination shows that the hare running ahead of the train was added late, over the finished paint of the track.
