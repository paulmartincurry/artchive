Edward Steichen · 1903 · Photogravure · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Rodin sits in profile in front of his sculpture of Victor Hugo. The gravure opened Camera Work number 2, published in April 1903. Steichen was twenty-four that spring, and he married Clara E. Smith the same year.
