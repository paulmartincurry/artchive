The Bewitched Mill

Franz Marc · 1913 · Oil on canvas · Without frame: 130.2 × 90.8 cm (51 5/16 × 35 3/4 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Marc painted this after a stay in the Italian town of Merano, in the South Tirol. He sold it himself in 1914 to Arthur Jerome Eddy of Chicago, whose widow and son gave it to the Art Institute in 1931. It is signed with a single M in the lower left corner. In 1936 and 1937 the Nazis ordered about 130 of Marc's works removed from German museums.
