Moresque Ornament from the Alhambra No. 5: Mosaics (The Grammar of Ornament)

Owen Jones · 1856 · Lithograph · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Owen Jones spent six months at the Alhambra in Granada with the architect Jules Goury, who died of cholera there at thirty-one. Colour printing in England could not reproduce what they had drawn, so Jones worked with chemists and printers on chromolithography and issued his Alhambra studies in twelve parts between 1836 and 1845. This plate comes from the later sourcebook, published in 1856.
