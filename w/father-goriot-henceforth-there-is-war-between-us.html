“Henceforth there is war between us.”

Father Goriot — Henceforth there is war between us (close)

Honoré de Balzac · 1835

Translated by Ellen Marriage

In the summer of 1834 Balzac began work on a tragic story about a father rejected by his daughters. The novel was first published as a serial during the winter of 1834–1835. Two stage productions followed in 1835, several months after the book.
