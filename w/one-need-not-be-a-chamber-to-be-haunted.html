One need not be a chamber to be haunted,
One need not be a house;
The brain has corridors surpassing
Material place.

One need not be a Chamber to be Haunted

Emily Dickinson · 1891

When Higginson urged Dickinson to come to Boston in 1868 so that they could meet, she declined, writing that she did not cross her father's ground to any house or town. Only 10 of her nearly 1,800 poems were published during her lifetime. Poems: Second Series followed the first selection in 1891 and ran to five editions by 1893.
