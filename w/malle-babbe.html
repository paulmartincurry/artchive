Malle Babbe

Frans Hals · c. 1633–1635 · Oil on canvas · Gemäldegalerie, Berlin

Free to use

For a long time the picture was mislabelled through a misreading of the inscription on the back of its frame. It is now often identified as a portrait of a particular woman of Haarlem, known as Malle Babbe, who may have been an alcoholic or suffered from a mental illness. Gustave Courbet made a copy of it in 1869 while it was on view in Munich.
