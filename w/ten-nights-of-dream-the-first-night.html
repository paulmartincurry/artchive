“Wait for me a hundred years. For a hundred years, sit waiting at my graveside. For, without fail, I'll come again to see you.”

Ten Nights of Dream — The First Night

Natsume Sōseki · 1908

Translated by Aiko Itō and Graeme Wilson · Short quotation from a translation still in copyright, credited to the translator. Not public domain; kept to a line or two on purpose.

The ten dreams ran in the Asahi Shimbun newspaper from 25 July to 5 August 1908. Natsume Sōseki was forty-one and had resigned his lectureship in English literature at Tokyo Imperial University the year before to write full time for the paper. Four of the ten pieces open with the same sentence.
