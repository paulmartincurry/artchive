Astronomicum Caesareum

Michael Ostendorfer · May 1540 · Hand-colored woodcuts · 17 7/8 × 12 11/16 × 1 5/16 in. (45.4 × 32.3 × 3.3 cm) · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The astronomer Petrus Apianus printed this book at his own press in Ingolstadt for the emperor Charles V and his brother Ferdinand, and it took eight years to produce. Twenty-one of its 36 woodcuts are volvelles, built on an Earth-centred model of the heavens, which could still be used to predict the movements of the planets. Copernicus published his Sun-centred system shortly afterwards. A study in 1997 counted 111 surviving copies; Tycho Brahe bought one in 1599.
