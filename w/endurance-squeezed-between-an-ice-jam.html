Endurance Squeezed between an Ice Jam

Frank Hurley · 1915, copyrighted c. 1916 · Photographic print · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Most of Hurley's equipment had to be abandoned with the loss of Endurance in 1915. He saved 120 of his glass-plate negatives and smashed about 400 others, then kept only a Vest Pocket Kodak and three rolls of film, with which he shot just 38 more images. This print, copyrighted around 1916, is linked in the Library of Congress record with Underwood & Underwood.
