Terracotta Kylix (Red-Figure Drinking Cup)

Euphronios · ca. 490 BCE · Terracotta · H. with handle 4 15/16 in. (12.6 cm) · The Met

Free to use

The museum records this cup under three accession numbers: a purchase in 1912, an anonymous gift in 1971 and a gift from E. D. Blake Vermeule in 1972. It stands 12.6 centimetres high with its handle. Euphronios was born in Athens around 535 BCE and was both a vase painter and a potter. The discovery of his signature in 1838 was the first sign that individual Greek vase painters could be named.
