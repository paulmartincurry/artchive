I, John Brown, am quite certain that the crimes of this guilty land will never be purged away but with blood. I had, as I now think vainly, flattered myself that without very much bloodshed it might be done.

I, John Brown, am quite certain (2 December 1859)

John Brown · December 2, 1859

Brown was well-read and believed that the last words of prominent people were valued. On the morning of his execution, December 2, 1859, he wrote these words and gave them to his jailer, Avis. He rode to the gallows sitting on his coffin in a furniture wagon, through a crowd of 2,000 soldiers.
