The Monkey Painter

Pierre-Louis de Surugue, after Jean-Baptiste-Siméon Chardin · 1743 · Etching with engraving in black on ivory laid paper · Image: 27.7 × 22.8 cm (10 15/16 × 9 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

The print copies Chardin's popular painting shown at the 1740 Salon. Its caption calls the monkey an exact imitator or parodist, and says some men paint the one who serves as model to the other.
