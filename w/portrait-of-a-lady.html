Portrait of a Lady

Rogier van der Weyden · c. 1460 · Oil on panel · 34 × 25.5 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

This is the only known portrait of a woman accepted as Rogier van der Weyden's own work, and the sitter's name is not recorded. She is probably in her late teens or early twenties. The panel probably belonged to the princes of Anhalt at Wörlitz, near Dessau, and Andrew Mellon bought it in December 1926.
