Canyon, Broadway and Exchange Place

Berenice Abbott · 16 July 1936 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

This is an 8 by 10 inch contact print, made on 16 July 1936 for the Federal Art Project, which sponsored Berenice Abbott's Changing New York. Abbott first showed the work in 1937 at the Museum of the City of New York, where her 305 photographs for the project were later deposited. The book came out in 1939 with captions by Elizabeth McCausland.
