Codex Manesse: Walther von Klingen (folio 52r)

Master of the Codex Manesse · c. 1305–1315 · Illuminated manuscript on parchment · Universitätsbibliothek Heidelberg

Free to use

The codex was produced in Zürich for the Manesse family. Its 137 miniatures form a series of portraits, one for each poet whose songs it gathers.
