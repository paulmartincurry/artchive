Judith and the Head of Holofernes

Gustav Klimt · 1901 · Oil and gold leaf on canvas · Belvedere, Vienna

Free to use

Critics took the woman for Salome, so Klimt had his brother Georg make a metal frame with Judith and Holofernes engraved on it. The canvas is 84 by 42 centimetres. Klimt was thirty-eight or thirty-nine, in the middle of the dispute over his ceiling paintings for the University of Vienna, and he painted the subject a second time in 1909. Both brothers were sons of a gold engraver.
