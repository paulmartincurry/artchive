Et de longs corbillards, sans tambours ni musique,
Défilent lentement dans mon âme; l'Espoir,
Vaincu, pleure, et l'Angoisse atroce, despotique,
Sur mon crâne incliné plante son drapeau noir.

Spleen (Quand le ciel bas et lourd)

Charles Baudelaire · 1857

Baudelaire was a slow and very attentive worker, often sidetracked by illness and distress, and Les Fleurs du mal did not come out until 1857. In the early 1850s he struggled with poor health and debts and moved from one lodging to another to escape his creditors. In those years he also finished translations of stories by Edgar Allan Poe.
