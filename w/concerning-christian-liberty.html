The Artchive
A Christian man is the most free lord of all, and subject to none; a Christian man is the most dutiful servant of all, and subject to every one.

Concerning Christian Liberty

Martin Luther · 1520

Translated by R. S. Grignon, 1883

In 1520 Luther held the chair of theology at the University of Wittenberg, and he turned thirty-seven that November. It was the year Pope Leo X demanded that he renounce all of his writings. He refused, and he was excommunicated in January 1521.
