Blue Morning

George Bellows · 1909 · Oil on canvas · 86.3 × 111.7 cm · National Gallery of Art, Washington

Free to use

Bellows kept this painting, and it passed at his death to his wife, Emma. Chester Dale bought it in June 1956 and left it to the National Gallery of Art in 1963.
