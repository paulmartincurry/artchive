Seventh Avenue Looking South from 35th Street

Berenice Abbott · 5 December 1935 · Gelatin silver print · 20.3 × 25.4 cm · The New York Public Library

Free to use

Abbott made this view on 5 December 1935, the year the Federal Art Project hired her to supervise Changing New York. The job paid for assistants in the field and in the office, which left her free to photograph, print and exhibit. The library's print is an 8 by 10 inch contact print.
