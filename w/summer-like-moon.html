Summer-Like Moon (One Hundred Aspects of the Moon)

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi · 1885–92 · Woodcut, color · 32.3 × 19 cm · Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Washington

Free to use

Akashi Gidayu, a retainer of Akechi Mitsuhide, writes his death poem before committing seppuku, with a tiger painted on the screen beside him. Yoshitoshi himself died in a rented room on 9 June 1892, three weeks after leaving a mental hospital. He was fifty-three.
