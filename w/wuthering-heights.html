Be with me always—take any form—drive me mad!
only do not leave me in this abyss, where I cannot find you!

Wuthering Heights (Ch. 16)

Emily Brontë · 1847

The publisher Thomas Newby accepted this novel together with Anne Brontë's Agnes Grey and issued them in 1847 as a three-volume set, with Wuthering Heights filling the first two volumes and Agnes Grey the third. It carried the pen name Ellis Bell. Newby had taken both books before their sister Charlotte's Jane Eyre had its success, but Jane Eyre came out first.
