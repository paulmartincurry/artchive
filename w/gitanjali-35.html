The Artchive
Where the mind is without fear and the head is held high;
Where knowledge is free.

Gitanjali 35

Rabindranath Tagore · 1910 (trans. 1912)

Rabindranath Tagore was forty-nine in 1910 and living at Santiniketan, where he had moved in 1901 to found an ashram with an experimental school, gardens and a library. His wife had died the year after the move. In 1913 he became the first non-European to win a Nobel Prize in any category.
