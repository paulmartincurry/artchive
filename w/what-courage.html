What Courage! (The Disasters of War, plate 7)

Francisco de Goya · 1810 (published 1863) · Etching, aquatint, drypoint, burin · Plate: 15.3 × 20.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

The woman at the gun is Agustina de Aragón. During the first siege of Zaragoza, after the gunners defending the city were killed, she loaded and fired a cannon into the French forces herself. Goya could not have seen it, and this is the one plate in the series that shows a known person rather than an anonymous casualty.
