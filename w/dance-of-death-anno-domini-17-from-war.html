Dance of Death, Anno Domini 17 (Dead Man's Hill), from War

Otto Dix · 1924 · Etching, aquatint and drypoint on cream laid paper · plate 24.5 × 30 cm · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Alongside the portfolios, Nierendorf published a cheap book of the war prints with an introduction by Henri Barbusse, in an edition of 10,000 at 1.20 Marks. Nierendorf worked with a pacifist to circulate the prints across Germany, and they were an immediate success. In 1937 a complete set from the Kupferstichkabinett Berlin hung in the Nazi Degenerate Art exhibition. A complete set of the fifty sold at Christie's in 2017 for £236,750.
