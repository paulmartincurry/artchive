The Artchive
Here sit I, forming mortals
After my image;
A race resembling me,
To suffer, to weep,
To enjoy, to be glad,
And thee to scorn,
As I!

Prometheus

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · c. 1772–1774

Translated by Edgar Alfred Bowring

Goethe wrote this poem between 1772 and 1774, drawing on a drama about Prometheus that he planned but did not complete. Friedrich Heinrich Jacobi published an anonymous and unauthorised version in 1785; the poem's first publication is dated 1789. Schubert set it to music in 1819.
