The Artchive
His eyes have all the seeming
of a demon’s that is dreaming.

The Raven

Edgar Allan Poe · 1845

Edgar Allan Poe first took the poem to his former employer George Rex Graham of Graham's Magazine in Philadelphia, who declined it and gave him 15 dollars as charity. The American Review then paid 9 dollars for it and printed it under the pseudonym Quarles. It was likely written in the summer of 1844, when Poe, his wife and his mother-in-law were boarding at a farmhouse in New York. The poem made him a national celebrity and did not bring him much money.
