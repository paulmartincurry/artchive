Christ on the Cross

El Greco · c. 1600–1610 · oil on canvas · Cleveland Museum of Art

This canvas hung in the Convent of the Salesas in Madrid from at least 1815 until 1952, the year it was sold and reached Cleveland by way of London and New York. It is 193 centimetres tall without its frame. The plaque at the top of the cross is lettered in Hebrew, Greek and Latin. El Greco had first trained on Crete as a painter of small icons.
