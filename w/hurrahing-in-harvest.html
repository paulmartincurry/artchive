Summer ends now; now, barbarous in beauty, the stooks rise
Around; up above, what wind-walks! what lovely behaviour
Of silk-sack clouds!

Hurrahing in Harvest

Gerard Manley Hopkins · 1877 (published 1918)

Hopkins dated this sonnet in the Vale of Clwyd on 1 September 1877, the month he was ordained. In a letter the following year he wrote that it was the outcome of half an hour of extreme enthusiasm as he walked home alone one day from fishing in the Elwy. He described its metre as sprung and outriding rhythm, and added a note on how outriding feet differ from dactyls.
