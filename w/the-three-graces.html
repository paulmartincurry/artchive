The Three Graces

Antonio Canova · 1814–17 · Marble · 173 × 97.2 × 57 cm · Victoria and Albert Museum, London, and National Galleries of Scotland, Edinburgh

Free to use with credit. Photo: ketrin1407, CC BY 2.0

John Russell, 6th Duke of Bedford, visited Canova's studio in Rome in 1814 and saw the Graces being carved for the Empress Josephine. When she died that May he tried to buy the group, failed, and commissioned this second version, which was installed at Woburn Abbey in 1819. It is cut from a single slab of white marble. Canova's assistants roughly blocked out the stone and left the final carving to him.
