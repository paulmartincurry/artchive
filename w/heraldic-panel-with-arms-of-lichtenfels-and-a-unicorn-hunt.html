Heraldic Panel with Arms of Lichtenfels and a Unicorn Hunt

Maker unknown · c. 1515 · White glass with silver stain and pot metal · 39.1 x 28.9 cm (15 3/8 x 11 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

The unicorn hunt above stands for the Annunciation: the virgin is Mary, and the hunter arriving with his hounds is the angel Gabriel. The red glass is laid over clear glass; on its own it would be too dark to see through.
