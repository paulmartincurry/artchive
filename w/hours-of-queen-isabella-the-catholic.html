Hours of Queen Isabella the Catholic

Master of the First Prayerbook of Maximilian · c. 1500 · ink, tempera, and gold on vellum · Codex: 23.5 × 17.3 × 4.8 cm (9 1/4 × 6 13/16 × 1 7/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

This book of hours was made in Bruges and Ghent and belonged to Isabella of Castile, the queen who paid for Christopher Columbus's voyage to the Americas. Its margins may hold one of the first daffodils painted in a manuscript. The lead illuminator is thought to be Alexander Bening. Four centuries later the book was in the hands of the Rothschilds, and a New York dealer held it for nine years before selling it to Cleveland in 1963.
