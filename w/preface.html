Preface

Rai Sanyō · 1817 · Double-leaf from a pair of folding albums; ink on silk · Each page: 25.9 x 18 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Rai Sanyō wrote this preface for a pair of albums put together to show the landscape along Lake Biwa near Hikone Castle; his father and two uncles each added a leaf. As a young man he tried to avoid training as a bureaucrat, and his father locked him in his room for three years. He finished his history of Japan, the Nihon Gaishi, in 22 volumes in 1827.
