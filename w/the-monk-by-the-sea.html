The Monk by the Sea (Der Mönch am Meer)

Caspar David Friedrich · 1808–1810 · Oil on canvas · Alte Nationalgalerie, Berlin

Free to use

Caspar David Friedrich worked on this canvas in Dresden for about two years and kept changing it right up to its exhibition. Scientific study has shown that he first painted two small sailing ships on the horizon and then took them out. The sky fills about three quarters of the picture. It was first shown at the Berlin Academy in 1810, hung at his request above The Abbey in the Oakwood, and King Frederick William III bought both paintings afterwards.
