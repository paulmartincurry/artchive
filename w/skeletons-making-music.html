Skeletons Making Music (The Dance of Death)

Hans Holbein the Younger · c. 1526, published 1538 · Woodcut · Sheet: 6.6 × 4.9 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Holbein drew the designs for this series in Basel in 1526, and the blockcutter Hans Lützelburger cut them in wood. The prints are tiny: this sheet measures 6.6 by 4.9 centimetres.
