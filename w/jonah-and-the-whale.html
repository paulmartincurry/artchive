Jonah and the Whale (Majma' al-Tavarikh of Hafiz-i Abru)

Maker unknown · c. 1425 · Opaque watercolor, silver, and gold on paper · 42.5 × 33.3 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Shah Rukh commissioned the historian Hafiz-i Abru in 1414 to assemble the world history this leaf comes from, and this copy was made in Herat about 1425. Hafiz-i Abru had entered Timur's court in the 1380s, was known as a good chess player, and died in June 1430.
