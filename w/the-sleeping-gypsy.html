The Sleeping Gypsy

Henri Rousseau · 1897 · Oil on canvas · Museum of Modern Art, New York

Free to use

Henri Rousseau showed this painting at the Salon des Indépendants in 1897, when he was in his early fifties, and then tried to sell it to the mayor of Laval, his hometown. The mayor did not buy it. It went instead to a Parisian charcoal merchant, who kept it until 1924; when it came back on the market that year there was a dispute over whether it was a forgery. The canvas is 129.5 centimetres by 200.7. Olga Guggenheim bought it in December 1939 and gave it to the Museum of Modern Art.
