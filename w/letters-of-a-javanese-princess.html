Oh, you do not know what it is to love this young, this new age with heart and soul, and yet to be bound hand and foot, chained by all the laws, customs, and conventions of one's land.

Letters of a Javanese Princess (25 May 1899)

Raden Adjeng Kartini · May 25, 1899 (Published 1911)

Translated by Agnes Louise Symmers, 1920

Kartini was twenty when she wrote this letter, in Dutch, a language few Javanese girls of her time were schooled in. From 1891 to 1895 she had been kept in the seclusion expected of a young noblewoman, and she educated herself by reading and by writing letters. She died in 1904 at twenty-five. J. H. Abendanon, one of her correspondents, collected the letters and published them in 1911, cutting references to polygamy in her family. He used some of the proceeds to found girls' schools.
