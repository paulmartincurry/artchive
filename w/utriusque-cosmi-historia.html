Utriusque Cosmi Historia

Robert Fludd & Matthäus Merian the Elder · 1617

Free to use

Robert Fludd was a London physician, forty-three when this volume came out in 1617. He had failed the College of Physicians examiners at least six times before they admitted him in 1609, and in 1618 he served as the College's Censor and helped write its Pharmacopoeia Londinensis. Much of his writing set out parallels between the cosmos and the human body, with the sun in the world playing the part of the heart in man. His number theory later brought him into a public exchange with Johannes Kepler.
