Headdress Ornament

Maker unknown · 1800s–1900s · Copper-silver alloy, gilding, kingfisher feathers, glass beads · 8.1 x 8.5 cm (3 3/16 x 3 3/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Ornaments like this, with flowers, butterflies or birds, were worn by women of the Manchu elite. The iridescent blue is real kingfisher feather, inlaid piece by piece.
