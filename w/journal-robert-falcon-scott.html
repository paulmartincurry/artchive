He slept through the night before last, hoping not to wake; but he woke in the morning—yesterday. It was blowing a blizzard. He said, 'I am just going outside and may be some time.' He went out into the blizzard and we have not seen him since.

Journal (Friday, March 16 or Saturday 17, 1912)

Robert Falcon Scott · March 1912

Lawrence Oates died on 17 March 1912, his 32nd birthday. He was suffering from gangrene and frostbite on the return from the Pole. Scott was unsure of the date of this entry but thought 17 March correct. Oates had applied to join the expedition in 1910 and was accepted mainly on the strength of his experience with horses.
