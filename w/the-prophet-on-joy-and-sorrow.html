Your joy is your sorrow unmasked.

And the selfsame well from which your laughter rises was oftentimes filled with your tears.

The Prophet — On Joy and Sorrow

Kahlil Gibran · 1923

Gibran was born in Bsharri, a village of Ottoman-ruled Mount Lebanon, and came to the United States with his mother and siblings in 1895. His mother worked as a seamstress while he went to school in Boston. His youngest sister died in 1902, and he lost his older half-brother and his mother the following year.
