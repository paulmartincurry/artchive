The Feast of Sada (Shahnama of Shah Tahmasp)

Attributed to Sultan Muhammad · c. 1525 · Opaque watercolor, ink, silver, and gold on paper · Painting 24.1 × 23 cm; page 47 × 31.8 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This is folio 22 verso of a copy of the Shahnama that held 759 pages, 258 of them paintings. The painting is attributed to Sultan Muhammad, the first director of the project, who had given painting lessons to Tahmasp when he was crown prince. Arthur A. Houghton Jr. gave the leaf to the Metropolitan Museum in 1970.
