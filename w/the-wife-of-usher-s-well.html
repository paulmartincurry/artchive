It fell about the Martinmass,
When nights are lang and mirk,
The carlin wife's three sons came hame,
And their hats were o the birk.

The Wife of Usher's Well

Anonymous Scottish Ballad · 1802

Walter Scott printed it in 1802 in his Minstrelsy of the Scottish Border, from the recitation of an old woman living near Kirkhill, in West Lothian. Cecil Sharp, collecting songs in Britain, had to go to the Appalachian Mountains to find it sung, and found 8 versions and 9 fragments there.
