Trellis

William Morris · 1864 · Block-printed in distemper colours · 68.6 × 54.6 cm · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

Trellis was the first wallpaper pattern William Morris designed for his company, after the roses he grew on the trellis at the Red House. Philip Webb drew the birds. Two years passed before Morris could print it to his satisfaction: he tried zinc plates, gave up, and had pearwood blocks cut by Barrett of Bethnal Green and the paper printed by Jeffrey & Company of Islington.
