A Monumental Portrait of a Monkey

Attributed to the Stipple Master · c. 1705–1710 · Opaque watercolor and gold on paper · Outermost border: 48.5 × 58.7 cm (19 × 23 in.) · Art Institute of Chicago

Free to use

Apart from the god Hanuman, monkeys are rarely the subject of portraits in India. An inscription on the back names this one Husaini, from Nawab Daud Khan, most likely the Mughal nobleman Daud Khan Panni.
