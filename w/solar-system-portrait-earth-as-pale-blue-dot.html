Solar System Portrait: Earth as Pale Blue Dot

Voyager 1, NASA/JPL · 14 February 1990 · Spacecraft image through violet, blue and green filters

Free to use with credit. Photo: NASA/JPL

Voyager 1 took this picture on 14 February 1990 from more than 6 billion kilometres away, as one of 60 frames for a portrait of the solar system. Carl Sagan had proposed in 1980 that the probe take one last picture of Earth. Earth fills 0.12 of a single pixel and sits by coincidence in the middle of a ray of scattered sunlight. The frame was shot through violet, blue and green filters.
