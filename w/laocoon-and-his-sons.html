Laocoön and His Sons

Agesander, Athenodoros and Polydorus of Rhodes · 1st Century BCE · Marble · Vatican Museums

Free to use

The group came out of the ground in a Roman vineyard in February 1506, and Michelangelo was called to the site at once. Pope Julius II acquired it the next month, paying the vineyard's owner with a job as a scribe and the customs revenue of one of the city gates. The priest's right arm was missing. A straight arm was added in 1532; a bent marble arm found in a builder's yard in 1905 was fitted in its place in 1957. The group is made of at least seven interlocking pieces of marble.
