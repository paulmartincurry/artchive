The School of Athens

Raphael (Raffaello Sanzio) · 1509–1511 · Fresco · Apostolic Palace, Vatican City

Pope Julius II commissioned this wall as part of the decoration of his rooms in the Apostolic Palace, and it is believed to be the third fresco Raphael finished in the first room. The title is not his, and no document from the time says who the figures are. Raphael put himself in beside Ptolemy, and Bramante is said by Vasari to have helped with the painted architecture. The cartoon, now in Milan, lacks the background and the figure of Heraclitus.
