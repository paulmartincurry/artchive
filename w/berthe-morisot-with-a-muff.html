Berthe Morisot with a Muff

Édouard Manet · c. 1871–72 · oil on canvas · Unframed: 74 × 60 cm (29 1/8 × 23 5/8 in.) · Cleveland Museum of Art

Free to use

Manet painted Berthe Morisot, herself a painter, twice in the same heavy winter coat and feathered hat; the other portrait also hangs in Cleveland. He kept this one all his life. It was listed in his studio inventory after his death, and when it came up in his estate sale at Drouot in February 1884, his widow bought it back.
