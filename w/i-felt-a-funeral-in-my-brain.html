As all the heavens were a bell,
And Being but an ear,
And I and silence some strange race,
Wrecked, solitary, here.

I felt a funeral in my brain

Emily Dickinson · ca. 1862 (published 1896)

About 1840 Dickinson's father bought a house on North Pleasant Street that overlooked Amherst's burial ground. In April 1844, when she was thirteen, her second cousin and close friend Sophia Holland died of typhus, and her parents sent her to stay with family in Boston to recover. She was thirty-one in 1862.
