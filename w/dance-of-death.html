Dance of Death (Folio CCLXIIIIv, Nuremberg Chronicle)

Michael Wolgemut & Wilhelm Pleydenwurff · 1493 · Woodcut and letterpress text · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Free to use

This page comes from Hartmann Schedel's Nuremberg Chronicle of 1493, which carried 1,809 woodcuts. Michael Wolgemut and his stepson Wilhelm Pleydenwurff were first engaged to make the illustrations in 1487 and 1488, and a contract of 1492 required the publisher, Anton Koberger, to keep the blocks in a locked room. Many blocks were printed more than once in the book with only the caption changed. The young Albrecht Dürer had served his apprenticeship in Wolgemut's workshop from 1486 to 1489. Pleydenwurff died in January 1494.
